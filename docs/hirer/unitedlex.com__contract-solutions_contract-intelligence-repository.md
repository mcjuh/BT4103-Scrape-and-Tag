Reduction in time associated with manual review ​
### Challenge
Our client, a Fortune 500 company within technology industry, had recently undergone a large acquisition. ​
The client had over 900,000 agreements from their own legacy systems and from the acquired company and was looking to centralize this data into a new CLM platform.​
Legacy data was spread across multiple sources, lacked searchable text, and was not stored in any format that would allow easy extraction of core contract provisions.​
### Solution
UnitedLex’s dedicated data science team deployed and configured Custom AI models to perform a first pass data extraction on 20 core contract provisions​
Our team of 75+ commercial attorneys and legal professionals reviewed the data to validate AI extraction results and establish parent-child hierarchies within the data set​.
### Value
Client is now able to make data driven decisions for their business and has gained real-time insights across their contract portfolio​.
