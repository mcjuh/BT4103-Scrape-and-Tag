# The Opus
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The Opus is commercial tower in the Business Bay district of Dubai, which designed by the award-winning architect Zaha Hadid. With an organic void right at the heart of the building, it is an architectural wonder made of engineering at its most impressive. Located in Dubai’s Burj Khalifa district, The Opus combines a variety of residences and a design hotel operated by the leading hotelier group ME by Melia Hotels International.
Services provided:
  * Leading and overseeing the commissioning process
  * Reviewing designs in order to optimise design solutions
  * Verifying pre-functional and functional checks of equipment
  * Witness testing of commissioned systems
  * Preparation of Systems Manuals
  * Preparation of Commissioning Reports
  * Reviewing of Commissioning results
  * Preparation of Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Manuals
  * Review of M.E.P Method statements and test documents
  * Construct and maintain the commissioning tracker documentation
  * Management of client training
  * Waste Engineering and Design
