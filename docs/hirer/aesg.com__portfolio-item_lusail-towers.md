# Lusail Towers
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
Lusail Towers is a four plot development in Lusail City, Qatar. The site lies on the coast at the eastern end of the Boulevard District and is bounded north and south by both the Waterfront and Fox Hills Districts. The project includes 2 towers with 80 floors and 2 towers with 50 floors together with 500,000 m2 low-rise buildings and 50,000 m2 retail, as well as basement parking areas to accommodate 3200 cars. Varying in height, the four towers create a distinctive and bold profile on the city’s skyline.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following:
  * Sustainability certification and management throughout design
  * Energy Modelling
  * Parametric Modelling
  * GSAS Consultancy
  * LEED Consultancy
