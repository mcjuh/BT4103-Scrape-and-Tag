# Building a future-ready LNG operation
How a global LNG producer achieved compliance and operational efficiency through integrated solutions 
## **The challenge: Balancing regulation, low-carbon goals, and capacity constraints**
A global liquefied natural gas (LNG) operator faced growing pressure to comply with evolving national regulatory permitting requirements and to respond to customer best-practice expectations for low-carbon solutions. These challenges required a partner capable of delivering integrated solutions that addressed compliance, operational efficiency, and strategic ESG planning and alignment.
Over a multi-year partnership, ERM partnered with a global LNG operator to advance its environmental and sustainability (E&S) objectives. Acting as an extension of their team, ERM combined strategic advisory with asset-level operational support to help meet regulatory requirements, deliver on low-carbon ambitions, and embed sustainability into core operations.
## **What we did: Integrated expertise for a multi-dimensional approach**
ERM deployed a program-based approach, integrating global subject matter experts across air quality, carbon management, digital systems, and organizational transformation. This provided the global LNG operator with targeted, high-impact solutions across multiple dimensions:
Emissions and decarbonization:
  * Enhanced energy efficiency and optimized operational performance through a suite of emissions and air-quality monitoring services.
  * Supported the development of digital tools including emissions monitoring dashboards.
  * Advanced GHG reduction measures and improved life cycle assessments to align with recognized industry frameworks.


Resource efficiency and circularity:
  * Improved waste and water data management systems to reduce costs and environmental impact. 
  * Supported circularity initiatives designed to minimize waste.
  * Enabled environmental teams to more effectively communicate compliance performance to internal stakeholders.


Safety and incident management:
  * Strengthened safety performance by developing field-based engagement tools and assurance applications that enabled real-time monitoring and verification.
  * Improved safety culture through more consistent data capture and process discipline. 


Risk andasset integrity:
  * Delivereddredging and remediation assessments, air dispersion modelling and climate risk assessments to de-risk operations and protect assets from value erosion.


Shareholder expectations:
  * Aligned sustainability objectives with shareholder priorities to unlock ESG-driven growththrough enhanced disclosure and strategic positioning.


## **The impact: Driving operational efficiency, compliance and environmental performance**
ERM’s integrated approach helped the global LNG operator maintain regulatory compliance, enhance environmental performance, improve operational efficiency, and advance decarbonization and ESG maturity. These outcomes aligned closely with their operational priorities:
  * **Safe and reliable operations:** Tactical asset-level support and strategic advisory to maintain operational continuity and resilience.
  * **Operational discipline:** Enhanced systems development, assurance advisory, and project management strengthened governance and compliance.
  * **Operational efficiency:** Digital systems and change management initiatives drove transformation and cost optimization.


By operating as a seamless extension of the global LNG operator’s team, ERM ensured continuity, efficiency, and strategic alignment, enabling the organization to meet stakeholder expectations and position itself for long-term success.
