Located between the Atlantic coast and the Sahara, Guelmim-Oud Noun covers 46,108 km², representing 6.49% of Morocco’s national territory. As one of the country’s largest regions by land area, it possesses significant economic and environmental assets. 
However, it faces growing pressures from severe water scarcity, increasing heat stress, uneven infrastructure provision and rapid urbanisation. Social and gender inequalities also continue to shape access to services, mobility and economic opportunities.
Building on ongoing investments in water supply and wastewater treatment infrastructure, the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD) commissioned Arup to develop a Green City Action Plan (GCAP) - the first regional scale plan within the EBRD Green Cities programme. The plan provides a comprehensive, evidence-based roadmap for sustainable infrastructure investment, policy reform and inclusive urban development.
Arup led the development of the GCAP through a highly participatory process, involving in-person workshops, online surveys and social media engagement. We undertook a detailed assessment of the region’s environmental, social and economic conditions, identifying local challenges, opportunities and planned investment programmes. 
The result comprises 29 bankable actions designed to address the region’s most pressing challenges. Supported by the identified delivery partners and quantified economic and climate benefits, the programme includes a high-impact portfolio of water investments spanning supply, distribution and wastewater treatment. 
Alongside the GCAP, we developed a dedicated Gender Action Plan to help ensure benefits are shared across all residents and user groups. Measures promoting accessibility, participation, skills development, employment opportunities and improved access to essential services were embedded throughout.
Bringing together four provinces and 53 communes, it provides a shared framework for strengthening water security, improving infrastructure resilience, creating green jobs and enhancing quality of life across diverse urban, rural, coastal, and oasis communities. 
The plan offers a practical and replicable model for regions across Morocco, North Africa and beyond, with 29 priority actions projected to support 5,900 jobs and reduce emissions by 730,000 tCO₂e annually. It shows how coordinated investment, long-term planning and inclusive governance can support a more resilient, sustainable and prosperous future.
The GCAP provides an opportunity to transform the environmental and economic challenges Guelmim-Oued Noun faces into drivers of development—strengthening water security, improving quality of life, creating opportunities for future generations, and building a more resilient, inclusive and prosperous future.
