# Unlocking $24M in Revenue Through Smarter Patient Access
  * July 03, 2025
  * Share


Following the acquisition of an industry leader in ambulatory infusion services that expanded its clinic footprint by 23%, Vivo Infusion experienced an influx of referral volumes and needed to address and prioritize a significant backlog of referrals and quickly drive value creation. FTI Consulting was engaged to unlock data-driven insights into the organization’s patient intake performance and provide project management office (“PMO”) support to align operations and scale patient access effectively across the expanded footprint.
Our Impact
  * Vivo Infusion’s expected revenue from booked appointments increased by $24 million (33%) from the first month to the last month of the engagement.
  * As part of the engagement, a new patient access operating model was implemented across 54 clinics, spanning eight different states, representing 33% of the total referral volume across the United States.
  * FTI Consulting reduced the total amount of handoffs in the referral intake process by approximately 60% as part of the update to the new patient access operating model.
  * Locations utilizing the new intake operating model experienced a $2 million (29%) increase in booked appointment expected revenue, and the enterprise experienced an $11 million (14%) increase over the same one-month duration.


### Our Role
  * FTI Consulting developed a process for rapid referral backlog reduction, refined reporting logic to more accurately measure referral completion times and eliminated time-consuming manual reporting.
  * Our experts designed a new intake operating model and consolidated the patient services process to decrease handoffs and streamline workflows, allowing for a more positive patient, referring provider and staff experience.
  * The team designed and implemented reporting to track patient intake performance using KPIs and leveraged analytics to effectively support business decisions.
  * FTI Consulting developed a comprehensive referral management dashboard containing seven new views and aligned dashboard reporting capabilities with both current operational and future-state pilot processes.
