# Accelerating IT Separation Readiness Following a $10.5B Carveout
Helping employees transition smoothly while preparing for IT separation

Following a $10.5 billion carveout, a global transportation technology company needed to transition employees into one unified ecosystem from four domains, three SSO environments, multiple collaboration platforms, and legacy systems. West Monroe partnered with them to lead IT separation readiness, TSA exit planning, and enterprise PMO coordination across global teams.
Together, we streamlined communications, strengthened governance, improved employee data quality, and aligned migration planning across the organization. Our collaborative effort reduced operational risk, improved employee readiness, and created a scalable foundation to support future growth, modernization, and long-term operational stability.

Ideas Into Reality
A $10.5B carveout left a global transportation technology company with one critical objective: unify employees across new technology platforms while keeping the business running. 
Read More 
The separation required thousands of employees to transition across multiple systems, domains, devices, and collaboration tools while maintaining business continuity. West Monroe quickly identified employee readiness, communication governance, and migration coordination as critical success factors for the broader IT separation effort. We established governance routines, mapped migration dependencies, aligned stakeholder communications, and developed readiness plans by employee wave. This early alignment created a clear roadmap for enterprise application transitions, TSA exit execution, and employee adoption while helping leadership proactively manage operational risks throughout the separation.

Tackling It Together
West Monroe partnered with leadership to keep a complex IT separation on track—aligning teams, preparing employees, and coordinating technology transitions across the organization.

West Monroe worked alongside the client's leadership team to plan and execute the IT separation while minimizing disruption to day-to-day operations. Drawing on expertise in organizational change, technology, and program leadership, we coordinated technology migrations, managed project risks, tracked key milestones, and aligned teams across the business.
We established clear decision-making processes, provided regular leadership updates, refreshed the IT intranet as a central source of information, and delivered more than 20 targeted communications through email, Slack, and other internal channels. By keeping employees informed and coordinating activities across workstreams, we helped the organization navigate each phase of the transition with greater confidence and consistency.
