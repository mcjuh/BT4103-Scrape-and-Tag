# Investment management company’s bet on legal support yields big returns
### Team of Axiom lawyers helped:
Institute standards and best practices as well as develop templates and playbooks related to NDA negotiations
Manage 100 NDAs per month across private equity and real estate teams
### Life is Good
**Picture this… you're an independent investment management firm in charge of more than $1.5T in client assets.** With specialized teams in 20+ countries around the globe working in a comprehensive range of asset types, you help your clients get more out of life.
### Big Challenge
**But, your NDA review process is a real bear.** There's no progress tracking, no standardization of outputs, slow turnaround times, and no understanding of the bottlenecks causing it. It's become a major distraction for your senior lawyers, yet past efforts to enlist outside service providers have come up short.
### Help Arrives
**So, you turn to Axiom. **We provide a remote team of high-performing lawyers with a designated lead to serve as your one point of contact for all NDA negotiations. The team quickly takes stock of the situation, instituting standards and best practices, developing templates and playbooks, and creating a tracker for workflow and frequently negotiated terms.
### Life is Even Better
**Now, you're managing 100 NDAs per month across your private equity and real estate investment teams,** with no interruptions to workflow. You have a cost-effective process that's scalable across various geographies and business units, and your in-house team can spend their valuable time on more strategic work. This is solution that will be paying dividends for years to come.
