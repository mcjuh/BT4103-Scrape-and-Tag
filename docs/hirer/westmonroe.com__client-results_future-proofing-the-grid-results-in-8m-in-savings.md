# Future-proofing the grid results in $8M in savings
Upgrading critical infrastructure to ensure long-term reliability

When telecom carriers announced plans to discontinue analog circuit support, a national investor-owned utility faced a critical challenge: modernizing its grid to maintain reliable operations and prevent outages. But upgrading wasn’t as simple as switching technologies—it required new infrastructure, adherence to engineering specifications, and powering new devices across a vast service area.
West Monroe stepped in to assess risks, secure regulatory funding, and deploy upgraded infrastructure. Over a five-year period, we helped implement a private telecom network, expand smart metering, and standardize operations. The result? An $8 million annual savings, improved network reliability, and a future-ready grid.

West Monroe helped the utility assess risk, secure funding, and deploy an upgraded private telecom network to help mitigate the risk of telecom network shutdowns.

Facing the phase-out of analog circuits, the utility needed a strategic, future-proofed solution. West Monroe began by assessing the business risks and crafting a compelling case for regulatory funding. Once funding was secured, we established a project management office to govern the modernization effort. The solution included expanding advanced metering infrastructure (AMI), upgrading the private telecom network, and implementing automation for operational efficiency. With a clear roadmap and expert execution, the utility was positioned to modernize without disrupting service—ensuring long-term reliability and efficiency.
