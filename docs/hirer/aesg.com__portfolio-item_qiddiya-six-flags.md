# Qiddiya Six Flags
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project shall be the best-themed amusement park in the GCC. A park that exceeds world-class standards in every area and provides an excellent guest experience and a project that is designed to sustainable principles. The project is intended to be an outdoor venue with minimal indoor activities. It comprises approximately 24 rides and 6 attractions. The scope includes Themed Amusement Park, inclusive of public realm and infrastructure within the plot.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following:
  * Sustainability Consultancy
  * Waste Management
  * Environmental Consultancy
  * Independent Commissioning Agent
  * Acoustics Consultancy
