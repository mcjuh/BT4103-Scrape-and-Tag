# ALVAREZ & MARSAL ADVISES ARMAH SPORTS COMPANY ON ITS TRANSFER TO THE MAIN MARKET OF THE SAUDI EXCHANGE (TADAWUL)
**Riyadh – 7th September, 2026 –** [Alvarez & Marsal (A&M)](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/), the global professional services firm known for its senior-led, operator-driven approach, has acted as Financial Due Diligence (FDD) advisor to Armah Sports Company (Armah), one of Saudi Arabia’s leading fitness and wellness operators, in connection with its transfer from the Parallel Market (Nomu) to the Main Market of the Saudi Exchange (Tadawul).
The transaction represents an important milestone in Armah’s development as a publicly listed company and marks one of A&M’s first equity markets mandates in Saudi Arabia.
A&M worked alongside Armah and its other advisors throughout the transfer process, providing FDD services in relation to the transaction and supporting key stages of the filing and regulatory review process with the relevant regulatory authorities and the exchange.
[Ali Anwar](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/ali-anwar), Managing Director and Middle East Head of Transaction Advisory, Alvarez & Marsal, said: _“We are proud to have supported Armah on this important milestone in the company’s journey. The successful transfer to the Main Market reflects Armah’s continued development and ambition, and we are pleased to have worked alongside the company and its advisors throughout the process.”_
The transaction further strengthens A&M’s track record in Saudi Arabia’s capital markets and reflects the firm’s continued expansion of its transaction advisory capabilities across the Kingdom. A&M supports corporates, sovereign wealth funds and private equity investors across the deal lifecycle, combining senior-led financial, tax, commercial, operational and technical due diligence expertise with broader M&A and capital markets experience.
