# Case Law Model Developed for Generative AI Startup
### Axiom attorneys helped to:
Train the Generative AI Model while allowing the client's legal team to focus on core business matters
Vet the AI model so that it could accurately assess regulatory compliance requirements within contracts
Save client 56% on outside support when comparing with equivalent law firm spend*
### Challenge
A startup is building a generative AI model focused on the legal profession. They are building a platform to support contract review, due diligence, and regulatory compliance. To effectively train a large language model (LLM), however, the **client needs a partner with an unprecedented mix of attributes– scale, legal experience, affordability, and diversity.**
### Approach
To help refine and strengthen its legal AI platform, the company engaged Axiom attorneys for hands-on product testing and structured feedback. In Phase 1, a 12-person team evaluated initial model questions and workflows. In Phase 2, the company expanded the program to 75 lawyers across a range of legal specialties, locations, and experience levels to test the model directly and identify opportunities for improvement. In the final phase, a continuing group of approximately 125 Axiom lawyers conducts deeper testing in specialized areas of law and regulation, helping the company fine-tune the product for its clients.
### Impact 
  * **Enables client’s legal team to focus on core business matters** by leveraging Axiom’s unique blend of size, experience, and diverse lawyer base to train the LLM
  * Highly qualified Axiom lawyers provide detailed data on specific regulations to **ensure the AI model accurately handles complex contract review and regulatory compliance requirements**.
  * 100+ total Axiom lawyers evaluating the LLM’s answers in real-time **rapidly****accelerates AI response reliability and accuracy**
