# Stabilizing a Leading Data and AI Services Firm Post-Acquisition
  * June 04, 2024
  * Share


FTI Consulting took over interim management responsibilities to manage the stabilization and transformation of a leading NASDAQ-listed digital, data and AI services firm after its acquisition by a private equity owner.
### Our Impact
  * FTI Consulting **reduced sales, general and administrative (“SG &A”)** and **operating costs by 35% and more than doubled the annualized EBIDTA percentage.**
  * Our experts improved commercials and delivery structure to drive a **more than 40% increase in average new deal gross margin** and **reduced the average days sales outstanding by 30%.**
  * We **stabilized quarter-over-quarter declining revenue** and positioned the client for **annualized growth above 12%.**


### Our Role
  * FTI Consulting experts assumed interim management roles for the client, including Chief Executive Officer, Chief Delivery Officer and transformation project management office lead, and evaluated a go-to-market strategy to identify growth opportunities, propositions and partnerships.
  * Our team performed an in-depth review of more than 25 large accounts to develop a strategic growth plan, in addition to meeting with executives of the client’s largest customers to share a new vision for the company, negotiate commercials and drive renewed business engagement.
  * We evaluated the operating model, including commercial, finance and delivery structures, to prepare a leaner, more agile and integrated sales, digital solutioning and services framework.
  * Our experts assessed the senior and extended leadership team to rebuild a high-performing management structure, develop a new incentive plan and support the transition to a permanent management team.
