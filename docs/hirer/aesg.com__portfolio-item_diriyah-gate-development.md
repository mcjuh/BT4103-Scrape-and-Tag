# Diriyah Gate Development
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
Diriyah Gate is a masterplan project that over an area of 7 sq km, with history and heritage assets at its core, enriched by entertainment, shopping, and residential elements. Diriyah Gate aspires to become: a cultural destination that will act as a catalyst for growth in both the local area and the wider region. It is an iconic project which embeds, reflects and celebrates the rich history of Diriyah and its unique architecture and heritage; a source of inspiration for Saudi citizens to reflect on the past, and be proud of their new future and place in the world.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following:
  * Certification Strategy Development for LEED and Mostadam Certification
  * Sustainability Plot Development Guidelines
  * Overall DGDA Sustainability Framework Program
