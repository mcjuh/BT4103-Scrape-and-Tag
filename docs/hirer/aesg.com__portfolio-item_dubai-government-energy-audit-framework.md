# Dubai Government Energy Audit Framework
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
As one of the first firms in the UAE to be officially recognised as an Accredited Energy Auditor by the Dubai Regulatory and Supervisory Bureau (RSB), AESG was appointed under a framework agreement to perform [energy audits](https://aesg.com/environment-sustainability/energy-audits/) for government buildings in Dubai.
The project followed the Dubai Supreme Council of Energy’s Directive number 1 of 2015, issued by His Highness Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum, to audit electricity and water consumption in government buildings in Dubai.
AESG carried out energy audits for multiple government buildings under this framework from January 2015, with the agreement renewed through to June 2018.
