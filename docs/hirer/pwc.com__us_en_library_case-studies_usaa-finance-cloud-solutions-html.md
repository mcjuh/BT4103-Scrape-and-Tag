Modernizing accounting and reporting with Oracle Cloud
# ​​​​​​​USAA _defines its new finance model_ with a cloud-powered solution
The United Services Automobile Association (USAA) is a financial services company providing multi-line insurance and banking products to members of the military, veterans and their families. Looking to update its financial system and enhance accounting and reporting, the association selected PwC for help implementing Oracle Cloud. As a result, USAA improved its systems, processes, collaboration and cost management with a trusted, connected cloud solution.

## Interview with
**Deep Raj**
VP, Application Development, USAA
###### What sparked the need for change?
“We operated off of a highly customized financial system that was more than 20 years old. This resulted in inefficient processes and data challenges. Our finance team needed more advanced technology. As we explored our options, we took the opportunity to use this tech upgrade for broader advancement and innovation across our finance operations. Our goal was to connect our systems, processes and people and automate where possible to focus on strategic areas of the business that ultimately enable us to better serve USAA Members.”
###### What solution did the teams unlock by working together?
“PwC helped us establish a multiyear transformation roadmap to migrate to an integrated [Oracle Cloud solution](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/technology/alliances/oracle-implementation.html), streamline processes and improve controls. PwC established a collaborative process across multiple functional areas to enable a successful outcome. Together, we built a new finance data model that will act as a springboard for our business’s future growth.”
######  Where did tech innovation meet human ingenuity?
“Cloud technology and people have [transformed our finance operations](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/business-transformation/finance-transformation.html), driving efficiency and delivering value. We implemented Oracle’s Financial Services Risk and Finance solutions, Oracle Fusion Cloud Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), Oracle Fusion Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) and Oracle Fusion Analytics Warehouse. By uniting various parts of our finance operations under one cloud, we gave our people more access to our data, insight to help guide their work and automation capabilities to greatly reduce manual, redundant tasks. An enterprise solution enabled by Oracle Cloud technology has unlocked a level of agility and scalability that we couldn’t achieve before. Moreover, the PwC team has helped upskill our people through tailored training.”
###### What was the real-world impact of approaching things differently?
“PwC helped us improve on our traditional approaches, as well as introduce new ideas to transform our business. Together, we implemented predictive analytics to anticipate member needs and proactively offer tailored solutions. We also introduced self-service options and digital platforms to enhance user convenience and accessibility. These advancements are helping us stay ahead of the competition and deliver exceptional value to our customers."


> “We’re building a more efficient and cost-effective finance function, which empowers us to drive our digital transformation agenda and ultimately better serve USAA Members.”
