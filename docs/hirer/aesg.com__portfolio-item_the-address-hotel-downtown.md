# The Address Hotel Downtown
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The Address Hotel located in Downtown Dubai is one of the most well-known hotels in Dubai. The hotel is located next to the Burj Khalifa. Following the fire that broke out on New Year’s Eve in the hotel back in 2015 AESG’s Commissioning team was assigned the job of the testing and commissioning of HVAC systems of the hotel.
