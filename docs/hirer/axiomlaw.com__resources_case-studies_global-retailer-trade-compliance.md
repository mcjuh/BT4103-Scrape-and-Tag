# Trade Compliance Initiative Completed for Top 5 Global Retailer
### Axiom attorneys helped:
Produce an updated export manual
Ensure trade compliance
Do more with existing budget
### Challenge
Global retailer needed attorney with deep knowledge of **trade compliance** to draft export manual, but **lacked experience in-house.**
Increased scrutiny under trade compliance regulations and the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act **(FCPA)** makes trade compliance more important than ever.
### Approach
Axiom deployed a **senior attorney with extensive knowledge** of trade compliance to provide strategic guidance and draft an export manual. Working closely with the retailer’s Vice President of Compliance and the in-house team, the Axiom attorney assessed current export operations, performed a gap analysis, and **developed a final set of export operational policies** to ensure compliance with export compliance regulations.
### Impact 
  * By **producing an updated export manual** , the Axiom attorney had helped the trade compliance department and legal department leadership ensure compliance in a critical regulatory area, **protecting the company’s reputation and business.**
  * **Flexible hourly billing** enabled the company’s trade compliance department to work with the Axiom attorney on an as-needed basis, including reengaging the attorney’s services to continually update and finetune successive versions of the manual.
  * Legal department was able to **do more with their existing budget** by working with Axiom rather than paying the premium rates that outside counsel would charge for this sort of service.
