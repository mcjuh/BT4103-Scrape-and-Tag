# Real Estate Support Centralized for VC Firm's Portfolio Companies
### Axiom attorneys helped:
Managed 100+ office lease terminations and renewals across the country
Partnered directly with client business teams to support both landlord and tenant disputes
Reduced outside spend on contracts review by 66% per hour
### Challenge
A small VC firm is facing a serious bandwidth crunch after losing their in-house real estate attorney. With **entities spanning across four core industries** , they need high-powered and ongoing real estate support for their eight portfolio companies
### Approach
Axiom swiftly integrated a seasoned real estate attorney into the client’s central legal team. The Axiom attorney, available to support the entire client portfolio, partners with the business team to **“churn and burn” renewals and terminations for 100+ office leases across the country**. With such high lease volume, the Axiom attorney is relied upon not just for legal review, but for triaging business needs and finalizing the deals. In addition to the ongoing lease pipeline, she **negotiates new construction contracts, handles disputes with landlords, and sends default letters** when required.
### Impact 
  * Axiom attorney handled**8-10 lease renewals or closure letters in any given week.**
  * Axiom attorney’s**in-house experience and practical business savvy** fostered seamless partnership with the client’s business team, enabling them to push forward on time-sensitive matters without delay.
  * Plugged a crucial real estate expertise gap for client after in-house attrition **without bogging down the remainder of the legal team.**
