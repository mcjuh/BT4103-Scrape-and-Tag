# River Park
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project consists of two residential buildings with associated retail and F&B outlets located in the heart of Dubai, adjacent to the Dubai Canal and Safa Park. As part of the building approvals, the environmental permitting process must be carried out. AESG was appointed by the lead consultant, Dewan Architects+Engineers to undertake an environmental impact assessment summary for submission to Dubai Municipality’s Environmental Planning and Studies Section (DM-EPSS).
  * Environmental Impact Assessment Summary Report
  * Securing the Environmental Clearance (EC)
