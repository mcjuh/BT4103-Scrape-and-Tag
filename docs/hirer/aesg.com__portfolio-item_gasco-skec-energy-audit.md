# GASCO SKEC Energy Audit
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
Sheikh Khalifa Energy Complex (SKEC) houses the headquarters of most of the ADNOC Group Companies. The complex is comprised of four office towers (two of which have 27 stories and another two towers of 20 stories each) in addition to a number of associated buildings including a car parking & main lobby.
AESG has completed a comprehensive energy & sustainability study for the complex.
  * Energy Audit
  * 3D-simulated Energy Model
  * LEED Assessment Study
  * Energy and Environmental Monitoring Study
  * Energy Baseline and Energy Balance for ISO 50001 implementation
  * In-site measurement of U-values
