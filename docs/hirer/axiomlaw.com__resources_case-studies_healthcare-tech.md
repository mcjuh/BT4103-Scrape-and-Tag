### Life is Good
**Picture this… you're a cutting-edge technology company accelerating the transformation of healthcare**. Providing data- and analytics-driven solutions to improve clinical, financial, administrative, and patient engagement outcomes, you're helping to create a stronger, more collaborative U.S. healthcare system.
### Big Challenge
**But, as a newly launched company formed from a merger, you have yet to build up your own internal legal and compliance organization.** You need support for a broad range of legal matters, yet you no longer have access to your parent company's shared service network. Traditional law firms offer the necessary expertise, but lack both the agility and the cost-effectiveness your business demands.
### Help Arrives
**So, you turn to Axiom.** After a primary provision of two lawyers, we quickly prescribe more than a dozen lawyers working across seven practice areas and numerous subject matters. You get support on policies and procedures, compliance, regulations, contracts, procurement, real estate, incidents, investigations, and more – all with analytics for operational metrics that enable you to track progress, improve processes, and optimally resource your internal staff.
### Life is Even Better
**Now, you have a team of legal specialists at the ready, and each engagement is saving you $100,000-$250,000 per month.** Using this fluid workforce to support new projects or fill in staffing gaps, you deliver improved outcomes with greater efficiency than ever before – and save an estimated $5M in your first two years as an independent company.
