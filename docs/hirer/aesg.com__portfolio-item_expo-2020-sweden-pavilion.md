# Expo 2020 Sweden Pavilion
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project development is a proposed (G+LM+UM+1) pavilion at Expo Dubai. The Scandinavian woodlands combine with Islamic geometric design to inspire the architecture and storytelling of the Pavilion. Visitors can stroll through its path, flanked by wooden trunks, and learn how the country is building smart cities, developing the next generation of travel, innovating life sciences and developing a bio-circular economy.
