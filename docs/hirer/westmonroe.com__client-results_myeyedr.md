## Transforming the Scheduling Experience
Facing challenges with its third-party scheduling system, MyEyeDr. needed a solution that could enhance patient experiences while offering deeper insights into scheduling behavior. With MyEyeDr. experiencing rapid growth, they turned to West Monroe to co-create a custom, human-centered scheduling platform that would address these challenges. Together, we built and deployed the system across 700 locations in just eight months, increasing online booking rates by 147% and improving patient onboarding by 360%. The new system eliminated key user experience issues such as double booking while empowering MyEyeDr. with actionable insights, enabling continuous optimization and delivering measurable improvements in customer satisfaction.

At the heart of the project was a human-centered design process that prioritized ease of use and efficiency. West Monroe’s team worked alongside MyEyeDr. to identify pain points, focusing on issues like double booking and the lack of visibility into patient behavior. After generating numerous ideas, we narrowed them down to impactful solutions, including digital intake forms for self-service. These forms reduced the workload for office staff and streamlined the patient journey, enabling faster onboarding and smoother scheduling. The business case became clear—these changes would directly drive patient satisfaction and create operational efficiencies.
