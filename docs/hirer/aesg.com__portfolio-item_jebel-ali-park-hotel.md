# Jebel Ali Park Hotel
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The Jebel Ali Park Hotel is an upcoming four-star development located within the Jebel Ali Golf Course, Dubai. The hotel will consist of 350 rooms, with most rooms facing the existing golf course.
AESG was appointed as the environmental consultant on this project, to obtain Environmental Clearance as per Dubai Municipality guidelines. A site visit was conducted to determine overall conditions as well as document and identify all flora and fauna. Following this, baseline testing was conducted for air, noise, soil, and groundwater parameters. A complete Environmental Impact Assessment Summary (EIAS) was submitted to the Dubai Municipality taking into account all site conditions and surrounding sensitive receptors, and final Environmental Clearance was obtained in September 2017.
