In just seven months, West Monroe and Rugs USA executed a fast-paced integration, leveraging **Intellio® Deliver** to guide nine targeted workstreams. We mobilized an Integration Management Office (IMO), setting up operational and cost-saving structures. Our team established a new organizational structure, consolidating distribution centers to reduce freight expenses by 70% and applying a revamped sourcing strategy that achieved 114% savings. This shift improved customer experience through faster delivery times while lowering costs, aligning with Rugs USA’s vision for long-term operational efficiency and future growth.
