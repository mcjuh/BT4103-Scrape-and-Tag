## A Smarter Intranet Delivered
Con Edison, one of the nation’s largest energy providers, wanted a single, intuitive digital platform to unite 15,000 employees across subsidiaries. With no centralized repository for operational data, employees faced fragmented information, limited access, and inconsistent communication. Together, we envisioned a scalable intranet with human-centered design at its core to streamline operations, boost engagement, and foster a safety-first culture. By analyzing employee needs, we consolidated over 1,200 pages into 120 and introduced intuitive features to improve navigation and communication. The results were immediate: a 50% increase in search use, 30% higher employee satisfaction, and daily engagement from 76% of employees one-year post-launch. This innovative platform earned recognition as a top 10 intranet by Nielsen Norman Group in 2020.

In 2018, Con Edison set out to transform its employee experience by building a centralized intranet. Our team began by conducting workshops, user interviews, and analyzing employee workflows. The insights revealed that fragmented information and lack of intuitive tools were key barriers. We used this understanding to design user personas and create a roadmap focused on simplicity and usability. We co-create with stakeholders at every step to ensure alignment and ownership of the changes. With clarity of purpose, we laid the groundwork for a secure, scalable platform tailored to employee needs.

This project required a multidisciplinary approach. We worked hand-in-hand with Con Edison’s internal teams over 12 months, combining deep industry insights with technical expertise. Using human-centered design principles, we addressed pain points through collaborative content strategy sessions and user testing. To future-proof the platform, we implemented a governance model to prevent duplication and clutter. The new intranet standardized communication with templates and intuitive navigation, ensuring employees had quick access to critical safety protocols, tools, and resources. Together, we delivered a secure, scalable, and human-centric portal that met the evolving needs of 15,000 employees.
