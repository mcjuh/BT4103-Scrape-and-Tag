Bega Cheese has enjoyed remarkable growth to become one of Australia's leading producers of cheese and dairy products. Pursuing a bold vision to be 'The Great Australian Food Company,' Bega recognized that its existing HR systems and processes lacked the flexibility and scale needed to manage the company's steadily growing workforce. 
Bega turned to KPMG Australia for help in launching its ambitious 'Future Today' HR transformation program, a strategic initiative to centralize and automate disparate HR operations and harmonize employee data to deliver timely new workforce insights and enhanced decision making.
Bega selected Workday's Human Capital Management cloud solution and worked closely with the KPMG Australia team to implement it - including the adoption of a KPMG Powered HR solution to help customize Workday's platform to Bega's needs.
