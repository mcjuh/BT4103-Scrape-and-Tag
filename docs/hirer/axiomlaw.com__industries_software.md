More Information on Axiom's Software Lawyers
## What can software lawyers help SaaS companies handle?
Software lawyers can help SaaS companies with customer agreements, procurement terms, MSAs, software licensing, reseller agreements, data processing terms, privacy compliance, incident response, employment policies, diligence, and integration work. Axiom can help legal teams add flexible software legal talent as workloads change.
## How can software lawyers support licensing and reseller agreements?
Software lawyers can support licensing and reseller agreements by clarifying usage rights, restrictions, sublicensing, payment terms, channel obligations, support commitments, data handling, IP ownership, termination, renewal, and liability allocation.
## What legal support is needed when software companies add AI features?
When software companies add AI features, legal teams may need support with data rights, model or vendor terms, customer disclosures, privacy notices, IP ownership, output risk, security review, product claims, and [governance processes for how AI is developed](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/artificial-intelligence), deployed, and monitored.
## How can software companies manage privacy and incident response obligations?
Software companies can manage [data privacy and incident response](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/data-privacy-cybersecurity) obligations by maintaining clear data processing terms, privacy notices, vendor controls, incident escalation workflows, response playbooks, training materials, and cross-functional coordination between legal, security, product, and customer-facing teams.
## When should software companies add commercial contracts support?
Software companies often add [commercial contracts support](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/commercial-contracts) when sales volume increases, enterprise negotiations slow revenue, procurement work creates a backlog, reseller agreements expand, renewals become more complex, or the legal team needs help standardizing templates and fallback positions.
## How can software lawyers support M&A, fundraising, or integration work?
Software lawyers can support [M&A](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/mergers-acquisitions), fundraising, and integration work by helping with due diligence, contract review, software and IP documentation, privacy and security issues, board materials, SEC-related support where applicable, acquisition documents, divestitures, and post-close integration workflows.
