# AI-Driven Modernization Cuts Call Abandonment by 50% for Health Plan
Transforming performance at scale by unifying 21 lines of business on an AI-powered contact center

A regional health plan faced frequent outages, rising call volumes, and inconsistent experiences across 21 disconnected lines of business—all driven by an outdated, unreliable contact center system. Human support teams were overwhelmed and lacked the tools and consistency to meet growing member and provider expectations. West Monroe partnered with the organization to align business, technology, and operations around a modern, cloud-based contact center. We led the selection and implementation of Genesys Cloud, redesigned the operating model, and introduced shared services to reduce fragmentation and enable consistency at scale. At the same time, we equipped agents with improved tools and workflows while deploying AI-driven capabilities to streamline routing, automate manual tasks, and improve speed.
The result: improved reliability, faster agent ramp-up, and a more efficient, consistent operation—establishing a stable foundation for continued AI and digital transformation.

Ideas into Reality
Facing outages and inefficiencies across 21 lines of business, the health plan partnered with West Monroe to deliver measurable improvements, including a 22% increase in IVR containment.

We began by aligning business and IT leaders around a future-state vision for customer service and a practical roadmap to achieve it. Before implementing new technology, we assessed current capabilities, identified hundreds of pain points, and defined initiatives across operations, data, and platforms. This clarity enabled the confident selection of Genesys as a best-in-class CCaaS (contact center as a service) solution and established a new standard for how the contact center would operate. With priorities aligned and outcomes clearly defined, the organization was positioned to move quickly while avoiding past implementation challenges and accelerate toward a more modern, scalable model.

Tackling it Together
Through a phased rollout, West Monroe and the client deployed Genesys to more than 2,200 users while delivering 27 features across four releases. 
Read More 
Working side by side, our healthcare and contact center teams partnered with business and IT leaders to co-create the solution. We redesigned IVR flows using natural language best practices, consolidated queues, and introduced AI-driven capabilities such as bots and Agent Copilot. In parallel, we established shared services for workforce management, reporting, and knowledge management to drive consistency and standardization across all 21 lines of business. This structured change approach—including readiness champions, role-based training, and ongoing support—ensured agents and supervisors were equipped to adopt new tools and ways of working at every stage.

Value Created
The program delivered a 22% increase in IVR containment, more than 50% reduction in call abandonment, a 12% decrease in average handle time, and $15M in projected cost savings from shared services by 2030.

By combining platform modernization with operating model transformation, the health plan achieved durable, measurable results. Contact center efficiency improved almost immediately, while shared services introduced forecasting accuracy, consistency, and scalability across the organization. Agent readiness also improved, with 88% reporting they felt prepared for the change—rebuilding trust after years of instability. Together, these outcomes reflect the program’s full value: measurable savings, better experiences for members and providers, and a resilient foundation ready to support future innovation.
