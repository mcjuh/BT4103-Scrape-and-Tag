## Make faster grid connections possible with AI
With the rapid adoption of electric vehicles and solar panels, a large electrical energy distribution company saw a sharp increase in grid-connection applications. Each application required a labor-intensive manual review of complex technical documents and diagrams, consuming valuable time and slowing customers’ grid connections.

### Complex manual application reviews 
With the rapid adoption of electric vehicles and solar panels, the company experienced a surge in grid-connection applications. Each request required a labor-intensive manual review of complex technical documents and diagrams, consuming valuable time and slowing customer response
A scalable solution to automate application reviews was required, while also maintaining compliance with strict safety standards. The goal was to increase efficiency and customer satisfaction through a transformative, AI-driven approach.
### Streamlined, automated reviews
A phased strategy leveraged AI technologies such as KPMG KymChat and erwin, to automate the grid connection application reviews. Custom AI agents were built to analyze technical documents and diagrams, reducing the average evaluation time from one week to just 30 minutes.
The solution was developed to integrate seamlessly with industry standards. In close collaboration with the company’s engineers, the KPMG Australia team helped validate the AI models, improve data consistency, and ensure full compliance with operational and safety requirements.
### Improved data consistency, efficiency and customer satisfaction 
Operational efficiency and customer satisfaction increased significantly, establishing a scalable foundation for future innovation. By streamlining routine document reviews, the system helped minimize the need for additional hiring and enabled the company’s engineers to focus on more strategic and higher-value tasks.
