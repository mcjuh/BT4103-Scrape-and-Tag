## Accelerating Data-Driven Decisions
A rapidly growing health plan required a scalable data platform to manage operations and meet increasing demands. West Monroe collaborated with them to design and implement a cloud-based solution using [**Databricks**](https://www.westmonroe.com/partnerships/databricks), improving data governance, consolidating analytics, and enabling AI use cases. This led to $2.5 million in first-year licensing cost savings, a 25-40% improvement in RFP technical scores, and 120,000 person hours saved annually through enhanced data analysis and reporting. Our strategic partnership not only modernized their data platform but also empowered their teams to innovate and stay competitive for the future.

West Monroe collaborated with the client's project team to develop and implement a comprehensive data platform strategy. We designed a system incorporating a data lake, integrated layer, and consumption layer capable of managing up to one billion claims. Together, we deployed Unity Catalog within Databricks to ensure robust data governance in their Microsoft Azure environment. Our phased migration approach connected multiple data sources into a unified platform, overcoming technical debt and on-premises limitations. This partnership allowed for faster execution and enhanced their ability to scale efficiently.
