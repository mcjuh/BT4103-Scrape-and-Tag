Opened 28 July 2026 beside Yanzi Lake in Shenzhen’s Pingshan District, the 105,300 m² Shenzhen Natural History Museum is South China's largest. Designed with B+H Architects, its Pearl River Delta–inspired form houses galleries, a dome theatre, LED cinema and nature theatres, and cave-inspired pathways. Each space demanded tailored acoustics for immersive multimedia, clear presentations and lasting visitor comfort.
We provided acoustic consultancy across the development, covering room acoustics, sound insulation and advanced computer modelling. Working closely with the project team, we balanced the distinct needs of each venue, optimising performance through detailed analysis and simulation to enhance speech intelligibility, audience comfort and sound isolation between neighbouring spaces.
Acoustics shape how visitors experience and understand the natural world. By tuning each space to its purpose, we help the museum deliver engaging, accessible educational experiences. The result supports Shenzhen’s ambitions as a cultural landmark advancing science education, environmental awareness and public engagement across the Greater Bay Area. 
Organic circulation routes shape how visitors move, and how sound travels, through the museum.
## Creating exceptional acoustic experiences
Arup provided acoustic consultancy services for the museum, helping create high-quality listening environments throughout its diverse range of spaces. The design encompassed room acoustics, sound insulation and advanced computer modelling across the venue's key spaces, including the 279-seat Dome Theatre, the 262-seat cinema featuring a 17m x 9m LED screen, the 508-seat Nature Theatre, and the 150-seat 4D theatre.
Special attention was given to balancing the varied acoustic requirements of the different facilities, from immersive multimedia experiences and educational presentations to live multimedia productions. Through detailed acoustic analysis and simulation, we worked closely with the project team to enhance speech intelligibility, audience comfort and sound quality, while ensuring appropriate sound isolation between neighbouring spaces. 
The integrated acoustic strategy supports the museum’s mission of delivering engaging and accessible educational experiences, reflecting our continued commitment to shaping meaningful visitor journeys through specialist acoustic design. 
Acoustics designed to bring exhibits to life while keeping spaces comfortable for every visitor.
Acoustic modelling helped optimise the audience experience across every performance space.
