# Consumer Litigation Claims for Automotive Company
### Axiom attorneys helped:
Handle cumbersome litigation claims from start-to-finish with minimal support
Cut average case time by systematizing tracking and collections
Reduce average arbitration cost by nearly $75,000 each
### Challenge
A distressed automotive company facing severe budget cuts faces a **major uptick in lemon law litigation** claims. Their internal legal team is undergoing major turnover, and they need robust support to keep up to properly address the volume of new claims coming in daily.
### Approach
A former AmLaw 20 Axiom lawyer onboards rapidly to support **State-by-State lemon law and Federal Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act (MMWA) claims**. With the legal team in a state of upheaval, the Axiom lawyer steps in to support the consumer litigation claims team. With minimal support, he handles customer claims from start to finish– **drafting initial response, prepping relevant case materials, and arguing case with arbitration committees**. He also partners with outside counsel to negotiate settlements for ongoing claims, prepare witnesses for depositions, and draft court filings.
### Impact 
  * In first 2 months of support, Axiom lawyer handled nearly 50 arbitrations, **saving the client ~ $75,000 each.**
  * Axiom lawyer developed systematized tracking collection processes for arbitrations and filings, **cutting average case time to ~4.5 hours**.
  * Offloaded time-sensitive and cumbersome litigation claims from in-house team to an experienced **Axiom attorney who could handle claims from start-to-finish without additional support**.
