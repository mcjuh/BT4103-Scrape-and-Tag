# Driving Digital Adoption to Achieve Compliance and Workforce Readiness
From initial risk to achieving full compliance

A **$2 billion-plus home care provider** was preparing to transition one of their markets—which had significant paper-based operations—onto a new EMR under a tight regulatory deadline. With just two months remaining, the rollout was at risk of missing the deadline, which would put their state compliance and caregiver pay at risk.
The provider turned to West Monroe to stabilize the project and lead them through the transition. Together, we replanned the implementation, led data migration across 11 offices, and executed a change management strategy that moved more than 2,000 employees from paper-based processes to digital workflows. Despite the tight timeline and a low digital readiness, the results made an impact: 95% mobile app adoption, more than 1,800 caregivers onboarded in the first week, and 99.6% of visits reviewed on time—ensuring timely caregiver pay and full compliance on Day 1.

Tackling It Together
Over eight weeks, we partnered to train 2,000 employees, including the onboarding of more than 1,800 caregivers to a brand-new mobile app—achieving 95% adoption in the first week.

Transitioning from paper required more than new systems—it demanded deep change management. West Monroe co-created a rollout strategy with branch leaders, combining in-person and remote training, field-ready communications, and on-demand support. Behind the scenes, we automated timekeeping, shift offers, and visit confirmations—saving staff critical hours each week. Our healthcare and tech experts worked together with the client to guide the change, aligning field, operations, and IT teams around a shared goal: go live with confidence, accuracy, and zero disruption to care.

Value Created
On day one, 99.6% of visits were confirmed, 72% achieved EVV compliance, and mobile adoption exceeded expectations in a paper-first environment.

The provider launched on time, fully compliant, and without service disruption. 99.6% of visits were confirmed in the first week—ensuring caregivers were paid promptly. EVV compliance hit 72% at launch and improved week over week. Field adoption exceeded expectations: 95% of caregivers downloaded the app and began using digital workflows, many for the first time. Readiness and adoption scores averaged 4 out of 5 or higher. With key manual processes now automated, staff reclaimed valuable time each week. The organization is now equipped with a scalable system—and a workforce ready for what’s next.
