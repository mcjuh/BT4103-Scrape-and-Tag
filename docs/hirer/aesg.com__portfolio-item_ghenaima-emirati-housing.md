# Ghenaima Emirati Housing
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The proposed Ghenaima (East) Emirati housing project, located in Al Ain, is a residential community comprised of more than 250 villas, two mosques and retail facilities. The design of the community aims to achieve a 2-Pearl rating under the Estidama PCRS.
AESG have been appointed to provide sustainability and Estidama consultancy on the project during both design and construction:
  * Sustainable design consulting services
  * Estidama design management
  * PCRS submission and approvals
