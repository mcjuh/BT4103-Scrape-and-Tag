A North American tire and parts distributor faced the same challenge many did during the pandemic—mounting supply chain disruptions, increased demand, and limited visibility into their end-to-end cost to serve. They needed a strategic reset to boost efficiency and drive meaningful cost reductions. Over 16 months, we partnered to co-create a transformation roadmap powered by [Alteryx](https://www.westmonroe.com/partnerships/alteryx) analytics, using real-time and predictive insights to optimize demand planning, inventory, and supplier performance. Building on this foundation, we optimized their network and routing efficiency to increase efficiency. The results? A $200M cumulative working capital reduction, $80M in overall EBITDA improvement, and an 18% reduction in cost per unit. By delivering immediate gains and long-term scalability, we built a smarter, more resilient supply chain.
