## Elevate’s Expertise Drives Dual Wins at RLLB 2025 Awards
**September 9, 2025** – Continuing its winning streak for the second consecutive year, LegalOps.com named two of Elevate’s customers – [Johnson & Johnson](https://www.jnj.com/) and [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/in/home) – as winners of the Running Legal Like a Business (RLLB) 2025 Awards, both in partnership with Elevate.
Johnson & Johnson received the Litigation Operations Excellence Award for its innovative Litigation Summit initiative, designed to foster collaboration between in-house and outside counsel to address rising litigation costs and emerging trends. Elevate partnered with J&J to support strategic planning, execution, and legal operations enhancements.
PayPal earned the Data-Driven Legal Department of the Year Award for its innovative use of spend, risk, and complexity data to optimise external legal spend. In partnership with Elevate, PayPal redesigned its legal spend strategy to cut routine costs and redirect resources to significant matters while maintaining quality across its portfolio, exemplifying how to ‘run legal like a business’.
These dual wins follow [Elevate’s 2024 success with FedEx](https://elevate.law/news/fortune-100-customer-of-elevate-wins-running-legal-like-a-business-award-for-work-using-elevates-legal-expertise-and-ai/), which was recognised for pioneering an [innovative solution](https://elevate.law/case-studies/elevates-ai-and-elm-solution-cuts-discovery-time-by-a-third/) to streamline litigation. Together, these accolades reflect Elevate’s continued commitment to helping leading legal teams rethink strategy, drive innovation, and deliver measurable business results.
Read more [here](https://www.theedgeroom.com/2025/09/04/rllb-2025-awards-celebrating-excellence-in-legal-operations/).
