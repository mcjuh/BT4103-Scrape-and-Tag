# Directorships and Chief Restructuring Officer
Experienced leadership, stability and transparency for distressed businesses and their stakeholders.
Kroll’s senior restructuring professionals act as Directors and Chief Restructuring Officers (CROs), taking leadership roles to stabilize businesses in crisis, restore transparency, and implement enforcement strategies.
Whether working alongside existing management or replacing them, we provide tailored interim management solutions to preserve, enhance and recover value.
We utilize share-charge receiverships combined with directorship appointments to execute enforcement strategies. This approach mitigates the operational and financial impact of formal insolvency proceedings while allowing us to take control of entities and assets that lack direct collateral or are in complex jurisdictions.

## How We Can Help
Kroll’s Directorships and CRO solutions provide experienced leadership in distressed situations. We stabilize and turnaround operations, manage stakeholders, and implement enforcements to protect value and drive recovery.
### Interim Management and Leadership
Appointed as Directors and CROs, we restore transparency, stabilize businesses, and navigate crises. Our professionals support or replace leadership, ensuring stakeholder confidence while optimizing cash flow and operational performance.
### Enforcement and Asset Realization
We use share-charge receiverships and directorship appointments to enforce strategies while preserving business value. This approach minimizes disruption, avoids insolvency risks, and ensures effective control over assets—even those without direct collateral or located in complex jurisdictions.
