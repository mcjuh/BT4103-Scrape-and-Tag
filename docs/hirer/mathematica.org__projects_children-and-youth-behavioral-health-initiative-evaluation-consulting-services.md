## Project Overview
Objective
Assess whether the Children and Youth Behavioral Health Initiative (CYBHI) is improving behavioral health outcomes for children and youth; work with the California Health and Human Services Agency to identify opportunities for more effective implementation to strengthen current and future behavioral health outcomes for children, youth, and communities in the state.
Project Motivation
The CYBHI is a $4+ billion investment in behavioral health for children, youth, and families across California and was the cornerstone of California Governor Newsom’s Master Plan for Kid’s Mental Health. The California Health and Human Services Agency needed a partner with deep subject matter expertise in children’s behavioral health, evaluation, education, healthcare, and disability policy.
Partners in Progress
  * Health Management Associates
  * UCLA Prevention Center for Excellence
  * James Bell Associates


Prepared For
California Health and Human Services Agency
Mathematica’s evaluation approach accounts for the complexity of the CYBHI and its more than 20 component workstreams, each designed to contribute to transforming the behavioral health ecosystem in California. It recognizes the importance of placing youth at the center of any efforts to transform the systems that serve them. 
Mathematica is conducting a rigorous, mixed-methods evaluation of the CYBHI to track its progress from design to implementation to outcomes. The findings will help California leaders understand what is working, what can be strengthened, and how to create lasting improvements in youth behavioral health.
The evaluation includes the following three studies:
  1. An **ecosystem study** to assess behavioral health system change from the CYBHI across workstreams and counties.
  2. An **implementation study** to understand how CYBHI implementation is progressing in select counties.
  3. An **outcomes study** to assess trends in metrics aligned with the key outcome objectives of the CYBHI.


Throughout the evaluation, Mathematica and its partners engage communities served by the CYBHI through focus groups, interviews, and listening sessions to ensure the evaluation reflects the varied experiences of children, youth, families, and providers at the core of the initiative. The evaluation also convenes and receives input from the CYBHI Evaluation Advisory Group, which includes California youth, families, frontline workers, local systems leaders, and researchers.
Insights from this evaluation will help California’s leaders make strategic, evidence-based decisions about ongoing implementation and support for key CYBHI workstreams. Reflecting CYBHI’s vision for collaborative ecosystem transformation, Mathematica is sharing findings transparently through public reports, webinars, local presentations, and an interactive data dashboard.
Explore the CYBHI Evaluation Hub for the latest collection of evaluation reports, case studies, and more: <https://cybhi.chhs.ca.gov/evaluation-hub>
