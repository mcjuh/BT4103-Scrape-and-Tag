# Strategic Review of Agentic AI-Enabled Business for BlackRock
  * July 03, 2025
  * Share


Considering an investment in a leading business process outsourcing (“BPO”) company, BlackRock engaged FTI Consulting to perform a comprehensive technical, strategic and commercial assessment of the target’s advanced AI systems.
### Our Impact
  * FTI Consulting identified risks and opportunities in the company’s forecasted synergies and recommended updated deal terms and risk mitigation strategies.
  * Our experts provided BlackRock with a comprehensive perspective of the target’s technical completeness through an evaluation of various AI components, including large language models (“LLMs”), system integrations and telemetry tools.
  * The team enabled clear visibility into the target‘s IP assets and the level of entanglement with other business units outside of the deal perimeter.
  * Our experts benchmarked the functional and technical capabilities of the target’s AI platforms and identified growth opportunities.


### Our Role
  * FTI Consulting conducted in-depth market research to identify core technical capabilities relevant to the target’s AI platform, focusing on agentic AI frameworks and their specific applications in the BPO’s processes.
  * Our experts held detailed diligence sessions with the target’s leadership to evaluate AI capabilities, key technical components, the AI roadmap and associated commercial, operational and financial performance enhancements.
  * The team developed a functional and technical scoring model to benchmark the target’s market position.
  * FTI Consulting analyzed the target’s technical AI architecture and R&D operating model to assess alignment with best practices and identify the level of IP entanglement between the target and its parent.
