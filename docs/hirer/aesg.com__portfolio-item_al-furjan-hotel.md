# Al Furjan Hotel
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project is a proposed hotel located within Al Furjan in Dubai. AESG was brought on board to manage the sustainability, environmental and waste management requirements for the project. AESG is providing consultancy services in regards to design review to ensure regulatory green building requirements are met. AESG is undertaking the environmental permitting and reporting for the project as well as designing the waste strategy and engineering the waste storage rooms for the hotel.
  * Sustainability Green Building Consultancy
  * Environmental Permetting
  * Initial Enviornmentla Examination (IEE)
  * Waste Management Consultancy
  * Waste Strategy Validation
  * Waste Engineering
