# EdTech firm overcomes alignment and migration issues with digital transformation
### Holistic RSM solutions unify key systems and processes and inspire global growth
August 31, 2026
##  Key takeaways 
Integrated systems enabled rapid growth and acquisitions with unified, scalable operations. 
Automated data migration and master data management boosted accuracy and control.
Streamlined order-to-cash and treasury processes drove efficiency and revenue impact.
# 
Strategy and planning NetSuite
Technology industry Salesforce Boomi Management consulting
Our client, an industry leader in educational technology (EdTech), is a private equity-backed portfolio company providing assessment, practice and data-driven solutions for educators worldwide. Following years of growth and acquisitions, the client operated with disconnected systems and region-specific processes that limited scalability. RSM worked with the client to lead a multiphase transformation focused on unifying customer engagement, streamlining operations and establishing a single source of truth across systems to lay the foundation for sustained global growth.
By working with a single provider to transform several key functions, the client increased efficiency and established a consistent vision for ongoing growth and success.

## The challenge: Fragmentation after growth
The client rapidly grew through strategic acquisitions, resulting in multiple disconnected systems and inconsistent processes across business units and geographies. Complexity extended beyond system sprawl. Fragmentation created inefficiencies for sales, services and accounting teams; reduced leadership visibility into accurate enterprise data; and required manual reconciliation across systems. This delayed decision making and limited the organization’s ability to respond to business challenges in real time.
As an EdTech provider, the client frequently sells at the district level while fulfilling subscriptions, pricing and billing at the individual school level. Manual billing, revenue recognition, tax and close processes required significant effort across multiple systems. As the organization continued to scale globally, these workarounds became increasingly difficult to sustain. Company leadership recognized the need to unify critical business processes and applications to support global operations while delivering consistent experiences for both customers and internal stakeholders.
"We realized we couldn’t scale globally with fragmented systems and processes. We needed a single source of truth."
Company Executive 
While the initial project scope encompassed enterprise resource planning (ERP) and customer relationship management (CRM) system selection and implementation for the client’s North American operations, it quickly grew to include several additional processes and applications.
“The project's original intent was to improve end-to-end processes that touch both CRM and ERP systems,” says RSM US Director Jacob Hulsey. “Through discovery and through mid-project acquisitions, the scope quickly expanded to better accommodate the client's global expansion.”
## Strategic goals for transformation: Elevating connectivity and scalability
The transformation strategy aimed to reconnect people, process and technology across the enterprise, unify operational and financial data, and future-proof the client’s operating model for continued growth. 
Given the organization’s acquisitive strategy, the solution was intentionally designed to operate in a dynamic environment capable of adapting to new business models, geographies, operating models and product offerings without disrupting the broader enterprise architecture.
RSM and the client adopted a business-led, technology-enabled strategy built on three pillars:
### **Business process unification**
Harmonizing workflows across sales, finance, customer success and operations
### **Technology architecture and data strategy**
Consolidating CRM and ERP platforms for seamless integration across billing, tax, treasury and reporting systems, supported by a scalable data model designed to accommodate ongoing acquisitions and global expansion
### **People and change management**
Embedding strong governance, executive sponsorship and user adoption programs to establish lasting success
## Discovery and design: Building alignment before the build
Rather than designing a net‑new solution in isolation, RSM began by closely evaluating the client’s existing processes across acquired entities. Teams worked collaboratively with client stakeholders to determine which processes should be retained, refined or retired and ensured alignment before entering the build phase.
During the discovery and design phases, RSM worked closely with client stakeholders to capture business requirements, perform fit-gap assessments against future-state systems and applications, develop future-state process flows, and design the future-state system architecture. The work focused on lead-to-cash processes, including supporting systems, integrations, data flows and related business capabilities.
A critical outcome of discovery was the recommendation to implement new [Salesforce](https://rsmus.com/technologies/salesforce.html "Salesforce") and [NetSuite](https://rsmus.com/technologies/netsuite/solutions.html "NetSuite") environments. This approach eliminated legacy data constraints, and enabled redesign of foundational elements like billing architecture and the global chart of accounts that would have otherwise limited automation and scalability over time.
Together with RSM’s discovery‑first approach and supported by strong client sponsorship, the teams designed a streamlined, scalable architecture that worked seamlessly across upstream and downstream systems while minimizing rework later in the program.
## Transformation highlights: Connectivity across key functions
The transformation delivered a unified Salesforce CRM experience that simplified workflows for sales and service teams through a centralized product catalog, consistent quoting processes and integrated routing for calls, email and service activities.
Leadership gained improved visibility into enterprise performance through consolidated, trusted data and eliminated the need for manual reporting and reconciliation previously required across multiple systems.
By designing and implementing new streamlined, connected processes, RSM and the client:
  * Specified data ownership and master data management processes for nine data domains with 40 stakeholders
  * Designed and implemented 50 enterprise-wide data flows
  * Migrated more than 20 million rows of data from seven sources to new NetSuite and Salesforce instances 
  * Conducted more than 175,000 billing transactions in NetSuite in five currencies, sent to more than 160 countries
  * Processed more than $875 million in payments using [Kyriba](https://rsmus.com/technologies/kyriba.html "Kyriba")
  * Migrated more than 1 million subscription and contract line items into NetSuite, preserving historical data while supporting scalable future growth


## Technology stack: Modern solutions for a unified enterprise
Before the project, the client operated five ERP systems and three CRM platforms; today, it runs on one ERP and one CRM solution. The transformation introduced a robust, best-in-class technology ecosystem, with [Salesforce](https://rsmus.com/technologies/salesforce.html "Salesforce") for CRM, NetSuite for ERP, ZoneBilling for advanced billing, Kyriba for treasury and cash management, Vertex for global transaction tax determination, and [Boomi](https://rsmus.com/technologies/boomi-services.html "Boomi") for application integration and data flow synchronization. Additional integrations with Recurly and Shopify ensured that all customer transactions, both business-to-business (B2B) and business-to-consumer (B2C) flow into NetSuite as a single system of record, enabling comprehensive enterprise‑wide reporting.
This new ecosystem establishes seamless data flow across customer, financial and operational systems and allows the client to maintain real-time inventory visibility while ensuring revenue-generating activities captured in Salesforce are accurately processed downstream in NetSuite—supporting both North American and global operations. After implementing the new processes and technology applications, the RSM team configured and tested the end-to-end solution in accordance with technical design specifications, completed additional rounds of data conversion and developed organizational management activities to drive user adoption.
## Governance and collaboration: Strong governance driving success
With a project of this scope and complexity, effective governance and collaboration from both parties was essential to maintaining momentum through leadership changes and global expansion. RSM and client leadership established a joint governance model with executive sponsorship across workstreams to maintain alignment. A cross-functional team from 34 RSM services worked seamlessly to deliver a single transformation roadmap. 
“We are seen as an arm of their business and not like outside consultants,” says RSM Supervisor Peter Bellantoino. “They are very comfortable with our deep knowledge of where they are and where they want to get, so they trust the guidance and recommendations we give them.”
## Impact and outcomes: Unlocking efficiency, visibility and growth
The transformation project delivered measurable business value across the organization.
From an executive perspective, consolidation into a single ERP environment replaced extended close cycles and manual consolidation with real‑time financial visibility in addition to the strategic advantage this transformation provided among competitors in the industry.
For finance and operations teams, automation across billing, amendments and reconciliations shifted effort away from manual processing toward review and exception management.
The flexibility of the unified architecture was demonstrated mid‑implementation when the client acquired a new business with physical inventory requirements. The solution absorbed these new operational needs without the need for the re‑architecture of core systems, enabling both organizations to operate on a shared global platform.
Connected systems support a seamless customer journey from sales through service, while a repeatable framework for future acquisitions positions the client for continued global expansion.
## The road ahead: A foundation for ongoing growth
This project was more than a technology upgrade; it redefined how the client operates. By reconnecting people, process and technology across every major function, RSM established a unified global framework that has increased efficiency, scalability and innovation. 
The North American transformation model laid the blueprint for the UK and other regions to follow, proving the strength of a unified architecture and governance framework. The phased go-live approach allowed RSM teams to learn from the North American go-live and implement tailored and strategic adjustments to further reduce day-to-day disruptions for the client and increase overall go-live success. With modern systems, standardized processes and a culture of collaboration, the EdTech company is now equipped to accelerate innovation, expand into new markets and deliver exceptional experiences to educators worldwide. 
"This transformation gave us the agility to scale globally and innovate faster than ever before."
Company Executive

Unified operations 
### **Unified operations**
RSM led a full unification of the company’s front‑, middle‑ and back‑office processes across all acquired entities. This included analyzing and rationalizing disparate workflows; designing standardized future‑state processes; and harmonizing sales, finance, customer success and operational activities across geographies. These unified processes created consistency, reduced manual work and enabled the organization to scale globally without fragmentation.
Modern enterprise core 
### **Modern enterprise core**
RSM replaced the client’s complex legacy environment, previously spanning multiple ERPs, CRMs, tax engines, integration tools and more than 100 ancillary applications with a streamlined, modern, connected enterprise architecture. This included selecting and implementing [Salesforce](https://rsmus.com/technologies/salesforce.html) as the unified CRM and [NetSuite](https://rsmus.com/technologies/netsuite/solutions.html) as the global ERP, supported by best‑in‑class tools like ZoneBilling, Vertex, [Boomi](https://rsmus.com/technologies/boomi-services.html), [Kyriba](https://rsmus.com/technologies/kyriba.html), Shopify and Recurly.
Enterprise data scale 
### **Enterprise data scale**
RSM executed a comprehensive data transformation program centered on ownership, governance, quality and scale. The team defined master data ownership across nine domains, established governance for 42 stakeholders, designed 46 enterprise data flows and migrated more than 20 million rows of data from seven systems, including over 1 million subscription and contract line items.
Revenue automation 
### **Revenue automation**
RSM redesigned one of the client’s most critical functions: its end‑to‑end order‑to‑cash (O2C) engine. By restructuring contract architecture, enabling automated billing, supporting accurate revenue recognition, and integrating [Salesforce](https://rsmus.com/technologies/salesforce.html) CPQ with [NetSuite](https://rsmus.com/technologies/netsuite/solutions.html) and ZoneBilling, RSM delivered a scalable, automated O2C engine that reduced manual effort and improved accuracy. The new workflow has already processed $5.88 million in quoted net revenue since go‑live.
Sales model transformation 
### **Sales model transformation**
RSM created a unified, highly customized [Salesforce](https://rsmus.com/technologies/salesforce.html) ecosystem tailored to the client’s unique sales model. This structure included enabling district‑level selling with school‑level contract fulfillment; introducing subscription architecture not natively supported in standard [Salesforce](https://rsmus.com/technologies/salesforce.html) CPQ; and creating configurable pricing, billing, shipping and amendment rules. These enhancements streamlined the sales cycle and improved usability for end users.
Global financial platform 
### **Global financial platform**
RSM implemented a fully redesigned global [NetSuite](https://rsmus.com/technologies/netsuite/solutions.html) environment serving as the client’s financial system of record. This included a new global chart of accounts, advanced billing through ZoneBilling, seamless treasury operations via [Kyriba](https://rsmus.com/technologies/kyriba.html), real‑time tax automation through Vertex and a unified financial platform supporting both North American and UK operations.
Connected enterprise 
### **Connected enterprise**
RSM deployed [Boomi](https://rsmus.com/technologies/boomi-services.html) as the client’s enterprise‑wide integration backbone, enabling synchronized data exchange across CRM, ERP, billing, e-commerce, tax, treasury and service delivery platforms. RSM also integrated B2B and B2C customer transactions into a single financial system of record, eliminating reconciliation complexity and improving enterprise visibility.
Transformation leadership 
### **Transformation leadership**
RSM worked hand in hand with client leadership through a joint governance structure with strong executive sponsorship across all workstreams. With 34 RSM service teams engaged, the program maintained alignment, momentum and strategic clarity through leadership changes and global expansion. RSM also provided organizational change management and adoption support to the successful, sustained transformation.
