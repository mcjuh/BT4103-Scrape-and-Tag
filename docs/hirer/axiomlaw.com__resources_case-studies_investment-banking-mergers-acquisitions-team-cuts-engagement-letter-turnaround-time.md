# Investment Banking M&A Team Cuts Engagement Letter Turnaround Time by 40%
[ Learn How ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/resources/case-studies/investment-banking-mergers-acquisitions-team-cuts-engagement-letter-turnaround-time#anchor) [ Discover DraftPilot + Axiom ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/solutions/tech-plus-talent/draftpilot)
### Axiom attorney helped:
Build and launch an M&A engagement-letter playbook in DraftPilot within one week
Cut markup turnaround by ~40%*, from 3 hours to under 2 hours per engagement letter 
Absorb overflow review during volume spikes without adding permanent headcount 
### Challenge
Engagement letters arrive in unpredictable spikes. When several client markups landed at once, the **five-person M &A legal team **faced slower turnaround on review and redlines—with no appetite to add permanent headcount.
### Approach
Axiom deployed **one attorney** with financial-services and AI experience. Working from the team's own negotiator's guide, they built a DraftPilot playbook for M&A engagement letters, **live within one week** ,then trained the team and absorbed overflow review directly.
### Impact 
  * **~40% less time** per engagement-letter markup, by the lead attorney's estimate.
  * Overflow absorbed **without a permanent hire or outside counsel**.
  * DraftPilot surfaced **redline points beyond the playbook** the team now plans to adopt.


_*Lead attorney's estimate._
