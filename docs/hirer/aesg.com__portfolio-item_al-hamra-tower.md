# Al Hamra Tower
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
Al Hamra Tower is a 1.9 million sqft sculptured building that houses shell and core office space constructed on above a luxury shopping centre in the heart of Kuwait city. The total construction cost of this facility was approximately $550 million.
The project began construction in 2006 and occupied in 2012. The project incorporates an energy efficient design with the goal of obtaining a high performance, low maintenance, low operating cost facility achieved through sustainable and energy efficient design features.
  * Investigation into issues with previous commissioning works, identification of root cause and the engineering of remedial actions
  * Verification and validation of commissioning of all MEP systems
  * Independent review of fire and life safety strategy
  * Fire and life safety system design review
  * Independent review of cause and effect matrix
  * Testing and commissioning of fire and life safety systems
