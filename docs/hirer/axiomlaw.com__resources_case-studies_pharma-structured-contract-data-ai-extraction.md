# Pharma Company Extracts Critical Data from Hundreds of Contracts using Tech+Talent
[ Learn How ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/resources/case-studies/pharma-structured-contract-data-ai-extraction#anchor) [ Discover Tech+Talent ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/solutions/tech-plus-talent)
### Axiom lawyer helped:
Cut project time from 1.5 months to 1.5 weeks by leveraging Axiom's AI-enabled lawyers
Save client $16,000 versus competing law firm project bid
Provide client maximum visibility on structured contract data, such as the time-bound obligations within the agreements
### Challenge
A global leader in generic medicine production needs to review a batch of hundreds of agreements after recent M&A activity. The contracts are time-sensitive, but their law firm provides a quote far exceeding their budget. The client **needs a cost-effective solution to complete their review ahead of the looming deadline**. 
### Approach
With limited time and over 200 contracts to analyze, Axiom pairs an experienced M&A lawyer with leading AI capabilities. The Axiom lawyer calibrates AI software with a sample set of client documents, verifying accuracy of each output by hand. Once set up, the **software instantly pulls 15 unique queries within each contract** to identify time-bound obligations, categorize document type, pull summaries, determine governing law (many deals were international), extract specific clauses, etc. **Within 2 weeks of receiving the request, Axiom completes the review** and flags the contracts which require further action. 
### Impact 
  * **Enabled the bootstrapped in-house team to focus on ongoing high-priority matters** by shifting high-volume work to external Axiom resources
  * **Completed entire contract review project in under 2 weeks** , a task which could have taken a lawyer manually reviewing the work nearly 2 months. 
  * **Structured contract data provided client clear visibility** on time-bound obligations within all of the agreements.
