# Elevating utility safety with edge AI drone inspection for field operations
## Making field operations smarter, safer, and faster
Deloitte and Baltimore Gas and Electric® (an Exelon℠ company) have teamed up to revolutionize aerial power line inspections with state-of-the-art AI-driven drone technology. Watch our client story video to learn how real-time data and insights are making a meaningful impact.

**Reimagining utility field operations with AI-enabled drone inspections**
Traditional drone inspections rely heavily on manual processes, often creating data overload and delaying critical analysis. The Deloitte IndustryAdvantage™ approach helped Exelon strategically integrate OptoAI™, an AI-powered drone analytics platform, to enhance efficiency and accuracy of field operations. OptoAI autonomously performs real-time asset inspections, accelerates preflight planning, and rapidly identifies potential issues—resulting in significant operational efficiencies:
  * **Streamlined workflows:** Automating data capture reduces manual steps and accelerates diagnostics.
  * **Real-time actionable intelligence:** Artificial intelligence (AI) helps instantly pinpoint anomalies to proactively mitigate risks.
  * **Improved reliability:** Predictive analytics support preventive maintenance, helping to reduce outages.


Learn how this targeted transformation of field operations, driven by IndustryAdvantage, can help utility providers address what matters at the heart of the business to impact efficiency, mitigate risks, and navigate today’s complex energy landscape more confidently.
