# Tripling Revenue Growth Without Increasing Costs Through Digital Transformation
  * December 19, 2023
  * Share


Private Equity Consulting  [Learn more](https://www.fticonsulting.com/industries/private-equity)
A leading fintech business, owned by one of the largest global Private Equity (“PE”) firms, was looking to embark upon a digital transformation to drive a new digitally enabled business model and triple revenues. Their current technology, business processes and organization were outmoded and unable to support such transformational growth.
### Our Impact
  * Setup the company for driving 3x revenue growth with flat cost basis.
  * Helped develop and launch new digitally enabled business model.
  * Original PE owner successfully exited midway through transformation due to the value creation theses supported by FTI Consulting. 


### Our Role
  * The PE firm and company engaged FTI Consulting to design a modern digital platform and a technical architecture that was flexible, agile, elastic and scalable and leveraged Artificial Intelligence.
  * Our experts redesigned key business processes with regulatory and compliance considerations and developed a strategic roadmap to execute the transformation mandate.
  * FTI Consulting was further retained to manage the digital transformation office with the CEO, CIO and COO across multiple workstreams involving six business units and eight technology partners.
