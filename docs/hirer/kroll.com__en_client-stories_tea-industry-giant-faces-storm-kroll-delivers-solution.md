# Tea Industry Giant Faces Perfect Storm: Kroll Delivers Strategic Solution
Discover how Kroll's restructuring team navigated unprecedented challenges to save Typhoo Tea. From shifting market dynamics to supply chain disruptions, learn about the strategic solutions that preserved the iconic brand, enhanced sale value, and saved 32 jobs. Read more on how Kroll delivers exceptional results in complex scenarios.
[ Talk to an Expert ](https://www.kroll.com/en/contactus)
The Challenge
Typhoo Tea, a stalwart of the British tea industry, faced unprecedented challenges as shifting consumer preferences and market dynamics created significant headwinds. A series of extraordinary events compounded its situation: a devastating factory break-in necessitated an acceleration of an intended shift from in-house production to a third-party manufacturing model, while 2024's geopolitical tensions disrupted their supply chain. Adding to these pressures, the company had invested in ethical sourcing initiatives to differentiate their product, which increased operational costs during an already challenging period. By November 2024, these combined factors had severely impacted the company's cash reserves.
Kroll's Solution
Acting on a significantly accelerated timescale, Kroll's restructuring team leveraged our expertise to navigate complex stakeholder relationships and implement a comprehensive solution that preserved the business and maximizing value. Through strategic negotiations and our deep understanding of the market, Kroll effectively mediated between stakeholders to secure the best possible outcome for creditors.
The Impact
Kroll's intervention resulted in a significantly enhanced sale value, substantially improving the recovery for key stakeholders. Our strategic approach and negotiation expertise led to a successful transaction that preserved the iconic Typhoo brand while maximizing value for creditors and preserving 32 jobs. The outcome demonstrated Kroll's ability to deliver exceptional results in complex situations, in time pressured scenarios, requiring careful stakeholder management and strategic thinking.
