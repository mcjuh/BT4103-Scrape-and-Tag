##  Key Findings 
  * Home visiting programs can strengthen engagement with families during all stages of interaction, including outreach and awareness, recruitment and enrollment, and retention and active participation.
  * To develop a comprehensive approach for each stage of family engagement, program staff can use practices based on seven key factors. 


This brief presents seven evidence-informed factors that promote family engagement in home visiting across all stages of interaction with families. The brief also provides examples of practices that can promote engagement, accounting for circumstances within and outside the home visiting program that can affect families’ awareness of these services and their decisions to enroll and actively participate. Home visiting program administrators and staff can use the factors and examples in this brief to inform their program planning and implementation.
This brief is one publication from the Understanding and Expanding the Reach of Home Visiting (HV-REACH) project. For more resources on family engagement in home visiting, see the HV-REACH project webpage.
