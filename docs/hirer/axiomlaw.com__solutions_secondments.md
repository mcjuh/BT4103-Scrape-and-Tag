## Legal Secondments FAQ
### What is a legal secondment?
A legal secondment is a temporary arrangement where a lawyer or legal professional is assigned to work within another organization for a specific period. This allows businesses to access legal knowledge and experience without long-term commitments.
### What are secondee lawyers?
Secondee lawyers are legal professionals who temporarily join an in-house legal team at another organization. These secondees work as part of the team, offering their legal skills and knowledge to address specific needs. 
### How long do legal secondments typically last?
Legal secondments can vary in duration, lasting anywhere from a few months to over a year, depending on the requirements of the in-house legal departments and the agreed period of time.

### What types of businesses typically use legal secondments? 
A wide range of organizations, including corporations, financial institutions, and government bodies, use secondments. They are particularly beneficial for businesses looking to strengthen or supplement their in-house legal departments during busy periods or when specific knowledge and experience is required.

## When should an in-house team use a legal secondment?
An in-house team may use a legal secondment when it needs dedicated legal capacity for a leave of absence, hiring delay, transaction, product launch, contract backlog, regulatory project, or temporary workload spike. A secondment can give the team continuity without requiring immediate permanent headcount.
## What work can a secondee lawyer handle?
A secondee lawyer can support work such as commercial contracts, privacy, compliance, employment, IP, product counseling, regulatory projects, litigation support, legal operations, and other in-house needs. The right scope depends on the secondee’s experience, the team’s priorities, and the structure of the engagement.
## How does a legal secondment compare to hiring full-time headcount?
A legal secondment can provide dedicated legal support for a defined period without the same timing, approval, or long-term commitment as a permanent hire. It can be useful when the need is urgent, temporary, uncertain, or project-based, while permanent headcount may be better for a stable, ongoing role.
## How do legal departments onboard secondee lawyers effectively?
Legal departments can onboard secondee lawyers effectively by defining scope, business contacts, systems access, matter priorities, reporting cadence, escalation rules, confidentiality requirements, and success measures before work begins. A structured onboarding process helps the secondee integrate quickly and contribute with less disruption.
## Can legal secondments support urgent coverage or a leave of absence?
Yes. Legal secondments can help cover urgent resourcing gaps, parental leave, medical leave, departures, delayed hiring, or temporary surges in work. Axiom can help legal teams access full-time or part-time legal talent with reserved availability based on the team’s coverage needs.
## How can Axiom help maintain continuity during a legal secondment?
Axiom can help maintain continuity by matching legal talent to the team’s needs, supporting engagement setup, aligning availability and scope, and helping legal departments structure work for predictable coverage. For longer engagements, continuity can be supported by keeping the same legal professional in place for the agreed term when possible.
