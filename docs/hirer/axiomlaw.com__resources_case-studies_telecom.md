# Large telecom provider in the UK gets a line on contract consolidation help, sees productivity jump 23%
### Team of Axiom lawyers helped:
Quickly review all contract templates in library
Develop a consolidated set of playbooks and create a single storage location easily accessible to everyone on the in-house team
Enable the team to work faster and smarter to drive a 23% jump in productivity
### Life is Good
**Picture this… you're a leading provider of global communications services,** supplying millions of people and businesses with fixed-line voice and broadband, mobile, security, cloud, and networking offerings. Operating in approximately 180 countries, you help keep the world connected.
### Big Challenge
**But, your multiple business units and geographic locations have caused your contracts library to grow unmanageably large.** We're talking about a complex compilation of playbooks, summaries, and NDA templates - not to mention a mix of current and out-of-date documents - all stored in different locations with varying accessibility. Your team struggles to find what they need, when they need it, and their work slows.
### Help Arrives
**So, you turn to Axiom. **We deploy a small team of lawyers who quickly review all the contract templates in your library. They give you recommendations for improvement, and gain alignment on a standard framework. Next, they develop a consolidated set of playbooks and create a single storage location that's easily accessible to everyone on your in-house team. Finally, they enact a formal process to ensure your new library is kept accurate and up to date.
### Life is Even Better
**Your team is able to work faster and smarter, leading to a 23% jump in productivity**. With no more crossed wires or miscommunications, your junior team members operate independently with fewer escalations, giving your senior team members the bandwidth to focus on more complex - and profitable - matters.
