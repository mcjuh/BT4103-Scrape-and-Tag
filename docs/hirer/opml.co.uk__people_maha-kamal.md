Maha Kamal is a Consultant in our Energy, Resources and Growth hub in the Climate, Resilience and Sustainability Practice.
Her work has been at the intersection of energy, gender and climate. She is co-chair, Women in Energy, a network that aims to increase gender equity in the energy and climate space in Pakistan. Earlier, she has worked with stakeholders across multilateral organisations like the World Bank, UNDP, USAID, and with the Government of Pakistan. She was awarded the Chevening Scholarship at the Queen Mary, University of London for Master's in International Public Policy, with distinction in dissertation on the Paris Agreement. She writes on policy issues. 
## Projects involving Maha Kamal
Project
###  [Evaluation of EU supported activities under the Warsak Hydroelectric Power Station rehabilitation project](https://www.opml.co.uk/projects/evaluation-eu-supported-activities-under-warsak-hydroelectric-power-station-rehabilitation "Go to Evaluation of EU supported activities under the Warsak Hydroelectric Power Station rehabilitation project")
We carried out an impact evaluation of activities performed under four sub-components of the European Union’s (EU) Grant for the Warsak HEPS Second Rehabilitation Project. The aim of the evaluation was to assess the impact and effectiveness of the grant activities in supporting hydropower rehabilitation and community development initiatives in the regions surrounding the dam.
