# Deep Dive Dubai
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
DDD is a unique world class privately owned recreational diving facility giving divers a unique experience of the under water world. The facility gives divers the opportunity to dive at least 60 meters deep. ISG assigned AESG to conduct a Gap analysis condition survey of MEP, ELV & FLS systems, the content of report was used to enable works to be tendered and completed.
