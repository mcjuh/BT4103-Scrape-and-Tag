# Jenners Refurbishment
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
AESG were appointed by Multiplex to carry out a Whole Life Carbon Assessment for the refurbishment and extension of the Jenners department store building in Edinburgh, Scotland. The project analysed 10 refurbishment scenarios to understand the impact from a Whole Life Carbon perspective and to guide the design team towards the optimum approach.
AESG are appointed to undertake the following:
  * Whole Life Carbon Assessment in accordance with RICS Guidance
  * Embodied Carbon Assessment using OneClick LCA
  * Operational Carbon Assessment through dynamic thermal modelling in IES
