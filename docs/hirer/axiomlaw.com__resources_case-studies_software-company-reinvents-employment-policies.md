# Employment Policies Reinvented for Software Company
### Axiom attorney helped:
Roll out robust set of new employment policies
Onboard and train new hires
Slash outside employment counsel spend in half
### Challenge
A data analytics company is in a period of rapid expansion. As their footprint grows, **so does the need for robust employment policies.** They trust their law firm to roll out policies for their international markets. But they still **need support at home in the US.**
### Approach
Axiom lawyer rapidly integrates and onboards into the client’s legal team in a matter of days. She partners with HR business partners to **advise on employment agreements, termination requests, performance issues, disability questions, etc.** She also implements new policies on areas such as military leave and social media communications. As the policies are rolled out and the company hires a full-time employment lawyer, **she helps train the new hire to minimize any management burden** on the in-house team. 
### Impact 
  * Following an Axiom-led gap analysis, the client rolled out a robust set of employment policies to **keep pace with rapid growth**
  * By **delegating onboarding and training of their new full-time hire to Axiom,** the client ensured a seamless hire and transition process
  * **Slashed outside counsel spend in half** on the employment matters Axiom attorney supported
