# The Hijaz Yacht Club
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The Hijaz Yacht Club stands to be an iconic building within Triple Bay, Amaala –A landmark development for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The Hijaz Yacht Club is located within the Marina Precinct of Triple Bay, Amaala. The Marina Basin will be carved from the natural coastline along the Red Sea of Saudi Arabia to enable yachts to dock in relatively calm waters within the precinct. Overall project is proposed to be used as restaurant and leisure development, with total of four floors along with a basement level.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following the concept design stage:
  * Fire & Life Safety Strategy Design
  * MEP Design
