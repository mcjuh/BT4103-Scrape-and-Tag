# RCU Sharaan Nature Reserve
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
AESG is preparing the conservation zoning strategy for the Sharaan Nature Reserve in AlUla and providing specialist ecology advisory input regarding IUCN conservation zoning and the different approaches to achieve the desired IUCN conservation category status. AESG is leading ecological conservation zoning reviews and discussions, contributing to the sensitive micro siting of assets within the nature reserve to minimise impact on the ecology.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following:
  * Conservation zoning strategy
  * Biodiversity sensitive design guidelines
  * Conservation zoning plan reviews
  * Compensation strategy workshops
