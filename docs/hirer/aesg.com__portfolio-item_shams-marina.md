# Shams Marina
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project is comprised of the following: 4 towers (Hotel-18 floors, Serviced Apartments-25 floors, 2x Branded Residences-17 and 21 floors with shared podium of 2 floors, Beach club and Basement Parking. The total GFA is approx. 94,000 m2. Shams Marina is located at Al Reem Island, Abu Dhabi, UAE.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following:
  * Fire & Life Safety Design
  * Fire fighting facilities – internally and site wide
  * Evacuation strategy
  * Structural fire resistance requirements
  * Space separation
  * Constructional requirements
  * Fire spread-internal and externally
  * Smoke control
  * Fire detection and alarm
  * Automation extinguishing System
  * Fire water supply requirements
