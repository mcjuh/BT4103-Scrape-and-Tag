# M&A Diligence and Corporate Filings Streamlined for Big Data Firm
### Axiom attorneys helped:
Support M&A diligence to free up in-house resources
Create cross-functional trackers and templates to accelerate entity management work
Optimize corporate legal spend on foreign and domestic filing fees
### Challenge
A data and analytics firm is active in the M&A market, but they **lack the bandwidth to support thorough due diligence and entity management.** They need a corporate lawyer to direct due diligence across numerous deals and support subsidiary compliance for over 100 entities worldwide.
### Approach
Axiom installs an M&A workhorse to power through due diligence on deals for four months. The Axiom attorney **drafts letters of intent** to secure first refusal on potential transactions and adjusts existing plans according to updated numbers and findings. He also optimizes their corporate processes by **conducting pre and post M &A cleanup, running cost/benefit analyses of foreign/domestic filing fees, and creating inter-departmental trackers** to monitor hundreds of foreign authorities and licenses.
### Impact 
  * **Boosted in-house M &A capacity, **freeing up resources to focus on time-sensitive contracts and core business initiatives
  * Accelerated entity management decision-making processes by **creating cross-functional trackers and templates**
  * **Optimized corporate spend** for the client by running cost-benefit analyses of foreign vs domestic filing fees (and related tax)
