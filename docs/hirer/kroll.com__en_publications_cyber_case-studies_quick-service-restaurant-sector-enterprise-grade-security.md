##  The Challenge
A global quick-service restaurant brand operates a highly distributed environment with a lean IT team, requiring security solutions that can be deployed rapidly, scale globally and minimize operational overhead. The company had a narrow window to dedicate internal resources to a major security initiative. It needed to quickly establish consistent, enterprise-grade protection and centralized visibility, without placing additional strain on its IT team.
##  Kroll’s Solution
The business turned to Kroll’s expertise to inform its security platform selection and deployment approach. Based on its distributed operating model, limited internal capacity and need for rapid deployment, Kroll recommended CrowdStrike as the optimal solution to deliver enterprise-grade protection, centralized visibility and scalable coverage across endpoints and environments.
Kroll’s deep CrowdStrike expertise and managed delivery model enabled the organization to achieve enterprise-grade security in under 24 hours, without diverting focus from core business priorities.
Key elements included:
  * Rapid rollout of CrowdStrike Falcon® across endpoints and environments
  * Full NG SIEM implementation, log source onboarding and operationalization
  * Platform configuration and tuning aligned to the company’s operating environment
  * Seamless coordination with third-party providers and geographically distributed locations


The entire deployment was completed in under 24 hours, delivering an exceptional turnaround for an enterprise environment of this scale.
##  What's Next 
As the company continues to grow and its digital footprint expands, it is working with Kroll to enhance visibility, strengthen resilience, and scale security operations—ensuring its environment remains protected as the business evolves.
##  The Impact
###  Accelerated Time to Value
The client gained immediate value, achieving enterprise-wide CrowdStrike deployment and SIEM implementation in under 24 hours, powered by Kroll’s deep CrowdStrike expertise and managed delivery model.

Kroll fully owned the deployment end-to-end, from planning and configuration through execution and operationalization, coordinating across third-party providers and regional locations. Kroll has significantly advanced the company’s security posture, positioning it to evolve its security strategy in alignment with evolving operational needs.
