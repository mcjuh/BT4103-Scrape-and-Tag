# 150 Holborn
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The new 150 Holborn development is an eight-storey landmark HQ replacing a 1980’s office block and incorporates ground floor retail and an adjacent residential block comprising 13 apartments. The 150 Holborn development is approximately 182,955ft2 and is a mixed-use scheme for international consultancy Dar Group. The building will feature a central entrance lobby, a street facing café, exhibition and reception space, an atrium with feature staircase and sweeping roof pavilion with roof gardens.
