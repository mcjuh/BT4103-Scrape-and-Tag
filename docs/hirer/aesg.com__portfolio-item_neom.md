# NEOM
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project forms a district of the larger NEOM masterplan. The overall area of the Silver Beach masterplan is 35 km2. The mixed-use development is to include residential, recreational, institutional and infrastructure facilities.
AESG was appointed by the masterplanner to conduct an Environmental and Social Impact Assessment following IFC Performance Standards.
  * Literature Review
  * Terrestrial and Marine Ecology Surveys
  * Hydrodynamic, flushing and sediment plume modelling
  * Air quality, soil and noise testing and measurements
  * Environmental and Social Impact Assessments
  * Environmental and Social Management Plans
