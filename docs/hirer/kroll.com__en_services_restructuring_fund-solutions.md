# Fund Solutions
Creating efficiencies, preserving value, and maximizing returns throughout a fund’s lifecycle. 
Kroll’s Fund Solutions practice provides fund lifecycle management expertise to help General Partners (GP) and Limited Partners (LP) address the critical middle and later stages of a fund’s life. We deliver end-of-fund solutions that create efficiencies, preserve value, and maximize realizations when traditional structures no longer serve investor interests.
Our services include establishing realization funds, offering replacement GP services, and delivering secondary market advisory strategies that extend timelines, reduce costs, and realign incentives. We also provide fund governance support and senior-led oversight to restore transparency, resolve disputes, and unlock liquidity.
In today’s private capital markets, fund managers and investors face mounting challenges as funds approach maturity. Rising costs, regulatory pressures, and governance concerns often converge, making it harder to protect and enhance value. Kroll’s expertise in cost-efficient fund wind-down, dispute resolution in funds, and restructuring underperforming investments ensures that stakeholders achieve stronger performance and investor value maximization.

## How We Can Help
Kroll supports funds through the critical middle and end stages of the lifecycle. Our tailored solutions preserve value, maximize realizations, and help clients navigate complex challenges. We focus on two phases — Realization and Secondaries — with five core offerings designed to address the unique issues that arise during these stages.
### Realization Phase
Focused on middle and end-of-life strategies that protect investor value and resolve challenges:
  * **Director Led Realizations:** Senior-led, hands-on oversight through independent directors or committee members to strengthen governance, resolve conflict, and drive performance.
  * **Contentious Situations:** Expert negotiation, dispute resolution, and forensic support to minimize value erosion and manage fraud, mismanagement, or disputes.
  * **GP Replacement Services:** Acting as a replacement GP or investment manager to restore trust, realign incentives, and safeguard investor interests.
  * **End of Fund Life Solutions:** Structural wind-down strategies that extend runway, reduce costs, and maximize realizations.


### Secondaries Phase
Designed to unlock liquidity and extend investment potential through market-driven solutions:
  * **Direct Sales and Continuation Funds:** Tailored strategies such as LP stake sales and continuation funds, providing liquidity for exiting LPs while enabling ongoing participation for those who remain.
