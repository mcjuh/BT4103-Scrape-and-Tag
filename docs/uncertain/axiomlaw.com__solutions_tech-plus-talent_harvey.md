### What types of work is Harvey best suited for?
Harvey excels at research-intensive and drafting-heavy workflows, including multi- jurisdictional analysis, regulatory tracking, policy development, and contract drafting/redrafting. It is particularly effective for early-stage research, document analysis, and synthesizing large volumes of legal information. 
### What kinds of tasks can Harvey support day-to-day?
Harvey supports legal research, document review, contract analysis, due diligence, and drafting of memos or policies. It enables lawyers to quickly extract insights, summarize materials, and generate structured first-pass outputs across a wide range of legal matters. 
### Can Harvey support multi-jurisdictional or global work?
Yes. Harvey is designed to handle work across jurisdictions and languages, making it well- suited for global regulatory analysis and cross-border legal matters. 
### How does Harvey fit into existing legal workflows?
Harvey is designed to integrate into existing legal processes, including research, drafting, and document review workflows, enabling teams to adopt AI without significant disruption. 
### What value can clients expect early in an engagement?
Clients typically see immediate time savings in research and drafting, with faster turnaround times for initial analysis. Efficiencies compound as workflows are refined and scaled over time. 
### How does Axiom legal talent use Harvey on engagements?
Axiom deploys Harvey within a lawyer-led engagement model where Axiom legal professionals partner with the client’s in-house team to design workflows, guide usage, and validate outputs—ensuring results are accurate, tailored, and aligned to client objectives.
### How quickly can Axiom deploy Harvey on a project?
Most engagements can begin within days. Axiom rapidly aligns legal talent who establish workflows and start delivering outputs, with iterative refinement as the engagement scales. 
### How do Axiom lawyers interact with Harvey?
Lawyers use Harvey through a natural language interface—asking questions, prompting analysis, and generating drafts—then refining outputs within structured workflows to meet client-specific requirements. 
### How accurate are the results?
Harvey produces strong first-pass outputs, including research summaries and draft documents. Accuracy is validated via Axiom legal professionals who review, refine, and contextualize outputs working with the client’s in-house team before final delivery. 
### Does Harvey replace lawyers?
No. Harvey enhances efficiency but does not replace legal judgment. Lawyers remain responsible for interpretation, validation, and final deliverables, with human oversight embedded throughout the workflow. 
### What does “first-pass analysis” mean in practice?
Harvey generates an initial, structured output—such as a research summary, issue list, or draft document—that accelerates the workflow. Axiom lawyers then refine and validate this output for final delivery. 
### How does the calibration period for AI enablement projects work with Harvey?
Harvey’s calibration period is a structured onboarding phase led by Axiom legal talent to tailor the system to a client’s specific legal workflows, standards, and content. During this period, Axiom legal talent configures and refines Harvey using the client’s documents, templates, and preferred ways of working—similar to how a new team member is trained on firm-specific practices. This typically involves:
  * Aligning outputs to firm standards (tone, formatting, legal reasoning)
  * Testing real use cases and workflows to ensure accuracy and usefulness
  * Iterative feedback loops between Axiom legal talent, users, and the Harvey team to improve performance
  * Validating results before broader rollout
  * The goal of calibration is to ensure that, by the time Harvey is deployed at scale, it produces high-quality, reliable, and context-aware outputs that reflect the client’s specific requirements— not just generic legal responses

### What security standards does Harvey meet?
Harvey meets leading enterprise-grade security and compliance standards, including SOC 2 Type II and ISO/IEC 27001 certifications, which are independently audited on an ongoing basis. In addition, Harvey aligns with key global privacy and regulatory frameworks such as GDPR and CCPA, supporting its use by large, multinational organizations.
Beyond certifications, Harvey employs a comprehensive security program that includes:
  * Encryption of data at rest and in transit
  * Strict role-based access controls and logical data separation
  * Zero data retention / no training on customer data by default
  * Regular third-party penetration testing and continuous monitoring


Together, these controls ensure that client data is protected to the highest industry standards and handled in accordance with enterprise security and privacy expectations.
### How is client data handled?
Client data is managed in accordance with enterprise-grade security and privacy standards. Harvey deployments are designed to protect sensitive information and support secure, compliant use in legal environments. 
### Will client data be used to train AI models?
No. Axiom’s approach prioritizes data privacy and confidentiality, with strict controls to ensure client data is not used to train public AI models. 
### How does Axiom ensure responsible AI use?
Axiom embeds Harvey within a controlled, human-in-the-loop framework. Lawyers guide workflows, validate outputs, and maintain accountability—ensuring AI is used responsibly and in line with professional standards. 
### What happens to client data when a project ends?
Legora follows defined data lifecycle and retention policies to ensure that client data is handled appropriately at the conclusion of an engagement, in line with contractual and regulatory requirements. 
### How does Axiom ensure responsible AI implementation?
Axiom takes a careful, research-driven approach to AI selection and deployment, focusing on tools that improve legal work while keeping lawyers in control, protecting client data, and delivering practical value in defined use cases. 
### How quickly can Axiom stand up a Legora-enabled project?
Most projects can begin within days, with an initial calibration phase completed on a small contract subset before scaling to the full population.
### How accurate are the results?
Accuracy is driven by the upfront calibration process. By refining the question set early, Axiom ensures high consistency across the full dataset rather than relying on post-hoc QA. 
### Can Legora integrate with our existing systems?
Yes. Legora integrates with common document management systems (e.g., iManage, SharePoint) and Microsoft Word, allowing Axiom to work within existing client environments. 
### Is this replacing lawyers?
No. Axiom’s model is explicitly lawyer-led. Legora enhances efficiency and consistency, while legal professionals supervised by the client’s in-house team remain responsible for judgment, interpretation, and final outputs.
