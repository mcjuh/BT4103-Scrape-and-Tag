### Education
  * MPhil, archaeology, University of Cambridge
  * BA, archaeology, University of Leicester


Vuk Trifkovic drives BCG X’s green growth acceleration coverage in Europe, the Middle East, and South America. He joined Boston Consulting Group in 2016 as part of BCG Digital Ventures. In 2020, Vuk co-founded the BCG Green Ventures offering with a mission to bring the business-building approach to urgent climate tech projects. Since then, he has led the BCG X sustainability agenda across a range of topics including carbon dioxide removal (CDR), large-scale renewables, industrial decarbonization, energy efficiency, heating electrification, circularity models in fashion, and shipping decarbonization.
In addition, he has 20 years’ startup experience in Berlin and London with a focus on geospatial data problems, as well as several tech industry analysis houses.
