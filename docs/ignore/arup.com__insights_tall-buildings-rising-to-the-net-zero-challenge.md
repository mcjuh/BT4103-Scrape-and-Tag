#  Tall buildings: rising to the net zero challenge 
This report investigates the key strategies that can be used to enable the City of London’s tall building cluster to become more environmentally sustainable, and to rise to the net zero challenge.
Last updated: July 2023

London and New York, renowned for their iconic city skylines, are recognised globally as centres of international business and commerce, attracting people from all over the world for both work and leisure.
This report explores why we build tall, and the opportunities and challenges that come with it. We compare the City of London’s Tall building cluster with Lower Manhattan’s to determine what they can learn from each other; what has been driving change and what are the key opportunities for improvement.
The report was produced in collaboration with EC BID.
