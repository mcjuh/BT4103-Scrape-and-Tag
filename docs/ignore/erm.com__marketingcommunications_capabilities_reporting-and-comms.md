# Reporting & Comms
####### 
Meticulous, beautifully-crafted reports from materiality to copy and design to amplification
## There’s a reason we’ve done hundreds of sustainability and impact reports for organizations all around the world.
We have been creating compelling sustainability reports since reporting first became an idea, and you can see our expertise in the integrity, craft and attention to detail we bring to every one of them. We also believe that it’s more than just a report. It’s a content hub. Your reports are full of compelling stories you and your team worked hard to make happen. But too often that content sits hidden inside the report and your organization doesn’t get the credit it deserves. Those stories could be pulling double duty in other channels from internal comms to social media. We’ll show you how to promote your report content to drive engagement.
##### Areas of Expertise
###### Content Strategy & Writing
Working together with your team, our expert writers and account leads start by developing an organizational outline and structure for your report based on key priorities and narratives, as well as all required content and data. We are fluent in the complexities of report creation and iteration and accustomed to sometimes dozens of SMEs contributing content and narrative. Reports are hard. We make them less hard.
###### Report Design
We take pride in our report designs and bring a high level of craft to each and every one. That's a lot of words and data, as they say. There is an art to making large amounts of copy and pages of data inviting, and to maintaining a cohesive visual thread throughout. We also understand the importance of branding and the need to ensure that reports are true to the larger brand look and feel.
###### Data Visualization
We have a strong respect and appreciation for a well-designed infographic and its potential to distill and communicate. And, as an added bonus, a great infographic is an important piece of communication that can pull double duty as content on social, websites and other communications.
###### Marketing Plan
Working with investor relations, HR and content teams, we identify relevant key audience activity in order to develop marketing channels and tactics driving owned, paid and earned media. We put that plan into action, testing, learning and iterating for optimization along the way.
###### Report Surround Sound
To further broadcast report content, we create landing pages and other website assets, external and internal digital campaigns, and ongoing thought leadership, from podcast episodes to speaking opportunities at key events.
