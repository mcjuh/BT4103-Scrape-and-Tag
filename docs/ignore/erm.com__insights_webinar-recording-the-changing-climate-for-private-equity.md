# Recording: The Changing Climate for Private Equity
**The SustainAbility Institute by ERM** and held a webinar on Thursday, June 24 to explore our new report, [_The Changing Climate for Private Equity_](https://www.erm.com/insights/changing-climate-for-private-equity/).
We presented the report’s findings, examining how private equity firms view climate-related risks and opportunities and what they expect of companies in their portfolios in terms of net zero goals and climate risk management and disclosure.This webinar featured a panel discussion and a Q&A with the following senior business leaders:
  * [Dazzle Bhujwala](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dazzle-bhujwala/ "Dazzle Bhujwala"), Director, Investor Network, Ceres
  * [Elizabeth Seeger](https://www.linkedin.com/in/elizabethseeger/), Managing Director, Sustainable Investing, KKR
  * [Jaideep Das](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jaideep-das-3299481/), Partner, Global M&A Services and Finance Sector Lead (EMEA), ERM
  * [Mátyás Csiky](https://www.linkedin.com/in/matyascsiky/), ESG & Sustainability, Regional Responsible Europe, Partners Group
  * [Michael Eisenberg](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michael-e-57202a53/), Head of ESG Integration, NY State Common
