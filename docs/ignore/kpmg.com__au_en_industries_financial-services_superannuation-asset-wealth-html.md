#  Superannuation, Asset & Wealth Management 
KPMG's Asset & Wealth Management practice offers a range of services to superannuation funds, life offices, employers and fund trustees. 


## We help you to understand, plan and respond to challenges today, and into the future
Drawing on our deep and extensive local and global expertise and insights, KPMG can provide advice and guidance in relation to:
  * Investment management 
  * Superannuation 
  * Product design and distribution 
  * Investor support services


Our clients include superannuation funds, managed funds across all asset classes, the wealth businesses of banks and life insurers, custodians and administrators (both internal and external).
###  How KPMG can support your sector 
Our team of professionals has deep experience in superannuation and wealth management to help you transform for the future.
###  Mergers & consolidation 
Our team of industry professionals help to you identify valuable consolidation opportunities and coordinate the end-to-end merger process, including undertaking due diligence to achieve the best outcomes for your organisation
###  Regulatory change 
Current regulatory themes include governance, accountability, sustainability and resilience, and KPMG analyses how changes will impact your organisation and helps you implement an effective change program to ensure compliance.
###  Insurance in Super 
From actuarial pricing to product development, tender management, governance and operations, our team specialises in all facets of insurance in super.
###  Retirement 
With expertise across the legal and actuarial environment, we provide clients with targeted advice on product development and service offerings to suit members’ retirement strategies.
