# Community Engagement
Helping people in our communities and beyond.
## A Commitment to Social Impact and Responsible Citizenship
At BDO, we believe that our success is inseparable from the success of the communities in which we operate and where our people live. We are committed to a vision of corporate citizenship in which we share responsibility for community well-being, a position we’ve proudly held for many years.
Our people play an essential part in delivering on our commitment to positive social impact. They contribute through firm-organized volunteer activities, their own commitments supporting causes they are passionate about, as leaders of non-profit and community organizations, and much more. 
## BDO Counts
Through our social impact program, BDO Counts, everyone has the opportunity to make a difference. 
BDO Counts is employee-driven and features a blend of nationwide programs as well as local service initiatives. Our network of BDO Counts coordinators serve as community engagement champions, helping to scale our programs by planning volunteer events and other charitable opportunities. 
By focusing on four cause areas chosen by our people, BDO Counts aligns personal interests with firm priorities. The cause areas enable us to focus our time, talents, and resources to give more intentionally to increase our impact.
### **Children & Youth**
We enhance youth well-being through supporting organizations that improve children’s academic, social, and emotional well-being.
### **Education & Literacy**
We drive equal educational and economic opportunities by supporting organizations helping people advance academically and professionally.
### **Human Rights**
We create thriving, inclusive communities by supporting organizations improving food security, housing, economic opportunity, and belonging.
### **Environment**
We accelerate environmental sustainability through support of environmental champions and innovators committed to helping the planet thrive.

## Our Cause Partner
BDO is proud to collaborate with our first cause partner, Big Brothers Big Sisters of America. This cause partnership marks a major milestone in BDO’s social impact strategy and will help strengthen our impact within our Children & Youth cause area by helping to create a pathway to economic empowerment and brighter prospects for the next generation through mentorship. Learn more about how we are nurturing tomorrow’s leaders together.
[Learn Morechevron_right](https://www.bdo.com/insights/press-releases/bdo-usa-announces-big-brothers-big-sisters-of-america-as-first-cause-partner)
Today we had about a dozen young people come in to spend time with about two dozen BDOers. They came very excited and very eager to learn. We wanted to get them to know one another, kind of get out of their comfort zones and stretch their networking skills. Oftentimes they're so busy with school and they don't have that special capital readily available to them. I wouldn't be here without the mentors that helped me throughout my career. Giving back and taking the time to help the students with their journey makes a difference. Being able to have someone older than you help guide the ropes because as young people, it's very important to have someone that we can look up to. It was awesome. The networking, the small groups, I got to meet so many great interns. I got to meet so many great people from like different career paths as well. I feel like the reason I'm here at BDO today was because one of the first lessons I learned from my mentor was accountability. Being accountable and understanding that like to be somewhere in life or to get to the thing that you want to get to, you have to work for it. A career path is not linear. There are going to be trials, tribulations, but it really does take the ability to ask someone else for help and for really having that person that's willing to give that help to them. The one big thing that I learned from today and would take with me for my future career is continue to work hard, be myself, and don't be afraid to ask questions. Collaborating with Big Brothers Big Sisters is so meaningful to BDO because we get to focus on the talent pipeline. It really allows us to prepare them to grow with confidence in a time that's really important in their lives. They've got big questions to answer as their futures are just right in front of them, and we really hope to spark some curiosity and inspire them to explore careers in this profession.
## Scholarships for Future CPAs
In alignment with our Education & Literacy cause area, we are proud to support the next generation of accounting professionals. Through two scholarship programs offered in conjunction with the AICPA Foundation, we’re dedicated to supporting students throughout different stages of their education journey.
### BDO USA AICPA Foundation Scholarship for Future CPAs
This scholarship provides financial assistance to outstanding students to encourage their pursuit of accounting as a major and their ultimate entry into the profession.
[Learn Morechevron_right](https://www.thiswaytocpa.com/education/scholarship-search/future-cpas-scholarship/)
### BDO USA AICPA Foundation Two-year Transfer Scholarship
This scholarship provides financial assistance to students looking to transfer from a two-year college to a four-year institution to complete their degree in accounting or an accounting-related field.
[Learn Morechevron_right](https://www.thiswaytocpa.com/education/scholarship-search/aicpa-foundation-two-year-transfer-scholarship/)
