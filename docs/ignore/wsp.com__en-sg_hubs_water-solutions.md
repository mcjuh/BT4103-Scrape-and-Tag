We are working closely with our clients to reshape resilience, by protecting water sources, optimizing use, and designing systems that are resilient and ready for change. Together, we safeguard water for future generations. From restoring ecosystems and remediating groundwater to treating wastewater and delivering infrastructure, we create sustainable solutions that work with nature and bring long-term value.
Global population growth, increasing water consumption, aging infrastructure, and the impacts of climate change are challenging the availability, quality and distribution of water.
We help governments, utilities, industries and communities to respond.
Our teams are supporting clients to manage, operate and maintain supply and distribution systems, optimize water use and reuse, and protect precious resources and environments.
We enable them to harness the awesome power of natural processes to enhance water quality and resilience, and create a sustainable, water-secure future for all.

## Empower industry with circular water solutions
Industries face increasing pressure to optimize water use and meet regulatory requirements. We support companies across all sectors in achieving compliance while improving operational efficiency through sustainable and circular water management.
Our work includes assessing environmental risks, guiding responsible water withdrawals, and supporting permitting and compliance strategies, especially in areas facing regulatory or contamination challenges. Our expert process engineering teams provide tailored solutions for water reuse, wastewater treatment, and resource recovery, helping industries reduce waste and extend the lifecycle of water.
By integrating circular economy principles, we enable businesses to minimize environmental impact, recover valuable resources, and build a resilient, future-ready operations.

#### Digital platforms
Our digital platforms perform a critical role in underpinning our advice and services. These world-leading tools allow us to collaborate across design teams, as well as with clients and the communities they serve; ultimately delivering stronger designs and advice.
FloodVue™ 
Visualizing flood risk through augmented reality
WSP’s FloodVue™ is an augmented reality tool that helps communities and landowners visualise flood scenarios by creating immersive images of severe weather impacts before they occur.
Smart Drains
Sensor-based drains to mitigate flood risk and boost resilience.
Smart Drain fits ultrasonic sensors in high-flood-risk gullies, sending real-time silt and flow data to IoT dashboards and flagging blockages so crews can clear the right drains at the right time.
Nature Vista
Turning nature-related risk into opportunity
WSP’s AI‑enabled platform that connects nature and asset data to turn complex environmental insight into clear, actionable decisions across the full project lifecycle.
Orbi INSIGHT
Digital asset management platform combining systems and applications
WSP’s Orbi INSIGHT is a digital platform for stakeholders across the organization, combining two back-end systems and four applications to support different workflows.
