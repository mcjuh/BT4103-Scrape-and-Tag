Our experienced energy team delivers tailored solutions to help clients meet energy efficiency targets and comply with international regulations and standards.
## The Importance of Energy Audits
To achieve net zero carbon in the built environment, addressing emissions from existing buildings is crucial. Globally, buildings contribute significantly to energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions. Energy audits play a key role in:
### Baseline Assessment
Understanding current energy performance to set benchmarks against industry standards.
### Regulatory Compliance
Meeting the requirements of international building codes and energy efficiency standards.
### Cost Savings
Identifying opportunities for energy efficiency improvements that lower operational costs.
### Sustainability Goals
Supporting corporate sustainability objectives and carbon reduction commitments.
## Our Approach
We conduct detailed energy audits following internationally recognised standards, covering buildings, industrial activities, and transport. By adopting a fabric-first approach, we prioritise improvements to the building envelope before considering mechanical and electrical systems. This ensures:
### Improved Energy Performance
Reducing energy consumption through efficient design and operation.
### Systematic Planning
Developing comprehensive retrofit strategies that deliver long-term results.
### Enhanced Comfort Conditions
Implementing solutions that improve indoor environmental quality, such as increased daylight and better thermal comfort.
### Accurate Measurement and Verification
Providing data to measure and report on the effectiveness of retrofit activities.

## Building Energy Efficiency Certificates (BEECs)
We help clients comply with global disclosure programs, which require valid energy efficiency certificates for the sale or lease of large commercial office spaces.
### Energy Disclosure Compliance
Ensuring clients meet their obligations under international disclosure programs.
### Tenancy Lighting Assessments (TLAs)
Conducting TLAs as part of energy efficiency certificate requirements.
## Carbon Footprinting
We calculate greenhouse gas emissions and support carbon reduction initiatives in line with international requirements.
### Greenhouse Gas Emissions Inventory

### Carbon Neutral Certification Support
Assisting clients with certification under international programmes like Climate Neutral.
### Carbon Reduction Strategies
Identifying opportunities to reduce emissions and achieve net zero targets.
