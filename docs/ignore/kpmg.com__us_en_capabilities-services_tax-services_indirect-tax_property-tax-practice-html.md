##  Cut through property tax complexity 
The KPMG Property Tax service helps companies keep up with local tax requirements, uncover opportunities for savings, and avoid penalties and overpayments. As businesses add locations or invest in new facilities, compliance can become more complex and cumbersome. Our property tax experience and knowledge of local regulations makes meeting this challenge simpler, faster, and more accurate.
Our property and compliance service teams provide property tax guidance and support to businesses of any size and in any industry. Key service offerings include:
### Property Tax Advisory Services
These may include initial property assessments and site reviews, exemption reviews, development projects, M&A planning, and property tax forecasting.
### Compliance Services
We can assist with property tax return preparation, assessment review, tax bill management, fixed asset reviews, and audit defense services.
### Tax Valuation Services
We can help conduct reacquisition-value due diligence, taxable value studies, purchase price allocations, full property tax appraisals, and tax valuation support..
### Complex Property Appeals Management
We support personal property tax appeals through tax valuation analysis and negotiations, value reviews and protest, board hearings, and litigation support.
### Real Estate Tax Services
We also perform real estate value reviews through the same mix of tax valuation analysis and negotiations, reviews and protest, board hearings, and litigation support.
