#  KPMG Global M&A Leadership Program 
A transformative client leadership experience for senior dealmakers across large corporate organizations and private equity funds.
##  Beyond the deal. Beyond transformation. 
We’re living through a time of extraordinary disruption and change — economic shifts, geopolitical tensions, regulatory changes, AI and tech disruption, and ESG considerations fundamentally alter the M&A landscape reshaping everything around us. And for leaders in M&A, that means needing to navigate complexity with clarity and conviction.
As a result, future dealmakers need to go beyond the technical skills required to execute the deal. They need deal-making capabilities that can reliably maximize value creation, adeptly exploit disruptive trends and shape strategies that align commercial ambition with long-term impact.
But when strategies fall short, it’s rarely just about execution. More often, it’s because we’ve underestimated the human side of leadership, stakeholder management, trust-building, the power of relationships and networks.
The KPMG Global M&A Leadership Program is designed for emerging leaders to focus on the human side of leadership — building belief, gaining consensus, and creating followership, so they can lead transformative deals today and in the future.
By bringing together the next generation of senior M&A leaders, we aim to create a supportive community that fosters confidence, influence, and a robust network. This program is about shaping the future of M&A leadership.

## Designed for future dealmakers
Each year, KPMG brings together a selected cohort of M&A leaders from corporates and private equity funds, across different sectors and geographies, who are ready to build their confidence in ambiguity, authority in the boardroom, and strategic vision beyond the deal thesis, while growing their network of global peers who will challenge and stretch their thinking.
Our program brings together a room of high-caliber professionals who are ready to step into the next chapter of their leadership. Attendees will engage in a curated curriculum across two immersive residential modules, followed up with hands-on coaching and ongoing connection.
The KPMG Global M&A leadership program can benefit both dealmakers and their organizations:
  * Understand the macro trends impacting dealmaking with a lens on value creation.
  * Accelerate leadership development, career progression and succession planning.
  * Build essential skills needed to effectively lead deals with C-suite members and investment committees.
  * Learn from and build trusted relationships with an active global network of industry peers and deal experts.
