#  How Councils are navigating financial pressures and building resilience 
Insights from the 2026 Public Sector Network Local Government Roadshow on how councils can strengthen financial resilience. 


## Harnessing opportunities to build financial resilience
In 2025–2026, KPMG and the Public Sector Network brought together local government leaders from across Australia, alongside private sector specialists, to explore the evolving challenges and opportunities facing the sector.
The roadshows highlighted the growing impact of financial constraints on councils, while surfacing practical pathways to strengthen resilience, through smarter resource management, targeted investment and through innovative thinking and the application of emerging AI technologies.
Attendees contributed to live polling across key themes, including financial sustainability, immediate and long-term pressures, and service optimisation.
These insights provide a clear view of the local government sector’s priorities and the actions required to build more sustainable, future-ready organisations.

## Key challenges facing local government
  1. ###### A structural funding gap
A growing gap between constrained revenue and rising costs is the defining issue for councils, with many unable to sustainably fund operations without external support.
  2. ###### Rising demand and community expectations
Councils are under increasing pressure to deliver more complex, higher-quality services, driven by population growth, ageing communities and changing expectations.
  3. ###### Ageing infrastructure and asset burden
A large, deteriorating asset base is driving significant maintenance and renewal costs, compounding long-term financial risk.
  4. ###### An operating model under strain
Skills shortages, rising cost-to-serve and legacy systems are limiting productivity and slowing councils’ ability to respond to change.
