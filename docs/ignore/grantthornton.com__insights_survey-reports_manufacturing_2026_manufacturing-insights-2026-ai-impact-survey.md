#  Manufacturing insights: 2026 AI Impact Survey
## Operational AI with high risks and low controls 
## Get more control, and get more value
Manufacturers are planning to focus AI on their operations, but many do not have a plan for when things go wrong. Our survey report shows how manufacturers need to close the gap between AI progress and control.

**What’s in the report?**
  * 48% of manufacturers are still just piloting AI, versus 34% across industries
  * 14% report accelerated innovation, versus 31% across industries
  * 12% are confident they could pass an independent AI governance audit, versus 22% across industries
  * 45% say competitive pressure is the primary force driving AI adoption
