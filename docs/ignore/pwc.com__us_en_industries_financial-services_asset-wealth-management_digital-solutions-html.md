## How PwC can help
Our team of professionals, with extensive experience in the asset management industry, employs a technology-driven, data-focused approach to tailor services to your operational and management needs.
With a consultative mindset, our specialists design your unique collection of services to complement each other, reducing duplicative processes and efforts by your people. Whether for your portfolio company or fund family operations, or your own finance functions, we work with you at your pace, ensuring that at each step, we have the right people to meet your present needs, and the bedrock established to expand capabilities, when you choose.
We use our data ecosystem, custom built for the asset management industry, to provide services in areas requiring specialized skills, helping streamline complex processes, enhance visibility, and enable everyone to focus on the future.
  * **Embedding regulatory specialists and technologies** in processes to keep ahead of the curve on evolving policies and timely reporting.
  * **Provide tech-enabled solutions to complex, specialized areas** (like private asset valuation) embedded with new technologies, such as generative AI, to reduce time and cost of current manual functions.
  * **Supporting and providing oversight functions** on behalf of the manager, that may be time consuming or require industry or business expertise freeing management for other functions.
  * **Enhance visibility and streamline complex processes** , including those with multiple providers, data sources, or multi-step actions, for faster outcomes.
  * **Develop a flexible talent model** that keeps you working with the people you know and trust, while supplementing your team with access to PwC's substantial network of professionals.

### External Reporting
We specialize in automatically crafting timely financial reports tailored for your external stakeholders, including investors, regulators, and analysts. Our experienced team collaborates with you to align your financial statements, regulatory filings, performance reports, and other essential disclosures with legal requirements, while building investor trust and aiding strategic decision-making. Trust PwC to enhance your reporting processes and keep you ahead in the dynamic AWM landscape.
**Benefits**
  * **Extensive knowledge and compliance proficiency:** Leverage PwC's deep understanding of financial reporting standards to effectively navigate the complexities of global regulations. We work to help your reports be accurate and compliant with evolving standards, positioning you advantageously.
  * **Enhanced quality through improved standardization:** With systemic data validation checks and business rule engines, our deliverables maintain high quality that supports thorough industry review. Our approach is designed to adapt to changing industry standards and technological advancements.
  * **Transform reporting with advanced technology:** Enhance your reporting process with PwC's innovative technology and proprietary tools. Achieve greater efficiency and accuracy, reducing errors and streamlining your reporting cycles for timely, precise results.


### Internal Reporting
In the fast-paced global markets, staying ahead requires more than just data — it demands clarity, precision, and strategic foresight. Our internal reporting service provides your management and board members with the resources needed to monitor performance, make informed strategic decisions, and effectively manage risk. With PwC, you receive precise and timely insights to assess operations, understand resource allocation, and align your operations with your strategic goals.
**Benefits**
  * **Expertise for strategic advantage:** Access to PwC’s expertise in managing and understanding asset managers and their big data to enhance collaboration within your organization, unlocking insights that support more informed strategic decisions and positioning for your firm.
  * **Customized services for dynamic growth:** Benefit from personalized services that adapt to your firm’s specific needs, supporting your internal reporting as your business grows and develops.
  * **Advanced technology for improved efficiency:** Enhance your data management with PwC’s innovative technology, increasing efficiency and accuracy to provide your firm with dependable insights for informed decision-making.


### NAV Oversight
Precision is everything. Our suite of NAV Oversight solutions simplifies the oversight of fund administration and NAV calculations. We bring industry and finance expertise on accounting and controls to your process, automating monitoring, streamlining reconciliations and recalculations, and assessing trends over time to improve the NAV signoff process, so your team can focus on growth and making the finance function a key strategic enabler of the asset management business.
**Benefits**
  * **Extensive knowledge and experience:** Rely on PwC’s deep understanding of the asset management sector to enhance your NAV reviews. Our experienced professionals are well-versed in complex NAV challenges and ever-evolving regulatory landscapes, providing accuracy and integrity that you can trust.
  * **Advanced technology and tools:** PwC’s innovative technology and proprietary tools automate routine tasks, helping to reduce human effort, allowing your team to focus on strategic activities.
  * **Grow with actionable insights:** Clear workflows and tailored disclosures help you to identify trends across your fund family, offering insights to improve your operations and manage costs effectively.


### Tax Managed Services

By providing access to resources, processes and technology, our scalable solutions can help fill the gaps in your tax department while delivering the cost certainty you need.
**Benefits**

  * **Advancing processes and technology:** Leverage data-driven process and single-workflow technology solutions to help enhance tax insights.
  * **A talent model that helps provide flexibility:** Keep working with the people you know and trust reducing risk and keeping ahead of regulatory changes, while maintaining legacy knowledge, all while supplementing your team with access to PwC’s substantial network of tax professionals.


### Valuation Solutions
Precise valuations are more than just numbers — they're the foundation of trust and strategic advantage. Our valuation services on hard to value assets get you faster, more transparent valuation reporting to help transform and grow your business.
**Benefits**
  * **Focus effort on analysis:** By bringing critical data together with prebuilt methodologies and models, automate valuations to spend less time on preparation and more time on analysis.
  * **Increased visibility:** Streamline your entire workflow to increase visibility for all stakeholders, with easy to scale valuations and comprehensive workflows that support collaboration and transparency throughout the end-to-end process.
  * **Extensive knowledge and compliance proficiency:** We know valuation governance because we do it ourselves. Access valuation specialists to help guide you on a wide variety of topics — from onboarding and execution to review and challenge processes.

**Benefits**

### AML/KYC
Financial Institutions have had anti-money laundering (AML) and economic sanctions processes in place for quite some time. But many still lack the integrated, risk-based, and cost-effective program that boards, regulators, and investors are increasingly seeking. Our specialized AML/KYC processes harness technology to create and maintain effective and sustainable AML/KYC programs for asset managers.
**Benefits**
  * **Comprehensive compliance:** Partner with PwC to leverage our deep regulatory knowledge and holistic AML/KYC solutions, integrating data-driven, technology enabled compliance with your risk management strategy for robust protection.
  * **Cutting-edge technology and efficiency:** Transform your AML/KYC processes with PwC's advanced data analytics and automation tools, ensuring precise client verification and streamlined transaction monitoring.
  * **Global Insight and continuous support:** Stay ahead of regulatory changes with PwC's global expertise, while benefiting from ongoing training and support to maintain a resilient compliance framework that earns client and regulatory trust.
