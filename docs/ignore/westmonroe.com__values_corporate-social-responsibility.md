# Corporate Social Responsibility
At West Monroe, we’re passionate about making a real impact—for our clients and the communities we serve. 
## The West Monroe Social Responsibility Approach
At West Monroe, we don’t just talk about social responsibility—we live it. We’re thankful for the opportunities we’ve had, and we believe in giving back to the communities that support us. By harnessing our collective talent, experience, and resources, we’re committed to making a tangible, lasting impact. Our approach blends large-scale, community-focused programs with grassroots, employee-driven initiatives that reflect the causes our people and communities care about. As we grow, we continue to evolve our scope to include governance frameworks, environmental sustainability, and a wholistic approach to supporting our communities.
## Here’s How We Do It
We believe in making giving and volunteerism personal. That’s why we support our employees and the causes they care about through our philanthropic programs. We focus our efforts around two key pillars that align with our company strategy: Building the Next Generation of Leaders and Increasing Equity. Together, these initiatives empower our people to create a meaningful impact.

### Individual Match Program​
We match employee donations to approved nonprofit organizations at 100% ​of their personal donation
### Dollars for Doers​
We encourage and reward employees who volunteer in their communities​ by making a donation based on the number of volunteer hours they log each year
### Giving Friday​
Every Friday we highlight organizations that employees care about and help them promote and request donations for those organizations
### Community Impact Fund
Aligned to our pillars, we select nonprofit partners and provide holistic support to their organizations as part of a three-year commitment​
### Day of Service
Once a year, all West Monroe employees come together to participate in a firmwide day of action where they volunteer with local organizations ​
### Pro Bono Projects
We actively support organizations with skills-based volunteerism at no cost​ to them, and encourage employees to seek these opportunities as well
## Sustainability at West Monroe
At West Monroe, we’re committed to building a sustainable future for our clients, communities, and firm. We approach sustainability as an ongoing journey grounded in continuous improvement and measurable impact. Since achieving carbon neutrality in 2019, we have continued to take a practical, business-aligned approach to understanding our environmental footprint, focusing on actions that reduce our impact and are both effective and sustainable across our operations.
Our commitment to reaching net-zero carbon emissions by 2040 through The Climate Pledge reflects this disciplined, forward-looking approach. Guided by a defined net-zero roadmap, we are focused on improving operational efficiency, reducing emissions where possible, and addressing remaining impacts through high-quality offsets.
## Making an Impact with Our Clients
At West Monroe, we collaborate with clients and communities in both pro-bono and market capacities, driving value and making the business case for sustainability. We take initiatives from concept to impact, applying a sustainability lens across all industries we serve to foster a competitive, responsible approach to business. 
## Making an Impact with Our People
Our passionate, cross-functional Sustainability Team drives educational and volunteer initiatives while working to minimize the company’s environmental impact. With programs such as Plastic Free July, knowledge sharing opportunities, and environmental stewardship, the team engages employees to support local and firmwide efforts.
West Monroe believes trust is built through consistent action and creating processes and ways of operating that reflect our values, reinforcing responsible practices across our operations and value chain.
