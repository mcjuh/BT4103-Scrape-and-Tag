###  [ Transaction Opinions  ](https://capitaladvisors.fticonsulting.com/our-offering/#)
**What we do:**
We render an opinion regarding the fairness of a transaction from a financial point of view or regarding the solvency of a company and are prepared to defend our opinions in the event of a subsequent bankruptcy or other legal proceeding.
**Why is FTI different in this service:**
  * Ability to issue and defend a wide range of complex fairness and solvency opinions.
  * Global depth of knowledge and experience across a broad number of dynamic industries and countries in which a transaction arise
  * Unconflicted opinions provide enhanced strength as primary and secondary opinion providers.
  * An independent and experienced perspective enables enhanced sensibility in the fairness opinions provided.
