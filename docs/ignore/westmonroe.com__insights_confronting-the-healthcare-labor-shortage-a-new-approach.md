## Quick Take
  * ### 14% of newly licensed nurses leave the profession within their first year.
  * ### Workforce modernization is key to enabling clinicians to work at the top of their licenses.
  * ### Delegating non-clinical tasks to the right support roles can reduce burnout and boost job satisfaction.
  * ### Approximately 30% of traditional bedside RN tasks can be completed virtually.
  * ### Strengthening workforce diversity directly correlates with improved patient outcomes and equity.
