### In the office
In the heart of the North Loop, our Minneapolis office is a space where collaboration meets community. Whether it’s enjoying happy hours on the rooftop (temperature-permitting, of course) with stunning views of the city or bringing your dog to the office, we create a work environment that blends productivity with relaxation. Our monthly lunch-and-learns foster growth and knowledge-sharing, keeping us connected and inspired as we tackle new challenges together.
### In the community
In Minneapolis, our commitment to sustainability starts with our Chief of Sustainability program, where team members volunteer to clean up local gardens and green spaces, making a direct impact on the city’s environment. We’re also proud to support organizations that uplift our community, including High Tech Kids, The Link, and Wildflyer Coffee, working together to foster innovation, provide support to youth, and create opportunities for all.
