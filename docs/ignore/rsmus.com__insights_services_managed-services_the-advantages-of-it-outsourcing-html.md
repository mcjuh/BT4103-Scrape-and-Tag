# Managed IT services buyer's guide: Realizing IT outsourcing advantages
April 18, 2025
#### [Download the guide ](https://rsmus.com/content/dam/rsm/pdf/services/managed-services/advantages-of-managed-IT-outsourcing-services-buyers-guide.inline.pdf)
#

IT infrastructure Managed IT services Financial services Digital evolution
In today’s digital landscape, companies need their systems to remain stable, protected and continuously available. Organizations must also have the experience and bandwidth to capitalize on innovative emerging technologies like automation and artificial intelligence that can drive their business forward.
But the continued IT and cybersecurity labor shortage and the growing complexity of IT create challenges for companies of any size. Savvy companies are turning to outsourced IT to access services within a predictable but flexible baseline cost structure.
### The right managed IT services provider will offer a variety of outsourcing options to meet your unique situation, including:
  * **Fully managed IT services:** A comprehensive offering combining essential IT operations with industry knowledge for a predictable monthly fee.
  * **Comanaged IT services:** Custom solutions that form a complete IT operation by combining your in-house IT team with your advisors’ IT professionals.
  * **Detect and respond:** Consulting services that help your organization quickly identify and respond to cyber threats.
  * **Managed cloud services:** World-class maintenance and management of your cloud environment.


In this guide, you’ll learn more about RSM’s capabilities in each of these areas. Our engagement starts with a comprehensive assessment to understand your company’s unique needs and priorities. We then apply our business and technology knowledge, consulting and outsourcing solutions, and proprietary solutions and insights to address those needs.
### We also include three specific case studies so you can see how:
A Western bank upgraded their IT operations to improve service delivery and efficiency.
A U.S. government contractor modernized their IT and security infrastructure.
