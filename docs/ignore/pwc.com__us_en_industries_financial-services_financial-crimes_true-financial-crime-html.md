# True financial crime video series
###### Step into the real world of financial crime with PwC leaders who’ve lived it.
This video series features first-hand stories and sharp insights from professionals who’ve navigated crises, led investigations and advised at the highest levels. Through dynamic interviews with others on the front lines, they unpack the threats shaping our world—and the strategies that work to counter them.
Season 1 kicks off with Sean Joyce—Principal, US and Global Cybersecurity and Privacy Leader and former FBI Deputy Director—who pulls back the curtain on today’s most audacious financial crime threats. From AI-generated fake receipts and deepfake scams to nation-state job fraud, “pig butchering” crypto cons, mortgage rings and more—these aren’t just wild stories. They’re red flags for what’s already here and what’s coming next.
