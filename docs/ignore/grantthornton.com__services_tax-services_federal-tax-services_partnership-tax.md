## Navigating the complexity of partnership tax
The benefits of a business partnership are many, including limited liability combined with flexibility in the way income, deductions and distributions are shared among partners. The flexibility of partnerships creates complexities in application of tax laws. That’s where we come in.
Our professionals bring consulting, formation and operational knowledge to help you effectively navigate all aspects of partnership tax by implementing tailor-made solutions for your business. The result? Economic and tax benefits for all partners.

Bipartisan Budget Act
The Bipartisan Budget Act of 2015 (BBA) ushered in a new era for tax partnerships by creating a new centralized partnership regime... Show more for IRS audits of partnerships and for partnership-initiated corrections to previously filed returns. The BBA-centralized partnership regime has added significant complexity to both examinations and the amended return process, including allowing the IRS to audit partnerships more easily. Our team of tax professionals help partnerships navigate the complex BBA rules and IRS examinations, helping navigate the procedures for adjusting previously filed returns.
