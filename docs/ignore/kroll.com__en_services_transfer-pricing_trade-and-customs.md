## Export Control and Compliance Solutions
Advanced Export Controls and Sanctions Advisory for Complex Technologies
Tailored guidance on EAR, ITAR, OFAC, and multilateral regimes for cutting edge sectors (e.g., semiconductors, AI, aerospace and defense, quantum computing), translating highly technical product attributes into precise jurisdiction, classification and licensing determinations.
Deep Technical Classification and Regulatory Interpretation
Combine engineering level expertise with regulatory analysis to resolve nuanced classification challenges, de minimis/foreign direct product rule implications, encryption controls and emerging technology controls across jurisdictions.
Global Compliance Program Design and Implementation
Build and enhance risk based export controls and sanctions compliance frameworks, including policies, procedures, screening protocols, internal controls, automation strategies and training tailored to complex multinational operations.
Cross-Border Risk Assessments and Transactional Due Diligence
Conduct end to end reviews of international business models, supply chains and customer/partner ecosystems to identify sanctions exposure, diversion risk and control gaps with a focus on high risk geographies and dual use technology environments.
Independent Audits, Investigations and Root Cause Analysis
Perform internal audits and forensic investigations of potential violations, leveraging data analytics and technical product knowledge to assess scope, identify control breakdowns and quantify risk exposure.
Voluntary Disclosures and Regulatory Engagement
Lead the preparation of voluntary self disclosures to U.S. and non U.S. authorities, manage regulator interactions and develop effective narratives that align technical facts with enforcement expectations.
Remediation, Corrective Action and Monitorship Support
Design and implement sustainable remediation plans, including control redesign, governance enhancements and compliance monitoring to support organizations under consent agreements or regulatory scrutiny.
End to End Licensing and Authorization Strategy
Develop and execute licensing strategies aligned with business objectives, including commodity jurisdiction requests, advisory opinions, license applications and coordination with government agencies.
Global Experience and Multi Jurisdictional Coordination
Advise across U.S., EU, UK, and Asia Pacific regimes, harmonizing compliance approaches to meet overlapping regulatory requirements while enabling compliant international growth.
