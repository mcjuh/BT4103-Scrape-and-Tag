### [Bridging the gap between PLM vision and reality ](https://www.capgemini.com/services/intelligent-industry/product-lifecycle-management-plm-reimagined/#)
Many organizations struggle to realize the full potential of PLM. Structural and cultural barriers often stand in the way. Systems remain siloed, underutilized, and lack the integration capabilities needed to support seamless collaboration and data flow across the product lifecycle.
### [Navigating legacy system dependencies ](https://www.capgemini.com/services/intelligent-industry/product-lifecycle-management-plm-reimagined/#)
Integrating PLM with legacy systems demands a careful balance between modernization and continuity. Successful PLM strategies recognize the need to bridge this gap—ensuring new capabilities can coexist with existing infrastructure without disrupting business continuity.
### [Balancing data quality and governance](https://www.capgemini.com/services/intelligent-industry/product-lifecycle-management-plm-reimagined/#)
Poor data governance influences data quality, consistency, and accessibility. Effective PLM implementation requires a strategic approach to data stewardship, ensuring that information remains trustworthy and actionable throughout the product lifecycle. 
### [Aligning business and sustainability](https://www.capgemini.com/services/intelligent-industry/product-lifecycle-management-plm-reimagined/#)
Integrating sustainability tools and metrics into PLM platforms helps organizations track impact, ensure compliance, and align product decisions with environmental goals.
### [Slowing innovation and adoption](https://www.capgemini.com/services/intelligent-industry/product-lifecycle-management-plm-reimagined/#)
Manual processes and ingrained habits hinder PLM standardization, delaying implementation and limiting innovation.
