# Loan Closing Services
Industry leading third-party loan closing desk with unmatched efficiency and cost savings
Kroll Agency and Trustee Services provides conflict-free, flexible and highly efficient agency services to the global corporate and M&A markets. We specialize in the administration of syndicated, bilateral, private credit and restructuring loan transactions.
Leveraging our global team, our loan administration expertise and our advanced processes and technology, Kroll provides industry-leading loan closing services. We offer critical support to our clients with loan transactions that improve accuracy and efficiency and significantly reduce settlement times– ultimately saving our clients’ time and money.

## How Kroll’s Loan Closing Services Desk Can Support
  * Dedicated team of professionals with extensive experience in the loan closing market 
  * Work with other counterparties and agents to settle trades in an effective and efficient manner, thereby reducing settlement times and increasing liquidity for clients 
  * Ability to help clients navigate the complex trade lifecycle, and a network to actively manage the risk of loans rather than the risk of process failures 
  * Unique understanding and expertise to review loan settlement documentation such as trade recaps, trade confirmations, transfer certificates/assignment, agreements/novation agreement and pricing letters
