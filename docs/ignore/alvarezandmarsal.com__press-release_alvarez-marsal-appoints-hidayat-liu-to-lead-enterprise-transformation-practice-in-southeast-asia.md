# ALVAREZ & MARSAL APPOINTS HIDAYAT LIU TO LEAD ENTERPRISE TRANSFORMATION PRACTICE IN SOUTHEAST ASIA
**SINGAPORE & JAKARTA, June 4, 2026: **Leading global professional services firm [Alvarez & Marsal](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com) (A&M) today announced the appointment of Hidayat Liu as Managing Director to lead the firm's Enterprise Transformation practice across Southeast Asia. The appointment signals A&M's intent to deepen client relationships in this dynamic, high-growth market.
Corporates across the region are moving up from legacy national champion models to competing globally with the backdrop of shifting global trade order and structural shifts driven by AI. The scale and complexity of this transformation demand advisors who go beyond strategy and can embed capabilities, technology and processes to drive measurable results. A&M's Enterprise Transformation practice is well positioned to meet that demand.
Mr. Liu brings complementary experience across key regional markets and sectors including energy, industrials, resources, logistics, financial services and healthcare - positioning the practice to serve clients across Southeast Asia from day one.
Mr. Liu has over 25 years of consulting and corporate experience leading enterprise transformation, operating model redesign and technology-enabled performance improvement programs across the resources, energy, industrial, logistics, financial services, and healthcare sectors. Prior to joining A&M, he served as a Partner with McKinsey & Company, served on the Executive Committee at Grab, and held leadership roles with Esquel Group and Advanced Micro Devices.
[**Utsav Garg**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/utsav-garg)**, Managing Director, Head of Southeast Asia & Australia, Alvarez & Marsal**, said, "Hidayat's track record of leading large-scale enterprise transformation across Southeast Asia's most complex sectors makes him uniquely placed to build and lead this practice. His appointment signals our belief that the demand for execution-led transformation advisory in the broader region is significant, and that A&M is the right firm to meet it."
[**Hidayat Liu**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/hidayat-liu)**, Managing Director, Head of Enterprise Transformation Practice, Southeast Asia, Alvarez & Marsal**, said, "Southeast Asia's corporates are at an inflection point, moving from national champions to regional and global competitors. What they need are advisors who can translate strategy into sustainable operational outcomes, embedding the right technology, processes, and capabilities to drive real performance breakthroughs. A&M's hands-on, execution-led model is built for exactly this moment, and I am excited to be part of building this practice from the ground up."
This announcement marks a significant step in A&M's continued expansion across Southeast Asia, building on its existing capabilities in Performance Improvement, Transaction Advisory, Tax and Corporate Finance services.
