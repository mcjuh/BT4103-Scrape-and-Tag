## Sustain FAQs 
What leaders want to understand before committing Sustain to the enterprise.
What is Sapient Sustain?
Sustain is an AI-powered operations platform that keeps enterprise systems running, optimized and resilient by detecting issues early and resolving them automatically instead of relying on manual, human-heavy support models.
How is Sustain different from traditional managed services?
Traditional managed services rely on people to monitor systems and respond after something breaks. Sustain replaces that model with agentic intelligence that predicts issues, resolves them automatically and continuously improves system performance.
Instead of scaling headcount to keep systems running, Sustain scales automation and learning. The result is higher uptime, fewer incidents and materially lower operating costs than people-heavy models.
Can Sustain work with our existing ITSM and monitoring tools?
Yes. Sustain is designed to sit on top of your current environment, not replace it. It integrates with existing ITSM, observability and infrastructure tools to correlate signals, detect patterns and trigger automated remediation. Teams keep their systems of record. Sustain adds intelligence and action across them.
