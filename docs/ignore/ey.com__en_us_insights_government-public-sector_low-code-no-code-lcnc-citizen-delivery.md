#  Innovate fast: low code – no code (LCNC) & citizen delivery

In today’s fast-paced digital world, implementing low-code or no-code (LCNC) platforms provide significant business value by enhancing the ability to develop and deploy municipal services quickly.1 The municipal and federal environments are dynamic, requiring nimble systems that can quickly meet changing citizen needs and administrative environments. LCNC platforms give agencies the ideal opportunity to meet these needs with limited budgets and IT staffing constraints.

Overall, the transformative power of LCNC platforms lies in its ability to empower users with little to no programming skills to quickly develop and deploy applications.2 These platforms offer the flexibility to customize applications to specific business needs without the need for extensive software development. This fosters innovation as specialists can translate their ideas into practical solutions, allowing them to bypass the traditional barriers of software development.
## Currents Trends
The trend toward LCNC platforms is permeating in the market. Organizations are increasingly shifting toward citizen developers — employees who create applications without formal development skills — to bridge the gap between business process knowledge and technical affinity.3
### Key Trends:
  * Prediction: By 2026, 80% of developers will be outside formal IT departments, according to Gartner.4
  * Benefits: Accelerate development time by up to 90%, lower project costs and enhance both business processes and user adoption.5


### Impact:
  * Empowerment: Increase employee contribution to solution development.
  * Efficiency: Quick adaptation to changing needs.
  * Innovation: Modular and reusable components for complex workflows.


## Barriers and Constraints
Despite their growing prevalence, many government agencies are only beginning to explore the potential of LCNC platforms and may not fully leverage their capabilities. Bridging this gap requires awareness and a strategic approach to adoption that maximizes the benefits of these innovative tools. Resistance to technology adoption usually falls within the six pillars outlined below.
Read more  Read less 
Chart description  Hide description 
Cultural resistance and change management, governance and compliance, training and awareness, transforming talent capabilities, poor business alignment, and short sighted strategy are six pillars for resistance to technology adoption.
## Utilizing a Strategic Approach
To overcome these challenges and unlock the full potential of LCNC solutions, agencies need a strategic approach that re-envisions innovation based on lessons-learned from previous technology adoptions. A strategic LCNC approach can easily adopt new technologies such as blockchain or artificial intelligence (AI) when the business case presents a need.6 In situations where cost, change management, lack of trainings, or the other resistance factors mentioned above are present, LCNC could bridge the technology barrier and help agencies transition between current operating models and the future state realities. Adopting LCNC will streamline workflows, boost efficiency, cut costs, and seamlessly integrate future technologies, unlocking agencies full potential.7

#### Summary 
LCNC platforms represent a transformative opportunity for government agencies to modernize their technological capabilities, streamline their operations and improve citizen services. By embracing LCNC solutions, agencies can overcome traditional barriers to software development, reduce costs, and accelerate project timelines.8 This strategic adoption not only empowers a broad range of employees to contribute to IT projects but also fosters a culture of continuous innovation. What new efficiencies and impactful change can your agency accomplish with LCNC?
