# Structured Finance
We stand ready to meet your asset-based lending, capital market, and securitization needs with enhanced technology solutions, data analytics, and cash-flow modeling.
The KPMG Structured Finance practice provides services to Capital Market Participants for asset-based lenders and for the asset-backed securitization life cycle, from pre-issuance to post-closing. Our services include due diligence for lenders, securitization deal execution, including green bonds, portfolio compliance, tax and regulatory reporting and compliance, accounting, valuation, and advisory services.
##  Structured Finance 
Advanced services for your asset-based lending, capital market, and securitization needs 
The KPMG Structured Finance practice provides a full range of capital market services. With more than 30 years’ experience, KPMG LLP works regularly with some of the world’s largest investment banks, government-sponsored enterprises, asset-based lenders, private equity sponsors, troubled company advisors, and insurers.
