# Fintech trends transforming financial services: What companies need to know
### How fintech investment is reshaping competition in financial services
July 10, 2026

Financial services
_This page has been updated from the original, published on Dec. 6, 2021, to reflect new events, conditions or research._
Fintech trends are reshaping financial services through increased investment, the expansion of digital assets and continued innovation in payments and lending. Consumer adoption has grown, and the competitive landscape continues to shift as traditional services adapt to new digital realities. These fintech trends are changing how companies deliver financial products and services.
Looking ahead, the sector is poised for further growth. Areas of prominence include artificial intelligence-enabled fintech startups and growth in stablecoin adoption and tokenization. Financial services professionals and stakeholders should prepare for continued transformation as fintech cements its role at the core of financial services.
Here are the latest data and insights on fintech trends and their impact on the continual digital transformation of the financial services industry.
