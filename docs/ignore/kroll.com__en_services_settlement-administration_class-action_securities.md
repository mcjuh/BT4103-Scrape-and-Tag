## The Original Securities Class Action Settlement Administrator
When we handled the Atlantic Department Stores securities class action settlement in 1974, our team was the first to do so. Since then, Kroll has administered hundreds of cases and distributed billions in settlement benefits. Many of the processes we created are still used in securities matters today, and we continue to innovate with industry-leading technology, a deep bench and fresh approaches. 
Specifically, our securities team has considerable experience handling a variety of securities administration matters with varying complexities, including but not limited to: 
  * Processing and calculating recovery amounts for several types of securities, including common stock, American Depositary Receipts, preferred stock, call and put options, mortgage-backed securities, LEAPS, and bonds
  * Processing and calculating recovery amounts for several types of distributions, such as multiple funds, foreign currencies, share distributions and electronic payment distributions and direct pay administrations that include interfacing with The Depository Trust Company (DTC)
  * Drafting and formulating notices and SEC plans of distribution
  * Crafting unique notice and administration programs to accommodate cases with large volumes of class members and limited budgets

## Securities Derivatives Settlements
Delaware Court of Chancery settlements require a specialized administration strategy that reflects the unique procedural and shareholder recordkeeping considerations of derivative, merger, and fiduciary duty litigation.
Kroll partners with counsel from the outset of an engagement to develop court-approved notice and administration protocols tailored to the structure of the settlement, the shareholder population and the available ownership records, ensuring efficient and defensible administration.
### Administration
Kroll administers both claims-made and direct distribution settlements utilizing transfer agent records, defendant-produced shareholder data, broker-based notification and payment protocols, as well as DTC participant information. We coordinate with a wide range of stakeholders, including transfer agents, Broadridge, custodial banks, brokers, and other nominees to facilitate the dissemination of notice to beneficial owners through established securities channels. Where appropriate, we administer electronic and mailed notice programs, manage shareholder communications and prepare comprehensive declarations and status reports for submission to the court.
### No Two Shareholder Settlements Are Alike
Kroll works closely with counsel to address complex administration issues, including record date determinations, beneficial ownership verification, nominee reimbursement programs, supplemental notice, and distribution methodologies. Our project management approach emphasizes accuracy, transparency, and compliance with court orders while providing counsel with real-time reporting of key metrics throughout the administration. We also leverage established industry relationships to minimize delays and keep settlements moving efficiently from notice to final distribution.
