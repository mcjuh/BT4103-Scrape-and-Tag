#  Cultivating profitable growth for private companies

## Overcome your obstacles and achieve your ambitions
Private companies have specialized needs that require relationships with professionals who truly understand them. Our services go beyond the expectations of our private company clients because we fully comprehend their challenges — and their opportunities.
Our experience serving many of the world’s largest private companies means that we’re familiar with your objectives — and we also are eager to learn about your unique needs. Meanwhile, our status as a top-five firm by revenue audited and market cap of SEC audit client base means you won’t outgrow us even as you meet and exceed your goals.

### Meeting needs across the private company spectrum
  1. PE-backed private companies
  2. Family-owned private companies
  3. Fast-growing private companies


More than 1,600 of our clients are backed by private equity, and we also serve some of the nation’s leading PE firms. We help portfolio companies and the firms that back them determine appropriate accounting policies, identify internal controls that should be instituted, and navigate through complexity related to M&A transactions.
Many family-owned businesses face challenges related to governance, succession planning and building effective, efficient operating models. Family dynamics can make these issues difficult, but our experience with many of the largest family-owned businesses in the United States can help you overcome these challenges and secure profitability today and sustainability for the future for you and your loved ones.
The model for many companies seeking rapid growth has shifted from a mostly revenue-based pursuit to a quest for profitable growth. When serving innovative companies and start-ups, we’re conscious of their need to build infrastructure to support the strategy and processes to achieve profitability.
