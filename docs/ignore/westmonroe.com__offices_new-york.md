### In the office
Our New York City office offers stunning 360-degree views of the city, including the vibrant theater district. Inside, you’ll find plenty of ways to unwind, with board games, video games, and a ping pong table always available. Our well-stocked kitchen has a variety of snacks and beverages to suit every taste, whether you're looking for something healthy or indulgent. We stay engaged with monthly lunch and learns, leadership breakfasts, and Director Days where leadership will host office hours on-site. Plus, we love celebrating fun holidays, like National Chocolate and Candy Corn Day, bringing an extra dose of festivity to the office.
### In the community
In New York City, we’re passionate about giving back and staying engaged with the vibrant communities around us. Whether we’re supporting local youth, helping underserved populations, or preserving the city’s natural spaces, our team is dedicated to making a lasting impact. We proudly partner with organizations like Bowery Mission, Network for Teaching Entrepreneurship, and Hudson River Park to help build a stronger, more resilient city for everyone.
