## Turn Product Lifecycle Management into a growth engine with EY PLM
Organizations today operate in a market where volatility is the norm. Consumer expectations shift overnight, supply chains must deliver with precision, and advantage can disappear in a single product cycle. Defensive tactics such as shrinkflation, rapid price changes or quick-fire reformulations may offer short-term relief, but they rarely create resilience or lasting growth — and can erode brand equity.
Sustained relevance now depends on innovation as a strategic capability, not a series of isolated initiatives. Yet many organizations struggle to turn bold ambition into scalable, repeatable outcomes. Product development remains fragmented across functions, insights sit in disconnected systems, and decision-making suffers from limited transparency and misaligned priorities, leading to delayed launches, rising costs, wasted effort and missed opportunities.
The EY PLM suite addresses these challenges head-on. We help organizations build the connected, insight-driven and execution-focused product engine required to lead in dynamic markets. By integrating proven operating models, advanced analytics, collaborative platforms and technology-enabled execution, our PLM capabilities bridge the gap between strategy and delivery — accelerating decisions, reducing complexity and advancing innovation at scale.
We bring together three powerful enablers that reshape how organizations conceive, develop, launch and optimize products end to end.

  * ##  1. The Innovation Excellence Operating Model
Innovation is rarely accidental — it is enabled by thoughtful design. To innovate reliably and repeatedly, organizations need the right governance, culture, processes and incentives. EY teams help clients embed a robust innovation operating blueprint designed to move beyond ad hoc ideas and into disciplined, enterprise-wide capability.
Grounded in a framework co-created with Procter & Gamble and enhanced through decades of cross-sector delivery, the Innovation Excellence framework provides the organizational muscle and operating practices companies need to shape portfolios, prioritize investments and execute efficiently across functions. Our approach covers:
    * Strategic portfolio management: focus resources where they deliver greater value
    * Structured governance mechanisms: streamline decision-making and maintain accountability
    * Clear roles, responsibilities and workflows: reduce friction and facilitate faster progression from idea to commercial reality
    * Product development methodologies: create a seamless technical community to sharpen consumer outcomes and manage risk during scale-up
    * Cultural enablers: build confidence, encourage experimentation and remove organizational blockers
The outcome is a more predictable and scalable innovation engine — accelerating time to market, reducing unnecessary complexity and helping organizations to focus on launches that truly drive growth and competitive advantage.

  * ##  2. EY Modular Innovation Experience
[EY Modular Innovation Experience](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/industries/consumer-products/modular-innovation-experience) is our next-generation innovation orchestration platform designed to help organizations break free from slow, siloed development.
Where traditional innovation systems create fragmentation, EY Modular Innovation Experience creates flow. It connects the entire product development process into one digital thread, bringing visibility, speed and coordination to every stage. Teams gain access to real-time data, guided workflows, automated governance steps and analytics-driven recommendations — all within a single platform.
Key capabilities include:
    * End-to-end workflow integration: keeping marketing, R&D, supply chain, finance and commercial teams aligned and informed
    * A single source of truth: facilitating rapid scenario planning, portfolio optimization and decision-making
    * Digital traceability: supporting compliance, risk management and faster iterations
    * Modular frameworks: allowing organizations to configure the platform to their distinctive operating model and product categories
EY Modular Innovation Experience transforms innovation from a disconnected series of tasks into a coherent, insight-led ecosystem. For chief supply chain officers and chief operating officers, it provides a level of visibility and orchestration that enhances speed, eliminates waste and dramatically improves launch reliability.

  * ##  3. SAP Integrated Product Development (IPD)
Technology integration is the backbone of modern PLM. SAP IPD unifies experimentation, product development, manufacturing transfer, product changes and regulatory information within a single digital environment.
EY teams help clients implement and embed SAP IPD seamlessly across their enterprise landscape, facilitating secure collaboration and accelerating decision-making.
Our SAP IPD-enabled PLM execution strengthens performance by:
    * Allowing controlled, role-based access to master data, formulations and specifications across R&D and manufacturing
    * Facilitating secure digital collaboration between global teams, suppliers and partners
    * Improving governance and version control through consistent identity validation and lifecycle traceability
    * Accelerating compliance activities by ensuring that only validated, authorized users interact with regulated processes and documentation
By establishing common working environment, SAP IPD supports faster and more reliable development cycles. When implemented as part of the broader EY PLM suite, it enhances operational performance, reduces risk and provides an integrated, secure foundation for scaling innovation from concept to commercialization.

## Benefits for your business
What differentiates the EY organization’s PLM capabilities is the integration of strategy, process, technology and execution. Rather than deploying isolated fixes, EY teams help organizations reimagine the entire product engine — supporting innovation investments that translate into operational outcomes and commercial impact.
Across industries, the EY PLM approach helps clients:
  * Reduce time-to-market and unlock capacity for more innovation
  * Improve margin through better portfolio choices and more efficient delivery
  * Enhance supply chain resilience with greater design-to-manufacture visibility
  * Strengthen brand competitiveness through faster, more relevant product releases
  * Enable sustainability and regulatory compliance through traceable, data-driven processes


From strategic ambition to technical activation, EY teams possess the methods, platforms and experience to help organizations build a modern PLM engine — one that delivers speed, reliability and growth in an increasingly complex world.
