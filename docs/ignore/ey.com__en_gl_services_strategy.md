#  Strategy Consulting by EY-Parthenon 
EY-Parthenon is a global strategy consulting firm that works with CEOs, boards, private equity investors and governments to help drive organic and inorganic growth, strategic transformation and value creation. 
In an environment defined by disruption, geopolitical uncertainty and accelerating technology change, our strategy consulting teams help organizations reimagine their future and execute with confidence — from defining ambition to delivering measurable results.
We have the breadth of perspectives and depth of experience to provide strategic advice to help achieve optimal — and sustainable — results. Our heritage of being a market leader in transactions helps us take an investor mindset to your strategic agenda – enabling us to help drive reinvention through capital, resources and execution to free up cash flow, build better business cases and deliver stronger total shareholder returns (TSR). Giving you the confidence to reimagine your enterprise and the capabilities to realize it.
