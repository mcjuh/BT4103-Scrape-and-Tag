#  Space industry 
## A market facing evolution, disruption — and new opportunities
Space is no longer a destination — it’s a catalyst for transformation. From exploration to domain awareness and launch to tourism, the space economy is expanding fast. And it’s not just fueling innovation in orbit. Across defense, agriculture, energy, finance — even food delivery — the impact is real and growing. As public and private sector players reshape what’s possible, space is becoming a launchpad for progress across the global economy.
As the space economy accelerates, so do the complexities. Organizations are balancing shifting policies, evolving governance and pressure to launch — fast. Add in persistent operating model constraints, supply chain volatility and the need for seamless ground support, and it’s clear: progress requires agility, clarity and collaboration.
We combine deep industry insight with leading edge tech to help you move faster, go further and scale smarter. From strategy to launch and beyond, our integrated consulting and trust services are built for what’s next.

## Solutions built for lift-off: PwC’s space services
Our space industry practice enables measurable results, launching our client’s most important outcomes.
A new space economy is taking shape — and the pace is accelerating. As public-sector funding gives way to a fast-growing ecosystem of private innovators, the market is more dynamic — and more competitive — than ever. PwC helps you move with confidence through it all. From business model reinvention to IPOs, divestitures, M&A and growth strategy, our space team brings cross-industry insight and deep A&D expertise to every engagement. We integrate audit and tax consulting with a powerful suite of AI, cloud and digital tools to increase productivity, spark innovation and unlock value — whether on Earth or in orbit.
Artificial intelligence (AI)
With deep experience in artificial intelligence and trusted collaborations across sectors, PwC is uniquely positioned to help space organizations unlock the full value of their data. We work side by side with clients to help augment decision-making, boost productivity, modernize legacy systems and deliver intelligence — securely and responsibly.
Engineering modernization
Aerospace engineering programs, tools, analysis and extensive human-in-the-loop processes have faced an inflection point. Discover how we’re helping clients modernize digital engineering, transform flight sciences and activate real-time program insights — while maintaining compliance and strengthening the digital thread from concept to launch.
Deals and private equity
PwC is a leader in trust services, providing our clients with deep experience and accelerated offerings to support space industry IPOs, divestitures, partnerships, mergers and acquisitions.
Tax, audit and law
With deep tax and audit experience and a sharp eye on space law and policy, we help you stay ahead of change, uncover savings and prepare with confidence. Together, we’ll navigate complexity and find opportunity in every filing.
Business to government
As opportunities in the space sector expand, so do the possibilities in government contracting. Our B2G consulting team helps space organizations unlock value from Federal collaborations — boosting R&D, improving cash flow and driving long-term commercial growth. We work with you to build smart sales strategies that match your risk profile and to design the people, processes and tech you need to win and manage Federal awards. From go-to-market to compliance, we connect your business objectives with public sector realities — turning complexity into competitive advantage.
