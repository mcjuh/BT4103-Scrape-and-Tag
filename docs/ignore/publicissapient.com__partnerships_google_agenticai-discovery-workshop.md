#  Unlock AI Value: Your Agentic AI Discovery Workshop 
[](https://www.publicissapient.com/partners/google-cloud/agenticai-discovery-workshop#request-a-workshop)
Our hands-on complimentary workshop helps your team rapidly identify, prioritize, and align high-impact AI agent use cases that deliver real business value. In just a few hours, gain clarity and an actionable plan.
## Transform your operations: Your workshop outcomes
  * **Pinpoint high-impact AI opportunities** : Clearly map where AI agents can solve your biggest challenges.
  * **Prioritized use case roadmap** : Walk away with 2-3 tailored AI use cases ready for action.
  * **Stakeholder alignment & buy-in**: Build consensus and identify champions for your AI initiatives.
  * **Clear next steps & feasibility insights**: Understand the path to pilot and production.


## What to expect
Understand your landscape
We map your current tools, workflows & pain points.
Uncover AI potential
We explore the "Find, Understand, Act" spectrum for AI agent impact.
Prioritize for value
We align use cases with your strategic goals and assess feasibility.
Define your action plan
We solidify pilot use cases and build momentum.
##  Prepare for success, what is needed for the workshop 
  * **Your team** : A cross-functional group of 3-5 key stakeholders from relevant departments, e.g., Operations, Customer Service, IT, relevant business units.
  * **Their time** : A dedicated 4-5 hour time slot for the interactive workshop.
  * **Their input** : Willingness to openly discuss current processes, challenges and objectives.
  * **Pre-work** : Completion of a short pre-workshop survey to maximize our time together.
