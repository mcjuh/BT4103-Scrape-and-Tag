## Benefits of Kroll Real Estate Advisory Group
Global Practice
As a global practice, we have the scale and geographic coverage to meet our clients’ needs wherever they may be. With over 300 dedicated real estate professionals operating out of 25 offices, Kroll has the local market expertise necessary to appraise and consult on major projects consistently throughout the world.
Independence
We offer independent valuation and consulting services in accordance with professional and ethical standards. We do not provide financial reporting audit services or equity research and ensure that we are not subject to conflicts of interest in performing our work.
Industry Expertise
As market leaders in real estate valuation and consulting, our professionals bring technical and industry expertise and local market knowledge to our clients. Kroll professionals are experts in all real estate sectors and in real estate funds; public and private REITs; corporate, institutional and franchise real estate; right of way; railroad and utilities; and alternative assets; among other areas.
Credentialed Experts
Our list of professionals includes real estate valuation and consulting experts, real estate investment bankers, chartered surveyors, architects, engineers and geologists. Their certifications and memberships include MAI, RICS, CRE, ASA, HypZert, LEED and BREEAM, among others. Our appraisals are USPAP, IVS and RICS compliant.
Market Leadership
As the largest independent global valuation practice specializing in valuations for financial reporting purposes, we have experience across all asset types and industries.
Technology-enabled Solutions
Best-in-class technology, analyses and reporting ensures consistency in our approach, results and outcomes.
Efficient Audit Reviews
We have experience working with audit teams, enabling an efficient review process.
