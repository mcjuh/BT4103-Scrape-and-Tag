## AI in Transportation
Generative and agentic AI solutions now enable companies to meet a wide range of longstanding challenges across the entire transportation and logistics value chain:
• Enhance resilience of global supply chains through predictive routing and alerts on potential disruptions from weather, natural disasters, and geopolitical events• Address a variety of security threats, from theft to cyberattacks• Alleviate a global shortage of skilled workers using AI-powered robots and computer vision systems• Reduce fuel, labor, and raw materials costs by optimizing carton usage, streamlining contract negotiations, and other applications.
