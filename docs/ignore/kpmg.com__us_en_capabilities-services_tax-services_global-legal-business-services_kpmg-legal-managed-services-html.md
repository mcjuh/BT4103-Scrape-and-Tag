##  Efficiently accelerate and scale global contracting and legal compliance 
Transforming routine legal work into strategic advantage 
In-house legal teams face unprecedented pressure as the volume of routine legal work rises in today’s fast-paced business environment. The contract management process becomes complex at scale, with thousands of contracts needing reviews, negotiation and execution across multiple jurisdictions. Traditional approaches to handling this workload often lead to backlogs that delay business opportunities and increase risk exposure.
KPMG Legal Managed Services addresses these challenges through a globally connected, technology-enabled delivery model that transforms legal and contracting operations. We combine people, process, and technology to deliver consistency, speed, and transparency, freeing up your in-house team to focus on higher-value work while maintaining control and compliance.
## Integrated solutions to transform your legal operations
KPMG Legal Managed Services works in concert with our **Legal Operations Consulting** and **Legal Technology** **Innovation** capabilities to deliver an end-to-end solution for legal function transformation.
High-volume contract management
From drafting and negotiation to execution and renewal, we manage high-volume contracting processes using standardized playbooks and workflow automation. Our approach accelerates turnaround times and reduces cost while maintaining quality and compliance.
Event-driven solutions
Dedicated teams support one-off or time-bound initiatives such as M&A-related contract harmonization, regulatory compliance projects and large-scale contract remediation.
Tech-enabled operations
Powered by Digital Gateway for Law and ContractPodAi’s LeAh platform, our services use AI-driven extraction, analytics, and redlining to accelerate review and improve accuracy. Insights from data help optimize terms and manage risk.
Global delivery model
Our tiered delivery structure combines legal professionals and contract specialists across multiple centers of excellence, ensuring consistency, scalability, and cost efficiency around the world.
