# A Call to Collective Action

**September 9, 2024**
The world is changing rapidly. New technologies are remaking society and work. Climate change is taking an ever-greater toll on people, economies, and nature. Challenges of this magnitude cannot be addressed by individual governments or companies. A new level of international cooperation is needed. BCG experts share where [global collaboration](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/social-impact/overview) is most critical—and how such action can help societies adapt and thrive in the face of disruption.
