#  Small island developing states 
Small island developing states (SIDS) are on the front line of global climate change. We look at how they can prepare and protect themselves.
Last updated: June 2024

Small island states are among the least responsible for climate change, and yet feel its impacts tangibly and directly.
The challenges SIDs are facing are interconnected and require integrated solutions. We take a systems-approach to strengthen the resilience of SIDS and to contribute to the ongoing wellbeing of island populations, maximising their ability to remain in place.
