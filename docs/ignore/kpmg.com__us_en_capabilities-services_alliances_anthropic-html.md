## Your Trusted Advisor, Now Powered by Frontier AI
Our alliance with Anthropic engineers AI directly into the platforms, controls, and workflows where professional judgment matters most. This integration of KPMG's deep domain experience with Anthropic's frontier AI delivers tangible, immediate value to your organization.
## Transforming Operations Across the Enterprise
Our alliance is designed to benefit clients by transforming how work gets done. Whether building agile, agentic systems that adapt to new regulations in real time or optimizing operations to unlock new avenues for value creation, we help you responsibly harness AI to accelerate your business goals. While we offer highly specialized capabilities for complex functions—such as Tax compliance and Private Equity strategy—the fundamental power of this alliance is its ability to scale across your entire organization.
## A Partnership Built on Trust
KPMG and Anthropic share a foundational commitment to security, accuracy, and trust. We embed robust cybersecurity, risk, and AI assurance protocols into the systems we design. Our work is guided by KPMG's Trusted AI Framework, ensuring that AI is not just powerful, but also safe, reliable, and deployed responsibly.
