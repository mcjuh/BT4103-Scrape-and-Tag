Every big idea deserves exceptional planning and execution. That’s why at Arup, we help organisations transform strategic thinking into efficient, deliverable programmes of work.
Our expertise enables robust governance, sharper decision-making, and greater certainty of delivery. Our teams bring deep experience in portfolio, programme and project delivery. Digitally enabled PMOs, AI analytics; and rigorous change and risk management are all supported by Arup’s wider capabilities in technical, operational, digital and sustainability expertise. Our strength lies in this combination of technical excellence and consultancy insights, enabling organisations to not only deliver on project and programme ambitions, but to also achieve far broader priorities. From buildings and estates to large-scale infrastructure, and through driving enterprise-wide transformation, we deliver high-quality, sustainable results, on time and within budget.
## How we can help you
Strategy implementation
Our teams assess whether strategic portfolios will deliver the intended outcomes, then guide impactful, lasting change.
Strategy implementation
Close
Many clients come to us with clear strategic goals but need support turning them into actionable plans and deliverable programmes of work. 
Working with a strategic partner is vital – especially during major investments, infrastructure developments, or business diversification – drawing on a wider knowledge base to better understand the full lifecycle of projects and programmes and their strategic importance as they interlock. Arup acts as that strategic partner bringing effective integration.
Our teams assess whether strategic portfolios will deliver the intended outcomes, then guide impactful, lasting change. Progress is tracked using established leading and lagging metrics and KPIs to ensure results are both measurable and meaningful.
Transformation programme delivery
We create clear roadmaps, mobilise the right capabilities, and manage workstreams to help organisations successfully navigate complexity.
Transformation programme delivery
Close
Successful transformation demands an effective combination of managing the big picture and attention to detail.
By defining strategic benefits, KPIs, and a Target Operating Model, we ensure everyone in the organisation has a shared understanding of the strategic goals and that progress remains measurable against a realistic plan.
We create clear roadmaps, mobilise the right capabilities, and manage workstreams to help organisations successfully navigate complexity and achieve the defined future state. Our structured project framework enables lasting change and focuses on unlocking real transformational value.
Capital programme delivery
We support clients from programme delivery to asset operations and management, including enabling Operational Readiness Activation and Transition (ORAT).
Capital programme delivery
Close
We help deliver complex capital programmes with increased confidence. Robust governance and best-practice delivery models enable effective decision-making to align programme vision with strategic outcomes.
Preparing for the transition into live operations is always a critical phase, and we support clients from programme delivery to asset operations and management, including enabling Operational Readiness Activation & Transition (ORAT). We design phased delivery roadmaps, implement Project and Programme Management Offices (PMOs), and design and establish appropriate frameworks for assurance and performance tracking. 
Post-delivery, we offer ongoing support through health-checks and maturity assessments, basing our approach on the clients’ programmatic context, to ensure continued success.
[Find out more about our ORAT service](https://www.arup.com/services/orat/)
Major infrastructure projects
We help clients achieve high performance across development, delivery, and operations, supported by strong controls and compliance.
Major infrastructure projects
Close
With the imperative for government funding to demonstrate value for money, budgets constrained by competing priorities; and complex governance requirements, the delivery of large-scale infrastructure requires exceptional programme and project management. 
We help clients achieve high performance across development, delivery, and operations, supported by strong controls and compliance.
Our approach integrates sustainability and regulatory requirements into our delivery models, drawing on Arup’s wider technical expertise. Our strength lies in managing multi-stakeholder interfaces and ensuring planning and consenting processes are built-in from the start.
Robust assurance and health-checks are tailored to the project, client, regulatory context and lifecycle stage, ensuring outcomes are resilient and aligned with strategic goals.
Buildings and estates projects
Our teams support clients in delivering sustainable buildings, repurposed facilities and estate developments - from initial appraisal to final delivery.
Buildings and estates projects
Close
We support clients in delivering sustainable buildings, repurposed facilities and estate-wide developments - from initial appraisal to final delivery. 
Our work ensures real estate decisions align with wider portfolio priorities, and broader strategic goals.
We are also committed to integrating net zero and sustainability into every stage, including energy, carbon, water, waste, and construction-stage environmental performance. Increasingly, we also help refurbish and repurpose existing assets to extend value and reduce embedded carbon impacts. Our experience is diverse and spans sectors including commercial, healthcare, education, transport, science and technology.
PMO and controls
We set up tailored portfolio, programme and project (P3) management solutions suited to the specific context and client needs, to enable data-driven decision-making across all stages of the project management journey.
PMO and controls
Close
We set up tailored portfolio, programme and project (P3) management solutions – including PMOs, P3M3 frameworks and robust programme and project controls - suited to the specific context and client needs, to enable data-driven decision making. 
Establishing and managing advanced controls solutions is a powerful way to navigate complexity and deal with change initiatives across design and implementation, while further supporting organisations in developing project management maturity.
PMOs with a digital-first approach, ensures alignment with strategic goals and organisational governance. Data-driven project insights - AI-enabled to drive greater efficiencies across a broad range of information sources - help improve decision making and generate stronger project outcomes.
Procurement advisory
We help organisations balance risk, drive value and enable successful delivery through strategic procurement advice and tailored sourcing strategies, to provide the insight and structure needed to make confident, data-driven decisions.
Procurement advisory
Close
Whether navigating complex supply chains, public sector frameworks, or major capital investments, effective procurement backed by a robust supply chain strategy is vital.
We help organisations balance risk, drive value, and enable successful delivery through strategic procurement advice and tailored sourcing strategies, to provide the insights and structure needed to make confident, data-driven decisions. Our breadth of technical experience enables our teams to develop a strategy that's founded on client organisations' current and future needs. We provide sound market analysis and engagement, maturity reviews and readiness assessments - including skills and capability gap analysis - to further develop and refine strategies in line with agreed commercial, quality and delivery objectives.
Tender management is critical, and we manage the process from initial preparation through to contract execution, ensuring efficiency, value and best practice throughout. We help clients develop and define the most robust commercial contract strategies, addressing relevant risks, and focused on the organisation's ultimate commercial performance, driving efficiency, resilience and long-term value.
