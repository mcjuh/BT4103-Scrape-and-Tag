#  AI for retail and consumer products 
How can you turn AI into measurable business value? Explore our latest insights and innovative capabilities designed to help you scale AI across your organization to unlock measurable growth and impact.
## From AI ambition to enterprise impact
AI is no longer just a buzzword—it’s a competitive advantage across the consumer industry. Some retailers, consumer products companies, and distributors are using AI to move faster, personalize at scale, strengthen supply networks, and make smarter decisions that improve profitability.
From intelligent forecasting to supply chain optimization and personalized customer engagement, Deloitte works with retail, consumer products, and wholesale distribution leaders to scale AI with speed, structure, and confidence.
