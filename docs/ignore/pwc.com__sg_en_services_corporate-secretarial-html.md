# Corporate Secretarial Services
Your needs, our commitment
Copy link Link copied to clipboard 
Our Corporate Support Services team provides practical and effective assistance to organisations from top-tier multinationals, to small and medium enterprises, as well as startups across a wide range of industries.
We aim to provide value-added services and solutions that are flexible and tailored to your needs, and more importantly, that solve your problems.
Our people are geared to assist you in navigating the setting up of a business in Singapore, through ongoing statutory compliance, financial reporting and managed payroll services.

Meeting your statutory obligations
### Meeting your statutory obligations
Corporate compliance in Singapore is a complex legal requirement for all Singapore registered entities. The Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority in Singapore governs the statutory obligations of entities and its officers. Our **entity governance and compliance** team helps you and your officers fulfil your statutory obligations in an efficient manner.
Resource shifting to core activities
### Resource shifting from non-core to core activities
Exponential growth in technology and globalisation has prompted organisations to rationalise their resources. Outsourcing payroll and related function frees up resources, including valuable human capital, to focus on and engage in projects that add value to your business.
Digitalising your finance function
### Digitalising and streamlining your finance function
Integrating finance and accounting support can prove to be a difficult task, especially amidst constantly evolving business' workflows. Outsourcing and using appropriate technology can help to not only alleviate any strain on resources but leverage on experienced professionals and services. Our dedicated and trusted **accounting and finance solutions** team helps you gain confidence in your financial numbers so you can make decisions based on the insights you receive.

### Entity governance and compliance
With increasing prominence in regulation, risk and legal entity management, and a constantly shifting regime of regulatory developments across different countries, board responsibilities have never been broader or greater. We are part of a global network of company secretaries, lawyers and governance specialists over 100 jurisdictions.
**How we deliver value**
#### Entity creation
  * Incorporation of entities
  * Registration of branches 
  * Redomiciliation of entities 


#### Entity management 
  * Board meeting faciliation
  * Advice and assistance with corporate changes
  * Advice on legal entity compliance obligations
  * Managing ongoing compliance and reporting obligations 


#### Entity dissolution
  * Pre-dissolution and restructuring
  * Dissolution of solvent and dormant entities 


#### Best practices and governance 
  * Directors' training
  * Advice on subsidiary risk and governance framework with global perspectives 


### Complete payroll
We work as one team with our employment tax, global mobility, legal and HR colleagues to provide compliance-centric, technology-driven managed payroll services that delivers a high quality and consistent experience to clients and their employees regardless of their location in the world. 
**How we deliver value**
#### Stress free payroll
We offer extensive experience in payroll processing functions and ensure that your employees are paid accurately and in a timely manner.
#### Cost-effective web-based payroll services
Our web-based payroll services help make it easier for businesses to get the payroll services they need at an affordable price.
#### Payroll-related compliance
Our team ensure that you, as an employer, comply with all the payroll-related statutory requirements and obligations.
### Accounting and finance solutions 
The combination of skills, technical knowledge, relevant experience and effective digital tools has become increasingly important as organisations continue to face new and complex events both locally and globally. We will partner you and provide you with the right support in areas including:
  * Dedicated business advisor
  * Full accounting services


  * Cloud accounting software with OCR 
  * Digital dashboards and tools 


**How we deliver value**
#### Partnering you as your business advisor
Benefit from the experience of our team during regular face-to-face meetings with your dedicated account manager.
#### Real time information with cloud tools
Management reporting and cashflow projection dashboards are integrated with the accounting software, providing you with real-time information.
#### A one-stop shop
Our partnership extends to other areas of support that you typically need, including
  * Entity governance and compliance support
  * Payroll and benefits management
  * Corporate and indirect taxes advisory and compilance 


#### Automate everyday processes
We focus on maximising efficiency through the use of digital tools so you can focus on driving your business forward.
