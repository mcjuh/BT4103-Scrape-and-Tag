# Fixed Asset Register Health Check
Kroll’s Fixed Asset Register Health Check is designed as a first-step diagnostic to help organizations understand the current state of their asset data.
Resilience and precision begin with clear, accurate information on asset value, status and age. Many organizations still operate with fixed asset registers (FARs) that are outdated, incomplete or fragmented, creating blind spots that undermine capex planning, decision making, and operational continuity.
Kroll’s Fixed Asset Register Health Check is precisely designed to help organizations assess their data quality, reliability and completeness.
It provides a concise, data-driven review that helps organizations identify gaps, uncover inconsistencies and establish a roadmap toward a unified, decision-ready register. This innovative Kroll’s solution provides clear actionable dashboards and executive reports for decision makers.
## Main Features of the FAR Health Check
The FAR Health Check helps organizations:
  * Evaluate data quality across asset categories and identify inconsistencies or missing information.
  * Quantify data completeness through a weighted scoring model.
  * Visualize historical acquisition and revaluation patterns to flag bulk capitalizations or legacy adjustments.
  * Compare accounting life to valuation life and assess asset age profiles.
  * Identify indicators for potential step-ups or step-downs between Net Book Value, Replacement Value and Fair Market Value.


## One Source of Truth for Many Needs
One of the biggest challenges organizations face is fragmented data—finance relies on accounting systems, engineers on maintenance platforms, and operations on local records.
Kroll’ Health Check is designed to help build a roadmap to unify all these perspectives and needs and create a single source of truth that all stakeholders can trust and apply. It enables the CFO to model depreciation accurately, the COO to plan maintenance strategically, and the CEO to optimize their asset performance and make confident, value-driven decisions.
