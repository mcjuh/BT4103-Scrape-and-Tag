## How EY tech delivery services drives growth with modernization and talent solutions
Financial services organizations frequently encounter significant obstacles as they strive to grow and enhance operational capabilities. These hurdles include:
  * Securing the right talent at the right time, leading to workforce inefficiencies.
  * A vendor landscape with varying commercial structures and delivery models that complicate effective delivery.
  * Difficulties in balancing internal and contingent workforce needs.

## Questions about tech delivery services
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  1. What are tech delivery services?
Tech delivery services encompass a range of solutions aimed at helping organizations implement and manage technology effectively. These services typically include strategic modernization, system integration and providing essential skill sets to support business operations. A key focus is on leveraging engineering talent to design and build robust technology solutions that align with organizational goals. This approach allows businesses to adapt to changing market demands while maintaining operational efficiency.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  2. How do tech delivery services integrate AI?
Tech delivery services integrate AI in several ways to enhance functionality and efficiency. This includes modernizing legacy data platforms to create AI-ready data environments, enabling organizations to harness the power of data analytics. Additionally, AI-enabled applications and middleware are developed to streamline processes and improve user experiences. Furthermore, incorporating AI into the SDLC allows for the use of intelligent tools that enhance coding, testing and deployment, ultimately improving the quality and speed of software delivery.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  3. What are the benefits of tech delivery services?
Tech delivery services offer numerous benefits, including increased operational efficiency, faster time-to-market for new features and improved scalability. By leveraging innovative technologies and skilled engineering talent, organizations can modernize their systems and processes effectively. The integration of AI capabilities further enhances these benefits by automating routine tasks, improving decision-making and enhancing resource allocation. These services also support better workforce management by providing access to advanced skills and cost-effective solutions that streamline development and data management.
