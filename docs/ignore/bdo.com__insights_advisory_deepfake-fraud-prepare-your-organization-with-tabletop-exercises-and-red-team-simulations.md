# Deepfake Fraud: Prepare Your Organization with Tabletop Exercises and Red-Team Simulations
Article
  * PRINT 
  * 

_This article was first published by[ACFE's Fraud Magazine](https://www.acfe.com/fraud-magazine) in the June 2026 issue._
To defend against AI-enabled deepfake fraud, organizations must be proactive and prepared. In an article for [Fraud Magazine](https://www.acfe.com/fraud-magazine), BDO's Karen Schuler and Steve Taylor emphasize the critical role of tabletop exercises and red-team simulations in preparing for sophisticated threats. These training exercises, which focus on verification, cross-functional coordination, and scenario design, provide a structured approach to identify weaknesses and strengthen defenses. By implementing these strategies, organizations can foster a culture of continuous improvement to stay ahead of potential fraudsters.
