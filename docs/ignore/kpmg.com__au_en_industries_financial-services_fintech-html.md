## KPMG can help throughout the Fintech Lifecycle
Our experts help fintechs at every stage of their lifecycle – from start-up through to scale-up and maturity – to grow and achieve their strategic ambitions. We also work extensively with established financial services organisations to better understand and work with the fintech eco-system to meet their own innovation, growth and operational targets.
In today’s fast-paced world of financial services, innovation is central to competitiveness and growth. The financial industry is transforming with the emergence of new products, channels and business models. This change is driven by evolving customer expectations, digitalisation, as well as continued regulatory and cost pressures.
A robust fintech industry is helping spearhead both technological and business model evolutions. KPMG's dedicated fintech advisors work with businesses across their lifecycle to help them to identify growth opportunities and to develop and execute on their strategic plans in local and global markets.
###  How KPMG can help fintechs 
We offer a full suite of advisory services for fintechs, including:
###  Strategic review and funding advice 
###  Tax services, including R&D incentives 
###  Actuarial services (insurance risk, claims, underwriting) 
###  External and internal audit services 
###  Advice on risk, regulatory and compliance matters 
###  Remuneration and reward advisory 
###  Environmental, social and governance (ESG) advisory 
###  Business, operational and tech transformation advisory 
## Why choose KPMG?
We combine a strong local presence with access to a global network of fintech professionals.
This positions us well to provide advice to established organisations on the best way to leverage value from fintech partnerships or acquisition of fintech technologies and platforms.
We help analyse the disruptive market and the potential implications of new technologies on business models – and pave the way to partnering or acquisition of fintech innovations.
