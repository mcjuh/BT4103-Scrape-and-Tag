# Lamina: Innovating Participation Loans
Seamlessly share deal information and manage every stage of participation loans in one place with Lamina
[Lamina](https://www.laminafs.com/), a West Monroe Company, was developed to redefine how syndication and participation loans are handled today, maximizing speed, efficiency, and transparency at every turn.
Syndication and participation loans are riddled with challenges ranging from origination to servicing—manual data entry, inefficient processes that drag out deal times, and limited access to deals confined by existing relationships.
With Lamina, banks, credit unions, and agricultural lenders can streamline the participation loan process from start to finish.
