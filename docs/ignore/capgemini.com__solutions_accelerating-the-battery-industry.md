### As the world accelerates toward electric mobility and clean energy, battery demand is set to grow exponentially in the next decade. Decarbonization is critical for the planet’s survival, and achieving it requires the development of highly efficient, cost-effective batteries.
_Battery manufacturers are racing to accelerate production, market entry, and revenue growth. Only those who successfully balance speed, cost and quality will thrive._
In a race where time is critical, Capgemini leverages digital simulation, engineering expertise, and a strong partner ecosystem to address key challenges in the battery industry with our accelerated solutions environment (ASE) workshops that will fast-track your progress.
As a trusted advisor, we bring deep knowledge across the full battery value chain, applying a comprehensive approach that integrates business, operations, technology, engineering, and chemistry. We can be your guide, providing insights around our proven capabilities:
  * **Scale & Time-to-Market** – From construction to operations, Capgemini accelerates deployment with digital and engineering capabilities implementing end-to-end optimized business processes such as ERP, MES, and MOM, leading to a hyper-efficient gigafactory.
  * **Talent** – The Capgemini Battery Academy provides an end-to-end solution for hiring, training, and certifying workers, ensuring gigafactories have the skilled talent needed for on-time production.
  * **End-to-End Visibility** – With AI-driven, data-backed solutions, Capgemini ensures regulatory compliance to circular economy practices, traceability, resilient supply chains and closed-loop operations, optimizing performance from enterprise level to shop floor.


Battery regulations are evolving fast. With Capgemini, you get end-to-end digital traceability—ensuring full Battery Passport compliance without slowing down innovation.
#### Capgemini is playing a key role in the battery industry
We cofounded the European battery alliance with InnoEnergy, bringing together more than 800 industrials and innovation actors. Together with key research institutions and industrial partners, we have developed a first-of-its-kind Technology Cluster Battery Cell to drive innovation in the domain. We deliver powerful impact to gigafactory players, from strategic design, R&D, and pilot line to gigafactory ramp-up.
