# Energy and Mining
Kroll helps energy and mining companies analyze and address issues relating to volatile commodity markets and evolving regulatory frameworks.
[ Contact Us ](https://www.kroll.com/en/contactus)[ Client Stories ](https://www.kroll.com/en/client-stories)
Global supply chain threats, aggressive regulatory and compliance issues, geopolitical turmoil and evolving energy business models have accelerated the challenges facing energy and mining companies.
Kroll’s multidisciplinary energy teams help clients stay ahead of these complex risks. With specialists located across North and South America, Europe, the Middle East, Asia and Australia, we offer truly global coverage and local market expertise. Our specialists can work with you to identify cost-saving opportunities, assess and mitigate risks, and maximize the value and performance of your asset portfolios. We can partner with you to address both the day-to-day operational challenges of your business and the long-term strategies needed to drive sustainable growth.
Kroll's suite of solutions includes interactive valuation maps and proprietary cost estimating and reporting tools. Our risk intelligence platforms also allow energy clients to protect vulnerable assets from the threat of cyberattacks.
