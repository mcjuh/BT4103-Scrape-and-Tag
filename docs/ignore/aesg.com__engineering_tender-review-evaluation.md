## Ensuring the Best Value and Compliance for Your Façade Projects
Tender review and evaluation are critical steps in the façade engineering process, ensuring that proposed solutions meet the project's technical requirements, budget, and schedule. At AESG, we provide a comprehensive approach to tender evaluation, examining every aspect of submissions to ensure that contractors and suppliers deliver on quality, compliance, and value. Our expert team conducts thorough assessments to help clients make informed decisions, minimising risks and optimising outcomes.By leveraging our expertise in façade design, materials, and construction methods, we offer a detailed analysis of tender submissions to ensure they align with the project's objectives. Our evaluations consider technical feasibility, cost-effectiveness, regulatory compliance, and contractor capability, helping to identify the most suitable partners for your project.
## What We Offer
[ Technical Evaluation ](https://aesg.com/engineering/tender-review-evaluation/#collapse-7abe8626aa069022ec1d)
We assess the technical aspects of each tender submission, including proposed materials, construction methods, and façade system designs. Our evaluation ensures that the solutions meet the project’s specifications and performance requirements.
[ Compliance Assessment ](https://aesg.com/engineering/tender-review-evaluation/#collapse-203a7c16aa069022ec1d)
We verify that all submissions adhere to relevant regulations, industry standards, and project-specific requirements. This includes checking compliance with fire safety standards, structural integrity, and energy performance criteria.
[ Cost Analysis ](https://aesg.com/engineering/tender-review-evaluation/#collapse-daeab306aa069022ec1d)
We analyse the cost breakdown of each tender to identify value for money, ensuring the selected proposal provides the best balance between cost and quality for UK projects.
[ Contractor Capability Review ](https://aesg.com/engineering/tender-review-evaluation/#collapse-7abe8626aa069022f37d)
We evaluate the experience, track record, and resources of each contractor to ensure they have the capability to deliver the project successfully. This includes assessing their past projects and qualifications.
[ Risk Identification and Mitigation ](https://aesg.com/engineering/tender-review-evaluation/#collapse-203a7c16aa069022f37d)
We identify potential risks associated with each tender submission, such as construction complexities, material availability and recommend strategies for mitigation.
