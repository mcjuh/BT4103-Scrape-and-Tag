#  Banking and capital markets 
Change is accelerating. Organisations are transforming to stay competitive and grow amid economic uncertainty and rising capital requirements. Disruptive technologies such as AI and blockchain are redefining the norm. Evolving customer expectations, sustainability demands and shifting regulations are reshaping business models across the industry. Sector and industry convergence is gaining momentum. Less regulated markets are enabling new models of capital access for corporate clients and, increasingly, retail investors—offering longer-term funding and greater flexibility.
Meeting today’s challenges requires fast innovation and deep expertise. With the right guidance and solutions, change becomes a catalyst for gaining a competitive edge. 
## How we can help
We invest the time to understand our clients’ unique pressures. Through our extensive network of advisory, assurance and tax services, we help you build resilience, drive transformation and increase stakeholder trust. 
We collaborate with you to manage rapid change and complexity. Leverage our deep industry insight and risk and regulatory knowledge across generative AI, digital transformation, emerging technologies and sustainability. Adapt, innovate and thrive in a dynamic environment. And drive long-term value while maintaining compliance and trust.

### Maximise market strategies 
We help you optimise deals and market structures. And we provide guidance on value creation, embedded finance and strategic business alliances. Our embedded finance expertise can help integrate financial services seamlessly into customer experiences.
