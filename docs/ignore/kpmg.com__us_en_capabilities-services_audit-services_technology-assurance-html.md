#### SOC Reporting
### Third-party confidence through SOC and other attestation reporting
While technologies like cloud computing and blockchain enable more efficient service delivery and boost the attractiveness of outsourcing, they may also raise financial and reputational risks. The KPMG suite of SOC attestation services helps your company build trust in their service delivery processes and controls to increase your stakeholders’ confidence in your data.
### Do your customers seek independent assurance and confidence in the processes you perform and technologies you provide on their behalf?
SOC and other attestation services can:
  * Provide insights to help your organization enhance their controls environment
  * Deliver transparent controls-related reporting to increase customer and stakeholder confidence
  * Allow for greater trust in outsourced internal controls over financial reporting
  * Demonstrate the effectiveness of controls related to the security, availability, confidentiality, processing integrity, and privacy of the systems and data your company hosts


#### Cyber Attestation
### Fortifying Trust: Navigating The World of Cyber Attestation
As the frequency and impact of cyber threats continue to rise, it is crucial for organizations to demonstrate their effective risk management capabilities. This not only fosters trust but also facilitates transparency and education regarding cybersecurity practices.
### Do you have independent assurance over your cybersecurity risk management program? 
Cyber attestation will help:
  * Demonstrate robust internal controls and cybersecurity risk management programs
  * Provide a higher level of assurance for shareholders 
  * Show commitment to cybersecurity efforts
  * Allow flexibility in assessing control effectiveness


#### Digital Transformation
### Manage change and risk throughout your transformation journey

### Is your organization undertaking an enterprise, digital, business model or performance transformation?

  * Feedback relative to the use of more effective and cost-efficient automated application controls
  * Identification of risks that can be remediated and keep your project on track
  * Improved understanding of financial reporting processes and the ability to adopt industry-leading practices
