# Reporting & Disclosure
ERM helps organizations take control of their sustainability narrative as they respond to voluntary reporting and regulatory-driven disclosure expectations. We engage and co-create with a broad range of stakeholders, making the process a streamlined, forward-looking, value-creating opportunity, that elevates brand, strengthens relationships and informs action.

## Engage stakeholders, create business value 
Markets expect ESG strategies to be built for real impact, rather than marketing purposes. ESG performance targets and carbon metrics need to be central to the realities of business operations.
Sustainability reporting is shifting from voluntary to mandatory (e.g., CSRD/ESRS, California, IFRS/ISSB, SEC). These disclosures have common objectives: communicating ESG risks; outlining strategy; disclosing long- and short-term targets and progress; engaging stakeholders across the value chain; and strengthening ESG governance.
The proliferation of sustainability disclosures has created a complex landscape of demands for both quantitative and qualitative data. To unlock value from disclosure, organizations need to align data-gathering processes, embrace digitized solutions, and develop metrics that offer operational insight.
In making decisions on climate and sustainability, leaders must balance credibility, reputation and profitability. Issues such as ‘greenwashing’ and ‘greenhushing’ remain key concerns for organizations grappling with what data they should release into the public domain.
