# Security Agent Services
We hold security interests on behalf of creditors in a variety of jurisdictions and asset classes.
As a leading provider of agency and trustee services, Kroll offers security and collateral agent services to creditors in various types of financing transactions. Our experienced team can handle complex and multi-jurisdictional structures, ensuring that the security interests of creditors are protected and enforced in accordance with the relevant agreements.
We act as security or collateral agent for a wide range of asset classes, including real estate, aircraft, ships, intellectual property, trade receivables, inventory, equipment, contracts, shares and bank accounts. We have the capability and expertise to hold and manage both physical and digital collateral on behalf of creditors.
Our security and collateral agent services include:
  * Acting as pledgee holding security on behalf of creditors
  * Safekeeping of physical collateral
  * Enforcing security on behalf of creditors
  * Holding proceeds of enforcement on behalf of creditors
  * Filing of creditor claims in bankruptcy proceedings
  * Allocation of distributions according to post-enforcement waterfalls
  * Distributing enforcement proceeds to lenders
  * Releasing security on loan repayment or bond financing on direction of creditors
