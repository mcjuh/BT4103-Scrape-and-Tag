# Agentforce Revenue Management Accelerator
KPMG and Salesforce help solve SaaS Lead to Cash challenges, reduce sales cycles, and automate complex quoting.
## Experience the power of the Agentforce revenue management accelerator enabled by KPMG LLP
Supercharge your sales cycle and simplify complex quoting, pricing, and ordering processes with Agentforce Revenue Management (ARM), Salesforce's newest, AI-enabled, CPQ-focused solution.
Our suite of AI-powered agents transforms sales execution from start to finish. Through an intuitive conversational interface, teams can rapidly create opportunities and quotes while receiving intelligent recommendations for product bundling and pricing. AI-driven insights help sales teams streamline approvals and make informed decisions with greater confidence.
These capabilities are just the beginning. Our growing library of agents is designed to automate and enhance critical sales and revenue management processes across the end-to-end sales lifecycle. The result is a streamlined experience that empowers teams to sell smarter, reduce operational costs, and accelerate sales cycles, ultimately increasing deal profitability and customer satisfaction.
Learn more about how KPMG and Salesforce can help transform your sales processes with AI-powered agents.
