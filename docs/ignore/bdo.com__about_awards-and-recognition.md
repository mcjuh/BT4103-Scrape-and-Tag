# Awards and Recognition
Honoring our caring culture and our prominence in the profession.
## Empowering Excellence
At BDO, we are committed to helping our people, clients and communities grow together. This is something we work together to fulfill every day. We are proud of the recognitions we have received and are filled with gratitude for our professionals who show up to work as their best selves, inspiring a culture of belonging, spirit of stewardship and dedication to exceptional service.
At BDO, our professionals are the foundation of our collective success. We take building a strong culture seriously, and our work is never done. We're constantly listening, improving, and investing. Doing right by our people helps us deliver measurable results to our clients and creates a stronger impact in the communities where we live and work.
Cathy Moy
Chief People Officer, BDO USA
## A Winning Workplace
Caring is one of our defining values – so, it is imperative that we nurture a collaborative, inclusive culture and celebrate our collective accomplishments. As a leader in the profession, we are immensely honored to receive national recognition for providing best-in-class service and cultivating an exceptional workplace.
We have actively invested time, efforts and resources into becoming a more inclusive organization, and I am proud of the steps we have made towards progress. Progress, however, would not be possible without our people and their passion for fostering a culture of belonging. It is our honor to be recognized for building a workplace where each of us can thrive as our authentic selves.
Bernie Pieters
People and Culture Principal and Chief Inclusion Officer, BDO USA
