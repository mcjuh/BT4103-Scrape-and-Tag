# Sports
Kroll delivers financial and risk advisory solutions to clients across the Sports industry, providing a global team with exceptional local market expertise.
Sports teams, governing bodies and associations, as well as private equity and other investors, are tackling a complicated array of challenges: heavy scrutiny surrounding the reputations of teams and players, cybercriminal targeting of consumers, the ever-changing landscape of sports gambling regulations, the complexities of cryptocurrencies in sports betting, the well-being of athletes and the rapid growth of esports. As sports organizations grow, the tasks of valuating assets, securing reliable tax guidance, maximizing M&A value and performing critical due diligence and restructuring work have never been more challenging.
Kroll is uniquely qualified to help sports industry clients stay ahead of these challenges. For decades, Kroll has partnered with the world’s largest and most prestigious sporting associations and governing bodies to provide a wide range of valuations, opinions, tax structuring, restructuring solutions, background checking and reputational due diligence services.
Our teams of multidisciplinary experts have deep relationships with major sports organizations and clubs, leading sports franchises and emerging brands. We combine Kroll’s global reach with local expertise, providing in every engagement regional experts who are familiar not only with the sporting landscape, but also with the complexities of the local political economy and the need for transparency within the business sector.

## Kroll Sports Advisory Solutions
The core of Kroll’s Sports practice is engagement with leading sporting organizations, teams and governing bodies to tackle challenges in sports. We have developed a range of services for clients, ranging from valuation solutions for the world’s leading franchises to pre-incident awareness of risk exposure and risk mitigation tactics. In contrast with our traditional client base, sporting entities are often structured in a less conventional way, sometimes drastically, and therefore face unique challenges and regulatory obstacles. Amid an ever-changing environment, we see continued demand for our investigative skill set and the value of our sports-related services.

### 
Financial Due Diligence for a European Football Club
The client hired Kroll to assess the veracity and reliability of suspicious transactions identified by an independent expert. We were also required to identify those directors and employees involved in the alleged scheme and evaluate the damage, if any, suffered by the client. Kroll conducted an in-depth analysis of financial records, client data, a market price assessment, a forensic analysis of relevant devices, corporate intelligence and internal interviews. The findings of the investigation informed the basis of the client’s filing of a complaint with the local prosecutor.
