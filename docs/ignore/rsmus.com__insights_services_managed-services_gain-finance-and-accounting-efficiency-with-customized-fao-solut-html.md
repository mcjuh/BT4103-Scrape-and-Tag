# Gain finance and accounting efficiency with customized FAO solutions
January 21, 2025
Finance outsourcing Managed services Financial consulting
As organizations increasingly embrace finance and accounting outsourcing (FAO), they are discovering significant gains in efficiency and effectiveness. FAO solutions enhance finance and accounting functions through process and technology improvements, allowing employees to concentrate on core business activities.
Customized FAO solutions go beyond standard technology offerings by integrating specialized workflows, business processes and organizational frameworks. This comprehensive approach helps companies tackle various finance and accounting challenges, such as hiring, training and developing staff, while ensuring the timely production of actionable financial information for critical decision making.
By leveraging outsourced resources and industry-specific knowledge, companies can establish more effective back-office functions without the burden of hiring and turnover issues. This allows businesses to focus on their core operations and strategic growth, while benefiting from increased financial visibility, efficiency and scalability.
RSM’s FAO advisors can provide a full-service solution or develop a hybrid model where clients retain some in-house capabilities while outsourcing others. This flexibility ensures an FAO solution is tailored to the specific needs of the client, rather than following a one-size-fits-all approach.
