## The Trends Shaping Private Equity in 2026
  * ### The AI-Led Fund is Already Here
PE firms are rapidly splitting into AI leaders and laggards, with LPs rewarding those who can show measurable, enterprise-wide adoption. 
  * ### Private equity is being rewired by a new capital ecosystem
Sovereign wealth funds and AI-driven investment models are reshaping competition, making operational excellence the cost of entry rather than a differentiator. 
  * ### AI Moves from Experimentation to Value Creation Mandate in the Portfolio
Buyers now expect portfolio-wide AI execution, pushing companies to move beyond pilots and deliver real impact within typical PE hold periods. 
  * ### The end of easy exits
Tightening debt markets and LP pressure demand verifiable operational improvements, with firms lacking credible AI roadmaps facing valuation penalties. 
  * ### Sector Shifts Worth Watching
Manufacturing, healthcare, and life sciences are commanding premiums for vertical-specific AI tools with proven adoption amid increasing regulatory scrutiny.
