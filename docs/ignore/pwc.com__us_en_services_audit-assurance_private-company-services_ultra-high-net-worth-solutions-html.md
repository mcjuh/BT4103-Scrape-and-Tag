#  Family and Private Wealth services 
## Built around what matters most
Your wealth, your business, and your legacy are deeply connected. As families grow, so do the opportunities and complexities of managing wealth, family enterprises, and family offices. You deserve advisors who understand the technical, financial, human, and emotional dimensions. For decades, we've helped individuals, families, and family offices navigate complexity with greater clarity and confidence by connecting capabilities across tax planning, governance, technology, and risk.

## Family and Private Wealth services
  1. Define your family strategy and legacy
  2. Navigate complex tax planning and compliance
  3. Preserve, grow, and transfer family wealth
  4. Modernize your family office operations
  5. Scale your family office through technology
  6. Strengthen family governance and compliance
  7. Stay ahead of cyber, privacy, and personal risk


### Define your family strategy and legacy
Staying true to your family’s [values and purpose](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/audit-assurance/private-company-services/family-business/values-philanthropy.html) is important for preserving wealth and creating lasting impact. We help families define what they stand for, fostering alignment across generations. Through [our next-generation development programs](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/services/family-business/next-gen.html), we help prepare future leaders with the judgment, leadership skills and financial fluency to navigate the responsibilities of wealth, business ownership and legacy.
### Navigate complex tax planning and compliance
Tax considerations should be part of significant decisions about your and your family’s wealth. We help you navigate complex federal, state and local, and cross-border tax regulations, advising on direct investment strategies, entity and ownership structuring, transfer pricing, regulatory compliance, and the tax treatment of real estate, luxury and collectible assets. The goal: greater clarity and more informed decisions designed around your life, legacy and ambitions.
### Preserve, grow, and transfer family wealth
For wealth owners, families, family offices, and family enterprises, wealth is rarely simple. It can span direct investments, significant transactions, real estate, philanthropy, and trust and estate planning. We advise across the overall picture, including wealth transfer strategies, corporate development, sell-side and buy-side due diligence, [succession planning](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/audit-assurance/private-company-services/family-business/business-transition-and-succession-planning.html), philanthropy and compliance. Our approach is built around your life, not just your balance sheet.
### Modernize your family office operations
As wealth grows more complex, families and family offices may need greater rigor, professionalism and data-driven insight. Whether you’re building a new family office, enhancing an existing one or managing shareholder services, we help structure your operating model, [outsourced accounting and consolidated reporting](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/audit-assurance/private-company-services/wealth-compass.html), and related functions to help support more informed decisions and your family’s personal and financial goals.
### Scale your family office through technology
Running a family office can come with real challenges, from limited resources and complex processes to competing for talent. The right technology can help change what’s possible. We assess your current state, design a tailored technology strategy and support implementation of fit-for-purpose systems to help streamline operations, scale more effectively, and prepare for what’s next.
### Strengthen family governance and compliance
Strong governance can help families and family offices navigate complex wealth structures with greater clarity and accountability. We help implement and sustain oversight practices, drawing on deep experience to design governance models, conduct independent audits, and create tailored policies that help safeguard what you’ve built. Because good governance isn’t just about managing risk. It can also help preserve trust across generations and across the table.
### Stay ahead of cyber, privacy, and personal risk
From cyber threats and privacy concerns to operational, financial and personal security risks, today’s risk landscape is increasingly complex for wealthy individuals and family offices. A proactive approach can help. We help strengthen cybersecurity, enhance privacy safeguards, and develop tailored digital solutions to support agility and help identify emerging risks early. The goal is to help better safeguard what you’ve built and what’s private.

## Frequently asked questions
What is a family office?
A family office is a structure established to help manage a family's wealth, operations, and long-term objectives. Family offices often coordinate services such as accounting and reporting, tax planning, governance, investments, technology and risk management, helping families address increasingly complex financial and administrative needs.
What does PwC do for family offices and family enterprises? 
PwC brings together the capabilities families, family offices and family enterprises need in one place, including tax, advisory, technology and risk. Our multidisciplinary teams help clients navigate interconnected decisions across wealth, business, governance, compliance, privacy and succession planning, providing a broader perspective as needs evolve.
How can families prepare for changing needs across generations?
As family and business needs evolve, PwC connects clients with the people, capabilities and experience most relevant to their situation. Your dedicated team serves as a front door to the broader PwC network, bringing together specialists across tax, governance, technology, risk and family office operations as priorities shift.
How do I make smarter tax decisions about my wealth?
Smarter tax decisions start with understanding the overall picture: your wealth, business, family, and jurisdictions. We advise on direct investments, ownership structuring, operating businesses, real estate, transfer pricing, regulatory compliance and long-term planning, helping you consider how decisions in one area may create implications in another.
How do you work with my existing advisors?
We work alongside your trusted advisors, including investment managers, attorneys, bankers and trustees, bringing perspective across tax, governance, risk, technology, family office operations and business issues. Our role is often to help connect the dots across disciplines, so decisions can be considered in the context of the broader picture.
