#  The 2025 Digital Citizen Report 
Australians are turning to AI for answers, but government sites aren’t keeping pace. It’s time to close the gap.
[](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/dam/ps-redesign/us/en/2026/01/resources/research/digital-citizen-report/doc/digital-citizens-insights-report.pdf)
## Overview
Australians are rapidly embracing generative AI. But despite the excitement, big questions about its safety and accuracy persist, and citizens are looking towards the government to provide leadership, regulation and faster, smarter AI-powered services. Our [2025 Digital Citizenship Report](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/dam/ps-redesign/us/en/2026/01/resources/research/digital-citizen-report/doc/digital-citizens-insights-report.pdf), one of Australia’s largest surveys of its kind, provides unique insights into how generative AI is shaking up digital interaction and what governments need to be doing now to prepare for the future. By embracing generative AI today, government has a unique chance to overhaul infrastructure, ensure information is safe and seriously amp up service delivery. **You’ve got the power to lead this change. Let’s make it happen.**
## What’s inside:
**51%** of Australians are using gen AI on a daily basis **21%** are comfortable enough to access public services via generative AI **94%** are concerned about AI as a whole **45%** mistrust the information AI provides
