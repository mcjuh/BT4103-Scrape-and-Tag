## Tools to help you be an effective board member
## Strengthen your board and audit committee
Serving on a not-for-profit board can be very rewarding. Seeing the impact your organization has on individuals, your community, or even the entire world, is heart-warming and gratifying. However, it’s not without its many challenges.
Grant Thornton serves some of the largest not-for-profit organizations and higher education institutions, working closely with both board members and institutional leadership. We understand the unique challenges and pressures you face as a board member. To help you excel in advancing your organization’s mission and meeting your fiduciary responsibilities, we’ve developed an exclusive series dedicated to your special role.
