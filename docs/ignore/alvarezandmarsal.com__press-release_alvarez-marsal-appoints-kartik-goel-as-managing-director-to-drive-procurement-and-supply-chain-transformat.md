# Alvarez & Marsal Appoints Kartik Goel as Managing Director to Drive Procurement and Supply Chain Transformation
**MUMBAI, INDIA, April 14, 2026 –** Leading professional services firm [Alvarez & Marsal (A&M)](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/) India has announced the appointment of Kartik Goel as Managing Director within its Business Transformation Services practice. Based in Mumbai, Mr. Goel will focus on strengthening A&M’s procurement and supply chain transformation offerings, with an emphasis on enterprise-wide cost transformation and execution-led impact.
Mr. Goel brings extensive experience driving large-scale procurement and supply chain transformations across India and the United States including cost transformation, operating model redesign, strategic sourcing, end-to-end process improvement, logistics and network strategy, and digital procurement. He has a strong track record of translating operational diagnostics into measurable P&L impact.
His notable engagements include procurement cost take-out and operating model redesign for leading footwear manufacturers and retailers, MRO (maintenance, repair, and operations) cost reduction for one of the world’s largest food and beverage players, and end-to-end operations diagnostics for a major used-car retailer in the US. He has also led digital twin strategy for a leading quick service restaurant chain.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Goel was a Partner at Boston Consulting Group (BCG), where he led operations and supply chain engagements across North America and India and served as North America Operations Pipeline Lead.
[_**Himanshu Bajaj**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/himanshu-bajaj) _**, Managing Director & Head – A&M India and GCC,** said, “Procurement and supply chain functions are becoming central to driving efficiency, resilience, and measurable financial outcomes. Clients are seeking partners who can move beyond strategy to execution. Kartik’s experience in driving cost transformation and operational improvements will be instrumental in strengthening our capabilities and delivering sustained impact.”_
[_**Kartik Goel,**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/kartik-goel)_**Managing Director, A &M India,** said, “Organizations are increasingly focused on optimizing costs, managing complexity, and embedding digital and AI capabilities into their operations. A&M’s hands-on, execution-led approach provides a strong platform to translate strategy into results. I look forward to working with clients to build more agile, data-driven supply chains and deliver lasting value.”_
