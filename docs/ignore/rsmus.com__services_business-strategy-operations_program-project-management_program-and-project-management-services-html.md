# Program and project management as a service
### Deliver complex initiatives on time and within budget
#### [Let’s talk about your next initiative ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/program-project-management/program-and-project-management-services.html#contact)
### **Enterprise execution through strategic program and project management services**
In today’s fast-moving business environment, successful execution is as critical as strategy. RSM’s program and project management services help organizations align initiatives with enterprise goals, manage risk and accelerate value realization. Whether launching an artificial intelligence transformation, integrating systems or scaling operations, our advisors provide the structure, discipline and insight to deliver results.
Many organizations struggle to translate strategy into action. Without the right governance, tools and leadership, even well-funded initiatives can stall. RSM helps bridge the gap between vision and execution, so your programs deliver measurable business outcomes.
### **Flexible delivery, proven methodology**
We tailor our project management services to your organization’s needs, whether you require full program oversight or targeted support. Our advisors bring deep experience in agile, waterfall and hybrid methodologies, and work collaboratively with your teams to embed best practices and build internal capability.
### **Built for the C-suite, trusted by the enterprise**
We work with chief financial officers, chief information officers and chief human resources officers and other senior leaders across industries to manage enterprise-wide initiatives, including enterprise resource planning implementations, M&A integrations, artificial intelligence and digital transformations, and regulatory compliance programs.
### **Deliverables of RSM’s program and project management services**
  * Program governance frameworks
  * Project charters and execution plans
  * Risk and issue management protocols
  * Stakeholder communication strategies
  * Resource and budget tracking tools
  * Project management office setup and optimization
  * Agile coaching and delivery support
  * Post-implementation reviews and insights


##  Frequently asked questions  

What is program and project management as a service?  
    
Program and project management as a service is a flexible, outsourced model where RSM provides experienced professionals and tools to manage your initiatives from start to finish. 

How does RSM tailor its approach to our organization?  
    
We assess your goals, culture and existing capabilities to design a delivery model that fits, whether agile, waterfall or hybrid. 

What industries does RSM support?  
    
We serve clients across manufacturing, health care, financial services, technology and more. 

Can RSM help us set up a PMO?  
    
Yes. We offer PMO design, implementation and optimization services to help you build sustainable project delivery capabilities. 

What tools and methodologies does RSM use?  
    
We are tool-agnostic and experienced in platforms like Microsoft Project, Smartsheet, Jira and ServiceNow, among others. 

How does RSM maintain stakeholder alignment?  
    
RSM achieves stakeholder alignment through structured communication plans, governance models and regular reporting tailored to executive and operational audiences. 

What’s the difference between program and project management?  
    
Projects are temporary efforts with specific goals, while programs are collections of related projects managed in a coordinated way. 

How quickly can RSM mobilize a team?  
    
We can typically deploy resources within days, depending on scope and availability.
