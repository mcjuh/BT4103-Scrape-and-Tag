# Streamline indirect tax with Avalara
## How we work with Avalara
Grant Thornton implements and customizes Avalara tax determination solutions with seasoned practitioners who provide proven business insight.
Here’s how Grant Thornton and Avalara work together to help clients achieve streamlined automation in global indirect tax determination and reporting:
### Avalara provides
  * A cloud-based leading indirect tax calculation and compliance solution
  * An exhaustive collection of prebuilt connectors for integration with billing, ERP, procurement, and e-commerce platforms
  * Continually updated tax rules and rates with industry-specific content
  * Rooftop address validation for customer addresses


### Grant Thornton provides
  * Enterprise tax specialists with experience as tax practitioners
  * Certified Avalara implementors to serve clients globally
  * End-to-end Avalara implementation services and ongoing support, including current state review, requirements gathering, design, system integration, configuration, testing and more for a range of host systems
  * A full spectrum of tax services, such as tax consulting, tax research, product taxability analysis and outsourcing
  * A product taxability matrix for your sales and purchases


Together, Grant Thornton and Avalara help you implement and tailor your North American and global indirect tax determination. It’s just one of the [**strategic alliances**](https://www.grantthornton.com/services/alliances) that help Grant Thornton drive more value for our customers.
