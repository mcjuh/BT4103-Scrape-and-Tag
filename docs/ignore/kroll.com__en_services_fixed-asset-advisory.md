# Fixed Asset Advisory Services
Kroll provides clients the transparency they need by identifying, managing and valuing their fixed assets.
Our valuation programs, portfolio review services, risk mapping and dashboard creation focuses on structured data collection and digital reporting that improves financial oversight, regulatory compliance and risk management.
We serve clients throughout the world, in virtually every industry, with our best practices approach to real estate, machinery and equipment valuation, property insurance appraisal and fixed asset inventory and reconciliation. As the leading independent valuation services provider, we have expertise in all classifications of real and personal property. Each year, we value assets valued at over $1 trillion for insurance. Additionally, we are an industry leader in fixed asset valuation, inventory tagging and reconciliation services, providing our clients with the data visibility needed for fixed asset management and improved financial reporting, capital budgeting and accurate property tax valuation. Our professionals draw upon deep global experience and leading-edge technology to constantly evolve and refine our services to assist clients with their financial and risk management responsibilities.
