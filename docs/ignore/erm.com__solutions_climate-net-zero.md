# Climate & Net Zero
Net zero demands a robust strategy that is practical, defensible and actionable by the organization, without compromising commercial objectives. ERM partners with clients to create long-term value by translating the corporate climate vision into reimagined business models and portfolios, as well as asset level implementation actions that enhance operational performance.
## Balance decarbonization with profitable growth 
Stakeholders are demanding net zero alignment. Organizations and governments are facing expectations from investors, regulators, customers and society for a just transition to a low-carbon economy that incorporates climate justice and human rights. 
Climate-related risks and opportunities are transforming the way organizations operate. Timely and purposeful identification, analysis and disclosure of climate impacts are critical for creating and protecting value. 
Market shifts, technology disruptors and policy changes introduce material financial risks and opportunities. An informed and agile response is needed to safeguard credibility, reputation and profitability. 
Financial compliance obligations, which initially focused on financial institutions, are now expanding to listed corporates and large privately-owned businesses.
## Safeguard revenue and reputation with a future-proofed net zero strategy.
Align to capital markets
ERM’s approach delivers accurate and transparent climate-related financial risk and opportunity analysis. We help align value chain carbon emission evaluations with capital market expectations and business plans, which facilitates access to larger pools of capital at a lower cost. 
Operationalize strategy
ERM leverages industrial and operational expertise to convert corporate net zero strategies into on-the-ground action. We assist clients with decarbonizing operations, evaluating products and services, and adjusting portfolios through mergers and acquisitions. 
Establish net zero mindsets and processes 
We accelerate organizational transformation by embedding change management into net zero strategy planning and roadmaps. By building carbon fluency with leaders and teams, tasks and initiatives can be contextualized and executed more effectively.
Unlock richer climate insights 
ERM helps organizations enhance and harness climate data and analytics. We establish the capabilities and IT systems needed to advance net zero goals and portfolios and to enable effective progress reporting to investors and regulators.
