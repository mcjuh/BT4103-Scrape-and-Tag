#  Digital Assets for Financial Services 
Innovative solutions for the evolving world of cryptocurrencies and blockchain technology 
Copy link Link copied to clipboard 
At PwC Singapore, we're at the forefront of delivering dynamic professional services in the digital assets space. Leveraging our robust network and expertise, we provide professional guidance and cutting-edge solutions to ensure our clients successfully navigate and excel in this ever-evolving ecosystem. 
## How can PwC help?
Whether you're an established financial institution or a startup challenging the status quo, we tailor our services to align with your unique needs and goals. We offer tailored, secure and cutting-edge digital asset solutions that help clients navigate complex regulations, manage risk and build trust in a rapidly changing global landscape. Our comprehensive suite of offerings is designed to meet the complex requirements of our clients, powered by deep expertise in digital assets, blockchain technology and regulatory frameworks. We empower informed decision-making through client-centric, customisable solutions and enhance the reach of our services through our global network.
Security is a fundamental aspect of our approach. We ensure your digital assets are safeguarded through advanced cybersecurity practices, alongside robust security reviews and testing. At the same time, we drive innovation by offering insights into emerging trends in cryptocurrency markets, stablecoins and regulatory developments. Our work is driven by a clear mission: to build trust in society and address the most significant challenges in the digital assets space.

Market research and strategy advisory
  * In-depth market research and competitive analysis on digital assets, blockchain and crypto trends to guide informed business decisions and industry benchmarking
  * Development of market entry and expansion strategies for blockchain technology adoption and integration
  * Long term vision setting and roadmap development for blockchain startups
  * Risk management and business continuation strategies


Deals advisory
  * Financial, operations, vendor, IT and HR due diligence services
  * Strategic mergers and acquisitions (M&A) advisory, including structuring of deals and navigating the regulatory complexities
  * Whitepaper preparation for token launching, including the strategy development, structuring of token sales, distribution models, regulatory aspects and governance systems
  * Fundraising strategy and support for startups and established businesses
  * Cost optimisation and value creation implementation


Regulatory licensing support
  * Assistance with planning, obtaining and maintaining Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) licenses for crypto businesses
  * Guidance on compliance with MAS’ cryptocurrency regulations (e.g. AML/KYC)
  * Risk assessments and legal frameworks for digital asset transactions
  * Technology risk management review
  * Guidance on meeting other regulatory, tax and financial reporting requirements for holders and issuers of cryptocurrencies


Controls and process readiness reviews
  * Review and assessment of blockchain technology solutions and digital asset processes
  * Evaluation of internal controls and security practices related to blockchain integration
  * Risk analysis and process improvements for digital asset custody
  * Development and implementation of governance frameworks


Attestation and audit
  * Statutory audits for financial statements or annual report
  * Internal audits over key business processes
  * Attestation related to digital payment token (DPT) licensing requirements
  * Stablecoin attestation
