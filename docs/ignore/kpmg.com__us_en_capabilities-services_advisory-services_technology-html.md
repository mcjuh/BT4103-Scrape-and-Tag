# Modern Technology
Our technology leaders help organizations implement artificial intelligence, improve data and analytics, enhance cybersecurity, leverage the cloud, and more, driving speed to modern technology.
## Leveraging technology for business growth, innovation, and resilience
Business leaders want to harness technology for strategic advantage and sustainable growth. But many challenges can arise on the transformation journey: technology complexity, balancing innovation with efficiency, cybersecurity and privacy, hiring the right talent, cost management, and aligning technology with business strategy.
KPMG’s technology solutions can help you achieve the following outcomes:
Business growth and profitability
To drive growth and profitability, businesses can leverage technology to streamline operations, expand market reach, improve customer experience, optimize supply chains, and implement automation and artificial intelligence. 
Innovation and competitive advantage
Businesses can encourage innovation, establish a competitive advantage, and stay ahead in a rapidly evolving marketplace by strategically leveraging technology related to research and development, collaboration and knowledge sharing, market research and consumer insights, customer-centric experiences, continuous learning, and upskilling. 
Resilience and risk mitigation
Businesses can improve resiliency and mitigate various risks by using technology that can facilitate data backup and recovery, cybersecurity, disaster recovery planning, predictive analytics, business continuity planning, and regulatory compliance. 
Effective decision making and strategic planning
Utilizing technology for business intelligence, decision support systems, real-time monitoring and reporting, scenario planning and modeling, and visualization tools can facilitate effective decision making and strategic planning for businesses.
##  Making technology work for you 
Business leaders look to technology to enhance operational efficiency, enable data-driven decision-making, improve customer experiences, foster agile workforces, optimize supply chains, and strengthen resilience. That’s way it’s crucial for organizations to align technology initiatives with overall business goals.
Over the last 15 years, we’ve built a leading technology organization designed specifically to help IT leaders succeed at the accelerated pace business now demands.
The combination of KPMG’s comprehensive expertise, strategic partnerships, innovative solutions, global reach, and cybersecurity excellence enables our professionals to help organizations adapt to evolving technological trends to stay competitive in today's fast-paced digital landscape.
