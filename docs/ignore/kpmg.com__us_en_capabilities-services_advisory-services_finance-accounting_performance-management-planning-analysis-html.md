# EPM, FP&A, and Analytics
Leading FP&A professionals leverage a holistic approach to align business strategy and KPIs to financial planning. Enabling an organization's performance management with advanced analytics increases speed and quality of analysis, improves decision-making and insights, and enables further integrated business planning across the organization.
Financial planning and analysis (FP&A) functions traditionally struggle with the time and resources required to create annual operating plans. Because much of this is due to continued manual forecasting, FP&A teams need to embrace more sophisticated technology solutions to enhance operations, create more accurate forecasts in the face of market uncertainty, and convey that information more effectively to organization leaders. Advanced predictive analytics can better anticipate changing business drivers, while rapid strategic modeling can help assess the impact of unexpected external events and business model shifts.
As a recognized leader in this space, KPMG brings a “business integrator” approach that leverages a broad set of capabilities. KPMG was named a “Vanguard Leader” in Finance Excellence Consulting in a recent ALM Intelligence report with “the deepest capabilities” in Finance Excellence amongst the organizations evaluated. Our services include enhancing existing enterprise performance management (EPM) technologies, defining FP&A strategy, assessing your plan-to-perform process, deploying a full-scale transformation of EPM technology, and more. Our approach, methodologies, and tools are time-tested across various industries and have consistently delivered enhanced strategic value to the finance function.
Integrated planning and analysis across functions
Digital EPM allows organizations to collaborate on planning and reporting related activities in a virtual environment. Finance organizations can more effectively collaborate with cross-functional partners and increase visibility into the operational drivers and key assumptions used in business forecasting.
Improved forecast accuracy
Tools with predictive analytics capability employ AI and machine learning to analyze previously unmanageable reams of data—both internal and external—to identify business trends and drivers that have a bearing on organizational objectives. These tools provide innovative insights through advanced statistical modeling techniques and enable a much higher level of transparency around business drivers. By decreasing the amount of manual activity, they improve shareholder value and enable a reallocation of revenue or cost savings to other growth initiatives.
Enhanced insights and decision making
EPM and other advanced data analytic (D&A) capabilities provide FP&A functions with an opportunity to influence the entire enterprise. They leverage data differently, integrate the most current internal and historical data with external signals, and incorporate innovative visualization tools that create vivid, user-friendly, and easily understood graphics that help CFOs, management, and other nonfinancial professionals understand the data. These capabilities can lend themselves to realtime decision-making based on the most up-to-date data.
Increased forecasting speed
Emerging technology and powerful D&A tools enable FP&A teams to analyze enormous sets of data far more efficiently and quickly than ever before. As a result, the teams can enhance and accelerate the speed of their scenario planning, forecasts, and actuals analyses. Earlier visibility to initial results drives faster resource, procurement, or investment decisions.
