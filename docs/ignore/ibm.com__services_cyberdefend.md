##  Safeguard data, identities, workloads and platforms across your hybrid cloud 
IBM CyberDefend® is a comprehensive cybersecurity services offering that empowers organizations to safeguard their digital assets in the face of evolving threats. We combine AI-infused consulting with cutting-edge tech to safeguard data, identities, workloads and platforms across your hybrid cloud, leading to: ​ 
  * Cloud adoption acceleration ​
  * Increase of organizational security return on investment (ROI) ​
  * Elimination of technical debt ​
  * Development of a security posture adaptable to evolving threats and technologies
  * Transformation of your security program into an operational enabler ​ ​


From protecting AI systems, to transforming cryptography, to IdOPS, to AppSec to cloud security, IBM CyberDefend assists your business in driving innovation without exposing itself to novel risks.

##  Strategic partnerships
###  Microsoft 
IBM Security® Services and Microsoft have formed a strategic alliance to deliver end‑to‑end threat management across enterprises. Their combined solutions support secure cloud migration, modernization and business transformation by using Microsoft Cloud and IBM’s security expertise. 
IBM contributes a full portfolio covering security strategy, risk consulting, threat protection and multi‑cloud solutions, enhanced by tools like ATOM and AskIAM™. Together, they help organizations address challenges such as identity protection, compliance and technical debt, while using gen AI‑powered security solutions to stay ahead of threats.
[ Learn more about our partnership with Microsoft  ](https://www.ibm.com/services/microsoft-azure-security)
###  Amazon Web Services 
IBM Consulting® Security Services is an AWS Level 1 managed security service provider (MSSP) Competency Partner. We work with your organization as an extension of your team, offering advanced solutions and expert guidance to help protect your AWS cloud environment. 
We enable you to embrace AWS native controls while aligning them with your enterprise security programs to simplify and centralize visibility.
[ Learn more about our partnership with AWS  ](https://www.ibm.com/aws/security)
###  Palo Alto Networks 
Organizations adopting AI and digital technologies face growing cybersecurity complexity due to fragmented tools, siloed teams and advanced threats—leading to increased risk and potential revenue loss.
To address these issues, many leaders are shifting their view of cybersecurity from a cost center to a driver of growth and innovation. This approach is accelerating the adoption of unified, AI-powered security platforms that improve visibility, speed and resilience.
IBM and Palo Alto Networks have collaborated to deliver such an approach, helping organizations simplify security, strengthen operations and unlock value from digital transformation.
