## Fire Risk Assessments (FRAs)
Our Fire Risk Assessments are conducted in accordance with the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005. We provide:
### Comprehensive Hazard Identification
We identify potential fire hazards specific to your premises.
### Actionable Recommendations
Our detailed reports include clear, prioritised recommendations for risk reduction and safety improvements.
### Risk Evaluation
We assess the likelihood of fire incidents and the potential impact on occupants and property.
### Compliance Documentation
We ensure your FRA is documented properly to meet legal obligations.
## External & Internal Fire Surveys
Our thorough fire surveys cover both external and internal aspects of your building:
### Internal Fire Surveys
We assess fire safety systems, escape routes, compartmentation, and emergency lighting.
### Holistic Approach
We identify vulnerabilities across all areas of your property to ensure a comprehensive understanding of fire risks.
### External Fire Surveys
Our inspections evaluate external fire safety measures, including access for emergency services.
