# Building Information Modelling (BIM) Management

## [Insights](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/bim-management/#Insights)
Building Information Modelling (BIM) is at the core of digital project delivery, ensuring seamless coordination, data accuracy, and interoperability across all project stages. AESG’s BIM Management service provides structured oversight and technical expertise to help clients define, manage, and optimize their BIM workflows. Our approach ensures compliance with industry standards while enabling efficient collaboration, data management, and clash-free design integration.
## Key Services
[ Development of Information Requirements (EIRs, AIRs, PIRs) ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/bim-management/#collapse-7abe8626aa068045c4a4)
Assisting clients in defining Exchange Information Requirements (EIRs), Asset Information Requirements (AIRs), and Project Information Requirements (PIRs).
[ BIM Strategy ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/bim-management/#collapse-203a7c16aa068045c4a4)
We develop bespoke BIM Execution Plans (BEPs), Master Information Delivery Plans (MIDPs) AND Task Information Delivery Plans (TIDPs) to establish workflows, responsibilities and standards to effectively drive BIM implementation on projects.
[ BIM Data Management ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/bim-management/#collapse-7abe8626aa068045db3c)
We ensure the validation and verification of BIM data across the project lifecycle.
[ BIM Coordination ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/bim-management/#collapse-ed0f8e36aa068045db3c)
We oversee and manage the clash detection and design conflicts to facilitate the coordination and accuracy in BIM Models.
By integrating BIM methodologies, strategic data management, and coordination workflows, AESG enables project teams to reduce risks, improve collaboration, and enhance the value of BIM Models produced on projects.
