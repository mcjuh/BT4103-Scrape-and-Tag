# AI Meets Reality Podcast
Real Leaders, Real Stories, Real-World AI
## About the Podcast
As artificial intelligence moves from concept to daily practice, organizations are discovering both its potential and its complexities. AI Meets Reality explores how AI is being used in real-world settings, highlighting practical applications, unexpected challenges, and successes. From automating routine tasks to supporting strategic innovation, AI is changing how businesses adapt and grow in a rapidly evolving landscape.
BDO’s AI Meets Reality is a podcast hosted by [Kirstie Tiernan](https://www.bdo.com/kirstie-tiernan), AI Solution Leader. Each episode features candid conversations with executives and innovators about the realities of AI - what’s working, what’s challenging, and what’s still uncertain.
Learn about emerging trends, practical applications, and real-world experiences to help your organization navigate the opportunities and complexities of artificial intelligence.

### Data, Governance, and the Road to Real AI
31 Min
#### SUMMARY
This episode features Jolanta Pomiotio, who shares her perspective on driving innovation at the intersection of operations, technology, and AI. 
#### NOTES
**Guest LinkedIn:**[Jolanta Pomiotlo | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jolanta-pomiotlo/)
**Podcast Key Takeaways:**
**Start with the Business Problem**
Jolanta Pomiotlo emphasizes that organizations should not pursue AI for its own sake; the best results come from identifying a real business problem first and then applying the right technology to solve it.
**Strong Data Foundations Enable AI Success**
The conversation highlights that data quality, context, and governance are essential to moving AI initiatives beyond pilot mode and into production, since poor or fragmented data leads to poor outcomes.
**Start Small to Build Momentum**
Rather than beginning with large, complex AI initiatives, the episode stresses the value of starting with smaller, more manageable use cases where data is stronger and value can be demonstrated quickly.
**Change Enablement Drives Adoption**
Successful AI rollout depends on tailored training, change champions, and helping employees rethink how they approach their work, while addressing resistance and building confidence in the tools.
**Governance and Security Must Come First**
As AI tools become embedded in daily workflows, organizations need clear guardrails around permissions, sensitive data, and platform governance to ensure secure and responsible use.
**AI Accelerates Productivity, Not Just Technology**
The episode explores how AI can help teams prototype faster, reduce manual effort, and work through larger backlogs, ultimately enabling organizations to deliver value more quickly.

### Beyond the Dashboard: How AI Agents Are Changing Work
26 Min
#### SUMMARY
This episode features Fiona Towner, who shares her perspective on leading AI and analytics strategy at Luck Companies. 
#### NOTES
[Guest LinkedIn: Fiona Towner | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fiona-towner-07273b256/)
**Podcast Key Takeaways:**
**Practical AI Adoption Matters**
Fiona Towner emphasizes that successful AI adoption starts with practical use cases, helping employees understand how the technology can support day-to-day work and deliver measurable value.
**Peer Learning Accelerates Change**
Organic, peer-to-peer knowledge sharing can be more effective than top-down training, building curiosity, confidence, and stronger adoption across the organization.
**Agentic AI Is Reshaping Analytics**
The conversation explores how AI agents are beginning to move users beyond static dashboards by enabling more dynamic analysis, recommendations, and action-oriented insights.
**Trust and Governance Remain Critical**
As AI becomes more embedded in workflows, organizations must balance speed and efficiency with validation, governance, and responsible use of data.
**Strategy and Budget Must Align**
Scaling AI requires leadership support, thoughtful investment, and a clear focus on where automation, copilots, or agents can create the greatest business impact.

### Healing with Heart and AI: St. Jude’s Fundraising Journey
32 Min
#### SUMMARY
This episode features Erica Albert, who shares her story as a cancer survivor and her role in advancing St. Jude’s mission. This podcast explores how AI tools enhance donor engagement, while authentic relationships remain at the core of fundraising. The episode looks at challenges with data responsibility and the hospital’s leadership in genomics research. The hospital’s commitment to hope, healing, and innovation is at the heart of the discussion. 
#### NOTES
**Guest Notes**
Erika Albert is the program strategist for major and principal gifts at ALSAC, the fundraising and awareness organization for St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital. In her role, she builds relationships with donors, develops fundraising strategies, and helps secure critical support to advance St. Jude’s mission of providing free care and advancing pediatric research. 
[Guest LinkedIn: Erika Albert](https://www.linkedin.com/in/erika-albert-04564213/)
**Podcast Key Takeaways**
**Personal Connection Drives Mission**
Erika Albert’s experience as a childhood cancer survivor deeply informs her passion and commitment to fundraising for St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital. 
**AI Enhances Fundraising**
The use of AI tools, such as Copilot and ChatGPT, helps Erica and her team streamline donor research, personalize outreach, and summarize complex medical information, slowing them to focus on building authentic relationships.
**Balancing Technology and Humanity**
While AI offers efficiency, maintain genuine human connection with donors remain essential, especially in the era of increasing autonomation and skepticism about authenticity. 
**Ethical and Data Challenges**
The team faces ongoing challenges around data privacy, model accuracy, and ethical use of donor information, highlighting the importance of responsible technology adoption in philanthropy
**St. Jude’s Global Impact**
St. Jude’s work extends beyond patient care, with innovative genomics research and international collaboration accelerating program progress in pediatric cancer treatment and benefiting children worldwide.

### From Aha! To AI: A Five-Step Journey with Carlin Power at JLL
35 Min
#### SUMMARY
Join host Kirstie Tiernan for the first episode of AI Meets Reality as she talks with Carlin Power about the real-world impact of artificial intelligence. Carlin shares personal and professional stories of AI in action, revealing the lessons learned and surprises encountered along the way. This podcast uncovers how today’s leaders are turning AI potential into practical results, transforming the way we work, lead, and innovate.
#### NOTES
Guest LinkedIn
[Carlin Power, MBA | LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carlin-power-mba-0174b199/)
**Key Takeaways:**
**Educate: Spark Curiosity and Confidence**
JLL began its AI journey by inspiring experimentation and providing practical training, making AI approachable and relevant for every employee.
**Define Strategy: Connect Vision to Impact**
A clear, company-wide AI strategy - centered on measurable goals and consistent messaging - ensured that every initiative aligned with business outcomes.
**Lay the Foundation: Build Security and Trust**
JLL prioritized data security, privacy, and governance from day one, creating a trusted environment for safe and scalable AI adoption.
**Prepare Your People: Empower and Engage**
Continuous feedback, tailored training, and user-centric design enabled employees to confidently adopt, use, and shape AI tools in their daily work.
**Go & Grow: Sustain Momentum and Scale Innovation**
JLL is investing in agentic AI and citizen development, scaling expertise and empowering teams to create future-ready solutions that drive ongoing impact.
