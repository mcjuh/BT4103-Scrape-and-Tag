#  Smash Through Tech Debt with AI 
Modern enterprises can’t afford incremental fixes. This report outlines how to use AI to dismantle legacy systems and reset your organization for speed, resilience and growth.
[](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/dam/ps-redesign/us/en/2026/01/resources/research/hfs-ai-tech-debt-report/doc/hfs-ps-report-smash-tech-debt.pdf)
## Legacy is holding back more than just your IT. Let’s fix that.
Legacy tech isn’t just slowing systems—it’s holding entire businesses back. This research report, created in partnership with HFS, is based on insights from more than 600 IT and business leaders across industries and reveals a growing recognition that breaking free from tech debt requires more than new technology. More than 80 percent of senior executives polled say AI is the breakthrough needed to finally overcome entrenched technical debt. But technology alone won’t get you there. Mindset and bold moves are required for this shift.

## Five moves for AI-native leaders
Inside this HFS Research Report you’ll find a blueprint for more than just technology overlays. We roadmap rebuilds from the ground up and provide practical guidance for the cost metrics that matter most. Here are the five moves that separate AI-native leaders from those automating their stagnation: 
  1. Treating tech debt like financial debt and tracking, prioritizing and eliminating it
  2. Building around AI rather than bolting it on
  3. Moving from labor-first outsourcing to outcome-based partners
  4. Pricing for business value, not effort
  5. Redesigning roles, processes and culture for continuous AI-driven reinvention
