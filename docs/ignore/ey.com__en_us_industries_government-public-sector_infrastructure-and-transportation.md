#  Government infrastructure and transportation 
Infrastructure and transportation agencies are under pressure to deliver more — faster, smarter and with greater accountability. EY professionals help public sector leaders turn funding, policy and ambition into real‑world results that strengthen mobility, resilience and public trust.

## What EY teams can do for you:
We help infrastructure and transportation agencies make smarter investment choices, secure and deploy funding, and deliver complex programs with confidence. EY teams bring strategy, delivery discipline and risk management together to help projects stay on track, strengthen resilience and earn public trust.
### Outcomes that matter
Let’s collaborate to achieve results such as:
  * Clear, defensible prioritization of capital investments
  * Faster movement from funding approval to project start
  * Fewer delivery disruptions through stronger controls and risk management
  * Greater transparency across programs, portfolios and stakeholders
  * Infrastructure investments that translate into visible, community‑level impact

Transportation capabilities: Our approach helps infrastructure and transportation agencies turn funding, policy and ambition into real-world results. The transportation team aligns smart investment prioritization, disciplined program delivery and transparent risk management to accelerate project execution, strengthen resilience and deliver visible community impact. Services include Department of Transportation (DOT), Tolling, Transit and Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA) and Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) modernization, helping agencies improve project delivery, modernize operations, enhance customer experience, strengthen governance, increase efficiency and build public trust.
