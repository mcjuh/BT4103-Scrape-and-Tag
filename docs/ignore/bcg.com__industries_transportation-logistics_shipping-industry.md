# Shipping Industry and Marine Ports
Volatile trade-flow dynamics and heightened emissions regulations mean many standard shipping industry approaches are no longer viable. Our international shipping consulting team helps shipping lines and port operators emerge leaner, cleaner, and more profitable.
To ensure long-term survival, the maritime shipping industry must undertake a transformation that digitizes business and fleet operations, automates end-to-end operations, leverages [GenAI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence/generative-ai), and prioritizes [decarbonization](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/climate-change-sustainability/decarbonization). We help clients devise new offerings and new ways of working to reduce emissions, save costs, and serve new markets and customers.

## Our Approach to Maritime Consulting
BCG’s proprietary [Shipping Benchmark Initiative](https://www.bcg.com/industries/transportation-logistics/shipping-benchmark-initiative/overview) provides the most comprehensive and reliable benchmarks in the shipping industry, delivering the data-driven insights companies need to get ahead in the volatile world of global shipping. Armed with this knowledge, companies are better prepared to tackle today’s most pressing industry challenges, including direct and indirect emissions, operational cost [digitalization](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/digital-transformation/overview), and global trade disruptions.

## Our Client Impact on Shipping Strategy
Our international shipping consulting team has worked with marine ports and shipping industry leaders around the world. Some of our recent successes include the following:
BCG has joined forces with several Danish transportation and logistics companies, the Danish government, and Danish-based engineering consulting firm COWI in a partnership to **develop a new hydrogen and e-fuel production facility** in Copenhagen. The facility now delivers more than 250,000 tons of sustainable fuel every year for buses, trucks, maritime vessels, and airplanes.
A container company had consistently been among the least profitable in the shipping industry. We helped **implement a transformation program for the container ship operator** , optimizing trade coverage, redesigning the sales organization, and identifying significant cost-cutting opportunities. By the second year of the effort, the company’s earnings before interest and taxes had risen above the market average.
BCG helped build a predictive maintenance capability within a large international shipping company. By installing monitoring sensors in its control systems and engine rooms, **the company boosted the reliability of its fleet** and cut maintenance costs.

[Our ESG Playbook for Shipping Decarbonization](https://www.zerocarbonshipping.com/publications/the-esg-playbook-for-shipping/)
The shipping industry is responsible for 3% of worldwide greenhouse gas emissions, but only 46% of the largest shipping companies have taken responsibility by committing to environmental, social, and governance (ESG) targets. That’s why BCG and the Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Center for Zero Carbon Shipping have developed an ESG Playbook for Shipping. Designed to help navigate the ESG landscape, this playbook describes tangible steps to help shipping companies set—and reach—ambitious ESG targets.
