What makes Kroll the preferred partner? With an industry-leading team of more than 185 experts based in London, New York and Bangalore, we are dedicated to providing the highest and most efficient levels of client service, making us the trusted provider for the restructuring and insolvency market’s leading lenders, debt advisors, sponsors and their advisors. How do Agency and Trustee Services Facilitate Corporate Debt Restructuring?
Having a competent, responsive and non-conflicted agent in place is critical to ensuring the successful facilitation of a debt restructuring or debt recapitalization
Clients choose Kroll to act as a commercial agent in restructurings to successfully implement all manner of consensual and nonconsensual restructuring and recapitalization strategies, from discussion on engagement and assigning a dedicated team of professionals to the transaction, to negotiation of deal documentation and enforcement of security.
