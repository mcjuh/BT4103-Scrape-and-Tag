## Meet our leaders
### Michael Schulte
CEO Engineering, Group Executive Board member
Michael has been CEO of Engineering since January 2026, and a member of the Group Executive Board since January 2021. Prior to this, from 2021, he was CEO of Northern Europe. Michael was the Managing Director of Capgemini’s Business Unit in Germany from July 2018 to December 2020, after having led the Application Services business in Germany since 2013. Under his leadership Capgemini reached market leading positions both in management consulting and in application development, and became a leading brand for digital talent.
### Alexandre Audoin
Executive Vice President, Global Head of Industries, Sales & Portfolio, Capgemini Engineering
Alexandre Audoin has led Capgemini and Capgemini Engineering global Automotive Industry for three years. Since July 2024, Alexandre is Capgemini Engineering Global Head of Industries, Sales & Portfolio with a special focus on the creation of Intelligent Industry, helping clients master the end-to-end software-driven transformation and do business in a new way through technologies like 5G, edge computing, artificial intelligence (AI), and the internet of things (IoT).
### Volkmar Varnhagen
Chief Operating Officer, Capgemini Engineering; Member of the Capgemini Group Executive Committee
Volkmar Varnhagen is Chief Operating Officer of Capgemini Engineering and a member of the Capgemini Group Executive Committee. He oversees operations, cross-regional collaboration and strategic growth. He holds a PhD in Mechanical Engineering and brings extensive expertise in business and engineering transformation, as well as operational excellence, with a strong track record across the automotive and manufacturing industries.
### Abdelmajid Boutayeb
Executive Vice-President, North America Cloud & Custom Applications Practice Head
A graduate of ENSEEIHT engineering school in France, Abdelmajid started his career as an engineer and consultant with Tractebel Engineering. He joined Capgemini in 2009 from Arjowiggins, worldwide leader of technical papers, where he was CIO. After diverse roles at Capgemini, and prior to his current role, he was global sales officer in digital engineering and manufacturing with responsibility for the North America region, with a focus on leveraging OT and IT to support legacy industrial businesses, and enter the connected and smart engineering products and digital manufacturing worlds.
### Igor Bieda

An experienced engineering-services leader, he focuses on engineering excellence, innovation and sustainable impact, following a 25-year career in the software engineering services industry. 
### Riccardo Dolfi
