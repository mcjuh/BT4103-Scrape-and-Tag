#  Prioritise green growth 
With climate change driving macro-economic change across all sectors and regions of th world economy, many towns and cities are considering what this agenda will mean for their future development. There are big implications for local trade and industry, levels of employment and productivity as well as population growth.
Arup’s Advisory Services team is helping city clients worldwide to explore the green growth potential their cities or regions already possess, or might develop. There’s a clear recognition that productivity can no longer come at any cost. Not every city will become a global centre of EV or wind turbine production, so it’s about thinking more deeply about the green growth that’s most relevant to your location, people and resources.
