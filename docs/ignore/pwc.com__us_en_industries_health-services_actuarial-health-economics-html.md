Our Health Actuarial & Economics team delivers outcome-focused solutions to the healthcare industry’s complex challenges. We utilize our expertise in actuarial science, health economics, and data analysis to help clients understand and manage their financial performance. Our team serves clients across the healthcare ecosystem, including payers, providers, and health services companies. We collaborate closely with our strategy and operations-focused colleagues to develop practical solutions for differentiated performance.
## Latest Healthcare Trends (Q3'2024)
Our team analyzes health plan-reported financial data each quarter to understand industry trends. As of September 2024, we observed:
  * Rising Industry-Wide Loss Ratios: Through Q3-2024, loss ratios across all lines of business have exceeded previous years, driven by various economic and regulatory headwinds. These trends signal a challenging financial landscape for insurers.
  * Potential Expiration of ARP Subsidies: The American Rescue Plan’s enhanced subsidies for the Individual market face an uncertain future under the new administration and a Republican-controlled Congress. According to Congressional Budget Office (CBO) estimates, this could leave approximately 2.2 million individuals uninsured by 2026 if the subsidies are not extended.
  * Medicaid Margins and Policy Risks: Medicaid margins have normalized to pre-COVID levels. However, potential funding cuts and structural changes to Medicaid financing under the Republican administration could hinder future growth and stability in this segment.
  * Medicare Advantage Pressures and Opportunities: Regulatory challenges in the Medicare Advantage (MA) space continue to pressure plan margins. Despite this, the incoming administration is anticipated to improve MA funding and reduce regulatory burdens, offering some relief and opportunities for growth.


These factors underscore the dynamic and evolving nature of the healthcare industry, emphasizing the need for strategic planning to navigate potential risks and capitalize on emerging opportunities.
