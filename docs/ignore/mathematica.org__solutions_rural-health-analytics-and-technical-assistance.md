# Rural Health Analytics and Technical Assistance
Rural healthcare leaders face growing pressure to preserve access to care, improve outcomes, and maintain financial stability.
Mathematica helps state agencies and rural healthcare organizations address complex challenges through analytics, policy expertise, and implementation support. We deliver practical solutions rooted in evidence and local needs, designed to expand access to care, strengthen financial sustainability, and support collaboration across systems and communities.

## See where access gaps, financial risks, and care opportunities exist
Mathematica's integrated analytics and advisory approach help states and healthcare leaders make informed decisions about care access, service delivery, and long-term sustainability. 
  * **Analytics platform:** We use advanced analytics, AI, machine learning, and clinical expertise to identify opportunities at the service-line level that can address unmet care needs and support rural hospital sustainability. 
  * **Reporting tools:** We provide customizable reports to support statewide collaboration across hospitals, healthcare delivery system planning, and Rural Health Transformation Program reporting requirements. 
  * **Advisory services:** We offer expert strategic and technical support to help state agencies and rural hospitals develop plans to close care gaps, guide investments, and strengthen hospital financial viability. 


## We help you meet CMS rural health transformation goals
Through the [Rural Health Transformation Program](https://www.mathematica.org/features/transforming-rural-health-through-innovation-and-evidence), the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) is helping states test new approaches to strengthen healthcare financing, expand access to care, and improve health outcomes. 
Mathematica's analytics platform and advisory services help CMS, state agencies, and rural hospitals meet [key goals of the program](https://www.cms.gov/priorities/rural-health-transformation-rht-program/overview), including sustainable access and innovative care. Together, these capabilities support a range of strategic planning and care transformation efforts, including:
  * **Support for appropriate care availability statewide** Mathematica provides integrated hospital- and state-level analytic reports to help rural hospitals and state agencies identify access gaps, coordinate care coverage, and guide infrastructure investments.
  * **Strengthening the financial viability of struggling rural hospitals** We deliver service line analytics, financial modeling, and technical assistance to help rural hospitals remain sustainable and improve performance. Mathematica has produced more than 100 service-line analytic reports and customized financial models for more than 80 rural hospitals considering REH conversion across 28 states.
  * **Understanding when and how to reopen rural hospitals** Our data-driven analysis helps states evaluate the feasibility and impact of reopening recently closed rural hospitals.
  * **Developing innovative care transformation models, including value-based care arrangements and alternative payment models** Our work supports care transformation strategies that optimize service-line delivery through collaboration among providers, payers, and technical experts.

### What types of data are available through this platform’s analytics framework?
The platform’s analytics framework provides access to comprehensive data to help you assess market share, hospital market areas, service lines, disease prevalence, unmet care needs, patients’ travel patterns, and professional services. Our platform integrates and analyzes data from a variety of sources, including: 
  * Medicaid and Medicare Advantage encounters 
  * Medicare fee-for-service claims 
  * Medicare cost reports 
  * Population-level analyses and community estimates 
  * American Community Survey 
  * Hospital input 


We can integrate state-specific data sources such as commercial claims data, state all-payer claims databases, and Geographic Information Systems data.
### What kinds of reports can I access through this platform?
States and hospitals can access ready-made and customizable reports featuring:
  * **Hospital service-line analysis:** Using custom market definitions, multipayer data, and machine learning, we identify undiscovered, high-impact service lines that meet community needs, and we deliver ranked service-line recommendations, utilization estimates, and community profiles.
  * **Statewide regional strategy:** We provide states with projections on Medicare and Medicaid enrollment and service use to help them regionalize care delivery. Our reports also offer metrics on quality, access, and patients’ travel time to help states ensure adequate access to care. 
  * **Financial modeling:** We assist hospitals considering alternative payment models by using financial models that project impacts on revenue, costs, and margins—incorporating reimbursement changes, cost savings, and hospital-specific factors. We also assess financial and community implications and offer policy insights.
