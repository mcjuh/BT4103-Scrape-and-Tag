# Investor FAQs
Main Content
##  WHAT IS ACCENTURE'S TICKER SYMBOL, AND ON WHAT EXCHANGE DO ITS SHARES TRADE?
Accenture's shares trade under the symbol ACN on the New York Stock Exchange.
##  WHEN WAS ACCENTURE'S INITIAL PUBLIC OFFERING (IPO), AND WHAT WAS THE OFFERING PRICE?
Accenture's IPO took place on July 19, 2001. The initial offering price was $14.50 per share.
##  HOW CAN ONE PURCHASE ACCENTURE STOCK?
To purchase shares of Accenture stock you will need to contact a licensed broker.
##  DOES ACCENTURE HAVE A DIVIDEND REINVESTMENT PLAN?
No. At this time Accenture does not have a dividend reinvestment plan.
