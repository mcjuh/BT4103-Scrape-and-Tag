# Retail Access Funds Solutions
Kroll helps managers and investors in alternative asset funds meet the complex demands of rigorous, timely and frequent valuations and navigate related-party considerations.
The emergence of new and innovative fund structures is enabling broader access to a wide array of private investment opportunities for individual retail investors. Private asset managers are seeing robust demand for such Retail Access Funds from a class of investors that have had limited access to illiquid investment strategies historically.
Greater access, together with heightened regulatory concerns by the SEC, investor scrutiny and complexity of deal structures, have led to accelerating demand for comprehensive valuation solutions and an increasing need for fairness opinions to support related party transactions.
Kroll is a market leader in providing portfolio valuation services and fairness opinions to the alternative investment community, working with more than 600 leading fund managers and investors globally.
