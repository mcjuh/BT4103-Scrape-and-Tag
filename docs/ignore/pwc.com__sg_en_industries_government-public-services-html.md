## Supporting your vision, making a difference
As demands of citizens and residents evolve, the challenges faced by the government and public services are becoming increasingly complex. Our government & public services practice work closely with our clients to provide professional support to forward the national agenda across the public domains. We aim to make a difference with our valuable insights on opportunities and challenges, progressive strategies and innovative solutions.

[Our service offering](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/industries/government-public-services.html#services)
## PwC in Government
We help governments around the world to understand and respond to increasingly complex and unpredictable challenges in the areas of public safety, justice, and security.
We partner you to develop practical and innovative solutions that make a difference in the public domain, such as education, defence and security, digital government, international development, local governance and public finance.
We put our best people on the job to bring you the right expertise, experience and deep insights into the sector-specific opportunities and challenges.
## Our 4C's approach
Our 4C’s approach to delivering value to our government and public services sector. 
### Curate
Your priorities, our professionalism. We curate our solutions to meet your specific needs.
### Collate
We evaluate your strategies and initiatives from data gathered, address key risks, conduct in-depth research, investigate priority initiatives to deliver innovative solutions.
### Complement
We respond to your needs by leveraging our domestic, regional and global expertise to share international best practices and adapt them in our deliveries to suit Singapore's context.
### Cultivate
We facilitate and curate dialogues and promote collaboration among businesses, the Government, the academics and the society at large.
