# Project management tool selection and configuration
### Find the right-fit project management platform
#### [Connect with our project management team ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/program-project-management/project-management-tool-selection-and-configuration.html#contact)
### **Aligning strategic tools with business goals for enterprise project success**
Choosing the right project management tool involves more than technical considerations. It is a strategic investment in how your organization plans, executes and governs initiatives. RSM assists executive teams in aligning tool capabilities with business goals, leveraging artificial intelligence, streamlining project workflows and enabling scalable governance through tailored configuration and training.
Also, RSM conducts a structured discovery process to assess your current project management environment, including legacy tools, stakeholder needs and project types. We work with business units to define tool requirements and identify platforms that provide enterprise-wide visibility, control and scalability.
### **Configuration for governance and usability**
Once a tool is selected, RSM configures access controls, builds reusable templates, incorporates AI and establishes a hierarchy that reflects your organization’s project structure. We migrate legacy data, set up dashboards and create accelerators to support consistent reporting and decision making.
### **Enablement through training and knowledge transfer**
To drive adoption, we provide tailored training for administrators and superusers, equipping your teams to manage projects independently. This approach promotes long-term usability and empowers internal teams to scale project management practices across departments.
### **Benefits of RSM’s project management tool selection and configuration services**
  * Consolidated and standardized project management environment
  * Improved cost efficiency and simplified architecture with AI integration
  * Consistent data and reporting across projects
  * Accelerated setup of new projects and users
  * Enhanced governance through dashboards and templates
  * Reduced change impact through structured training


##  Frequently asked questions  

What types of project management tools does RSM evaluate?  
    
RSM assesses a wide range of platforms, including SaaS-based tools, enterprise PM suites and industry-specific solutions. 

How does RSM determine the best-fit tool for our organization?  
    
We conduct stakeholder interviews, review legacy systems and align tool capabilities with your strategic goals. 

Can RSM help migrate data from our current tool?  
    
Yes. We manage legacy data migration and the setup of new projects within the selected platform. 

Do you provide training for internal teams?  
    
We offer tailored training and knowledge transfer for administrators, superusers and project leaders. 

What deliverables are included in this service?  
    
Tool analysis, evaluation matrices, configuration templates, dashboards, user setup and training materials are included in the service. 

How long does a typical engagement take?  
    
Timelines vary based on scope, but most tool selection and configuration projects are completed within several weeks. 

Can this service support multiple departments or business units?  
    
Yes. Our approach is designed to scale across functions and support enterprise-wide governance. 

Is this service available as part of a broader transformation initiative?  
    
Yes. Tool selection and configuration can be integrated with PMO advisory, portfolio management or system implementation services.
