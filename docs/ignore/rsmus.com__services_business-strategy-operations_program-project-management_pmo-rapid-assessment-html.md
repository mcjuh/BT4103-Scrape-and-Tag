# PMO Rapid Assessment
### Evaluate your PMO, align governance and reduce risk with a targeted roadmap
#### [Contact us  ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/program-project-management/pmo-rapid-assessment.html#contact)
##  Why RSM’s rapid PMO assessment goes beyond diagnostics 
Many project management office (PMO) assessments focus solely on diagnostics. RSM’s PMO Rapid Assessment is built for action. Designed for the C-suite and other senior leaders, this assessment guides organizations from insight to implementation through a structured three-step process: assessing the current state, designing a future-state operating model and delivering a tactical roadmap.
### **A structured 3-step approach to PMO excellence**
The assessment incorporates stakeholder interviews, benchmarking, temperature-check surveys and operational technology reviews to create a shared fact base. This cross-functional, evidence-based approach supports adoption and reduces risk. Recommendations are prioritized and costed, providing executives with a clear set of options. Service packages are tailored to each organization’s context, balancing structure with flexibility.
### **Benefits of RSM’s PMO Rapid Assessment**
Clients typically see improvements in portfolio governance, delivery risk reduction and resource optimization. The assessment clarifies decision rights, identifies opportunities for artificial intelligence, highlights control gaps, and points to changes in tooling or processes that unlock throughput. It is especially effective for organizations dealing with fragmented metrics, unclear prioritization, constrained capacity or PMOs focused on tactical roles.
### **Tangible outcomes include:**
  * A current-state PMO assessment based on stakeholder input and benchmarking
  * A future-state target operating model aligned with strategic goals
  * A tactical roadmap with sequenced, cost-aware recommendations
  * Visibility into risks, gaps and opportunities, including AI
  * Practical options for improving governance, tools and resource alignment


##  Frequently asked questions  

What is a PMO Rapid Assessment?  
    
A PMO Rapid Assessment is a structured engagement that evaluates your project management office’s (PMO’s) current state, designs a future-state operating model and delivers a tactical roadmap with prioritized, costed recommendations. 

How does this differ from a traditional PMO maturity review?  
    
Unlike other maturity reviews that focus on diagnostics, the PMO Rapid Assessment is built for action. The process incorporates stakeholder input, benchmarking and operational analysis to deliver practical next steps. 

Who should consider a PMO Rapid Assessment?  
    
Senior leaders, such as the chief financial officers (CFOs), chief human resources officers (CHROs), chief information officers (CIOs) and heads of transformation, who need better portfolio governance, reduced delivery risk and improved resource alignment may benefit from this assessment. 

What are the outcomes?  
    
Outcomes include clearer decision rights, stronger governance structures, reduced risk of project failure and optimized use of tools, AI and resources. 

How long does the assessment take?  
    
Timelines vary based on scope. The structured three-step approach is designed to move efficiently from insight to implementation. 

Can the assessment be customized?  
    
Yes. Service packages are tailored to your organization’s needs, allowing flexibility in scope, depth and delivery format.
