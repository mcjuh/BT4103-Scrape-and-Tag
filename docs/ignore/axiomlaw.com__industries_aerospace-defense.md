## Hire an Aerospace & Defense Lawyer
Aerospace and defense lawyers provide legal services to companies operating within the highly regulated aerospace and defense industry. These lawyers have extensive knowledge of the laws and regulations governing the sector, particularly those enforced by the Department of Defense (DoD) and other federal agencies. They handle a broad range of matters, including litigation, compliance, and transactions. For aerospace and defense companies, these lawyers play a crucial role in protecting trade secrets, managing intellectual property, and ensuring compliance with export control regulations. They also assist in structuring joint ventures, advising on private equity investments, and navigating complex bid protests related to government contracts.
Hiring aerospace and defense lawyers can significantly benefit businesses in this sector by helping them manage legal risks and maintain regulatory compliance. Axiom’s lawyers can assist defense contractors and other companies in establishing robust compliance programs that meet the stringent requirements of the DoD and other regulatory bodies. They can conduct internal investigations to identify potential legal or regulatory violations, advise on corrective actions, and represent clients in litigation matters, such as disputes over contracts, intellectual property rights, or regulatory compliance. Additionally, aerospace and defense lawyers are adept at handling bid protests, which are common in the industry when companies challenge the awarding of government contracts.
In the competitive aerospace and defense industry, where protecting intellectual property and trade secrets is critical, aerospace and defense lawyers provide invaluable support in safeguarding these assets. They help companies navigate complex legal issues related to technology transfers, licensing agreements, and collaborations with other entities, ensuring that their intellectual property rights are fully protected. By partnering with Axiom’s experienced aerospace and defense lawyers, businesses can better navigate the legal complexities of the industry, minimize risks, and achieve their strategic goals while maintaining compliance with all relevant laws and regulations.

## What can aerospace and defense lawyers help companies handle?
Aerospace and defense lawyers can help companies manage government contracts, federal procurements, FAR and DFARS requirements, subcontractor obligations, supply chain compliance, export controls, cybersecurity, ethics, conflicts, investigations, and procurement disputes.
## How can aerospace and defense lawyers support government contracts?
Aerospace and defense lawyers can [support government contracts](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/government-contracts) by helping with procurement strategy, proposal requirements, FAR and agency supplement obligations, pricing issues, subcontractor terms, requests for equitable adjustment, claims, bid protests, and contract administration across the program lifecycle.
## What legal support is needed for ITAR, EAR, and sanctions compliance?
Aerospace and defense companies may need [export controls legal support](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/export-controls) to classify controlled items or technical data, review exports and reexports, assess licensing requirements, screen counterparties, update policies, train employees, manage third-party risk, and coordinate remediation if a potential [ITAR & EAR,](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/ear-itar) customs, or sanctions issue arises.
## How can lawyers support CMMC, DFARS, and defense cybersecurity requirements?
Lawyers can support CMMC, [DFARs](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/dfars), and defense cybersecurity requirements by helping legal and compliance teams understand contract obligations, flow-down terms, controlled information handling, vendor requirements, security attestations, incident response terms, and documentation needed to support compliance across prime and subcontractor relationships.
## How can aerospace and defense companies manage subcontractor and supply chain compliance?
Aerospace and defense companies can manage subcontractor and [supply chain compliance](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/supply-chain) by standardizing flow-down terms, reviewing supplier codes of conduct, monitoring product safety requirements, tracking cybersecurity obligations, documenting CPSR-related controls, and creating escalation paths for procurement, ethics, export, or security issues.
## When should aerospace and defense companies add flexible legal talent?
Aerospace and defense companies often add flexible legal talent during proposal cycles, audits, investigations, contract disputes, cybersecurity readiness projects, export-control reviews, supply chain remediation, [M&A activity](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/mergers-acquisitions), or temporary gaps in government contracts or compliance teams.
