# Modernizing Federal HR with Next-Gen Technology 
Build a strategic, data-driven and resilient federal HR function with innovative technology and AI to advance the mission
##  Enabling the Modern Federal Workforce 
The federal workforce is at a pivotal moment. Emerging technologies, AI policy requirements, and talent shortages are forcing agencies to rethink how HR operates and delivers value. Yet many federal government HR environments remain anchored in legacy platforms that limit automation, insight, and employee experience. Modernizing HR IT is no longer optional—it is foundational to workforce transformation. To build resilience and readiness, agencies must modernize both their HR technology and operating approach. 
### Navigating a New Era of Federal HR
Federal HR leaders are navigating a complex landscape of workforce disruption, fiscal pressure, and rapidly evolving technology expectations. Legacy HR systems often struggle to keep pace, creating barriers to workforce planning, talent development, and change adoption.
KPMG works alongside federal organizations to deliver integrated HR transformation—combining modern platforms, workforce strategy, and change enablement to help agencies focus on what matters most: their people.
KPMG can help your agency in the following areas:
Human Resources Transformation
Modernize your agency’s HR backbone by implementing HR information technology systems, integrating HR managed services, and providing HR operational excellence guidance. 
Workforce Transformation
Align your agency for mission success through strategic organizational design, assessment of current workforce capabilities, and develop top talent through integrated performance management and tailored learning solutions. 
Change & Tech Adoption
Empower your agency to drive cultural change, deliver strategic communications, and accelerate tech adoption of leading technology platforms through training and upskilling.
