# Win the moment by mastering customer intent
In partnership with Adobe, we explore how brands can reclaim relevance—and customer loyalty—in the age of AI

AI has rewritten customer expectations almost overnight. Customers are done exploring and comparing—they’re now delegating.
A [recent study](https://www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/en-us/report/agentic-commerce) from the IBM Institute for Business Value (IBM IBV) found the use of AI applications such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and other chatbots to aid in purchasing decisions surged 62% over the last two years, and even more for Gen X (82%) and Boomers (92%). And with the rise of agentic AI, customers are now able to compare vendors, book appointments, resolve service issues, and complete transactions—often without ever visiting a brand's website.
This has created a new baseline for how customers express their intent to brands. Every interaction that doesn’t recognize who customers are, understand what they want to accomplish, and eliminate the work for them now stands out as a poor experience.
> “We’re trying to identify how we can take care of the customer _before_ they wind up in a situation they could have planned for. It’s about becoming predictive and then personalized.”
> **Steve Fournier,** CMO, Discount Tire
For organizations, it’s a fundamental shift. The ability to respond meaningfully to customer intent is now currency. Beyond traditional marketing signals, if an organization can’t detect intent and act appropriately in the moment, the opportunity fades and loyalty drifts to a competitor (or AI agent) that can meet those expectations.
To compete in today’s landscape, brands must protect the customer relationship—and their competitive advantage—by delivering contextual continuity, proactive service, and personalized experiences rooted in deep understanding. 
**Industry leaders are capturing intent—and value—before it dissolves** How long does it take organizations to decipher and act on customer signals? We examine industry averages against the fastest 20% in each sector.
According to new IBM IBV research, conducted in partnership with Adobe, the challenge is now owning the customer relationship in a world where AI intermediaries are everywhere. And that requires more than visibility. It demands moving proactively and precisely.
Most enterprises aren’t equipped to compete. According to our global survey of 1,000 senior tech and marketing executives, organizations are flooded with data but can’t use most of it, as it sits trapped in silos with no unified view. They express grand AI aspirations but lack the foundations to make AI work at scale. And as customer expectations accelerate, the gap between delivery and demand grows ever more costly.
> Only 34% of the customer data organizations collect is actually used for CX decisions.
In other words? AI doesn't win customer loyalty—orchestrated experiences do. Orchestration connects data collection, decisioning, activation, measurement, and optimization into one continuous, closed loop. Each customer signal informs the next action, and each action feeds learning back into the system. 
**In this new reality, the loyalty advantage hinges on three components:**  
| Intent  | Can you recognize the customer's specific goal across channels, before it no longer matters?  |  
| --- | --- |  
| Anticipation  | Can you create a personalized, end-to-end experience that goes beyond what the customer asked for?  |  
| Confidence  | Can you act with precision, balancing speed and trust at scale?  |  
Agentic AI makes recognition, anticipation, and confidence possible at scale—but only when orchestrated across the right data, workflows, technology, and cross-functional alignment. 
In this report, we investigate how enterprises are restructuring operations around the intent economy. We explore three imperatives: turning fleeting signals into actionable intent, orchestrating personalized experiences before customers ask, and pairing speed with governance to compound advantage.
**FAQ**
What is the cost of failing to decode customer intent?
Organizations that take longer to detect and act on customer signals see return on marketing investment plunge by 30-40 percentage points compared to industry peers. Operating waste from excessive delay costs an average of USD 29 million annually, while fragmented customer views multiply manual handoffs, rework, and exceptions—adding cost without adding customer value. 
Why does personalization at scale create a coordination tax?
As organizations move from foundational personalization into predictive and real-time stages, they often hit a hidden coordination "tax," generated by multiple handoffs and systems making decisions. The spike in performance comes only with agentic orchestration. Without it, extra personalization multiplies friction. With it, the payoff can be dramatic—revenue and operating margin gains surge at full maturity. 
How does governance compound advantage?
Those that pair speed with governance turn early wins into enduring growth. These balanced organizations report 7% lower customer acquisition cost, 1% higher conversion rate, 38% higher customer lifetime value, and 12% better marketing ROI. They also share cross-functional ownership of technology and ROI metrics, reducing duplicative tools and forcing tradeoff decisions into the open.
