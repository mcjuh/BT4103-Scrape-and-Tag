# Engineering, Fire Safety and ESG Consultants in Saudi Arabia
Delivering Innovative Solutions for the KSA's Built Environment
AESG KSA stands at the forefront of sculpting the Kingdom’s vibrant urban future. Merging global insight with an intimate understanding of local heritage, our team weaves innovative solutions across engineering, sustainability, energy, environmental management, fire and life safety, and commissioning, propelling the Kingdom's boldest aspirations into reality. With every collaboration - from visionary developers to forward-thinking architects, contractors, and government leaders - we offer the expertise to turn intricate challenges into pioneering opportunities. We don’t just bring ideas to life; we forge a resilient and sustainable future for Saudi Arabia, one where tomorrow’s dreams are shaped by today’s boldest endeavours.
## Our Commitment to KSA
At AESG, we stand firmly alongside Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030 and the National Transformation Plan (NTP 2020), dedicated to shaping a future where sustainable, smart cities bloom across the Kingdom. As proud signatories of the WorldGBC’s Net Zero Buildings Commitment, we are driven by a singular vision — to achieve carbon neutrality across our projects by 2030, illuminating the path to a cleaner, greener tomorrow. With unwavering focus, we offer expertise in three essential realms: **Environment and Sustainability, Engineering, and Project Delivery Management**. Together, these pillars create projects that are not only resilient and efficient but also poised to meet the challenges of the future. Through our work, we contribute to the Kingdom’s ambitious goals, weaving long-term value into every endeavour, and advancing Saudi Arabia’s journey toward a sustainable, prosperous tomorrow.
## Our Commitment to the Middle East
From Dubai and Abu Dhabi to key cities across the GCC and wider Middle East, AESG is the preferred partner for governments, leading real estate developers, and forward-thinking private sector clients. Our regional presence and global expertise enable us to consistently deliver impact-driven results that align with national visions and long-term development strategies. With a steadfast commitment to sustainability, innovation, and resilience, AESG is proud to be shaping the future of the built environment across the Middle East - supporting the region’s ambitious growth with solutions that stand the test of time.
