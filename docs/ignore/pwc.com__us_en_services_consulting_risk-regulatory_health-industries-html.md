## Overview
### From compliance-driven to resilience-led
The health industry is facing significant policy change, regulatory complexity, cost pressures, and a need to continue to innovate. We help providers /academic institutions, payers, pharmaceutical, medtech and life sciences companies anticipate, navigate and manage strategic, financial, operational and regulatory compliance risk. 
By combining deep industry expertise, innovative technology, and exceptional client experience, we help organizations get ahead of risk and opportunity to achieve their strategic objectives.

## Capabilities
  1. Internal audit and controls
  2. Regulatory risk and compliance


### Internal audit and controls
Advance internal audit with precision and purpose. We tech-enable the internal audit life cycle so that effort and focus is spent providing our deep health industry expertise—to help organizations strengthen governance, internal controls and regulatory compliance so that the organization can achieve its strategic objectives and enable positive outcomes.
### Regulatory risk and compliance
Healthcare organizations operate in a complex regulatory environment while sustaining rising cost pressures to do more, with less. We advise providers, payers and pharmaceutical and medical technology companies in more efficiently and effectively navigating and managing regulatory risk and compliance programs. Our tech-enabled services span enterprise risk, regulatory change management, through compliance strategy and transformation—helping organizations stay compliant while limiting overhead and unnecessary expense to business operations.
