# Harness the power of data to modernize operations
By harnessing data, your business can produce data products, tools, systems, and applications that drive business decision-making and help modernize operations. 
Our approach 
##  Data done right 
Getting your data right is foundational to modernizing your operations. Data, structured or unstructured, must be secure, compliant, and usable. For chief data officers, it means leveraging tools like tagging and archiving to get more data in the hands of stakeholders. The goal is data optimized for AI, which may require data catalogs or data platform consolidation. Ultimately, harnessing data leads to data products that drive business outcomes and AI agents that can carry out process activities, both examples of modernized operations.
