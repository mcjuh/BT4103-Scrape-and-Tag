# Transform your business with Oracle
Grant Thornton does more than implement technology. We implement change.
We start by understanding how your business runs today, and where your leaders want to see improvement they can measure. Then, we help you implement a solution with AI and other capabilities that fit your size, maturity and risk profile — embedding intelligence into your workflows for finance, operations and other functions. When technology drives better decisions, faster execution, stronger control and more business value, it transforms your business.
In every project, our integrated capabilities combine deep Oracle Cloud and embedded AI expertise with financial, operational, regulatory and industry leadership. Our solutions go beyond platform feature activations to apply technology-driven business operating models for finance, supply chain, HR and planning, with platform‑agnostic data governance, responsible AI design and long‑term managed services agility. That’s why our solutions are proven to improve margin, speed and transparency for audit readiness.
We design modernization that aligns with Oracle ERP, EPM, HCM, SCM and AI-driven enterprise workflows, with proven results. We embed AI and other emerging capabilities into your processes for close, planning, procurement, workforce and more. The result is an enterprise solution that strengthens your business performance today and fortifies your business resilience for the future.
## Grant Thornton delivers
  * Business-led modernization that ties Oracle investments directly to financial and operational outcomes
  * Deep expertise across ERP, EPM, HCM, SCM and embedded AI, supported by proven client success across industries, across geographies and across years of implementation results
  * Practical activation of Oracle AI within existing workflows, identifying business opportunities with a focus on ROI and governance
  * Integrated advisory depth across finance, risk, compliance and industry needs
  * An expandable approach that supports growth across functions as needs evolve
  * Scalable delivery and managed services that support long-term strategy alignment, optimization and accountability
