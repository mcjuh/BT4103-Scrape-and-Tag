# Business Leaders Guide to a Just Climate Transition
_WBCSD_ and _ERM_ have launched a new report, the _Business Leaders Guide to a Just Climate Transition_. This practical guide aims to support senior corporate leaders as they embed people considerations into their climate strategies to ensure business resilience, while building trust with stakeholders and contributing to address systemic risks.
[ Get the full report ](https://www.erm.com/insights/business-leaders-guide-to-a-just-climate-transition/#jtform "Get the full report")
### **Why this guide matters**
In today's evolving business landscape, integrating people considerations into climate action is a strategic imperative. The transition to a net-zero, nature-positive economy is not only technical – it is deeply social. Employees, suppliers, communities, and consumers are central to this transformation.
Overlooking people can have serious financial consequences. From litigation, project delays, and reputational harm to missed opportunities in new markets, supply chains, and access to capital.
For Boards and C-Suite leaders, this guide helps to close a critical gap. Until now, most climate strategies have focused on emissions and technology, leaving workforce and community impacts fragmented or reactive. This guide helps companies integrate people priorities into transition plans, ensuring business resilience, while supporting fair and inclusive climate action.
### **What’s included:**
  * _A clarification of boundaries for corporate responsibility_ on climate-related social impacts, risks, and opportunities.
  * _A clear business case_ for a just transition
  * _Insights on sectoral and regional dynamics_ to inform place-based approaches
  * _Recommended actions for Boards and executives_ to align fiduciary duties with emerging due diligence expectations
  * _Practical steps_ to embed just transition thinking into climate strategies and transition plans
  * _Case studies and best practices_ from leading companies
