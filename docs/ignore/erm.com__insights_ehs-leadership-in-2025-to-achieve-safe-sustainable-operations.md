# EHS Leadership in 2025 to Achieve Safe & Sustainable Operations
In a world where operational risk, regulatory complexity, and workforce expectations are accelerating, the role of EHS leadership is being redefined. The most forward-thinking organizations are no longer treating EHS as a compliance function—they’re turning it into a strategic engine for resilience, innovation, and value creation.
ERM’s latest report, EHS Leadership in 2025 to Achieve Safe & Sustainable Operations, reveals how top-performing companies are transforming their EHS programs to meet the moment.
[ Get the report now ](https://www.erm.com/insights/ehs-leadership-in-2025-to-achieve-safe-sustainable-operations/#form "Get the report now")
**What you’ll learn:**
  * How to implement a **data-driven, digitally enabled** approach to EHS performance
  * Ways to **leverage regulatory intelligence** to cut through the noise and act with confidence
  * Why **executive engagement** is the most critical driver in reducing Serious Injury and Fatality risks
  * How to **mitigate Process Safety and Machine Safety risks** in increasingly automated environments
  * Where **AI can deliver real, measurable impact** in EHS operations
  * How to **recalibrate your EHS org model** to optimize capability, fluency, and cost-efficiency


**Why This Report Matters**
Based on ERM’s deep collaboration with global clients, this briefing distils six practical, high-impact actions that EHS leaders can take today to future-proof their programs. Whether you're navigating digital transformation, regulatory upheaval, or cultural change, this report offers the clarity and direction you need.
