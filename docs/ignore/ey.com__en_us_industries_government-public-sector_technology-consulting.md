#  Technology, data and AI in government 
We help government organizations with digital transformation services, providing an elevated constituent experience that matches private sector quality. To help federal, state and local governments with their technology transformation journey, our approach focuses on discovering, designing and implementing the right technology to provide accessible and user friendly systems.

  * ##  Service summary
EY Government and Public Sector Technology Consulting helps federal, state and local government agencies modernize technology, data and operations to improve mission outcomes, service delivery and constituent experience.
#### Key offerings:
    * Technology modernization across applications, cloud platforms, low‑code solutions and enterprise operating models to replace legacy systems and improve agility
    * Data, analytics and artificial intelligence (AI) solutions that enable better decision-making, insight generation and workforce upskilling
    * Cybersecurity and digital trust services, including digital identity, data protection and cyber program transformation, to protect public systems and data

## What EY can do for you 
Technology plays a vital role in addressing agency and constituent goals, with our approach prioritizing human-centric solutions that harness the power of technology to meet client aspirations. Our service offerings include modernizing legacy systems and integrating data strategy and governance, advanced analytics and innovative GenAI capabilities to enhance decision-making, drive operational efficiencies and empower a knowledgeable workforce adept in the era of exponential data growth. This could lead to improved workforce productivity and scalability, streamlined operations and successful achievement of agency objectives. 
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Data, insights and AI in government
To help achieve desired outcomes when using data, you first need to break down the barriers and silos across government agencies. By maximizing results realized from technology and analytics investments, and working to build and improve scalable ecosystems to match needs, our goal is to help government agencies fully realize the potential of their data. To transform into data-driven organizations, it’s important to continually work to ensure data privacy and regulatory compliance. Our team is equipped to help our clients rise above these data and technology complexities:
    * Data design and management
    * Data engineering and platforms
    * Advanced analytics and insights as a service
    * Data literacy: employee training and upskilling
    * Artificial intelligence and machine learning
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Technology modernization in government
Legacy platforms often rely on dated technology that is difficult to access, expensive to maintain and unable to meet evolving business objectives. The EY team’s approach puts humans at the center – it is predicated on bringing the best of technology to address your organization’s current and future objectives. This approach includes:
    * Application modernization
    * Cloud economics and strategy
    * Enterprise security
    * Cloud platform and cloud operating model
    * Low/no-code applications
    * Intelligent automation
Whether your agency desires full scale modernizations or micro modernizations, we are equipped to help your organization achieve desired outcomes. Technology is a critical enabler, but decisions are based on business objectives and outcomes, which our solutions are customized to facilitate.
