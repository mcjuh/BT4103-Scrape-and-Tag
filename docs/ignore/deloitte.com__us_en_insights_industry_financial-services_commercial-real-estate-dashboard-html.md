# Commercial Real Estate Outlook Survey Dashboard
The Commercial Real Estate Outlook Survey, conducted by the Deloitte Center for Financial Services, is an annual flagship research effort designed to help real estate leaders strategically plan for the year ahead.
Last fielded in June and July 2025, the survey gathered perspectives from more than 850 C-suite executives and their direct reports at commercial real estate owner and investment organizations. Respondents from 13 countries across North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific shared insights on growth prospects, operations, technology plans, investment priorities, and anticipated shifts in commercial real estate fundamentals over the next 12 to 18 months (see methodology), published in our [2026 outlook](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/financial-services/financial-services-industry-outlooks/commercial-real-estate-outlook.html).
The survey’s findings offer a data-rich snapshot of industry sentiment, emerging opportunities, and key risks facing the global CRE market as leaders navigate a complex global macroeconomic landscape.

###### key takeaway
Globally, respondent sentiment points to increasing M&A selectivity in CRE markets, with fewer of those surveyed indicating their firms plan to accelerate deal activity over the near term. Respondents also note that strategic partnerships could increasingly be leveraged as an alternative to traditional M&A to support yield and diversification objectives.
