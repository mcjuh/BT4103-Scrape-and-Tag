## Financial services industry trends
Financial services organizations are navigating a period of significant transformation. New technologies, evolving regulatory expectations and evolving customer demands are reshaping business models and operating strategies. Institutions that take a proactive approach to risk, modernization and talent development may be better positioned to compete and adapt.
Key financial services industry trends and issues include:
**Technology modernization and innovation**
Many organizations are accelerating efforts to modernize [core systems](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/key-considerations-for-financial-institutions-core-system-strate.html), migrate to cloud-based platforms and improve data quality. These investments are intended to create more efficient operations, support real-time decision making and establish a stronger foundation for future innovation.
[ Learn more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/banking-technology-modernization-improve-customer-experience.html "Learn more")
**Artificial intelligence and automation**
Financial services firms are moving beyond AI experimentation and embedding intelligent automation into fraud detection, compliance monitoring, customer service, software development and operational workflows. Realizing value from AI requires strong data governance, clear oversight and responsible implementation practices.
[ Learn more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/ai-for-financial-services-organizations-in-2026.html "Learn more")
**Digital assets, payments and investment innovation**
[Stablecoins](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/how-banks-can-prepare-to-embrace-payment-stablecoins.html), tokenization, real-time payments, private credit and evergreen fund structures are influencing how capital is deployed and how financial transactions occur. As these markets evolve, organizations are evaluating opportunities to expand product offerings while managing liquidity, valuation, regulatory and operational considerations.
[ Learn more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/fintech-trends-are-transforming-the-financial-services-industry.html "Learn more")
**Risk management, regulatory compliance and operational resilience**
Financial services organizations navigate complex regulatory requirements while managing cybersecurity threats, fraud, data privacy expectations and [third-party risk](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/enhancing-third-party-risk-management-for-bank-fintech-partnersh.html). As supervisory scrutiny evolves, institutions are strengthening governance, compliance and resilience programs to protect critical operations and maintain stakeholder confidence. 
[ Learn more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/2026-cybersecurity-mmbi-financial-services-snapshot.html "Learn more")
**Growth and profitability**
Economic uncertainty, changing customer expectations and increased competition are prompting financial services organizations to reevaluate growth strategies. Companies are exploring new products, markets and strategic partnerships while focusing on operational performance, customer experience and long-term value creation.
[ Learn more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/how-banks-can-drive-successful-m-a-integration.html "Learn more")
**Talent, workforce and operating model transformation**
Financial services organizations continue to face workforce capacity constraints as experienced professionals retire, employee expectations evolve and demand for specialized skills continues. Institutions are investing in workforce planning, process redesign, automation and AI-enabled productivity tools to improve efficiency, preserve institutional knowledge and support sustainable growth while maintaining service quality and regulatory compliance.
[ Learn more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/financial-services/the-financial-services-workforce-of-the-future.html "Learn more")

## FAQs 

What is the financial services industry?  
    
The financial services industry encompasses organizations that manage, move, invest, lend and protect money. It includes banks, asset managers, insurance companies, capital markets organizations, specialty finance firms and fintech companies that provide products and services to consumers, businesses and institutional investors. 

How are financial services organizations addressing regulatory expectations?  
    
Financial services organizations are strengthening governance, compliance and risk management programs as regulatory requirements continue to evolve. Many institutions are also investing in technology and data capabilities to improve transparency, reporting and oversight. 

What role is technology playing in the future of financial services?  
    
Technology is reshaping how financial services organizations operate, serve customers and manage risk. From artificial intelligence and automation to cloud platforms and advanced analytics, organizations are evaluating how digital investments can improve efficiency, decision making and customer experiences. 

Why is operational resilience a growing priority for financial institutions?  
    
Financial services organizations face increasing pressure to maintain business continuity during cybersecurity incidents, market disruptions and other unexpected events. Organizations are focusing on resilience strategies that strengthen critical operations, third-party risk management and response capabilities.
