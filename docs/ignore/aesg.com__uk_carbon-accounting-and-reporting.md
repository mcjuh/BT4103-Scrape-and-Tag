At AESG UK, we assist businesses in accurately quantifying and reporting their carbon emissions across all operational activities, covering Scopes 1, 2, and 3 emissions in accordance with the Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol and UK standards.
## Why Carbon Accounting Matters in the UK
The UK is committed to achieving net zero emissions by 2050, aligning with global climate goals and meeting increasing regulatory requirements and stakeholder expectations. Many UK businesses, especially publicly listed companies, are mandated to disclose their carbon emissions annually. Additionally, as part of their net zero strategies, organisations are setting ambitious targets to address Scope 3 emissions throughout their supply chains, making comprehensive carbon accounting essential for compliance and sustainability.
## Our Approach

AESG UK provides comprehensive Carbon Accounting and Reporting services tailored to the UK market. Our expert team guides you through the complexities of carbon measurement and reporting, ensuring compliance with both international frameworks and local regulations.

Detailed analysis of your direct (Scope 1), indirect (Scope 2), and value chain (Scope 3) emissions to gain a complete understanding of your carbon footprint.
[ Benchmarking Assessments ](https://aesg.com/uk/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-203a7c16aa06a333d225)
Comparing your emissions data against industry standards and UK regulatory requirements to highlight areas for improvement.
[ Net Zero Roadmaps ](https://aesg.com/uk/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-8b2a72e6aa06a333d225)
Developing strategic plans to achieve your net zero targets, incorporating reductions in both operational and supply chain emissions.
[ Stakeholder Engagement ](https://aesg.com/uk/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-10432486aa06a333d225)
Working with internal and external stakeholders to collect essential data and ensure alignment with your sustainability goals.
[ Whole Life Carbon Assessments ](https://aesg.com/uk/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-7abe8626aa06a333d7c8)
Assessing the carbon impact of your projects from design through decommissioning, supporting sustainable decision-making throughout the lifecycle.
[ Circular Economy Principles ](https://aesg.com/uk/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-203a7c16aa06a333d7c8)
Replacing traditional linear processes with circular design principles, allowing for measurable benefits of circularity in processes and systems.
[ Embodied Carbon Assessments ](https://aesg.com/uk/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-15019716aa06a333d7c8)
Measuring carbon emissions associated with building materials and construction, helping you select sustainable options.
[ Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) ](https://aesg.com/uk/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-6c232cd6aa06a333d7c8)
Providing standardised information on the environmental performance of your products to support transparent reporting and sustainable procurement.
