# Mergers, Acquisitions & Transactions
ERM partners with clients to navigate fast-paced and competitive M&A transaction environments. During the due diligence process, we focus on highlighting value-creation opportunities, identifying sustainability risks while taking a commercial view on technical factors.
## Unlock the value of data-driven insight for more informed investment decisions
Sustainability is increasingly important to a company’s equity story, with investors generating premium value for companies that demonstrate strong sustainability performance. Embedding sustainability into business models and investment decisions means navigating an evolving landscape of sustainability topics and approaches.
Decarbonization is a key imperative for the energy transition and needs to be evaluated during the diligence phase of any M&A activity. Technical due diligence approaches need to focus on identifying decarbonization opportunities, risks and pathways.
With greater sustainability disclosure necessary during the transaction process, robust exit readiness and vendor due diligence can help attract more buyers and facilitate a more competitive sale process and price.
New technologies are disrupting the due diligence process. Leveraging digital solutions and AI insights provides a rapid and deeper understanding of sustainability issues and simplifies the management of large volumes of data. 
## Maximize return on investment and safeguard operations, financial performance and brand reputation throughout the transaction process. 
Execute efficient buy-side transactions
ERM provides advisory services to help clients deliver M&A transactions that align with sustainability goals, unlock strategic opportunities and maximize value creation. Our approaches draw on data-driven insights and digital tools to expedite due diligence and mitigate risk. 
Streamline post-acquisition integration
Clients leverage our expertise post-acquisition to drive sustained sustainability value realization and enable operational compliance with complex regulatory environments. We develop and implement post-deal sustainability value creation plans, which help to reduce complexity and unlock the full potential of new investments. 
Maximize return on investment at exit
ERM’s sell-side advisory and exit readiness capabilities assist financial sponsors and corporations in preparing for divestitures, optimizing asset portfolios, and executing transactions that align with their strategic objectives, while minimizing risks and maximizing value. 
Gain advantage with data-driven insights
We harness the power of digital and AI-based tools, such as ERM’s Climate Impact Platform, to expedite our due diligence process. Our approach is tailored to each client’s transaction needs, providing them with valuable insights that are relevant to their industry and investment goals.
