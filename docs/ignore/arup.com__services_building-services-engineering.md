#  Building services engineering 
Get in touch with our team
Contact our experts
Offering integrated expertise in mechanical, electrical and public health engineering, we design the key systems that keep buildings operational and create a delightful environment for the people who use them.
Our building services engineers represent all the disciplines required to create, design and implement the systems and infrastructure for ventilation, heating and cooling, lighting and all the other systems a building needs.
### Learn more about building services engineering:
[ Mechanical engineering Energy efficient MEP design and implementation, including heating and cooling systems ](https://www.arup.com/services/mechanical-engineering/)
[ Electrical engineering Power system design and engineering for buildings and infrastructure ](https://www.arup.com/services/electrical-engineering/)
[ Public health engineering Design and engineering of water supply, drainage and sanitation systems ](https://www.arup.com/services/public-health-engineering/)
