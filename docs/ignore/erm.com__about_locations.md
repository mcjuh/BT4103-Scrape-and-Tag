##  ERM statement on operations in Russia 
Following the invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, we made the decision to close ERM’s operations in Russia. We took the necessary steps to wind down that part of our business as quickly as possible. As a consequence, ERM has no employees based in Russia and we no longer offer consulting services or have any contractual obligations in Russia.
