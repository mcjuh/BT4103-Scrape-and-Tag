###  Need More Antitrust Information? 
Contact Us 
Companies and counsel need trusted and independent experts and advisers to address increasingly complex antitrust issues and to help manage and resolve antitrust and competition challenges. FTI Consulting and our subsidiary Compass Lexecon provide antitrust consulting services that include investigations, litigation, transaction and compliance advisory across multiple industries and continents. Our dynamic blend of proactive strategies and responsive solutions empowers clients to navigate the complexities of antitrust law and to address issues more seamlessly. With a proven track record in handling some of the most significant antitrust investigations, litigation matters and merger reviews, our team of antitrust experts provides strategic insights and persuasive evidence to private and public sector clients in an ever-evolving regulatory and enforcement framework.
Clients rely on us to help develop a thorough understanding of the relevant antitrust issues, apply our regulatory, enforcement and technical knowledge with relevant economic theory and advanced methods to those issues, and deliver analyses supported by solid and persuasive empirical evidence. Our antitrust experts are continually called upon to present the most complex antitrust issues and analyses in understandable terms for clients both in and out of the courtroom or in regulatory and enforcement agency settings. 
Our experts provide expert testimony and submissions in jurisdictions around the world, in a wide variety of court, regulatory and enforcement contexts, including mergers, market inquiries, regulatory proceedings, enforcement [investigations and litigation](https://www.fticonsulting.com/services/antitrust/merger-control). In the U.S., we provide expert testimony and presentations before state and federal courts, and in settings involving state regulatory agencies, arbitrators and federal agencies, including the Department of Justice (“DOJ”), the Federal Trade Commission (“FTC”) and the Federal Communications Commission (“FCC”). We also have experience in several high profile cross-border investigations in Europe initiated by the European Commission (“EC”), Competition & Markets Authority (“CMA”) and Bundeskartellamt, amongst others. When expert support and advisory on competition matters is required, many of the largest corporations, government agencies and legal firms engage FTI Consulting.
