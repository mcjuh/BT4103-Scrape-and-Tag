## Wendy’s recipe for a high-quality HR transformation
**Challenges**
Wendy’s most important assets are its people, but its legacy patchwork HR systems risked undermining their advantage in the battle for talent in the food services industry. 
**Outcomes**
KPMG helped move Wendy’s onto a unified, full-service, cloud-based platform powered by Oracle’s leading HCM system. The seamless deployment across 400+ stores and 15,000+ employees reinforced Wendy’s brand as a company that genuinely cares about food, family, and community.

## Integrating Monsanto into Bayer’s Crop Science
**Challenges**
After the $66 billion acquisition of Monsanto, Bayer’s Crop Science (BCS) finance team needed to efficiently integrate and align the two companies. They wanted to overcome change fatigue and jumpstart a transformation.
**Outcomes**
KPMG used an 18-month transformation roadmap incorporating multiple functions, processes, and workstreams to create a single, globally aligned vision that empowered BCS finance to deliver value to the entire Bayer organization.
Get the full story about how we helped Bayer realize the full promise of their merger. [Read more>](https://kpmg.com/us/en/how-we-work/client-stories/bayer-crop-science-named-story.html)
## A winning rebound for a leading fitness company
**Challenges**
When a leader in fitness recovery equipment was forced into default by Covid and frozen supply chains, its primary lender recommended engaging KPMG as a financial advisor. 
**Outcomes**
KPMG helped the company improve performance, obtain new financing, repay all lenders, and return to health. The wide-ranging services led to $30 million in cost reductions, $75 million in inventory monetization, newfound stability, and new processes for moving forward.
Get the full story about how we helped a fitness company recover its own financial health. [Read more>](https://kpmg.com/us/en/how-we-work/client-stories/winning-rebound-for-leading-fitness-company.html)
