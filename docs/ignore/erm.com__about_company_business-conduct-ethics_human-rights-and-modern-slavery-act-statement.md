#  Human Rights & Modern Slavery Act Statement
##  Human Rights, Modern Slavery, and Child Labor Policy 
The purpose of this policy is to outline ERM’s commitment and actions in support of international efforts to uphold human rights and against modern slavery and child labor. 
  * [Human Rights Modern Slavery and Child Labor Policy 2025](https://www.erm.com/globalassets/about-us/policies-2024/human-rights-modern-slavery-and-child-labor-policy.pdf "Download now")


#### Respecting and promoting human rights is central to our purpose and values, a priority for management and employees, and a central tenant to our strategic approach to ESG.
**Our approach to Human Rights**
We are committed to respecting the rights of all those with whom we engage through our own operations and across our value chain, this including our work with clients, the ERM Foundation, the SustainAbility Institute by ERM and our engagement with wider stakeholders, in recognition of our individual and collective responsibility to respect and promote human rights in society. ERM’s approach to human rights is set out in the document below.
[Download our Human Rights Strategy](https://www.erm.com/globalassets/sr2024/erm-sustainability-report-2024_human-rights-strategy.pdf "Download our approach to Human Rights")
**Modern Slavery Statement**
ERM provides our 2025 Modern Slavery Statement wherein we provide an overview of the approach and tools we have in place to prevent modern slavery in our business and supply chains and describe the specific actions that we have taken in the past fiscal year.
[Download ERM's 2025 Statement on Modern Slavery](https://www.erm.com/globalassets/sr2025/erm_modern_slavery_2025_final.pdf "Download ERM's 2024 Statement on Modern Slavery")
This Statement covers all subsidiaries of the ERM Group for the fiscal year ending 31 March 2025.
