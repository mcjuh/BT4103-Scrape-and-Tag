#  Healthcare Strategy and Transactions Consulting 
The EY-Parthenon Healthcare Strategy and Transactions Consulting team has deep sector and functional experience in the dynamic healthcare industry. The EY-Parthenon team develops and provides strategies for enterprise and operational transformation, mergers and acquisitions (M&A), joint ventures and investing for growth, as well as venture building, value creation and financial viability.

## What the EY-Parthenon Healthcare Strategy and Transactions Consulting team can do for you
As the healthcare industry adapts to changing regulations and new expectations from patients and other stakeholders, the EY-Parthenon healthcare team works with leaders across the healthcare ecosystem to tackle their biggest challenges, develop and drive strategies and solutions, and help execute meaningful changes to achieve lasting organizational strength.
The EY-Parthenon team embraces the complexity of healthcare strategy, M&A and joint ventures, adopting an investor mindset while collaborating with chief executive officers, boards, private equity firms and government entities to shape the future of healthcare.
Our strategy, transactions and value creation solutions work in practice, not just on paper:

  * ##  Corporate strategy and transformation
Health entities today need strategies that propel future growth. EY-Parthenon analysis and tested methodologies, customized for payers and providers, can help execute short-term projects, as well as multiyear strategic programs. From new operating models to creating new ventures to go-to-market and international market entry strategies, we use a whole enterprise lens to open transformative possibilities for health organizations.
[Learn more.](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/corporate-growth-strategy)

  * ##  M&A, divestment, partnerships and investing
Providers, payers and health services organizations alike can use partnerships, M&A, divestment and investing to transform and grow. EY-Parthenon Healthcare teams can analyze and advise on markets, potential targets and related actions, whether the goal is strategic alignment, access to new markets and capabilities, or transformative growth.

  * ##  Value creation and financial sustainability
Value in healthcare can take many forms. Whether an organization needs to shore up finances and manage liquidity, decrease the cost of care, enhance patient and member experiences, improve outcomes, and/or assess other strategic options to remain competitive, EY-Parthenon teams can help identify and unlock value. The EY-Parthenon [Edge](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy-transactions/connected-capital-technologies) suite of proprietary technologies combines sector data and decades of industry experience to produce actionable insights.

## We help healthcare leaders answer critical questions around strategy and growth:
Transformative strategy
  * How should I refine my business strategy to meet evolving customer needs amid the ever-changing health landscape?
  * How can I reinvent my operating model and go-to-market strategy?
  * How can I hone my organization’s strategy to strengthen operational resilience and enhance value?


Transformative transactions
  * What type of transaction or partnership will propel our organization’s growth and meet patient and member needs?
  * How can I grow market share through investment in AI and other technologies? How can I leverage the ecosystem to scale my products and services?
  * What is the right strategy — buy, build or partner — to solve the innovation deficit and enhance value?


In the [health sector](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/industries/health), setting and executing a strategy to create value for all stakeholders has become increasingly complex. Leaders must take bold and transformative actions — often under intense financial pressure. The EY-Parthenon team can help organizations become more resilient while creating seamless and simplified processes, faster engagement, and virtual and on-site care that is convenient, responsive and effective for patients.
