## Outcome-driven finance transformation
Our KPMG Powered Enterprise | Finance offering takes a practical approach to transform your finance function and enhance value. ## Make your supply chain operations smarter
With KPMG Powered Enterprise | Supply Chain, you can focus on improving customer experience metrics and bringing uniformity to the function. KPMG Powered Supply Chain enabled by Microsoft Dynamics gives you immediate access to leading practices, processes and supply chain service delivery models. By looking at the overall demand and supply picture in real time, you can quickly identify and respond to emerging supply issues. We help you make the most of your data, reduce implementation risks and deliver efficiency gains using technology.
