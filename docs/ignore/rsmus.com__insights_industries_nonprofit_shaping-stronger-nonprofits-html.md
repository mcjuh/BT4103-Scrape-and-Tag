# Shaping stronger nonprofits: A look at our impact on the nonprofit community
December 16, 2025
Nonprofit
For more than 80 years, RSM has had the privilege of working alongside nonprofit organizations dedicated to fighting poverty, advancing education, protecting the environment and promoting equity. The achievements of these organizations inspire us daily, and we are honored to support their efforts as they navigate complex challenges and seize new opportunities. Navigating today’s nonprofit landscape requires resilience, adaptability and a deep commitment to mission. Organizations are being asked to do more—demonstrate impact, maintain financial stability, comply with evolving regulations and embrace new technologies—all while working with limited resources. In a time of both pressing need and meaningful opportunity, nonprofits benefit from advisors who truly understand their world and are invested in their success.
At RSM, we’re proud to support nonprofit organizations with assurance, tax and consulting services tailored to their unique needs. Our goal is to help mission-driven organizations operate with greater efficiency and confidence, so they can focus on what matters most: serving their communities and advancing their missions. When equipped with the right tools and insights, nonprofits are empowered to make a deeper, lasting impact.
This overview highlights RSM’s ongoing commitment to the nonprofit sector and the meaningful work we’re honored to support. It reflects our passion for and dedication to helping organizations build the strength and capacity they need to thrive. We’re grateful for the opportunity to walk alongside nonprofits on their journey—and we look forward to continuing to help turn vision into reality.
## Driving outcomes
At RSM, we believe that meaningful results come from deep understanding and tailored support. The following examples illustrate how our services have helped nonprofit organizations improve efficiency, enhance compliance and achieve strategic goals. 

Student Veterans of America  
    
Student Veterans of America (SVA) is a nationwide nonprofit that supports military veterans as they transition into higher education. SVA needed to balance operational efficiency with rising costs while staying focused on their core mission. The organization was looking to improve their back-office functions to free up staff members from tasks outside their experience.
SVA hired RSM to manage the nonprofit’s essential but noncore functions such as information technology and accounting. Outsourcing provided SVA with RSM professionals who had a broad range of experience, enabling the organization’s staff members to focus on their mission. The approach saved SVA money, improved the nonprofit’s daily operations and boosted employee morale. In addition, RSM’s management of the organization’s IT platform meant SVA had more advanced protection against cyberattacks—an increasing risk for national nonprofits. This alleviated SVA leadership’s concerns about security and compliance, allowing them to expand the technical tools and resources they can offer to student veterans while keeping vital data secure.
"Our staff has a better working experience because of the services that RSM provides. I really don’t know what we’d look like as an organization without the support that we receive for our mission from RSM."
**Jared Lyon,** President and CEO, Student Veterans of America  

Catholic Social Services  
    
Catholic Social Services (CSS), based in Edmonton, Canada, is one of the largest social service providers in the nation. CSS needed to modernize their operations and improve case management as part of a comprehensive digital transformation. By upgrading their system, CSS sought to improve the efficiency of their staff members, eliminate manual tasks and help employees focus on value-added interactions and the nonprofit’s core mission.
RSM executed the project in multiple phases, taking care to guide CSS through key decisions. Throughout the engagement, RSM focused on addressing any challenges promptly and efficiently. The result was that the working relationship extended beyond the technical details, fostering trust and a mutual investment in success. Upon implementation, the nonprofit quickly realized significant improvements, including streamlined case management and analytics. Through the engagement with RSM, the nonprofit not only achieved their goals but also set the foundation for long-term growth and operational excellence. RSM’s technical experience and innovative approach helped CSS improve their efficiency and increase employee satisfaction.
"The reason we were successful and worked so well together is because of the relationship that we developed. It's just a testament to the connection that we formed with RSM."
**Christian Vezina,** Vice President of Technology and Quality Improvement, Catholic Social Services  

Lutheran Social Service of Minnesota  
    
Lutheran Social Service of Minnesota (LSS) has been making a positive impact since 1865. The organization supports more than 91,000 Minnesotans annually—including youth and families, individuals with identified needs, and older adults—and serves with a vision that all people have the opportunity to live and work in a community with full and abundant lives.
When faced with the challenge of navigating an immense archive, including almost 100,000 files and millions of pages spanning over 70 years that a project covered, LSS looked to RSM for assistance.
RSM, through innovative technology and problem-solving, helped implement a document digitization framework using optical character recognition, Microsoft automation and advanced artificial intelligence tools. This comprehensive solution not only converted data into a digital format—it improved search capabilities, standardized information and offered more streamlined, actionable results.
As a result of RSM’s approach, LSS gained more reliable access to their historical records, making it easier than ever to find crucial information. With this AI-enabled transformation, LSS team members can now focus their energy where it matters most—on providing services that inspire hope, change lives and build community—instead of being weighed down by manual data searches and paperwork.
With this breakthrough, LSS is reclaiming stories and gaining productivity, ensuring that their mission will continue to thrive and grow for generations to come.
"RSM and Microsoft gave us more than a prototype; they gave us the tools and guidance to continue the work on our own. It became something sustainable that we could build on long-term."
**Heidi Leach,** Senior Director of Business Technology, Lutheran Social Service of Minnesota  

Bob Woodruff Foundation  
    
The Bob Woodruff Foundation (BWF) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to ensuring that veterans, service members and their families have stable and successful futures. The foundation invests in programs and services that address the needs of these communities.
BWF faced significant operational challenges with their legacy Salesforce environment. Years of patchwork fixes had created a system that was difficult to manage and nearing obsolescence as Salesforce phased out support of certain functions.
RSM assessed the foundation’s data environment and designed a migration path to a more reliable platform. The engagement focused on stabilizing operations by transitioning the system to a stronger platform, which would also help ensure long-term technical viability for the foundation. In addition, RSM wanted to improve BWF’s data quality and processes to enable more strategic decision making.
RSM’s approach improved billing clarity for BWF, providing detailed invoices that showed where resources were going. Furthermore, the foundation’s employees appreciated the more user-friendly system, which gave the organization a standardized data lexicon that eliminated confusion across teams.
Moving from a fragmented system to a robust platform positioned BWF for long-term growth. With a solid foundation and enhanced platform security in place, the nonprofit has become more efficient and better positioned to serve their stakeholders.
"We started this relationship with trust up front. We said either we’re going to be all in or we're not. RSM said yes to that, and their communication with us has been clear the whole time. That builds a good relationship, and relationships matter with big projects like this."
**Keith Whitcomb,** Senior Director of Operations, Bob Woodruff Foundation 
## Making an impact through community: Celebrating 10 years of impact
### **RSM US Foundation**
#### Empowering confident futures for youth…one community at a time
In 2014, we established the [RSM US Foundation](https://rsmus.com/about/stewardship/foundation.html) to enhance the giving activities that were already happening in our offices across the country. A tax-exempt public charity, our foundation is dedicated to empowering our people to make a difference for youth through community-based programming focused on education, hunger, housing and health.
#### 2024 charitable giving
The RSM US Foundation, our firm and our people contributed $8.7 million to charitable causes in 2024.
## Impact
As our firm continues to grow, so does our foundation’s impact, which totals $51.1 million in support to organizations and programming in the areas where our people live and work, including the U.S., Canada, El Salvador and India.
