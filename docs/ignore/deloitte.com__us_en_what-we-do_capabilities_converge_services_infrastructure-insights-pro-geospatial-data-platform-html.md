#  Infrastructure Insights Pro: AI-powered geospatial solution 
## Plan proactively. Mitigate risk. Govern confidently 
Infrastructure Insights Pro is Deloitte's AI-powered geospatial solution built for the future of infrastructure. By leveraging AI and spatial intelligence, it assists planners and engineers to visualize conditions on the ground, anticipate challenges, recommend infrastructure improvements, and allocate resources with precision. Whether enhancing transportation routes, managing vegetation risk, improving asset performance, or optimizing land use, Infrastructure Insights Pro transforms large volumes of complex data into actionable results.

## Key features
**Interactive maps and dashboards**
Our solution delivers a seamless experience by integrating interactive map visualizations and customizable dashboards. Built to be user friendly, it empowers you to efficiently explore and analyze large volumes of geospatial data using plain language and intuitive navigation. Some of our visualization capabilities are powered by external vendors, ensuring you have access to advanced tools for uncovering patterns, gaining actionable insights, and making informed decisions with confidence.

### Solutions
#####  [ Transportation management ](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-zone3/us/en/docs/services/consulting/2025/llp-for-transportation-approved-for-external-use.pdf)
Enhances road safety, improves traffic operations, and enables strategic planning by identifying areas of interest, predicting and evaluating anticipated impacts, and recommending priority infrastructure improvements and countermeasures. 
#####  [ Vegetation management ](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-zone3/us/en/docs/services/consulting/2025/llp-for-utilities-placemat-approved-for-external-use-june2025.pdf)
Enables proactive planning, monitoring, and maintenance of vegetation growth around critical field assets to help improve infrastructure safety, reliability, and efficiency, integrating geospatial and remote sensing data with the power of predictive AI modeling. 
#####  [ Environmental planning and permitting ](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-zone3/us/en/docs/services/consulting/2025/us-permitting-placemat.pdf)
Accelerates environmental planning and permitting by identifying constraints early, integrating geospatial and environmental data, and streamlining documentation, agency coordination, and review workflows to improve compliance and transparency, reduce rework and risk, and shortens approval timelines.
