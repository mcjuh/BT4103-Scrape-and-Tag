#  Audit Technology 
## Enhancing quality through world-class technology and innovation
Introduction  Hide page index 


## Your team is empowered with tech-enabled insights
We embed advanced technology and innovative processes into our audits, allowing us to deliver high quality and value-enhancing insights while also reducing the burden on you. By combining predictive modeling, our patented ledger analytics technology and artificial intelligence, our audits are known for:
**Quality** : When we use our technology to test full populations of data rather than using sampling, we bring more value to the audit.
**Customization** : Our technology and experience enables us to tailor your audit to your highest risks and your industry.
**Efficiency** : We streamline our audit process through innovation, reducing the time and resources you spend gathering data and information.
**Insights** : Combining technology with our deep market and industry knowledge, we arm you with advice and analysis that matters most to your business and investors.

## Creating a better audit experience for you
Streamlining project management
Enjoy seamless communication and document exchange while staying up to date on task completion thanks to technology platforms that make audits less of an effort for your team.
Unleashing data analytics
In our secure data hub, we incorporate your data across service lines to uncover personalized insights that add value to your business.
Tapping into AI
We apply AI to augment the auditors' document review and provide benchmarking and predictive analytics using linguistic and document analysis—customizing your audit for your business, industry and market.

Linguistic analysis
Our models call attention to certain words or phrases that can have predictive properties that can be helpful to you. For example, linguistic cues in earnings calls have been correlated to earnings restatements. 
Text analysis
