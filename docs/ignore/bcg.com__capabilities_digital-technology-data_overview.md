## How BCG Supports Clients in Creating Digital Capabilities
  * [**BCG X**](https://www.bcg.com/x/)**.** BCG X—the tech build and design unit of BCG—disrupts the present and creates the future by building bold new tech products, services, and businesses.
  * [**BCG Platinion**](https://www.bcg.com/beyond-consulting/bcg-platinion/default)**.** As an integrated part of the BCG network, BCG Platinion combines BCG’s strategic perspectives with tech know-how to implement and secure large-scale tech transformations.
  * **Strategy & Purpose. **A powerful, purpose-driven strategy allows companies to align and inspire teams, launch the right digital initiatives, and build the capabilities, processes, and mindsets necessary to sustain a digital advantage. Using our “articulate, activate, embed” methodology, our digital consulting services help organizations uncover their purpose—and translate that purpose into growth.
  * **Future-Built Outcomes.** Three types of outcomes will shape companies: personalized customer experiences and relationships; operational excellence (in which business processes and operations are heavily augmented by machines and AI); and innovative offerings, services, and business models.
  * **Technology and Data Enablers.** Fast-moving digital technology leaders are gaining an insurmountable advantage over the competition—powered by cutting-edge technologies and data-driven insights. Our digital consulting team helps clients uncover, incubate, and deliver game-changing digital businesses and products.
  * **Human Enablers.** The real strength of a company lies in unleashing the power of human creativity. BCG helps companies reset their organization and implement new ways of working to harness that potential at scale, while upskilling and reskilling digital talent to meet demands.
