# KPMG and Oracle
Delivering meaningful transformations
KPMG with our alliance partner Oracle is uniquely positioned to help companies gain a competitive advantage in this data and AI era by delivering successful transformation programs that bring a balanced approach of business expertise and AI technology to the table. KPMG industry-centric approach is driven by our experience and enabled by next-generation technologies to accelerate innovation and time to value.
KPMG has been an award-winning Oracle partner for over thirty years. Our delivery teams have experience across all Oracle products and modules (ERP, HCM, EPM, SCM, Procurement, BI/Analytics and CX), including developing unique PaaS solutions and deep experience implementing Oracle Accounting Hub and Subscription Management modules.
By leveraging Oracle's advanced AI capabilities, such as those found in the Oracle AI Agent Studio and OCI Generative AI Service, KPMG empowers clients to fuse intelligent automation and generative AI into their core business processes, enhancing data management, streamlining operations, and driving innovation. The result is a more intelligent and predictive enterprise, capable of anticipating market shifts, automating at scale, and outmaneuvering the competition..
Embarking on a cloud transformation journey with KPMG and our alliance partner Oracle, **delivers value** , **drives sustainable change** , and **accelerates innovation** – across the front, middle and back-office.
