## [ Government services, reimagined A playbook for turning digital transformation into citizen value ](https://www.pwc.com/ca/en/industries/government-and-public-sector/citizen-and-core-government-services/government-services-reimagined.html)
Defence organizations in Canada are at a crossroads of unprecedented change. Geopolitical tensions and conflicts are increasing. Security alliances are shifting. Threats are emerging on multiple fronts—not just physical ones. Cyber attacks are escalating in scope, reach, and consequences.
As global uncertainties mount and instabilities rise, Canada’s defence organizations—entrusted with safeguarding our sovereignty by protecting our people, our territory, our waters, our natural resources, and our strategic interests—face challenges that are more complex and interconnected than ever before. Their operational readiness relies on having the people, the equipment, the technology, and the data needed to respond in real time.
Success will depend on more than modernizing our existing defence infrastructure. It’ll take rethinking and reinventing the backbone of national defence and how defence organizations operate. And it’ll take agility, innovation, and increasing collaboration and partnerships with the Canadian private sector industrial base.
## Working with you to make changes that matter 
We’re a Canadian firm with deep experience working with the Canadian government, armed forces, and intelligence community. Furthermore, our global network allows us to share perspectives and experiences from other allied jurisdictions that inform decision making and accelerate outcomes. 
Working collaboratively with you, we’ll help lay the foundations you need to face tomorrow’s conflicts head-on—from rethinking operating models and workforce strategies to accelerating procurement processes and building sovereign and resilient supply chains. We’ll also help you navigate digital transformation and interoperability end-to-end so you can make the most of your data, use innovative tools like AI, and make time sensitive decisions with confidence.

### Operating as your trusted business advisor 
We are your trusted business advisor, supporting both minor and major capital acquisitions using cross-domain capability development experience gained across allied jurisdictions. We help you navigate complex acquisition programs with confidence based on our significant expertise, understanding of leading practices, and knowledge of innovative solutions in other advanced defence environments. By drawing on our Canadian experience and global knowledge base, we help you streamline project delivery, manage risks proactively, and unlock greater value from your investments in defense related assets.
### Growing the defence workforce 
We help you rethink workforce strategies, streamline organizational structures, understand evolving capability needs—including digital and AI skill sets—develop training programs, and build tools that equip your teams with the capabilities they need to meet future challenges and respond to a dynamic environment.
### Building future-ready operating models
We work with you to design agile and resilient operating models that aren’t reliant on legacy processes and technologies so that you can make stronger, data-driven decisions and drive innovation.
### Supporting data-driven investment decisions 
We use data-driven approaches to help you prioritize investments based on value while balancing risk so you can get more out of every dollar you spend. 
### Powering procurement 
We work with you to simplify and streamline your procurement processes and to embrace technologies that accelerate procurement activities, boost efficiencies, and enhance transparency and accountability so you can secure high-quality products faster. 
### Transforming enterprise outcomes with digital capabilities and AI 
We help you transition to secure and scalable cloud and protected environments and build the data and technology backbone you need to underpin seamless operations, enable interoperability with alliance partners, embrace innovative capabilities like AI, and empower your decision making with data analytics and real-time intelligence. 
### Fostering partnerships and ecosystem collaboration 
We help you establish relationships and partnerships with the Canadian industrial base to attract investment, enhance your domestic ecosystem, and advance joint objectives and innovation priorities.
### Enhancing supply chain sovereignty and resilience 
We work with you to assess the security and stability of your supply chains, build sovereign and dependable supply chains—particularly for critical inputs—and enhance the resilience of your supply chains using advanced automation and logistics practices (like digital twinning) so you have the trusted supply flow to be mission ready when it matters most.
### Providing seamless managed services 
We make it easy for you to focus on your critical operations by providing seamless IT infrastructure, back-office operations, and supply-chain management services so you can free up your workforce and enhance your performance.
