# Law Firm Employment Spend Reduced by 55% for Software Company
### Axiom attorney helped:
Cut outside employment law firm spend by 55%
Counsel on performance improvement plans and terminations
Resolve employment litigation claims
### Challenge
A lean legal team for an online software marketplace struggles to keep up with rapid business growth. **Relying on elite law firms for litigation and employment matters causes department spend to quickly grow out of hand.** However, they don't have enough work to commit to a full-time hire.
Approach
Although litigation claims arise infrequently, the company’s law firm rates make it nearly cost-prohibitive to fight back. After a sequence of expensive law firm bills, the General Counsel (GC) visits Axiom’s website to explore a lower cost solution.
She clicks into[Access Legal Talent](https://www.axiomlaw.com/access-legal-talent), their online lawyer marketplace, and searches for US employment lawyers with software experience, litigation backgrounds, and 12-15 years of experience. **Instantly, she discovers 3 top attorneys, one of whom she knows from her prior tenure at an AmLaw 10 firm.**
Impact
  * Axiom attorney pays for himself in no time, reduces their **first litigation settlement from $250,000 to $15,000**
  * Axiom attorney **works hand in hand with the HR team** to walk through each claim and produce cohesive business-oriented guidance
  * Axiom attorney **counsels on performance improvement plans and terminations,** drafts collection letter templates, and updates state-by-state disclosures in employee handbooks
