At AESG UK, our Threat and Risk Assessment services adhere to the principles of ISO 31000, ensuring a systematic, structured, and proactive approach to risk management. We specialise in helping organisations identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks, protecting their assets, operations, and people in an increasingly dynamic security landscape. Our assessments follow the ISO 31000 framework, which focuses on integrating risk management into all aspects of organisational decision-making. This ensures a robust foundation for addressing potential threats, vulnerabilities, and asset criticality, while supporting operational resilience and long-term strategic goals.
## Why Threat and Risk Assessments are Essential
In today's dynamic risk landscape, understanding the threats your organisation faces is crucial for maintaining operational resilience and protecting critical assets. Threat and Risk Assessments offer a structured approach to:
### Identify Potential Threats
From terrorism and cyber-attacks to natural disasters and insider threats.
### Assess Vulnerabilities
Evaluate weaknesses in physical structures, technological systems, processes, or policies that could be exploited.
### Determine Asset Criticality
Prioritise assets based on their importance and the potential impact of their compromise.
### Develop Risk Mitigation Strategies
Create actionable plans to reduce, transfer, or eliminate risks.

## Criticality and Impact Assessment
### Evaluate the significance of assets and business functions
### Determine potential operational, financial, and reputational impacts
## Risk Evaluation and Prioritisation
### Combine threat and vulnerability data to assess risk levels
### Prioritise risks based on likelihood and potential impact
## Strategic Risk Mitigation Planning
### Develop tailored strategies for risk acceptance, reduction, avoidance, or transfer

## Implementation Support
### Assist with integrating mitigation strategies into existing operations
### Provide ongoing support and monitoring to adapt to evolving threats
