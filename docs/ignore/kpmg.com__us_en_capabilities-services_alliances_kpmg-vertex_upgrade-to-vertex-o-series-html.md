# Now is the time to upgrade to Vertex O Series
Key benefits of migrating from a legacy L Series and Q Series tax engine
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2023/vertex-o-series.pdf)
Share
Increasing tax requirements, tax regulations, and automation demands on the indirect tax function now require a complete indirect tax system built on cloud andAI technologies for scalability and smarter decision-making. The newest offering, Vertex O Series, has been enhanced over the years through the product lifecycle of 6.0 through 9.0 version enhancements collected from Vertex customer improvement requests. Each version contains customer-requested enhancements, resulting in added functionality that can deliver valuable benefits to the tax department.
One of the key differences between the older versions—L Series and Q Series—and the newer version—O Series—is their architecture. Vertex has evolved their products along with the technology as users have moved from mainframes to PCs and now to the cloud.
Our latest slip sheet in the Vertex O Series, from KPMG Tax alliances, highlights the enhancements and benefits for clients considering an upgrade to Vertex O Series from Vertex Sales Tax L Series or Q Series.
