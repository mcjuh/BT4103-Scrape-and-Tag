# Government Contract Vehicles
Enabling agencies to be more flexible, agile and efficient
Publicis Sapient supports the operation of US Government agencies and provides vital strategies designed to fundamentally transform the way they work. Our expertise includes strategy, program management, technology and creative services.
To enable this work, we possess several Government-wide Acquisition Contracts (GWAC), which can be used by any federal agency, plus other Agency-specific Indefinite Delivery/Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract vehicles.
These currently include:
### **1) US General Services Administration: Multiple Award Schedule (GSA MAS)**
The GSA MAS means our Information Technology Services (IT-70) and Professional Services Schedules are provided through a streamlined acquisition vehicle. We’re able to provide comprehensive technology, management, strategy, marketing, advertising, design, graphic support and public relations solutions that leverage commercial best practices.
It also means federal agencies can leverage one of the most widely used procurement vehicles and benefit from our 20+ years of delivering successful technology and consulting solutions for the public and private sectors.

### **2) US National Institutes of Health (NIH) CIO-SP3***
CIO-SP3 is a GWAC from NITAAC (National Institutes of Health Information Technology Acquisition and Assessment Center). It can be used by any federal civilian or Department of Defense agency to fulfill a broad range of mission critical IT requirements. The CIO-SP3 vehicle:
**- Has a $20 billion ceiling**
**- Incorporates performance-based features**
**- Can award work in modular increments**
**- Allows for flexible contract types**

### **3) US Department of Homeland Security Enterprise Acquisition Gateway for Leading Edge Solutions II (EAGLE II)**
We offer the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) a wide variety of products and services, from software to the design, development, integration and operational maintenance of IT systems.
The goal of EAGLE II is to provide a broad mix of IT services that help DHS meet mission goals, consolidate IT services, provide innovative solutions and save money. EAGLE II is available to all of DHS, as well as other federal agencies working on homeland security-related projects.
