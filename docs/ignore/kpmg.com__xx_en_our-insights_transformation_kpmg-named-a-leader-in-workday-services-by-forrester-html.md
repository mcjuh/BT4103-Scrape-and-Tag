KPMG has been named a “Leader” in [The Forrester Wave™: Workday Services, Q2 2024opens in a new tab](https://reprints2.forrester.com/#/assets/2/2428/RES180706/report) .
“KPMG is well known for its business and strategy consulting for companies undergoing complex transactions like mergers, acquisitions, divestures, private equity, and large finance and operations transformation — enabled by the firm’s strong brand and capabilities in risk, technology, change management, and managed services. While it excels at Workday HCM, Finance, and Spend Management implementations, KPMG truly stands out in its industry accelerators in banking and capital markets, insurance and healthcare, and Workday packaged solutions,” the report states. 
The report highlights the breadth of collaboration and trust throughout the KPMG network of firms. “KPMG has 300-plus Workday enterprise clients globally. The firm uses its advisory and audit background to spearhead its ambition to be the most trusted provider supporting the C-suite, and this has greatly benefitted the firm in winning some of the highest-range, large-scale Workday deals in healthcare and financial services. Its most innovative asset is the Powered Enterprise solution and framework used across all aspects of its Workday delivery. What sets KPMG apart is its own CFO research and survey, which validates market needs before the firm builds a package or industry solution.”
[ The Forrester Wave™: Workday Services, Q2 2024 opens in a new tab](https://reprints2.forrester.com/#/assets/2/2428/RES180706/report)
[ Click here to know about the KPMG and Workday alliance ](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/what-we-do/alliances/workday.html)
Together, KPMG firms and Workday can help organizations accelerate business innovation and competitive differentiation by designing and deploying effective, cloud-based solutions. KPMG Powered Enterprise enabled by Workday leverages decades of business experience and Workday technology to help organizations transform their back office faster and achieve a greater step change in value.
The report continues, KPMG invests heavily in its talent for Workday training, certifications, and education in its Powered Enterprise asset, ensuring a standardized delivery experience for all clients.
To conclude, “reference customers noted that KPMG brings strong benchmarking data to help clients understand their positioning against peers, and it delivers the “A team” and a holistic approach to transformation.” Remarking, “reference customers noted that KPMG is worth the value and offers unparalleled industry expertise. KPMG is well suited for clients looking for a prescribed approach to transformation.”
**"Being recognized by Forrester as a “Leader” in Workday Services is a testament to KPMG professionals helping clients around the world transform their back-office systems and processes with Workday’s market leading technology."**
**David Zinn, Global Workday Platform Leader, KPMG International**
