# Tax Value Creation for Private Equity
Helping portfolio companies identify high-impact tax opportunities
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2026/tax-value-creation-private-equity.pdf)
Share
In today's competitive landscape, every margin matters. KPMG’s tax value creation offering helps portfolio companies quantify and prioritize high-impact opportunities to drive real results. By taking a holistic, opportunity-focused approach across both direct and indirect taxes, organizations can uncover hidden savings, mitigate risk, and drive measurable improvements to their bottom line.
## **Key Benefits:**
Opportunity-driven review
Uncovers value across all areas of tax, including credits, refunds, and structure optimization.
Low investment
A collaborative approach that typically requires two hours or less of a portfolio company’s time.
AI-powered
Leverages GenAI and advanced analytics to accelerate insight discovery and enable faster decisions.
Value centric
Designed to support cash flow, EBITDA, and margin performance.
