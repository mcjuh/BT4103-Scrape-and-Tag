## Healthcare Industry Trends in 2026
  * ### Workflow redesigns fuel AI value
AI adoption is accelerating, but only organizations that redesign workflows around people and process will unlock meaningful improvements in access, efficiency, and care quality.
  * ### Home is the new health hub
Home-based care is becoming healthcare’s most scalable channel, with hybrid virtual and in-person models expanding reach while balancing cost, equity, and trust.
  * ### Fix operations before adding bots
Automation won’t restore margins unless payers and providers first standardize data, align decision rules, and collaborate to eliminate waste and speed reimbursement.
  * ### OBBBA reshapes access, equity, and operations
The One Big Beautiful Bill Act brings new compliance demands and tightens margins, requiring redesigned intake, grant funding strategies, and regional coordination to maintain patient access.


_“Healthcare enters 2026 at a pivotal moment. Workforce shortages, reimbursement pressure, and rising patient expectations are straining systems built for another era. The organizations that thrive will be those bold enough to fundamentally redesign how care is delivered and financed._
_This transformation isn’t about adding more tools or pilots. It’s about modernizing workflows, aligning data across payers and providers, and re-engineering operating models for speed, access, and trust. The coming year will reward those who treat technology as a catalyst for redesign, not a substitute for it."_
