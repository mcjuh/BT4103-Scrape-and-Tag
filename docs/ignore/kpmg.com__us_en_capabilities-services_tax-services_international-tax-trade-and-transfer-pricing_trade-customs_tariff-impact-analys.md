# Tariff Impact Analysis
Tariffs create uncertainty. We deliver perspective. 
In today's dynamic global trade environment, the impact of tariffs can significantly affect your business's financial stability and supply chain operations. Being prepared is not just a best practice; it's a necessity. KPMG Trade & Customs provides clients with tariff impact analysis services, designed to help you navigate these complexities and maintain a competitive edge.
**Being prepared for tariff impact is crucial to help ensure:**
  * **Financial Stability** : Tariffs can lead to increased costs, affecting your bottom line. Being prepared allows you to mitigate these financial impacts and maintain profitability.
  * **Supply Chain Integrity** : Tariffs can disrupt supply chains, leading to delays and shortages. Preparedness ensures that your supply chain remains robust and reliable.
  * **Compliance** : Navigating trade regulations and compliance requirements can be daunting. Being prepared helps you stay compliant and avoid penalties.


KPMG can help you:
**Assess your tariff exposure** : We analyze your import and export data using our proprietary KPMG Tariff Modeler to identify areas of high exposure.
**Prepare detailed scenario planning** : Our scenario planning tools help you balance cost, quality, and availability, helping to ensure you are prepared for various trade scenarios.
**Create phased implementation plans** : We create detailed, phased plans with clear timelines to help you implement tariff mitigation initiatives effectively, to prioritize high-impact areas (e.g., China-sourced materials) with timelines
**Fulfill tariff strategy implementation** : Support tariff strategy implementation of sourcing shifts or tariff mitigation initiatives
**Drive and accelerate value** : Develop cost-saving strategies to mitigate tariff impacts
