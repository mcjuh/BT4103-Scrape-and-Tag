# Income and Franchise Tax
Comply with state and local regulations more effectively.
##  Comply with regulations and plan ahead 
All businesses must pay state corporate income tax, and many also pay franchise tax for the right to operate in certain states. Constant legislative changes and requirements that vary by state make compliance a complex and time-consuming process. And for companies making acquisitions or expanding into new states, the potential impact on income and franchise tax can be significant.
The KPMG Income and Franchise Tax practice can help make compliance and tax planning more effective. Working with our State Tax Resource Network, our specialized teams deliver timely insight and guidance on your changing tax requirements. And we can show you how to adapt processes and apply technology to make compliance more automated.
With offices from coast to coast, KPMG Income and Franchise Tax professionals are available wherever you do business.
###  Income and franchise tax services
Nexus and voluntary disclosure
To help meet nexus rules, KPMG can analyze your in-state activities and contacts to determine filing requirements and resolve any past exposures.
Apportionment and return reviews 
By analyzing data gathered from your company’s systems, we can define an apportionment methodology and allocate revenue to comply with state regulations. 
Restructuring and tax planning 
Our specialists can analyze your current financial and operational situation and explore how restructuring may affect future costs, income, and tax requirements.
Compliance 
KPMG professionals can help you prepare state income tax estimates, extensions, annual returns, and amended returns.
Dispute resolution 
We can assist with state tax audits, as well as rulings requests to secure guidance.
Credit and incentive reviews 
We can help to identify significant state tax credits and incentives available for multistate operations.
