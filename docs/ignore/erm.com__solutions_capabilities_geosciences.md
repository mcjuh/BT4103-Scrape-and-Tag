#  Geosciences
At ERM, we offer specialized geoscience services combining highly experienced personnel with state-of-the-art technology to provide complete value chain support from ground selection and targeting to discovery and definition of mineral resources. The team has expertise in:
  * Commodities including critical minerals (Cu, Ni, Co, Li, REEs, U), gold, iron ore and, critical and industrial minerals.
  * Geological disciplines including structural geology, geochemistry, data analytics, geometallurgy and integrated mineral systems; and
  * Specialized deposits, including Porphyry Cu-Mo-Au; Sedimentary Hosted Cu; Iron Oxide Copper Gold (IOCG); Intrusion Related Gold (IRG); Orogenic Gold; Nickel deposits (magmatic, komatiite and laterite): Iron Ore (Hematite, Goethite, CID, Magnetite and Itabirite deposits); Uranium (unconformity and sedimentary hosted deposits); REE deposits (ionic clay, Alkaline igneous hosted and Carbonatite hosted).


Given our depth of experience we can supply a complete exploration management service comprising planning, supervision, data collection, management, safety services and reporting.
Example offerings:
  * Regional targeting and project generation.
  * Program implementation, including flied work (drilling supervision, field program design, regional to prospect scale mapping, etc.)
  * Project management (including permitting, compliance and subcontractor screening)
  * Expert Reporting, due diligence, and geo-corporate advice (including JORC, PERC and NI 43-101 reporting, with experience in Chinese and Russian systems, VALMIN, input to acquisitions and mergers etc.)
  * Integrated data management (including drilling and GIS database design and management, 3D modelling etc.)
  * Safety Management, training and mentoring
