# Tax policy, legislation, and controversy
Staying abreast of tax policy developments, legislation, and regulations is a major challenge for organizations operating in multiple jurisdictions. Let KPMG help you keep pace and manage potential tax controversy.
##  Informed advice on compliance, planning, and controversy 
In a complex, constantly changing tax landscape, businesses need to monitor tax-related developments at every level to understand how they may affect tax planning and compliance. KPMG professionals provide informed insight and advice ahead of possible legislative or regulatory changes. We also offer guidance to help avoid disputes and effectively manage audits or tax controversies when they arise.
