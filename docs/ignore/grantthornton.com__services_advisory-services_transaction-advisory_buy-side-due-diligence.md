## **Insight-driven diligence that builds investment confidence**
Grant Thornton Stax’s buy-side due diligence is grounded in a longstanding history of partnership with private equity clients, combining deep industry and asset expertise across software and technology, business services, industrials, consumer, healthcare, events and education sectors. The approach at Grant Thornton Stax is collaborative, transparent and efficient — designed to maximize client value. Each engagement examines market dynamics and opportunities, target business model alignment, competitive positioning, key risks and value creation levers that drive future exit success. This process equips investors with the insights needed to make well-informed investment decisions and identify opportunities for revenue growth and sustained value creation post-acquisition.
