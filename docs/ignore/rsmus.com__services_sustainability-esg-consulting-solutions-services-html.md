##  Sustainability and ESG: A competitive advantage for middle market businesses 
Environmental, social and governance (ESG) initiatives are a business imperative. Investors, customers and employees alike demand transparency and a commitment to sustainability. Creating a sustainability strategy that meets those demands is an iterative process that evolves as companies learn from their performance, adjust to changes in their operating environment and respond to emerging sustainability trends and stakeholder expectations.
RSM tailors sustainability and ESG-related services to help you navigate the stages of ESG adoption and maturity. We facilitate your comprehensive and seamless ESG journey—from strategy development, to ESG assessment and planning, and on through implementation, supply chain redesign, ESG data analysis, climate risk modeling and voluntary or required ESG data reporting.
We support that journey with sustainability due diligence, ESG technology implementations, internal audit, tax credits and incentives, and limited and reasonable assurance across the global regulatory reporting environment.
Our tailored ESG solutions help you meet regulatory requirements, build stronger relationships with stakeholders, enhance your brand reputation and strengthen long-term business resilience. You can turn your ESG commitments into meaningful, measurable outcomes that support sustainable growth and innovation. 
Sustainability services help organizations assess, manage and validate ESG practices by combining advisory support, tax strategy and independent assurance to support regulatory compliance, align sustainability outcomes with business and growth objectives and strengthen the credibility of ESG data and stakeholder trust.

ESG consulting  
    
RSM’s sustainability advisors help private and public companies with every step of their ESG journey. This includes assessing current practices, developing strategies, implementing solutions, calculating impacts, reporting results and integrating ESG metrics into corporate decisions. Integrate ESG resources into your business. RSM's experienced professionals assist you with regulatory compliance, materiality and risk assessments, greenhouse gas modeling and calculations, voluntary and mandatory reporting, supplier surveys, stakeholder communications, and training.

Sustainability and ESG tax advisory services

Sustainability and ESG assurance services  
    
With various sustainability mandates in effect to some extent around the world, comprehensive ESG assurance is critical for maintaining stakeholder trust and confidence. RSM’s high-quality, industry-tailored assurance services verify your ESG reporting, helping to mitigate risk and providing you with peace of mind that your company is prepared for reporting requirements.

Internal audit services  
    
Compliance with global and domestic regulations, such as the European Union’s Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) and California Senate Bills 253 and 261, is essential for companies, as these regulations mandate the disclosure of various sustainability topics, including greenhouse gas emissions and climate-related financial risks. RSM's internal audits support accurate ESG data collection, controls and reporting systems to meet these requirements. Additionally, by standardizing ESG data collection processes and providing independent reviews, RSM’s internal audits enhance the accuracy and reliability of ESG data, reducing the risk of noncompliance.
[ Learn more about our internal audit services ](https://rsmus.com/services/risk-fraud-cybersecurity/governance-risk-compliance/internal-audit-controls-services.html "Learn more about our internal audit services") 

Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance services  
    
RSM helps public companies and those planning to go public navigate the complexities of SOX compliance. Our SOX advisors use a modern, risk-based approach and advanced technologies like data analytics and automation to improve efficiency and reduce the burden on internal teams. This helps ensure accurate financial and nonfinancial reporting, mitigating the risk of noncompliance.
In addition to traditional SOX compliance, RSM’s ESG and sustainability-related services focus on regulatory compliance, data accuracy and meeting international standards. This manages your risks, enhances ESG data integrity and helps you stay ahead of evolving ESG requirements, providing comprehensive support for your sustainability strategy.
[ Learn more about our SOX compliance services ](https://rsmus.com/services/risk-fraud-cybersecurity/governance-risk-compliance/sarbanes-oxley-sox-compliance.html "Learn more about our SOX compliance services") 

ESG software  
    
Navigating the complexities of sustainability and ESG data, approvals, calculations, regulatory reporting, risk management and systems integration can be challenging. RSM offers specialized knowledge along with many ESG software solutions designed to simplify and automate this journey. We help you achieve your unique sustainability objectives and meet your reporting requirements with a comprehensive approach to identifying and optimizing your data and technology architecture.

Supply chain advisory services  
    
Greater visibility into potential vendor/supplier risk is no longer simply nice to have—it’s imperative. The concerns they address include safety, traceability, labor and human rights, deforestation, conflict minerals, and extreme weather event risk. RSM's supply chain professionals help you address these concerns while building resilient operations that optimize cost and mitigate risk. Leveraging our strategic relationship with the artificial intelligence-driven Equifax Supplier Risk Solution further enhances the effectiveness of your efforts.
