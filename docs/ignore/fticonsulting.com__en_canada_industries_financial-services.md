Challenges faced by today's banking leaders include a continually evolving competitive and operating environment, mounting regulatory and compliance pressures, and the emergence of disruptive new technologies. FTI Consulting advises banks and financial institutions at all stages of their development on every aspect of their business, including risk management, financial and operational performance, compliance, regulatory inquiries, enforcement actions and litigation threats, and enterprise value creation and protection.
### How We Help
In addition to working with banks and other financial institutions, our experts work with the world’s leading private equity firms, hedge funds and investment banks, providing pragmatic transaction and execution-oriented services throughout the deal life cycle. From origination to exit, we use knowledge-driven skill and experience, focusing on critical financial, commercial and operational opportunities to help create and enhance enterprise value. Across the financial services industry, our professionals build on rich practical experience and multifaceted expertise to generate positive outcomes. We deliver both standalone offerings and comprehensive solutions to address the interconnected issues our clients face. Our team comprises highly respected bankers, restructuring and turnaround professionals, experts in business transformation, forensic specialists, CPAs, CFAs, certified anti-money laundering specialists, attorneys, economists, technology professionals, strategic communications experts, former operators, policy makers and former bank and securities regulators — all with extensive industry knowledge and expertise.
