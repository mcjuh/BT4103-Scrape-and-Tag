# Podcast: Microsoft 365 Mergers and Divestitures
Frank Lesniak explores how to navigate Microsoft 365 mergers and divestitures—from due diligence through migration and cutover
February 16, 2026
Social Share
[Mergers and divestitures](https://www.westmonroe.com/services/mergers-acquisitions) introduce significant complexity into Microsoft 365 environments—requiring organizations to rethink everything from tenant strategy to security, licensing, and user experience.
In this episode of the _Microsoft Cloud IT Pro Podcast_ , Senior Architect in our [Technology & Experience practice](https://www.westmonroe.com/services/technology-experience) Frank Lesniak breaks down what it takes to successfully navigate these transitions. He walks through the full lifecycle of a transaction—from early-stage due diligence and environment design to migration, cutover, and post-integration stabilization—highlighting where organizations most often run into challenges.
The discussion also dives into the technical and operational nuances that can make or break a transformation, including aligning disparate licensing models, managing endpoints and applications, and maintaining security throughout the process. A key theme: success depends on having a clear, structured approach that connects technical execution with broader business objectives.
