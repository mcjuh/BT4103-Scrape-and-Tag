# Phase 1: Strategic Advisory
[ Contact Us Today ](https://aesg.com/contact-us/)
## Introduction
Every successful data centre begins with the right strategic foundation. In this phase, we support clients in evaluating opportunities, assessing potential sites and defining a clear development strategy. Through comprehensive due diligence, infrastructure assessments and masterplanning, we help identify locations that offer the optimal balance of power availability, connectivity, scalability and long-term operational viability.
## Our Services
## Strategic Feasibility & Due Diligence
We provide independent technical and commercial assessments focused on power infrastructure viability, utility capacity availability, and grid connection feasibility for proposed data centre developments. Our analysis evaluates HV/MV supply options, substation requirements, and regulatory pathways to secure capacity, ensuring informed decision-making and reducing investment risk before significant capital is committed.
### Reduces development and investment risk
### Enables informed, data-driven decision-making
### Identifies technical, commercial and regulatory constraints early
### Improves project viability and financial confidence
## Site Selection & Infrastructure Appraisal
We evaluate potential sites with particular focus on power availability, fibre connectivity, infrastructure capacity, and grid connection feasibility. Our appraisals assess substation proximity, available voltage levels, utility provider capabilities, and the regulatory pathway to secure required capacity, helping clients identify locations that support reliable high-density operations and future campus expansion.
### Identifies sites best suited for resilient data centre operations
### Ensures access to critical infrastructure such as power and fibre
### Supports long-term scalability and campus expansion
### Minimises future operational and infrastructure constraints
## Power & Connectivity Assessments
Our power systems specialists conduct detailed assessments of grid infrastructure, supply capacity, and connection requirements to confirm power availability for current and future data centre loads. We analyse HV/MV supply options, evaluate substation upgrade requirements, assess grid resilience and redundancy, and identify infrastructure constraints that could impact project viability, schedule, or operational performance.
### Confirms availability of reliable power and network capacity
### Identifies infrastructure limitations early in the development process
### Supports uptime, redundancy and operational resilience
### Reduces the risk of delays or redesigns during later stages
## Data Centre Masterplanning & Capacity Strategy
We develop masterplans and capacity deployment strategies for data centre campuses, from initial test fits and layout concepts through to detailed infrastructure deployment roadmaps. Our masterplanning defines data hall configurations, building footprints and phasing, substation locations, power distribution strategies, cooling plant placement, utility connection points, and campus circulation for single buildings or multi-gigawatt developments. We integrate critical system requirements with site development constraints, optimise space utilisation and establish frameworks for securing incremental utility capacity, enabling efficient capital deployment, scalable growth, and long-term operational flexibility.
### Enables scalable and phased development
### Optimises infrastructure efficiency and land use
### Supports long-term growth and expansion planning
### Improves overall project value and operational flexibility
## Planning, Regulatory & Development Advisory
We provide regulatory and development advisory services focused on permitting, infrastructure approvals, and utility agreements. Our services include coordination with regional and local authorities, navigating connection processes, supporting substation planning applications, and management of capacity reservation agreements, helping clients accelerate critical approvals and secure long-lead utility commitments.
### Streamlines planning approvals and permitting processes
### Reduces regulatory and compliance risks
### Minimises potential development delays
### Provides clarity on jurisdictional and regulatory requirements
## Sustainability & Carbon Strategy
We develop power infrastructure strategies that enable renewable energy integration, grid decarbonisation pathways, and energy resilience for data centre operations. Our approach focuses on renewable supply procurement options, energy storage system integration, power purchase agreements, and utility-scale renewable connection strategies, supporting clients' net zero commitments while maintaining the reliability and redundancy required for mission critical operations.
### Reduces operational carbon and energy consumption
### Supports ESG and net zero commitments
### Enhances long-term asset resilience and compliance
### Improves environmental performance and investor confidence
