# Unlocking CSRD's Business Value: Early cross-sector lessons
**CSRD insights you can’t miss**
Hear from ERM experts and technology company TeamViewer and Yondr leaders on how organizations are unlocking business value through CSRD. Our expert panel explores how companies can take a more strategic approach to CSRD reporting—one that uncovers hidden risks, identifies opportunities for growth and efficiency, and builds greater resilience across the value chain.
**In the session, we covered:**
  * The most important takeaways from our Wave 1 analysis of 41 leading global companies 


  * Surprising trends and gaps in early CSRD readiness 


  * Practical guidance for adjusting your reporting and compliance processes moving forward 


**Watch the recording to gain actionable insights and cross-sector lessons.**
