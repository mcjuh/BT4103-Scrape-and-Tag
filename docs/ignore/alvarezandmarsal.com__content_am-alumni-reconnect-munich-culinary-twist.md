# A&M Alumni Reconnect in Munich with a Culinary Twist
## **May 22, 2025 – A Night of Connections, Conversations and Cuisine**
On May 22, more than 40 A&M alumni and current team members gathered in Munich for a unique twist on Germany’s annual alumni event: a “Dinner Hopping” experience through the heart of the city. Hosted by Managing Directors [Steffen Kroner](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/steffen-kroner), [Patrick Siebert](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/patrick-siebert) and [Jürgen Zapf](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/jurgen-zapf), the evening brought together exceptional food and a spirit of reconnection and camaraderie that reflects the strength of the A&M alumni community.
The evening began with a warm welcome and drinks at Alvarez & Marsal’s recently opened Munich office on Sonnenstraße 20 where guests had the chance to tour the new space.
Then the group boarded a private bus to begin a progressive dinner through Munich’s vibrant food scene. Stops included Ottantanove for starters, main courses at Il Cigno and ended the evening at Rustic.
As Steffen Kroner shared, “Every year, the Alumni Event is a special evening to look forward to. Reconnecting with former colleagues to reflect on past experiences, discuss current challenges and exchange ideas for the future feels like coming home.”
From the first welcome to the final dessert, the evening delivered meaningful connections, fresh perspectives and the unmistakable A&M spirit of collaboration and reenergized professional relationships.
