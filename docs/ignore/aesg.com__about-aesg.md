# About Us
AESG is a world-leading specialist consultancy, engineering, and advisory firm with headquarters in London, Singapore, Riyadh, Dubai, and Abu Dhabi. We operate on a global scale, delivering projects across Europe, the Middle East, Asia, Africa, Australia, and the United Kingdom. Driving advanced performance within the built environment, we pride ourselves on being industry leaders in each of the services we offer. Our expansive team comprises some of the largest dedicated specialist consultancy professionals, working on projects within the building, urban planning, infrastructure, and strategic advisory sectors.
## Mission
To partner with ambitious clients in the built and natural environment, solving the world’s most complex challenges by combining deep technical expertise, collaboration and innovation.
## Vision
To redefine consultancy by delivering impact that accelerates sustainable progress and sets a new global frontier for excellence and innovation.
## Strategic Solutions
By combining technical expertise with practical experience, our team of management consultants and subject matter experts provide strategic policy and planning solutions. We develop strategies and implementation plans that enable governments and private sector organisations to adapt to the ever-evolving global market shifts, embedding the objectives of resiliency and sustainability.
## Commitment to Sustainability
As signatories to the WorldGBC’s advancing net zero buildings commitment, AESG is dedicated to supporting the construction industry in achieving Net Zero Carbon Emission targets by 2030 and 2050. We are proud members of the Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat, and the Emirates Green Building Council, actively leading the global sustainable building movement.
## Our People
To achieve our ambitions, we prioritise growing and strengthening our greatest asset: our people. Through rigorous recruitment, training, mentorship, and professional development programmes, our highly skilled team of experts delivers exemplary consultancy services, ensuring the delivery of high-performance developments. AESG brings experience and expertise to deliver projects that stand the test of time.

## Our Global Presence
Operating from multiple strategic locations worldwide, AESG leverages its international footprint to deliver bespoke solutions tailored to diverse markets. Our global network ensures that we can provide local insights with a comprehensive understanding of global best practices, making us the ideal partner for complex, multinational projects.
