## How We Can Help
Recovering assets demands intelligence, legal enforcement, and strategic execution. Kroll uncovers misappropriated funds, secures ownership structures, and enforces claims globally. Using forensic accounting and investigations, we maximize recoveries and safeguard financial interests efficiently.
  * **Investigations and Asset Tracing:** We uncover fraud and misappropriation through intelligence-led investigations. Using forensic accounting, we trace dissipated funds, establish asset positions, and develop targeted recovery strategies.
  * **Enforcement and Tandem Investigations:** Our team executes recovery through court actions and insolvency appointments. We step into counterparties’ roles, accessing financial records and identifying misappropriated assets.
  * **Recovery and Asset Control:** We take control of assets across jurisdictions, replacing directors and securing ownership structures. Our cross-border enforcement strategies ensure effective asset realization.
  * **Claims and Realizations:** We maximize recoveries with court appointments, clawback actions, and liquidation strategies. By pursuing claims against counterparties, we optimize asset realization through market-driven processes.
