# Unlocking growth catalysts 
## Longer holds demand a sharper strategy, with growth and tech-focused innovation driving lasting returns.
Authors: 
[ Print ](javascript:window.print\(\); "Print")
[ Share  ](https://www.deloitte.com/global/en/Industries/financial-services/perspectives/unlocking-growth-catalysts.html# "Share")
Unlocking growth catalysts
[Download PDF (1MB)](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-shared/docs/industries/financial-services/2025/unlocking-growth-catalyst.pdf) [View PDF](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-shared/docs/industries/financial-services/2025/unlocking-growth-catalyst.pdf)
This article explores how extended hold periods are reshaping private equity’s approach to value creation. Venki Seshaadri, Private Equity Software Sector leader at Deloitte US, and Emma Cox, Deloitte’s Global Private Equity leader, share their perspectives on how the industry’s shift from relying primarily on multiple expansion to prioritizing operational excellence, technology adoption, especially AI, and strategic talent management. Their discussion highlights both the challenges and opportunities facing private equity firms as they seek to drive growth, strengthen leadership, and communicate value to investors.
Discover how private equity firms are evolving their strategies to build greater resilience and deliver stronger performance across their portfolios by reading **Unlocking growth catalysts,** featured in Private Equity International’s report.
