## Life Sciences trends in 2026
  * ### Pricing power belongs to products that prove superiority
Affordability pressures and rising development costs are redefining value. Only therapies and devices that demonstrate measurable, payer-accepted superiority will maintain pricing power.
  * ### Rebuilding supply chains for U.S. capacity with global flexibility
Tariffs and volatility demand selective U.S. investment while maintaining trusted global networks, creating hybrid models that balance resilience, cost, and regulatory complexity.
  * ### AI adoption accelerates—human judgment becomes the edge
AI is reshaping research, manufacturing, and commercialization, but progress hinges on clean data, strong governance, and teams trained to apply human oversight where it matters most.
