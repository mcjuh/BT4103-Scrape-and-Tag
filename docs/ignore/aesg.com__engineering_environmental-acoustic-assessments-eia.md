At AESG, we deliver bespoke environmental acoustic services that are essential for assessing, managing, and mitigating the impact of noise on surrounding environments. With a focus on precision and sustainability, we apply cutting-edge technology and advanced risk assessment techniques to provide holistic solutions that not only meet regulatory requirements but also enhance the quality of life in and around your projects.We support our clients across all stages of a project—whether you are seeking to comply with environmental regulations, reduce community noise exposure, or manage ongoing operational impacts. Our services span across industries, from urban development to transportation infrastructure and industrial facilities.
## Key Services
[ Noise Impact Assessments ](https://aesg.com/engineering/environmental-acoustic-assessments-eia/#collapse-7abe8626aa068e944e68)
We specialise in noise impact assessments that evaluate the potential impact of new developments on the surrounding environment. These assessments are critical for understanding noise sources and ensuring that appropriate mitigation strategies are in place to protect both residents and wildlife.
[ Baseline Noise Monitoring ](https://aesg.com/engineering/environmental-acoustic-assessments-eia/#collapse-203a7c16aa068e944e68)
We provide baseline noise monitoring to establish the current noise environment at your project site. This data serves as the foundation for accurate environmental noise modelling and future impact predictions.
[ Predictive Noise and Vibration Modelling ](https://aesg.com/engineering/environmental-acoustic-assessments-eia/#collapse-8b2a72e6aa068e944e68)
Using industry-leading software, we conduct predictive noise and vibration modelling, simulating how noise will behave across a variety of environments. This allows us to assess potential noise issues early and recommend mitigation measures to prevent future complications.
[ Noise Source Identification and Monitoring ](https://aesg.com/engineering/environmental-acoustic-assessments-eia/#collapse-7abe8626aa068e945396)
Accurate identification of noise sources is crucial for effective noise management. Our team conducts noise source monitoring for point, line, and area sources, offering insights into how individual noise sources contribute to the overall noise footprint of your project.
[ Environmental Compliance and Best Practices ](https://aesg.com/engineering/environmental-acoustic-assessments-eia/#collapse-203a7c16aa068e945396)
Our environmental compliance services ensure that your project adheres to local, national, and international noise regulations. We conduct assessments in line with guidelines such as the Environmental Noise Directive (END) and provide recommendations to meet environmental objectives.
[ Acoustic Risk Assessments for Health and Safety ](https://aesg.com/engineering/environmental-acoustic-assessments-eia/#collapse-06ae2e96aa068e945396)
Understanding the potential health risks associated with noise is vital for industrial and urban projects. We conduct acoustic risk assessments to determine exposure levels and ensure that both human health and operational integrity are safeguarded.
