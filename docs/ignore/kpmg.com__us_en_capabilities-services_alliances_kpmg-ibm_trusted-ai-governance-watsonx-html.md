# Trusted AI powered by watsonx.governance
Accelerating AI innovation with Trusted AI governance
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2025/new-model-ai-governance.pdf)
Share
As organizations increasingly turn to artificial intelligence (AI) to drive critical business decisions, three concerns become paramount: fostering compliance, securing data, and enhancing ethical AI practices—all while empowering innovation and building unshakeable trust.
KPMG LLP and IBM have joined forces to address these concerns. The KPMG Trusted AI framework, powered by the IBM watsonx portfolio, enables clients to monitor and govern their AI solutions effectively, reducing risk and making AI management more efficient.
IBM watsonx® is a portfolio of AI products designed to spur productivity and accelerate the impact of generative AI in core workflows. Watsonx.governance, part of this portfolio, is an end-to-end toolkit for AI governance that helps organizations manage, direct, and monitor their AI activities.
Together, the IBM watsonx portfolio and governance toolkit address the key challenges and benefits CEOs identify when implementing generative AI (GenAI) solutions.
**The power of the alliance partnership**
The collaboration between KPMG and IBM combines deep industry experience with advanced technology solutions, delivering a transformative approach to AI governance. Together, KPMG Trusted AI framework and IBM watsonx.governance can deliver:
  * Effective AI program and model lifecycle governance—Implementing robust frameworks to manage the end-to-end lifecycle with automation
  * Compliance and accountability—Establishing clear processes to adhere to regulatory and internal requirements
  * Scalability and innovation—Future-proofing the enterprise governance program to manage increasing data and user demands.


To learn more about IBM watsonx products, visit [IBM watsonx.governance](https://www.ibm.com/products/watsonx-governance)
