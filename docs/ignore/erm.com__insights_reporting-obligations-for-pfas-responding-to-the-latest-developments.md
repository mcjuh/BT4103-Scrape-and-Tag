# Reporting Obligations for PFAS: Responding to the Latest Developments
The PFAS reporting landscape is changing rapidly in the United States, with the latest federal developments delaying reporting and signaling intent to rewrite the rule. Despite the lack of clarity at the federal level, and proposed changes to EU rules, PFAS bans and restrictions continue to roll out from state legislatures, and global regulations still require significant investment in PFAS data collection and reporting. Now is the time to create resilient response strategies that support compliance and maintain market access critical to business success.
