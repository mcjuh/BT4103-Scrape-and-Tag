# Technology, Media and Telecom
Kroll provides innovative risk and finance solutions for the technology, media and telecom sectors.
For technology, media and telecom (TMT) companies, the pace of disruption has never been more intense. From breakthroughs in artificial intelligence to breakdowns in supply chains, from evolutions in e-commerce strategies to revolutions in semi-conductor development, the most successful TMT companies leverage sound solutions to maximize innovation.
Unprecedented innovation creates unprecedented risks. With over 100 years of valuation, investment banking, compliance and operational expertise, Kroll has advised TMT clients through eras of great disruption, unlocking and protecting value while navigating change. Our client-first approach combines industry expertise with cyber risk solutions to protect valuable data and intellectual property. Kroll is also the single-most experienced claims and noticing agent in the TMT sector.
Addressing critical risk, governance and strategy issues, Kroll’s team of professionals helps you tackle any challenge.
