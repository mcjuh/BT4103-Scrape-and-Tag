## How do modern managed services go beyond traditional outsourcing?
Modern managed services are a critical lever for cost reduction, resilience, and ongoing transformation. The [KPMG Managed Services Outlook 2026](https://kpmg.com/us/en/articles/2026/managed-services-outlook-survey-gated.html), with research and analysis by IDC, finds that 87 percent of US companies say managed services are highly integrated in their digital transformation strategy, with the share expecting high or extremely high value from these services set to grow to 71 percent in the next two years.
Long-standing approaches to service delivery can only take you so far. KPMG Managed Services propel the shift from transactional outsourcing to a strategic, modern business operations, integrating expanded capabilities to help you gain and sustain advantage. 
  * **Scope** : Covers knowledge-intensive processes, not just basic tasks, handled by highly skilled teams.
  * **Technology** : Uses sophisticated technologies like AI, automation, and advanced data to continually optimize performance.
  * **Model** : Delivered as an as-a-service subscription with predictable costs and clear, outcome-focused SLAs.
  * **Goal** : Designed to support ongoing transformation and create new value, not just reduce costs.

Modern managed services span front-, middle-, and back-office processes so you can transform customer-facing, decision-support, and operational functions in one model.
