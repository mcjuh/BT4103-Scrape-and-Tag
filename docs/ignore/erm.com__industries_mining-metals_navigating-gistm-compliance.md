#  Navigating GISTMCompliance
People. Planet. Performance. And a Plan.
[ Get the document ](https://www.erm.com/industries/mining-metals/navigating-gistm-compliance/#form "Get the document")
Tailings management just got a whole lot more serious—and smarter. With the August 2025 GISTM disclosure deadline looming, mining companies are under pressure to do more than tick boxes. They need to lead with integrity, resilience, and a rock-solid plan.
That’s where this guide comes in.
**“Navigating GISTM Compliance - Best Practice Guide”** is your high-impact roadmap to mastering the Global Industry Standard on Tailings Management. Whether you're a facility owner, ESG lead, or compliance champion, this report breaks down the complex into the actionable.
Inside, you’ll find:
  * How to turn internal audits into strategic power tools
  * Why human rights aren’t just a chapter—they’re the core
  * What it takes to build a living knowledge base (and ditch the dusty PDFs)
  * How climate change is rewriting the rules of tailings risk
  * The truth about legacy assets—and why closure is just the beginning
  * What disaster recovery really means (hint: switching focus to people might shift effectiveness!)


Plus, we go beyond compliance to explore what’s next: smarter tech, sustainable engineering, and a new generation of tailings thinkers.
**Download the guide** and discover how to move from reactive to resilient, from compliant to confident. Because in mining, the real legacy isn’t what you extract—it’s what you leave behind.
