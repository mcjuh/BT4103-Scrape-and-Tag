# Fabien Terraillot Joins Alvarez & Marsal’s Financial & Operational Restructuring Team in Paris


## _The new Managing Director brings more than 15 years of experience in managing high-impact industrial projects_
**Paris, 3 rd June 2025 – **Global professional services firm [Alvarez & Marsal](http://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/) (A&M) announces the hire of [**Fabien Terraillot**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/fabien-terraillot)as a Managing Director, effective immediately. The strategic appointment furthers the ongoing expansion of A&M’s [Financial & Operational Restructuring](https://emea-restructuring.alvarezandmarsal.com/) practice to meet growing demand for its turnaround expertise in France and across Europe.
Mr. Terraillot focuses on complex turnaround projects requiring operational, social and financial expertise, and specialises in social re-organisation and cash-excellence programmes. Working closely with Managing Directors [Thomas Marcorelles](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/thomas-marcorelles) and [Benoit Hochet](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/benoit-hochet), he supports companies facing stressed and distressed situations.
“A&M offers clients an independent, multi-disciplinary alternative to the current advisory market,” said Mr Terraillot. “I look forward to being part of its global, integrated network of professionals to deliver on the most complex local and cross-border projects.”
Bringing more than 15 years of experience in managing high-impact industrial projects, Mr. Terraillot has supported clients across sectors – from aeronautics, automotive, and healthcare to retail, technology, media, and telecommunications. .
He joins A&M from EY Parthenon, where he spent almost three years as a Partner. Before that he worked at Alix Partners and the French Ministry of Economy, including as a Ministerial adviser and restructuring case handler. He is an active member of the Association pour le Retournement des Entreprises (“ARE”), the restructuring practitioners’ primary professional association in France.
Mr. Marcorelles added: “Fabien’s arrival is a great addition to our Turnaround practice. Fabien brings a wealth of experience and strengthens even more our leadership position in that space of the French market.”
