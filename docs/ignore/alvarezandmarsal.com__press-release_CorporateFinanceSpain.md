# Alvarez & Marsal’s Corporate Finance Practice Expands to Spain
  * _Miguel Kindelán, technology investment banker of more than 25 years of experience, joins in Madrid to lead the growth of the business_


**Madrid, 1 st April, 2025 – **Global professional services firm [Alvarez & Marsal](http://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/) (A&M) has established the latest footprint of its [Corporate Finance](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/global-corporate-finance) practice in Madrid. Seasoned technology investment banker and mergers and acquisitions (M&A) expert [**Miguel Kindelán**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/miguel-kindelan) has been appointed Managing Director to lead and grow the new business unit, and to co-lead the technology M&A business in EMEA.
The launch builds on sustained growth of the firm’s Corporate Finance practice, which has recently made senior hires in [France](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/mark-wyatt), [Germany](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/press-release/CorporateFinanceGermany), [India](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/alvarez-marsal-expands-its-corporate-finance-practice-india-appoints-mohit-khullar-0), [Ireland](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/alvarez-marsal-opens-first-office-ireland-corporate-finance-appointment) and throughout the regions of the [UK](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/alvarez-marsal-hires-first-corporate-finance-managing-director-birmingham). The practice now counts on more than 110 experts across EMEA and 170 worldwide.
“I am excited to join A&M’s global network, channeling our collected expertise on behalf of our clients globally,” said Mr. Kindelán. “The deals market in Spain is full of potential, especially in the technology sector, and we have a real opportunity to create value by working closely with founders and investors.”
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Kindelán was the Head of Spain and Portugal Advisory at GP Bullhound. He also spent 18 years at Lazard advising clients across multiple sectors, including technology, telecommunications, transportation, and infrastructure, and working on high-profile restructuring deals in Spain and Latin America.
[Jonathan Boyers](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/jonathan-boyers), Managing Director and Head of A&M’s Corporate Finance practice in EMEA, commented: “Expanding to the Spanish market is a natural next step, where we see increased demand among founders, entrepreneurs, investors and boards for M&A strategy. Miguel’s industry experience will be crucial to help clients add value when selling their business, raising capital or acquiring assets.”
The news also comes as A&M continues its growth in Spain. The firm recently celebrated [a decade’s presence in the country](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/alvarez-marsal-marks-decade-growth-spain-addition-new-sector-expertise), marked a fivefold increase in headcount over the past five years, and [added Tax, Insurance and Corporate Transformation advisory to its suite of services](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/global-locations/spain#gl_services).
[Cristina Almeida](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/cristina-almeida), Managing Director and Co-Head of A&M Spain, added: “While A&M Spain continues its growth – having become the firm’s fourth-largest presence in Europe – our commitment to meeting our clients’ needs remains. Diversifying our services into Corporate Finance, and attracting leaders of Miguel’s standing and expertise, is critical to this mission.”
