### Our work with clients, stakeholders, and partners
The projects we work on can range from small desk-based research projects, to large consortia frameworks, to long-term programmes that can span several countries, last many years, and have complex technical and project management requirements.
We apply our expert knowledge and experience across this wide range of projects, working collaboratively with our clients, stakeholders, and partners, and tailoring our approach to the specific requirements of the project design. Our responsive approach also enables us to adapt to needs of our government clients, funders, and other stakeholders that may emerge during the course of a project.
