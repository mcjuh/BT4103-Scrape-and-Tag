###  Asset management
From fund launch to wind-down, asset managers are faced with intensifying cross-border complexity defined by regulatory scrutiny and shifting investor expectations. Against a backdrop of evolving ESG priorities and transparency requirements, the KPMG Law asset management practice helps shape legal frameworks that are both technically sound and commercially viable.

Across asset classes and jurisdictions, our guidance helps asset managers manage risk, meet regulatory expectations and pursue their growth objectives.
