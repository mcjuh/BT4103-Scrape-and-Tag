## Learning Strategy and Transformation
### Build a modern learning strategy and build your future.
The way we work has a significant impact on the way we learn. Traditional operating models and learning strategies are failing to deliver consumer-grade, empathetic, and data-driven experiences that delivers on long-term organizational transformation goals while meeting short-term upskilling needs. To keep pace with business and workforce expectations, learning organizations are rethinking their learning strategies.
Developing a learning strategy takes more than implementing technology, filling out a form, or providing one-off sessions. A strategy grounded in the skills of the future, employee preferences, and rich analytics is the foundation of a digitalized and modernized program that takes into consideration the learning technology stack, upskilling and content priorities, and operational efficiencies to deliver differentiated learning experiences that can be decisive in attracting and retaining top talent.
In a world of increasing automation, AI, and chatbots, KPMG LLP (KPMG) can guide you toward building a sustainable and impactful learning and development organization.
### How KPMG can help
KPMG works with organizations to design and deliver learning strategies that are personal to the learner, strategic to the business, and scalable to the organization. Our goal is to enable your people to adopt skillsets at an appropriate rate of change and in the flow of work.
As a trusted advisor and digital transformation leader, we leverage tested approaches and the latest innovations in our Ignition Centers. To start, we engage you in an immersive, curated design-thinking session to develop learning strategies that are rooted in learner empathy and experience. We then test the proofs of concept and measure their impact using data analytics. By doing so, we help you build a business case to drive leadership buy-in and scale the learning strategy across the organization.
Let us help equip your teams with the right skills, knowledge, and competencies for the future. View our case studies to see how KPMG has helped organizations across various industries with their learning transformations.
## Learning Managed Services
### A learning modernization study tailored to you
The KPMG experienced learning modernization team collaborates with your learning and business leaders to conduct a learning modernization study unique to your organization. The result is an optimized learning experience that uses leading tools and techniques across automation, AI, and digitalization. The study will identify ways to reduce overall spend, improve operational quality and efficiency, and introduce innovative learning elements with the following objectives:
  * Identify ways to optimize and innovate overall learning operations
  * Automate LMS administration and instructor management using AI
  * Improve instructor utilization and effectiveness
  * Reduce travel expenses
  * Identify ways to optimize vendor management models and reduce spend
  * Optimize content design and development using AI
  * Provide a business case for optimization with guaranteed cost savings
  * Present the business case for a variable supplemental instructor pool
  * Provide analytics model to leverage your learning data
  * Reduce overall costs by an average of 30 percent


### A business-first approach
Our technology-agnostic approach is based on the idea that business processes and operating models should drive learning solutions, rather than the other way around. Technology, however, plays a significant role in our approach. Using data and analytics, we help learning organizations increase engagement and streamline operations.
The KPMG suite of services can be plugged into existing training ecosystems to bring learning into the flow of work. These services can help transform your organization through a focus on learner centricity, organizational capabilities, and operational efficiency. 
### The case for progress
KPMG can help support your business case through our learning modernization study. The resulting business case can assist you with defining the value your organization can expect to receive from implementing LaaS. Using our Learning Maturity Model, we will take a deep dive into your organization by reviewing six functional areas across four dimensions.
As a result of our rapid analysis, you will walk away with your business case and roadmap for implementing LaaS.
