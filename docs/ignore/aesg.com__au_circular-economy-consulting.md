At AESG Australia, we provide Circular Economy Consulting services designed to optimise the reuse of materials and minimise waste throughout the construction supply chain. Our strategies focus on creating resource-efficient solutions that reduce embodied carbon emissions and promote sustainability across the Australian construction industry.
## Why Circular Economy Consulting Matters in Australia
Adopting a circular economy is vital for reducing embodied carbon and addressing supply chain challenges in Australia’s construction sector. With growing environmental regulations and market demand for sustainable practices, organisations are seeking ways to minimise waste, improve resource efficiency, and transition towards a circular economy model. Key Drivers for Circular Economy Practices in Australia:
### National Waste Policy Action Plan 2019
Australia’s commitment to waste reduction and resource recovery.
### State-Level Initiatives
Policies such as the NSW Circular Economy Policy Statement and Victoria’s Recycling Victoria policy.
### Sustainability Goals
Aligning with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), particularly SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production).
