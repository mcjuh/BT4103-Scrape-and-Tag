##### Aspire brings together the unique experiences, perspectives, and cultures of our Asian, Asian American, Native Hawaiians, and Pacific Islander team members, allies, and those interested to learn more to attain new heights.
## Mission
To connect, cultivate, and empower a community of Asian, Asian American, and Pacific Islander (API) team members.
## Objectives
  * Raise awareness and celebrate cultural heritage. 
  * Drive internal connection and collaboration globally at Slalom.
  * Create a sense of belonging. 


> I wholeheartedly love being Asian! I really love the community and connection we have and how easily it is to relate to others even if they are from a different country.
**Paulina Le** Senior Engineer, Charlotte
> Celebrating the culture, traditions, and history of Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders has always been extremely important to me.
**Brenda Yoo-Young** Managing Director, Seattle
> My ultimate goal in this lifetime is to contribute to laying the foundation for fostering healing and empathetic growth within our community, even if it's just a small step forward.
**Tiffany Shen** Consultant, Seattle
