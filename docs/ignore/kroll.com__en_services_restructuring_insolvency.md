# Insolvency
Maximizing recoveries for complex cross-border insolvencies and enforcements.
Kroll delivers insolvency and enforcement solutions that prioritize business continuity, preserve value, and maximize creditor recoveries. As liquidators, administrators, receivers, and bankruptcy trustees, we take swift and decisive action to safeguard, stabilize, and monetize assets while pursuing recoveries through complex litigation on behalf of creditors.
With licensed professionals on the ground across all major developed, offshore, and emerging markets, we seamlessly navigate complex insolvency cases across jurisdictions. Our expertise includes pursuing recoveries through litigation, investigating governance failures, and resolving disputes and claims—all with a focus on delivering optimal financial outcomes for stakeholders.
