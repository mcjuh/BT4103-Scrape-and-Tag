## Unlock the power of your data to streamline your portfolio operations
Get extensive insights into portfolios, resources and budgets. Our solution can help drive portfolio delivery, identify operational needs, address portfolio constraints, and improve your pipeline performance.
## Get a thorough view of your portfolio by rapidly ingesting and integrating your data
Integrate data from various sources and formats into our platform, regardless of your R&D environment. Our proprietary ontology maps your data model, freeing you to focus on gaining valuable insights.
## Generate predictive resource and financial insights with our forecasting engine
Leverage our machine learning-powered forecasting engine to gain predictive insights into resource and financial needs, supporting both headcount and direct spend for your portfolio strategy.
## Enhance design-making through GenAI-tailored visualizations
Experience immersive data visualization with GenAI. Quickly surface and dive into critical portfolio insights. Our GenAI engine can provide tailored insights, and our R&D AI chatbot can promptly answer questions.
## Rapidly model portfolio scenarios to support strategic portfolio decisions
Strategic simulations: Using advanced AI and machine learning models, our platform enables scenario planning to help leaders visualize and evaluate potential scenarios and trade-offs of their R&D portfolio. By simulating different variables such as budget adjustments, timeline shifts, and resource reallocation, leaders can make more informed decisions.
## Strategic collaboration: PwC and AWS
Our alliance with AWS merges PwC's deep industry experience with the latest in cloud technology, revolutionizing R&D operations for pharmaceutical companies. AWS’s robust and scalable infrastructure helps establish that our solutions are not only secure but also remain at the cutting edge of innovation. This powerful combination enables us to provide predictive insights and create immersive data experiences that are crucial for strategic decision-making and operational success in the pharmaceutical sector.
1. Connect and standardize data across your R&D organization 
PwC’s R&D Operations Platform can quickly and easily ingest your R&D data with our flexible data model. It can also seamlessly integrate additional data sources, such as custom portfolio prioritization and strategic goal data, to meet your specific needs. Our R&D business and technology professionals can work with you to identify and standardize data aligned with your business requirements.
2. Drive insights-driven R&D portfolio decision making
After configuring data and user-facing views, PwC collaboratively works with you to develop a business launch, change management, and user support strategy. This helps deliver a smooth platform launch for your wide range of end users, from R&D leaders to functional and operational teams. PwC helps both business users and the platform's technical back-end, enhancing your resources to drive business adoption of the platform.
3. Enable transparency and information exchange between leaders 
Enhance transparency across your R&D portfolio with our platform, which provides user persona-specific views to contextualize relevant information. Aggregate and synthesize data at the desired level, allowing users to spend less time searching and scrutinizing data and more time making informed decisions.
4. Empower stakeholders to predict resource and budget needs
The modular PwC R&D Operations Platform can help you meet your organization's needs, including R&D portfolio reporting, parametric-based resource and financial forecasting models and portfolio scenario models. PwC’s R&D business professionals can work with you to configure forecast models for each relevant R&D function and role so that they align with your business practices.
5. Scale your R&D insights to serve more users and needs
Extend the platform's utility to various use cases with guidance from PwC’s business and technology professionals. We work with you to collect and incorporate business user feedback to drive the platform's evolution and expansion, meeting your growing needs.

#### Forecast and analyze demand for non-clinical functions
Over a twelve-week period, we created a tailored forecasting solution for Development, Regulatory, and Medical Affairs roles, which enabled portfolio analytics.
#### Major systems upgrade to refresh and maintain forecasting capabilities
In twelve weeks, we migrated and enhanced our client’s resource algorithms and enabled a new suite of resource and portfolio-level analytics.

For over twenty years, PwC has delivered strategic, advisory and implementation services to R&D organizations, successfully enabling R&D operational planning capabilities. Our extensive experience supporting R&D operations across the R&D lifecycle allows our teams to help clients rapidly deliver solutions and generate insights through the platform, tailored to your strategic objectives and organizational needs.
