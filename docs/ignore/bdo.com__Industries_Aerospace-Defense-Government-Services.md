# Aerospace, Defense & Government Services
## Driving growth for reinvestment and fair market returns for shareholders.
Many government contractors are faced with increased competition and pressures from changes in regulatory priorities with each new administration.
The industry is also seeing a shrinking pool of clearance, or clearance-eligible, employees. These factors have led to a growth in ESOP transactions within the Aerospace, Defense & Government Services industry and created the need for a sophisticated level of expertise among ESOP advisors. 
While ESOPs are common in the Aerospace, Defense & Government Services space, there is no one common structure that achieves all stakeholders’ goals. Engaging the right financial advisor is critical in such a complex and highly regulated industry. Our team’s deep and extensive history as advisor to many complex M&A transactions allows us to work with clients to structure deals that meet the goals of the seller and the company.
