#### Transform asset management with data-driven strategies that enhance collaboration, agility, and innovation for a competitive edge.
Read more  Read less 
When I first entered the world of asset management, it became clear that data was both our most powerful asset and our most complex challenge. Siloed systems, fragmented insights, and legacy infrastructure often stood in the way of delivering the seamless, data-driven experiences clients now expect. But that’s changing - rapidly.
Read more  Read less 
How EY can help 
  * [ EY-Snowflake Alliance ](https://www.ey.com/en_au/alliances/snowflake)
Discover how the EY-Snowflake Alliance helps companies seamlessly transform their data into business value. Learn more.

In our latest whitepaper, “[Transforming asset management with data (pdf)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-gl/insights/financial-servicesemeia/documents/ey-gl-ebook-transforming-asset-management-with-data-design-considerations-for-enterprise-data-strategy-08-2025.pdf)”, developed in collaboration with Snowflake, we explore how the industry is undergoing a profound transformation. The catalyst? A new breed of enterprise data strategies that prioritize secure data sharing and marketplace models. 
What’s most exciting is how these innovations are finally dismantling the long-standing barriers to collaboration and agility. Secure data sharing eliminates internal silos, enabling real-time connections with partners, clients, and platforms - unlocking insights that were once out of reach. Meanwhile, marketplace models are opening new avenues to access and monetize data in ways that were unimaginable just a few years ago. 
Yet this transformation goes beyond technology. It requires a fundamental shift in how firms design and execute their data strategies — from aligning architecture with business goals to embedding governance and compliance at every level. Just as importantly, it calls for a culture that treats data as a strategic asset, not just an operational tool.
The whitepaper dives into these design considerations in detail, offering practical guidance for firms looking to modernize their approach. Whether you’re just beginning your data journey or looking to scale your capabilities, the insights shared by EY and Snowflake teams provide a roadmap for success.
The most powerful takeaway? The future of asset management will be shaped by those who can harness data not just to inform decisions, but to drive innovation. With the right strategy in place, that future is well within reach.
Transforming asset management with data: design considerations for an enterprise data strategy

#### Summary
The whitepaper Transforming Asset Management with Data, created in collaboration with Snowflake and EY, highlights the industry's shift toward innovative enterprise data strategies. By prioritizing secure data sharing and marketplace models, firms can break down silos and enhance collaboration. This transformation requires a cultural shift to view data as a strategic asset and align data strategies with business goals. The insights provided serve as a roadmap for firms at any stage of their data journey, emphasizing that the future of asset management will be defined by those who leverage data to drive innovation and informed decision-making.
