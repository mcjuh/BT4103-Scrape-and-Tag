## Why Carbon Accounting and Reporting is Essential
As global regulations around greenhouse gas emissions tighten, businesses are increasingly required to disclose their carbon emissions and take steps towards reducing them. Carbon accounting provides the foundation for developing effective strategies to manage emissions, ensuring compliance with environmental standards and improving operational efficiency. Accurately measuring carbon emissions is not just about regulatory compliance, but about identifying opportunities for cost savings, enhancing corporate reputation, and contributing to long-term sustainability.
Our services support organisations in understanding their emissions across all operational activities and supply chains, helping you take a proactive role in reducing your environmental footprint. This insight is key to achieving net zero targets and meeting the expectations of stakeholders, investors, and regulators.
## Our Approach
At AESG, we work closely with your team to deliver accurate, data-driven carbon accounting and reporting. Our approach ensures compliance with the Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol and other international frameworks, providing a clear understanding of your carbon footprint and actionable insights for improvement. From initial carbon footprint assessments to the creation of long-term net zero strategies, our team is committed to supporting your organisation at every stage of its sustainability journey.
