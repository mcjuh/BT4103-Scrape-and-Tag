# Webinar: Smarter, Faster Healthcare with Databricks Genie
Cameron Cross and Brett MacLaren share how West Monroe, Databricks, and CareSource use Genie to make faster, data-driven decisions
April 15, 2026
Social Share
Healthcare organizations have more data than ever—but too often, the people who need it most can’t access insights quickly enough to make informed decisions.
In this webinar, West Monroe, [Databricks](https://www.westmonroe.com/partnerships/databricks), and CareSource explore how Databricks Genie is helping healthcare teams close that gap. By allowing users to ask questions in plain language and receive real-time answers from trusted enterprise data, Genie makes analytics more accessible across the organization—without requiring SQL expertise or reliance on static dashboards.
The discussion offers a practical look at how healthcare organizations can improve decision-making, reduce bottlenecks, and empower teams to use data more confidently in their day-to-day work.
