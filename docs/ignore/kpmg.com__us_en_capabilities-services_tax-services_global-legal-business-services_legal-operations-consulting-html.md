# KPMG Legal Operations Consulting
Increase the value your legal function delivers
##  Want to align your legal and business priorities to create real value? 
The KPMG Legal Operations Consulting practice helps legal teams navigate today’s complex and challenging environment through a strategic approach to people, processes, data, and technology.
## Integrated capabilities for legal operations consulting 
KPMG Legal Operations Consulting works in concert with our **[Legal Technology Innovation](https://kpmg.com/us/en/capabilities-services/tax-services/global-legal-business-services/legal-technology-innovation.html)** and [**Legal Managed Services**](https://kpmg.com/us/en/capabilities-services/tax-services/global-legal-business-services/kpmg-legal-managed-services.html) capabilities to deliver a thorough approach for your legal function transformation. 
Assessment and operating model design
Assess your current legal operations against leading practices to design a target operating model that aligns people, processes, and technology to business priorities. This covers organizational design, work allocation, talent structure, contracting, and performance metrics that drive accountability and transparency.
Process improvement and governance
Stabilizes daily delivery, streamlines workflows, and establishes governance to run routine work through efficient, repeatable processes. Supported by lawyer-centric change management to drive adoption across legal and the business—resulting in a more predictable function and freeing teams to focus on strategic counsel, complex risk, and critical enterprise decisions.
Technology, data, and AI enablement
Legal technology, data, and AI drive measurable impact through strategy, solution selection, and change enablement. This ensures tools align to legal use cases, integrate with enterprise systems, and achieve adoption at scale—prioritizing high-value generative and agentic AI, strengthening data readiness, and supporting end-to-end implementation.
## Addressing legal technology challenges across your legal operating model
We help legal teams overcome operational, strategic, and technology challenges to unlock measurable value from their legal function. 
#### Your challenges
  * Demand outpacing capacity and limiting strategic impact, creating a need to improve service quality while reducing overall costs
  * Misalignment between legal team’s ideal and actual roles in solving business problems causing change friction
  * Difficulty defining, measuring, and communicating the legal team’s value and risk posture in business terms
  * Limited data and visibility into legal work, spend, cycle times, and risk
  * Fragmented legal and contracting processes across geographies, functions, and business units
  * Technology and knowledge investments not aligned to priority use cases or not adopted consistently


#### Our solutions
  * A clear, data-driven legal operating model that scales with business growth
  * Enhanced processes that reduce cycle times and boost consistency
  * Technology, data, and AI strategy and roadmap aligned to legal and enterprise priorities
  * A governance model that strengthens accountability and transparency
  * Better allocation of work so teams focus on strategic activities
  * A modern legal function with enhancements in cost, performance, and risk management

#### Legal operations FAQs 
**Q: What does a modern legal operating model look like?** A leading legal operating model is structured to improve transparency, accountability, and scalability by aligning legal talent, workflows, technology, governance and metrics to enterprise and legal priorities. 
**Q: How can legal operations improve efficiency in the legal department?** Legal operations can improve efficiency and service delivery by standardizing workflows, clarifying work allocation, strengthening governance, strengthening data visibility and aligning technology to high-value legal use cases.
**Q: How do legal leaders choose the right technology for their department?** Legal leaders choose the right technology by assessing business and legal requirements, prioritizing use cases, and selecting solutions that integrate with enterprise systems and support adoption.
