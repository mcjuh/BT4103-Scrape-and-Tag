# EU Policy Regulation Alert - On your marks
The EU’s mission to simplify its sustainability regulations has reached its final stage—and the results are clear: less complexity, fewer companies in scope, and more freedom for companies to focus on the material risks and opportunities relevant to their business.
[ download policy alert ](https://www.erm.com/insights/eu-policy-regulation-alert-on-your-marks/#csrdform "download policy alert")
Companies can no longer hit pause. Our latest policy alert breaks down the most important updates across the EU regulatory landscape and what they mean for businesses. 
It also includes in-depth recommendations for next steps and how to leverage simplified frameworks for commercial gain, as well as ways to prepare digital systems, assurance, and communication to support these efforts. 
The update delves into the following regulations: 
  * CSRD/ESRS 
  * CSDDD 
  * EUDR 
  * CBAM 
  * SFDR 2.0 


Download the full policy alert to explore what’s changing—and how your organization can get ahead.
