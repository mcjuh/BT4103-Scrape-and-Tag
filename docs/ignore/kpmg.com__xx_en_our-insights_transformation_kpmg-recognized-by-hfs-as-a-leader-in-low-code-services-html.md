We are excited to share that KPMG has been named a Leader in the HFS Horizons report: Low-Code Services, 2023.
The report states “KPMG brings a low-code led mentality to business transformation that incorporates automation, agility, and assurance across a modernized application architecture.”
We are proud of the commitment KPMG member firms have demonstrated in building leading low-code practices focused on empowering clients with software models that can convert legacy software architecture into more adaptable business platforms.
The report notes: “KPMG’s low-code solution is based on a business-led transformation need to empower employees with the tools they need while shedding legacy IT-centric approaches.”
KPMG’s Low-Code Center of Excellence, working with the Data & Analytics and Emerging Technology Center of Excellence, KPMG Global Lighthouse, helps member firm clients reach their low-code goals, building the future of application development and automation. KPMG professionals work with clients to utilize low-code platforms to speed up the creation of sophisticated enterprise-class applications that incorporate complex business logic, automate workflow and case management activities, integrate with existing information systems, and enable a slick user experience.
This helps to create scalable business applications in response to changing business demands, more important than ever in today’s digital first environment – helping to accelerate enterprise modernization, agility, and efficiency.
According to the report, “KPMG helped a global bank rearchitect its legacy software applications using low-code tools to replace nearly all the custom-build solutions with packaged SaaS and composable applications and used low-code to provide workflow integrations and microservices tailored to the functionality needed by employees.”
“The Appian Partner Award recognized KPMG’s outstanding results in global strategic program delivery on the Appian Low-Code Platform. KPMG pushes the boundaries of low-code to innovate and help customers rapidly unlock value.”
_KPMG firms bring the power of their network to help clients enable their strategy by harnessing the Connected. Powered. Trusted. Elevate. set of technology solutions to enable enterprise and functional transformation, laser focused on value creation and the delivery of differentiated customer experiences. We believe that our approach, technology solutions, and commitment to clients set us apart._
HFS analysis concludes that “KPMG offers its low-code solution (Sofy) for governance. It brings domain knowledge to provide a combined software development and deployment model for converting legacy software architectures into more adaptable to business platforms.”
