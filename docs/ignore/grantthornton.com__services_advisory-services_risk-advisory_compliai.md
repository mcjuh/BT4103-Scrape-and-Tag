## Value you see from Day 1
  1. Day 1
  2. Early cycle
  3. Midcycle
  4. Mature state


Day 1: Standardized procedures, automatically executed
CompliAI launches engagements with standardized, methodology‑aligned work programs already in place. Core procedures — walkthroughs, testing steps and evidence requests — are instantiated efficiently, eliminating manual setup and reducing variation across teams from the start.
Early cycle: AI‑generated testing and documentation
As execution begins, CompliAI applies AI agents to perform defined testing procedures, analyze evidence and generate documented test results directly within the work program. Supporting narratives, annotations and workpapers are produced as part of execution — ready for review and professional judgment without recreating documentation offline.
Midcycle: Reuse instead of rebuild
Approved procedures, testing logic and AI‑generated documentation can be reused across entities, processes and reporting cycles. Teams carry forward validated work programs and results, accelerating execution while maintaining consistency and audit readiness.
Mature state: Predictable, repeatable SOX execution
Over time, CompliAI enables a repeatable operating model where standardized procedures, AI‑assisted execution and reusable documentation materially reduce rework, shorten cycle times and improve consistency across complex, multi‑entity environments — without sacrificing judgment or control quality.

## Coverage across the SOX lifecycle
Designed to support both executive oversight and day-to-day SOX execution as complexity grows
End-to-end coverage built on standardized execution
CompliAI supports the full SOX lifecycle — from planning and walkthroughs through testing, reporting and governance — using standardized, reusable work programs that scale across entities and cycles.
Planning and design
Standardized procedures and templates are deployed consistently across in‑scope processes and entities, creating a common execution baseline while allowing risk‑based tailoring where needed.
Operating effectiveness testing
AI agents execute defined testing procedures, evaluate evidence and generate documented results within the work program. Outputs remain fully traceable and reviewable, supporting internal quality standards and external audit reliance.
Reporting and governance
AI‑generated testing results and standardized documentation flow directly into reporting and governance activities, supporting transparency, review and audit readiness while reinforcing strong AI governance and methodology adherence.
Scales across environments
Whether applied to a single entity or a decentralized global program, CompliAI enables consistent execution through reusable work programs and AI‑generated documentation — driving predictability across cycles without re‑creating work.
