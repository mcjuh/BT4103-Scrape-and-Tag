# Industry Solutions
KPMG Industry solutions enabled by Oracle
KPMG is an award-winning Oracle alliance partner with more than 30 years of experience bringing balanced approach of business strategy, industry-specific knowledge, and technology to our clients. Together with Oracle, we have successfully deployed more than 350 customers on Oracle Cloud.
KPMG Industry solutions enabled by Oracle is the foundation that delivers pre-built industry-specific operating models, processes, methodologies, and technologies that enable organizations to modernize and integrate their front, middle, and back-office operations to drive accelerated digital transformation for Finance and Enterprise Performance Management, Supply Chain Management, Customer Experience, Human Resources, and Data and Analytics.
Our Oracle solutions are further enhanced by the power of Artificial Intelligence (AI), enabling organizations to move from reactive reporting to proactive, predictive insights. By integrating KPMG's industry-specific AI models into Oracle Cloud, we help businesses anticipate market shifts, create dynamically personalized customer journeys, and transform their operations with intelligent automation.
Embarking on a cloud transformation journey with KPMG delivers value, drives sustainable change, and accelerates innovation.
