## Insights of the Quarter
Have you ever considered how the Subcontractor Portal could streamline your operations? Unanet's Subcontractor Portal is designed to simplify and expedite the management of your subcontractor workforce, from onboarding to invoicing, thereby strengthening the relationship between Prime contractors and their Subcontractors. By leveraging the Subcontractor Portal, you can introduce significant efficiencies into your management processes:
  * Accelerates the transition from labor incurred to invoicing and revenue recognition.
  * Minimizes manual tasks and reduces the need for manual adjustments.
  * Lowers accrual amounts, saving time on reconciliation.
  * Enhances project execution with real-time visibility and control.
  * Allows assignment of subcontractor employees to specific projects with controlled authorized hours.
  * Centralizes subcontractor time and expense tracking.
  * Boosts enterprise-wide visibility of information.
  * Optimizes utilization, resources, funding, cash flow, revenue, and profit.


Our team has deep expertise in this area and is ready to help you enhance your subcontractor management capabilities. For any questions or additional information about the Subcontractor Portal in Unanet, please contact Stephanie Lianos.
