## Bodhi FAQs 
What leaders want to understand before committing Bodhi to the enterprise.
What is Sapient Bodhi?
Unlike generic agent frameworks, Bodhi is an enterprise agentic AI platform engineered to put agents into production. They're pre-built with a shared context layer that governs how they reason and act inside complex systems. Bodhi embeds business rules, workflows and controls directly into every agent so your teams can act faster and see value sooner, all without cloud or data lock-in. Agents coordinate across functions, execute multi-step work and stay adaptable over time so that AI can evolve with your business and keep working for you.
How is Bodhi different from AI copilots or task-based agents?
Copilots assist individuals. Bodhi orchestrates agents across enterprise workflows. While most AI tools focus on isolated tasks like writing code or generating content, Bodhi coordinates multiple agents that plan, act and collaborate across systems and teams. This makes it possible to automate end-to-end business processes, not just individual steps.
Can Bodhi handle complex, multi-step enterprise tasks?
Bodhi Deep Research ranked #3 on the Hugging Face DeepResearch Bench Leaderboard, confirming that Sapient Bodhi performs among the best AI agents in advanced, multi-step reasoning and analysis.
