#  Audit 
Audits are the fundamental building blocks of effective capital markets.
EY auditors use their knowledge, skills and experience to deliver high-quality audits with independence, integrity, objectivity and professional skepticism. As part of the All in strategy, the EY organization’s multi-billion-dollar commitment to audit quality, technology and people is making a fundamental shift toward AI-transformed audits, alongside a modernized audit approach.

EY Assurance isn’t waiting – it’s shaping the future with confidence. By combining top talent with innovative technology, a continuous improvement mindset and a streamlined audit methodology, EY is redefining what it means to bring assurance to the capital markets. With quality as the foundation, EY is elevating the audit experience for EY people and clients.

Future of Audit. Now. EY Audit teams are supported by a modern approach, streamlined process, and a single, global Assurance technology platform that has been augmented with agentic AI. 
Modern approachProviding clear audit methodology focused on risk assessment and confidence in the audit
Streamlined process Embedding specialized teams to facilitate industry knowledge and smarter delivery
AI-powered platform Facilitating guided workflows, advanced analytics and accelerating use of AI at scale
This transformation includes an ongoing worldwide structured training program to upskill all global audit and technology risk professionals in line with developments to auditing standards, regulation and technology — including the responsible development and use of AI.
The EY organization’s modernized audit methodology and frameworks support greater depth, relevance and confidence in the audit.
