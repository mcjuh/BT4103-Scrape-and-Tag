#  Personalising the student experience 
How university leaders are ‘making a difference’ to the student experience.
[Download the reportopens in a new tab](https://kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/uk/pdf/2026/07/personalising-the-student-experience.pdf)
Student expectations of their university experience are changing. To keep up with the first and future generations of digital natives, experiences need to be tailored to the individual, informed by AI and real time data. No longer are universities able to segment students based on broad categories, they must analyse individual behaviours in real time and use this to deliver impactful personalised experiences across the student lifecycle.
Download the full report to explore key findings from our global survey of more than 400 university leaders and students, and learn how institutions are delivering personalised student experiences.

###  93% of universities are already using AI 
Adoption is accelerating rapidly, with **93 percent** of universities already using AI to personalise student experiences —positioning AI as a key enabler of scale, but not a substitute for strategy, governance and design.
###  58% of university leaders believe AI will transform how universities operate and students learn 
Adaptive learning platforms, virtual tutors and early alert systems tailor content and interventions to individual needs, helping students stay on track academically.
###  66% of university leaders say that personalising the student experience is a high priority 
As personalisation moves from aspiration to expectation, the challenge for many universities is no longer whether to act, but how to act and at what scale.

> Universities struggle to keep up with changing student expectations. The sector needs to prepare for future generations-Generation Beta-who will be ‘AI naturals’ with AI being integral to their upbringing. Globally, university leaders recognise the impact AI will have on teaching and learning and how they operate, and understand the need to prioritise the student experience at scale. They need to break down workforce silos to re-design the end to end student journey, ensuring they deliver seamless, personalised, digital first experiences. No small task to keep up with the pace of change, but in order to grow and differentiate in a highly competitive market it is essential to invest in the student experience.

## 5 key takeaways
  1. ###### Treat student experience as a whole-of-university strategy
It must span teaching and learning, services, data and governance—not sit within a single function.
  2. ###### Focus on outcomes, not activity
Leading universities align investments to measurable student and institutional value.
  3. ###### Use AI to understand the student
AI enables better decisions at scale, enabling institutions to hyper-personalise student experiences.
  4. ###### Be deliberate about where to personalise
Prioritise high-impact moments across the student lifecycle.
  5. ###### Address culture, and ways of working
A lack of governance and siloed ways of working are often the biggest barriers to scaling personalisation.
