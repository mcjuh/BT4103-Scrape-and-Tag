## What is ISO 9001?
ISO 9001 sets out a structured framework for managing quality across an organization. The standard is based on key management principles, including customer focus, leadership engagement, a process-based approach, risk-based thinking, and continual improvement.
Organizations that implement a quality management system aligned with ISO 9001 can improve internal consistency, reduce errors and inefficiencies, and strengthen governance across processes and functions.
Because ISO 9001 is internationally recognized, certification is widely used to demonstrate credibility and reliability in domestic and global markets.

## Frequently Asked Questions
##  Is ISO 9001 certification mandatory?
ISO 9001 certification is voluntary. However, it is often required by customers, supply chains, or public tenders, and is widely used to demonstrate quality management capability.
##  Who can be certified to ISO 9001?
Any organization, regardless of size or sector, can be certified to ISO 9001 if it meets the standard’s requirements.
##  How long does ISO 9001 certification last?
Certification is typically valid for three years, with surveillance audits conducted during the cycle and a recertification audit required at the end of the cycle.
##  What does the ISO 9001 certification process involve?
The process includes defining the certification scope, a Stage 1 readiness audit, a Stage 2 implementation audit, an independent certification decision, and ongoing surveillance audits.
##  Does ISO 9001 certification guarantee quality outcomes?
Certification does not guarantee specific products, services, or outcomes. It provides assurance that a structured quality management system is in place and operating in line with ISO 9001 requirements.
##  Can ISO 9001 be integrated with other ISO standards?
Yes. ISO 9001 can be integrated with other management system standards such as ISO 14001, ISO 45001, ISO 50001, or ISO/IEC 27001.
##  Can an existing ISO 9001 certificate be transferred to ERM CVS?
In many cases, yes. Certificates issued by other accredited certification bodies may be transferred, subject to review in accordance with accreditation requirements.
##  What is ERM CVS’s role in ISO 9001 certification?
ERM CVS acts as an independent certification body, assessing conformity against ISO 9001 requirements and making impartial certification decisions in line with accreditation rules.
##  What is the ISO 9001 Certification Process?
Certification to ISO 9001 through ERM CVS follows a structured and transparent process aligned with international accreditation requirements.
  1. **Application and Scope Definition** : Definition of certification scope, organizational boundaries, and applicable activities.
  2. **Stage 1 Audit** : Review of management system documentation and readiness for certification.
  3. **Stage 2 Audit** : Assessment of implementation and effectiveness of the quality management system.
  4. **Certification Decision** : Independent technical review and certification decision.
  5. **Surveillance Audits** : Periodic audits to verify continued conformity during the certification cycle.
  6. **Recertification:** Full reassessment at the end of the certification cycle.


##  What are the changes expected for ISO 9001:2026?
The revision of the ISO 9001 standard is underway and is just at draft stage of the process so still subject to change, the Final Draft International Standard (FDIS) is expected in early 2026 and this will provide more certainty on the expected changes prior to final publication expected later in 2026.
