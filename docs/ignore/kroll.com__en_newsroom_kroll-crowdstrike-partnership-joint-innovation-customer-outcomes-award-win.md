# Kroll Accelerates CrowdStrike Partnership with Joint Innovation, Customer Outcomes and Award Win
## Key Takeaways
  * _Kroll joins CrowdStrike’s Project QuiltWorks alongside leading partners_
  * _Kroll partners with CrowdStrike on Charlotte AI AgentWorks Ecosystem and CrowdStrike Falcon® Flex for Services_
  * _Kroll named CrowdStrike’s 2026 Americas MSSP Partner of the Year_


New York, NY – [Kroll](https://www.kroll.com/en/), the leading independent provider of global financial and risk advisory solutions, announced today the recent milestones achieved during the first 150 days of its strategic partnership with [CrowdStrike](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/) and its latest recognition as CrowdStrike’s 2026 Americas MSSP Partner of the Year. Kroll clients utilizing the [CrowdStrike Falcon® platform](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/platform/) are seeing substantial reductions in incident recovery times, operational overhead and are gaining access to joint innovation initiatives, such as [Project QuiltWorks](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/partner-program/project-quiltworks/).
Dave Burg, Global Group Head of Cyber and Data Resilience at Kroll says, "Our strategic partnership with CrowdStrike is designed to deliver immediate improvements in cyber resilience while positioning our customers for what’s ahead. Kroll’s deep involvement in initiatives such as Project QuiltWorks and the Charlotte AI AgentWorks Ecosystem, addresses the evolving nature of cyber risk at its root. Bringing Kroll’s deep advisory and response expertise to these market leading technologies and initiatives will set customers up for a more resilient future, backed by proven leaders in the cyber industry.”
## Kroll and CrowdStrike Joint Innovation Projects
Kroll, CrowdStrike and a groundbreaking coalition of top technology partners have launched Project QuiltWorks in response to the sharp rise in attacks exploiting AI-discovered vulnerabilities. According to Kroll research, these attacks are [now linked to over 90% of reported cyber incidents](https://www.kroll.com/en/publications/cyber/state-of-cyber-resilience-2026). It brings together expertise in incident response, AI and vulnerability testing to rapidly identify and remediate security gaps in production code. Project QuiltWorks aims to set a new standard for vulnerability discovery and collaborative cybersecurity in the AI era.
Project QuiltWorks follows other joint initiatives from Kroll and CrowdStrike, including collaboration on the [Charlotte AI AgentWorks Ecosystem](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-launches-charlotte-ai-agentworks-ecosystem-for-building-secure-agents/) and [CrowdStrike Falcon® Flex for Services](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-launches-flex-for-services-bringing-flexible-cybersecurity-services-consumption-to-the-agentic-era/). 
## Customer Outcomes and Industry Recognition
As CrowdStrike 2026 Americas MSSP of the Year, Kroll delivers significant value to customers through its world-class cyber advisory and incident response services. With [tens of thousands of endpoints migrated to the CrowdStrike Falcon® platform](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/press-releases/crowdstrike-kroll-partnership-elevates-mdr-services/), Kroll’s clients are seeing restoration times reduced to a matter of hours, and total cost of ownership reduced by as much as 30 percent, in some cases. Many clients have undergone complete modernization of their cyber operations in less than 30 days as a result of the partnership.
“Kroll is a key partner in helping organizations modernize security and accelerate response with the Falcon platform,” said Daniel Bernard, Chief Business Officer, CrowdStrike. “By combining their incident response expertise with innovations like Project QuiltWorks and the Charlotte AI AgentWorks Ecosystem, we’re delivering measurable outcomes for customers – from faster recovery times to reduced operational complexity. This recognition as our 2026 Americas MSSP of the Year reflects the impact we’re driving together.”
