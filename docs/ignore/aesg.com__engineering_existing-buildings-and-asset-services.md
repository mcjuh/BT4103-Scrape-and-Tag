# Existing Buildings & Asset Services
[ Contact Us Today ](https://aesg.com/contact-us/)
## Structural Assessment, Retrofit & Optimisation for Existing Assets
We assess, retrofit, and optimise existing structures to enhance safety, performance, and long-term value. AESG’s services support adaptive reuse, change-of-use projects, and asset management, ensuring buildings remain resilient and fit for purpose throughout their lifecycle.
## Our Services
## Structural Condition Assessments
Evaluating the condition of existing structures to identify risks, deterioration, and maintenance needs, ensuring safety and longevity.
### Detects structural issues before they become critical
### Supports maintenance and repair planning
### Enhances safety for occupants and users
### Informs investment and asset management decisions
## Retrofitting & Strengthening Design
Designing targeted retrofits and strengthening solutions to improve structural capacity, resilience, and performance of existing buildings.
### Increases load-bearing capacity and safety
### Extends asset life and usability
### Optimises materials and construction cost
### Minimises disruption during implementation
## Change-of-Use & Adaptive Reuse Assessments
Assessing existing buildings for change-of-use or adaptive reuse projects, ensuring compliance, safety, and functional performance.
### Supports safe and compliant reuse of structures
### Identifies necessary modifications and enhancements
### Enables efficient and cost-effective adaptation
### Preserves asset value and sustainability
## Structural Due Diligence for Acquisitions
Providing independent technical assessments and reports for property acquisitions to evaluate structural integrity, risks, and compliance.
### Reduces financial and operational risk
### Confirms structural compliance and safety
### Informs investment and decision-making
### Identifies future maintenance or retrofit needs
## Residual Life & Asset Performance Assessments
Estimating remaining service life and performance of structures to inform maintenance, refurbishment, and long-term planning.
### Provides accurate insights on asset longevity
### Supports strategic maintenance and refurbishment planning
### Enhances safety and operational reliability
### Optimises long-term investment and lifecycle costs
