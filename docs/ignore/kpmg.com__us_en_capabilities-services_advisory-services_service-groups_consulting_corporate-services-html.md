# Corporate services
High performing firms focus on advancing their business functions to surpass the competition. They look for opportunities to leverage AI, digital labor, analytics, and service delivery options to create greater agility.
In our increasingly complex world, a holistic view of business functions can yield great returns. By leveraging AI-powered tools, KPMG services help organizations transform back office functions so clients can focus more on strategy and insights. To drive continued improvement, executives should be asking:
  * How can innovative technology tools like AI better serve my business strategy?
  * What processes can be automated?
  * Are my teams deployed effectively?
  * What regulatory changes require attention?
  * Are there tax implications to be considered?
