ERM partners with technology leaders and their suppliers to design and implement resilient supply chain strategies—turning sustainability transformation into enterprise value by de-risking, decarbonizing, and circularizing everything from critical materials to finished hardware, enabling reliable scale at pace in the AI era.
Supply chain solutions that create competitive advantage
## Building resilient, scalable, low‑carbon AI supply chains across the full lifecycle
Responsibly sourced critical materials
End‑to‑end supply chain due diligence and audits to assess and ensure responsible sourcing of critical materials, addressing human rights, environmental impacts, supplier risk, and regulatory expectations.
Supplier readiness & circular design
Assessing and strengthening supplier capabilities while embedding circular‑by‑design principles upstream, translating design intent into practical implementation across materials, manufacturing, and sourcing decisions.
Product carbon, waste & scope 3 reduction
Creating n-tier supply chain transparency by building credible product‑level inventories and roadmaps, then implementing decarbonization levers across the value chain—from design and sourcing to manufacturing and logistics—while addressing hard‑to‑abate emissions through targeted carbon removal strategies.
End of life, reuse & recovery
Designing and implementing end‑of‑life strategies that go beyond compliant disposal to enable usable life-extension, safe recovery, reuse, and material reintegration, while engaging suppliers to establish reliable circular feedstocks and closed‑loop supply chains at scale.
