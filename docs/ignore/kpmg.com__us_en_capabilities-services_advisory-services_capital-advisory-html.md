# Infrastructure, Capital Projects and Climate Advisory
Implement successful, efficient and sustainable infrastructure and capital programs – from strategies to delivery and ongoing management. 
Today’s leaders face unprecedented pressure to deliver value for stakeholders, meet environmental goals, and foster a positive workforce culture—all while operating within tight budgets, limited resources, and increased accountability. At KPMG, we help asset intensive organizations develop strategies, finance, deliver, and manage capital asset portfolios and projects. We also help evaluate transactions and improve asset operations to enhance the services delivered to customers and constituents.
Our vision is to be a driving force for the responsible renewal of America’s physical infrastructure. By elevating the services and outcomes they enable, we contribute to a more sustainable future— driving economic growth, improving quality of life, enhancing resilience, and improving connectivity.
