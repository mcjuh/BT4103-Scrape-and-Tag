#  12th Asia Offshore Wind Day: Focus on South Korea
September 3, 2024 
Park Hyatt, Busan South Korea - [Get Directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1JniKFgFXxYPud4T6)
[ register ](https://www.asiawind.org/12th-asia-offshore-wind-day/)
ERM is proud to participate in the 12th Asia Offshore Wind Day, hosted by the Asia Wind Energy Association at the Park Hyatt Hotel in Busan on September 3, 2024. This event is a key gathering for industry leaders to explore the rapidly growing offshore wind market in Korea and across Asia, including emerging markets such as Japan, Australia, Vietnam, Taiwan, and the Philippines.
Our Country Managing Partner, Seung Hyun Kwak, will contribute to Session 7: **Environment, Communities & People**. During this session, Seung Hyun will present **"Securing the Future: Safety Solutions in Offshore Wind Development,"** focusing on strategies to ensure the safe and sustainable growth of offshore wind projects.
We look forward to connecting with industry peers and sharing our insights on the future of offshore wind, with a focus on environmental stewardship and community engagement.
Don’t miss this opportunity to join the conversation and learn more about the innovations driving the offshore wind sector forward.
##  ERM Speaker
##  Seung Hyun Kwak 
Country Managing Partner, ERM Korea 
Seung Hyun Kwak is the Country Managing Partner at ERM Korea, where he oversees a wide range of services focused on Environmental, Safety & Health management, as well as broader ESG and sustainability initiatives. He began his career as an HSE Manager at Hyundai Construction & Engineering, where he played a key role in developing EHS management systems and achieving ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 certifications. With 27 years of experience, he has conducted comprehensive safety and health assessments for both global and domestic companies, ensuring compliance and best practices across various industries.
