#  Publicis Sapient Names Dan Pitchenik as Industry Lead for Financial Services in North America 
Appointment reinforces Publicis Sapient’s commitment to helping financial services clients thrive through digital business transformation powered by AI.
May 1, 2025 
Share
## NEW YORK, NY, May 1, 2025
Publicis Sapient, a digital business transformation company, today announced the appointment of Dan Pitchenik as Industry Lead for Financial Services in North America.
In this role, Pitchenik will lead Publicis Sapient’s Financial Services business in North America across the banking, capital markets, insurance, and wealth and asset management sectors. Based in New York, he will be responsible for driving growth and deepening client relationships, bringing together Publicis Sapient’s SPEED – Strategy, Product, Experience, Engineering, and Data & AI – capabilities to help clients navigate their digital transformations journeys and create long-term value.
“We’re thrilled to welcome Dan to Publicis Sapient,” said **Nigel Vaz, CEO of Publicis Sapient.** “He brings a strong track record of advising C-suite leaders across the financial services industry, and his leadership experience across strategy and transformation programs makes him an invaluable addition to our team. As clients face unprecedented shifts in customer expectations and unprecedented technological shifts powered by AI, Dan’s industry expertise and passion for client impact will be critical in helping them successfully transform for the future.”
Pitchenik joins Publicis Sapient from Accenture, where he held senior leadership roles in both global and US Financial Services. His career also includes leadership positions at PwC and Diamond Management and Technology Consultants, where he advised major investment banking and asset management clients on large-scale business and technology transformations.
“I’m excited to join Publicis Sapient, whose heritage is rooted in helping clients navigate secular shifts driven by evolving customer behaviors and new technologies,” said **Pitchenik**. “We are in such a moment now – with advancing technologies like AI and data-driven platforms reshaping the landscape at a time of global macroeconomic uncertainty. Publicis Sapient is uniquely positioned to help clients balance near-term pressures while building future-ready businesses. I look forward to partnering with our clients to lead them through their transformations.”
## About Publicis Sapient
Publicis Sapient, the global technology hub of Publicis Groupe, provides enterprise AI platforms and services. With over 30 years of digital business transformation experience, we enable enterprise clients to transform how they operate and serve their customers, unlocking new value and enabling them to thrive in an AI-driven world. Our platforms [Sapient Slingshot](https://www.publicissapient.com/platforms/slingshot), [Sapient Bodhi](https://www.publicissapient.com/platforms/bodhi) and [Sapient Sustain](https://www.publicissapient.com/platforms/sustain) use AI built off this deep enterprise context to help organizations modernize their legacy tech systems, build agentic solutions, and automate their IT operations. The combination of our AI platforms and the expertise of our people enables us to deliver faster and more effective outcomes through solutions that are specific to the unique needs of our clients’ businesses, their industries and their customers.
