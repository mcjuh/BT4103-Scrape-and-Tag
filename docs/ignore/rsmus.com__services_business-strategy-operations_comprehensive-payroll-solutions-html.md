Payroll services and solutions–how they differ  
    
Payroll consulting is a specialized service that helps businesses navigate the complexities of payroll, ensuring compliance with laws and regulations while optimizing their payroll processes. Consultants provide advice on various aspects of payroll, including tax compliance, data integration and process efficiency. 
Payroll solutions are systems and methods that help businesses manage employee compensation accurately, on time and in compliance with tax and labor regulations. These solutions aim to ensure timely pay, regulatory compliance, efficient record-keeping, business continuity and employee satisfaction. 

Typical services provided by payroll providers include  
    
  * **Payroll processing:** Calculating wages, deducting taxes and generating paychecks or direct deposits.
  * **Tax filing:** Managing and filing federal, state and local payroll taxes.
  * **Benefits administration:** Handling employee benefits like health insurance and retirement plans.
  * **Time and attendance tracking:** Some providers offer solutions for tracking employee hours and managing time-off requests.
  * **Reporting:** Generating reports on payroll data and tax summaries.
  * **Employee self-service:** Allowing employees to access their pay stubs and tax forms online. 



Benefits of using payroll services  
    
  * **Time savings:** Outsourcing payroll tasks frees up business owners' time to focus on other aspects of their business.
  * **Reduced errors:** Payroll services use automation and expertise to minimize errors in calculations and filings.
  * **Tax compliance:** They ensure compliance with complex tax laws and regulations, reducing the risk of penalties.
  * **Streamlined processes:** Payroll providers offer integrated platforms for payroll, HR, and other related functions.
  * **Specialized support:** They provide access to customer support and assistance when needed.
