##### Horizons is dedicated to creating a world where infinite variations in mind and body are represented, empowered, and celebrated.
## Mission
We amplify the voices of disabled, neurodiverse, and mental health communities. We are committed to being educators and advisers to Slalom through best practice development, user experience enhancements and learning opportunities through cross-functional partnerships. 
## Objectives
  * **Community:** We work to build community, network, and foster spaces of collaboration both within and external to Slalom. This includes building bridges across other Slalom communities, as our team member experiences are unique and deeply personal. 
  * **Impact & Educate:** We develop, support, and lead internal educational programs and campaigns. We also serve in an advisory capacity to Slalom. We seek to create a healthier workplace that promotes and protects the health and safety of all team members.
