## Operational due diligence support tailored to your investment needs
We understand the importance of operational due diligence (“ODD”) for you to make investment decisions, and the challenges you face:
  * Growing costs of conducting due diligence, especially across borders
  * Shortage of resources to monitor or regularly review targets’ operation readiness
  * Increased complexity of operational due diligence in specific targets and asset classes requires industry expertise
  * Lack of independence and objectivity for in-house or investment advisors’ due diligence programmes


At PwC, we are a team of professionals with extensive industry experience and expertise across various alternative assets, strategies, and investor types. With our vast PwC global network and industry knowledge, we always stay at the forefront of new regulations and industry trends in different continents. We are here to provide you with operational due diligence services that adhere to the highest industry standards and catered to your unique requirements.
## Our expertise covers the full lifecycle, regardless which stage you are right now
Through our rigorous ODD process, we strive to assist you in making informed investment decisions and selecting assets, managers, or funds that align with your risk tolerance and investment objectives.
