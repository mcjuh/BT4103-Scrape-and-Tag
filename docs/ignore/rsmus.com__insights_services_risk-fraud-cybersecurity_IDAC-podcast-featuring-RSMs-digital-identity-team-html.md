##  What is the Identity at the Center podcast? 
[Identity at the Center (IDAC)](https://www.identityatthecenter.com/) is a weekly podcast dedicated to identity security and identity and access management (IAM). Podcast hosts [Jim McDonald](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jimmcdonaldpmp/) and [Jeff Steadman](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jeffsteadman/), who are directors at RSM US LLP, bring decades of real-world IAM experience.
The podcast covers modern IAM, including:
  * **Identity governance and administration (IGA):** Access control, risk and compliance.
  * **Digital identity news:** Emerging threats, trends and regulations.
  * **IAM best practices:** Securing users, systems and data.
  * **Identity insights:** Extensive experience and analysis, and real‑world lessons.
  * **Privileged access management (PAM):** Protecting high‑risk accounts.
  * **Cloud identity:** Managing access in hybrid and cloud environments.
  * **AI in identity and access management:** Automation, intelligence and the future of identity security.


Digital identity sits at the core of modern cybersecurity. Most attacks now exploit identity—credentials, privileges and access—not just systems. As identity threats evolve across cloud, AI and hybrid environments, staying informed is critical to reducing risk, strengthening controls and making smarter security decisions.
As a sponsor of the **IDAC** podcast, RSM US LLP supports meaningful conversations that help organizations strengthen identity security and stay ahead of evolving threats.

##  Frequently asked questions (FAQs)  

What is identity and access management, and why is it important?  
    
Identity and access management (IAM) is the framework of policies, processes and technologies that ensures the right people—and systems—have the right access to the right resources at the right time. It governs how digital identities are created, authenticated, authorized and monitored across applications, data and infrastructure.
IAM is critical because identity is now the primary attack surface in cybersecurity. Most breaches exploit stolen credentials, excessive privileges or unmanaged access rather than technical vulnerabilities alone. Strong IAM reduces risk, limits the blast radius of attacks, supports compliance and enables secure adoption of cloud, remote work and AI‑driven systems. 

How can IAM leaders stay informed about the evolving topic?  
    
  * **Follow identity‑focused podcasts and briefings.** Hear directly from practitioners and vendors on emerging threats and solutions.
  * **Engage with the IAM community.** Participate in conferences, user groups and online forums to learn from real‑world experiences.
  * **Monitor regulatory and security news.** Stay current on privacy laws, compliance requirements and identity-related breach patterns.
  * **Invest in ongoing education.** Certifications, labs and hands‑on [cybersecurity workshops](https://rsmus.com/services/risk-fraud-cybersecurity/cybersecurity-business-vulnerability/cybersecurity-workshops.html) help leaders keep pace with cloud, PAM and AI‑driven IAM changes.


Staying informed enables IAM leaders to anticipate risk, modernize controls and make smarter identity decisions as the threat landscape evolves. 

How can identity and access management consulting services improve compliance?  
    
IAM consulting services can improve compliance by helping organizations design, implement and govern access controls that align with regulatory requirements and audit expectations. Experienced consultants bring structure, knowledge and repeatable frameworks to reduce identity-related compliance gaps.
Key ways IAM consulting strengthens compliance include:
  * **Policy alignment and enforcement.** IAM consultants map access policies to regulations such as SOX, HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR and state privacy laws, then enforce them consistently across systems.
  * **Least‑privilege access.** Reduces excessive permissions by ensuring users and systems only have access required for their roles—a core compliance expectation.
  * **Automated access reviews and certifications**. Streamlines periodic reviews, approvals and attestations required by auditors.
  * **Improved identity governance.** Establishes clear ownership, segregation of duties and lifecycle controls for joiners, movers and leavers.
  * **Audit readiness and reporting.** Provides centralized visibility, logging and evidence to support audits and reduce manual effort.
  * **Risk reduction for privileged access.** Strengthens controls around administration and service accounts, which are heavily scrutinized in compliance assessments.


By modernizing identity controls and governance, IAM consultants help organizations meet regulatory requirements more efficiently while reducing audit risk and operational overhead. 

Why is digital identity critical to modern cybersecurity?  
    
Digital identity is critical to modern cybersecurity because it controls who and what can access systems and data—and most cyberattacks now exploit stolen credentials, excessive privileges or unmanaged access rather than technical vulnerabilities.
