#  AI for Future Cities 
AI is changing the way we move, live and work in our cities. In this publication series, we explore the opportunities and risks of AI for future cities, and we ask how we can use it to shape cities that are liveable, productive and sustainable.
AI for Future Cities is for all sorts of AI users: planners, policymakers, urbanists, designers and sustainability advocates. The series focuses on helping cities use AI to tackle changing climates, resource scarcity, wellbeing, population growth and inequity.
