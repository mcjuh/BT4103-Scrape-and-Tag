#  Strategic transactions and reinsurance solutions 
We help insurers navigate complex reinsurance strategies with end-to-end solutions — from strategic consulting and operational setup to managed services and ongoing compliance. Our integrated experience in actuarial, finance, risk and technology enables faster execution, cost efficiency and sustainable growth.

## Our Strategic Transactions and Reinsurance team offers services to evaluate and implement reinsurance transactions, from assessments to operational setup
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  End-to-end managed services for establishing sidecars, affiliates and captives
We offer integrated solutions in actuarial, accounting, treasury, management reporting, risk management and compliance.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Life yearly renewable term (YRT) reinsurance management
We provide focused management services for life YRT reinsurance.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Reinsurance target operating model development
We craft tailored operating models that align with your business goals and regulatory requirements.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Reinsurance operations management
We help streamline and enhance operational efficiency in reinsurance activities.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Capital adequacy ratio management
Our experienced professionals provide guidance on US risk-based capital (RBC), the Bermuda Solvency Capital Requirement (BSCR) and other required capital regime strategies to establish regulatory compliance and financial stability.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Actuarial appraisal for buy-side due diligence
We provide actuarial insights and evaluations as part of the due diligence process for acquisitions.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Purchase GAAP (PGAAP) fair value assessment and actuarial balances
We help establish fair value and actuarial balances for accurate opening balance sheets and financial reporting.
Read more  Read less 
  * ###  Knowledgeable transaction support
We offer actuarial appraisals, PGAAP fair value assessments and buy-side due diligence for strategic transactions.
