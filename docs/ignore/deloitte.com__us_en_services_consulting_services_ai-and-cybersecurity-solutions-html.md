## Cyber accelerated
Whatever the challenge, Deloitte can help you innovate, accelerate, and achieve your cybersecurity objectives. Below are just a few examples of how we’re putting our experience and tailored approach to work across industries. Contact us for a use case analysis.
#####  Enhanced security for financial services 
Deloitte's GenAI and LLM-based SSDL framework provided a financial services company with a broad set of solutions for securing AI-based applications, enhancing security and operational efficiency. From user inquiries and intent context to guardrails, rule application, workflow automation, and user experience, each stage was fortified. 
#####  Guardrail deployment for technology, media and telecom 
Deloitte helped a global publishing company secure its customer-facing GenAI chatbot to protect the company’s extensive library of proprietary content. The company sought additional safeguards against prompt injection and jailbreak attacks. We evaluated and quantitatively tested commercial and open-source solutions to identify a scalable guardrail solution to prevent and monitor anomalous and problematic user activity for production deployment. 
#####  AI strategy for life sciences and health care 
Deloitte helped a US health care company develop an AI security and governance strategy to support its AI modernization goals. Outcomes included a tailored AI governance structure that accounted for the evolving AI regulatory landscape as well as an approach to development that balanced opportunity and risk in support of AI innovation. 
#####  GenAI transformation for energy and resources 
A large public utility was modernizing while integrating renewable energy into the grid. It aimed to create an enterprise strategy to lead the industry with responsible GenAI technologies, including use case ideation and secure implementation across business units. Emphasis was placed on governance to balance innovation and AI risk mitigation, helping this utility company address AI's broader implications in a highly regulated industry. 
#####  Integrating AI risk management for a consumer organization 
Deloitte assisted a leading US casino enterprise in modernizing IT policies, enterprise procedures, and tactical processes for AI adoption, development and management. Our work included developing an enterprise AI policy that governed AI capabilities across casino operations as well as tactical recommendations for updating IT evaluation, intake, development and management capabilities for AI solutions.
