# 2025 Sustainability Report 
Our approach to sustainability is rooted in our longstanding belief that responsible business is good business. Focusing on the issues most important to our stakeholders, we build strategies and programs that drive short-term and long-term value for our people, our communities and the environment. [Learn more about our sustainability journey](https://www.bdo.com/sustainability-esg).
## 2025 Sustainability Highlights
Elected Matt Becker as next CEO of BDO USA, effective July 1, 2026 
Completed largest strategic expansion to date, adding more than 1,300 professionals from HORNE LLP to better meet client needs, including expanded state and local government services
Mobilized over 23,000 volunteer hours 
Donated $3.5M to nonprofit organizations and communities 
Offered 177 inclusion events 
Achieved 84% in the Employee Engagement Index within our annual employee Pulse survey 
Validated our greenhouse gas (GHG) emission reduction targets by the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) 
Procured renewable energy certificates (RECs) to match our firm's electricity consumption for calendar year 2025 
Completed our first climate scenario analysis, sharpening our insights around climate risks and opportunities throughout our operations
Continued the integration of sustainability levers into performance improvement solutions that help drive efficiency and cost reductions for our clients
Stewardship is about continuity — honoring the vision and dedication that has made BDO a resilient organization, while preparing the firm to grow together into the future. Together, we are aligned on the priorities that matter: focusing on client centricity, supporting our professionals, and advancing responsible practices that strengthen BDO for the long term.
Matthew Becker, BDO USA CEO and Wayne Berson, BDO USA Former CEO
