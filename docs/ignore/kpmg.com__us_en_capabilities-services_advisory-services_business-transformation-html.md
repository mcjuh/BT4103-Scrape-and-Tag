###  AI is an accelerator, not the goal 
### AI is an accelerator, not the goal
We view artificial intelligence as a powerful enabler of transformation, not the final destination.
That’s why we developed KPMG Velocity as an extensive transformation approach, not a technology platform, product, or tool focused on AI enablement alone.
Unlike other approaches that start with the technology, KPMG Velocity strategically applies AI only where it directly accelerates decision-making, improves productivity, and enhances customer and employee experiences—all while maintaining strong governance, resilience, and trust.
###  Built-in trust, risk management, and resilience 
### Built-in trust, risk management, and resilience
Organizations achieve sustainable growth through transformation only when trust, risk management, and resilience are embedded from the start—not added later. Because trust is foundational to everything we do, it is never treated as a separate workstream. Rather than viewing risk, regulation, and resilience as constraints that slow you down, we frame them as essential enablers of sustainable, long-term transformation.
###  Modular and scalable across the full enterprise 
### Modular and scalable across the full enterprise

  * Start where it matters most to your immediate strategic goals
  * Scale transformation efficiently across functions and global geographies
  * Evolve over time, adapting to market shifts without major business disruption.

#### FAQs: Transformation and KPMG Velocity
**What is business transformation?**
Business transformation is the process of helping an organization unlock sustainable growth by aligning its strategy, operating models, people, and technology. More than just simple change, it is an ongoing journey to create lasting value by reimagining operations, optimizing performance, and unlocking growth across all critical business functions.
**How does KPMG help companies grow through transformation?**
KPMG guides companies through complex challenges to achieve sustainable success using deep industry insights, innovative capabilities, and an extensive methodology called KPMG Velocity. By integrating services across functions, KPMG helps organizations reimagine operations, optimize performance, and unlock growth. This approach helps ensure that trust, risk management, and resilience are embedded from the start, framing them as essential enablers of long-term value.
**What is KPMG Velocity?**
KPMG Velocity is an extensive, unified methodology for business transformation. It is not a technology platform or a tool but an approach that brings together our industry-leading methods, capabilities, and insights. KPMG Velocity is designed to help organizations transform faster and more deliberately by balancing speed, direction, discipline, and trust in an AI-driven world.
**How does AI fit into KPMG’s transformation approach?**
KPMG views artificial intelligence as a powerful enabler of transformation, not the final destination. Within the KPMG Velocity approach, AI is applied strategically and practically, but only where it directly accelerates decision-making, improves productivity, and enhances customer and employee experiences. This is done while maintaining strong governance, resilience, and trust.
