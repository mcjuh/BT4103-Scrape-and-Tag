#  ERM Foundation Future Fund Program
##  Accelerating impact through our regional partnerships
Thanks to the generous donations of ERM Shareholders, in 2024 the ERM Foundation launched a three-year Future Fund program focused on accelerating progress towards the UN Sustainable Development Goals. The program enabled us to invest in longer-term projects and programs at the intersection of biodiversity protection, climate action and investing in female livelihoods across our regions. 
One flagship program was supported per ERM region, each chosen and run by a team of dedicated ERM employees.
See our partner organizations below.
##  SolarAid
**Location: Malawi**
SolarAid’s mission is to light up every home, school and clinic in Africa by 2030, using safe, clean solar power. This project will support 40 new Mayi Walas solar entrepreneur groups from early stages of recruitment and training to the launch of self-sustaining businesses to help provide economic independence and safe solar light for over 100,000 people in rural communities.

**900** Female entrepreneurs supported
**29,000+** Solar lights sold by the female entrepreneurs 
**155,000+** People reached with clean, safe and affordable solar light

##  The Ocean Foundation
**Location: Xcalak Reefs National Park, Quintana Roo, Mexico**
The Ocean Foundation’s mission is to support, strengthen, and promote organizations dedicated to reversing the trend of destruction of ocean environments around the world. They focus on emerging threats to generate cutting edge solutions and better implementation strategies. This project will invest in community capacity building to protect over 160 hectares of mangrove restoration in the Xcalak Reefs National Park, with the ultimate goal to protect the national park and achieve a blue carbon certification.
[ Watch the Video ](https://vimeo.com/1181910807/26ff2da5e3 "Watch the Video")
**160+** Hectares of mangrove habitat enhanced
**50+** Community members trained in mangrove restoration
**One** Blue carbon certified project under Plan Vivo standard
##  Ocean Wise
**Location: Metlakatla First Nation waters in Prince Rupert, British Columbia, Canada**
The Ocean Wise Association’s mission is to empower communities and individuals to take action to protect and restore the world’s oceans from climate change, overfishing and ocean pollution. This support backs the seaforestation project to research and develop cost-effective and scalable kelp regeneration techniques to ensure these ecosystems flourish for future generations.
[ Watch the Video ](https://vimeo.com/1181911533/3668e2eb90 "Watch the Video")
**20+** Hectares of kelp restored
**30+** Community members engaged and trained
[Read more](https://ocean.org/ "Read more")
##  Center for Rural Development in Central Vietnam (CRD)
**Location: A Luoi District, Thua Thien Hue province, Vietnam**
CRD mission is to conduct research, training, technological transfer and policy advocacy for sustainable rural development. They focus on natural resources governance and biodiversity conservation, disaster risk management and climate change adaption to ensure sustainable livelihood development. This project will support the conservation of 50,000 medicinal plants and the protection of up to 15,000 hectares of natural forest in association with livelihood improvements for ethnic minorities in mountainous areas.
[ Watch the Video ](https://vimeo.com/1181911339/c1b7aafa49 "Watch the Video")
**50,000** Medicial plants being planted under the forest canopy
**15,000** Hectares of natural forest managed and protected
**10,000+** Community members impacted through capacity building and awareness training
