#  Converge by Deloitte 
## Software and data products that converge with services to accelerate outcomes 
Our production-grade Converge products, built on a common engineering foundation, combine Deloitte’s deep sector experience, a robust data fabric, forward deployed engineering, and proprietary and ecosystem-curated innovation to move from issue to outcomes faster. 
## Solving for the most nuanced industry challenges
Most failed technology investments aren’t just a build problem. They’re often the consequence of not fully uncovering, appreciating, or addressing the real “problem.” 
With Converge, we start where it matters most: an acute grasp of sector-nuanced workflows, data architectures, competitive dynamics, regulatory realities, and the root causes of issues that off-the-shelf SaaS solutions and AI-led advice can gloss over. 
We’ve long been at the forefront of leading transformations across all major sectors, where successful outcomes were the consequence of understanding the particulars. And it’s this experience that provides the due diligence needed to build solutions that fit and embed within how your business works.

#####  Why is Deloitte in the business of selling software-based solutions? 
For 180 years, Deloitte has delivered trusted outcomes. As companies evolve how they achieve value from investments, expectations have changed along with it. When outcomes are anticipated in months, not years, it’s only natural we’d codify our deep sector experience into products and platforms to unlock faster, stronger outcomes. 
#####  Do Converge products only work with and on specific enterprise platforms, hyperscaler technologies, and AI models? 
Converge products are built with and on many of the leading platforms and AI technologies companies use to run and manage their business today. Specific architecture decisions are based on common needs and tech stacks within specific sectors.
#####  How does AI factor into Converge solutions, and is it just a buzzword here? 
AI, generative and agentic, is embedded across our products to provide value in the context of specific workflows and technology architectures to create outcomes that matter. 
#####  What does the Converge product roadmap look like? 
We expect that our Converge storefront will continue to expand into different sectors and that our existing sector product lines will grow with additional products. We constantly apply rigorous innovation methodologies to identify, engineer, test, and validate new solutions for broad market consumption.
#####  Do Converge products share a common engineering foundation? 
Yes. Converge products are built and run on a shared platform and common runtime services, which means faster deployment, connection to Deloitte’s broader AI and delivery capabilities, and greater interoperability between products, inclusive of our Zora AI™ digital workforces.
