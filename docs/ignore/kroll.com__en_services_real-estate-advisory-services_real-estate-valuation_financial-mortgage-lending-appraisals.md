# Mortgage Lending Appraisals
Kroll provides independent real estate appraisals to support mortgage lending decisions. We deliver objective, market-based valuations of real property serving as collateral for loans, helping lenders assess risk and meet underwriting requirements.
Our appraisal services are designed specifically for banks and other financial institutions originating or holding loans secured by commercial real estate. We value a broad range of property types and asset classes, supporting lending decisions across stabilized and transitional assets. All appraisals are developed in accordance with applicable regulatory and professional standards, ensuring credible and defensible results aligned with current market conditions.
