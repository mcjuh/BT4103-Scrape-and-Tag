# Benchmarking Air Emissions of the 100 largest power producers in the United States
Working with NRDC and Constellation, the 21st Edition of the Benchmarking Air Emissions report has now been released.
The Benchmarking series began in 1997 and uses publicly reported data to compare the emissions performance of the 100 largest power producers in the United States. Company rankings and aggregate industry trends reflect 2024 generation and emissions data.
