## Our media & communications _expertise_
#### Communications 
From network transformation to data-driven customer experiences, we help communications leaders put the latest technology and thinking to work for their customers, employees, and organizations. 
#### Gaming 
From publishers and streaming platforms to PC and console manufacturers, we work with leading gaming companies around the globe to lead high-impact change together. In driving player engagement, accelerating media and sponsorship sales, and more, we believe in doing things differently. 
#### Marketing & advertising 
The industry faces unprecedented disruption: cookie deprecation, new privacy laws, and machine-learning breakthroughs. We partner with leading brands to navigate this shifting landscape and meet customers where they are. From building audience targeting solutions to leveraging data science, Slalom has the proven expertise to meet advertisers' unique business needs. 
#### Media & entertainment 
The media and entertainment industry is rapidly evolving through digital transformation. Companies are transforming content creation and distribution while rethinking the customer experience. We partner with the world’s leading streaming, broadcast, music, and publishing companies to differentiate and drive profitable growth amid turbulence. 
#### Sports & live entertainment 
Within live entertainment and sports, fan experience continues to take center stage. Slalom partners with top sports leagues, teams, venues, and technology providers to deliver best-in-class fan experiences, leveraging the latest industry solutions and technologies such as frictionless retail, personalization, next-generation security, and interconnected stadiums.
