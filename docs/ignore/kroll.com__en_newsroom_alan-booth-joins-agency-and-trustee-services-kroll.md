# Alan Booth Joins Kroll to Head Agency and Trustee Services Practice
London – Kroll, the leading independent provider of global financial and risk advisory solutions, has appointed [Alan Booth](https://www.kroll.com/en/our-experts/alan-booth) as managing director and Head of its [Agency and Trustee Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/agency-and-trustee-services) practice.
Kroll’s Agency and Trustee Services practice is a market leader in providing conflict free, flexible and highly efficient agency and trustee services to the global loan and bond markets. Booth, who is based in London, brings two decades of expertise in the financial services industry, is a qualified English Solicitor and a licensed attorney with the New York State Bar. His career has been marked by significant leadership experience at Ocorian and Deutsche Bank, and his extensive background encompasses expertise in corporate trust and agency, corporate services and fund administration.
[Jessica Stamelman](https://www.kroll.com/en/our-experts/jessica%20stamelman), President of Kroll Business Services, said, "Our Agency and Trustee Services practice has experienced tremendous growth over the last few years. We are thrilled to have Alan join Kroll to lead our next stage of expansion and reinforce our leading market position.”
From its inception as Lucid Agency and Trustee Services in 2017, Kroll’s Agency and Trustee Services practice has grown to boast 700+ deals. The founders, Emma Foster (nee Hamley), [John Traynor](https://www.kroll.com/en/our-experts/john-traynor) and Paul Barton, remain an integral part of our leadership team and will work closely with Booth as he transitions into his new role.
“Kroll is well known for its exceptional client service and expertise in the loan and bond markets,” Booth stated. “I'm honored to lead the Agency and Trustee Services practice during a time when the business is focused on growth, and I look forward to building upon Kroll’s great foundation to more effectively support our clients globally.”
The Kroll Business Services practice includes the following suite of services: [Agency and Trustee Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/agency-and-trustee-services), [Issuer Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/issuer-services), [Restructuring Administration](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/restructuring-administration), [Settlement Administration](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/settlement-administration), [Government Solutions](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/government-solutions), [Notice Media Solutions](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/notice-media-solutions) and [Business Support Solutions](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/business-support-solutions).
