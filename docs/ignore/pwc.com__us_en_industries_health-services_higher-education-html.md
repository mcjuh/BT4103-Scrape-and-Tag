#  Higher education 
## Higher education institutions face a confluence of external and internal challenges
PwC’s higher education practice is built to help address the opportunities and challenges faced by higher education institutions today. With dedicated professionals working with colleges and universities across the nation, PwC assists our higher education clients in identifying opportunities to increase accountability, transparency, and help to transform to align resources to support their missions.

## Opportunity and challenge focus areas for higher education
PwC believes there are four business imperatives facing higher education institutions today:
**Affordability** Rising tuition levels have already exacerbated student debt levels, making it difficult to continue to increase tuition. This in addition to existing administrative inefficiencies and the need to update technology, is leading institutions to look for ways to streamline.
**Innovation** As the pace of change increases, higher education finds itself needing to move faster to remain on the cutting edge, including business transformation enabled through technology (ERPs) and are considering strategies around research, growth and new education models.
**Demonstrate value** Increasing access to data analytics is creating opportunity to measure return on investment and demonstrate value in academic compliance and resource optimization.
**Risk mitigation** The public is focused on safety and security more than ever, particularly on campuses. Institutions find themselves needing to demonstrate that they are getting ahead of these risks.
