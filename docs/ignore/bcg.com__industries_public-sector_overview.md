# Public Sector

Governments must act faster than ever to improve economic outcomes, strengthen social systems, and build resilience for billions of citizens today and tomorrow. BCG brings the AI capabilities, digital tools, and implementation speed combined with deep public sector expertise to help leaders deliver transformative, lasting impact at scale.

## How BCG Helps Transform Public Sector Organizations
BCG’s public sector consultants support governments around the world by bringing together our insights and capabilities from the private sector and from decades of experience supporting public sector clients. Our government consulting teams have completed more than a thousand public sector consulting projects in the past five years. We also founded, and continue to support, the independent [Centre for Public Impact](https://www.centreforpublicimpact.org/); a learning partner for governments, public servants, and those leading the charge to reimagine government so that it works for everyone.
  * Public sector transformations equip organizations with the right delivery models to realize their vision and achieve stakeholder buy-in, as well as the communication strategies necessary to support the transformation journey. Our framework for transformation in the public sector focuses on process, progress, transparency, and people. 
  * Embracing new technologies such as [AI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence) and [blockchain](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/blockchain) is only part of successful digital transformation in the public sector. Changes to culture, organization structures, capabilities, governance, and workflows are also critical to long-term success. BCG’s Center for [Digital Government](https://www.bcg.com/industries/public-sector/digital-government) can assess organizations’ [digital maturity](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/digital-maturity) and identify opportunities to enhance public sector digital strategy.
  * Talent is one of government’s greatest assets, particularly in the digital age. BCG works with public sector clients to develop world-class [people and organizational capabilities](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/people-strategy/overview), build [agile](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/agile-at-scale) organizations, support leadership development, manage change, and undertake HR transformations.
  * At all levels, public sector institutions can do more with less. We help optimize cost and productivity, [manage costs](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/cost-management), deliver higher quality through lean approaches, and professionalize the [procurement](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/procurement) function. BCG’s market-informed design and sourcing framework (MIDAS) is one tool that improves public sector procurement and creates greater value. 


## Our Clients' Success in Public Sector Strategy
A large government agency needed to change its business processes to become more efficient. Over the course of eight months, BCG designed and implemented a new operating model for several of the agency’s business lines and BCG coached the organization’s senior leaders to drive change. The transformation achieved a 15% increase in productivity.
Given continued economic growth and the need to be adaptive and agile, a government treasury agency in Asia sought to transform its organization and operations. Using a BCG framework for change, the agency created a fiscal policy office to enforce discipline across the government, increased process efficiency, and streamlined overlaps across functions.
Client Impact
[How Michigan Is Turning Economic Transition into Community Resilience](https://www.bcg.com/about/client-impact/economic-transition-helps-build-community-resilience)
BCG supported the State of Michigan in standing up a new Community & Worker Economic Transition Office to address shifts in the utility and auto sectors, which together make up more than 20% of the state’s economy.
Client Impact
[GIFT: Upskilling Malaysia’s Young Tech Talent for a High-Wage Future](https://www.bcg.com/about/client-impact/upskilling-malaysias-young-tech-talent-for-the-future)
BCG partnered with Khazanah Nasional Berhad to design and launch GIFT: a national workforce transformation program that equips Malaysia’s TVET graduates with industry-ready capabilities, unlocks higher-value career pathways, and supports the country’s ambition to compete at the frontier of the global innovation economy.
