# Real Estate Investment Advisory
Kroll Real Estate Advisory Group assists clients with commercial real estate investments ranging from a single property to large portfolios with many assets. 
We help our clients identify investments that meet their needs; perform feasibility assessments; conduct due diligence; and support them through all phases of the transaction.

  * [Property investment and portfolio analysis](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/real-estate-advisory-services/real%20estate%20investment%20advisory/property%20investment%20and%20portfolio%20analysis)

  * Technical advisory/property condition reports
  * Feasibility and site selection studies
  * Cash flow analytics and modelling (Argus)
  * Lease abstraction and analysis
