##  See our services 
Streamline. Automate. Accelerate. 
As a multinational company with a complex cross-border transaction structure, your transfer pricing policies can create intercompany accounting challenges unique to your business. KPMG Operational Transfer Pricing (OTP) services precisely calibrate your intercompany transaction processes to successfully execute your policies. Predictable, reliable results help to eliminate large true up adjustments, avoiding unexpected deviations from policies, improving ETR forecasting, and providing agility to respond to unexpected audits. 
Your organization benefits from:
  * More accurate cash and treasury planning due to better legal entity forecasts
  * Improved coordination with customs
  * Simplified intercompany accounting processes
  * Valuable supply chain insights


As your business changes and faces increasing demands from the global regulatory environment, you require accuracy, efficiency, and reduced risk. KPMG helps ensure data integrity, integration with other key functional areas, and reporting transparency into the implementation of your transfer pricing strategies.

### Introducing Operational Transfer Pricing powered by KPMG Digital Gateway
By harnessing the power and advanced modeling framework of the KPMG Digital Gateway platform, you can seamlessly connect to numerous data sources, effectively breaking down silos and enhancing your global transfer pricing function.
### Capabilities
The proprietary modeling framework of Operational Transfer Pricing, powered by KPMG Digital Gateway, provides:
Automated flexibility and scalability for transfer pricing monitoring and SKU-based pricing calculations.
Interactive dashboards to monitor entity profitability against transfer pricing policies and flux results against prior periods.
Workflows to manage and assign required tasks, centrally track completion progress, and document review and signoff to support internal controls.
Multiple data sourcing options and a common data model that enable connectivity to downstream tax processes and to KPMG tpEngineTM for transfer pricing documentation.
