# Coupa Procure-to-Invoice System Training & Certification 
KPMG offers Coupa training and certification programs, tailored for professionals to elevate their platform administration skills.
### **Elevate Your Coupa Administration process with Certified Training from KPMG**
Empower your system administrators and other Coupa users with the skills necessary to enhance their use of Coupa. KPMG is pleased to offer multi-day Coupa trainings and certification program, tailored for professionals aiming to elevate their platform administration skills. Our Coupa Certified Training specialists have in-depth experience with platform administration, implementation, procurement, and invoicing. They provide practical insights and solutions to real-life scenarios.
Participants will engage with interactive training modules that promote hands-on learning. Upon completion of the course, participants are eligible to take the Coupa Procure-to-Invoice System Administration Certification exam. Successful completion earns an official certification and badge. Join us for this training that will equip you with the knowledge and expertise to fully utilize Coupa’s powerful platform & administration.
At KPMG, our team of Coupa implementation specialists includes over 720 certified professionals with extensive industry experience located around the world. Having collaborated with Coupa for more than a decade on more than 175 successful global Coupa deployments, we are proud to have the longest-standing relationship of any Coupa partner. Trust us to deliver top-notch training to help you achieve your business goals.
**By the end of the course, participants should be able to:**
  * Describe the key features of Coupa's Business Spend Management platform
  * Manage users, roles, permissions, and content groups
  * Create, configure, and maintain system-wide data
  * Configure and customize Coupa settings to meet your organization's specific needs and preferences
  * Perform basic tasks such as creating requisitions, approving orders, and submitting invoices
  * Analyze Coupa performance and usage by leveraging insights and reports
