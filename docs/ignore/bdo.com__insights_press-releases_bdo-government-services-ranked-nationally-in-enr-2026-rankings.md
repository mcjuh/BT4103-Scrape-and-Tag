#  BDO Government Services Ranked Nationally in ENR 2026 Rankings
Press Release
  * PRINT 
  * 

**Key Facts:**
  * BDO Government Services was recognized in ENR’s 2026 national rankings, highlighting its standing in the government services market.
  * The ENR rankings are a widely recognized industry benchmark for evaluating leading firms in the engineering and construction sectors.
  * This recognition demonstrates BDO Government Services’ continued support for public sector clients through advisory, compliance and mission-focused services. 


**CHICAGO, June 23, 2026** — BDO Government Services, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of BDO USA and a leading provider of disaster recovery and program management services, has earned national recognition in [Engineering News Record’s (ENR) 2026 Top Rankings](https://www.enr.com/ext/resources/Issues/National_Issues/2026/22-June/ENR06222026_TopPSF_compressed.pdf), building on the integration of [HORNE LLP’s government services](https://www.bdo.com/insights/press-releases/bdo-usa-expands-professional-services-capabilities-through-horne) practice in 2025. 
BDO Government Services ranked 20th among the Top 50 Program Management Firms and 30th among the Top 100 Construction Management-for-Fee Firms nationwide. 
The 2026 rankings reflect continued growth and market momentum following the launch of BDO Government Services, which expanded the firm’s capabilities in federally funded programs.
“Being recognized by ENR reflects how effectively our professionals have come together to scale impact for our clients. We are delivering comprehensive solutions that help governments execute complex programs with accountability, speed and measurable results.”
Neil Forbes
National Practice Leader of BDO Government Services
This recognition for BDO Government Services reflects the experience and capabilities brought to the practice through the incorporation of HORNE’s government services team, whose knowledge and track record in delivering complex, federally funded programs have helped shape its foundation. It underscores BDO Government Services’ ability to combine that experience with sector knowledge, national scale, technical resources and advisory insight.
“While our clients ultimately measure success by outcomes, this recognition speaks to the strength and consistency behind those results. We are focused on helping governments deliver programs that create lasting value for communities.”
Andrea Espinola Wilson
Managing Principal of Industry Specialty Services at BDO USA
