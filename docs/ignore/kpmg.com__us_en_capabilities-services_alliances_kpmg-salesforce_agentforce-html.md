# Transform your customer experience with Agentforce
Where AI innovation meets implementation excellence
Use the KPMG AI Incubator to learn where Agentforce can add the most value to your business.
## Welcome to the _era of Agentforce_
Salesforce leads the market in cloud-based platforms that connect businesses with their customers. Now enhanced by Agentforce, they can deploy the agentic layer—autonomous AI agents equipped with tools, tailoring, and a suite of out-of-the-box functions—to bring you unprecedented capabilities:
  * **Autonomous customer service** can handle complex customer inquiries, qualify sales leads, and optimize marketing campaigns while maintaining a personal touch. 
  * **Seamless data integration** with the Salesforce data cloud unifies customer data across all touchpoints, with “zero copy” integration, enabling Agentforce to operate with complete context and precision without the hassle of managing new data sets.
  * **Intelligent workflow** automates routine tasks while escalating complex issues to human experts, to deliver immediate impact, scalability and solutions that amplify human potential.


### Take the leap into autonomous agents with KPMG
The collaboration between Salesforce and KPMG is helping clients design, develop, and govern these AI agents to boost efficiency and unlock new value in several ways:
  * **The AI Incubator,**[launched in 2024](https://kpmg.com/us/en/capabilities-services/alliances/kpmg-salesforce/kpmg-ai-incubator-for-agentforce.html), is an industry-driven innovation laboratory that offers a launch pad environment for ideation and validation.
  * **Proprietary frameworks for data and AI strategy adoption** enforce balance between rapid value and sustained improvements, ethics, and trustworthiness.
  * **Industry-aligned AI agents** help maximize value with prioritization on regulations and industry-specific, scalable roadmaps.
