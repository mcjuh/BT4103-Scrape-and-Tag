#  Webcast Q&A – The SF-425 Reporting, Order of Operating Expenses & Returning Interest Income
Article 6 min read
  * PRINT 
  * 

The following represents the questions we received and the answers we provided on our webcast “The SF-425 Reporting, Order of Operating Expenses & Returning Interest Income” held on March 5, 2026
If you are in need of a private one-hour training/consulting session on these topics, please [register for our SF-425 Consultation/Training](https://bdophafinance.matrixlms.com/visitor_catalog_class/show/2303823). 
### 
How would excess utility charges be treated for SF-425 reporting and for the order of operating expenditures?
add
Per HUD guidance, excess utility collections would be reported on the SF-425 as a program income deduction alternative and would take the same priority of rental collections for the order of operating expenditures.
### 
How would fraud recovery be treated for SF-425 reporting and for the order of operating expenditures? 
add
Per HUD guidance, fraud recovery collections would be reported on the SF-425 as a program income addition alternative and would be spent before operating subsidy if not expended for expanded uses.
### 
How would bad debt collections be treated for SF-425 reporting and for the order of operating expenditures?
add
Per HUD guidance, bad debt collections would be reported on the SF-425 as a program income addition alternative and would take the same priority of rental collections for the order of operating expenditures.
### 
We did not budget for any 1406 funds. Can we revise our Capital Fund budget?
add
Yes, a Public Housing Authority (PHA) may revise their Capital Funds budget. We recommend you review the [Capital Fund Guidebook](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/PIH-GUIDE-CF.PDF) for guidance.
### 
The beginning cash being calculated as of January 1, 2026, for SF-425, why is Budget Line Item (BLI) 1406 money not being assumed to be operating subsidy?
add
Capital Funds have different rules and regulations than public housing operating subsidies.
### 
Some of the nonrental income in the past was considered nonfederal funds that could be spent on anything you deem necessary. 
add
Pursuant to Section 9(k) of the 1937 Act (42 USC 1437g(k)), as amended, the PHA may retain nonrental income, and such amounts will not decrease the amounts PHAs receive under the Capital or Operating Fund. Nonrental income may be used for eligible purposes within the Public Housing program or the Section 8 program or to benefit the residents assisted by the PHA.
### 
When do you spend 2026 operating subsidy reserves? 
add
Operating subsidies can be spent after rental collections have been spent. The operating subsidy must be fully expended in seven years or returned to HUD per [PIH Notice 2025-20](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/PIH_2025-20.pdf).

Do we have to return interest on unencumbered funds from a lawsuit that is not from government subsidy?
add
### 
When will we get a billing statement for the interest we have to pay back? 
add
The PHA will not receive a billing statement. The PHA is responsible for tracking their grant funds and the interest earned on the grant funds. 
### 
We have USDA RD-15 program and earned interest in 2024 and 2025 on its Maintenance Reserve Account. Do we have to send that interest income to HUD? 
add
No, interest earned on restricted funds is not included. In addition, interest earned on Section 8 administrative fees is excluded from returning interest. 
### 
Could an Unliquidated Obligation be our Payment In Lieu of Taxes (PILOT)? 
add
Yes.
### 
Can you transfer funds to other Asset Management Projects (AMPs) that lose money to show you spent the subsidy in total? 
add
If a PHA transfers cash from an AMP to another AMP per Excess Cash guidance, then the initial AMP providing the cash to the receiving AMP will only report the cash as expended once the receiving AMP spends the cash.
### 
We have a cell tower on a hi-rise building. Can the income from that be used for HCV purposes? 
add
Pursuant to Section 9(k) of the 1937 Act (42 USC 1437g(k)), as amended, the PHA may retain nonrental income, and such amounts will not decrease the amounts PHAs receive under the Capital or Operating Fund. Nonrental income may be used for eligible purposes within the Public Housing program or the Section 8 program or to benefit the residents assisted by the PHA.
### 
Can nonrental income be used only for HAP or can it be for administrative purposes in regards to Section 8? 
add
Pursuant to Section 9(k) of the 1937 Act (42 USC 1437g(k)), as amended, the PHA may retain nonrental income, and such amounts will not decrease the amounts PHAs receive under the Capital or Operating Fund. Nonrental income may be used for eligible purposes within the Public Housing program or the Section 8 program, or to benefit the residents assisted by the PHA. Furthermore, cash from sources other than HAP funding must be approved by HUD to prevent terminations of housing assistance for HCV participants per [PIH Notice 2013-28](https://www.hud.gov/sites/documents/13-28pihn.pdf).
### 
How long can Capital Funds BLI 1406 funds stay in reserves before they expire? 
add
Currently, the Capital Funds don’t expire. 
### 
Why does interest income on grant funds get returned to the Department of Health and Human Services? 
add
2 CFR 200.305 (b)12 states “The recipient or subrecipient may retain up to $500 per year of interest earned on federal funds to use for administrative expenses of the recipient or subrecipient. Any additional interest earned on federal funds must be returned annually to the Department of Health and Human Services Payment Management System (PMS) through either the Automated Clearing House (ACH) network or a Fedwire Funds Service payment. All interest in excess of $500 per year must be returned to PMS regardless of whether the recipient or subrecipient was paid through PMS.” Find more information on [Returning interest income](https://pms.psc.gov/grant-recipients/returning-funds-interest.html). 
### 
Is there HUD language regarding unearned revenue? 
add
HUD implements the Government Accounting Standards Board (GASB) guidance.
### 
When did the returning of interest over $500 rule start?
add
The Cash Management Improvement Act of 1990 amended the Intergovernmental Cooperation Act of 1968. The PHA may only retain $500 of interest in total for interest earned on all federal grants. Interest earned on administrative fees in the Section 8 programs are excluded from returning interest income because they are considered a fee for service.
### 
Can you provide the steps and/or address to return the interest earnings to HHS?
add
Interest earned on grant funds must be returned annually. No specific reporting deadline, just annually. I would recommend that when the audit is complete, the PHA can return the funds. Please see the response to [question 13](https://www.bdo.com/insights/industries/government-public-sector/webcast-q-a-the-sf-425-reporting-order-of-operating-expenses-returning-interest-income#13).
### 
Are late fees considered rental income? 
add
No, please refer to [PIH Notice 2025-20](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/PIH_2025-20.pdf).
### 
Do you have to submit an SF-425 for each AMP or consolidate all the AMP activity into one SF-425?
add
A SF-425 will be submitted for each AMP each year until the subsidy is fully expended for the grant or until the end of the Period of Performance (PoP).
### 
Do you have to have expenses for the entire rental collected amount or just up to the subsidy amount?
add
The PHA is required to spend the rental collections first prior to spending the operating subsidy.
### 
The PHA must spend its rental collections before spending operating subsidy. We make a large amount of money doing third party management at year end if we have a surplus, but the overall surplus is less than our other non-operating fund income for the year. Do we need to even track this detail like you just displayed? Can't we just assume our surplus comes from other income? 
add
Per 2 CFR 200.302 and 24 CFR 990 Subpart H, a PHA must maintain all source documentation.
### 
Can't you just analyze your cash account? Why go through the cash flow process?
add
Please refer to question 20. The PHA has the discretion to track their activity that complies with the grant requirements. 
### 
It seems like the PHA would want to retain our Capital Fund BLI 1406 and pre-2026 reserves as long as possible. Is that true? 
add
The PHA must spend their rental income first, then their operating subsidy. It is HUD’s expectation that nonrental income is expended in the same funding year in which operating funds are appropriated. Retaining BLI 1406 funds and pre-2026 reserves seems like a practical methodology.
### 
Our PHA applies for operating subsidy for properties that we do not own (these properties are owned by developers and have public housing units in them). We draw the operating subsidy from eLOCCS and send to these properties. Are we responsible for ensuring that these properties are complying with cash management and the order of operating expenditures?
add
HUD will be publishing FAQs in the near future and will address this. 
### 
When are SF- 425's due?
add
PHAs will be required to submit an annual SF-425 to report on the status of funds for the period ending December 31 each year. The SF-425 is due by the following April 30. PHAs must submit SF-425s annually until the PHA submits an SF-425 that reports a zero balance of unobligated funds, unliquidated obligations, or cash on hand, which may be earlier than the end of the Period of Performance identified in the Annual Operating Subsidy Processing Notice.
### 
The suggestion to continue drawing operating subsidy 1/12 monthly is not consistent with PIH Notice 2025-20 that says the op sub money has to be immediately expended. 
add
24 CFR 990 allows the PHA to draw 1/12 of their operating subsidy monthly. PIH Notice 2025-20 does not state that a PHA cannot draw 1/12 of the subsidy monthly. 
### 
What if my PHA doesn’t follow asset management because we are under 400 units, but we do have three AMPS though?
add
All of the requirements for SF-425 reporting, returning interest income on grant funds, and the order of operating expenditures still apply. 
### 
When do we start the deferred revenue recording process for operating subsidies? 
add
The process will start with calendar 2026 operating subsidy. For example, if the PHA is March 2026 year-end, then the year-end reporting would require a liability as of March 31, 2026, for any 2026 operating subsidy drawn from eLOCCS and not spent. 
### 
Is there still a requirement to have three months of total expenditures held in reserves? If so, should that three months of reserve be exempt from these requirements and assumption? 
add
Currently, HUD plans to maintain the Financial Assessment Sub-System (FASS) ratios and not adjust scoring. HUD also plans to adjust the Financial Data Schedule (FDS) to score the FASS ratios for the new methodology of reporting deferred revenue/liability.
### 
How do funds from Section 18 dispositions impact these new rules?
add
Please refer to PIH Notice 2025-20 section seven regarding non-operating fund program income. 
### 
Does each AMP keep $500 of interest earned on its operating subsidy? 
add
Please see response to [question 15](https://www.bdo.com/insights/industries/government-public-sector/webcast-q-a-the-sf-425-reporting-order-of-operating-expenses-returning-interest-income#15). 
### 
Do you have to spend your current year operating subsidy prior to spending excess operating subsidy from prior year?
add
That is not required. A best practice would be to spend your oldest operating subsidy first.
### 
Do we reach out to BDO to get assistance with building the spreadsheet? 
add
Yes, PHAs can contact BDO and schedule a one-hour personal training/consulting session. Please feel free to [schedule a time](https://bdophafinance.matrixlms.com/visitor_class_catalog). 
### 
If a PHA has antenna income for an AMP, how would the PHA separate that income from the others, or do you need to return interest on the income as well? 
add
The PHA is only required to return interest income on federal grant funding. 
### 
Is interest income on grant funds returned for public housing and Section 8? 
add
Interest income is returned for all interest earned over $500 on federal grants that the PHA administers. Section 8 administrative fees are excluded from this requirement because the administrative fees earned are considered a fee-for-service. 
### 
Would payments received from collection loss count as rental income?
add
As of now, HUD has not commented on this. 
### 
Are we allowed to keep current and future interest earned on reserve balance prior to January 1, 2026? 
add
No, prior reserves are considered an operating subsidy due to the order of operating expenditures, and the amount of interest earned on those reserves would be included for payment back to HUD. 
### 
If you must pay back interest from reserves, is it a five-year lookback or from the beginning of time? Should that be recorded as a liability at December 31, 2025? 
add
The PHA is not required to pay back interest that has been received in past years. Please see response to [question 36](https://www.bdo.com/insights/industries/government-public-sector/webcast-q-a-the-sf-425-reporting-order-of-operating-expenses-returning-interest-income#36). 
### 
Does any interest we earned on any type of account in 2025 over the $500 need to be sent back to HUD? Our agency has many CDs from very long ago that earn a large amount in interest. Does it all need to be sent back to HUD? 
add
See response to [question 34](https://www.bdo.com/insights/industries/government-public-sector/webcast-q-a-the-sf-425-reporting-order-of-operating-expenses-returning-interest-income#34). 
### 
Can you offset bank fees with interest earned from investment? 
add
We recommend you follow GAAP/GASB principles for accounting and reporting. The PHA can negotiate interest rates and fees for the best interest of the PHA.
### 
For a RAD conversion to LIHTC with a PBV HAP contract, how should the portion of operating subsidy transferred to Section 8 to fund PBV HAP be reported through December 31? How is this reported on the SF-425? 
add
[HUD Accounting Brief# 22](https://ahacpa.org/resources/brief_22.pdf) provides clear guidance to account and report operating subsidy transfers for RAD. The amount of the transfer(s) to the RAD entity would be reported on the SF-425 as cash disbursed. 
### 
Do June 30, 2025, through December 31, 2025, year-ends with audited submissions have to report a payable to HUD for interest. 
add
A payable would only start for March 31, 2026, year-ends? [PIH Notice 2024-25](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/2024-25pihn.pdf) requires the returning of interest income on grant funds over $500. Yes, it is required for 2025. 
### 
Nonrental income is NOT included in the calculation of subsidy to draw, correct? 
add
Rental income (collections) must be spent prior to spending the operating subsidy. Nonrental income does not follow the order of operating expenditures per [PIH Notice 2025-20](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/PIH_2025-20.pdf).
### 
What is considered rental income? Is it tenant rent only or does it include garbage fees, late fees, etc.? 
add
Rental income currently consists of tenant rental charges only.
### 
Since my PHA also serves as the Central Office Cost Center (COCC) for a nonprofit and for Section 8, can I defend the idea that all of my PHA reserves are a result of reimbursements?
add
I believe I can prove $0 in reserves is considered subsidy. Cash management and the order of operating expenditures applies to the public housing program and not the COCC. Any reserves prior to 2026 in the Public Housing program would be considered operating subsidy unless the PHA can prove otherwise. 
### 
How would HUD determine that operating reserve balances were derived from operating subsidy and not other sources of funds? 
add
This responsibility would reside with the PHA to prove the pre-2026 reserves are not operating subsidy. 
### 
Do you suggest we keep these funds in separate accounts to keep track of this, instead of putting it all into one account? 
add
The PHA has the flexibility to decide how to account and report activity. Please see response to [question 20](https://www.bdo.com/insights/industries/government-public-sector/webcast-q-a-the-sf-425-reporting-order-of-operating-expenses-returning-interest-income#20). 
### 
Are there FDS line items with respect to program income vs non-operating subsidy program income, etc.? 
add
Per the [FDS Line Definition Guide](https://efpradvisory.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/FDS-Line-Definition-Guide-May-2012.pdf), FDS line 70300 is for rental income charges, FDS 70400 is other tenant income, and FDS line 71500 is other income. There are several other categories of revenue as well located in the guide. In addition, we are reporting on cash collections and expenditures on the SF-425, and many of these FDS line items are reported on an accrued basis. This would not be a clean reconciliation. 
### 
Does collection loss income have to be spent before operating subsidy?
add
Currently, only rental collections must be spent first prior to spending operating subsidy per [PIH Notice 2025-20](https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/PIH_2025-20.pdf). This can be subject to change as HUD implements the cash management requirements.
