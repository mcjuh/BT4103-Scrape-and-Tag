# Construction Expert Services
Expert analysis, testimony and advice for construction and engineering disputes and projects.
Recognized for the quality and independence of our insight, we work on a wide variety of construction and engineering disputes across six continents. Our delay, quantum, project and construction management and technical experts have been appointed on some of the world’s largest and most complex projects. This integrated capability enables a single, joined-up service offering that delivers coherent and consistent opinions across technical, program, and financial issues in disputes and complex project advisory engagements.
We have extensive testifying experience in litigation and international and domestic arbitration under various rules, including those of the ICC, ICSID, LCIA, ICDR, HKIAC, and SIAC. Our team provides clear guidance on all aspects of construction-related issues, from delay and disruption, project and construction management, through to disruption-related costs, valuation of variations and change, termination, and insolvency. We are engaged at every stage of the dispute process, from pre-action advice through to oral and written expert testimony.
## Construction Expert Services
We help businesses and asset owners manage challenges and uncertainty throughout the project lifecycle.
Our end-to-end services include project structuring, risk management, operational due diligence and restructuring, project turnaround, project finance and debt restructuring and M&A evaluation and integration. We also provide interim management services, including business plan development and implementation and executive placement.
Our experts draw on extensive experience managing major projects and disputes to anticipate, mitigate, and resolve issues before they escalate. By combining technical, program, and commercial expertise, we help parties avoid delays, reduce risk, and control costs so they can achieve their objectives.
## Delay and Disruption 
Our delay experts have extensive international experience, having been appointed on many leading, global projects.
We deliver independent expert advice on matters of delay and disruption, program management, planning and programming in construction and engineering disputes worldwide. In the event of a project delay, our team of independent experts are adept at establishing the critical path and undertaking an objective analysis of the issues.
Our professionals provide independent expert determination, expert witness and arbitration services in all dispute resolution forums, including litigation, arbitration and adjudication. We are recognized for the quality, integrity and independence of our insight and analysis, and our delay experts are acknowledged by Who’s Who Legal as leading practitioners in their fields.
## Quantum
Our quantum experts have been appointed on many of the world's most complex and high-value construction and engineering disputes.
We undertake rigorous and thorough quantum analysis and present clear and cogent independent evidence. Our oral evidence and written reports are called on in various dispute forums, including adjudication, arbitration and litigation.
Drawing on a wealth of industry experience, our quantum experts deliver independent expert advice on valuation and costs management (including costs arising from delay and disruption), professional negligence and termination. We also advise on the valuation of variations or compensation events and insurance-related issues. Our quantum experts are acknowledged by Who’s Who Legal as leading practitioners in the field and are recognized for their outstanding work on disputes across a wide range of sectors.
## Project Management 
Drawing on in-depth industry experience across the full project lifecycle, from inception to handover and operation, we assess the performance of professional project managers.
Our project management experts have years of practical experience delivering complex projects as professional project managers. We use this to confidently assess how an appropriately qualified and competent project manager would interpret and implement its contractual obligations.
In evaluating the project manager’s performance, we establish whether the professional has exercised all the reasonable skill, care and diligence to be expected. Our expert opinion informs counsels’ liability and negligence assessments of the project managers’ performance.
## Construction Management
Drawing on practical experience of delivering projects as professional construction managers, we provide independent advice in dispute avoidance, dispute resolution and strategic decision-making. 
We assist at any stage of the project to maximize value and reduce exposure to the risks associated with a construction management approach. Issues we are regularly appointed to advise on include engineering, procurement and construction management performance on large-scale, high-value and complex EPCM contracts.
Our practical experience in delivering some of the most complex, global projects provides us with unrivaled expertise and insight. 
## Damages
Our experts are regularly instructed and give evidence in construction disputes and arbitration. We have experience working on projects globally, both in relation to investment treaty claims acting for and against the state, and in commercial and contractual disputes between companies.
Whether it is delays in the development of mines, consequential losses from the delay of property development or losses arising from aborted construction projects, we provide specialist expertise in evaluating wasted costs and valuing historical and future losses.
## Technical Experts
Our technical experts possess extensive international engineering knowledge and substantial practical experience across complex construction, infrastructure, energy and industrial projects. Working in close collaboration with our delay, disruption, quantum and project management specialists, we offer a fully integrated and methodical approach to resolving technical issues.
We provide independent and rigorously objective advice on matters arising from design, construction, operation and performance. Our expertise encompasses materials and corrosion, mechanical integrity, fire engineering, MEP, rail infrastructure, telecommunications, electrical engineering, rotating equipment and structural engineering.
Our professionals are skilled in evaluating defects, failures and non-compliance, delivering clear, authoritative opinions. We are recognized for the quality, integrity and independence of our technical analysis and for our ability to present complex engineering issues clearly and consistently to tribunals and courts.
