## **How we can help**
HKA’s depth of expertise and global reach ensure a rapid and flexible response to the complex requirements of our clients, regardless of scale or scope. Assignments typically demand specialist capabilities in areas such as:
### ****Conduct****
Our investigations and regulatory experts help firms take control when misconduct has been identified. We help firms to establish the facts, manage regulatory communications, and enhance control environments to safeguard against future misconduct.
### **Digital Forensics & Data Security**
We support complex digital investigations, including eDiscovery, analytics, high-tech crime, blockchain forensics, data breaches and cybersecurity. Our AI-enabled tools help clients mitigate risk and respond to litigation, regulatory, and security challenges.
### **Economic Damages & Valuation**
We provide robust assessment of economic damages, business valuations and asse impacts – including IP – in disputes involving joint ventures, mergers and acquisitions, and competition. We provide independent assessment of end-to-end financial crime risk management programmes (including [section 166 reports in the UK](https://www.hka.com/flyer/s166-skilled-person-reviews/)) and work with firms to remediate system, controls and governance frameworks. As an independent firm, we offer conflict-free access to top-tier talent. We investigate fraud, regulatory breaches, white-collar crime and bankruptcy across jurisdictions, combining deep accounting knowledge with advanced financial forensics and data analytics.

Acting for both insured parties and insurers, our specialists use sophisticated valuation methods to quantify losses. With in-house technical expertise in architecture, engineering, fire safety, and an array of other disciplines, we help prove root causes and extent of damage.
