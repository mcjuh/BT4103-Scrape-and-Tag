## Key M&A Trends for 2026: What’s Shaping the Next Deal Cycle
  * ### The Return of Middle-Market Momentum
After lagging in 2025, middle-market M&A activity is expected to rebound in 2026 as valuations stabilize, financing conditions improve, and buyer confidence returns—reigniting deal flow across priority sectors.
  * ### AI as a Competitive Differentiator
AI is reshaping the M&A process by accelerating target identification, diligence, and deal execution. These capabilities are widening the speed and insight gap between private equity firms and corporate acquirers that lag in AI adoption.
  * ### Greater Selectivity and Strategic Discipline
In a more competitive environment, leading acquirers are prioritizing strategic fit, synergy realization, and long-term value creation over deal volume—raising the bar for both targets and deal theses.
  * ### Execution and Integration Under Pressure
Post-close execution remains a critical risk area. Process gaps, unclear ownership, and weak integration handoffs continue to be the biggest sources of value leakage in M&A, increasing pressure on integration readiness.
  * ### Rising Competitive and Regulatory Complexity
New buyer profiles, heightened regulatory scrutiny, and shifting trade and industrial policies are adding complexity to the M&A landscape—reshaping deal timelines, diligence priorities, and risk management strategies.
