### Bringing the future of healthcare delivery into focus
There are hundreds of ways that technology is transforming the way healthcare is delivered and managed. We’re here to help you understand which could have the biggest impact on patient outcomes and your operations, and which are most deserving of your attention and your modernization budgets.
Earlier this year, we created our first [Looking Glass](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/looking-glass) report, examining more than 120 technology trends through six “lenses” to help businesses understand what those trends could mean for them. Now, we’re taking that analysis a level deeper, looking specifically at what some of these trends could mean for the healthcare sector, and how they’re shaping the future of healthcare delivery.
