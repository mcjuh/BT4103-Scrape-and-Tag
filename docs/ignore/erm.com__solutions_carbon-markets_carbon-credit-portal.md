## **Build a better carbon credit portfolio with less stress**
Your company may need to buy carbon credits to achieve a rapidly approaching climate goal.But the complexity and riskiness of today’s carbon markets often leave sustainability and procurement teams overwhelmed—and too often, on the sidelines, out of the carbon credit market.ERM, the world’s largest sustainability consulting firm, has built a carbon credit purchasing solution that takes the guesswork and anxiety out of these important transactions. With ERM’s Carbon Credit Portal, corporate buyers can make progress towards their climate goals with confidence and ease. That’s great news for the climate and communities around the world that benefit from carbon credit projects.
Why ERM's Carbon Credit Portal
## Fifty years working with corporate sustainability, procurement, and environmental compliance professionals means we know what you need in a credit procurement solution. 
### Your carbon credit journey is streamlined and easy
You do not need experience with sourcing, evaluating, contracting, or managing carbon credits to use ERM’s Carbon Credit Portal. Built on the CEEZER platform, our portal is secure and intuitive. Every user receives personalized support from ERM’s expert advisors and can tap into their technical expertise to understand project risks, methodologies, and co-benefits. 
### Your carbon credit options come pre-screened for integrity
Many carbon credits have questionable impact and can leave your company vulnerable to allegations of greenwashing. Every credit for sale on ERM’s portal has been screened by ERM’s carbon market experts, providing you with greater confidence. 
### Your options are curated according to your priorities
Don’t waste time browsing credits that have hidden dealbreakers. When you buy credits with ERM, you’ll see a curated list of projects designed to match your unique selection criteria, including geography, project type, and price point. 
### Your expert advisors are impartial 
Unlike other advisors, we do not own the credits we help sell. We have no financial stake in the credits we recommend or the credits you buy. That means that our recommendations are based on only one thing: your success. 
### You can build and manage your whole portfolio in one place
Portal users can import credits purchased off platform giving a complete picture of your carbon credit portfolio. Users can plan for future procurements and track total emission reductions. 
### Our pricing is simple, transparent, and risk-free
Our pricing model is simple: When you purchase credits on the ERM Carbon Credit Portal, you pay the price charged by the credits’ seller, plus a transparent transaction fee to ERM. If you are not satisfied with your options and do not complete a transaction, you are not billed.
