### Frequently Asked Questions
### 
What is an ESOP?
  * An Employee Stock Ownership Plan or ESOP is a qualified defined contribution benefit plan (like a 401K) with two special benefits: 
    * ESOPs are designed to invest primarily in the stock of the sponsoring company and, as such, ESOPs are exempt from the diversification requirements of other qualified plans. 
    * ESOPs are allowed to borrow money to fund the purchase of employer securities
  * Created by Congress in 1974 to enable employees to participant in the wealth creation process
  * To encourage the formation of ESOPs, Congress put in place significant tax incentives
