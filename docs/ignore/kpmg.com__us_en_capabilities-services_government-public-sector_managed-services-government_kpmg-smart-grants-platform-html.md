## KPMG Smart Grants Platform
Power your grants programs to streamline work, accelerate decisions, and move funding to communities faster. 
Grants programs face growing challenges and expanding expectations. Agencies are juggling more applicants, more regulations, and more pressure to deliver results—and often with systems that can’t keep up with today’s demands. 
**The KPMG Smart Grants Platform helps organizations meet that challenge.** Built for and by practitioners, this fully managed approach brings automation, AI, and built-in controls into a single, modern environment that streamlines grant administration and helps agencies accelerate the process with greater accuracy and transparency across multiple programs.
## A unified platform for modern grants management
The KPMG Smart Grants Platform is a managed, extensive digital solution that helps to improve crucial stages of the grants lifecycle. Built with automation, AI, and intuitive design, it simplifies complex processes while strengthening accuracy, compliance, and visibility. We designed the platform to help bridge the gap between rigid off-the-shelf tools and fully custom-built systems, with a configurable solution that adapts to program needs without the complexity or overhead of building from scratch.
The KPMG Smart Grants Platform supports program administration from application intake through execution and closeout. The platform includes integrated case management, automation-enabled workflows, and a user-friendly interface that can be tailored to the needs of each program. And it’s built to support multiple grant programs within a single environment, helping agencies scale efficiently without adding new systems or vendors.

### Delivering essential capabilities
Application intake
Create a faster, more intuitive application process with configurable forms, guided learning, and automation that help improve data throughput and reduce the burden for both applicants and staff.
Application review and award
Strengthen the integrity and speed of reviews with case-level views, automated scoring and decision-support tools, and workflows that streamline selection and award processes.
Risk assessments
Programmatically determines the level of compliance risk through dynamic questionnaires and scoring rubrics. Enables agencies to focus oversight efforts where risk is highest and apply consistent, risk-based decision making.
Compliance monitoring
Supports ongoing, risk-driven compliance monitoring across the grant lifecycle. Centralizes milestone tracking, invoice review, and document validation to help ensure adherence to program requirements and audit readiness.
Grant closeout
Simplify closeouts with structured workflows, checklists, and document management – helping teams close cleanly and accurately with audit-ready documentation.
