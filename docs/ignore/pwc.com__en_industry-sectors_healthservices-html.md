### Strategy
We support **hospitals** in preparing to meet the challenges of the future. In doing so, we work with you to strengthen your strategic positioning so that you can focus on providing a clearly defined range of services. We outline how you can occupy an attractive niche or otherwise stand out in the market, and suggest which organisational and business models will boost your positioning. Through our tried-and-tested strategy advice, we give you the appropriate tools to develop a forward-looking hospital strategy, backed up by data and the necessary experience, and enable you to stay on track while implementing it together with your employees.
As an **outpatient service provider** , you need to future-proof your outpatient and intermediate services, pursue an appropriate services strategy and develop the associated operating concepts. For example, you should formulate ideal models for outpatient locations, outpatient units within your hospital and other outpatient services and optimise them according to needs.
We help improve your efficiency in the outpatient and intermediate service spectrum and ensure you make optimal use of your resources, including employees and infrastructure. At the same time, it’s important that your facility remains attractive for employees and patients. We present you with the latest market developments, trends and demand forecasts in a clear format, explain new forms of care, and use workshops to develop innovative concepts for an outpatient and intermediate care structure that is perfectly adapted to your situation. We work with you to align your outpatient service delivery so that you stand out against the competition and continue to be a frontrunner in the outpatient market.

### Data protection
Digitisation in healthcare continues to progress at all levels. Administrative processes are increasingly being executed digitally in hospitals and at outpatient service providers, and digital data exchange between hospitals and outpatient service providers and health insurers is already established. This **digital networking of the healthcare sector** will continue to expand in future and become the driving force behind **more effective and efficient patient care**. Hospitals and outpatient service providers are also seeking to outsource their data processing to the **cloud** for reasons of efficiency and effectiveness. Alongside the opportunities, this also poses **risks, such as unauthorised disclosure of data to unauthorised third parties or hacker attacks** , which give rise to a variety of complex issues in the area of data privacy and information security.
We’re here to support you on your legally compliant journey into the cloud, and will clear a path through the jungle of regulatory requirements. As the first step, we’ll carry out our tried-and-tested data protection gap assessment to show you where you are on your journey and what data protection risks you’re exposed to. In a second step, we’ll help you take concrete action to implement your data protection and information security strategy, as part of your intended move to the cloud.

### Additional benefit concepts
The Swiss Insurance Association (SIA) has adopted principles for the definition, valuation, billing and future of additional benefits in accordance with the Swiss Insurance Contracts Act (VVG; SICA) by introducing the industry framework ‘Mehrleistungen VVG’ (additional benefits under SICA). **PwC helped to develop these principles.** Supplementary health insurers are responsible for implementing them in cooperation with service providers. As a leading advisory firm for additional benefits concepts, we’ll assist you in developing a plan of action for your additional benefits and help you plan how to implement the most important measures. This could include introducing a valuation mechanism, establishing effective controlling, developing negotiation strategies, making adjustments to the model contract, drawing up a combined controlling and governance concept, and reporting to the regulator.
