# Diversified Industrials Investment Banking
Industrials expertise for middle-market M&A transactions.
Featured Case Studies
Kroll's Industrials Investment Banking practice focuses on the middle-market, advising privately-owned businesses, financial sponsors and public corporations on buy-side, sell-side and capital-raising transactions.
We are known for our creative and hands-on approach leveraging deep industry knowledge and global expertise to elicit robust interest from both financial and strategic investors or financing partners. Kroll manages tight, cohesive processes that support strong outcomes for the shareholders and management of industrial companies.
Our international presence and wide-reaching resources differentiate Kroll from other middle-market M&A advisors, providing our clients with valuable global transaction resources.
