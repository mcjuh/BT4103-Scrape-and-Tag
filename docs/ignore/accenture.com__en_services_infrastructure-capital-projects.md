##  Develop and optimize capital projects for successful execution 
Close
Capital projects succeed or fail based on the quality of the decisions made upfront - around scope, cost, risk, and how the project will be delivered. We help you move from idea to approval with clearer options and fewer surprises, combining human experience with AI insights to bring greater clarity and confident decision‑making.
  * **Project organization, governance, and processes** -We help project leaders stand up a fit-for-purpose project organization, governance, and cadence that drives accountability from day one, roles, decision rights, stage gates, and risk ownership that work at scale. 
  * **Project planning, scoping and feasibility** -We use a structured early phase project definition process to develop a robust scope and business case, run data driven analyses to test assumptions and trade-offs, and identify the decisions that matter most so your project is ready for approval. 
  * **Licensing and permitting** -From site evaluation through construction, we help manage permitting end-to-end by translating regulatory requirements into integrated plans and coordinating studies and compliance processes that reduce risk and protect timelines. 
  * **Integrate the project supply chain** -Engage contractors and suppliers early to influence design, secure long-lead capacity, and reduce commercial uncertainty. By mapping critical materials and deploying technology, you can predict and mitigate supply chain constraints and delivery risks. 
  * **Delivery method and contracting strategy** -Select the right delivery method and contracting approach based on scope, risk appetite, market conditions, and owner capability. We help you design risk allocation and commercial incentives that align owner, contractor, and supply chain outcomes within a governance framework. 
  * **Estimating project cost and schedule** -From early planning to detailed estimates we use our experience alongside AI and historical benchmark data from comparable projects to develop transparent, detailed and investment ready cost estimates and schedules that improve accuracy and target value improvements. 
  * **Stakeholder and process integration** -We design engagement strategies grounded in stakeholder mapping, consultation planning, and clear communications. We synthesize feedback into data-driven actions and use sentiment monitoring to detect issues early and maintain support.

##  Integrate sustainability and social responsibility from the start 
Close
From changing government policies to the rapid evolution of net-zero technologies, organizations face uncertainty around requirements, which is increasing risk. Sustainability and social responsibility are now non-negotiable and must be embedded at the heart of every project.
  * **Design and build sustainably** -Sustainability decisions made late in design can increase cost and limit impact. We help you integrate sustainability into project design and construction, optimizing materials, energy use, sourcing, and construction methods, so your assets are both designed for energy efficient operations and built sustainably. 
  * **Compliance and ESG reporting** -Accuracy in reporting and ESG compliance is crucial. Digital tools such as carbon accounting tools, smart sensors, and digital twins can help reduce the compliance burden that often slows down projects. 
  * **Net zero infrastructure** - We aim to exceed carbon neutrality standards by focusing on minimizing rework and waste, using low energy designs, and efficient prefabrication techniques. 
  * **Stakeholder and community engagement** - Using tools like stakeholder analysis, community engagement, and sentiment analysis, we help you work closely with stakeholders and local communities to spot and reduce risks early, keeping projects on time, on budget, and trusted by the people they affect. 
  * **Carbon cost management** -We go beyond basic carbon accounting, integrating advanced carbon analytics with industry-leading cost management to provide data-driven insight into embodied and operational carbon, enabling informed, financially viable sustainability decisions across the full asset lifecycle.

These cookies don't collect information that identifies a visitor. All information these cookies collect is anonymous and is only used to improve how the website works. Third party vendors may have access to this data and may use it to improve their overall services and offerings. _Functionality cookies_ allow a site to remember choices you make (such as your username, language or the region you are in) and provide more enhanced, personal features. These cookies cannot track your browsing activity on other websites. They don’t gather any information about you that could be used for advertising or remembering where you’ve been on the Internet outside our site.

#### Advertising and Social Media Cookies
Advertising and Social Media Cookies
Advertising and social media cookies (including web beacons and other tracking and storage technologies) are used to (1) deliver advertisements more relevant to you and your interests; (2) limit the number of times you see an advertisement; (3) help measure the effectiveness of the advertising campaign; (4) retargeting to Accenture websites/information and (5) understand people’s behavior after they view an advertisement. They are usually placed on behalf of advertising networks with the site operator’s permission. They remember that you have visited a site and quite often they will be linked to site functionality provided by the other organization. This may impact the content and messages you see on other websites you visit. If you do not allow these cookies you may not be able to use or see these sharing tools or play certain videos on our site.
