The Forrester Wave™: AI Services, Q2 2024
[**BCG was named a leader in AI services**](https://reprints2.forrester.com/#/assets/2/1878/RES180736/report) in  _The Forrester Wave™: AI Services, Q2 2024_ , an evaluation of the 9 most significant AI service providers, deeming participating vendors Leaders, Strong Performers, Contenders, or Challengers. Based on its performance, BCG was one of the two companies named a Leader.Forrester reported, “Boston Consulting Group excels at delivering AI with business impact. BCG challenges its clients’ assumptions of AI impact by developing innovative AI solutions with a pragmatic approach led by business expertise… [BCG’s] vision stands out, focused on its clients becoming more competitive with AI while bringing notable clarity about the investments in areas such as R&D, technical development, talent quality, and sector-specific capabilities. BCG provides a robust community to clients that includes peer-to-peer opportunities and networking with AI luminaries.”
The Forrester Wave™: Digital Transformation Services, Q4 2023
**BCG was named a leader in digital transformation services** in _The Forrester Wave™: Digital Transformation Services Q4 2023,_ an evaluation assessing 15 digital transformation providers that they deemed “the top vendors in the market.” Participating vendors were categorized as Leaders, Strong Performers, Contenders, and Challengers. BCG was one of five vendors identified as a Leader.The report stated that “BCG’s emphasis on business strategy consulting and organizational transformation is reflected in its strong capabilities in these areas, relying on established C-suite relationships to create buy-in and collaboration across the organization. With BCG X (the firm’s tech build and design unit), it has established a strong differentiator in business model innovation based on a vast catalog of prebuilt components to quickly assemble new business units while co-innovating with clients to further expand the IP catalog.”

ALM Vanguard: Organization Strategy Consulting
**BCG was ranked as the number one leader in 2019 by ALM Intelligence,** which assesses firms on their ability to create impact for their clients**.** BCG ranked number one across breadth of capabilities, depth of capabilities, and client impact. BCG also retained its best-practice status in the “Enabling Tools” category for the second year running because of the strength of the proprietary tools underpinning its organization strategy consulting methodologies. In addition, BCG stood out for its ability to connect the products, services, and capabilities of its broader ecosystem to leverage data and [emerging technologies](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/emerging-technologies/deep-tech) as it helps clients to execute large-scale organization strategies.
ALM Vanguard: Communications and Change Management Consulting
[**BCG was named a leading change management provider in 2019**](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/organization-strategy/culture-change-management)**.** ALM Intelligence recognized BCG’s comprehensive approach, noting that the firm excels in its ability to steer clients through large-scale change initiatives from strategy through execution with agile transformation management. The approach stands out for its holistic nature, enabling clients to manage three journeys simultaneously: leader, people, and organization. It instills discipline and data science into program management and creates an “engine room” for change with an innovative design for a physical space. Clients value BCG’s hands-on, practical, and technical approach to change, as well as its coaching capabilities.
ALM Vanguard: Joint Ventures and Alliances Consulting

2024 SIIA CODiE Award: Best Marketing Solution - Fabriq by BCG
**BCG and**[**BCG X**](https://www.bcg.com/x/)**won the 2024 CODiE Award for Best Marketing Solution for**[**Fabriq by BCG**](https://www.bcg.com/x/product-library/fabriq-marketing-personalization-platform)**.** The award recognizes the best solution that enables companies to plan, target, market, and measure the effectiveness of campaigns promoting products and services to consumers and/or businesses online, off-line and via mobile devices. The CODiE Awards judges noted, "Fabriq empowers marketers with a powerful suite of tools for real-time personalization, media spend optimization, and seamless campaign management with existing tech stack. Its ability to bridge the tech gap, cater to diverse campaign goals, and deliver measurable results makes it a super competitive solution for brands looking to personalize customer experiences and maximize marketing ROI."
2023 SIIA CODiE Award: Best Artificial Intelligence Driven Technology Solution - Growth AI By BCG
**BCG and**[**BCG X**](https://www.bcg.com/x/)**won the CODiE Award for Best Artificial Intelligence Driven Technology Solution for its tool Growth AI by BCG.** The awardrecognizes the best native artificial intelligence, machine, or deep learning solution that learns, performs, and excels at specific automated tasks. Growth AI’s holistic suite of AI offerings maximizes the growth potential of consumer packaged goods companies by supporting data-driven decisions across and within standard commercial functions, including pricing and promotion, marketing allocation, product, and more.
