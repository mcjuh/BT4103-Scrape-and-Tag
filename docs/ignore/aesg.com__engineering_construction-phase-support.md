## Construction Engineering Support & Risk Management
AESG provides practical, on-site and remote engineering support during construction to ensure designs are implemented safely, efficiently, and to the highest quality. Our expertise helps manage risk, resolve technical challenges, and optimise constructability.
## Our Services
## Construction Stage Engineering Support
Providing on-site and remote engineering support during construction to ensure designs are implemented safely and effectively.
### Ensures design compliance and safety during construction
### Supports efficient problem-solving on site
### Minimises delays and rework
### Enhances overall project quality and performance
## Site Inspections & Compliance Reviews
Conducting inspections and reviews to verify construction quality, code compliance, and adherence to design specifications.
### Confirms workmanship meets design standards
### Ensures regulatory and code compliance
### Reduces construction risks and potential defects
### Provides confidence to clients and stakeholders
## Value Engineering & Constructability Advice
Advising on construction methods and design optimisations to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and mitigate risks.
### Optimises materials, time, and construction costs
### Improves buildability and workflow efficiency
### Reduces project risk and potential rework
### Enhances overall project value and quality
## Technical Responses to RFIs & Site Queries
Providing rapid, expert technical responses to construction queries to maintain progress and mitigate risks.
### Minimises construction delays
### Ensures issues are resolved in line with design intent
### Reduces risk of costly errors or rework
### Supports smooth communication between design and site teams
