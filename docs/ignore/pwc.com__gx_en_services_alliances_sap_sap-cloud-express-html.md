## Cloud Express: Empowering transformation through AI
We’re delighted to launch Cloud Express - a suite of solutions enabling us to flexibly deploy [SAP Business Suite](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/services/alliances/sap/pwc-sap-ai-business-transformation.html) in weeks. Crafted with our trusted expertise and industry-leading practices, they have been honed over decades of collaborating with organisations worldwide, providing businesses with a reliable way forward. The core offering of our Cloud Express suite comprises of:
  * Cloud ERP Express: Establishes a solid business foundation on SAP Cloud ERP in just eight weeks, all while ensuring adherence to regulatory and tax requirements.
  * Human Experience Management Express (HXM Express): Utilises SAP SuccessFactors to streamline HR processes comprehensively.
  * Integration and Extensibility Express: Pre-defined integrations and extensions built on years of project experience to meet evolving business needs.
  * Industry ready solutions: Supported by industry-specific deployment templates, starting with professional services, manufacturing and retail, helping address nuanced challenges.
