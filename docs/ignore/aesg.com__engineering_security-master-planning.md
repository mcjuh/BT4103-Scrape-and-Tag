At AESG, our Security Master Planning Services are delivered by our expert Security Consultants, following a thorough Security[ _Threat & Risk Assessment_. ](https://aesg.com/engineering/threat-and-risk-assessments/)A Security Master Plan is a comprehensive document that includes reports, drawings, and illustrations outlining your organisation’s security strategies, goals, plans, policies, and procedures. This plan provides a detailed overview of security risks and the mitigation strategies agreed upon by all stakeholders.
## Why Early Security Planning Matters
For urban design professionals—including landscape architects, civil engineers, architects, and urban planners—as well as creators of public or private spaces such as developers, builders, corporate organisations, and government departments, integrating security during the earliest development phases is both efficient and cost-effective. Implementing a Security Master Planning document at the conceptual stages of a project ensures robust security measures are embedded within the built environment from the ground up.

## Security Zones and Space Classification
Our Security Master Planning Services focus on the demarcation and optimal use of space throughout your project. AESG categorises spaces based on:
## Intended Use and Function
### Defining the purpose of each area.
## User Groups and Access Permissions
### Determining who can access specific spaces.
## Asset Value, Sensitivity, and Attractiveness
### Assessing the importance and vulnerability of assets within each zone.
We employ security zones to group spaces with similar access management levels, ensuring a structured and effective security framework.
