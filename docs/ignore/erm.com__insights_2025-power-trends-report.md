# 2025 Power Trends Report
The power sector is undergoing a transformative shift. After years of stagnant power demand, power systems are now facing increased pressure due to rising demand growth driven by emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) and the growing electrification of energy systems. Concurrently, the energy transition and the integration of renewable energy sources into the grid are further straining power systems.
At this time of change, power sector actors must embrace innovative solutions that enhance reliability, bolster workforce safety, and drive the energy transition. By doing so, they can build a resilient, sustainable, and future-ready power system that meets the demands of the evolving energy landscape.
