#  TMT Strategy and Transactions Consulting 
EY-Parthenon TMT Strategy and Transactions Consulting teams help develop and provide transformative growth, transaction, digital and value creation strategies. We help technology, media and entertainment, and telecommunications organizations build greater financial and operational resilience and technical agility, supporting their future growth within an ever-changing environment.

## What the EY-Parthenon TMT Strategy and Transactions Consulting teams can do for you
Technology, media and telecommunications (TMT) companies are under constant pressure to reinvent themselves to stay relevant and seize emerging opportunities. Staying ahead of the competition demands more than adaptation — it requires a bold embrace of rapid technological innovation, a keen understanding of evolving consumer expectations and a commitment to pioneering new products and services. By navigating disruptive business models, adapting to shifting regulations and harnessing the power of analytics and artificial intelligence (AI) for sharper insights and smarter decisions, TMT businesses can thrive in an ever-changing marketplace.
EY-Parthenon teams can help you address your most important questions about excelling in a highly competitive environment:
## Transformative strategy
  * How can we develop a cloud, cybersecurity and AI growth strategy to accelerate enterprise value?
  * How can we transform digital streaming strategy, content personalization and advertising revenue models?
  * How can we scale 5G and IoT strategy, enhance customer experience, and reduce churn?


## Transformative transactions
  * How do we identify and execute M&A strategy for digital innovation and competitive advantage?
  * How can transactions unlock value through content expansion, audience growth and revenue synergies?
  * How can M&A support telecom diversification beyond traditional voice and data services?


EY-Parthenon TMT Strategy and Transactions Consulting teams assist clients in crafting transformative growth strategies by offering insights into the market, competition and customer behavior. Our teams support organizations in implementing their strategies through significant operational changes. In addition, we assist businesses in transforming their vision into reality by leveraging strategic, financial and operational insights, as well as utilizing extensive industry experience and detailed knowledge to achieve overall execution. EY-Parthenon teams can help TMT organizations become more agile and innovative by helping them develop integrated and streamlined operations, accelerated market entry, and digital solutions that are adaptable, scalable and responsive to dynamic industry demands.
