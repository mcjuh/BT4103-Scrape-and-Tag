# Sales and Use Tax Services
Kroll provides a comprehensive suite of sales and use tax services to assist companies in complying with their sales and use tax issues and obligations.
Our tax practice applies proven controversy, recovery and compliance methodologies, while functioning as a tightly integrated consulting team mindful of our clients’ needs. Kroll is comprised of dedicated sales tax professionals with decades of experience and technical knowledge across all state and local tax jurisdictions to address the most challenging sales and use tax issues companies face.
We offer a customized approach when generating high-value tax saving ideas, solutions and process improvements. To minimize tax and related costs, we work to ensure our clients are utilizing the appropriate exemptions and planning opportunities. Our services include:
  * Transactional analysis/tax planning
  * Physical and economic nexus reviews
  * Audit representation and negotiation
  * Tax controversy and appeals
  * Turnkey compliance outsourcing services including treasury and a dedicated account representative for timely responses
  * Operational review
  * Risk reduction through voluntary disclosure agreements (VDA)
  * Tax registration and business license management
