### What We Do
Kroll is a global leader in financial services compliance, providing practical support across the full regulatory lifecycle — from authorisation and licensing to ongoing advisory. We help clients navigate FCA, PRA, FCA/PRC, HMRC and cross-border regulatory rules, requirements and registrations. Our services include building robust compliance programmes, structuring tailored risk-mitigation policies and procedures, conducting testing and mock examinations, preparing for regulatory visits and conducting operational due diligence.
Our tailored solutions include:
  * FCA and PRA authorisation, registration and variation of permissions
  * Appointed Representative and regulatory hosting solutions
  * Retained and ad hoc compliance consulting for asset managers, brokers and financial institutions
  * AML/CTF programme development, independent testing and risk assessments
  * KYC/CDD, transaction monitoring and sanctions screening optimisation
  * Consumer Duty gap analysis, implementation and ongoing compliance support
  * Mock regulatory examination and FCA visit readiness services
  * Senior Managers and Certification Regime (SM&CR) advisory and implementation
  * Skilled person (Section 166) report support and remediation
  * Regulatory due diligence for M&A transactions
  * Enforcement and investigative support
  * Independent assessments
  * Remediation plans and corrective action reviews
