## The realities of transportation and mobility
Transportation and mobility organizations face rising cost pressure, shifting customer expectations and increasing ecosystem complexity while relying on legacy systems that limit visibility, speed and innovation.
### Legacy systems slow innovation
Fragmented, aging platforms make it difficult to coordinate engineering, manufacturing, retail and service operations or launch new digital experiences.
### Disconnected data limits engagement
Customer, vehicle and operational data often live in silos, preventing timely personalization and smarter decision-making across sales, service and ownership journeys.
### Rising costs and technical waste
Technical debt, inefficient infrastructure and inconsistent data practices drive up costs and undermine scalability across the mobility value chain.

## How transportation and mobility organizations apply AI across the business
### Unify data for personalization
Connect customer, vehicle and financial data for personalized experiences across OEM, dealer and finance touchpoints.
### Improve service diagnostics
Apply AI to analyze repair history, telematics and guidance content to reduce service times and improve first-time fix rates. 
### Automate engineering documentation
Generate specifications, architectures and test assets automatically to accelerate software development and modernization.
### Personalize sales and finance decisions
Use AI to tailor offers, pricing and financing options while reducing approval times and manual effort.
### Optimize parts and inventory
Predict parts demand using service history and regional patterns to reduce shortages and excess inventory.
### Detect quality and warranty risks
Analyze engineering, field and warranty data to identify emerging quality issues earlier and reduce investigation costs.
