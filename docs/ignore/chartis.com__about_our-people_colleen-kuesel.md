# Colleen Kuesel
Chief Marketing Officer 
Colleen Kuesel serves as Chief Marketing Officer of Chartis where she is responsible for advancing the firm’s marketing infrastructure and activities and driving the firm’s collective efforts to build Chartis’ brand equity. She brings to the firm 20 years of experience in branding, marketing, and communications for professional services organizations. 
Prior to joining Chartis, Colleen spent nearly 17 years with Ernst & Young LLP (EY) where she led brand, industry, and field marketing, internal and external communications, and business development operations functions in regional and Americas leadership roles. At EY, she was instrumental in the build of its marketing and account management functions in the US and globally and partnered with leadership teams to achieve significant change and transformation initiatives. 
Colleen has been active in speaking on marketing transformation and account-based marketing and is certified in account-based marketing by the IT Services Marketing Association (ITSMA). 
She graduated summa cum laude from Drake University with a Bachelor of Arts in journalism.

###  Why did you pick Chartis? 
There’s a unique blend of traits you find among Chartisans. Everyone’s brilliant, but you can find that many places—it’s that plus the intense passion to improve healthcare, the empathy and humility colleagues of all levels display, and the opportunity and nimbleness in a smaller firm to create big change—whether it’s internally or on behalf of our clients. 
###  Where’s your favorite place to visit and why? 
We love Grand Case, St. Martin. It’s the perfect mix of beautiful Caribbean beaches and water, culture, French food and wine, and warm and welcoming people. It’s a special place with some of the most amazing French restaurants outside of France; it’s one of the few places we return to again and again. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
Chasing after my son (quite literally!), planning my next vacation, and trying to perfect pizza in our wood fired oven.
