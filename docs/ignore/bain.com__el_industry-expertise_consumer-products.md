The consumer products industry has faced a relentless wave of disruption—from a global pandemic to inflation, supply chain breakdowns, and mounting environmental pressure. Category volumes remain soft. Pricing power is hitting its limit. And consumer behavior keeps evolving, while private label and insurgent brands gain traction across the globe.
But the best consumer packaged goods companies (CPGs) aren’t standing still. They’re leveraging technology, streamlining portfolios, and reinventing their go-to-market models to outpace the competition. Our consumer products consulting expertise enables you to navigate complexity, anticipate risk, and unlock new sources of growth. 
On your journey to full potential, we can help you:
  * Rethink your **growth algorithm** by maximizing current profit pools—through superior execution and a sharper [understanding of consumer shifts](https://www.bain.com/el/industry-expertise/consumer-products/consumer-lab/)—while making [bold portfolio moves](https://www.bain.com/insights/?filters=industries\(170\)%7cservices\(291,219423,219424,219431\)&invisibleFilters=articleRelevance\(\)) that expand existing categories or unlock entirely new ones
  * Reinvent for**sustained productivity gains** by simplifying today’s portfolio and [operating model,](https://www.bain.com/el/insights/winning-operating-models/) strengthening [supply chain](https://www.bain.com/insights/?filters=industries\(170\)%7cservices\(219419\)&invisibleFilters=articleRelevance\(\)) resilience, and sharpening the strategic focus for tomorrow’s business
  * Redefine an [AI-led](https://www.bain.com/el/vector-digital/ai-insights-and-solutions/artificial-intelligence/) **and technology-driven model** by honing your ability to roll out value-generating [digital tools](https://www.bain.com/el/industry-expertise/consumer-products/digitalization-in-consumer-products/) at scale today and reimagining your entire business for tomorrow
  * Build [sustainability](https://www.bain.com/el/consulting-services/sustainability-corporate-responsibility/) into your strategy, making a challenge an opportunity


Our clients include companies in the food, beverage, personal and household care, consumer durables, luxury goods, apparel and footwear, recreational products, and over-the-counter medicines sectors. The breadth of our experience and expertise, combined with our broad ecosystem of best-in-class partners, ensures that we tailor powerful solutions that meet your exact needs.
