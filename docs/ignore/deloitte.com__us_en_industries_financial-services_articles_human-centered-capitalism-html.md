# Human-centered capitalism
## What does the future of financial services hold? We think it’s a higher bottom line.

### The bottom line is evolving, and so is our approach to it.
In the future of financial services, the bottom line will no longer be simply a sum total of earnings and losses—it will be much more. It will be the blurred line between greater equality and more shared wealth. The line in the sand drawn by the company that lives and breathes its values rather than just publicizing them. And the line toed by organizations who realize that environmental stewardship isn’t just good for the planet—it’s good for business.
Join us in adopting a more human-centered form of capitalism. [And elevate your bottom line until it’s one that truly sets your business apart.](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/industries/financial-services/articles/human-centered-capitalism.html#financial)
#####  A more inclusive world 
Where all the communities will have equal access to financial opportunities 
#####  A more educated world 
where financial providers will finance the re- and upskilling of America’s workforce, empowering displaced workers to help to drive growth and prosperity in the new, digital economy. 
#####  A more sustainable world 
where citizens and firms will view financial services players as the leaders in building green, harnessing clean energy, reversing emissions, and adapting to more extreme weather. 
#####  A more collaborative world 
where incumbent financial services firms, large tech companies, fintech startups, and companies from other industries view each other as allies, collaborators, and partners in serving humanity. 
#####  A more profitable financial services industry 
where financial institutions will perform their amplified roles without having to make significant trade-offs between profit and positive social impact.

###  Vision for a higher bottom line 
With the future of financial services rapidly taking shape, firms must heed the call to embrace human-centered capitalism by engaging in more direct, personalized, meaningful, and socially responsible ways, leading to:
**A more inclusive world**
where all communities will have equal access to financial opportunities.
**A more educated world**
where financial providers will finance the re- and upskilling of America’s workforce, empowering displaced workers to help to drive growth and prosperity in the new, digital economy. 
**A more sustainable world**
where citizens and firms will view financial services players as the leaders in building green, harnessing clean energy, reversing emissions, and adapting to more extreme weather. 
**A more collaborative world**
where incumbent financial services firms, large tech companies, fintech startups, and companies from other industries view each other as allies, collaborators, and partners in serving humanity.
**A more profitable financial services industry**
where financial institutions will perform their amplified roles without having to make significant trade-offs between profit and positive social impact.
