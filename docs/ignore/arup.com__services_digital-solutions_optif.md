#  OPTIF

Contact our experts
OPTIF is Arup’s proprietary offshore wind foundation design tool, used by our experts to deliver services across the development cycle. Leveraging extensive experience in designing offshore structures, OPTIF combines rapid analysis, advanced optimisation, and a flexible approach for efficient foundation design.
Specifically developed for offshore wind monopile and jacket foundations, innovative optimisation algorithms and analysis can lead to significant primary steel weight reductions and faster delivery. The flexibility offered by OPTIF enables Arup experts to adapt analysis to meet project-specific requirements.
## Key benefits:
primary steel weight savings through advanced optimisation*
faster design delivery due to rapid analysis and automated design outputs
existing use cases across a wide range of different foundation design projects
*Relative to commercial software. Depending on site and project-specific conditions
Our experts use OPTIF to deliver [offshore wind foundation design services](https://www.arup.com/markets/energy/renewable-energy/offshore-wind/offshore-foundation-design/) across the development cycle. Leveraging Arup’s extensive experience in designing offshore structures, OPTIF combines rapid analysis, advanced optimisation, and a flexible approach for efficient foundation design.
OPTIF applies powerful loads analysis methods and automated workflows to rapidly iterate designs, allowing for a complex design space to be explored considering many thousands of different scenarios. This allows an optimal design configuration to be identified, enhances confidence in designs at an early stage, and can result in fewer load iterations being required with the wind turbine manufacturer and reduced risk of changes in later design stages.
Building on Arup’s structural and geotechnical experience from the wider built environment, OPTIF uses purpose-built optimisation algorithms to identify primary steel configurations that minimise steel weights and reduce fabrication costs. Using industry best-practice, detailed finite element models of key components can be developed and analysed quickly and efficiently.
The solution is designed to be flexible and can be adapted to meet project specific requirements. It is solver-agnostic, facilitates transparency in delivery, and ensures consistency in approach and data formats from feasibility assessments through to detailed design.
OPTIF brings an integrated approach to offshore wind turbine foundation design, with end-to-end workflows that allow expert users to generate consistent and high-quality design outputs, drawings and reporting.
Case study 
Rampion 2: advancing offshore wind in the UK
Rampion 2 is a proposed extension to the existing Rampion offshore wind farm, located 13km off the Sussex coast. With up to 90 new turbines planned, the project is expected to generate enough renewable electricity to power over one million UK homes—supporting national efforts to transition to clean energy.
Arup was commissioned to deliver an early Front End Engineering Design (FEED) study for the wind turbine foundations. Using OPTIF, Arup’s proprietary design tool, engineers rapidly analysed thousands of foundation configurations. This enabled robust, optimised designs that accounted for complex site conditions and supply chain constraints, while remaining flexible to client input.
Arup also applied its Integrated Load Analysis (ILA) capability, developing a bespoke aeroelastic model of the wind turbine generator. This improved load estimate accuracy and reduced design uncertainty ahead of turbine supplier engagement. Sensitivity studies across tower configurations enabled holistic optimisation of the full support structure.
The FEED study provided RWE with a high-confidence substructure assessment, helping to inform key development decisions as Rampion 2 progresses. Arup’s advanced digital tools and collaborative approach ensured technically sound, cost-effective solutions aligned with project delivery needs.
