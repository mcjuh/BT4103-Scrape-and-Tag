## Why Carbon and Decarbonisation Advisory Matters in Australia
As the global economy transitions to net zero, it is critical for Australian organisations to establish robust carbon management strategies and implement carbon pricing mechanisms. Key tools such as carbon credits, offsetting, and emissions trading schemes enable both public and private sector organisations to reduce their carbon footprint and comply with regulatory requirements. Australia's commitment to net zero emissions by 2050 is supported by various government policies and mechanisms, including:
### Emissions Reduction Fund (ERF)
A voluntary scheme that incentivises businesses, farmers, and land managers to adopt practices and technologies that lower greenhouse gas emissions, such as carbon capture, reforestation, and energy efficiency.
### Safeguard Mechanism
A policy ensuring that emissions reductions achieved under the ERF are not offset by increases from Australia's largest emitters, by setting emissions baselines and requiring companies to reduce emissions or purchase carbon credits if they exceed these limits.
### National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting (NGER) Scheme
A mandatory reporting framework for tracking greenhouse gas emissions, energy production, and consumption. This data supports the management of Australia's overall emissions profile.
AESG Australia offers carbon advisory services that help clients navigate these frameworks, implement offsetting strategies, and achieve emission reduction targets. By aligning with Australian policies and international agreements such as the Paris Agreement, we provide clear guidance for organisations on the path to net zero.

## Decarbonisation Strategies for a Sustainable Future
As Australia and global industries push towards the Paris Agreement’s goal of limiting global warming to well below 2°C, the demand for actionable decarbonisation strategies has never been greater. AESG Australia partners with organisations across major sectors, including energy, infrastructure, manufacturing, and real estate, to create long-term solutions for reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
## Our Approach to Decarbonisation Advisory
### Energy Audits & Efficiency Upgrades
Identifying energy-saving opportunities through comprehensive audits, helping organisations reduce costs and emissions in line with Australian Standards (AS/NZS 3598:2014).
### Deep Retrofit Solutions
Revitalising existing infrastructure with high-impact energy retrofits that drastically improve efficiency and reduce carbon footprints.
### Renewable Energy Integration
Strategically incorporating renewable energy sources like solar and wind, transitioning businesses away from fossil fuel dependence.
### Climate Risk Assessment & Resilience Planning
Evaluating climate risks and preparing organisations to adapt to future environmental challenges with robust resilience strategies.
### Net Zero Carbon Roadmaps
Crafting customised pathways to guide organisations towards net zero emissions by 2050 or earlier, with clearly defined and actionable milestones.
