## [Insights](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-acoustics-design-engineering/#Insights)
At AESG, we understand that the quality of acoustics within a building significantly impacts the experience of its occupants. Our Building Acoustics Design & Engineering services are designed to deliver optimal sound environments, ensuring comfort, enhancing communication, and promoting well-being. By offering bespoke acoustic solutions, we help developers, architects, and engineers create spaces that meet stringent regulatory standards while delivering exceptional acoustic performance.Whether it’s reducing noise pollution in commercial buildings, improving acoustic performance in residential spaces, or mitigating vibration in sensitive environments, our global expertise allows us to deliver tailored solutions across various sectors. From initial design through to commissioning, our services cover the entire lifecycle of a project.
## Key Services
Our Building Acoustics Design & Engineering services include a range of acoustic solutions tailored to meet the unique needs of your project:
[ Environmental Acoustic Assessments (EIA) ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-acoustics-design-engineering/#collapse-7abe8626aa068b619fb1)
Our environmental noise impact assessments are designed to ensure compliance with environmental regulations. We measure and assess noise levels to minimise environmental impact, helping you achieve regulatory approval for your project.
[ Masterplanning ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-acoustics-design-engineering/#collapse-203a7c16aa068b619fb1)
We conduct detailed noise and vibration measurement surveys to evaluate existing conditions on-site. Our accurate measurements help ensure compliance with required standards and inform acoustic design decisions.
[ Overheating (Approved Document O) Assessment ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-acoustics-design-engineering/#collapse-8b2a72e6aa068b619fb1)
In line with the UK’s Approved Document O, we assess buildings for overheating risks, ensuring that acoustic factors are considered alongside thermal comfort and ventilation strategies to improve occupant well-being.
[ Site Noise & Vibration Measurement Surveys ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-acoustics-design-engineering/#collapse-10432486aa068b619fb1)
Our comprehensive site noise and vibration surveys provide an accurate assessment of existing conditions. We use precise measurements to inform acoustic design decisions and ensure compliance with relevant standards, including BS 8233 and BS 4142.
[ Construction Supervision ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-acoustics-design-engineering/#collapse-7abe8626aa068b61adb0)
We oversee the implementation of acoustic designs during the construction phase, ensuring that the intended acoustic performance is achieved. Our team works closely with contractors to ensure that noise control measures are properly integrated.
[ Peer Reviews ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-acoustics-design-engineering/#collapse-203a7c16aa068b61adb0)
Using advanced noise modelling and analysis techniques, we simulate noise propagation within your project’s environment. This allows us to predict noise levels and adjust the design to mitigate potential issues before they arise.
[ Noise Modelling & Analysis ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-acoustics-design-engineering/#collapse-15019716aa068b61adb0)
Using state-of-the-art noise modelling software, we predict and analyse noise propagation to identify potential issues before they arise. This proactive approach allows us to mitigate noise impact early in the design process, ensuring a high standard of acoustic comfort.
