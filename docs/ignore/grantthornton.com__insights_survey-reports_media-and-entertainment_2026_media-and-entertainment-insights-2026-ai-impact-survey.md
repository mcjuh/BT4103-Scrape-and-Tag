#  Media & Entertainment insights: 2026 AI Impact Survey
## Boards committed early, front lines are still learning
## Commercial value must catch up to commitment
Media and entertainment organizations have board-level AI commitment that leads the marketplace. Adoption support for writers, editors and production staff is the most urgent need.
**say frontline workers need the most AI adoption support**
**have already fully integrated agentic AI into workflows**
**What’s in the report?**
  * Boards are approving major investments that aren’t getting fully adopted
  * Role-specific workflows are critical to delivering measurable returns
  * The accountability implications of a buy-first strategy are coming into focus
  * Agentic AI is scaling faster than the controls
