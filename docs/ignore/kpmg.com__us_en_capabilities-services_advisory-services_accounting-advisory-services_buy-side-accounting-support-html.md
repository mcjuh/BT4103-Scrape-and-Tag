# Buy-side accounting support
From financial reporting and valuation to regulatory compliance and integration, acquiring a business is a high-stakes endeavor.
Successfully executing an acquisition requires navigating a host of complex accounting and financial reporting challenges. Companies must correctly apply purchase accounting rules (ASC 805), manage the intricacies of SEC reporting if public, and determine the fair value of acquired assets and liabilities.
Furthermore, a successful deal hinges on the seamless integration of the target’s finance functions, including aligning accounting policies and consolidating reporting systems, all while managing the project to mitigate risks and ensure a smooth transition.
## What companies need to do to address the challenge
The strategic CAO
New expectations, new opportunities for today’s Corporate Controller and Chief Accounting Officer
  * Address the complexities of purchase accounting (ASC 805), prepare for SEC reporting requirements, and manage financial statement preparation.
  * Assess the fair value of acquired assets and liabilities and coordinate with tax teams to ensure an efficient transaction structure.
  * Align accounting policies between the acquirer and target, consolidate reporting systems, and ensure the finance function is operationally ready from Day 1.
  * Streamline processes across all workstreams involved in the acquisition, helping to mitigate risks and ensure the overall success of the deal.
  * Receive expert advice on purchase price adjustments and support in resolving post-deal financial disputes.


By leveraging this specialized support, companies can execute acquisitions with greater confidence and clarity, in order to address critical financial reporting, valuation, and integration challenges to unlock long-term value.
