#  2025 Annual Trends Webinar (APAC)
February 20, 2025 - 12pm SGT 
Webinar 
Discover what’s shaping the future of sustainable business in Asia Pacific at our tailored webinar, that explores The Sustainability Institute’s [**2025 Annual Trends Report: The Path Forward for Sustainable Business.**](https://www.erm.com/insights/2025-annual-trends-report-the-path-forward-for-sustainable-business/ "trends report")
##  About the Webinar
Andrew Angle, co-author of the report, presented key insights and our expert panelists delved into the trends for Asia Pacific, including on climate change, advancing sustainability disclosures, sustainable production and consumption in supply chains, leveraging technology for sustainability, and navigating anon the ever-evolving political landscape. Anchored by insights from the report, this webinar offered actionable guidance for building resilience and driving success in the Asia Pacific region and beyond.
