## Re-establish And Optimise Existing Building Performance with AESG's Multi Discipline Existing Building Commissioning Team
As buildings age, their systems drift from optimal performance, leading to increased energy consumption and decreased occupant comfort. AESG provides Existing Building Commissioning (EBCx), also known as Retro Commissioning, services to evaluate and enhance the performance of your existing facilities.
## What is Existing Building Commissioning (EBCx)?
Existing Building Commissioning is a systematic process that seeks to improve a building's operations by identifying and implementing low-cost or no-cost operational and maintenance improvements. EBCx focuses on:
### System Evaluation
Assess current performance of HVAC, lighting, and control systems.
### Performance Enhancement
Optimise system settings and controls for efficiency.
### Operational Improvement
Train staff and implement best practices for maintenance.
## Benefits of Retro Commissioning
### Optimised Building Performance
Ensure all systems operate optimally, reducing energy consumption and operational costs.
### Quality Assurance
