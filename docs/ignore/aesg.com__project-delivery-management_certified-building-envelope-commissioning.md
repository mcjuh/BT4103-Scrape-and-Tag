## Ensuring Optimal Building Performance with AESG's Certified Building Envelope Commissioning Team
The building envelope is the most consequential building system, it is the physical barrier between the interior and exterior environments and plays a pivotal role in a building's overall performance. AESG offers Certified Building Envelope Commissioning (BECx) services to ensure your building envelope meets design intentions and performance criteria, enhancing durability, energy efficiency, and occupant comfort.
## What is Building Envelope Commissioning (BECx)?
Building Envelope Commissioning is a systematic, quality-focused process that verifies and documents the performance of the building envelope components, including walls, roofs, windows, doors, and foundations. BECx aims to:
### Verify Design and Construction
### Enhance Performance
### Extend Lifespan
## Benefits of Building Envelope Commissioning
### Energy Efficiency
  * Achieve significant energy savings by reducing thermal losses
  * Meet or exceed energy codes and standards, such as ASHRAE and local regulations.


### Moisture Control
  * Avoid health hazards associated with moisture accumulation.
  * Prevent damage to building materials caused by water intrusion.


### Occupant Comfort
  * Maintain stable indoor temperatures regardless of external conditions.
  * Reduce external noise infiltration for a comfortable indoor environment.


### Cost Savings
  * Lower utility bills through improved energy performance.
  * Early detection and correction of issues prevent expensive future repairs.


### Compliance and Certification
  * Meet criteria for certifications like LEED, BREEAM, and WELL Building Standard.
  * Ensure adherence to international building codes and standards.
