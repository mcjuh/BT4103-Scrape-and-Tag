# Value-Added Tax
Local support for managing global tax
##  Manage requirements and increase savings 
Although it isn’t used in the United States, value-added tax (VAT) is levied by more than 170 other countries, making it the most common form of tax worldwide. U.S. companies with overseas operations must pay this indirect tax and keep accurate records to receive credits for the VAT paid at every stage in the supply chain, from production to final sale.
Based in the U.S. as part of our State and Local Tax practice, the KPMG VAT team offers an international perspective delivered locally. Our professionals have hands-on experience working with VAT regimes around the world, and with companies in a wide range of industries. Services include:
  * Defining and managing key VAT risks
  * Identifying opportunities to reduce absolute VAT costs and cash flow impacts
  * Reviewing an organization’s locations, businesses, and supply chain to identify VAT registration requirements
  * Assisting with compliance requirements in multiple regions
  * Implementing and maintaining technology platforms to automate VAT payment and compliance
  * Assisting with audits and recoveries from local tax authorities


Our U.S. VAT team works closely with KPMG Global Indirect Tax professionals from KPMG International member firms around the world. Wherever you are, we can mobilize a team to address your needs effectively.
Reverse audit
KPMG can conduct a reverse audit of your purchase or sales transactions to uncover VAT overpayments and address the issues that caused them.
VAT recovery
VAT overpayments are common, but the recovery process can be complex and slow. KPMG uses local knowledge and the latest technology to help improve the VAT recovery process.
Compliance management
Our teams use proprietary and third-party technology solutions to help U.S.-based companies review, automate, and improve VAT compliance.

## Dive into our thinking: 
Global indirect tax obligations in the digital economy
Over 170 countries employ a value-added tax (VAT) or goods and services tax (GST). An increasing number of these VAT/GST jurisdictions have introduced, or are in the process of introducing, VAT/GST rules aimed at the digital economy. U.S.-headquartered multinational companies who operate in these countries need to understand their global tax obligations.
