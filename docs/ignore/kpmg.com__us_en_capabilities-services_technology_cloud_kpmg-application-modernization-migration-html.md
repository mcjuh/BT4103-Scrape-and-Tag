##  Accelerate your journey to a modern enterprise 
KPMG Cloud Application Modernization and Migration is our field-tested, repeatable approach powered by Generative AI to implement complex application and cloud modernization initiatives in a reliable, scalable and cost-effective way, established through years of successful engagements.
This approach includes our suite of advanced methodologies, services, applications and accelerators designed to help you build and migrate to a modern digital architecture and make the operational and organizational changes you need to take full advantage of it.
KPMG Cloud Application Modernization and Migration is more than just migrating you to the cloud. Our highly skilled and experienced cloud, data and business professionals can help you reduce technical debt, embrace automation, leverage new technologies, develop new operating models, empower new ways of working and reorganize your workforce to cohesively accelerate your cloud transformation initiatives and achieve your business goals.

## Security Benchmarks and Assessment Frameworks
The purpose of security assessment tool is to identify vulnerabilities in architectures or applications and their deployment environment, monitor activity within the containers, and detect possible attacks on them. These tools help ensure that applications are secure and compliant with industry regulations and standards. They also assist in mitigating risks associated with misconfigurations, unauthorized access, and malware attacks on containerized environments. Overall, security assessment tools are essential for maintaining the security and reliability of applications and infrastructure. The assessment is designed to help organizations handle risks and enjoy the benefits of using this technology.
