KPMG has been named a "Leader” in the  _IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Microsoft Implementation Services 2024 Vendor Assessment_ (doc #US50539623, April 2024).
The report states, “KPMG, in partnership with Microsoft, helps clients adapt to the fast-changing AI and technology landscape, and the firm offers its clients a range of technical accelerators and digital innovations on Microsoft technology. Currently, KPMG has 12 priority solutions with Microsoft originating from over 50 solutions it has developed since 2017. The company continues to enhance these solutions to ensure relevancy in the current GenAI landscape.” 
This IDC study represents a vendor assessment of the 2024 Microsoft implementation services market through the IDC MarketScape model. This research is a quantitative and qualitative assessment of the characteristics that explain a vendor's success in the marketplace.
_Source: ”IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Microsoft Implementation Services 2024 Vendor Assessment”_
_IDC MarketScape vendor analysis model is designed to provide an overview of the competitive fitness of ICT suppliers in a given market. The research methodology utilizes a rigorous scoring methodology based on both qualitative and quantitative criteria that results in a single graphical illustration of each vendor’s position within a given market. The Capabilities score measures vendor product, go-to-market and business execution in the short-term. The Strategy score measures alignment of vendor strategies with customer requirements in a 3-5-year timeframe. Vendor market share is represented by the size of the circles._
###### [Read the IDC MarketScape: Worldwide Microsoft Implementation Services 2024 Vendor Assessment excerpt hereOpens in a new window](https://idcdocserv.com/US50539623e_KPMG)
The global KPMG and Microsoft alliance combines Microsoft’s technological excellence, trusted cloud platform, and AI leadership with KPMG professionals’ leading industry and technical advisory experience to help clients accelerate innovation, drive efficiencies, create value and improve customer experiences.
KPMG firms work with Microsoft to help clients transform their front, middle and back offices. The KPMG suite of digital transformation services, including Enterprise and Powered Enterprise, can help organizations achieve business-led and technology-driven transformation. By utilizing the Microsoft Common Data Model, these industry-specific frameworks maintain a strong focus on key strategic areas and help digitize in a way that is designed to create fast, predictable value from the transformation while working to drive long-term business value through real-time insights. 
KPMG is a member of the 2023-2024 Microsoft Business Applications Inner Circle, ranking KPMG in the top echelon of Microsoft’s Business Applications global network of alliance partners.
The report mentions: “KPMG understands the trend toward multicloud environments and offers expertise and accelerators for cross-platform integration. Its assets support delivery and become useful tools for its clients to use after implementation. KPMG's Microsoft practice services span the entire project life cycle, from strategy to architecture to implementation to managed services.”
The report concludes, “Buyers had positive feedback about KPMG's ability to integrate vendor project teams with internal teams; provide customer service, particularly onsite; and resolve problems/issues related to customer service. Strengths of KPMG are functionality or offering road map and cost management strategy.”

Together, we leverage the latest technologies, such as generative AI, to help clients drive innovation and transform the way they work to generate better business outcomes.
