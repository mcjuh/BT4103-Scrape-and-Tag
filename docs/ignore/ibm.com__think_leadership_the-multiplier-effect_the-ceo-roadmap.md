##  The hidden cost of waiting for proof 
Most organizations treat uncertainty as a reason to wait. The leaders pulling ahead see it as the price of admission. Instead of viewing ambiguity as a barrier, they approach it as a leadership challenge, knowing their advantage comes from acting while the future is still taking shape.
