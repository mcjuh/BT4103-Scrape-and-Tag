# Privately Speaking Series: Insights on private company growth
Practical insights for private market leaders from private company advisors.
## What you need to know, when you need to know it
_Privately Speaking_ tackles the issues that privately held entities, including private equity- and venture capital-backed companies, care about most. In each edition, we share insights, as well as practical, actionable tips to help boards and executive management grow, strengthen, and transition their privately held businesses.
Every day, professionals from KPMG Private Enterprise help many of the world’s most successful entrepreneurs and leaders from the fastest-growing private companies and family businesses navigate challenges, grow their businesses, and create value for investors.
In this series, we will offer perspective on how private company leaders can create new competitive advantages by examining emerging issues, evolving trends, and the latest research on key topics, including business and tax planning, talent management, succession planning, operational efficiency, and IPO readiness.
Whether your company is starting out and growing or strengthening and transitioning, this series promises to explore ways to help companies be successful at any stage of the business journey.
