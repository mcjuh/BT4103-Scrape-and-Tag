# Transportation & Logistics
[ Talk to us to learn more ](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/#block-connect-with-us)
Cruise to value and profitability.
The transport and infrastructure sector is highly fragmented with thousands of small to medium businesses dominated by several large market players. The industry environment is magnified by the global reach of major players that can leverage volume and add services to generate profit. The sector is also very capital intensive and often relies on the investment community for funding and growth.
By advising or joining your management team, A&M can help you improve processes and controls, reduce costs, improve performance, maximize working capital and create shareholder value.
[ Transport & Infrastructure ](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/node/6726)
People and their goods are moving at record numbers, and you face knife-edged competition for their business. Add a high-test mixture of rising fuel prices and tightening credit, and your engine to profitability can stall. A&M’s dynamic and experienced team will soon have you back on the road and into the fast lane. 
[ Airlines & Airports ](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/node/6731)
Following a smooth ascent into an extended period of growth, there’s a bit of turbulence in the Airlines & Airports sector. Fuel prices are lower, but you need to offset ongoing cost pressures to maintain fiscal altitude and improve yields. Our experts help you navigate the challenges to maximize revenues and cut costs. 
[ Shipping & Ports ](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/node/6736)
Staying afloat in a sea of revenue and operational cost pressures is no easy task. An oversupply of capacity, coupled with the need to invest in logistics and support increasingly larger vessels, can stretch your resources. With A&M aboard, you’ll optimize your financial returns. 
Combine high fuel prices, a record number of rail passengers, millions of vehicles on the road, and a huge demand for home delivery, and your plans can jam or derail. Clear the track to profitability with A&M.
