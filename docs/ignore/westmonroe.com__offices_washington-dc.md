### In the office
Our Washington, D.C. office offers the perfect blend of work and community, starting with our 3rd-floor lounge, where collaboration comes naturally. From Tuesday lunches to our monthly social hours, we prioritize connection and camaraderie. And don’t miss the highlight of the year—our famous annual chili cook-off, a beloved tradition that brings everyone together for some friendly competition.
### In the community
We believe in making a meaningful impact beyond the office. Our team is actively involved in supporting local initiatives that strengthen the community and preserve the city’s rich history and environment. From volunteering our time to lending our expertise, we take pride in working alongside organizations that address critical needs across education, housing, and environmental protection. Some of the groups we proudly partner with include Martha’s Table, Habitat for Humanity of Washington D.C., and Potomac Riverkeeper.
