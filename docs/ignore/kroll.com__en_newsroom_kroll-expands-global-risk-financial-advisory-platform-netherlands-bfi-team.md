# Kroll Significantly Expands Its Global Financial and Risk Advisory Platform in the Netherlands with the Talent Acquisition of the BFI Team
Amsterdam – Kroll, the leading independent provider of global risk and financial advisory solutions, today announced the talent acquisition of the BFI team to Kroll’s [Valuation Advisory Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/valuation-services) Practice in the Netherlands. As leading specialists in complex business valuations, financial investigations and disputes, the BFI team significantly expands our service offerings in the Netherlands. 
BFI was founded in 2016 by Jan Adriaanse and Marc Broekema to support shareholders, corporate leaders and legal advisors with high quality independent advice. In 2021, Alexander Bregonje joined Jan and Marc in the business to grow BFI’s footprint in the Netherlands. 
Among other areas of expertise, the BFI team has a particularly strong reputation in the rapidly expanding restructuring related valuation space and an impressive track-record of supporting major shareholder disputes and investigations in multinational-corporate bankruptcies and liability investigations and analyses. Notable recent representations include acting as valuation adviser to Steinhoff International Holdings (SIHNV) and securing court sanction of its WHOA Restructuring Plan in the Netherlands.
Marc and Alexander will co-lead Kroll’s Netherlands [Valuation Advisory Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/valuation-services) team and report to Michael Weaver, Managing Director and Head of International Valuation Advisory Services. This move also marks a return to the firm for Alexander who previously worked at Kroll (previously named Duff & Phelps) for 15 years. In addition, Jan Adriaanse will join as director along with the entire team of valuation experts from BFI. 
Kroll’s [Amsterdam](https://www.kroll.com/en/about-us/global%20locations/europe%20and%20middle%20east/amsterdam) office maintains a team of seven managing directors specializing in [Valuation Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/valuation-services), [Expert Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/expert-services) and [Forensic Investigations and Intelligence Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/forensic-investigations-monitorships) and will have a total of 40 employees following the talent acquisition of the BFI team.
Michael Weaver, Managing Director and Head of International [Valuation Advisory Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/valuation-services) at Kroll, said: “I am delighted to welcome BFI to Kroll and to work with the brilliant team they have built over the last seven years. Throughout all of our discussions, it was clear that BFI and Kroll are ideal business partners and share a vision for providing the highest-quality services to support their clients on complex engagements. With the talent acquisition of BFI, Kroll is gaining a wealth of expertise, deep technical skills, unparalleled local market knowledge and relationships that will further cement our leadership position in the local market and enhance our wider EMEA valuation practice.”
Alexander Bregonje, Managing Director and Co-Head of the Netherlands [Valuation Advisory Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/valuation-services) team at Kroll, said: “I am excited to be rejoining Kroll during this time of growth and investment in data and technology. Kroll has a strong reputation as the leading global independent valuation provider. The team and I are looking forward to partnering with our global Kroll colleagues to further enhance how we support our clients in the Netherlands.” 
Marc Broekema, Managing Director and Co-Head of the Netherlands [Valuation Advisory Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/valuation-services) team at Kroll, said: “As a trusted advisor to the leading law firms on complex and sensitive dispute, litigation and restructuring valuation cases, I am excited to work closely with Kroll’s leadership to enhance and expand critical valuation solutions for our clients. I am confident that our clients will benefit from this unparalleled service offering.”
