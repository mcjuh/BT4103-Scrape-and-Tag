## Your one-stop shop for technology solutions in asset and wealth management
**Chief Technology Officer (CTO) as-a-Service** is aimed to be a one-stop-shop for all technology related needs of the target client segments including data, cyber security, modern technology, enterprise systems and cloud.
#### Target client segments:
Small family office (SFO)
Multi family office (MFO)
Boutique asset managers
Small asset owners
We are committed to providing comprehensive assistance and support throughout the entire lifecycle of asset and wealth management and fund-related enterprises, encompassing the entire spectrum from inception, strategy to IT operations management. Thereby, our key outcomes for the different target client segments are as follows:
##### Obtain data-driven insights to simplify complexity and make informed decisions
##### Unlock speed-to-market to maintain competitive edge
##### Free up capacity to focus on higher value work
##### Enable greater innovation and higher performance
##### Embed new skills across tax, risk, compliance, legal, cloud engineering and data management
Our team of specialised professionals in the AWM industry, functional, technology, data, cloud, and cyber security and is poised to facilitate the establishment of a robust technology roadmap and assist in its operationalisation, ensuring optimal efficiency and effectiveness. In addition, with a blend of both business and technology skill sets, PwC is able to provide a holistic view of your future enterprise transformation.

New businesses
### New businesses
**Our four target client segments are:** SFO, MFO, boutique asset managers and small asset owners
  * Day 0 and day 1 readiness [set-up hardware and software]
  * Third party vendor contract management
  * Service management and maintenance support


Existing business
### Existing business
**Our four target client segments are:** SFO, MFO, boutique asset managers and small asset owners
#### Assess and strategise
  * Technology function maturity assessment
  * Risk, compliance and controls assessment
  * Data protection and cyber security assessment
  * Enterprise architecture assessment
  * Anti money laundering and know your customer assessment
  * Tech roadmap preparation and/or prioritisation


New and existing business
### New and existing business
**Our four target client segments are:** SFO, MFO, boutique asset managers and small asset owners
#### Mobilise and design
  * Design IT governance framework and technology target operating model
  * Manage and run end to end vendor selection process
  * Design future proof process framework [risk, compliance, controls, AML/KYC, data protection and cyber security]
  * Design 3rd party vendor management process


#### Implement
  * End to end technology implementation [investment management, AML/KYC, cyber security, CRM, ERP]
  * Technology implementation advisory
  * Programme governance and management
  * Change impact assessment and plan
  * Testing and data migration data migration


#### Maintain and optimise
  * Service management support
  * Third party vendor management and vendor performance review
  * Crisis management and breach response
  * Continuous improvements [periodic people, process and technology assessments]
