# Finance Function Excellence
The finance function's role is rapidly evolving into a strategic partner, driving business performance and stewarding value direction. BCG works with CFOs and their teams to create a future-ready finance function while ensuring operational excellence.

## Our Approach to Finance Function Excellence
  * **Finance Vision and Strategy.** We align the finance function targets and roadmap with future business needs. This entails defining a North Star and fundamentally rethinking finance strategy, organization, processes, technology, and people. Our CFO Excellence services include setting the agenda for incoming CFOs and guiding them on best practices and cutting-edge finance trends. Additionally, our finance function consulting team helps CFOs become chief performance officers, driving greater profitability in response to today's volatile business environment.
  * **Finance Operating Model and Organization.** We transform the finance function operating model to drive greater efficiency and effectiveness. This includes reshaping the talent strategy and organization structure, location and sourcing model, and ways of working. We support finance [cost transformations](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/cost-management) by implementing actions that realize durable savings and ensure finance functions can increase focus on higher value-add activities. In post-merger management, we apply this approach to realize significant synergies and value-creation opportunities.
  * **Digitization in Finance.** The transition of the CFOs' role impacts their use of digital, moving from optimizing core functionality to supporting value creation in the business. To support this shift, we help define a robust digital strategy, including the use of [generative AI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence/generative-ai) and the next generation ERP strategy. We help our clients create their technology roadmap; identify advanced digital use cases; develop pilots and scaling them rapidly; and support them through the detailed design and implementation of new digital capabilities, such as financial planning and forecasting tools.
  * **Financial Steering Excellence.** We leverage AI and advanced analytics to help build world-class capabilities in performance steering—including revamping the budgeting and forecasting processes through automated, driver-based planning and enhanced decision support with next-generation business intelligence.

## Our Clients’ Success in Finance Function Transformation
$120m
in cost savings
An energy company worked with BCG to envision its future finance function. The revamped operating model introduced centers of excellence (including talent and digital) to drive best practices and continuous improvement; significantly increased the use of shared services; and allowed for leaner embedded finance teams to focus on enhanced decision support and partnership with the business. The finance function led the transformation of the overall enterprise in terms of speed and impact, driving **$120 million in cost savings**.
~40%
freed capacity
The CFO at a fashion and luxury store turned to BCG for help transforming its planning, forecasting, and business intelligence capabilities, including driver-based business logic, modern digital tooling with greater data automation, and streamlined planning and forecasting processes. As a result, finance and business leaders gained more real-time visibility into the drivers of performance and **freed up 30% to 40% of the financial planning and analysis teams’ capacity** to redeploy to business advisory and decision support.
~$40m
in efficiency improvements
BCG developed an AI- and machine-learning-based forecasting engine for a large industrial goods manufacturer. This new tool, based on vast internal and external data sets, improved the accuracy of revenue forecasts by 50% while drastically reducing variance and bias and providing better explainability into the drivers of demand. Better forecasting enabled **$25 million to $40 million in efficiency improvements** across business operations, through improved labor and production scheduling, procurement efficiencies, and better working capital.
~30%
freed capacity
BCG worked with a CFO of a global health care company to develop a vision for the finance function and to support its design and implementation. This included a radical rethink of the planning process, the agile operating model, and organizational design. As a result, the finance teams became more flexible and efficient, **freeing up 20% to 30% of their capacity**.
