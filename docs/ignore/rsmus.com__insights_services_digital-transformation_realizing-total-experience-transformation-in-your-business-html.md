##  What is human-centered design?
Human-centered design is a strategic approach that aligns your products, processes and services with how your customers and employees interact with them. It’s not a one-time activity but an ongoing process. You should continually leverage new information about how your customers and employees interact with your organization to create more personalized and valuable experiences.
A human-centered design approach brings behavioral science, user research and analytics together to gain a deep understanding of how a customer or employee currently interacts with a process, product or service, uncovering opportunities to make engagement more intuitive and impactful. Human-centered design also helps your organization create a culture of iteration capable of evolving along with the needs and preferences of its users. Using these insights, designers can generate new thinking that fully takes user behavior into account while enabling a more adaptive and responsive design ethos.
> Human-centered design is a strategic approach that aligns your products, processes and services with how your customers and employees interact with them.
By eliminating ambiguity, real-world pain points and friction in customers and employees’ daily lives, you can turn the human experience and user expectations into a dynamic driver of business outcomes. In addition, you can generate more empathy from your teams for the needs of users to create a forward-thinking work environment focused on solving real-world problems. Human-centered design can also help you better understand the human element of your business problems so you can incorporate the right technology solution in the most effective manner possible.

## Customer acquisition and retention
### **The challenge**
Almost every business faces a saturated market with fierce competition from larger, well-funded enterprises on one end and agile startups on the other. As a result, many organizations either struggle to stand out or find themselves in a race to the bottom to compete solely on price, limiting their ability to create a customer experience that inspires ongoing loyalty and word-of-mouth.
### **The opportunity**
RSM enables your business to understand the true needs, motivations and behaviors of your customers so products, solutions and marketing strategies can be designed to drive revenue growth. By digging deeply into the mind of the customer, your organization can create a sustainable, defendable market position that lays the foundation for inspiring repeat purchases and recommendations.
## Employee recruitment and engagement
### **The challenge**
Many organizations struggle with a tight labor market that continues to shrink due to retirement at the same time that a younger generation is demanding more engagement from their work. As modern workplace dynamics evolve, organizations need to rethink how they can meet employee demands for meaningful work, an inclusive culture and opportunities for personal growth.
### **The opportunity**
RSM enables your company to uncover, understand and improve the daily experiences that most influence recruitment and employee retention. By identifying the core issues that may be hindering employee engagement and retention, your organization can create a work environment that resonates with your current workforce while enhancing your brand as an employer.
##  The RSM human-centered design approach
RSM uses a proven framework to design and implement human-centered design strategies that improve the customer and employee experience. This structured process ensures that your products, services and work processes are tailored to meet the real-world needs of prospects, customers and employees to maximize impact and drive business outcomes. 
### Discover
Our multidisciplinary team engages in extensive research, user interviews and site observations to gain a deep understanding of the needs, motivations and challenges of the people you want to reach. This creates the foundation for insights by ensuring a multi-faceted exploration of the user’s needs and preferences, helping your organization transcend surface-level ideas or misleading metrics.
### Define
Taking the initial learnings, our team synthesizes and distills findings into clear user insights that pinpoint the exact issue to be addressed. By precisely defining the problem at hand, your organization can better focus on solutions that resonate with the user, ensuring a higher probability of success.
### Develop
Once the issue is defined, our team leverages collaborative ideation, rapid prototyping and iterative testing to design, create and validate a solution. This approach ensures that the right solution is found quickly and is then refined until it meets the exact needs of the user.
### Deliver
We work side-by-side with your organization to develop and launch the most effective approach and then leverage post-launch feedback to continuously adapt the solution. This process helps the solution stay relevant to the changing needs and expectations of your audience, helping ensure that it’s sustainable over the long term and delivers maximum ROI and impact.
