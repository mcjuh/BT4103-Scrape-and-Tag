# An Agile Framework for Navigating Economic Shifts
## Inflation, interest rates and tariffs have set a course for economic uncertainty. No economist can predict the depth or duration of a downturn with perfect precision. 
Our experience shows that weathering economic uncertainty is predicated on business resilience: the capacity to withstand business shocks and pivot to new opportunities. Resilience isn’t just for companies in dire financial straits or those vulnerable to unpredictable macroeconomic conditions. 
To illustrate, organizations with strong balance sheets, innovative strategies, [sustainable business practices](https://www.bdo.com/services/esg), and growth potential can achieve long term resilience. Strategic resilience transcends simple preparation for potential disruptions; it also involves being poised to seize emerging opportunities with agility and foresight. By leaning into resilience principles such as strategic [scenario planning](https://www.bdo.com/services/advisory/management-consulting/strategy-innovation-consulting) and [risk mitigation](https://www.bdo.com/enterprise-risk-management), companies in all stages of business health can find opportunities in economic adversity and accelerate growth coming out of it.
BDO helps businesses transform challenges into opportunities for growth. We help identify crucial focus areas, suggest low-risk and high-return actions, and make recommendations to adapt to external factors. We provide a comprehensive suite of agile insights, resources, and tools that enable business leaders to make decisions and act with confidence in an evolving business environment. 
  * **Persevere:** Business is unstable and market share is declining. Developing a crisis management plan is essential for companies to respond quickly and effectively navigate economic volatility. 
  * **Maintain:** Business is under control, but growth is stagnating. Cross-department collaboration is necessary for developing strategies to achieve operational efficiency. 
  * **Recover:** Business is stable and growth is ready. Implementing business recovery strategies can help organizations stabilize and plan for profitable growth. 
  * **Thrive:** Business fundamentals and growth are strong. Organizational resilience is crucial for companies to find opportunities and accelerate growth in economic adversity.
