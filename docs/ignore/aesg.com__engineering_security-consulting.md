## Comprehensive Security Risk Assessments
Our security consultancy services begin with detailed assessments of credible threats, site-specific vulnerabilities, and asset criticality. These evaluations form the foundation for a tailored security risk mitigation plan, guiding clients through decisions on risk acceptance, avoidance, reduction, or transfer. We collaborate closely to create a cohesive Asset Protection Program and a comprehensive Security Strategy that aligns with your operations, design, and management structures.
## Integrated Security Solutions
AESG focuses on integrating physical, electronic, and operational security measures into a unified strategy. Our expert team covers a broad spectrum of security disciplines, developing strategies that deter, detect, delay, and deny unauthorised access to critical assets. By implementing advanced technologies and proven methodologies, we ensure optimal protection for our clients.

Our team works with architects, engineers, and site managers to ensure that security measures are seamlessly integrated into the planning, construction, and operational phases of a project. From early-stage master planning to final implementation and testing, AESG ensures that security is an inherent part of the built environment, delivering lasting protection and peace of mind.
