# U.S. Inbound Tax Network
Keeping foreign companies well-informed of tax requirements for their U.S. investments
Navigating the shifting U.S. landscape 
##  Keep pace with changing tax requirements 
Foreign-based companies, funds, and investors doing business in the United States, often called “inbound companies,” can face unique tax issues. As the U.S. tax system is complex and multilayered, timely and knowledgeable tax advice across U.S. federal, state, and local tax is essential to help you address the complexities of U.S. tax law and keep pace with changing tax requirements.
Our U.S. Inbound Tax Network is made up of Tax professionals who specialize in specific U.S. tax areas, focus on inbound and cross-border tax issues, and have deep knowledge of U.S. business practices. Working collaboratively with colleagues from various disciplines, they leverage their experience to meet the full range of client needs. 
Invest with confidence
Foreign-based companies can invest with confidence in the United States when they leverage the knowledge and experience of the U.S. Inbound Tax Network professionals who focus on U.S. tax and trade planning and compliance.
Cut the cross-border complexity
Working closely with colleagues from other KPMG International firms, our U.S. Inbound Tax Network professionals coordinate tax and trade advice that addresses the interconnected tax requirements among the United States, headquarter country, and other jurisdictions in which a company operates. 
Stay on top of new developments with ease
U.S. Inbound Tax Network professionals provide insights and analysis on ongoing U.S. tax and trade legislative and regulatory developments that may affect inbound companies and advise on how to stay compliant and turn challenges into potential opportunities.
