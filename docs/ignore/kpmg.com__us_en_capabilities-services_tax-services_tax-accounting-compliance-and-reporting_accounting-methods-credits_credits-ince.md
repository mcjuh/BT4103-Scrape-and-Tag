# Realize tax savings for qualified research activities
Discover efficient, smarter, AI-powered R&D credit solutions. 
## Uncover R&D tax credit opportunities with advanced technologies
KPMG utilizes advanced technologies, including our proprietary Digital Gateway Platform and Research Credit Navigator software, to efficiently analyze and document your company’s R&D activities for federal and state tax credit programs. Our automated solutions streamline documentation review, quickly identify potential credit opportunities, and deliver comprehensive, audit-ready results for tax authorities. This approach can enhance cash flow, reduce tax liability, and allow your team to focus on strategic priorities.
