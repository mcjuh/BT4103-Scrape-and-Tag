## The realities of travel and hospitality
Travel and hospitality brands face rising traveler expectations for connected experiences while managing legacy platforms, operational complexity and constant pressure to modernize across digital and physical touchpoints.
### Legacy platforms fragment journeys
Disconnected booking, loyalty and operational systems make it difficult to deliver consistent experiences from planning through post-trip engagement.
### Real time personalization required
Guests expect relevant recommendations, offers and support at every moment, but siloed data limits personalization across channels and locations.
### Operations must stay flexible
Travel demand shifts quickly, requiring platforms that can scale, adapt and perform reliably during peak periods and disruptions.
