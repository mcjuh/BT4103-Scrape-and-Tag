## Complete Monitoring Features
### Triple-Bureau Credit Monitoring
Tracks credit activity across Experian, Equifax and TransUnion and notifies individuals of any new inquiries, accounts or changes. Monitoring is view-only and does not affect credit scores.
### Single-Bureau Credit Report
Provides a credit report from one of the national credit bureaus to individuals who have activated their monitoring services online.
### Dark Web Monitoring
Scans hidden online marketplaces, forums and criminal sources for exposed personal or financial information. If an individual’s data is found, an alert is sent immediately.
### Social Security Number Scan
Monitors public records to identify when new names, aliases or addresses become linked to an individual’s Social Security number, issuing alerts if unexpected associations appear.
### Payday Loan Monitoring
Checks high-risk lending sources (including online, rent-to-own and payday lenders) for new loans or inquiries tied to an individual’s personal information and notifies them of any activity.
### Bank Account Alerts
Detects new bank account openings or changes to existing accounts using an individual’s personal information across a broad network of financial institutions, alerting users when new activity occurs.
