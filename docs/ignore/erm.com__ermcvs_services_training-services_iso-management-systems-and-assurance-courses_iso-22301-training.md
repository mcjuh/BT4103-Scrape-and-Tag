## Expand Your Credentials Through HLS Compatibility 
ISO 22301 conforms to the High-Level Structure (HLS), a standardized framework employed across many ISO management standards including ISO 9001 (Quality), ISO 14001 (Environmental), and ISO/IEC 27001 (Information Security). This shared architecture creates a clear advantage: upon finishing Lead Auditor training for ISO 22301, you can certify as a lead auditor for other HLS-aligned standards through brief conversion courses rather than full training programs. You've already learned the fundamental audit methodology and system architecture.
Certification agencies, consulting practices, and organizations managing integrated frameworks actively seek auditors with multi-standard abilities.
