### Cloud strategy and design
Helps organizations define secure, scalable multi-cloud and hybrid infrastructure strategies aligned to business goals. Our capabilities span cloud strategy, AI and platform selection, operating model design, migration planning, agile transformation, data center and network modernization, financial governance, TCO and FinOps—enabling resilient architectures, efficient operations and agility. We help clients accelerate and realize economic value from strategic investments.
### AWS Deployment
Modernize AWS cloud environments through accelerated design, deployment and management of secure, scalable infrastructure. Support AWS adoption through migrations, foundational configuration and landing zone design that enables advanced cloud capabilities and AI workloads. Design and implement enterprise resiliency solutions to support business continuity, with ongoing management to ensure reliable performance and alignment with enterprise objectives.
### Microsoft Azure deployment
Establish secure, scalable Microsoft Azure environments through accelerated design, deployment and management of cloud foundations. Enable Azure modernization through migrations, configuring core services and implementing landing zones that support advanced services and AI workloads. Strengthen operational resilience with enterprise continuity solutions, supported by ongoing cloud operations that maintain performance, reliability and business alignment.
### Google Cloud deployment
Build secure, scalable Google Cloud environments through accelerated design and deployment of cloud foundations. Execute migrations, configure core Google Cloud services and implement landing zones that support advanced cloud capabilities and AI workloads. Deliver enterprise resiliency and continuity solutions for key operations, supported by ongoing cloud management to maintain performance, reliability and alignment with enterprise objectives.
### Oracle Cloud Infrastructure deployment
Deploy secure, scalable Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) environments through cloud foundation configuration, migrations and landing zone implementation. Enable cloud adoption by establishing core OCI services and architectures designed for enterprise performance and scalability. Provide ongoing operations management to ensure reliable performance and alignment with enterprise objectives.
### ServiceNow deployment
Design, deploy and operate scalable AI-powered solutions on the ServiceNow platform with ecosystem technology providers to modernize business and IT operations and deliver value. Enable modern service delivery by aligning processes and capabilities across core ServiceNow modules, including ITSM, ITAM, ITOM, CSM, HRSD, SPM, GRC, SecOps, BCM, CPQ, NowAssist, MoveWorks and App Engine. Enhance efficiency, transparency and experience through integrated workflows and continuous optimization to drive sustained performance and value.
### Business Process Management and low-code/no-code deployment
Design, build, and operate AI-enabled business processes that drive revenue growth, profitability, innovation and competitive advantage through workflow management and intelligent automation powered by low-code platforms. Leveraging Appian, Pega, Celonis, UiPath, Microsoft Power Platform, Alteryx, Automation Anywhere and AWS AgentCore and Quick Suite, scalable digital workflows enhance execution and decision-making. Deliver end-to-end industry solutions using AI-driven agentic automation across enterprise workflow, case management, robotic process automation and intelligent document processing.
### Productivity suite deployment
Enhance collaboration and productivity through AI-enabled deployment, migration and integration of modern platforms, including Microsoft Suite with Copilot, SharePoint and Teams, and Google Workspace with Gemini. Design, deliver and operate enterprise implementations across commercial and regulated environments, supported by governance, security and change enablement to drive adoption and reduce overhead. Provide scalable AI-enabled digital workplace solutions that modernize cloud and device ecosystems, improving performance, cost efficiency and employee experience, including support for mergers, acquisitions and tenant consolidations or separations.
