# Featured in Nikkei & Nikkei Asia: A&M’s Strategic Entry into Japan
Alvarez & Marsal’s (A&M) expansion into Japan was recently featured in Nikkei and Nikkei Asia following the firm’s October 16th press release. In the article, Co-Founder and Co-CEO Bryan Marsal discussed Japan’s growing wave of restructuring, divestitures, and cross-border M&A activity, which has created new opportunities for challenger consultancies. 
A&M’s new offices in Tokyo and Osaka will focus on transaction advisory and tax services, supporting companies navigating corporate reform and strategic transformation. A&M plans to grow its Japan team from 20 to 500 professionals over the next five years as it deepens its commitment to the region.
