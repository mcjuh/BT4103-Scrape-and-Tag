# Risk & Compliance Managed Services
Strengthen compliance operations with AI-enabled services that cover growing complexity and demands across critical risk domains.
Compliance requirements are evolving at an unprecedented pace, highlighting the need for more resilient and technology-enabled risk management operations. Amid expanding complexities and the rapid rise of AI, many organizations are shifting their focus from traditional compliance functions toward a modernized model built for speed and consistency.
KPMG Managed Services helps companies accelerate that shift by taking on the operational load directly, enabling our clients to harness the latest AI and technology capabilities while we manage the underlying systems and maintenance. With skilled professionals, AI-driven automation, and advanced delivery methods, we help organizations move from labor-intensive tasks to high-value decision-making—and help improve the accuracy, and productivity of their compliance needs across complex domains.
Our services cover a wide range of compliance areas to give organizations an integrated, modern way to manage risk with confidence.
Our Services 
A connected suite of compliance managed services
KPMG delivers a modern, intelligence-driven approach to compliance across essential domains. Each service is fully managed, built on established delivery methods, and supported by our skilled professionals. Our services include AI tools and automation that improve speed and efficiency and reduce operational burdens. Together, this coordinated approach across all of our services helps our clients create a more repeatable and adaptable compliance environment.
Know Your Customer (KYC) 
Strengthen customer due diligence with AI-enabled workflows workflows that automate data acquisition, and standardize quality checks. KYC Managed Services help our clients improve accuracy and speed across onboarding, periodic reviews, and high-risk investigations.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) 
Modernize transaction monitoring with analytics-driven detection, intelligent alert processing, and automated reporting. Our AML Managed Services help institutions reduce false positives, accelerate detection cycles, and maintain regulatory confidence.
Financial Crimes 
Perform complex investigations and regulatory reporting through automated data collection and synthesis. Our services help companies arrive at faster, fully documented suspicious activity determinations.
Sanctions and Fraud 
Identify suspicious activity among high volumes of transactions by leveraging data-driven AI detection models. These services help our clients increase consistency and improve the speed of fraud adjudication.
Managed Control Testing 
Enhance internal controls through standardized processes, automated testing, and transparent reporting. We help organizations strengthen governance and reduce manual workloads across their financial, operational, and regulatory controls.
Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) 
Improve visibility and oversight across the vendor lifecycle with continuous monitoring, structured assessments, and automated analytics. We help organizations manage risk across increasingly complex supplier ecosystems and support timely onboarding activities.
## Outcomes that strengthen compliance from every angle
KPMG Compliance Managed Services help organizations improve consistency and control across their most resource-intensive compliance functions. By integrating advanced AI and automation throughout the compliance lifecycle, our services help organizations bridge the gap between traditional operations and a modern, tech-forward risk model. Typical outcomes include:
**Cost reductions of as much as 50 percent** through optimized operations and automation-led delivery that reduce manual effort and improve cost predictability.
**Faster processing times** via AI-enabled workflows that accelerate onboarding, investigations, assessments, and reporting—often cutting cycle times by 40 percent or more.
**Higher-quality decisions** that are driven by improved accuracy, standardized reviews, automated checks, and clear audit trails.
**Greater transparency** through unified dashboards and data insights that enhance visibility across compliance operations, risks, and performance trends.
**Stronger regulatory confidence** from consistent execution, documented controls, and ongoing improvements.
