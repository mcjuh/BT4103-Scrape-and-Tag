# Research Credits & Incentives
Maximizing your federal and state research credits is more important than ever now that Congress has made the credits permanent.
**Don’t overlook any opportunity.** Our highly experienced tax professionals work hard to identify every area within your firm where qualified credit expenses exist. Our goal is to sustain and, wherever possible, increase your research credits and deductions. We have the tools necessary to complete even the most complicated credit calculations, and we assist in preserving the documentation required to support qualifying activities and departments moving forward.
**We’re here as your advocate.** Audits happen, but their effects can be minimized. If you are required to respond to tax authorities, our audit specialists serve as your partners, communicating with professionals at every level of your organization to collect the required documentation while minimizing disruption.
No matter how large or small your business, we listen and bring our best people and practices to get the job done right.
