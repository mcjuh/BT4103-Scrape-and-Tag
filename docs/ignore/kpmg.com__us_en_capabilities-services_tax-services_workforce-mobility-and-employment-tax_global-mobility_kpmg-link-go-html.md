# KPMG LINK Go
The global mobility platform built for change 
##  Scalable mobility for modern workforces 
Times are changing – and global mobility is changing with them. In a fast-paced, increasingly complex world, your organization must continuously reimagine how to support a growing and diverse set of stakeholders, from employees and travelers to tax, HR, finance, and legal teams. KPMG LINK Go is built for this moment, delivering an adaptable, scalable, cloud-based platform that evolves as business needs change. By combining configurable workflows, intelligent automation, and centralized data, LINK Go helps your organization meet the demands of today while staying ready for what’s next.

### Introducing KPMG LINK Go
##  What is KPMG LINK Go? 
KPMG LINK Go brings global mobility together in a single, connected platform – centralizing processes and data while efficiently linking you with your vendors partners and KPMG LLP. Designed to modernize the mobility experience, the platform supports both legacy and emerging move types and policies, giving you the flexibility to evolve without disruption. Available anytime, anywhere, and on any device, LINK Go equips stakeholders with real-time insight to make efficient, informed decisions – helping you move people to the right places, in the right way, with confidence. 
**Human-Centric Automation** Automation is applied where it creates the most value, while human oversight helps ensure your instance of LINK Go is thoughtfully designed, tailored and configured to adapt to the complexities of any global mobility program.
**Scalability & Integration** An integration-friendly architecture creates a scalable, centralized hub for all mobility operations, unifying data, workflows, and reporting - while supporting growth and evolving policies without adding complexity.
**Effortless Experience** Simplify global mobility for everyone, with intuitive workflows, role-based dashboards, task driven notifications and actionable insights. Reduce complexity and manual effort to focus on supporting people, making informed decisions, and achieving successful outcomes.
**Transparency & Security** Protect sensitive data with role-based access controls, built-in governance, and data integrity measures. Ensure every stakeholder has the right information at the right time, supporting confident decision making.
**Purposeful Connectivity** Centralized case management and communications connect mobility teams, employees, and vendors for greater clarity and accountability. Streamlined case visibility and real-time collaboration reduce misalignment and deliver a more connected experience.
**Continuous Innovation** Ongoing enhancements based on industry trends, client feedback, and compliance standards future proof LINK Go, delivering value today and adapting to tomorrow’s global mobility expectations.
