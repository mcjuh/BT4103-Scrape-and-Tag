# ERP Modernization Readiness Self-Assessment
Tool
  * PRINT 
  * 

ERP modernization initiatives often face delays, budget overruns, and adoption challenges when critical readiness factors are overlooked. BDO's ERP Modernization Readiness Self-Assessment helps organizations evaluate whether they have the strategic alignment, technology foundation, organizational capacity, and change management capabilities needed for a successful ERP transformation. 
The assessment provides a structured framework to identify potential risks, uncover readiness gaps, and prioritize actions before ERP selection or implementation begins. Organizations already undergoing an ERP transformation can also use the assessment as a diagnostic tool to validate assumptions, identify misalignments, and focus course-correction efforts where they can have the greatest impact. 
Complete the assessment to better understand your organization's readiness and gain insight into the areas that may require additional planning, investment, or alignment to support a successful ERP modernization journey.
