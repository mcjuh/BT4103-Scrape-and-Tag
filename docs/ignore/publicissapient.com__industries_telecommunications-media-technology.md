## The realities of telco, media and technology
Telco, media and technology organizations face constant pressure to move faster, scale reliably and stay relevant while managing legacy platforms, fragmented data and rising infrastructure costs. 
### Legacy platforms slow delivery
Aging, tightly coupled platforms limit release velocity, increase operational risk and make it harder to innovate across digital services and content.
### Disconnected data limits relevance
Customer, content and usage data often live in silos, making real-time personalization and intelligent decision-making difficult at scale.
### Costs rise as complexity grows
Expanding infrastructure, duplicated tooling and inefficient workflows drive up costs and erode margins over time.
