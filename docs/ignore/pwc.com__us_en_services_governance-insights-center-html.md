## Navigate with confidence. Stay ahead of emerging corporate governance trends.
We provide holistic governance solutions — including new director onboarding, assessments and advisory services. Our insights and tools keep you informed, strengthen effectiveness and keep you prepared for your oversight role.
## Learn more about the Governance Insights Center
  1. About us
  2. How we can help
  3. Meet the team


### About us
We are independent, trusted professionals dedicated to helping companies navigate corporate governance challenges with the insights and solutions you need.
Our team collaborates with boards of directors, investors and management teams to bring a global perspective across industries. We deliver insights and tools to help you stay ahead and make informed decisions.
### How we can help
We can help you navigate the complexities of corporate governance with confidence by offering a holistic suite of governance solutions tailored to meet the needs of boards and executive teams.
[Governance Educational Events and Networking](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/governance-insights-center/director-investor-education.html)
Advisory services:
  * Board dynamics and leading practices, including culture assessments


  * Board and committee structure and assessments in alignment with charters and leading practices


  * Corporate governance assessments and remediation


  * Risk oversight and ERM leading practices


  * Succession planning


  * Tailored governance solutions to meet your specific needs
