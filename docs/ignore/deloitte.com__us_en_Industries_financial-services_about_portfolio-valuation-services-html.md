  * ###  Regulatory and compliance challenges 
#### Navigating complexities in valuation requirements 
The complexity of portfolio valuations continues to grow, while simultaneously, demands for more consistent and in-depth documented valuations are intensifying due to increased scrutiny from regulators, auditors, and investors.
  * ###  Enhancing transparency 
#### Navigating complexities in valuation requirements 
Private investment entities often own a variety of securities that are often illiquid and not publicly traded. In such cases, establishing their value can be a challenging task due to the absence of a market to determine the price. Portfolio valuation services can significantly enhance transparency and uniformity, meet stakeholder expectations, decrease perceived conflicts of interest, and reduce organizational risk.
  * ###  Maintaining independence 
#### Navigating complexities in valuation requirements 
Valuation services are especially valuable when investment managers need an unbiased valuation for their various illiquid investments. A private equity portfolio valuation performed by a disinterested third party not only provides a credible value but also equips the fund with the required information to finalize its financial statements.
