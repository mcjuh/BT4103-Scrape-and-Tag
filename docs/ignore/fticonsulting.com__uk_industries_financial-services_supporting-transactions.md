Banks are facing unprecedented change as result of regulatory and financial pressure. The result is that they must operate in a higher volume corporate transactional environment. As they seek to reduce the size of their balance sheets and stimulate asset migration to meet risk and capital strategies, FTI Consulting assists in stimulating liquidity, valuation and transaction efficiency. We advise on complex transactions in the financial services sector, including portfolio sales, debt and funding restructuring, hive offs and M&A. Whether representing buyers, sellers or stakeholders, we offer a comprehensive financial and operational due diligence service leading to value-related advice and hands-on support.
**Our Services include:**
  * Financial and operational due diligence
  * Vendor due diligence and assistance services
  * Transaction tax advisory
  * Fair value opinions and valuation services
  * Debt restructuring advice and implementation
  * Post transaction integration and performance improvement
  * Buy side portfolio advisory services
  * Sell side portfolio advisory services
  * Short term cash and liquidity management
  * Accelerated M&A
