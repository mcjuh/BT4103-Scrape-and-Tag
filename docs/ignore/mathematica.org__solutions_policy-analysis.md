# Policy Analysis
Strengthen your programs and strategy with rigorous policy analysis that reveals what works. 
Not all evidence is equal. We conduct rigorous research across [our focus areas](https://www.mathematica.org/our-focus-areas) to further evidence-based decision making and to help federal agencies, state and local governments, foundations, educational institutions, professional associations, and businesses understand what works.

## Overview
We are subject matter experts at the intersection of data science and social science. 
Objectivity is built into our research frameworks. 
Our data collection and research capacity spans cultures and continents. 
We conduct systematic reviews to assess the quality of existing evidence and summarize findings. 
We apply [advanced statistical methods](https://www.mathematica.org/solutions/~/link.aspx?_id=0A3196B49EB341BDBC68D0FB099294CB&_z=z), including machine learning and Bayesian methods. 
We disseminate findings through reports, presentations, videos, webinars, data visualization, and blog posts. 
Architects of Policy Research 
## Over 50 years shaping federal policymaking 
We conducted the first social policy experiment in the United States: the New Jersey Negative Income Tax Experiment. Our work has expanded and evolved over time, using rigorous analysis as its foundation, to provide decision makers with policy-relevant evidence and recommendations.
