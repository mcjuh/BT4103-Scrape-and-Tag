## KPMG Ventures builds enduring relationships with start-ups who share our commitment to serving large, enterprise clients 
KPMG Ventures is dedicated to collaborating with and investing in early-stage start-ups in areas like artificial intelligence; data; cybersecurity; environmental, social, and governance; and more.
We invest in what we consider to be the world’s most promising start-ups, with a focus on those that solve for the needs of our clients and fit into our long-term strategy. We collaborate with start-ups and find new ways to work together. By bridging the gap between start-ups and our clients, KPMG Ventures creates durable relationships that help future-proof the businesses we serve, while building the KPMG of the future. KPMG Ventures is part of the strategic innovation ecosystem at KPMG LLP.

## For clients: Tailored innovation and a window into the future
KPMG Ventures helps future-proof both the firm and our client base, amplifies our commitment to innovation and infuses new technology into our services - better supporting our clients while building the KPMG of the future.
By sharing insights from the start-up world in their domains of interest—e.g., where capital is flowing, which set of priorities they need to supercharge, and eventually what investments will help meet their expanding challenges—we can help companies gain a competitive edge.
### Future-proofing our client base
Tailored innovation for specific needs
Our investment philosophy starts by identifying start-ups solving the challenges enterprise organizations face—both today and in the future. By working together, we help start-ups mature to better meet the needs of the businesses we serve.
Access to a dynamic ecosystem
KPMG clients gain access to leading solutions, disruptive technologies, and transformative services. KPMG Ventures also acts as a signal-sensing tool in the market, helping companies understand and stay ahead of key trends.
Early adoption of breakthrough technologies
Our clients gain the opportunity to implement breakthrough technologies and advanced solutions ahead of competitors, see a first-mover advantage, and cement their businesses as industry leaders.
Reduced risk in innovation
With KPMG Ventures’ extensive vetting and deep commitment to our ecosystem of start-ups, enterprise companies can adopt emerging technologies with fewer challenges usually associated with early-stage innovations.
Collaborate with corporate venture capital teams
We welcome the opportunity to work with the businesses we support in new ways, including sharing investment perspectives with their corporate venture capital teams.

Accelerating growth for start-ups
KPMG Ventures is the strategic investor of choice for start-ups looking to solve complex business challenges and transform the way our clients operate. We make minority equity investments in early-stage start-ups (primarily Seed and Series A) and offer more than just capital – we provide a platform for growth, differentiation, and access to transformative opportunities.
Our size helps start-ups scale – with 265,000 partners and employees and a global network of technology alliances and clients, we can help amplify the impact of your business, drive industry-changing transformation and propel your company to new heights.
We’re always looking for ambitious founders building new things for enterprise clients. We’re focused on areas like artificial intelligence, data, cybersecurity, ESG and more.
**Durability, agility, and alignment through the use case**
**Strategic investor of choice:** KPMG Ventures provides a platform through which you can build powerful relationships that accelerate your growth and amplify the impact of your business.
**More than just a check:** Once a start-up is within our ecosystem, we open access to a range of experts—from specialists who can help create a more enterprise-ready solution to client-delivery teams able to make introductions.
**Driven by business value:** While many investors focus on the feature, functionality, or founder when evaluating a start-up, the heart of our investment thesis is always the use case—the business problem that the start-up is trying to solve. As start-ups mature, KPMG Ventures accelerates this evolution and supports start-ups as they refine their products.
**Durability and trust:** KPMG Ventures invests for the long run. We build relationships that are durable across economic cycles because we’re committed to our start-up ecosystem—providing support, guidance, and sales channels.
**Move at deal speed:** We combine durability with agility, because we understand that everyone needs to move with speed and purpose.
**Values and culture (a/k/a we’re nice):** Commitment, values, and culture matter. The most common informal feedback we receive is that we’re “really nice” and “easy to work with.” It’s a very human comment that can get lost when translated into the language of investments-but we believe people, relationships, and kindness matter.
