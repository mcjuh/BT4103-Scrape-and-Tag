# EHS Management
ERM provides solutions that integrate EHS compliance, sustainability commitments and business performance to enable sustainable operations. We help organizations strengthen their EHS management programs so they can be the foundation for risk management, sustainability performance and value creation.
## A shifting context for EHS Management
Operational leaders need to deliver commercial objectives while complying with an ever-expanding mass of EHS and sustainability regulations. Maintaining confidence in compliance depends on translating regulations into clear, relevant and operationally actionable content that targets real risks.
Erosion of institutional knowledge and intellectual capital has led to blinds spots on key areas of EHS risk management at corporate and asset levels. Heightened focus on sustainability at C-suite level has led to under-funding for EHS resourcing and innovation.
EHS organizational structures, hierarchies and resource allocations need to be recalibrated to current day risks and competency needs. Unintegrated tools and manual processes have led to sub-optimal uses of resources and more risks of error.
Inconsistencies between sustainability reporting and EHS data put into the public domain exacerbates compliance risks and greenwashing concerns. Lack of data governance and inconsistent data quality can lead to unreliable data being used for decision-making.
