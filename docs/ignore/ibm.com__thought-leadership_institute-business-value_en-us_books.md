### The CEO’s Guide to Generative AI
## The CEO’s Guide to Generative AI
Over the next three years, [CEOs expect gen AI to support innovative business models](https://www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/en-us/report/ceo-generative-ai) and operating models that push the envelope. They believe AI-powered people will skyrocket productivity, shifting business from a labor-based model to one that is asset-enabled. AI technology will also open up new markets by enabling employees to create high-value solutions and workflows that previously weren’t feasible or affordable. 
And things are changing at lightning speed. This book captures the latest insights the IBM Institute for Business Value has collected from rapid response research, as well as direct CEO conversations and use case pilots. We’ve distilled our in-depth knowledge into specific insights CEOs can use to responsibly scale generative AI adoption, transforming both the enterprise and the customer experience with this new technology.

### The Great Tech Reset
## The Great Tech Reset
The Great Tech Reset leverages “hybrid by design” – a strategic approach that goes beyond simply combining technologies. It’s about aligning IT with business goals, breaking down silos, and unlocking the power of cloud and AI synergy. This translates to real results: hybrid-by-design companies can generate 3x higher ROI over five years. 
Our book serves as your roadmap to navigate the Great Tech Reset and unlock the transformative power of hybrid by design.
