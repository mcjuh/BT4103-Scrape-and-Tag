# Corporate Compliance and Business Ethics Advisory
Kroll’s Corporate Compliance and Business Ethics Advisory team helps businesses turn compliance and ethics into a strategic advantage in the moments that matter most.
Against a backdrop of heightened geopolitical volatility and uncertainty, corporates and their global supply chains, joint ventures and subsidiaries must navigate complex political environments while managing evolving and—at times—conflicting compliance and ethics obligations. The efficacy of your compliance strategy can make the difference between market leadership and market exit. Companies that seek longevity, investor trust, and client and consumer confidence must demonstrate how their values, business ethics and compliance programs work in practice and must minimize negative impacts on both the bottom line and the communities in which they operate.
**One breach. One headline. That’s all it takes to lose trust. By the time a crisis hits, it may be too late.**
Companies that ignore or overestimate their compliance controls expose themselves to serious risk:
  * Significant financial and reputational impact
  * Reduced access to loans and investors
  * Consumer mistrust
  * Fines
  * Commercial disruption
  * Loss of talent
  * Criminal sanctions


> **Don’t wait for a crisis. Protect your business now. Kroll helps you reduce enforcement and reputational exposure through targeted controls.**

## What We Do
We build compliance programs that work. Our team delivers end-to-end support across your compliance program, helping you anticipate risk, build organizational trust, meet evolving regulatory expectations and safeguard your business. Our experts include specialists in anti-bribery and corruption, fraud and sanctions, data and AI governance, whistleblowing, third-party risk, human rights and environmental due diligence, environmental, social and governance (ESG) reporting, and everything in between.
### Anticipate the Threat
We provide early identification of vulnerabilities, expert due diligence, and compliance and ESG assessments tailored to your needs. From Pre-IPO, investment and M&A evaluations to tone-from-the-top culture checks and enterprise risk management, we help eliminate blind spots before they impact your business and people. Our business intelligence and external threat assessments deliver the insight you need to act with confidence and clarity.
### Design and Build the Architecture
We design commercially astute frameworks across governance, risk, compliance, privacy, AI and ethics aligned to regulatory standards and your unique risk appetite. From gap analysis and materiality assessments to fractional compliance officer services and bespoke training, we deliver practical policies, board-ready reporting, and clear ownership structures. Our experts provide strategic compliance leadership and integrate practical technology and AI solutions, ensuring your compliance architecture is built to perform in the real world.
### Assess Your Third Parties
We deliver end-to-end third-party risk management your teams can confidently execute, from beneficial ownership screening and enhanced due diligence to jurisdiction and sector risk mapping, value chain analysis, and regulatory readiness. Our full-service approach covers everything from sanctions, human rights, privacy, and environmental assessments, supported by automated workflows and easy-to-follow playbooks. Whether navigating M&A, distributor governance, or evolving global regulations, we provide proportionate, practical controls that protect your business at every level.
### Assure and Monitor
We provide monitorship, readiness assessments, and independent verification across legal, audit, compliance, and ESG functions. Our technical effectiveness testing, data lineage mapping, and dashboard-driven integrity checks ensure your controls perform as intended. We benchmark against industry and regulatory standards, verify ESG claims, disclosures, and reporting aligned to the latest laws and standards. We design and assess control frameworks to keep them commercially pragmatic and aligned to your evolving business needs.
### Respond in a Crisis
We provide expert incident response with cultural recovery across HR, compliance, legal, AI governance, and ESG issues. We manage sensitive internal and external investigations into fraud, bribery, sanctions, misconduct, human rights, greenwashing, ESG controversies and cybersecurity incidents. Our team delivers prioritized mitigation strategies, remediation road maps, and regulator-ready documentation to close gaps and restore trust. From whistleblowing protocol reviews to corporate intelligence and crisis strategy, we help you navigate the most challenging moments with confidence and control.
### Promote Your Programs
We help you communicate your ethics and compliance initiatives with clarity and confidence, both internally and externally. From board-ready materials covering strategy, budget, and ROI to market-facing communications that position compliance as a competitive advantage, we tailor every message to your audience. Our approach is sensitive to conflicting geopolitical attitudes, ensuring your program resonates across stakeholders, cultures, and markets while reinforcing trust and long-term value.
