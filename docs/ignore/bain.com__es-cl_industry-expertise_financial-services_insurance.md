## AI in Insurance
As an industry that’s heavily reliant on large amounts of unstructured data, insurance has unlocked tremendous value from AI. Insurers are improving efficiencies across the property and casualty (P&C), life, and health insurance value chains, delivering:
• Smarter marketing and product innovation through AI-powered content creation and • personalized customer engagement• Elevated customer experience with real-time sentiment analysis and personalized recommendations• Operational efficiency at scale via automated underwriting• Stronger enterprise enablement across legal, risk, finance, HR, and IT—streamlining everything from contract and invoice generation to fraud detection and compliance
