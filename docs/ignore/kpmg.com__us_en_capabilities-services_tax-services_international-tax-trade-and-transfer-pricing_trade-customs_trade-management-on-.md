# KPMG Trade Management on Demand 
Our managed services allow you to focus on what’s important to you. 
##  Service overview 
Global trade is undergoing rapid change. Our managed services extend beyond importers. We help your company deliver strategic value to higher- priority work by outsourcing repetitive tasks. KPMG can help you do this efficiently, compliantly, and with full transparency.
The KPMG difference 
Extensive trade and industry knowledge and experience with global reach to help provide clients with creative methodologies and skilled leadership utilizing innovative technology. 
Cut through complexity 
Managed services at competitive costs to help navigate and simplify operations with specialists dedicated to quality and detail. 
Improve efficiency 
Continual investment in technology and innovative solutions to help keep your operations running nimbly and effectively. 
Mitigate risks 
Achieve strong results in regulatory and compliance processes to assess, manage, and reduce trade risks. 
### KPMG Trade Management includes:
Free trade agreements (FTA) 
We help you increase savings and meet regulatory requirements for every FTA claimed.
Classification 
Our team utilizes artificial intelligence (AI) to assist in performing cost-effective and accurate import and export classifications. 
Trade data management 
Our teams use an automated validation process to identify errors on import entries before they become systemic.
Automated value reconciliation 
Our team’s experience, combined with process mapping, enables us to break down adjustments impacting value, calculate weighted allocations, and recalculate duties and fees supported by an auditable trail.
Restricted party screening 
We perform customized screening to help confirm that only lawful transactions occur.
Foreign trade zone (FTZ) management 
We help your company benefit from duty-deferral without performing the day-to-day tasks of running a zone.
