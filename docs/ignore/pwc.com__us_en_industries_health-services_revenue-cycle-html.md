### Operating Model and Data-Driven Insights
PwC redesigns revenue cycle operating models grounded in data, automation, and predictive insight to enable faster, smarter decision-making across RCM functions. 
**Key Solutions Delivered**
  * AI-enabled operating model design that translates insight into action 
  * Predictive and prescriptive analytics embedded into workflows to drive real-time decisions and measurable performance improvement


### EHR / RCM Transformation
PwC enables organizations to maximize the value of their EHR and RCM platforms by optimizing native capabilities, improving configuration, and integrating complementary technologies. 
**Key Solutions Delivered**
  * EHR optimization and integration with payer and third-party systems 
  * Workflow standardization and orchestration for clinical-financial alignment


### Clinical Alignment and Optimization
PwC bridges clinical and financial workflows and incentives to improve revenue capture while maintaining high standards of quality and compliance. 
**Key Solutions Delivered**
  * Technology-enabled coding quality platform to strengthen revenue integrity and compliance
  * Automated coding workflows for accelerated billing cycles 
  * Market-based strategic pricing and value-based care reporting capabilities and analysis


### Intelligent Automation and AI
PwC embeds AI and intelligent automation across the revenue cycle to eliminate manual work, deliver real-time decision support, and unlock capacity at scale. 
**Key Solutions Delivered**
  * End-to-end process automation 
  * Generative and agentic AI enablement 
  * Denials prevention and next-best-action analytics


### Digital Patient Engagement
PwC scales consumer-first experiences that improve access, financial transparency, and patient satisfaction. 
**Key Solutions Delivered**
  * Omni-channel patient engagement 
  * Proactive, personalized outreach for care continuity


### Strategic Access
PwC drives revenue growth and margin improvement by aligning patient access strategies with demand, capacity, and reimbursement realities. 
**Key Solutions Delivered**
  * Access growth strategy and capacity alignment 
  * Demand forecasting and utilization insights


### Revenue Cycle Managed Services
PwC provides revenue cycle managed services that stabilize performance, address workforce constraints, and continuously improve revenue cycle outcomes.
**Key Solutions Delivered**
  * AI-enabled end-to-end RCM business process outsourcing services with a proven track record of improving financial performance
  * Exception-based workflow leadership, managing $40BN+ in AR


### Revenue Cycle Strategic Reimbursement
PwC strengthens reimbursement performance by optimizing payer strategies and improving contract outcomes, delivering sustainable margin improvement while navigating increasing payer and regulatory complexity. 
**Key Solutions Delivered**
  * Contract strategy and intelligence platforms 
  * Reimbursement performance predictive analytics


### Join PwC’s Revenue Cycle Consortium
Connect with revenue cycle leaders from health systems across the country to exchange insights, explore timely industry topics, and benchmark key financial and operational metrics. PwC’s Revenue Cycle Consortium helps members surface leading practices, strengthen performance, and drive continuous improvement in a rapidly evolving healthcare landscape.
