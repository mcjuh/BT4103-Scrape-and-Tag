## [Meet the Team](https://aesg.com/environment-sustainability/sahel-accessibility-rating-system-consultancy/#Team)
AESG provides specialist consultancy services to support the implementation of the **SAHEL Rating System** across new developments and existing assets in Abu Dhabi. Our qualified SAHEL professionals combine accessibility expertise with extensive experience in multidisciplinary design integration, regulatory compliance, sustainability certification, construction monitoring, and operational performance.
Introduced by Abu Dhabi’s Department of Municipalities and Transport, SAHEL establishes a consistent framework for creating environments that are accessible, inclusive, adaptable, and easy to navigate. The system addresses the needs of people of determination, senior citizens, parents with young children, and individuals with temporary or permanent accessibility requirements. It applies across three principal categories—Communities, Public Realm, and Buildings—with projects assessed against four performance levels: Accessible, Adaptable, Inclusive, and Exemplar.
## Why It Matters
## Globally, an estimated 1 in 6 people live with some form of disability – a figure that grows further once senior citizens, parents with young children, and people with temporary mobility needs are considered. Designing for accessibility means designing for the majority of end users at some point in their lives, not a narrow segment of them.
## As SAHEL becomes an established reference point for accessibility performance in Abu Dhabi, projects that embed it from the outset gain a measurable advantage: fewer late-stage redesigns, stronger asset resale and leasing appeal, and a stronger position when engaging institutional and government occupiers who increasingly expect inclusive-design credentials as standard.
## Our Approach
Our approach embeds accessibility requirements into the project from the earliest stages rather than treating compliance as a retrospective assessment. Through structured design coordination, progressive compliance reviews, site verification, and evidence management, AESG helps project teams identify risks early, minimise redesign, control cost impacts, and deliver practical accessibility outcomes that remain effective throughout the asset’s operation.By integrating SAHEL consultancy with AESG’s wider expertise in sustainability, building performance, mobility, human-centric design, commissioning, digital modelling, and asset operations, we provide clients with a coordinated and efficient pathway toward inclusive, compliant, and high-performing developments across Abu Dhabi.
## Our Services
## AESG supports clients, developers, designers, contractors, and asset operators throughout the complete project lifecycle, from early planning and design through construction, handover, and operation. Our services include:
  * SAHEL applicability assessment and target-rating definition
  * Project coordination with the relevant authorities
  * Baseline reviews, gap assessments, and development of project-specific compliance strategies
  * Translation of SAHEL requirements into clear architectural, interior design, landscape, public realm, mobility, wayfinding, and operational requirements
  * Accessibility workshops with client and multidisciplinary design teams
  * Detailed design reviews, compliance trackers, action registers, and technical recommendations


  * Integration of accessibility requirements into specifications, drawings, schedules, employer’s requirements, and tender documentation
  * Review of accessible routes, entrances, vertical circulation, sanitary facilities, parking, drop-off areas, signage, wayfinding, emergency provisions, and user interfaces
  * Construction-stage inspections and verification of installed accessibility measures
  * Review and compilation of supporting evidence, testing records, as-built information, and closeout documentation
  * Support during authority reviews, assessments, audits, and final rating submissions
  * Operational accessibility reviews and recommendations for existing buildings and public spaces
