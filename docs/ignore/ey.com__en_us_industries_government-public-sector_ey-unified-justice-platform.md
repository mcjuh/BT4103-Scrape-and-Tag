Across criminal justice, public safety and law enforcement agencies, archaic case management systems and dated technologies create significant barriers to effective information sharing and collaboration. Issues such as data inconsistency, redundancy and difficulty in linking case information across the justice consortium have led to inefficiencies in case management and reduction of confidence in judicial institutions. By simplifying the inherent complexity of integration along with modern technology capabilities, the Unified Justice Platform helps agencies connect the dots across the criminal justice continuum.
Read more  Read less 
### Unified Justice Platform components
Read more  Read less 
  1. Event-driven architecture 
  2. NIEM knowledge graph 
  3. Justice Hub 
  4. AI analysis assistant 


Experience the power of real-time data sharing and process automation across justice and public safety organizations. Our architecture supports instant communication and processing of events, driving efficiency and responsiveness.
Read more  Read less 
Unlock seamless data sharing and interoperability with a knowledge graph based on the National Information Exchange Model (NIEM). Entity resolution based on artificial intelligence (AI) is baked in to connect persons and cases across the justice landscape.
Read more  Read less 
A centralized application provides a single point of access to justice-related information and data across organizations. Our Justice Hub provides data integrity, security and accessibility for authorized users, streamlining your operations.
Read more  Read less 
Harness the power of advanced AI. You can ask questions in natural language and request case summaries and reports via an AI chat interface. You can also use our AI video analyzer to analyze bodycam/dashcam footage, create event timelines and generate incident reports.
Read more  Read less 
The EY Unified Justice Platform empowers agencies to navigate the criminal justice continuum more effectively through:
Read more  Read less 
  * ##  Standards-based interoperability
Achieve seamless information exchange across various source systems, enabling timely access to accurate data for quicker, informed decision-making.
Read more  Read less 
  * ##  Run-anywhere container platform
Designed to provide unparalleled flexibility and scalability, the platform allows you to package your applications and to run consistently across any on-premises, hybrid or cloud environment.
Read more  Read less 
  * ##  Enhanced operational efficiency
Automating repetitive tasks and embedding intelligence into processes streamline workflows, significantly increasing efficiency.
Read more  Read less 
  * ##  Person-centric information model
The use of an entity-resolved knowledge graph enhances understanding of justice data and accelerates insights across systems or data domains.
Read more  Read less 
  * ##  Strengthened security posture
Integrated Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) security controls protect sensitive data and reduce noncompliance risk.
Read more  Read less 
  * ##  Reduced total cost of ownership
By leveraging commodity tools and adhering to open standards, our vendor-agnostic platform manages both initial and ongoing ownership costs for justice systems.
