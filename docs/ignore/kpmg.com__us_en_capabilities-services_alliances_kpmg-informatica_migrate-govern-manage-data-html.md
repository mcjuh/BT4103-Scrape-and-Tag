# Informatica | Migrate, govern, and manage your data
Intelligent Data Management
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2022/informatica-intelligent-data-management.pdf)
Share
Data has the power to create great value by driving better decisions. Yet, the quality and the speed at which key decisions can be derived depends on your ability to:
  * Leverage automation and cloud-based architectures to maintain agility and flexibility
  * Govern data according to new regulations and evolving technologies
  * Manage and maintain trusted data throughout the organization.


More and more enterprises store data in legacy, on- premises warehouses or platforms and are considering a move to the cloud. IT leaders must identify what assets to migrate and then efficiently onboard the data, files, and databases to cloud-based platforms. 
Privacy regulations—such as General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)—require enterprises to know where personally identifiable information (PII) resides and be able to access or delete it. To be compliant, executives need a centralized platform with updated local and global privacy regulations.
To reduce errors and streamline business processes, enterprises seek to create a single, master source for all critical business data. Master data management (MDM) helps ensure policies are maintained and adhered to, builds confidence in data quality, and provides access to trusted data required for analysis.
The joint Intelligent Data Management offering combines KPMG D&A advisory services with Informatica’s enterprise data management products. It supports cloud migration, data governance, and MDM across various data platforms. By emphasizing data- driven, rapid decision-making, the offering changes the way organizations interact with data.
KPMG and Informatica also help clients manage their in-cloud and on-premises data needs across cataloging, lineage, usage, transformation, storage, and privacy. By leveraging advanced technologies such as AI and machine learning, KPMG and Informatica can bring additional value through searchable catalogs, data marketplaces, and predictive data quality.
