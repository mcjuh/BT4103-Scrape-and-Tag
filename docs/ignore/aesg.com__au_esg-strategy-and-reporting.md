At AESG Australia, we specialise in developing customised ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) strategies and reporting frameworks that align with Australian regulations and your project’s specific goals. Our approach focuses on identifying key performance indicators (KPIs) to enhance sustainability, meet stakeholder expectations, and support long-term value creation.
## The Importance of ESG in Australia
With increasingly stringent regulations and the rise of sustainable finance, an effective ESG strategy is essential for organisations in both the public and private sectors. Addressing environmental, social, and governance issues not only ensures regulatory compliance but also strengthens investor confidence and meets the demands of key stakeholders.
Our ESG strategies are tailored to align with the expectations of investors, regulators, Indigenous communities, and the wider public. We ensure your project contributes positively to environmental protection, social well-being, and ethical governance practices, while transparent ESG reporting demonstrates accountability and progress.
## Australian Regulatory Context
We ensure compliance with key frameworks such as:
### Australian Sustainability Reporting Standard (ASRS)
Mandatory climate disclosure.
### ASX Corporate Governance Principles
Managing ESG risks and opportunities.
### Modern Slavery Act 2018
Reporting on actions to address modern slavery in operations and supply chains.
### NGER Scheme
Mandatory reporting of greenhouse gas emissions and energy use for large corporations.
### ASIC and APRA Guidance
Climate risk disclosure and management of financial risks related to climate change.

## ESG Reporting
Our ESG reporting frameworks are designed to monitor, evaluate, and communicate your sustainability performance effectively. We ensure that your reports meet global and local standards, including:
• Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards • Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) • Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) Recommendations • Task Force on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) Recommendations • Integrated Reporting (< IR >) Framework • United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs)
Our reports provide transparent, data-driven insights that demonstrate your commitment to sustainability and ethical governance, helping you build trust with stakeholders and investors.
