#  Leaders Solving for Tomorrow 
Today’s leaders face an era of profound transformation. Megatrends - from climate change and technological disruption to shifting demographics and global instability - are reshaping industries, societies, and economies at an unprecedented pace. The challenge isn’t just to adapt, but to lead with confidence, inspire trust, and drive meaningful change and deliver sustained outcomes for your organisation and society.
The imperative to act is clear. CEOs anticipate greater pressure in the coming years, with [PwC’s CEO Survey 2025](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/issues/c-suite-insights/archive/ceo-survey-2025.html) revealing that 42% believe their companies won’t be viable in ten years if they stay on their current path. The world demands reinvention, and no single business, leader, or sector can tackle these challenges alone.​
PwC's award-winning Leaders Solving for Tomorrow programme is a seven-week experience designed to equip senior leaders with the insights, capabilities, and strategic connections needed to shape the future. Through dynamic discussions, professional-led sessions, and cross-industry collaboration, LSFT helps leaders make sense of global Megatrends and harness transformation for long-term success.​
Leadership today isn’t just about managing complexity—it’s about reinvention, trust, and the courage to shape what’s next. Are you ready to lead into the future?​

## Shaping tomorrow’s transformative leaders
Our programme equips leaders with dynamic insights and innovative perspectives that enable them to proactively navigate and shape disruption, create sustained value and foster growth in motion.
Through a mix of self-paced and live modules, leaders are steered through the programme – applying their learning to their experience, needs and challenges. The programme is fully digital, making it accessible to all leaders, regardless of their location or background.
## The programme aims to:
  * **Broadened Perspective:** Sharpen your awareness of urgent global issues and their impact on industries and organisations worldwide.​
  * **Strategic Relevance:** Connect Megatrends to your own business context, identifying what must change now to stay ahead.​
  * **Actionable Foresight:** Recognise critical shifts and immediate actions required to ensure ongoing relevance and economic viability.​
  * **Leadership Effectiveness:** Deepen self-awareness and develop strategies to elevate your leadership impact in times of uncertainty.​
  * **Systemic Thinking:** Strengthen your ability to lead organisational change, drive cross-functional collab.
