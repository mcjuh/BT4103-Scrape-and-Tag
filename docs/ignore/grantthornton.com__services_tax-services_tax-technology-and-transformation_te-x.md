## Frequently asked questions
What are the installation requirements?
There is no installation needed. The application is hosted by Grant Thornton and used by Grant Thornton employees for your benefit.
Does te.x integrate with other solutions?
The application interacts with [**Vertex**](https://www.grantthornton.com/services/alliances/vertex) O Series on-demand or internally hosted Grant Thornton environments. For clients that use Vertex on-premise, we will replicate your environment on Grant Thornton servers in order to perform testing.
Does te.x replace the need for an implementation consultant?
te.x helps you complete the implementation requirements, design, configuration, and testing process, but it does not replace the need for a professional to interpret and design best practices.
Will te.x support complex tax requirements?
te.x is a highly customizable solution that works for clients of all sizes. It can support the simplest to the most complex tax and system integration requirements.
