## How we can help

The automotive and industrial industry is going through a major transformation driven by product and process technology shifts, onshoring of supply chains and the rapidly growing influence of EV manufacturers and governments looking to diversify away from dependence on overseas battery and electronic components manufacturers. The transformation affects each part of the automotive and mobility value chain, from OEMs, suppliers, dealers and aftermarket players to new technology and mobility players. The growing importance of electric, autonomous and connected vehicles has made it more important than ever to partner with industry experts who can help navigate these rapidly changing markets.
### How We Help 
Our local Canadian experts and global automotive team leverage decades of industry experience and knowledge of the challenges and opportunities to advise management teams in this ever-evolving industry. FTI Consulting offers a comprehensive range of business transformation services to vehicle manufacturers, suppliers, retailers, financers and other automotive and clean/smart mobility subsectors, as well as their creditors, lenders and other stakeholders.
Highly experienced financial and operational advisors lead our industry team and focus on key issues, value and solutions for our clients. We support our Canadian clients from our offices in Toronto, Montreal, Calgary and Vancouver and operate a full network across North America. This cross-border, integrated approach is designed to address the unique situations that both local and large multinational companies face and provide customized communications and support on the ground in different markets.
