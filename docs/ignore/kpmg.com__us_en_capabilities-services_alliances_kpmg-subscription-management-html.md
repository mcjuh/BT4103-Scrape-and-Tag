# KPMG Subscription Management, enabled by Oracle 
Thrive in the subscription economy: optimize service, enhance customer experience, and boost efficiency with KPMG and Oracle.

The software-as-a-service (SaaS) model did more than transform how software was consumed by businesses and individuals alike; it ignited the subscription economy. Today, as the everything-as-a-service (XaaS) revolution continues to obliterate limitations on what can be sold by subscription, companies pivoting to a subscription model are discovering that it can be more transformative for them than for their customers.
### Thrive in the Subscription Economy
KPMG Subscription Management, enabled by Oracle, can help businesses that offer subscription-based services make the technology and operating model transformations necessary to succeed in the subscription economy.
Instead of a sale, a subscription is an ongoing relationship with your customer. With the Oracle Subscription Management solution as the foundation, we can help you manage and optimize subscription services, create seamless customer-centric experiences, improve operational efficiency and billing accuracy, and meet financial reporting and regulatory compliance objectives.
### Key Benefits:
  * Automate billing, invoicing, and revenue recognition
  * Improve renewal management
  * Enhance customer interactions
  * Improve customer acquisition and retention:
  * Open a window into performance
  * Maintain compliance and security


**Why KPMG?**
Oracle Subscription Management is the only fully integrated, cloud-based subscription management system that simplifies the management of contracts, billing, and revenue. Unlocking its full value requires an advisor that understands you, your business, and your industry; an advisor that can help you make the strategic, technology, and organizational changes necessary to tear down barriers to continuous innovation.
