#  2025 Annual Trends Webinar
February 5, 2025 
Webinar recording 
What's next for sustainable business? Join a global panel of ERM experts on to hear their insights as they discussed the highlights from the ERM Sustainability Institute's [**2025 Annual Trends Report: The Path Forward for Sustainable Business.**](https://www.erm.com/insights/2025-annual-trends-report-the-path-forward-for-sustainable-business/)
##  About the webinar
The discussion, hosted by ERM Sustainability Institute Director Aiste Brackley and Research & Networks Manager Andrew Angle on 5th February, explored:
  * The biggest sustainability trends in 2025 and their impact on global companies
  * Expected developments in climate change, sustainability disclosure, nature, technology, supply chains, sustainable consumption, and other areas
  * Recommendations for companies on how to respond to the expected developments and stay ahead of the curve


We were joined by an ERM expert panel:
  * Alison Dury, Senior Partner, Global Industry Lead, Technology
  * Paul Simpson, OBE, Consulting Partner, Corporate Sustainability and Climate Change
  * Sarah Bostwick, Principal Consultant, Corporate Sustainability
