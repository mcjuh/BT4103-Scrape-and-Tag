# KPMG Digital Gateway Valuation Catalyst
KPMG Digital Gateway Valuation Catalyst redefines how valuations are delivered — centralizing data, connecting workflows, and embedding AI capabilities across the entire valuation lifecycle. Greater insights. Stronger governance. A more connected experience.
Platform overview 
## A single, integrated valuation environment
Built on KPMG Digital Gateway, Valuation Catalyst brings together data, workflows, and people into one connected environment — transforming how valuations are delivered, enhancing the process from start to finish.
The platform centralizes valuation data and connects each step of the process — from information requests through modeling, reviewing, and reporting — creating a more transparent, structured, and consistent way to deliver valuations. Every output is guided by professional judgement, with controls and governance built in from the start.

Core capabilities
## Three pillars of the Valuation Catalyst platform
Valuation Catalyst addresses the most critical challenges in the valuation lifecycle — from data fragmentation to limited client transparency — through three interconnected capabilities.
Centralized data 
Integrated workflow 
AI-enabled intelligence 
## Centralized data
A single source of truth for every engagement. All documentation, requests, and outputs are organized within a structured environment, enabling consistent visibility across projects and portfolios.
**Key features:**
  * Eliminates fragmented files, emails, and shared drives
  * Improves traceability and audit readiness
  * Supports AI-analytics layered on a clean data foundation
  * Teams and clients work from the same complete, up-to-date information


## Integrated workflow
Connects core valuation activities within a single environment — improving consistency and reducing the need for manual reconciliation while preserving the flexibility of established tools.
**Key features:**
  * Links modeling, review, and reporting seamlessly
  * Reduces manual coordination across teams
  * Information request templates that can be customized for every project
  * Real-time visibility into data request progress and overall project status, with direct client document uploads as an option


## AI-enabled intelligence
Intelligent capabilities that help teams and clients engage with valuation data more efficiently — supporting summarization, navigation of complex content, and dynamic interaction with results.
**Key features:**
  * AI-assisted summarization of valuation content
  * Dynamic exploration of underlying data
  * Accelerates response to questions on complex matters
  * All outputs subject to professional judgement and review

## More than a platform. A KPMG delivery model.
Valuation Catalyst was purpose-built — not adapted from a general-purpose platform. It reflects the leading valuation practices from KPMG with technology-enabled delivery and a commitment to innovation.
  * **Proven methodologies and governance:** Every process embedded in Valuation Catalyst reflects KPMG’s leading valuation practices — standardized, audit-ready, and designed for regulatory environments where accuracy, transparency, and accountability are non-negotiable. Built-in quality review, version control, and traceability help ensure the analysis is defensible.
  * **Technology-enabled delivery model:** KPMG is not adapting a general-purpose platform to valuation — Valuation Catalyst was purpose-built on KPMG Digital Gateway to connect data, workflows, and analysis within a single environment. Integrated modeling, centralized data, and AI-enabled capabilities support how work is performed, while KPMG professionals are accountable at every step.
  * **Future-ready innovation:** Valuation Catalyst is designed to evolve continuously — with expanded use cases, advanced AI capabilities, and an ongoing commitment to improving how valuations are delivered to meet evolving market, regulatory, and client demands.
