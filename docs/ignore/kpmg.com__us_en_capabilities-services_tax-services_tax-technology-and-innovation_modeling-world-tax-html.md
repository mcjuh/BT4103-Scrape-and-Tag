# Digital Gateway Modeling Suite 
Discover the far-reaching power of tax models with KPMG Digital Gateway.
## Put the power of modeling in your tax team’s hands with KPMG Digital Gateway
In a fast-evolving tax landscape marked by unprecedented uncertainties and unknowns, proactive and robust tax modeling and scenario planning have never been more critical—nor more complex. That’s why the KPMG Digital Gateway platform serves as a vital platform for today’s enterprise tax function leaders. Its suite of advanced, data-driven, and AI-enabled modeling tools equips tax departments to position themselves for success as they navigate the evolving tax landscape shaped by the [**Tax Policy Trifecta**](https://kpmg.com/us/en/insights-by-topic/tax-policy-trifecta.html).
Major tax department challenges:
**US tax legislation** : Understanding and planning for the implications stemming from OB3, and anticipating and preparing for future legislation.
**Global reform** : The implementation of BEPS 2.0 Pillar Two, future developments post-Pillar One, and global trends like the UN's agenda on cross-border services withholding tax all demand strategic attention and adaptation.
**Regulatory disruption** : New guidance under the Inflation Reduction Act and the Corporate Alternative Minimum Tax, alongside potential new regulations, require agile responses.
To help clients prepare and respond to regulatory changes, including the bill commonly known as the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OB3), BEPS Pillar Two, and CAMT, our scenario-comparison overlay provides detailed and transparent results, showing the cash tax and effective tax rate impacts of different choices under the new laws. 
Turn uncertainty into opportunity
### Sophisticated scenario-comparison overlays are designed to help clients navigate the complexities of new tax provisions like One Big Beautiful Bill.
## Key benefits:
Digital Gateway modeling tools empower the tax function to simulate scenarios, evaluate impacts and devise strategies to mitigate risks and seize opportunities of the Tax Policy Trifecta.
Dynamic scenario modeling
Enable strategic and informed decision-making by evaluating various tax scenarios set by the user, including Section 174 expensing vs. amortization under OB3, and other tax planning options – allowing tax departments to see the multi-year effects of planning choices using real data for transparent and detailed analysis.
Integrated data aggregation and tax calculations
Consolidate and connect outputs from multiple complex models into a single, cohesive view helping to ensure accuracy and consistency across tax models.
Transparency and traceability
Excel-based calculations allow users to trace inputs to outputs, enhancing reliability.
Detailed dashboard and overlays
Visualize the outcomes and impacts of different customizable scenarios in an easy-to-understand dashboard - Facilitating dynamic discussions with key stakeholders with complex information in a clear and accessible format.
Timely updates
Automatic updates for tax law changes keep businesses compliant and informed.
Effective collaboration
Facilitate efficient operation across teams and borders, crucial for managing global tax complexities.
About KPMG Digital Gateway
KPMG Digital Gateway is a centralized, cloud-based access portal to a suite of tax tools that facilitate and accelerate a wide range of tax operations, including robust support for tax modeling and scenario planning. With capabilities for managing, analyzing and extracting insights from data, Digital Gateway helps tax leaders identify and mitigate risks, respond swiftly to regulatory changes, plan for future scenarios, and turn the challenges of the Tax Policy Trifecta into opportunities for strategic growth and resilience.
