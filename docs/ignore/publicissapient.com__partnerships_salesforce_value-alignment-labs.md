#  The Value Alignment Lab 
Our Value Alignment Labs help organizations connect Salesforce investments to business outcomes. Each lab is tailored to your goals and delivered virtually or in person. Publicis Sapient experts work with key stakeholders to identify blockers, define near-term actions and build a practical roadmap for governed, measurable progress across your Salesforce environment.
[](https://www.publicissapient.com/partners/salesforce/value-alignment-labs#request-a-workshop)
## What to expect
Customer engagement and personalization
Publicis Sapient and Salesforce help organizations deliver connected, real-time customer engagement powered by unified data, AI and journey orchestration. Through our Customer Engagement Lab, we help clients define the right personalization strategy across marketing, commerce, service and loyalty experiences. Using Salesforce Data Cloud, Marketing Cloud and Agentforce, we enable organizations to activate first-party data at scale — creating more relevant, predictive and conversational customer interactions across every touchpoint. Our teams help clients accelerate:
  * AI-powered personalization
  * Cross-channel journey orchestration
  * Loyalty and lifecycle engagement
  * Real-time audience activation
  * Connected customer experiences across CRM, commerce and service


With customers increasingly expecting seamless and personalized interactions, the Customer Engagement Lab helps organizations move from fragmented engagement to connected, intelligent experiences that drive measurable business value.
Total commerce
Publicis Sapient and Salesforce partner to help organizations build connected, AI-enabled commerce experiences across the full customer journey. Through the Commerce Value Alignment Lab, we help clients identify opportunities to modernize commerce operations, improve conversion and unlock more agile digital growth.
Leveraging Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Agentforce and Data Cloud, we help brands create scalable commerce ecosystems that connect product discovery, personalization, fulfillment, customer service and post-purchase engagement.
The lab focuses on helping organizations:
  * Increase conversion and basket size
  * Reduce friction across digital journeys
  * Enable conversational and agentic commerce experiences
  * Improve operational agility and speed-to-market
  * Connect commerce data across marketing, service and loyalty


As consumer expectations continue to evolve, organizations need commerce experiences that are intelligent, adaptive and deeply connected across channels. Our Commerce Lab helps define the roadmap to achieve that transformation.
Agentic commerce lab
Publicis Sapient and Salesforce help organizations prepare for the next evolution of digital commerce — where AI agents become a new interface to customer engagement, product discovery and purchasing journeys. Through the Agentic Commerce Lab, we help clients identify how autonomous agents, connected data and AI-powered experiences can unlock new growth opportunities across the commerce ecosystem.
Leveraging Salesforce Commerce Cloud, Agentforce and Data Cloud, the lab helps organizations design and activate intelligent commerce experiences that combine conversational interfaces, personalization and real-time decisioning across every stage of the customer journey.
The Agentic Commerce Lab focuses on helping organizations:
  * Explore agent-powered shopping and service experiences
  * Enable conversational and AI-assisted commerce journeys
  * Activate real-time personalization using unified customer data
  * Connect commerce, loyalty, marketing and service interactions
  * Increase API-driven commerce flexibility and scalability
  * Improve conversion, retention and customer lifetime value
  * Identify operational efficiencies through AI-enabled automation


As AI reshapes how customers discover, evaluate and purchase products, organizations need commerce experiences that are adaptive, connected and intelligent by design. The Agentic Commerce Lab helps define the strategic roadmap, technical architecture and business priorities required to compete in an increasingly agent-driven economy.
Identity resolution
Publicis Sapient, Publicis Media and Salesforce help organizations build trusted, privacy-first data foundations for the AI era. Through the Identity Resolution Lab, stakeholders collaborate to define how customer data can be unified, governed and activated across the enterprise.
Powered by Salesforce Data Cloud and Publicis Groupe’s data capabilities, the lab helps organizations create a connected customer graph that improves personalization, audience intelligence and marketing effectiveness — while reducing reliance on third-party identifiers.
Key focus areas include:
  * First-party data strategy
  * Identity resolution and customer unification
  * Consent and privacy-aware activation
  * Data Cloud enablement
  * Audience and segmentation strategy
  * Measurement and media optimization


The outcome is a scalable, future-ready data foundation that supports connected experiences, AI activation and more effective decision-making across marketing, commerce and customer engagement.
