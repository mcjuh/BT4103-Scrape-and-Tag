#  Capital Markets and Accounting Advisory Services 
Share
Copy Link Link Copied
Close
Capital markets are undergoing a transformative phase where technology, regulations, and business transformation intersect, creating both opportunities and complexities for companies pursuing growth through IPOs or SPAC mergers.
We go beyond traditional audit services to act as your trusted accounting advisor, combining deep technical expertise with AI‑enabled insights to deliver tailored solutions for complex accounting issues and transaction structures.
## Our vision
At PwC Singapore, we provide you with clear technical answers to capital market and corporate reporting challenges. Whether you're looking to de-risk your most complex transactions, preparing for IPO, or seeking corporate reporting guidance, our Capital Markets and Accounting Advisory Services (CMAAS) team is here to support you every step of the way.
With deep technical expertise and AI-enabled solutions, we empower businesses to navigate transitions with confidence—helping you unlock value, strengthen trust, and prepare for sustainable success in this rapidly changing market.

Capital markets strategies
### We execute capital-markets strategies that helps your business succeed.
Companies embarking on new business or capital market transactions, whether through an initial public offering (IPO) or via a merger with a special purpose acquisition company (SPAC), or by obtaining green and sustainable financing through schemes announced by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), face new challenges due to increasingly stringent compliance and regulatory requirements. With the evolving reporting requirements, the scrutiny on accounting and financial reporting has never been so high and the digitalisation of the finance function is also playing an increasingly important role.
Whether the ultimate choice is through an IPO, SPAC, or direct listing, businesses need to objectively assess their readiness for operation as a public company.
Digital finance and accounting
### We build trust through accounting advisory.
At PwC Singapore, we help organisations tackle accounting and reporting challenges with confidence. Our Accounting Advisory team combines deep technical expertise with cutting-edge digital finance solutions to streamline processes and enhance compliance. From resolving complex accounting issues to providing flexible managed services, we deliver end-to-end support that drives efficiency and strategic insight.

Initial Public Offering (IPO)
### Initial Public Offering (IPO)
An IPO marks a pivotal milestone for any organisation, and preparing to be a public company is just as critical as starting up the business itself. Achieving the full value of an IPO demands confidence, clear decision‑making, and a strong foundation for long‑term success — our team is here to assist you every step of the way.
[Find out more](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/services/assurance/capital-markets-and-accounting-advisory/ipo.html)
Special Purpose Acquisition Company (SPAC)
### Special Purpose Acquisition Company (SPAC)
SPACs are increasingly attractive as a liquidity pathway, offering a significantly faster route to going public than a traditional IPO — but they also come with their own complexities. Our PwC team helps you navigate these challenges with confidence, providing the specialised insights and support needed to maximise value throughout your SPAC journey.
[Find out more](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/services/assurance/capital-markets-and-accounting-advisory/spac.html)
Accounting advisory
### Accounting advisory
We help organisations navigate complex accounting challenges arising from crises, regulatory changes and evolving standards by delivering precise, practical and compliant solutions. Supported by deep technical expertise and AI‑enabled analytics, our specialists support you across the full accounting lifecycle — from setting foundational structures to enabling smooth operations and responding to complex transactions — backed by deep expertise across key accounting areas. We provide targeted, compliance‑focused support to identify gaps and align your application with the latest accounting requirements and industry best practices.
###### Our transformation approach includes:
  1. Setting up accounting policies
  2. Setting up and implement processes for periodic closing and reporting
  3. Implementing General Ledger modules and reporting systems
  4. Accounting support for deals and post‑deal reporting support
  5. Advisory in specific accounting issues
  6. GAAP conversion and implementation
  7. End‑to‑end accounting and reporting implementation for new business set‑up
  8. Finance and accounting training


[Find out more](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/services/assurance/ifrs-18-redefining-financial-performance-reporting.html)
Managed services
### Managed services
Over two‑thirds of top‑performing companies now rely on managed services to gain strategic advantage and address critical capability gaps. We offer a flexible resourcing solution — enhanced by AI‑enabled insights and automation — that provides you access to specialised resources exactly when needed for you to maximise your operational efficiency. 
###### Our service offerings include:
  1. **Complex technical accounting**
     * Lease accounting
     * Hedge accounting
     * Revenue accounting
     * Contract review
  2. **Financial close and financial reporting**
     * Pre-close preparation
     * Data collection and validation
     * Intercompany reconciliation
     * Multi-entity and multi-GAAP reporting
     * Consolidation process
     * Financial statement and disclosures
     * Reporting and analysis
  3. **Finance operations (AR/AP management)**
     * Invoicing
     * Accounts receivable/payable management
     * Cash application
     * Reporting and analysis
     * Accounting journals
     * Process improvement
  4. **Financial planning and analysis (FP &A)**
     * Budgeting and forecasting
     * Management reporting
     * Performance analysis
     * Strategic support
