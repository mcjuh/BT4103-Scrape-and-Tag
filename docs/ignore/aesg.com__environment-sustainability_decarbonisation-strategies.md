In response to the urgent need to combat climate change, our Decarbonisation Strategies services provide organisations with the expertise and solutions required to significantly reduce their carbon footprints and foster sustainable growth.
## Why Decarbonisation Strategies Are Essential
Governments worldwide, adhering to the COP21 Paris Agreement, are collectively striving to maintain global warming below 2°C, in alignment with the recommendations of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC). The tangible effects of climate change have compelled governments to develop long-term greenhouse gas emission strategies and climate change adaptation plans across various sectors, including the built environment, transportation, and industries. These initiatives are fundamental in managing and reducing greenhouse gas emissions, ensuring a sustainable future for all.
However, existing built assets present a significant challenge to the net zero agenda. Many structures require deep retrofits to meet zero carbon performance criteria, necessitating specialised engineering and advisory services to facilitate this transformation. AESG’s sustainability team is adept at developing efficient Decarbonisation Strategies at building, estate, and portfolio levels, ensuring that organisations can navigate the complexities of carbon reduction with confidence and efficacy.
## Our Approach
AESG adopts a holistic and tailored approach to Decarbonisation Strategies, ensuring that each client's unique needs and circumstances are meticulously addressed. Our methodology encompasses the following key elements:
### Energy, Water and Waste Auditing
Establishing baseline performance through audits to identify inefficiencies and opportunities for savings and reductions.
### Climate Modelling
Utilising climate modelling techniques to project future climate scenarios and assess their potential impacts on developments.
### Retrofit Strategies
Designing and implementing solutions to enhance energy efficiency in existing structures, thereby reducing energy consumption and carbon emissions.
### Climate Risk and Resilience Assessment
Identifying and analysing potential climate-related risks to develop strategies that enhance the resilience of operations and infrastructure against adverse climate impacts.
### Decarbonisation Investment Strategies
Developing strategic investment plans that prioritise carbon reduction initiatives and ensure financial viability.
### Sustainable Energy Solutions
Integrating renewable energy sources and sustainable technologies to support long-term carbon neutrality.
### Carbon Accounting & Reporting
Providing accurate tracking and reporting of carbon emissions to facilitate informed decision-making and regulatory compliance.
