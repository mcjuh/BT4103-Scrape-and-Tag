# Federal energy policy shifts: Insights from recent Executive Orders
On April 8th and 9th, U.S. President Donald Trump signed a series of Executive Orders (EO), some of which are directly relevant to the U.S. power grid and various decarbonization efforts. The Executive Orders were:
  * 1 - Strengthening the Reliability and Security of the United States Electric Grid (“Grid Reliability”)
  * 2 - Protecting American Energy From State Overreach(“State Regulations”)
  * 3 - Reinvigorating America’s Beautiful Clean Coal Industry and Amending Executive Order 14241 (“Coal Industry”)
  * 4 - Zero-based Regulatory Budgeting to Unleash American Energy(“Zero-based Regulation”)
