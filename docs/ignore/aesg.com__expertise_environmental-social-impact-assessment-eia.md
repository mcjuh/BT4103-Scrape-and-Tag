## Why is an ESIA Important?
The ESIA process is essential for evaluating how a proposed project will affect its surrounding environment and communities. It involves identifying both environmental impacts and social considerations, such as effects on local communities and ecosystems. Conducting an ESIA allows you to proactively address key concerns—like air quality, water pollution, noise levels, biodiversity, and socio-economic effects—ensuring all necessary mitigation measures are in place before project execution.
