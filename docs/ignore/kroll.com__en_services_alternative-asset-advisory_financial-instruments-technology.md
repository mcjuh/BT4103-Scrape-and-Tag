# Financial Instruments and Technology
The Kroll Financial Instruments and Technology practice is a leading solutions provider for asset managers, hedge funds, fund administrators, banks, insurers, private equity firms, commodity trading and investment firms, and corporations. 
[ Contact Us ](https://www.kroll.com/en/contactus)
We provide independent valuation services and risk & financial advisory across a broad range of structured, illiquid investments and complex assets. We assess complex financial instruments through the lens of asset managers’ risk underwriting processes, paired with Kroll’s deep regulatory, accounting, and financial reporting expertise.
Kroll’s Financial Instruments & Technology team is comprised of professionals from across the complex asset ecosystem, including former buy side investors, sell side structuring and capital markets agents, rating agency leaders, collateral managers, and advisory experts. 
**Our asset class expertise includes:**
Structured Products:
  * CLOs & CLO risk retention
  * ABS
  * RMBS
  * CMBS & CRE CLOs
  * Collateralized fund obligations (CFOs)
  * Credit-linked notes (CLNs)
  * Rated note feeder funds
  * SRT & CRT


Complex & Esoteric Securities:
  * Derivatives, options & hedging instruments
  * Synthetic securities
  * Transportation receivables (aircraft, shipping, rail)
  * Life settlements & esoteric receivables
  * SPACs
  * PIPEs & founder shares


Private Structured Investments:
  * Private securitized assets
  * Convertible bonds & preferreds
  * Warrants
  * Private structured credit
  * Structured equity
  * Contingent claims analyses (CCA)
  * Transfer rights & offtake agreements


Loan Pools:
  * Whole loans & warehouses
  * Consumer whole loans
  * Consumer loan servicing rights
  * Student loan pools
  * Auto loans
  * Agency & non-agency mortgage pools
  * Solar and esoteric pools
  * Factoring receivables


Special Situations Investments:
  * Bankruptcy claims
  * Distressed debt & equity
  * Part-out investments
  * Trade claims
  * Royalty claims
  * Litigation receivables
  * Media streaming rights
  * Life settlements


**We provide bespoke advisory services to our clients, including:**
  * Independent valuations of financial instruments for financial & LP reporting
  * Daily pricing for interval funds & related regulatory reporting (e.g., NPORT)
  * Transaction-supportive valuations & risk retentions
  * Model validations & model reviews
  * Model stress testing
  * Code reviews
  * Valuation policies & procedures advisory
