## What are HPDs?
Health Product Declarations (HPDs) provide essential information about the health and environmental impact of building products. HPDs offer a transparent and standardised method to report the chemical ingredients of products and their associated health effects, enabling stakeholders to make informed decisions about product selection. Additional details include the quantity and role or function of each ingredient, recycled content, VOC content and emissions, and other relevant information.
## Why Choose HPDs?
### Improved Product Selection
HPDs enable architects, designers, builders, and consumers to make informed product choices by providing clear information about the health impacts of materials.
### Reduced Health Risk
Understanding the health implications of product ingredients allows stakeholders to select materials that minimise health risks for occupants.
### Enhanced Sustainability
HPDs support the selection of products that align with sustainability goals and third-party certifications, promoting healthier and more sustainable built environments.
## Regulatory Compliance and Green Building Ratings
The HPD Open Standard is the authoritative reference for preparing an HPD, providing a framework for product manufacturers and their ingredient suppliers to report and disclose information about products and associated health data. HPDs are crucial for compliance with various regulations and certifications, such as LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design). They play a significant role in earning points in green building rating systems like LEED, WELL, and the Living Building Challenge, thereby contributing to the achievement of sustainability certifications.
