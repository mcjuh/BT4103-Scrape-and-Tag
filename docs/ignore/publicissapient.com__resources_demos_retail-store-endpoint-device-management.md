#  Retail Store Endpoint Device Management Demo 
Retail stores rely on thousands of endpoint devices—from POS terminals and tablets to kiosks and IoT devices—to keep stores running. But even a single outage or noncompliant device can impact store operations, employee productivity and customer experience. Sapient Sustain continuously monitors endpoint health across all stores, identifies potential issues before they escalate and autonomously orchestrates remediation before they affect the business.

What types of endpoint issues can Sustain detect?
Sustain continuously monitors endpoint health to identify a wide range of operational issues, including performance degradation, software problems, configuration changes, connectivity issues, missing updates, policy violations and other conditions that could impact business operations. The demo illustrates this with a software update compliance issue, but the platform supports broader retail store IT operations monitoring.
How does Sustain troubleshoot endpoint problems?
Sustain correlates operational data across dashboards, incidents, endpoint telemetry and historical resolutions to identify the affected endpoint, determine likely root causes and recommend or initiate the appropriate remediation workflow.
Can AI resolve issues autonomously?
Yes. Sustain can trigger AI agents that execute remediation workflows, monitor progress, validate successful recovery and confirm return to a healthy operational state.
