## How do you turn legal complexity into business confidence?
When your business is changing at pace, how legal work gets delivered matters as much as the advice itself. EY Law teams support you with integrated services and solutions designed to work in practice — embedded into your business, scalable across your organization, and provided as one connected approach.
  * Holistic, not one‑off: legal services and solutions that help enable transformation from decision through execution
  * Built into operations: legal and regulatory advice translated into practical, business-oriented outcomes
  * Technology enabled at scale: AI, data and technology that drive speed, consistency and control
  * Globally aligned, locally grounded: with 3,000+ legal professionals across 75+ jurisdictions, combining globally consistent ways of working with deep local knowledge and experience
  * Connected across EY: integrated legal, business and technology capabilities powered by 400,000 EY professionals
