# International tax, transfer pricing, and trade
For companies operating in multiple countries, understanding the consequences of tax planning decisions at a global level is essential to managing finance and achieving wider business goals.
##  Manage the complexities of international tax 
KPMG provides tax planning, transformation, and compliance services for international businesses—from those just entering foreign markets to established multinationals. Our specialists can assist you with navigating complex tax and trade laws and regulations and identifying opportunities to modify structures and operating models. Let us help you unlock value with effective services that support international tax, transfer pricing, trade and customs, and value chain risk and cost management.
