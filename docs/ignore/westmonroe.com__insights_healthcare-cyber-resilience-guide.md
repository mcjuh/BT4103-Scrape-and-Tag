## Cyber Attacks are The Threat Healthcare Can’t Ignore 
Cyber threats are no longer a question of if but when—and healthcare organizations are feeling the impact. Every attack threatens patient data, trust, and the ability to deliver care. And with breaches costing up to $9.77 million per incident, the stakes have never been higher. The challenge isn’t awareness—it’s execution. 
### Healthcare leaders must navigate tight budgets, aging systems, and increasing cyber risks while ensuring continuity of care. 
To stay ahead, organizations must: 
  * Shift from prevention to resilience—building response muscle and testing systems before an attack happens. 
  * Strengthen vendor security to reduce third-party vulnerabilities. 
  * Modernize with a security-first mindset, ensuring technology upgrades embed protection at every level. 
  * Collaborate smarter, aligning payers, providers, and regulators.
