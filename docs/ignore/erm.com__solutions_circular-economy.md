# Circular Economy
ERM empowers clients to design, make and market sustainable products and services, by combining innovative portfolio management strategies with circular economy practices.
## Circular economy drivers and regulations intensify
There is an imperative to decouple economic growth from resource consumption. Embracing circularity and product innovation can help convert operational constraints into market differentiators. 
The growing threat of raw material shortages can be mitigated by transitioning to a circular economy. Promoting the use of sustainable resources and innovative manufacturing techniques creates more resilient supply chains.
A rise in policies and regulations mandating sustainable practices means more organizations are exploring circularity, with earlier adopters gaining a competitive advantage.
The convergence of consumer demand for sustainable products with ESG investment priorities is accelerating the transition to a circular economy. This shift is being supported with dedicated investments.
## Boost resilience and sustainability by adopting circular business models.
Assess readiness and build roadmaps
ERM’s experts help clients assess the circularity of suppliers and value chains, pinpointing areas for enhancement. Our guidance enables organizations to go beyond decarbonization to improve resource efficiency and reduce waste.
Design and implement circular products and supply chains
We collaborate with clients to develop and deliver commercially viable circular products and services. By focusing on innovation, organizations can maintain their competitive edge and ensure their offerings align with evolving consumer demands and industry trends.
Navigate a changing legislative landscape 
ERM helps organizations adhere to current and upcoming regulations related to circular business practices, such as ESPR and SSbD. We offer trusted and strategic advice on compliance management, enabling clients to take a proactive approach.
Accelerate circularity with digitalization
We specialize in aiding the transition to the circular economy with digital tools and robust data management strategies that simplify product disclosures and performance management. Our expertise enhances operational efficiency and reduces risks and costs.
