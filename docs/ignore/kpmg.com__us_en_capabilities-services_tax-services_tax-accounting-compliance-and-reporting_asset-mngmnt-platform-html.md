# KPMG Asset Management Platform
Automate all phases of the tax compliance and planning process, from data collection, to tax form generation and distribution. 
## **Empower your organization to navigate complex tax landscapes with confidence and precision**
The KPMG Asset Management Platform (AMP) is leading-edge technology that can help you reimagine cost-effective, innovative ways to execute tax compliance and planning.
Informed by our extensive, in-depth tax knowledge of asset management entities such as passthroughs, Regulated Investments Companies (RICs), and Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs), the Asset Management Platform enables efficiency and transparency and helps normalize big data so you can save time, improve accuracy, and discover new tax value-adds for your organization.
## Your Impact: Features and Benefits
Accessibility, collaboration & workflow
Web-based and mobile accessible – available anytime, anywhere. Real-time document collaboration and milestone tracking. Shared access for client, KPMG, and authorized third parties.
Transparency
Drill-down views to analyze source amounts & adjustments. Customized reports. Audit trail of changes and updates. Real-time visualization of your fund’s organizational structure.
Validation & Efficiency
Supports all major asset classes for both Federal & State. Seamless allocation & tiering for multi-level structure. Easy setup process across entities with similar attributes. Automate book/tax adjustment via percentage or change in balance.
Automation & Analytics
What-if scenarios using copied projects and/or trades & holdings. On-demand generation of tax estimates in custom reports. Graphic data visualizations for decision support. Quantitative relationships for patterns and trends in your tax data. Predictive analytics in the use of models and visualizations for decision support.
Document Delivery Portals
Investor & Partner collaboration tools. Gather and track data and documents from investors. Customization of user access and permissions. Collect digital signatures.
GenAI
Tax Data analysis and research. Gain AI insights. K-1 & K-3 scanning, document generation, preliminary review functions and other administrative tasks.

##  Maximize the Potential of Your Tax Operations 
Select the service arrangement that aligns with your needs and expertise:
  * **Traditional Engagement Model** – Delegate tax preparation and review to KPMG, enabling your team to concentrate on strategic planning and value-added activities, while still maintaining oversight with visibility into your data and the status of deliverables as well as streamlining your review process with custom analytics in the KPMG Asset Management Platform.
  * **Co-sourcing** – Opt for a collaborative approach with the KPMG Asset Management Platform. This hybrid model allows your department to choose which steps of the preparation and review process to keep in-house while outsourcing other steps to KPMG. You get the benefit of KPMG’s ongoing investment in technology and tax technical expertise along with the flexibility to shift responsibilities between your department and KPMG to handle changes such as growth or attrition.
  * **Licensing** – If your tax department would like to retain full control of the tax compliance process, consider licensing KPMG Asset Management Platform to increase efficiency and scalability while reducing the cost and risk of maintaining custom workpapers and systems.
