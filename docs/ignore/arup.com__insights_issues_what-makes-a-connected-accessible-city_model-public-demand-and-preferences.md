#  Model public demand and preferences 
In the smartphone era, there are powerful ways to learn from commuter and public transport behaviours. Anonymised travel data enables levels of modelling that help transport network owners to understand how, where and when the population is travelling.
We help test policies and proposals, looking at how different packages of transport interventions might lead to major changes in travel behaviour. We are able to simulate the complex interactions that arise in everyone’s day accurately and rapidly. We explore the role of pricing techniques to manage congestion and develop financially sustainable transport operations.
Learn more about [City Modelling Lab](https://www.arup.com/services/city-modelling-lab/).
Commuters, families, scientists and decision makers collaborate on future green transport policy in Berlin. [Find out more](https://www.arup.com/link/9f6660905d2648838e51568ffbb7b30d.aspx "Agents of change") about how our digital and city modelling teams made it happen.
