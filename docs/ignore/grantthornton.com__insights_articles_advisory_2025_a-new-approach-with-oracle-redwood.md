# A new approach with Oracle Redwood
May 14, 2025 
Enterprise solutions have matured. While enterprise solutions have always offered powerful connectivity, many organizations have struggled with usability, integration and scalability. Now, organizations are changing even faster. They need systems with the agility to empower change rather than resisting it.
## The Oracle Redwood approach
Oracle Redwood is a new evolution in enterprise solutions, pairing an advanced design system and user interface (UI) strategy. This approach addresses the needs for usability, integration and scalability by providing an intuitive, seamless experience across all Oracle Cloud applications. Redwood unlocks Oracle Cloud to let users streamline tasks, personalize interfaces, follow guided journeys and discover new capabilities like embedded AI tools. The sleek, minimalist design of Redwood helps users focus on what’s most important without distraction.
### Strategic considerations
Many organizations are pursuing digital transformation to optimize business operations, enhance collaboration and streamline workflows. Oracle Redwood helps organizations accelerate their digital transformations across the Oracle Cloud, whether they have new or existing implementations.
Some business benefits of Oracle Redwood:
  1. **Efficient and fast** : Redwood enhances the speed and performance of Oracle applications, accelerating access to critical business data and applications. The interface offers elastic search pages, simplified in-app navigation and a design that minimizes the clicks required.
  2. **Engaging and modern user experience** : The consistent and intuitive design across Oracle applications can accelerate adoption and boost user engagement. With a design that is focused on intuitive navigation and mobile access, users can focus more time on tasks that drive business value.
  3. **GenAI capabilities** : Oracle has offered AI capabilities before, but now its catalog has expanded with GenAI capabilities. Redwood lets users take full advantage of these new tools.
  4. **Personalized adaptability** : Redwood provides several options across the platform to improve operational efficiency. With guided journeys and guided learning, users are empowered to perform their tasks with minimal friction.
