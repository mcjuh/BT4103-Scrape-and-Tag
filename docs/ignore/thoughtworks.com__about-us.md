##  Design. Engineering. AI
**For more than 30 years, Thoughtworks has helped shape how the world builds technology.**
Today, by harnessing AI to scale what is possible, guided by human judgment and experience, we aim to be the force behind the world’s most consequential technology transformations.
We combine our engineering and design expertise with AI to modernize systems, unlock data and create AI-powered products.
[AI/works™](https://www.thoughtworks.com/ai/works) is our agentic development platform and [Agent/works™](https://www.thoughtworks.com/agent/works) is our enterprise platform for governing and running autonomous AI agents. Together they bring decades of our engineering know-how to every stage of AI software delivery, from development through to deployment and governance.

**Thoughtworks was founded in Chicago in 1993. A lot has changed since then, but our unwavering commitment to engineering excellence has seen us grow to become leaders in the AI-first software delivery and data engineering space.**
We apply hands-on experience to help our clients better navigate change, create adaptable technology platforms that align seamlessly with business objectives and rapidly design, deliver and evolve market-leading products and experiences at scale.
[ See our history  ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/about-us/history)
**Our purpose is to create an extraordinary impact on the world through our culture and ﻿technology excellence.**
This is more than just a statement for us. It’s a constant challenge against which we measure how we show up for our people, clients and society. The decisions we make, the behaviors we demonstrate, the ways in which we work, the innovations we lead and the solutions we deliver all feed back to our purpose. To us, this is what makes Thoughtworks extraordinary.

We’re wired to see our work as an opportunity to continuously share knowledge and grow as we challenge the possibility in tech. From our industry pioneering work in open source, responsible tech and data mesh, to AI-first software delivery and data engineering, we’re proud of our history leading the charge in technology innovation.
