## The future starts now. Are you ready?
The challenges that leaders face today are more significant and complex than they’ve been in generations. Global crises such as increasing wealth disparity, massive technological disruption, demographic pressure and declining confidence in governing institutions all demand urgent action. At the same time, a breakdown in global consensus with growing nationalism and populism has influenced the political agenda of many historical donor countries.
This context has a direct impact on the capacity of organisations to respond to these global crises. PwC has done various surveys in which we’ve gained valuable insights into the specific challenges organisations face as they look to build, equip and support tomorrow’s workforce.
[Get in touch with our experts](https://www.pwc.ch/en/services/people-organisation/workforce-of-the-future.html#contact-us)
Similar to organisations across all industries globally, the leaders of government and public services organisations face challenges around digitalisation, automation, workforce diversity, equity and inclusion, culture and sustainability. These stumbling blocks highlight the importance of shoring up both the financial and the human capital elements of the equation.
## Our services and solutions
#### Making your workforce ready for the future
At PwC Switzerland we follow a people-centred approach to tackling workforce change and transformation. We focus on two interlinked topics:
  * The **new world of work** that’s emerging from megatrends such as automation, demographic shifts and the imprints that global crises such as the pandemic and various geopolitical uncertainties have left on society. 
  * The **new skills** and behaviours that are required from every single person to align with the changes facing the world of work. 


Our aim is to pick up where you’ve already started. The future of work can’t be pinned to a specific start and end date. It’s a continuous process of planning – implementing – rethinking – adapting. We endeavour to understand your individual approach to tackling the challenges of the future. Getting to know your vision, your plans and how you’re implementing them helps us find a clear direction to travel in, build a path that’s future ready, and work with you to make it tangible and sustainable.
Also called people, talent or human capital strategy – is all about preparing your workforce to deliver on your business strategy.
Depending on the business imperatives triggering action on workforce planning, we help you develop and implement responses using a broad and varied set of tools and mechanisms.
Your decision to start on a workforce strategy journey can be prompted by many factors:
  * Sometimes a need to **optimise your business and cost model** necessitates work in areas such as product & service innovation, revenue recovery & cost management, integrated business & performance management, supply chain resilience or deals/M&A.
  * The pressure of **regulatory change** can trigger adjustments in areas such as product plan resources and skills to adapt to regulations and requirements, managing legal & regulatory change, planning for changes in federal and cantonal workforce policies, and SEC/ESG reporting and disclosures.
  * The rapid evolution of a **hybrid workforce** is often forcing the agenda in terms of planning for a hybrid workforce, real estate strategy, employee experience, safety & wellbeing, capacity & [workforce planning](https://www.pwc.ch/en/services/people-organisation/workforce-capability.html).
  * Employees’ need for **purpose & inclusion** is driving change under the banner of purpose & trust, diversity, inclusion & equity, employability & skills & good jobs, mobility & tax and employee engagement.
  * And then of course there’s **digital transformation** and using it to address areas such as future skills & workforce models, redesign of work & workplace, adoption of digital channels & tools, business case realisation, talent strategy & programmes, and sustainable productivity.


### Workforce strategy
Mapping your way forward
Modern human resource functions are expected to drive business impact and enable organisations to adapt to a rapidly evolving landscape. Our HR transformation service helps you develop and implement detailed actions to concrete challenges while always keeping the broader HR and business landscape in mind.
When you approach us with a problem or objective seeking a concrete solution, we have a broad range of approaches to choose from, carefully coordinated and taking account of your organisation’s HR maturity:
  * Approaches based on **talent acquisition and retention** can span everything from an inclusive workplace and roles supporting work/life balance to strategic hiring to access skill sets in the gig economy – all designed to harness opportunities to attract and retain the best people.
  * Solutions revolving around **HR[technology](https://www.pwc.ch/en/services/people-organisation/hr-technology-digital-strategy.html), [analytics](https://www.pwc.ch/en/insights/people-analytics.html), and automation** involve harnessing HR tech, redesigned roles and big-data-backed decisionmaking to help you achieve business growth.
  * By helping you focus on we can help you achieve collaboration across HR and the business as well as create everyday development opportunities and transparency on your performance management processes, all facilitating experiential learning that accelerates the development of your people.
  * Our **total rewards** solutions enable you to optimise your operating model and encourage the use of benefits, also promoting experiential learning and facilitating the accelerated development of your people.
