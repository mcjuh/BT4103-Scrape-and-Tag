#  COP16 & COP29 Outcomes: Insights and Implications for Asia Pacific
December 4, 2024 
Webinar 
[ register ](https://erm.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_wctam-koTi6wXP4OgGmIlg#/registration)
Explore the significant outcomes of the most recent Biodiversity COP16 and Climate COP29 and their implications for businesses in the Asia Pacific region. The session will include insights, guidance, and actionable strategies that help companies respond to emerging expectations and harness new opportunities. It aims to empower businesses to take a holistic approach to climate and nature, providing the tools needed to stay competitive, compliant, and resilient in the rapidly evolving sustainability landscape.
This exclusive webinar seeks to provide:
  * An overview of the COP16 and COP29 outcomes and the convergence of climate and nature commitments.
  * APAC implications, national and industry-specific.
  * Strategies for transition planning, integrated disclosures, and sustainability reporting.
  * Guidance on managing risks and seizing opportunities tied to international developments.
