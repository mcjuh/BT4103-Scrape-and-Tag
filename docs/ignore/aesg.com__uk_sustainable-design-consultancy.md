## Why Sustainable Design Matters
The built environment is a major contributor to carbon emissions and resource depletion. By adopting sustainable design principles, organisations can:
### Reduce Environmental Impact
Minimise energy consumption, carbon emissions, raw materials use and waste generation, as well as enhancing biodiversity, wellbeing and social value.
### Enhance Building Performance
Improve energy efficiency and indoor environmental quality.
### Meet Regulatory Standards
Comply with UK regulations and sustainability certifications such as BREEAM, LEED, WELL, NABERS UK and Passivhaus.
### Increase Market Value
Boost the marketability of properties through sustainable features.
