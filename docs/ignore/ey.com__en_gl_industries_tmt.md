#  Technology, media & entertainment, and telecommunications 
When industries converge, new ecosystems thrive.
Technology, media and entertainment, and telecommunications (TMT) companies are the building blocks of transformation and include many of the world's most prolific brands driving innovation across all industries. Organizations are racing to solidify their niche in the TMT marketplace but, as they grow, they must differentiate themselves from their competitors and from up-and-coming startups. They must become adaptive digital enterprises that can quickly flex to changing markets and economic environments in today's rapid, unpredictable landscape.
EY can support the navigation of this landscape by helping to:
  * Create compelling employee and customer experiences to set you apart in the marketplace
  * Retain skills and talent so you can provide leading-class products and services
  * Protect your data, brand and reputation
  * Support your growth agenda by bolstering your M&A pursuits
  * Create and connect ecosystems of industries, partners, service providers and customers to build systemic agility


Discover EY's TMT insights and services and learn how our teams can help your business create compelling customer experiences and achieve operational excellence.
