##  The digital assets space looks vastly different today than it did just a few years ago 
Digitally native companies that custody digital assets or have platforms for trading need to be able to adapt their vision and strategy as the landscape changes.
"The question is, how do you build despite regulatory uncertainty and have the agility to adapt to constantly changing regulations?"
**Jay Schulman,** RSM US LLP, blockchain and digital assets practice leader 
Along with a clear strategy and a comprehensive regulatory understanding, companies in the digital asset space need to assess what the shifting landscape means for their security, audit and accounting, and tax functions, as well as their technology systems and processes. This e-book examines important considerations in each of these areas.
