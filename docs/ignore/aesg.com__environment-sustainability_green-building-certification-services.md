At AESG, our Green Building Certifications and Sustainability Rating Systems services assist organisations in achieving recognised green building certifications. We provide guidance to obtain certifications such as LEED, BREEAM, WELL, Estidama, Mostadam and others, enhancing your project's sustainability, marketability, and compliance with international standards.
