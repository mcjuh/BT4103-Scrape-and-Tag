## Guiding growth in a new era
For construction firms, REITs and real estate companies, preparation is key. When navigating risks such as fluctuations in market conditions and interest rates, it helps to have a partner who understands how to put a solid foundation in place — and who can apply data and AI-driven insights to improve decision-making, manage risk and respond with speed when challenges arise.
With teams that specialize in tax, audit and assurance, and issues such as government regulations, M&A, and fraud and false claims, we help our clients prepare and grow by combining deep industry experience with technology and AI-enabled capabilities to strengthen performance and support confident growth.

### Selected sectors we serve
  1. Construction
  2. Real Estate
  3. REITs


Leaders in the construction industry need to have their operations, processes and internal controls in place as they confront risks like market changes and new tax rules. Our construction professionals guide general contractors and subcontractors; home builders and road builders; heavy construction firms; architecture and engineering firms; design/build companies and suppliers — domestic to international — to be prepared for whatever comes their way.
From tax compliance to valuation, our specialized real estate team works with office, retail, and industrial owners and developers, as well as property management firms, assisting them in mitigating risks and finding the right solutions. With lessons learned from the global financial crisis, we’re ready to guide you through the right processes so you can get ahead and stay ahead.
In the ever-changing landscape of real estate, REITs need a partner that can help them navigate fluctuations in market conditions, interest rates and taxes. Get guidance from our specialists in corporate tax, audit and assurance and advisory so that you’re prepared to adapt and grow.
