# KPMG & ServiceNow
Reimagining enterprise workflows with Trusted AI and ServiceNow innovation
KPMG and ServiceNow team up to help organizations modernize how work gets done across the enterprise, with AI at the core. Our specialists combine sector insights with a business-led, tech-enabled approach to transformation on the ServiceNow AI Platform. That’s how we have earned the title of ServiceNow’s Worldwide Transformation Partner of the Year three times over.
From strategy through implementation and optimization, our solutions drive game-changing outcomes. Working side by side with clients, our specialists translate platform innovation into sustainable results—helping elevate their customer and employee experiences, strengthen risk management and security, improve back-office operations, and infuse trust in AI.
From connected healthcare and life sciences to financial services operations, to life sciences and government, our industry-specific solutions make the difference in all industries. So, whether a client is new to ServiceNow or a veteran of the platform, KPMG is here to help clients realize the full potential of the platform and deliver sustainable value well into the future.
