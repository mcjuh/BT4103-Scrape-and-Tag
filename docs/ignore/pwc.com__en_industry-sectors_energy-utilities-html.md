## The energy industry is facing major challenges
Ever-dwindling resources and climate change mean that the issue of sustainability is a core consideration for all businesses. In addition, the industry is undergoing major legal framework changes. Success in such market conditions requires the services of a reliable partner to help ensure your response is optimal.
At PwC, we have experts with many years of experience within the energy-industry. Our experts can advise you in areas including; the development of renewable energies, strategic design for navigating the decentralised energy environment, improved process management, demand-oriented personnel planning and cost reduction.
Naturally, in conjunction with our consulting services, we take all relevant tax and legal aspects into account.
## Our expertise
  1. Energy strategies for the future
  2. Cost optimisation and risk management
  3. Process optimisation
  4. Operational monitoring and coaching
  5. Transaction support
  6. Digitalisation


### Energy strategies for the future
We will work alongside you to develop new energy strategies, from devising the optimal approach and obtaining the necessary resources, to implementing your new energy concept.
### Cost optimisation and risk management
Our experts will help you to save costs and enhance the quality of your products and services. We will also provide a transparent and customer-oriented risk management across the entire value chain.
### Process optimisation
We can optimise your business processes by creating a lean and flexible company structure, which may involve, for instance, your business organisation and management, through to your IT systems and personnel planning. Our approach is holistic in nature, whereby we continuously take all areas of your business into account.
### Operational monitoring and coaching
Our experts have many years of experience within the energy-industry. They will guide you through the entire process and train your employees, thus ensuring a smooth transition.
### Transaction support
We advise energy providers on any financial and/or tax issues relating to company transactions. Our experts cover the areas of M&A consultation, assessment, Due Diligence, as well as Post-Merger integration.
### Digitalisation
We support you in the development of your own digitalised strategy and accompany you throughout the digital transformation. Through the merger of our Management Consulting and Experience Center, we bring together the best of the advisory and digital worlds – from strategy right through to implementation.
