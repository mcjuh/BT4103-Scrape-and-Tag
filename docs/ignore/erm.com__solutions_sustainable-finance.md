# Sustainable Finance
ERM helps clients embed sustainability into investment decisions and advance sustainable finance approaches. We work across the entire financial sector ecosystem, from private markets, financial institutions and corporates, to public and civil society organizations.
## A deepening connection between strategy, finance and sustainability
The sustainable finance revolution demands action and focus from the entire investor ecosystem. The financial sector needs to assume leadership by setting new benchmarks and standards to drive better impact and alignment. 
For companies seeking capital and sustainable finance, the increasing sustainability imperative means rethinking business strategies and capital investments to support future success.
Climate, supply chain, human rights, DEI and technology disruptors and policy changes present material financial opportunity and risk. Integrating sustainability into business is crucial to drive profitable growth and enterprise value creation. 
To cope with growing investor and lender demands and increased reporting and regulatory obligations, organizations need to embrace approaches that demonstrate measurable sustainability credentials. 
## Maximize value and impact by proactively embedding sustainability throughout the investment lifecycle.
Private market strategy development
ERM provides advice to fund managers, general partners and their portfolio companies on sustainability strategy, implementation roadmaps, and best practices for integrating it to drive growth. We work with strategy, operational, digital marketing and go-to-market teams focusing on value creation and risk mitigation. 
Private market implementation support
ERM provides practical support to enable clients to embed sustainability into business processes and cultures in order to meet investor expectations. Our approach enables clients to demonstrate considered and credible planning to future investors.
Transaction preparation and exit advisory
ERM supports financial and corporate clients in maximizing return on investment via pre-exit planning, Initial Public Offerings (IPOs), carve outs, spins outs and new infrastructure platforms. We proactively support general partners and portfolio companies in advance of any exit to optimize value creation. 
Sustainable debt instruments
ERM helps financial institutions and borrowers/issuers mobilize capital to support the transition to an inclusive low-carbon economy. Our support includes Second Party Opinions (SPOs), technical advisory, sustainable financing frameworks, and transition finance development assistance. We also help financial institutions advance their own decarbonization efforts.
