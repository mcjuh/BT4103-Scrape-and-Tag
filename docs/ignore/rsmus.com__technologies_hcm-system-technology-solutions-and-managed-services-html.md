# Optimize your HCM system performance 
### RSM’s comprehensive HCM technology solutions and managed services
#### [Contact our HCM technology team ](https://rsmus.com/technologies/hcm-system-technology-solutions-and-managed-services.html#contact)
It is common for organizations to think their human capital management (HCM) system has reached its limit when, in reality, it just hasn’t been fully utilized or optimized. Upon review, it is often possible to achieve increased performance, enhanced capabilities, improved process automation and expanded product capability, enabling longer-term value in line with organizational needs and goals.
Obtain improved outcomes from your HCM technology with RSM’s human capital consulting and managed application services (MAS). Specifically tailored for organizations using [UKG](https://rsmus.com/insights/technology/ukg/unlock-your-ukg-potential.html) or [Dayforce](https://rsmus.com/insights/technology/dayforce/dayforce-optimization-guide.html), our solutions deliver enhanced performance, modernized processes, automation to maintain data integrity and improved employee experience—all while helping you achieve your strategic goals. Don’t replace your system—optimize it with RSM.
## Why optimize your HCM system?
With RSM’s human capital solutions, you can revamp your existing system to:
  * Enable workforce transformation by leveraging all product capabilities and automating business workflows
  * Fine-tune talent management to align with trending talent strategies
  * Mitigate compliance risk for labor regulations, pay practices, employee leave accrual and tracking, and changes to tax withholding
  * Enhance decision making with robust reporting and analytics capabilities, including AI-enabled solutions
  * Reduce the risk of exposure of employee data through system access
  * Maximize efficiencies for front-line management processes for scheduling, time collection and resolution of time data discrepancies


By [optimizing your system](https://rsmus.com/insights/technology/ukg/blount-fine-foods-discovers-a-shortcut-to-human-capital-manageme.html), you can have confidence in the integrity of system data, increase end-user adoption and productivity, and maintain compliance in a changing world, which will allow you to have continuous return on investment—without having to budget, evaluate and implement a new system.

## FAQs 

What is an HCM system?  
    
An HCM system, also known as a human resource information system (HRIS), is a comprehensive software solution designed to manage and optimize an organization's workforce. It integrates various HR functions such as recruitment, onboarding, payroll, performance management, compensation, learning management, time and attendance, and employee development. By centralizing HR data and automating processes, an HCM system enhances efficiency, improves decision making and supports strategic HR initiatives. 

Why does an HCM system need to be optimized?  
    
It is common for businesses to need help accurately diagnosing HCM system problems that cause manual workarounds, data issues, payroll processing errors and many other frustrations that can crop up as your system ages or your business evolves.
Your HCM technology might require configuration changes, updates or system integration as you add or change other technology systems. Plus, HCM technology vendors are continually adding improvements and new modules that can be enabled for better system performance. You may also need to address any process improvements or identify training opportunities to get the most out of your system.
Just as you would take your car for a tune-up when something feels amiss, you can bring your HCM system problems to RSM’s professionals for a fast track to fixing what’s truly not working optimally. 

How does a system utilization review work?  
    
RSM’s HCM technology teams have system certifications plus knowledge and industry experience from working with a diverse group of clients. Our HCM system optimization specialists consider the key stakeholders across departments as system configuration is examined for tune-up opportunities. Attention is given to considering how changes made in one part of your HCM system might affect another.
Our HR technology professionals can align your system’s capabilities with your business objectives, enabling your use of your system to adapt along with your organization to support change and growth.
