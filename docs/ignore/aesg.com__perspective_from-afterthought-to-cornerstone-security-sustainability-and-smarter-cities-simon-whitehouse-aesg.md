## The Future of Resilience:
## A Holistic Approach
In today’s landscape, defined by escalating cyber threats, climate change imperatives, and rapid urbanisation, integrating security with sustainability is not merely a choice—it is a necessity.
In this exclusive feature for Influencers Edition 2026, Simon Whitehouse, Divisional Director of Security Consulting at AESG, offers his expert perspective on the evolving global standards for risk and resilience.
**Key Insights** : The article challenges industry leaders to move beyond outdated, traditional risk models. It highlights the growing importance of holistic, human-centred strategies, exemplified by the rise of security-by-design principles (as seen in the UK) and pioneering built environment certifications like SABRE and SHIELD.
Learn how AESG is working with clients globally to transform security from a historical afterthought into a foundational cornerstone of sustainable development.

Simon Whitehouse is a preeminent figure in the security sector, bringing to AESG a legacy of leadership as the founder of SGW, recently acquired by AESG.
