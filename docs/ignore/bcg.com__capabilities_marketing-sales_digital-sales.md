# Digital Sales
Next-generation sales brings together the power of analytics, digital tools, agile processes, and people expertise to create an efficient go-to-market strategy—creating [cost effective](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/cost-management), organizational efficiencies. BCG helps clients unleash the full potential of digital sales.

## BCG’s Approach to Digital Sales
Our successful digital sales transformation is built upon five principles:
  1. **Understand the**[**customer journey**](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/customer-insights/customer-journey)**.** Leverage key digital tools, demand analytics, and the [LTV/CAC ratio](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2017/corporate-development-one-ratio-subscription-business-needs-to-know) to gain full transparency into the customer journey.
  2. **Optimize sales processes.** Integrate marketing and sales with digital tools, such as digital inbound marketing, account-based marketing, and data-driven, customized offers.
  3. **Optimize individual**[**sales channels**](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/marketing-sales/sales-channel-strategy)**.** Create high-performing channels with a digitally enabled sales force, [AI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence)-driven call centers, and data-driven [inside sales.](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2016/sales-channels-marketing-sales-five-selling-secrets-todays-digital-b2b-leaders)
  4. **Integrate channels.** Align online and offline channels using next-generation sales management, shared incentives, and a real-time sales cockpit.
  5. **Implement sales enablers.** Ensure that key enablers are in place, including a centralized global demand center, advanced analytics, and the right culture and talent.


Companies that master these five principles not only gain, on average, a 3% EBITDA margin advantage over competitors, they also outperform in terms of profits, revenue, and growth.
