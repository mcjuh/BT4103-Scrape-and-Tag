## Our Building Safety & Advisory Services
AESG offers a full suite of Building Safety & Advisory Services designed to provide solutions for all aspects of external wall safety and compliance. Our services include:
[ Fire Risk Appraisal for External Walls (FRAEW) ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-7abe8626aa06a38830bc)
Utilising the [PAS 9980 methodology](https://aesg.com/uk/ews1-form-pas-9980-assessments/), we conduct detailed fire risk appraisals to assess and mitigate fire hazards associated with external walls. Our FRAEW services ensure that your building achieves a tolerable level of safety with a proportionate risk-based approach, providing peace of mind and reduced risk to occupants.
#### [EWS1 Form Production](https://aesg.com/uk/ews1-form-pas-9980-assessments/)
#### With our in-house specialists we can facilitate the production of [ EWS1 Forms](https://aesg.com/uk/ews1-form-pas-9980-assessments/) for all building heights and outcomes. Our expertise ensures accurate and compliant documentation inclusive of supporting substantiation, enabling smoother property transactions and meeting lender requirements.
[ Peer Reviews ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-8b2a72e6aa06a3885500)
Our peer review services cover both FRAEW assessments and technical packages, ensuring thorough evaluation and adherence to industry standards. This critical step enhances the reliability and credibility of your fire safety documentation.
[ Expert Advisory and Recovery Support ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-10432486aa06a3885500)
AESG provides strategic advisory services and recovery support, guiding you through cladding remediation and other fire safety measures. Our team offers insights to help you navigate complex safety matters effectively while being cognizant of wider project context.
[ Transactional Due Diligence and Technical Advisory ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-c9e9cc06aa06a3885500)
We assist with transactional due diligence and technical advisory support, ensuring that all fire safety aspects are meticulously evaluated during property transactions. This service supports informed decision-making and risk management.
[ Building Safety Cases ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-e2700de6aa06a3885500)
Developing building safety cases is essential for regulatory compliance and demonstrating a proactive approach to fire safety. Our team crafts detailed safety cases that cover all necessary aspects to meet stringent safety standards.
[ Remediation Packages and Technical Tender Reviews ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-7abe8626aa06a3886211)
AESG designs remediation packages and conducts technical tender reviews, streamlining the remediation process and ensuring technical accuracy. Our services facilitate effective and compliant fire safety interventions to achieve the required outcomes.
[ Remediation Construction Monitoring ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-203a7c16aa06a3886211)
We oversee remediation construction, ensuring that all fire safety improvements are implemented correctly and adhere to safety specifications. Our monitoring guarantees that remediation work meets the necessary safety objectives.
[ Fire Assurance Inspections ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-15019716aa06a3886211)
For new builds under construction, AESG provides fire assurance inspections to ensure that all fire safety measures are integrated from the ground up. This proactive approach helps prevent safety and compliance issues before they arise.
[ Documentation Review and External Wall Fire Safety Reports ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-c4e26dd6aa06a3886211)
Our documentation review and external wall fire safety reports offer thorough assessments and actionable insights. We ensure that all safety documentation is accurate, comprehensive, and cognizant of regulatory requirements.
[ Investigation, Scoping, and Inspections of As-built External Walls ](https://aesg.com/uk/external-wall-advisory/#collapse-cc9ac5d6aa06a3886211)
AESG conducts investigation, scoping, and inspections of as-built external walls which are used to identify potential fire risks and recommend necessary safety enhancements. Our detailed inspections are carried out in person by AESG’s experienced and technically trained staff. This allows for the required insight and detailed understanding which facilitates our proportionate and technically measured approach to challenges.
## Why Choose AESG for Building Safety & Advisory?
With a dedicated team of professionally qualified fire engineering consultants, façade engineers, building surveyors, and project managers, AESG has successfully executed numerous external wall fire safety assessments, advisory services, and cladding remediation projects both in the UK and internationally. Our multidisciplinary expertise allows us to address the unique challenges of fire safety in diverse building environments. Our team is specially qualified and suitably competent for the nature of the challenging works encountered, this includes Chartered Engineers (C.Eng) and members of Institution of Fire Engineers (MIFireE) along with a holder of the recently introduced Higher Risk Building Registration with the Engineering Council.
## Our Commitment to Quality and Compliance
AESG is committed to delivering exceptional service quality and ensuring compliance with all relevant standards. We hold professional indemnity insurance and are fully insured to produce EWS1 Forms under the same policy. Our stringent internal review and QA/QC processes are accredited to ISO 9001, 14001, and 45001 standards. Additionally, we are registered with Safety Schemes in Procurement (SSIP) and hold accreditations from CHAS, Constructionline, and Acclaim.

Our track record speaks for itself. AESG has successfully delivered numerous projects through the Building Safety Fund (BSF) and is a proud member of the Homes England Cladding Safety Scheme (CSS) Panel. Our clients trust us for our expertise, reliability, and commitment to enhancing fire safety in their buildings.
