#  Measuring community engagement and sharing lessons learnt
4 July, 2024 
Minter Ellison, 20/447 Collins Street, Melbourne, Australia - [Get Directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/77TmX97e38LMmfd5A)
[ Register here ](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=02v-8kqcW0iuaeGIIKiBMPDu3TKCGOlOus1-zoA66rVUNVgzWVhMTzFOREhBUlVKVUVUUEE3SUkySC4u)
Delivering meaningful involvement from stakeholders and communities is crucial for successfully executing and managing projects within set timelines and budgets. As professionals, our goal is to implement engagement strategies consistently, efficiently, and comprehensively, while customizing our approach to meet local community requirements and regulations. How can we confirm and demonstrate our effectiveness, and what proven methods and tools from other sectors can guide us in this endeavour?
**Date:** Thursday, 4 July
Arrival from 7:45am for an 8:00am start with a 9:30am finish (Light breakfast, tea and coffee provided)
**Location:** MinterEllison, 20/447 Collins Street, Melbourne VIC 3000
**Event Recap:**
During this event, ERM, in partnership with MinterEllison and IAP2 Australasia, hosted a panel discussion moderated by ERM Partner Tricia Wunsch. This excellent discussion measured the quality of community engagement and sharing lessons learned, spanning diverse perspectives by representatives from government, construction, renewables, mining and legal.
