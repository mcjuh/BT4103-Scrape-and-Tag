## Ensuring Acoustic Excellence During Construction
At AESG, our Acoustic Construction Supervision services ensure that your project’s acoustic designs and noise control measures are properly implemented during the construction phase. With on-site supervision, we ensure that all acoustic requirements are adhered to, minimising the risk of non-compliance or acoustic failures. Our team works closely with construction teams, architects, and engineers to provide real-time feedback and guidance, ensuring that the project meets all relevant acoustic standards and performance goals.We understand the complexities of acoustic design, and our involvement during construction helps ensure that sound insulation, vibration control, and noise mitigation strategies are properly executed, saving you from costly rework or post-construction issues.
## Key Services
[ On-Site Acoustic Supervision ](https://aesg.com/engineering/construction-supervision/#collapse-7abe8626aa068dd552d1)
Our team of acoustic consultants is present on-site during critical construction stages to supervise the installation of acoustic treatments and systems. We monitor the process closely to ensure that sound insulation, noise barriers, and vibration dampening systems are installed correctly and according to the project’s specifications.
[ Quality Assurance and Compliance Checks ](https://aesg.com/engineering/construction-supervision/#collapse-203a7c16aa068dd552d1)
We conduct regular quality assurance checks to ensure compliance with industry standards, such as BS 5228 for noise control during construction and BS 4142 for industrial noise impacts. Our team provides detailed feedback to the construction crew, ensuring that all acoustic goals are met.
[ Coordination with Contractors and Project Teams ](https://aesg.com/engineering/construction-supervision/#collapse-8b2a72e6aa068dd552d1)
Our acoustic engineers work closely with contractors, project managers, and architects to ensure that noise control measures are integrated seamlessly into the broader construction process. By coordinating across teams, we help avoid conflicts between acoustic requirements and other building systems.
[ Acoustic Testing and Verification ](https://aesg.com/engineering/construction-supervision/#collapse-7abe8626aa068dd55893)
As part of our construction supervision services, we conduct on-site acoustic testing to verify that the installed systems meet performance criteria. We measure noise levels, sound insulation performance, and vibration control to ensure that the project complies with design expectations and regulations.
[ Construction Noise and Vibration Monitoring ](https://aesg.com/engineering/construction-supervision/#collapse-203a7c16aa068dd55893)
Our team also provides real-time construction noise and vibration monitoring to track the impact of site activities on surrounding areas. This helps manage noise pollution and ensures that the project complies with local noise ordinances and regulations throughout the construction period.
