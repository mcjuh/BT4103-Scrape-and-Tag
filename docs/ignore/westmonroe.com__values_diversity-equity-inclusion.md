# Diversity, Equity & Inclusion 
At West Monroe, we believe that diversity, equity, and inclusion drive meaningful impact for our people, our clients, and our communities. 
## Bringing Our Best to Work
At West Monroe, diversity, equity, and inclusion are core to how we work. We’re committed to fostering an environment where everyone feels valued, included, and empowered to reach their full potential—embracing perspectives across race, background, gender, religion, age, ability, and more.
We know that the best outcomes are achieved by bringing diverse voices together and embedding inclusion and equity into how we operate. Our goal is to drive connection by making confident, thoughtful, people-centered decisions, that meet employees where they are, to enhance employee experience and support the firm’s growth.
## Our Employee Resource Groups
At West Monroe, we’re committed to fostering a culture of connection and collaboration. Our Employee Resource Groups create spaces where employees can build relationships, share experiences, and learn from one another. These groups are central to our mission of strengthening communities—both inside and outside our organization.
