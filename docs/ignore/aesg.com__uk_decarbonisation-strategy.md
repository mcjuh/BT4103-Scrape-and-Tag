At AESG UK, we are committed to supporting both public and private sectors in achieving Net Zero Carbon Emission targets. Our expertise in developing effective decarbonisation strategies enables organisations to reduce their carbon footprint, comply with UK regulations, and contribute to a sustainable future.
## The Importance of Decarbonisation Strategies in the UK
The UK is leading the fight against climate change, with a legally binding commitment to reach net zero emissions by 2050. This goal involves collaborative action by government, businesses, investors, and communities, all striving towards a more sustainable future.
### Key Drivers
### Government Leadership
The UK government has established clear commitments to reduce emissions and reach net zero by 2050, providing a strong regulatory framework for businesses.
### Regulatory Compliance
Programmes such as the Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR) scheme and the UK Emissions Trading Scheme (UK ETS) require organisations to report on emissions and energy use, ensuring accountability.
### Market Forces
Investors and consumers increasingly prioritise sustainability, encouraging businesses to adopt responsible practices and minimise their carbon footprint.
### Technological Advancements
Innovations in energy efficiency, renewable energy, and low-carbon technologies are making it easier for organisations to meet their decarbonisation goals.
### Sustainable Finance
Financial institutions are increasingly using carbon reduction metrics as part of sustainable finance offerings, supporting the transition to a low-carbon economy.
## Key Services

## Baseline Performance Assessment
### Energy Auditing
Conducting energy audits aligned with BS EN 16247 standards to establish current energy performance.
### Carbon Footprinting
Calculating Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions in accordance with SECR and GHG Protocol guidelines.
## Retrofit Identification and Planning
### Deep Retrofit Strategies
Developing plans for substantial upgrades to improve energy efficiency and reduce emissions.
### Building Energy Modelling
Using simulation tools to identify optimal retrofit measures for carbon reduction.
## Decarbonisation Investment Strategies
### Cost-Benefit Analysis
Evaluating the financial implications of proposed decarbonisation measures.
### Funding and Incentives
Assisting clients in accessing UK government grants, sustainable finance, and incentives for energy efficiency improvements.
### Prioritisation of Actions
Identifying quick wins and long-term investments for maximum return.
## Net Zero Pathway Development
### Strategic Roadmapping
Creating tailored pathways to achieve net zero emissions by 2050 or earlier.
### Electrification Strategies
Transitioning from fossil fuel-based systems to electric alternatives.
### Renewable Energy Integration
Assessing and implementing on-site renewable energy solutions such as solar PV systems.
## Regulatory Compliance and Reporting
### SECR Reporting Support
Assisting with compliance under the Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting scheme.
### ESG Reporting
Providing support for environmental, social, and governance reporting requirements.
### Carbon Neutral Certification
Guiding organisations through achieving carbon neutrality under schemes such as the UK Green Building Council’s Net Zero Carbon Buildings Framework.
## Monitoring and Verification
### Performance Tracking
Implementing systems to monitor energy consumption and emissions over time.
### Measurement and Verification
Ensuring that implemented measures deliver the expected benefits.
