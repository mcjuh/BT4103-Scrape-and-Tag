#  Telecommunications 
Telecom operators face mounting pressure to boost performance and returns while sustaining future investment. They must modernize legacy networks, platforms and operating models to drive efficiency and value, while continuing to invest in next-generation infrastructure, digital services and AI. This demands a more disciplined approach to funding, prioritization and execution.
Through value-led programs from strategy to execution, direction is translated into action across business, operating and technology priorities. Combining deep sector experience, advanced technologies including agentic AI at scale and global alliances, we help modernize networks, platforms and operating models to improve performance and resilience, supporting leaders to deliver measurable results.
Together, we are all in to help telcos shape the next phase of their evolution so that they create more value and better meet the needs of customers and society.

AI is reshaping customer expectations and buying behaviors, while competitive and supplier ecosystems are evolving at pace. To address this, more compelling value propositions, greater service personalization and winning customer experiences are essential.

### Scaling AI adoption to optimize operating models and enhance agility, efficiency and productivity

**HOW EY CAN HELP**

### Optimizing telco asset portfolios and M&A strategies to drive scale and unlock long-term value
As consolidation accelerates, telcos must reassess core and non-core assets and capital allocation to unlock scale and value balancing infrastructure ownership and strategic optionality, while maximizing value capture and synergy realization.
Read More  Read Less 
**HOW EY CAN HELP**
Refining capital allocation and M&A strategy, EY helps telcos realize scale and value and strengthen infrastructure positions, identifying opportunities for acquisitions and potential divestitures as new consolidation scenarios form.
  * **Portfolio strategy aligned to future value:** reshaping core and non-core assets across networks and digital infrastructure to support portfolio objectives, capital allocation priorities and organizational flexibility as consolidation accelerates


  * **M &A to drive scale and repositioning:** defining and executing M&A strategies to support consolidation, access capabilities in areas such as data centers and digital infrastructure, and capture synergies that strengthen long-term competitive positioning


  * **Disciplined capital allocation:** applying insight and scenario modeling to inform capital allocation decisions across infrastructure, markets and services, in line with long-term value creation priorities


  * **End-to-end transaction execution:** supporting the deal lifecycle from strategy and diligence to integration and separation to help organizations manage complex transactions, navigate risk and advance their financial and strategic objectives

###### **CHALLENGE**
### Reduce enterprise risks to maximize stakeholder trust
Telcos must navigate escalating cyber threats, growing regulatory scrutiny and rising ESG expectations while protecting critical infrastructure and maintaining customer trust. Failure to strengthen data governance, resilience, and security increases risk across increasingly connected ecosystems.
Read More  Read Less 
**HOW EY CAN HELP**
Advancing cyber resilience, AI governance and sustainability performance, EY helps telcos protect critical assets and enhance stakeholder trust, reducing risk exposure while supporting compliance, operational resilience and long-term growth.
  * **Advanced threat detection and rapid response:** designing security operations that combine intelligence, automation and monitoring to identify, contain and recover from cyber threats, strengthening resilience and protecting critical infrastructure


  * **Integrated risk governance and resilience:** embedding data, technology and enterprise risk management to support data governance, controls and regulatory compliance, while addressing continuity, recovery and long-term operational resilience


  * **Trusted AI through robust governance:** supporting responsible AI adoption through transparency, risk controls and lifecycle monitoring, helping address regulatory expectations, decision-making and stakeholder trust in AI-driven services


  * **Sustainable transformation and ESG performance:** embedding sustainability into strategy and operations to address climate and supply chain risks, strengthen ESG performance and disclosures, and increase stakeholder trust
