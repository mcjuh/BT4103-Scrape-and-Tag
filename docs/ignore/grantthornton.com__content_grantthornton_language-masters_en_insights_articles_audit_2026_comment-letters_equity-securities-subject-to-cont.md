# Comment: Equity securities subject to contractual sale restrictions
July 28, 2026 
Grant Thornton recently submitted a comment letter to the FASB in response to its proposed Accounting Standards Update, _Fair Value Measurement (Topic 820) – Investment Companies with Equity Securities Subject to Contractual Sale Restrictions_. The proposed guidance would require investment companies to include the economic effect of contractual sale restrictions in their measurement of an equity security’s fair value.
In our letter, we express agreement with the proposal insofar as it addresses the practical concerns raised on behalf of investment companies with the FASB. We also highlight several conceptual concerns and encourage the Board to take on a broader project to address those concerns.
