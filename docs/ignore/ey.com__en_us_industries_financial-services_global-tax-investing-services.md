#  Global Tax Investing Services 
EY's [**global tax investing services**](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-us/industries/financial-services/documents/ey-global-tax-investing-services-2025.pdf) can help custodians, broker dealers, private banks, asset managers, pension funds, family offices, endowments and high net worth individuals be more proactive in meeting, monitoring and managing risks of the expanding and evolving tax requirements in markets where they invest.

## What EY Global Tax Investing Services can do for you
As authorities worldwide enforce new tax laws and increase transparency, keeping up with new and evolving taxation regimes and treaties can be overwhelming. Global investors must identify and coordinate with numerous local tax service providers to meet their compliance and reporting requirements. The growing complexity of this responsibility is driving demand for globally coordinated services with agility and responsiveness to a dynamic marketplace.
Our professionals understand the role of the tax service provider in various non-US markets, the taxpayers' interactions with global and local custodians, and the compliance and reporting responsibilities that our clients face. With our suite of tax technology tools, we can help you gain efficiencies and insights with resources designed to address your global investing tax management issues.

[➜ Corporate Actions Tax Analyzer (CATA)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-us/industries/financial-services/documents/ey-cata-slip-sheet-2025.pdf) Proactively identify and review publicly available, complex corporate actions and understand their tax implications.
[➜ Foreign Tax Form Completion (FTFC) service](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-us/industries/financial-services/documents/ey-ftfc-service-slipsheet-2025.pdf) Complete and track foreign withholding tax forms to obtain relief-at-source or file reclaims.
[➜ Global Investor Services (GIS)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-us/industries/financial-services/documents/ey-gis-slipsheet-2025.pdf) Reduce risk, improve quality and efficiency through a centralized focus on nonresident tax compliance around the globe.
[➜ GWTR Transfer Tax](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-us/industries/financial-services/documents/ey-stamp-transfer-tax-flyer-gwtr-2025.pdf) Consider potential transfer tax implications of new fund launches or transitions.
[➜ Global Withholding Tax Reporter (GWTR)](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-us/industries/financial-services/documents/ey-gwtr-brochure-2025.pdf) Discover your one-stop information source for withholding tax rates, rules and procedures worldwide.
