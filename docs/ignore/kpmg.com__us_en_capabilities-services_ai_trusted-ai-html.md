## Manage risks from AI integration
As your AI program grows, so do the potential risks and challenges.
Trust
Trust risk is putting the external reputation of an organization at stake. This can arise from bias, discrimination, misinformation, or opaqueness of its AI systems. 
Compliance
Compliance risk can stem from the rising number of global AI and ethics regulations and internal enterprise policies and controls. 
Security and Privacy
Cyber and adversarial threats, as well as the need for transparency and consumer privacy can create security and privacy risks due to the fast pace of development. 
Value
Value is at risk when there is insufficient awareness and training on AI; data integrity, statistical validity, or model accuracy concerns; or when there is a lack of technology assessment tools.
Speed
Program speed can slow as models and pipelines become more diverse and complex; data, storage, and computing needs grow; and the need for AI/ML talent increases.
