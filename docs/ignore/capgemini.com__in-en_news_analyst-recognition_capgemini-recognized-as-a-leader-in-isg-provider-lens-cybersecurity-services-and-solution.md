## For the fifth consecutive year, Capgemini has been named a Leader in the ISG Provider Lens™ Cybersecurity – Services and Solutions 2025 report, affirming our continued excellence and trusted position in the global cybersecurity landscape.
This prestigious recognition spans multiple regions, including the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, and France, and highlights our leadership across four critical cybersecurity domains:
  * Technical Security Services (TSS)
  * Strategic Security Services (SSS)
  * Next-Gen SOC/MDR Services
  * Risk-based Vulnerability Management


**Why Capgemini stands out**
The ISG report underscores Capgemini’s ability to deliver **future-ready, AI-powered cybersecurity solutions** that scale with our clients’ evolving needs. Our key differentiators as highlighted in the report includes:
  * **Comprehensive, end-to-end cybersecurity services** tailored to industry and region
  * **Operationalization of GenAI** and advanced threat intelligence
  * **Seamless IT/OT/cloud integration** for holistic protection
  * **Global-local delivery model** with deep industry expertise
  * **Strong ecosystem partnerships** with leading technology providers
  * **Contextualized MDR** and industrial-grade SOC capabilities
  * **Alignment with regulatory, ESG, and sector-specific mandates**


> “Capgemini exemplifies cybersecurity leadership by combining deep domain expertise, industrial-grade SOC capabilities, and a scalable, intelligence-driven service model tailored to regional market needs. Its ability to operationalize advanced technologies like GenAI, integrate IT and OT environments, and deliver contextualized MDR through a globally coordinated but locally delivered framework positions it as a formidable partner in strategic and technical security transformation”
> Gowtham Sampath- Assistant Director and Principal Analyst
> “This recognition by ISG as a Leader across key cybersecurity domains and regions reinforces our global strategy. Cybersecurity today is a catalyst for business transformation, and our approach is anchored in trust, speed, and resilience. Our clients count on us to deliver outcomes that are not only technically strong but also aligned with their strategic goals. We do this at scale—combining deep expertise, AI-powered cyber defense, and industrialized SOC capabilities tailored to regional and sector-specific needs”
