April 21, 2026 _—_ KPMG is proud to announce it has been named the **2026 Google Cloud Partner of the Year for Global Industry Solutions: Breakthrough** , recognizing its leadership in one of the largest and fastest enterprise-scale deployments of Gemini Enterprise.
Through the rapid integration of KPMG professionals’ expertise into Google’s AI platform, KPMG demonstrated how agentic AI can be adopted securely and at scale across a complex global enterprise. This internal blueprint now underpins KPMG’s Gemini Enterprise fast track activation framework and industry-specific agentic AI solutions, helping clients accelerate adoption and deliver measurable outcomes.
As part of its own transformation, KPMG became one of the first global professional services organizations to deploy Gemini Enterprise at an enterprise scale. Within two weeks of launch, more than 90% of KPMG US professionals were actively using the platform. By enabling more than 55,000 employees, KPMG US validated Gemini Enterprise in real‑world conditions and helped establish a repeatable blueprint that enables KPMG member firms to actively support clients in their adoption of Gemini Enterprise.
Building on this foundation, KPMG professionals have created over 150 agents for clients around the world, including launching a suite of AI agents designed to transform critical client functions across finance, audit, risk, and operations. These agents are already delivering measurable outcomes, including helping a telecom client in adopting agentic AI within its internal audit function. KPMG firms also launched Google Cloud Experience Centers in several countries, helping clients to explore real-world agentic AI use cases and learn how to translate agentic capabilities into measurable business outcomes.
> KPMG has made a significant impact by applying Google Cloud technology to solve complex challenges across key industries. We’re honored to celebrate KPMG as our Global Industry Solutions Breakthrough Partner of the Year and recognize the innovative changes they’ve enabled.
Kevin Ichhpurani 
President, Global Partner Ecosystem 
Google Cloud 
> What matters to clients isn’t just experimentation, it’s whether AI works at scale under real enterprise conditions. This award recognizes a shift in how enterprises are approaching AI. By deploying Gemini Enterprise internally, we have shown that agentic AI can be adopted rapidly, governed responsibly, and scaled securely. That experience now directly informs how KPMG firms can help clients move from pilots to measurable, enterprise‑wide outcomes with the KPMG Trusted AI framework.
Kevin Bogle 
Global Lead Partner for Alphabet 
KPMG US
