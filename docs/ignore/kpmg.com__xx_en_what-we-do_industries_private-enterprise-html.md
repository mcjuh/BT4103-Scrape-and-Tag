#  Private Enterprise 
Collaborating with private companies of all sizes as they look to grow, exit or transfer ownership.


## Shaping strategy that leaves a legacy
Private companies fuel the world’s economies, driving innovation, employment and investment across industries and geographies. Yet against a backdrop of succession pressures, shifting tax landscapes, capital constraints and talent shortages, owners are finding it harder than ever to protect what they’ve built while realizing future growth potential. From family businesses and family offices to fast-growing startups with high growth potential, it’s a complexity that requires agility and resilience to overcome. KPMG Private Enterprise is here to help.
Working with private companies at every stage of their journey, our sector specialists support with global reach and local understanding. Whether you’re preparing for a succession event, structuring a family office, entering new markets or strengthening controls, we help you grow with confidence while safeguarding your legacy.
Our global Private Enterprise network unites professionals from KPMG firms around the world to deliver coordinated and multidisciplinary approaches. We work with owners and management teams to solve challenges, unlock future value and shape strategies for years to come.
