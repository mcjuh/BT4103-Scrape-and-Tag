#  Product ClaimsAssurance
[ Get in touch ](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/contact/ "Get in touch")
ERM CVS provides independent assurance for product claims made by organizations, such as low carbon, carbon intensity, product carbon footprint and recycled content. This third-party assurance helps to boost credibility and trust with customers. It also:
  * Reduces the risk of ‘green washing’ accusations
  * Increases confidence with key stakeholders, including shareholders, customers and partners
  * Unlocks insights into how products measure up against the competition
  * Provides the information needed for scope 3 product carbon footprint emissions statements
  * Enables B2B customers to more accurately report their emissions


## Boost competitive advantage, drive efficiency and reduce risk by demonstrating the real impact of products and services. 
Environmental and social impacts and credentials are increasingly influencing consumers’ purchasing decisions for products and services. 
Integrity and transparency are vital for building trust. Organizations are seeking to reduce the risk of making accidental false claims about products. 
Supply chain decarbonization is gaining momentum. Organizations are increasingly tackling scope 3 emissions by using supplier data to prove the carbon intensity of products as well as achieved reductions. 
More regulations are emerging to cover the social and environmental impact of an organization’s own operations and supply chain. 
## Mitigate the risk of financial and reputational damage with trusted product claims. 
Product carbon footprint (PCF) and life cycle assessment (LCA)
ERM CVS provides trusted third-party assurance of PCF statements, which helps organizations prove decarbonization claims and make accurate external disclosures. Our assurance and life cycle assessment processes focus on the accuracy of input data to deliver a reliable output.
[ read more ](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/product-claims-assurance/#product "read more")
Environmental claims
ERM CVS helps to evaluate a range of environmental claims, including recyclability, recycled content, circularity, energy savings and water savings. Our assurance services are aligned to key global reporting standards and regulatory frameworks, such as ISO 14021, US Green Guides, UK Green Claims Code, and the EU and Australia Green Claims Directives.
Carbon intensity assurance
Industries with high carbon footprints assess the role of raw materials and inputs in their decarbonization strategies. ERM CVS has the deep sector, technical knowledge and alignment to global standards (e.g GIIGNL Framework, and Statement of Greenhouse Gas Emissions (SGE) Methodology), to assure the carbon intensity of feedstocks, such as liquid natural gas, ammonia, hydrogen, methane and other materials. 
Carbon neutral and avoided emissions claims 
ERM CVS helps organizations demonstrate their achievement of carbon neutrality, net zero and avoided emissions key climate change goals. Our robust assurance processes, critical analysis and technical sector knowledge brings integrity to decarbonization claims. Example reporting criteria includes PAS 2060 and WBCSD Avoided Emissions Guidance.
##  PFC assurance and product LCA
Measuring the carbon footprint of products across the full life cycle enables organizations to reduce scope 3 GHG emissions, identify cost-saving opportunities, demonstrate leadership, meet customer expectations and differentiate their products against competitors. It also provides emission impact insights that can help to shape supplier selection, product design and manufacturing processes.
ERM CVS assures that the PCF methodology used by organizations focuses on the appropriateness of assumptions and emission factors as well as any allocations and cut-off criteria. We also evaluate the overall approach employed to calculate PCFs.
Our experts can perform critical reviews of life cycle assessments to validate the integrity of the processes used and the reliability of the results achieved. Standards applied for PCF and LCA assurance include ISO 14067: 2018 Carbon footprint of products, ISO 14040/44, PAS 2050, GHG Protocol Product Life Cycle Accounting and Reporting Standard, Together for Sustainability, and ISO 14071.
