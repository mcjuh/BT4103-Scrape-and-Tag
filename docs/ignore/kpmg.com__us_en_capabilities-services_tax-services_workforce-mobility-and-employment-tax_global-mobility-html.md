##  Solutions for a global workplace 
Less risk, more satisfaction 
Managing a global and evolving workforce comes with challenges. But it also comes with rewards when you send your best talent anywhere in the world. The key is to keep risk and cost low while keeping employees excited and able to do their best work. KPMG can help you confidently manage the complexities of being globally mobile.
Attract top talent
Gain competitive advantage by designing policies and processes that engage and retain top talent and support a diverse and inclusive work environment. 
Make it less taxing
Navigate the complexities of global payroll and employment tax with leading- practice strategies, and support employees with income tax returns and settlements.
Seize each opportunity
Manage risk, reduce costs, and enhance employee experiences so you can be ready to take on new ventures, customer requests, and projects— wherever they are.
