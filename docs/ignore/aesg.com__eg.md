# Sustainability and Engineering Consultants in Egypt
Delivering Innovative Solutions for Egypt's Built Environment
In the heart of Egypt’s evolving landscape, AESG stands as a trusted partner in building a sustainable, low-carbon future. As a leading sustainability consultancy and engineering advisory firm in Egypt, we are proud to contribute to the nation’s climate ambitions - combining global expertise with deep local insight to transform vision into action. We specialise in **façade engineering, fire and life safety, building commissioning, and carbon management** , crafting bespoke **net-zero strategies** that respond to Egypt’s unique built environment. At AESG, we guide our clients toward **Green Pyramid certification** through the **Egypt Green Building Council (EGBC)** , ensuring each development meets rigorous sustainability standards while respecting the local context.​ Our mission is clear: to deliver high-performance, future-ready solutions that redefine what it means to build for generations to come.
## Our Commitment to Egypt
Our multidisciplinary expertise spans three core service areas: **Environment and Sustainability, Engineering, and Project Delivery Management**. From fire-safe façades to energy-efficient building envelopes and advanced fire protection systems, we ensure every structure we touch aligns with Egypt’s national building codes and local regulations. We work hand-in-hand with developers, architects, engineers, and public sector partners - uniting vision with precision to deliver projects that matter. Each step we take contributes to Egypt’s sustainability goals, from achieving Green Pyramid certification through the Egypt Green Building Council (EGBC) to supporting Vision 2030 and the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). AESG Egypt is not just a consultancy—we are collaborators in a national mission to build a greener, safer, and more sustainable Egypt.
## Our Commitment to Africa
From our foundation in South Africa and Egypt, AESG is rapidly growing across the African continent, partnering with governments, visionary real estate developers, and forward-thinking private sector leaders. With a deep respect for Africa’s rich diversity and boundless potential, we blend local knowledge with global expertise to craft solutions that reflect the continent’s aspirations for sustainable growth and thriving communities. AESG is honoured to play a role in shaping Africa’s future - one where the built environment harmonises with nature, resilience is built into every structure, and every project paves the way for a sustainable tomorrow.
