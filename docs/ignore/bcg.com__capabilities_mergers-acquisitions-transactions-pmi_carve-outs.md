# Carve-Out
A successful carve-out creates lasting value, not just a completed transaction. BCG’s carve-out consulting teams guide clients from strategy through execution, helping maximize value, minimize disruption, and position businesses—and their people—for long-term success.
A corporate carve-out is an opportunity to strengthen both the business being separated and the business that remains. Whether an organization is navigating the sell side or buy side, BCG delivers end-to-end expertise to manage complexity, accelerate execution, and maximize value, tailoring our support to the transaction priorities.BCG’s carve-out consultants combine deep separation and integration expertise with industry and functional capabilities to help clients navigate transactions with confidence, ensuring continuity and mitigating risks.

Sell-Side Carve-Outs
Preparing a business for separation requires balancing transaction readiness with long-term value creation for both the carved-out business and the remaining organization. Our carve-out consultants support clients across every stage of the separation journey, including:
  * **Carve-out strategy.** Define the separation vision, scope, value case, governance, and roadmap while assessing deal complexity and key risks.
  * **Transition planning.** Map functional and cross-functional interdependencies, define the separation perimeter, and create the execution blueprint.
  * **Transition service agreements (TSAs).** Structure, negotiate, and manage TSAs to support business continuity while enabling an efficient transition.
  * **Operational readiness.** Coordinate people, processes, technology, and communications to enable a seamless operational separation.
  * **RemainCo optimization.** Identify and mitigate stranded costs, redesign operating models, and strengthen the remaining business for future growth.
  * **Separation execution.** Stand up RemainCo and SpinCo capabilities, enable operational independence, and minimize long-term entanglements.


Buy-Side Carve-Outs
Acquiring a carved-out business requires more than successful integration. It demands early planning, disciplined execution, and a clear path to value realization. Our carve-out consulting approach helps corporate and private equity investors accelerate performance, from signing through stabilization, by supporting:
  * **Carve-out strategy.** Assess value creation opportunities and integration risks during diligence while defining transition priorities and the long-term value creation agenda.
  * **Target operating model design.** Define the future organization, governance, and capability requirements needed to execute the investment thesis.
  * **Transition planning.** Identify operational dependencies, establish execution priorities, and prepare for a successful transition.
  * **TSAs.** Design and negotiate TSA arrangements that reduce operational risk while accelerating independence.
  * **Integration readiness.** Coordinate business, technology, operations, and people activities to ensure uninterrupted operations from close.
  * **Synergy realization planning.** Identify and size synergies, define ownership, and establish execution roadmaps through post-close value capture.
  * **Separation execution.** Stand up NewCo capabilities, establish control, and enable operational independence aligned with strategic objectives.
