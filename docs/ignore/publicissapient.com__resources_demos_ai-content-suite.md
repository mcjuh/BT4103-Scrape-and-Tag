How does Bodhi power the AI content supply chain?
Bodhi is the decision engine behind our content supply chain solution. It uses AI agents to determine what content to create, how to adapt it, who needs to review it and where it should go next. Those decisions are made using real context, not static rules or manual hand-offs. That’s what keeps content flowing.
How does the AI content supply chain work with existing CMS and MarTech tools?
Our content supply chain solution is designed to work with the tools teams already rely on. Bodhi connects into existing CMS, DAM and MarTech systems. Its agents coordinate decisions across your systems instead of replacing them. They create content, personalize it and route it through a single flow while execution happens where it already lives. How does the AI content supply chain make sure content is on brand?
Our content supply chain solution treats brand rules as part of the system, not a checklist at the end. Bodhi embeds tone, messaging guidelines and approval requirements directly into how content is created, adapted and routed. AI agents check content as it moves forward, flagging issues or adjusting outputs automatically. That keeps brand consistency intact and prevents content from stalling in review loops.
