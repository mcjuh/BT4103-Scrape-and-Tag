#  Piyush Gandhi 
##  Specialist Leader | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
Piyush Gandhi is a Specialist Leader in Deloitte Consulting LLP’s Customer Practice, focused on the Industrial Products & Construction sector within the Energy, Resources & Industrials (ER&I) industry.
Piyush helps organizations deliver outstanding customer experiences and drive more effective sales and service engagements across digital and in-person channels. Piyush works with enterprise architects, project delivery leads, and developers to implement advanced mobile, commerce, sales, and service solutions that enable digital transformation.
With over 23 years of experience across SAP platforms, Piyush is passionate about sharing leading practices and advising clients on the evolving landscape of customer expectations.
