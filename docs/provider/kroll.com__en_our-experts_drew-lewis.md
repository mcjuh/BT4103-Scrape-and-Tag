AT A GLANCE 
Drew Lewis is a Senior Director in the Settlement Administration practice, based in Chattanooga, TN.
Drew has spent the past 10 years in the class action and mass tort space, focusing on eDiscovery. He worked on matters such as Opioids Litigation, Interest Rate Swaps Antitrust Litigation, Social Media Adolescent Addiction Litigation, FICO Antitrust Litigation, Real Page Antitrust Litigation, WWE Securities Litigation, and NEC Baby Formula Litigation.
Prior to joining Kroll, Drew began his career as an Associate at Baker Donelson (AmLaw 100) and then as eDiscovery Counsel at Recommind. Drew then ran his own business focusing on class actions and mass torts before joining DISCO, where he co-founded and lead the Class Action and Mass Tort Practice Group.
Drew has a J.D. from the University of Mississippi School of Law.
