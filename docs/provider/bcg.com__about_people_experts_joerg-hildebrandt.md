Green Public Procurement: Catalyzing the Net-Zero Economy
Public procurement accounts for 15% of global greenhouse gas emissions annually. Because governments can create demand for clean energy technologies, they can have a direct impact on reducing the world’s carbon footprint.
### Education
  * Business administration and engineering degree, University of Stuttgart


Joerg Hildebrandt leads the global Public Sector practice at Boston Consulting Group. He advises senior leaders and board members of government entities on strategic transformations to deliver future success. His mission is to unlock the full potential of large corporations and public sector institutions. Joerg has particular expertise in large-scale transformations, strategy, climate and environment, operating models, digitization, marketing, organizational design, governance, and change management. In addition to his commitment to his BCG clients, Joerg has supported non-profit organizations and NGOs in their strategy development and execution.
Joerg regularly shapes new thought leadership to share insights. One of his recent publications stems from his work with the World Economic Forum on Green Public Procurement.
