### Education
  * MBA, Yale University
  * Master of Forestry, Yale University
  * BA, human biology, Stanford University


Lucyann Murray is a core member of Boston Consulting Group’s Climate & Sustainability and Industrial Goods practices. She maintains a passionate advocacy for nature in her work, concentrating on how water, land use, forests, ecosystems, and biodiversity are intricately intertwined with operations, supply chains, and surrounding communities.
Lucyann has worked in a range of land-based, heavy asset, and processing industries, including mining and metals, forest products, agriculture, energy, and chemicals. Her functional experience covers the entire value chain, with a focus on business strategy, change management, and operations. She focuses on nature-based strategies that can provide a regenerative future for companies and communities.
Before joining BCG, Lucyann worked at Accenture’s Chemicals and Natural Resources (CNR) practice, where she led sustainability for CNR in North America.
