#  Venkat Govindan 
##  Managing Director | Deloitte & Touche LLP 
Venkat has 25 years of expertise in elevating the value of IT services internally within enterprises and through a Fortune 500 consultancy. He specializes in data engineering and analytics across industries. Venkat is savvy in envisioning the big-picture strategy and operationalizing the execution roadmap while anticipating and proactively mitigating challenges. He is a people-oriented, collaborative leader who builds relationships and partners across the executive team to align solutions and project portfolios with organizational priorities. He enjoys fostering a culture of innovation, collaboration, and shared success across a global team.
