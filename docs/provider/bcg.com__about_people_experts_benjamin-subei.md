### Education
  * Dipl. Wirt.-Ing, Industrial Engineering, RWTH Aachen and Imperial College London


Benjamin Subei joined Boston Consulting Group in 2018 with a mission to expand the firm’s agriculture business. He is a core member of the Industrial Goods practice, specializing in agribusiness and the food value chain, with an emphasis on business and growth strategy development, large-scale transformation, cost optimization, and digital business model development and digital farming. 
Ben has in-depth experience in all segments of the agribusiness value chain, covering farm input suppliers in seeds, crop protection, fertilizer and machinery, farm distributors, agriculture holdings, agtech and farming software players, and primary crop and livestock processing companies. 
Ben coordinates the firm’s climate and sustainability work in agriculture, and has deep experience in, and a passion for, regenerative agriculture, decarbonization of farming, and sustainable upstream food production.
