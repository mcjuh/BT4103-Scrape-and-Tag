Christoph Schweizer is CEO of Boston Consulting Group and a member of the firm’s Executive and Operating Committees. Previously, he was BCG’s Chair for Central and Eastern Europe and the Middle East.
Since joining BCG in 1997, Christoph has supported clients across health care, private equity, and multiple other industries on major transformational programs, including AI/GenAI initiatives, growth strategies, M&A, and post-merger integrations. Under his leadership, BCG has significantly expanded its AI and broader tech capabilities, including through the launch of BCG X, the firm’s tech build and design unit.
Christoph is a member of several leading organizations, including the Business Roundtable, the Singapore Economic Development Board’s International Advisory Council, and the World Economic Forum’s International Business Council.
