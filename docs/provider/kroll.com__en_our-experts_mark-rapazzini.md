AT A GLANCE 
Mark Rapazzini is a Senior Director in the Settlement Administration practice. He is based in Silicon Valley.
Mark has over 25 years of legal experience in cases ranging from individual personal injury litigation to class actions and complex mass torts. 
Mark consults with clients in the government enforcement actions, consumer, environmental, , finance and mass tort practice areas. He has over 25 years of experience managing complex settlement administration matters.
Before joining Kroll, Mark worked as an attorney at The Boccardo Law Firm; Alexander, Rapazzini & Graham; and Duane Morris LLP. He was also a founding partner at Rapazzini & Graham, LLP.
While still practicing law, Mark and his law partner founded RG2 Claims Administration, LLC (RG2), where he served as the chief operating officer and was responsible for all aspects of the business development and strategic direction. In 2008, Mark and his RG2 co-founder joined a national claims administration company, where he served as a senior vice president in client services and consulting.
Mark holds a bachelor’s degree in political science from the University of California and a J.D. from Santa Clara University School of Law. He is admitted to the State of California Bar; U.S. Courts of Appeal 9th Circuit and 4th Circuit; U.S. Northern District Court of California; Central District Court of California; Eastern District Court of California; and Southern District Court of California. Mark is also a member of the State Bar of California and has served as a court-appointed mediator, court-appointed arbitrator, settlement judge pro tem, as well as an independent arbiter for an Assurance of Discontinuance by the New York Attorney General and an independent adjudicator for a Consumer Financial Protection Bureau Consent Order. Mark has also served as Special Master in MDL proceedings.
