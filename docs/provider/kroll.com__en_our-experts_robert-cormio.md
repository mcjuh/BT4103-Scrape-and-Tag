# Robert Cormio
##### Senior Director

AT A GLANCE 
Robert Cormio is a Senior Director Kroll’s Settlement Administration practice and leads the Securities Settlement Administration team. He is based in New York.
Rob has been in the administration of securities class action settlements since 2006 and has managed hundreds of securities class action matters including the _Nortel I_ and _Nortel II_ securities litigations (over $1.1 billion settlements and over 470,000 claims combined), the Lehman Brothers Securities Litigation (over 100 different securities and multiple pools of settlements) and the Tyco Settlement (a $3.2 billion settlement with over 500,000 claims).
Rob has held leadership roles in several nationally recognized class action claims administration companies. He maintains a hands-on business approach by personally overseeing engagements, ensuring the highest quality product and expert handling. Prior to joining Kroll, Rob led a securities settlement team of a start-up claims administrator and helped build a securities platform.
Rob began his career in the financial services field where he worked in trading and back office roles. He received a B.S in mathematics with a minor in secondary education from Dowling College.
