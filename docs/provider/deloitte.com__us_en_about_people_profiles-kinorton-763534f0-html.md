#  Kieran Norton 
##  Principal | US Cyber Innovation & Assets Leader 
[ San Francisco, US ](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/offices/office-locator/san-francisco.html)
Kieran Norton is a Principal in Deloitte Consulting and serves as Deloitte’s US Cyber Innovation & Assets Leader. He helps organizations modernize cybersecurity and manage AI and technology risk with pragmatic, risk-aware approaches that enable digital transformation while addressing rapidly evolving threats.
  * Leads AI-enabled transformation for Deloitte’s US Cyber practice, focused on scalable and responsible adoption.
  * Oversees Deloitte’s cyber innovation and assets portfolio—from product strategy and engineering through deployment and go-to-market—to accelerate measurable outcomes.
  * Advises executives on governance, controls, and risk mitigation for GenAI and emerging technologies.
  * Helps clients modernize infrastructure and cloud security to support digital transformation, supply chain modernization, and speed-to-market.
  * Supports cost reduction and capability uplift by operationalizing secure AI and accelerating AI adoption within cyber operations to deliver faster, higher-value outcomes.


Kieran has been a leader within Deloitte’s Cyber practice for more than 20 years, with roles spanning Cyber Threat Management, Technology Risk, Mobile Security, Infrastructure & Cloud Security, and Emerging Technology & Strategy. He has also built and led strategic alliance relationships and serves in multiple account leadership roles across the financial services and technology sectors.
Market: Deloitte United States
