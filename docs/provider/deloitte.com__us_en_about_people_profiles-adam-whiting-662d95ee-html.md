#  Adam Whiting 
##  M&A Consultative Services Leader 
Adam is a leader in Deloitte Consulting LLP’s M&A and Restructuring practice. He has expertise in both pre/post-deal environments, strategic cost transformation programs and cross-functional synergy delivery. He primarily serves Deloitte’s retail and consumer products clients. Adam has advised clients on strategic acquisitions/divestitures, commercial and operational diligence / synergy identification, pricing analysis, large global program management, and integration / divestiture delivery. Adam holds a Bachelors in Economics from the University of Notre Dame and a MBA in Strategy and Finance from the Fuqua School of Business at Duke University.
