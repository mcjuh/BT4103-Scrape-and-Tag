# Barbara Bryan
Senior Partner 
Barbara Bryan is a Senior Partner in Performance Transformation. Barbara brings over 25 years of healthcare consulting experience including leadership, operational, and business development roles. A practiced healthcare thought leader with a focus on innovation, Barbara specializes in clinical and financial process improvement and critical analysis of potential improvements in cost, quality, and service. With a background in nursing and technology, Barbara brings a clinical perspective to her clients and is known as an industry expert in capacity management, throughput, care delivery, and technology-enabled solutions. Barbara leverages her deep expertise leading financial and clinical transformation engagements with a focus on IT integration to enable sustainable outcomes. 
Prior to joining Chartis, Barbara was a Managing Director at Impact Advisors; a Vice President at Premier Performance Services where she lead Clinical Operations and Workforce solutions; a Principal within GE Healthcare’s Command Center practice; a Managing Director and clinical practice leader at a national healthcare consulting firm; an executive at a healthcare technology company; and founded an independent healthcare consulting firm that was acquired by a global consulting firm. 
Barbara is frequently asked to share her knowledge through articles and presentations for distinguished industry associations and publications. Speaking engagements include forums such as ACHE Congress, HIMSS, Becker’s Healthcare, and various industry conferences. Barbara’s publications have been featured in Becker’s Hospital Review, Healthcare Purchasing News, Healthcare Executive Magazine, and Hospital & Health Networks Magazine. 
Barbara received her Master of Business of Administration from Saint Francis University, where she was awarded the Academic Excellence distinction, and her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Georgetown University, where she graduated Magna Cum Laude.

###  Why Chartis? 
Being in the healthcare consulting industry for over 25 years, I know that the heart of any consulting firm is the people. Chartis has an incredible reputation in the industry for delivering high quality strategies and operational solutions for the changing environment. These outcomes are a direct result of the talent, knowledge, and dedication of Chartis colleagues who work side by side with clients every day. Beyond this, Chartis is known for a culture of kindness that resonates across the firm and with clients. 
###  How do your personal goals align to Chartis’ mission and vision? 
At Chartis, there is a shared passion for improving healthcare and caring for one another. LOVE what you do. LOVE who you do it with. LOVE who you do it for. I relate to this mission in both my professional and personal life. One of my professional goals is to be a mentor for the next generation of consulting leadership, and I am passionate about this purpose. I truly appreciate all the mentors that I have had in my career, and I want to pay it forward through mentorship of others. Personally, I approach my passions in life with this same attitude, whether that is running, cooking, traveling, or simply being a hockey mom…LOVE what you do. LOVE who you do it with. LOVE who you do it for. 
###  Where’s your favorite place to visit and why? 
Without a doubt, Italy is my favorite place to travel due to the wonderful memories that I have of the beautiful country. My husband asked me to marry him in a piazza in the gorgeous lakeside town of Cuomo. It was a magical moment! We honeymooned in Italy, including Venice, Rome, Florence, and Sicily. We visited once again on our 10-year anniversary to the Amalfi Coast and the Puglia region – the “boot of Italy.” Perhaps the most meaningful trip was during a Christmas vacation when we took our family to Italy. All three of our sons fell in love with the country and the people, and our youngest will be studying in Rome this summer.
