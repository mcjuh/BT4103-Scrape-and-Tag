AT A GLANCE 
Matthew Wright is Managing Director in the Fixed Asset Advisory Services practice, based in London. Matthew leverages more than 17 years of experience in the valuation of machinery and equipment, industrial and commercial property, and large civil works across several industries, including oil and gas, power, manufacturing, steelmaking and fabrication, cement plants, shipyards, ports, hospitals, pharmaceuticals, and food and beverage.
Matthew also worked for 18 months in Kroll’s Austin, TX, office. Prior to joining Kroll, he was an associate director of the Asset Advisory team at Lambert Smith Hampton in London. In that role, he provided a range of consultancy services to insolvency practitioners and asset-based lenders. These services included valuations, sales advice and disposal of distressed businesses and all classes of industrial and commercial business assets.
Matthew has provided valuations for insurance, claims, asset-based lending, purchase price allocation, restructuring and insolvency, financial reporting and taxation purposes. He has undertaken valuations in over 50 countries, with projects ranging from single sites to multiple locations. He has also worked with small and medium enterprises and global conglomerates. These clients’ valuations have ranged from thousands to billions of dollars.
Matthew cofounded a start-up mapping and software service that uses satellite imagery and geospatial data to serve the energy insurance market.
He holds a bachelor’s degree in civil and structural engineering from the University of Leeds and a master’s degree in quantity surveying from the University College of Estate Management. Matthew is an Accredited Senior Appraiser with the American Society of Appraisers.
