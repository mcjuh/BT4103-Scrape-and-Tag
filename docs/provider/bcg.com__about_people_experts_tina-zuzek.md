### Education
  * MBA, Harvard Business School 
  * BA, government, Harvard College


Tina Zuzek-Arden is a leader in Boston Consulting Group’s Energy and Public Sector practices. She has advised some of the largest public utilities in the US, large-scale energy developers, hyperscalers, large corporations, and the US federal government, on a variety of energy topics.
Tina is BCG’s lead for energy work in the US federal government, as well as for physical risk and resiliency in North America. She works with clients on defining baselines, energy, and physical resiliency; advanced energy technologies; energy procurement; and broad-scale transformations of procurement and operations, while remaining mission-focused.
In addition, Tina works with public utilities and other companies on a wide range of topics, including distributed energy resources, innovative advanced energy technologies, energy efficiency, grid-scale renewables, and electrification.
