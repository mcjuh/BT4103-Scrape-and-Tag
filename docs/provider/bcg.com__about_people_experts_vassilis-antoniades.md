Vassilis Antoniades is a regional leader of the Financial Institutions practice in Central Europe and the Middle East, and he is a member of BCG’s Global CEO Advisory Council. He has had a key role in the development of the Athens office since joining BCG in 2003 and also advises the CEOs of financial services, energy, and various other sectors clients in Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. Vassilis led the Athens office from 2010 to 2018. Currently, he leads large regional client relationships on behalf of BCG.
Vassilis is a government-appointed board member of Greece’s National Council for Research and Innovation (NCRI) and advises the Greek government on green transition and post-pandemic economic recovery. He also serves on the boards of the Bodossaki Philanthropic Foundation and the Benaki Museum.
