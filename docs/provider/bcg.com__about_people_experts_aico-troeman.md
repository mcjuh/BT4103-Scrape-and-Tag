### Education
  * PhD, applied physics and nanotechnology, University of Twente
  * MSc, applied physics, University of Twente


Aico Troeman is a core member of Boston Consulting Group’s Energy and Industrial Goods practices. He joined BCG in 2011 and has supported multiple clients across these industries.
Aico is passionate about the intertwined domains of strategy, technology, and change. As such, the nature of Aico’s work is often grounded in the rethinking of the evolution and disruption of an industry’s value chain. With this as a foundation, he supports clients on defining their mid- to long-term strategies and on transforming and future-proofing their organizations.
Along with his client work, Aico leads the recruiting activities for BCG in the Netherlands.
Before joining BCG, Aico gained experience as a global program manager at Océ Technologies, a global subsidiary of Canon INC. In this role, he focused on R&D and manufacturing productivity and post merger integration.
