#  Jay Khatwani 
##  Managing Director | Deloitte & Touche LLP 
###  Jay brings over 25 years of technology leadership experience, primarily within the Financial Services sector. Throughout his career, he has successfully developed and expanded large global banking relationships, growing accounts to over $200 million annually and managing substantial P&Ls for more than a decade. 
His deep expertise in technology, combined with a passion for understanding and addressing client challenges, has enabled him to design and deliver transformative programs that position technology as a true business differentiator. This approach has allowed him to establish and maintain trusted partnerships with CXOs at leading financial institutions.
Additionally, he has a proven track record of building, motivating, and coaching high-performing global teams of more than 1,000 professionals, driving sustained growth and organizational success
