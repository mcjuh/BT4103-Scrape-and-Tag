### Education
  * École des Ponts ParisTech 


Mouhcine Berrada leads Boston Consulting Group's Operations practice for France, as well as the practice's work in AI for the EMESA region (Europe, Middle East, South America, Africa). He is a core member of the Industrial Goods (notably aerospace and aluminum) and Consumer practices. 
Since joining BCG in 2014, Mouhcine has led multiple engagements on supply chain AI (control tower, inventory optimization, etc.) as well as manufacturing AI (advanced process control, batch tracking, etc.).
Before joining the firm, Mouhcine worked for ten years at Saint-Gobain, where he held several positions including Supply Chain Manager in France and Plant Director in the UK.
