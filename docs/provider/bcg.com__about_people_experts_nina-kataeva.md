# Nina Kataeva
Managing Director & Partner [ Zurich ](https://www.bcg.com/offices/zurich/default)


### Education
  * Master's degree, digital arts, University of Applied Arts, Vienna
  * Master of Management of Science, business administration and financial mathematics, Vienna University of Economics and Business.


Nina Kataeva joined Boston Consulting Group in 2009. She is a is the global co-leader of BCG’s organization design, operating model, and cost work, and leads the People & Organization practice in Central Europe. She is an expert in agile at scale for the Central and Eastern Europe, Middle East, and Africa (CEMA) region.
Nina has led a variety of large-scale agile transformations focusing on full organization transformation, new ways of working, digitization, and build-up of new digital capabilities. She designed and implemented a multi-year digital capability development with over 60 individualized learning journeys and grid-specific use cases, as well as personally conducting selected expert trainings on digital skills.
She also led development of a big data model with over 6 million data sets and identified optimization levers including digital use cases; and developed a new target operating model for a large retail business unit in the energy industry. Nina is fluent in English, German and Russian.
