## Executive summary
Joseph Pacello is a Partner in the NYC office of Grant Thornton Advisors LLC. Joe has extensive experience providing tax services to private equity funds, hedge funds, venture capital funds and other alternative investment vehicles. He has been an asset management tax professional for over 25 years, including experience as a Big 4 tax partner.
In addition, Joe has taught graduate courses at Fairleigh Dickinson University and Grand Canyon University on the topics of financial products taxation and entity taxation. He also is a frequent speaker at industry conferences and is regulary quoted in the financial press on tax matters impacting the industry.
