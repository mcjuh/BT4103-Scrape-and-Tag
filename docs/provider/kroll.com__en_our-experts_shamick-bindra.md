AT A GLANCE 
Shamick Bindra is the Chief Operating Officer of Kroll Business Services. 
In this capacity, he oversees the global operations for the business unit, which includes [Restructuring Administration](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/restructuring-administration), [Settlement Administration](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/settlement-administration), [Issuer Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/issuer-services), [Agency and Trustee Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/agency-and-trustee-services), and [Notice Media Solutions](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/notice-media-solutions). Shamick leverages over 20 years of experience.
Prior to joining Kroll, Shamick worked at KCC, a claims and noticing agent, as the director of application development. Prior to KCC, he was a software developer at Chevron, designing and developing software for internal business applications.
Shamick graduated from UC Santa Cruz School of Engineering with a B.S. in Computer Science.
