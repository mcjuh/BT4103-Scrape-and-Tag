#  Pinaki Dhal 
##  Private Markets Technology and Data Leader | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
##  Managing Director 
Pinaki co-leads Deloitte’s US Consulting Private Equity practice, advising private equity sponsors and investment managers on modernization to drive scalable growth. He works with General Partners (GPs), corporate-owned investment managers, and traditional managers building private capabilities to assess end-to-end investment and operating processes and redesign operating models that improve speed to invest, decision quality, transparency, and cost to serve. His work is enabled by automation, modern platforms, stronger data foundations, and selective outsourcing—helping clients strengthen execution and controls across the private capital lifecycle.
Pinaki brings 20 years of experience serving leading sell-side and buy-side institutions across commercial real estate, private credit, asset-based finance, securitized products, trade and supply chain finance, infrastructure and fund finance, and private debt. 
Market: Deloitte United States
