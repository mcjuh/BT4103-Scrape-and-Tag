Florence Gatome has more than twenty years’ experience in international development. She has experience in climate change, governance, health, education, and policy, bringing a wealth of expertise to her global role leading our Policy and Technical Assistance Practice. She is based in our Kenya office.
Florence has worked collaboratively with a diverse range of stakeholders, including bilateral partners, multilateral organisations, development finance institutions, government agencies, nonprofit organisations, foundations, and the private sector across the globe.
Florence holds an MSc in Development Finance from the University of Manchester and is currently pursuing a PhD in Climate Change and Adaptation at the University of Nairobi. Her career is marked by a commitment to driving impactful policy engagement and delivering technical assistance.
