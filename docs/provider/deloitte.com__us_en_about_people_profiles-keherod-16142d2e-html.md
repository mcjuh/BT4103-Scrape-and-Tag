#  Kelly Herod 
##  Chief Client Officer 
As the US Chief Client Officer, Kelly leads Deloitte’s client engagement strategy, delivering outcomes-oriented consulting services to clients across its six industries and private equity businesses. In this role, Kelly advances Deloitte’s IndustryAdvantage™ capabilities and go-to-market strategy, develops the industry depth of Deloitte’s US professionals, as well as defines and executes Deloitte's US consulting business and innovation agenda.
Kelly is a principal in Deloitte Consulting LLP. Previously, Kelly served as Deloitte’s US Energy, Resources & Industrials Industry Leader, leading the industry practice’s strategy and collaborating with client services teams to drive value. Prior to that, Kelly served as the leader for Deloitte’s US SAP practice and alliance teams – helping clients activate intelligent, cloud-enabled technologies and working with SAP to reimagine client solutions through co-innovation.
She brings 25 years of experience leading global ERP-enabled business transformations for clients across industries. She currently serves as the Business Transformation Executive Sponsor for a portfolio of clients, advising C-suite leaders on transformation strategy, program governance, and execution to help organizations navigate complex change.
Kelly is an innovator and value-generation leader, having presented in marketplace forums such as the World Economic Forum, the Consumer Electronics Show, SAP Sapphire, and various other industry panels. She is passionate about building the next generation of leaders, working with Texas Christian University’s MBA Consulting Advisory Group, and supporting children and STEM education through the Ella Project.
Market: Deloitte United States
