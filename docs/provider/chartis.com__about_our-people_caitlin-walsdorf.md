# Caitlin Walsdorf
Partner, HealthScape Advisors 
Caitlin is a Partner at HealthScape Advisors, a Chartis company. Caitlin co-leads HealthScape’s Ancillary Center of Excellence. In her role, she supports payers – including stand-alone ancillary plans, health plans, and multi-line carriers – across the Commercial, Medicare, Medicaid, and Federal lines of business. 
In her nearly 15 years of consulting and industry experience, she has supported plans across a range of financial and operational engagements including health plan-ancillary plan partnerships; market entry support; diversification strategy; sales excellence strategy; product strategy; network and cost of care analytics and management; operational assessment and improvement; and transaction advisory.
Caitlin is responsible for working with HealthScape’s Analytics & Technology Team to manage several ancillary-specific business intelligence products including the Commercial Dental Insights Dashboard (network analytics), the Medicare Advantage Dental Tool, and the Medicare Advantage Vision Tool.
She is also a regular contributor to and co-author of HealthScape’s thought leadership in the Ancillary domain. Recently, she co-authored the Pathway to Value survey and paper in partnership with the National Association of Dental Plans (NADP) & CareQuest Institute. 
Prior to joining HealthScape, Caitlin led Government Programs business development at EyeMed Vision Care. During her tenure, she executed a strategic expansion initiative in Medicaid by contracting and implementing six new partners representing 1M+ members. She also focused on business development and operational excellence for Medicare Advantage, contracting and implementing five new partners. To support Government Programs at scale, Caitlin led cross-functional teams to deploy system enhancements, improve platform capabilities, and refine operating procedures.
Caitlin began her career at HealthScape Advisors after earning her bachelor's degree in science-business from the University of Notre Dame.

###  What do you hope to help our clients achieve? 
Solve problems. The problems in healthcare financing and delivery are innumerable and complicated. I hope to help our clients design and execute pragmatic solutions that position them for continued growth and success, while making a positive impact on the experience of receiving and paying for healthcare in our country. 
###  Do you have a motto or personal mantra? 
I set a mantra for myself at the beginning of each year. Last year’s was “words matter” – a reminder that clear and concise communication increases credibility and effectiveness in both personal and professional settings. This year’s is “less but better” – a prompt to sharpen focus on the highest value activities that progress our clients, the firm, my teammates, and myself, closer to achieving goals and making impact. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
Dreaming up and planning our next adventure. I love to both see new places and revisit sentimental favorites – whether it’s having an intense mini-golf putt-off with my brother over the high stakes ice cream bill in Door County, Wisconsin, going for a long boat ride in Crosslake, Minnesota, or tackling a new trail in a bucket list country.
