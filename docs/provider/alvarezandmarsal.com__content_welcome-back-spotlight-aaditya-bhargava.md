# Welcome Back Spotlight: Aaditya Bhargava
### **Returning to A &M Singapore: The Power of Relationships in a Global Career **
Aaditya Bhargava has rejoined Alvarez & Marsal as a Manager in [Corporate Performance Improvement](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/corporate-performance-improvement) (CPI) in Singapore, having previously been part of the firm’s early expansion in Australia. His return reflects both a global career journey and the lasting impact of the relationships built at A&M. 
In this Q&A, Aaditya shares what brought him back, how his move to Singapore has expanded his perspective, and how relationships and travel have shaped his career. 
### **From Australia to Singapore**
**What brought you back to A &M? **
I missed consulting’s fast-paced, multifaceted environment, where no two days look the same, and every challenge demands your best thinking. Beyond the work, I missed the people at A&M: smart, driven, and genuinely collaborative colleagues, who make the experience meaningful. That pull to return aligned with my wife’s secondment to Singapore, which made it the right moment to come back to A&M in a new market. 
**You were part of A &M’s early expansion in Australia. What was it like helping build the brand from the ground up? **
Incredibly rewarding. I joined when A&M was growing our presence in Australia, well before the regular features in the Australian Financial Review. I still remember sitting on the floor assembling a whiteboard with [Nick Reid](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/nick-reid), Managing Director, in our temporary Perth office, then getting straight back to building proposals. That early energy and sense of ownership were unmatched. Being part of A&M’s first projects in Australia is something I am still proud of. 
### **A New Market, A Broader Perspective**
**How has your move to Singapore expanded your experience within Corporate Performance Improvement?**
It has pushed me beyond my traditional focus on energy and mining into a broader range of industries and service offerings. I have especially valued working with private equity clients across sectors, from data centers to consumer businesses. 
**How did you approach building credibility in a new office and market?**
A&M’s one firm, one team mindset meant I was reconnecting with friends in the region, not walking into a room of strangers. Beyond that, I focused on striking the right balance: confidence in the experience I bring and genuine curiosity about what this market values. Taking the time to listen and learn has been critical. 
### **The Power of Relationships and Staying Connected to A &M **
**You have emphasized the power of relationships. What advice would you give others about staying connected to A &M? **
Never underestimate the relationships you build at A&M, especially those forged through close collaboration and high trust. For me, those connections helped me relocate to another part of the world and opened possibilities I could not have imagined. My advice is simple: stay in touch. You never know what door will open. 
**You travel frequently for both work and pleasure. How has travel influenced your perspective, both personally and professionally?**
Travel fills me with immense gratitude and has been one of the greatest shaping forces in my life. The opportunity to see the world and engage with a wide range of people is invaluable. It pushes you, humbles you, and reminds you that perfection is not the goal, growth is.
