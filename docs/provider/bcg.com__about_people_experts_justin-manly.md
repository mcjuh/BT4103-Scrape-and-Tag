### Education
  * MBA, with high distinction, Ross School of Business, University of Michigan
  * BA, economics, Dartmouth College


Justin Manly has held a number of leadership roles in his 17 years with Boston Consulting Group. He leads growth and innovation for BCG globally, and is a core member of the firm's Consumer, Public Sector, Corporate Finance & Strategy, and Marketing, Sales & Pricing practices.
Justin has collaborated with leading public and private sector organizations as they seek to transform and make step-change improvements to drive growth. He has consulted with his clients on growth, transformation, innovation, post-merger integration, and how to reimagine operating models to drive results.
Justin leads Marketing and Alumni Relations for BCG Chicago, and previously led alumni relations for North America. He is a founding board member of BCG's Center for Illinois' Future, which provides consulting support to non-profit organizations in Illinois. He also serves on the boards of Intrinsic Charter Schools and Thrive Chicago.
