  * [Principal Investors and Private Equity](https://www.bcg.com/industries/principal-investors-private-equity/overview)

### Education
  * MBA, Tuck School of Business, Dartmouth College
  * BA, Wesleyan University


Peter Czerepak leads Boston Consulting Group's Boston global asset management work for the firm. He supports asset managers (including public and private equity and hedge funds) and wealth managers on strategy, fund operations, commercial due diligence (buy-side and sell-side), and portfolio company growth transformations. Peter has significant experience in investing topics related to industries including industrial goods, technology, infrastructure, and business services.
Peter leads the global asset and wealth team and contributes to BCG’s annual asset and wealth reports. He also leads the private equity knowledge team globally. In his over 20 years in consulting, he has worked with leading asset managers and private equity sponsors, and has conducted hundreds of due diligence, strategy, and performance improvement engagements for clients across industries. In addition, he serves as relationship lead to a number of BCG's asset management, infrastructure, and private equity clients.
