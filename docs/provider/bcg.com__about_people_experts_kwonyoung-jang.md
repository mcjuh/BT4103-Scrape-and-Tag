### Education
  * BA, Yonsei University


Kwonyoung Jang is a core member of Boston Consulting Group’s Insurance and Financial Institutions practices, and the firm’s Transformation & TURN work. He focuses on large-scale change. Kwonyoung is also active with the Principal Investors & Private Equity (PIPE) and Corporate Finance practices. 
Since joining BCG in 2008, Kwonyoung has led multiple strategy, turnaround and restructuring, and M&A/PMI projects for Korean insurance and financial sector clients. His main focus is helping his clients’ top management redefine their strategy and operation capability and steer them toward value creation opportunities. He also specializes in interim C-suite roles for insurers undergoing distressed turnaround or other major business/operation transformations. 
Prior to joining BCG, Kwonyoung worked for a media and entertainment company focusing on internet marketing.
