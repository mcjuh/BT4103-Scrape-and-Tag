AT A GLANCE 
Nenad Cvjetkovic is a Managing Director in the Restructuring Practice, based in the Netherlands.
He leverages over 15 years of restructuring experience in both advisory and CFO roles across multiple sectors, specializing in financial and operational restructurings. His extensive background includes leading numerous debt and capital transactions during critical and high-demand situations. This diverse experience enables him to evaluate various stakeholders’ perspectives swiftly and effectively.
Nenad's primary focus is to accelerate the development of Kroll's restructuring practice in the Netherlands, aiming to provide cross-border solutions for both existing and new clients. Specific growth initiatives include leveraging his strong relationships with major Dutch banks, direct debt providers, and private equity firms.
Prior to joining Kroll, Nenad was a partner at ACE Advisory, which underwent a talent acquisition by Kroll for the team in the Netherlands. He played a pivotal role in leading the business and delivered a number of exceptional projects, including the financial and operational restructuring of a leading Dutch retailer and a food production company. His unique blend of skills as a CFO, advisor, and financier allows him to navigate complex challenges and drive successful outcomes.
Currently, Nenad serves on the Supervisory Board of an international transportation company, where he contributes his financial acumen and strategic insights to guide the organization’s growth and development.
Beyond his professional career, Nenad has a background in basketball, having played full-time at a high level, demonstrating how he thrives in high-pressure, dynamic environments.
