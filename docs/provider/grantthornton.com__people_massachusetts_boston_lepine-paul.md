## Executive summary
Paul Lepine serves as Grant Thornton's Alliance Director for the firm's Oracle and NetSuite Alliance Partnership across North America. With over 15 years of sales and marketing experience, Paul provides the practice with a proven track record of transitions / multi-pillar business transformations, campaign development, application sales training outreach, customer engagement, and ecosystem visibility.
With a proven track record of success in driving strategic partnerships since joining the firm in June 2017, Paul is responsible for overseeing alliance operations and spearheading joint-marketing endeavors that contribute to Grant Thornton’s growth and revenue generation goals. His approach is founded on a successful triangulation of customer engagement, ecosystem visibility, and pipeline generation across Grant Thornton’s Oracle & NetSuite practice.
Grant Thornton's Oracle practice addresses a broad range of process, technology, and organizational needs across finance, supply chain and HR functions. From strategy, through design, implementation, upgrades and managed services, Grant Thornton help our clients improve business performance with Oracle’s leading-edge technology.
