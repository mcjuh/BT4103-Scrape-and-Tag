### Expertise
Brett Swanson is an accountant with almost 20 years of experience in the construction industry. He has acted as an assistant expert on numerous occasions.
Brett has been engaged in the drafting of reports in preparation for trial regarding commercial valuations and delay. He has supported the named expert on commissions with disputes valued over US$1 billion.
Brett specializes in the analysis of construction contracts and their cost accumulation provisions to audit contractors’ costs. He has reviewed and analyzed labor, equipment, subcontractor, materials, and job site and home office overhead cost documentation, including job cost reports, invoices, timecards, change orders, bid documentation, and contractors’ financial statements.
### Background
Brett has extensive experience of performing contract cost audits and analyzing construction claims. This includes general contractor/construction manager (GC/CM) contracts with a guaranteed maximum price (GMP), GC/CM design-build contracts with a GMP, cost-reimbursable contracts, and firm fixed price (FFP) contracts, as well audits of requested changes.
Brett is a Certified Management Accountant and Certified Construction Auditor and holds a bachelor’s in business administration with a concentration in finance. He is a member of the Institute of Management Accountants and the National Association of Construction Auditors.
