### Expertise
Damien Beven is a Chartered Accountant with over 10 years of experience. He has acted as a forensic accounting expert.
Damien has provided expert testimony in the Federal Court of Australia, including giving concurrent expert evidence (“hot-tubbing”), and has also provided expert evidence before the NSW Civil and Administrative Tribunal in litigation matters. He has authored, co-authored, and assisted in the preparation of a wide range of expert reports relating to the quantification of financial loss, business valuation, and insolvency matters, on disputes with values of up to AUD$30 million.
Damien specialises in quantifying economic and financial loss regarding loss of profits and business interruption, particularly in insurance and commercial litigation matters. He provides expert opinion in insolvency matters, including assessing the date on which a company became insolvent.
### Background
Damien has led complex financial misconduct investigations and has extensive experience in forensic accounting and restructuring. He has acted for major insurers, law firms, banks, accountants, and federal government agencies.
Damien holds a degree in accounting and corporate governance and an advanced certification from the Australian Restructuring Insolvency and Turnaround Association.
