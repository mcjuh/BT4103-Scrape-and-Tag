#  Angie Gorman 
##  Managing Director 
## About Angie
Angie Gorman brings deep experience in managing organizational changes in a way that enables stakeholders to be productive during times of transition. Angie is a senior leader in FTI Consulting’s People & Transformation practice and the team’s primary subject matter expert in compliance. Partnering with colleagues and clients, she helps ensure leaders and employees understand the “what, why and how” of adopting new policies and procedures and enables culture evolution to minimize regulatory risk over the long term.
For more than 30 years, Angie has provided communications and change management counsel to clients across multiple industry sectors to help their employees navigate change during transactions and performance improvement initiatives. Additionally, she helps clients engage employees and evolve their cultures in support of business strategy, adopt new processes, recover following crises and enable leaders through learning and development programs. Her strategic planning and implementation work supports and enhances executive sponsorship, communications, leader alignment, resistance management and organizational change capabilities in companies of all sizes.
Angie’s experience includes 25 years in consulting roles and seven years leading corporate communications for Fortune 1000 companies. Prior to FTI Consulting, she served as senior director of associate communications at OfficeMax, where she played an integral role in merger communications to employees when the company was acquired by a competitor. Previously, she was the director of communications at Solo Cup Company, leading all internal and external communication for the organization as it implemented numerous performance improvement and growth initiatives. Additionally, she spent 10 years at Burson-Marsteller in Chicago, leading client business in the corporate communications practice, including the agency’s change management specialty group, and four years at Hill & Knowlton in Chicago.
