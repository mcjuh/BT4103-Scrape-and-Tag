#  Gene Reznik
Chief Strategy Officer and Global Head of Service Lines
Pronouns: He / Him
I’ve always been fascinated by how advanced technologies foster innovation and create step-changes in human performance. 
Trained as an electrical and computer engineer at Northwestern University, I spent the early stages of my career building global communications networks, which became the foundation of the modern internet. I then joined Accenture, where I worked and lived across the US, Europe, LATAM, and Asia, advancing to serve as the company’s Chief Strategy Officer. After Accenture, I held leadership positions at leading data and AI SaaS companies, applying advanced technologies to improve business performance. I am excited to be at Thoughtworks during this transformative time in the technology industry. Cloud, data, and AI are driving new software architectures, advanced approaches to building software, and many new players and ecosystems. Thoughtworks' extensive knowledge and pioneering spirit enable our company to guide clients through this disruptive time of change. 
I live in the San Francisco Bay Area with my wife and am a proud father of three sons.
