# Austin Gresham
Partner 
Austin Gresham is a Partner in Digital & Technology Transformation, bringing over 30 years of experience in Information Technology spanning from operations to strategy. In his current role he advises healthcare organizations the alignment of digital and technology strategies with clinical, operational, and enterprise priorities. Since joining Chartis in 2022, he has led engagements in digital strategy development, agile product management, and intelligent inpatient room design as part of the firm’s Hospital of the Future offering. His work emphasizes measurable impact, from improving patient acquisition and clinical outcomes to enhancing clinician satisfaction and reducing operational costs. Austin is recognized for his ability to translate complex technology portfolios into actionable roadmaps that drive sustainable transformation across health systems.
Prior to Chartis, Austin held senior leadership and advisory roles across several major technology firms, consistently bridging business strategy with digital innovation in healthcare and life sciences. At Dell Technologies, he partnered with global healthcare and life sciences clients to design multi-year transformation strategies, including cloud modernization and data management programs. As Field CTO at AHEAD and Principal at RoundTower Technologies, he helped organizations adopt AI/ML, IoT, and automation solutions, incubating new offerings and practices. There he also helped develop triage solutions for early COVID detection in ED's and Urgent Care, piloted an early hospital-at-home program, and led a team researching novel approaches to senior care in independent living aided by AI, ML, and IoT. Earlier in his career, Austin served in technical and consulting leadership roles, developing a reputation for architecting resilient, high-value enterprise systems. Austin's consulting experience dates back to 1999 when he founded a boutique consulting firm called Avail Technologies which he sold in 2008.
Austin holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Information Systems from DeVry Institute of Technology. He has held multiple technology certifications from AWS, Google, Cisco and others and has been recognized with multiple industry awards. Beyond his client work, Austin contributes thought leadership through speaking engagements and publications on digital transformation, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies in healthcare.

###  Why healthcare? 
Why healthcare? That’s easy! What industry, other than healthcare, literally affects every person on the planet? The difference between having access to healthcare or not means the difference in your quality of life, how long you live, and, by extension, the lives of everyone around you. Choosing healthcare means I am privileged to positively impact the lives of potentially billions of people. Using technology to advance access to care, the quality of care, and patient outcomes is the most rewarding career I can think of. I was blessed to be part of a team years ago that was involved in an early precision medicine pilot. When I got a glimpse into the future of what technology could do for healthcare, and how it could impact actual people, I was hooked! 
###  Do you have a motto or personal mantra? 
I don’t know if it’s a mantra or motto, but when presented with two options—one being the “safe,” “you got this” option and the other being the “scary,” “can I really do this?” option—I always take the latter. You can’t grow if you don’t stretch yourself! Sometimes you fail, but that’s where the best learning comes from. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
When I'm not working, you can find me in the woods somewhere, usually running or sometimes hiking. When busy calendars align with my family of five, you can also find us on the trail, but in Jeeps.
