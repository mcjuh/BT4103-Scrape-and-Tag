Chuck brings more than 30 years of strategic technology consulting experience to his role as a Senior Partner in West Monroe’s Technology & Experience work. As leader of the firm’s award-winning Chicago office, he guides our largest office and HQ – consistently recognized as one of the city’s top workplaces.
At West Monroe, Chuck has spearheaded IT, security, and cloud transformations for Fortune 1000 companies, delivering cost savings and strategic value. With a focus on leadership and collaboration, he builds high-performing teams and drives impactful results. Before joining West Monroe in 2016, Chuck was Vice President at Forsythe Solutions Group. He holds a degree from the University of Iowa.
