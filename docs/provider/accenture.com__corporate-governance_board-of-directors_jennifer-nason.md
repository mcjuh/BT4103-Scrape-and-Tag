# Jennifer Nason
About Jennifer
Jennifer Nason, a member of Accenture’s board of directors since February 2025, was Global Chair, Investment Banking at JPMorgan, a global financial services firm, from 2012 until her retirement in February 2025. In that role, Jennifer was responsible for many of the firm’s key investment banking relationships around the world.
Jennifer has a deep knowledge of banking and capital markets, technology and telecommunications from her nearly 40-year career at JPMorgan where she served as Global Head of Technology, Media and Telecommunications Investment Banking from 2004 to 2012. Prior to that Jennifer focused on Telecommunications Investment Banking as Managing Director from 1998 to 2004. She also established JPMorgan’s first IT Business Development Group, a partnership between Technology Banking and the company’s IT organization, which has helped shape its technology roadmap and facilitated multiple direct investments in technology companies. She began her career working for the Commonwealth of Australia as a Project Analyst in the Department of Management and Budget in Victoria.
Jennifer is a director of Rio Tinto plc and a trustee of Dodge & Cox Funds.
Jennifer holds a Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Science and Economic History as well as a Bachelor of Commerce in Economics – both from the University of Melbourne, Australia.
