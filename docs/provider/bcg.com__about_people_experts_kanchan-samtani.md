### Education
  * MBA, Indian Institute of Management, Calcutta


Kanchan Samtani founded and leads Boston Consulting Group's Principal Investors & Private Equity practice in India, and the Corporate Finance & Strategy practice across Asia Pacific. She is a member of BCG’s global Executive Committee.
Since joining the firm in 2001, Kanchan has worked in a number of industries. In particular, she partners with private equity funds, investors, and global organizations on supporting M&A, transactions, post-merger support, corporate finance, and in portfolio value creation.
