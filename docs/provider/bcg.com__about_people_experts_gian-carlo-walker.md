### Education
  * MBA, honors, Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania
  * BA, business administration, University of St. Gallen
  * BA, economics, University of St. Gallen


Gian-Carlo Walker is a core member of the Health Care practice at Boston Consulting Group. His work at BCG focuses on supply chain operations and transformation— primarily with biopharmaceutical and medical devices companies. Gian-Carlo has experience in digital supply chain and advanced analytics for biopharma operations and contract development and manufacturing organizations (CDMOs), supply chain planning, operating model design, strategic collaborations, and other topics. He has also led due diligence and post-merger integration planning engagements.
Prior to joining BCG, Gian-Carlo worked in investment banking at Credit Suisse in Zurich.
