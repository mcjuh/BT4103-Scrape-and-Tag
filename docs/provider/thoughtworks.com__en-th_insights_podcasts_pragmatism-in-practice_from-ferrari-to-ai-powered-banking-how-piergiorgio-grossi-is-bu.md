## Brief summary
Alla Gancz joins Piergiorgio Grossi, Chief Innovation & Data Officer at Credem, to discuss his extraordinary career spanning Ferrari Formula 1, data and AI ventures, Ducati and banking. Piergiorgio explains what high-performance engineering can teach banks about data, feedback loops and decision-making; how Credem’s AINEXT model moves AI from experimentation into business execution; and why successful transformation depends as much on people, culture and trust as it does on technology. The discussion explores Credem’s adoption of AI, and the challenge of giving teams greater speed and autonomy while maintaining the control, resilience and accountability required of a bank.
