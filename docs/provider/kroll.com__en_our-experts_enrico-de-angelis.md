AT A GLANCE 
Enrico De Angelis is a managing director in the Milan Office and leads Kroll Transfer Pricing practice in Italy. Enrico has more than 18 years of specialized advisory experience in assisting multinationals of all sizes with their transfer pricing and custom valuation policies.
Prior to joining Kroll, Enrico served as a managing director for Quantera Global with responsibility for the Italian market. Before that, he was a partner at a boutique transfer pricing firm in Zurich and Milan. Enrico started his career with McKinsey & Company, followed by serving as a senior associate at a Big 4 accounting firm.
Enrico is a frequent speaker at national and international transfer pricing conferences and seminars and has authored a number of articles in specialized magazines and book chapters dealing with transfer pricing and custom valuation.
Enrico holds a Ph.D. in economics from IMT School of Advanced Studies, an M.A. in international economics from the World Trade Institute and a B.A. in economics from LUISS University.
