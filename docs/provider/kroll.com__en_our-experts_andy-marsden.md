AT A GLANCE 
Andy Marsden is Managing Director and Global Co-Leader of Kroll's Decision Intelligence practice, specializing in business modelling and analytics.
He leverages more than 16 years of modelling experience, assisting clients across their full spectrum of modelling and data analytics needs – deals, raising of finance, bid support, financial planning, valuation and at-scale investment portfolio solutions. His clients include sovereign wealth funds, major global alternatives investors, corporates and financial institutions.
Prior to joining the firm, Andy spent 20 years with Ernst & Young where he was an associate partner in the Strategy and Transactions practice, leading the Financial Services Business Modelling team in London. During this time, he worked with banking clients across Europe with significant exposure to bank restructuring and insolvency, performing and non-performing loan transactions and regulatory driven change.
Andy has developed tools to support and streamline financial planning and reporting as well as specialized models to inform restructuring and liquidation, facilitate the raising of debt and equity and to support the business case for new products, businesses, operating model design or other strategic decisions.
Andy’s expertise includes providing model assurance to providers of finance and other stakeholders, securitization models to support the design and issuance of asset backed securities, and developing systems to support regulatory reporting (e.g., stress-testing or valuation in resolution).
Andy holds a Master’s Degree in Mathematics and is a Fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales.
