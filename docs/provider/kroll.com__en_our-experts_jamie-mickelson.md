AT A GLANCE 
Jamie Mickelson is Managing Director in the Investigations, Diligence and Compliance practice, based in Mexico City.
Jamie is an attorney with nearly two decades of experience in managing complex investigations.
She has experience working with banks, financial intelligence units, public prosecutors and other government agencies across the U.S. and Latin America.
Before joining Kroll, Jamie spent nearly fifteen years with the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) as a federal prosecutor. She investigated and litigated complex financial crimes, including fraud, corruption and money laundering cases. Jamie has worked in Latin America for nearly a decade. She served as the DOJ Attaché in Bogota, Colombia, where she coordinated international investigations and extraditions from Colombia to the United States and represented DOJ in the bilateral law enforcement relationship. She also served as a DOJ Legal Advisor at the U.S. Embassies in Guatemala and Mexico, where she advised public prosecutors and other government entities on best practices in complex investigations and devised and delivered training programs for judges, prosecutors, analysts and investigators. Jamie began her career as a litigation associate with Latham & Watkins, LLP, in Chicago, Illinois.
Jamie holds a J.D. from the College of William and Mary and a B.A. from James Madison University. She is a licensed attorney in Illinois and Georgia and is fluent in Spanish.
