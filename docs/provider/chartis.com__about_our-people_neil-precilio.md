# Neil Precilio
Partner, HealthScape Advisors 
Neil Precilio is a Partner at HealthScape Advisors, a Chartis Company. He has over 12 years of diverse consulting experience providing both strategy and execution support to help clients grow, perform, and transform across multiple industries.
Neil has led a variety of projects for healthcare clients; including health plans, large employer/retiree groups, specialty healthcare providers, and healthcare investors. His consulting experience has included strategy development, operating model design, and corporate advisory for mergers and acquisitions.
Neil is dedicated to helping clients execute strategic, operational, and analytical engagements, with focus on health plan operating model transformation, go-to-market strategy, member engagement / advocacy, integrated care models, product strategy, behavioral health innovation, transaction advisory, and vendor procurement management. 
Neil co-leads HealthScape's Medicare Center of Excellence and has led numerous engagements helping plans grow, perform, and transform in the Medicare Advantage market. This work includes market entry, partnership model development, and product portfolio strategy. Additionally, Neil actively supports the firm’s Medicare thought leadership.
Prior to joining HealthScape, Neil worked in Crowe’s Financial Services Consulting practice where he focused on program implementation and management for clients in the banking industry.
Neil earned his bachelor’s degree in Financial Planning from Purdue University.

###  What has been your favorite project at HealthScape and why? 
My favorite project at HealthScape was helping a health plan and its provider partners design a future-state operating and partnership model with aligned incentives to better serve individuals with acute physical and behavioral health needs. It was especially meaningful to support collaboration that drove improved outcomes and care experiences within the client’s local community. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
When I’m not working, you’ll likely find me on a walk with my wife, two kids and our dog, watching a Purdue basketball game, or squeezing in a round of golf (and subsequently searching for my ball) when I can. 
###  Where’s your favorite place to visit and why? 
My favorite place to visit is Positano, where my wife and I got engaged. The people are great, the food is delicious, and the views are incredible.
