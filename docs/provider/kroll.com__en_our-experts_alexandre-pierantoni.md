AT A GLANCE 
Alexandre Pierantoni is a Managing Director and Head of Latin America and Brazil Investment Banking practice of Kroll, based in the Sao Paulo/ Brazil office. Alexandre has more than 35 years of corporate finance experience, with particular expertise in consumer, industrial, IT, healthcare and educational sectors.
Alexandre has a strong M&A background in both origination and execution of buy and sell side mandates for financial and strategic investors, as well as economic valuations and strategic analysis for corporate development. His international experience is grounded by living and working experiences in Latin America, the U.S. and Europe.
Responsible for the origination and execution of several acquisition and sale transactions in Brazil and Latin America, Alexandre has represented large national and multinational companies, as well as financial investors in buy and sell side mandates such as Piemonte Holding/ Elea Digital, Apax Partners/ Ericsson, Carlyle, Vinci Partners, Gualapack Group, Viajanet (investment portfolio Company of Monashees and Redpoint), Bacardi-Martini, Grupo JD/ Labrunier, Petrobrás, CCL Industries, Arlington Group, Damovo Group, Diebold, Grupo Multicoisas, Graffo Embalagens, Coremal Industries, Faculdades Metropolitanas Unidas/ FMU, Centro Universitário Brasilia/ CEUB, Universidade Tiradentes/ UNIT, Mars, Sonoco, NEC, Rakuten, Boasso Global, Ploeger-Oxbo, Yamaha Motor da Amazônia, Maximatech and Unilever.
Alexandre started his working career in 1992 at PricewaterhouseCoopers M&A practice, where he became a partner in 2007. From 2014 to 2017 he was a partner in a local M&A boutique firm, joining Kroll in 2017. 
Alexandre holds a degree in business administration from Germany and a degree in economics in Brazil, where he also holds an MBA with a specialization in Finance from Fundação Getulio Vargas (FGV-EAESP). He has also participated in executive programs at Northwestern University/Kellogg School of Management, New York Institute of Finance, MIT - Massachusetts Institute of Technology and FGV - Fundação Getúlio Vargas. Alexandre is a Board Member of the Brazilian-American Chamber of Commerce, where he also chairs the Investment & Trade Committee. He has been a volunteer member of ENDEAVOR in Brazil since 2001.
Alexandre is fluent in English and German, in addition to his native language, Portuguese.
