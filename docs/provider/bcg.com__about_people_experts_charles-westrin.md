### Education
  * BBA, University of Michigan, Ross School of Business


Charles Westrin is managing director of BCG U, a specialty business within Boston Consulting Group that provides transformative upskilling offerings and capability building to clients to unlock organizational impact at speed and scale, and with a measurable ROI.
Charles has in-depth strategic, product, and business-building expertise from a career in online edtech, having held leadership roles across higher education, global tech platforms, and content providers.
Prior to BCG, he led Learning Product Innovation at 2U/edX, launching new business models and learning products that reached over 80 million learners worldwide. Charles has also led global education businesses and driven large-scale digital transformation efforts at some of the largest education companies in the world.
