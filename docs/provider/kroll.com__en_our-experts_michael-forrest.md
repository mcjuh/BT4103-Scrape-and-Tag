AT A GLANCE 
Michael Forrest is a Director in the Settlement Administration practice, based in St. Louis, Missouri.
Michael brings more than 15 years of experience in the class action industry, including as a class action attorney. He brings valuable understanding into the complexities of modern litigation resolution, enabling him to effectively handle client needs. Michael consults with attorneys to develop successful administrations on a wide range of complex legal matters.
Prior to joining Kroll Michael worked as a vice president of business development at a settlement administrator. In his previous work as a class action attorney, he represented clients in Senne v. Office of the Commissioner of Baseball and the National Credit Union Administration RMBS Litigation.
Michael has a J.D. from Saint Louis University School of Law, and a B.A. from Loyola University Chicago.
