# Matthew Marchingo
Managing Director & Partner [ Houston ](https://www.bcg.com/offices/houston/default)


### Education
  * Master of Management, Monash University
  * BA, multimedia business and computing, Monash University


A core member of Boston Consulting Group's People & Organization practice, Matt Marchingo specializes in next-generation support functions. He focuses on the intersection of digital, process, and technology to deliver transformed service operations in all support functions.
Matt is an expert in organizational transformations, digital support functions, and E2E service management including order to cash (O2C) and record to report (R2R). He works with clients in the consumer, retail, manufacturing, industrial goods, technology and telecommunications, and energy industries.
Matt's recent projects have covered operational turnaround, supply chain, product strategy development, digital commerce, market segmentation, operating model design, org design, labor systems, economic regulation, and post-merger integration.
Before joining BCG, Matt was a project consultant at Booz & Company leading the Operating Model and Organizational Design platforms for the Asia Pacific and Middle East.
