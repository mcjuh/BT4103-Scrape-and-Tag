AT A GLANCE 
Jesus Garza is a Vice President in the Operational Advisory Services practice based in the New York Office. Jesus has over 9 years of experience in management consulting and operations management spanning product and service businesses in industries like manufacturing, supply chain, software, retail, logistics, telecommunications, banking and insurance.
He specializes in operations improvement and cost reduction analysis through the application of Lean Six Sigma methodologies and a thorough understanding of data mining and business analytics. Jesus is deeply knowledgeable in manufacturing, operations, supply chain, statistical modeling & analysis and IT / Ops architecture, including SDLC management & execution.
Jesus has extensive experience in a wide range of projects, including process design & reengineering, Lean Six Sigma, shop floor cost reduction and process optimization, operations and IT due diligence, financial & operational modeling, ERP and CRM configuration and implementation, software deployment, business intelligence dashboard design, and machine learning implementation.
Jesus previously worked as a management consultant for Deloitte in their Strategy and Operations practice, where besides delivering projects, was part of the Data Science and Analytics internal taskforce, leading internal training sessions and delivering proposals & projects.
Jesus earned a Bachelor of Science in Industrial Engineering with a minor in systems at Tecnologico de Monterrey in Mexico and Master of Science from New York University in Industrial Engineering with a focus on data science.
