Kainat Faheem Abbasi is a Consultant in our Health Practice, where she contributes to a range of health sector projects. With a Bachelor's in Dental Surgery from Army Medical College Rawalpindi and a Master's in Public Health from King's College London, Kainat brings a blend of clinical and public health expertise to her role. Over the years, she has contributed to a range of health projects in Pakistan, with a particular focus on digital health innovations and immunization programs. Her work spans from supporting on-the-ground health interventions to developing strategic approaches that address gaps in health systems.

The project aims to enhance immunisation training in Pakistan by standardising content, developing a competency-based curriculum, and introducing an interactive Learning Management System (LMS) to improve health worker capacity, data quality, and vaccine uptake, to strengthen immunisation coverage and disease surveillance.
