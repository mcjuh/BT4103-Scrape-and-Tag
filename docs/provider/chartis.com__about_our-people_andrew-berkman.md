# Andrew Berkman
Associate Partner 
Andrew Berkman is an Associate Partner at HealthScape Advisors, a Chartis company. Andrew has 10 years of healthcare consulting experience providing strategic and execution leadership and support for transformational initiatives. His work focused on regional health plan strategy and planning, ancillary strategy and execution, business transformation, product design and implementation, provider partnerships, value-based care, and medical cost reduction.​ 
Andrew managed several complex programs for health plans including a regional health plan’s enterprise transformation focused on member engagement, clinical and community partnerships, operational excellence and care models, and facilitating a regional health plan and provider collaboration focused on launching a tiered network product, increasing primary care engagement and directing care to high quality and lower cost providers. ​ 
Andrew’s recent ancillary experience includes a multiple Commercial dental strategy and execution efforts to drive growth and profitability and post-merger integration support for Commercial dental plans.​ 
Prior to joining HealthScape, Andrew was a healthcare consultant for PwC. Andrew graduated from Boston University with a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration and a concentration in Finance.
