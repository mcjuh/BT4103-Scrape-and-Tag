Mark Wyatt is a Managing Director and Head of the Alvarez Marsal Corporate Finance practice in Paris. He specializes in mid-market mergers and acquisitions for owner managed businesses, private equity, and large corporates, particularly within the healthcare and sports and leisure sectors.
With over 36 years of experience, Mr. Wyatt brings a rich multicultural background and deep expertise in multiple service lines, including audit, transaction services, valuations, and corporate finance. His comprehensive technical knowledge equips him to create significant value for clients throughout the M&A process. Having advised on over a hundred transactions, he excels in navigating complex deal dynamics, adeptly resolving conflicts to drive successful outcomes and create lasting value for clients.
Mr. Wyatt has advised a wide array of clients, overseeing financial transactions that encompass company disposals, acquisitions, mergers, and IPOs for major corporates, medium-sized owner managed businesses, and private equity firms. His extensive sector experience includes financial services, real estate, consumer markets, technology, and transport and logistics. Mr. Wyatt also served as a Finance Director for a bank for two years, further enhancing his financial acumen.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Wyatt worked with KPMG in London, Hong Kong, and Paris. For the past 18 years, he led the corporate finance business in France, guiding the practice through a period of exceptional growth and diversification.
Mr. Wyatt earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics from King’s College London and is a Statutory Auditor accredited by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of England and Wales (ICAEW).
