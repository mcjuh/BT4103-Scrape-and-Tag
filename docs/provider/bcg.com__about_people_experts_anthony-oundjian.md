### Education
  * MBA, INSEAD—Singapore and France
  * MSc, Ecole Centrale Paris


Anthony Oundjian started his consulting career in 1999 and joined the Brussels office of Boston Consulting Group in 2004. He subsequently moved to Southeast Asia in 2008 and opened the Manila office in 2017. Today he supports clients in telecommunications, conglomerates, multilateral development institutions, and financial institutions on a variety of topics including strategy, payments, digital businesses, and digital transformations.
Anthony previously led BCG’s Technology, Media, & Telecommunications practice in Southeast Asia and has served fixed and mobile telecom operators across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia. Among other topics, his extensive telecom experience has covered growth strategy, M&A, network sharing, efficiency transformation, customer experience, and pricing.
Anthony is BCG’s Asia-Pacific Chair for Alumni & Career Services, and Senior Advisors. Prior to consulting, Anthony worked for Electricite de France–first based in Paris and then in Montreal.
