### Education
  * MBA, ESSEC Paris (Ecole Supérieure des Sciences Economiques et Commerciales)


Benjamin Entraygues leads Boston Consulting Group's Principal Investors and Private Equity practice area globally. Joining BCG in 2017, he has deep expertise in the industrial goods, health care, and retail sectors. 
A consultant since 2000, Benjamin brings his in-depth experience leading cases for private equity funds and corporations, primarily on mid-cap and large-cap assets, at every stage of the investment value chain. He has worked with companies across a wide range of industries. 
Benjamin was previously with Roland Berger GmbH, acting as Senior Partner in the Paris team of the Global Investor Support Group. He has led major cases in venture due diligence, carve outs, and leveraged buyouts.
