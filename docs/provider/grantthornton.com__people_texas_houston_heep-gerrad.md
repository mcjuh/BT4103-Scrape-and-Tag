## Executive summary
Gerrad Heep has more than 14 years of experience serving private and publicly traded corporations and partnerships in the energy industry, including natural gas pipelines and storage, exploration and production, trading and marketing, chemical manufacturing and refining, oil field services, and seismic acquisition. Heep has advised clients on equity and debt offerings for publicly traded energy companies, and due diligence projects relating to various M&As within the energy industry.
