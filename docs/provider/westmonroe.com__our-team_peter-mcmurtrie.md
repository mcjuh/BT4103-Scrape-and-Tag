Peter brings over three decades of experience guiding organizations through complex transformations that optimize performance and drive tangible results. Recently, he helped a national commercial lines carrier implement process and data initiatives that reduced expenses by $200 million over three years. Peter’s expertise spans sales, marketing, claims, and customer interactions, with extensive experience working with C-suites and boards of directors. 
Before joining West Monroe, Peter was president of Property & Casualty Commercial Insurance at Nationwide, where he oversaw key national broker relationships. He holds a bachelor’s degree from the University of South Florida and has completed executive education programs at Wharton.
