# Aman Sabharwal, MD 
Managing Partner 
Aman Sabharwal, MD, is a Managing Partner at Chartis. Aman has over 20 years of healthcare experience and is a board-certified internal medicine hospitalist. His areas of expertise and professional skills include optimizing clinical value analysis programs, hospitalist programs (and other physician practices), care coordination services, clinical documentation improvement services, clinical quality and utilization, value-based care, high reliability, and hospital and health system transformational consulting services. 
Before joining Chartis, Aman spent nearly 14 years at Vizient and most recently served as the practice leader for Clinical Consulting, where he led complete hospital transformation engagements. Prior to that, he served as the Chief Utilization Officer at Jackson Health System in Miami, Florida. 
Aman has authored and been featured in numerous articles and webcasts, including: “Life as a nocturnist”; “New roles in bed utilization”; “Cardiac telemetry guidelines improve bed utilization and resources”; “Make the most of staffing to minimize weekend effect”; and “The future of clinical documentation improvement: data and analytics will drive CDI and support population health management.” 
Aman holds a Bachelor of Liberal Arts and Doctor of Medicine degrees from the University of Missouri, and a Master of Health Administration from the University of North Carolina – Chapel Hill.

###  Why healthcare? 
Ever since I was a child, I wanted to be a doctor. I have always had a passion for science and pathophysiology. As I pursued my passion, I realized how much I enjoyed impacting a patients journey from the ER to the inpatient setting and then back to home. During my career, the administrative and operational aspects of healthcare grew on me, to where I was able to impact multiple patient outcomes simultaneously by employing both clinical and process improvement skills. This brought me to consulting, where my passion has been improving overall health system operations and quality. 
###  Why did you pick Chartis? 
I chose to grow my career at Chartis because the people and culture are collegial and collaborative. The mission here also fits with my ideals: Believe in better. As a healthcare professional, not only do we need to do better, but we can be/do better, to ultimately improve patient outcomes.

The one thing I can’t live without is my morning soy latte. I am definitely a morning person and am up early, however, I have to have at least 2-3 shots of espresso in my latte every morning.
