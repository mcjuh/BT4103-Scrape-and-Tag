### Education
  * MA, economics and government, Maastricht University


Jochen Schönfelder leads Boston Consulting Group’s Transform practice in Central Europe. BCG Transform helps clients deliver rapid, visible performance improvements in the short term while strengthening their organizations and positioning them to win in the future. Jochen has supported multiple large corporations and mid-cap clients in distressed situations and successfully helped them to restructure both their strategic and structural setup as well as their balance sheet. He is also a member of the Industrial Goods, Consumer, and Private Equity practices.
Since joining BCG in 2014, Jochen’s client work has focused on the automotive, consumer goods/fashion, engineered products, and renewable energy industries. He has acquired considerable experience in cost management strategy and restructuring and turnaround management.
Prior to joining BCG, Jochen was a partner at Roland Berger.
