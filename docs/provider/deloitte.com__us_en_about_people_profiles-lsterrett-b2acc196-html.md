#  Lynne Sterrett 
##  Vice Chair | US Clients & Industries Leader 
Leveraging her 25 years of consulting experience, Lynne Sterrett, as Clients, Industries, and Insights leader, is responsible for key elements of how Deloitte goes to market. Passionate about delivering impact for clients, she helps deliver an integrated client support model, innovative solutions across industries and sectors, and the latest insights, research, and trends produced by Deloitte’s thought leadership publishing operation. In addition, through her previous experience as the National Life Sciences and Health Care Consulting leader, Sterrett sees creativity, technology, and innovation as fundamental to solving the most pressing problems in business.
