Ronald Maalouf is a core member of the Principal Investors & Private Equity (PIPE) practice at Boston Consulting Group. His clients are located primarily in the Middle East.
Ronald has worked with leading sovereign wealth funds, investment funds, and family-owned investment groups on all aspects of fund investment strategy and operations, transaction support (due diligences), and portfolio value creation, including business plans, innovation, digital, and transformations.
Before joining the firm, Ronald worked as a senior associate of financial services at Booz & Company, and as a manager of advisory services at PriceWaterhouseCoopers.
Ronald is a CFA and a CPA certificate holder. He speaks fluent Arabic, English, and French.
