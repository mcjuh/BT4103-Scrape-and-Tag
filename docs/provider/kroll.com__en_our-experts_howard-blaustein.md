AT A GLANCE 
Howard Blaustein is a managing director of the Restructuring Administration practice, based in New York. Howard, who has more than 30 years of experience in the corporate restructuring industry, joined Kroll in 2019 through the firm’s acquisition of Prime Clerk, a business he co-founded.
Howard started his legal career at Weil, Gotshal & Manges and after Weil, he worked at Mayer Brown and O’Melveny & Myers, continuing his focus on corporate finance and restructuring. During his legal career, Howard worked on dozens of large restructurings, including Eastern Airlines, Inc. and R.H. Macy & Co.
From 2004 to 2013, Howard worked at KCC, a claims and noticing agent, as Director of Corporate Restructuring Services.
Howard graduated from Vassar College and holds a J.D. from New York Law School.
