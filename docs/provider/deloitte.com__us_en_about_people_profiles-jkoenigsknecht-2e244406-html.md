#  Jack Koenigsknecht 
##  Energy, Resources & Industrials Industry Leader | Merger & Acquisition Services | Partner | Deloitte & Touche LLP 
With more than 20 years of experience, Jack is the US Automotive Industry and Aerospace & Defense Industry leader for M&A Transaction Services at Deloitte & Touche LLP. Jack specializes in buy-side and sell-side due diligence, the preparation of carve-out financial statements, and the application of purchase accounting. Jack has delivered hundreds of successful M&A transaction projects for buyers and sellers across the industrial products space globally.
