### Education
  * Post-doctoral fellowship, Embryology, Carnegie Institute of Washington
  * PhD, Biochemistry, University of Utah, School of Medicine
  * BS, Biological Sciences, University and research honors, Carnegie Mellon University


Becky Frederick is a core member of the Tech & Digital Advantage and Technology, Media & Telecommunications practices at Boston Consulting Group. She helps clients in the telecommunications, health care, technology, and financial services industries build their digital and modern technology capabilities.
She has led large-scale AI and digital transformations, helping clients shift how they build and deliver technology, modernize legacy landscapes, and unlock value with AI. She has a passion for driving true changes with clients -- activating human-AI collaboration to reshape technology capabilities. Becky is a passionate leader and advocate for women in digital and tech.
Before joining BCG in 2013, Becky was an academic scientist and science policy fellow.
