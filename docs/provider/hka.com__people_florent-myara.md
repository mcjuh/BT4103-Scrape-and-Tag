### Expertise
Florent Myara is a financial expert with 20 years of experience. He has been involved in nearly 40 business valuation and economic damages expert matters, whether appointed as expert or assisting the named expert.
Florent has testified on financial analysis and valuation matters and has acted as a senior member of expert teams in litigation and international arbitration. This has included ICSID and ICC proceedings, French commercial, administrative and criminal proceedings, AMF-related matters, and court-ordered expert investigations. He has provided case assessments, reviews of opposing expert analyses, financial modelling, preparation of expert evidence, and coordination of analytical and drafting work on disputes with values approaching $500 million.
Florent’s expert work includes business and share valuation, damages quantification, lost profits analysis, event studies, share-price impact analysis, valuation of complex financial instruments, and counterfactual financial modelling. He has expertise in post-M&A issues, shareholder disputes, capital markets, banking, regulatory matters, financial restructurings, and option-like instruments.
### Background
Florent has advised clients in banking, construction, real estate, telecoms, media, sports, renewable-energy and restructuring-related disputes. He is a former lecturer at Paris Dauphine University, teaching corporate finance and financial engineering at master’s level.
Florent holds a bachelor’s degree in management and a master’s in management, with a specialisation in finance, from HEC Paris. He is a member of the French Society of Valuation Experts and is fluent in English and French.
