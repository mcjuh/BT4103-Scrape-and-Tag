John Suckow is a Senior Advisor with Alvarez & Marsal in New York. 
At A&M, Mr. Suckow served as Managing Director from 2002 through 2022, holding various positions including A&M’s Chief Operating Officer, CEO of Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc., and Chief Restructuring Officer of Interstate Bakeries Corp.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Suckow worked with Arthur Andersen, having most recently served as the leader of Andersen’s U.S. Restructuring practice.
Mr. Suckow earned a bachelor's degree in accountancy from Miami University (Oxford).
