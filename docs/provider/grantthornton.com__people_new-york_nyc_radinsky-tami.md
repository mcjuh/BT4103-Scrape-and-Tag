## Executive summary
Tami Radinsky is an audit partner in Grant Thornton’s New York office. Tami brings 20 years of experience in the healthcare, higher education, and not-for-profit industries, working with clients as part of both external and internal audit practices.
Tami’s broad-based experience in accounting, auditing, and business advisory services includes helping clients in the areas of risk assessments, operational improvement, internal control reviews, internal audit projects, and regulatory compliance. She has worked with hospital systems, complex universities, academic medical centers, foundations, and not-for-profit organizations.
Prior to joining Grant Thornton, Tami spent 16 years at PwC in numerous roles, including an accounting oversight role providing client teams with guidance to resolve complex accounting issues in a variety of industries.
She is a lead instructor for healthcare and educational not-for-profit courses and presents on healthcare technical matters. Tami leverages deep technical and industry expertise to add value to clients that produce immediate and tangible results.
When she is not assisting clients and engagement teams, Tami enjoys spending time with her husband and four children as well as giving to the community through volunteer efforts with various local schools and charities.
