## Erica Greulich, PhD

[+1 415 975 3227](tel:+1%20415%20975%203227) egreulich@secretariat-intl.com
Erica Greulich specializes in empirical microeconomics and quantitative analysis, including the estimation of damages and lost profits. She has testified in Federal and state courts on issues relating to class certification, damages, labor market conditions and lost earnings. She has more than 20 years of experience developing econometric models, assessing economic injury, estimating damages and conducting proactive audits, and her consulting engagements frequently concern class action, breach of contract, employment, and antitrust matters.
Prior to joining Secretariat, Dr. Greulich’s research and experience focused on the nexus between regulation, housing markets, and wage and employment outcomes. Her research has been published in the  _Brookings-Wharton Papers on Urban Affairs_ and _Regional Science and Urban Economics_.
While at UC Berkeley, Dr. Greulich taught courses in microeconomics and game theory.
