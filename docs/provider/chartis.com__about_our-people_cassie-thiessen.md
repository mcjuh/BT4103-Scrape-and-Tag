# Cassie Thiessen
Partner and Vice President of Talent Acquisition 
Cassie Thiessen is a Partner and Vice President of Talent Acquisition at Chartis. She leads the firm’s Talent Acquisition function, responsible for attracting and hiring talent across the organization and supporting the firm’s continued growth at scale. Cassie has nearly 20 years of recruiting experience spanning agency and corporate environments, with a focus on healthcare IT, healthcare consulting, and executive recruitment.
Cassie is recognized for her expertise in executive recruitment, talent acquisition operations, employer branding, and recruiter development. She has played a key role in supporting the firm’s unprecedented hiring growth by implementing scalable recruiting strategies, building high-performing recruiting teams, and driving operational excellence across the talent acquisition function. Known for her authentic leadership style, she partners closely with firm leadership to align recruiting efforts with organizational priorities and growth objectives.
Prior to joining Chartis, Cassie served in talent acquisition roles at Impact Advisors. Earlier in her career, she worked in Healthcare IT agency recruiting.
Cassie holds a Bachelor of Science in Economics and a Bachelor of Arts in Spanish Language from the University of Idaho.

###  Why healthcare? 
I’ve been the primary caregiver for two of my immediate family members who went through breast and head & neck cancers. We all need healthcare and based on my experiences, if we can make accessing, navigating, and receiving healthcare easier, particularly in times of acute disease, we’ll all be better for it. 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
Jobs truly change lives. We all spend a lot of time at work, so when we make great hires that love their work, love the firm, and achieve their own career goals, that’s very rewarding. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
Driving sketchy forest service roads while listening to Neil Young.
