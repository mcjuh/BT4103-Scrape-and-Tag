## Career Summary
[Curriculum Vitae](https://www.brookesbell.com/fileadmin/uploads/brookesbell/CVs/SCALES_CV.pdf)
Adrian Scales is a Chartered Master Mariner (CMMar) with extensive maritime expertise. He joined Brookes Bell as a Marine Consultant in January 2007, following his tenure as Master with P&O Ferries (Irish Sea) Ltd. Adrian was appointed Director of Nautical in 2021 and subsequently Director of Europe in 2024.
With significant ship-handling experience across a wide range of vessel types, Adrian also obtained pilotage exemption certificates for ports regularly visited during his time with P&O. Over an 18-year seagoing career, he served on a number of vessels including reefer/container ships, general cargo carriers, freight ferries, Ro-Pax ferries, and High-Speed Passenger Craft (HSC).
## Background
Adrian has carried out a variety of surveys and opinion-based work since joining Brookes Bell; these are listed at the end of this CV. His particular specialisations are in the areas of salvage and wreck removal; ship handling; ro-ro vessels and cargoes; high-speed craft; refrigerated vessels and cargoes; stowage and securing. Adrian also specialises in the assessment of crew actions following emergency incidents, especially fire, with respect to compliance with training and familiarisation on board Ro-Ro and general cargo vessels. 
He has also been extensively involved in the investigation, assessment and overseeing of a number of recent salvage related/grounding incidents and wreck removals, including: GOODFAITH (2015), SITEAM ANJA (SCR - 2016), NANCY GLEN (2018), PRISCILLA (2018), NEARCHOS (SCR - 2018), YANTIAN EXPRESS (2019), HABIB EXPRESS (SCR - 2019), ZELEK STAR (SCR – 2020), STELLAR BANNER (SCR - 2020), KAAMI (2020), ZEBU (2021), NAZMIYE ANA (2021), RIVER THAMES (2021), EUROFERRY OLYMPIA (SCR - 2022) and FREMANTLE HIGHWAY (SCR – 2023).
In November 2015, Adrian was appointed to the Lloyd's Salvage Arbitration Branch SCR Panel as Special Casualty Representative (SCR). Adrian has provided expert reports for, and given expert evidence in, high court, arbitration and mediation proceedings in the UK.
Adrian was awarded Chartered Master Mariner status in November 2017.
