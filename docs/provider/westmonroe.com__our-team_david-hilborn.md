David helps clients align their workforce strategies with business goals, whether through navigating the cultural impacts of M&A or transforming HR functions with cutting-edge technology. 
As the Organization, People & Change lead, David brings extensive expertise in organizational design, HR operations, and workforce transformation. He has a proven track record advising global and domestic organizations across industries and sizes. David joined West Monroe after 27 years at Mercer, where he held senior leadership roles and guided some of the firm’s largest clients through complex human capital challenges. 
## _David's All-In Mindset_
David is an amateur military historian and co-authored The Fighting 30th Division. He’s also an active volunteer, serving as CEO Performance Committee chair for the Institute for Study Abroad and as a former board chairman for the March of Dimes.
