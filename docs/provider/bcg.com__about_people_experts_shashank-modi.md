### Education
  * MBA, Kenan-Flagler Business School, University of North Carolina/IESE Business School
  * BTech, mechanical engineering, Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology


Shashank Modi is a core member of Boston Consulting Group’s Technology, Media & Telecommunications; Technology & Digital Advantage; and Global Advantage practices. He recently moved from BCG’s Atlanta office to New York. He has also previously been part of the London office. Shashank works mostly with telecom and technology clients on digital, operations, network, and strategy topics. He is the North America topic lead for digital ecosystems.
Shashank is currently co-lead for the North America Asian Diversity Network (ADN), and formerly served as the Atlanta ADN node for several years. He is passionate about the mission of the ADN, and within that group, has a strong track record of serving people and driving impact.
