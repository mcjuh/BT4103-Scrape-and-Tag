## Darcy Dague
##### Managing Director – Insurance, North America
Darcy Dague is a managing director in Accenture’s Insurance practice. She has more than 17 years of experience working in insurance, with the past 12 focused on claims in North America. Dague brings a wealth of experience in claims strategy, software, delivery and business process design for large property and casualty (P&C) carriers, as well as mid-market clients. She holds a bachelor’s degree in computer science from Western Illinois University.
