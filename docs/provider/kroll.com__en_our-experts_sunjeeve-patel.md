# Sunjeeve D. Patel
##### Managing Director and Head of Issuer Services
[Sunjeeve D. Patel](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sunjeevepatel)[+ 44 (0)20 7704 0880](tel:+%2044%20\(0\)20%207704%200880)Sunjeeve.patel@kroll.com
Sunjeeve Patel is a managing director in the [Issuer Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/issuer-services) practice, based in London. Sunjeeve has more than 20 years of experience in the finance sector.
Sunjeeve founded Lucid Issuer Services Limited (now [Kroll Issuer Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/issuer-services)) in 2004, using his industry expertise to build an organization with a focus on service, technical expertise, transparent processes and integrity. Sunjeeve was responsible for many of the significant transactions that have made Issuer Services a market-leading practice.
Prior to joining Kroll, Sunjeeve worked at The Bank of New York and The First National Bank of Chicago NBD.
Sunjeeve is also the co-founder of the [Agency and Trustee Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/agency-and-trustee-services) practice (formerly known as Lucid Agency and Trustee Services), which specializes in loan and bond agency services across multiple markets.
Sunjeeve studied law at the University of Nottingham and completed the legal practice course at Nottingham Law School.
