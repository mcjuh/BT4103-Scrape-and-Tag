AT A GLANCE 
Masha Lewis is Managing Director, APAC Regional Leader in the Fixed Asset Advisory Services practice, based in Melbourne, Australia.
Masha leverages more than 18 years of asset valuation and advisory experience in assisting capital-intensive clients with insurance, mergers & acquisition, financial and tax reporting (purchase price allocations), asset impairments, leasing studies, due diligence and asset audits, asset register reconciliation, reconstruction and componentization and asset management.
Prior to joining Kroll, Masha served as Partner at PwC Australia. Before that, she worked at PwC U.S., servicing clients globally.
Masha holds a bachelor’s degree in finance and international business from New York University – Stern School of Business. She is also a Chartered Member (MRICS) of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors and an Accredited Senior Appraiser of the American Society of Appraisers.
