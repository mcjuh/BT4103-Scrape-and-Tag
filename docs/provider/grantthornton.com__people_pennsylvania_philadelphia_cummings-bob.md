## Executive summary
Bob Cummings is a Partner in Grant Thornton’s Financial Management Practice providing services to global property/casualty, life and health insurance industry clients.
Bob is a consulting professional with over 20 years of experience focused on business process management and performance optimization, finance transformation, enterprise risk management, and internal control remediation within the (Re)insurance industry.
Bob has managed process and technology enhancement initiatives in all aspects of the insurance value chain from Underwriting through Claims management and Actuarial as well as in support functions such as Finance/Accounting, Financial Planning and Analysis, Treasury, Tax and Human Resources. With a strong focus on the finance function within the insurance industry Bob has extensive experience in identifying, quantifying and remediating financial statement risk. Along with his significant experience in the (Re)Insurance industry Bob has also worked with clients in the Not For Profit Healthcare, Real Estate and Manufacturing & Distribution industries.
