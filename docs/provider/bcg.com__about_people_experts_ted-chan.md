### Education
  * MS, technology and policy, MIT
  * BS, computer and systems engineering, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute
  * BS, computer science, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute


Ted Chan is the head of Boston Consulting Group in Hong Kong. He also leads BCG's Climate & Sustainability practice and the Travel, Cities & Infrastructure (TCI) practice in Greater China. He led BCG’s Corporate Finance & Strategy practice in Greater China between 2009 and 2014.
With over 25 years of experience across industrial, technology, consumer, and health care companies, Ted has developed profound expertise in guiding leading Chinese companies through significant transformation and globalization efforts. He also assists multinational corporations in formulating and executing strategies tailored to the Greater China market. Notably, in the climate and sustainability arena, Ted has supported clients on issues related to decarbonization, green energy, emerging green business opportunities, and navigating the global regulatory landscape.
From 2014 to 2017, Ted left BCG to serve as Group Chief Strategy Officer at SF Express, a leading express delivery and logistics company in China. As a key member of the senior management team, he played a key role in the company's successful public listing in early 2017, overseeing all strategic, investment, and partnership initiatives for the group. He returned to BCG in 2017.
Ted is on the board of directors of Save The Children in Hong Kong.
