## Executive summary
Paul oversees Grant Thornton's industry, geography, commercial platforms and analytics, and global account efforts. He leads Grant Thornton's AI, Data and Technology practice and has over 30 years of experience in advising clients. He has advised stakeholders, including bank groups, customers, suppliers and shareholders, in several different scenarios, including company viability, reconstructions, debt restructuring and strategic options.
