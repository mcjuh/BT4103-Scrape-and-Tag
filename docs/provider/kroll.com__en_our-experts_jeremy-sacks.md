AT A GLANCE 
Jeremy Sacks joined Kroll (formerly Duff & Phelps) in 2005. He is a managing director in the Los Angeles office and part of the firm’s Financial Reporting practice. He has more than 34 years of experience in consulting and performing valuations of businesses and assets for acquisition, divestiture, financing, financial reporting and tax. He has also served as an expert witness.
Prior to joining Duff & Phelps, Jeremy was a managing director with Standard & Poor’s Corporate Value Consulting and a partner with PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP. He specializes in financial services, retail and franchised businesses and other consumer products-based companies. Jeremy has served some of the world’s largest financial institutions, title insurers, insurance brokerages and restaurant companies. He has also spoken on a wide variety of real estate topics including franchise valuations, real estate securitization, financing trends, domestic privatization and market segmentation. Jeremy holds an M.B.A. in banking and finance and a B.B.A. in public accounting from Hofstra University. He is also a certified public accountant and is accredited in Business Valuation.
