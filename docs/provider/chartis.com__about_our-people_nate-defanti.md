# Nate DeFanti
Partner 
Nate DeFanti is a Partner at Chartis with more than 25 years of healthcare executive leadership experience helping provider organizations leverage technology to improve clinical, operational, and financial performance. A former clinician, healthcare executive, and management consultant, he specializes in digital transformation, enterprise technology strategy, Epic implementation and optimization, governance, informatics, and organizational change. 
Throughout his career, Nate has advised and led healthcare organizations ranging from integrated delivery networks to multihospital health systems, driving large-scale transformation initiatives that improve quality, efficiency, and long-term sustainability. He is recognized for helping healthcare organizations balance innovation with practical execution, leveraging artificial intelligence, automation, and digital transformation capabilities to create measurable clinical, operational, and financial value. He bridges clinical, operational, and technology perspectives to help healthcare leaders navigate complex technology investment decisions. His experience spans enterprise governance, Epic strategy, application portfolio management, technology modernization, and performance improvement, with a focus on aligning technology investments to organizational strategy and measurable outcomes.
Prior to joining Chartis, Nate served as Chief Information Officer for Conemaugh Health System, where he led enterprise technology strategy, informatics, and application delivery across a four-hospital health system. Previously, he served as the Vice President of Applications for Allegheny Health Network, overseeing enterprise application strategy and IT operations across 13 hospitals and more than 500 ambulatory locations. He also held leadership positions at UPMC and consulting roles with Deloitte Consulting and Cap Gemini Ernst & Young.
Nate earned a Master of Business Administration in Health Administration from the University of Pittsburgh, a Master of Physician Assistant degree from Duquesne University, and a Bachelor of Science in Health Sciences from Duquesne University.

###  Why healthcare? 
I have always been fascinated by science and the complexities of the human body, which led me to pursue a career in medicine. I am equally driven by the complexities of our healthcare system and the opportunity to help simplify it for caregivers and patients. 
###  What do you hope to help our clients achieve? 
Efficiency. By helping provider organizations operate with less friction, we can reduce cost and effort while ultimately improving patient outcomes. 
###  Do you have a motto or personal mantra? 
“Do or do not. There is no try.” Though spoken by a fictional character, Yoda, this quote captures a belief I hold deeply: meaningful achievements come from commitment, not intention alone. The greatest impact is made when we fully dedicate ourselves to a goal.
