### Education
  * MBA, Indian Institute of Management, Indore
  * BEng, electronics and communication engineering, Delhi College of Engineering.


Ishang Jawa has over 12 years of experience as a core member of Boston Consulting Group's Industrial Goods and Marketing, Sales & Pricing practices. He leads the firm's building materials business in Asia Pacific. Ishang is an expert in large-scale turnarounds, sales acceleration, digital transformation, and manufacturing excellence. He works with clients in building materials, power and renewables, and metals and mining across India, Southeast Asia, and Canada.
Prior to joining BCG, Ishang worked in investment banking at JP Morgan as part of the consumer, health care, and retail coverage team.
