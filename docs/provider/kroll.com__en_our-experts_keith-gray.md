AT A GLANCE 
Keith Gray is a Managing Director in the Transaction Opinions practice at Kroll and is based in Chicago. He has extensive experience executing engagements for fairness opinions, solvency opinions and business valuation opinions.
Keith specializes in providing [fairness opinions](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/transaction-opinions/fairness-opinions), [solvency opinions](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/transaction-opinions/solvency-opinions), valuations and other corporate finance advisory services to clients involved in a variety of public and private company transactions, including GP-led secondary recapitalizations, leveraged buyouts, related-party transactions, asset sales, spin-offs, going-private transactions, tender offers, share repurchases, dividend recapitalizations, and ESOP transactions.
Keith has experience across a broad range of industries, including real estate, agriculture, consumer products, food and beverage, healthcare, and manufacturing, among others.
