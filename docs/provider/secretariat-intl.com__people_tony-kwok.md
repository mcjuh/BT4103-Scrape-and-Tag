## Tony Kwok
#### Director
Tony Kwok is a Chartered Quantity Surveyor, who possesses more than 20 years of deep experience on construction claims, dispute resolution matters and contract administration in both international and local projects for building, civil engineering and oil & gas sectors.
Hong Kong
[+852 2979 2225](tel:+852%202979%202225) tkwok@secretariat-intl.com
Tony Kwok is a Chartered Quantity Surveyor with over 20 years of experience in construction claims, dispute resolution, and contract administration across international and domestic projects in the building, civil engineering, and oil & gas sectors. As a Director with Secretariat, he leads teams and works closely with Quantum Experts in the preparation of expert reports, joint statements, and detailed quantum analyses. His expertise covers a wide range of standard forms of contract, including FIDIC, JCT, NEC, IChemE, the Hong Kong Standard Form, EPC, and other bespoke agreements.
Mr. Kwok has advised leading blue-chip companies on numerous high-profile disputes, including the quantum assessment of prolongation, disruption, variation orders, termination losses, defects, and third-party claims. He is recognised for his ability to combine technical precision with clear strategic insight, providing clients with robust and reliable support in complex arbitration proceedings.
