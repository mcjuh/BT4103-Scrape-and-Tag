# Mitesh Modha
## Managing Director, Business Development, International
### About Mitesh
Mitesh brings more than 10 years of experience in business development, origination, stakeholder engagement, and cross-border commercial strategy. His career spans the UK, Europe, and the Middle East, with a strong track record of creating new market opportunities, shaping strategic partnerships, and supporting clients involved in high-profile and complex commercial claims, corporate governance matters, and class or group actions.
Mitesh has operated at the intersection of legal strategy, corporate governance, financial risk, and commercial recovery. Prior to HKA, he worked for a leading, global litigation funder with a particular focus on competition actions and securities litigation. He also spent 9 years at a leading legal costs firm, initially as an in-house advocate and eventually as a Director. 
Bringing valuable international experience and commercial insight, Mitesh focuses on strengthening client relationships and supporting the firm’s expansion across competition, group litigation and commercial disputes. His breadth of experience aligns closely with HKA’s multidisciplinary expertise and services across competition, group litigation, and commercial dispute mandates.
