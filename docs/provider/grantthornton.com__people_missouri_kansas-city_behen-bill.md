## Executive summary
In his 20 years of experience in professional services, Bill has become a recognized thought leader in the selection, implementation and adoption of enterprise applications. Bill specializes in the program management and delivery of transformational projects involving the implementation of on premise and Cloud based technologies. Bill has extensive experience in the enabling technologies for the functional areas of Finance/Accounting, Procurement/Supply Chain, Project Accounting, HR, Order Processing/Customer Servicing, and Marketing. Prior to joining Grant Thornton, he worked for ConAgra Foods and Arthur Andersen. Bill has a breadth of industry experience with a primary emphasis servicing clients in the Financials Services, Hospitality/Gaming, Telecommunications, Retail, Professional Services, Distribution, Real Estate, and Manufacturing sectors. Over his career, Bill has serviced many multi-national organizations with operations in AMERICAS, EMEA, and APAC.
