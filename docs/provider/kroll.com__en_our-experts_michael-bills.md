AT A GLANCE 
Michael Bills is a Managing Director in the Restructuring practice, based in London. Michael leverages over two decades of experience in corporate advisory and restructuring, specializing in providing strategic financial advice and planning to borrowers and lenders with asset-based lending facilities. 
His advisory work includes:
  * The analysis of financial information
  * Financial forecast modelling
  * Working capital management optimization
  * Contingency and exit planning
  * Refinance and debt advisory services
  * Tax arrears solutions
  * Stakeholder management


Michael has completed a significant number of independent business, viability and pre-lending reviews across a wide range of business sectors including manufacturing, engineering, recruitment, construction and hospitality. These engagements have involved extensive financial due diligence and working alongside management teams and lenders in both growth and distressed scenarios.
Michael has completed two secondments to leading asset-based lending institutions, is a chartered accountant and is JIEB qualified.
