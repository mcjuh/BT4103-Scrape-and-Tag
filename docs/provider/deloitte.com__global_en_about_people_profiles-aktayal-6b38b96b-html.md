#  Akash Tayal 
##  Principal | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
###  Akash Tayal leads US Engineering-as-a-Service at Deloitte, helping organizations modernize engineering practices and accelerate product delivery. 
With 23 years of experience in software engineering and business-process consulting across financial services, health care, retail, and other sectors, he guides Fortune 500 clients through cloud transformations, DevSecOps and SRE adoption, and the design of resilient, secure platforms.
As a principal in Deloitte Consulting LLP, Akash partners with C-suite executives to shape technology strategy, establish global engineering centers of excellence, and embed outcome-based operating models that enhance developer experience and time to value. A frequent speaker on modern engineering and platform strategy, he is known for building high-performing teams and cultures of continuous improvement.
When he’s not advising clients, Akash enjoys playing cricket, working on his golf swing, and exploring new destinations on family road-trip adventures.
