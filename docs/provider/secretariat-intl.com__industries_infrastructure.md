# Infrastructure
## Addressing disputes in the world’s largest infrastructure projects.
Major infrastructure projects are complex and require significant time and resources to complete. Lack of early planning, outmoded transactional approaches to procurement, immature designs, and poor management of subcontractor interfaces and digital information are just some of the pitfalls that can create risk when it comes to these high-profile projects.
The challenges for promoters and contractors are compounded amid strong cost inflation, supply chain disruption, skills shortages and, in some markets, overheating.
Secretariat has supported some of the world’s most prestigious infrastructure megaprojects, and with decades of cumulative experience, our people are soughtout as leading experts and advisors when it comes to addressing the lifecycle of infrastructure initiatives.

#### Managing Director – Sydney 
Mr. Larkin has more than 30 years of experience in the construction industry. He is a chartered civil engineer and holds a law degree with deep arbitration qualifications.
