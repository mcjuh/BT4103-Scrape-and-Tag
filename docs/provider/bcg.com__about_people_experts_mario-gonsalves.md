Mario Gonsalves joined Boston Consulting Group in 2010. He leads BCG’s Public Sector practice in India, and the transportation, infrastructure, travel, tech, and lodging work for Asia Pacific.
Mario has considerable expertise in large-scale transformation, growth strategy, infrastructure development and financing, go-to-market, and LEAN operations. He has led major projects in the public sector across Economic Development, Defence & Security and Transport Infrastructure. In the private sector, he has worked across infrastructure, real estate, automotive, mining, cement, iron ore, refractories and agricultural inputs. Mario has worked on projects across North America, South Africa, India, and Sri Lanka, including spending a year in the Philadelphia office as part of BCG’s Global Ambassador program. 
Mario is a 2024 honoree of the prestigious Economic Times 40 Under 40 list of leaders of tomorrow to watch for in India. Before joining the firm, he worked at Siemens India Ltd. where he marketed and executed turnkey power distribution projects in the paper, food, and water industries.
