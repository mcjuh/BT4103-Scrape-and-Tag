AT A GLANCE 
Graham is Global Head of Expert Services at Kroll. Based in the Hong Kong office, Graham leads the global expansion of Kroll’s construction and commercial dispute and advisory capabilities in addition to the growth of its multi-disciplinary team of experts. He works closely with other practices to support Kroll’s growing list of clients in Asia Pacific, Europe and the US markets.
Graham has worked in Asia for over 25 years and has extensive experience in the preparation, defense, negotiation and settlement of contractual claims. He works alongside, and in support of, solicitors and barristers and has been recognized by Who’s Who Legal as a “Global Elite Thought Leader” and “top class expert who is highly respected in the international markets”. He is also cited for his “very comprehensive and well-reasoned reports” and his “excellent performance on the stand”. He has been appointed as Quantum Expert/Advisor on numerous occasions both Internationally and in Hong Kong, giving evidence in Mediation, Conciliation and Arbitration. Having worked as a PQS, Contractor and Consultant Graham is able to provide strategic advice to a range of Clients.
Graham is an energetic and motivated Chartered Quantity Surveyor with further qualifications and experience in Construction Law, Arbitration, Mediation and Project Management. In addition, Graham has worked as Dispute Manager on a number of high-profile construction disputes.
Graham is also an accredited Mediator.
