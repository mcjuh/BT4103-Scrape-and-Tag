AT A GLANCE 
Gareth is a Managing Director and Global Head of Kroll’s Technical Accounting practice in New York, recognized for delivering market-leading, independent, and commercial-focused accounting advice across the deal life continuum.
He is an expert technical accounting and transaction structuring expert with over a decade of experience advising on complex transactions. His expertise spans M&A, joint venture arrangements, and Chapter 11 bankruptcy fresh start reporting.
He is a trusted advisor specializing in technical accounting and transaction structuring, providing expert guidance on financial due diligence for both buy-side and vendor engagements across diverse industries and global markets.
With deep expertise in the commercial application of technical accounting standards, Gareth advises on complex matters such as GAAP framework assessments, business combination support, debt and equity financings, stock-based compensation, and the adoption of new accounting standards. He delivers strategic, on-call technical support under both US GAAP and IFRS, ensuring compliance and optimizing financial outcomes.
Having delivered hundreds of business combination engagements, he helps clients navigate the complexities of purchase accounting, including multiple entity pushdown (ASC 805 / IFRS 3), GAAP conversions and the preparation of technical memorandums. He works seamlessly with Kroll’s market leading valuation practice regularly delivering integrated solutions to clients. 
**Recent experience**
  * **Fresh Start Reporting:** Advised a $1.8 billion global cloud communications and workstream collaboration company on the application of fresh start reporting following its emergence from Chapter 11 bankruptcy.
  * **PE Take-Private Transaction:** Provided ASC 805 purchase accounting guidance for a multi-billion-dollar private equity acquisition of a global publicly traded engineering firm.
  * **Retail Sector Acquisition:** Advised on the ASC 805 technical accounting aspects for a multi-billion-dollar acquisition of a leading U.S. retail chain.
  * **Complex Debt & Equity Transactions:** Supported a NASDAQ-listed company in assessing complex financing transactions, including warrant amendments and the issuance of convertible debt.
  * **Private Equity Platform Company Support:** Assisted several private equity platform companies with financial reporting and technical accounting for add-on acquisitions.


Prior to joining Kroll, Gareth advised in EY’s Financial Accounting Advisory Service group, where he specialized in transaction accounting and financial instruments. Before EY, Gareth worked in corporate finance and private equity.
Gareth holds a Bachelor of Business Science (finance and accounting) and is a registered Chartered Accountant.
