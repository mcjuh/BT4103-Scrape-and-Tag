# Catharine Wilder
Partner and Vice President of Consulting Operations 
Catharine Wilder is a Partner and Vice President of Consulting Operations for Performance Transformation. She is a healthcare consulting and operations professional with over 25 years of experience in management and healthcare including project management, hospital operations, and management consulting. Catharine has experience designing and implementing revenue cycle process improvements at multiple academic medical centers, including revised workflows, policies and procedures, technology enhancements, job descriptions, performance monitoring/measurement tools, training programs, staffing models, and other supporting resources. Catharine excels in the areas of hospital revenue cycle information technology and operations, with specific expertise in patient access and denial management. She brings excellent leadership, project management, organizational, communication, strategy, change management, and staff development skills which further enhance her healthcare operations and information technology knowledge and experience. 
In her current role, Catharine leads the advancement of the firm’s revenue cycle advisory solutions and provides overall management for the practice. She plays a key role in development of the practice area’s intellectual property and thought leadership. She also manages the sales and marketing efforts for the practice area, including scoping and staffing projects to align the firm’s expertise and capabilities with the needs of each client. 
Prior to joining Chartis, Catharine worked as an independent healthcare leader advising on strategic revenue cycle initiatives and as a Director at a large consulting firm overseeing large revenue cycle transformation projects. She also served as a Vice President of Patient Access leading strategic direction and operational oversight over Patient Access within a 400-bed academic medical center in New York City. Catharine received her Master of Education in Counseling Psychology from Seton Hall University, and her Bachelor of Arts in Psychology and Sociology from Villanova University.

###  How do your personal goals align to Chartis’ mission and vision? 
I am the daughter of a Nurse Practitioner who was a pioneer in her vision for healthcare when I was growing up. Over 25 years ago, she was working to have telemedicine used as common practice in home care and other settings when there were many skeptics. I have engrained in me the philosophy that we need to innovate and work to materially make healthcare better. It felt like a daunting task to move that needle and really make an impact; however, now being part of an organization that carries that mission, it seems so much more achievable. 
###  Where’s your favorite place to visit and why? 
While it’s been quite some time since I have traveled there, my favorite place to visit would have to be Italy. I may love it just because it’s in my blood (both of my grandfathers come from there—one from Turin in the North and one from Sicily in the South), but I think it’s more likely the food, the wine, the art, the landscape, the architecture, and so much more. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
When I’m not working, you will likely find me doing something that involves my two daughters (packing lunches, homework oversight, taking to/from activities, making meals, doing hair, logistics management, watching them perform/dance etc.) or spinning on the Peloton bike, which is a daily event for me and a great stress reliever!
