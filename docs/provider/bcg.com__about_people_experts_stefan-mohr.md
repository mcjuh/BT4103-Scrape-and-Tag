Taking a Portfolio Approach to Technology Investments
For a technology to be an enabler of business success, we need to see a pathway to real, tangible value. BCG’s Stefan Mohr explains that we must look through the lenses of desirability, feasibility, and viability to establish the connection between the technology and the business value.
### Education
  * Diploma, Business Administration/Computer Science, Karlsruhe University, Germany


Stefan is the leader of DigitalBCG in the Asia-Pacific region. He also leads DigitalBCG’s accelerator systems globally. 
Stefan helps business and technology leaders use existing and emerging technologies to innovate customer propositions and transform business operations. 
Since joining BCG, Stefan has worked with organizations to develop digitally driven business strategies to design and scale digital solutions for transforming sales, marketing, operations and management processes. These solutions simplify and modernize technology environments and build the organizational capabilities to succeed in a digital world. 
Prior to BCG, Stefan worked at BASF in Germany as a global product manager.
