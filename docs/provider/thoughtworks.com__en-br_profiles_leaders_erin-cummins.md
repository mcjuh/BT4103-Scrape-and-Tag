#  Erin Cummins
Chief Financial Officer
Pronouns: She / Her
As a leader, I am passionate about enabling people to do amazing things, and that’s what led me to join Thoughtworks in 2004: the people I talked to at Thoughtworks had such passion for the company and for technology that I knew I wanted to be a part of it. I became Head of Finance for Europe in 2011 and CFO in 2014, and I have supported the business and our people through many phases of growth and expansion. I am incredibly proud of our enduring commitment to nurturing our technologists and growing their careers.
I am from the US but now live in London, which I love for its global culture.
