Mohit Khullar is Managing Director and Leader of Alvarez & Marsal Corporate Finance in India. He specializes in private equity, M&A, and structured finance transactions.
With over 21 years of experience, Mr. Khullar has an established track record in the transaction advisory space. He has led marquee transactions across healthcare, consumer, business services, and industrials sectors. He has developed and led high-performing teams while also cultivating strong relationships with founders, CXOs, and financial sponsors.Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Khullar served as Managing Director at o3 Capital and worked with Equirus Capital and Standard Chartered Bank. Additionally, he cofounded a healthcare technology business, where he served as CEO, garnering multiple industry accolades.Mr. Khullar earned a bachelor’s degree in electronics and communications from Netaji Subhas University of Technology, Delhi, and a postgraduate diploma in management from the Indian Institute of Management, Lucknow. He is an alumnus of the Rashtriya Indian Military College and has served on the Governing Council of NATHEALTH, a leading Indian healthcare industry federation.
