#  Sachin Sondhi 
##  Universal Banking & Capital Markets Consulting Leader 
Sachin is a principal with Deloitte Consulting LLP based in New York. He leads the US Universal Banking & Capital Markets Consulting practice and drives Deloitte’s global strategic program on Financial Services Operating Model Transformation. Sachin has worked with a diverse set of financial institutions helping them better organize and operate their businesses in the face of a rapidly changing marketplace. His primary focus is on business and operations transformation, mergers and acquisitions, and performance management.
Sachin has more than 20 years of experience at Deloitte includes five years in Asia where he led the development of the firm’s financial services and strategy and operations practice.
