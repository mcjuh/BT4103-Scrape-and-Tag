#  Dan Simmonds 
##  Principal | US Investment & Wealth Mgmt. Solutions Leader 
[ Minneapolis, US ](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/offices/office-locator/minneapolis.html)
###  Dan Simmonds serves as the US Investment & Wealth Mgmt. Solutions leader for Deloitte’s AI & Engineering Industry Solutions. 
Within this role, Dan helps clients streamline their operations and technologies to modernize investments, wealth, retirement, before real estate core operations, and more. As a princial at Deloitte Consulting LLP, Dan is passionate about advising on operating model and technology solutions, increasing operating value, and improving investment sophistication for asset and wealth managers/services.
In his previous role, Dan focused on front and middle office transformation, leading many platform assessment and selection initiatives across the full value chain and crossing traditional and alternative asset classes. Along with the technology-based transformations, he has also led clients through insourcing/outsourcing operations, data management, and reporting solution implementation and trading platform integration.
Dan received his B.A. in Business Marketing from the University of St. Thomas – Opus College of Business. In his free time, Dan enjoys spending time with family on the lake, playing/coaching hockey, and golfing.
