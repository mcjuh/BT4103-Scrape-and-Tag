AT A GLANCE 
Juan Carlos Díaz is an Associate Managing Director and EMEA & APAC Lead for Enterprise Risk Retainer within Kroll’s Cyber and Data Resilience practice, based in Madrid. With more than 20 years of experience, Juan Carlos advises executive leadership teams on managing complex cyber and enterprise risks, combining deep technical expertise with strategic, board level insight.
Juan Carlos specializes in helping organizations strengthen cyber resilience through long term risk governance, proactive security programs and incident preparedness. His experience spans both proactive and reactive cybersecurity services, including Red Team and Purple Team exercises, cyber crisis simulations, counterintelligence, security architecture assessments, product evaluations, threat research and executive level security training. In recent years, he has played a key role in the development and leadership of enterprise cybersecurity retainers, supporting organizations in aligning cybersecurity with broader business and risk management objectives.
Throughout his career, Juan Carlos has supported a wide range of clients, including government entities, global financial institutions and multinational private sector organizations, across EMEA, APAC, Latin America and the Middle East. He has led and overseen complex security engagements, advising organizations operating in highly regulated and critical infrastructure environments.
Before joining Kroll, Juan Carlos spent over a decade at a leading information security firm, where he led technical audit, incident response and cybersecurity consulting practices. He was appointed Cybersecurity Director for Spain in 2017 and later became Leader of Counterintelligence Services for the EMEA region in 2019, overseeing multidisciplinary teams and regional service delivery.
Juan Carlos holds a degree in Computer Engineering from the University of Extremadura and maintains industry recognized certifications, including CISSP, CISA and CISM. He is an active member of ISACA and (ISC)² in Madrid.
