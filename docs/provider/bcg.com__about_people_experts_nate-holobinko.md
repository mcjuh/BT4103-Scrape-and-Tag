### Education
  * BS, business administration, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill


Nate Holobinko is the North America leader for Health Care Services (HCS) within Boston Consulting Group's Health Care practice. He previously led BCG’s Health Care practice for the Western US.
Nate has over 20 years of experience helping health care companies develop new strategies and transform their business models. He has hands-on experience across the health care landscape including national payers, major academic medical centers, integrated health systems, Blue Cross Blue Shield Plans, and private equity investors.
Nate focuses on the intersection of insurance and care delivery where the rapid evolution in therapeutics, analytics, and regulation is creating new opportunities for growth, improved economics, and better patient outcomes. In addition to helping many of the nations leading health care institutions with their overall strategies, he helps lead organizations through their most significant transformations including cost-out at scale, operational stabilization, digital enablement, and M&A.
Nate has also been a thought leader in the practice and his perspectives have been featured broadly in the media including _Strategy + Business_ , the _Harvard Business Review_ , _BenefitsPro_ , the World Healthcare Congress, and NIHCM, among others.
Prior to joining BCG, Nate was a principal in Booz & Company's health care strategy practice.
