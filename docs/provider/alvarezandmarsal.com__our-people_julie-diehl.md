Julie Diehl is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal’s Corporate Performance Improvement practice in Houston. She specializes in strategic sourcing (including low cost country sourcing), profit and margin improvement, go-to market pricing strategies, supply chain management, cost reduction, supplier relationship management, vendor management, operations improvement, logistics network optimization, procure-to-pay, sales and marketing and IT sourcing strategy. Ms. Diehl has worked with a range of private and public clients across the manufacturing, energy / oil and gas, automotive, airlines, building and construction, state and city government and distribution industries.
With more than 25 years of experience, Ms. Diehl has helped companies drive profit improvement through business process and organization transformation.
**[Watch: Delivering Real Value through Strategic Sourcing](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/delivering-real-value-through-strategic-sourcing)**
Ms. Diehl started the supply chain practice for A&M more than 11 years ago with the mindset of combining industry-experienced practitioners with problem-solving consultants to share knowledge and train clients so that the performance improvement realized is sustainable for the long term. Having spent half of her career in industry and the other half in consulting, she has found this combination has helped clients execute and achieve quick results.
In her role with the firm’s global professional services, she has assisted in delivering business performance improvement, turnaround management and advisory services to organizations seeking to transform operations, catapult growth and accelerate results through decisive action and utilize the restructuring heritage of A&M’s senior professionals who have helped leaders turn the need for change into a business asset, manage risk and unlock value at every stage.
Prior to joining A&M, Ms. Diehl served in several senior management positions with Pennzoil-Quaker State Company. As Vice President of Global Strategic Sourcing, she led the U.S. and Asia sourcing teams. As Vice President of National Account Sales, she managed the sales team serving retailers throughout the U.S.
She earned a bachelor’s degree in finance from the University of Houston and is a certified purchasing manager (CPM).
