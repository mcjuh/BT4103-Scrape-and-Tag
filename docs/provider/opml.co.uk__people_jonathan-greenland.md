Jonathan Greenland is a Senior Consultant and Public Financial Management (PFM) expert and economist, with over 12 years' experience in PFM reform, quantitative and qualitative research, analytical Skills, data analysis, and project management. He holds an MSc in Economics for Development from the University of Oxford, and speaks English, French, German, and Russian. He has a history of working as a senior economist in government as an ODI Fellow, PNG, and in multilaterals including ADB and the World Bank.
## Projects involving Jonathan Greenland
Project
###  [Assessment for designing Kenya Social and Economic Inclusion Programme (KSEIP2)](https://www.opml.co.uk/projects/assessment-designing-kenya-social-and-economic-inclusion-programme-kseip2 "Go to Assessment for designing Kenya Social and Economic Inclusion Programme \(KSEIP2\)")
The World Bank commissioned us to inform the design of the second Kenya Social and Economic Inclusion Programme (KSEIP2). We assessed Kenya’s social protection system, explored options to scale up the shock-responsive Hunger Safety Net Programme (HSNP), and analysed cost data from the Economic Inclusion Project (EIP), focusing on how cost structures changed as implementation shifted from NGOs to the Government of Kenya (GoK).
