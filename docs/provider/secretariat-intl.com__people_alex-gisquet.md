## Alex Gisquet
#### Manager
​​Mr. Gisquet is an economic consultant with experience in quantifying damages for complex, high-stakes litigation matters involving intellectual property, forensic accounting, breach of contract, and corporate governance.​ 
San Diego
[+1 ​​480 822 0981​](tel:+1%20%E2%80%8B%E2%80%8B480%20822%200981%E2%80%8B) agisquet@secretariat-intl.com
​​Mr. Gisquet is a seasoned professional specializing in the quantification of damages relating to intellectual property matters including patents, trade secrets, and trademarks. In addition to intellectual property, Mr. Gisquet’s expertise has been applied to forensic accounting, breach of contract, and corporate governance matters. Mr. Gisquet’s work has been used in connection across a wide variety of industries including life sciences, financial services, healthcare, media and telecommunications, nonprofits, and consumer goods.
​Examples of Mr. Gisquet’s work include the following projects:
  * **​Reasonable royalty analysis for COVID-19 vaccine technology.** Evaluated marketplace dynamics, licensing policies, competitive relationships, sales and profitability, and the economic contributions of the patented technology to determine the quantification of a reasonable royalty for technology within a COVID-19 vaccine.
  * **​Disgorgement analysis for an online learning trademark.** Apportioned the value of a trademark used in connection with an online learning platform to determine the profit earned from consumers likely to have been confused by a competing trademark.
  * **​Forensic accounting of capital gains earned by a mutual fund.** Investigated accounting records to locate and track capital gains to determine the appropriate distribution among partners within a mutual fund.
  * **​Evaluation of domestic industry and public interest for consumer electronics.** Analyzed investments in domestic industry and assessed marketplace dynamics to determine the impact on domestic industry and public interest in the event that certain consumer electronics were excluded from the market.
