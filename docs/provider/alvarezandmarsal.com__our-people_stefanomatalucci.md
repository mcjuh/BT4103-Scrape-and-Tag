Stefano Matalucci is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Private Equity Performance Improvement in Milan. He specializes in strategy and performance improvement for strategic buyers for corporates and private equity firms. 
Mr. Matalucci brings more than 20 years of industry and consulting experience across a range of sectors, including business information, information and communication technology (ICT), manufacturing, sports and entertainment, financial services, healthcare and the public sector.
Mr. Matalucci’s notable recent engagements include serving as interim CEO for a private equity owned manufacturer. He realized a three-year strategic plan that included consolidating business across different countries, value chain streamlining, new product development and realizing cost synergies. Mr. Matalucci served in several executive roles for a private equity owned company in the business information sector, managing the post-merger integration process and leading value creation initiatives including the turnaround of a business unit, the creation of a unique product portfolio across the legal entities, new product development, M&A support, and cost synergy realization.
Mr. Matalucci also advised a private equity firm on creating a media company to maximize media rights for Lega Serie A (the association of Serie A soccer clubs in Italy). He managed the M&A sell-side process for a privately owned Serie A Club, after advising and supporting the owners for more than a year with strategic planning and value creation across all functions and business areas. Mr. Matalucci also led projects for large international private equities, focusing on business and commercial due diligence, value creation initiatives and post-acquisition planning.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Matalucci was a Partner with EY in the Strategy and Transaction business unit, where he was responsible for PE value creation. Previously, he worked with McKinsey & Company serving private equities and central banks.
Mr. Matalucci earned a bachelor's degree in aerospace engineering from Politecnico di Milano and an MBA (magna cum laude) from SDA Bocconi in Milan.
