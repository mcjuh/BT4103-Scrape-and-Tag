### Expertise
Jose Omar Nuñez is a Professional Engineer with 20 years of experience. He has been disclosed as a technical expert on over 75 cases.
Jose has acted as a forensic structural engineer at depositions in litigation matters and has performed over 3000 cause-and-origin assessments. He has produced expert reports on disputes with values of up to $1 million.
Jose specializes in the transport industry and in structural evaluations of residential and commercial buildings. His expertise includes the repair and rehabilitation of cable-stayed and movable bridges, water intrusion investigations, roof evaluations, and evaluating construction vibration claims.
### Background
Jose has experience in plan review, code compliance, and coordinating multi-disciplinary teams across public infrastructure and private development projects. He has managed multimillion-dollar programs, negotiated consultant contracts, and ensured adherence to federal, state, and local standards.
Jose holds a certificate in public administration, a degree in civil engineering, and is a Certified English XL Tribometrist. He is a member of the National Society of Professional Engineers and is bilingual in English and Spanish.
