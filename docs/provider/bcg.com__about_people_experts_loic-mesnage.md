### Education
  * MBA, University of Chicago, Booth School of Business
  * Master's degree, finance and audit, ISC, Paris


Loïc Mesnage joined The Boston Consulting Group in January 2018 after 21 years in consulting. He is a member of the Technology Advantage and Industrial Goods practices, and he specializes in digital transformations and large technology driven transformation.
Loïc works with clients in industrial goods and aerospace and defense, as well as the utility and energy sectors to solve complex business problems in IT strategy and digital transformation. He uses many levers of change including technology, data, and new ways of working.
Prior to joining the firm, Loïc worked for PwC for 16 years, where he created and led technology consulting in France as well as the data analytics practice.
