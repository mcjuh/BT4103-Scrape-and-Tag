Leveraging Technology to Improve Container Shipping
BCG’s Camille Egloff, the leader of the firm’s shipping sector, cautions that the shipping industry isn’t likely to return to growth in the high single digits. But there are several areas of opportunity to improve shipping operations, including using digital solutions to increase efficiency.
### Education
  * MBA, HEC Paris School of Management


Camille Egloff leads Boston Consulting Group’s transportation and logistics sector globally. She is also the global leader for the Center for Digital in Transportation and specializes in transportation and logistics topics while working within BCG’s Center for Mobility Innovation, a team of urban mobility experts and digital business builders. 
Since joining BCG in 1998, Camille has acquired deep knowledge and experience advising [shipping](https://www.bcg.com/industries/transportation-logistics/shipping-industry) clients across geographies and vessel segments, with a focus on large-scale transformation programs. She is a thought leader in shipping and publishes often on the subjects of the container transportation market, energy management, and governance of family businesses. She has also been pioneering advanced analytics in shipping. 
Before joining BCG, Camille worked for French retailer Carrefour and for BNP Paribas.
