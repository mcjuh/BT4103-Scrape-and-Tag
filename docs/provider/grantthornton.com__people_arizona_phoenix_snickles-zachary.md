## Executive summary
Zach is a partner in Risk Advisory Services. He has spent his career in professional services performing advisory and assurance services and oversees the firm’s Anti-Fraud and fraud risk solutions. He specializes in fraud risk assessments and consulting, regulatory compliance, investigations, business disputes, litigation, and Anti-Fraud maturity. Zach has been awarded the Standing Ovation Award from the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) for exemplary personal achievement in his efforts to advance the forensic and valuation services profession.
