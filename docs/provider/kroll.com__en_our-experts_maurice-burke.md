AT A GLANCE 
Maurice Burke is a Managing Director in the Investigations, Diligence and Compliance practice, based in Australia. He leverages more than 20 years of experience in dispute resolution, investigations and contentious regulatory matters across the Asia Pacific region, including Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia and the Philippines.
Maurice solves complex problems for his clients and has advised a broad range of multinational clients on strategies to meet operational and commercial goals. He has a deep understanding of legal systems, the critical role of local culture in identifying and remediating compliance issues, and the challenges of cross and multijurisdictional issues. 
He has been described by clients as ‘adept at complex dispute resolution and anti-corruption investigations’, ‘a practitioner paying close attention to Indonesia and the region’ and ‘a seasoned disputes and regulatory compliance lawyer, with extensive expertise in Southeast Asia’.
Prior to joining Kroll, Maurice served as Asia Head of Investigations and Head of Litigation and Regulatory at DLA Piper, Singapore. He has been consistently recognized as a leading practitioner by legal directories; he has been ranked as a Band 1 practitioner for Corporate Investigations/Anti-Corruption International 2018-2024 and a Band 1 practitioner for Dispute Resolution (International Firms) (Indonesia) by Chambers Asia Pacific and Chambers Global. He has also been ranked as Band 1 practitioner for White Collar Crime – Foreign Firms, 2021-2023 by Legal 500.
In short, Maurice helps clients identify, address and move past the most complex operational and compliance issues.
