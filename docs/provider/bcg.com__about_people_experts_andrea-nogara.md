### Education
  * MA, Economics, University of Padua
  * MBA, Insead Business School


Andrea Nogara joined The Boston Consulting Group in 2011. As a member of the BCG Industrial Goods Practice for Italy, Greece, and Turkey, he has been involved in several strategic and operational cases focusing on the construction and automotive industries.
Andrea has deep experience in the industrial goods sector, from business planning to commercial strategy and operations. His main focus has been improving performance and operational processes. He led the implementation of lean production techniques in several projects for both commodity producers and manufacturers.
In the construction sector, Andrea supported a leading company on all aspects of its national and international acquisitions, and helped improve its operational performance.
Prior to joining BCG, Andrea worked for another consulting firm on industrial goods and private equity.
