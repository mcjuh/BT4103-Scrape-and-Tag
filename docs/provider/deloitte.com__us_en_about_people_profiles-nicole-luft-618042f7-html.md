#  Nicole Luft 
##  Audit Partner | Deloitte’s financial services practice 
Nicole is an Audit partner in Deloitte’s financial services practice, focused on helping her clients and teams succeed. She has significant experience in the banking and capital markets industry having worked with commercial and retail banks and broker-dealers of all sizes for more than 15 years. Nicole was part of Deloitte’s National Office and served as the point of contact for capital markets audits across the Americas, authored guidance on audit and industry procedures and organized and led multiple industry trainings for practitioners of all levels. She currently serves some of the most diverse banking clients at Deloitte and has vast experience in SEC reporting, internal controls, operational processes, CECL and complex accounting and regulatory issues.
