### Education
  * Engineering Degree (MS), computer science, telecommunications & network engineering, Telecom Paris
  * Engineering Degree (MS), computer Science, mathematics and physics, Ecole Polytechnique


Jean-François Bobier is a member of both BCG X and Boston Consulting Group's Tech & Digital Advantage practice. 
Since joining BCG in 2004, Jean-François has partnered with clients in multiple industries on projects related to technology, cyber, and quantum. He has in-depth expertise on data, digital, and AI platforms that enable business use cases at scale, cost, and stability. 
Jean-François leads the BCG X AI Institute's research on quantum technologies, which has contributed over 15 publications and multiple other offerings to the quantum ecosystem, including organizing the QuantX European hackathon at BCG's Paris offices. He is also a core member of the BCG X AI Platforms group. 
Prior to joining the firm, Jean-François worked as an IT architect at IBM Business Consulting Services.
