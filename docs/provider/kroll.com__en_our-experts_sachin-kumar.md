AT A GLANCE 
Sachin Kumar is an Associate Managing Director in the Cyber Risk practice, based in New Delhi.
Sachin leverages more than 16 years of information security experience, assisting in the financial, technology, automobile and gaming industries, with notable emphasis on cloud security, application security and large-scale penetration testing programs.
Prior to the [acquisition](https://www.kroll.com/en/newsroom/kroll-acquires-security-compass-advisory) of Security Compass Advisory (SCA) by Kroll in December 2021, Sachin served as a director at SCA. Before that, he served as Director and Principal Security Consultant at SCA.
Sachin received a B.Tech. in electronics and a degree in communication from Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology, Gorakhpur.
