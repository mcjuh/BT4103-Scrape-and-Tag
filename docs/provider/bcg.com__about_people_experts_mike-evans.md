### Education
  * MBA, Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley
  * BA, economics, Colgate University


Mike Evans is a managing director and partner at BCG X.
Mike is responsible for partnering with large corporations to help them identify new sources of growth and build businesses that leverage their assets to create advantage. He's launched seven startup businesses since joining BCG. Mike leads multidisciplinary teams of entrepreneurs, innovators, and operators, drawing on his core expertise in marketing and digital, and serves as a board observer on several of his ventures. He leads the retail business for BCG X and is a core member of BCG’s ecommerce leadership team.
Mike has previous experience as a retail operator in chief marketing officer, head of digital, and strategy roles, and as a startup entrepreneur, strategy consultant, and investment banker.
