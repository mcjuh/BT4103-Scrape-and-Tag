AT A GLANCE 
Steven Haley is a Managing Director and head of the [Government Solutions](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/government-solutions) practice. He is based out of the New York office.
Prior to the [acquisition](https://www.kroll.com/en/newsroom/kroll-acquires-verus-analytics) of Verus Analytics by Kroll in July 2020, Steven served as the COO of Verus, where he oversaw all the business and data-related aspects of the company’s services.
He has managed the performance of hundreds of complex examinations, with a focus on processing and evaluation of all data and processes. Steven partnered with technologists and developed innovative data analysis methodologies. Steve has also served as the COO of Kroll’s Governance Risk Advisory practice.
Prior to joining Verus, Steven was a member of KPMG’s National Unclaimed Property practice, advising clients on their unclaimed property procedures and reporting obligations. Inc.
Steven received a B.S. in business management and finance from Salve Regina University.
