#  Daniel Soo 
##  Principal | Deloitte Risk & Financial Advisory 
Daniel, a principal at Deloitte & Touche LLP, is a part of the Cyber Risk Services Infrastructure practice of Deloitte Risk & Financial Advisory. He works with many of Deloitte’s largest and most complex clients to help them assess, enhance, and sustain their technology strategies, operations, and risk management capabilities in the financial services industry.
Daniel has led many strategic and transformation engagements to help clients address their diverse strategic, operational, and regulatory challenges. Additionally, he has worked with many leading financial institutions with specific focus on integrating risk and control elements into operations and implementing reporting/oversight efforts.
Daniel received a Bachelor of Applied Science in Computer Engineering from the University of Waterloo.
