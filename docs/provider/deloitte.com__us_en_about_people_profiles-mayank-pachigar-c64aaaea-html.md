#  Mayank Pachigar 
##  Manager | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
Mayank Pachigar is a manager in Deloitte Consulting LLP’s Customer Practice, focused on the Industrial Products & Construction sector within the Energy, Resources & Industrials (ER&I) industry. 
Mayank supports clients in enhancing digital commerce and customer engagement strategies, working across B2B and B2C channels. Mayank collaborates with multidisciplinary delivery teams to implement commerce and front-end solutions that advance digital transformation efforts.
With nearly fifteen years of experience across SAP commerce and front-end technologies, Mayank brings a blend of technical and delivery experience and has advised clients as they adapt to evolving customer expectations.
