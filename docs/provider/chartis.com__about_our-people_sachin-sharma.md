# Sachin Sharma
Senior Partner 
Sachin Sharma is a Senior Partner in Performance Transformation at Chartis. He brings more than 15 years of experience helping hospitals and health systems improve financial, operational, and clinical performance through large-scale transformation initiatives. Sachin specializes in leading complex financial and operational improvement programs that drive sustainable margin improvement, operational excellence, and long-term organizational resilience. He has partnered with academic medical centers, integrated delivery systems, children’s hospitals, community hospitals, and faith-based health systems across the country, serving as a trusted advisor to executive leadership teams navigating financial turnaround, performance transformation, and enterprise-wide change.
Sachin brings deep expertise in workforce operations, labor productivity, and enterprise performance improvement. He has extensive experience designing and implementing labor management infrastructure programs, including productivity measurement and benchmarking methodologies, target-setting frameworks, position review processes, and management systems that enable organizations to achieve and sustain performance goals. He works closely with executive leadership teams to identify, prioritize, and implement strategies that improve margins, strengthen operational performance, and build sustainable organizational capabilities.
Throughout his consulting career, Sachin has advised more than 50 healthcare provider organizations and led multidisciplinary teams through financial turnaround, system integration, and enterprise transformation initiatives. He partners closely with executive leadership teams to identify, prioritize, and implement strategies that drive sustainable margin improvement and operational excellence.
Prior to joining Chartis, Sachin was a Director at Berkeley Research Group (BRG), where he led large-scale performance improvement engagements for healthcare provider organizations nationwide.
Sachin earned a Bachelor of Science in Health Information Management Systems from The Ohio State University.

###  Why healthcare? 
One of my first jobs was as a patient transporter at a local hospital, and I have not looked back since. That experience allowed me to interact with patients, fulfill my passion for helping others, and realize that possibilities within the healthcare field are endless. 
###  Do you have a motto or personal mantra? 
“Pay it forward.” I am grateful for what others have done for me and my family, and I use that gratitude as motivation to help others. 
###  One item on your bucket list? 
Visiting Antarctica. It’s one of the most remote places in the world and an adventure that I would like to experience.
