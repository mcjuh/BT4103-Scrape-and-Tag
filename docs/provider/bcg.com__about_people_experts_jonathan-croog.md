### Education
  * MBA, The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania
  * AB, Princeton University


Since joining Boston Consulting Group in 2005, Jonathan Croog has focused on private equity, helping clients evaluate potential acquisitions and improve the performance of their portfolio companies. He leads the "PE ring fence" at BCG, a dedicated team of consultants who support the Financial Institutions practice and business development efforts.
Jonathan has led due diligence projects across a range of industries and topics. In the financial services sector, he has led projects in personal lending and insurance, wealth management, consumer lending, auto lending and credit, and mortgages.
In retail, his due diligence work has focused on multichannel shopping (television, catalogue, and online) as well as building-products businesses (hardwood and softwood flooring). He has also worked on due diligence projects in business services and media.
Prior to joining the firm, Jonathan worked at Mercer Management Consulting, where he focused largely on financial services clients, and at Bain Capital, where he focused on leveraged buyout transactions in industrial and other businesses.
