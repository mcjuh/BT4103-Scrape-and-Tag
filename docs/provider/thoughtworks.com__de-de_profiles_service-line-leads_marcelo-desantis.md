#  Marcelo De Santis
Executive Advisor, Digital Transformation
Alumni
Marcelo is Executive Advisor at Thoughtworks, in this role he works with executives to shape and advance their digital transformation and corporate innovation strategies. As an ICF certified executive coach he is committed to unleash the potential of technology leaders and entrepreneurs. Prior to joining Thoughtworks, he was Chief Information Officer of Mondelez and Pirelli.
Marcelo serves on the Boards of UNICEF, Hispanic IT Executive Council (HITEC) and Angeles Investors.
