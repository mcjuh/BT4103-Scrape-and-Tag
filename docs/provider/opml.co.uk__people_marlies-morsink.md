Marlies Morsink is Oxford Policy Management’s Deputy Programme Director for Thrive, a multi-country research programme supporting the scaling up of early childhood development (ECD) services in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). 
She has extensive experience conducting impact evaluations in low- and middle-income countries and is co-author of _Attributing Development Impact: The Qualitative Impact Protocol (2019)_. She holds a Master’s in Social Policy Research from the University of Bath and is a Fellow in the Department of Social and Policy Sciences. 
Marlies also holds an MBA from the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, and had a career in international investment finance prior to transitioning into international development.

Project
###  [Thrive: Evidence for Scaling Childhood Development](https://www.opml.co.uk/projects/thrive-early-childhood-development "Go to Thrive: Evidence for Scaling Childhood Development")
Thrive is a large-scale, multi-country research and policy programme which aims to build understanding of Early Childhood Development (ECD) service delivery models, at scale, and how they can transform to significantly improve childhood health, nutrition, education and wellbeing outcomes in low- and middle-income countries. Thrive seeks comprehensive, practical answers about how ECD systems innovate, improve, and better serve children and communities.
