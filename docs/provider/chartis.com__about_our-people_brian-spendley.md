# Brian Spendley
Senior Partner 
Brian Spendley is a Senior Partner in Strategic Transformation. He has more than 10 years of experience in healthcare and management consulting, the majority of which has focused on providing strategic advisory services to leading health systems, physician organizations, private equity investors, and their platform companies. Brian’s primary areas of focus are transaction diligence, enterprise strategy and growth planning, mergers and acquisitions, partnership development, economic alignment, value-based care, and network development, as well as capital asset and facility planning. 
Brian’s most recent engagements have focused on partnership and transaction support for healthcare investors and health system clients. He has led the strategic and operational evaluation for multiple healthcare investments to support due diligence for private equity clients, and he played an instrumental role in facilitating the affiliation discussions for a $15B transaction between one of the nation’s largest community health systems and an academic medical center in the Southeast. Further, he has led the development of innovative partnerships to align PE-backed physician groups with health systems across multiple specialties. 
Before joining Chartis, Brian was a managing associate with a consultancy in Washington, DC focused on emergency management and public health preparedness within the public sector. Prior to his career in management consulting, Brian served in a variety of healthcare and nonprofit leadership roles. With Project Health Baltimore (now Health Leads), a nonprofit focused on addressing all patients’ basic resource needs (e.g., housing, food, employment) as a standard of quality care, he provided administrative, financial, and program management support to the Baltimore and National Site. 
Brian earned a Master of Science in Public Health (MSPH) with a concentration in Health Policy from the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health. He also holds a Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Engineering from Syracuse University.

###  Why healthcare? 
Having started my career in healthcare at the age of 16, I’ve seen the direct impact healthcare can have on patients. Over time, I’ve grown to appreciate the immense challenges the industry faces and the opportunity to not only tackle some of these complex challenges, but to do so surrounded by fantastic, passionate colleagues and incredible clients who share a common mission to improve healthcare and the health of our communities. 
###  What has been your favorite project at Chartis and why? 
I recently worked on a partnership between a health system and a private equity backed physician group. From ideation to negotiation to execution, it was a privilege to be engaged and work on a novel partnership that has the potential to serve as a model for other healthcare providers. 
###  One item on your bucket list? 
I would really like to learn Spanish as a second language over the next few years.
