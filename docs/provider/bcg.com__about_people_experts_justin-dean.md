Powering the Energy Transition
Justin Dean 
Justin Dean reflects on the future of energy—and how GenAI is driving transformation, unlocking efficiency, and expanding access to affordable solutions.
### Education
  * MBA, Kellogg School of Management
  * Masters of Engineering Management, Northwestern University
  * BS, mechanical engineering, University of Virginia


### Honors and Awards
  * Top 100 Most Influential Blacks in Corporate America, 2018, Savoy Magazine
  * University of Virginia, School of Engineering, board of trustees


Justin Dean is a leader in Boston Consulting Group’s Energy practice with 30 years of experience working in power and utility sector. Justin oversees BCG North America’s Mid-Atlantic System and is responsible for operations for the firm’s Washington DC, Philadelphia, New Jersey, and Pittsburgh offices. As a member of BCG’s Global Executive Committee, Justin helps shape BCG’s own business and people strategies. 
Within power and utilities, Justin has experience across the value chain—with a focus on electric and gas utility strategy/M&A, field operations improvement, digital/product management, distributed energy resources, large scale renewables, and energy access/affordability. This includes advancing affordable energy access for all, fostering a more inclusive workforce, and addressing environmental considerations. 
Prior to joining BCG, Justin led distribution engineering at NV Energy in Southern Las Vegas and started his career at ABB/ALSTOM Power as a generator repairs engineer. Justin serves on the Global Advisory Board at the Kellogg School of Management and the Board of Trustees at the University of Virginia School of Engineering, and is an active member of the Executive Leadership Council.
