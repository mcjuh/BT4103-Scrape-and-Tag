### Education
  * PhD, chemistry, Imperial College London
  * Dipl-Kfm, business administration, RWTH Aachen


Frank Breitling is a member of the global leadership team for the People & Organization practice at Boston Consulting Group. He is passionate about helping his clients prepare for the future through building world-class, AI-enabled organizations—ones that integrate the best of human ingenuity and skills, advanced data and analytics, digital/AI/GenAI capabilities, and flexible organization design, to optimize employee opportunities and enterprise value.
Frank has extensive experience in large-scale transformation and organization optimization supporting major global financial services, insurance, and health care companies, as well as companies in many other industries.
Prior to joining BCG, Frank worked for Bayer and BASF.
