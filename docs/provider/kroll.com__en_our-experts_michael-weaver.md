# Michael Weaver
##### Managing Director and International Valuation Advisory Services Leader, Testifying Expert
[Michael Weaver](https://www.linkedin.com/in/michael-weaver-a696a78)[+44 (0)20 7089 4773](tel:+44%20\(0\)20%207089%204773)michael.weaver@kroll.com
AT A GLANCE 
Mike Weaver is Managing Director and the International Financial Advisory Leader for EMEA, Latin America and APAC, based in London.
He has over 30 years of valuation and advisory experience, having started his career at PwC in 1995. He left PwC's Valuation and Strategy division in 2002 to start his own valuation business with a number of senior PwC colleagues taking advantage of the continuing move towards independent consulting advice. His business was purchased by American Appraisal in 2005. Mike was with American Appraisal for nine years, eventually leading the European business. He joined Kroll (formerly Duff & Phelps), following the purchase of American Appraisal in February 2015.
Mike has served on the Committee of the Valuation Special Interest Group at the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales. He is a Member of the Royal Institute of Chartered Surveyors' Business Valuation division, and he is an active participant in driving the future of the valuation profession globally through the International Valuation Standards Council (IVSC) and other bodies. He is regularly appointed as an expert witness in international arbitration and valuation related court cases and also by the President of the Institute of Chartered Accountants.
