AT A GLANCE 
Benjamin Wiles is a Managing Director in Kroll’s London office and a senior member of the Restructuring practice. With over two decades of experience in corporate finance and restructuring, Benjamin has led complex engagements across a wide spectrum of industries and financial distress levels.
His career includes extensive review and advisory assignments, supporting businesses through operational and financial challenges. Benjamin brings a unique client-side perspective gained during a secondment at one of the UK’s leading clearing banks, where he worked directly with stakeholders navigating high-pressure restructuring scenarios.
Benjamin’s sector expertise spans retail, leisure, technology, construction, import and distribution, automotive, recruitment and renewable energy. He is a licensed Insolvency Practitioner, a Fellow Chartered Accountant, and is frequently invited to speak at professional education seminars and industry events.
Benjamin led the redevelopment and regeneration of Dreamland Margate, oversaw the strategic administration of [PaintWell](https://www.kroll.com/en/client-stories/historic-painting-retailer-rescued-through-strategic-administration), and managed the cross-disciplinary solution that uncovered over £285 million in missing assets and restore transparency in the largest asset-based lending fraud in UK history - [Arena Television](https://www.kroll.com/en/client-stories/exposing-uks-largest-asset-based-lending-fraud-arena)
Benjamin’s work continues to demonstrate Kroll’s commitment to delivering value-driven solutions in time-sensitive and complex restructuring environments.
