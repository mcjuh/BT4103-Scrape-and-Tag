AT A GLANCE 
Craig Parkin is Associate Managing Director in the Cyber Risk EMEA Security Advisory business, based in London.
Craig leverages more than 20 years of experience in financial services and consulting sectors, with a focus on cyber security, architecture, risk, governance and regulatory frameworks. He has developed and delivered cyber strategies for small and large companies at varying stages of their lifecycle through startup, acquisition, carve out and divestiture.
Prior to joining Kroll, Craig served as Chief Information Security Officer at Global Screening Services (GSS), a regulatory technology startup that delivers software-as-a-service sanction screening to filter banks’ transactions against international sanction lists. Prior to this, he worked as a consultant—most recently for global consulting firm, AlixPartners—before having led highly technical e-trading infrastructure and security teams at RBS investment bank (now NatWest Markets) and Morgan Stanley.
Craig holds a Ph.D. from the University of Cambridge as well as certifications in information security, including Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) and Certified Cloud Security Professional (CCSP).
