### Education
  * Master's, economics, L. Bocconi University
  * Bachelor's, Esade University


Beatrice Lemucchi, a member of Boston Consulting Group's Consumer practice, joined the firm in 2006. She has led a variety of high-profile projects in the luxury and retail arena.
Among her many engagements in apparel (men's, women's, directly-owned stores, franchise, luxury, and wholesale), she has led the transformation of a major Italian luxury brand with the objective of increasing the company's valuation, by leveraging strategic planning, brand assessment, design and setup of the transformation office, development of the new collection, and org review. In addition, she led the transformation of an historical Italian brand to elevate the brand and the product against peers.
Beatrice has over 15 years of experience in management consulting in seven different countries. Prior to joining BCG, she worked as analyst in the Stock Lending department of Banca IMI Sec., New York branch.
