### Expertise
​Ali Cherri is a civil engineer with over 10 years of experience. He has been appointed as a delay expert and has assisted the named expert on more than 15 occasions. 
​​Ali has acted as the lead delay assistant to the testifying expert in arbitration and litigation and has attended multiple hearings. His engagements have covered construction project matters across the Middle East and North America, with disputed values of up to $40 million.
Ali specializes in critical path and delay analyses and can analyse, simplify and present complex construction dispute matters by using concise visual representations to arrive at clear conclusions. He has provided expert witness services and delay analysis work for major construction projects across infrastructure, high-rise buildings, schools, hospitals, mining refineries, and marine.
### Background
Ali has experience working both on-site and within an office for contractors and consultants. He has assisted clients with project management and claims services.
Ali holds a degree in civil engineering with a specialization in project management. He is a member of the Order of Engineers of Quebec, Canada, and is fluent in English, French and Arabic.​
