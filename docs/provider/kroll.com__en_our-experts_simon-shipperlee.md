AT A GLANCE 
Simon Shipperlee is a Senior Director in the Restructuring practice, based in London. He has over 17 years of experience in corporate restructuring and formal insolvency work.
Simon has extensive experience in mid-market corporate insolvency across various sectors and in both formal insolvency processes (principally administrations) and restructuring work. During his career, Simon has developed a specialization in trading administrations, having led assignments across many sectors, including retail, hospitality and sports. He has a particular focus on football, having worked on trading administrations, due diligence reviews and other advisory assignments in this sector.
Prior to joining Kroll, he worked at Grant Thornton where he gained experience in formal insolvencies, fraud investigation and independent business reviews.
Simon is a Fellow of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants and a qualified insolvency practitioner.
