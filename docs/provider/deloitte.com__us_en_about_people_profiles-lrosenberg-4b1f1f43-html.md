#  Larry Rosenberg 
##  Banking & Capital Markets | Audit & Assurance Partner 
Larry is a partner in Deloitte & Touche LLP’s Audit & Assurance practice. He has more than 25 years of experience serving some of Deloitte’s most prominent clients and has extensive experience in leading global audit engagements for multinational SEC registrants. Larry leads Deloitte’s US Audit & Assurance Capital Markets practice.
