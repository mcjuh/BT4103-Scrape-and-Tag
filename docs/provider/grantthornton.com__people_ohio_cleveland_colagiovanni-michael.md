## Executive summary
Mike is a Corporate Tax Partner and Tax Practice Leader of Grant Thornton’s Cleveland office. He specializes in corporate taxes, including income tax accounting and financial reporting matters. Mike is Grant Thornton’s national leader of Rightsourcing services helping clients operate a tax model that delivers elevated value and efficiency to the organization.

### Experience
Mike has over 20 years of corporate tax experience, working with both public and private companies. Mike serves a diverse spectrum of companies in a number of industries, including consumer and industrial manufacturing, distribution, and retail companies. Mike specializes in federal and state tax consulting areas including expertise in accounting methods, research tax credit, multi-state taxation, and international taxes.
