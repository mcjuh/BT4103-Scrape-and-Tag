# Dean Frankle
Managing Director & Partner [ London ](https://www.bcg.com/offices/london/default)


### Education
  * MBA, Harvard Business School
  * BSC, government and economics, London School of Economics (First class)


Dean Frankle leads Boston Consulting Group's Financial Institutions practice for the UK, the Netherlands, and Belgium. He focuses on strategy and large-scale transformation engagements with asset management, wealth management, and retail banking clients. In addition, he leads the coordination of the firm's private equity work with financial institutions for the UK, Europe, the Middle East, South America, and Africa.
