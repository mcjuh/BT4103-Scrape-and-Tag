## Executive summary
Katie is a Partner within the firm's Business Consulting practice, primarily serving technology clients in SaaS, networking and hardware, ecommerce, telecommunications and cloud computing services. She enables large scale, customer-centric business transformations, and delivers solutions that unlock new value streams and position clients for their next phase of organic growth. She helps clients optimize their capabilities, amplify growth in market and accelerate value realization by driving clear visibility to customer needs, increased organizational agility and faster pace to market.
With nearly 15 years of experience in strategy, Katie has served large Enterprises and rapidly growing startups across a wide range of diverse clients. With a background in mathematics and psychology, Katie thrives at the intersection of data-driven insights and enabling customer-centric experiences. She understands how to tailor solutions to meet clients’ needs and has a proven track record of driving significant value creation for clients on a global scale.
Katie is a thoughtful, energetic leader who excels in the full range of management from strategy to execution. She doesn't hesitate to go above and beyond, roll up her sleeves and ensure her teams deliver with excellence to accelerate clients forward.
