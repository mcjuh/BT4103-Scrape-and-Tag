#  Andrea DiGregorio 
Vice President; Director of Accounting Operations 
As vice president and director of accounting operations, Andrea DiGregorio is responsible for the company’s finance operations and reporting, compliance, pricing, procurements, as well as strategic alignment and tactical coordination of administrative issues. She joined Mathematica in 2016 as the controller.
She came to Mathematica from Lockheed Martin, and has held many senior accounting and finance-related positions, mostly with other federal contractors. Andrea holds an M.B.A. in finance, and is certified in project management and cost accounting.
