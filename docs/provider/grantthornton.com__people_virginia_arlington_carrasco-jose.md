## Executive summary
Jose is a partner in the partnership tax group of Grant Thornton’s Washington National Tax Office. In this role, Jose advises client service teams and clients on complex partnership tax matters, including transactions and structuring involving partnerships and technical issues affecting partnerships and their owners, such as disguised sales, mergers, divisions, leveraged acquisitions and distributions, and compensatory partnership interests. He also supports the firm's partnership tax practice through technical training, thought leadership, and the development of resources designed to enhance consistency, quality, and technical excellence across engagement teams.
