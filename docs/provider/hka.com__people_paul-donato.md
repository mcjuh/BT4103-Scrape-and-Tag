### Expertise
Paul Donato has over 20 years of experience in forensic accounting and commercial damages. He has been appointed as an expert on forensic accounting matters on 25 occasions.
Paul has been an expert in actions before the American Arbitration Association (AAA) and United Nations Commission on International Trade Law (UNCITRAL) arbitration, State Courts, and before regulatory bodies such as the SEC, the FBI and the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ). He has conducted financial investigations with transactional activity in excess of US$10 billion and acted as expert on projects with disputed values of up to US$150,000,000.
Paul’s expertise includes forensic accounting matters involving large data sets and complex accounting analysis. His expert services include asset tracing, fraud investigations, employee malfeasance, executive breaches of fiduciary duties, shareholder disputes, construction damages, and complex commercial damages calculations and analyses.
