### Education
  * MBA, business administration and management, SRH Riedlingen


Benjamin Rehberg is the global offer leader for Boston Consulting Group's Tech and Digital Advantage practice and is a member of the global leadership teams of the Tech and Digital Advantage and People & Organization practices. He was formerly leader of the Technology Advantage practice for North America.
Benjamin focuses on the financial services industry, including banking, insurance, and payments, on multiple digital, technology, and operational issues. In addition, he works on technology topics in automotive. He is an expert on AI and GenAI and platform operating models, as well as digital and technology transformation, broader technology, agile, and organization and governance topics.
Prior to his current role, Benjamin was global co-lead for BCG's agile transformation business. He has coauthored numerous articles about GenAI, digital and technology transformation, platform operating model, and agility. Before joining BCG, Benjamin worked as a consultant with IBM Global Services in Germany.
