AT A GLANCE 
Dafydd Evans is a Managing director in the Investment Banking practice and a Board Director of Kroll Securities Ltd, based in the London office. Dafydd leverages more than 25 years of corporate finance experience in assisting UK and European corporates, private equity and owner-managed businesses. He has experience across both M&A and restructuring across the Industrials, Engineering & Materials and Aerospace & Defense sub-sectors.
Prior to joining Kroll, Dafydd served as a leading member of the European Industrials M&A team at DC Advisory Partners (formerly Close Brothers Corporate Finance). Before that he worked for the Industrials Corporate Finance team at PricewaterhouseCoopers. Prior to his career in Investment Banking, Dafydd gained operational experience as a Product Engineer in the steel industry with Corus plc (now TATA Europe) and Alcan Rolled Products Ltd.
During Dafydd’s career, he has completed several high-profile transactions. Most notably the recent sale of Independent Forgings & Alloys (IFA) to Trive Capital, the sale of CSi Palletising to MPac plc, the sale of ecube to Unical Aviation (a Platinum Equity company) and the £212m sale of Pirtek Europe to Franchise Brands plc. 
Dafydd received his M.A. in Materials Science from St. Anne’s College, Oxford University and gained three Oxford Blues in the 1991, ’92 and ’93 Varsity matches. A former rugby player having represented his country, Wales, at Schools, Students and u21 level, Dafydd went onto play for both Pontypool and Newport RFC. Dafydd is a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales.

Investor Appetite in Aerospace & Defense Continues to Grow
Speaking at an industry event focused on aerospace, defense and M&A trends, Dafydd Evans shares his insights on the growing investor appetite for the sector, highlighting the significant increase in interest from both private equity and debt investors over the past two years. He highlights the UK's strengths in innovation, advanced technology and intellectual property, emphasizing the need for continued investment to maintain a competitive edge and support future defense capabilities. Dafydd also explores the evolving M&A landscape and examines the growing involvement of UK and U.S. private equity firms as investment activity across the sector continues to accelerate.
