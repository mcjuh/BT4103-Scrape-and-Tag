# Aesthetic medicine company gets injection of legal support, reducing templates by 84%, and boosting their bottom line
### Axiom lawyers helped:
Consolidate library of Services and Consulting, Confidentiality, and Master Services Agreement templates
Legal team spend less time searching for the right template
### Life is Good
**Picture this… you're a developer, manufacturer, and marketer of the most recognizable aesthetic brands in the world.** Backed by science and driven by innovation, your in-house research and development team creates the products that empower confidence.
### Big Challenge
**But, as you've grown, you've amassed an enormous, complex library of Services and Consulting, Confidentiality, and Master Services Agreement templates.** Your legal team struggles to find what they need, when they need it, and projects are being delayed. You know it's costing you time and money, but consolidating these intricate agreements is a weighty task that would strain your legal team even more.
### Help Arrives
**So, you turn to Axiom.** We send you three highly skilled lawyers who quickly step in, so your legal team can concentrate on the high-value work that aligns with your enterprise goals. Tackling the assignment with surgical precision, they remove duplicate and out-of-date templates, identify key differences, and provide insightful recommendations for improvements. Then, they create new standard templates and fallback clauses, and house them all in one simplified, time-saving repository.
### Life is Even Better
**Now, your mammoth template library is 84% leaner** , with the once-chaotic collection transformed into a smooth, orderly, self-service system. Able to find and update exactly what they need with efficiency and ease, your legal team spends less time searching for the right template and more time focused on project completion – which means greater productivity and profitability for you.
