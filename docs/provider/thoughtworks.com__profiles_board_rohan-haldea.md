#  Rohan Haldea
Director
Pronouns: He / Him
Rohan has served as a member of our board since 2017. He joined Apax Partners in 2007 and is based in the London office. He is currently a Partner in the Tech & Telco team, and oversees Apax India. He also serves as a Trustee of the Apax Foundation, a non-profit organisation affiliated with Apax Partners.
He has both led and participated in a number of key deals including Infogain, Herjavec Group, Coalfire Systems, Fractal Analytics, Grupo Engineering, EVRY, GlobalLogic, iGATE, Tivit S.A., NXP and Orange Switzerland. 
Prior to joining Apax, Rohan was an Associate at Bain Capital in their North American private equity division. Prior to that, Rohan was a consultant at McKinsey and Co.
Rohan holds a Bachelor of Technology in Manufacturing Sciences and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology, New Delhi and an MBA from Harvard Business School.
