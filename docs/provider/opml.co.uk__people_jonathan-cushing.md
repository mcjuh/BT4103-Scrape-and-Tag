Jonathan is a Principal Consultant, and Hub Lead, Health Systems Governance at OPM. 
Jonathan has over 20 years of experience in health governance and health systems strengthening. He has worked on topics including, public private partnerships, policy reforms, procurement systems strengthening and strengthening transparency and accountability within systems. 
Jonathan brings experience of working with a range of partners, including UN agencies, governments, civil society and the private sector in Asia and Africa, including long-term experience in Bangladesh, Malawi, and Nepal. He previously worked with GIZ, and led Transparency International’s Global Health Programme. 
Jonathan holds an MSc in Health Policy, Planning and Financing from LSE/LSHTM, and a BA in Geography and Development Studies from the University of Sussex.
