  * Master’s degree, HEC School of Management

Vinciane Beauchene leads Boston Consulting Group’s global offer on Enterprise AI Transformation, helping CEOs turn AI from a set of use cases into value-creating, enterprise-scale transformations in which humans matter.
Working with BCG X, BCG’s technology build and design unit, Vinciane brings deep functional expertise to the intersection of AI, operating models, and people transformation. She focuses on the non-tech dimensions that determine whether AI delivers real business value at scale, including AI ambition and value priorities, governance and steering, enterprise operating models, end-to-end process rewiring, leadership activation, and workforce implications such as skills, ways of working, and teaming.
Her work centers on AI at scale, enterprise agility, and large digital transformations, with a strong bias toward making the future of human value creation explicit and actionable. She helps organizations clarify how roles evolve in an AI-powered enterprise and build the capabilities required to shift at speed, including reskilling at scale and enabling new ways of working.
A recognized business builder and thought leader, Vinciane has scaled BCG’s Talent & Skills offering into a global platform and has led major AI and digital transformation programs delivering measurable value for clients across industries. She is a frequent speaker and contributor on the impact of AI on work, workforce, and organizations.
Vinciane is a core member of BCG’s People & Organization and Industrial Goods practices. Since joining BCG in 2008, she has worked extensively with clients in energy management, automotive, aerospace, and defense.
