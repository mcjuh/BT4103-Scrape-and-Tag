### Education
  * INSEAD, MBA
  * Msc, Industrial Engineering, Eindhoven University of Technology


Jacqueline Govers joined Boston Consulting Group in 2007. She is a core member of the firm’s Technology & Digital Advantage practice specializing in transactions, and mergers and acquisitions. She also serves as the firm's global product lead for technology in transactions.
Jacqueline’s recent work focuses on technology in transactions (carve-outs and post-merger integrations) and digital and data transformation more broadly. She has led BCG's tech team on several large-scale buy-side carve-outs, and has led digital transformations across industries. She also has hands-on experience running one of these programs as a program director.
