### Education
  * JD, with honors, New York University School of Law
  * BA, with honors, University of Pennsylvania


Jared Feiger is a leading member of Boston Consulting Group's transactions and integrations team and the BCG Transform practice. He is the firm's Asia Pacific leader for carve-outs. Jared has expertise in wholesale enterprise transformation, M&A, integrations, carveout, cost-out, customer experience, people topics, pricing, growth, and value creation.
Jared has acted as the COO, CTO, and overall integration and program lead for multiple mergers, acquisitions, carveouts, transformations, turnarounds, and value creation programs for clients including a Fortune 50 consumer firm, a Fortune 100 high tech firm, an iconic global automaker, a high-growth global services firm, a major travel firm, and several banks and telcos.
Before joining BCG, Jared was the founder and owner of Consulutions, advising clients on business and legal strategy. He also managed several businesses in the automotive, consumer, and technology sectors, worked as an attorney, spent three years at Accenture, and later was the CEO of another professional services firm.
