AT A GLANCE 
Ezequiel is a Managing Director in the Valuation Advisory Services practice in Madrid. He has more than 15 years of experience in corporate finance, valuations and M&A. Currently, he focuses on valuation services on a national and international level. Ezequiel has extensive experience in transactions, having advised in acquisition processes, divestment, mergers, privatizations, etc
Ezequiel has led valuation projects related to transactions (business valuations and acquisition valuations), fairness opinions, intangible assets, impairment tests and purchase price allocations, as well as modeling services for transaction processes. He has performed relevant projects in a wide range of industry sectors. Ezequiel joined American Appraisal (now Kroll ) in 2012. Previously, he worked for Ernst & Young as a senior manager in the corporate finance department and recently for the valuation and business modelling practice. He also worked for a large utility group (Unión Fenosa) as a business development analyst and in a corporate finance boutique. Ezequiel is a speaker at corporate valuation and corporate finance related seminars and conferences, and he has been invited as a guest to renewable energy and transport and logistics sector conferences. **Education**

  * Universitat de les Illes Balears, Economics and Business Administration
