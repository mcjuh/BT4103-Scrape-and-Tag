# Kroll Interactive Valuation Maps
Kroll’s mapping service allows clients to visualize and interpret their location-based data.

## Features
  * Custom online maps incorporate and highlight business data.
  * Accurately positioned and color-coded overlays combine with satellite imagery and other geographic views.
  * Any type and amount of data may be attached to a map, including documents, reports, photos and videos.
  * Intelligent heat map representations automatically emphasize areas of interest or concern.
  * KML exports of all content and geographic data are available for easy loading into risk analysis software, Google Earth or other GIS tools.
  * System is securely accessible online via any web browser, including on mobile devices.
  * User friendly and intuitive design encourages interaction with the map and understanding of the data.
  * Interactive maps make it easy to navigate the system and can include links to attachments, including the following: 
    * Major item asset listing (in any format)
    * Major item asset listing with individual values (in any format)
    * Reports/presentations
    * Photographs
    * Maintenance schedules
    * Asset registers
    * Any other document


## Solutions
  * Insurance valuation maps show facility layouts with clearly identified blocks overlaid on satellite imagery, annotated with values, easing the understanding of complex information and assisting the insured, insurers and brokers.
  * Asset registers/equipment lists can be linked to relevant areas of a facility. This is a great tool for progressive asset management.
  * Heat maps are color-coded to value per meter square, ideal for risk engineers searching for high-value areas.
  * Complete risk portfolio plotting is possible on one global map, aiding accumulation and natural catastrophic scenarios.


  * Key item location plotting is available, ideal for plotting location and values of major items, e.g., machinery breakdown schedule item locations and critical plant schedules.
  * Online satellite imagery plot plans displaying any business-specific information, e.g., muster points, are featured. This tool is ideal for presentations to shareholders, contractors, visitors, etc.
