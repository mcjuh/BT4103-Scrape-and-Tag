#  Infrastructure & transport 
Helping organizations empower communities, move economies and accelerate sustainable business growth 


## Laying the foundations for future transformation
More than merely sectors, infrastructure and transport underpin economies, enable communities and create new opportunities for society to explore and grow. Yet the pressure to deliver faster and smarter has never been greater. As aging assets, funding shortfalls and fractured supply chains collide with demands for decarbonization and digital integration, organizations face the challenge of mobilizing capital and capability at scale. Not just for today, but for a more resilient tomorrow.
Working with public authorities, investors, developers and operators to translate strategy into delivery, we help overcome barriers to accelerate action. Whether shaping national infrastructure programs, securing funding or harnessing the full potential of AI and technology, our teams combine policy insight with data analytics and engineering know-how to help drive performance, from planning through to operation.
Our global infrastructure and transport network unites Audit, Tax and Advisory professionals from KPMG firms around the world to deliver coordinated, multidisciplinary approaches. With deep sector experience across energy, utilities, mobility, logistics and construction, we’re here to help you lay the foundations for future transformation.
