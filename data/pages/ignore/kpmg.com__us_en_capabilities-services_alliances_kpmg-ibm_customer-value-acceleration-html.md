# Customer Value Acceleration
A customized approach to IBM software asset management and reporting
##  What is the IBM Customer Value Acceleration program? 
The Client Value Acceleration Program (CVA) is designed to help IBM clients in optimizing software deployments, understanding contracts, managing product lifecycles and reducing cyber security risk.
KPMG utilizes its ITAM framework to consider the people, process, technology and data necessary to manage IBM assets through their lifecycle while providing the foundational data to support other key functions across the business.
