## Why Climate Change Risk Assessments Are Critical
The effects of climate change are increasingly evident, with more frequent and severe weather events such as flooding, rising temperatures, and heatwaves. These changes, coupled with the urban heat island effect in built-up areas, lead to higher ambient temperatures and increased energy demand for cooling. Integrating flood risk management and climate resilience strategies early in the project lifecycle is essential to manage these risks effectively and avoid costly interventions later on.
Conducting a Climate Risk Assessment during the planning or transaction stage allows developers to understand potential financial exposures and necessary interventions to ensure long-term resilience. These assessments facilitate the development of targeted strategies that reduce the vulnerability of both infrastructure and communities to climate-related impacts.
## Our Approach
AESG’s team comprises climate modelling specialists who utilise the latest data to project future climate scenarios accurately. This enables us to forecast potential climate impacts effectively, integrating climate resilience strategies into your developments. Our holistic approach combines flood risk assessment, overheating assessment, and urban heat island modelling to provide an understanding of climate risks. We also assist clients in developing practical solutions to mitigate these risks, whether through nature-based solutions that enhance the resilience of local ecosystems or net zero carbon strategies that reduce a project’s overall environmental footprint. By addressing climate risks proactively, AESG ensures that developments remain resilient and compliant with evolving environmental regulations.
