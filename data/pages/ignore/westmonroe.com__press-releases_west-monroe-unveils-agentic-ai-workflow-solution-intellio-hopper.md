# West Monroe Unveils Agentic AI Workflow Solution, Intellio® Hopper, to Accelerate Data Teams by 80%
Firm’s newest asset is a groundbreaking tool for companies adopting AI 
CHICAGO – February 4, 2024 – West Monroe, a global business and technology consulting firm, has launched its Agentic AI Workflow solution, [Intellio® Hopper](https://www.westmonroe.com/services/intellio-hopper), to revolutionize how companies modernize their technology and data platforms. As companies across industries expedite their digital transformation to adopt AI, they face highly complex, manual tasks in data conversion and migration. Intellio® Hopper’s cutting-edge technology automates and simplifies this process, reducing the time and effort required for data tasks by 80% and driving significant cost reductions.
Intellio® Hopper is the latest addition to West Monroe’s [Intellio® suite](https://www.westmonroe.com/about/intellio), a proprietary set of AI and data solutions that combines advanced technology with West Monroe’s business expertise to address clients' most pressing challenges. The suite includes tools designed to accelerate business outcomes in data, analytics, and AI, as well as operational technology and mergers. Intellio® Hopper focuses on solving the most time-consuming aspects of data transformation, including code conversion and migration.
“West Monroe’s business-minded technologists are leaning into new technology and fundamentally up-ending how we have approached this work for years,” said **Casey Foss, Chief Commercial Officer and leader of West Monroe’s AI Center of Excellence**.
Intellio® Hopper exemplifies our commitment to building innovative solutions that deliver measurable impact and help organizations adapt to the pace of change.
Casey Foss
Chief Commercial Officer and leader of West Monroe’s AI Center of Excellence
Unlike traditional manual data engineering processes, Intellio® Hopper integrates **Agentic AI** workflows with human oversight, automating tasks such as data modeling, replatforming, migration, and code conversion. Intellio® Hopper seamlessly integrates with modern data platforms like Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, Snowflake, and others, strengthening data foundations and enhancing overall performance. By addressing common data challenges—including complexity, high error rates, and costs—the solution enhances **accuracy** , **consistency** , and **speed** , freeing data engineers to focus on high-value innovation and strategic initiatives.
“At West Monroe, we are focused on practical use cases for LLMs, and our data engineers have leveraged incredible creativity in applying this approach to solve common data challenges as they work alongside our clients’ data teams,” said [**Cameron Cross**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cam-cross-90822727/)**, partner in West Monroe’s**[**Technology & Experience**](https://www.westmonroe.com/services/technology-experience)**practice and co-inventor of the solution**.
Our clients see the value and are adopting these patterns. GenAI is a game-changer for their developers, and Intellio® Hopper is the framework that helps them capitalize and drive efficiency.
Cameron Cross
Partner in West Monroe’s Technology & Experience practice and co-inventor of Intellio® Hopper
West Monroe has seen strong demand and a growing pipeline of clients eager to modernize their data ecosystems. Early adopters of Intellio® Hopper across **healthcare, financial services, and manufacturing** have already reported:
