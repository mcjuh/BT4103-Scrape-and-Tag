# Staff augmentation services
### Access skilled managers and scale your team with confidence
## Strategic staffing solutions for complex project demands
RSM’s staff augmentation services help organizations meet evolving project demands with agility and precision. Whether you're navigating a major transformation, implementing artificial intelligence or managing in-flight initiatives, our experienced project professionals integrate seamlessly into your team, delivering governance, transparency and execution excellence without the delays of traditional hiring.
## Determine business needs and match resources
We begin by understanding your project goals, timelines and internal capacity. Based on this assessment, we match you with qualified project managers, coordinators or program leaders who align with your delivery model and business culture.
## Execute with confidence and control
Our professionals support a wide range of activities, from project kickoff and stakeholder alignment to detailed planning, reporting and risk mitigation. You retain oversight while gaining executional strength and strategic clarity.
### **Benefits of RSM’s staff augmentation services**
  * Accelerated scalability without hiring delays
  * Scope, timeline and budget control
  * Transparent and timely reporting
  * Reduced project risk and issue escalation
  * Seamless integration with internal and external teams


##  Frequently asked questions  

What types of roles can RSM provide through staff augmentation?  
    
RSM offers project managers, coordinators, program managers and portfolio managers across various capacities. 

How quickly can resources be deployed?  
    
Our scalable model allows for rapid onboarding, often within days, depending on project scope and availability. 

Do RSM professionals work under client direction?  
    
Yes. Clients retain oversight and direction, which allows seamless integration and alignment with internal processes. 

Can RSM support third-party or vendor-led projects?  
    
Yes. Our professionals can manage or coordinate efforts across internal teams and external stakeholders. 

What deliverables are included in a typical engagement?  
    
Common outputs include kickoff decks, RAID logs, status reports, stakeholder lists, reporting templates and transition plans. 

Is staff augmentation suitable for short-term projects?  
    
Yes. We support both short-term and long-term engagements based on your needs. 

How does RSM maintain quality and consistency?  
    
Our professionals follow RSM’s Unified Portfolio, Program and Project Management Framework and proven methodologies to deliver quality and consistency. 

Can staff augmentation be combined with other RSM services?  
    
Yes. It often complements advisory, technology and transformation services for holistic delivery.
