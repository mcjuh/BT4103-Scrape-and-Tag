## Green Building Certification Commissioning with AESG's International, Multi Discipline Commissioning Team
With the built environments requirement to respond to Climate Change and energy reduction targets, verifying that building systems perform as intended is crucial for efficiency, sustainability, and occupant comfort. At AESG, we provide commissioning services for a variety of international green building certification programs, including LEED, BREEAM, Estidama, Mostadam, Green Star, and more. Our global experience ensures that your project meets the highest standards of sustainability and performance, no matter where it's located.
## Our Commissioning Services for Green Building Certifications
We understand that each certification program has its unique requirements and regional nuances. Our team is adept at navigating these complexities to deliver commissioning services that align with your project's goals and the specific criteria of the certification you're pursuing.

## Our CommissioningProcess
## Pre-Design Phase
  * Review of the Owner's Project Requirements (OPR) and Basis of Design (BOD).
  * Integration of sustainability goals into the design process.


## Construction Phase
  * Monitoring of installation practices to ensure compliance.
  * Coordination of functional performance testing for building systems.


## Acceptance and Handover
  * Verification that systems meet the performance criteria of the chosen certification.
  * Compilation of comprehensive commissioning reports and manuals.


## Post-Occupancy Evaluation
  * Ongoing monitoring to ensure systems continue to operate efficiently.
  * Assistance with fine-tuning and optimisation based on real-world usage.
