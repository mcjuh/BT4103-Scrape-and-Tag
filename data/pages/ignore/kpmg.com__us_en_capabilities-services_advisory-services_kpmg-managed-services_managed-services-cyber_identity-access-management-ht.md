## Why identity management is the linchpin of enterprise security
Every digital interaction starts with identity. As organizations expand across cloud, mobile, and hybrid environments, managing the “who, what, when” of user access has become more complex—and more critical. Many enterprises still rely on fragmented systems, manual access reviews, and limited visibility, leaving openings that increase both security and compliance risks.
Leading-edge identity programs must enable agility and trust, not slow them down. KPMG helps organizations close the gap between protection and productivity by modernizing how identities are governed, secured, and automated across the enterprise.
## An intelligent, integrated approach to identity management
KPMG Managed Identity Services provides skilled professionals, advanced analytics, and intelligent automation to modernize how organizations create, manage, and control digital identities. Our managed services model delivers full oversight—from workforce and privileged users to customers and partners—helping companies enhance security, efficiency, and compliance while reducing complexity and cost.
**We help clients:**
  * **Strengthen access governance and control** with centralized visibility across users and applications.
  * **Reduce risk and operational cost** through continuous validation and automated enforcement.
  * **Improve user experience and oversight** with frictionless onboarding and self-service capabilities.
  * **Accelerate modernization** with platform-agnostic integration and scalable cloud delivery models.


Core capabilities of KPMG Managed Identity Services
Our managed services model supports the full identity lifecycle with integrated capabilities designed to improve both protection and performance. Core services include:
Access management 
Secure, efficient authentication and authorization for workforce and customer identities. 
Privileged access management 
Continuous monitoring and protection of high-risk accounts, credentials, and infrastructure.
Identity governance and administration 
Centralized provisioning, certification, and policy enforcement for consistent access control across the enterprise.
Analytics and AI-automation 
Intelligent analytics and process automation to detect anomalies, flag excess privileges, and streamline requests.
Platform management and optimization 
Ongoing maintenance and tuning of identity innovations that support evolving business and compliance needs.
## Turning identity control into a business advantage
In today’s cyber climate, digital identity is more than a back-office function. It’s a driver of enterprise speed, security, and trust.
KPMG Managed Identity Services helps organizations:
  * Lower operating costs and complexity through standardized, automated delivery.
  * Strengthen compliance and audit readiness.
  * Improve operational scalability and user experiences.
  * Gain continuous visibility into access patterns and emerging risks.
  * Refocus internal teams on strategic priorities.
