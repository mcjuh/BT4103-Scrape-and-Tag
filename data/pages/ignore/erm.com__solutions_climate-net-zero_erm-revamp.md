## **Decarbonization can unlock hidden business value. The same initiatives that reduce emissions can also reduce operating costs via energy savings and lower maintenance spend, as well as cut the cost of debt via green financing. It can also improve performance, strengthen operational and supply chain resilience, boost energy security and drive enterprise-wide value.**
**How REVAMP unlocks financial value**
ERM REVAMP is our decarbonization value diagnostic to move organizations beyond compliance – delivering measurable sustainability gains and bottom-line impact in the short term. It’s where climate strategy meets business strategy. As broader market and economic uncertainty continue, the ability to leverage decarbonization to drive operational savings and increase resiliency is a significant source of untapped competitive advantage.
**Clients face three main questions** : 
  1. **Is our decarbonization business case financially quantified & investment-grade** **from the executive to the operating level?**_(Confidence to deliver Opex reductions, alignment on how to execute, with clarity across corporate and site level on delivery)_
  2. **Are we unlocking the full commercial value of decarbonization - or leaving strategic benefits on the table? ** _(Beyond cost savings: resilience, customer retention, capital access, and efficiency gains)_
  3. **Are we making the right capital planning choices to accelerate impact, drive savings, and maximize shareholder value?**_(Capex quantum, Capital efficiency, execution readiness, and resource prioritization)_


Successfully answering these questions requires the following building blocks to be in place, to ensure effective delivery of your decarbonization roadmap.
## We focus on program fundamentals to ensure effective delivery of your decarbonization roadmap
### Credible business case
Robust and financially quantified business case, fully informed by and integrated into the business plan.
### Minimise payback period for initial interventions to build momentum
First round of actions must deliver ROI in under 3-4 years, utilising latest technologies, and data insights – efficiency, fleet, heat, buildings, renewables, digital tools.
### Capex carefully calibrated and stress tested
Sufficient capital allocation and financial structuring for decarbonization investments, timed to asset replacement cycles.
### Solid & sustained stakeholder buy-in to deliver the ROI
Consistent buy-in from operational teams at site level, complemented by strong C-level sponsorship.
### Impact demonstration
Ability to track performance against the business plan to demonstrate successful value delivery and maintain commercial momentum.
