# KPMG Private – Tax 
Tax services for privately-owned businesses 
## Special focus on private companies
KPMG Private - Tax Insights
Stay current on the changing landscape to help achieve tax efficiency for privately-owned businesses and family offices
Private business owners know full well that leading their business in times of economic pressures or in times of rapid growth is an all-consuming endeavor. At the end of the day, the last thing you should worry about are tax inefficiencies that could negatively affect your progress. 
Whether you’re a high-growth VC, a PE-backed company, family business, or family office, KPMG Private – Tax is focused on your success, beginning at the entity level through the ultimate shareholder. We offer a full suite of tax services across family-owned and private business structures to help you achieve tax efficiency, no matter where you are in your business journey. 
With changes to tax regulations being the only constant, it’s difficult to stay abreast of the latest local, federal, and global regulations. You can rely on KPMG LLP to help drive tax efficiencies across your business, from the entity level to the ultimate shareholders.
