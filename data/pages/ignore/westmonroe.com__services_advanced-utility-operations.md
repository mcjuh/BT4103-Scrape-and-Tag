# Advanced Utility Operations
Modern utilities need ADMS and AMI to enhance grid reliability, customer experience, and operational efficiency. Are your systems ready?
## Advanced Distribution Management Systems (ADMS) for Digital Grid Operations
The shift to digital grid management requires systems that can handle increasing complexity from distributed energy resources (DERs) and smart technology investments. Our team brings the unique expertise needed to plan, execute, and optimize ADMS and Distributed Energy Resource Management Systems (DERMS) programs. We work closely with utilities to overcome challenges such as aligning stakeholders, improving data accuracy, and developing a DER strategy that meets organizational and regulatory needs. Our approach ensures that your ADMS implementation drives resilient, efficient power distribution with enhanced situational awareness and grid control.
## Next-Gen Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI)
Next-Gen AMI enables a connected, responsive grid with real-time data and advanced applications like load disaggregation, managed EV charging, and predictive maintenance. Our team partners with utilities to develop AMI roadmaps that align with strategic goals, conduct vendor evaluations, and manage multi-year implementations. By optimizing grid-edge capabilities, we help you unlock new use cases and value streams that drive tangible financial benefits for both your organization and your customers.
