# Restaurant Industry
Restaurants face a challenging environment shaped by industry headwinds, evolving consumer expectations, and rising competition. As traffic softens and spending becomes more deliberate, we help leaders stand out and create experiences that keep customers coming back.
Delivering value depends on getting hundreds of small decisions right. Restaurant goers are more digital and less forgiving than ever, while restaurant leaders are being challenged to do more with less. BCG’s experts in consulting for restaurants support brands across marketing, [operations](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/overview), digital, and technology, integrating AI across those businesses. In quick service restaurant consulting and fast casual restaurant consulting in particular, this shift is enabling more consistent execution and stronger results.

## How We Help Clients in the Restaurant Industry
Winning in today’s restaurant environment requires a coordinated approach, powered by [AI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence), where decisions across marketing, price/menu, operations, [supply chain](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/supply-chain-management), technology, and development reinforce one another in real time. Our AI restaurant consulting work helps leaders embed this system across the full value chain.

BCG’s restaurant consulting teams work alongside client leadership teams to drive impact in six priority areas:
Expand All 
Consumer Demand and Growth Strategy
We identify the most valuable customer segments and dining occasions based on a brand’s distinct right to win, then align the organization against a prioritized set of initiatives, brand positioning, and [marketing](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/marketing-sales/overview) to capture demand in an increasingly zero-sum market.
Menu Architecture and the Price/Value Equation
Our data-driven approach moves leaders beyond price-led competition by using AI to optimize menu architecture, [pricing](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/pricing-revenue-management/overview), and promotions, balancing transactions and margins to optimize profitability. By linking these decisions to real-time demand signals, we help brands strengthen perceived value without relying on excessive discounting.
Digital and Guest Experience
An integrated platform across mobile, loyalty, CRM, and ordering creates a data-rich ecosystem. This foundation enables [personalization](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/marketing-sales/personalization) at scale, improves return on marketing investment, and strengthens direct relationships with guests.
Smarter, AI-Enabled Operations
From labor scheduling and demand forecasting to inventory and order accuracy, we embed AI into daily operations. The result is more consistent execution, improved throughput, and performance management systems that ensure every location delivers on the brand promise, every day.
Network and Growth Decisions
We apply AI to site selection, store performance, and format strategy, helping leaders decide where to expand, where to optimize, and where to exit with confidence.
AI and New Ways of Working
Lasting advantage comes from adoption, not pilots. We redesign operating models, decision rights, and capabilities so AI is fully integrated into how the organization works, and can power sustained bottom-line impact.
