##  KPMG and Coupa: Transforming the mid market. 
Mid‑market organizations face a unique set of challenges (both internally and externally) when transforming their procurement operations. Internally, unlike large enterprises with expansive budgets and dedicated transformation teams, mid‑market organizations must deliver on transformational goals with demonstrable ROI across leaner resources and tighter timelines. Externally, these organizations are faced with the challenge of finding the right partner to guide them toward strategic outcomes with tailored solutions that are set to scale as the business continues to expand.
KPMG LLP (KPMG) is proud to introduce our Coupa Accelerate Offering, a dynamic approach specifically designed to empower mid‑market businesses by optimizing their Coupa implementations with business‑led outcomes. Our reimagined delivery model leverages a dynamic team empowered by AI and other proprietary technologies to help ensure a smooth transformation, from kickoff to go‑live and beyond.
## How KPMG can help
KPMG Accelerate provides a structured yet adaptable framework to deliver Coupa engagements efficiently, helping ensure high‑quality outcomes with round‑the‑clock support throughout the engagement. Leveraging our global networks and technology capabilities, we aim to bring the KPMG perspective, experience and “white‑glove” service to mid‑market organizations looking to transform their procure‑to‑pay function.
