Major schemes involve hundreds of suppliers and partners, on projects that often take years to complete. There’s never been greater need for a unified project data platform that helps people to collaborate efficiently.
We developed Fuse to enable a greater level of coordination, collaboration and control of project data. The system provides geographic information, enables spatial analysis and can be customised to present whatever information is most relevant and important to the project team.
