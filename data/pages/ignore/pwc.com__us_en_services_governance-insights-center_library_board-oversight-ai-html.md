# Board oversight of AI transformation
  * July 31, 2026


#### Key takeaways: 
  * **AI oversight must extend beyond technology.** Boards should approach AI as an enterprise transformation affecting strategy, capital allocation, operations, talent, culture, and risk. Clear responsibilities across the full board, committees, and management can help keep oversight coordinated and outcomes focused.
  * **AI will reshape how work gets done.** As companies embed AI and agents into core workflows, boards should challenge management to redesign roles, decision rights, skills, and accountability—not simply automate existing processes. Human judgment, workforce readiness, and employee trust will remain central to successful adoption.
  * **Stronger governance can help AI scale with confidence.** Boards should expect management to prioritize high-risk uses, embed controls from the outset, escalate significant incidents, and report on business value as well as risk. Effective oversight depends on understanding whether AI investments are producing measurable outcomes and supporting the company’s long-term strategy.


##  Six actions for effective AI oversight
AI transformation is reshaping strategy, operations, talent, and risk across the enterprise. Boards can help management stay focused on the decisions that matter most by overseeing AI as a business transformation, challenging where the company should invest, and monitoring whether adoption is creating value responsibly.
  1. Govern AI like a transformation, not a tech initiative
  2. Align on where to lead, lag, or exit
  3. Steer the talent and culture needed to unlock AI
  4. Guide the shift to a workforce of people and AI agents
  5. Oversee the risks and controls that let AI scale
  6. Monitor outcomes and risks
