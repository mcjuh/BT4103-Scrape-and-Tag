## Move beyond conventional outsourcing 
The days of one-size-fits-all operating models are over. Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) and agentic AI are rewriting how the financial services industry functions. Your managed services should do more than just cut costs—they should serve as an engine for innovation and growth.
Deloitte’s Operate to Transform approach can enable just that. We combine industry experience with advanced automation. Our approach is not only flexible, it’s focused on your outcomes—aligning operations to effectiveness metrics. By unlocking new value, we can enable you to pursue your modernization goals.

###  Featured Operate managed services
  * ####  AI and Data Operations 
In banking and capital markets, strong data operations provide competitive advantage. We help firms operate and continuously evolve their data ecosystems, delivering timely, accurate, and auditable data through standardized, automated, and AI-enabled capabilities. The result: a reliable foundation that supports running the bank today while enabling the continuous modernization needed to change it tomorrow.
Offerings:
**•** Data Foundry (Foundation): Build secure and reliable data foundation for timely, confident decision-making
**•** Trusted and Governed Data: Deliver trusted, accurate, and reliable data and processes across the enterprise life cycle
**•** Intelligent Data Operate: Elevate data operations with automation, AI, and user-centric delivery
  * ####  Tax Operate Services 
Advisory and implementation services to help support your organization with an efficient tax operating model design. We can help you with:
**•** Tax department assessments and benchmarking
**•** Transformation through automation and technology solutions
**•** Tax talent and resourcing considerations
**•** Change management support
**•** Cost-savings alternatives
  * ####  Anti-Money Laundering (AML) as a service 
Deloitte's cost-effective AML, Sanctions, and Know Your Customer compliance operations solutions leverage technology and GenAI to help our clients transform their AML compliance operations.
  * ####  Client Reporting 
Client Reporting Operate is an AI-enabled, scalable approach to modernizing client reporting—helping organizations reduce reliance on legacy platforms, manual processes, and technology-heavy change cycles. We offer configurable report design, automated report generation, and QA support to improve accuracy, accelerate time to market, and enable a more self-service reporting model for business teams.
