#  Why Publicis Sapient 
Publicis Sapient is a tech company that provides enterprise AI platforms and services that modernize, run and sustain the world’s most complex and regulated industries.
[](https://www.publicissapient.com/platforms)
## We modernize enterprises, build and orchestrate agentic solutions and keep technology resilient as businesses scale.

## Here’s why our clients choose us:
#1
### We’re committed to outcomes
Our commitment shows up in results delivered for clients: 75% faster modernization, 50% cost savings, 99% code-to-spec accuracy and billions of dollars in new revenue.
[Sapient Slingshot](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/platforms/slingshot.html) takes on the heavy lifting of writing, testing and validating code that teams normally do manually, allowing modernization to move faster and cost less.
#2
### We’re built for execution
We’ve spent 30 years building, running and optimizing systems. We’re singularly focused on [AI that ships, scales and sustains](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/platforms.html) in production. 
Sapient Slingshot deploys and [Sapient Bodhi](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/platforms/bodhi.html) orchestrates agents within live enterprise environments. [Sapient Sustain](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/platforms/sustain.html) keeps systems running once they’re in production. Work ships, stays up and keeps improving over time.
#3
### We’re grounded in experience
Our integrated capability model brings together [20,000+ practitioners](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/company/capabilities.html) across strategy, product, experience, engineering and data & AI—operating as one system rather than isolated functions. 
By pairing people and platforms, we orchestrate human and AI work, embedding intelligence into your enterprise and delivering continuous, outcome-driven transformation.
#4
### Our platforms integrate into the enterprise
[Our platforms](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/platforms.html) run inside and integrate with systems you already rely on—ERP, CRM, data platforms, cloud infrastructure and security controls. 
They deploy securely in your private cloud, on-prem or in a hybrid model. With modular architecture and multi-cloud, multi-LLM support, you can [orchestrate agents across vendors](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/solutions/partners.html) without data lock-in.
#5
### Our platforms are built for complexity
Our platforms are built for [how enterprises actually run](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/customers/stories/rwe-slingshot-legacy-modernization.html)—with legacy systems, regulatory constraints, fragmented data and global scale. 
Instead of forcing enterprises to clean everything up first, the platforms work as-is, which is why they hold up in complex, global environments.
#6
### Our platforms compound knowledge
Each deployment makes the platform smarter by retaining business context and operating knowledge. That means less rework, lower risk and better results the longer you use it. 
Slingshot, Bodhi and Sustain connect to [a living map](https://www.publicissapient.com/content/ps-redesign/us/en/resources/blog/enterprise-context-graph.html) of how your systems, rules and decisions fit together. This means teams can modernize, decide and use AI safely without losing critical knowledge.
