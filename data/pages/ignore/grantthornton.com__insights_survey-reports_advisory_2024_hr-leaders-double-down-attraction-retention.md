#  HR leaders still focused on securing talent 
## Survey-takers also tout value of training, wellbeing
## HR survey report focuses on remodeling, AI use
In our HR Leaders survey, industry pros revealed how their companies are recognizing and responding to today’s workforce challenges through AI use, attention to wellbeing and talent development.
### What's in the report?
  * AI use is increasingly being used in training and development in order to better retain valued employees.
  * Ensuring employee wellbeing is a primary focus, though results may not match expectations.
  * Leaders expect to remodeling their company’s HR functions in the near future.
  * HR plays a crucial role in M&A preparedness, at a time when transactions are expected to increase.
