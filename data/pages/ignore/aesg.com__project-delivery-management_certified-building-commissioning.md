## Ensuring Optimal Building Performance with AESG's Certified Commissioning Team
With the built environments requirement to respond to Climate Change and energy reduction targets, verifying that building systems perform as intended is crucial for efficiency, sustainability, and occupant comfort. At AESG, we offer Certified Commissioning (CxA) services, providing independent third-party verification of your building systems' performance. As a certified commissioning firm, we are uniquely positioned to deliver commissioning services that meet the highest industry standards.
## What is Certified Commissioning (CxA)?
Certified Commissioning (CxA) is a systematic process that verifies and documents that all building systems are planned, designed, installed, tested, operated, and maintained to meet the owner's project requirements. This independent verification ensures that the building performs according to its design intent, enhancing efficiency, reliability, and sustainability.
### Independent Verification
  * Objective assessment of building systems without conflicts of interest.
  * Assurance that systems function as intended and meet performance criteria.


### Process
  * Involvement from the pre-design phase through to post-occupancy.
  * Detailed documentation and reporting at every stage.
