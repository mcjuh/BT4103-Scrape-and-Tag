## Precision Engineering for Complex and Innovative Façade Designs
Geometric modelling and analysis are essential in modern façade engineering, where intricate shapes and unconventional designs are becoming the norm. At AESG, we utilise advanced geometric modelling techniques to turn architectural visions into buildable and high-performance façade solutions. Our expertise in modelling complex geometries ensures that even the most ambitious designs can be realised accurately, efficiently, and sustainably.Using cutting-edge software and computational methods, our team performs detailed analysis to optimise the structural and thermal performance of façades while minimising risks during fabrication and installation. Our approach integrates precision engineering with design flexibility, enabling architects and developers to push the boundaries of what is possible.
## Why Geometric Modelling & Analysis is Crucial
Geometric modelling and analysis serve as the foundation for transforming complex architectural concepts into practical façade solutions:
### Feasibility and Buildability
Ensures that innovative and non-standard façade geometries can be constructed within the system, material, budget and timeline constraints.
### Structural Integrity
Evaluates the structural behaviour of façades under various loads, including wind, seismic, and thermal forces, ensuring safety and durability.
## Thermal Performance
Assesses heat transfer, insulation, and solar gain to optimise energy efficiency and occupant comfort.
### Precision in Fabrication
Reduces errors in the fabrication process by providing accurate geometric data, which leads to better quality control and reduced wastage.
### Cost Optimisation
Identifies opportunities for value engineering by refining complex shapes for more efficient material use and installation processes.
## Our Approach to Geometric Modelling & Analysis
### 3D Parametric Modelling
We use parametric design tools to create flexible 3D models that can be easily adjusted to explore different design options and optimise façade performance. This allows for rapid iteration and refinement of complex geometries.
### Thermal and Energy Simulations
Our team performs thermal modelling to evaluate how the façade interacts with environmental factors, such as sunlight and wind. This informs decisions on materials, insulation, and shading devices.
### Finite Element Analysis (FEA)
We conduct structural analysis using FEA to simulate the response of façade components to various forces. This ensures that façades meet safety and performance standards while supporting architectural intent.
