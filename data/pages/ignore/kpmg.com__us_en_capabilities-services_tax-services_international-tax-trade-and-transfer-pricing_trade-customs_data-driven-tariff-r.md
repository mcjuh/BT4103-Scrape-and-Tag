# Data-driven tariff recovery services
Unlock speed, accuracy, and savings 
Many importers are discovering significant opportunities to recover overpaid tariffs through post summary corrections (PSCs). However, the traditional process is manual, slow, and costly. Original entry filers can charge a high per-amendment cost, and process each one individually, leading to long delays and diminished value, especially for companies with thousands of entries with refund opportunities.
KPMG Trade & Customs offers data-driven tariff recovery services, providing a thorough view of the scope and cost of tariff impacts on your organization. We offer an artificial intelligence (AI) and technology-enabled solution that transforms the tariff refund process, delivering accelerated results, cost-efficient processes, and accurate, compliant outcomes.
##  Our tariff recovery services 
Tariff impact analysis
KPMG helps clients develop strategies to lessen the impact of tariffs by analyzing import and export data. Our proprietary modeling tool, the KPMG Tariff Modeler, identifies areas for risk mitigation and opportunities for savings. We assist companies in assessing the impact of tariffs, including those announced by the U.S. and retaliatory tariffs anticipated on U.S. products globally.
Learn more about how we can help you navigate the complexities of tariff awareness and maintain a competitive edge.
[ Explore our tariff impact analysis capabilities ](https://kpmg.com/us/en/capabilities-services/tax-services/international-tax-trade-and-transfer-pricing/trade-customs/tariff-impact-analysis.html)
Tariff refund identification, automated PSC filing, and recovery services
KPMG has developed a technology-enabled tool that transforms the tariff refund process. Leveraging our proprietary Digital Gateway, we automate the filing of PSCs directly with US Customs and Border Protection (CBP), eliminating manual data entry and dramatically increasing speed and accuracy. This service is ideal for high-volume importers in sectors such as automotive, aerospace and defense, manufacturing, oil and gas, technology, and chemicals. Our quality checks and experienced team provide high-quality filings, reducing compliance risk and increasing refund opportunities.

## The KPMG Tariff Modeler: Your engine for success
The KPMG Tariff Modeler is a proprietary tool that considers the challenges businesses face in monitoring the impact of tariffs for today and plans for tomorrow. This tool provides a differentiated experience, offering:
