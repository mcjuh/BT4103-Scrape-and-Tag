## Where does your enterprise land?
At every stage, what holds enterprises back is structural: disconnected systems, fragmented data, governance that can't keep up with AI. Most land somewhere in the middle, where AI is everywhere, but it isn't creating value at scale yet.

The operating model 
##  AI works inside teams, but it breaks between them 
Product, risk and compliance each run their own AI tools. The trouble starts when a decision has to move between them—there's no shared layer to carry it across, so work gets handed off, slowed or lost. That's the enterprise bottleneck. The enterprises pulling ahead have rebuilt how work crosses functions: modernizing the systems underneath and connecting teams that used to operate in isolation.
