# Slalom Awarded Three Databricks Partner of the Year Awards at 2025 Data + AI Summit
June 10, 2025
[ Share ](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/newsroom/databricks-poy-ai-summit-awards?=2024-financial-services-outlook#shareModal)
**Seattle, June 10, 2025—** Slalom, a leader in business and technology consulting, is honored to announce its recognition as the 2025 Databricks SLED Partner of the Year, Databricks Canada Partner of the Year, and Databricks AMER Industries Business Unit Partner of the Year. Presented at the annual , these awards highlight Slalom's exceptional contributions and innovations in state and local government, Canada and across all industries.
Over the past year, Slalom has demonstrated an unparalleled commitment to helping enterprise organizations build data intelligence through its collaboration with , the Data and AI company. Slalom's approach to developing innovative solutions, enhancing capabilities, and ensuring client success has positioned it as a key partner in the industry. Joint efforts with [Databricks](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/partners/databricks?=2024-financial-services-outlook) have included creating cutting-edge solutions that improve data accessibility and enable organizations to make informed decisions.
"Our partnership with Databricks is vital to delivering transformative data solutions for our clients, and these awards underscore the impact of our collaboration," said Joe Berg, General Manager, Data and AI at Slalom. "We are proud to be recognized for our achievements and look forward to continuing to drive innovation in the field."
“We are thrilled to recognize Slalom with not one but three Partner of the Year awards,” said Kori O’Brien, SVP of Global Partnerships at Databricks. “Over the past year, Slalom has demonstrated an exceptional commitment to helping enterprises unlock data intelligence through innovative, client-focused solutions built on the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform. Their dedication and impact make them an invaluable partner as we continue to empower enterprises across industries to turn data into actionable insights.”
To learn more about the partnership with Databricks, please visit Slalom at booth #642 at [Data + AI Summit](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/events/data-and-ai-summit?=2024-financial-services-outlook) in San Francisco, June 9-12.
**About Slalom**
Slalom is a fiercely human business and technology consulting company that leads with outcomes and champions leaders to dream big and build now. We are trusted by leaders across the Global 1000, many successful enterprise and mid-market companies, and 500+ public sector organizations to improve operations, drive growth, and create value. At Slalom, we believe that together, we can move faster, dream bigger, and build better tomorrows for all.
