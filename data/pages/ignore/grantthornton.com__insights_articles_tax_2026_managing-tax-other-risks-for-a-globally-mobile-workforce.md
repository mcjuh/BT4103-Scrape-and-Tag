# Managing tax, other risks for a globally mobile workforce
August 18, 2026 
Listen to the podcast
Global mobility used to mean formal expat assignments. Today, though, it’s possible for employees at many companies to work from almost anywhere in the world. Remote work, hybrid work, digital nomads and distributed leadership teams have become a permanent part of the workforce landscape, and companies are recruiting talent across borders. As this flexibility increases, so do the tax, payroll, compliance and governance challenges that organizations must manage.
In the latest episode of Grant Thornton’s _D.C. Dispatch_ podcast, tax advisors David Sites, Richard Tonge and Storme Sixeas explore how global mobility has evolved from a niche HR concern into a strategic business issue that demands collaboration between tax, legal, payroll and leadership. Drawing upon their experience advising multinational organizations, they explore why “legacy” mobility policies often fail to address the way people actually work today.
Central to the discussion is the update to the OECD Model Tax Convention commentary, aimed at modernizing an element of the tax system that was codified long before today’s borderless workforce existed. Sites, Tonge and Sixeas unpack recent OECD efforts to address how and when remote work arrangements create a taxable corporate presence in another country, break down what the move towards simplification means for organizations with global workforces and discuss where there is still uncertainty.
The conversation goes beyond policy developments to examine practical implications for business leaders. Our advisors stress the importance of modernizing workforce mobility policies, exploring how companies can balance compliance obligations with the need to attract and retain talent in an increasingly global labor market. In today’s world of digital nomads, travel for employee convenience, cross-border remote work and globally distributed teams, businesses must respond to new questions these situations raise about tax exposure, payroll obligations and regulatory compliance.
Listen to our latest D.C. Dispatch episode to find out more about how the tax landscape is changing and how your organization can better navigate the new complexities of global mobility.
