# Slalom Awarded Two Databricks Partner of the Year Awards at 2026 Data + AI Summit 
June 15, 2026
[ Share ](https://www.slalom.com/gb/en/who-we-are/newsroom/slalom-awarded-two-databricks-partner-of-the-year-awards#shareModal)
Databricks Data + AI Summit – June 15, 2026 – Slalom, the global business and technology consulting company, was recognised as the 2026 Data + AI Platform C&SI Partner of the Year and 2026 Databricks C&SI Insurance Partner of the Year by Databricks at the annual [Data + AI Summit](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit). These awards recognise Slalom’s exceptional contributions and innovations in delivering industry-leading data, analytics, and AI solutions across the Databricks platform. This is Slalom’s 11th Databricks Partner of the Year award. 
"Our partnership with Databricks helps organisations unlock the full value of their data and AI investments," said Joe Berg, Senior Managing Director, Data & AI at Slalom. "These awards reflect the strength of our collaboration and the meaningful outcomes we’re delivering together for clients across industries." 
Over the past year, Slalom has demonstrated an unparalleled commitment to helping enterprise organisations build and scale data analytics and AI applications and agents through its collaboration with [Databricks](https://www.databricks.com/). Through its Databricks Centre of Excellence, Slalom helps organisations modernise legacy data environments, establish governed AI-ready data foundations, and operationalise AI at enterprise scale. Joint efforts with Databricks have included delivering AI and data modernisation solutions powered by the Databricks platform, including governed GenAI frameworks, AI-powered document intelligence, enterprise lakehouse modernisation programs, and industry-specific Brickbuilder accelerators. Together, Slalom and Databricks are helping organisations accelerate AI adoption, improve operational efficiency, and unlock faster access to trusted insights. 
One example of this innovation is Slalom’s [Insurance 360 solution](https://go.slalom.com/insurance-360), a Databricks Brickbuilder accelerator purpose-built for the insurance industry. Developed by Slalom’s Databricks Centre of Excellence, Insurance 360 helps insurers rapidly establish governed, AI-ready data foundations with Databricks-native analytics and AI capabilities. The solution enables organisations to accelerate data democratization, modernise analytics environments, and support future AI-driven customer and operational experiences. 
“The most successful organisations today are those that can turn raw data into real business impact. Slalom, our 2026 Insurance Partner of the Year and 2026 Data + AI Platform Partner of the Year, stands out in helping customers make that leap,” said Kori O’Brien, SVP, Global Partnerships at Databricks. “Their ability to execute complex transformations on the Databricks platform helps our joint customers move faster and put AI to work in meaningful, measurable ways.” 
Learn more about the [Slalom and Databricks](https://www.slalom.com/gb/en/who-we-are/partners/databricks) and visit Slalom at Booth #645 at [Data + AI Summit](https://www.databricks.com/dataaisummit) in San Francisco, June 15-18.
