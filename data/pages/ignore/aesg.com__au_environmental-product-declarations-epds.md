## Empowering Sustainable Choices with Environmental Product Declarations
In today's world, sustainability is not just an option—it's a necessity. At AESG, we are committed to driving sustainability forward by providing Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) services. Our expertise enables manufacturers, suppliers, and project teams to transparently communicate the environmental impacts of their products, fostering informed decision-making and promoting sustainable practices across the industry.
## What are Environmental Product Declarations?
An Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) is a standardised, third-party verified and internationally recognised document that provides quantified environmental impact data for a product across multiple life cycle stages. Based on the principles of Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) and adhering to ISO 14025 and EN 15804/ISO 21930 (for construction products and services) standards, EPDs offer transparent information on the environmental performance of products, including the following mandatory impact categories (as per EN 15804):
• Climate Change • Ozone Depletion • Acidification • Eutrophication • Photochemical Ozone Formation • Depletion of Abiotic Resources (minerals, metals, fossil fuels) • Water Use
EPDs serve as a critical tool for architects, engineers, developers, and consumers to make environmentally responsible choices by enabling the comparison of different products that adhere to the same set of rules/standards (Product Category Rules).

## Key Services
Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)  EPD Development and Verification  EPD Updates and Renewals  Customised EPD Solutions 
## Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)
### Data Collection and Analysis
Gathering data on raw materials, manufacturing processes, transportation, usage, and end-of-life disposal.
### Scenario Modelling
Exploring alternative materials or processes to identify opportunities for impact reduction.
### Impact Assessment
Evaluating environmental impacts across the product's life cycle in accordance to applicable standards and Product Category Rules.
## EPD Development and Verification
### EPD Preparation
Compiling LCA results into a clear and concise EPD document in accordance with relevant standards and rules of Programme Operators.
### Program Operator Liaison
Managing submissions to EPD program operators for registration and publication.
### Third-Party Verification
Coordinating with accredited third-party verification bodies or individual verifiers to verify the LCA and EPD, ensuring credibility and compliance.
## EPD Updates and Renewals
### Periodic Reviews
Updating EPDs to reflect changes in products or processes, maintaining the validity and relevance of the information.
### Re-verification Support
Assisting with the re-verification process as required by program operators.
## Customised EPD Solutions
### Product Category Rules (PCR) Development
Creating PCRs or Complementary-PCRs, where a suitable PCR does not yet exist, enabling the development of EPDs.
### Digital EPDs
Developing machine-readable EPDs compatible with digital platforms and Building Information Modeling (BIM) systems.
### Corporate Sustainability Reporting
Integrating EPDs into broader sustainability strategies and reporting frameworks.
