# Unleash AI with UiPath
Agentic AI can help your organization achieve its goals — if it’s aligned to your goals. We provide that alignment.
Grant Thornton has a clear understanding of strategic business processes across finance, tax, compliance and operations. We use that understanding to design automations for each organization’s size, maturity and regulatory environment. Our designs are guided by governance, data integrity and control frameworks, targeting measurable results without over‑engineering or platform sprawl. This business‑first perspective helps organizations prioritize investment, maximize returns, and maintain value as they evolve.
As a UiPath Diamond Partner, and Agentic Automation Fast Track Partner, we know how to make UiPath empower transformation across finance, supply chain, HR, compliance and other functions. Our end‑to‑end experience encompasses strategy, advisory, implementation and ongoing optimization, with managed services and health checks that can maintain performance and accountability over time.
Grant Thornton and UiPath provide a combination of technology and service that operationalizes AI with explainable and auditable solutions. UiPath capabilities, with Grant Thornton advisory depth, create solutions that perform today and adapt for the growth, change and risks of tomorrow.
**Grant Thornton delivers**
  * Business‑first automation aligned to strategic outcomes and financial impact
  * Deep industry and regulatory insight that informs solution design
  * Integrated strategy, implementation and managed services under one accountable team
  * Responsible AI frameworks embedded into automation from the start
  * Enterprise perspective that supports broader modernization goals
  * Scalable solutions designed to evolve with growth, change and risk
  * Long‑term optimization and support that focus on sustained value
