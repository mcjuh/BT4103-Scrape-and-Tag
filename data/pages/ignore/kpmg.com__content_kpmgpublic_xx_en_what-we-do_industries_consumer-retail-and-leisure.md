#  Consumer, Retail & Leisure 
Helping organizations navigate complexity, unlock growth and build consumer trust in a changing global landscape


## Supporting sustainable growth in a world of shifting expectations
Consumer, retail and leisure organizations are under ever-evolving pressure: margins are tightening, customer behaviors are shifting, and the pace of AI-driven disruption is reshaping how brands connect, sell and grow. For those leading the charge, success is no longer just about agility: it’s about becoming masters of navigating change.
From helping accelerate digital transformation to strengthening supply chain resilience and reimagining the customer experience, we work across the entire consumer goods, retail and leisure sectors to drive sustainable growth built on data, purpose and loyalty.
Our consumer, retail and leisure network unites Audit, Tax and Advisory professionals from KPMG firms around the world to deliver connected, multidisciplinary support. Combining big-picture thinking with tailored transformation, we’re here to help you modernize, stay competitive and build experiences that make the difference.
