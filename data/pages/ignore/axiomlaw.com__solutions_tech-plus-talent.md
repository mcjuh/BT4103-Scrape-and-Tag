More Information on Tech+Talent
## What is Axiom Tech+Talent?
Axiom Tech+Talent combines experienced Axiom legal professionals with rigorously vetted AI tools to help in-house legal teams complete legal work faster, manage greater volumes, and keep lawyers involved in review and decision-making. 
## What are AI-enabled lawyers?
AI-enabled lawyers and legal talent are experienced legal professionals who use approved AI tools to complete legal work more efficiently. Axiom lawyers combine legal judgment with AI-supported research, review, drafting, analysis, and workflow automation, while remaining responsible for reviewing and validating the work they deliver. 
## How is Tech+Talent different from purchasing legal AI software?
Tech+Talent is not simply a software license. Axiom pairs AI technology with trained legal talent and practical workflows, helping clients reduce the time and internal resources required to select, configure, deploy, and operationalize legal AI. 
## What work can AI-enabled legal talent perform?
AI-enabled legal talent can support contract review and negotiation, M&A due diligence, regulatory response, legal research, bulk lease abstraction, policy analysis, document abstraction, contract migration, metadata extraction, and other high-volume or repeatable legal work. 
## Who is responsible for AI policy decisions and the supervision of AI-enabled legal work?
Client legal leadership retains ownership of AI policy interpretation and legal risk decisions. Axiom lawyers translate those decisions into practical operating guidance, while Axiom-managed workflows enforce the agreed review, escalation, and approval requirements. 
## Can AI-enabled legal talent work within existing legal processes?
Client legal leadership retains ownership of AI policy interpretation and legal risk decisions. Axiom lawyers translate those decisions into practical operating guidance, while Axiom-managed workflows enforce the agreed review, escalation, and approval requirements. 
What is Axiom? 
Axiom is a global alternative legal services provider delivering on-demand legal talent, secondments, and AI-enabled legal services to in-house legal departments, supporting both ongoing work and complex legal projects while reducing legal costs and outside counsel spend.
