## Ensure Building Operational Performance with AESG's Multi Discipline Ongoing Building Commissioning Team
Maintaining optimal building performance isn't a one-time effort, it's an ongoing process. You cannot manage what you do not measure and analyse. At AESG, we offer Ongoing Building Commissioning (OCx) services to ensure your building continues to operate efficiently, sustainably, and reliably throughout its lifecycle. AESG has a senior team member working on the ASHRAE SSPC 300 Committee writing the ground breaking new ASHRAE Guideline 0.3, Ongoing Commissioning Process.
## What is Ongoing Building Commissioning (OCx)?
Ongoing Building Commissioning (OCx) is a proactive, continuous process that monitors and tunes building systems to maintain peak performance. Unlike traditional commissioning, which occurs during construction or retrofitting, OCx extends into the operational phase, ensuring systems adapt to changing conditions and usage patterns.
## AESG's Approach to Ongoing Commissioning
At AESG, we employ a systematic and technology-driven approach to OCx consistent with ASHRAE’s developing Guideline 0.3:
### Integration of Advanced Technologies
Implement IoT sensors and smart building systems for real-time monitoring.
### Customised Action Plans
Develop specific measures to enhance performance based on data insights.
### Data Analytics and Reporting
Design strategies for data aggregation, reporting and analysis.
### Continuous Improvement
Regularly review and adjust commissioning strategies to align with goals.
### Collaborative Solutions
Work closely with facility management teams to tailor strategies.
