### Most leaders overestimate what agentic AI can do today
### and underestimate the impact it will have tomorrow.
### Don’t fall into the same trap.
Retail is on the cusp of a transformation unlike any before. Agentic AI, where natural language understanding meets autonomous action, is reshaping how businesses connect with customers, streamline operations, and deliver value.
This e-book explores how agentic AI is redefining the retail landscape, from product discovery and customer journeys to behind-the-scenes operations. Whether you’re a retailer, technologist, or business leader, you’ll find actionable insights to turn disruption into your competitive advantage.
[ Read now ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/content/dam/thoughtworks/documents/e-book/tw_Agentic_AI_in_retail_ebook_digital.pdf)
##  Why this matters 
By 2030, spending on agentic AI is projected to grow from $5 billion to $47 billion, with retail at the forefront of this transformation. This is your opportunity to lead the charge, adapt to disruption, and turn change into strategic advantage. 
## What you’ll learn
#### Structural shifts you can’t ignore
Discover how agentic AI is automating workflows, enabling hyperproductive startups, and giving large enterprises the tools to dominate their ecosystems.
#### Spot the signals, seize the advantage
Uncover the latest developments in autonomous commerce and agent-driven customer journeys to outpace competitors and build stronger customer connections.
#### Future-proof your business
Learn actionable strategies to boost productivity, enhance management with conversational insights, and create loyalty-driven customer experiences powered by agentic AI.
