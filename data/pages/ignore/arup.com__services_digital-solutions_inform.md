InForm is Arup’s data-driven generative design platform that we use on a wide variety of building, masterplanning, and infrastructure projects. It is a cloud-based decision-support platform that connects parametric models, simulations, and performance metrics so teams can explore, evaluate, and compare thousands of design options quickly.
## Key benefits:
  1. **Make better decisions earlier:** Rapidly explore thousands of design options for faster, evidence‑based decisions.
  2. **Reduce risk, delay and costly rework:** Integrate multidisciplinary engineering performance insights early to reduce risk and improve outcomes.
  3. **Build faster alignment around a shared vision:** Collaborate in real time through an intuitive cloud interface for clearer alignment.


At its core, InForm captures a wide range of client ambitions, site constraints, regulatory requirements, sustainability targets, and performance KPIs, enabling designers to generate diverse design options that respond intelligently to complex and often competing criteria. Its powerful analytics engine evaluates each option across critical factors, including daylight, wind comfort, structural performance, energy use, carbon, noise, cost and commercial value, to support evidence‑based decision‑making from the outset.
Through an interactive 3D web interface, clients and stakeholders can explore scenarios, compare alternatives and understand how changing priorities or assumptions influence project outcomes. This creates a transparent basis for discussion, helping teams align around a preferred direction with greater clarity and confidence. By testing more possibilities earlier, InForm can reduce design risk, limit costly redesign and respond more effectively as project objectives evolve. It helps clients identify solutions that balance sustainability, comfort, resilience, technical feasibility and commercial performance, turning complex project requirements into clear, evidence-based decisions.

Case study 
Waterfront Rivium, Rotterdam
For the Rivium Waterfront residential development in Rotterdam, we used InForm to deliver a data‑driven feasibility and early‑stage design study for a highly constrained waterfront site.
The platform enabled rapid exploration of millions of design options, integrating structural, environmental, comfort and commercial performance within a single parametric model.
This evidence‑based approach supported informed decision‑making, leading to an optimised tower massing that reduced risk, improved urban quality and strengthened the project’s business case. As a result, both the client and municipality adopted Arup’s data‑led recommendations and progressed confidently to the next design phase. 
By using Arup InForm, advanced parametric modelling, and AI, we provided the client with a holistic approach and clear, actionable recommendations to designing two building towers. 
Case study 
Elements, Amsterdam
InForm was used to evaluate Elements’ tower geometry against six sustainability‑led KPIs including daylight, sun exposure, greening, wind comfort, energy and embodied carbon.
Multiple parametric studies were run to compare design options, allowing the team to identify the shape that delivered the best balance of performance and environmental quality. This data‑driven process strengthened the project’s sustainability narrative and ultimately helped secure planning approval.

Case study 
Madrid Nuevo Norte, Madrid
For Madrid’s new neighbourhood, Madrid Nuevo Norte, InForm enabled the team to support carbon‑focused urban policy decisions by modelling large‑scale scenarios and quantifying potential impacts.
The tool helped evaluate how neighbourhood‑level planning choices influence embodied and operational carbon, giving stakeholders clear, evidence‑based insight into the implications of different development strategies.
