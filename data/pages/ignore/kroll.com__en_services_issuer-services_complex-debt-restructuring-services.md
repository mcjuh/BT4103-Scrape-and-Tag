# Complex Debt Restructuring Services
Kroll’s Issuer Services practice provides high-quality, comprehensive debt restructuring services by proactively managing the implementation of any restructuring or refinancing initiative.
At Kroll, we understand that navigating complex debt restructurings can be challenging and requires a partner who is responsive, transparent, and focused on your needs. Our Issuer Services team offers custom, comprehensive support for clients through all phases of restructuring or refinancing, delivering clarity, efficiency, and assurance at every stage. We take pride in delivering high-quality, comprehensive solutions that address your unique circumstances and empower you to achieve successful outcomes, no matter how intricate the restructuring initiative.

## Debt Restructuring Services
  * Assisting legal counsel with restructuring mechanics, including drafting any documentation 
  * Identification exercise in advance of the launch of the restructuring 
  * Any lock up process on behalf of the client and any ad hoc committee 
  * Excluding unnecessary paperwork to facilitate participation by investors 
  * Newspaper publications when and where necessary 
  * Online access to documentation via a bespoke and secure website 
  * Information agent and central point of contact for all investors 
  * Communicating terms of the restructuring to back-office personnel 
  * Tabulating and processing investor details with a high level of attention to detail 
  * Paying specific attention to those investors who have agreed to support the restructuring 
  * Authenticating all claims made by investors via the clearing systems/ issuer authorized sources 
  * Calculating entitlements due to investors per position in accordance with the restructuring


## Uncleared Asset Administration
  * Issuance of uncleared and/or unlisted securities as part of liability management exercises or restructuring initiatives 
  * Serving as the official transfer agent for newly issued private securities 
  * Providing administrative agent services to support the entire lifecycle of securities 
  * Granting creditors access to a secure online platform for viewing holdings, downloading documents, requesting statements, processing transfers, and responding to general meetings
  * Managing client service fees, with administrative expenses covered by creditors
