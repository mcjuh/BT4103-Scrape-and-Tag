## Overview
### Built for high-growth companies scaling toward what’s next.
How does PwC help emerging companies scale? By empowering the high-growth and startup ecosystem with deep industry experience and an integrated suite of audit, tax, deal, and advisory solutions purpose-built for high-growth environments. From early-stage funding rounds through IPO readiness and beyond, our multidisciplinary teams deliver the financial rigor, transaction support, and strategic counsel that innovative companies need to navigate complexity, accelerate growth, and make the leap from startup ventures to sector-defining public companies.

### Helping high-growth companies navigate scale and complexity 
PwC’s Emerging Company Solutions team supports emerging and high-growth companies as they scale operations, prepare for financing events, strengthen reporting processes, and develop infrastructure designed for long-term growth.
  * **Audit & Assurance related services** - Audit support for early-stage companies navigating the path to IPO success. We use methodologies tailored to your business risks and stakeholders today and tomorrow.
  * **Tax Strategy & Compliance** - Support growth with tax structures and compliance strategies aligned to business objectives.
  * **IPO & Financing Readiness **- Prepare for investor expectations, public company readiness, and growth-stage financing events.
  * **Technical Accounting & Reporting** - Navigate evolving reporting requirements and complex accounting matters with confidence.
  * **Scalable Finance Infrastructure** - Develop operational processes and finance functions designed to support rapid growth.
  * **Governance & Institutional Readiness **- Strengthen controls, processes, and operational maturity as expectations increase.
  * **Operational Efficiency** - Improve visibility, scalability, and decision-making across the organization.


### Supporting companies through transformative growth moments
High-growth companies face increasing expectations from investors, regulators, and the market. PwC helps companies prepare for critical growth milestones with scalable infrastructure and deep technical experience.
##### During Q4 2025, PwC served:
  * 100% of IPOs > $1B raised 
  * 85% of all IPOs by deal value 
  * 91% of unicorn IPOs by deal value 
  * 4 of the top 5 IPOs 


Source: PwC analysis
### Navigating the journey from startup to IPO and beyond
How can startups prepare for an IPO? It’s complex, resource-intensive and can pull focus from running the business. And it starts with preparation. We bring the experience to help you plan ahead, avoid roadblocks and stay in control from day one.
