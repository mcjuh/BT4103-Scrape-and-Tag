#  Environment Analyst Summit and Awards
23 - 24 August 2022 
Grand Hyatt Denver, Colorado 
[ Learn more ](https://www.globalbusiness-summit.environment-analyst.com/)
Tom Reichert, Global CEO, ERM will be giving the Industry Keynote Address at the Environment Analyst Global Business Summit in Colorado.
The Environmental Analyst Global Business Summit will see leaders and stakeholders in the sustainability industry, coming together to discover how to leverage ESG and net zero opportunities to drive substantial environmental and economic change.
##  ERM at the Summit and Awards
Tom Reichert, Global CEO, ERM will be speaking at 10.15am on 23 August. Read Tom Reichert's interview with Environment Analyst ahead of the Global Business Summit [here.](https://environment-analyst.com/global/108401/global-business-summit-erms-ceo-on-digital-transformation-esg-and-key-megatrends)
ERM has been shortlisted for awards in the following categories:
ESG Innovation - ESG Fusion and CRISP (Climate Risk, Impacts & Solutions Platform)Net Zero Accelerator - Sustainability at the heart Rising Star - Isabella Kong, Assistant Consultant (Corporate Sustainability), ERMRising Star - Archana Somasekharan, Sustainability & Foundation Advisor, ERM
##  ERM Speaker
##  Tom Reichert 
Global CEO, ERM 
Tom Reichert is the Global CEO of ERM as of February 2022, leading a team of 6,500 professionals in over 40 countries worldwide. He is an expert with 30 years of experience in the technology, business, and sustainability consulting services industry.
He has lived and worked extensively across North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia, helping clients transform their businesses. He has delivered outstanding, rapid growth for global businesses and industries, with a particular focus on large-scale change, transformation, enablement, and technology.
