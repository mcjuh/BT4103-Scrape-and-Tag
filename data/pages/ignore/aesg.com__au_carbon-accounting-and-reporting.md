At AESG Australia, we assist businesses in accurately quantifying and reporting their carbon emissions across all operational activities, covering Scopes 1, 2, and 3 emissions in accordance with the Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Protocol and Australian standards.
## Why Carbon Accounting Matters in Australia
Australia is committed to achieving net zero emissions by 2050, aligning with global climate goals and responding to increasing regulatory requirements and stakeholder expectations. Many Australian businesses, particularly publicly listed companies, are now mandated to disclose their carbon emissions annually. Additionally, as part of their net zero strategies, organisations are setting new targets to address Scope 3 emissions through their supply chains, making comprehensive carbon accounting essential for compliance and sustainability.
## Our Approach

Comprehensive analysis of your direct (Scope 1), indirect (Scope 2), and value chain (Scope 3) emissions to provide a clear understanding of your carbon footprint.
[ Benchmarking Assessments ](https://aesg.com/au/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-203a7c16aa0694cddc3e)
Comparing your emissions data against industry standards and Australian regulatory requirements to identify areas for improvement.
[ Net Zero Roadmaps ](https://aesg.com/au/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-daeab306aa0694cddc3e)
Developing strategic plans to achieve your net zero targets, incorporating both operational and supply chain emissions reductions.
[ Stakeholder Engagement ](https://aesg.com/au/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-2142b726aa0694cddc3e)
Collaborating with internal and external stakeholders to gather necessary data and ensure alignment with your sustainability goals.
[ Whole Life Carbon Assessments ](https://aesg.com/au/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-7abe8626aa0694cde1bd)
Evaluating the carbon impact of your projects from design to decommissioning, supporting sustainable decision-making throughout the lifecycle
[ Circular Economy ](https://aesg.com/au/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-203a7c16aa0694cde1bd)
Replace the traditional linear thinking with circular design principles into your processes, systems and design and realise the benefits of circularity that is measurable. 
[ Embodied Carbon Assessments ](https://aesg.com/au/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-daeab306aa0694cde1bd)
Measuring the carbon emissions associated with building materials and construction processes, helping you choose sustainable options.
[ Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) ](https://aesg.com/au/carbon-accounting-and-reporting/#collapse-2142b726aa0694cde1bd)
Providing standardised information on the environmental performance of your products to support transparent reporting and sustainable procurement.
