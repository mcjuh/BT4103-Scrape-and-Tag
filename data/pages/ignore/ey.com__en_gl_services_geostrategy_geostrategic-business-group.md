## What EY-Parthenon teams can do for you
The Geostrategic Business Group (GBG) helps you translate geopolitical insights into business strategy.
The Geopolitical Business Group works with other EY teams to assess the potential impact of geopolitical risk on investments, supply chains, data and other areas of a business. With the breadth of our strategic and operational knowledge, the teams develop actionable plans to help you better identify, assess, help manage and strategize around the political risks you face.
We also help you design and implement a geostrategy at your organization, putting in place the governance and processes that adapt business strategy and broader risk management practices to overcome challenges and seize opportunities associated with an increasingly dynamic geopolitical environment.
To thrive in today’s era of geopolitical volatility, all organizations need a geostrategy — the cross-functional integration of political risk management into broader risk management, strategy and governance.
## Our Political Risk Consulting Services
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Insights briefings
Tailored briefings and facilitated discussions for Boards of Directors, C-suites and their teams, to identify and understand specific political risk developments as well as global trends.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Political risk assessment
Assessment of the likelihood and potential impact of relevant geopolitical developments and other political risks on a company’s business functions and global footprint. Such assessments can be performed on a one-off or recurring basis and can be used to inform risk management, market entry or exit and other strategic decisions.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Geostrategy maturity assessment
Based on the EY-Parthenon geostrategy framework, analysis of an organization’s current geostrategy to conduct a gap assessment, standards against peers and provide recommendations for improvement.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Quantification and dashboards
Identification and curation of key risk indicators (KRIs) to track the probability and impact of political risks occurring and the creation of political risk dashboards to monitor these developments. Such KRIs and dashboards can be used to integrate political risk into broader risk monitoring and reporting systems and communicate to the C-suite and the Board.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Geopolitical strategy
Integration of political risk assessments into an organization’s corporate and growth strategy, including strategic questions around operating models, global supply chains and other business planning considerations.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Political risk due diligence
Bespoke political risk analysis and country risk assessments to integrate into broader diligence processes, such as when deciding whether to enter and exit markets or pursue transactions such as M&As.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Scenario analysis
Exploration of multiple, alternative futures for a geopolitical uncertainty to design, test and refine an organization’s risk management, operational resilience and strategy.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Tabletop exercises
Design and facilitation of a bespoke workshop for Boards, C-suites or other teams to explore the potential impacts and likely crisis responses to a hypothetical political risk scenario. This can include the creation of a crisis response playbook.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Stakeholder engagement
Mapping of relevant stakeholders and associated issues in a particular market, develop a stakeholder engagement strategy and an associated toolbox to reduce the impact of potential political risks.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ##  Geostrategy governance
Creation or refinement of an organization’s geostrategy governance model, from the Board level to various functional teams, following the EY-Parthenon geostrategy framework.
