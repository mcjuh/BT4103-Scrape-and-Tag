#  100 Days In: How Renewable Energy Developers Are Adapting to a New Policy Landscape
April 30, 2025, 12:00 ET 
Webinar 
[ Register ](https://erm.zoom.us/webinar/register/6617443154721/WN_h4fPWvkdSRG71HcLeuQLNg)
Are you tracking the latest developments in US renewable energy policy?
Renewable energy developers in the US face new permitting, regulatory, and development challenges since the new US presidential administration took office in January. But thanks to the industry’s powerful momentum, developers can continue delivering renewable energy projects at pace and scale if they stay agile and well informed.
We invite you to join our upcoming webinar to hear expert insights on how your projects may or may not be affected.
What to Expect: Experts from ERM and Sidley Austin LLP will explore the impacts of the White House's Executive Orders, DOGE, tariffs, and possible changes to the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA). They will draw on their extensive experience supporting the full lifecycle of renewable energy projects to explore the permitting, regulatory, and development challenges that may arise across different technologies.
Who Should Attend?
  * Renewable energy project developers
  * Environmental compliance and permitting professionals
  * Energy strategy and sustainability leaders


##  Speakers
##  Nikki Payne 
Renewables Lead for North America at ERM 
With nearly 20 years at ERM, Nikki leads the North American renewable energy team. She oversees ERM's delivery of technical and advisory services for wind, solar, battery storage, hydrogen, hydroelectric, and nuclear projects. Nikki's team thrives in complex scenario and provides a wide range of skilled support, including siting support, critical issues analyses, impact assessment, international standards, planning, permitting, and engineering.
##  Todd Hall 
Global Renewables Lead, ERM 
Todd oversees service delivery for ERM's global network of clients that develop, build, generate, buy, and sell renewable energy. Todd has approximately 30 years of experience in consulting enabling the delivery of major capital projects.
##  Peter Whitfield 
Partner, Sidley Austin LLP 
Peter is an environmental attorney who assists energy, transportation, oil/gas, and other highly regulated industries w/ regulatory compliance and litigation. He has counseled clients on compliance/permitting obligations with various environmental statutes including the Clean Air Act (CAA), National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA), the Federal Land Policy and Management Act (FLPMA), the Endangered Species Act (ESA), Clean Water Act, and the National Forest Management Act (NFMA), Marine Mammal Protection Act, and the National Historic Preservation Act.
