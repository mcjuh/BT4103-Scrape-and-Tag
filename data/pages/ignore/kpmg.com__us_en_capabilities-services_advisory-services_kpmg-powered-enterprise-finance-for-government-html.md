# KPMG Powered Enterprise | Finance for Government
Transform your finance function faster and with confidence—so you’re ready for whatever challenge comes next.
Infrastructure funding
State and local government finance organizations are under pressure to meet today’s demands while adapting to future expectations. Modern CFOs are looking to extend the role of the finance function beyond transaction processing and reporting to a next-generation provider of insight and strategic value for governments.
You need agility, more value from your technology, and access to the latest data, delivered in real-time, to make critical decisions and to drive financial performance. And, with effective remote collaboration now a mission-critical priority, you need the tools to extract maximum value from your cloud investments to facilitate new ways of working and a people experience that enables sustainable change.
Many have started finance transformation efforts, but teams are stretched thin, many face budget constraints, and transformation can take years. Transformation is complex, but governments are not alone.
## Jump-start your finance transformation
We used our deep public sector finance experience to tailor KPMG Powered Enterprise | Finance specifically to help state and local governments jump-start finance modernization efforts and derive maximum value from your move to the cloud.
With **Powered Finance for Government** , you have immediate access to leading public sector finance practices and processes that help transform critical finance functions with less risk, faster speed to value, and a human-centric experience. The dynamic, pre-configured solution is enabled by market leading platforms and designed to accelerate financial operations, while reducing accounting and forecasting costs, improving working capital and freeing up resources by cutting time spent on manual tasks.
The result is a finance organization that moves smarter, faster, and ultimately provides greater value to government workers, citizens, and communities.
