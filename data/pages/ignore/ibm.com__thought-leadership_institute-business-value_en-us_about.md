# About the IBM Institute for Business Value
[Subscribe for more insights from IBM IBV (will open in a new window)](https://www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/en-us/newsletters)
## What is the IBM Institute for Business Value?
Founded in 2002, the IBM Institute for Business Value (IBM IBV) is IBM’s thought leadership think tank. We provide distinctive, evidence-based intelligence that gives leaders the insight they need to make better business decisions, and the inspiration to act.
IBM IBV has been ranked #1 in thought leadership quality by [Source Global Research](https://linkedin.com/posts/salima-lin-b17bb71_thoughtleadership-content-professionalservices-activity-7313953656985251841-Cgyz?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAAQlWYEB5UYI0Q7lkLuy6G6qc8L1mdsF-0k) for two consecutive years and is the recipient of prestigious awards, including a Gold Stevie Award in thought leadership from The International Business Awards®.
## What is our mission?
We survey and interview 50,000 global executives a year to deliver data-driven insights that enables business leaders to:
  * Make informed strategic decisions based on comprehensive market analysis
  * Navigate emerging technology trends and their business implications
  * Transform their organizations for sustainable growth and competitive advantage
  * Address industry-specific challenges with targeted solutions
  * Benchmark their performance based on industry and regional performance indicators


## What principles guide IBM IBV’s research?
Our research is built on five core principles that ensure credibility and impact:
  * Credibility through evidence
We rely on rigorous methodologies and trusted, credible data to ensure every insight is backed by sound analysis and robust research practices.
  * Authenticity in perspective
We draw from real-world experiences, client engagements, and industry expertise to deliver insights that are relevant, practical, and reflective of today’s dynamic business environment.
  * Reliability in results
Our research is repeatable, consistent, and trusted—built to withstand scrutiny and inform confident decision-making across industries, geographies, and business functions.
  * Objectivity and independence
Our thought leadership is driven by facts and data—not agendas. This independence ensures our insights are balanced and useful for any enterprise.
  * Impact through action
Every research study and benchmarking analysis is designed to not only inform but to enable action, providing clear guidance on what business leaders need to know and what they need to do next.

  * With an analysis of market trends and performance benchmarks that identify opportunities and challenges facing modern businesses
  * In order to deliver strategic recommendations and practical actions based on real-world data

### Component Business Modeling
Component Business Modeling (CBM) is a framework developed by IBM IBV that breaks down business activities into individual components. This approach helps companies visualize their business and identify areas where they can reduce costs, improve performance, and better manage risk.IBM clients can work with their account teams to implement CBM.
