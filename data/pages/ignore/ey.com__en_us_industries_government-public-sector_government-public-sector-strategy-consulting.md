What the EY-Parthenon Government and Public Sector Strategy Consulting team can do for you 
Governments and the public sector are under significant pressure due to economic constraints, changing demographics and rapid technological disruption. To effectively meet mandates for services and diverse population needs, it’s critical that they evolve by adopting innovative approaches, improving transparency and leveraging technology. This evolution is essential for maintaining public trust, enhancing service quality and addressing global challenges.
Read more  Read less 
As the government and public sector industry transforms, EY-Parthenon teams can help you answer some of your most critical questions:
Transformative strategy
  * In what innovative ways can we reimagine our operating model to better serve the public and adapt to evolving societal needs? 


  * How can we transform our strategic approach to build greater operational resilience across government services and public infrastructure?


  * What transformative applications of advanced technology can we harness to drive efficiency and create more effective outcomes for the public?


Transformative transactions
  * What is the right strategy to help reverse the innovation deficit: buy, build or partner? 


  * How can government entities form joint ventures or alliances with private companies to enhance operations and improve public service?


  * How can selling or leasing government-owned assets to private entities generate revenue and encourage private investment in public infrastructure?


Read more  Read less 
EY-Parthenon government and public sector strategy consulting professionals can advise clients on developing transformative strategies by providing insights regarding enhancing operating models, increasing operational resilience or building cutting-edge technology tools. But a strategy alone is not enough: We also help organizations execute strategy through real operational change. We help them transform the vision into reality by leveraging strategic, financial and operational perspectives, based on deep sector experience and firmwide knowledge to help enable end-to-end execution.
We can help government and public sector organizations become more agile and innovative by enabling integrated and efficient operations, accelerating the implementation of public initiatives and providing digital solutions that are adaptable, scalable and responsive to evolving community needs.

  * ##  Federal government
Our federal government team has experience in strategic planning, public policy analysis and operational improvement in government agencies. They focus on enhancing efficiency, driving innovation and maintaining compliance with regulations to better serve public interests. Additionally, the team leverages data analytics and technology solutions to support informed decision-making and effective program implementation.

  * ##  State and local government
The EY-Parthenon state and local government teams specialize in areas such as policy development, program evaluation and financial management to enhance the effectiveness of government operations. They focus on improving service delivery, driving innovation and fostering community engagement while maintaining compliance with regulations. In addition, the teams leverage data-driven insights to support strategic decision-making and enhance resource allocation.

  * ##  Education
The EY-Parthenon public education team specializes in providing strategic insights and solutions to enhance educational outcomes and operational efficiency. With a deep understanding of the education sector, they leverage data analytics and industry best practices to support policymakers, educational institutions and stakeholders in navigating complex challenges. Their knowledge encompasses areas such as curriculum development, performance improvement and resource allocation, so that educational initiatives are both effective and sustainable.
