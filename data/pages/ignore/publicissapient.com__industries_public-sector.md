## The realities of public sector
Public sector agencies are under pressure to deliver modern, equitable digital services that meet rising citizen expectations while overcoming legacy systems, budget constraints and complex regulatory environments.
### Legacy systems limit service delivery
Aging platforms and fragmented processes make it difficult to modernize services, share data and respond quickly to citizen needs. 
### Citizen experiences are inconsistent
Disconnected systems and manual workflows create friction for residents trying to access services, submit requests or receive timely support.
### Scaling data-driven decisions is hard
Without integrated data and analytics, agencies struggle to forecast demand, allocate resources and measure program outcomes effectively.

Deploy assistants to help residents find information, navigate services and complete requests more easily.
