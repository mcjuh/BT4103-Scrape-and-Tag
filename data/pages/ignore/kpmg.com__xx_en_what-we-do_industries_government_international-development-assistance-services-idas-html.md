## A multidisciplinary approach to complex challenges
KPMG firms’ dedicated International Development Assistance Services (IDAS) practice works to address challenges faced by the international development community. Working with development organizations on a variety of projects; helping them run effectively and efficiently as organizations, and to realize their aims and deliver programmatic impact, the IDAS practice brings leading global and local professionals to work together to deliver a range of services that are tailored to meet the development sector’s unique needs.​
The IDAS Institute is our Global Center of Excellence that supports KPMG firms in the pursuit of international development opportunities, primarily with:​
  * multilateral development banks,
  * bilateral donors,
  * the United Nations System​,
  * and nonprofit organizations (e.g., foundations and NGOs).


KPMG’s global network of member firms operates in 139 countries and territories and has more than 275,000 people around the world, providing Advisory, Tax, and Audit services to industry and global capital markets and are committed to supporting the global effort to reach the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) by 2030.
