# Tax function transformation
In a digital-first world, forward-looking businesses need to transform the tax function to reduce costs, streamline workflows, and maintain a competitive advantage. 
##  Strategies and solutions for tax transformation 
Effective tax function transformation means more than rethinking old processes and adopting new technology. It starts by defining a target operating model for tax that aligns with your organization’s capabilities and strategic objectives. KPMG has the in-depth tax knowledge and technical experience to help you make the right choices—from deciding what functions should be outsourced to implementing innovative tax technology to improve performance.
