# KPMG and Saviynt
Cloud identity and governance built for simplicity and scale.
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2022/kpmg-and-saviynt-alliance.pdf)
Share
As the explosive growth of digital identities across the IT landscape continues, combining multiple identity management capabilities into a single cohesive platform becomes critical.
Unifying controls and risk management for every identity, app, and cloud is more than a nice to have; it forms the basis of continuous compliance. Onboarding people, applications, and machines in minutes and selectively turning on access and governance functionality as needed following principles of least privilege helps to secure your critical data and resources.
KPMG LLP (KPMG) helps large, complex organizations with a wide variety of Identity and Access Management (IAM) - services spanning across assessment, strategy, implementation, and managed services.
KPMG and Saviynt have established an alliance to bring the Saviynt Enterprise Identity Cloud to organizations that want rapid deployment, based on zero trust principles, to help simplify identity audits with an identity governance framework, cross-application Statement of Direction (SoD) management, and automated controls monitoring.
To learn more, visit [Saviynt](https://saviynt.com/ "Saviynt")
