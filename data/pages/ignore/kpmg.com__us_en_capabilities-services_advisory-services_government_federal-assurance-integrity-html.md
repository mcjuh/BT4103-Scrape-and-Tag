## Elevating Federal risk management
Enhance public trust in the Federal government’s resilience and mission success with cutting-edge risk management solutions, program governance, and oversight strategies for accountability.
##  Building trust in Federal assurance and integrity 
Federal government agencies face mounting pressure to proactively and efficiently manage risk, fortify against fraud, and enhance program integrity while simultaneously achieving their mission and maintaining the public’s trust. So where do Federal leaders begin? 
### Transforming risk management
KPMG LLP (KPMG) professional consulting services enhance risk management across government organizations, promoting a culture of integrated, risk-aware decision-making and bolstering operational resilience. KPMG professionals specialize in optimizing government program design, operations, and oversight to ensure mission success, heighten public trust, and increase accountability. 
KPMG can help your agency transform at the enterprise and programmatic levels in the following areas:
Risk Management
Modernize your approach to risk identification, assessment, and management into an integrated, risk-aware strategy that bolsters operational resilience. We guide this transformation with industry-leading risk operating models and partner with top technology providers, elevating your risk and program governance to unparalleled heights. Our suite of services encompasses enterprise risk management; governance, risk, and compliance; and, internal controls transformation.
Program Integrity
Integrate the design, operations, and oversight of government programs to achieve mission success and improve public trust, accountability, and oversight. Our services include program governance, eligibility, and operations; oversight and monitoring; and, performance audits.
Fraud Risk Management
Our Forensic Services enhance your fraud detection and prevention capabilities by utilizing a custom Fraud Risk Management framework, allowing your agency to craft a robust anti-fraud strategy, informed by data insights and strengthened by the latest technology. Our services include fraud risk management maturity model assessments, fraud risk management services, and fraud data analytics and technology.
Investigations
Acting quickly and decisively to prevent, detect, and respond to fraud, misconduct, and noncompliance concerns is essential to minimize disruption and loss. We work with you to mitigate risk, restore confidence, and preserve stakeholder value. Our services include Investigative program setup, reactive investigations, and ongoing compliance investigations.
