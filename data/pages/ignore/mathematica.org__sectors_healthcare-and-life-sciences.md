# Healthcare and Life Sciences
## Data governance and analytics to strengthen decisions and outcomes
[Connect with an Expert](https://www.mathematica.org/sectors/healthcare-and-life-sciences#landing_contactusform)
  * [Related Staff](https://www.mathematica.org/sectors/healthcare-and-life-sciences#landing_staff)


Mathematica helps healthcare and life sciences organizations make confident, evidence-based decisions in complex, highly regulated environments. With more than 50 years of experience in health policy and data analytics, we support leaders as they navigate change while maintaining trust, quality, and compliance.
By combining deep domain expertise with advanced analytics and responsibly governed AI, we work alongside partners to modernize data systems, improve interoperability, and strengthen governance. Our teams deliver research, advisory services, and technology solutions that help organizations generate objective insights and operate effectively within clinical, operational, and regulatory constraints.
Across the healthcare ecosystem—from providers and health systems to life sciences companies and risk-bearing entities—we help organizations integrate data, set strategy, and advance solutions that improve health outcomes.
