#  Private Equity Investing 
Through all M&A cycles, KPMG can help Private Equity investors enhance value and returns. 


We fully understand the expectations and rigorous demands relating to all aspects of private equity ownership. With the need to generate real value for your investors being clear, we can help you make smarter decisions and achieve your goals.
Our practice advises Private Equity clients across all aspects of the investment lifecycle. This includes raising funds, deal sourcing and investment in new assets, portfolio company performance improvement, exit planning and realisation, and audit, tax and regulatory advice. We go to market with a multi-disciplinary approach coupled with deep sector-specific expertise. Our UK client base ranges from domestic mid-market PE funds, to the largest global multi-asset alternative investment firms.
