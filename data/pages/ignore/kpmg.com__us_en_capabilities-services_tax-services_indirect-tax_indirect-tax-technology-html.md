##  Transform your indirect tax function 
Today's indirect tax landscape across the globe is changing at record pace. Tax administrations are constantly striving for visibility into the end-to-end supply process through technology tools that automate the tax reporting process, from e-invoicing to digital reporting to e-accounting. In addition, today's competitive business environment is requiring companies to thing outside the box for new revenue streams as well as leveraging new technology to automate business processes to drive increased revenue and a reduction in costs. Indirect tax touches all of these areas.
KPMG Indirect Tax Technology professionals can help transform your corporate tax organization from merely providing reactive administrative support services to delivering proactive thought leadership and bottom-line contributions. Our team has diverse backgrounds and skill sets that enable us to help build you a leading, thorough tool that will meet your indirect tax needs.

##  Brazil tax reform is here - are you prepared? 
The Brazilian tax reform introduced the most sweeping changes to the country's indirect tax system in decades. The seven-year transition to a dual value-added tax system began in October 2025 and businesses are facing a complex transformation that impacts nearly every part of their operations.
KPMG has developed a Brazil tax reform simulator which can help organizations anticipate tax impacts, strategize pricing, comprehend operational changes, and manage tax complexities during this transition. By using data-driven insights and interactive dashboards, the simulator supports proactive planning to help mitigate risks and identify opportunities arising from the reform.
