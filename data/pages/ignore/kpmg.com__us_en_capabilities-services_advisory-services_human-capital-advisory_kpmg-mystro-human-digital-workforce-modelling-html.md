# Orchestrate your human + AI workforce with KPMG Mystro
Unlock the potential of your workforce with AI-driven insights from KPMG Mystro™. Transform the way you manage human and digital labor for sustainable growth. Mystro enables AI-driven analysis and activation across human and digital labor, allowing organizations to rapidly model their total workforce, including both human and digital employees. By improving visibility of your Total Workforce, Mystro helps you reimagine, realize, and reinvent the workforce to unlock growth. 
##  Normalize and optimize human and digital work across the organization 
KPMG Mystro uses 3 billion labor market data points across 15,000 roles to deconstruct human and digital labor into tasks and activities. You can see who’s doing what, where it’s being done, and what it costs, then spot the AI opportunities that matter most. That’s how you find savings, efficiency, and better outcomes, without guessing.
