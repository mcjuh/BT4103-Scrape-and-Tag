# Transform your business with a focus on people
### Take a human-centered design approach
June 13, 2024
## What is human-centered design? 
### It’s a strategic approach that prioritizes the needs and behaviors of users when developing products, processes and services. HCD leverages behavioral science, user research and analytics to provide new insight into how to make user experiences more intuitive and impactful.
In a world of rapidly advancing technology, a focus on people could offer your business a real competitive advantage. That’s true whether you’re struggling to retain market share, improve customer interfaces or build the right team for growing your organization. Human-centered design (HCD) puts your customers and employees at the center of each process and interaction, delivering experiences that feel more satisfying and drive deep brand loyalty.
Your organization can leverage HCD to:
  * Attract and keep top talent
  * Gain new customers and increase customer loyalty
  * Develop the products and services customers want
  * Create brand messaging that resonates with key audiences
