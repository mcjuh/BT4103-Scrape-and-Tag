# Digital Marketing
In an ever-changing world, consumer needs and expectations are rapidly evolving. And digital marketing organizations must understand these shifts, and respond quickly to them. BCG helps companies develop the human and technical capabilities that foster speed, precision, and growth.

Digital channels now function as core social and economic infrastructure, enabling brand continuity in online spaces. As expectations for digital engagement deepen, [CMOs](https://www.bcg.com/executives/cmo-agenda) face growing pressure to elevate the performance of their digital marketing strategy and capabilities.
The key is to be built for the future, leveraging the right mix of human and technical enablers: the skills, processes, marketing analytics, KPIs, and ways of working that create value and growth. With so many pieces to fit together, finding the ideal framework for digital marketing might seem like an impossible goal. But it can be done.
By taking a bionic approach, we help identify, develop, and implement all the components for digital marketing transformation. Our digital marketing consultants work with clients to define outcomes—and build an [agile](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/agile-at-scale), integrated, sustainable digital marketing framework for achieving them. We can do this because our expertise spans the breadth of strategy, technology, and digital optimization:
Expand All 
Digital Marketing Strategy
Mastering digital marketing and [personalization](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/marketing-sales/personalization) means reaching the right customer with the right message through the right channel. This raises tough questions: How do we target customers effectively? How do we segment our customer base? How do we leverage digital marketing trends? We help companies uncover the answers—and develop an end-to-end digital marketing strategy.
Marketing Technology
The vast array of technical enablers offers many possibilities—but a lot of ways to get it wrong. We match companies with the tools that boost their capabilities in the ways that matter most. We help clients decide when to build, buy, or rent. And we ensure that their marketing technology stack is never more complex—or costly—than it needs to be.
First-Party Data
Companies collect an unprecedented amount of data directly from their customers: transaction histories, online browsing behavior, loyalty program activity—the list goes on. This revolution in first-party data enables more personalized and more compelling marketing, but only when companies use the data in responsible and innovative ways. Our digital marketing consultants—working with technologists, scientists, programmers, engineers, and designers from [BCG X](https://www.bcg.com/x/)—help clients develop the use cases, data governance, tech platforms, and ways of working that let them access, integrate, and unleash first-person data. So they can create both trust and value. 
Vendor Evaluation
With thousands of providers in the marketing technology space, companies can quickly feel lost as they look for the right partners. We serve as a conduit between clients and vendors—something we’ve long done in our nonmarketing engagements as well. We constantly evaluate what is available, as well as the provider landscape, to identify the right vendor for the situation.
Acceleration and Optimization
Marketers must adapt to rapidly shifting customer behavior and expectations. We work with companies to develop the processes and ways of working that foster speed and responsiveness. We bring a test-and-learn approach to marketing—so businesses can incorporate feedback and make changes quickly—while instilling an agile mindset.
Measurement
Brand marketing is a powerful financial lever—yet most companies still can't connect spend to future revenue, or get the real-time signals needed to optimize campaigns before budgets are spent. Which campaigns drove real purchasing decisions, and which touchpoints moved the needle?
Through our comprehensive solution, First-Fast Response (FFR), we help companies cut through the noise with rigorous measurement that ties brand marketing activity to financial outcomes. FFR delivers rapid, needs-based insights and strategic guidance on consumers’ likelihood to choose a brand across key purchasing contexts—enabling organizations to refine creative strategy, sharpen campaign effectiveness, and drive stronger brand performance and ROI.
A Foundation for Growth
Digital marketing will always be an evolving discipline. We lay the foundation for continual improvement: developing the [platforms](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/digital-platform), [skills](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/people-strategy/talent-skills-strategy), and governance that support marketing personalization and data-driven marketing. We also deploy best-in-class measurement, so CMOs can gauge how their marketing initiatives are performing and know where to focus to maximize returns.
