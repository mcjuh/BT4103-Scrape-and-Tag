# Real Estate Valuation
Kroll Real Estate Advisory Group combines accounting, finance and real estate expertise in performing real estate valuation and consulting for an array of purposes.
As one of the market leaders in real estate valuation, Kroll offers extensive technical and industry expertise, as well as local market knowledge. Because we are a global practice, we have the scale and geographic coverage to meet our clients’ needs.
We provide valuable support for acquisitions, dispositions and financing, enabling clients to make business decisions based on accurate, timely and defensible information.
The [Kroll Real Estate Valuation Solution (REVS)](https://www.kroll.com/en/tools-and-platforms/kroll-revs-real-estate-valuation-solution) is a single point of access for the full valuation process—combining portfolio insights, benchmarking tools, workflow automation, valuation management and embedded expertise in one integrated environment. Real Estate Valuation Services
Valuation Management
Kroll’s valuation management services deliver clarity, consistency and control across complex real estate portfolios through independent valuations, portfolio analytics and regulatory‑aligned reporting.
Financial Reporting for Real Estate Transactions
Kroll provides independent real estate valuations and purchase price allocations to support ASC 805 and ASC 820 financial reporting with accuracy, transparency and regulatory compliance.
Tax Reporting for Real Estate Transactions
Kroll delivers independent real estate valuations to support tax reporting based on fair market value, ensuring defensible positions and regulatory compliance.
Cost Segregation
Cost segregation services offer companies the opportunity to maximize tax depreciation benefits related to the construction or acquisition of real estate and minimize the risk of audit exposure.
Fairness & Solvency Opinions for Real Estate Transactions
Kroll provides independent fairness and solvency opinions for real estate transactions, helping boards and stakeholders meet fiduciary obligations, manage risk and support defensible decision‑making.
Mortgage Lending Appraisals
Kroll Real Estate Advisory Group provides an array of mortgage lending services, including asset valuation, as well as searches and surveys for verification and certification of compliance with various standards and regulations.
Right of Way Appraisals
Kroll Real Estate Advisory Group provides a full array of independent right of way appraisal services.
Financing and Debt Advisory
Kroll Real Estate Advisory Group assists clients in securing initial financing or restructuring of their existing debt obligations secured by real estate holdings.
Portfolio Valuation
Kroll specializes in assisting clients with the valuation of alternative investments, specifically securities and positions for which there are no "active market" quotations.
Non-Performing Loans Services
For the large and growing distressed market, Kroll Real Estate Advisory Group provides non-performing loan services which bring together business, financial, legal, technical and real estate competencies into a single solution of exceptional quality and effectiveness.
Real Estate Restructuring/Repositioning
Kroll provides real estate consulting and restructuring services to companies with real estate holdings that are financially distressed.
Real Estate Debt Valuation Services
Kroll provides independent real estate debt valuation services, supporting investors, lenders and asset managers in an evolving credit environment.
