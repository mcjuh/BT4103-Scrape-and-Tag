AESG conducts Air Quality Assessments (AQA) to evaluate the impact of proposed developments on local air quality. Our assessments identify existing pollutants and establish a baseline against which potential impacts from new developments can be measured. By proactively managing air quality concerns, we ensure that your projects not only meet regulatory standards but also contribute to enhancing environmental health.
## Why Air Quality Assessments Are Important
With growing concerns over urban air pollution and its effects on public health, integrating air quality management into project planning is essential. Air Quality Assessments are required by local and national planning authorities to determine whether a proposed development could negatively affect local air quality. These assessments help developers minimise their contribution to air pollutants such as particulate matter (PM10, PM2.5) and nitrogen dioxide (NO₂), ensuring safer and healthier environments for communities.
## How AESG Supports Air Quality Management
AESG’s Air Quality Assessments are designed to meet both planning requirements and environmental goals. We offer standalone assessments or integrate them as part of a broader Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) to ensure a holistic approach to environmental compliance. Our team works closely with developers to propose practical, science-based solutions that mitigate negative impacts on air quality, safeguarding the well-being of local communities and ecosystems.
