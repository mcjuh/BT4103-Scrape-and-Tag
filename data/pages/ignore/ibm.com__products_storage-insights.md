Optimize data for at least 40% cost savings by managing with AI recommendations, business SLAs and policies.

User-friendly interface 
An intuitive, user-friendly interface allows you to manage dashboards, reports, automations and data-bound SLAs as needed, while also monitoring the performance, health and capacity of all your storage systems.
AI-powered analytics 
By analyzing trend and data patterns, proactive AI-powered analytics can predict potential issues ad failures before they happen, preventing downtime and improving performance.
Automated problem resolution 
With automated alerts and troubleshooting, built-in AI recommendations guide users through problem resolution, helping to reduce manual intervention and improve operational efficiency.
Integration across storage solutions 
With support for a wide range of storage devices, including IBM and non-IBM systems and solutions, IT teams can assemble a central, comprehensive view of their entire storage estate within a single observability dashboard.

##  Our products 
IBM® Storage Insights 
The data observability dashboard for monitoring basic health, capacity and performance of your block storage.
IBM® Storage Insights Pro 
Get more detailed data storage management for proactively monitoring your storage environment with automated alerts and inline threat detection for safeguarding against potential ransomware attacks.
IBM Spectrum® Control 
The most comprehensive data observability dashboard package provides monitoring and analytics on premises for multivendor storage solutions and in the cloud with Storage Insights Pro.
##  Other use cases 
Data protection 
Recover data quickly and without interruption in the event of a cyberattack. IBM Storage FlashSystem uses IBM Safeguarded Copy and IBM Cyber Vault to create air-gapped copies of data and restore production fast.
Lights out data center 
View all your data in one place and rely on AI-driven data tiering. Powered by IBM Storage Virtualize, FlashSystem makes it easy to manage data across all systems while modernizing your legacy storage capabilities.
Storage data analytics 
Faster storage analytics means faster time to insight. Process your data and gain deeper insights at lightning speed, whether on premises or in the cloud, by using tools built with flash storage analytics.
