## On-demand webcast description 
This webcast will explore key findings from the Q1 2026 CFO Survey, a quarterly pulse study of senior finance executives across industries. The discussion will examine how CFOs are responding to shifting economic conditions, evolving cost pressures, and continued investment in technology and digital transformation.
Drawing on survey responses from more than 200 senior finance leaders, the webcast will highlight trends in economic outlook, investment priorities, workforce planning, and finance operating models. Participants will gain insight into how finance leaders are balancing growth objectives with cost management while navigating persistent inflation, customer price sensitivity, and changing expectations around outsourcing and digital transformation.
