##  Government contractors need experienced advisors who understand their industry and will deliver customized solutions that fit their needs. 
Government contractors face a range of challenges when navigating a multifaceted business and regulatory environment. The good news is you don’t have to go it alone. Work with the right advisor, one who understands the nuances of working with the federal government. 
Being successful is about more than just financing, it’s about mitigating business and operational risk. You must adapt to shifts and trends in technology and cybersecurity. You need guidance on filing bid protests and addressing employment-related issues such as the expansion of the mobile workforce and working with subcontractors. Critically, contractors must keep pace with changes in government administration and its evolving policies, rules and regulations. It’s a balancing act, but RSM can help. 
We understand the complex challenges you face and provide in-depth, industry-specific knowledge to solve them successfully. Our government contracting services include guiding clients through regulatory compliance issues, assisting in an acquisition or divestiture, enhancing value during the yearly audit and assessing the tax ramifications of regulatory changes. Our government contracting-focused professionals can also advise you across a wide range of disciplines including valuation, M&A, cybersecurity and technology to increase efficiencies and improve performance. 
##  Government contractors benefit from our specialized experience in:  

Technology  
    
We advise technology clients in establishing the cost and regulatory infrastructure to secure funds and execute contracts in their pursuit of developing next-gen technology. We’ll also provide guidance on protecting your company’s valuable intellectual property. 

Business and professional services  
    
Business and professional services firms face steep competition for federal contracts. Our indirect rate optimization strategies help government contractors offer more competitive pricing to win more contracts. 

Manufacturing  
    
Complex cost accounting requirements and an increased regulatory focus on supply chain transparency has complicated business for manufacturing contractors at all tiers. Our professionals offer in-depth strategies and services customized for this key sector. 

Life sciences  
    
Funding from agencies like HHS, BARDA and NIH for R&D efforts and clinical trials introduce USG compliance requirements on otherwise commercial companies. We’re highly experienced at standing up compliance infrastructures so you can focus on your practice. 

Nonprofit  
    
Our in-depth experience with complex 2 CFR 200 interpretations, F&A rate/NICRA preparation and negotiations and USG procurement standards will help you maximize cost recovery and comply with award terms and conditions. 

Construction  
    
Navigating prevailing wage requirements, negotiating change order proposals, Request for Equitable Adjustments (REA) and other contract disputes claims are business as usual for construction contractors. We’ll help you reach successful contracting resolutions.
