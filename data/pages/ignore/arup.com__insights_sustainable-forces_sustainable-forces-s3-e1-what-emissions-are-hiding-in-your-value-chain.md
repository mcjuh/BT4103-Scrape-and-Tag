#  Sustainable Forces S3 E1: What emissions are hiding in your value chain? 
The property industry is a major emitter, with the built environment responsible for nearly 40 per cent of global greenhouse gas emissions. But what’s driving this massive footprint? Scope 3 emissions – those hidden, indirect emissions hidden within complex value chains.
