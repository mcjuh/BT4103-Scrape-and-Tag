# KPMG AI Technology
Building a scalable and trusted foundation for AI adoption
The opportunity to operationalize AI at scale begins with building a strong AI Technology Foundation that integrates AI with Cloud and Data. High-quality data, advanced technology and trust come together to help to ensure that AI and other emerging technologies can deliver actionable, accurate and reliable outcomes that create real value.
As your business scales and adopts AI, it is crucial to develop a sustainable and adaptable AI Technology Foundation that helps you transition from an intelligent organization to an autonomous enterprise, ensuring it remains future-ready to meet evolving business needs.
##  Anchoring on a few focus areas can get your AI technology foundation started on the right foot.
Invest in AI Infrastructure for scalability, orchestration and trust
Investing in AI infrastructure is essential for effective orchestration of resources, ensuring seamless integration and optimized workflows, while also fostering trust through robust security and compliance measures. 
Establish the AI Technical Architecture
Strategically define a robust foundational tech architecture to maximize performance and ensure adaptability. A modern and common set of patterns, tools and technologies ensures consistency, enhances efficiency and scalability, streamlines development and deployment, and optimize costs.
Build the data foundation for AI
Evaluate your data landscape to ensure you have the foundations needed for your AI journey. A continuously updated, robustly enriched, and contextually self-verified data corpus ensures accurate, up-to-date responses for evolving business needs.
Implement AI Operations & Governance
With robust AI Operations in place, AI systems can maintain consistent performance and deliver ongoing value, handling complexity, improve efficiency and enhance outputs. This includes implementing strong risk and governance frameworks, embedding security, and continuous monitoring.
