# Process Agent
Global Process agency services to support companies entering legal contracts governed by New York, English, Hong Kong or Singapore law.
Navigating legal contracts can be complex, particularly when those contracts are governed by laws from jurisdictions where your company does not have a physical address. This is where process agency services come into play.
Kroll provides vital process agency services for companies entering legal contracts governed by New York, English, Hong Kong or Singapore law who do not have an address in the Country. Process agencies, also known as process agents or agents for service of process, provide specialized services designed to receive legal documents and notices on behalf of a company.
When companies engage in international contracts, it is often a legal requirement to appoint a process agent in the jurisdiction where the contract is governed. This ensures that all legal documents, such as court summons, legal notices and other critical documents, are received and handled promptly, even if the company does not have a physical presence in that jurisdiction.
Kroll receives documents and notices related to legal proceedings on behalf of our client and promptly sends them to the client, ensuring they can fulfill their legal responsibilities efficiently and effectively.
