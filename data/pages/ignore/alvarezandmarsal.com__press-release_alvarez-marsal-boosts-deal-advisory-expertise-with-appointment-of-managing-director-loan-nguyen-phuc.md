# Alvarez & Marsal Boosts Deal Advisory Expertise with Appointment of Managing Director Loan Nguyen-Phuc


_The senior hire brings more than two decades’ experience across sectors to meet sustained client demand in France for pre- and post-deal services_
**Paris, 19 th September, 2025 – **Global professional services firm [Alvarez & Marsal](http://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/) (A&M) has appointed[**Loan Nguyen-Phuc**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/loan-nguyen-phuc "Loan Nguyen-Phuc") as a Managing Director to the Deals team of its [Corporate Transformation Services](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/corporate-transformation) practice. The senior hire, made with immediate effect, marks the latest expansion of the business unit in [France](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/global-locations/france) – now at 46 team members – as A&M responds to continued demand from corporates and private equity firms for complex transformation programmes.
Working closely with [**Guillaume Martinez**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/guillaume-martinez), Managing Director and Corporate Transformation Services Practice Lead in Paris, Mrs. Nguyen-Phuc specialises in pre- and post-deal services to help clients create value.
Most recently a partner in KPMG’s Integration and Separation practice, where she created and led the Finance M&A Advisory team, she has helped CFOs and finance professionals navigate complex transactional environments, transformation of the finance function in periods of hypergrowth, and performance optimisation.
**Mr Martinez commented** : ‘We continue to attract top senior talent in Paris to meet sustained client demand for value creation in both organic and inorganic situations. We’re delighted to welcome Loan to our fast-growing Deals team – her in-depth expertise will be vital to leading and executing complex transformation programmes across the transaction journey.’
With more than two decades’ experience in finance, industry and advisory firms, Mrs. Nguyen-Phuc brings insights from the Media, Software, Technology, Hospitality and Pharma sectors. The seasoned professional has also held several senior positions as agency CFO, Group Controller and EMEA Internal Audit lead for a major player of the Communication sector
**Mrs Nguyen-Phuc said** : ‘A&M is known for its hands-on, delivery-focused approach to transformation. Having acted as an interim CFO in transactions myself, and led major finance transformation projects, including ERP implementation and cost optimisation programmes, I know the value clients place on results and accountability. I’m keen to join a Deals team of such like-minded professionals.’
