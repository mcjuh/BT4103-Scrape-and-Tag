Axiom can help in-house teams add flexible real estate legal capacity for day-to-day leasing work and larger portfolio initiatives.
## How can legal teams manage real estate work during restructuring or distressed markets?
During restructuring or distressed market conditions, legal teams may need help with lease defaults, workouts, foreclosures, REO matters, amendments, renewals, subleases, terminations, and portfolio rationalization. Axiom can help legal departments add real estate support for urgent matters, overflow work, and portfolio reviews.
## What should legal teams review in commercial lease negotiations?
Commercial lease negotiations often require review of rent, term, renewal rights, assignment and sublease rights, default remedies, operating expenses, maintenance, indemnity, insurance, exclusivity, use restrictions, termination rights, and dispute provisions. Axiom can help in-house teams manage lease review and negotiation workflows at scale.
## How can legal teams support construction, planning, and development projects?
Legal support for construction, planning, and development can include review of design and construction agreements, AIA documents, zoning or planning issues, permitting support, project risk allocation, financing documents, tax incentives, and dispute avoidance. Axiom can provide additional capacity for transactional, operational, or project-based real estate work.
## What real estate compliance issues should companies monitor?
Real estate legal teams may need to monitor environmental, health and safety, OSHA, disclosure, reporting, zoning, permitting, finance, and transaction-related compliance issues. Requirements can change quickly, so in-house teams often benefit from added support for regulatory tracking, contract updates, and issue-specific review.
