# Public Safety and Justice Services
People first, mission always. We help you serve and protect your communities in a better, faster, safer, and more efficient way.
At KPMG LLP, we understand that the public safety and justice ecosystem is changing rapidly to meet the complexity of today’s evolving societal challenges, and staying ahead requires a strategic approach that puts people, safety, and mission above all else.
We have a team of over 100 partners and professionals that are serving state, county, and city agencies across law enforcement, corrections and community supervision, rehabilitation and reentry, and judicial/legal sectors. As a team comprised of former practitioners, senior executives, and leading consultants, we bring a deep appreciation of the challenges you face and the capabilities of a multi-disciplinary firm to help you deliver practical, measurable results. 
Driving actionable strategies and measurable outcomes.
We help you elevate impact and efficiency in keeping your officers, staff, and communities safer. We help you overcome challenges across facilities, workforce, populations, operations, programs, technology, and budgets. We bring you scale, speed, skill, and a track-record of demonstrated results.
Law enforcement & first responders

## Our offerings:
#### Law enforcement & first responders:
  * _Defining Strategy_ : Strategic organizational and facilities master planning development and delivery (Districts/Stations/Posts)
  * _Improving Operations_ : Staffing/overtime/labor market analysis and response time reduction programs
  * _Delivering Solutions_ : CAD/NG911/RMS/CMS systems modernization and analytic/automation/AI use cases


#### Corrections & supervision:
  * _Defining Strategy_ : Strategic organizational and facilities master planning and development and delivery (Prisons/Jails)
  * _Improving Operations_ : Staffing/overtime/labor market analysis and consent decree/settlement implementation
  * _Delivering Solutions_ : OMS/JMS systems modernization and analytics/automation/AI use cases


#### Rehabilitation & re-entry:
  * _Defining Strategy_ : Strategic organizational and facilities master planning and development and delivery (Clinics/Treatment Centers)
  * _Improving Operations_ : Clinical productivity, rapid diagnostics, and efficiency programs (Clinic and Community/Mobile teams)
  * _Delivering Solutions_ : EHR/CMS systems modernization and analytics/automation/AI use cases


#### Judicial & legal:
  * _Defining Strategy_ : Strategic organizational and facilities master planning development and delivery (Courts/Complexes)
  * _Improving Operations_ : Case processing improvement and high-utilizer program analytics/implementation
  * _Delivering Solutions_ : CMS/ERP systems modernization and analytics/automation/AI use cases
