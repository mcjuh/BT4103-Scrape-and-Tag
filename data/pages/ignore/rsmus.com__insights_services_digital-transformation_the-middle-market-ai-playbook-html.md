##  Key takeaways 
AI is rapidly transforming key operations in all industries, creating new avenues for growth.
There is no single strategy for AI success—several factors combine to drive AI value.
Thoughtful planning is important for readiness, governance and alignment with business goals. 
Artificial intelligence tools and solutions have quickly become essential for sustainability and growth in the middle market, driving opportunities to enhance innovation and build a competitive advantage. While AI is now a critical element of business success, there is no one-size-fits-all approach to optimize value from the technology. Instead, effective AI strategies require a combination of several factors, including confirming data readiness, establishing responsible governance, identifying compelling use cases and leveraging the right skills and experience.
"Companies recognize that AI is not a fad, and it's not a trend. Artificial intelligence is here, and it's going to change the way everyone operates, the way things work in the world. Companies don’t want to be left behind."
**Joseph Fontanazza,** Risk Consulting AI Governance Leader, RSM US LLP

##  Frequently asked questions (FAQs)  

What is the RSM middle market AI playbook?  
    
Our AI playbook provides key insights and advice to guide organizations through the adoption, implementation, governance and management of AI solutions. The playbook analyzes how AI is advancing in the middle market, with key considerations for AI readiness, use cases, strategy, governance, implementation, integration, managed services and support. 

How can CXOs achieve enterprise AI adoption?  
    
[C-suite executives can prepare companies for successful AI implementation](https://rsmus.com/insights/services/digital-transformation/ai-for-cxos.html "C-suite executives can prepare companies for successful AI implementation") by focusing on several trends and themes that can inspire successful AI deployment with measurable long-term results. These include developing an AI implementation plan that aligns with business goals, establishing effective AI governance, integrating AI into existing workflows, and focusing on change management and user adoption. 

What is an AI readiness assessment?  
    
An [AI readiness assessment](https://rsmus.com/services/digital-transformation/ai-readiness-assessment.html "AI readiness assessment") is a tool to help evaluate how prepared an organization is to adopt and scale AI. It typically measures areas like data quality, infrastructure, talent, strategy and governance. The results help identify gaps and guide planning for successful AI implementation. 

What are the risks of AI for a growing business?  
    
[Responsible AI implementation](https://rsmus.com/services/digital-transformation/artificial-intelligence/ai-governance.html "Responsible AI implementation") and ongoing governance is essential to achieve anticipated value from the technology. Potential risks include data privacy concerns, regulatory compliance challenges, ethical issues and model bias.  

What are the first steps for bringing AI into my organization?  
    
Effective AI implementation is not a one-size-fits-all project. [Key considerations for AI success](https://rsmus.com/insights/services/digital-transformation/4-steps-to-integrating-ai-strategy-and-implementation.html "Key considerations for AI success") include understanding what business problems to target with AI, what solutions can best address those issues, how to analyze the return on investment and how to put your strategy into action.

Our $1 billion investment in AI is not just for advising clients, but for transforming how we deliver tax, audit and compliance services. Examples include our [myRSM Tax ecosystem](https://rsmus.com/services/business-tax/myrsm-tax-ai-powered-tax-technology-ecosystem.html) with its licensable [PartnerSight®](https://rsmus.com/technologies/partnersight.html) platform, our [RSM Luca](https://rsmus.com/services/audit-services/rsm-luca.html) digital audit ecosystem and our RSM Atlas compliance automation platform.
