#  Serving on a higher education institution board 
## Gain the foundational knowledge you need to succeed
## Higher education board guidebook
Serving on the board of a college or university is both an honor and a responsibility. As a trustee, you must remain focused on providing good stewardship of the institution’s mission, reputation and resources. The responsibility that comes with providing this stewardship need not be overwhelming; when you begin your board tenure with a solid overview of what you need to know and what will be expected of you, your orientation to trusteeship should go smoothly.
**What’s in the guidebook?**
  * Trustee roles, responsibilities, and liabilities 
  * Keeping the board effective
  * Accountability and integrity
  * Financial stewardship 
  * Operating procedures
