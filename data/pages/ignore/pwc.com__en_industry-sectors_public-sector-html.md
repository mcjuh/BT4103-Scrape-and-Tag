### Transformation
As a public sector manager, you’re faced with some momentous challenges. You need to make your administrative practices more flexible, carry out complex transformations of your operating model, use new technologies and protect sensitive data. At the same time, you need a targeted approach to develop your organisational culture, your employees and yourself. We help you master these tasks and shape change in your industry for the long term – drawing on our many years of experience, our tried-and-tested services and our broad network of experts, both in Switzerland and worldwide.

### **Sustainability**
Global warming, water shortages, a scarcity of raw materials, health problems, migration, conflicts – the list of problems caused by climate change is long, and is getting longer every year. First and foremost, sustainability doesn’t only refer to environmental issues, but also includes social and governance components (ESG). The demand for ESG-compliant behaviour also requires sustainable solutions from the public sector. This is essential in order to meet the increasing demands of citizens and other stakeholder groups. Through our experience and our active approach, we help you make the most of the potential offered by sustainability and further develop your organisation in all areas involving ESG.

### Employer branding
The shortage of skilled workers is affecting all sectors, and the public sector is no exception. It’s becoming increasingly difficult for you as a manager of a public authority to fill job vacancies. As a result of the growing staff shortage, there’s a risk that the provision of public services will decline in terms of performance and quality. That’s why we’ll show you how you can use targeted employer branding to promote the well-being of your employees, increase their willingness to perform, create an inclusive and integrative working environment and position yourself as an attractive employer organisation. This way you can position your organisation for the work of the future and tackle the shortage of skilled workers head on.

### **Cybersecurity & data protection**

### **Indirect taxes & value added taxes**
What is the input tax deduction for investments in the community? These and similarly complex tax issues are increasingly occupying public administrations. Our VAT team has obtained a landmark ruling from the Swiss Federal Supreme Court for a municipality in the canton of Zurich. According to the ruling, municipalities, cities and cantons can claim input tax from the Federal Tax Administration on investments which have already been made that have resulted in taxable income. This also opens up VAT opportunities for new investments. Our experts across Switzerland will help you implement regulatory changes so that you can ease the burden on your operations.
