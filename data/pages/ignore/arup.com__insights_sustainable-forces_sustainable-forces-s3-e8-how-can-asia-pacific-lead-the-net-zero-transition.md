#  Sustainable Forces Special Episode Momentus 2025: How can Asia Pacific lead the net zero transition? 
Arup, with support from the Sydney Opera House, was proud to host Momentus – a special annual event that connects great minds and explores bold actions for a sustainable future.

In this special edition of Sustainable Forces podcast, we take you live to the event where Dr Michelle Dickinson is joined by [Hilde Tonne](https://www.arup.com/contact-us/hilde-tonne/), Chair of the Arup Group Board, and Zoe Whitton, Managing Director, Head of Strategy and Impact at Pollination, who share their unique perspectives on how the Asia Pacific region can assume global net zero leadership.
Highly vulnerable to climate impacts and in need of productivity levers to fuel future economic growth, the region is uniquely positioned to lead the net zero charge.
Hear from our panellists as they share their insights and respond to questions from leaders across the private sector, government, community and academia in a thought-provoking discussion on how we can collectively create momentum to address the challenges of climate change.
