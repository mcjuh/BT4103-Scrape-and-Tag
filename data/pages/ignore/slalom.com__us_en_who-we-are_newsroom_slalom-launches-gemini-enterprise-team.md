# Slalom Launches Gemini Enterprise Team 
June 11, 2026
[ Share ](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/newsroom/slalom-launches-gemini-enterprise-team?=2024-financial-services-outlook#shareModal)
**SEATTLE — June 11, 2026** — Slalom, the global business and technology consulting company, today announced the launch of a new team dedicated to helping businesses adopt and scale Gemini Enterprise, built through a deep collaboration with Google Cloud. The team is designed to help organizations integrate the latest Gemini Enterprise capabilities into the products, services, and experiences that shape how their customers engage.
Slalom’s relationship with Google Cloud spans more than decade, during which Slalom has received nine Google Cloud Partner of the Year Awards, earned an [advanced competency in Artificial intelligence](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/services/artificial-intelligence?=2024-financial-services-outlook) and built a team of more than 500 certified Google Cloud consultants.
“Slalom’s new team focused on Gemini Enterprise is helping customers tackle one of their most urgent questions, ‘How do we use AI to deliver something better to the people we serve?’” said Sean Everhart, Managing Director at Slalom. “Google Cloud’s investment, enablement, and support help us bring the right resources to clients and answer this question at scale.”
The enterprise AI conversation to date has largely focused on internal efficiencies, transforming business processes, and reducing cost. An even larger opportunity, and harder problem to solve, is using agentic AI to reimagine the employee and customer experience. The organizations getting there fastest are tackling three things at once:
  * Modernizing legacy systems so AI has the right foundation to scale


  * Building AI-ready data pipelines that turn enterprise context into real-time intelligence


  * Creating adaptive operating models that [move AI from pilot to production](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/services/artificial-intelligence/ai-transformation?=2024-financial-services-outlook) in weeks instead of quarters.


Success shows up in AI that reaches customers and employees, agentic workflows that accelerate how products are designed and brought to market and modernized platforms that give development teams the context they need to ship faster.
Slalom's Gemini Enterprise team brings these elements together, helping clients realize outcomes that can drive real, measurable benefits for the people they serve.
"Organizations are moving past AI experimentation and demanding solutions that deliver tangible value for their customers," said Victor Morales, Vice President, Global System Integrators Partnerships, Google Cloud. "By deepening our partnership with Slalom, we will help businesses build and deploy enterprise-grade AI solutions that drive real growth and transformation."
“Backed by Gemini Enterprise, our relationship with Google Cloud, and go-to-market support, and amplified by Slalom’s industry expertise and presence across 30+ markets, the collaboration helps clients build and scale enterprise-grade agentic solutions that can deliver measurable impact,” said Everhart.
Learn more about [Slalom’s collaboration with Google Cloud](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/partners/google-cloud?=2024-financial-services-outlook).
