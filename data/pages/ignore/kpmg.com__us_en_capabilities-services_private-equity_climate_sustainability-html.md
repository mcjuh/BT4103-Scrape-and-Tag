# Sustainability in private equity
Driving value for private equity firms and their portfolios
KPMG understands the investment lifecycle and has experience embedding ESG across all phases. Our established approach to support private equity firms, funds, and portfolios meet regulatory and other stakeholder demands while unlocking value and creating future-fit firms and portfolios.
## Our service offerings include:
ESG and climate strategy
Includes a clear ESG policy and framework for the firm, fund, and portfolio companies. The focus is on the integration of ESG into investment processes and ownership practices and integrating ambition into the tone at the top.
Fundraising strategy
The focus is on fund-level ESG and climate strategy aligning to the firm and limited partner (LP) requirements as well as best practice (reporting) frameworks.
Origination and investment
Includes the integration of ESG and climate factors into origination and pre-investment decision-making processes, including compliance considerations.
Portfolio company onboarding and 100-day plan
The application of an ESG and climate lens is designed to create and preserve value. Findings from diligence is integrated into post-investment 100-day and value creation plans.
Ownership period
Involves working with portfolio companies to execute the ESG and climate value creation plan / roadmap and implement processes to monitor and report progress and comply with regulatory and other stakeholder reporting requirements. The application of an ESG and climate lens is used for deal re-financing / debt.
Exit
ESG and climate has become a strategic deal value driver and key part of the equity story, resulting in higher exit multiples. KPMG can apply exit readiness experience to help yield value from your ESG story.
