## Employees’ expectations about where, when and how they work have changed
We know our people can thrive when their daily experience matches what they value and makes them happy — meaningful relationships, a varied workload, support for flexibility and a sense of purpose and belonging. Our brand-defining people experience is designed to fundamentally rethink the work experience in our profession to attract, retain and develop leading talent so that we can serve our clients, fuel our growth and set a new standard for the business community. Just as we’re relentlessly client focused, we’re unwavering in our ongoing investment in our people experience.

## How we support our people
We are committed to reimagining the way we work — using technology to personalize careers while keeping client impact front and center. Our people are the heartbeat of our firm, and we’ve listened closely to understand what they need to grow, thrive and succeed. We’re creating a more personalized career experience — one that evolves with our people. From tailored growth and rewards to built-in well-being and flexibility, we support every stage of their journey.
Well-being
Our people will be supported by different career paths, development curricula and pay, including longer-term incentives and protected time off to recharge between workload or career sprints.
Development
Our people will have greater opportunities to grow, to practice new skills, and to then work on different teams and new assignments of interest. We will elevate leadership and coaching skills for everyone at all levels.
Total Rewards
Our people can easily choose from a wide range of benefits — designed to support every stage of life and career. From physical and mental health to financial, social and emotional well-being, there's something for everyone.
Always a PwCer
Our people can pursue professional paths that excite them — whether inside or outside our firm, and we'll invest in the incredible community of individuals who have worked at PwC.
## Explore our careers
We’re inspiring and empowering our people to change the world. Powered by the technology of today, you’ll work with diverse teams to build trust and create new client solutions. The only way we can tackle the challenges of a fast-changing world is with people like you.
## Awards and accomplishments
Our commitment to well-being, total rewards, development and alumni connectivity makes our firm one of the best places to work, learn and excel. Our commitment to building a culture of belonging — rooted in purpose and values — is consistently recognized by our people and by organizations across the industry.
