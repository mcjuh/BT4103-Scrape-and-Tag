## Legislative and Best Practice Compliance Schemes
AESG, is qualified and experienced to support a variety of legislative and best practice recognised assessment, certification, and compliance schemes:
[ International Best Practice ](https://aesg.com/uk/security-assessment-compliance/#collapse-7abe8626aa06a4a119a9)
**• Building Research Establishment (BRE) SABRE Security Assessment and Certification Scheme**
SABRE is a security assessment and certification scheme for new and existing buildings and built infrastructure assets. It promotes a systematic and risk-based approach to security, leading to operationally and cost-effective solutions. AESG, licensed by BRE, allows our Registered Assessors and Professionals to display the SABRE Badge of Recognition and provide professional services under the scheme’s terms of reference.**• Building Research Establishment (BRE) BREEAM HEA 06 Safety and Security Needs Assessment**
One BREEAM credit is available to developers concerning site and building security, requiring a Suitably Qualified Security Specialist (SQSS) to conduct an evidence-based Security Needs Assessment (SNA) during or prior to Concept Design (RIBA Stage 2 or equivalent).
[ Kingdom of Saudi Arabia ](https://aesg.com/uk/security-assessment-compliance/#collapse-203a7c16aa06a4a119a9)
**• Ministry of Interior – High Commission for Industrial Security (HCIS)**
HCIS is the national regulatory agency for Security, Safety & Fire Protection requirements at industrial facilities and designated critical infrastructure nationwide. The HCIS Security Directives are mandated by the Minister of Interior, Saudi Arabia, ensuring stringent compliance across industries.
[ United Arab Emirates ](https://aesg.com/uk/security-assessment-compliance/#collapse-5b7161a6aa06a4a119a9)
**• Security Industry Regulatory Agency (SIRA) SIRA**
regulates the Security Industry in Dubai. AESG is a SIRA-licensed consulting firm for the Security Consultancies & Studies category, with a licensed branch office at DWC in Dubai. We support organisations in ensuring their CCTV and electronic security systems meet SIRA’s legislative requirements.**• Abu Dhabi Urban Planning Council – Safety & Security Planning Manual (SSPM)**
Commissioned by the Abu Dhabi Urban Planning Council (UPC), the SSPM guides the development of safe and secure communities. AESG is experienced in guiding developers and architects through SSPM compliance during both concept and detailed master plan stages.
[ United Kingdom ](https://aesg.com/uk/security-assessment-compliance/#collapse-7abe8626aa06a4a11f5d)
**• The National Security Inspectorate (NSI) Associate Consultancy Programme (ACP)**
NSI is the leading certification body for the security and fire protection sectors in the UK. The NSI ACP is a referral facility for businesses seeking help with Certification and business development. AESG, as an NSI-licensed associate consultancy, supports businesses in obtaining independent certification to industry standards, enhancing credibility and competitive edge.
[ Additional Compliance ](https://aesg.com/uk/security-assessment-compliance/#collapse-203a7c16aa06a4a11f5d)
**• Security Overlay to the RIBA Plan of Work**
AESG adheres to the Security Overlay to the RIBA Plan of Work, ensuring that security considerations are seamlessly integrated into architectural and construction projects.
