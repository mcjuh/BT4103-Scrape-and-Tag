Frequently asked questions
How do I get started?
Submit an [**interest form**](https://www.grantthornton.com/services/tax-services/federal-tax-services/corporate-tax/fa-x#get-in-touch) and a member of our team will get in touch with you. We’ll then start by holding a discovery session where we learn about your specific requirements. Then, we’ll customize a fixed asset solution that aligns with your unique needs.
What type of access will I have to fa.x?
You’ll have access to a data repository that houses all of your depreciation reports and IRS fileable forms. You’ll also have a visualization dashboard that shows you meaningful insights into your current fixed asset activity.
Can you run IRS fileable tax forms?
Yes. Our standard deliverable generates the tax forms 4562 and 4797 in PDFs that can be attached to your return.
Can you maintain multiple fixed asset books?
Yes. We can customize additional depreciation books, to track computations for various jurisdictions and reporting requirements.
Can you maintain book / GAAP depreciation?
Yes. We can maintain and calculate GAAP depreciation where independence rules permit.
