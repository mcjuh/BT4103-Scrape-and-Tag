President, Asia-Pacific & Japan 
Mike has over 30 years of consulting and professional services experience running teams and client accounts in the United States, Germany, Japan, and Australia. His extensive experience in growing markets across the broader Asia-Pacific (APAC) region is critical to Slalom’s plans for significant growth in those regions.

Upon graduation, he joined Accenture (Andersen Consulting) and quickly gained valuable program delivery experience. Complex program delivery took Mike across the US to Europe, Japan, and eventually to Australia in 2006, where he settled with his wife, Kelly, to raise their three sons.
