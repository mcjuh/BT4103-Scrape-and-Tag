#  Financial Services Tax 
Managing tax obligations responsibly and proactively can make a critical difference in reducing inefficiencies and improving opportunities.
We provide comprehensive tax services tailored to the financial services industries, including banking and capital markets; insurance; wealth and asset management; and real estate. By understanding the specific tax challenges these sectors face, we offer strategic guidance and compliance solutions to navigate the complex tax landscape.
EY US is the only accounting firm with a dedicated business unit for the financial services industry. Our team specializes in business tax, indirect tax, international tax and in many other tax-related issues associated with operations, compliance, reporting, technology, estate planning and law.
