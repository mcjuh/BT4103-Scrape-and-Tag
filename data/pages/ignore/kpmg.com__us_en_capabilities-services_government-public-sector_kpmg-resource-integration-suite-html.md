# The KPMG Resource Integration Suite
Future-ready, modular program and enterprise integration solutions
## Modern solutions for modular government modernization
### Connect, modernize, and deliver better outcomes—faster.
The KPMG Resource Integration Suite (KRIS) is a comprehensive set of cloud-based Software as a Service (SaaS) solutions designed for seamless program and enterprise integration. This powerful suite combines a future-ready enterprise integration platform with high-impact business applications specifically tailored for government program needs—from integrated eligibility and customer communications to Medicaid community engagement verification. Our adaptable solutions are engineered to enhance standardization, interoperability, and programmatic improvements across government departments and agencies, including health and human services.
The integrated solution suite empowers government agencies to move from siloed legacy systems to a connected, modern service delivery model—without the disruption of rip and replace. Built for modular modernization, our solutions help agencies meet today’s demands while laying the groundwork for tomorrow’s innovation. So, you can modernize incrementally, reduce risk, and accelerate time to value.
## The Platform: the core of the KRIS portfolio
The KRIS Connected Platform is a cloud native enterprise interoperability and integration hub, supporting application, shared services, and data interoperability through multiple integration patterns. It contains leading third-party software, cloud services, and accelerators designed to support the enterprise needs of the public sector. Its key components include:
  * **Integration Fabric** : An interoperability framework that promotes data exchange across the enterprise and supports multiple integration methods and patterns.
  * **Services Fabric** : A set of centralized services that can be reused across the enterprise for efficiency and standardization.
  * **Data Fabric** : A comprehensive data platform that helps manage, store, analyze, and leverage data more effectively.


## The KRIS Data Hub: a standalone data platform, purpose-built for enterprise data services
The KRIS Data Hub is designed to power enterprise data services, equipping agencies with the tools they need to manage, monitor, and leverage data effectively. It helps stakeholders track data movement, identify data quality issues early, and deliver trusted data extracts—helping agencies shift from reactive reporting to proactive insights that drive better outcomes.

### The industry packs
Tailored to your program needs. Designed for impact.
The KRIS Connected Platform comes equipped with specialized industry packs designed for rapid, effective deployment across key government domains and programs, including Medicaid and child welfare.
###  Medicaid 
### Medicaid
The KRIS Connected Platform accelerates the implementation of states’ Medicaid enterprise by providing standards-based integrations based on the Medicaid Information Technology Architecture (MITA) framework. Using a standards-based interoperability approach, this industry pack contains pre-built APIs, data and message models, and facilitation guides across each of the MITA domains to improve the effectiveness of Medicaid modular modernization.
###  Child welfare 
### Child welfare
The KRIS Connected Platform accelerates the implementation of your child welfare enterprise by providing standards-based integrations based on the Comprehensive Child Welfare System (CCWIS) and former Statewide Automated Child Welfare Information System (SACWIS) guidelines. Using a standards-based interoperability approach, this industry pack contains pre-built APIs, data and message models, and facilitation guides across each of the major child welfare business functions to improve the integration with internal and external stakeholders.
## The business applications
KRIS Connected business applications are purpose-built for governments’ unique needs such as integrated eligibility and customer communication management. The configurable solutions, built on the KRIS Connected Platform, accelerate time-to-market with lower total cost of ownership​
KRIS Benefits Management
A scalable, flexible, and business-driven integrated eligibility solution powered by Salesforce. It combines KPMG leading practices and pre-configured enterprise architecture assets with the power of industry-leading third-party products to meet states’ public benefits management and integration needs.
KRIS Customer Communications Management
An AI-powered solution powered by Messagepoint that creates personalized, human-centered interactions that address the evolving expectations and experiences of constituents across health and human services.
KRIS Community Engagement Verification
A modular, configurable solution designed to help state Medicaid agencies maintain eligible member coverage and meet federal community engagement requirements under H.R.1. The portal streamlines and automates Medicaid work requirements verification / community engagement compliance through configurable workflows and integration with existing eligibility and income verification systems. Implementation-ready and scalable, the AI-enabled solution provides an intuitive experience for both members and caseworkers—simplifying compliance, reducing administrative burden, and empowering members and your workforce.
