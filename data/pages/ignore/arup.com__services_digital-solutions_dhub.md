d.Hub is a digital asset portfolio management solution, providing project teams with real-time insights to facilitate decision-making.
## Key benefits:
  1. **Real-time project and asset portfolio tracking:** Clients can monitor projects and assets in real-time, and quickly address issues.
  2. **Seamless collaboration:** d.Hub streamlines work methodology for all stakeholders involved, centralising data and generating workspaces focused on the specific portfolio transformation project. d.Hub allows for worldwide collaboration.
  3. **Total customisation:** Many ready to use and customisable modules tailored to your project needs are already available, including Site Audits (issues and assessment), Sustainability Solutions (EU taxonomy, LEED certification, BREEAM certification, TRUE certification, ESG reporting), and Project Management (Project tracker, risk management, RFI tracker, submittal tracker). If unavailable, our team will work with you to develop tailor-made modules that align with unique project requirements. 


The platform allows public and private portfolio owners to collect accurate data of their asset portfolio (site inspections and audits, asset portfolio maintenance, sustainability transformation and more), plan actions to achieve long-term organisational objectives, all in one platform. By integrating all data in a single platform, d.Hub facilitates clients’ ability to plan for the future – identifying investment opportunities while prioritising and predicting asset maintenance. 
To centralise and unify critical information on projects, assets and investment portfolios, d.Hub offers an array of ready-to-use and on demand modules that are designed to create more certainty around project performance and value, while also improving reporting accuracy. Additionally, d.Hub offers its Forms mobile app, this allows users to capture asset data, log issues and set assessment actions onsite directly via a mobile device.

Adif – digitalising program management
d.Hub is being used for the program management for over 5,000 assets for Adif, Spain’s railway infrastructure manager. Streamlining the intensive maintenance programme for the vast national rail network is crucial to Adif’s successful operations. d.Hub offers operators real-time data through a single source of truth, enabling better informed asset maintenance decision-making. This allows for better asset management, cost savings, detailed asset portfolio data, as well as efficient designs for inspection, maintenance, and refurbishment strategies.
Kraft Heinz – centralised asset management worldwide
Kraft Heinz is using d.Hub to centralise the gathered data and facility management for their global assets across four main workstreams: Programme and Project Management, Digital, Cost Management and Site Structural Surveys. The data collection process for structural engineers was streamlined by automating some data entry and automated PDF reporting was set up to enable the client to view a site summary and an overview of the results from the Structural Surveys. This tool allows the client to prioritise assets that require investment as part of their global capital expenditure program. d.Hub is being used on over 65 sites and over 1000 buildings for inspections, maintenance works tracking, and cost management. d.Hub is being used as a central point of truth unifiying data from multiple inspection companies, Arup included.
