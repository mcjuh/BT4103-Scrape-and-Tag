## Why Sustainable Procurement Matters
Operations and procurement offer significant opportunities for organisations to reduce their overall carbon footprint. Sustainable procurement focuses not only on internal processes but also on the entire supply chain, including the sourcing of materials, product lifecycle, and environmental impact. By implementing sustainable procurement policies and utilising digital tools, organisations can improve the sustainability of their operations while increasing the inherent value of their products.
Through a comprehensive assessment of a company’s supply chain and procurement practices, AESG helps identify areas for improvement, ensuring that both suppliers' and internal processes align with Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) goals. This approach enhances sustainability across the supply chain, helping clients meet regulatory requirements and market demands for eco-friendly, socially responsible products.
## How AESG Supports Sustainable Procurement
AESG assists clients by providing detailed supply chain mapping and carbon footprint calculations, offering a full analysis of suppliers' materials and products, as well as internal procurement policies. Our team of thought leaders, including chemical, environmental, and service engineers, works closely with clients to develop tailored solutions that optimise their procurement processes. We ensure that each solution fully integrates sustainability, from sourcing materials to the final delivery of products.
Our services also include the development of Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs) and support for the implementation of sustainable procurement policies. Through the use of sustainability tools, we make the integration of sustainable practices seamless and efficient, offering clients clear, actionable insights and cost-benefit analyses that allow for informed decision-making.
