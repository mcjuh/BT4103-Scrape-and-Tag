# BDO’s 8-Minute Value-Based Care Readiness Assessment
Take the assessment and receive your VBC transition guide
The pressure is on for healthcare providers to shift to value-based care (VBC) models, driven by rising costs, tighter margins, and the need to deliver high quality patient care.
But shifting to VBC isn’t as simple as flipping a switch — it requires a major overhaul, not just of payment models, but also of how patient care is delivered, monitored, and improved. Without the right preparation, healthcare providers may fail to realize the expected ROI of the transition.
Our 8-minute VBC assessment can help you discover how prepared you are for the shift to VBC. By assessing your organizational maturity across a wide range of functions, we can help you determine:
  * Where your greatest strengths lie
  * What outstanding gaps could destabilize your organization during the transition period
  * How to address current gaps and prepare for success
