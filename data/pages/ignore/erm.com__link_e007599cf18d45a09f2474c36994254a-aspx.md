# Episode 43: Strategy to delivery - Lessons from London Climate Action Week 2026
In this episode, ERM partners Irem Yerdelen, Anna Jakobsen, and Jason Smith join host Mark Lee to reflect on key insights from London Climate Action Week 2026. Together, they explore what the week signaled about the state of the climate agenda, from the shift toward implementation and delivery, to the growing importance of collaboration, finance, and system‑level change.
Their conversation covers:
  * Just Transition - embedding social value into climate strategy and investment decisions
  * Supply chain decarbonization - moving from compliance to collaboration, enablement and investment across value chains
  * Electrification - scaling the transition to clean energy systems and overcoming infrastructure and cost barriers
  * Sustainable packaging - unlocking innovation, regulatory compliance, and commercial value
