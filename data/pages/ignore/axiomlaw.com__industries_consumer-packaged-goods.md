## What legal support do CPG companies need for supply chain, logistics, and procurement contracts?
CPG companies often need [legal support for direct and indirect procurement](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/procurement), [supply chain agreements](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/supply-chain-agreements), logistics contracts, equipment leasing, technology contracts, software, licensing, and vendor terms. Axiom can help in-house teams [manage contract volume](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/commercial-contracts), review business-critical agreements, and support procurement workflows during inflation or supply chain disruption.
## How should CPG brands review marketing, advertising, and product claims?
CPG brands should review marketing and advertising claims for substantiation, disclosures, competitive risk, environmental claims, influencer or promotion terms, and product-specific regulatory requirements. Axiom's [marketing and advertising lawyers](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/marketing-advertising) can help legal teams support claims review, advertising workflows, and coordination with marketing, compliance, and product teams.
## What regulatory compliance support do CPG companies need?
CPG companies may need [regulatory support](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/regulatory-compliance) with FDA, FTC, CPSC, environmental compliance, trade compliance, [ESG compliance](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/esg-compliance), product labeling, recalls, inspections, and regulatory response. Axiom can help legal teams review policies, update documentation, manage cross-functional regulatory issues, and support business teams during product or packaging changes.
## How can CPG companies protect brands, trademarks, and product IP?
CPG companies can protect brands and product IP by reviewing licenses, trademark clearances, applications, registrations, renewals, patent workstreams, and freedom-to-operate analysis. Axiom's [intellectual property lawyers](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/intellectual-property) can help in-house teams manage IP portfolios, product launches, and brand-protection tasks across changing product lines.
## What privacy and cybersecurity support do CPG legal teams need?
CPG legal teams may need support with consumer data, ecommerce data flows, loyalty programs, [GDPR](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/gdpr), CCPA, DPIAs, DPAs, DTAs, policies, training, and incident response. Axiom can help legal departments [manage data privacy documentation](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/data-privacy-cybersecurity), vendor reviews, and incident response readiness as consumer data programs expand.
## How can CPG legal teams improve legal operations and reduce outside spend?
CPG legal teams can improve legal operations by organizing intake, streamlining contract workflows, reviewing outside counsel panels, tracking matter volume, and building repeatable processes for high-volume work. Axiom can help in-house teams add flexible [legal operations support ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/legal-operations-support)and improve efficiency without increasing fixed headcount.
