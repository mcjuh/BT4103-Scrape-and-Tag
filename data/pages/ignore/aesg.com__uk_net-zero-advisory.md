## Why Net Zero Advisory Matters in the UK
AESG provides expert advice in sustainable design, minimising both embodied and operational carbon across the entire lifespan of the development. As the UK moves towards a net zero economy, it is also important to establish strong carbon management strategies and carbon pricing mechanisms, which will be crucial for organisations across sectors. Tools like carbon credits, offsetting, and emissions trading schemes empower public and private sector organisations to lower their carbon footprint and comply with regulatory mandates. The UK's commitment to net zero by 2050 is backed by various policies and mechanisms, including:
### UK Emissions Trading Scheme (UK ETS)
A cap-and-trade system designed to reduce emissions across sectors, promoting cost-effective carbon reduction.
### Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR)
A framework requiring qualifying companies to report energy use, carbon emissions, and emissions intensity, ensuring transparency and accountability.
### Climate Change Levy (CCL)
A tax on energy delivered to non-domestic users, incentivising energy efficiency and carbon reduction.
### Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD)
Mandatory climate risk disclosure for large companies and financial institutions to align with the UK’s net zero ambitions.
AESG UK offers advisory services to help clients navigate these frameworks, implement offsetting solutions, and achieve emissions reduction targets. By aligning with UK regulations and global agreements like the Paris Agreement, we provide clear guidance on the path to net zero.

## Decarbonisation Strategies for a Sustainable Future
With the UK's drive to meet the Paris Agreement goal of limiting global warming to well below 2°C, the need for actionable decarbonisation strategies has never been more critical. AESG UK partners with organisations across key sectors such as energy, infrastructure, manufacturing, and real estate to design long-term solutions for reducing greenhouse gas emissions. Our tailored approach includes:
### Energy Audits & Efficiency Upgrades
Identifying energy-saving opportunities through detailed audits, helping organisations cut costs and emissions in line with UK standards.
### Deep Retrofit Solutions
Enhancing existing infrastructure with energy-efficient retrofits that significantly lower carbon emissions.
### Renewable Energy Integration
Strategically incorporating renewable sources such as solar and wind to transition businesses away from fossil fuel reliance.
### Climate Risk Assessment & Resilience Planning
Assessing climate risks and equipping organisations with robust resilience strategies for future environmental challenges.
### Net Zero Carbon Roadmaps
Developing customised pathways to guide organisations toward achieving net zero by 2050 or earlier, with defined milestones.
