# International Business
The rules of international business are being rewritten, creating opportunities for companies that are nimble enough to capitalize. BCG’s international business consultants help companies recognize the economic patterns in globalization and seize the future.
As digital integration accelerates and geopolitical complexity deepens, companies must navigate a multipolar world: the US turns inward, China shifts focus, Europe seeks a new path, and the [Global South](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/in-a-multipolar-world-global-south-finds-its-moment) gains influence. Trade wars, [tariff](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/international-business/navigating-international-trade/what-is-a-tariff) policies, [economic nationalism](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2024/economic-statecraft-is-back), and regional conflicts are redrawing the map of international business.Our international business consultants help companies respond with tailored strategies—adapting products, models, and operations to withstand shocks, manage risk, and stay competitive. From securing reliable supply chains to unlocking growth in fragmented markets, we guide no-regret moves that build globally resilient businesses.

## Our Approach to International Business
We help companies absorb the shock of global volatility by building capabilities and structures that enable data integration, flexibility, and agility across borders and businesses. As an international strategy consultancy, we advise the following types of clients on how to navigate a fragmented global ecosystem:
  * **Multinational Corporations.** We help MNCs unlock growth in emerging markets through both global and local opportunities. Our tools position MNCs ahead of evolving consumer trends, dynamic supply chains, evolving operating models, and rising local competitors.
  * **Global Challengers and Other Local Competitors.** Our international consulting group works with a class of rapidly growing companies in emerging markets that we call “global challengers”—and also with the local companies that aspire to compete at that level. Our strategies help them both win at home and compete with leaders on the global stage.
  * **Governments.** We team with national and regional authorities to promote economic and international development. Our experts evaluate local business climates and map out the ways to attract more FDI and build public–private partnerships.

## Our Clients’ Success in International Business
We developed a tariff mitigation strategy for a global life sciences firm, **reducing potential tariff exposure by 40%–50% in 2025 and 100% in 2026,** restoring investor confidence and supporting earnings outlook.
We**drove a group-wide ESG transformation** for an industrial conglomerate, aligning strategic priorities with stakeholder expectations and delivering a clear roadmap through a centralized TMO.
We developed a comprehensive green investment strategy for an Asian government that was aligned with the nation’s net zero goals, delivering an estimated **$19 billion uplift and** **creating 350,000 high-skilled** **jobs**.
We worked with a global telecom giant to unlock growth beyond connectivity by institutionalizing a strategic partnership model—**powering Tier 1 alliances with OpenAI, Google, and Meta** and transforming how the business drives innovation at scale.
We helped a leading electrical goods company **achieve 1.5x market growth** through a sales transformation program.
We designed a global brand growth strategy for a leading apparel and footwear brand, **reversing two years of stagnant revenues and delivering double-digit growth**.
