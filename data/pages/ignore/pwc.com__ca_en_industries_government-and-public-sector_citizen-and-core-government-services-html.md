## [ Build your future public sector workforce Empowering people to thrive in an era of reinvention and AI ](https://www.pwc.com/ca/en/industries/government-and-public-sector/citizen-and-core-government-services/build-trust-build-your-future-public-sector-workforce.html)
The future of government services—from citizen and business-facing activities to internal client services and operations—depends on more than incremental change. It relies on having the right public sector workforce, future-forward operating models, secure and resilient technologies, and the enabling tools required to turn government priorities into measurable citizen value. 
Today’s challenges are unprecedented—but so are the opportunities. Now is the time to reimagine what’s possible—to rethink strategy, accelerate digital transformation, and modernize government services and underlying operations so they are future ready. This includes equipping teams with AI-enabled solutions so they can work smarter, strengthening cybersecurity and operational resilience, modernizing procurement and supply chains, enabling new ways of working across the public sector, and developing robust workforce upskilling programs. 
Done well, these shifts can help governments collaborate more effectively with other levels of government and industry, respond faster to changing circumstances, and spark new pathways for sustainable economic growth. 
## Collaborating to modernize government services and deliver citizen value
We’re a Canadian firm with deep experience supporting core government organizations at the federal, provincial, and municipal levels, helping them modernize government services and strengthen the foundations that power effective service delivery.
Through our global network, we also bring insights and leading practices from other jurisdictions—perspectives that can help governments improve efficiencies, enhance operational resilience, and deliver greater value for citizens and businesses.
At the same time, we work closely with Canadian businesses across sectors and regions. This gives us a unique, holistic view of how the economy is evolving, how industries are adapting, and how government services can be transformed to be more agile, more efficient, and more closely aligned to value-based outcomes. 
We’ll support your digital transformation activities end to end, bringing together people with the capabilities you need when you need them. This includes economists who understand economic and geopolitical trends; specialists in international tax, trade, and regulatory environments; and teams with deep experience in complex, large-scale ERP transformations, government operations modernization, and public sector workforce transformation. 
And—vitally important today—we’ll collaborate with you to rethink government services and your [workforce](https://www.pwc.com/ca/en/industries/government-and-public-sector/citizen-and-core-government-services/build-trust-build-your-future-public-sector-workforce.html) in an era of reinvention and AI. We’ll help you apply [AI responsibly](https://www.pwc.com/ca/en/services/artificial-intelligence/trust-in-ai.html) to enable new ways of working, augment the work your people do so they can make a bigger impact, and create measurable value across your services and operations.
## Driving measurable impact across government services and operations
The opportunity for governments to deliver meaningful change—for citizens, for industry, and for Canada—has never been greater, or more critical. By modernizing government services and strengthening the core operations that support them, governments can build resilience and become more future ready.
Let’s work together to redefine what’s possible.
  1. Applying a holistic lens to transformation value and impact 
  2. Building future-ready operating models
  3. Strengthening enterprise technology and digital capabilities 
  4. Accelerating AI transformation in government
  5. Supporting data-driven investment decisions
  6. Modernizing procurement
  7. Strengthening supply chain resilience and sovereignty
  8. Empowering public sector workforce transformation and upskilling 
  9. Building cybersecurity and operational resilience
  10. Providing seamless managed services


### Applying a holistic lens to transformation value and impact 
We’ll help you define the full picture of your digital transformation initiatives so you can better understand their impact on the Canadian economy, businesses, and citizens, make more informed decisions, and drive measurable value creation. 
### Building future-ready operating models
We’ll collaborate with you to turn your vision for the future of your organization into reality, helping you define the value you want to deliver, clarify the outcomes you want to achieve, determine what needs to change—including operating models, people, processes, and technologies—manage your transformation activities from end to end, and measure the value of your activities so you can adapt as needed. 
### Strengthening enterprise technology and digital capabilities 
We’ll help you navigate your enterprise technology journey end to end—working with you to establish a clear digital strategy and build the secure data and technology backbone you need to underpin seamless operations, embrace innovative capabilities like AI, get your data house in order, and enable confident, data-driven decision-making. 
### Accelerating AI transformation in government
We’ll help you design and build agile AI-enabled solutions grounded in trust, governance, responsible AI, security, and compliance—so AI can augment the work your people do, support new ways of delivering services, and allow you to deliver greater value across government services.
### Supporting data-driven investment decisions
We’ll use data-driven approaches to help you prioritize investments based on value while balancing risk so you can get more impact from every dollar you spend. 
### Modernizing procurement
We’ll work with you to simplify and modernize your procurement processes—including implementing technologies that accelerate procurement activities, boost efficiencies, and enhance transparency and accountability—so you can secure high-quality products and services faster.
### Strengthening supply chain resilience and sovereignty
We’ll help you assess the security and stability of your supply chains, build sovereign and dependable supply chains, and enhance your supply chain resilience using advanced automation and logistics practices.
### Empowering public sector workforce transformation and upskilling 
We’ll help you reevaluate workforce strategies, design more resilient organizational structures, understand changing capability needs—including digital and AI skill sets—and develop upskilling programs so your people can confidently use AI-enabled solutions in their day-to-day work. 
### Building cybersecurity and operational resilience
We’ll help you understand evolving cybersecurity threats—from insider risk and cyber-disruptions to cyber prepositioning—get the most out of your cybersecurity solutions, and strengthen your cybersecurity posture by enhancing threat intelligence, reducing vulnerabilities, managing your risk exposure, and responding quickly when cyber threat activity occurs.
### Providing seamless managed services
We’ll make it easier for you to focus on your core operations by providing seamless IT infrastructure, back-office, and supply chain managed services—freeing up your workforce to focus on higher value activities so you can drive better citizen outcomes.
