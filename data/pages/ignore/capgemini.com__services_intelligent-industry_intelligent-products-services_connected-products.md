# Connected product engineering
## Turn connected products into intelligent devices that drive lifetime value
From wearables to home routers, to smart appliances, connected products increasingly shape how consumers live, work and interact with brands. Products that were once standalone assets are becoming connected, intelligent, continuously evolving platforms that generate data, create new services, and deliver ongoing customer value.
Advances in Wi-Fi, 5G, Matter, edge AI, cloud, and silicon are expanding what these devices can do. Consumers now expect simple setup, reliable connectivity, and seamless interoperability. They quickly lose patience with products that are complex to get connected, do not interact properly with other devices, or make it hard to perform software updates. Connectivity is a key product differentiator.
### Managing complexity from device to cloud
As products evolve into software-defined systems, engineering teams need expertise that spans the entire technology stack. Delivering seamless connected experiences requires deep integration across electronics, embedded software, Wi-Fi and other wireless technologies, edge intelligence, cloud services, and user applications. Every layer must work together seamlessly – across the device, different networks, and product ecosystems. Developing and maintaining these capabilities in-house can increase complexity, slow innovation and delay time to market.
Once products are deployed, manufactures must ensure connectivity performs reliability in real world environments, remains compatible with evolving technologies, and meets stringent requirements for security, privacy, performance and certification. They must also manage devices at scale, deliver secure over-the-air updates and support products throughout increasingly long lifecycles.
Designing, validating and testing these interactions end-to-end requires specialist facilities, tools, and expertise that can be slow and costly to build internally.
### Our solution: end-to-end connected product engineering
From chip to cloud, Capgemini Engineering helps organizations design, secure, test and continuously evolve connected products. We combine product engineering, embedded systems, connectivity, edge AI, cloud and lifecycle management with deep Wi-Fi expertise, AI assets, and advanced testing facilities. This integrated approach reduces complexity and brings secure, certified and future-ready consumer devices to market faster.
## Our capabilities
### Product and device engineering
We establish the product foundations, bringing together electronics, embedded software, connectivity, cloud services, and security from architecture and technology selection through to system integration.
### Device OS and middleware engineering
End-to-end engineering across device operating systems, middleware, frameworks, and platform layers for connected devices, gateways, STBs, OTT devices, and smart TVs. Enables rapid platform modernization, integration, optimization, and validation across ecosystems such as RDK, OpenWRT, prpl, Android TV, Android Middleware, and Linux.
### Connectivity and wireless engineering services
We engineer reliable connectivity across Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Matter, cellular, and low-rate wireless technologies including IEEE 802.15.4. Our capabilities cover protocol integration, performance optimization, interoperability, device onboarding, and connectivity management.
### Edge AI and intelligent products
We enable devices to interpret data and respond locally through edge AI, computer vision, audio intelligence, predictive analytics, and TinyML.
### Device and cloud services
We create the APIs, cloud services, analytics and management tools needed to monitor, manage, and improve connected-device fleets after deployment.
### Lab-as-a-Service and end-to-end product testing
Our labs test complete connected-product experiences across the device, connectivity, applications and cloud. We provide functional, performance, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and IEEE 802.15.4 testing, alongside interoperability validation, automation frameworks, and certification support.
### Security and compliance
We engineer security into products through secure architectures, threat modelling, encryption, vulnerability assessment, and regulatory-compliance support.
### Product sustenance and lifecycle management
We keep products secure, effective and valuable through over-the-air updates, remote diagnostics, monitoring, predictive maintenance, and support for mature and end-of-life products.
