# Master Data Management

Our Master Data Management (MDM) offering focuses on MDM strategy and business case development in alignment with organizational priorities, as well as MDM program delivery experience and accelerators to decrease time to value when implementing MDM solutions.
KPMG and Informatica work together to help solve our clients’ most complex data challenges. We collaborate through the MDM project lifecycle, from strategy and design, through implementation of Informatica’s industry-leading MDM technology.
### KPMG Master Data Management enabled by Informatica
KPMG has aligned with Informatica to present an MDM offering that combines our data and analytics advisory services with Informatica's industry-leading technology.
#### Transcript
Do you struggle to manage multiple sources of data, maintain data quality, and comply with data privacy regulations?
Do you need help solving challenges in multiple data domains such as product, supplier, and finance data?
Are you seeking to understand customers on a deeper level, enhance service, and boost sales?
If you answered yes to any of these questions, then you need a master data management, or MDM solution.
KPMG has partnered with Informatica to present an MDM offering that combines our data and analytics advisory services with Informatica's industry-leading technology.
Our offering manages MDM data throughout its lifecycle, providing a single reliable source of information. It increases accuracy, consistency, and enables real-time access and analysis for actionable insights and personalized customer experiences.
We help organizations navigate complex data ecosystems, unlock value from critical information, and implement comprehensive MDM solutions. We also help shape and execute client strategies so they can get the most value from Informatica.
[Dan Fisher]: “Many clients come to us because they struggle with manual, time-consuming master data management processes that often lead to confusion and eroding consumer trust.
[Barry Raghunathan]: Using tailored, industry-specific master data strategies, we help them automate their processes, correct inconsistencies, and pave the way for data-driven decision-making that saves valuable time and effort.”
Our KPMG / Informatica MDM offering can help you:
  * Increase revenue and profitability
  * Reduce operational costs
  * Increase customer satisfaction and loyalty 
  * Foster repeat business and referrals
  * Enhance regulatory compliance for data privacy


As Informatica’s Innovation Partner of the Year in 2022 and 2023, KPMG is a market leader in data management.
Our team brings deep experience and tried-and-true accelerators to streamline your experience when solving master data challenges.
Contact us today to learn how our MDM offering can help give you a 360-degree view of your customers, get ahead of their needs, and positively impact your business.
