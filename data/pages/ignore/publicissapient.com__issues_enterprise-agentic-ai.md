## Enterprise agentic AI FAQs
What is enterprise agentic AI?
Enterprise agentic AI uses agents to carry out work across systems, data and workflows under clear business rules. Unlike a standalone assistant, it can plan tasks, take action, coordinate with other agents and keep people in control where judgment is required.
How do you move AI from pilots into daily operations?
Start with business outcomes, not isolated use cases. Then connect agents to the systems, data and workflows where work happens, apply governance and monitor performance so AI becomes part of the operating model rather than another experiment.
How do you govern AI agents across the enterprise?
Governance requires one place to see which agents are running, what they can access, how they perform and whether they follow policy. Sapient Bodhi provides centralized monitoring, role-based access and human oversight across agent workflows.
