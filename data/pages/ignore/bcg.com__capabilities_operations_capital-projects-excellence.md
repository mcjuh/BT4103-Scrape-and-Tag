# Capital Projects Excellence
Less than 1% of capital projects deliver on the value promised and are completed on time and on budget. BCG helps organizations change that by aligning strategy, execution, and data to turn capital projects into lasting competitive advantage.
An anticipated $7 trillion-plus will be deployed by 2030 in capital projects, from clean energy and electrification to digital infrastructure and transportation. But with this unprecedented scale comes equally unprecedented risk. The vast majority of capital projects struggle to deliver as planned, resulting in delayed impact, lost value, and eroded trust.

## Our Approach to Capital Projects Excellence
BCG’s capital projects excellence consultants combine strategic foresight, advanced analytics, and [AI-driven](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence) insights to help organizations master every stage of the capital project lifecycle. In an industry where promised benefits often fail to materialize, our capital projects management services integrate technology and human expertise to improve decision making, transparency, and performance. We embed value creation and execution discipline from strategy through delivery to reverse a trend of underperformance.Each BCG capital projects excellence consultant partners closely with client teams to ensure delivery excellence, embedding data-driven insights and operational rigor into every phase of the project lifecycle. Our capital projects excellence consulting approach focuses on three interconnected pillars that ensure alignment, visibility, and measurable value creation.
Expand All 
Capital Portfolio Strategy
We help clients unlock the full potential of their capital portfolios by aligning capital strategy with execution. Our approach leverages AI and human expertise to strengthen governance, capital planning, and performance discipline, driving agility and accountability across complex program ecosystems.
Project Design and Development
We support clients in enhancing diligence and planning during early project stages, using data and [generative AI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence/generative-ai) to assess feasibility and evaluate risk. This ensures that only the most viable, value-accretive projects advance, laying a solid foundation for execution success.
Capital Projects Execution
We enable organizations to deliver projects efficiently, safely, and on schedule by combining AI and predictive analytics, with proven operational excellence methodologies. This integration allows for real-time visibility, risk mitigation, and cost optimization throughout delivery.
Together, these pillars and technologies deliver the clarity, control, and confidence required to achieve enduring capital excellence and transform capital projects into high-performing assets that deliver sustained portfolio value.
## Our Clients’ Impact in Capital Projects Excellence
Our capital projects excellence work spans industries with visionary organizations that are redefining how capital projects are planned, financed, and delivered. Together, they deliver the complex, high-impact programs that power economies, enable sustainable growth, and advance sectors such as [infrastructure](https://www.bcg.com/industries/urban-planning/infrastructure), [energy](https://www.bcg.com/industries/energy/overview), [industrial goods](https://www.bcg.com/industries/industrial-goods/overview), and beyond.
Before launching the large-scale entertainment project, the client needed to ensure operational readiness across functions. BCG led a two-phase program: first, establishing readiness scorecards, cross-asset roadmaps, and risk interventions; then, coordinating dry runs, walkthroughs, and command center setup. This work enabled the client to anticipate operational risks early, coordinate execution across functions, and establish a scalable model to transition complex assets into operations on time and without unnecessary cost or schedule overruns.
A major transmission system operator faced cost pressures while procuring partners for underground lines. In a tight market, BCG led a structured tendering process, established robust governance, and supported vendor negotiations. The result: transparent selection, over 30% in projected cost savings, and up to a 17% reduction in final offers—unlocking value before construction began.
