## Accelerating your transformation journey
The EY Transformation Platforms are designed to streamline transformation journeys, forging a digital pathway from strategy to execution. The platforms support organizations to unlock their full potential, establishing clear objectives and harnessing data for actionable insights.
Read More  Read Less 
##  Identify value 
Identify new business models and growth opportunities, assess technology and AI investments for real impact, and leverage strategic insights to maximize enterprise value.
##  Orchestrate value 
Align teams and innovation to deliver high-impact transformations, enhance human experience to boost adoption, and manage risk strategically to build confidence and agility.
##  Realize value 
Embed new ways of working for lasting change, verify and optimize transformation outcomes, and capture sustained value while minimizing future costs.
## The technologies that enable transformation
Two suites of products to help clients address recurring challenges in transformation programs.
Read More  Read Less 
Value identification suite 
Technologies that help to uncover new avenues for value creation, accurately assess and quantify potential opportunities, and establish a solid value baseline for your transformation initiatives – allowing for informed decisions before implementation.


Value orchestration and realization suite 
Technologies that enable seamless integration of diverse capabilities, tools and assets to address complex transformation challenges, embedding new ways of working and helping deliver sustainable, measurable outcomes.
