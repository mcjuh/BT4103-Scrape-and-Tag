# ERM launches new digital platform to help companies deliver projects faster, better and safer
07 December 2018
Share this page:
**ERM announced today the launch of ERM Nexus, a new technology platform designed to streamline and enhance how it delivers projects around the world to provide faster, better and safer outcomes for its clients.**
Companies often acquire and manage significant amounts of detailed data, particularly on Environmental, Health and Safety (EHS) issues, but this information is often stored in different server locations, software systems, directories, files and format types. By harnessing the data on one platform, ERM Nexus helps companies achieve greater levels of EHS and operational performance, realize new value and gain critical business insights.
Since August 2018, ERM Nexus has been piloted live on numerous site investigation and remediation projects and programs in the US, Brazil, Europe, South Africa, India and Australia. For these projects, the ERM Nexus platform builds on industry-leading software, such as Earthsoft’s EQuIS™, ESRI’s ArcGIS™ and Microsoft™, so providing the necessary linkages to create an integrated digital workflow. This includes project scoping and on-site data collection; data management and validation; delivery of progress reports, dashboards, data insights and analytics; and automated reporting.
“ERM Nexus offers a seamless way to integrate project data with business data,” says Oliver Phipps, Lead Partner, ERM Nexus. “For site investigation and remediation, this platform enables our clients to build a robust data asset that improves the management of legacy sites and programs, optimizes remediation costs and drives more effective and faster outcomes, such as exit from liability or sale of a property.”
ERM Nexus is just one of the ways the company is helping its clients harness the power of data and technology to realize new value. It is striving to be the leading digital EHS service provider in the market. 
In alignment with that ambition, it is technology-enabling core services one by one and adding new service offerings in Digital Advisory and Data Analytics.
