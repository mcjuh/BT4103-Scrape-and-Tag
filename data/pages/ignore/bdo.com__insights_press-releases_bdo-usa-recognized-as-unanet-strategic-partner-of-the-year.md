# BDO USA Recognized as Unanet Strategic Partner of the Year
Press Release
  * PRINT 
  * 

**Key Facts:**
  * Recognized by Unanet as 2026 Strategic Partner of the Year
  * Marks BDO’s fifth year receiving a Unanet partner award
  * Highlights BDO’s support for government contractors with Unanet implementation, software optimization, compliance, accounting and operations


**CHICAGO, July 10, 2026** — BDO USA, one of the nation’s leading accounting and advisory firms, has been named Unanet’s “Strategic Partner of the Year,” marking the fifth time the firm has received a partner award from the government contracting-focused enterprise resource planning (ERP) and customer relationship management (CRM) provider. The recognition reinforces the strength of BDO’s strategic relationship with Unanet and its continued focus on helping organizations maximize the value of their technology investments.
### Built on Experience and Collaboration
[BDO’s Unanet team](https://www.bdo.com/strategic-partner-ecosystem/unanet-implementation-integration-services) has established a track record of delivering long-term value to organizations navigating complex operational and financial environments. This recognition highlights a sustained commitment to delivering high-quality support and guidance in a specialized and evolving sector.
Being recognized for the fifth year is especially meaningful because it reflects the consistency of our team’s work, the strength of our collaboration with Unanet, and the trust organizations place in us. We are passionate about helping our clients achieve their goals and about working alongside a talented and dedicated team.
Stephanie Lianos
Managing Director and Unanet Team Leader, BDO
### Supporting Long-Term Success
The award also reflects BDO’s emphasis on responsive service and long-term client relationships. In addition to implementation and consulting support, BDO provides [government contracting advisory services](https://www.bdo.com/services/industry-specialty-consulting/government-contracting-consulting) across strategy, compliance, accounting and operations — helping organizations strengthen processes, improve day-to-day performance and realize sustained value from their Unanet investment. 
> “Every day, we work with organizations to leverage Unanet more effectively across the full lifecycle, from implementation through software optimization, to enhance operations and achieve measurable outcomes,” said Lianos. “This recognition reflects the impact our team strives to deliver, and the collaboration that makes it possible.” 
As BDO looks ahead, the firm remains focused on its collaboration with Unanet to support continued innovation and help clients address evolving challenges across the government contracting landscape.
