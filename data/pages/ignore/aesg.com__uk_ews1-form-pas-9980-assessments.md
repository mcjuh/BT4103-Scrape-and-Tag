Our qualified fire safety professionals conduct thorough assessments of external wall systems to identify fire risks, support regulatory compliance, and facilitate property transactions.
## What is the EWS1 Form?
The EWS1 form (External Wall System 1) is a standard assessment process used to evaluate the fire safety of cladding and external wall materials on residential buildings. It was introduced in response to the Grenfell Tower fire to address concerns about combustible cladding. The assessment is typically required for buildings over 18 meters tall or those with specific risk factors.
An EWS1 form is completed by a qualified fire safety expert, who determines whether the materials and construction of the external wall system pose a fire risk. This assessment is crucial for mortgage lenders, insurers, and property owners when buying, selling, or refinancing residential buildings.
## What is PAS 9980?
PAS 9980 is a UK code of practice that guides the fire risk appraisal and assessment of external wall construction for multistorey, multi-occupied residential buildings. It provides a methodology for evaluating the risk associated with different types of cladding and external wall systems, including the materials' combustibility and potential to spread fire.
PAS 9980 assessments help identify necessary remediation work to reduce fire risks to an acceptable level. This appraisal considers factors such as the condition of the materials, the building's height and layout, and existing fire safety measures like sprinklers and compartmentation.
