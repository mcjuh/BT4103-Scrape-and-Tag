# Distressed deal support services
We aim to maximize value by assessing the risks and opportunities with a divestiture or acquisition of a distressed asset.
### When a company is in distress, a step in the wrong direction can sometimes have significant effects on corporate performance and company value.
KPMG can help turn difficult situations into opportunistic results. We assist in assessing identified risks and pitfalls in dealing with acquisitions and sales of distressed assets by providing a realistic analysis of current options. With insight into the potential impacts and risks of those options, a path that will maximize value can be found.
We can assist with efforts around project management, financial and operational diligence, implementation or separation of the transaction, and growth strategies. Whether dealing with out-of-court, in court, or Section 363 sale, KPMG can help guide you throughout the transaction. In addition, our team is well-versed in wind-down/liquidation assessments for domestic and international exits.
