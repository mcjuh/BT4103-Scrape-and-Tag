# Audit Quality Report
### RSM’s commitment to audit quality and innovation
May 05, 2026
#### [View the report ](https://rsmus.com/content/dam/rsm/insights/services/audit/1pdf/audit-quality-report.inline.pdf)
## The foundation of RSM audit quality
Audit quality at RSM is built on a foundation of integrity, competence, objectivity and independence—essential elements that instill confidence and drive our commitment to excellence.
Delivering [high-quality audit services](https://rsmus.com/services/audit-services.html "high-quality audit services") involves more than just adhering to rigorous standards; it requires a comprehensive approach that integrates effective governance, ethical adherence, client engagement management and robust monitoring systems.
At the core of this commitment is our people. Their knowledge and dedication to upholding the highest principles of integrity and independence are crucial to our audit quality. As gatekeepers for the public trust, quality is job one as our auditors prioritize their professional responsibilities and uphold the values of our profession.
## Quality, excellence and insights driven by RSM Luca
Enhancing audit quality is a continuous priority at RSM, with a strong focus on innovation and digital transformation. RSM invests in audit technology to perform audits as efficiently and effectively as possible.
A cornerstone of this strategy is [RSM Luca, our global digital audit ecosystem](https://rsmus.com/services/audit-services/rsm-luca.html "RSM Luca, our global digital audit ecosystem"), built on four key pillars: automation, analytics, artificial intelligence and digital experience.
These pillars strengthen our audit processes by improving efficiency, minimizing disruption and delivering insights tailored to our clients’ needs. RSM Luca equips our audit and assurance professionals with cutting-edge tools that allow us to meet and exceed client expectations while maintaining rigorous quality standards.
## Planning and performing the audit
Our audit approach emphasizes a comprehensive understanding of the client's business environment, including [financial reporting](https://rsmus.com/insights/financial-reporting.html "financial reporting") and internal controls.
We use a global methodology to tailor audit procedures to our clients’ specific risks and complexities, promoting consistency and quality. The audit plan is aligned with risk assessments, and procedures are adjusted as needed based on changes in risk factors.
Our teams leverage specialized knowledge and consult with subject matter experts and our National Professional Standards Group (NPSG) to enhance audit quality.
Advanced tools are utilized to prepare detailed documentation, support the audit opinion and facilitate an effective review.
## Adherence to quality management frameworks
RSM’s commitment to quality is reflected in our adherence to established quality management frameworks, as well as our ongoing monitoring and annual evaluation of progress toward meeting quality objectives.
To further strengthen our quality framework, we launched the RSM US Audit Quality Board (AQB) in 2025. The AQB’s three independent members advise and evaluate our assurance practice, focusing on quality control and the system of quality management. They also provide advice and recommendations on audit quality-related aspects of the firm’s operations, culture, governance and risk management.
## Audit quality is deeply embedded in our culture
[Leadership at RSM](https://rsmus.com/about/leadership.html "Leadership at RSM") plays a critical role in embedding quality into our organizational culture. The governance structure, including the [board of directors](https://rsmus.com/about/stewardship/governance.html#board "board of directors") and the Risk Oversight Committee, oversees risk management and supports adherence to policies.
Managing Partner and Chief Executive Officer Brian Becker, alongside a dedicated leadership team, emphasizes that quality management is integral to the firm’s strategy.
Responsibilities are delegated to specialized groups, such as the Office of Risk Management and NPSG, which focus on maintaining high standards in auditing and compliance.
## Delivering innovative audit services through a commitment to continuous improvement
At RSM, we demonstrate our commitment to audit quality through adherence to rigorous quality management standards, our innovative approach driven by RSM Luca and a leadership team dedicated to maintaining the highest standards of excellence.
Quality is woven into every aspect of our practice, enabling us to deliver exceptional, industry-tailored audit services while continuously advancing our methods and tools.
