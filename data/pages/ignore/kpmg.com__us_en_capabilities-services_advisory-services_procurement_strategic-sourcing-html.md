# Value Generation
Transforming procurement and enabling cross-functional value opportunities.
Value Generation is about finding value through procurement. It encompasses developing and executing an approach that continuously improves and assesses the purchasing activities of an organization while extracting the best possible value. Value can be defined as market related, risk reduction, tax efficiency and efficiency savings, all of which are captured within strategic sourcing and are an essential component of the journey to procurement excellence.
(1) Source: KPMG US, “KPMG 2023 Global Procurement Survey” (December 2023). | (2) Based on the findings of KPMG LLP.
## Driving impact through the value levers
Procurement Analytics  Operating Model  Cost of Goods Sold Impact  Working Capital  Tax Efficient Procurement  Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)  Market Intelligence  Supplier Management 
Procurement Analytics
#### Procurement Analytics
Gaining data-driven insights and visibility into spend and processes to optimize strategies.
Operating Model
#### Operating Model
Designing an effective operating model streamlines processes, improves operational effectiveness, and generates cost savings.
Cost of Goods Sold Impact
#### Cost of Goods Sold Impact
Significantly reducing COGS through strategic sourcing, negotiation, and demand management to improve profitability.
Working Capital
#### Working Capital
Optimizing financial management by extending supplier payment terms, establishing beneficial payment trigger dates and payment runs.
Tax Efficient Procurement
#### Tax Efficient Procurement
Implementing Pro-Co or Captive Purchasing with Tax-Advantaged Sourcing strategies can optimize procurement and financial operations.
Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)
#### Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)
Assessing risk related to business continuity, data, information, and IT security, as well as enhancing supplier compliance.
Market Intelligence
#### Market Intelligence
Utilizing data-driven insights to rapidly identify cost savings and efficiency gains.
Supplier Management
#### Supplier Management
Driving innovation, delivering value and managing risk through effective supplier relationships.

## Procurement trends & focus areas
Sustainable Sourcing & Social Responsibility
How should we manage our supplier relationships to ensure transparency, traceability, and build diversity?
GenAI
How can we generate powerful insights to enable data-driven decisions when creating value, improving efficiency and decreasing costs?
Agile Operating Model
How do we future-proof procurement? How do you apply continuous improvement to procurement’s operating model?
Category Innovation
How can we enable insight-based sourcing and driver-based planning to enable better category management? 
Insights & Analytics
How can insights drive better actions and, in turn, results? What new ways of thinking about data can drive fast, predictive and prescriptive actions?
Digital
How can we incorporate digital tools such as RPA, AI, and Cognitive Innovation, to improve business engagement and end user experience?
Supplier Innovation
What is necessary to optimize our supplier relationships to drive top-line growth and compliance? 
Risk Management
How can we create enhanced strategies to identify, assess, and mitigate supply chain risks, including geopolitical and cybersecurity threats?
