# Oracle JD Edwards Partner Excellence awards for Grant Thornton
In recognition of its JD Edwards practice, Grant Thornton has received Oracle JD Edwards 2020 Partner Excellence Awards in three categories: Application Optimization, Process Automation, and Building a Cloud Culture. The awards recognize Grant Thornton’s ability to combine business strategy with JD Edwards’ functional and technical capabilities to deliver value.
The trio of Partner Excellence honors, each rooted in a customer success story, demonstrates extensive experience implementing JD Edwards’ enterprise resource planning tools to help clients manage complex, large-scale projects and programs.
“As the business climate transforms in near real time, companies need advisors to help them match their application strategy with their business goals, while also realizing cost-savings and other upside benefits,” said Greg Davis, Technology Transformation principal and JD Edwards practice leader. “That’s exactly what we’re providing with JD Edwards. These awards exemplify Grant Thornton’s ability to deliver transformative services and solutions.”
The 2020 Partner Excellence Awards are the latest in a long line of accolades Grant Thornton has received over decades of collaborating with JD Edwards. For more information about Grant Thornton’s JD Edwards service offerings, visit [www.grantthornton.com/services/advisory](https://www.grantthornton.com/services/advisory-services).
