#  Sustainable Forces S3 E7: Can cities thrive on the water we waste? 
Cities are generating more wastewater and stormwater than ever before. Could this untapped resource help meet our growing demand for water? Listen now to find out.

Growing cities face increasing demand for freshwater, yet many urban water systems still follow a linear process of take, use, pollute and dispose. Circular thinking offers a smarter approach by turning wasted resources into new value for our cities.
In this episode, Dr Michelle Dickinson is joined by Chris Chesterfield, Professor of Practice at the Monash Sustainable Development Institute and Celeste Morgan, our Integrated Water Management Lead to explore ways cities can capture and reuse water to improve liveability, sustainability and resilience.
Together, we will discover how urban water systems are evolving, what it takes to build a circular water city, and how collaboration can create cleaner, healthier and more sustainable waterways for the communities of tomorrow.
