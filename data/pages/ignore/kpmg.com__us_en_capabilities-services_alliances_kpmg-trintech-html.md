# KPMG and Trintech
Technology-enabled record to report
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2022/kpmg-and-trintech-exploiting-technology.pdf)
Share
Today’s market requires strong governance over finance processes, people and technologies. However companies are challenged with decentralization, lack of real-time visibility into status and risk-related issues, heavily manual and spreadsheet-intensive processes, and inadequate and error-prone reporting.
KPMG and Trintech help clients improve performance by enhancing visibility, standardizing financial reporting processes, and simplifying workflow. The business process and technology prowess of KPMG combined with Trintech’s Cadency application helps establish an operational framework to empower companies to cut costs and better manage organizational expectations.
As a leader in Record-to-Report process improvement and enabling technologies, KPMG assists organizations with automation of account reconciliation and financial close, and enhancing value from Trintech’s applications.
To learn more, visit [Trintech](https://www.trintech.com/).
