# OnDemand Indirect Tax Services
A managed service providing ongoing tax engine configuration maintenance and support
##  An outsourced approach to indirect tax 
Regardless of which tax engine software you license, when it was implemented, or who implemented it, KPMG Indirect Tax Technology professionals can help your company focus on more important priorities by maintaining your tax engine for you. Our top differentiators include:
**Consistency:** Our service can reduce the risk of inconsistency often experienced by organizations going through periods of workforce change. KPMG will maintain documentation of all aspects of your tax policy configurations.
**Vendor alliances:** KPMG has strong connections with tax engine vendors and our professionals have developed deep, technical knowledge working with all types of third-party tax engines. Our frequent communication and dedicated access channels with key vendors allow us to escalate software-related concerns when needed.
**Value:** KPMG can remove the burden of tax engine configuration, troubleshooting, documentation, and overall tax policy maintenance from your list of responsibilities.
