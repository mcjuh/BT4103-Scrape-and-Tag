Our Solutions
Kroll Economics helps the C-suite act with confidence and clarity—wherever economic questions arise. With rigorous scenario analysis and practical solutions, we can support with:
Geopolitical and Macroeconomic Stress Testing
We use our proprietary, market-leading geoeconomic models to quantify how geopolitical and macroeconomic shocks and trends affect corporate resilience, portfolio performance and strategic plans. 
Scenario and Risk Analysis
We develop tailored, data driven scenarios that translate complex macro, market and policy risks into clear implications for clients’ strategies, investments, and operations. 
Trade and Economic Policy
We provide evidence based analysis and modelling to help governments and corporates navigate trade dynamics, tariff changes and economic policy decisions. 
Policy and Regulatory Insight
We assess evolving regulatory frameworks and policy reforms to inform and positively impact economic, financial and operational outcomes for firms, regulators and policymakers. 
Economic Impact Assessments
We quantify the economy-wide GDP, employment, fiscal and social contributions of investments, assets or policies to inform strategy, funding and stakeholder engagement. 
Market Intelligence
We combine real time data, advanced analytics and structured market assessment to deliver clear insights on market opportunities, competitive dynamics, sector trends and commercial opportunities. 
Climate and Energy Impact Modelling Policy
We evaluate transition and physical climate risks using advanced economic and energy system modelling to assess their impacts on economies, sectors and firms in order to inform strategy, investment theses and risk mitigation.
