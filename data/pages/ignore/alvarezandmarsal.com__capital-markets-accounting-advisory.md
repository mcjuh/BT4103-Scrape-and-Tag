# Capital Markets & Accounting Advisory
[ Talk to us to learn more ](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/#block-connect-with-us)
Our Capital Markets and Accounting Advisory team has the expertise to assist boards, management teams and company functions in addressing the challenges faced in today’s evolving capital market’s environment. We support companies throughout the entire investment lifecycle and our service areas span from addressing complex technical accounting issues in acquisitions, carve-outs and restatements to financial management, transformation and digitalization as well as supporting in every stage of an IPO process.
