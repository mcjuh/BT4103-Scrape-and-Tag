# KPMG and IBM
Empowering clients with the power of AI
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2024/kpmg-ibm-client-first-slipsheet.pdf)
Share
As organizations advance their transformation agendas and embrace technologies to remain competitive, it becomes imperative for them to unlock value. A strategic collaboration can help improve efficiency, develop insights from the available ecosystems, and lower costs. However, the search for one that understands the intricacies of the diverse technologies at play can be challenging. 
Through a client-first approach, KPMG LLP and IBM bring innovative offerings to market across Hybrid Cloud, Data, AI, and other emerging technologies, including quantum computing and post-quantum cryptography, that help clients accelerate digital transformation. The regulatory, risk, and finance experience of KPMG, combined with the robust consultancy and technology competencies of IBM, can help clients address challenges with knowledge-driven and actionable solutions.
## Trusting in the Power of AI
KPMG and IBM can help clients move forward in their digital transformation journey with confidence. Alliance teams will work to ally concerns relating to the ethical and responsible development, deployment, and use of Gen AI technologies in digital transformation programs, and create trust in the tools that can ultimately help reduce operational risk, drive value creation, and strengthen the management of regulatory risk.
## Better Together
The process of digital transformation of a client’s ERP system – its resources, data, and operations across multiple departments – relies on the capabilities that are rooted in trust and certainty. Those two attributes form the core of the KPMG and IBM alliance, which seeks to enable agility and competitiveness in a business environment rife with uncertainties. 
The KPMG-IBM alliance can help deliver secure and data-driven innovation for our clients. We are prepared to put it to work creating greater value for you. 
To learn more, visit [IBM](http://www.ibm.com/) or [Red Hat](https://www.redhat.com/en).
