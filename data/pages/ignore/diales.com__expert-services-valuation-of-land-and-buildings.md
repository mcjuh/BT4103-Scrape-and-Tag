#  Valuation of Land and Buildings
Content
Content
#### Diales has an experienced team of valuers with a wide range of sector experience, including commercial buildings (industrial, leisure, office and retail), a wide variety of residential premises (houses to large scale flatted schemes), mixed-use developments and land. 
Our valuers have extensive UK market knowledge and a wealth of technical expertise. Our integrity and willingness to explain and discuss our approach and methodology is central to our professional advice. These principles enable us to both create and build trusted relationships. 
We have extensive experience throughout the property life cycle, from valuing land to completed and let schemes to buildings requiring refurbishment and urban regeneration projects. 
We provide clients with a truly independent view of our opinion of value(s). Our experience helps clients to better understand both the technical and market related issues around a variety of disputes. We provide clear and detailed insights that lead to informed decisions based on our approach and methodology in the preparation of our valuation opinions. 
Our role as an expert witness means we provide high quality valuation advice in either defence or support of litigation, negligence and other specialist matters. 
Working collaboratively with the other in-house technical capabilities and expertise in architecture, safety and fire, structural engineering, and MEP, Diales can deliver a unique, integrated, and trusted valuation service offering for our clients.
This offering enables us to incorporate the impact on building asset value into our wider capabilities to provide a fully rounded service.
**Our experts’ sector experience includes:**
  * Industrial and warehouses 
  * Offices 
  * Retail – including high street, shopping centres, out of town and supermarkets 
  * Leisure – including restaurants, cafes, cinemas, pubs and gyms 
  * Residential – including houses, flats and large purpose built blocks 
  * Mixed-use 
  * Land - both with and without planning permission


**Common instructions our experts are requested to opine on include:**
  * Professional negligence 
  * Fiduciary claims 
  * Dilapidations 
  * General litigation
