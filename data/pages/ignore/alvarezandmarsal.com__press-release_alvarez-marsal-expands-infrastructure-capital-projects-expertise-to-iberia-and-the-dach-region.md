# Alvarez & Marsal Expands Infrastructure & Capital Projects Expertise to Iberia and the DACH Region


**Hamburg & Madrid, 26th June 2025 -- **Global professional services firm [Alvarez & Marsal](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/) ([A&M](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/)) announces the appointment of two Managing Directors, effective immediately, to the growing roster of talent in its [Infrastructure & Capital Projects practice (A&M INFRA)](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/infra):
  * [**Matthias Laue**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/matthias-laue)  _joins in Hamburg to lead the practice in the DACH region_
  * [**Gabriel Valtueña-Ramos**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/gabriel-valtuena) _joins in A &M’s Madrid office to lead the practice across Iberia_


Iberia and DACH are already established, core markets for A&M. The senior hires are in response to growing client demand from clients in both regions for sector-specific, operational leadership across the infrastructure value chain, including in power, energy, transport, construction and public-private partnerships (PPPs). The moves also build on sustained global expansion of A&M INFRA over the past year, including in [India](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/alvarez-marsal-expands-its-infrastructure-capital-projects-group-india-pankaj-bhagat), [Saudi Arabia](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/james-irvine), the [UK](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/craig-forrest) and [US](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/alvarez-marsal-launches-new-transportation-and-logisitics-infrastructure-group-north).
[**Marcos Ganut**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/marcos-ganut)**, Managing Director and Global Head of Infrastructure and Capital Projects at A &M, said: **‘We are thrilled to announce the continued expansion of A&M INFRA across Europe. Matthias is an expert known for his understanding of the entire energy value chain. Gabriel has helped private clients and public authorities worldwide in the origination and delivery of major construction and infrastructure projects. Both are outstanding professionals who will strengthen our presence and capabilities in these key markets.’
Mr. Laue brings more than 30 years of experience in transaction advisory and dispute resolution within the power, heat, and gas sectors. He joins A&M from 15 years with AFRY Management Consulting, where he served as Global Head of Transaction Advisory and Head of Dispute Resolution. Having advised on many landmark transactions (market, commercial, regulatory, technical, and ESG due diligence) in the European power, heat, and gas markets, he has also given expert witness testimony in a large number of high-stake disputes. Recognised as an energy expert by Who’s Who Legal/Lexology, earlier in his career Mr. Laue held expert and management roles at Fortum Oil & Gas and in ExxonMobil.
Mr Laue noted: ‘I am glad to be part of the A&M Infra team, as the A&M platform in Europe follows a successful growth model. I look forward to helping clients create value through M&A, growth strategy, and dispute resolution.’
With 25 years’ experience in the construction and infrastructure industry, Mr. Valtueña-Ramos joins A&M from construction firm CABBSA where, as interim CEO, he redefined the company’s strategy to drive greater growth and diversification. He previously co-led McKINSEY’s Construction & Infrastructure practice in Iberia for seven years. Before that he spent nearly 14 years at CINTRA (FERROVIAL), working on the development, financing, construction, and operation of major international infrastructure projects, including eight years as CEO of several CINTRA subsidiaries in Spain, the UK and Australia. A qualified civil engineer, he began his career at construction companies such as DRAGADOS, ACS, and SANDO, where he was involved in the execution of major infrastructure projects.
Mr. Valtueña-Ramos commented: ‘A&M INFRA brings together a team not only of sector experts, but industry leaders. I am keen to draw on my experience to help tackle clients’ challenges and bring holistic transformation to their projects.’
