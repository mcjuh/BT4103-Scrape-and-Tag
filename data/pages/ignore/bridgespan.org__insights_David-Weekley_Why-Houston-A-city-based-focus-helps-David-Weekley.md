# Why Houston? A city-based focus helps David Weekley add unique value to the organizations he supports
“I just naturally gravitate to those [organizations] where my time and expertise and check can have more impact,” says David Weekley. Being “all about results,” Weekley notes that a hometown focus makes keeping tabs on and influencing outcomes easier.
