## Early-stage drug discovery and development have famously always been time-consuming, costly, and risky. But not in the age of AI and digitalization.
Ask anyone in pharma about the challenges of early-stage drug discovery and developmentand you’ll get the same answer:  _it’s notoriously time-consuming, costly, and risky_. It’s always been this way. In the first part of this Pharma Convergence series, “[**The rise of TechBio and R &D’s evolving operating models**](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/expert-perspectives/the-rise-of-techbio-and-rds-evolving-operating-models/),” we identified the traditional siloed approach as a contributing factor, comparing it with TechBio’s emerging advantages. But despite technological advances, the industry still struggles to overcome longstanding [disheartening statistics](https://ai.creative-biolabs.com/blog/enhancing-lead-optimization-in-small-molecule-drug-discovery-using-artificial-intelligence/): drug development timelines of 10-15 years, success rates below 10%, and a staggering average cost of $1-2 billion. 
At the heart of this challenge lies the early R&D phase, in which scientists explore disease biology, find chemical “hits,” and refine them into viable candidates for treatment. These foundational steps are critical, yet traditionally inefficient. The struggle, however, is now coming to an end. Today, digital transformation isn’t just enhancing the early R&D process; it’s reinventing it. 
### AI in drug discovery and development 
Leading pharmaceutical companies are leveraging AI, automation, and advanced analytics to make significant gains: accelerating discovery timelines by **20-50% and reducing early development costs by up to 50%**. These gains improve efficiency, unlock capacity for more innovation, and fast-track the delivery of life-changing treatments to patients. As digital transformation reshapes early-stage drug development, knowing where to focus, what to measure, and how to act is a strategic imperative for competitiveness, innovation, and results.

### Six pivotal phases of AI in drug development 
#### Phase 1: Understanding disease biology 
Drug discovery begins with  _understanding the disease biology_ – identifying key mechanisms, pathways, and potential targets like proteins or genes. Digitalization is reshaping this phase by integrating genomic, proteomic, clinical, and literature data into unified platforms, enabling faster and deeper insights. 
AI models can now cross-analyze multi-omics datasets to uncover novel targets that manual methods might miss. Tools like AlphaFold have transformed structural biology by [predicting structures](https://www.drugdiscoverytrends.com/7-ways-deepmind-alphafold-used-life-sciences/) for  _virtually all catalogued proteins_ , helping assess “druggability” in minutes. 
#### Phase 2: Digital decision-making in modality selection 
Once a target is identified, the next step is choosing the best therapeutic modality (e.g., small molecule, biologic, gene therapy, etc.). Traditionally based on expert judgment, this decision is now being enhanced by AI and predictive modelling. 
Digital tools analyze target properties (e.g., 3D structure, cellular location, pathway role) and historical data to recommend the most suitable modality. Additionally, silico modelling allows researchers to simulate how different modalities might interact with the target, enabling faster, more informed decisions. 
#### Phase 3: Accelerating hit Identification 
Hit identification involves finding compounds that interact with the target, traditionally done via high-throughput lab screening. Digital transformation introduces AI-driven virtual screening and intelligent automation, dramatically improving speed, cost-efficiency, and hit quality. 
AI-driven virtual screening can rapidly scan massive compound libraries to [predict likely binders](https://ai.creative-biolabs.com/blog/enhancing-lead-optimization-in-small-molecule-drug-discovery-using-artificial-intelligence/), streamlining candidate selection before lab testing. By screening billions of compounds, AI dramatically improves hit rates and reduces lab workload. 
**Industry success story – Numerion Labs:** [Numerion Labs’s platform](https://numerionlabs.ai/) screened 10 billion molecules in two days, with a ~7% hit rate, which by far exceeds traditional methods. Robotics and real-time data loops accelerate assays and feed Al for continuous learning. The end result: Faster, cheaper, more productive hit discovery. This is the power of AI in drug discovery. 
#### Phase 4: AI-accelerated hit-to-lead development 
The focus of this phase is on transforming promising “hits” into optimized lead compounds – candidates with strong efficacy, minimal side effects, and favorable pharmacokinetics. Traditionally slow and iterative, it is now accelerated by AI-driven design and automated synthesis. 
Machine learning models can predict how structural changes impact a [compound’s performance](https://www.ijpsjournal.com/assetsbackoffice/uploads/article/Lead+Optimization+Integrating+Experimental+Computational+and+AIML+Approaches.pdf), such as binding affinity or solubility, helping chemists prioritize promising modifications. Generative AI (Gen AI) can even propose novel analogues tailored to desired properties. 
By filtering out low-potential compounds in silico and leveraging automated synthesis and testing, the traditionally slow design-and-test cycle becomes a fast, data-driven loop. This tight human-AI chemistry accelerates convergence on high-quality lead compounds with fewer iteration. 
#### Phase 5: Refining leads into drug candidates to drug candidate 
Here, the top lead is refined and nominated as a drug candidate for preclinical testing. The focus is on de-risking to ensure efficacy, safety, and pharmacokinetics are acceptable before major investment. 
Digital tools streamline this process through in silico Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion, Toxicity (ADMET) predictions; simulation models, and integrated data dashboards. These help flag issues early, guide final tweaks, and enable faster, evidence-based decisions. 
#### Phase 6: Investigational new drug (IND) enablement through digital integration 
This final phase prepares the Investigational New Drug (IND) application, consolidating safety data, manufacturing details, and regulatory documentation. Traditionally complex and time-consuming, it is now streamlined through digital project management, AI-assisted documentation, and centralized data systems. 
AI tools help auto-generate dossier sections, check data consistency, and simulate key decisions (e.g., human starting dose). Integrated dashboards align toxicology, Chemistry, Manufacturing, and Controls (CMC); and regulatory teams, reducing delays and errors.
### Accelerating digitalization in pharma: The Payoff 
Across all six phases of early drug R&D, digital transformation is proving to be a gamechanger. From AI-powered target discovery to automated IND preparation, each step becomes faster, smarter, and more cost-effective. 
By tracking both high-level KPIs and granular sub-metrics, organizations can quantify improvements in speed, cost, and success rates. Real-world examples show dramatic gains, such as reducing discovery-to-candidate timelines [from four years to one](https://greyb.com/blog/ai-in-biotech/) and cutting IND prep time by [30%.](https://www.ijpsjournal.com/assetsbackoffice/uploads/article/Lead+Optimization+Integrating+Experimental+Computational+and+AIML+Approaches.pdf) In the next part of this Pharma Convergence series, “Unlocking system-wide productivity gains in R&D,” we’ll take a closer look at how to reverse the productivity crisis in drug discovery. 
The ultimate beneficiaries are patients and healthcare systems, as promising therapies reach clinical trials and the market sooner. By embracing digital tools, pharma R&D evolves from a slow, manual process into an agile, data-driven enterprise, accelerating innovation without compromising scientific rigor.

## FAQs
### Explain how machine learning accelerates hit identification in drug discovery.
Machine learning enables pharma companies to more rapidly screen billions of compounds virtually, leading to predicted probable binders, improved hit rates, reduced laboratory workload, and the identification of promising candidates.
### How can AI improve the efficiency of clinical trials in pharma?
AI improves clinical trials by identifying suitable patient populations, predicting outcomes, optimizing study design, and accelerating data analysis. This results in reduced timelines and operational costs.
### How do AI algorithms help in personalized medicine during drug development?
AI analyzes genomic, clinical, and biomarker data to identify patient subgroups. This enables companies to identify more targeted therapies, improved treatment effectiveness, and better safety.
### How does AI support target discovery in early drug R&D?
AI integrates multi-omics, clinical, and scientific literature data to uncover novel disease targets, while tools like AlphaFold rapidly assess protein structures and druggability.
### How are pharma companies addressing the productivity crisis in drug discovery?
Pharma companies are adopting AI, automation, predictive modelling, and integrated digital platforms to accelerate drug discovery, reduce costs, improve success rates, and streamline decision-making.
