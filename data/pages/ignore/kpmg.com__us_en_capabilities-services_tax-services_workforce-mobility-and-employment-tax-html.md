# Workforce mobility and employment tax
Managing a mobile workforce across multiple countries and jurisdictions is more than just a human resources challenge. It also means meeting local tax requirements for employees wherever they go.
##  Tax services for today’s mobile workforce 
KPMG offers a full range of tax solutions for organizations with mobile, hybrid, and/or remote workers in virtually any country. Our specialists can help you manage tax reporting, risk, and compliance more effectively while allowing your employees to work more flexibly and travel more widely. Let us help you keep pace with regulatory requirements and cross-border complexities with greater confidence and foresight.
