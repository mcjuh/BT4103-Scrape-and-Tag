## Frequently asked questions
How does SOC.x benefit me?
SOC.x is a leading-edge solution specifically built to deliver SOC reports efficiently. That helps us avoid duplication, drive consistency, and provide you with insights about your compliance assessment journey.
Who is Hyperproof?
Hyperproof Inc. is a leading provider of SaaS compliance operations for mid-sized to large enterprises. We have joined forces with Hyperproof to help our clients better manage risk and enhance the efficiency of compliance programs and reports.
How does SOC.x integrate with Hyperproof?
Through its proprietary end-to-end operation platform, Hyperproof helps customers understand numerous compliance requirements, manage internal controls, define ideal compliance/audit processes and workflows, automate manual tasks, and monitor and report on their compliance posture. We have jointly developed seamless data exchange capability between SOC.x and Hyperproof to empower our clients to collaborate with their Grant Thornton professionals before, during, and after an audit.
