# Bring an open mind: Desh Deshpande says the best solutions come from “catalyzing” not “designing”
The best solutions to social problems come from the community members that will benefit from the innovation, says Desh Deshpande. “Let people living in those societies come up with solutions and play a catalyzing role, as opposed to being the architect.” [See More Remarkable Givers](https://www.bridgespan.org/Insights/Library/Remarkable-Givers/Profiles)
