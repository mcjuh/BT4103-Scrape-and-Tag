#  Diversity, Inclusion, Belonging
Creating a diverse and inclusive work environment where everyone belongs is an essential part of making ERM a great place to build a career. We formalized our diversity, inclusion, and belonging (DIB) mission over a decade ago, and it is integral to our ability to foster a culture where every employee feels valued, respected, and empowered. This long-held commitment ensures that diverse perspectives are not only welcomed but also actively integrated into our business strategies, leading to more innovative solutions.
By embedding DIB into everything we do, we attract and retain top talent, enhance employee engagement, and build a workplace where all employees can thrive authentically.
Our Approach
## At ERM, Diversity, Inclusion and Belonging (DIB): 
Integrates strategy, data analysis, benchmarking, and ERM’s business goals to drive inclusive excellence and organizational effectiveness. 
Fosters a culture of diversity, inclusion, and belonging across all levels of the organization. 
Highlights our commitment to creating an environment where every employee feels valued and empowered to contribute their unique perspectives and talents.
**DIB Mission Statement**
Our diversity, inclusion, and belonging mission is to be the leading global force in environmental and sustainability consulting, leveraging the power of diversity, inclusion, and belonging to drive innovation and deliver unparalleled solutions that positively impact our clients, employees, and the communities we serve.
**Employee Resource Groups**
Our global employee resource groups support and create space for all employees to build connections and thrive. These include the Asian Professional Network, Black Professionals Network, Caregivers @ ERM, EnAble (disability and neurodiversity), GLOW (Global Women), Hispanic/Latinx Professionals Network, North American Veterans, and PRIDE (LGBTQ+).
Annual Observances
## Every year, we celebrate various occasions to foster inclusion among all our employees. Our observances strengthen a culture that celebrates diversity and cultivates belonging for everyone.
### Asian Cultural Exchange
A celebration of the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of Asian people.
### Black History Month
A celebration of the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of Black people.
### Carers Month
A celebration of caregivers and the experience of caregiving.
### Hispanic/Latinx Heritage Month
A celebration of the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of Hispanic and Latinx people.
### Global Inclusion Month
A celebration of diversity and inclusion throughout our workplace. 
### International Day of Persons with Disabilities
An occasion to promote the rights and well-being of people with disabilities and raise awareness of the barriers they face in all aspects of society.
### Pride Month
A celebration of the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of LGBTQ+ people.
### Veterans and Remembrance Day
A time to honor and remember the service, dedication, and courage of military veterans and those who offered the ultimate sacrifice.
### Women’s History Month and International Women's Day
A celebration of the social, economic, cultural, and political achievements of women.
**Sponsorship Program**
LEAD, our sponsorship program, was created to help employees build a successful ERM career. Through meaningful, long-term relationships between employees seeking professional growth and senior leaders who provide advocacy and guidance, participants develop their careers, expand internal networks, and deepen their understanding of ERM. LEAD encompasses four stages of developing a beneficial relationship: Listen (to each other), Elevate (a skill), Advocate (for opportunity), and Dedicate (new experiences).
**Learning Programs**
Passport to DIB is a collection of learning programs that supports employees on an exciting pathway towards a more diverse and inclusive environment. Each learning journey is designed to support personal and career growth at ERM by building awareness and understanding of key topics and behaviors and encouraging the incorporation of DIB into our daily ways of working.
