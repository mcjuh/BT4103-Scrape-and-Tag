#  Revolutionizing Enterprise Software Deployment 
For organizations looking to create a strategic advantage with AI and technology automation, the launch of Nectar Works by PS Hummingbird marks a significant milestone. This innovative AI platform is set to transform the way enterprise software is deployed, offering unprecedented efficiency, speed, and quality.
## Introducing Nectar Works
Nectar Works is a proprietary AI platform designed to streamline the project lifecycle of Dynamics 365CE and Power Platform projects. As Adam Piercy, the Director of AI Transformation for PS Hummingbird, explains, “Nectar Works is an interface for managing various AI agents that store elements of the project lifecycle, from sales to delivery handover. Simply said, Nectar Works helps the implementation teams understand and deliver exactly what is in the project scope without endless handoff discussions. The delivery team can query the Nectar Works Sales Agent instead of taking time away from the sales team.”
The delivery team can also use Nectar Work generative AI agents to develop the deliverables needed in each phase of the project lifecycle. When used for tasks like workshop planning, blueprint creation, backlog management, and sprint planning. Nectar Works significantly accelerates time to value, making it an invaluable asset for any project.
## The Synergy with Sapient Slingshot
Publicis Sapient, PS Hummingbird’s parent company, developed a proprietary AI platform called Sapient Slingshot. While Nectar Works focuses on the project lifecycle, Sapient Slingshot is designed for software development. The two platforms work hand in hand to deliver exceptional results.
Sapient Slingshot aids developers in writing code, analyzing Power Automate flows and working with JSON, among other tasks. It enhances the efficiency of the software development lifecycle, On the other hand, Nectar Works uses generative AI to accelerate the project implementation. Together, these platforms create a seamless and efficient workflow, ensuring that projects are delivered on time and with the highest quality.
## Unprecedented productivity and quality
The integration of Nectar Works and Sapient Slingshot has led to remarkable improvements in productivity and quality. According to Adam Piercy, “We estimate that using these platforms results in a 40% increase in speed from design to code, iterate, build, and test; allowing organizations to reap the benefits of their projects sooner.”
In terms of code verification, the platforms have shown a 50-60% increase in identifying and correcting software defects. This improvement in quality ensures that the final product is robust and reliable. Additionally, the platforms reduce the time spent on administrative tasks and meetings, allowing project teams to focus more on strategy and delivery. This shift in focus leads to a more consultative approach, where the real pain points of the business are identified and addressed.
## Industry-specific expertise
One of the standout features of Nectar Works is its ability to adapt to different industries. The platform can be customized to incorporate industry-specific knowledge, making it an expert in the field it is deployed in. Nectar Works industry AI agents build the industry standard 80% of the project, allowing the team to focus on that last 20% that creates competitive advantage.
Nectar Works retains all the history and knowledge accumulated over time, ensuring that valuable insights are not lost due to team member turnover. This continuity is crucial for long-term projects, where maintaining a consistent knowledge base is essential for success.
##  A new era of AI consulting 
PS Hummingbird prides itself on being an AI-native company. Formed after the advent of ChatGPT, the company has embraced AI as a core component of its consulting services. As Adam Piercy puts it, “PS Hummingbird aims to be an AI consulting firm, not just a consulting firm that uses AI.”
This forward-thinking approach sets the company apart and positions it as a leader in the field of AI-driven enterprise software deployment.
