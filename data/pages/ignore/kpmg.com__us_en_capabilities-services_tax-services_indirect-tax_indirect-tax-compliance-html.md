# Indirect Tax Compliance
Better management of your indirect tax compliance obligations is a necessity
##  Better compliance for any indirect tax 
Reporting and filing indirect taxes accurately can be a major challenge, and changes to tax laws and accounting standards mean that staying informed and up to date is essential to avoid penalties or overpayments.
The KPMG Indirect Tax Compliance practice can help you manage your indirect tax processes and controls more effectively. We take the time to understand the specific challenges you face, and we apply technology, data, and automation to make compliance faster, easier, and more accurate. KPMG has the scale and flexibility to offer options that include:
  * Full outsourcing of sales and use tax compliance
  * Cosourcing for specific taxes and services
  * Access to data and resources from our national compliance centers.


Offloading your compliance burden also means you’ll have more time to focus on higher-value activities such as tax planning and risk management.

A top indirect tax team
KPMG has one of the largest indirect tax practices in the U.S., with more than 300 indirect tax professionals and national compliance centers in Atlanta and Chicago.
In-depth local knowledge
Our sales and use tax professionals bring a detailed understanding of local statutory reporting rules and practices for all states and jurisdictions.
Leading technology
KPMG uses technology to manage and automate compliance. We combine third-party software with proprietary KPMG technology and robotic process automation (RPA) tools.
Accessible reporting

### Improve compliance for any indirect tax
We offer a full suite of technology enabled indirect tax compliance services to help organizations meet indirect tax reporting and filing obligations for all indirect tax types.
Sales and use tax
KPMG is one of the largest providers of indirect tax services in the world. Our specialists can help you achieve sales and use tax compliance across thousands of jurisdictions.
With decades of dedicated business license experience, as well as specialized knowledge of state and local regulations, KPMG helps make adhering to licensing requirements less complex.
With decades of dedicated property tax experience and specialized knowledge of state and local regulations, KPMG helps achieve property tax compliance across thousands of state and local jurisdictions.
Motor fuels excise tax
With states and localities changing rules and increasing audits, complying with motor fuels excise taxes is more complex than ever. KPMG helps make compliance more automated and accurate.
With hands-on experience working through complex VAT regimes in many countries, our specialists know how to make compliance more complete and less time-consuming.
Unclaimed property regulations are complex, and compliance failures can be expensive. Our professionals keep up by staying in touch with state unclaimed property administrators.
Across multiple states and jurisdictions, KPMG Severance Tax and Royalty Services professionals provide clarity in helping you interpret your severance taxes and royalties to help ensure you realize the full value of your upstream assets.
