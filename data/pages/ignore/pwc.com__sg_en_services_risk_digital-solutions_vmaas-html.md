With the evolving threat landscape and the growing complexity of technology ecosystems, any organisation needs to know their assets and understand their weak points. Early detection of security holes is crucial for preventing cyber attacks and for identifying effective solutions for your organisation. There is, however, inefficiency in the vulnerability process of many organisations. The traditional vulnerability management process is a continuously evolving set of information security that is time-consuming, resource-intensive and inefficient. 
## How we can help you 
Our Vulnerability Management as a Service (VMaaS) helps you to increase the visibility of your assets and minimise your threats in a fast, scalable, intelligent and automated manner. We help clients with end-to-end vulnerability management from the perspectives of people, process and technology. 
  1. Hackbot


### Hackbot
Hackbot is an integrated platform that utilises robotics technology to automate and strengthen your threat and vulnerability management process. It automates threat scanning and report generation, prioritises vulnerabilities based on the likelihood and impact of risks, and identifies common vulnerabilities across applications. Through Hackbot, you can achieve:
  * Greater efficiency through the automation of routine tasks 
  * A faster vulnerability assessment process 
  * A strategic prioritisation of tasks to be done in the vulnerability management process.
