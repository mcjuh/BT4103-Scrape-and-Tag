# State and Local Tax
Stay ahead of tax rules across the country.
##  Keep pace with constant change 
Complying with state and local tax requirements has always been challenging, especially for businesses that operate in multiple states and municipalities. Regulations vary widely and change frequently, and the rise of online commerce means that even companies with no physical presence in a state may still owe tax on local sales.
The KPMG State and Local Tax (SALT) practice makes navigating a sprawling, constantly changing tax landscape less difficult and more efficient. Our experience and local knowledge can help you pay the right amount of tax in every state and locality. And we also provide careful, proactive tax planning and compliance advice.
