Our cross-cutting expertise allows us to work more effectively with policymakers, creating stronger public policies that support economic growth, reduce poverty, and address social disadvantage. 
  * [ Gender equality and social inclusion ](https://www.opml.co.uk/gender-equality-and-social-inclusion)
  * [ Office of the Chief Economist ](https://www.opml.co.uk/office-chief-economist)


As well as sector specialisms, we are also able to draw on the cross-cutting expertise of our dedicated teams throughout the business. This multi-sectoral approach gives us a deep understanding of a county’s political economy, meaning we can better research, design, implement, and evaluate impactful public policy.
Our teams of cross-cutting specialists, include:
  * [Research and evidence](https://www.opml.co.uk/our-expertise/research-and-evidence): our team members in monitoring, evaluation, and learning; research management; and data innovation, bring together communities of practice to bridge the gap between evidence and practical policy delivery. 
  * [Macroeconomics and diagnostics](https://www.opml.co.uk/office-chief-economist): delivered through the Office of the Chief Economist, enhancing the impact of our work in development economics.
  * [Gender equality and social inclusion](https://www.opml.co.uk/gender-equality-and-social-inclusion): shaping just, inclusive, and sustainable public policies that empower women and marginalised groups across sectors we work in.
