KPMG Powered Evolution helps you extend the value of your transformation, beyond a successful ServiceNow go-live. As your business evolves, so should your platform. Powered Evolution delivers the agility, governance, and expertise needed to keep your ServiceNow environment optimized, secure, and aligned with the latest innovations.
## The challenge
Transformation doesn’t end at implementation. Disruptive technologies, shifting market dynamics, and evolving organizational needs demand continuous adaptation. Without a proactive strategy, your ServiceNow platform risks becoming outdated. A lack of proper support and governance can turn today’s best practices into tomorrow’s legacy platform.
Twice-yearly ServiceNow releases require careful planning and execution. Retaining specialized talent for periodic updates is costly and inefficient. By integrating AI-driven insights and automation into release management, organizations can streamline impact assessments, accelerate adoption, and reduce manual overhead. KPMG Powered Evolution offers a scalable, cost-effective solution to stay current and continuously drive value from your ServiceNow investment.
## **The KPMG approach**
As a Global Elite Partner and the 2025 ServiceNow Worldwide Transformation Partner of the Year, KPMG combines deep ServiceNow know-how with leading practices that help you stay up-to-speed with the platform’s latest updates and can help you stay ahead with its innovative technologies, including AI. Our flexible support model can provide the right resources at the right time, whether you need strategic guidance, operational support, or just technical enhancements.
We can help you:
  * Realize sustained value from your transformation investment
  * Mitigate risk through proactive governance and controls
  * Accelerate adoption of new ServiceNow features
  * Maintain agility in a rapidly changing business environment
  * Prepare for AI features and capabilities
