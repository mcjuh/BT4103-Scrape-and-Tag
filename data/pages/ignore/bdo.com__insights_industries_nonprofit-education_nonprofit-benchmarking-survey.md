# 2025 Nonprofit Benchmarking Survey
## Where Do You Stack Up Against Similar Organizations?
Nonprofits face adversity but are strengthened by their purpose.
BDO's ninth annual Nonprofit Standards Benchmarking Report analyzes responses from 250 nonprofit executives. Learn how nonprofit organizations are navigating economic uncertainty, threats to funding, and a volatile federal policy landscape while simultaneously pursuing growth, partnerships and their missions. 
Read the study of all 250 respondents, as well as the studies of the 50 higher education leaders and 50 grantmaking organizations.
