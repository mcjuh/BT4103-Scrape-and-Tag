# Slalom named one of Canada’s Best Workplaces™ with Most Trusted Executive Teams for 2023
February 28, 2023
[ Share ](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/newsroom/canada-most-trusted-executive-team?=2024-financial-services-outlook#shareModal)
**Canada - February 28, 2023** – Slalom, the global business and technology consulting company, has been named [one of Canada’s Best Workplaces™ with Most Trusted Executive Teams for 2023 by Great Place to Work®](https://www.greatplacetowork.ca/en/best-workplaces/best-workplaces-with-most-trusted-executive-teams-2023#2023-most-trusted-executive-teams/view-sub-list-details70/63597da816ca460021e6972f/). The award recognizes executive teams who have excelled in their management abilities by building effective relationships and confidence among their employees.
Inclusion in the list is entirely based on the results of anonymous responses to the [Great Place to Work](https://www.greatplacetowork.ca/en/)®’s Trust Index by Slalom employees, which measures leadership effectiveness and employee trust in their executive teams.
“For our Canada leadership team, one of our greatest privileges is to work alongside our 900+ employees across Canada, building an amazing culture where we can all love our life and work. We are incredibly honoured to foster an environment where our people feel both supported and motivated to dream bigger, move faster, and build better tomorrows for all,” says Wayne Ingram, President, Slalom Canada.
You can learn more about our Canada teams at these links: [Calgary](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/locations/calgary?=2024-financial-services-outlook), [Montréal](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/locations/montreal?=2024-financial-services-outlook), [Toronto,](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/locations/toronto?=2024-financial-services-outlook) and [Vancouver](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/who-we-are/locations/vancouver?=2024-financial-services-outlook), and more about our recognition on the [Great Place to Work](https://www.greatplacetowork.ca/en)® page.
**About Slalom**
Slalom is a purpose-led, global business and technology consulting company. From strategy to implementation, our approach is fiercely human. In seven countries and 44 markets, we deeply understand our customers—and their customers—to deliver practical, end-to-end solutions that drive meaningful impact. Backed by close partnerships with over 400 leading technology providers, our 13,000+ strong team helps people and organizations dream bigger, move faster, and build better tomorrows for all. We’re honoured to be consistently recognized as a great place to work, including being one of Fortune’s 100 Best Companies to Work For seven years running.
