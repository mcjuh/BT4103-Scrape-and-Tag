# Alvarez & Marsal's Global Transaction Advisory Group Continues Building in Germany as EMEA Market Demand Strengthens


### _New Managing Director Silvio Ritz joins as firm continues to expand its transactions capacity in the region_
Düsseldorf – 29 July 2026 – **Alvarez & Marsal (A&M**), a leading global professional services firm, today announced the appointment of [Silvio Ritz](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/silvio-ritz) as a Managing Director within its [Global Transaction Advisory Group](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/global-transaction-advisory) (Global TAG). Based in Düsseldorf, Mr. Ritz’s geographic appointment further expands A&M’s transaction advisory footprint in Germany, reinforcing the firm’s continued investment in one of Europe's most active M&A markets and enhancing its ability to support corporate and private equity clients across the region.
Mr. Ritz brings nearly 20 years of experience advising corporate and private equity clients throughout the M&A lifecycle – helping them identify key value drivers, assess transaction risks and uncover value creation opportunities. Specializing in financial due diligence – advising on both buy-side and sell-side transactions, vendor assistance, business plan assessments and refinancing processes across mid-market and larger transactions – Mr. Ritz has focused on multiple industries with a concentration in retail and wholesale, e-commerce, consumer goods and consumer services. 
[Paul Aversano](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/paul-aversano), Managing Director and Global Practice Leader of Global TAG, said, "Our growth centres on strategic talent investment and leveraging A&M’s integrated platform to solve challenges for clients. As market demand rises, we stay several steps ahead – investing in the key markets where clients seek our differentiated service model, backed by deep local knowledge. Silvio's appointment reflects this commitment and reinforces our transaction advisory strength across Germany and the broader European market.”
"Silvio brings exactly the combination of technical excellence and commercial, industry-specific insight our clients look for," said Managing Directors and Global TAG EMEA Co-Leaders [Jonathan Gibbons](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/jonathan-gibbons) & [Philip Mitchell](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/philip-mitchell). "His depth across the consumer industries and his pragmatic, execution-focused style strengthens our ability to deliver differentiated transaction advice as we continue to grow our practice across Germany and the region."
Commenting on his appointment, Mr. Ritz said: "A&M's entrepreneurial, execution-focused culture and its strong momentum in the transaction advisory market were a natural draw. I look forward to deepening client relationships across Germany and internationally, bringing a pragmatic, data-driven approach to financial due diligence, and helping to accelerate Global TAG's growth in the consumer industries."
## **About Alvarez & Marsal's Global Transaction Advisory Group**
A&M's Global Transaction Advisory Group (Global TAG) provides investors and lenders with the answers needed to get the deal done. We combine our firm's deep operational, industry and functional resources with Big Four-quality financial accounting and tax expertise to assess key deal drivers and focus on the root cause of any critical deal issues. As the largest transaction advisory practice outside the Big Four, our global integrated teams help private equity, sovereign wealth funds, family offices and hedge funds as well as corporate acquirers unlock value across the investment lifecycle.
The firm's Global Transaction Advisory Group includes over 1,300 professionals in 45 offices worldwide. To learn more, visit [AlvarezandMarsal.com](http://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/).
