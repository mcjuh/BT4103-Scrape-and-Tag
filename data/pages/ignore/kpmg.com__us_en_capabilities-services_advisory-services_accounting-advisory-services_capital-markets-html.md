# Capital Markets Readiness | KPMG
Capital Markets Readiness offers services and consulting for every stage of an IPO or SPAC transaction - from discovery to reporting.
What Capital Markets Readiness looks like: strategic intent, end-to-end visibility
**Assessment. Insight. Opportunity.**
Strong equity markets, higher valuations, and easing interest rates are positively impacting market conditions, convincing companies and sponsors to seriously consider exits via IPO as a viable path to raising capital.
In this environment of measured optimism, C-suite executives are taking a deliberate approach and looking beyond a narrow focus on immediate steps ahead. They recognize that the most successful transitions to public ownership require thorough assessment of the entire journey: Strategic rationales. Careful due diligence. And the continued build-out of Finance, IT, Tax, Controls and HR infrastructure to meet ongoing, post launch requirements.
> **"We are emerging from an extended moment of fluidity and uncertainty, and companies are engaging with the capital markets, with a focus on intention, timing and direction."**_Shari Mager, Partner, U.S. National Leader, Capital_ _Markets Readiness_
##  **The long view:** Every stage of an IPO or SPAC transaction is connected. 
When your goal is obtaining maximum transaction value, next-step details matter. So does an end-to-end perspective.
**Click below** to assess your step-by-step readiness. 
  * **When & Why** Pre-deal strategy 
  * **How** Pre-launch due diligence 
  * **What** Steady-state operations 


###  1. When & Why 
#### Pre-deal strategy
Establish the strategic purpose, method and timing of your transaction. Craft the investment case narrative used to communicate to the markets.
#### Identify investors/targets
  * **IPO:** Define investor profile (e.g., retail or institutional).
  * **SPAC:** Identify suitable targets.


Identify investors/targets  Due diligence  Deal negotiations  Secure financing  Tax structuring  Set timing 
###  2. How 
#### Pre-launch due diligence
Conduct regulatory due diligence, including information gathering, preliminary analysis and filing preparation.
#### Assess readiness
Evaluate operational readiness for public company requirements, including timeline to resolve gaps. Develop a detailed timeline. 
Assess readiness  Debt or PIPE financing  SEC filing requirements  Complex accounting  Financial statements  S-1/S-4 filings 
###  3. What 
#### Steady-state operations
Continue to build out operational infrastructure to meet the ongoing reporting and regulatory obligations of a public company. 
#### Post-close SEC reporting 
  * **IPO:** S-1 declared effective.
  * **SPAC:** File the Super 8-K and S-1 to register shares issued at transaction to PIPE or target equity holders.


Post-close SEC reporting  Investor relations  Public company requirements  SOX controls 
## Your strategic path to capital markets
Private companies, new to the IPO market 
For C-suites intent on moving companies past their entrepreneurial origins, articulating a strategic vision that will secure stakeholder buy-in is critical – from regulators, target investor groups, current and future employees, business partners and customers.
Private equity firms, practiced at IPOs, SPACs, 144a Private Placements
For experienced investors, demonstrated excellence at pre-transaction due-diligence steps - supported by advanced AI tools for data analysis and insights - like modeling and valuation are table stakes. They also look for “what good looks like” operational insights -- process, systems, talent -- that will position companies for long-term, post-transaction success.
Public companies, looking to divest
Successful carve-outs or spin-offs rest on strategy that addresses financial, operational and tax priorities for both the parent company and the spin-off. And then granular implementation of processes, systems and skillsets that will allow the new entity to function independently.
### Our structured approach to capital markets transactions
Our approach, combined with our experienced team and AI technology—helps to ensure the strategy you choose is the best for your company, that your record keeping is compliant, that SEC filings are accurate and thorough, and that your deals are completed in a timely and efficient manner. Some of the services we provide:
Initial public offering (IPO) services
  * Initial public offering (IPO) services
We help evaluate whether an IPO is the right strategy to generate capital and expand your company, or if there’s a better approach. If an IPO is right for you, we’ll help you get ready for it by leveraging advanced technologies like GenAI to enhance your “equity story” development, preparing financial statements, and transforming operations and internal controls prior to launch. We can also help you develop the infrastructure to operate as a public company and prepare you for other “being public” activities.
Step 1: Evaluate | We help evaluate whether an IPO is the right strategy to generate capital and expand your company, or if there’s a better approach. If an IPO is right for you, we’ll help you get ready for it by helping you develop your “equity story”, preparing financial statements, and transforming operations and internal controls prior to launch.
Step 2: Execute | We can also help you develop the infrastructure to operate as a public company and prepare you for other “being public” activities. And finally, you go public.
Step 3: Examine | In this post-IPO period, we re-examine the strategy and goals and ensure you are on track to deliver. If not, we also help you pivot to get you back on track.


Private Capital Raises
  * Private Capital Raises
We can help you raise capital in the private capital markets to effect pre-IPO liquidity events for current shareholders such as share repurchases and special cash dividends. We can also arrange capital to support acquisitions ahead of the IPO. With deep relationships across the full spectrum of financing alternatives, we design and execute a competitive process among potential lenders/ investors to construct a tailored capital markets solution that solves your current capital structure objectives while laying the foundation to drive long-term shareholder value.


Debt or equity offerings and 144a private placements
  * Debt or equity offerings and 144a private placements
In addtion to raising capital, our professionals can help you prepare transaction-compliant financial statements and can help you communicate with regulators and investors, including benchmarking of key performance indicators (KPIs) and measuring business performance for annual reporting purposes. Further, we can advise on whether individuals and entities are “qualified” investors under tax and SEC rules.


Spin-offs
  * Spin-offs
Your company may be considering spinning off or selling a division. We can help you determine the optimal strategy from a financial, operations and tax standpoint. We then help you implement the strategy and, if appropriate, create new accounting, operations, and technology/infrastructure systems for your organization and the spin-off company.


Mergers and acquisitions (M&A)
  * Mergers and acquisitions (M&A)
We support companies in all aspects of M&A transactions, including cash flow modeling, legal entity rationalization and accounting for the impact of the U.S. Tax Reform Law, SEC requirements, and international tax and compliance regulations. We also assist in all aspects of pre- and post-closing activities to support financial reporting and operational activities, including valuation, change-management, and organizational culture issues.


Internal audit and enterprise risk
  * Internal audit and enterprise risk
Our professionals bring vast experience in financial and operational internal auditing, IT evaluation, fraud analytics and risk assessment, shared services review, finance management, treasury, and supply chain troubleshooting. We can document and test key processes and controls for SOX compliance purposes and help organizations develop an internal audit process that delivers strategic business assurance, identifies business opportunities and enhances organizational value.


Tax structuring/compliance services
  * Tax structuring/compliance services
We help companies involved in capital markets transactions develop effective tax strategies and tax compliance processes. These actions include complex modeling assistance, capital raising or asset divestitures, corporate restructuring, operations and infrastructure transformation, and human resources management. This enables companies to navigate their way through and around tax, SEC, and other compliance laws and regulations.


Technology and intelligent automation (IA) resources
  * Technology and intelligent automation (IA) resources
Through our industry-leading technology and IA resources, including innovative data and analytics (D&A) tools, extensive benchmarking records, and flexible financial reporting templates, we team with our clients to develop customized strategies that increase efficiency through automation. This allows clients to design and execute capital markets transactions more efficiently and accurately, perform operational and administrative tasks more quickly, reduce mistakes due to human error, and devote more time to their primary business, whether it’s manufacturing, energy, technology innovation, or asset management.


Securitization
  * Securitization
In a fluid and evolving regulatory environment, issuers of and investors in structured debt products need discerning insight and execution support. The challenge may be keeping up with emerging reporting frameworks for ESG. Or use of product-specific issuance platforms such as social bonds or carbon credits. Regardless of the objective, KPMG offers client-specific expertise, tools and support to meet any pre- or post-issuance need.
