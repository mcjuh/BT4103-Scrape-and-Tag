#  Informing the deployment of carbon capture, utilisation, and storage 
This report provides evidence based guidance on the deployment of carbon capture, utilisation, and storage (CCUS) to navigate common concerns and foster informed dialogue about these proven, regulated technologies.

Our report, developed by Arup and the World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD), offers evidence from current deployments, regulatory frameworks and technological advances, which demonstrate the viability and value of CCUS as a materially significant contributor to the decarbonisation of hard to abate sectors.
Key highlights include:
  * **Clear, science-backed responses** to misconceptions about safety, cost, and effectiveness of CCUS. 


  * A **review of CCUS policy mechanisms and the affordability** of CCUS today together with key cost reduction variables to improve competitiveness. 


  * **Practical focus areas to enable commercially self-sustaining projects** in the next decade. 


This is a resource is for industry leaders, investors, policymakers, and NGOs seeking clarity on the role of CCUS in achieving net zero.
