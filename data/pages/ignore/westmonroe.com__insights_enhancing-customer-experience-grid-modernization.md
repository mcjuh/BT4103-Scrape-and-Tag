# Bridging the Gap: Enhancing Customer Experience Through Grid Modernization
Discover how grid modernization is transforming the energy landscape and revolutionizing the way utilities serve their customers
May 14, 2025
Social Share
Beyond the technical aspects of grid modernization, this webinar highlights the real-world benefits for customers. From improving reliability and affordability to empowering consumers with greater control over their energy usage, modern grid technologies are reshaping the utility-customer relationship in exciting ways.
### Key themes: 
  * How emerging technologies are redefining customer experiences and interactions with utilities
  * The latest trends driving grid modernization and their impact on energy accessibility
  * Insights into how state energy policies are fostering customer-centric solutions and accelerating clean energy adoption
  * Opportunities to address affordability while delivering value to customers
