## AI in Construction & Infrastructure
Generative and agentic AI in the construction and infrastructure sectors, most notably in core engineering processes, can drive a material EBITDA uplift by supporting or accelerating:
• Repetitive task automation, which frees up human resources for more strategic and high-value activities while reducing costs• Capability augmentation, which amplifies the impact of scarce engineering talent by extending their expertise across critical themes• Zero-defect deliverables, which upgrades the quality of deliverables through automated data consistency checks and accurate estimates• Schedule acceleration, which enables schedule compression through task acceleration, schedule optimization, and predictive design
