At AESG UK, we specialise in creating bespoke ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) strategies and reporting frameworks that align with UK-specific regulations and your project’s objectives. Our approach emphasises identifying key performance indicators (KPIs) to drive sustainability, fulfil stakeholder expectations, and support long-term value creation.
## The Importance of ESG in the UK
With evolving regulations and the growth of sustainable finance, a robust ESG strategy is essential for both public and private organisations. Addressing environmental, social, and governance challenges not only ensures compliance but also builds investor confidence and meets stakeholder demands. Our ESG strategies align with the expectations of investors, regulatory bodies, local communities, and the wider public, ensuring that your projects contribute positively to environmental protection, social well-being, and sound governance. Transparent ESG reporting further demonstrates accountability and progress toward these commitments.
## UK Regulatory Context
We ensure compliance with key UK frameworks such as:
### UK Corporate Governance Code
Governance and ethical standards for listed companies.
### Modern Slavery Act 2015
Reporting requirements on actions to prevent modern slavery within operations and supply chains.
### Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR)
Mandatory reporting of energy use and carbon emissions.
### Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) Guidance
Climate risk disclosure and management.
### UK Stewardship Code
Promoting sustainable investments and responsible business practices.

## ESG Reporting
Our ESG reporting frameworks help monitor, evaluate, and communicate sustainability performance effectively. We ensure that your reports meet both local and global standards, including:
• Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards • Sustainability Accounting Standards Board (SASB) • Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) Recommendations • Task Force on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) Recommendations • Integrated Reporting (IR) Framework • United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs)
Our reports provide transparent, data-driven insights that showcase your commitment to sustainability and governance, fostering trust with stakeholders and investors.
