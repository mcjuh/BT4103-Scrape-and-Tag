# Measure, report and assure sustainability data
We’ll help you navigate sustainability standards, metrics and ratings frameworks to deliver accurate disclosures.
### How does reporting help me deliver on my sustainability strategy?
At KPMG, we believe businesses should tell their own story instead of letting others telling it for them. We know from talking to CEOs that this not a compliance exercise, but a unique opportunity to unlock value and build trust with key stakeholders. In order to unlock that value, sustainability strategy must be embedded in the business and authentically communicated to all stakeholders.
Robust and reliable reporting that both meets regulatory requirements and supports internal decision making is critical to delivering on your sustainability strategy. Measuring your progress involves an understanding of the different metrics and frameworks to measure the success of your sustainability initiatives. KPMG sustainability delivers insights that help propel you on your sustainability journey to accurate, reliable and rigorously prepared disclosures and reporting.
### The Sustainability Reporting Journey
Assess
Identify current state of reporting on climate and sustainability initiatives
Operationalize
Design and implement sustainability reporting initiatives to improve quality and realize value
Report
Issue consistent, comparable, reliable, and assurance-ready reporting to realize value
