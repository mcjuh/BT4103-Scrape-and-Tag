Case studies 
##  Case studies 
Postal savings bank 
The IBM team provided a secure, cost-effective cloud-based solution for the integration and monitoring of a postal savings bank’s transformed business and service components. The team advocated for the use of AWS to support integration, service operations and security services while meeting government and financial sector requirements and regulations.
Global logistics and distribution company 
The IBM team helped the company—with over 1700 package sorting facilities critical to their logistics infrastructure—create and validate a solution to contain and minimize the impact of potential ransomware attacks. This process helped protect their enterprise-scale server and endpoint infrastructure across 1200+ physical sites.
European financial institution 
In collaboration with the IBM team, the bank received an end-to-end digital transformation including security services deployed across all cloud instances, the overhaul of identity and access processes and increased visibility on endpoints. This process resulted in improved risk posture, increased threat detection capabilities and GDPR and FINMA compliance.
