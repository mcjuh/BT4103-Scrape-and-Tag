**Clients rank KPMG #1 for overall Quality of work in Technology, Media & Telecoms Consulting**
According to analyst group Source’s Perceptions of Consulting in Technology, Media and Telecoms 2025 (TMT) report.
KPMG firms have received top rankings across multiple categories in the Source client perceptions study: Perceptions of Consulting in Technology, Media and Telecoms 2025. According to buyers of TMT consulting services and KPMG clients who participated in the study, KPMG firms ranked 1st in multiple categories, including:
  * 1st for overall quality of work in TMT.
  * 1st for first choice for financial management in TMT.
  * 1st for quality for HR & change management in TMT.


> Being recognized as the top performer for delivering high quality work underscores the dedication of KPMG professionals and our unwavering commitment to empowering technology, media and telecoms businesses to achieve their ambitions with confidence. This recognition from Source is a powerful validation of the trust that clients place in KPMG firms. It reflects our commitment to combining deep sector knowledge, trusted delivery and innovation by supporting clients as they navigate complexity and shape their future. Looking ahead to 2026, we remain focused on building on this momentum and continuing to deliver meaningful value across the organizations we serve.
[ Anna Scally ](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/contacts/s/anna-scally.html)
Global Head of Technology, Media & Telecommunications

Additionally, the report indicates that according to clients, KPMG firms are among the top scorers in TMT for quality of work in AI advice & implementation, improving legacy IT infrastructure, and HR & change management.
## About the study
Source’s Perceptions of Consulting in Technology, Media and Telecoms 2025 reveals what 678 executives, directors, and senior managers in TMT think about the leading consulting services firms and examines how clients see firms differently as they move from awareness, to shortlisting a firm, to their experience as a direct client. Respondents were asked to identify three firms they’re familiar with, giving Source 2,034 responses about different firms. Respondents represent a wide range of countries and business functions, and 83% work in organizations that generate more than $500 million in revenue.
