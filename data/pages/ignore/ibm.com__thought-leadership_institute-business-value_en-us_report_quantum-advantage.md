# Journey to quantum advantage
Around the world, quantum leaders are placing strategic bets and pushing forward. This report spotlights the frontlines of innovation.

Quantum computing stands at a pivotal moment. The science has moved decisively beyond theory: roadmaps detail the path to quantum advantage and organizations are pursuing specific use cases that promise to redefine a wide range of industries.
_Journey to Quantum Advantage_ captures the momentum now steadily building around the world. It collects real-world stories from across sectors—aerospace, finance, life sciences, energy, government, and more—underscoring the fact that quantum’s potential is now clearly wide-ranging.
This report celebrates the innovators and risk takers who seized frontier opportunities before the business case was clear. Spurred by visions of new capabilities, these leaders have grappled with emergent complexities involving algorithms, hardware, or talent. Now they are advancing out front, creating new competitive advantages.

What is quantum advantage?
Quantum advantage is when quantum computers can run a computation more accurately, cheaply, or efficiently than any classical computing method. Momentum is building toward this milestone, which will be reached at different times across different industries depending on the particular quantum use case. 
Why are organizations pursuing quantum advantage?
The reasons leaders pursue quantum advantage vary by industry and organization, depending on the specific strategic vision and need for new capabilities. But each quantum journey begins when leaders recognize quantum's powerful potential. _Journey to Quantum Advantage_ collects stories about innovators and risk takers who seized frontier opportunities and are now advancing out front.
What are examples of quantum use cases?
Examples of quantum use cases included in  _Journey to Quantum Advantage_ vary by industry and include:
Finance and banking: portfolio optimization, risk modeling and analysis, macroeconomic variable forecasting
Chemicals: chemical and material design screening, drug discovery
Life science: Pangenome-guided sequence alignment, phylogeny, data encoding
Energy: Optimization, quantum machine learning, scenario modeling
Electronics: Materials simulation for e-drives, fuel cells, electrolyzers, and advanced sensors, equipment configuration and semiconductor process optimization
How do quantum partnerships and ecosystems support innovation?
When organizations share quantum hardware, talent, and knowledge insights and innovations are more likely to accelerate. Convinced that partnerships power quantum progress, organizations have invested in ecosystems around the world connecting industry, government, universities, hardware providers, and other stakeholders. Many of these ecosystems didn’t exist a few years ago—a measure of growing maturity in the quantum space.
_Journey to Quantum Advantage_ illustrates how quantum readiness grows where ecosystems flourish and resources align. Examples of quantum ecosystems featured in the report include Basque Quantum and the Republic of Singapore.
