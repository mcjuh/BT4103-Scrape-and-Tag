##  Do you need to jump start your move to Oracle Redwood? 
Oracle Redwood is Oracle Cloud HCM's next-generation design system, offering consumer-grade experiences, streamlined workflows, and advanced data visualizations. Deploying Oracle Redwood now provides access to innovative features and a competitive edge in human capital management. Starting your transformation today minimizes adoption risks and helps to ensure an efficient integration.
Oracle Redwood is further enhanced by Oracle Cloud's embedded AI capabilities which provides intelligent, conversational interactions, and Oracle Machine Learning, which enables predictive analytics and informed decision-making. This integration of AI technologies empowers organizations to leverage data-driven insights and automation, enhancing the overall HCM experience.
### Three-Week Accelerator
KPMG offers a repeatable process that aligns your adoption with Oracle’s update schedule, giving you the flexibility to integrate new features at your own pace. 
  * Week 1: Understand the Oracle HCM landscape.
  * Week 2: Configuration analysis and recommendations using the Redwood Customization Helper Tool.
  * Week 3: Report out and determine next steps, including a scorecard and implementation roadmap.


### How KPMG Supports Redwood Adoption
Redwood adoption involves quarterly releases that provide new capabilities and enhanced functionality. Our toolkit accelerates your journey to Redwood and supports you throughout the release lifecycle by:
  * Describing your Oracle Cloud HCM footprint
  * Assessing Redwood’s impact on applications
  * Exploring security and change management implications
  * Recommending and implementing features like 'nudges', Oracle Search, and Visual Builder Express.


KPMG can help you harness the power of AI-driven features within Oracle Redwood, such as predictive analytics and intelligent automation, to maximize your ROI and drive business value.
### Key Deliverables of Our Program:
  * Customized Adoption Scorecard: Identifies challenges and opportunities in Redwood implementation.
  * Implementation Roadmap: Provides transparency on steps and timing.
  * Knowledge Transfer: Empowers your organization to integrate and leverage Redwood effectively.


Begin your Cloud HCM Redwood journey now to stay ahead, capitalize on new features, and ensure your team’s readiness ahead of the 2025 deadline.
