# Capital Projects & Infrastructure
ERM helps organizations shape, permit, construct and operate sustainable and investable capital projects and infrastructure assets. Across the infrastructure lifecycle, we identify and manage risks and streamline processes by aligning with international standards, investor requirements and stakeholder expectations on sustainability.
## Meeting demand for sustainable capital projects and infrastructure 
Meeting energy needs will drive unprecedented demand for sustainable capital projects and infrastructure assets. Considerable investment will be needed, aligned with international and/or sustainable finance standards.
Delivering increasingly complex capital projects means navigating sustainability demands from societal, regulatory and investor stakeholders. In addition, developers need to plan for unforeseen challenges which can give rise to delays and costly disruptions. 
Successful capital project and infrastructure development requires a programmatic mindset to actively shape projects that will meet regulatory, financial and societal expectations and protect and enhance corporate reputation. 
Strategic alignment and effective collaboration between internal teams and external stakeholders is critical to enable the timely identification and management of factors that could derail or jeopardize project delivery. 
## Secure funding, avoid delays and deliver resilient capital projects and infrastructure assets by proactively managing sustainability risks and critical success factors. 
Meet investment and funding demands 
We help project sponsors and investors shape projects to meet financial institution and regulator expectations, leveraging our expertise and experience of international lending requirements, sustainable finance needs, and regional government funding opportunities.
[ Read more ](https://www.erm.com/solutions/sustainable-finance/ "Read more")
Deliver successful and sustainable capital projects 
ERM takes an interconnected and programmatic approach to capital project and infrastructure development and delivery. We work with clients to embed sustainability considerations into decision-making at every stage of the lifecycle, which helps to simplify processes, such as securing permits, accessing sustainable finance and maintaining social license to operate. 
Enhance engagement for social acceptance 
Transparent and collaborative two-way engagement with stakeholders and communities is key to achieve regulatory and social acceptance for a capital project. ERM’s teams of stakeholder engagement experts design engagement programs that help build trust and enhance outcomes.
[ read more ](https://www.erm.com/solutions/social-impact-human-rights/ "read more")
Enable safe, sustainable construction and operations 
From providing advice on obtaining and maintaining permits to simplifying the process for developing operational management systems, ERM assists clients at all project stages. We provide a seamless handover in our support through project design, permitting, construction, operations and closure.
