#  Transport and infrastructure 2025 CEO Outlook 
Insights from transport and infrastructure CEOs on growth, digital transformation and AI.
[Download the reportopens in a new tab](https://kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/xx/pdf/2026/02/infrastructure-and-transport-ceo-outlook.pdf)
The 2025 Infrastructure and Transport CEO Outlook finds leaders confident on growth prospects, with a rebound in strategic mergers and acquisitions, to consolidate and enhance digital and sustainable capabilities. There are high hopes for AI, to drive improvements in project delivery, asset performance and energy efficiency. Supply chain resilience is another big priority, as organisations pivot to new regions in response to geopolitical change.
Whilst optimism is high, execution gaps remain. The winners will be those who turn bold headlines into actionable strategies — especially in AI, talent, project performance and financing.
Download the report to find out more about the key trends shaping the sector in 2026 and beyond.
