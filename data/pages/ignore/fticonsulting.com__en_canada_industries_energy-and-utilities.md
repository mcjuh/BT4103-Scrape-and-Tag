Our Canadian Energy team provides a wide array of advisory services that address the strategic, financial, regulatory and legal needs of clients involved in upstream and downstream production of crude oil, natural gas, refined products, chemicals, coal and electric power, as well as clients involved in emerging technologies and clean energy.
### How We Help 
Our main areas of expertise in the energy industry are in corporate finance and restructuring, and arbitration, litigation and dispute resolution. We help our clients design, navigate and implement creative solutions to complex, distressed situations in the energy industry.
###  [ Corporate Finance  ](https://www.fticonsulting.com/en/canada/industries/energy-and-utilities)
The Energy Turnaround & Restructuring experts at FTI Consulting help clients stabilize finances and operations...
The Energy Turnaround & Restructuring experts at FTI Consulting help clients stabilize finances and operations to reassure all parties that proactive steps are being taken to navigate complex situations and enhance value. Our deep expertise across the energy industry enables us to quickly determine the key issues facing our clients and react immediately to their challenges.
For clients in crisis, our team can develop liquidity forecasts, improve cash flow management, obtain additional financing, negotiate loan covenant waivers and guide complex debt restructuring. We also provide analytical and advisory services to primary lenders and unsecured creditors of distressed borrowers. Our extensive experience and relationships within the Canadian energy industry allow us to understand the perspectives of various stakeholder groups, identify roadblocks to a successful restructuring and design and implement creative restructuring solutions for our clients.
###  [ Arbitration & Litigation  ](https://www.fticonsulting.com/en/canada/industries/energy-and-utilities)

International arbitration and litigation are part of FTI Consulting’s DNA. According to _Who’s Who Legal_ , we have the largest number of experts in this area of any consulting firm.
Our energy specialists are frequently called on to act as industry experts in arbitrations and litigation, providing market analysis or advice on industry practices and standards. In addition, they act as damages assessment experts in commercial and investment disputes. We also assist our clients in the negotiation phases prior to, or concurrent with, litigation.
