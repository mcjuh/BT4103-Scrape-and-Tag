## One team. Every angle. Stronger exits.
Grant Thornton and Stax have united to provide end-to-end insight across the full M&A value chain — helping sellers address the questions that matter most: _Is the company positioned to win? Are the numbers credible? Is the team built for growth?_ Our coordinated approach builds buyer confidence, reduces surprises and accelerates deal execution. As you pursue transformative growth, our responsive, industry-focused teams guide you through every phase of the deal lifecycle with clarity and confidence.
Our integrated model delivers consistency that inspires confidence in every market and every deal. Senior-led, high-touch engagement ensures expert oversight and elevated quality, powered by data-driven insights and technology. Leveraging Grant Thornton Stax’s holistic transaction advisory experience — across financial, operational, tax, IT, commercial and human capital dimensions — we help identify opportunities, mitigate risks and craft buyer-ready narratives that drive stronger exits.

Transaction Management
We help you prepare for Day 1 and beyond. Our team helps keep clients from overpaying and overestimating cost synergies. We identify... Show more the risks, validate the value drivers, and recommend integration and separation considerations during diligence to make sure you’re ready to move toward long-term performance. If your transaction involves a carve-out that requires an integration or a separation, we assist you with the transitional services agreements (TSAs), planning and even the post-close management of the project to help make sure the newly configured organization delivers the value and synergies expected. A further benefit of working with Grant Thornton is our “one firm” approach that puts client experience at its center, allowing us to connect our pre-closing insights and findings to expert teams from our large Consulting practice to augment our client’s teams during post-closing value capture if specific technical, process, HR, or other skills are required. 
Transaction Structuring and Consulting 
We work with you to structure your deal and purchase or sale agreement to drive effective operations while minimizing... Show more tax liability. We understand the complex tax effects of deal structures, and we gather and assess various data inputs and assumptions. We have the experience and technical background to consider inputs including purchase or sale price, enterprise value, tax basis of assets, LLC interests, assumed liabilities, transaction costs and working capital adjustments as we help you create a structure that’s perfectly tailored for your needs whether you’re buying or selling an asset.
