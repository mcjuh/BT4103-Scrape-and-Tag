#  HR Cloud Operate 
## Innovative HCM cloud solutions 
Are you maximizing your HCM cloud investment and running HR operations efficiently? Our HR Cloud Operate services help you deliver, modernize, and evolve your end-to-end HR processes and cloud HCM solution with deep technical know-how, next-gen tools and global talent, enabling you to innovate while delivering a modern HR experience for your people.
## Our HR Cloud Operate services support:
  * Payroll and time
  * Core HR
  * Benefits
  * Compensation
  * Talent management
  * Recruiting
  * Learning


### HR Cloud Operate services
As part of your HR operating model, we provide both HCM cloud application management services and HR process support to ensure your platforms and your HR function runs smoothly.
Cloud HCM application management services 
  * Functional and technical support including reporting and analytics
  * Release management
  * Health checks
  * New module deployment
  * Geographic expansion and M&A
  * Application adoption


HR process services 
  * HR delivery optimization
  * Business process execution
  * Process standardization and automation
  * Inquiry and transactional support
  * Leading practice adoption
  * Data management
