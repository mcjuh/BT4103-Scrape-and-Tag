# KPMG Powered Enterprise Supply Chain
Making supply chains seamless
## With one click, customers set your supply chain in motion. Design yours to deliver with resilience.
Your supply chain is all about connecting the dots—and delivering on your brand’s promise. From marketing to production to fulfillment, you are the engine that keeps the customer happy. And in today’s environment, the challenges you face are daunting.
With the spotlight squarely on supply chains, KPMG Powered Supply Chain gives you the strategy and tools to connect with your whole business—from front to back—and use customer insights to forecast demand so you can mitigate the impact of disruption and manage costs.
You’ll gain access to our leading practices learned from working with many organizations, and our pre-configured tools and processes, which can be customized to your needs to help you rapidly implement the latest supply chain technologies.
