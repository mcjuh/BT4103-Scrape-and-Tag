# Financial and Operational Restructuring
Global leader in local and international cross-border restructurings
Kroll’s Restructuring team provides expert solutions for complex cross-border financial and operational restructurings, combining global expertise with local market knowledge to drive stability and recovery.

We don’t just advise—we implement. As Directors, CROs, and Monitoring Accountants, we lead restructurings with transparency and alignment to stakeholder objectives. ## How We Can Help
  * ### Independent Financial Advisory: 
We act as Independent Financial Advisors IFAs, guiding stakeholders through restructuring strategies that optimize outcomes and preserve value. 
  * ### Financial and Operational Restructuring: 
We drive the implementation of financial and operational restructurings, ensuring distressed businesses regain stability while optimizing long-term performance. 
  * ### Restructuring Solutions: 
We lead both informal and court-based restructurings. 
  * ### Liability Management Advisory: 
We provide integrated liability management solutions, including tender offers, exchange offerings, and consent solicitations to restructure financial obligations efficiently and effectively. 
  * ### Relevant Alternative Assessments:
Our deep insolvency and restructuring experience, combined with Kroll’s leading valuation expertise, supports court-approved restructuring plans globally.
