# IMpact: An investment management podcast series
As financial markets continue to shift, Deloitte’s investment management practice is here to provide insights. Tune in and hear from industry leaders as they share their perspectives on challenges and opportunities taking place in the investment management sector.
## About the podcast 
Welcome to _IMpact_ , a podcast series from Deloitte’s investment management practice, hosted by Reese Blair. Throughout the series, we’ll hold enriching conversations with industry leaders where we’ll explore challenges, opportunities and overall trends that are top of mind for investment management professionals. 
Be sure to tune in and learn more about the road ahead for the investment management sector, ways markets are adapting to new challenges, and unique ways to make more out of industry insights.
