## Creating value
The Deals M&A Operations team at PwC Singapore helps clients navigate and successfully deliver on transactions by creating value through a range of merger and acquisition services across the deal continuum. We help your business identify, quantify, and deliver full deal value, with greater speed, insight, and confidence.

  * [ Post-deal ](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/services/deals/merger-and-acquisitions-operations.html#tab_2-1-root_container_content-free-container_section-339989968-co-1542408988_secondarynavigation-)


Pre-deal
At the pre-deal stage, we identify potential areas of risks and opportunities through due diligence across operations, technology, and people-related aspects of a deal. This can include assessing the readiness and compatibility, identifying potential synergies, cost savings opportunities, taking control and red flags that impact the transaction.
### What we can offer
  * Functional due diligence report across finance and control, human resources, operations and technology.
  * Synergy assessment and business case modelling.
  * Carve out readiness and/ or integration day one strategy.


Post-deal
Post deal we help clients to take steps to preserve value and improve the efficiency, effectiveness, and value of their operations and strategy. Typically, we help with integration/ carve out planning, operating model and organisation design and drive operational improvements.
### What we can offer
  * Day one operating model and value creation plan.
  * Post-merger integration/ separation project management office.
