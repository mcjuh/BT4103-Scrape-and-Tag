#  Regulatory Risk & Compliance – Health Industries

## Overview
Health industries organizations face rising regulatory and compliance demands while operating under increased economic pressure. Compliance requirements continue to grow more complex across providers, payers, and life sciences—and regulatory burden is expanding faster than most compliance programs can evolve. 
PwC helps modernize compliance by simplifying processes, improving data and technology use, and reducing duplicative effort—lowering the cost of compliance while strengthening oversight, quality, and performance.
## Market trends
60%
of payers fall into the “laggard” category, citing fragmented oversight and outdated compliance processes.
97%
of payers view risk management as critical, yet only 63% believe their compliance programs are effective.
76%
of providers cite regulatory complexity and evolving requirements as their top compliance challenge.
## Capabilities
  1. Payer compliance
  2. Provider compliance
  3. Pharma and life sciences compliance
  4. Regulatory & quality
  5. Integrated risk transformation
  6. AI governance


### Payer compliance
Strengthen regulatory alignment across health plans. PwC helps health plans address the full spectrum of CMS, state, and contractual requirements through a holistic, technology-enabled framework designed to support cost-efficient compliance. The result: a connected compliance framework that helps reduce costs while meeting evolving regulatory obligations.
### Provider compliance
Enhance governance and audit readiness for providers. PwC helps providers streamline regulatory monitoring, strengthen governance, and support operational activities that promote accurate billing, quality care delivery, and adherence to evolving federal and state requirements. This enables streamlined oversight across clinical and administrative workflows—improving quality while reducing compliance burden.
### Pharma and life sciences compliance
PwC helps pharmaceutical and life sciences organizations design, implement, and sustain modern compliance programs that keep pace with evolving regulations, increasing enforcement scrutiny, and complex global operations. We combine deep industry knowledge with risk, regulatory, and technology capabilities to help companies move from reactive compliance to proactive, data-driven oversight.
### Regulatory & quality
Navigate evolving pharmaceutical and device regulations. PwC supports life sciences companies in navigating the regulatory requirements underpinning pharmaceutical and medical device research, development, and commercialization—including regulatory labeling, change management, and operating model design. The outcome: clarity on evolving requirements that helps accelerate development and commercialization timelines.
### Integrated risk transformation
Integrate capabilities across legal, compliance, and quality. PwC helps organizations tech-enable and integrate capabilities across legal, compliance, regulatory, quality, and related functions to deliver more cost-effective risk coverage. Cross-functional teams shape integrated compliance ecosystems to help improve visibility, support timely decisions, and streamline oversight across clinical and administrative workflows. This delivers integrated risk intelligence across functions—lowering the overall cost of compliance.
### AI governance
Balance speed and safety in AI adoption. PwC supports organizations in the adoption of AI technology while meeting demanding regulatory requirements and standards. The result: confident AI adoption with clear regulatory alignment and appropriate safeguards in place.
