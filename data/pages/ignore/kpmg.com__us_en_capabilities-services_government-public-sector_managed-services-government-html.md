# Managed Services for Government
Tap the power of dynamic service models to help optimize costs, transform operations, and strengthen community outcomes.
Government agencies at every level are under growing pressure to modernize services, improve productivity, and meet rising stakeholder expectations—all while reducing costs and operating with fewer resources. Traditional service delivery models can’t keep up.
KPMG Managed Services offers a more flexible, cost-efficient way forward. Our scalable, technology-enabled solutions help agencies address workforce limitations, streamline operations, ensure compliance, and reduce legacy costs and long-term capital commitments. It’s an approach that helps governments deliver more responsive and resilient services—and better outcomes for the people and communities they serve.
## Delivering value with modern managed services
Across government, priorities are shifting. Agencies must deliver more—faster, more efficiently, and often with fewer people and less funding. Yet the decades-old model of transactional outsourcing isn’t built for today’s public sector demands.
Modern managed services offer a better path forward. It’s a model that prioritizes strategic value, not just offloading tasks and cutting costs. And according to recent KPMG International research, it’s an approach that is gaining momentum with organizational leaders:
  * 89 percent want providers who can drive strategic outcomes
  * An overwhelming majority see enhanced services as an opportunity to rapidly access new technologies (93 percent) and domain expertise (87 percent)
  * 8 in 10 say scaling these services across the enterprise will deliver long-term value
  * Organizations deploying this approach are seeing operational cost reductions of as much as 45 percent

###  Grants management 
### Grants management
The KPMG Smart Grants Platform is a custom-built digital solution that modernizes and streamlines the full grants lifecycle—from intake to closeout. It combines automation, analytics, and case management with essential support services, such as funding validations, compliance monitoring, and reporting. Agencies get the expertise and tools they need to help distribute funding faster, improve compliance, and enhance transparency. Key features include built-in compliance tools, intuitive dashboards, and configurable workflows for programs like FEMA Public Assistance, HUD Community Development Block Grant Disaster Recovery, and Solar for All.
