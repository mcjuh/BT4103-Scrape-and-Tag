#  PwC at InsureTech Connect 
September 29 - October 1, 2026 | Mandalay Bay, Las Vegas 
Copy link Link copied to clipboard 
## Meet us in Las Vegas!
[InsureTech Connect (ITC Vegas)](https://vegas.insuretechconnect.com/) is the world’s largest gathering of insurance innovators, bringing together more than 9,000 industry participants from across the globe.​
PwC is proud to once again sponsor this year’s event, where we’ll be engaging in discussions on how the latest technological innovations are transforming the insurance sector. We invite you to join the activities we’re hosting and participating in throughout the event.
Please use the form below to let us know if we can expect to see you there and don’t forget to explore our related content, where you’ll find the latest on the key topics shaping the future of insurance.​
## PwC at ITC Vegas 2026
**Visit PwC at booth #2719** September 30 - October 1, 9am-5pm PST | Mandalay Bay ITC Expo Hall​
Stop by booth #2719 to learn about PwC’s capabilities. Meet our insurance specialists and take advantage of the opportunity to explore our latest insights, thought leadership and innovative solutions.
### _Day 1_
**Panel discussion | The Strategic Rise of Claims**
Tuesday, September 29 | 9:05 – 9:25 am PST | Life, Health & Benefits Stage - Mandalay Bay Ballroom D 
Session Description​
​For decades, claims was viewed primarily as a service function measured by efficiency and cost control. Today, leading insurers are redefining it as a source of competitive differentiation. In this opening conversation, we explore how claims performance influences retention, brand trust, operational insight, and long-term profitability. What distinguishes a modern claims organization from a traditional one? How are carriers elevating claims leadership to the strategic table? And how can claims excellence shape enterprise-wide performance? This session sets the tone for the summit by positioning claims transformation as a growth lever, not just an operational upgrade.
**​Moderator:** Alena Kharkavets, Head of U.S. Claims, WTW​ **Panelists:** Chris Kotcamp, Principal, PwC US​ Curt Hess, U.S. Executive President, Vitesse
**Pega | Women in Insurance**
Tuesday, September 29 | 3:00 – 4:00 pm PST | Mandalay Bay Ballroom I & L
Session Description​
AI is transforming the insurance industry, reshaping how organizations serve customers, empower employees, and drive business outcomes. Join senior leaders as they discuss AI-driven transformation, modernizing insurance, and the leadership practices that build resilient, high-performing teams. Gain practical insights on leading through disruption, fostering innovation, and creating cultures that enable people and businesses to thrive.
**Participants:** Elizabeth Sherman, VP of Internal Communications & Engagement, Pega ​Julia Lamm, Principal, Workforce Solutions, PwC US Jill Baker, Director, PwC US Carie Whalen, VP, Solution Consulting, Pega ​Emily LaMay, Co-Head of Individual Markets, Global Atlantic
### _Day 2_
**Panel discussion | Beyond Automation: Building the AI-Native Insurance Operating Model​**
Wednesday, September 30 | 2:40 – 3:15 pm PST | Life, Health & Benefits Stage, ITC Expo Hall
Session Description​
AI and hyperautomation are no longer future concepts; they are reshaping how insurers operate today. This session will explore how life, annuity, group and voluntary benefits carriers are rethinking their operating models to drive efficiency, improve customer and advisor experiences, accelerate decision- making and unlock new growth. We’ll discuss practical use cases, emerging capabilities, and the organizational shifts required to move insurers beyond isolated automation toward a more agile, scalable and intelligent enterprise.
**Moderator:​** Josh Schwartz, Principal, PwC US **Panelists:** TBA
