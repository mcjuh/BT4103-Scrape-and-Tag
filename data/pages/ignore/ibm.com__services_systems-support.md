##  Multivendor support 
IBM multivendor support offers an enhanced and simplified maintenance experience by serving as a single point of contact for a wide range of OEM hardware products, software and networking. These services are designed to streamline operations, improve problem management and accelerate error resolution—creating more resilient IT environments. Centralized support can also lead to significant cost savings and increased staff productivity for businesses.
IBM Support Services for Multivendor Server, Storage, Network and Security, offer predictive insights through IBM Support Insights* and IBM Electronic Service Agent (IBM ESA)*, delivering reliable recommendations across all environments.
Benefit from IBM’s 35+ years of experience in multivendor environments, renowned technical expertise and continuous investments in AI to help improve resiliency and enhance the customer experience.
