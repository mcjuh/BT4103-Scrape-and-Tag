## About the study
Source’s Perceptions of Consulting in Energy & Resources in 2025 report reveals what 333 executives, directors, and senior managers in Energy & Resources think about the leading consulting services firms. The report examines clients’ perceptions of the quality, value, and attributes of different firms as they move from awareness, to shortlisting a firm, to their experience as a direct client. Respondents were asked to identify three firms they’re familiar with, giving Source 999 detailed responses about different firms. Respondents represent a wide range of countries and business functions, and 96% work in organizations that generate more than $500 million in revenue.
