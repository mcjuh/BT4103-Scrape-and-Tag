# Deloitte Named a Leader in Worldwide Industry Cloud Professional Services by IDC MarketScape
[ Print ](javascript:window.print\(\); "Print")
[ Share  ](https://www.deloitte.com/global/en/about/press-room/industry-cloud-professional-services-by-idc-marketscape.html# "Share")
**NEW YORK, NY, 21 January, 2025** — Deloitte was recently positioned in the Leaders category of the IDC MarketScape: Industry Cloud Professional Services 2024 Vendor Assessment (doc #US51036624, December 2024). This report evaluates vendors in the industry cloud professional services market, and it is the second time in which Deloitte has been named a Leader in the report for this market.
The report noted, “Deloitte continues to make strategic investments and acquisitions to deepen its capabilities, including a US$2 billion IndustryAdvantage program for developing talent, capabilities, assets, and partners that can build innovative solutions to solve mission-critical issues affecting clients' core business.” Through [IndustryAdvantage™](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/pages/about-deloitte/solutions/industry-advantage-business-transformation.html), Deloitte is positioning their clients with the advice, solutions, technologies, and tools they require to get ahead of evolving markets and gain a competitive advantage in their respective industries, helping to drive growth and realize their strategic goals.
The report further noted that, “Central to the development of Deloitte's industry solutions is the Converge platform... and related offerings that meet its clients' technology needs. These offerings are built on a secure, industry-compliant, and modular foundation.” The report continued, “With the data platform and data-driven engagement strategy teams, Deloitte's clients have been able to unlock record-breaking digital engagement.” Deloitte also believes that the [Converge™ by Deloitte](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/pages/consulting/solutions/converge.html) industry platforms, which combine its experience and intellectual property with software, AI, and data from its alliance ecosystem, are central to successfully solving a variety of industry challenges in areas as diverse as automotive, banking, healthcare, insurance, life sciences, public sector, retail, sports, travel, and hospitality.
“Deloitte is dedicated to helping clients get ahead of changes in their industries and build the businesses of the future, faster. We are focused on bringing strategy, business, and technology together to enable large-scale transformations in a way that allows our clients to constantly evolve as their industries and technologies change. Industry-specific solutions are helping to do this faster, and our IndustryAdvantage portfolio, enabled via our Converge platforms, is core to our ability to support our clients globally,” **said Brian Campbell, principal and IndustryAdvantage leader, Deloitte Consulting LLP**.
In recent years, Deloitte Consulting LLP has made significant advancements in the software engineering space, launching [Deloitte Engineering](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/pages/consulting/solutions/software-engineering-services.html) to help clients across industries define, shape, build, and scale their solutions for maximum business value.
For more about Deloitte Analyst Relations, please visit Deloitte’s [Analyst Recognitions page](https://www.deloitte.com/global/en/about/recognition/analyst-relations.html).
**About IDC MarketScape:**
IDC MarketScape vendor assessment model is designed to provide an overview of the competitive fitness of technology and service suppliers in a given market. The research utilizes a rigorous scoring methodology based on both qualitative and quantitative criteria that results in a single graphical illustration of each supplier’s position within a given market. IDC MarketScape provides a clear framework in which the product and service offerings, capabilities and strategies, and current and future market success factors of technology suppliers can be meaningfully compared. The framework also provides technology buyers with a 360-degree assessment of the strengths and weaknesses of current and prospective suppliers.
