#  Roadmap to success: Steering Portfolio Toward Sustainable Solutions
February 19, 2025 
Webinar 
[ register here ](https://wbcsd.my.salesforce-sites.com/GuestEventPageV2?aId=a5sVj0000004cZl)
Join WBCSD and ERM for the second webinar in our series, focusing on how product portfolio transformation can guide organizations toward sustainable, just, net-zero, and nature-positive chemicals. This series is ideal for decision-makers across the value chains relying on chemicals.
From healthcare to energy, communication to mobility, chemicals play a crucial role in the global economy. Their contribution to global greenhouse gas emissions (~6.3% in 2019) and environmental degradation are undeniable. The chemical sector stands at a crossroads, confronted with challenging geopolitical shifts and evolving political landscapes and the unique opportunity to enable progress for all while addressing intensifying climate impacts and reversing nature loss. This demands cooperation across value chains to transform how chemicals are produced, used, disposed of, and recycled while innovating value-generation models that will deliver the solutions for a more sustainable and equitable future. 
In this context,**how could organizations create sustainable value while transitioning their current supply, asset base offering toward equitable, net zero, nature positive solutions?**
Building on the recently released [Towards Planet Positive Chemicals: A Chemical Transformation Roadmap Enabled by the Circular Economy](https://www.erm.com/industries/chemical/towards-planet-positive-chemicals-wbcsds-chemical-transformation-roadmap/), **WBCSD and ERM** are hosting a **two-part webinar series** to explore how companies producing and relying on chemicals can leverage opportunities across the value chain to drive this transformative shift.
Join WBCSD and ERM for the second webinarof the series which will take place on **Wednesday 19 February, 4:00-5:00 PM CET**.
[**Register here.**](https://wbcsd.my.salesforce-sites.com/GuestEventPageV2?aId=a5sVj0000004cZl)
We will explore the role of **product portfolio transformation** to help organizations navigate the complexities of sustainable product portfolio steering toward just, net zero, nature positive chemicals.
