## The realities of energy and commodities
Energy and commodities companies are navigating volatile markets, decarbonization goals and complex supply chains while relying on legacy systems that limit visibility, speed and operational resilience.
### Legacy systems limit visibility
Operational, trading and asset systems can be fragmented across the value chain, making it hard to access data and make informed decisions quickly.
### Decisions require intelligence
From production and maintenance to trading and risk, energy organizations struggle to apply analytics and AI consistently across workflows in real time.
### Operations must stay resilient
Downtime, equipment failures and performance issues can disrupt supply, increase risk and impact safety, sustainability and customer trust.
