## What is Employers' / Owners' Independent Verification (CxV)?
Employers' or Owners' Independent Verification (CxV) is a process where an independent third party verifies that the commissioning activities of a building project meet the owner's project requirements (OPR) and design intent. Acting as your representative, we ensure that all systems and components are designed, installed, tested, operated, and maintained according to specified standards.
### Independent Oversight
  * Objective evaluation free from conflicts of interest.
  * Representation of your interests throughout the project lifecycle.


### Verification of Commissioning Activities
  * Review and confirm that all procedures are properly executed.
  * Ensure systems function as intended and meet performance criteria.


### Compliance Assurance
  * Verify adherence to codes, regulations, and certification requirements.
  * Identify discrepancies between design intent and actual performance.
