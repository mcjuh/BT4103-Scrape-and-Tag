#  Sustainability through delivery - Leveraging portfolio, programme and project management to drive stronger outcomes 
Addressing the climate emergency has never been more paramount. This white paper explores the role project, programme, and portfolio management (P3M) professionals need to play in the delivery of sustainability outcomes.

In an era where the urgency of addressing the climate emergency is paramount, the role of project, programme and portfolio management (P3M) in driving sustainability outcomes cannot be overstated.
“Sustainability through delivery - Leveraging portfolio, programme and project management to drive stronger outcomes” explores the transformative potential of P3M methodologies in achieving critical sustainability goals for our clients.
From blockers for why these practices aren’t being fully implemented, through to tangible solutions and real-life case studies that demonstrate the actions that can be taken to overcome them, this white paper highlights the significant impact P3M managers can have in promoting sustainability at every stage of a project’s lifecycle.
Intended to serve as catalyst for change within the project management community, this paper calls for a paradigm shift in how we approach project management, emphasising the need for a collaborative effort between P3M managers and sustainability professionals.
