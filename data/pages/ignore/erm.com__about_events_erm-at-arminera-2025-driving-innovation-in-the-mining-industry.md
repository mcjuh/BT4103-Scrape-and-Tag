#  ERM at ARMINERA 2025
May 20-22, 2025 
Buenos Aires, Argentina 
[ Additional Information ](https://arminera.ar.messefrankfurt.com/buenosaires/es.html#info)
**About the event**
Argentina will host one of the most important events in the mining sector this week: the International Mining Exhibition, **ARMINERA 2025.** For three days, ARMINERA will become the hub for key national and international decision-makers in the mining industry. The event will showcase the latest global technological advancements that are shaping the future of mining. Attendees will have access to talks and panels on innovation across all stages of the mining process, highlighting the individuals and organizations leading the way with new solutions and forward-thinking strategies.
**ERM Participation**
ERM is proud to be part of this significant event. Our Partner and Leader for Capital Projects Delivery, Miguel Sainz, will represent ERM on Thursday, May 22, participating in the “Transparency and Sustainability of the Mining Industry” panel. Block 2 at 12:00: **Toward Sustainable Mining – What Standards Are Being Implemented in Argentina?**_Analyzing from the perspective of civil society, auditors, and companies, the initiatives being implemented to ensure the sustainability of Argentina’s mining industry._ **Room: Ceibo C**.
##  Panelist
##  Miguel Sainz 
Partner in charge of Capital Project Delivery for Argentina and Chile 
Miguel joined ERM as a Partner for the Andean region, based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. He brings more than 20 years of experience in environmental sciences and as a health and safety specialist in management positions locally (Patagonia, Central, and NOA Region) and regionally (Chile, Peru, and Mexico). Solid knowledge in diverse industries and a strategic role in the Mining, Oil & Gas, and Energy sectors. Linked to consulting, private and public sector. He has designed, led, and executed projects related to Environmental & Social, and Health & Safety issues.
