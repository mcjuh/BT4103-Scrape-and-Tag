## Ensuring Safe and Compliant Façade Systems
Fire and safety compliance are critical aspects of façade design, affecting not only the safety of occupants but also the overall resilience of the building. At AESG, our façade engineering team collaborates closely with our Fire Life Safety division to deliver façade solutions that meet stringent fire safety regulations. By integrating fire safety considerations from the early design stages, we help clients achieve façades that not only look impressive but also provide reliable protection against fire risks. Our approach involves a thorough assessment of materials, design features, and installation methods to ensure compliance with fire safety standards. We address key factors such as material combustibility, fire compartmentation, smoke control, and external fire spread, providing façade solutions that minimise risks and meet regulatory requirements across diverse markets.
## What We Offer
[ Material Fire Performance Assessment ](https://aesg.com/uk/fire-safety-compliance/#collapse-7abe8626aa06a3e6da00)
We evaluate the fire properties of façade materials, including combustibility, fire resistance, and reaction to heat. Our team ensures that selected materials meet or exceed local and international fire safety standards.
[ Façade Fire Risk Assessment ](https://aesg.com/uk/fire-safety-compliance/#collapse-203a7c16aa06a3e6da00)
We conduct detailed assessments of façade systems to identify potential fire risks, focusing on factors such as insulation, cladding, and glazing systems. Our assessments help guide the selection of fire-safe materials and design features.
[ Fire Compartmentation and Barriers ](https://aesg.com/uk/fire-safety-compliance/#collapse-daeab306aa06a3e6da00)
We design and specify fire barriers and compartmentation measures within façade systems to prevent the spread of fire between building areas. This includes the strategic use of fire-resistant panels, cavity barriers, and fire-stopping solutions.
[ Collaboration with Fire Life Safety Experts ](https://aesg.com/uk/fire-safety-compliance/#collapse-7abe8626aa06a3e6e716)
Our façade engineers work closely with AESG’s Fire Life Safety division to ensure that façade designs integrate seamlessly with the building’s overall fire protection strategy. This collaboration enables us to address issues such as smoke control, emergency egress, and compliance with local fire codes.
[ Compliance Reviews and Testing Oversight ](https://aesg.com/uk/fire-safety-compliance/#collapse-203a7c16aa06a3e6e716)
We conduct compliance reviews of façade designs and installations, verifying that all fire safety measures align with regulatory requirements. Our team also oversees fire testing for façade systems, such as large-scale fire performance tests, to validate compliance.
## How We Deliver Fire & Safety Compliance
### Design Stage Assessment
We evaluate façade designs from the outset to identify any fire safety concerns and recommend suitable solutions. This includes selecting fire-rated materials and integrating fire barriers where needed.
### Material and System Specification
We specify materials and systems that comply with fire safety standards, considering factors such as combustibility, thermal properties, and durability.
### Compliance Testing and Certification
Our team oversees fire testing and certification for façade systems, ensuring that all components meet regulatory requirements.
### Ongoing Support During Construction
We provide guidance during the construction phase to ensure that fire safety measures are implemented as specified and address any issues that arise.
