###### Banking Performance Navigator


In today’s volatile market, bank CFOs face mounting pressure to raise the bar on performance monitoring. But traditional planning tools and off-the-shelf software often fall short, proving disjointed, inefficient, or difficult to scale enterprise wide.
Banking Performance Navigator replaces fragmented tools with a single, integrated platform—and swaps reactive, bottom-line-driven processes with a strategic, forward-looking approach to balance sheet management. Flexible and tailored to your organization’s specific needs, the tool analyzes key economic and capital ratios and enables simplified calculations of risk-weighted assets and absorbed capital.
Banking Performance Navigator significantly reduces coordination time with other functions—cutting simulation cycles **from several days to just minutes** while lowering operating effort by 20%. Users can quickly assess the impact of strategic decisions, simulating scenarios with a view of:
  * Economic and capital outcomes, with embedded sensitivity analysis
  * Future performance, based on shifting macro conditions
  * Alignment with the bank’s defined risk profile
  * Consistency with evolving regulatory requirements


Bank CFOs are no longer relegated to accounting and compliance; they’re strategic leaders who tap data analytics and AI to help steer the business. Banking Performance Navigator helps them provide CEOs with a sharp, real-time view of where the business is going.
