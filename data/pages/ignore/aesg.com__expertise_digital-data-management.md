Our integrated, multidisciplinary teams combine engineering expertise with operational insight to address the complex demands of data centre environments, from redundancy and uptime to compliance, safety, and future scalability.

## Commissioning
Commissioning is essential to ensuring that building systems are designed, installed, and tested to perform according to the operational requirements of a project. At AESG, we offer a range of commissioning management services, from independent verification to ongoing and retro-commissioning, ensuring that buildings operate at peak efficiency. Our expertise covers sustainability and operational certifications, including LEED, BREEAM, Estidama, Mostadam, and other regional standards, ensuring compliance with international and local sustainability and operational requirements.

### Digital Delivery
The future of the built environment relies on the seamless digital delivery of structured, high-quality data that enhances collaboration, efficiency, and operational performance. AESG’s Digital Delivery services ensure that project data is well-managed, accessible, and interoperable from design to handover and beyond. By leveraging industry best practices, automated workflows, and our in-house information management platform (DATA+), we help clients navigate the complexities of digital handover, COBie management, and BIM implementation—ensuring that data remains an asset throughout the entire lifecycle of a building or infrastructure project.
