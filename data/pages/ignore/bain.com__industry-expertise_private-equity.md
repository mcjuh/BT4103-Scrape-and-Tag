## AI in Private Equity
AI is a transformational technology for alternative investors and their portfolio companies and will increasingly become table stakes to maintain competitiveness. We can help you unlock AI’s full potential across the PE life cycle, including:
• **Sharper diligence**. We help investors assess how AI could shape a target’s competitive advantage while also identifying risks around disruption, data readiness, and execution. This leads to higher conviction in a business’s full potential and smarter investment decisions.
• **Stronger portfolio performance**. We partner with portfolio companies to embed AI across their value chain, driving topline growth through product development and commercial excellence and expanding margins through intelligent automation and operational efficiency.
• **More efficient investor operations**. We help LPs and GPs apply AI across their internal operations and workflows, strengthening and streamlining investment processes, knowledge management, and back-office functions while enabling greater focus on higher-value insight generation and strategic decision making.
[Harnessing generative AI in private equity](https://www.bain.com/insights/harnessing-generative-ai-global-private-equity-report-2024/)
