# From connectivity provider to growth partner
Together with GSMA Intelligence, we explore how telcos can unlock opportunity with SMEs through integration, visible reliability, and managed security.

Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) are an undeniable powerhouse in the global economy, making up [roughly 90%](https://initiatives.weforum.org/sme-resource-hub/home) of all firms. They also represent a major technology market, with SME IT spending projected to reach [USD 1.1 trillion](https://www.marketresearch.com/Global-Industry-Analysts-v1039/SMB-Spending-42601418/) by 2030. Yet research from [GSMA Intelligence](https://www.gsmaintelligence.com/research/operators-in-focus-enterprise-opportunity-survey-dashboard-2025) shows that for many communication service providers (CSPs), this segment remains under-monetized beyond foundational connectivity. SMEs rely on CSPs for essential network services, but their broader digital needs—and the outcome-driven revenue opportunities they represent—are inconsistently addressed.
> "Many SMEs still see CSPs as providers of telephony and bandwidth, despite telcos offering far more, from lower-cost cloud services and cybersecurity to a true one-stop shop.” 
> **Sharat Sinha** Chief Executive Officer, Airtel Business, India
To uncover these opportunities, the IBM Institute for Business Value (IBM IBV) surveyed 800 SMEs and 200 telecom providers across 22 countries and interviewed executives from leading CSPs. Our research reveals an SME customer base willing to spend and eager to partner with their CSPs for advanced digital transformation. What limits CSPs growth is not capability, but a misalignment between what CSPs emphasize and what SMEs value most in driving adoption, willingness to pay, and converting trust into uptake. This misalignment is often reinforced by relying on size alone to define SMEs, masking critical differences in operational exposure and digital dependency among businesses of similar scale.
CSPs have built a strong foundation of trust with SMEs, earning high satisfaction ratings across service dimensions such as installation, repair, uptime and support. This credibility is a powerful springboard into a market craving more sophisticated solutions. Yet CSP portfolios still lean toward a product-centric mindset, prioritizing connectivity and internal efficiency over integration, assurance, and outcome ownership. 
**Top offerings that would encourage SMEs to adopt digital solutions from CSPs**
Revenue expectations reflect this pattern. CSPs anticipate half (50%) of their SME revenue in 2028 will still come from mobile connectivity and nearly one‑fifth (19%) from fixed connectivity—indicating uncertainty about how to expand into higher‑value digital services. This echoes findings from [earlier IBM IBV research](https://www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/en-us/report/telecoms-revenue-opportunities) with GSMA Intelligence, which identified a recurring opportunity to better align with evolving B2B demand: CSPs have historically over‑indexed on connectivity while underestimating SME willingness to pay for integrated, outcome‑driven services. Two years on, SME revenue expectations suggest that opportunity remains.
Now is the moment for CSPs to convert SME satisfaction into growth. By strategically embracing AI and automation, CSPs can move beyond bandwidth to integrated, outcome-driven solutions that directly address SME priorities. In this report, we spotlight three imperatives that define how CSPs can close the trust‑to‑adoption gap with SMEs. 
#### 1. Simplify integration to reduce operational friction.
SMEs trust their CSPs and would pay 34% more for turnkey digital bundles that reduce manual effort, strengthen compliance, and drive business outcomes. But trust alone isn’t translating into adoption. CSPs often overestimate cost sensitivity while underestimating SME concerns about operational risk and disruption. Reducing friction—not adding more products—is critical to converting SME trust into uptake. 
#### 2. To monetize reliability, make it visible.
Reliability only creates value when SMEs can see it. 80% say proactive visibility into performance would increase trust and adoption, and 83% want AI-enabled assurance. By making performance transparent—through dashboards, predictive assurance, and ROI translation—CSPs can transform SLAs from static commitments into differentiated, monetizable outcomes. 
#### 3. Shift toward delivering AI-enabled, managed protection.
82% of SMEs suffered a cyber incident in the past year, and they’re nearly twice as likely to rely on IT and cloud providers for security as CSPs. Yet SMEs would pay 30% more for AI-enabled, managed security bundles with continuous monitoring and demonstrated ROI—reinforcing a broader shift toward outcome ownership across security, service assurance, and integration.
> SMEs say they would pay 34% more per month for turnkey digital bundles that reduce manual effort, strengthen compliance, and drive business outcomes.
SMEs are ready for more—and willing to pay providers who simplify operations, make reliability measurable, and take accountability for outcomes. Download the full report to explore how telcos can unlock the next wave of SME growth.
