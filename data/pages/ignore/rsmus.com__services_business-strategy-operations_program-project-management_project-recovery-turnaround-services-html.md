# Project recovery and turnaround services
### Regain control of stalled projects with proven governance and delivery frameworks
#### [Let’s discuss your project recovery needs. ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/program-project-management/project-recovery-turnaround-services.html#form)
##  Recover value from troubled projects with structured turnaround support 
When critical projects stall or veer off course, the impact can ripple across your organization, affecting budgets, timelines and stakeholder confidence. RSM’s project recovery and turnaround services help you regain control, realign objectives and deliver outcomes that matter. Our structured approach combines governance, risk management and stakeholder engagement to restore momentum and minimize disruption.
### **Review and assess project health**
We begin with a rapid diagnostic of your in-flight or failed project. The process includes reviewing existing project documentation, identifying gaps in governance, scope, schedule and stakeholder alignment and providing actionable recommendations.
### **Realign scope, governance and delivery**
Our team implements proven project management practices to reestablish control. This includes defining scope, sequencing activities, optimizing resource assignments and establishing clear escalation paths and reporting cadences. At RSM, we help you move forward with confidence through updated plans, stakeholder buy-in and transparent reporting. Our focus remains on minimizing risk, proactively managing issues and keeping project outcomes aligned with business goals.
### **Benefits of RSM’s project recovery and turnaround services**
  * Regain control over scope, timeline and budget
  * Rebuild stakeholder confidence
  * Improve transparency through timely reporting
  * Reduce risk and manage issues effectively
  * Deliver business-critical objectives


##  Frequently asked questions  

What is project recovery and turnaround?  
    
Project recovery and turnaround refers to a structured approach to rescuing failing or stalled projects by addressing governance, scope, schedule and stakeholder issues. 

When should I consider a project recovery service?  
    
If your project is over budget, behind schedule or facing stakeholder misalignment, it may benefit from a recovery assessment. 

What types of projects does RSM recover?  
    
We support technology implementations, AI initiatives, business transformations and cross-functional projects across industries 

How long does a typical recovery engagement take?  
    
The duration of a recovery engagement varies based on project complexity. An audit may take a few weeks, while a full turnaround can extend over several months. 

What deliverables can I expect?  
    
You’ll receive updated project plans, governance frameworks, risk logs, stakeholder maps and reporting dashboards. 

Can RSM take over a project midstream?  
    
Yes. RSM can assume project leadership or provide your internal team with targeted advisory and delivery services. 

How does RSM maintain stakeholder alignment?  
    
We define roles, establish communication cadences and facilitate decision making to rebuild trust and clarity among stakeholders. 

What industries does RSM support with recovery services?  
    
RSM serves clients across manufacturing, health care, financial services, technology and other industries.
