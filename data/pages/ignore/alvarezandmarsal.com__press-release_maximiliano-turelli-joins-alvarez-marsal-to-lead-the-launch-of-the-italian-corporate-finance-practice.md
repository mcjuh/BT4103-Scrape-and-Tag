# Maximiliano Turelli joins Alvarez & Marsal to lead the launch of the Italian Corporate Finance practice


**Milan, 26 th June 2025 – **Global professional services firm [Alvarez & Marsal](http://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/) (A&M) announces the opening of its [Corporate Finance](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/global-corporate-finance) office in Italy, led by [**Maximiliano Turelli**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/maximiliano-turelli). The senior appointment represents the latest expansion of A&M’s Corporate Finance practice this year, building on sustained growth in [Ireland](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/alvarez-marsal-opens-first-office-ireland-corporate-finance-appointment), [France](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/mark-wyatt), [Germany](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/press-release/alvarez-marsal-continues-expansion-of-german-corporate-finance-practice-with-hire-of-christian-ramme), [Spain](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/press-release/CorporateFinanceSpain), [Switzerland](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/CorporateFinanceSwitzerland), and throughout the regions of the [UK](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/insights/alvarez-marsal-hires-first-corporate-finance-managing-director-birmingham).
With over 20 years of experience in corporate finance and more than 50 M&A transactions completed, Mr. Turelli has advised private equity funds, entrepreneurs, and corporates across a wide range of sectors. He previously held senior roles at Banca Akros, Equita Group, and K Finance / Clairfield International.
Mr. Turelli is responsible for establishing and growing A&M’s Corporate Finance and M&A advisory offering in Italy. His focus is on serving financial sponsors, family-owned businesses, and mid-market companies, particularly in the industrial sector.
**Commenting on the launch, Mr. Turelli said:** ‘We see strong and increasing demand for high-quality, independent corporate finance advice in Italy, especially in the mid-market segment. Clients are looking for senior-led execution, sector insight, and international reach – and there is a growing need for a truly global adviser that can seamlessly connect Italian businesses and private equity funds with international opportunities. Known for its operational excellence and hands-on approach, that’s exactly what A&M brings to the table.
‘The Italian M&A landscape is evolving, with private equity playing a growing role in supporting business transformations and succession planning, particularly in the industrial and B2B sectors.’
**Expecting to build on strong market momentum, the firm is actively working to expand its Italian Corporate Finance team to meet growing client demand.**
‘We are in the process of hiring additional mid-senior professionals with complementary expertise, who will join the team by this autumn,’ added Turelli. ‘Our goal is to build a best-in-class platform capable of serving clients across a broader range of sectors and transaction types.’
[**Jonathan Boyers**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/jonathan-boyers)**, Managing Director and Head of A &M’s Corporate Finance practice in EMEA, explained: **‘Italy is a key jurisdiction in the European mid-market M&A space. It holds strategic importance in our growth plans as we continue to build our integrated, cross-border offering, underpinned by our network of 135+ Corporate Finance professionals across EMEA. Maximiliano’s expertise in the industrial sector, especially, will help us support a key pillar of the Italian economy, which boasts deep entrepreneurial roots and internationally recognised know-how.’
[**Stefano Matalucci**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/stefanomatalucci)**, Managing Director at A &M’s Private Equity Performance Improvement practice and Head of A&M in Italy, added:** ‘Maximiliano has built a solid track record supporting clients across sectors – we’re delighted to welcome a leader of his calibre to our growing team in Milan. The launch of the Corporate Finance practice will enhance the strength and visibility of the A&M brand in Italy, complement our [range of existing service lines](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/global-locations/italy#gl_services), and stand the firm in good stead as we grow and evolve to support clients for the long term.’
