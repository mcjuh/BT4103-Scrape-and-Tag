## The KPMG roadmap to transformation value
Whether you’re pursuing value in the form of performance improvement, operational agility, technology innovation, or market growth, KPMG helps organizations realize full transformation potential. We emphasize the following four elements of success:
Value at the center
Making value central to transformation efforts at every stage helps organizations minimize risk while maximizing rewards. Our work with clients helps identify value drivers critical to each sector to help clients reach their strategic, financial, and operational goals.
Championing change
Although transformation initiatives can engender excitement, they can also result in fatigue and fear. Ensuring that buy-in and ownership across all levels of your workforce will help ensure that the entire organization promotes and drives sustainable innovation and transformation.
Harmonizing the moving parts
KPMG recommends managing concurrent transformation initiatives by creating a coordinated, cross-functional transformation program in order to execute with speed and capture more value.
Fast forward with technology
From AI to the Cloud to digital enablement, technology and data are critical engines of transformation. 
By focusing on these elements, KPMG helps clients realize the full potential of their transformation initiatives, turning great expectations into real transformation value.
