# Powered Evolution
Extend the performance and value of your cloud enterprise systems with managed services that deliver continuous improvements.
In today’s digital-first environment, simply adopting SaaS is not enough—success depends on how effectively it is managed. A **Forrester Total Economic Impact™** study commissioned by KPMG reveals how **KPMG Powered Evolution** transforms the SaaS management experience, turning operational overhead into a strategic advantage.
The Forrester study evaluated the tangible benefits organizations achieve when utilizing Powered Evolution to manage their SaaS ecosystems. Key findings include:
  * **80% Reduction in End-User Downtime:** By proactively managing updates and stabilizing the environment, Powered Evolution removes the friction that slows your workforce down.
  * **ROI of Over 200%:** The efficiency and cost-savings realized through our managed service model provided one organization with a return on investment that more than tripled their initial outlay.


**Why KPMG Powered Evolution?**
SaaS platforms move fast. Powered Evolution supports organizations to keep up and stay ahead. KPMG provides the continuous innovation, functional and technical support so your technology investment delivers its maximum potential long after the initial implementation.

## **Specialized skills and services that keep your platforms advancing**
Powered Evolution blends platform expertise with automation, analytics, and proven operating disciplines to keep your cloud systems aligned with business needs. The model brings together key capabilities that support ongoing improvement, including:
  * Platform and function-specific specialists
  * Solution and operating-model architects
  * Governance and release management expertise
  * Configuration, integration, and development support
  * Platform administration and technical resources
  * Testing resources and automation tools


These capabilities work together to streamline updates, accelerate adoption, and help organizations get more value from the features already built into their platforms.
