### Monetizing platforms through integration of energy, mobility and digital services
As autonomy, electrification and connectivity converge, vehicles are becoming platforms. Aerospace, defense and mobility companies must monetize ecosystems of partners, data and services to unlock new revenue beyond traditional product sales.
Read More  Read Less 
**HOW EY CAN HELP**
EY helps companies rebalance portfolios and monetize ecosystems — scaling electrification and capturing value from circularity and software innovation.
  * **Balanced portfolios:** creating balanced portfolios to manage the duality of business growth and navigate demand fluctuations 
  * **Software-driven value:** building the digital and data foundations for connected, autonomous and electrified vehicles 
  * **Collaborations that scale innovation:** identifying high-impact partnerships to build new capabilities and scale new mobility solutions 
  * **Circular advantage:** designing reuse and recycling models that reduce costs and emissions while meeting sustainability goals
