# March 2026 Quarterly Trends Report
ERM’s quarterly trends reports deliver clear, up‑to‑date overviews of the top sustainability issues shaping private-sector strategy. In this March 2026 edition, we highlight key Q1 signals across the four trends covered in ERM’s 2026 Annual Trends Report that offer a window into where corporate sustainability is going next.
Our accompanying Quarterly Trends Webinar, 'A Spotlight on Energy', can also be found below.
#### **Trends include:**
##  Trend 1: Sustainability that pays
  * Signal 1: Strong performance amid pressures defines sustainable finance landscape
  * Signal 2: Sustainability reporting undergoes simplification and expansion


##  Trend 2: Energy conundrums
  * Signal 1: U.S. policy changes reshape energy landscape
  * Signal 2: Splintering geopolitical environment spotlights energy security concerns
  * Signal 3: Power infrastructure needs in focus amid demand surges


##  Trend 3: Sustainability in the digital era
  * Signal 1: Question of who pays defines U.S. data center power conversation
  * Signal 2: Emerging oversight needs shape AI’s role in sustainability programs


##  Trend 4: EHS transformation
  * Signal 1: EHS programs need to evolve to deliver strategic impact
  * Signal 2: Environmental regulations expand and contract
