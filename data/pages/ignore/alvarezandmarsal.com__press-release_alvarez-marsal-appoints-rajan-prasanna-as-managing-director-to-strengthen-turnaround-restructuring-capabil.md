# ALVAREZ & MARSAL APPOINTS RAJAN PRASANNA AS MANAGING DIRECTOR TO STRENGTHEN TURNAROUND & RESTRUCTURING CAPABILITIES IN INDIA
**BENGALURU, INDIA, 31 JULY 2026 –** Leading global professional services firm [Alvarez & Marsal (A&M)](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/) India has announced the appointment of Rajan Prasanna as Managing Director within its Turnaround & Restructuring practice. Based in Bengaluru, he will advise clients on operational and financial turnarounds, business model redesign and performance improvement initiatives.
Mr. Prasanna brings more than 20 years of corporate leadership, operating and consulting experience across India, the UAE and the United States. He has advised and led organizations on business turnarounds, cost reduction, customer-centric growth strategies and large-scale performance improvement and value creation initiatives across consumer, retail, industrial, automotive, digital native and real estate sectors. 
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Prasanna held senior leadership positions at the Flipkart Group, including as Senior Vice President, Strategy and Analytics at Myntra, where he led multiple new business and cost transformation programs that contributed to the company's market-leading growth and profitability. He has advised global clients as an Associate Principal at Bain & Company and led Customer Excellence for Emaar Group in the UAE.
[_**Himanshu Bajaj**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/himanshu-bajaj) _**, Managing Director & Head – A&M India and GCC, **_said, _“Today's business environment is being shaped by geopolitical volatility and rapid technological disruption, making restructuring and turnaround capabilities more critical than ever. As demand for operational and financial transformation continues to grow, we remain focused on investing in senior talent that strengthens our ability to solve our clients' most complex challenges. Prasanna’s appointment is a significant addition to our continued efforts to expand our Turnaround & Restructuring capabilities in India.”_
[_**Nikhil Shah**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/nikhil-shah) _**, Managing Director and Financial Advisory & Special Situations Leader**_**,**_**A &M India**_**,** said, _“Prasanna brings a distinctive combination of consulting expertise and hands-on operator capabilities, having led significant change management initiatives in complex situations. He brings a practical, execution-focused perspective that helps clients turnaround and improve performance rapidly.”_
[_**Rajan Prasanna**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/rajan-prasanna) _**Managing Director, Turnaround & Restructuring**, **A &M India,**_ said, _“Businesses today are looking for practical solutions that deliver measurable results as they navigate increasingly complex transformation challenges. A &M's execution-led approach and deep expertise in turnaround, restructuring, and performance improvement provide a strong platform to help clients solve complex business challenges. I look forward to working with my colleagues and our clients to improve performance, build resilient businesses, and create long-term value.”_
