## Leading the Way in Eco-Friendly and High-Performance Façade Design
At AESG, we recognise the unique challenges and opportunities presented by Australia's diverse climate zones in façade design. Our sustainable façade solutions are tailored to minimise environmental impact, optimise energy efficiency, and enhance occupant comfort across the continent, from the tropical north to the temperate south.
## The Australian Context for Sustainable Façades
Recent changes to Australia's National Construction Code have introduced new energy efficiency standards for buildings. These changes, which include a minimum 7-star energy rating for new homes, are likely to impact façade design as part of overall building performance. This transition aligns with the nation's decarbonisation goals and presents opportunities for innovation in building design, including façades, to meet the new energy efficiency requirements.
## Why Sustainable Façades Matter
Sustainable façade design delivers multiple benefits for Australian projects, including:
### Energy Efficiency
By optimising insulation, natural ventilation, and shading, sustainable façades reduce a building’s energy consumption and operational costs, which is essential for meeting Australia's energy codes.
### Reduced Environmental Impact
Using eco-friendly materials and construction techniques lowers the carbon footprint and resource use, aligning with Australian sustainability practices and green building standards.
### Improved Occupant Comfort
Sustainable façades enhance thermal comfort, natural lighting, and indoor air quality, leading to healthier and more pleasant indoor environments for Australia’s varied climatic conditions.
### Compliance with Green Building Standards
Sustainable façades help Australian buildings meet certification requirements, such as Green Star, NABERS, Passivhaus and WELL, promoting market recognition and increasing building value.
## Our Approach to Sustainable Façade Innovation in Australia
### Passive Design Integration
We emphasise passive design strategies that maximise natural light, provide effective shading, and optimise thermal performance, reducing reliance on mechanical heater systems, particularly important in Australia’s cold regions such as New South Wales, Victoria and Tasmania.
### Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)
We conduct life cycle assessments to evaluate the environmental impact of materials and design choices, ensuring that façade solutions are sustainable from production through to disposal or recycling. This process is crucial for reducing embodied carbon, as it allows us to identify and select materials with lower carbon footprints, optimising design choices for a more sustainable future.
### Advanced Material Selection
Our team carefully selects materials with low embodied energy, high durability, and excellent thermal properties. We also prioritise recycled and renewable materials to support sustainable construction practices in Australia.
### Passivhaus Design
Our team has extensive experience in specifying performance standards and designing details for Passivhaus design. We collaborate with certified Passivhaus assessors to deliver weather-tight, highly insulated façade solutions that fully meet Passivhaus design criteria.
### Dynamic and Adaptive Façade Technologies
Incorporating technologies like electrochromic glazing, dynamic shading systems, and double-skin façades allows façades to respond to changing environmental conditions, enhancing energy performance across diverse Australian climates.
## The Process of Delivering Sustainable Façade Innovation
#### Sustainability Assessment
Evaluating project goals, site conditions, and Australian regulatory requirements to develop a sustainable façade strategy.
#### Design Development and Simulation
Using energy modelling and performance simulation tools to optimise façade design for thermal efficiency, natural light, and ventilation, aligning with Australia’s Green Star and NABERS standards.
#### Material Selection and Specification
Choosing materials that offer a balance between sustainability, performance, and aesthetics for Australian projects.
#### Life Cycle Analysis and Performance Evaluation
Conducting a comprehensive assessment of the façade’s long-term environmental impact, from construction to end-of-life.
