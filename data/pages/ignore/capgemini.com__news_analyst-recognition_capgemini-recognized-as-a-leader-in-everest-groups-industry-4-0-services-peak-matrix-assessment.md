## Capgemini is thrilled to announce being named as the highest-designated Leader in Everest Group’s Industry 4.0 Services PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025.
This research is the third edition of Everest Group’s Industry 4.0 Services PEAK Matrix® Assessment, and it covers key tenets of Industry 4.0 engineering services encompassing multiple relevant enterprise use cases.
> “Capgemini provides a strong combination of end-to-end and point solution-based IP solutions for promising use cases such as smart energy management, asset tracking, and predictive maintenance. It effectively covers all deal-origination channels to boost Industry 4.0 services. The client portfolio is diverse, with significant representation in aerospace, defense, CPG, FMCG, and food processing sectors, including WEF Lighthouse clients. Clients value the robust project management support that addresses any deviations from committed outcomes. Moreover, Capgemini is open to engaging in alternative commercial models like risk and reward arrangements.”
> Nishant Udupa, Practice Director, Everest Group
> “Industry 4.0 is not just about technology—it’s about transformation. This recognition reaffirms Capgemini’s commitment to delivering tangible business outcomes through innovation, co-creation, and cutting-edge digital engineering. We are proud to be recognized as a Leader by Everest Group, and we remain dedicated to shaping the future of intelligent industry alongside our clients and partners.”
> _Nicolas Rousseau, EVP, Chief Digital Engineering & Manufacturing Officer at Capgemini Engineering_
