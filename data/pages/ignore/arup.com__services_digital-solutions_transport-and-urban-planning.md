#  Transport and urban planning solutions 
The planning and design of our cities requires forensic understanding of that complex thing: human behaviour. 
To aid the planning process, we have developed a range of software that models how people navigate the urban environment, creating a detailed picture of an area’s transport needs. These tools strengthen public engagement and debate, while providing planners with a clearer picture of the future needs of a community, city or region.
[ City Modelling Lab Better transport and urban planning using agent-based modelling ](https://www.arup.com/services/city-modelling-lab/) [ MassMotion Model and analyse pedestrian and crowd movement through public spaces and buildings ](https://www.arup.com/services/digital-solutions/massmotion/) [ Staterra Gain real-time insights into people’s active travel preferences to drive better planning ](https://www.arup.com/services/digital-solutions/staterra/) [ Charge4Fleets Digital planning tool to enable effective development of public EV charging infrastructure ](https://www.arup.com/services/digital-solutions/charge4fleets/)
