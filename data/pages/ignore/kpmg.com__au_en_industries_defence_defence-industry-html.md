###  What are Defence Industries?
Defence Industries comprise prime contractors and subcontractors who deliver products and services to the areas of government supporting the Defence portfolio.
###  What types of industries are Defence Industries?
Defence Industries are broad and cover many sectors, including (but not limited to):
  * aerospace defence: including companies involved in the production and service of commercial and military aircraft, defence equipment, space vehicles, and related components
  * naval defence: responsible for the design, production, and maintenance of naval vessels, submarines, and associated naval equipment
  * land defence: involved in the production and service of land vehicles, artillery, armoured vehicles, small arms, and related systems
  * cyber defence: focusing on protecting information systems from theft or damage to the hardware, the software, as well as the information on them
  * defence electronics: including companies that design, manufacture, and maintain radar systems, electronic warfare systems, communications systems, and other defence-oriented electronics
