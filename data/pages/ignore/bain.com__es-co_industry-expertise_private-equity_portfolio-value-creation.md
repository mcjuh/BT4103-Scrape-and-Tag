With multiple expansion slowing, private equity firms must help portfolio companies make the strategic and operational improvements that generate sustained revenue and earnings growth.​
We lead the market in supporting an active approach to ownership, and the earlier we are engaged, the better. We develop a robust value-creation plan that covers critical decisions for the portfolio company around ambition, where to play, how to win, and the path to delivery.
