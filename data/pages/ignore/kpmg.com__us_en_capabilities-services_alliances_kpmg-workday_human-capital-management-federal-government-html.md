The new reality of human resources
Human capital transformation, enabled by Workday, is imperative for federal agencies
The intersection of evolving employee demographics and expectations with technology innovations creates a new opportunity for the federal government to increase digital human capital. Digital human capital means that employees can have multiple self-service options and a positive experience interacting with their employer, while increasing automation and reducing labor intense, repetitive tasks. It also means that leaders and staff can be more strategic and productive, using new capabilities to plan for the future of HR, while reducing technical debt and costs.
