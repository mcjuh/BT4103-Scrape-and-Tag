## Reimagining resilience across borders
Industrial manufacturing has long been a cornerstone of global industry, driving advancements from robotics and AI to defense, additive manufacturing, self-healing materials, and the new space economy. However, today, it finds itself under pressure. 
Persistent supply chain vulnerabilities, shifting trade policies and geopolitical disruption are testing established operating models.
Manufacturers must manage increasingly fragile material flows, control costs, and ensure compliance in a complex landscape. At the same time, there is a growing demand for more transparent and circular practices and resiliency expectations across the value chain. Labor shortages are converging with the rise of digital skills and advancing technologies, pushing companies to modernize not just operations, but entire workforce strategies.
