## What legal support do utilities need for power purchase and EPC agreements?
Utilities often need legal support for power purchase agreements, EPC agreements, [government contracts](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/government-contracts), SOWs, [NDAs](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/nda), engagement letters, and technology licensing. Axiom can help in-house teams review, negotiate, and manage utility contracts tied to energy infrastructure, renewable projects, operations, and vendor relationships.
## How can utilities manage real estate rights for wind, solar, and infrastructure projects?
Utilities can manage real estate rights by reviewing leases, title work, easements, registrations, SNDAs, and wind or solar project documentation. Axiom can help legal teams support land rights, project diligence, and documentation workflows for infrastructure development, [renewable energy](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/renewable-energy) assets, and ongoing portfolio management.
## What regulatory compliance support do utilities need?
Utilities may need [legal support for regulatory filings](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/regulatory-compliance), reliability obligations, environmental rules, cybersecurity expectations, compliance monitoring, and vendor documentation. Axiom can help legal departments coordinate regulatory workstreams, support internal policies, and add flexible capacity for recurring or urgent utility compliance needs.
## How can utilities manage M&A, divestiture, and entity reorganization work?
Utilities managing transactions may need support with due diligence, carve-outs, divestitures, legal entity reorganizations, separations, governance updates, capital markets materials, and corporate documentation. Axiom can help in-house legal teams add [M&A and transaction legal support ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/mergers-acquisitions)without overextending internal resources during complex deal cycles.
## What data privacy and technology issues should utilities legal teams review?
Utilities legal teams should review consumer data obligations, vendor data-use terms, technology licensing, cybersecurity obligations, incident response workflows, and privacy requirements tied to customer platforms or operational technology. Axiom can help legal teams by providing [legal support for data privacy](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/data-privacy-cybersecurity), [technology](https://www.axiomlaw.com/industries/technology), and vendor review needs as utilities modernize infrastructure.
## How can utilities strengthen trade compliance and vendor certification processes?
Utilities can strengthen trade compliance by reviewing supplier documentation, certification letters, vendor diligence, import/export classifications, sanctions controls, and internal policy updates. Axiom can help legal teams [support trade compliance](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/trade-compliance-sanctions-export-controls) processes and manage regulatory documentation for international vendors or high-tech utility operations.
