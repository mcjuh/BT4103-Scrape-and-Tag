###### **CHALLENGE**
### Managing capital and portfolios
Rapid geopolitical, technology and demand shifts are reshaping where and how value is created. Capital allocation and portfolio decisions are increasingly critical but more difficult to get right.
Read More  Read Less 
**HOW EY CAN HELP**
By grounding capital and portfolio decisions in cash flow resilience, execution capability and risk adjusted optionality, EY helps companies focus on the full capital lifecycle, helping enable near term cash preservation through long-term position-taking.
  * **Optimizing the asset footprint** : repositioning portfolios based on geopolitical exposure, shipping and logistics dependencies, restart feasibility, and regional demand dynamics 
  * **Aligning portfolios to new sources of value** : prioritizing portfolios, products and processes with high-growth and high-margin markets including through developing specialty capabilities 
  * **Capital solutions that balance growth and discipline** : deploying capital to protect downside risk while preserving upside optionality — investing through volatility, sequencing growth realistically and aligning capital programs with execution constraints 
  * **Innovative M &A and partnerships**: leveraging sector-specific support to identify and execute transactional opportunities that drive efficiency, scale, cost benefits and market presence

### Innovating through AI and technology
AI, machine learning and advanced technologies offer huge potential, but success requires linking digital strategy to operations to deliver real impact.

**HOW EY CAN HELP**
From subsurface to refining, EY helps companies harness the potential of AI and emerging technology to innovate, transform and realize tangible business value across the value chain.
