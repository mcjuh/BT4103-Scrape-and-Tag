#  Space Capabilities 
Working with organizations to navigate the new space economy, and pioneer the next generation of digital, data and infrastructure 


## Catalyzing transformation across all industries
Space may be an unlimited domain of commerce, innovation, and exploration, no longer confined to governments and space agencies; From agriculture and consumer goods to healthcare, life sciences, automotive, manufacturing, finance, and insurance, space and its related technologies provide unparalleled opportunities for innovation and growth. At KPMG, we view space as a catalyst for transformation across all industries.
We believe in empowering organizations to navigate the complexities of the space economy and collaborate with them to achieve their strategic objectives.
Our professionals excel in shaping resilient business models, operations, and supply chains to help withstand space ecosystem challenges. Our specialists can support strategic transactions, strengthen data resilience, and streamline procurement. We also help navigate regulatory complexities, secure space operations, and expand technological reach. We have built deep relationships with leaders in space, enabling us to service clients with the latest innovation across the space ecosystem.
