### Alation
#### Data catalog platform
Alation is a prominent data catalog software platform that assists organizations in consolidating and organizing metadata. It offers users a unified interface to comprehend and review data assets effectively. It acts as a repository for data knowledge, providing users with a comprehensive view of available data sources, descriptions, and usage. The platform offers features like automated data profiling, data lineage visualization, and collaborative data governance, enabling users to gain data insights.
**Core focus areas**
  * Data search
  * Data lineage 
  * Impact analysis 
  * Data governance and compliance 


### Alteryx
#### AI, analytics and automation software
Alteryx offers an AI Platform for Enterprise Analytics that empowers users of all skill levels to gain data-driven insights through an intuitive, AI-assisted experience and automated analytics. Its end-to-end platform supports self-service data preparation, data science, machine learning, and AI-generated insights, all while ensuring enterprise-grade governance and security. Globally trusted, Alteryx is used by approximately 50% of the Global 2000.
PwC, a [Global Elite Partner](https://www.alteryx.com/partners/pwc) and major user of Alteryx, leverages Alteryx for internal efficiencies and client solutions across our global network. We have developed a rich repository of best practices and assets, which we share within our network to maximise Alteryx's potential.
PwC emphasizes that technology alone isn't enough for transformation; people and process are equally important. We focus on empowering teams with the right skills and fostering a data-driven culture, as well as optimizing processes for efficiency. Robust data governance is crucial for accurate, secure, and compliant data. Our approach combines Alteryx expertise with an understanding of people, process, and governance to ensure sustainable and scalable success in data initiatives.
**Core focus areas**
  * Data analytics
  * Data integration 
  * Data migration 
  * Data science
