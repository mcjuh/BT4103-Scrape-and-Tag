What EY teams can do for you
Read more  Read less 
Health and human services agencies are working to deliver more responsive, accountable and effective programs amid rising demand, evolving federal requirements, workforce constraints and aging systems.
Across Medicaid, human services, rural health, public health and behavioral health, EY teams bring together program knowledge, operational experience, technology capabilities and implementation discipline to help agencies improve performance and outcomes.
The work spans program stabilization, digital and platform modernization, program integrity, provider ecosystem improvement, compliance support and large-scale transformation — all focused on helping agencies move from strategy to measurable results.

Safeguarding state child care programs
On January 6, 2026, the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) took action to prevent additional program integrity issues related to programs overseen by HHS’s Administration for Children and Families (ACF) — Child Care and Development Fund, Temporary Assistance for Needy Families, and Social Services Block Grant. ACF notified impacted states that access to these funding streams is now at risk pending further review — putting all states on notice to be prepared for similar scrutiny across all federally funded programs and putting families at risk of losing essential social services. (Source: HHS Freezes Child Care and Family Assistance Grants in Five States for Fraud Concerns, January 6, 2026). 
Effective program integrity requires a multidisciplinary team. The EY team — including former HHS agency executives, OIG and DOJ federal regulators, federal law enforcement, technology professionals and forensic accountants — delivers the insights needed to mitigate risk, strengthen compliance and maintain integrity of public funds. We have helped over 100 state and local agencies navigate complex, rapidly changing programs and maintain integrity and stewardship of funds on behalf of their constituents.
