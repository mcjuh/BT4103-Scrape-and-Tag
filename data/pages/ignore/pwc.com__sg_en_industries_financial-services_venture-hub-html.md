## One-stop shop for entrepreneurs and investors
PwC Venture Hub works closely with entrepreneurs, venture capitalists, incubators and accelerators. We adopt a one-stop-shop approach, providing solutions and services to enable businesses and investors to build resilience, achieve sustainable growth and expand into key markets.
## A trusted advisor in the venture ecosystem
Complex regulations, stiff competition as well as expectations from investors, industry partners and other stakeholders are common challenges in this rapidly developing ecosystem.
Tapping on our global network and deep industry knowledge, we can provide a suite of tailored advisory services for both startups and investors to help spur innovation and expedite growth, while enabling your business to remain competitive and compliant.
## How we can help you
Venture Hub is made up of a team of highly motivated and experienced professionals that provide a range of services from supporting tech businesses in their growth acceleration and scale-up efforts to working closely with both buy-side and sell-side through the entire venture deal journey. Venture Hub supports from the start of the deal process in preparation of the business plan, to venture due diligence or vendor assistance support and more. We strive to go beyond the market expectations in this rapidly developing tech ecosystem.

## Meeting your needs

For investors
### For investors
  * **Venture due diligence:** The crowded tech industry can sometimes be challenging to navigate. We can help you cut through the noise, assessing and validating startup potential so that you can make the right investments with speed and accuracy.
  * **Assurance services:** From licensing service for your new fund set-up to audit service, we can help you with end-to-end assurance services while you focus on your core investment business. 


For tech businesses
### For tech businesses
  * **Strategic planning:** We help tech businesses refine their strategy and gear toward an investor ready mindset. We help investigate the right opportunities, co-develop the appropriate growth trajectory and help your business to access the right markets through our growth acceleration advisory service and Align and Connect Programme.
  * **Venture support:** Whether you consider an acquisition to extend into new markets or as an option to exit, we often come across that tech companies face difficulty from the initial idea and initiation of a deal to closing the deal due to the rapidly changing market landscape. We will accompany you on your journey from the development of your acquisition plans to the execution of the deal though our vendor due diligence and assistance services.
