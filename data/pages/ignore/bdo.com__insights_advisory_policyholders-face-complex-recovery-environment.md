# Policyholders Face Complex Recovery Environment 
Article
  * PRINT 
  * 

As claims surge in volume and intensity, policyholders must adapt to a complex recovery environment. Property markets are raising the bar on valuation accuracy, cyber insurers are pulling back on once-standard coverage, and new regulations are reshaping timelines across every line of insurance. 
In Business Insurance magazine, BDO’s Claims Recovery Leader Daniel Ruehle explains why companies must audit their coverage now, verify valuations, confirm cyber contingencies and stress-test recall limits against tariff inflation.
