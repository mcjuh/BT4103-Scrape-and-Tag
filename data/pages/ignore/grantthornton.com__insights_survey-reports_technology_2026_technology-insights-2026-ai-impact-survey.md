#  Technology industry: 2026 AI Impact Survey
## AI adoption runs ahead of controls
## Speed without control
Most tech firms are already scaling agentic AI across the enterprise, but many are limited by a lack of internal governance. Those that govern AI and prove results are pulling away from those that that do not.
**of tech firms are scaling agentic AI across multiple business units or fully integrating it into workflows, compared to 38% in all industries**

**What’s in the report?**

  * 43% say leaders need a risk, controls and compliance mindset, compared to 37% across all industries
