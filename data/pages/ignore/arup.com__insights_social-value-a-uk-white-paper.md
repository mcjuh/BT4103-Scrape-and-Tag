#  Social value: a UK white paper 
This white paper sets out how we think about social value, and our approach to embedding it into our projects. 
Last updated: August 2023

Social value, social equity, social impact or social responsibility – whatever you call it, we know that today it is more important than ever before. Those of us working in the built environment, including both the private and public sector, have a duty of care to the communities we work in to create places, infrastructure and cities that support and give back to those who are most in need. This is not always easy, and there is not a one-size-fits-all solution.
Our team has developed this UK-focused white paper to set out how we think about social value, and our approach to embedding it into our projects. We believe that taking this ‘theory of change’ based approach is a powerful framework that enables us to focus on real change.
