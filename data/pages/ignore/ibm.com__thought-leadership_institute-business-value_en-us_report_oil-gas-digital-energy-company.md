How leaders use tech to balance today's demands with a low-carbon tomorrow
# 
# Upstream, midstream, and downstream oil and gas operations are considered major factors in global warming.
Together, they contribute [15%](https://iea.blob.core.windows.net/assets/2f65984e-73ee-40ba-a4d5-bb2e2c94cecb/EmissionsfromOilandGasOperationinNetZeroTransitions.pdf) of total energy-related greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. Under pressure from the public and regulators, they need to evolve into digital energy companies by synthesizing sustainability and digitalization. Yet, few have even set a net-zero emissions goal.

## Actions to take
  1. Partner with the finance department to estimate the value hybrid cloud can deliver.
  2. Use blueprinting to visualize how critical parts of the business are performing today and determine how cloud supports business-critical value streams.
  3. Actively engage a business sponsor to turn the cloud vision into a fully funded and staffed business experiment that tests hypotheses.
