# Foodservice Distribution Industry
Foodservice distribution margins are under pressure from rising costs, tight labor, and fragmented operator demand. BCG partners with leading distributors to rewire profit pools, improve service, and build capabilities that drive lasting profitable growth.
For foodservice distributors, profitable growth can come from seeing the business as an interconnected system. Decisions about what to buy and how to source it influence network operations, service, and commercial performance.Our foodservice distribution consulting approach connects these opportunities across the value chain, helping leaders identify where value sits and turn it into measurable results.

## How We Help Clients in the Foodservice Distribution Industry
Every foodservice distribution player runs on three connected activities: what they buy, how it’s moved, and how it’s sold. A decision in one area will show up quickly as a cost, a service issue, or a margin opportunity elsewhere. BCG’s foodservice consulting teams treat these actions as one interconnected system, identifying opportunities across functions to create greater value.Our foodservice consultants start with a rapid, data-driven diagnostic that locates where value sits across buy, move, and sell. BCG and client teams execute together, progressively transferring ownership from BCG's foodservice consulting teams and building the client's capabilities to sustain results.

Expand All 
We help distributors strengthen decisions about what they buy, and how they buy it. Our work supports merchandising teams, and spans category and sourcing strategies focused on COGS, [cost management](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/cost-management), assortment, and private label strategies that drive customer share and expand margins.
Move
We improve how products flow from suppliers through distribution centers and to customers. Our end-to-end [supply chain](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/supply-chain-management) work connects sourcing, inventory management and replenishment, warehouse operations, and outbound. We optimize demand planning, improve visibility in the supply chain, and deliver optimal service levels to end customers. Through warehouse [operational excellence](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/end-to-end-operational-excellence) and workforce management, we increase warehouse throughput, optimize labor scheduling and productivity, and drive automation.
Sell
We strengthen how distributors serve and grow their business from independent customers to chains and national accounts. Our [pricing strategy](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/pricing-revenue-management/overview) work uses customer segmentation and cost-to-serve analytics to support more precise, differentiated pricing decisions and respond to changes in input costs and demand. We also help align sales teams and commercial activation around the customers they serve and the products and services they offer to drive profitable growth.
