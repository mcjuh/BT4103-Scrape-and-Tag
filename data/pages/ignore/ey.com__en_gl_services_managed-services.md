EY Managed Services transforms and runs business functions as strategic capabilities, unlocking enterprise-wide value and enabling clients to realize their vision with confidence.

Business leaders face complex challenges of greater volatility and velocity – but unlocking value is still within reach. Growth-focused leaders are leaning into transformation. Realizing success requires turning business functions into strategic capabilities aligned to strategic business goals. 
### The EY Managed Services Advantage
With EY as your strategic managed services partner, you gain a powerful advantage through:
  * Access to talent empowered with AI, along with sector-infused, deep domain experience across industries, regulations and geographies
  * Modular, connected solutions designed for flexibility and speed, integrated seamlessly to advance your strategic objectives
  * Advanced technology and insight-driven analytics to turn complexity into clarity and drive smarter decisions that create a competitive edge
  * Access to ecosystem partners that help you scale, innovate and realize outcomes faster

  * ###  What are managed services?
In a managed services arrangement, a third party transforms one or more of an organization’s business functions, including finance, tax, risk and compliance. Managed services gives organizations a new way of operating, to better navigate more complex conditions and build a platform for long-term value creation. Leading managed services providers bring deep domain experience and specialist talent across critical functions, enabled by advanced technology and proven processes, to collaborate with internal teams and integrate with existing technology to elevate performance. By delivering better outcomes for business functions, managed services can give organizations the capabilities, flexibility and freedom to achieve enterprise-wide, enduring value.
