##  Our approach 
As the world continues to face rapid and profound change, maintaining a precise understanding of market dynamics and the impact of economic volatility on the value of your business or assets is crucial. Today more than ever, the experience, and insights of dedicated valuation and sector specialists can help keep you on the path to opportunity and success.
Are you considering an acquisition or disposal, creating a joint venture, or involved in a commercial dispute or litigation? Are you determining value for financial reporting or tax purposes, or raising funds to expand core capabilities? Whatever the challenge, KPMG valuation specialists are here to help. KPMG member firms’ global network of more than 2,700 valuation professionals working in more than 70 countries are providing robust and informed guidance on valuation matters across multiple sectors, deal stages and client types. Using the latest data analytics, simulations and visualization tools, KPMG professionals have the capabilities to address complex modeling and valuation issues.
## What we do
We can help companies manage their transfer pricing issues through such services as:
  * ###### Independent board advice and fairness opinions
KPMG firms advise senior management and boards of public and private companies on how they can raise capital, prioritize market opportunities, or consider mergers, acquisitions, joint ventures and divestitures.
  * ###### Accounting, tax, and regulatory valuations
We assist in determining the fair (market) value of equity shares, assets or complex financial instruments under accounting, tax or other regulatory standards such as IPEV, IVS or OECD.
  * ###### Investment and M&A advice
We provide timely and impartial advice, often in close cooperation with other deal advisory service lines, based on advanced data analytics, benchmarking, modelling and state-of-the art valuation concepts for deal pricing, capital-allocation strategies, synergy assessment strategic-option assessment, and value creation opportunities. 
  * ###### Dispute, litigation, and expert valuations
KPMG member firms have extensive experience in providing expert witness evidence across a range of dispute-resolution processes, either as a party-appointed, jointly engaged or court/tribunal-appointed independent expert witness. We provide damage quantifications on contract breaches, IP infringements or shareholder/joint-venture partner disputes.
  * ###### Venture capital, early stage, and LBO valuations
We offer independent analysis and valuation of equity shares and IP when you are raising equity or debt financing, including modelling robust financial business plans based on insights gained thorough commercial due diligence. 
  * ###### Restructuring
We help lenders, companies and administrators in the context of restructurings by providing valuation advice and strategic disposal options in connection with independent business reviews and independent valuation opinions.
