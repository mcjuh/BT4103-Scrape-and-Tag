### Introducing an innovation laboratory that offers an immersive approach to help organizations leverage AI
Agentforce is the agentic layer of the Salesforce platform for deploying autonomous AI agents equipped with tools, tailoring, and a suite of out-of-the-box functions. The KPMG AI incubator takes it a step further by offering **a launch pad environment for ideation and validation**. Test it at your chosen location, a Salesforce World Tour event, or at a KPMG main office—and bring AI strategies from concept through prototype and into full production.
Explore how AI can transform business by turning theoretical concepts into reality with speed and precision, with KPMG and Salesforce specialists as your guides. Then experience for yourself why KPMG is the top-ranked provider for quality AI advice and implementation.
