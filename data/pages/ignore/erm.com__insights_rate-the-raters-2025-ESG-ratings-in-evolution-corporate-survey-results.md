# Rate the Raters 2025: ESG ratings in evolution - Corporate survey results
Since the ERM Sustainability Institute’s last [_Rate the Raters_ report in 2023](https://www.erm.com/insights/rate-the-raters-2023/), ESG rating agencies have faced mounting pressure on several fronts – from new regulatory expectations to scrutiny over transparency, consistency, and market relevance.
[ Watch webinar ](https://www.erm.com/insights/rate-the-raters-2025-ESG-ratings-in-evolution-corporate-survey-results/#webinar "Watch webinar")
Against this backdrop, the ERM Sustainability Institute conducted the latest  _Rate the Raters_ survey of global companies. Key findings based on insights from several hundred respondents include: 
  * **Shift in drivers** : Investor demand remains the strongest driver of corporate engagement with ESG rating agencies, though customer expectations are rapidly growing in importance. 


  * **Quality and usefulness leaders** : EcoVadis, CDP, and S&P Global ESG top the rankings as the highest quality and most useful. 


  * **Increasing selectivity:** The number of ESG ratings that companies typically engage has dropped significantly, suggesting that companies are taking a more selective and strategic approach. 


This research is part of a multi‑year initiative designed to understand how companies and investors perceive and engage with ESG rating providers. The Investor edition will be published in 2026. Together, the Corporate and Investor editions will provide a comprehensive view of how ESG ratings are perceived and used across the market.
