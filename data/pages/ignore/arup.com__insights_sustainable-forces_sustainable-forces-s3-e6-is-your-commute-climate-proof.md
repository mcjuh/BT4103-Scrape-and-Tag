#  Sustainable Forces S3 E6: Is your commute climate proof? 
From sea level rise and flooding to wildfires and cyclones – climate change is impacting our transport systems now. So, how do we prepare them for an uncertain future? Listen now to find out.

[ Issue  How do we make cities resilient to climate change?  ](https://www.arup.com/insights/issues/how-do-we-make-cities-resilient-to-climate-change/)
From rail networks to everyday footpaths, transport keeps our cities moving and our communities connected. But as extreme weather events and natural disasters become more frequent, how prepared is our transport infrastructure to withstand them?
In this episode, Dr Michelle Dickinson is joined by Peter Reidy, Chief Executive Officer at KiwiRail and Mayurie Gunatilaka, our New Zealand Country Leader, to discuss how we can build transport systems that stand up to a changing climate.
Together, we will unpack resilience frameworks, investment approaches, real-world examples and the concept of ‘now proofing’ offering insights into how we can prepare the networks we rely on every day.
