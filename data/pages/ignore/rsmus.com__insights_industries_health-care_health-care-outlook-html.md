# Health care industry outlook
June 01, 2026
##  Key takeaways 
In health care, rising capital costs make complexity a decisive factor in investment decisions.
Winning providers govern complexity with value roadmaps aligning mission, capital and care.
Health care providers should use supply chain intelligence to map and anticipate disruptions.
## What is the health care industry outlook?
RSM’s health care industry outlook provides insights into the major trends shaping the U.S. health care sector throughout the year. In our latest edition, we explore how rising capital constraints and escalating operational complexity are reshaping decision making for middle market health systems, eroding the traditional divide between clinical priorities and financial performance. We examine how leaders can manage this inflection point—using enterprise value roadmaps, disciplined capital allocation and integrated operating models—to navigate structural workforce shortages, digital transformation, site-of-care shifts and the transition to value‑based care.
##  TREND: Rising business complexity 
For health care organizations, operational complexity is no longer an incidental condition to navigate around. It is becoming the defining variable for shaping where leaders invest, what they standardize and, increasingly, what they divest.

##  TREND: Supply chain resiliency 
As supply chains grow more complex for health care organizations, with deep relationship tiers involving contract manufacturers, distributors and specialized logistical vendors, the probability of disruption rises, too. Historically, this complexity leads to more frequent and longer‑tail interruptions, not fewer, especially as policy and geopolitical dynamics shift.
