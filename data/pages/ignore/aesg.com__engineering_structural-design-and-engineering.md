# Structural Design & Engineering

## Safe, Efficient Structural Design from Concept to Delivery
At AESG, our Structural Design & Engineering team delivers innovative, robust and practical solutions tailored to each project’s unique requirements. From early concept through detailed design, we combine advanced analysis, technical expertise, and constructability insight to create structures that are safe, efficient, resilient, and sustainable.
## Our Services
## Concept, Schematic & Detailed Structural Design
Delivering innovative and practical structural solutions through concept, schematic, and detailed design to ensure safety, efficiency, and constructability.
### Ensures safety, compliance, and long-term performance
### Optimises materials, efficiency, and cost
### Supports constructability and coordinated documentation
### Reduces risk through early identification and independent review
## Structural Analysis & Calculations
Providing precise analysis and calculations for all structural systems to ensure safety, efficiency, and compliance with design codes.
### Ensures structural safety and regulatory compliance
### Optimises material use and design efficiency
### Identifies potential risks and performance issues early
### Supports accurate, constructible, and cost-effective designs
## High-Rise, Complex & Specialist Structures
Designing high-rise and architecturally complex structures with advanced engineering solutions that meet performance, safety, and constructability requirements.
### Enables innovative and safe structural solutions for challenging designs
### Ensures compliance with international codes and standards
### Supports efficient construction and risk management
### Enhances long-term durability and performance
## Substructure Engineering
Delivering robust substructure solutions tailored to site conditions and structural requirements for safe and durable buildings.
### Ensures stability and load-bearing performance
### Optimises foundation design for cost and material efficiency
### Mitigates geotechnical and construction risks Supports long-term asset durability and resilience
## Structural Drawings, Specifications & Design Reports
Producing coordinated drawings, detailed specifications, and comprehensive design reports to guide construction and ensure project success.
### Provides clear and accurate design documentation
### Supports efficient construction and coordination
### Reduces errors, rework, and delays
### Enhances regulatory compliance and client confidence
## Independent Peer Review & Design Verification
Offering expert independent review and verification of designs to ensure quality, compliance, and risk mitigation across all structural systems.
### Confirms design accuracy and code compliance
### Identifies potential risks and improvement opportunities
### Enhances confidence for clients, contractors, and regulators
### Supports high-quality, constructible, and cost-effective outcomes
