## What legal support do diversified financial services companies need for AML, KYC, and BSA compliance?
Diversified financial services companies often need [legal support for AML/KYC](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/kyc-aml) controls, BSA obligations, customer due diligence, transaction monitoring, policy updates, and regulatory response. Axiom can help in-house legal and compliance teams support risk-based programs, documentation, and higher-volume review needs across business lines.
## How can financial services teams prepare for regulatory exams or enforcement response?
Financial services teams can prepare for exams or enforcement response by organizing policies, controls, training records, disclosures, remediation plans, and regulator-facing documentation. Axiom can help legal teams[support regulatory response](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/regulatory-compliance), coordinate internal stakeholders, and provide flexible coverage for document review, issue tracking, and [remediation work](https://www.axiomlaw.com/solutions/large-projects/regulatory-compliance-review).
## What legal support is needed for funds and investment management work?
Funds and investment management teams may need support with registered funds, mutual funds, private funds, [fund formation](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/fund-formation), structuring, governance, disclosures, and fund management operations. Axiom can help in-house legal departments access [flexible legal support for fund-related issues](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/funds), workstreams, documentation, and compliance-driven review needs.
## How can legal teams support capital markets and lending workstreams?
Legal teams can [support capital markets](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/capital-markets) and lending work by reviewing debt and equity capital markets documentation, bank lending arrangements, private credit terms, borrower finance materials, covenants, diligence requests, and closing deliverables. Axiom can provide flexible legal talent for transaction support, peak-volume work, and ongoing financing needs.
## What legal support helps financial services teams manage workouts, restructurings, and special assets?
Financial services teams managing workouts, restructurings, or special assets may need support reviewing credit documents, assessing borrower obligations, coordinating remediation steps, and managing high-volume documentation. Axiom can help legal departments add flexible capacity for restructuring-related work while internal teams focus on strategy and business continuity.
## How can global financial services teams maintain compliance coverage across regions?
Global financial services teams can [maintain compliance coverage](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/regulatory-compliance) by mapping jurisdictional obligations, assigning local review work, coordinating escalation paths, and documenting regulatory updates. Axiom can help legal departments add flexible legal coverage across regions, time zones, and compliance workflows when internal capacity is limited.
