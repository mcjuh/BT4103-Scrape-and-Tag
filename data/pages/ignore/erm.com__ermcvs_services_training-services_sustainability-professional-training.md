Our Sustainability Professional Training Courses
## We provide a comprehensive learning pathway through five specialized programs designed to build your sustainability and climate expertise
### ESG Internal Auditor (2 Days) 
Develop the skills to evaluate and validate your organization’s ESG performance systems. This course teaches audit professionals to assess data integrity, identify material risks, and strengthen controls. Learn systematic approaches to reviewing sustainability claims, reporting processes, and providing assurance that enhances credibility, compliance, and ESG risk management.
[ See upcoming classes ](https://www.ermlearning.com/shop/Course.aspx?id=5035&name=ESG+Internal+Auditor)
### Carbon Accounting Fundamentals (2 Days) 
Gain practical skills to measure, manage, and report greenhouse gas emissions. This course introduces carbon accounting training using the Greenhouse Gas Protocol. Learn to calculate Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions, explore Scope 3 basics, build emission inventories, apply accurate emission factors, and prepare GHG reporting for compliance and sustainability goals.
[ See upcoming classes ](https://www.ermlearning.com/shop/Course.aspx?id=5055&name=Carbon+Accounting+Fundamentals)
### ISO 14064 – Greenhouse Gas Lead Verifier (4 Days) 
Earn globally recognized credentials to lead greenhouse gas verification audits. This intensive program covers ISO 14064 and ISO 14065 standards, advanced verification methodologies, materiality assessment, evidence gathering, and opinion formulation. Build technical skills to evaluate complex emissions, strengthen compliance, and position yourself for career growth in the expanding climate assurance sector.
[ See upcoming classes ](https://www.ermlearning.com/shop/Course.aspx?id=5034&name=ISO+14064+-+Greenhouse+Gas+Lead+Verifier)
### Life Cycle Assessment Fundamentals (2 Days) 
Learn Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) training to evaluate environmental impacts across the full product lifecycle, from raw materials to end-of-life. This course covers LCA principles, methodologies, and practical applications. Identify environmental hotspots, compare product alternatives, and support sustainable design decisions for circular economy initiatives and environmental product declarations.
[ See upcoming classes ](https://www.ermlearning.com/shop/Course.aspx?id=5036&name=Life+Cycle+Assessment+Fundamentals)
### Scope 3 Deep Dive (2 Days) 
Master advanced Scope 3 emissions training to tackle the largest share of corporate carbon footprints. Learn methodologies for quantifying all 15 categories, engage suppliers for data collection, apply credible estimation techniques, and align reporting with the GHG Protocol and Science Based Targets (SBTi) for robust climate compliance and performance.
[ See upcoming classes ](https://www.ermlearning.com/shop/Course.aspx?id=5053&name=Scope+3+Deep+Dive)
### Product Carbon Footprint (1 Day)
Learn how to measure, understand, and reduce product-level greenhouse gas emissions. This course introduces Product Carbon Footprint (PCF) concepts and methodologies, helping professionals calculate emissions, identify hotspots, and use carbon footprint data to support climate and sustainability goals.
[ See upcoming classes ](https://www.ermlearning.com/shop/Course.aspx?id=5089&name=Product+Carbon+Footprint)
## The Sustainability Imperative: Why Training Matters Now 
Sustainability is no longer optional, it’s a business-critical priority. Global regulations and stakeholder expectations are accelerating, with mandatory environmental reporting, social responsibility frameworks, and ESG disclosure requirements reshaping corporate obligations. Companies face growing pressure from investors demanding transparent sustainability data, supply chain partners enforcing environmental and social commitments, customers prioritizing sustainable products and practices, and regulators mandating compliance.
This shift creates an urgent need for professionals who can assess sustainability performance, implement credible programs, and navigate complex frameworks. Without internal expertise, businesses risk compliance gaps, penalties, and reputational damage. Investing in sustainability training courses equips teams with validated skills in ESG compliance, corporate sustainability strategies, and environmental stewardship, critical capabilities as sustainability moves from voluntary initiative to core business imperative.
Each program integrates theoretical frameworks with practical application through realistic scenarios, interactive group exercises, and industry case examples, ensuring participants can immediately apply learning to advance their organization's sustainability journey.
