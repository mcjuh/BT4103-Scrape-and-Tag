# PricePrism™: Advanced Price Transparency Intelligence Platform 
Tools, analysis, and data intelligence to unlock competitive advantages and identify growth opportunities 
Leverage comprehensive pricing data to create opportunities, enhance positioning, drive growth, boost advocacy and advance research. 
Healthcare leaders today face the dual challenge of ensuring financial growth while navigating a landscape filled with massive, disorganized pricing data. Whether your focus is securing competitive reimbursement rates, optimizing value-based care payment opportunities, or expanding service lines, the stakes are high and your margin for error is slim. You need more than just organized data to make decisions that lead to results.
**Data analysis acumen _and_ health policy expertise: **Our teams have decades of collective experience in healthcare markets, federal and state policy, and payer-provider ecosystems to deliver transformative insights that can truly foster smart decision-making.
**Access to a deep well of data:** Get access to critical reimbursement rates from all national and many regional health plans, provider-specific Medicare rates, and, if desired, 100% access to Medicare and Medicaid claims.
**Powerful tools and expert guidance:** Custom professional services engagements as well as access to our price transparency database and a customizable dashboard offer you the tools necessary to answer your organization’s most pressing questions

## A Robust Database, Customized Dashboard, and Unrivaled Expertise 
To deliver actionable insights, you need access to the right data, customizable visualizations, and expert analysis. With Mathematica’s price transparency tools you get: 
**Trusted, Organized Data** : With more than five decades of experience, Mathematica transforms massive, disorganized data into structured, reliable insights you can trust.
**Comprehensive Access:** Gain clarity from a vast array of data sources, including national and regional health plans, provider-specific Medicare rates, and Medicare and Medicaid claims data. Upload new plan files for on-demand ingestion.
**Tools to Analyze the Value of Care** : Analyze the value of care using reimbursement rates integrated with data on service volume, utilization, and provider performance.
**Actionable Insights:** With our custom dashboards, get a real-time understanding of pricing visualizations and quality of care in your market.
**Expertise Across Markets and Policies** : Rely on our deep understanding of healthcare markets and policy landscapes to navigate negotiations with confidence, backed by evidence and insights tailored to your organization’s goals.

## Frequently Asked Questions
###  Q: How can I be sure your data is reliable?
A: Mathematica has been a trusted partner in data analytics for more than 50 years, serving healthcare organizations and government agencies. Our comprehensive price transparency database is built on reliable sources, including national and regional health plans and Medicare data. 
### Q: Will I be able to draw conclusions that address my organization’s goals?
A: Absolutely. Our team of data visualization experts will work with you to create custom visualizations that provide clear, actionable insights, ensuring you can make informed decisions that directly support your organization's objectives. 
### Q: What kind of ROI can I expect?
A: Our services are designed to maximize your negotiating power, leading to more competitive rates and improved financial outcomes. We work closely with you to ensure that the strategies we develop deliver measurable value.
