# Mergers & acquisitions tax and valuations 
In a constantly evolving regulatory environment, companies engaged in mergers, acquisitions, and other transactions need to fully assess the tax implications of any deal.
##  Tax and valuation services for any transaction 
KPMG offers a full range of tax services designed to uncover value and help mitigate risk from mergers and acquisitions (M&A). Our M&A tax professionals can help guide you through the entire lifecycle of a deal, from establishing the value of businesses or assets to long-term tax planning and analysis. From smaller domestic deals to major international transactions, we offer the experience, scale, and in-depth tax knowledge to drive tax efficiency and unlock value throughout the M&A process.
