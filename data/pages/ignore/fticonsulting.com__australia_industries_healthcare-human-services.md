In FTI Consulting’s Health practice, our purpose is simple: to deliver meaningful, measurable and sustainable improvements in healthcare and human services. We combine a boutique approach with the strength of a global network, partnering with health sector stakeholders to drive meaningful improvements across the full spectrum of healthcare disciplines.
Together, we can shape a stronger, more resilient health system for generations to come. 
Australia’s healthcare and human services systems are amongst the world’s finest, delivering exceptional care and support to millions of individuals every day. Yet, these systems are at a crossroads, shaped by the collision of groundbreaking innovation and technology, growing community needs and escalating financial pressures. From workforce shortages to rising costs, from system reforms to market disruptions, today’s environment requires bold solutions and a renewed focus on sustainable outcomes. 
Governments, providers, funders and investors all face unique challenges and opportunities. FTI Consulting’s Healthcare and Human Services consulting professionals provide expert guidance to navigate these complexities, ensuring successful change is achieved and optimising value recovery, protection and growth.
