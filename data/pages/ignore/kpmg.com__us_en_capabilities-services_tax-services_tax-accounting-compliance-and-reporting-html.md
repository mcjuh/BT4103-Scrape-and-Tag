# Tax accounting, compliance, and reporting
Changing regulations, accelerated reporting deadlines, and new accounting standards pose fresh challenges for tax departments. KPMG can help you stay compliant while improving efficiency and helping to mitigate risk.
##  Meet today’s tax requirements and plan for tomorrow 
Achieving domestic and international tax compliance accurately, efficiently, and quickly is essential for businesses in every industry. Our tax professionals can help you adapt to complex compliance and reporting requirements today, and plan for emerging legislation and regulatory changes in the future. We’ll also show you how technology and automation can make accounting, reporting, and compliance easier to manage.
