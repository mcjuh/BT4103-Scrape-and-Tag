**Axiom can help you create, review, and update your compliance infrastructure, ensuring smooth integration of the latest regulations.**

## How Axiom Can Help with Compliance
  * ### Strategy and planning
Review your current compliance infrastructure, advise on how new regulations may impact your current compliance program, create a project plan to address any gaps or updates required.
  * ### Regulatory mapping
Align regulations against product set and understand which regulations apply to which product set(s) and how to ensure compliance.
  * ### Reviewing laws and regulations for applicability
Determine which laws and regulations apply to which product sets and where certain jurisdictions’ regulations may take priority over others.
  * ### Drafting policies and procedures
Create and/or update policies and procedures that are impacted by new regulations or tightened regulatory requirements.
  * ### Ongoing compliance monitoring
Monitor for and address potential breaches of compliance procedures and work with internal teams to address them.
