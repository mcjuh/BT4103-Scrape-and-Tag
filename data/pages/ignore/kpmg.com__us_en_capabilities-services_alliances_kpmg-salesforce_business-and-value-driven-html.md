# Financial Services | KPMG and Salesforce
Transform customer experiences and drive growth with GenAI and Agentforce
Long-term leadership in the age of AI Salesforce & Informatica
As the financial services landscape continues to rapidly evolve, institutions must balance personalized client experiences with complex regulatory demands in an increasingly competitive market. With rising customer expectations, banks, insurers, and asset managers need to connect front, middle, and back offices to create seamless interactions. The introduction of generative AI and autonomous agents like Salesforce’s Agentforce offers a powerful solution, enabling financial firms to analyze data at scale, anticipate client needs, and respond to thousands of inquiries simultaneously.
Today’s financial clients expect secure, personalized, and integrated services across all touchpoints. By implementing AI-powered solutions that enhance efficiency without sacrificing the human relationships essential to financial services, institutions can streamline operations, improve decision-making, and deliver exceptional customer experiences. The result is a more agile organization that can adapt to market shifts, maintain regulatory compliance, and build lasting competitive advantage.
**Accelerating finance**
Financial services leaders are investing in AI to unlock real-time data insights and enable a level of personalization that puts clients at the center of every interaction. Through our alliance with Salesforce, KPMG helps clients implement these capabilities responsibly and ethically. Our combined solution stack—including Salesforce Financial Cloud, Data Stack, Einstein GPT, and Agentforce—enables you to transform customer engagement, streamline operations, and meet regulatory requirements while maintaining the trust that financial relationships demand.
With deep industry knowledge and extensive experience implementing Salesforce solutions for financial institutions, KPMG presents tailored approaches that address the specific challenges across the financial sector. Our work focuses on creating measurable business value through:
  * Personalized customer experiences that strengthen relationships
  * Operational efficiency through process automation and optimization 
  * Secure, compliant technology integration
  * Data modernization for AI-powered insights


**Building the future together**
The KPMG [award-winning approach](https://kpmg.com/us/en/media/news/kpmg-ranks-for-quality-ai-advice-implementation-source-2024.html) to digital labor, combined with our Trusted AI framework and [dedicated AI Incubator](https://kpmg.com/us/en/capabilities-services/alliances/kpmg-salesforce/kpmg-ai-incubator-for-agentforce.html) helps clients maximize Salesforce platform to achieve full value from your investment. Through MuleSoft integration and Tableau visual analytics, we deliver extensive capabilities that help transform customer engagement while maintaining the personal touch that builds lasting relationships. Our lauded approach supports rapid implementation while protecting your existing investments, avoiding large data moves, and empowering your employees to become engines of innovation.
Together, KPMG and Salesforce can help you build solutions that accelerate financial services transformation, improve client satisfaction, and drive sustainable growth—while keeping you ahead of the future.
To learn more, visit [Salesforce Customer 360](https://www.salesforce.com/products/).
