## Building capacity across Australia’s national security and justice industry
Organisations within the national security and justice sector are striving to be lean and dynamic while striking a delicate balance between technology adoption, risk minimisation and complex legislative compliance. Improving performance and impact while reducing cost remains a critical challenge, and KPMG Australia offers the deep sector expertise required to build organisational capability, develop leaders and drive real cultural change.
###  How we support your sector 
KPMG offers expertise across police, courts and corrective services as well as emergency management, youth justice, customs and immigration.
###  Cyber security 
KPMG supports the national security sector with capabilities spanning cyber security strategies, security architecture, security risk and technical assessments and managing and delivering information security projects and tools.
###  Forensic investigations 
KPMG’s forensic investigators and integrity specialists have extensive skills and experience in areas including policing and criminology and are skilled in conducting in-depth fraud, corruption and misconduct investigations.
###  Digital transformation 
From major IT change through to the development of IT strategy, vendor selection and implementation assurance, we work with the industry to build organisational capability through technological change.
###  Risk and compliance 
From government agencies to emergency management and beyond, we work with organisations within security and justice sectors to identify, assess and manage risks while ensuring complying with complex legislative frameworks
