### In the office
Founded in 2002, West Monroe’s Chicago office is at the heart of our growth and innovation. Located in the Loop, our HQ blends the city’s spirit with our passion for technology and business. More than just a workspace, it's where we host client events, celebrate achievements on our 14th floor, and explore shared hobbies through our Chiefs program, like Basketball, Tacos, Chess, and Fitness. You might even catch us in our basement bowling alley rolling a few frames.
### In the community
We're not just in Chicago. We're part of Chicago. Our team actively supports a number of local organizations such as United Way of Metro Chicago, Posse Chicago, Girls on the Run Chicago, and Nourishing Hope. With a deep passion for making a difference, we’re committed to uplifting our neighbors and strengthening the community through meaningful, hands-on involvement.
