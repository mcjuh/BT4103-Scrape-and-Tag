# Moving Forward with Intention and Nurturing a Culture that Allows People to Thrive

  * PRINT

**A Message from BDO Public Sector, LLCFrank Landefeld, President and CEO**
Helping people thrive every day means we are committed to our team members at all levels and all functions, to our clients and their missions, and delivering services with quality and integrity. More than ever, public sector agencies are being asked to do more and with greater accountability – modernize systems while protecting critical data, advance innovation while maintaining compliance, and deliver results in environments defined by constant change. These new realities shape how we serve our clients and how we think about the future. 
## Our Priorities for 2026
BDO’s 2026 priorities for BDO Public Sector are clear. We are focusing on impact, leading with integrity and investing in our people.
### Making an impact.
Our clients rely on us not just for our experience, but to help them deliver outcomes that strengthen mission performance and public trust.
### Leading with integrity.
Trust is essential in public sector work, and it must be earned every day—through transparency, accountability, and professionalism.
### Investing in our people.
Our success depends on building teams who are supported, challenged, and given opportunities to grow.
## Talent for a Changing Government
Government is evolving, and so is the talent that is required to deliver quality results that last. Technical skills matter, but they are only part of the equation. We look for people who are thoughtful, curious, and accountable – professionals who take ownership, collaborate across teams, and understand the responsibility that comes with serving the public. We value diverse experiences and perspectives, and believe the best results are achieved when people are empowered to contribute and lead.
