# Green Growth Accelerator
Building a green business is no longer a niche ambition; it’s a practical path to longevity. Our green growth consultants work side-by-side with clients to build businesses that deliver both enterprise value and climate-positive impact.
Green growth is a core driver of enterprise value. Market signals are clear: **green tech costs are falling by up to 90%** and **up to $7.4 trillion in annual capital investment is needed** to meet climate goals.But while the momentum for [decarbonization](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/climate-change-sustainability/decarbonization) is clear, uncertainty remains. The opportunity belongs to those prepared to navigate complexity and build scalable green business models that lead the market.

## Our Approach to Green Growth
Today’s CEOs are prioritizing green growth as a core driver of resilience, competitive advantage, and profitable growth in an increasingly climate-defined economy. BCG’s green growth accelerator helps clients seize this opportunity through scalable green business models that deliver both [climate impact](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/climate-change-sustainability/overview) and competitive advantage. We bring strategy, speed, and execution rigor to every stage of green growth. Here’s how we ensure our clients’ success:
Expand All 
Purpose and Strategy
We help clients define the ambition behind their green growth, anchoring it in purpose, vision, and long-term strategy. That includes clarifying sustainability goals, setting credible targets, and identifying strategic initiatives that will drive both growth and impact. This early alignment sets the tone for execution and ensures each move serves a larger business and climate agenda. 
Value Proposition
We support clients in defining a sharp market position, building differentiation, and shaping tailored offerings based on customer needs. Our teams help craft compelling business cases, identify price-premium green business growth opportunities, and de-risk growth through early off-take agreements that validate market demand and ensure revenue certainty. From MVP development to go-to-market planning and commercial strategy, we help ensure that the business is viable, scalable, and positioned to lead.
Operating Model
To deliver and grow, green businesses need agile, execution-focused organizations. We help design the right operating model—including [organizational structure](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/organization-strategy/organization-design), talent strategy, [supply chain](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/supply-chain-management) management, and technology tools—to ensure the business is equipped to move with speed, accountability, and focus.
Growth Accelerators
New ventures succeed when their foundations are built to scale. We work with clients to unlock smart capital, achieve tech maturity and cost efficiency of nascent solutions, and shape ecosystems to support sustainable green growth. Our teams design effective funding strategies, advise on navigating complex regulations, and identify where customers will pay a green premium versus where a strong business case is essential.
We work with corporates, scale-ups, investors, and developers to build and grow new green business ventures. Whether the goal is to activate existing assets, commercialize emerging green tech, or launch into new markets, our focus is the same: creating viable businesses that deliver value and impact from day one.

Client Impact
