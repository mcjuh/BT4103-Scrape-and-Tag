## Overview
### Raise the bar on service delivery at scale
Professional business services are under pressure to move faster, deliver consistently and adapt continuously. Clients expect more insight, more transparency and more impact—without added complexity. We work with service providers to reimagine how work gets done, from operating models and global delivery to data, AI and talent strategies, so you can strengthen performance today and build momentum for what comes next.

### Modernize infrastructure-driven service models
Built environment services (BES) providers are navigating cost pressures, asset intensity and rising demand for digitally enabled delivery. From facilities management to infrastructure support and field services, performance depends on productivity, margin discipline and capital efficiency. We work with BES organizations to modernize operating models, embed digital tools and strengthen portfolio strategy. Improve service reliability, unlock asset-light opportunities and position your business to scale as infrastructure and sustainability demands evolve.
### Reinvent workforce solutions for what’s next
Human capital management (HCM) providers are reshaping their offerings as workforce expectations shift and AI transforms recruitment, talent management and HR operations. Margin pressure and changing client demand require a sharper focus on differentiated, higher-value services. We help HCM companies redesign business models, expand digitally enabled solutions, and improve global delivery efficiency. Strengthen recurring revenue streams, enhance productivity, and position your workforce solutions for long-term resilience.
### Scale smarter with AI-enabled operations
Business process outsourcing (BPO) providers face rising expectations for speed, insight and cost transparency. AI and automation present opportunities to shift toward higher-margin, digitally enabled service models. We support BPO firms in modernizing delivery platforms, optimizing cost structures, and expanding into adjacent service domains. Improve productivity, strengthen cash flow discipline, and build scalable operating models that drive sustainable growth in a competitive market.
### Compete for talent and lead with technology
Professional services (PS) firms are seeing resilient demand for core offerings—but the landscape is shifting. Digital services are driving new growth, while competition for skilled talent intensifies. We help PS firms navigate this evolving environment, reinventing delivery models, enhancing digital capabilities, and recalibrating talent strategies. From GenAI adoption to global delivery optimization, we work with you to improve productivity, protect margins, and position for sustained growth.
### Capture the AI opportunity in learning and data
Digital and education (D&E) providers are turning AI into a growth engine. AI-enabled services are driving revenue, higher-margin digital offerings are improving profitability, and automation is boosting productivity. But staying ahead requires continuous adaptation. We help D&E companies leverage new technologies, explore new markets, and meet changing client demands. From inward-facing AI tools to customer-facing solutions, we help you build a business that leads.
### Adapt to evolving regulatory and demand shifts
Legal services organizations are experiencing shifting demand patterns, pricing pressure, and increasing regulatory complexity. Digital enablement, AI-assisted review and alternative service models are redefining how value is delivered. We support legal services providers in modernizing operating structures, expanding higher-margin advisory capabilities, and strengthening governance frameworks. Improve productivity, enhance billing realization, and build resilience in a rapidly evolving legal landscape.
### Build resilient, tech-enabled supply chains
Logistics and distribution (L&D) providers operate at the center of global value chains, facing cost volatility, geopolitical uncertainty, and demand fluctuations. Performance depends on digital visibility, capital efficiency, and scalable operations. We work with L&D organizations to enhance forecasting, optimize network design, and strengthen service reliability. Improve asset utilization, diversify revenue streams and position your logistics platform for cross-sector growth opportunities.
### Strengthen performance in regulated markets
Testing, inspection, and certification (TICC) providers play a critical role in regulated industries where trust, compliance, and technical rigor matter. Margin pressure and evolving regulatory standards require disciplined cost management and digital modernization. We help TICC organizations streamline operations, embed advanced analytics, and refine service portfolios. Improve profitability, strengthen recurring revenue and scale efficiently in markets where certification and compliance remain mission critical.
## Capabilities
  1. Modernize operations and delivery models
  2. Align strategy, portfolio, and growth
  3. Build the workforce for AI-enabled services
  4. Scale AI with governance and trust


### Modernize operations and delivery models
Professional business services firms are under pressure to reduce cost-to-serve while scaling new offerings. We help you modernize your operating model and digital core— from finance, billing and entitlement systems to global delivery networks and shared services structures. By simplifying complexity and aligning delivery to value creation, you can lower structural costs, improve visibility into profitability, and create the operational foundation required to scale new revenue models.
### Align strategy, portfolio, and growth
Growth in professional business services increasingly depends on disciplined capital allocation and portfolio clarity. We help you assess where to invest, divest or reposition — using transactions and strategic partnerships as levers for measurable value creation, not just scale. From evaluating acquisitions and carve-outs to aligning pricing, offerings and market focus, we help you sharpen your portfolio strategy so you can drive profitable growth and reduce unnecessary complexity.
### Build the workforce for AI-enabled services
Your industry is being reshaped by AI-enabled delivery, automation, and evolving client expectations. We help you redesign roles, reskill talent and embed AI into workflows so your workforce can operate at higher productivity and insight. From capability assessments to new incentive structures and operating rhythms, we help you align people, process, and technology—enabling human-AI collaboration that supports long-term growth and margin resilience.
### Scale AI with governance and trust
AI is redefining how your organization delivers insight and efficiency. We help you move from pilots to enterprise-wide adoption—embedding governance, data controls and risk management into every phase of deployment. By aligning AI strategy with regulatory expectations, client trust and operational resilience, you can scale AI responsibly while strengthening competitiveness and protecting brand credibility.
