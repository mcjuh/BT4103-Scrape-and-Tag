Get in touch
ERM CVS provides assurance, verification, certification and training services for the world’s leading organizations. We strive to make a positive difference to the future of the planet by adding credibility to our clients’ sustainability performance.
We have been supporting organizations since 1996, and view our work as more than just "ticking the box." Our team of experts works broad-ranging industries and are passionate about supporting clients achieve their goals. In all cases, utilizing our industry experience, subject-matter and standards expertise, we identify opportunities for improvement, and challenge our clients to help them transform their sustainability performance and achieve their goals.
## Our services
ESG & Sustainability Reporting Assurance
Sustainability performance data is a vital source of information for anyone seeking a holistic understanding of an organization’s activities. Independent assurance adds credibility and can help identify areas for improvement while signaling a desire for transparency and meaningful reporting.
[Learn more](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/esg-sustainability-reporting-assurance/)
Certification of Management Systems
Delivering independent single site and multi-site conformity assessment and certification services, for connected operations and controls and scope to boost organizational performance.
[Learn more](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/certification-management-systems/)
Training Services
ERM CVS offers public and private flexible and efficient training programmes that help organizations and individuals improve their knowledge and understanding of standards, regulations and sustainability assurance requirements.
[Learn more](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/training-services/)
Sustainable Finance Assurance
ERM CVS offers a range of independent assurance services to support sustainable or green finance. Whether it’s assurance of Sustainability Performance Targets (SPTs) for Sustainability-Linked Loans (SLLs), assurance of Impact Reporting, or annual verification of performance against Green Bond commitments, our robust assurance approach helps add integrity and credibility.
[Learn more](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/sustainable-finance-assurance/)
Sector Specific Certification Schemes
ERM CVS provides sector specific assessment and certification services, enabling organizations to combine management system needs with sector specific schemes, providing greater efficiency and performance opportunities.
[Learn more](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/sector-specific-certification-schemes/)
Product Claims Assurance
ERM CVS provides independent assurance for product claims made by organizations, such as low carbon, carbon intensity, product carbon footprint and recycled content. This third-party assurance helps to boost credibility and trust with customers. 
[Learn more](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/product-claims-assurance/)
Carbon Market Assurance
ERM CVS is an independent verification and validation body (VVB) delivering a range of services to project developers, carbon credit buyers and investors looking to de-risk their investments and ensure trust and credibility in the carbon markets.
[Learn more](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/carbon-market-assurance/)
Internal and Supplier Audit Services
ERM CVS provides internal and supplier audit and verification services to help organizations improve ESG resilience and performance. Our services enable clients to understand operational risks, strengthen regulatory compliance, meet investor and consumer expectations and improve stakeholder engagement.
[Learn more](https://www.erm.com/ermcvs/services/internal-supplier-audit-services/)
