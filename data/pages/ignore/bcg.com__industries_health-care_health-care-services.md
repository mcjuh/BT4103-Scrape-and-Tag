# Health Care Services Industry
AI, new care models, and ecosystem shifts are changing health care delivery and financing. BCG’s health care services consultants partner with provider, payer, and health system executives to deliver better patient outcomes and greater organizational value.
Health care leaders must simultaneously improve productivity, expand access to care, and strengthen financial sustainability in an increasingly complex, regulated environment. Success is driven by reimagining care delivery, [operations](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/overview), and business models supported by the latest [technology](https://www.bcg.com/industries/technology-media-telecommunications/overview) while maintaining clinical excellence. Our health care services consultants help health care providers, payers, and systems deliver lasting transformation.

## How We Help Clients in the Health Care Services Industry
Health care transformation extends beyond any single organization. Providers, payers, and health systems face distinct priorities, but sustainable change depends on how they work together across the broader health care ecosystem.Our health care services consulting teams bring deep sector expertise with digital, [AI,](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence) and operational capabilities to help clients improve outcomes, strengthen performance, and build more resilient organizations. We partner with private and [public sector](https://www.bcg.com/industries/public-sector/overview) organizations across the health care ecosystem to address organization-specific priorities while improving how the system works as a whole.
Expand All 
Health Care Providers
Providers are redefining care delivery to improve patient outcomes, increase capacity, and achieve sustainable growth. Our health care provider consulting work includes:
  * **AI-Enabled Clinical Transformation.** Optimizing clinical pathways, resource utilization, and care delivery through AI and advanced analytics.
  * **Digital Access to Care and Patient Navigation.** Expanding access through digital engagement, virtual care, and connected patient experiences.
  * **Growth, Business Model, and**[**M &A Strategy**](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/mergers-acquisitions-transactions-pmi/m-and-a)**.** Developing growth strategies, evaluating M&A opportunities, and redesigning operating models to strengthen long-term performance.


Health Care Payers
Payers are using AI and new operating models to improve affordability, accelerate innovation, and deliver more coordinated care. Drawing on deep experience across payer organizations, our health care payer consulting team engages clients to improve performance while strengthening coordination across the health care ecosystem through:
  * **AI-Driven Cost Transformation.** Improving medical and administrative [cost management](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/cost-management) through AI, automation, and advanced analytics.
  * **Payer-Provider Integration.** Strengthening coordination, optimizing operations, and improving member and patient experiences.
  * **Technology Modernization and Modular Architecture.** Modernizing technology and operating models to accelerate innovation and improve organizational agility.


Health Care Systems
Health care systems must improve access, affordability, and resilience while preparing for future health challenges. Our health care systems consultants partner with governments, ministries of health, and public agencies to redesign systems that deliver better outcomes for populations while creating sustainable models for care delivery in areas such as:
  * **Health System Redesign.** Transforming reimbursement, service delivery, workforce strategies, and system governance.
  * **Digital Infrastructure.** Building connected data and digital platforms that improve coordination, expand access, and enable more effective care delivery.
  * **Policy and Legislative Implementation.** Translating health policy into practical implementation that strengthens system performance and improves population outcomes.


## Our Impact With Clients in the Health Care Services Industry
20%
additional clinical capacity
BCG helped a health care provider develop and implement an AI-enabled clinical pathway tool that analyzes electronic health records to recommend personalized care pathways and optimize appointment scheduling. The solution increased clinical capacity by 10% to 20%, enabling more efficient care delivery while supporting better patient outcomes.
20%
lower average handling time
A European health care provider engaged BCG to transform patient services with a GenAI-powered virtual agent that automates appointment scheduling and routine patient inquiries. The solution reduced average handling time by approximately 20%, improved service levels with shorter wait times and 24/7 availability, and enhanced the overall patient experience.
7%
cost savings within one year
To restore sustainable growth, a leading health care payer worked with BCG on a comprehensive transformation that included new health insurance products, an expanded care management program, and customer-focused brand repositioning, and a modern technological basis. The effort launched seven new products, reversed the decline in supplementary insurance, delivered >5% year-over-year growth, and reduced costs in targeted care management by 7%.
1st
national health strategy
BCG partnered with a Middle Eastern government to develop the country’s first comprehensive national health strategy. Spanning governance, funding, care delivery, workforce, public health, and digital transformation, the strategy created a shared roadmap to expand access to care, strengthen public health and prevention, advance value-based payments, and build a more sustainable health system.
10-year
health system strategy
An Australian state health care system partnered with BCG to develop a future health strategy that would reimagine care delivery over the next decade. BCG helped define a new vision for a more sustainable, patient-centered, and digitally enabled health system while creating an implementation roadmap to build stakeholder alignment and support delivery at scale.
Client Impact
[How Rochester Regional Health Is Healing Itself and the Community](https://www.bcg.com/about/client-impact/how-a-western-new-york-health-provider-is-healing-itself-and-the-community)
By overhauling strategy and operations and rediscovering its core values, vision, and purpose, the Western New York health provider halted $1 million in daily losses, reinvigorated its workforce, and improved access to care.
