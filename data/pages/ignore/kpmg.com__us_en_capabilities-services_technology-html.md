##  HP’s M&A 
Engineering HP’s smooth integration 
**Challenges**
M&A integration can be complex, especially when merging technologies, systems, and data across different organizations. HP needed to efficiently integrate and align systems, maintain business continuity, and optimize the value created from the merger.
**Outcomes**
KPMG LLP assembled a global team of AI-enabled specialists to tackle merger complexities. We integrated customized data sites and accelerated real-time tracking, testing, and budgeting. Our smart technology approach improved visibility and accountability throughout the process, driving productivity and innovation.

## AI in finance
### Banking on Trusted AI
**Challenges**
A leading US financial services company recognized the growing risks associated with its AI and machine learning (ML) systems. To improve reliability, establish trust in its AI-driven decisions, and pre-empt ethical, legal, or compliance issues, it turned to KPMG.
**Outcomes**
KPMG brought together the people and technology to design a responsible AI program, developing a proof of concept for processes to validate AI/ML models and data. We helped the client centralize its AI/ML governance across multiple business units, mitigating risks by providing a more complete responsible AI solution.
