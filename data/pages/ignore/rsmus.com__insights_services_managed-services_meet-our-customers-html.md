#### Government contractor: CMMC compliance with managed IT and cybersecurity
Prior to working with RSM, a government contractor faced significant challenges managing a fragmented IT environment created through multiple acquisitions, making it difficult to maintain security, compliance and operational efficiency. Guided by RSM’s managed IT and cybersecurity services team, the organization modernized their IT infrastructure, strengthened cybersecurity controls, and established a sustainable path to Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification compliance. Today, the company benefits from a more secure, scalable and reliable technology environment, allowing internal teams to focus on core business goals while RSM proactively manages IT operations and ongoing compliance requirements.
[ Read more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/services/managed-services/government-contractor-strengthens-compliance-and-cybersecurity-by-outsourcing.html "Read more")
**Global energy enterprise: Back-office and technology solutions**
As their U.S. energy operations expanded and regulatory and operational complexity increased, B.Grimm Power turned to RSM Catamaran’s outsourcing solutions to manage critical back‑office and technology needs. Through scalable support across accounting, payroll, IT, tax and cybersecurity, RSM provided the local resources and operational consistency the multinational organization needed to operate efficiently in the U.S. market. With RSM’s integrated outsourcing solutions in place, B.Grimm gained greater control, improved efficiency and achieved lasting peace of mind—allowing leadership to stay focused on strategic growth while trusting RSM to support day‑to‑day operations.
[ Read more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/services/managed-services/outsourcing-fuels-success-for-global-energy-business.html "Read more")
**Biopharma company: Multifunctional support**
As Geron Corporation moved into commercialization of their blood cancer treatment, growing operational demands and limited internal capacity led the company to engage RSM Catamaran for multifunctional outsourcing support. Through scalable services across finance, IT and tax—along with the integration of launch operations—RSM delivered the consistency and efficiency needed to navigate this critical phase. With RSM’s integrated model in place, Geron gained improved efficiency, stronger reporting and greater flexibility—allowing leadership to stay focused on advancing their therapies while RSM supports day‑to‑day operations.
[ Read more ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/life-sciences/from-science-to-scale-how-biopharma-geron-transformed-with-rsm.html "Read more")
**Real estate and private equity firm: Fund administration and accounting services**
A leading real estate and private equity firm turned to RSM’s managed services team to address staffing challenges and streamline fund administration. By leveraging RSM Fund Services+ and the firm’s Sage Intacct capabilities, the organization strengthened core financial processes, improved reporting reliability, and gained ongoing support across fund administration, technology and operations—all through a single, trusted advisor.
