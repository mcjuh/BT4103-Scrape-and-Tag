**HOW EY CAN HELP**
EY supports technology companies to shape finance strategies and deliver AI infrastructure at scale while balancing performance, resilience, sustainability and risk.
  * **More effective capital allocation:** Provide structured financing, risk assessment and valuation to guide infrastructure investment decisions. 
  * **Sustainable energy strategies:** Support energy sourcing, efficiency planning and sustainability integration to secure reliable power for AI growth. 
  * **Faster infrastructure deployment:** Refine planning, supplier strategies and delivery models to reduce delays and accelerate capacity build-out. 
  * **Compliant, scalable architecture** : Design data center and AI solutions aligned with data sovereignty, regulation and multi-region requirements.

###### **CHALLENGE**
### Bridging the gap between AI investment and finance adoption
While technology companies invest heavily in AI-enabled products, many finance functions lag in adoption — relying on manual processes and fragmented data, which limits insight, slows decision-making and reduces overall business agility.
Read More  Read Less 
**HOW EY CAN HELP**
EY professionals help technology companies transform finance functions by embedding AI across data, processes and decision-making — integrating systems, automating workflows and enabling more timely, insight-driven performance management for CFOs and finance leaders.
  * **Faster, insight-driven decision-making:** Integrate finance data with AI analytics to deliver real-time, explainable insights that support faster decisions and value creation. 
  * **Improved forecast accuracy and confidence:** Support predictive modeling and scenario analysis by aligning internal and external data to strengthen planning and forecasting outcomes. 
  * **Reduced manual effort and increased efficiency:** Automate data validation, reporting and core finance processes to reduce manual intervention and improve operational efficiency. 
  * **Greater strategic capacity in finance teams:** Redesign processes and embed AI capabilities to free up capacity, helping finance teams to focus on higher-value, strategic activities.
