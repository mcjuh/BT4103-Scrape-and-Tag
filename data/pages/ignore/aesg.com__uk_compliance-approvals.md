## Navigate the Regulatory Landscape with Confidence
In today's complex building environment, compliance is critical. AESG offers a suite of services designed to help you navigate the intricate web of UK building regulations, ensuring your project meets all necessary standards and approvals.
## Our Compliance and Approvals Services
Third-Party Verification & Value Engineering  Tender Evaluation & Due Diligence Review  Building Control & Gateway Approval Advice  Building Safety Case Development 
## Third-Party Verification & Value Engineering
We provide independent verification to ensure your project meets all required standards:
### Comprehensive review of design and construction plans
### Cost-effective solutions that maintain safety and quality
### Identification of potential compliance issues
### Optimisation of designs for improved performance and value
## Tender Evaluation & Due Diligence Review
Our expert team assists in evaluating tenders and conducting thorough due diligence:
### Detailed analysis of tender submissions
### Identification of potential risks and liabilities
### Assessment of contractor capabilities and compliance history
### Recommendations for selecting the most suitable contractors
## Building Control & Gateway Approval Advice
We offer expert guidance on navigating the Building Safety Act's gateway process:
### Assistance with Gateway One planning applications and fire statements
### Guidance on Gateway Three completion certificate requirements
### Support for Gateway Two building control approval submissions
### Advice on meeting Building Safety Regulator expectations
## Building Safety Case Development
Creating PCRs or Complementary-PCRs where a suitable PCR does not yet exist, enabling the development of EPDs.
### Comprehensive risk assessments
### Creation of the 'golden thread' of building information
### Development of safety management systems
### Ongoing support for maintaining and updating the Safety Case
