# KPMG Legal Technology Innovation
From strategy to optimization, we deliver integrated, automated solutions for legal and contracting workflows.
##  Is your legal technology stack delivering measurable business impact? 
Legal departments are investing in technology at an unprecedented speed, but many fail to achieve real operational gains. Tools remain underused, data fragmented, and workflows disconnected. 
KPMG Legal Technology Innovation can help turn these investments into measurable business impact.
## Integrated capabilities to drive legal technology innovation and transformation
KPMG brings together Legal Technology Innovation, Legal Operations Consulting, and [Legal Managed Services](https://kpmg.com/us/en/capabilities-services/tax-services/global-legal-business-services/kpmg-legal-managed-services.html) to help deliver connected, measurable results.
Strategy and system selection
Define your legal technology strategy, requirements, and vendor landscape by identifying where agentic AI can safely automate and select platforms aligned to your workflows and enterprise architecture. From contract lifecycle management to matter management and e-billing, we help you choose the right tools for your needs.
Implementation and integration
Implement, configure, and integrate legal technology with ERP, CRM, procurement, finance, and data platforms. Our approach covers requirements for definition, configuration, and testing, to help ensure adoption, oversight, alignment, and measurable results.
AI-enabled legal workflows
Design workflows that apply GenAI, agentic AI, and automation where they deliver real value. We help enable document automation, AI-driven analytics, and the controls required for responsible use across the legal function.
