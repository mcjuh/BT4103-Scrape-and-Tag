# Deliver differentiated quality with quality engineering services
KPMG Quality Engineering (QE) services combine thought leadership and NexGen solutions to deliver accelerated quality.
## AI is disrupting how work gets done. Are you ready to maximize its value?
### KPMG LLP quality engineering services leverage our knowledge and experience with AI-powered testing strategies, governance, automated test execution, testing tools, and monitoring methods to help you deliver accelerated quality.
As the world continues to evolve towards AI-driven strategies to achieve higher efficiency, better decision-making, and new revenue streams, it’s imperative that your testing strategies embrace the latest tools and approaches to support your goals.
Modern quality engineering practices can help propel your organization forward, moving you from reactive validation to advanced, smart automation. You can expand test coverage, reduce manual tasks, and release software with more confidence.
We provide a team of professionals who help clients reduce risk and increase speed to market by offering tools and approaches that can transform testing from fragmented efforts into a centralized, governed, and optimized testing powerhouse. We are here to show you the _future of quality engineering_.
