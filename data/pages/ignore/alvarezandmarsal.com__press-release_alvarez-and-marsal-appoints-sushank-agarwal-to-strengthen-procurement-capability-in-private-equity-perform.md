# Alvarez and Marsal appoints Sushank Agarwal to strengthen procurement capability in Private Equity Performance Improvement team
_Senior hire from BCG Inverto to build European procurement capability across PEPI_
**London, 19 January 2026** – Global professional services firm Alvarez & Marsal (A&M) has appointed Sushank Agarwal as a Managing Director in its [Private Equity Performance Improvement](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/private-equity-performance-improvement) (PEPI) practice, based in London.
[Sushank](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/sushank-agarwal) joins A&M from BCG Inverto, where as Global Private Equity Business Lead he advised private equity firms and portfolio companies on large-scale procurement transformation and value creation programmes. His appointment reflects A&M’s continued investment in strengthening its operational capabilities across Europe, with a particular focus on procurement, supply chain resilience and cost transformation for PE-backed businesses.
With more than 25 years’ experience delivering procurement-led value across a broad range of industries, Sushank has worked extensively with private equity-owned companies to drive rapid, sustainable savings. Alongside near-term value delivery, he is known for helping management teams embed stronger procurement capabilities that endure well beyond the initial transformation.
Prior to BCG Inverto, Sushank held senior leadership roles at Kearney, Accenture Strategy and Efficio, building and leading high-performing procurement teams and delivering complex, multi-country transformation programmes.
Sushank will lead A&M’s PE procurement practice, working closely with the European PEPI leadership team to scale A&M’s procurement capability across the region, partnering with private equity sponsors and portfolio company management teams to accelerate value creation through disciplined execution.
[**Mike Trenouth**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/mike-trenouth)**, Managing Director and European Co-Head of Private Equity Performance Improvement at Alvarez & Marsal, said**: _“Procurement remains a powerful lever for value creation in private equity. Sushank brings rare depth across both strategic procurement and hands-on delivery, with a track record of achieving rapid impact while building capabilities that last. His arrival significantly strengthens our offering to clients across Europe.”_
[**Kevin Garvey**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/kevin-garvey)**, Managing Director and European Lead, Operations, Supply Chain & Procurement team, said:** _“As we continue to build out our supply chain and operations capabilities across Europe, Sushank’s experience working closely with investors and management teams will be invaluable. His understanding of how procurement, operations and value creation intersect in PE-backed environments aligns closely with how we work with clients.”_
**On joining A &M, Sushank said:** _“A &M offers clients a service that combines strategic insight with operational execution. Private equity clients are increasingly focused on turning procurement into a sustained source of competitive advantage, not just a one-off cost exercise. I’m excited to join the firm at a time of strong momentum and to help build a best-in-class procurement capability across Europe.”_
