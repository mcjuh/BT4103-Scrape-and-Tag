Your idea. Bigger.

## A lasting legacy
KPMG Studio is a fast-to-market incubator that helps ultra-determined pioneers transform a big dream into an even bigger reality.
Anu Puvvada, KPMG Studio Leader, discusses how the business-building program helps pioneers leave a lasting legacy for generations to come.
OUR APPROACH 
At KPMG Studio, we think big. Big ideas, big potential, big business. 
We’ve designed a rigorous and guided process that pushes an idea’s potential beyond its original boundaries. Ideas that enter the Studio are put to the test in a business-building experience designed to hone a product or service into a viable business model.
By working alongside a core team and applying data-driven insights, pioneers have the opportunity to test their idea’s viability in the market and pivot their approach in real time.
As the idea moves from the incubator to the accelerator, we provide the foundational materials and expertise to scale the business. At the end of the program, pioneers are equipped to lead their business into the future.
Incubator 
Seed, test, and shape early stage ideas
Accelerator 
Develop solutions and business model, drive early adopter growth, and mentor
Center of excellence 
Scale, activate, and prepare for spin-in or spin-out
