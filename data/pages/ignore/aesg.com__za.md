# Sustainability and Engineering Consultants in South Africa
Delivering Innovative Solutions for South Africa's Built Environment
AESG South Africa is rapidly expanding as a leading sustainability consultancy and engineering advisory firm, delivering cutting-edge solutions in façade engineering, fire and life safety, building commissioning, carbon management, and net-zero strategies across South Africa and the African continent. Our growing presence reflects a strong commitment to addressing the region’s evolving built environment needs. With in-depth knowledge of South Africa’s unique challenges and opportunities, our expert team supports clients in achieving Green Building Council South Africa (GBCSA) Green Star certifications and aligning with international sustainability standards.
## Our Commitment to South Africa
As we scale our operations, we remain focused on advancing energy-efficient façades, fire-resilient buildings, and climate-adaptive infrastructure that meet both local regulations and the broader goals of South Africa’s low-carbon development agenda. We specialize in four core service areas: **Environment and Sustainability, Engineering, and Project Delivery Management.** We work closely with developers, architects, engineers, and government bodies to ensure that each project delivers measurable impact, contributing to South Africa’s sustainability goals, including the National Climate Change Response Policy, GBCSA Green Star Program, and the country's commitments to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
## Our Commitment to Africa
From our foundation in South Africa and Egypt, AESG is rapidly growing across the African continent, partnering with governments, visionary real estate developers, and forward-thinking private sector leaders. With a deep respect for Africa’s rich diversity and boundless potential, we blend local knowledge with global expertise to craft solutions that reflect the continent’s aspirations for sustainable growth and thriving communities. AESG is honoured to play a role in shaping Africa’s future - one where the built environment harmonises with nature, resilience is built into every structure, and every project paves the way for a sustainable tomorrow.
