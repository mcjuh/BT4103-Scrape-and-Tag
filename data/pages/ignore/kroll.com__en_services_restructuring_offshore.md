Global restructuring leadership delivering offshore solutions that protect and recover value
## Who We Are
Kroll’s Offshore Restructuring practice is a solution-driven advisor in restructuring and financial disputes, focused on navigating the complexities of offshore environments. Our team is a trusted partner for clients facing financial distress, enforcement actions, and operational disruption. We help clients achieve long-term success by identifying core issues and implementing tailored solutions aligned with their goals. Our experience in assessing financial and operational integrity enables us to act decisively in complex scenarios, whether advising management or stepping into leadership roles. 
Our multidisciplinary team combines deep industry knowledge with regulatory expertise to manage cross-border restructuring cases with precision. We handle contentious and distressed matters while ensuring fair stakeholder representation. With Kroll's Offshore Restructuring practice, clients gain a dedicated partner committed to protecting value and delivering results.

## What We Do
Financial and Operational Restructuring
Kroll leads complex restructurings across borders, acting as Chief Restructuring Officers and Directors. We align stakeholders, enforce solutions, and restore stability through valuation, due diligence, and transparent execution tailored to financial and operational challenges.
Insolvency
Kroll acts as liquidators, administrators, receivers, and trustees. We safeguard assets, resolve disputes, and maximize creditor recoveries across jurisdictions. Our licensed experts stabilize distressed entities with speed, precision, and litigation strategies in complex insolvency matters.
Asset Recovery and Investigations
We trace hidden assets and enforce remedies in fraud and financial disputes. Acting as receivers or liquidators, Kroll applies forensic accounting and global intelligence to recover value through cross-border investigations and strategic enforcement.
Turnaround and Transformation
Kroll designs targeted restructuring plans to improve liquidity, profitability, and operations. We assess challenges, intervene strategically, and guide clients through distress to recovery, restoring confidence and unlocking long-term potential with deep industry insight.
Controlled and Solvent Wind-Downs
Kroll advises on orderly wind-downs that protect fiduciary duties and stakeholder interests. We maximize asset value, minimize risk, and use court systems strategically to ensure fair, transparent outcomes without unnecessary disruption or litigation.
Scheme of Company Arrangements
We guide companies through schemes of arrangement to restructure obligations with creditor approval. Kroll provides planning, negotiation, and documentation expertise to ensure compliance, preserve value, and support long-term sustainability.
Receiverships
Kroll steps into distressed situations to manage operations, protect interests, and optimize performance. Our receivership experience ensures continuity, transparency, and recovery through best practices in complex restructuring scenarios.
Claims Management
We help clients navigate bankruptcy and liability claims with precision. Kroll maximizes recovery through legal and insolvency expertise, strategic advocacy, regulatory insight, and simplified processes that deliver results and exceed expectations.
