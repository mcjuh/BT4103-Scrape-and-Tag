# 2024 Financial and Operating Trends in Private Clubs report
### Report highlights trends of more than 200 clubs throughout Florida
November 18, 2024
Private clubs
##  Private clubs in 2024: Future-ready insights for a changing landscape 
Explore the latest operating and financial trends for over 200 private clubs across Florida, a state known for its vibrant private clubs. This 2024 report, drawing from the most recent audited annual reports of private clubs and associations compiled by RSM US LLP, provides essential insights into the challenges and opportunities within this evolving industry. Our rigorous methodology ensures both the integrity and reliability of the data, empowering clubs to make informed, strategic decisions.
In a year marked by economic shifts, board members and managers at golf and country clubs are navigating heightened pressures—from elevated interest rates and workforce shortages to supply chain constraints and housing affordability issues. These challenges are expected to persist, affecting operational stability and financial performance across the sector.
This report not only examines these factors but also delivers industry benchmarks, enabling clubs to gauge their financial and operational health against peers. We also encourage clubs to focus on aligning their performance with their own strategic goals and budget objectives rather than solely comparing with other organizations.
Gain a strategic advantage with this detailed analysis of the financial and operational trends shaping the private club industry in 2024. Don’t miss out on the opportunity to future-proof your club.
