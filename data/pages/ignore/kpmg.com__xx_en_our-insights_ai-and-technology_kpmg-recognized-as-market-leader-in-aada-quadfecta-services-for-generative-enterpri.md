KPMG has been recognized as a Market Leader in the [HFS Horizons AADA Quadfecta Services for the Generative Enterprise 2024 reportopens in a new tab](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/xx/pdf/2025/03/kpmg-hfs-horizons-aadaquadfecta-services-2024.pdf).
The report relies on a range of sources to help HFS obtain a well-rounded perspective on 27 service providers. The aim of the report is to assess service providers’ ability to demonstrate the continuous value delivered to their enterprise clients, leading to them becoming a Generative Enterprisevia the AADA quadfecta (i.e., analytics, AI, data platforms and automation).
To be acknowledged as a market leader (known as Horizon Three), providers must demonstrate the ability to “deliver integrated solutions that leverage the full power of _analytics_ , _AI_ , _data platforms_ , and _automation_ [AADA] and create entirely new sources of value, including forecasting future business models and enabling real-time decision-making. The aim is to create a Generative Enterprise”.
The report summarizes KPMG’s strengths as offering: “Holistic execution, IP development, and strategic partnering in the AADA space to drive cohesive business transformations.”
In relation to our capability, it states that “KPMG delivers comprehensive transformation and value creation…”, with an “emphasis on integrated teams and change management ensures that transformations are technical and aligned with client-specific business models”.
We are proud to have received this recognition, and of the commitment KPMG firms have shown in building leading practices that helps clients apply AADA technologies to create differentiated experiences for their own customers and employees. KPMG Lighthouse, a global network of centers of excellence in data & analytics, has helped accelerate our capability in AADA related technologies.
We believe that our approach, technology solutions, and commitment to clients sets the global organization apart. By starting with the model answer, KPMG firms can help accelerate change, and reduce risk.
