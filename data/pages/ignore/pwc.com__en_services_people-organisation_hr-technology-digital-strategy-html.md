#  HR Tech: HR Technology & Digital Strategy 
## HR Technology & Digital HR
Allow HR technology to be the driving force for transformation in your organisation, while ensuring that your employees embrace the new system for its clear benefits. We employ experienced specialists in systems like Workday, Oracle HCM and SAP SuccessFactors.
#### Contact us...
  * If you want to transform your organisation using HR technology.
  * If you want to simplify your localised HR systems and applications in a cloud.
  * If you want to integrate HR Technology with internal and external systems, such as salary administration or LinkedIn.
  * If you have concerns about risk, control and governance during implementation.


## Our capabilities include
  1. HR technology strategy and selection
  2. HR solution architecture and integration
  3. HR technology implementation
  4. HR disruptive technologies


### HR technology strategy and selection
We define strategies for deploying HR Technology to enable organisations to make the most of their employees and HR strategies, developing business cases for change and assisting with the evaluation and selection of systems. What's more, we work with you to ensure that transformational HR elements are taken into consideration, addressed and incorporated into any HR technology plan to provide the right foundation for success.
### HR solution architecture and integration
We define the HR systems landscape and align it with overall solution architecture so that organisations can ensure that their people and HR strategies are working effectively. We design and implement systems integration strategies to enable process and data integration.
### HR technology implementation
Technology is implemented in line with the HR function service delivery model and the organisation's personnel requirements. We have extensive experience of implementation and key SaaS and on-premises solutions from Workday, SAP and Oracle.
### HR disruptive technologies
Assisting organisations with identifying and deploying disruptive digital technology that has a significant impact on their HR and personnel issues.
