#  Private equity services that deliver operational confidence from acquisition to exit 
## Serving the PE ecosystem. Solving at scale. 
From diligence to daily operations, we help middle market portfolio companies move with speed, confidence and clarity. We bring insights from thousands of deals each year, trusted relationships with more than half of America’s largest private companies and experience working with every one of the top 25 global PE firms. Supporting over 1,100 portfolio companies annually, we help you unlock value faster and drive results that last.
Creating value takes more than speed — it takes consistency. We help private equity firms and their portfolio companies move faster with fewer surprises, delivering standardized, scalable solutions that drive performance across the portfolio. Value is realized at exit, but built every day — and we help make sure you're ready.

## Accelerating value, from diligence to exit
We help private equity firms and portfolio companies accelerate value creation with integrated, tech-enabled solutions tailored to every stage — from diligence to exit.
  1. Preferred provider simplicity
  2. Deal advisory and diligence support
  3. Private equity audit services
  4. Tax structuring and compliance for PE
  5. Portfolio company transformation
  6. Exit readiness and IPO advisory
  7. CFO enablement and finance team training
  8. Technology-enabled delivery


### Preferred provider simplicity
PwC’s preferred provider model streamlines portfolio-wide delivery of audit, tax and advisory services. With centralized coordination, standardized execution and a phased rollout approach, we help private equity firms reduce complexity and unlock value faster — without re-onboarding a new team for every portfolio company.
### Deal advisory and diligence support
Rapid financial, tax and operational due diligence to uncover risks and opportunities pre-acquisition.
### Private equity audit services
Scalable audit solutions tailored for PE-backed companies, designed to support IPO readiness and compliance with speed.
### Tax structuring and compliance for PE
Efficient tax structuring, global compliance and K-1 automation across funds and portfolio companies.
### Portfolio company transformation
Performance improvement, digital enablement and cost optimization to enhance EBITDA and unlock value.
### Exit readiness and IPO advisory
Strategic support to prepare for exits, including audit readiness, financial reporting and deal execution.
### CFO enablement and finance team training
Upskilling and advisory for portfolio CFOs and controllers through ongoing training, webcasts and toolkits.
### Technology-enabled delivery
Real-time insights and portfolio coordination through PwC’s proprietary platforms, like SightLine.
