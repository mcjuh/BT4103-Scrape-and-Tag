# End-to-End Restructuring
Kroll’s end-to-end restructuring services include preparation, filing, communications, notice, administration, reconciliation, distribution, and more.
### Prefiling Preparation Services
Intake, analyze and format unstructured and structured data to create a list of all actual and potential creditors, including utilization of our industry-leading proprietary contract summarization and abstraction solutions. Create customized case-specific websites and provide virtual data room services.
### Case Filing
Execute first-day noticing by providing necessary case information to the public and affected parties through a customized case-specific website and arrange for service of required notices.
### Call Center and Communications Support
Develop a customized strategic communications plan. We provide 24/7 call center, live chat and email support to properly assist parties of interest with questions about a case. Our capabilities are global, scalable and multi-lingual. 
### Creditor Notifications
Corporate restructurings can involve millions of individuals and entities – customers, vendors, employees, retirees, shareholdersand others – who must receive key filings and notice of important deadlines, especially in the case of a deadline to file a claim. Our noticing capabilities not only disseminate key deliverables, but also stand up to any scrutiny from the court as serving the best interests of all parties. 
### Preparation of SOFAs and Schedules
Work with clients and their advisors to aggregate data, properly identify various constituents and create necessary court filings, including Statements of Financial Affairs and Schedules of Assets and Liabilities.
### Claims Administration and Case Management
Review and process all incoming claims to create and maintain the official register of all filed claims. Our proprietary system enables us to maintain an auditable trail that accounts for every claim, including any amendment, objection or other interaction between debtors and creditors. Our system also produces customized reports while utilizing data analytics to better understand case trends. Creditors can take advantage of our first-of-its kind electronic claims filing platform. 
### Plan Solicitation and Vote Tabulation
Assist clients and their advisors to implement a restructuring plan where creditors vote using a personalized ballot prepared by our team, which utilizes our proprietary electronic ballot platform. We then process and tabulate the returned ballots to prepare a voting certification that sets forth the results of each voting class.
### Claim Reconciliation
Utilize our proprietary system to efficiently analyze claims to provide an accurate picture of the amount and nature of the liabilities. We also can prepare, serve and process both substantive and procedural objections.
### Plan Distributions
Prepare for and distribute cash, securities or other consideration on behalf of our clients. Facilitate issuance of required tax documentation such as a 1099.
