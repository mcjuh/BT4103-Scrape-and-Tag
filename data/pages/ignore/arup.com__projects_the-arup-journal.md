#  The Arup Journal 
Since 1966, The Arup Journal has been our firm’s ‘journal of record’, documenting some of our most challenging projects and explaining how we approached them. Useful for research into the built environment, and valuable to anyone with an interest in design and engineering, The Arup Journal reveals the breadth, depth and quality of the work we do.
