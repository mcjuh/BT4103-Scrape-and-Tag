# Restructuring Advisory
Detailed diligence and practical solutions with firm recommendations
Kroll is a specialist provider of restructuring advisory services supporting corporate and government clients and their stakeholders both locally and internationally throughout the business cycle.
Our advisory services support clients across the entire business lifecycle, including pre-lend financial assessments, financial and cash flow forecast modeling and reviews, working capital management and optimization, options assessments, contingency planning, and accelerated sale processes.
We provide restructuring advice to government departments regarding stressed and distressed providers of public services, where failure could result in the cessation of a key public service or lead to significant regional hardship and unemployment. We serve on key government panels, maintain strong connections with major departments, and help both public entities and corporations navigate complex restructuring situations.
Working closely with our corporate finance and valuation advisory teams, we also offer transaction, debt, and capital advisory services. We deliver pragmatic advice to corporate boards at critical points in their business journey, including acquisitions, fundraising, restructuring, and exit strategies.
Drawing on our expert knowledge and situational experience, along with a hands-on approach, we deliver practical solutions for local, cross-border, and emerging market transactions. Our local and global networks give clients access to deal flow, strategic investments, and tailored options assessments.
More than advisors, we implement our recommendations to help you protect, restore, and maximize value.
