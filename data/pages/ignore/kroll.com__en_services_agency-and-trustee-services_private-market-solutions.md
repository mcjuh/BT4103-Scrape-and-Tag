# Private Market Solutions 
Kroll delivers efficient, market-leading Agency and Trustee Services for global private market firms.
Kroll delivers efficient, market-leading Agency and Trustee Services for global private equity firms. We specialize in administering private credit loans, syndicated debt, private placements, restructurings, escrow for M&A, acquisitions, bespoke continuation fund banking and other capital market solutions.
Mid-market firms are increasingly required to close deals quickly while adhering to rigorous compliance standards. As transactions grow more complex, often involving multiple lenders, customized financing structures and compressed timelines, the need for a reliable loan agent partner has become even more critical than ever.
We are committed to providing each client with personalized service, advanced technology and expertise from our professionals, no matter the size or complexity of the transaction.

### 
Administrative / Facility Agent
Kroll specializes in loan administration supporting banks, private equity firms, direct lenders and multinationals. Our Agency and Trustee Services Team utilizes best-in-class technology and personalized consultation to provide clients with unmatched conflict-free expertise across the entire loan lifecycle.
  * Finance documentation review and negotiation
  * Access to debt domain for pre and post-closing transactional needs (such as document archiving/sharing with deal parties and running consents)
  * Administration of funds flows and highly efficient processing of drawdowns and funding notices calculating amounts due under each loan via our loan system, Loan IQ
  * Payment receipt and fund distribution on each payment date via our loan system, Loan IQ
  * Management of amendments, consents and waiver processes
  * Market-leading trade settlement timelines via industry leading software, Clearpar
