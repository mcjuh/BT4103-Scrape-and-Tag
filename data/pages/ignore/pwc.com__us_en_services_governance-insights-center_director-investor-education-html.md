# Events and webcasts
Learning about and staying up to speed on the latest corporate governance trends is a continuous journey. Every board director starts from a different place and brings a unique background. Board governance training programs are essential to enhance director performance and drive impact in the boardroom.
Whether you prefer attending a webcast, reading a newsletter or joining us at a live event, we strive to provide a wide range of director education programs that address critical issues that boards, management teams, investors and audit committee members face.
##  Events
Our events provide valuable insights on a wide range of topics, including significant governance developments, leading practices, key regulatory, accounting, and auditing updates, and emerging shareholder issues. Additionally, we conduct seminars focused on industry-specific challenges and actively participate as featured speakers and sponsors at numerous events.
Corporate Directors Exchange
PwC’s Corporate Directors Exchange is our premier multi-day, interactive program bringing together Fortune 1000 public company directors to explore the latest business and governance perspectives. Participants hear from PwC leaders and distinguished industry voices while engaging in candid peer discussions and small-group dialogue with fellow directors.
The 2027 program will be offered in two locations:
**January 12–13, 2027** Key Biscayne, FL
**March 15–16, 2027** Half Moon Bay, CA
Reach out to us for more information on the program.
The Directors' Table
Our most exclusive director-led roundtable event gathers Fortune 250 directors for a unique, two-day program. Participants engage in private peer-to-peer dialogues on key governance issues, enabling them to swiftly identify critical trends, explore solutions, and develop lasting relationships.
**October 6-7, 2026** New York, NY
**January 2027** Key Biscayne, FL
**May 4-5, 2027** New York, NY
**October 5-6, 2027** New York, NY
Committee Peer Exchanges
One of our more intimate events, this one-day program, for F1000 US public company directors, is centered around small, peer group discussions that focus on the key issues committees face in fulfilling their responsibilities.
**October 5, 2026** New York, NY
**May 6, 2027** New York, NY
