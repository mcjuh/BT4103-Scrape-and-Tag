KPMG is proud to announce that we have been awarded the prestigious [IDC 2024 Customer Satisfaction Award for Enterprise Intelligence Servicesopens in a new tab](https://idcdocserv.com/2024_CSAT_Award__KPMG). We believe this recognition from IDC, a leading global provider of market intelligence, underscores KPMG firms’ unwavering commitment to delivering exceptional value and driving success for clients.
## What the Award Means
The IDC 2024 Services CSAT Award is based on direct feedback from clients through IDC's comprehensive Services Path Survey. This survey assesses customer satisfaction across more than 25 different metrics. KPMG's placement in the highest scoring group demonstrates member firms’ ability to consistently exceed client expectations in the delivery of enterprise intelligence services.
[ Read the full report opens in a new tab](https://idcdocserv.com/2024_CSAT_Award__KPMG)
## Key findings from IDC's research
  * ###### Client-centric approach
KPMG's high customer satisfaction scores reflect our dedication to understanding and addressing the unique needs of each client.
  * ###### Growing demand for Enterprise Intelligence
IDC's research indicates a strong and growing demand for enterprise intelligence services, with nearly 80% of companies planning to increase their investment in this area.
  * ###### KPMG's strengths
The award highlights KPMG firms’ strengths in providing end-to-end business and IT services, leveraging advanced automation (AI/ML), and possessing deep experience in modern application delivery – all critical factors in client success.
