## Private Equity and Venture Capital
Private equity (PE) and venture capital (VC) firms often encounter unique risks due to their diverse holdings and portfolios. While firms routinely seek assistance after an incident occurs, Kroll collaborates with our PE and VC partners to proactively understand the uniqueness of their business, culture, operations, risk tolerance and exposure across the firm and portfolio companies before incidents occur. 
We assist our partners in fully understanding the capabilities of portfolio companies or investment recipients, both during the pre-transaction period and after a transaction has occurred, by planning for standardization and devising cost-effective enterprise security risk mitigation strategies. Whether you are seeking pre-transactional due diligence on an entity’s enterprise security framework or standardizing the posture of portfolio companies for more cost-effective risk mitigation, Kroll is your fully informed partner throughout the lifecycle of your investments.
Our PE and VC services also include master planning, workplace violence and active assailant preparedness, as well as outsourced personnel and [threat management](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/enterprise-security-risk-management/threat-management-workplace-violence-active-assailant-advisory).

## Healthcare
Ensuring the safety and security of patients, staff and sensitive data is an absolute necessity. Our security consulting services are tailored to meet the unique physical and cybersecurity challenges faced by health care facilities of all sizes, compositions and geographies, providing comprehensive solutions that prioritize patient care and compliance.
Our latest report _[The State of Cyber Defense: Diagnosing Cyber Threats in Healthcare](https://www.kroll.com/en/insights/publications/cyber/state-cyber-defense-healthcare)_ , offers a holistic overview of the health care sector including insights from threat intelligence, data breach statistics, offensive security considerations and insight into the maturity of health care organizations’ cybersecurity programs.
### Why Invest in Security Consulting for Your Health Care Facility?
  * **Patient Safety:** Ensure the well-being and security of patients during their health care journey, creating a supportive and healing environment
  * **Compliance and Data Protection:** Maintain compliance with health care regulations, such as HIPAA, to protect patient data and avoid costly penalties
  * **Preserve Reputation:** A safe and secure health care facility enhances your reputation and instills confidence in patients and their families


  * **Risk Reduction:** Identify and address security vulnerabilities before they escalate into incidents, minimizing disruption to health care services
  * **Emergency Response Efficiency:** Streamline your facility's response to safety and security emergencies, ensuring the continuity of care even in the most challenging of situations


Our health care services include, but are not limited to, acute threat management, security program assessments, workplace violence and active assailant preparedness and security master planning.
