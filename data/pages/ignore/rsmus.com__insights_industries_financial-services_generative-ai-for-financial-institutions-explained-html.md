Tune in for an insightful discussion on generative AI with Microsoft's cutting-edge platform and automation suite. RSM’s Arjun Sud and Peter Brady demystify the technology and share practical generative AI use cases for financial institutions.
## Key topics include:
**Introduction to generative AI:** Understanding the basics and the potential applications in financial institutions
**Use cases:** Real-world examples of generative AI use in financial institutions, including fraud detection, customer service automation and personalized financial advice
**Benefits:** Enhanced efficiency, improved accuracy and cost savings
**Challenges:** Addressing concerns related to data privacy, ethical considerations and regulatory compliance
**Future outlook:** Predictions for the evolution of generative AI in finance and its long-term impact on the industry
