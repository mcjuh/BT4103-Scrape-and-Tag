# Advanced Analytics
Predict impact, manage costs, and make better decisions with advanced analytics. 
We help you identify opportunities for program and process improvement, predict risks and impacts, manage costs, study social challenges, and more with advanced analytics. We employ analysts and data scientists with expertise in several focus areas, including public and commercial health, global development, education, and human services. We use a variety of technologies to collect and analyze data, including remote sensor and satellite data collection, to help you understand the bigger picture. 
## Get deeper insights from your data.
We analyze your data to develop reports, analyses, and models that help you make decisions that have a greater positive impact.
[CONNECT WITH A DATA EXPERT](https://www.mathematica.org/solutions/advanced-analytics#landing_contactusform)
## Capabilities
**Machine learning:** Make the shift toward more automated processes with supervised and unsupervised models. 
**Predictive Analytics:** Understand the impacts of program and policy decisions before you implement them. 
**Spatial analysis:** Understand and visualize geographic relationships with siloed data by layering it over maps to see patterns more clearly. 
**Bayesian methods:** Better understand potential outcomes by integrating data from other studies with your own. [Learn more](https://www.mathematica.org/solutions/Bayesian-Methods)
**Factor analysis:** Uncover patterns in large data sets and identify opportunities and areas for improvement. 
**Network analysis:** Work toward solving complex social problems by understanding the broader system in which they exist, using network analysis to connect data points across systems. 
**Natural language processing:** Automate text review to speed up survey and evaluation processes or uncover unobserved themes from documents to guide your strategy.
