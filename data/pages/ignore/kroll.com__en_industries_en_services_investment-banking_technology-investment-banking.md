# Technology Investment Banking
Technology expertise for middle-market M&A transactions
Featured Transactions
Our Technology M&A Advisory practice Investment Banking practice has extensive experience advising public corporations, financial sponsors, privately-owned businesses and other middle-market companies on buy-side and sell-side M&A transactions, and capital raising services for clients worldwide.
Our strength stems from our deep domain expertise across key sub-sectors of technology, our extensive transaction experience, our tenured relationships with strategic buyers and private equity firms, and our global reach. The team is focused on working with middle-market technology clients on M&A and capital raises, leveraging Kroll’s global strategic advisory capabilities delivered.
