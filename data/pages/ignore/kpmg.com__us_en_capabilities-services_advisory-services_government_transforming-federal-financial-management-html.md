# Transforming Federal financial management
Through automation and transformation, Federal agencies and military service branches can proactively drive value, innovation, and mission performance.
##  A new lens on financial transformation 
There is a significant opportunity for Federal agencies to overhaul their existing financial management systems and functions. By integrating strategic planning, automation, and data analytics, agencies and military branches can drive innovation, accelerate informed decision-making, and enhance mission execution.
### Simplify financial transformation
KPMG knows what it takes to transform your Federal agencies and military service branches into the next generation of financial management.
Whether you need tech-enabled financial transformation, audit management and readiness capabilities, or risk management tactics, we work with you to deliver custom tools and methodologies, develop innovative strategies, leverage automation, and connect all systems and data points for greater insights. Our focus is to help agencies strategize and implement a transformation roadmap to make better decisions, meet regulatory demands, monitor performance, and promote accountability – all while being effective stewards of Federal funds.
KPMG can help your agency transform its finance operations in the following areas: 
Functional financial management
Build the next generation of finance management functions and system modules to maximize spend value and streamline financial planning and management.
Re-engineering business processes
Implement a standardized and technology-enabled solution to assist your agency with an end-to-end business process transformation tool.
Integrate Enterprise Governance, Risk, and Compliance (eGRC)
Apply a trusted roadmap to implement eGRC and audit management capabilities, effectively monitor risks, proactively address internal control deficiencies, and provide insights to efficiently manage internal and external audits.
Technology risk management
Modernize your approach to risk identification, assessment, and management into an integrated, risk-aware strategy that bolsters operational resilience.
