## Section 508 and VPAT Information
Making Products and Services Accessible - Voluntary Product Accessibility Templates
(Section 508 and Section 255 VPATS)
The Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT) is a widely used document format intended to permit contracting officials, customers and users to easily assess the accessibility of BDO’s products (if any) and services against the standards set forth in Section 508 of the United States Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as amended (29 U.S.C. § 794d), as well as the guidelines set forth in Section 255 of the Communications Act of 1934, as amended by the Twenty-First Century Communications and Video Accessibility Act of 2010 (47 U.S.C. § 255).
If you have any questions about accessibility relating to BDO products and services, or if you are unable to find a VPAT for the product or service of interest, please contact BDOPS-VPAT@bdo.com.
