## RSM and NetSuite address the most common pain points for law firms
RSM’s NetSuite legal industry team combines cross-functional experience in legal services with technological innovations and AI capabilities that deepen client relationships, giving your firm a competitive edge. We use AI for advanced process automation and intelligent operational support throughout the project life cycle to help implement a dedicated NetSuite ERP solution for law firms that can address the critical challenges legal practices face today.
Limited functionality 
Law firms commonly face limited accounting software choices, often QuickBooks solutions that can be outgrown or inflexible legal-specific options. NetSuite offers a scalable platform that keeps you at the forefront of advancements, including AI-enabled tools.
Multiple legal entity support 
Many firms are expanding and may operate multiple legal entities, which most popular law firm software options don't support. NetSuite has strong support for managing multiple legal entities and accessing consolidated reporting.
Lack of Filevine integration 
Many firms using Filevine seek more advanced and robust accounting options. NetSuite and RSM’s connector seamlessly integrate the two systems. Cases and expenses from Filevine sync with NetSuite, while NetSuite financials are accessible within Filevine.
Inefficient trust accounting 
Managing trust balances is essential. NetSuite's built-in trust account management streamlines reconciliation, reporting and operations. RSM configures your system to fit your firm's specific needs.
Ineffective reporting and business insights 
Law firms often struggle to obtain clear metrics on case profitability, office performance and financial breakdowns by case type or attorney. NetSuite's flexible reporting structure and RSM’s understanding of your specific needs give you the visibility to answer those questions on demand.
