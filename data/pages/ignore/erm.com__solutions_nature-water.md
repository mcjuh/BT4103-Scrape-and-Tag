# Nature & Water
ERM works with organizations to help them factor nature and water into their strategies and transition to a nature positive future. We partner with clients to turn financial data and scientific insights into meaningful and rapid action.
## Nature depletion threatens business continuity
The majority of the world’s GDP is dependent on nature and its services. It is critical for organizations who rely on nature and water in their value chains to have a clear understanding of their related risks and opportunities. 
Water scarcity, quality and control are becoming significant challenges due to climate change and rising demand. Rapid and meaningful action is needed to ensure business continuity. 
Valuing nature and water as assets rather than resources is crucial to business resiliency. Proactively addressing these risks can prevent costly delays to capital projects, strengthen supply chains and support local communities. 
To become ‘nature positive’, organizations need access to accurate data and integrated solutions. This helps to simplify disclosure, drive transformation and maximize value creation.
