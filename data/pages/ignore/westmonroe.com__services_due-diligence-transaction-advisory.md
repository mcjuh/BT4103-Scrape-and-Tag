## Hold Period Health Check
Looking to boost value before an exit? Our Hold Period Health Check identifies the most impactful opportunities to enhance EBITDA, strengthen technology and operations, and reduce risk. ## Comprehensive, Industry Expert Diligence
Our diligence approach is built on over 20 years of advising on thousands of transactions, giving us a unique database of benchmarks across spend, headcount, and other operational metrics. Organized by specialty, we dive deep into the specifics of your target’s industry to deliver fast, highly nuanced assessments. Our diligence framework balances risk identification with value creation to provide a comprehensive perspective:
  * **Technology:** We evaluate infrastructure, scalability, risks, and necessary investments. Our proprietary tools highlight where future-proofing is critical to support growth.
  * **Cybersecurity:** We identify vulnerabilities, ensure regulatory compliance, and safeguard against post-close risks.
  * **Operational:** We assess operational efficiency, scalability, and alignment with growth plans while uncovering potential cost-saving opportunities.
  * **Commercial:** We analyze market positioning, customer retention, revenue potential, and competitive differentiation.
  * **Compliance:** We ensure alignment with specific industry regulations and identify potential liabilities that could derail success.
  * **People/Org:** We evaluate the organizational model, leadership alignment, and talent retention risks, ensuring cultural fit and operational readiness.

## Frequently Asked Questions
  * What is M&A due diligence?-
M&A due diligence is the process of evaluating a target company before a transaction closes to identify risks, uncover opportunities, and confirm whether the deal supports the buyer's investment thesis. It typically assesses operations, technology, cybersecurity, compliance, commercial performance, and people so decision-makers can move forward with greater confidence.
