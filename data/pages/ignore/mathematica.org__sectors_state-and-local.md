# State and Local
## Data, analytics, and partnerships that improve programs and services
[Discuss your goals](https://www.mathematica.org/sectors/state-and-local#landing_contactusform)
  * [Related Staff](https://www.mathematica.org/sectors/state-and-local#landing_staff)


Mathematica partners with state and local leaders to improve programs and modernize systems where decisions are high-stakes and timelines are tight. We help agencies move from data to action by bringing together data science, social science, and technology. 
Working alongside our partners, we strengthen data foundations, apply analytics and AI aligned with state priorities, and translate insight into practical solutions. Our approach is grounded in the realities of legacy systems and operational constraints, helping agencies focus on what will work now and scale over time. 
As a trusted partner, we support states in improving day-to-day operations, identifying data issues earlier, and making informed decisions about where to scale, adapt, or redesign programs. This work helps agencies improve outcomes, strengthen accountability, and deliver better services to the people they serve.
