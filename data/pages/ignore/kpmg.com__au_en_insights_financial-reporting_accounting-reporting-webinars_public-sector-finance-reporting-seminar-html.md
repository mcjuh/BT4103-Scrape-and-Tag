## 2026 virtual seminar
KPMG's Finance Advisory team ran their 2026 annual public sector finance reporting seminar on 31 March 2026. 
The seminar is designed to help prepare public sector finance professionals for annual financial reporting, helping ensure they are on track for implementing new reporting standards applicable in future years and to provide insights into the future direction of finance and reporting in the public sector.
The 2026 seminar covered the following topics:
  * financial reporting updates for the 30 June 2026 reporting season including new and amended Australian Accounting Standards and IFRIC agenda decisions
  * key accounting issues, including uncertainties, leases and public‑private arrangements
  * ESG and climate‑related reporting developments affecting government 
  * observations from the first year of public sector sustainability reporting
  * the role of artificial intelligence in finance, including use cases and governance considerations.
