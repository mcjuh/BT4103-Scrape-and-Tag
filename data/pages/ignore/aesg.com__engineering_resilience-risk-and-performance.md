## Performance-Based Design for Seismic, Wind, Fire & Robustness
Our team delivers performance-based and risk-informed structural solutions to ensure buildings withstand natural hazards, fire, and other extreme events. AESG enhances resilience, reduces risk, and ensures long-term structural integrity across all projects.
## Our Services
## Seismic & Wind Engineering
Designing structures to withstand seismic and wind forces, ensuring safety, stability, and long-term performance.
### Ensures structural safety under extreme loads
### Complies with international codes and standards
### Reduces risk of structural failure
### Enhances resilience and occupant confidence
## Progressive Collapse & Robustness Assessments
Evaluating structural systems for robustness and resilience against unexpected loads or failures to prevent disproportionate collapse.
### Identifies vulnerabilities in structural systems
### Supports design of resilient and safe buildings
### Minimises risk of catastrophic failure
### Improves long-term durability and reliability
## Structural Fire Engineering Coordination
Integrating fire performance into structural design to ensure safety, compliance, and minimal structural damage during fire events.
### Enhances occupant safety in fire scenarios
### Ensures compliance with fire codes and standards
### Minimises structural damage and post-fire costs
### Supports integrated fire and structural design solutions
## Performance-Based Structural Design
Developing designs based on targeted performance criteria, balancing safety, functionality, and cost-efficiency for complex structures.
### Delivers tailored, performance-driven solutions
### Optimises materials, efficiency, and cost
### Ensures structures meet specific functional requirements
### Reduces risk through evidence-based design decisions
## Risk Mitigation & Resilience Strategies
Providing expert guidance to identify, assess, and mitigate structural risks, enhancing resilience and long-term asset performance.
### Reduces operational and structural risks
### Supports strategic decision-making for asset management
### Enhances long-term durability and reliability
### Improves overall safety and performance of structures
