###  [ Regulatory and Business Change  ](https://www.fticonsulting.com/uk/industries/financial-services/governance-regulatory-risk-and-compliance)
Across the financial services sector, the role of compliance functions continues to expand and evolve whilst...
Across the financial services sector, the role of compliance functions continues to expand and evolve whilst needing to stay apace with, and support, new agile ways of working.
We understand how critical effective risk and compliance frameworks are in supporting a firm’s ability to operate, protect reputation and strengthen business relationships.
Aided by the onset of technological innovation within the risk and compliance space, our implementation strategies combine technology solutions to support process efficiency and advanced analytics, which strive to support a more proactive and client-centric approach to managing compliance risks.
  * **Regulatory Change.** In the face of an increasingly demanding and interventionist regulatory environment (both domestically and internationally), it can be a challenge for firms to consistently monitor regulatory change and ensure that their compliance frameworks remain effective and fit for purpose. We recognise there is no single “right way” to “do” compliance and the implications of non-compliance are business critical with the potential for far-reaching consequences. We apply a holistic lens to minimise regulatory risk and provide practical support to clients in establishing compliance frameworks that meet required standards.
  * **Business Change.** As businesses move to expand their offerings, be it through new business lines or products, entering new geographies or through strategic mergers and acquisitions, it is vital to fully assess the impact of new or different regulatory requirements and implement policies and procedures to remain compliant. There may also be a need to apply for and obtain new regulatory licences, which can be a complex and time-consuming process. Our team supports clients in assessing the impacts of change and successfully implementing the required changes or updates to risk and compliance frameworks.
