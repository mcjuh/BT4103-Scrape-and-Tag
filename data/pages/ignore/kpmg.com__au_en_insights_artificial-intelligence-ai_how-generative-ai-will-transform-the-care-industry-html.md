## Care organisations face the challenge of balancing escalating demands for care with finite resources?
Governments and care organisations worldwide are striving to reconcile the disparity between the growing need for health care services against limitations imposed by economic constraints, rising expenses, and workforce shortages.
While the transformative power of data and technology in reshaping care has long been advocated, progress has not kept pace. Enter Generative AI (Gen AI) – an emerging powerhouse with enormous potential for transformation in the care industry.
_Care Reimagined 2025_ explores what is currently achievable for the care industry with established AI technologies, and provides insight into the nascent applications of Gen AI.

## AI focus areas for care organisations
  * ###### Care resources under stress
    * Staffing shortages on the rise
    * Infrastructure facing increasing presssure
    * Intensified financial pressure
  * ###### AI and machine learning
    * Health and wellbeing focus
    * Data utilisation
    * Seamless care network
    * Personalised care
  * ###### Generative AI opportunities
    * Patient/client experience
    * Population health
    * Operational efficiency
    * Clinical experience
    * Equity
  * ###### Working differently with AI
    * Appropriate care supported digitally
    * Integrated and collaborative transformation of care processes
    * Governance and leadership
