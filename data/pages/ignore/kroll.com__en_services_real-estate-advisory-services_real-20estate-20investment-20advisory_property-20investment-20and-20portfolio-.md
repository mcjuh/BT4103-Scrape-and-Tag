# Property Investment and Portfolio Analysis
Kroll Real Estate Advisory Group provides Property Investment and Portfolio Analysis Services that involve the analysis of the various components of property investment as well as the identification of possible scenarios for the positioning of an asset.
The primary objectives are the identification of potential utilization and the determination of the highest and best use for the subject property. Kroll offers a full range of consultancy services for single assets, portfolios and development projects, including:
  * Investment analysis
  * Portfolio analysis
  * Pre-feasibility studies
  * Feasibility studies
  * Rezoning assistance
  * Market and sector studies
  * Analysis of investment returns (sensitivity and SWOT analysis)
