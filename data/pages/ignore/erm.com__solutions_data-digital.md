# Data & Digital
ERM designs and delivers the data and digital solutions that clients need to navigate the evolving sustainability landscape. Our digital tools and transformations help organizations reduce reputational risk and boost operational performance, establishing a unified source of sustainability data.
## Maximize the value of data and digital
Digital technologies and data insights enable organizations to optimize their operations and improve sustainability outcomes. Identifying the right solutions and vendors can be time-consuming and costly. 
The volume, variety and velocity of sustainability data requires highly scalable digital tools that can be adapted for different industries and geographies as well as changing goals and regulations. 
AI is increasingly being used as part of digital solutions. Applying governance controls and integrating responsible AI principles helps to encourage sustainable use, avoid AI bias and align with sustainability goals. 
People and process are just as important as technology for a successful digital transformation. This often requires new mindsets, workflows and targets.
