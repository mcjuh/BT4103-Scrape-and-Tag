# Extending a Leading Banking ERP Solution to Deepen Customer Engagement
Article 3 min read
  * PRINT 
  * 

Many financial institutions rely on a leading banking ERP solution to manage transactional data and core operations. While this platform is effective for operational tasks, today’s financial landscape demands more. With customer expectations evolving, financial institutions must prioritize deeper insights and relationship management to remain competitive.
Although some banking ERP systems include a basic CRM option, it may not provide the depth needed to foster strong customer relationships or build loyalty. Instead of replacing these systems, integrating Microsoft Dynamics 365 can significantly elevate and extend existing capabilities. This approach preserves system reliability while opening opportunities to enhance customer experiences, streamline operations, and support data-driven marketing and loan management strategies.
## Understanding Process Alignment Before Integration
Successfully integrating technology like Microsoft Dynamics 365 requires more than deploying software across departments. A careful [review of existing processes](https://www.bdo.com/insights/digital/avoid-platform-chaos-by-streamlining-modernization-with-a-business-process-catalog) , along with documentation and training, it’s essential to help ensure effective use of new tools across the organization.
For example, one institution attempted to enhance CRM functions within its banking ERP solution but faced setbacks due to inadequate planning. The challenges included:
  * **Data Modeling Gaps:** Resulted in scattered customer data and duplicate records.
  * **Adoption Challenges:** Led to inefficiencies and decreased team morale.
  * **Planning Shortfalls:** Created unexpected costs with limited CRM functionality.

## CRM Remodeling with Microsoft Dynamics
For institutions looking to modernize without abandoning trusted ERP solutions, remodeling a CRM integration with Microsoft Dynamics 365 can be highly effective. This extension enhances the ERP’s core functionality, enabling institutions to modernize customer engagement, marketing, and operations.
## Unified Data for Smarter Decisions
By connecting transactional data from a banking ERP solution with Microsoft Dataverse, institutions can create a unified view of customer profiles. 
This 360-degree view of customers allows teams across departments to:
  * **Data-driven decision-making** across departments.
  * **Improved customer service** by reducing data silos.
  * **New growth opportunities** based on customer insights.


## Personalization Deepens Customer Engagement
A comprehensive view of each customer allows institutions to move beyond generic messaging and offer experiences that resonate with individual needs. Predictive analytics enables:
Based on deep data and predictive analytics, it’s possible to:
  * **Targeted marketing campaigns** that align with customer behavior.
  * **Customized financial products** based on individual histories.
  * **Enhanced loyalty** by addressing specific financial goals.


## Automated Processes for Operational Efficiency
Integrating Microsoft Dynamics 365 makes it easier to streamline and automate manual tasks and improves cross-departmental productivity and communication. With tools like Power Automate, routine processes like loan processing, compliance tasks, and customer service workflows become faster and more accurate.
## Strategic ERP Integration Considerations
Successful platform modernization requires careful planning. Financial leaders should consider these key factors:
  1. **Define Strategic Objectives:** Establish clear goals for integration to gain alignment and focus.
  2. **Identify Priorities:** Start with high-impact functions, rolling out integration in phases.
  3. **Set KPIs:** Metrics such as customer satisfaction and operational efficiency help track progress. 
  4. **Help Ensure Data Readiness:** Early data assessment and cleansing are essential for accurate customer profiles.
  5. **Choose the Right Partner:** Aligning tech with business processes is crucial. BDO Digital’s experience helps to ensure a smooth integration process.
