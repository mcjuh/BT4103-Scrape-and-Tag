# Performance Management
Track program progress and optimize resources with expert support in performance management. 
Grant program administrators require modern data management systems, analytics, and the right performance measures to make real-time decisions that drive impact. Our policy, data analytics, and technology experts work together to develop customized data management systems that help funders and thousands of grantees collaborate to measure and improve performance. 
We work with foundations and agencies to tackle common yet challenging issues, starting with developing a robust set of performance measures to effectively measure impact. Next, we work collaboratively to understand how the data for those measures will be captured by grantees and program providers and develop systems to collect and share data across diverse platforms. Our expertise continues with our data scientists who help develop interactive data visualizations to assist in analyzing performance data, which can inform and direct technical assistance and training. With flexible performance management systems, agencies and foundations can use data to develop strategies for continuous improvement and deploy scarce resources for maximum benefit.

## Our performance-management experts work with program administrators and stakeholders to: 
Operationalize program strategy, including developing theories of change and logic models to identify the performance drivers that lead to the desired outcomes. 
Develop an integrated set of performance measures to assess the strategy and inform data-driven decision making for program management and improvement. 
Perform a needs assessment to identify the data, data quality, and information system needs and provide options for addressing gaps. 
Analyze performance measures and associated data using descriptive, econometric, and statistical techniques to support program improvement. 
Develop information systems to automate performance measure calculation and provide results in understandable formats and data visualizations. 
Provide technical assistance to third-party system developers to outline business requirements, specify data elements, and test software. 
Maintain, update, and improve program strategy, operations, and the performance management system using data and the best available research to identify potential changes. 
Provide consulting and analysis services to define performance measures and collaborate to address challenges.
