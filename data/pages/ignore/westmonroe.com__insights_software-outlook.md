## Software trends in 2026
  * ### Software enters a new battle for IT budget share
Cloud-only is no longer enough. Software providers are moving to hybrid models that blend cloud, edge, and on-device AI to cut latency, control costs, meet compliance needs, and support emerging agentic architectures.
  * ### AI redefines software creation
From planning through deployment, AI is transforming the SDLC, boosting engineering productivity and velocity for teams that pair AI-first workflows with strong governance and clear metrics.
  * ### AI success comes from better data, not bigger models
With access to similar foundation models, software companies are differentiating through proprietary, AI-ready data using modern platforms, governance, and human oversight to turn trust into a sustainable moat.
  * ### Disruption risk rewrites the M&A playbook
Investors are valuing software targets on the distinctiveness of their AI capabilities, data assets, and vertical expertise, driving consolidation toward platforms with clear, defensible AI advantages.
  * ### AI forces a rethink of labor-heavy delivery models
AI-augmented services are upending traditional, labor-based outsourcing as buyers and providers renegotiate contracts around outcomes, automation, and managed services built on smarter AI, not cheaper headcount.
