# KPMG and Ivalua
Transform your procurement organization to be digitally driven and efficient
KPMG Powered Enterprise | Procurement enabled by Ivalua is an outcome-driven business transformation solution that combines deep procurement knowledge, well-established delivery capability and leading technologies such as AI to drive sustainable change, rising performance and lasting value.
The Powered Procurement solution provides an out of the box operating model for Source-to-Pay that helps clients transform their S2P process – accelerating delivery and enabling clients to maximize the value of their AI investment.
A pre-configured cloud solution, embedded with years of KPMG leading practice and enhanced by AI, Powered Procurement can help you to quickly transform and derive value from your move to the cloud.
To learn more, visit [Ivalua](https://www.ivalua.com/ "Ivalua").
