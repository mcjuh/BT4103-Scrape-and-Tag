# Real Estate Debt Valuation Services
Kroll provides independent real estate debt valuation services, supporting investors, lenders and asset managers in an evolving credit environment.
Our experts deliver thorough analysis of commercial real estate (CRE) debt instruments, including mortgages, mezzanine loans, construction financing and distressed or non-performing portfolios.
We combine deep real estate expertise with advanced valuation methodologies to assess loan-level and portfolio-level risk, incorporating market dynamics, property fundamentals, borrower credit profiles and capital structure considerations. Our valuations are grounded in discounted cash flow analysis, comparable market data and scenario-based stress testing to reflect current and forward-looking conditions.
Kroll supports a wide range of use cases, including financial reporting, transaction support, risk management, regulatory compliance and strategic decision-making. We work closely with private equity firms, debt funds, banks, insurance companies and special servicers to provide transparent, audit-ready valuations designed to withstand scrutiny.
