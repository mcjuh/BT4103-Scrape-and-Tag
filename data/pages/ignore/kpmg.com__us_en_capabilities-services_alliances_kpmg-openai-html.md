## KPMG and OpenAI
Building the AI-First Enterprise 
KPMG and OpenAI have formed a strategic alliance, with KPMG being named an OpenAI Elite Partner—the highest tier in the OpenAI Partner Network.
KPMG LLP and OpenAI are enabling AI-native experiences that transform how organizations interact with enterprise data, workflows, and systems. Our collaboration is designed to help organizations move toward a new enterprise operating model—one where AI serves as an intuitive system of engagement, while existing enterprise applications safely continue to serve as the system of record.
> As an OpenAI Elite Partner, KPMG brings deep transformation, industry and implementation experience that can help organizations deploy OpenAI's technology in new and impactful ways. Together, we’re applying OpenAI’s models directly within enterprise systems and public-sector platforms so organizations can use AI where work actually happens.
Colleen Kapase 
Vice President of Strategic Global Partnerships and Ecosystems at OpenAI 
**Reinventing Enterprise Software Through AI-First Experiences**
Traditional, application-centric software is shifting toward more integrated, agent-led platforms. We help organizations explore this transition by connecting data, complex workflows, and actions through a single conversational interface and intelligent agents. As a powerful proof point of our capabilities, OpenAI selected KPMG to design and deploy an AI-native Supply Chain & Fulfillment Orchestration platform that unifies supply chain operations, fulfillment, and finance through intelligent workflows and agents. By adopting these next-generation, headless SaaS experiences, your teams can streamline how they access information, make data-driven decisions, and execute daily work.
**Accelerating State and Local Government Modernization**
To help state and local government agencies modernize, we integrate advanced AI directly into [KRIS Connected, the KPMG suite of SaaS solutions](https://kpmg.com/us/en/capabilities-services/government-public-sector/kpmg-resource-integration-suite.html). Transitioning from legacy, siloed environments to connected case management, service delivery, and decision support is designed to empower public-sector teams to elevate critical workflows. 
**Strengthening Cyber Defense Through Trusted AI**
Building on our firm's participation in [the OpenAI Daybreak Cyber Partner Program](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/we-are-working-closely-with-openai-as-a-design-ugcPost-7474903062462214144-2fO1/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAAmj9cEBBISmxEaq3qfPL0g0dcKwczgQghU), we are co-developing next-generation, AI-powered cybersecurity solutions. This effort combines OpenAI’s innovative capabilities with the firm’s deep cyber experience and Trusted AI framework to help your business build resilience in a complex threat landscape.
