#  Integration & Separation 
From early value identification through due diligence, to deal execution and realization, KPMG’s strategy team drives focus, clarity, and momentum across your journey.


## Helping you secure lasting value
Our integration and separation (I&S) professionals work closely with you helping you address critical areas within the deal process, such as:
  * ###### Supporting pre-deal due diligence for an early view of I&S approaches, key risks, synergy opportunities, and deal value drivers.
  * ###### Conducting synergy assessments with assistance from evaluation to sustained value capture.
  * ###### Protecting business continuity and reducing disruption during the transition phase.
  * ###### Planning for I&S readiness, to attain operational integrity on Day 1 and beyond.
  * ###### Establishing a structured deal management process and providing necessary tools, while aligning with I&S strategy and principles.
  * ###### Creating a program strategy and governance process to achieve integration and synergy targets.


## Delivering support
### Lifecycle
  1. Pre-sign 
  2. Sign-to-close 
  3. Post-close 


During the pre-sign phase, the emphasis is on due diligence to confirm initial findings, establish a foundation for execution, and negotiate / sign the deal. Potential support may include:
  * Due diligence support
  * Standalone cost and ‘red flag’ assessment
  * Identification of strategic intent and key value drivers (e.g., preliminary / top-down synergies)
  * Development of execution approach and preliminary timeline (e.g., execution readiness framework and guiding principles)
  * Identification of preliminary operational risks and issues
  * Transitional service agreement (TSA) support
  * Integration / separation program governance and management.


As the deal phase progresses, the focus shifts to preparing and completing execution planning in readiness for deal close, including:
  * Launch of execution program with key workstream leads (e.g., program kickoff and workstream charters)
  * Entanglement identification and log
  * Current state, Day 1 / interim state, and target operating model
  * Bottom-up synergy assessment
  * Organizational design
  * Pre-close, Day 1 readiness, and post-close plans
  * Synergy opportunity list and dashboard
  * TSA considerations and governance
  * Change management and communications planning
  * Execution roadmap and deal scorecard
  * Preparation for Day 1 cutover
  * Functional conversations and deal support.


During this phase, we can help you realize deal value, by developing an execution plan aligned with the deal thesis and driving towards the targeted outcome. We work with you from Day 0 to Day 100, tackling key issues, reducing surprises, identifying synergies throughout the process, and stabilizing operations. Support may include:
  * Day 1 and post-close execution
  * Post-close hyper-care support
  * TSA administration
  * Functional / workstream support
  * Execution on post-close task plans
  * Capturing and tracking synergies
  * Capturing and tracking stranded cost
  * Post-close optimization identification and implementation.

### Our value-driven approach and delivery
Through a structured, professional approach, we can help you deliver long-term value and reduce risk, while maintaining a continuous process throughout the transaction lifecycle. Through effective operational planning and execution, KPMG firms leverage our I&S Advisory network to achieve a successful delivery. This requires coordination across various aspects of the deal.
  * ###### A trusted advisor and known leader
We bring relevant experience and guidance to execute on what is most important – while maintaining focus on the people, processes, data, tech solutions, and ways of working that are designed to deliver outcomes that matter and can be measured. 
  * ###### An integrated team working at deal speed
Our integrated, cross-functional approach helps drive positive change, powered by our tools and capabilities to plan, execute, and sustain the outcomes sought by you and your stakeholders. And, by working closely across our global network, KPMG firms can help implement cross-border transactions and provide practical, hands-on delivery. 
  * ###### A holistic approach to integration planning and execution
We recognize that acquisitions and divestitures are complex and focus on preserving and delivering value through the completion of a transaction and beyond. Our framework considers operational insights, the imperative of control, and evaluation of culture and personnel, taking into account deal-specific nuances such as joint ventures, carve-outs, etc. 
  * ###### Next-level, global insights using advanced analytics and data science 
KPMG firms have invested in technology, data, and analytics on a global scale to serve clients in all sectors. Our library of proprietary and external data sets is designed to bring access to real-time and high-frequency data, enabling us to uncover the key indicators of potential value and insights.
  * ###### Execution power
Our teams have extensive I&S experience and takes a holistic view of execution power. We tackle required changes to the business and operating models, while retaining focus on the one-time and recurring financial impact during and after the transaction – while understanding the deep cultural and change aspects of the transaction.
