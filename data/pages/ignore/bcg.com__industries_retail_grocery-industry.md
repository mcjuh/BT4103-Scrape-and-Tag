# Grocery Industry
The grocery industry is evolving as customer expectations change, competition intensifies, and AI creates new opportunities for growth. From merchandising and store operations to supply chain and private label, BCG helps grocery leaders create value where it matters most.
Food retailers make thousands of daily decisions that shape customer demand, product availability, and profitability. Leading grocery businesses are using AI to make those decisions faster and at greater scale across merchandising, pricing, operations, and supply chain.BCG partners with leaders across the grocery industry to turn those decisions into an opportunity—combining deep sector expertise with hands-on experience to drive tangible business outcomes, improve performance, and accelerate transformations.

Our dedicated grocery retail consulting team helps clients throughout the industry drive impact in six key areas:
Expand All 
AI and Technology
BCG helps grocery leaders apply AI across both growth and productivity initiatives, from merchandising, pricing, promotions, and [personalization](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/marketing-sales/personalization) to labor planning, store operations, and supply-demand forecasting. We work with clients to embed AI into everyday decision-making, helping organizations improve performance and build the capabilities needed to scale AI across the enterprise.
Merchandising
Through our grocery retail consulting work, we help clients optimize assortment, space, category strategy, and product performance, using advanced analytics and deep commercial expertise. By improving category economics, simplifying complexity, and aligning assortments with customer demand, we work with merchandising organizations to strengthen sales performance, improve margins, and create more compelling assortments for customers.
Pricing and Promotions
BCG’s grocery consulting teams develop [pricing](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/pricing-revenue-management/overview) and promotion strategies that improve competitiveness, strengthen customer loyalty, and maximize return on investment. Combining market insights, AI-enabled decision making, and deep pricing expertise, we help grocery leaders optimize pricing architecture, promotional effectiveness, and investment across categories.
Store Operations and Supply Chain
We improve labor productivity, product availability, inventory performance, and [supply chain](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/supply-chain-management) efficiency through process redesign, digital tools, and AI-enabled decision making. From workforce planning and forecasting to inventory management and replenishment, we help clients improve store and supply chain performance, reduce complexity, and build more resilient operations.
Own Brands and Private Label
From portfolio architecture and sourcing to positioning and customer experience, our grocery consulting teams develop end-to-end private label strategies for clients. We work with grocery retailers to increase penetration, strengthen quality perception, and create distinctive offerings that support both growth and margin objectives.
Cost Optimization
BCG partners with grocery leaders to identify and capture structural cost opportunities across [procurement](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/procurement), operations, and support functions. Our approach combines the latest AI tools, rigorous cost diagnostics, and hands-on execution support to reduce costs while maintaining customer value, operational performance, and long-term competitiveness.
## Our Clients’ Impact in the Grocery Industry
~20%
reduction in total loss
A grocery wholesaler and retailer partnered with BCG to design a total loss reduction program across its stores and distribution centers. The initiative reduced total loss by approximately 20% and delivered ~$20 million in annual run-rate savings after full rollout.
~3%
cost savings
A regional grocery retailer partnered with BCG to modernize merchandising analytics, improve pricing and promotion effectiveness, and transform supplier negotiations. Within six months, the company achieved approximately 3% cost savings in fresh categories while establishing new capabilities to support sustained margin improvement.
3 to 4 pts
of profit improvement via enhanced category management
A regional food retailer worked with BCG to redesign its category planning approach—including category roles, assortment design, supplier funding, and promo architecture. The effort generated 3–4 points of profit improvement while strengthening category performance and commercial decision-making across the merchandising function.
