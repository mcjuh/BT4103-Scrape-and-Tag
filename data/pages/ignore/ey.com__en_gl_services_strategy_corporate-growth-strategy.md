## The Growth imperative:
CEOs are not waiting for stability – uncertainty is the new normal, and executives are rethinking their approach to transformation in today’s [nonlinear, accelerated, volatile and interconnected (NAVI)](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/megatrends) world. According to [EY-Parthenon’s CEO Survey](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/ceo/ceo-outlook-global-report), growth is top of mind for leaders, driven by both organic and inorganic growth, as well as AI and transformation.

Today, leaders face mounting pressure to reimagine their business models and create value as disruption accelerates along macro and micro dimensions including:

## What EY-Parthenon can do for you
EY-Parthenon teams can help business leaders answer the following questions:
  * What initiatives or investments can generate the highest ROI and value?
  * How can we scale successfully while managing risk and uncertainty?
  * How can you help me transform our business for growth and efficiency?
  * How do I use our data and market data to uncover growth big bets quickly?
  * How can I envision disruptive futures and define bold bets that we must take? 


Our clients are under sustained pressure to deliver enduring growth despite headwinds through technology turbulence, shifting customer expectations and new entrants. They need to find new sources of growth and decide which to pursue as well as when and how to build them.
Whether uncovering new growth opportunities, reimagining business models or building ventures from concept to scale, our integrated solutions exist to create net-new growth for clients amid disruption, not just improve the core business.

## Corporate and Growth Strategy Consulting: Frequently Asked Questions
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  What is corporate and growth strategy?
Corporate and growth strategy defines how organizations create long-term value by identifying, prioritizing and executing growth opportunities. It includes business model reinvention, market expansion, M&A and [venture creation](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/corporate-venture-building), often powered by data and AI.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  How can companies achieve sustainable growth in today’s volatile environment?
Companies achieve sustainable growth by combining organic and inorganic strategies, [reimagining business models](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/disruptive-strategy-consulting), and using AI and data to identify high-value opportunities while balancing speed, risk and long-term value creation.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  What are the key drivers of business growth today?
Growth is driven by [AI and data](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/neurosymbolic-ai), changing customer expectations, new market opportunities, ecosystem partnerships and business model innovation, alongside external forces such as geopolitics, regulation and competitive disruption.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  How does EY-Parthenon support corporate and growth strategy?
EY-Parthenon helps organizations identify, orchestrate and realize growth by combining AI-powered insights, business model reinvention and full-cycle venture creation, connecting strategy with execution to deliver measurable outcomes.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  What makes EY-Parthenon’s growth strategy approach different?
EY-Parthenon combines corporate strategy, AI-powered insight and venture building to help organizations not just plan for growth but build and scale new growth engines. The focus is on unlocking long-term value creation and turning bold strategic ambition into measurable results.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  How do companies prioritize growth investments and initiatives?
Companies prioritize growth by evaluating ROI, strategic fit, risk and scalability, using data and scenario analysis to focus on the [highest-value opportunities](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/commercial-strategy-consulting) while balancing short- and long-term performance.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  How is AI used in growth strategy?
AI is used in growth strategy to analyze market and customer data, identify emerging opportunities, and prioritize high-impact initiatives. It enables faster decision-making, better forecasting, and more [scalable execution of growth strategies](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/neurosymbolic-ai).
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  What is business model reinvention in strategy consulting?
Business model reinvention in [strategy consulting](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy) involves redefining how a company creates and captures value. This can include portfolio optimization, new revenue models, market expansion and operational transformation to unlock new sources of growth.
