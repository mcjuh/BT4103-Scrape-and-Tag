Use cases 
##  Business priorities 
Production and reservoir optimization
Declining field productivity, rising drilling costs and aging assets are eroding margins. Most operators have ambition but lack the data infrastructure to act at scale. Use AI to optimize reservoir analysis, well placement and production performance—and recover more from every well.
Oil and gas supply chain resilience
Oil and gas supply chain resilience are among the most complex in the world—global, capital-intensive and exposed to commodity volatility and geopolitical disruption. Most still rely on fragmented, manual processes. Use AI to build resilient, adaptive supply chains and protect every capital project.
Refinery and downstream optimization
Refineries operate on tight margins under mounting pressure from volatile feedstocks, aging equipment and tightening environmental standards. Optimizing throughput and managing unplanned shutdowns requires real-time intelligence. Use AI and automation to reduce downtime and lower operational costs. 
Scaling AI across oil and gas operations
Roughly 70% of digital AI initiatives in oil and gas fail to scale—not because the AI doesn't work, but because the underlying data is too fragmented to build on. Operators end up with SCADA, ERP and operational data in separate silos. Build the AI-ready foundation that turns isolated pilots into enterprise-wide results.
