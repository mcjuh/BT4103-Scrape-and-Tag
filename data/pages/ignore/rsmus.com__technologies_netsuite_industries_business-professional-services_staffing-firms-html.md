## Centralize your data and operations with a connected business operations platform
Staffing firms often rely on multiple tools, such as applicant tracking systems (ATS), project management software, customer relationship management (CRM) and human capital management (HCM) platforms, which store valuable data in separate systems. Oracle NetSuite offers a range of prebuilt connectors and a robust core application programming interface to integrate these tools into a single platform.
NetSuite serves as a central hub, merging ATS, CRM and HCM into a streamlined enterprise resource planning (ERP) system that automates staffing and executive search services. With its cloud-based software, NetSuite replaces manual processes and consolidates reporting into a digital hub, providing real-time access to key data on profitability, expenses, revenue and more.

## NetSuite and RSM address staffing firms’ most common pain points
RSM’s NetSuite staffing and executive search industry team combines years of cross-functional experience in the staffing industry with technological innovations that automate tedious processes and deepen client relationships, giving your business a competitive edge.
Margin tracking and profitability 
NetSuite centralizes client information, placement details, billing records and documents into a single system, eliminating the need for disconnected software. RSM’s NetSuite solution tracks and manages placement data, including staffing, contracts, locations, bill rates, labor costs and fees. It enables accurate reporting for operations and finance, helping your firm bill on time and understand margins at the placement level.
Revenue allocation at the placement level 
NetSuite streamlines revenue allocation at the placement level by reducing errors, maintaining compliance, improving efficiency and enhancing client trust. 
RSM’s NetSuite solution for staffing firms is configured to automate system controls, provide robust audit trails with adherence to guidelines, streamline manual processes to save time and resources, and provide transparent reporting and reliable fund management across all legal services.
Billing flexibility 
NetSuite automates many routine tasks such as time and expense tracking, invoicing and report generation. This frees up valuable time and reduces the potential for human error in data entry.
RSM’s NetSuite solution is configured to track and manage your time entries and expenses with approvals, consolidate invoicing and automate billing based on custom rates.
Invoicing format flexibility 
NetSuite brings all your data into one place, fostering improved team communication.
RSM’s NetSuite solution enables customized PDF templates, attachments and automated workflow processes to meet customer invoicing requirements. Manage your accounts receivable communication process from a single source of truth in NetSuite. 
ATS and payroll integration 
NetSuite offers various out-of-the-box connectors for automated integrations with ATS and payroll systems, facilitating seamless data flow and streamlining recruitment, onboarding, payroll management and employee data tracking.
ATS integrations typically automate transferring candidate data, time entries and job placements into NetSuite. Payroll connectors automate compensation details, tax calculations and compliance information, syncing payroll journals with financials. These integrations reduce errors and manual entry while enhancing efficiency.
