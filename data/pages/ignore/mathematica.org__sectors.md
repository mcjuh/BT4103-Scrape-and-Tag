# Sectors We Serve
Mathematica partners with organizations across the public, private, and foundation sectors to improve programs, strengthen decisions, and deliver lasting impact.
## Our Sector-focused Solutions
Explore how we help clients, partners, and communities achieve their goals and improve outcomes with evidence-driven insights and practical solutions. 
### Federal
Partnering with **federal leaders** to address urgent social challenges with rigorous research, action-ready evidence, and objective insights that strengthen programs and improve lives.
### Foundations
Supporting **philanthropy partners** in developing and scaling innovative ideas, curricula, and approaches that create measurable community impact.
### Global Health and Development
Working with **NGOs** , **foundations** , and **international organizations** to advance cost-effective, evidence-based solutions that improve lives, promote economic growth, and strengthen global stability. 
### Healthcare and Life Sciences
Providing data-driven insights and analytic expertise that help **providers** , **payers** , **life sciences and med tech** , and **medical colleges and societies** make informed decisions and enhance care and outcomes.
### State and Local
Helping **state and local leaders** use data and evidence to make programs and policies more efficient, effective, and impactful for the communities they serve.
