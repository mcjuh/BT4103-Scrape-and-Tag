#  Extended Workforce Solutions 
## Contingent workforce and talent management 
## Streamline your workforce: Optimize, comply, and scale
As the definition of ‘workforce’ expands, many organizations struggle to gain visibility and control of their total workforce. It requires integrated technology, robust governance, and a strategic approach to managing and sourcing talent, both contingent and full-time. Organizations have to balance cost efficiency with compliance, disparate systems, and complex workforce planning while maintaining a cohesive culture and worker experience.
Deloitte’s deep experience managing an extended workforce of our own makes us uniquely positioned to help organizations find the right solutions to their challenges. Our Extended Workforce Solutions (EWS) use advanced technologies and AI to deliver strategic insights and cost savings, transforming contingent workforce management from a reactive function into a proactive, value-driving capability that empowers your organization to stay competitive and compliant.
