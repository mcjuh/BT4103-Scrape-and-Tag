#  Acoustics
#### Noise and vibration are inevitable consequences of a developed society.
Often the effects of noise are small, but for some of our clients they can become in an major issue, particularly when promoting new infrastructure such as roads, railways, airports or industrial facilities.
We work closely with clients and stakeholders to assess acoustic emissions and the effects these may have on people and wildlife. We strive to anticipate concerns and design integrated and sustainable solutions before problems arise.
The breadth of our work across sectors and around the world means we are able to provide insight from a number of perspectives, whatever the problem in hand. Our acoustics offering encompasses:
  * 30 years of company experience across all noise producing sectors; transport, oil and gas, energy, renewables, manufacturing, construction etc.
  * Experience in integrating noise assessment into major projects, from High Speed Rail and new airport developments, to working for the IFC and the European Commission developing regional and international noise policy
  * A team of 40 qualified professionals in 12 countries networked to share expertise and pool resources
