## AI governance: Maximizing benefits, minimizing risks
Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming our world and presenting endless opportunities for businesses to transform strategies and operations, boosting efficiency and cutting costs.
Yet without cultural and ethical alignment, AI can introduce harmful bias and have unintended consequences for workforces and communities. Boards are key in ensuring AI’s benefits are maximized and risks are minimized, fostering a culture of responsibility and trust.
