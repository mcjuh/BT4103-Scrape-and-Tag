# EU Omnibus Policy Alert: Recommendations for companies on how to prepare for the upcoming changes
In February 2025, the European Commission introduced the EU Omnibus, its proposal to overhaul existing European sustainability regulations. The principal regulations the EU Omnibus aims to simplify and streamline are the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD), the EU Taxonomy, and the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM).
[ download the policy alert ](https://www.erm.com/globalassets/insights/omnibus_policyalert_v6b.pdf "download the policy alert")
The Omnibus is part of efforts by the EU to increase European competitiveness and decrease regulatory complexity and compliance burdens. According to the Commission, the proposed rules will make sustainability reporting more effective and efficient, simplify due diligence for responsible business practices, and unlock opportunities in European investment programs.
This Policy Alert provides an overview of suggested revisions to CSRD, EU Taxonomy, CSDDD, and CBAM, and shares recommendations for companies on how to prepare for the upcoming changes.
