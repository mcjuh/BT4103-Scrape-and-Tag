### KPMG Accounting Advisory Services for asset management
KPMG Accounting Advisory Services (AAS) offers an extensive range of services to help asset management clients achieve their business and operational objectives across all reporting levels, from registered investment advisors and management companies to fund-level, legal entity, partnership, and portfolio company/asset-level reporting. With a full suite of fund reporting and oversight services, as well as advisory and consulting services, our experienced professionals work closely with clients to optimize their fund accounting and reporting functions in today's complex environment.

###  Assisting management’s NAV oversight with an innovative approach 
### Assisting management’s NAV oversight with an innovative approach
Our managed services solution is designed to enhance and streamline the Net Asset Value (NAV) review process by combining advanced technology with the deep accounting expertise of a Big 4 firm. Powered by the Asset Management Accounting Platform (AMAP), which integrates automation, advanced analytics, and AI, our customizable services provide end-to-end support across the fund accounting lifecycle—transforming complex data into actionable insights and helping clients maintain accurate, timely NAVs with greater confidence. 
[Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2025/kpmg-nav-oversight-services.pdf)
###  Streamlining processes with fund reporting solutions 
### Streamlining processes with fund reporting solutions
As part of our managed services offering, the fund reporting solution—powered by Workiva—helps asset management clients streamline financial and investor reporting across all products, structures, and asset classes. By combining our firm’s deep industry expertise with Workiva’s integrated platform, we deliver customized, high-quality reporting through automated workflows, extensive collaboration, and real-time data.
