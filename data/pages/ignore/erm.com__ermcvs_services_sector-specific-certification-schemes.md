#  Sector SpecificCertification Schemes

ERM CVS provides sector-specific assessment and certification services that help organizations meet evolving regulatory and stakeholder demands. We enable clients to identify risks and opportunities in their management systems and achieve independent certification based on international standards.
## Unlock greater value with stronger accountability and transparency
To meet stakeholder expectations on a global scale, organizations increasingly need to demonstrate responsible practices and performance goals that align with multiple sets of internal standards, external frameworks and international regulations. 
In a world where transparency, trust and credibility play a paramount role in a brand’s reputation, sector-specific certifications help organizations to safeguard competitive advantage and their license to operate.
Mining sector clients are increasingly pressured by evolving regulations, specific performance standards, industry benchmarks, and rising expectations from downstream buyers, local communities, investors, labor unions, and NGOs. 
Sector-specific certifications enable organizations to demonstrate they are following best practices that help to reduce operational and environmental risks, improve quality control and enhance overall sustainability performance.

UK Emission Trading Scheme (ETS) certification
ERM CVS is an accredited verification body for the UK’s Emission Trading Scheme (ETS), where we provide independent verification of annual emissions reports and ETS GHG verification as required for compliance. In addition, we verify data required for free allocation: this includes baseline and activity level change reports as well as new entrant reserve applications. Our verification approach follows ISO 14064:3.
Oil, gas and offshore wind certifications
Safety and Environmental Management Systems (SEMS) certification of offshore activities is a performance-based approach to integrating and managing offshore operations in the Gulf of Mexico. SEMS, which can also be applied voluntarily to operations in other locations, helps to enhance safety and environmental performance by reducing the frequency and severity of incidents.

##  IRMA - Initiative for Responsible Mining Assurance
IRMA is a voluntary Initiative for Responsible Mining Assurance which aims to increase transparency and improve the environmental and social performance of mining operations.
ERM CVS provides independent, third-party assessments against IRMA’s comprehensive, internationally recognized Standard of Responsible Mining applicable to all sized of industrial mines around the world, covering all mined materials except for energy fuels.
Results of IRMA assessments are publicly available on the IRMA website (<https://responsiblemining.net/>), allowing interested parties to understand how the mine scored against each of the over 400 requirements of the IRMA standard, covering topics such as legal compliance, occupational health and safety, human rights, water and waste management, as well as the overall achievement level of the mine.
For more information about the IRMA Standard and the assessment process, please visit the IRMA website here: <https://responsiblemining.net/what-we-do/assessment/>
To view IRMA audit announcements of ERM CVS assessed mines, use the ‘Public announcements’ button. To provide feedback about a mine near you, please click the ‘Contact us’ button below.

##  Approved mining ESG standards
ERM CVS is approved to deliver a number of mining ESG standards including:
  * Initiative for Responsible Mining Assurance (IRMA), as an IRMA provider, ERM CVS delivers IRMA verification and certification services
  * The Copper Mark – ERM CVS is a Copper Mark provider
  * Aluminum Stewardship Initiative (ASI) – ERM CVS can conduct ASI certifications
  * ICMM Assurance and Validation requirements (Sustainability Report Assurance and Performance Expectation (PE) validation)
  * National Mining Associations CORE Safety, a voluntary mine safety and health initiative
  * Global Standard on Tailings Management (GISTM)
  * International Cyanide Management Code (Cyanide Code)


##  The US Center for Offshore Safety (COS)
The US Center for Offshore Safety (COS) offers SEMS certifications for offshore wind operations outside of the US outer continental shelf. ERM CVS has been a COS Accredited Audit Service Provider since 2013. Our deep oil and gas expertise, especially in the Gulf of Mexico, means we are uniquely placed to help clients meet their obligations and achieve certification.
