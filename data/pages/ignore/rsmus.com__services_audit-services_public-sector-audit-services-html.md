#### [Contact audit professionals ](https://rsmus.com/services/audit-services/public-sector-audit-services.html#contact)
##  Ensuring accountability with audits tailored for government, nonprofit and education 
State and local governments, tribal governments, nonprofit organizations and educational institutions face special challenges in managing their operations, including the need for independent and objective audit procedures. These entities must address a variety of operational and technological issues while simultaneously ensuring accountability and transparency to diverse stakeholders. They also bear the responsibility of managing public funds and grants effectively, while delivering high-quality services efficiently.
With our experience and industry-specific knowledge, RSM is well equipped to help you navigate these complex financial and operational challenges. We offer a comprehensive range of services tailored to your specific needs, whether at the federal, state, local or tribal level. From technology and operational efficiencies to board training and financial projections, we have you covered. Our team of professionals is committed to providing you and your organization with an independent and objective audit of your financial statements.
For organizations receiving federal funding, compliance with single audit requirements is paramount. We are well-versed in the Single Audit Act and Uniform Guidance, ensuring obligations are met accordingly. We also recognize the importance of financial strategies in maximizing grants and donor dollars. We leverage our digital audit ecosystem to enhance the effectiveness and efficiency of your audit, minimize disruption and uncover valuable information for better decision making.
As a trusted advisor, RSM provides tailored insights to help you and your stakeholders make critical business decisions and confidently utilize financial information. We strive to provide you with the knowledge and guidance necessary to navigate complex challenges and seize opportunities, ensuring the long-term success and sustainability of your organization.
