# Antitrust Class Action Administration
Expert notice and administration of antitrust settlements.
Kroll has administered hundreds of antitrust settlements, including some of the most complex and high-profile cases in history, with billions of dollars in settlements funds distributed. Our team has experience across all types of antitrust cases, including monopolies, price-fixing, price discrimination, product tying and complex financial instruments.
### Work with a Leading Administrator to Navigate Complex Antitrust Class Action Settlements
Antitrust class action settlements frequently involve multiple classes and sophisticated plans of allocation. Our innovative technology and extensive experience make us the preferred administrator for these complex cases. Our proprietary platform provides real-time checks and balances that ensure confidence that all deadlines will be met without deviation from the settlement agreement.
Learn more about our [comprehensive class action services.](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/settlement-administration/class-action)
### In-House Staff with Practical Experience in Antitrust Class Action Matters 
Each member of our team is well-versed in every aspect of settlement administration, allowing us to foresee potential problems before they occur and recommend proven and tried solutions. Our [in-house media team](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/notice-media-solutions) is led by an internationally recognized notice expert and is the most experienced legal notice team in the industry. The team’s experience is unparalleled in the design and execution of worldwide antitrust notice programs, including one of the largest court-approved international notice programs, In Re: Air Cargo Shipping Antitrust Litigation. 
Our team also includes experienced professionals performing comprehensive auditing procedures and processes to ensure that the highest degree of accuracy is applied to all facets of the administration process. We also serve as class action settlement funds escrow agent for many antitrust matters, working closely with our banking partners to oversee funds in the billions of dollars and to ensure tax compliance. We leverage our longstanding relationships with leading domestic and international financial institutions to offer the most competitive market rates.
