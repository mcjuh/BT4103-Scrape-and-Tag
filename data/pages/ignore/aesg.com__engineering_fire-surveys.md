## Ensuring Comprehensive Fire Safety Through Expert Fire Surveys
Fire surveys are essential for identifying and mitigating fire risks in both new and existing developments. At AESG, our expert fire survey services help clients maintain compliance with fire safety regulations while optimising the safety and resilience of their buildings. We provide a thorough evaluation of fire hazards, active and passive fire protection measures, and emergency systems to deliver actionable insights and recommendations tailored to the specific needs of each project.
## Why Conduct Fire Surveys?
Fire surveys play a critical role in safeguarding buildings and occupants. They help in:
### Identifying Potential Hazards
Assessing fire risks associated with materials, equipment, building layout, and occupant behaviours.
### Evaluating Fire Protection Systems
Checking the adequacy and maintenance of fire alarms, sprinklers, fire extinguishers, emergency lighting, and evacuation systems.
### Ensuring Regulatory Compliance
Verifying that fire safety measures align with local and international codes, standards, and legislation.
### Enhancing Building Resilience
Providing guidance for upgrades or modifications to fire safety systems to meet the latest requirements.
### Supporting Fire Safety Management Plans
Integrating findings into overall safety strategies to improve risk management and emergency preparedness.
## Our Fire Survey Services
[ Fire Risk Assessments ](https://aesg.com/engineering/fire-surveys/#collapse-7abe8626aa068f11c425)
In-depth evaluations of fire hazards and safety measures, taking into account the unique characteristics of each building or site. Our fire risk assessments prioritise both life safety and asset protection, providing a balanced approach to fire risk management.
[ Building Fire Safety Audits ](https://aesg.com/engineering/fire-surveys/#collapse-203a7c16aa068f11c425)
Detailed audits to assess compliance with relevant fire safety codes and regulations. Our audits cover everything from fire doors and compartmentation to emergency escape routes and fire-fighting equipment.
[ Passive Fire Protection Assessments ](https://aesg.com/engineering/fire-surveys/#collapse-8b2a72e6aa068f11c425)
Evaluating the condition and adequacy of passive fire protection systems, such as fire-rated walls, doors, and dampers. We identify any deficiencies and recommend solutions to ensure the integrity of compartmentation.
[ Active Fire Protection Inspections ](https://aesg.com/engineering/fire-surveys/#collapse-7abe8626aa068f11c9c4)
Reviewing the installation and operation of active fire protection systems, including alarms, sprinklers, smoke detectors, and emergency lighting, to ensure they are functioning as intended.
[ Evacuation and Emergency Preparedness Reviews ](https://aesg.com/engineering/fire-surveys/#collapse-203a7c16aa068f11c9c4)
Analysing evacuation procedures, emergency lighting, signage, and assembly points to optimise the building's emergency response capabilities.
[ External Wall Systems (EWS1) Surveys ](https://aesg.com/engineering/fire-surveys/#collapse-e3ba31f6aa068f11c9c4)
Conducting assessments of external wall materials and fire safety measures to comply with industry standards, ensuring that façade materials are safe and meet current regulatory requirements.

## When Should Fire Surveys Be Conducted?​
## Pre-Construction
Identifying potential fire risks and ensuring that designs meet fire safety requirements.
## During Construction
Assessing fire safety measures to ensure compliance with regulations before project completion.
## Post-Construction
Conducting final inspections before occupancy to verify all fire safety systems are in place and operational.
## Regular Intervals
Scheduling periodic surveys to maintain compliance and adapt to any changes in building use, layout, or fire safety regulations.
