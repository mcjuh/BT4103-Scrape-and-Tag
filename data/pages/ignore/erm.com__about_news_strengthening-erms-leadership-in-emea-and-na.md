# Strengthening ERM’s leadership in EMEA and NA
05 July 2016
Share this page:
ERM has made two senior appointments in North America and Europe, the Middle East and Africa (EMEA) to further strengthen our capabilities in these regions.
As the new Regional CEO for North America, **Charles “Chuck” Leichner** joins ERM with 30-years’ experience in the engineering and construction industry and as a registered Professional Engineer. He has held a variety of executive operational and client-facing leadership positions for multi-billion dollar global and national firms, of which his most recent positions include serving as the Chief Strategy and Sales Officer for ARCADIS North America and Global Director of Corporate Development for ARCADIS. His experience includes leading strategy development and implementation, mergers and acquisitions, post-merger integrations, business transformations, and leading new business initiatives. As the new Regional CEO for EMEA, **Jean-François Bolduc** is a senior executive and business leader who joins ERM from an 18-year career with Golder Associates. At Golder he was President of Europe and the Middle East overseeing business units in 14 countries and more than 900 staff. Jean-François has assumed various leadership roles throughout his career in Canada and Europe, building and leading professional and design-build delivery teams around environmental and ground engineering solutions and services in the Infrastructure, Oil & Gas, Mining and Power sectors. With a depth of experience in working in multi-cultural environments, his belief in building strong and lasting relationships with colleagues helps to create a foundation of cohesive and lasting teams that benefit both colleagues and clients alike. 
**Keryn James** , ERM’s Global Director of Operations, said: _“These two appointments signify the importance ERM places on excellent leadership of our people while maintaining a clear line of sight to the needs and expectations of our clients._
_“Chuck has a strong understanding of the North American market, as well as a wealth of global experience. He also brings an experienced strategic and market/client focus to the role that will help ERM to continue to deliver excellent service to our clients._
_“Jean-Francois has an excellent level of experience in the consulting business. Combining that with a strong approach to understanding the value of people in a professional services environment in helping to ensure we continue to provide our clients with the excellent level of service they expect from us, these two appointments continue to support our commitment to shaping a sustainable future with the world’s leading organizations.”_
