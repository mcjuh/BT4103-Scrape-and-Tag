# Class Action Settlement Administration
Providing exceptional results for all types of class action administrations through our expert team, consultative approach and unrivaled proprietary data security and technology.
Our class action administration team is comprised of subject matter experts with decades of experience managing some of the largest and most complex settlements in U.S. history.
We’ve processed millions of claims, mailed tens of millions of notices, expedited hundreds of thousands of calls and distributed billions of dollars in compensation to class members worldwide. This experience, coupled with our state-of-the-art technology and superior data security, enables us to deliver a full-service class action notice and administration solution that drives efficiency, speed in delivery, accuracy, quality control, transparency and cost savings.
Understanding that not all settlements are the same, we partner with clients early in the settlement phase to design customized processes and procedures that meet the unique intricacies of each case. Our consultative approach for each engagement sets the tone for a smooth and well-planned administration that leads to successful outcomes for all stakeholders. 
##### Our Services
We leverage our technology and subject matter expertise to deliver top-quality results through our in-house services:
  * Pre-settlement consultation services

  * Website and database design and management
