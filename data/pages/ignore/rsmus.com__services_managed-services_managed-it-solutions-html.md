# Managed IT and Applications
### RSM Catamaran
Information technology
## Technology is just the beginning
You need IT that can scale with you—that evolves to give you a competitive edge as you grow. This kind of IT involves much more than the latest technology; it involves an integrated team of RSM professionals that manage cloud and infrastructure, business applications, and data analytics in accordance with leading industry practices. With RSM managing your IT function, your technology can provide a solid foundation on which to grow your business.

## Level up your tech knowledge with managed IT
RSM manages your IT team with process knowledge and best-of-breed third-party solutions, including IT service management, operations and monitoring, backup and recovery, cybersecurity, and end-user training. We recommend IT solutions for your business based on industry regulations and your unique operational needs. We manage these solutions for you so you can enjoy an efficient and customizable IT function. We also offer a 24/7/365 service desk capability with multilanguage options, minimizing disruptions to your workflow. 
While you can expect a predictable monthly fee, our RSM team is designed to flexibly scale as your IT landscape evolves so that we can cover the full breadth of your business’s needs. 
[ Learn more about managed IT services ](https://rsmus.com/services/digital-transformation/managed-it-services.html "Learn more about managed IT services")
## Get the most from your applications with managed application services
Resource constraints can hinder IT teams, stealing time from deeply understanding and proactively managing complex business applications. RSM can provide those critical resources, expanding your applications team and maximizing their full capabilities. 
Our team of highly qualified RSM professionals advise and execute on new features, optimizations, integrations and extended capabilities for many application types, including enterprise resource planning, customer relationship management, procurement, finance and accounting, treasury management, and workflow systems. With a deep understanding of the market’s growth opportunities and industry best practices, our team can bring your business vision to life through enhanced management of these powerful tools. 
[ Learn more about managed application services ](https://rsmus.com/services/digital-transformation/managed-application-services.html "Learn more about managed application services")
## Benefit from managed data analytics
RSM offers a dependable, flexible and expandable team to manage your data analytics needs while you keep your business focused and lean. From data migration and integration to master data management, reporting, and advanced analytics, our end-to-end outsourced solutions provide seamless support for data optimization, governance, and analysis. With RSM’s Data analytics managed services, your business can access the extensive technology and knowledge bank of our data professionals, poised to support your business in all matters of data analytics.
