##  Drive AI-powered storage resiliency
Modern organizations are constantly battling threats. From preventing ransomware attacks and other sophisticated cyberattacks to managing hardware failures and natural disasters, it’s one battle after another. Data resilience—the ability to prevent data loss, detect threats early and recover rapidly from disruptions—is what you need for business survival.
Take advantage of advanced storage solutions that deliver AI-powered data resilience and provide comprehensive protection across your entire data lifecycle. With data resiliency embedded at the core of modern storage architectures—spanning scale-out and scale-up hardware systems, software-defined storage, mainframe storage and tape—you gain defense-in-depth protection that safeguards your most valuable asset: your data.
Detect threats before they strike 
Activate AI-based monitoring of your primary and auxiliary storage systems.
Integrate security across your stack 
Integrate your IBM Security® solutions and data-resilient storage systems.
Recover from tamper-proof snapshots 
Speed up reliable recovery with immutable snapshots that can’t be altered or erased.
Accelerate backup and recovery 
Perform backup and recovery with hardware snapshots and copy data management.
