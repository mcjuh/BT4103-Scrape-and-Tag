# Carveouts & Divestitures
Carveouts demand clarity and control. Together, we’ll simplify the complexities, protect the bottom line, and separate for long-term success.
## Carveout Diligence and Sell-Side Readiness
Whether you’re preparing to divest or evaluating a carveout opportunity, our team ensures you’re ready to act. We assess entanglements across systems, people, and operations, modeling standalone run rates and separation costs for confidence at every stage. For sellers, we stress-test your operations, identify vulnerabilities early, and develop comprehensive standalone operating cost models. This ensures a cleaner, more efficient process, protecting value and reducing risk during negotiations.
## TSA Management and Execution
Transition Service Agreements (TSAs) often add complexity and cost to carveouts. Our experts design efficient TSA schedules, leveraging pre-built templates and accelerators to expedite agreement. We define clear governance and escalation processes to ensure smooth execution. Using our proprietary tools, we help clients rapidly exit TSAs, often reducing stranded costs by up to 30%.
## Target Operating Model and NewCo Definition
A scalable Target Operating Model is the backbone of a successful carveout. We define a clear operating and capability model, balancing optimization and risk mitigation through our proprietary Value to Risk (V2R) framework. From skills and organization structure to metrics, systems, and culture, we help you establish a NewCo ready to thrive post-separation.
## Separation Management Office (SMO) Program
Our Separation Management Office (SMO) ensures execution with precision and minimal disruption. Using proven carveout methodologies, digital dashboards, and lean teams, we track progress and streamline administrative burdens. Acting as program leaders, we orchestrate cross-functional efforts to meet Day 1 readiness and long-term success milestones.

## Frequently Asked Questions
  * What is a carveout?-
A carveout is the separation of part of a business from its parent company so it can operate independently or be sold. It often requires separating systems, teams, operations, and shared services so the new business can successfully operate as a standalone organization.
