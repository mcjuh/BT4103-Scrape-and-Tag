## Asset and wealth management sector trends
  1. Pressure on performance and profitability
  2. Transforming the business model
  3. Transforming through technology
  4. The battle for talent
  5. Rise of digital assets


### Pressure on performance and profitability
With muted active returns, increased transparency and ease of access to the market, the asset and wealth management industry is experiencing fee pressure and changing investor preferences, leaving firms searching for ways to transform and improve performance while cutting costs.
### Transforming the business model
  * Rising concentration


Asset and wealth managers are making a host of deliberate and aggressive strategic decisions to succeed in the face of today’s challenges, including making acquisitions and adding new product capabilities to increase scale.
  * Value propositions matter


Value-for-money demands are upending the economics of the industry, which has seen asset-weighted mutual fund and ETF fees fall in recent years, driven by investors switching to lower-cost products.
### Transforming through technology
Asset and wealth managers are investing in digital platforms to enhance the client online experience by improving access to products and supporting the administration of investment vehicles.
### The battle for talent
Firms are adapting their corporate culture to attract tech-savvy talent while upskilling and retaining current talent to address the industry’s ongoing staffing challenges.
### Rise of digital assets
With the maturation of exchanges and custody, the avenues for obtaining and holding digital assets are becoming more secure and offer a simplified approach. As a sign of its growing popularity, regulators have an increased focus in this area, and firms and their service providers are embedding digital assets into strategies.
