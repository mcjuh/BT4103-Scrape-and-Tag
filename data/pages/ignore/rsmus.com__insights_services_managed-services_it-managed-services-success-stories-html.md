# IT managed services success stories
### How Four Financial Service Organizations Accelerated their Digital Transformation
August 29, 2021
#### [Download the guide ](https://rsmus.com/content/dam/rsm/insights/services/managed-services/it-mngd-srvc/it-managed-services-success-storiesV2.inline.pdf)
Labor and workforce Managed IT services Financial services Managed cloud and IT
### Digital transformation should be top of mind for any financial organization interested in improving customer experience and remaining competitive against nimble new entrants into the field.
In our latest guide, we highlight the successes that four financial organizations found with IT managed services from RSM. Here’s a sampling of the results they collectively achieved:
  * Implemented a more effective cloud infrastructure management solution
  * Established a secure outsourced environment to host sensitive client data
  * Improved cybersecurity procedures
  * Reduced IT complexity and achieved greater clarity into technology investments
  * Improved customer satisfaction with self-service technology
  * Established more extensive disaster recovery and business continuity processes
  * Enabled internal IT resources to focus on more strategic initiatives
  * Established flexible, scalable technology resources to accommodate anticipated growth


These companies all shifted their digital transformation into high gear with a modern, cloud-based infrastructure and hands-off maintenance that freed their teams to create memorable experiences for their customers.
