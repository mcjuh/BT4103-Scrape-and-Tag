#  Energy, Industry and Resources 
We help our clients maximise assets, improve efficiencies and translate the latest process technologies into workable designs - whatever the field or the scale of their operations. Our extensive experience spans traditional power generation technologies, through to emerging renewable technologies and associated systems. We provide a full range of specialist services to owners, operators and lenders including feasibility studies, advisory services on permitting, financial, technical, environmental issues, to engineering design and project & construction management consulting.
