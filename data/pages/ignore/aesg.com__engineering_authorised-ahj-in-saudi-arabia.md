## Navigating Compliance with Saudi Arabia's Fire and Safety Regulations
In Saudi Arabia, obtaining approval from an authorised Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) is a critical step in ensuring that building projects comply with local fire and life safety regulations. The AHJ is responsible for enforcing standards set by the Saudi Building Code (SBC), overseeing fire safety measures, and granting the necessary certifications for building occupancy and operation.AESG offers expert guidance and support in liaising with AHJs throughout the project lifecycle. Our team’s extensive knowledge of local regulations, combined with a track record of successful approvals, ensures a seamless process for our clients.
## What is an Authorised AHJ in Saudi Arabia?
An Authorised AHJ in Saudi Arabia is typically a governmental or semi-governmental body empowered to:
### Enforce Fire SafetyCodes and Standards
Such as the Saudi Building Code, SBC 801 (Fire Protection), and other local regulations.
### Review and ApproveBuilding Designs
Ensuring that fire safety systems, structural elements, and materials meet safety requirements.
### Conduct Inspectionsand Certifications
Assessing compliance during construction and before granting occupancy permits.
### Oversee Fire Safety Measuresand Emergency Plans
Approving fire protection systems like fire alarms, sprinklers, and evacuation strategies.
The Saudi Civil Defence is the primary AHJ for fire safety, but other agencies may be involved for specialised requirements, such as industrial safety or healthcare facilities.
