#  KPMG named a “Leader” in Oracle Services by Forrester 
KPMG firms recognized for helping clients deliver large-scale, complex digital transformations with Oracle.

KPMG firms have been named a “Leader”, the highest designation, in [The Forrester Wave™: Oracle Services, Q1 2025 reportopens in a new tab](https://reprint.forrester.com/reports/the-forrester-wavetm-oracle-services-q1-2025-76b7ab33/index.html). Forrester evaluated 12 firms, assessing them in three categories: Strategy, Capabilities and Customer Feedback. The report noted KPMG as a “an excellent choice for enterprises looking for large-scale Oracle implementations and transformations that need complex delivery frameworks, mature change management, and industry best practices.”
[ The Forrester Wave: Oracle Services, Q1 2025 report opens in a new tab](https://reprint.forrester.com/reprints/the-forrester-wavetm-oracle-services-q1-2025-76b7ab33)
> To us, this recognition underscores KPMG firms’ commitment to delivering exceptional value to clients through our longstanding strategic alliance with Oracle. Leveraging extensive capabilities across industries and functions, together with Oracle’s leading cloud technologies, KPMG professionals help clients unlock their full potential and achieve remarkable outcomes.
Sanjay Sehgal 
Oracle Global Alliance Executive Sponsor & Head of Markets, Advisory 
KPMG in the US 
The Forrester report also noted that the referenced customers cite KPMG firms as “outstanding in implementations, its Powered Execution delivery methodology, change management, and talent quality. Customers state that the provider brings unmatched insights and industry benchmarks and is a great culture fit.”
> We see this recognition as a testament to the strategic vision of our leadership team and the exceptional work KPMG practitioners deliver around the globe for clients. Across KPMG firms, our teams consistently strive to deliver actionable insights, deep sector expertise and lasting value, while working closely with clients to instill confidence throughout their entire transformation journey.
Jennifer Chilton 
US Oracle Practice Leader 
KPMG in the US 
KPMG was also acknowledged for its standout vision in helping clients to “tackle complex digital transformations that accompany Oracle Cloud Applications with its Powered Enterprise asset and sector-specific value models. It has a highly mature talent strategy driven by demand, investments, a business plan of markets to penetrate, pipeline, and a complex talent development framework. It earned top scores for its global delivery strategy, which centers on scalability and availability of the right skill set with the right localization, price, and culture fit. It has an industry-standard AI-powered innovation strategy.”
> We believe our position as a “Leader” in Oracle Services reflects our unwavering dedication to delivering value, driving change and accelerating innovation through proven methodologies and strategic solutions. By leveraging the KPMG digital transformation suite of solutions enabled by Oracle, alongside our wide range of change management capabilities, KPMG professionals help empower clients to effectively navigate complex challenges and achieve sustainable, transformative results.
Thomas Gall 
Oracle Global Platform Lead 
KPMG 
On KPMG’s capabilities, the report notes that “KPMG excels in business and strategy consulting enabled by its strong brand and capabilities in risk, technology, program management, change management, and managed services. It shines in performance improvement, complex M&A transactions, and technology modernization in its implementations due to its assets and methodologies. KPMG is rated high for change management, driven by experience design and journeys. It earned top scores for OCI and platform support with unique offerings such as a finance data lake, retail data warehouse, intelligent forecasting, and more. Its Oracle CX and NetSuite offerings are below par relative to others evaluated.”
