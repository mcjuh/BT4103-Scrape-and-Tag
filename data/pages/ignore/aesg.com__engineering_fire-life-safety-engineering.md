## Delivering Practical and Resilient Fire Safety Solutions
At AESG, our fire safety engineering team specialises in developing risk-based fire safety strategies that are not only technically feasible but also tailored to meet the unique needs of each project. Our approach emphasises resilience and practicality, ensuring that fire safety measures are seamlessly integrated into every stage of a building's lifecycle.With decades of experience across various sectors—including building, aerospace, industrial, and oil and gas—our team combines technical expertise with a deep understanding of evolving fire safety requirements. We stay at the forefront of industry developments to provide innovative solutions for challenges such as evacuation planning, façade fire safety, smoke control systems, and compliance with stringent fire safety codes.
## Expertise Across InternationalStandards and Regulations
Our knowledge spans a wide range of international and local fire safety standards, including:
  * International Building Code (IBC)
  * International Fire Code (IFC)
  * NFPA Codes and Standards
  * EU & British Standards (e.g., Approved Document B, BS 9999, BS 7974)
  * Regional standards, such as the Singapore Fire Code, UAE Fire and Life Safety Code, Saudi Building Codes, and GCC Codes


By understanding the latest requirements from authorities and adapting to advancements in performance-based and risk-based fire engineering, AESG ensures that our clients’ projects meet the highest levels of safety and regulatory compliance.
## Fire Safety Services for Every Stage
We provide tailored strategies to enhance the operability and maintainability of both passive and active fire safety systems. Our services extend across new developments, ongoing construction projects, and operational buildings, offering a holistic approach to fire safety that includes:
### Strategic coordination for phased handovers, ensuring smooth project delivery
### Integration with façade engineering and materials expertise for enhanced fire protection
### Guidance through regulatory processes, including compliance with new frameworks such as the Gateway process
### Fire safety audits and inspections throughout the building's operational lifecycle
