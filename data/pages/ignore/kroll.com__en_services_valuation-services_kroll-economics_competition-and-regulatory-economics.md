# Competition and Regulatory Economics
Leading economic expertise for competition, regulatory and disputes matters
As economic evidence plays an increasingly central role in litigation, arbitration, transactions and regulatory scrutiny, Kroll's Competition and Regulatory Economics team delivers independent economic and econometric advice to help clients navigate complex, contested and high-stakes matters.
Combining scientific rigor, advanced quantitative analysis and deep sector expertise, we help clients develop robust, defensible economic arguments before competition authorities, regulators, courts and arbitral tribunals worldwide.
## Economic Insight for Decisions That Matter
Kroll's Competition and Regulatory Economics practice builds on the proven expertise and reputation of ABC economics, which joined Kroll in 2026. Combined with Kroll's established competition expertise, the practice further enhances our ability to support clients across key jurisdictions and cross-border matters.
Our economists combine rigorous academic analysis with a practical understanding of legal, regulatory and commercial realities—ensuring economic evidence is both technically robust and clearly communicated. By bringing together the strengths of ABC economics and Kroll's global platform, we help clients address their most complex regulatory, competition and disputes challenges with confidence.

## Clients and Sectors
Energy, Utilities and Natural Resources
We advise clients in markets where regulation, infrastructure, investment and competition interact. Our experience covers electricity and gas, energy-related arbitration and industrial energy markets, drawing on economic and econometric tools to address complex commercial and regulatory questions.
Telecommunications and Digital Markets
We work in technology-driven markets shaped by networks, data, infrastructure access and rapid innovation. Our experience includes mobile and broadband telecommunications, data infrastructure and digital markets, where economic evidence is often central to competition and regulatory assessment.
Transport, Aviation and Logistics
We advise clients across transport and logistics markets, including aviation, air cargo, rail and cargo shipping. These sectors often raise complex economic questions around market structure, state support, damages and regulation.
Healthcare, Pharmaceuticals and Life Sciences
We advise clients in healthcare and life sciences markets where innovation, regulation, intellectual property and competition intersect. Our experience includes healthcare, pharmaceuticals, medical equipment and patent-related disputes.
Financial Services, Banking and Insurance
We work on economic questions in financial services, banking and insurance, including matters involving competition, regulation, state aid and dispute-related analysis. These markets often require careful assessment of incentives, risk, market structure and counterfactual outcomes.
Automotive and Automotive Supply
We advise in automotive and supplier markets, where competition issues often arise across complex value chains and procurement structures. Our experience includes the automotive sector and the automotive supply industry.
Retail, Consumer Goods and Food
We work with clients in consumer-facing markets including food, supermarkets, consumer products and fast-moving consumer goods. These sectors often require close analysis of market definition, pricing, bargaining relationships and consumer choice.
Industrials, Chemicals and Materials
We advise in industrial markets that are often characterised by complex supply chains, capacity constraints and differentiated production processes. Our experience includes steel, chemicals, heavy industry and industrial manufacturing.
