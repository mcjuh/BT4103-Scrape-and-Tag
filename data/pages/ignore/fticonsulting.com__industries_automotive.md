The Automotive and Industrial industry is going through a major transformation driven by product and process technology shifts and compounded by the effects of the Covid-19 pandemic. The transformation affects each part of the automotive and mobility value chain — from OEMs, suppliers, dealers and aftermarket players to new technology and mobility players in the sector. The growing influence of electric, autonomous and connected vehicles has made it more important than ever to partner with industry experts who help navigate these rapidly changing markets.
### How We Leverage Auto-Industry Experience
Our global automotive consulting team leverages decades of industry experience and knowledge of the challenges and opportunities to effectively advise management teams in this ever-evolving industry. FTI Consulting offers a comprehensive range of business transformation services to vehicle manufacturers, suppliers, retailers, financers and other automotive and clean/smart mobility sub-sectors, as well as their creditors, lenders and other stakeholders.
Senior, experienced financial and operational advisors lead our industry team and focus on key issues, value and solutions for our clients from our offices in the heart of the U.S. automotive industry — Detroit, Michigan — as well as other key offices in North America, Latin America, Europe and Asia. We have expertise across all sub-sectors and vehicle systems and industry operator mindset and approach, and the ability to leverage an extensive network of relationships cultivated from our roles in numerous complex situations and transactions in the automotive industry.
