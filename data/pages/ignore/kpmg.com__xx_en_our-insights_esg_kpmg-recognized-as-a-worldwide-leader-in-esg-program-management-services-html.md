We are excited to share that KPMG has been named a worldwide leader in the [IDC MarketScape: Worldwide ESG Program Management Services 2023-2024 Vendor Assessment.](https://idcdocserv.com/US50608423e_KPMG) (PDF 215KB)
The report notes: “KPMG’s service’s adjacent software was one of the strongest in the assessment. By offering a wide range of software to supplement its services engagements, KPMG empowers its clients to continually drive impact even after the services’ engagement has ended.”
We are proud of the commitment KPMG has demonstrated in building a leading ESG team focused on helping clients harness the power of ESG to transform their business. KPMG’s ESG solutions are both holistic and practical, and designed to help organizations turn their ESG aspirations into action. Through industry-leading expertise, data-driven technology and global alliances, KPMG’s experience across critical ESG issues means our professionals can assist clients in creating the right roadmap for their ESG journey.
According to the report, "Customers should consider KPMG in situations where managed software and services are of a high priority. KPMG’s suite of adjacent software extends the impacts of a project past the end of the engagement period.”
> I am delighted that KPMG firms have been recognized by the IDC MarketScape for our experience that goes beyond strategy, working side-by-side with clients at each step of their ESG transformation. With capabilities that span the enterprise, KPMG professionals are committed to guiding clients in driving sustainable innovation across their business as they build a sustainable future.
