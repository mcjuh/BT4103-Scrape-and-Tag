## Event description 
Despite the challenges posed by geopolitical upheaval and tariffs, finance leaders continue to have a positive outlook on their own organizations' capabilities for growing profits.
Join Grant Thornton professionals for a discussion on insights from our Q3 2026 CFO Survey and hear what senior finance leaders said about the trends shaping business strategy, investment priorities and organizational performance. During this webcast, we'll explore the issues top finance executives are watching most closely, including the changing role of AI, business transformation, capital allocation, tax and trade developments, and the opportunities and challenges that may influence decision-making in the months ahead.
Gain practical insights into how finance leaders are approaching today's rapidly changing environment and what the latest survey results may signal for the future.
