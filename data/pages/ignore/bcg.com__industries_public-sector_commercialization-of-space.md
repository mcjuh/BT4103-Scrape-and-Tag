# Space Sector
Space is no longer the final frontier. There’s a new space economy where commercial companies and public sector organizations are gaining competitive advantage. BCG’s space sector consulting experts help clients enter the race—and win.
The commercial space sector represents a tremendous opportunity for a diverse range of public and private sector organizations. Governments are looking to spur growth in space, and organizations such as NASA and the European Space Agency are leading the way in the commercialization of space. Incumbent space industry companies are taking advantage of new technologies that will give them a competitive edge here on Earth, while other businesses are seeking to leverage the benefits of space-based technologies.Meanwhile, there’s a new breed of companies built around satellites, spaceports, and in-orbit infrastructure. BCG is uniquely positioned to help clients across industries achieve their commercial space goals and explore new possibilities of the space economy that may have once seemed impossible.

## How We Support the Space Industry
The space economy is taking off, but that doesn’t mean players will have an easy time launching their business or leveraging space technologies. We provide consulting to space industry leaders, and we work with governments, established companies, and new market entrants to unlock opportunities in space industry areas across the value chain—from strategy and [infrastructure](https://www.bcg.com/industries/urban-planning/infrastructure) to [operations](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/operations/overview) and end-user services.
In today's new space age, our mission is to advance a responsible and sustainable space economy, generate deeper insights about our planet, and enable scientific discovery. BCG’s space sector consulting experts—which include former space executives, engineers, technologists, and [manufacturing](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/manufacturing/overview) leaders—have the experience and insights required to help clients realize powerful opportunities in the space industry.

## Our Clients’ Success in the Space Sector
5x
ROI in GDP growth
A European space agency wanted to support a multibillion-Euro investment by quantifying the benefits of space exploration. Our space industry experts’ methodology validated the investment strategy, drawing on more than 15 business cases to map the economic impact of the space sector. The analysis showed that a major European space exploration program would generate a GDP multiplier effect of more than five times the overall budget. 
~8
org layers leaner
A new military space organization was tasked with standing up an organization structure within 15 months; the new organization sought to be leaner than its predecessor to enable efficiency and quick decision making. BCG helped deliver the full organization structure **five months ahead of schedule** , while **eliminating about eight organizational layers** from the previous organization model, with clearly defined roles for all billets.
12%
over goal
A large satellite-manufacturing company was struggling to complete a multiyear effort to reduce its cost structure. To help the business get back on track, BCG’s experts established a project management office and enhanced governance processes. With a renewed focus on clear ownership, streamlined decision making, and creative troubleshooting, the manufacturer **exceeded its original transformation goals by 12%**.
