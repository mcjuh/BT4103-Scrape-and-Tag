## Diligent. Timely. Results-driven.
Structured products and securitizations are important sources of funding for many companies and an integral part of the investment strategy for institutional investors. The review and modeling of these securities requires detailed asset-specific expertise and process-specific knowledge. Grant Thornton's Structured Products group works with issuers, banks, and portfolio managers to help them consider all factors related to agreed-upon procedures (AUP).
