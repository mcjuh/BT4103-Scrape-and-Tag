#  Insights to Enablement 
## Help your people develop the Salesforce skills that drive better outcomes
  * [Measurement and monitoring ](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/insights-to-enablement.html#root_container_content-free-container-1_section-32054184_tabshome1)
  * [Implementation and adoption](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/insights-to-enablement.html#root_container_content-free-container-1_section-32054184_tabshome2)


### **Use innovative training tools to drive high-value Salesforce use**
Increase the effectiveness of your Salesforce implementation by using interactive learning and gamification tools that help users quickly master the features that drive higher ROI for your business.
### Continually improve adoption with measurement and monitoring tools
As your people continue to learn and explore, you can score adoption and behaviors to make the connection between what they’re doing, what they’re learning and how much value they deliver to your bottom line.
### Gain expert assistance from PwC with your global implementation and adoption
PwC launched the largest global Salesforce implementation––across 158 countries. Our proven approach is grounded in experience, so you can count on our industry knowledge every step of the way.
## Insights to Enablement, combined with PwC’s service, has helped our clients achieve on average:
  * ~68% increase in Salesforce activity: show your people the benefits of improving how they use Salesforce through cutting-edge scoring algorithms and dashboards, and reinforce their skills with daily, bite-size learning.
  * ~140% increase in team collaboration: we put your people’s collaborative ways of working at the center of gamification, encouraging individual and team use of Salesforce.
  * ~30% increase in Salesforce use in just four weeks: we help you accelerate adoption by identifying the ‘critical few’ behaviors that enable change management and business benefits.
