## AI in Technology
No stranger to disruption or speed, the tech sector is an AI adoption frontrunner compared with other industries. Technology firms, including software, hardware, semiconductor, and IT services companies, are harnessing AI to:
• Improve software speed to market, quality, and cost with AI coding assistants• Automate lead generation and targeted marketing• Increase levels of IT service desk support• Identify patterns to proactively track security threats• Make data quality control more efficient
