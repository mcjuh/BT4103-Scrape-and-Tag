# 2026 Private Clubs Compensation Survey Report
### How does your club stack up?
August 21, 2026
#### [Order the report ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/private-clubs/2026-private-clubs-compensation-survey-report.html#contact)
Labor and workforce Private clubs Real estate
Private clubs continued to navigate a complex operating environment throughout 2025 and into 2026, with labor and workforce challenges remaining at the forefront for many clubs.
As compensation continues to represent the largest controllable expense for private clubs, understanding evolving salary and wage trends is more important than ever to support effective budgeting, talent retention and long-term financial sustainability.
RSM US LLP's annual compensation survey encompasses salary data for 97 roles across Florida's private clubs. Carefully crafted to include essential compensation insights, this report will contribute to effective financial management within your club.
Download your copy of the 2026 compensation report and gain insight into the industry’s local salaries and wages to ensure your club is competitive and on target.
