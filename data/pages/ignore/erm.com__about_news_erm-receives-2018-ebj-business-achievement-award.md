# ERM Receives 2018 EBJ Business Achievement Award
29 April 2019
Share this page:
**Environmental Business Journal® (EBJ)[has recognized ERM with a Business Achievement Award](https://ebionline.org/2019/01/25/2018-ebj-business-achievement-awards/), highlighting ERM’s ongoing commitment to transforming the way we deliver services to meet our clients’ changing needs. **
ERM was recognized in the technology merit category for the global deployment of NEXUS by ERM, a digital platform designed to streamline and enhance how we deliver projects to provide faster, better and safer outcomes for our clients. The implementation of NEXUS for Site Investigation and Remediation projects was launched in July 2018 and is now being used on over 100 projects around the world.
> " In what is widely regarded as a stable environmental consulting market, a number of companies exceeded the norms of low single-digit growth with double-digit growth or ambitious ventures into new practice areas or technology development. "
Grant Ferrier
president of Environmental Business International Inc. (EBI, Inc.), publisher of Environmental Business Journal.
Shawn Doherty, Director of Digital Business & Transformation added: “We are honored by this industry recognition as it demonstrates our commitment to improving client service, innovation and developing new methods of creating value for our clients. We would like to recognize all the hard work and dedication from many ERM employees, our development partners and clients for their excellent feedback and guidance to get to this point.
“We have recently started the roll-out of NEXUS for Due Diligence to deliver better M&A insights and project outcomes for our clients. The NEXUS for Due Diligence approach also creates a platform to harness data insights, peer benchmarking, emerging issue and sectoral trends analysis to inform our clients’ organizational M&A/growth strategy, transaction planning, execution and negotiation.”
