# Revisions to CSRD and CSDDD finalized
April 02, 2026 
Snapshot 2026-05, _Impact of final changes to European sustainability reporting requirements_ , summarizes the final Omnibus revisions to the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive and the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive, as well as related revisions to the European Sustainability Reporting Standards and changes to EU Taxonomy reporting. The final directive was officially published on February 26, 2026 and entered into force 20 days thereafter; EU member states have been given one year to transpose it into national legislation, which may include incorporating additional country-level reporting requirements or noncompliance penalties.
The Snapshot is complete with tables outlining the final revisions at-a-glance along with insights for potential next steps to help affected companies move forward in complying with the new sustainability requirements.
