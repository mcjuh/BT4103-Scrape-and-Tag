# The intrepid COO: 5 strategies to shatter enterprise inertia
AI has moved beyond basic automation and productivity. Seven out of ten COOs report embracing a revolutionary approach called agentic AI—intelligent systems that don't just follow commands but actively reason, plan, and execute complex business strategies autonomously.

Chief Operating Officers (COOs) need to think like strategists and execute like realists. They must strengthen organizational resilience through robust cybersecurity measures and operational excellence, while driving aggressive business expansion that delivers exceptional customer experiences.
And they need to do all of this in a world buffeted by geopolitical volatility and AI-driven disruption that’s driving [two-thirds of CEOs](https://www.ibm.com/thought-leadership/institute-business-value/en-us/c-suite-study/ceo) to say they’re willing to take on even more risk to maintain competitive advantage. 
### Welcome, agentic AI
At the same time, organizations are grappling with agentic AI. As operations shift from automating tasks to orchestrating self-adapting processes, operations leaders are navigating unprecedented challenges—along with unprecedented opportunities. 
> The most forward-thinking COOs are creating an entirely new operating model. What’s changed?
Traditional operating models weren’t built for AI that can reason, act, and learn autonomously. The agentic AI enterprise operating model transforms how work gets done—by enabling AI agents to collaborate with people and systems in real time, driving exponential gains in productivity, speed, and innovation. It’s not just automation—it’s a new way to operate. 
To gain a deeper understanding of enterprise operations today and tomorrow, the IBM Institute for Business Value (IBM IBV) surveyed more than 1,900 Chief Operating Officers (COOs) and Chief Supply Chain Officers (CSCOs) in 25 industries and 33 countries. The survey, conducted in May and June 2025, reveals how reimagining operations can accelerate growth.
