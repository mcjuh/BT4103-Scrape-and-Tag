## Progress by industry: Financial services
Second only to technology, media, and telecoms, the financial services industry is seeing more benefits than most other industries in AI, recognizing the potential for using GenAI in financial reporting, and will prioritize more (66%) than others over the next three years. 
### According to our research, financial services is the second-most-advanced industry in using AI in reporting.
