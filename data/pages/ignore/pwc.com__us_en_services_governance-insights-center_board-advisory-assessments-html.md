#  Board advisory and assessment 
Boards need more than a check-the-box evaluation or one-size-fits-all education. PwC helps boards assess effectiveness, deepen fluency on emerging issues, and turn insight into action through independent assessments, immersive director sessions, and tailored education grounded in our latest governance thought leadership. 
## How we can help
[ Board effectiveness assessments ](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/governance-insights-center/board-advisory-assessments/board-assessments.html) [ Immersive AI board governance sessions for directors ](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/governance-insights-center/board-advisory-assessments/immersive-ai-governance-directors.html) [ Board and committee education  ](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/governance-insights-center/board-advisory-assessments/board-committee-education.html)
## Why PwC
Boards turn to PwC when they want an independent perspective, current market insight, and practical recommendations. Our Governance Insights Center brings together governance specialists and multi-disciplinary subject matter professionals to help boards strengthen effectiveness, navigate emerging issues, and stay prepared for pivotal moments.
  * Independent, boardroom-native perspective


  * Governance, risk, strategy, and AI insight on one team 


  * Tailored engagement designed around the board’s agenda


  * Recommendations that support follow-through
