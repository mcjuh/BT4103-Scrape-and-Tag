#  Powering Data Center
Strategic energy solutions for scalable, sustainable expansion 
ERM brings strategic and technical advisory, as well as procurement support, across the capital asset lifecycle, to help our clients to access secure and sustainable energy when and where it is needed, scale energy-efficient solutions to reduce costs, and retain a license to operate through compliance and integration with local energy systems. 
## Speed to market and scalable growth of the data center industry requires secure and sustainable access to power in locations of choice. 
Surging demand for data centers
Rapid growth in cloud, AI, machine learning, and edge computing is fueling strong demand for data center capacity.
Power access as a critical bottleneck
Timely access to grid power is a major challenge, prompting developers to seek markets with ready grid access or explore alternatives like on-site generation.
Clean energy expectations
Regulatory pressures, investor expectations, and customer demands are accelerating the shift toward clean and low-carbon energy sources.
Strategic, market-specific energy planning:
Developers require a holistic view of site impacts and are pursuing diverse energy strategies tailored to each market’s infrastructure and sustainability context.

##  Data center power crunch: Meeting the power demands of the AI era 
Data centers are rapidly increasing their electricity consumption, driven primarily by the growth of AI and digital infrastructure, placing unprecedented strain on power grids globally. This surge necessitates transformative collaboration between the power and technology sectors to ensure sustainable, reliable energy supply while meeting aggressive carbon-free goals.
