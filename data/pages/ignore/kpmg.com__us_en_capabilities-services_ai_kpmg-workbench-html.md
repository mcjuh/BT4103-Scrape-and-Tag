# **Intelligent AI agents**
At the heart of KPMG Workbench is a network of 50 AI assistants (agents) and chatbots, with nearly a thousand in development. These agents work alongside KPMG professionals, providing faster, high-quality, digital solutions across multiple sectors.
KPMG has collaborated with Microsoft to build KPMG Workbench on Microsoft Azure AI Foundry services, allowing for interoperable, agent-to-agent communications. In addition, it brings together capabilities from across the KPMG ecosystem of alliance partners, such as ServiceNow, Salesforce and Workday, so clients accessing it can choose the model or AI agent that fits their task.
# **Client-centric innovation**
KPMG Workbench can enable the next generation of KPMG AI-enabled client service delivery, including innovative and content rich ‘Services as Software’ (SaaS) that will provide our clients with AI tools steeped in our deep industry expertise and experience.
Clients include:
**A global bank** that requires data sovereignty and processing of agents to help the organization verify the identity and legitimacy of its customers and mitigate risk, including fraud
**A telecommunications and technology organization** with a need to enhance their compliance management with AI agents in a highly regulated industry where trust built into the platform is imperative
**A global household retailer** looking to drive back-office productivity by growing a digital workforce using multiple AI models
KPMG Workbench has built-in data sovereignty, meaning clients can maintain full control of how their data is stored and processed and manage diverse risk and governance needs, helping them to meet local and global regulatory requirements. **KPMG is also the first organization in the world to achieve BSI/ISO 42001* certification for AI Management Systems.**
