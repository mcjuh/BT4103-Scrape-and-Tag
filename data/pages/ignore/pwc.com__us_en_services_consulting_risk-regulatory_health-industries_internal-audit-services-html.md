## Overview
Many health organizations are constrained by traditional internal audit models that fall short of today’s risk demands. With increased scrutiny, evolving IT systems, and strategic transformation underway, there is a need to elevate internal audit from a control checker to a trusted risk advisor. PwC helps internal audit become digitally enabled and insight-driven, so it can monitor risk continuously, respond quickly, and align with enterprise priorities.

## Capabilities
  1. PwC’s Internal Audit Orchestration Suite
  2. Dynamic risk assessments and continuous monitoring
  3. Multi-modal delivery
  4. Digital audit lifecycle integration
  5. Thought leadership and benchmarking
  6. Embedded provider and payer expertise


### PwC’s Internal Audit Orchestration Suite
PwC’s [Internal Audit Orchestration Suite](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/risk-regulatory/library/internal-audit/internal-audit-orchestration-suite.html) is a modular, AI-first platform that helps transform how internal audits are planned, executed and delivered — beginning with fieldwork enabled by Dynamic Testing and our control design assessment AI Toolkit.
The [Fieldwork module](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/risk-regulatory/library/internal-audit/internal-audit-orchestration-suite/fieldwork-dynamic-testing.html) performs AI-enabled control testing that dynamically generates test procedures and control attributes, performs evidence review, improves traceability, and produces audit-ready testing outputs — while helping keep auditor judgment in the lead.
### Dynamic risk assessments and continuous monitoring
PwC uses data-driven assessments informed by benchmarking and stakeholder input to align audit scope with emerging healthcare risks and transformation areas. PwC uses tools to continuously monitor data and processes for potential risk flags.
### Multi-modal delivery
From full-source to client-led engagements, the delivery model flexes to meet staffing, budget, and knowledge transfer goals—enhancing audit maturity and reducing disruption.
### Digital audit lifecycle integration
From data ingestion to real-time reporting, PwC’s technology suite streamlines risk identification, speeds fieldwork, and supports continuous monitoring—reducing manual burden.
### Thought leadership and benchmarking
Clients gain access to PwC’s top healthcare audit leaders and events, bringing outside-in perspective and peer-aligned insight to internal audit planning.
### Embedded provider and payer expertise
Internal audit teams include professionals with health system, payer, and IT experience, delivering credible and efficient audits across revenue cycle, claims, clinical operations, and compliance domains.
