#  Labor and Employment Law 
EY teams can help you address complex labor and employment law projects across geographies. We can support you throughout the entire evolution of your business, helping you to manage the people-related legal aspects of restructuring, mergers, takeovers and all types of business transformation.

## What EY can do for you
Multinational companies face growing pressure to not only comply with diverse labor and employment laws but also build workplaces that reflect their values and attract the best talent. Aligning employment practices with your organization's purpose — while managing legal complexity across jurisdictions — is essential to becoming an employer of choice in every market.
Many organizations are asking themselves how to:
  * Attract and retain top talent through competitive, legally sound and locally relevant employment frameworks.
  * Build a strong and credible employer brand while navigating complex labor and employment laws globally.
  * Enable and navigate workforce transformation, mobility and evolving work patterns while addressing legal risk.


EY Law professionals have multijurisdictional experience and knowledge to assist organizations in managing day-to-day workforce issues. They can provide overall support for transformation projects including feasibility checks, design, coordination and implementation.
### EY Law teams can help you navigate:
Workforce transactions and transformation Conduct thorough legal checks on key employment documents, communications, consultations with employee representatives, workforce changes and employee arrangements to identify risks and maintain compliance across the organization.
Workforce risk and sustainability Provide guidance on global workforce arrangements such as hiring, pay, leave, safety and other policies to manage workforce compliance risks and regulations for evolving work patterns.
Employment operate Support compliance, risk management, case handling, employee relations, investigations and documentation to help organizations follow employment laws and run HR processes smoothly.
### Benefits of a solid compliance foundation
Your organization can focus on building workplaces that reflect your values, attract talent and drive competitive advantage globally. The services and solutions across labor and employment provide: 
  * Confidence in meeting regulatory requirements across multiple jurisdictions and managing exposure to legal and financial risk.


  * Clear, actionable guidance on compliance obligations across diverse legal landscapes. 


  * Integrated support across the full employee lifecycle — from hiring and mobility to restructuring — in every jurisdiction where you operate.

Labor and employment services and solutions help multinational organizations navigate complex employment laws across jurisdictions, reducing legal and financial risk. By providing clear guidance on compliance obligations and supporting the full employee lifecycle — from hiring and mobility to restructuring — these offerings help enable organizations to build workplaces that reflect their values, attract top talent and drive competitive advantage globally.

Beyond meeting regulatory requirements, these offerings help organizations align employment practices with their broader business strategy and purpose. This includes supporting workforce transformation, managing evolving work patterns and enabling mobility — all while managing legal risk. The result is a more resilient, agile workforce and a stronger employer brand across every market in which the organization operates.
