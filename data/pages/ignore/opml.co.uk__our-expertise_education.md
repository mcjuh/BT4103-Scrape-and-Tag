Equipping children and youth with education and life skills is critical to transitioning into a more inclusive, equitable, and prosperous world. To achieve this, we support governments, development partners, foundations, and private sector to drive better education and life outcomes for all children and youth.

We are committed to high-quality, lifelong learning opportunities for all in low- and middle-income countries. We support education systems to become adaptive, self-improving, and focused on building the skills, values, and attitudes needed to achieve development goals, especially for the most hard-to-reach and marginalised children and youth.
We do this by providing high-quality, responsive, impact-oriented, and contextually relevant policy research, technical assistance and consulting services to governments and their development partners.
