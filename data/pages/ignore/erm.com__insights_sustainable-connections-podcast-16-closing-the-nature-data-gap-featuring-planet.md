# Episode 16: Closing the nature data gap featuring Planet
In our sixteenth episode, Mark Lee talks to Amy Rosenthal, Senior Global Director for Conservation Initiatives at leading satellite imagery provider, Planet, and Michael Mangiante, Global Climate Risk Lead at ERM, about how nature mapping and data analytics can enable companies and governments to operationalize their nature strategies. Their conversation covers:
  * How to use satellite data to enable nature stewardship and disclosure
  * Identifying and taking action against nature crimes
  * Monitoring and verification of nature restoration projects
