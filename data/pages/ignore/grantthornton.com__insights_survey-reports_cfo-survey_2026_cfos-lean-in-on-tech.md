#  CFOs lean in on tech as market pressure mounts 
## AI optimism drives spending despite rising performance risks
## Gap between ambition, execution is growing
Grant Thornton’s 2026 Q2 CFO survey shows a steady pursuit of tech enablement despite concerns about the U.S. economy.
**What’s in the report?**
  * Confidence in the U.S. economy has plummeted to a five-year low
  * CFOs are turning to tech to ease the sting of economic pessimism
  * Returns on tech investments require strong governance
  * A warming M&A environment shows appetite for value-focused dealmaking
