## Patient Access Expansion | Digital Engagement | Performance Improvement
### Dynamic Scheduling & Referral Solution
Automatically identify cancelled appointments that can be offered to patients on your waitlist and identify inbound referrals that need to be scheduled; text patients with an offer, and if accepted, schedule the appointment within the EMR and confirm the booking via text.
### **Rapid Deployment**
Pre-built solution that deploys with EMR integration, typically within a few months
### **Quick Time to Value**
Payback expected within 3 months of deploying with positive impact to patient lives
### **Value-Based Fees**
Install / set-up costs are recuperated via a small % of revenue from filled appointments
