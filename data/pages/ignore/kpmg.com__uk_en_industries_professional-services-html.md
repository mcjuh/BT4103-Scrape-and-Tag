#  Professional services 
Assisting law firms and other professional practices in tackling challenges senior stakeholders face. 
Professional services firms are navigating a period of profound transformation. From evolving client expectations and digital disruption to increasing regulatory scrutiny and talent pressures, the sector is being reshaped by forces that demand agility, innovation and resilience. At KPMG, we work shoulder-to-shoulder with law firms, accountancy practices, and other professional services organisations to help them thrive in this dynamic environment.
Our multidisciplinary teams bring deep sector knowledge and practical experience to help clients unlock growth, realise greater value from their operations, protect their businesses from emerging risks, and attract and retain top talent. Whether you're looking to transform your front office, optimise your cost base, or future-proof your firm through strategic M&A or digital enablement, we’re here to help you lead with confidence.

## We Can Help You
At KPMG, we understand the unique challenges and opportunities facing professional services firms. Our solutions are designed to support your ambitions across four critical dimensions: Growth, Value, Protect, and Talent.
  * ###### Achieve Sustainable Growth
We help firms expand into new markets, improve client retention, and enhance profitability through front office transformation including use of AI, strategic M&A, and working capital optimisation. Our teams have supported leading firms with mergers, international expansion, and private equity investment strategies.
  * ###### Unlock Value from Your Operations
From legal operations and back-office transformation to technology modernisation and data governance, we help firms streamline processes, reduce costs, and improve decision-making. Our Powered Enterprise solutions and alliance partnerships with Microsoft, SAP, and Workday enable scalable, future-ready operations.
  * ###### Protect Your Firm
In an increasingly complex risk landscape, we help clients stay ahead of regulatory change, strengthen internal controls, and build cyber resilience. Our risk transformation and cyber teams work with firms to embed robust governance and quantify risk exposure in meaningful ways.
  * ###### Attract and Retain Top Talent
We support firms in designing compelling employee value propositions, modernising reward structures, and embedding inclusive employment practices. From partner remuneration to pay transparency and career path mapping, we help you build a workforce strategy that aligns with your business goals.

#### We Value Your Privacy
When you visit any website, it may store or retrieve information on your browser, mostly in the form of cookies. This information might be about you, your preferences or your device and is mostly used to make the site work as you expect it to. The information does not usually directly identify you, but it can give you a more personalised web experience. Because we respect your right to privacy, you can choose not to allow some types of cookies. Click on the different category headings to find out more and change our default settings. However, blocking some types of cookies may impact your experience of the site and the services we are able to offer. [More information](https://home.kpmg/uk/en/home/misc/cookies.html)
#### Functional Cookies
Functional Cookies
These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third party providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow these cookies then some or all of these services may not function properly.
#### Targeting Cookies
Targeting Cookies
These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies through for instance collecting data from videos embedded in websites and aggregating it with other data in order display targeted advertising to web visitors across a broad range of their own and other websites. They do not store directly personal information, but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and internet device. If you do not allow these cookies, you will experience less targeted advertising.
#### Performance Cookies
Performance Cookies
These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us to know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous. If you do not allow these cookies we will not know when you have visited our site, and will not be able to monitor its performance.
#### Strictly Necessary Cookies
Always Active
These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which essentially amount to a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in or filling in forms. You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site will not then work.
