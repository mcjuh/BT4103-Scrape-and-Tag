Your Coupa implementation was just the beginning. KPMG Powered Evolution helps you sustain momentum and unlock ongoing value from your procurement transformation to help prevent your platform from becoming tomorrow’s legacy tool.
### The challenge
As your business evolves, so do the demands on your Coupa platform. New releases arrive three times a year, each bringing opportunities to improve and requiring specialized knowledge to assess, configure, and deploy them. Without a dedicated team, these enhancements often go unused, and your platform risks becoming outdated. Retaining skilled resources for periodic needs isn’t cost-effective, and falling behind can mean losing competitive advantage.
### The KPMG approach
Named Coupa’s 2024 North American Strategic Partner of the Year, KPMG combines deep platform experience with leading practices to help you stay current, optimize performance, and adapt to change without the cost of maintaining a full-time team to manage the platform. Our Powered Evolution framework offers a flexible, scalable support model, tailored to your specific needs and centered around your goals and overall business strategy.
We provide:
  * Continuity of resources to reduce transition risk
  * Client-specific insights to guide upgrade decisions
  * Operational support so you can focus on core priorities
  * Access to KPMG’s global network for the latest leading practices
