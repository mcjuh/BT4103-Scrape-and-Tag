# Valuation Management
Kroll’s valuation management services deliver clarity, consistency and control across complex real estate portfolios through independent valuations, portfolio analytics and regulatory‑aligned reporting.
Kroll supports its clients in their reporting of valuation results and execution of their entire valuation process across all varieties of CRE portfolio products, as well as lending activities. Our experience, insight and access to information contribute to confident investment decision-making, timely fulfillment of reporting requirements and the ability to control costs and scale through partnership.
Kroll’s valuation management services are designed to provide clarity and consistency across complex real estate portfolios. By leveraging our global network of professionals and proprietary data resources, we deliver independent, defensible valuations that align with regulatory standards and investor expectations. Our team works closely with clients to streamline the valuation process, ensuring that reporting deadlines are met without compromising accuracy or transparency.
Beyond compliance, we focus on creating long-term value by helping clients optimize their valuation strategies. Whether managing diverse commercial real estate holdings, supporting lending activities or advising on acquisitions and dispositions, we tailor our approach to each client’s objectives. Through proactive communication, advanced analytics and industry-leading expertise, Kroll empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions, mitigate risk and achieve operational efficiency in today’s dynamic real estate market.
## Valuation Management Services
  * Appraisal management (bid and engage) 
  * Appraisal review and third-party appraiser contracting 
  * Net asset value and fund reporting 
  * Performance attribution 
  * Portfolio analytics 
  * Collateral monitoring (valuation, review) 
  * Direct consulting (assurance, valuation, review, audit) 
  * Valuation policy construction (collaboration and advice)
