# Liability Management and Corporate Action Services
Turnkey Expertise for Exchange Offers, Tender Offers, Consent Solicitations, and Strategic Corporate Actions
With Kroll Issuer Services, you gain access to the largest and most experienced public securities team, dedicated to supporting your unique needs and objectives. ## Service Offerings
From start to finish, we streamline the process, minimize complexity, and focus on achieving successful outcomes for all stakeholders.
  * Consent solicitations 
  * Rights offerings 
  * Tender and exchange offers for equity and debt 
  * Mandatory exchanges 
  * Distribution elections 
  * CUSIP eligibility for new issuances in DTC 
  * Bondholder identification 
  * Private placement and settlement agent services 


## Pre-launch Services
  * Collaborating with legal counsel to draft offer and consent documentation 
  * Eliminating unnecessary paperwork to maximize efficiency, including providing a dedicated online platform for instruction collection when needed 
  * Coordinating newspaper and stock exchange publications as required


## Post-launch Services
  * Providing secure online access to documentation and instruction submission 
  * Informing clearing systems promptly to ensure a successful and timely launch 
  * Acting as the information agent and central contact for investors and intermediaries 
  * Communicating deal terms to back-office personnel at major custodians and brokers 
  * Verifying investor eligibility before distributing offer documents to comply with restrictions 
  * Delivering regular reports on information flow and instructions to dealer managers 
  * Assisting with calculation of settlement amounts


## Settlement Services
  * Overseeing and organizing the settlement process 
  * Coordinating delivery of new securities to investors in exchange offers 
  * Providing payment details directly to clearing systems, reducing settlement risk 
  * Preparing necessary certificates and confirmations for trustees, agents, and depositaries 
  * Arranging marking down, cancellation, or transfer of accepted securities 
  * Adapting services to the procedures of local clearing systems or depositaries where needed
