# Intelligent compliance for Life Sciences
Turning regulatory obligation into operational advantage
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2026/intelligent-compliance-for-life-science-final.pdf)
Share
Life sciences organizations are operating in an environment where compliance expectations are expanding, data volumes are increasing significantly, and third‑party ecosystems are growing more complex. Traditional, rules-based compliance models are struggling to keep pace. Compliance can no longer function as a standalone control activity, it must be integrated, intelligent, AI-enabled and scalable.
KPMG helps life sciences organizations evolve compliance from a reactive requirement into a data‑driven, insight‑led business capability —using AI, advanced analytics, and automation, to help organizations anticipate risk earlier, respond with confidence, and operate more efficiently at scale.
## **How we help**
KPMG’s Life Sciences Risk & Compliance Consulting team supports organizations across the compliance and quality lifecycle—from program design and execution to AI‑enabled monitoring, advanced analytics, investigations, and digital enablement. Our teams work alongside compliance, legal, quality, and commercial stakeholders to embed intelligence directly into compliance operations, addressing today’s challenges while preparing for what’s next.
## Strengthening compliance programs
### Build programs that scale with your business
We help organizations take a fresh look at how compliance programs are structured and operated—focusing on practicality, sustainability, and regulatory alignmentwhile leveraging AI to improve visibility, consistency, and decision making.
Our work includes:
  * Evaluating the effectiveness of existing compliance frameworks and controls
  * Redesigning processes to reduce complexity and manual effort
  * Using data, analytics, and AI to surface emerging risk patterns and anomalies
  * Embedding intelligent automation to support consistency, accuracy, and audit readiness


The result is a compliance program that supports growth, adapts to change and continuously learns from data—while standing up to regulatory scrutiny.

## Responding to compliance issues and investigations
### Act quickly. Maintain credibility. Protect the organization.
When allegations, misconduct, or regulatory inquiries arise, organizations need experienced support that can respond efficiently without disrupting the business. Speed, defensibility, and data integrity are critical.
KPMG supports life sciences clients by:
  * Augmenting internal teams during investigations
  * Managing and analyzing large, complex data sets using advanced analytics and AI‑assisted review techniques
  * Applying advanced analytics to accelerate insight generation, triage issues, and prioritize investigative actions
  * Helping organizations remediate issues and strengthen controls through data‑informed root cause analysis


Our approach emphasizes defensibility, speed, and transparency—helping organizations move forward with confidence.

## Managing third‑party and contract compliance risk
### Visibility and control across an expanding ecosystem
Third parties—from patient support vendors to distributors—play a critical role in life sciences operations, while also introducing regulatory and financial risk. Managing this ecosystem requires continuous, insight‑driven oversight.
KPMG helps organizations:
  * Assess compliance risk before engaging third parties
  * Apply data analytics and AI‑enabled screening to identify elevated risk relationships
  * Monitor vendor adherence to contractual and regulatory requirements
  * Conduct targeted compliance audits across commercial and patient‑facing programs
  * Design remediation plans informed by risk prioritization and predictive insights


Our services help organizations proactively manage risk across complex, global third‑party networks—moving from periodic reviews to continuous monitoring.

## Digitizing compliance operations
### Technology that works the way compliance teams work
Manual processes, disconnected systems, and spreadsheet‑driven workflows limit the effectiveness of compliance programs. KPMG helps life sciences organizations modernize compliance through AI‑enabled, purpose‑built technology solutions that embed intelligence directly into day‑to‑day operations.
Our digital offerings support areas such as:
  * HCP engagement and transparency reporting
  * 340B compliance
  * Intelligent commercial contracting automation
  * Health authority inquiry management with workflow automation and analytics
  * Enterprise risk management with real‑time dashboards and predictive insights


These solutions are designed to increase efficiency, improve data quality, and deliver real‑time insight—freeing compliance teams to focus on higher‑value, strategic work.
