Vendor and Technology Selection
### Vendor and Technology Selection
Purchasing large and complex systems directly affect how organisations conduct business, as they require significant investments in time, money, and employee trainings. Additionally, organisations must choose a solution that not only satisfies current needs but also supports future growth.
Software solutions must be scalable to support ever increasing volumes and complexity of data from business growth. This makes the software selection process critical to laying the groundwork for a successful implementation for the long term. Spending the time to ‘do it right’ the first time can save time, money, and increase employee satisfaction. We work with you to decide on a customised level of services which include Requirement Analysis, RFP Development, Vendor & Technology Selection and Implementation Support. 
Technology Implementation
### Technology Implementation
Our Advisory services will help you in the implementation of technology that is aligned with our HR function service delivery model and the needs of your employees. With our extensive experience in the implementation of Software as a Service, or SaaS, and on-premise solutions in SAP and Workday, we will enable you to leverage on leading practices using cloud technology. With a global perspective, our approach will focus on designing simple, standardised and sustainable solutions. 
Data Migration and System Integration
### Data Migration and System Integration
Defining your HR system landscape such that it is aligned with the overall solution architecture is imperative. Leveraging on our pre-built system integration solutions, our experts provide you with just the guidance you need to ensure seamless integration of processes and data across different systems.
Reduce time and resources while increasing precision by using automated tools for data migration which can be used at every step of the process- Extraction, Transformation and Loading. Our global presence will enable you to gain value from both our local and global capabilities. 
Data Intelligence and Workforce Analytics
### Data Intelligence and Workforce Analytics
We connect data assets to derive critical insights that have a potential to positively impact your business. We provide a comprehensive solution to drive data-driven innovation and intelligence across the enterprise, unifying scalable enterprise AI and intelligent information management systems. Reliable real-time data and multidisciplinary HR dashboards ensure better decision making and talent management. Allow technology to develop a performance driven culture in your organization. 
Application Maintenance and Support
### Application Maintenance and Support
Managing your business ‘as usual’, becomes even more critical when operating in a cloud environment. With a constant focus on functional solutions and business process knowledge, alongside mandatory patching cycles and multiple releases per year, the capabilities and responsibilities of your application management services team become essential to help you navigate the changes. Thus, our AMS package combines all the essential activities required for a seamless operation of applications on the cloud while focusing on continual service improvement.
Our AMS package is built with a blend of structure and flexibility to ensure great experience and value for the client through continual service improvement. We will bring to the table, our full suite of cloud AMS activities along with dedicated resources for Change Management, Release Management, Problem Management and Ticketing Intelligence.
