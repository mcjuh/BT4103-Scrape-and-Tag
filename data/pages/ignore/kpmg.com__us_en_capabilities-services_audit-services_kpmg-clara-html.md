# AI‑enabled. Human‑led. Audit quality—delivered through KPMG Clara AI
KPMG Clara is our global intelligent platform where governed agents support continuous analysis and data‑intensive procedures, so auditors can focus on judgment, skepticism, and complex risk.​ 
##  Quality and risk—architected with KPMG Clara AI 
KPMG Clara sharpens audit focus through automated, population-level analysis. The agents document their reasoning at every step, creating a clear, auditable trail. This transparency empowers auditors to supervise results, easily verify the process, challenge anomalies, and apply critical human professional judgment where it matters most.
