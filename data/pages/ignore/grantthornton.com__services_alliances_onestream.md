# Modernize finance with OneStream
## How we work with OneStream
Grant Thornton is a OneStream Global Advisory System Integration Partner, delivering strategic solutions that onboard and integrate OneStream’s platform into your business operations. Here’s how Grant Thornton and OneStream work together to help companies optimize their corporate performance management with a focus on quality:
### OneStream provides
  * A unified financial platform to simplify and streamline corporate performance management processes such as planning, financial close and consolidation, reporting and analytics, account reconciliation, tax provisioning, customer and product profitability
  * Financial and operational insights to support faster and more informed decision-making
  * A cloud-based solution designed to evolve and scale with your organization


### Grant Thornton provides
  * A dedicated OneStream practice with certified professionals who have an average of 18 years of process and technology experience and more than 5 years of OneStream experience
  * Specializations in providing strategic direction, system implementation and managed services support
  * Proven experience in transformation implementation for enterprise applications, even for companies that have distributed and decentralized management, and multiple ERP transactional systems, but need to view comprehensive financial, customer and product content
  * Personal collaboration to help you meet changing business objectives, gain operational efficiencies, improve performance and transform data into action


Together, Grant Thornton and OneStream will help you digitally transform and modernize financial operations. It’s just one of the [**strategic alliances**](https://www.grantthornton.com/services/alliances) that help Grant Thornton drive more value for our customers.
