# A roadmap to auditing cloud security
December 01, 2025 
As organizations accelerate their adoption of hybrid and multi-cloud environments, internal audit functions face mounting challenges in keeping pace with evolving security risks. This article from The Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA) and Grant Thornton offers a practical roadmap for auditing cloud security, emphasizing unified visibility, robust identity governance, and proactive risk management. It explores how internal audit can strengthen assurance in AI-powered ecosystems and outlines key frameworks and strategies for chief audit executives to build resilient, secure operations in the cloud era.
