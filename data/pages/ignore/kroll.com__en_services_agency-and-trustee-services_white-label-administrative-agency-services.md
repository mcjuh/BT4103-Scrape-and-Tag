# White-Label Administrative Agency Services
Expert white-label services customized to meet your administrative agency needs, from modular solutions to full-service operations.
When you engage Kroll for white label administrative agency services, you remove the burden of managing your loan portfolio yet retain full oversight. Kroll offers a wide range of these services, from a modular to a full-service extension of your operations. Our customizable white-label administrative agency services allow your operations team to spend more time analyzing your loan portfolio than managing it.
Using our cutting-edge technology, we maintain stringent controls on data integrity to safeguard all the information we manage for our clients. This involves adhering to detailed written operational procedures and continually providing adequate training and supervision to all staff members. Task ownership and accountability are essential to our success.
Additionally, we maintain a segregated cash/payments team within operations. The team is staffed by payments experts with many years of experience.
