## Ensure Your Projects Stay on Budget
At AESG, our Cost Management Services are designed to provide accurate, reliable, and strategic financial oversight for your projects. Whether you’re embarking on a new development, refurbishing an existing asset, or conducting a financial feasibility study, our experienced team ensures your projects are delivered within budget and aligned with your financial goals.
## Our Cost Management Services
Pre-Contract Cost Management  Post-Contract Cost Management  Specialist Services 
## Pre-Contract Cost Management
## Pre-Contract Cost Management ensures efficient budget allocation and cost control from project inception.
### Feasibility Estimating and Strategic Advice
## Feasibility Estimating and Strategic Advice provides crucial insights for informed decision-making in construction projects.
### Risk Management
## Potential financial threats and uncertainties in construction projects are mitigated through proactive management.
### Budget Validation
## Budget Validation confirms the accuracy and reliability of project cost estimates.
### Change Management
## Modifications to project scope, timeline, or budget are efficiently handled to minimise disruptions.
### Cost Modelling
## Detailed financial projections are created to optimise project expenses and enhance financial performance.
### Procurement Strategy and Contract Advice
## Resource acquisition and contractual agreements are optimised to ensure smooth project execution.
### Cost Planning
## Comprehensive strategies are developed to manage expenses throughout the construction lifecycle, ensuring cost-effectiveness.
### Tender Action (Documentation, Management, Evaluation, Negotiation, Recommendation)
## A thorough process is implemented for selecting contractors, including documentation, management, evaluation, negotiation, and recommendation.
### Benchmarking (both design criteria and cost)
## Industry standards are used to compare design criteria and costs, ensuring competitive pricing and optimal value.
### Bills of Quantities (BOQ)
## Detailed itemised lists of materials, parts, and labor are provided for accurate cost estimation and project planning.
### Value Engineering
## Cost-effective alternatives are identified without compromising quality or functionality, enhancing project efficiency.
## Post-Contract Cost Management
## Our team implements strategic processes to manage and regulate costs associated with construction projects after the contract has been signed, ensuring the project stays within the approved budget while maintaining quality standards.
### Contract Administration
## We oversee contracts between all parties involved in the construction project, handling documentation, terms, conditions, and changes to ensure obligations are fulfilled and risks are managed effectively.
### Cost Reporting
## Detailed documentation and analysis of the project's financial aspects are provided, offering insights into current costs and projections of future expenses to guide decision-making and maintain fiscal health. 
### Change Control
## A structured process is implemented for assessing, documenting, and implementing changes in the project, minimising impacts on timeline, budget, and objectives while maintaining transparency.
### Claims Management
## Our expertise helps minimise the impact of external factors on your budget by formally tracking all contract variations and keeping you informed of your forecast to completion.
### Valuation and Payment
## Interim valuations are conducted to accurately assess the value of completed works, ensuring payments align with project progress and facilitating fair compensation for contractors, subcontractors, and suppliers.
### Final Account Negotiation and Settlement
## We assist in the negotiation and settlement of the final account, ensuring all costs are accurately accounted for and any disputes are resolved efficiently to close out the project successfully.
