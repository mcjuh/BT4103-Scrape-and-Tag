BDO presents a highlights summary of the significant cost-of-living adjustments (COLA) effective for 2026. These adjustments recently announced by the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and the Social Security Administration (SSA) have a wide-ranging impact, including the savings rate for retirement plans.
