# Automate, integrate and optimize
Integrated risk management and compliance program delivery, with KPMG and Workiva
[ Download PDF ](https://kpmg.com/kpmg-us/content/dam/kpmg/pdf/2022/workiva-integrated-risk-compliance.pdf)
Share
Organizations turning to a risk management solution typically underestimate the complexities associated with implementation and integration with the existing processes and technology environments. Successfully transitioning a functional area to technology is dependent on establishing key elements at the project’s onset, such as goals and objectives, process and data evaluation, proper change management, and technology enablement activities.
KPMG LLP (KPMG) and Workiva can help clients address these challenges through a broad technology-enabled solution that combines the leading risk and compliance frameworks of KPMG and the AI-powered Workiva platform. The established approach of KPMG for risk management and compliance program development and enhancement includes developing a vision and aligning risks, controls, and reporting processes, as well as training resources.
The holistic implementation and transformation capabilities of KPMG coupled with the innovative Workiva AI-powered platform can help our clients efficiently and effectively satisfy their requirements to achieve their risk and compliance objectives.
