## [Insights](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/cobie-management/#Insights)
Effective COBie (Construction Operations Building information exchange) management is essential for ensuring accurate, structured, interoperable & meaningful asset data is produced at handover for use by facility & asset management teams. AESG’s COBie Management service streamlines the process of defining, collecting, validating, and delivering asset information using DATA+, our in-house digital information management platform. Our process-driven approach ensures compliance with industry standards such as ISO 19650 while enabling seamless integration with BIM Models and CAFM systems.
## Key Services
[ COBie Information Delivery Plan ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/cobie-management/#collapse-7abe8626aa0680421aec)
We define the framework for COBie implementation, ensuring a structured approach for data exchange across the delivery of the project.
[ Asset Information Matrix ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/cobie-management/#collapse-203a7c16aa0680421aec)
We prepare a project-specific LOI (Level of Information) matrix that summarises the 5 Ws (i.e., who, what, when, where and why) for the development and production of COBie data across all asset types.
[ Asset Data Templates ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/cobie-management/#collapse-daeab306aa0680421aec)
Standardised data templates are created for each asset type to capture the relevant COBie information efficiently.
[ COBie Data Integration ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/cobie-management/#collapse-189b8c46aa0680421aec)
Oversee COBie data integration and validation within the BIM Models for each information exchange.
DATA+ Information Management Platform
## Data+ is AESG’s web-based information management platform that serves as a central source of truth and offers a secure, collaborative environment for all stakeholders and project teams for all COBie information. By adopting a process-driven, user-friendly interface, we empower projects to produce accurate and validated information from design to operation aligned with ISO 19650.
### Automated COBie Data Validation to reduce errors and enhance accuracy.
### Integration of COBie data with BIM models & facility management software for interoperability.
### Federation of COBie data for centralised and structured asset information management.
By implementing COBie best practices, automated validation, and structured data management, AESG ensures that project teams, asset owners, and facility managers receive high-quality, meaningful asset information to support long-term operational efficiency.
