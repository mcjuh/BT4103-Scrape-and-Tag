# Smart operations for energy and chemicals
## Enhancing digital automation for a competitive advantage
Amid an unprecedented global energy transition, energy and chemicals companies are seeking out industry experience and capabilities to help optimize operations, enhance efficiency, and reduce costs—all while maintaining safety and reliability.

[ Share  ](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/industries/energy/articles/smart-operations-in-energy-and-chemicals-industry-expertise.html# "Share")
That’s why energy and chemicals companies turn to Deloitte. We combine technology, data, and experience to help your business operations work smarter. Utilizing our IndustryAdvantage™ framework, your business can unlock the full potential of its technology investments through targeted transformation.
  * **End-to-end approach:** Leveraging multidisciplinary industry experience and a distinct toolset, we can design, implement, and run custom solutions tailored to your company’s specific needs. Across the value chain, we support your company at each step on its digital transformation journey—helping you drive growth and competitive advantage.
  * **Assets and ecosystems:** We utilize our talent, experience, and network of relationships to address complex operational challenges. Clients can test solutions in Deloitte’s secure, simulated environments, minimizing risk before real-world implementation.
  * **Innovation:** Deloitte can help companies in the energy and chemicals industry transform their operations—from smart utilities to control centers to connected aftermarkets—using cutting-edge solutions and technologies like AI that create data-driven decision-making.
  * **Sustainability:** Deloitte embeds sustainability and integrates it throughout the value chain. This includes helping clients make better capital allocation decisions to improve asset economics and lessen the environmental impact through waste reduction and energy efficiency.
