At AESG Australia, we are dedicated to helping organisations reduce their carbon footprint and support a more sustainable future. Our Whole Life Carbon Strategy services offer solutions to assess, manage, and minimise carbon emissions throughout the entire lifecycle of a project or asset. By aligning with Australia’s environmental goals and implementing innovative strategies, we empower our clients to achieve significant carbon reductions and enhance their overall sustainability performance.
## Key Services
## Ensuring strategies comply with Australian regulations on waste reduction and resource efficiency.
Whole Life Carbon Assessment  Circular Economy Studies  Energy Strategies  Carbon Management Strategy and Design Integration 
### Whole Life Carbon Assessment
We conduct detailed Whole Life Carbon Assessments to measure the total greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions associated with a project or asset over its entire lifecycle, from material extraction to end-of-life disposal.
#### Embodied Carbon Analysis
Assessing emissions from raw material extraction, manufacturing, transportation, and construction.
#### Carbon Hotspot Identification
Pinpointing areas with the highest emissions to prioritise reduction efforts.
#### Operational Carbon Assessment
Evaluating emissions from energy use during the operational phase.
#### Reporting and Compliance
Preparing reports in line with Australian standards, such as the Green Building Council of Australia (GBCA) guidelines, and international standards like ISO 14040/44.
#### End-of-Life Carbon Evaluation
Estimating emissions from demolition, waste processing, and recycling or disposal.
### Key Benefits
### A complete understanding of carbon emissions across the project lifecycle
### Compliance with Australian sustainability regulations and certifications
### Informed decision-making for design and material choices
### Contribution to Australia’s Net Zero Emissions target by 2050
### Circular Economy Studies
Our Circular Economy Studies help organisations transition from a linear "take-make-dispose" model to a regenerative circular approach, maximising resource efficiency and minimising waste.
#### Material Flow Analysis
Mapping resource inputs and outputs to identify circular opportunities.
#### Secondary Material Integration
Incorporating recycled or reclaimed materials into projects.
#### Waste Reduction Strategies
## Developing plans to minimise waste during construction and operation.
#### Lifecycle Extension Strategies
Implementing practices to extend the useful life of assets and components.
#### Design for Disassembly and Reuse
Creating designs that enable easy deconstruction and material reuse at the end of an asset’s life.
### Key Benefits
### Reduced environmental impact through efficient resource use
### Cost savings from waste reduction and material reuse
### Compliance with Australia’s National Waste Policy and circular economy initiatives
### Enhanced sustainability credentials and market differentiation
### Energy Strategies
We develop tailored Energy Strategies that optimise energy performance, reduce consumption, and integrate renewable energy into projects.
#### Energy Audits and Benchmarking
Assessing current energy use and comparing it to industry best practices.
#### Energy Management Systems
Implementing systems to monitor and optimise energy use.
#### Energy Efficiency Measures
Identifying and implementing improvements in building systems, including HVAC, lighting, and controls.
#### Regulatory Compliance
Implementing battery solutions within buildings to optimise their storage capacity and ability to interact with the grid.
#### Renewable Energy Integration
Evaluating and incorporating solar, wind, or other renewable energy sources.
#### Regulatory Compliance
Ensuring adherence to the National Construction Code (NCC) Section J and state-specific energy efficiency standards.
### Key Benefits
### Lower operational energy costs
### Improved energy security and resilience
### Reduced carbon emissions from energy use
### Contribution to state and federal renewable energy targets
### Carbon Management Strategy and Design Integration
We help organisations develop Carbon Management Strategies integrated into the design and operational phases of their projects.
## Carbon Reduction Targets
Setting achievable goals aligned with Science-Based Targets and Australia’s climate commitments.
#### Carbon Offsetting Strategies
Identifying reputable offset projects to compensate for unavoidable emissions.
## Low-Carbon Design Solutions
Incorporating strategies such as passive design, material selection, and efficient systems to reduce emissions.
#### Monitoring and Reporting
Establishing processes to track and report carbon performance.
#### Supply Chain Engagement
Working with suppliers to source low-carbon materials and services.
### Key Benefits
### A systematic approach to reducing carbon emissions
### Improved competitiveness in a carbon-conscious market
### Enhanced ability to meet stakeholder and investor expectations
### Alignment with global best practices, such as the Carbon Neutral Standard for Buildings and Precincts
