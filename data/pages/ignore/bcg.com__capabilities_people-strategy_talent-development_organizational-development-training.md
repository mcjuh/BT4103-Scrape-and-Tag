# BCG U: A Capability-Building and Enablement Solution
BCG U delivers scalable, high-impact upskilling that enables enterprise-wide transformation. Grounded in learning science and strategic execution, our capability-building and enablement solution helps organizations build future-ready workforces—supporting sustained organizational talent that’s ready to deliver on strategic ambition.
Organizations today face compounding talent and transformation challenges: 74% struggle to scale GenAI value, 70% of transformations fall short, and 59% of employees will need reskilling by 2030. Meeting this moment requires more than strategy—it demands capability building at scale.BCG U unlocks the potential of organizations by deploying capability-building consulting experts who deeply understand each client’s industry, business model, and strategic ambition. The result of our work is a workforce that’s equipped with the high-impact capabilities necessary to create the new value required to activate [AI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence), accelerate transformation, and reskill workforces for the future.At the center of this work is BCG’s AI Fluency Ladder, which has proven itself across BCG’s 33,500-person workforce and can now help CEOs build AI readiness across their own organizations. The AI Fluency Ladder develops capabilities from foundational knowledge to the ability to build sophisticated AI systems. Each competency level assesses distinct, enduring skills, from basic AI literacy to full system ownership and deployment. Self-paced learning and hands-on practice build workforce value, with progress measured and certified along the way.

## How BCG U Works
BCG U uses a three-phase approach to deliver high-impact upskilling, starting with business priorities and working backward to design and scale contextualized learning experiences that drive results.
Expand All 
Design a Blueprint for Business Value
We design tailored capability-building and enablement strategies that are aligned to business goals, defining the foundation for impact:
  * Define key outcomes for each program.
  * Segment learner personas based on role-specific needs.
  * Create a roadmap for capability development.


Build and Configure
Our capability-building consulting experts develop efficient, relevant experiences that are tailored to an organization’s executive context and strategic challenges:
  * Design learning journeys by role and level, defining content, modality, and delivery.
  * Build and configure learning assets and delivery tools.


Deliver and Measure
We launch programs with real-time monitoring and governance, ensuring ongoing impact:
  * Roll out phased implementation across an organization.
  * Implement tracking tools and set regular impact review meetings.
  * Measure business outcomes and adjust in order to maximize ROI.


This approach is turbocharged by the BCG U Platform, which combines world-class industry experts and learning specialists with a best-in-class technology ecosystem and learning science. This organizational talent development platform enables us to rapidly create and seamlessly deliver contextualized, high-quality capability-building and enablement programs that generate measurable business impact with speed, at scale, and clear ROI. It also powers our suite of specialized “accelerators”—focused programs that fast-track skills in high-impact areas such as [GenAI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence/generative-ai), climate and sustainability, and [marketing](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/marketing-sales/overview)—enabling organizations to utilize our capability-building platform to build future-critical capabilities faster.
## Our Clients’ Success with High Impact Upskilling 
10x
reduction in learning time
To support a state agency in improving pediatric patient outcomes, a North American university partnered with BCG U and [BCG X](https://www.bcg.com/x/) to design, build, and launch a digital portal for 180,000 caregivers and families. Using a human-centered learning design, the university **reduced learning time from over 150 hours of dense academic content to just 15 hours** of bite-sized, digestible, and engaging content.
69%
increase in AI adoption
To accelerate AI adoption, a global health care company partnered with BCG U’s capability-building consulting experts to build a scalable learning ecosystem. Tailored upskilling journeys equipped employees across roles with the tools and capabilities to apply new technologies to their daily work—**driving AI adoption from 18% to 87%** , and embedding it into the organization’s way of working.
2,000
professionals upskilled
Singapore’s digital-talent gap has been a key challenge for companies, often hindering the country’s ambition to be a tech hub. Together with SkillsFuture Singapore, BCG U set about shrinking that gap by launching a unique train-and-place program called Rapid and Immersive Skill Enhancement. As of early 2023**, the program had upskilled more than 2,000 mid-career Singaporean professionals** , enabling them to pivot their career paths into in-demand data and digital roles. The upskilling training program is on track to teach at least 8,000 more over the next two years.
90%
increase in tool utilization 
A global consumer goods company partnered with BCG U to drive enterprise-wide adoption of proprietary AI tools. By activating senior leaders as champions and delivering targeted enablement across teams, **the company achieved a 90% increase in AI tool utilization.**
