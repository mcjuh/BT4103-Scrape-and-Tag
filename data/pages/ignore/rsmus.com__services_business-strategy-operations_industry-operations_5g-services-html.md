## Unlock the power of 5G
5G is the innovative platform that virtually connects everyone and everything. Advancements in 5G have transformed our lives, economy, jobs, industries and even our communities. In today’s world, 5G goes well beyond mobile communications, with the power to connect the world around us and create potent new business opportunities.
Compared to its predecessors, 5G technologies unleash exceptionally high-speed connectivity, a fully realized Internet of Things services framework and lower latency (regardless of the user’s location). These advancements are needed to support immersive applications, with both spectrum and energy efficiency, as well as a uniform quality of experience.
Immersive services are fundamentally revolutionizing health care, education, entertainment and other industry sectors, with virtual reality, augmented reality, autonomous vehicles, cellular-to-everything connectivity, robots and artificial intelligence creating new dynamics for humans and machines, and automated end-to-end manufacturing processes.
