#  Financial Services Built to Scale Safely 
Scaling financial services requires systems that protect compliance, security and trust
[](https://www.publicissapient.com/industries/financial-services#connect)
## The realities of financial services
Financial services organizations operate under constant pressure to modernize legacy systems, meet regulatory requirements and deliver better customer experiences while managing risk at scale.
### Legacy systems limit progress
Embedded business logic and aging infrastructure slow innovation, increase costs and make modernization complex across sectors.
### AI pilots fail to scale
Many institutions experiment with AI but struggle to move beyond pilots due to fragmented data, governance concerns and lack of enterprise-wide orchestration.
### Risk and compliance are required
Any change must meet strict regulatory, security and audit standards, making speed and innovation difficult without the right controls in place.

## How financial services organizations apply AI across the business
### Modernize core banking platforms
Decouple business processes from monolithic cores to enable faster delivery, improved reliability and cleaner architecture.
### Unify data for personalized banking
Unify customer data for personalized engagement, proactive risk alerts and more confident decision-making across products.
### Interpret investment guidelines at speed
Standardize how investment mandates are interpreted to flag risks, reduce errors and ensure compliance across portfolios.
### Streamline lending operations
Automate post-approval lending workflows to reduce time to cash, improve accuracy and handle exceptions efficiently.
### Enable real-time payments
Move from batch to real-time processing to support instant settlement, better liquidity management and regulatory compliance.
### Automate back-office operations
Apply AI to reconciliations, fraud checks and claims to lower costs, reduce errors and speed cycle times.
