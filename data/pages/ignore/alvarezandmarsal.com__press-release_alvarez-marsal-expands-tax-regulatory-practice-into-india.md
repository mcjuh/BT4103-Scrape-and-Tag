# ALVAREZ & MARSAL EXPANDS TAX & REGULATORY PRACTICE INTO INDIA
**MUMBAI, INDIA, 30 July 2026** : [Alvarez & Marsal](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/) (A&M), a leading global professional services firm, is scaling its global Tax & Regulatory practice into India, reinforcing its position in one of the world's most complex and high-growth markets for international investors and financial institutions. 
The firm launches with a founding team of five Managing Directors spanning international tax, M&A and PE taxation, transfer pricing, indirect tax, transaction advisory, and regulatory structuring — with a vision to create a best-in-class Tax practice. 
## **Aggressive Growth in a High-Priority Market**
India represents one of A&M’s most significant strategic bets. The firm’s expansion reflects its broader commitment to bringing operator-led, execution-focused advisory to markets where regulatory and transactional complexity demands more than conventional advice. A&M’s model — senior practitioners leading every engagement, free from audit conflict constraints — offers a distinctive value proposition to clients navigating India’s fast-evolving tax and regulatory environment.
The India build-out is part of A&M’s wider global expansion. The firm’s growth strategy is anchored in attracting premier talent who seek an entrepreneurial environment — where client outcomes and value delivery are the priority. 
[_**Ernesto Perez**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/ernesto-perez) _, Managing Director and Global Tax Practice Leader, Alvarez & Marsal, said, “India demands advice that is technically rigorous and commercially grounded — and this team is built to deliver exactly that. We are just getting started.”_
[_**Himanshu Bajaj**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/himanshu-bajaj) _, Managing Director & Head – Alvarez & Marsal India and GCC, said, “Our clients need advisors who can move at the pace of India's evolving market — with the expertise and judgment to execute. The ambition here is significant, and we're building accordingly.”_
[_**Keyur Shah**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/keyur-shah-0) _, Managing Director and Head of Tax – India, Alvarez & Marsal, said, “A&M's entrepreneurial culture and commitment to senior-led delivery is what drew me here — the opportunity to build something meaningful in a market where technical depth and commercial pragmatism genuinely matter.”_
[_**James Badenach**_](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/james-badenach) _, Managing Director and Head of Tax, Asia Pacific, Alvarez & Marsal, said, "India is a large and complex market, and our ambition is to add real value where clients need it most. This team gives us the depth to do exactly that."_
## **Founding Team**
A&M’s India Tax & Regulatory practice is led by five Managing Directors:
[**Keyur Shah**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/keyur-shah-0), Managing Director and Head of Tax & Regulatory Practice — leads the practice with over three decades of experience in international and corporate taxation, tax structuring, and regulatory advisory for global financial services clients.
[**Hasina Chhil**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/hasina-chhil), Managing Director — specializes in RBI licensing, India entry and exit strategies, and tax compliance for banks, NBFCs, fintech companies, and investment funds, with more than 20 years of experience.
[**Monika Arora**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/monika-arora), Managing Director — a New Delhi-based indirect tax specialist with more than 24 years of experience advising on GST, customs, compliance automation, and controversy management across manufacturing, e-commerce, and financial services.
[**Vishal Hakani**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/vishal-hakani), Managing Director — brings over 22 years of M&A tax and transaction advisory experience, with a particular focus on private equity, joining from Deloitte India.
[**Amod Khare**](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/amod-khare), Managing Director — a transfer pricing and international tax specialist with more than 30 years of experience advising multinationals across technology, financial services, and pharmaceuticals.
A&M Tax’s India launch is part of a sustained period of significant investment in the formation of a truly global tax platform. In recent months, A&M Tax has entered several key European markets, including [Italy](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/press-release/alvarez-marsal-launches-tax-practice-in-italy-appoints-valentina-santini-to-lead), [Sweden](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/press-release/alvarez-marsal-expands-tax-practice-to-sweden) and [Ireland](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/press-release/alvarez-marsal-tax-expands-into-ireland-with-senior-leadership-appointments). This growth reflects A&M Tax’s strategy to offer clients fully integrated, conflict-free, global solutions.
A&M’s Tax expansion in India was also covered by International Tax Review - [Read the article here](https://www.internationaltaxreview.com/article/2gkapl51d87lshf43hd6o/direct-tax/large-complex-difficult-a-m-tax-leaders-reveal-india-expansion-challenges).
