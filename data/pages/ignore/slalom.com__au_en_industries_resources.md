## Our energy & natural resource  _expertise_
#### Agriculture 
New reports showing the surge in agricultural input prices are raising concerns about global food security. The industry shifting focus to reducing food waste and loss while still fighting supply issues means investing in technology and infrastructure. We’re here to help tackle these ambitious projects and enable long-term success.
#### Energy & utilities
The global power and utilities sector faces historic transformation as decarbonization accelerates, electrification drives demand; and AI pressures the grid. Slalom partners with energy leaders using a people-centric, data and outcomes-driven approach, aligning vision with analytics, cloud, and AI to unlock value while balancing reliability, affordability, and sustainability. We put both customers and employees at the heart of the energy transition.
#### Environmental services 
We have a responsibility and opportunity to do good in the world, including bringing critical awareness to how organisations impact both people and the planet. We consciously consider the entire business footprint to guide sustainable growth and build resilience, driving change that will positively impact communities for generations. 
#### Natural resources 
In today’s changing environment, natural resource companies face immense pressure to prioritise the greater good. With workplace transformation, transitions to new technologies, and new regulations to consider, we can help navigate change, improve operations, and drive efficiency on a global scale.

The Power Up Podcast is a show where we shine a light on the people, trends, and technology transforming the power and utilities industry.
