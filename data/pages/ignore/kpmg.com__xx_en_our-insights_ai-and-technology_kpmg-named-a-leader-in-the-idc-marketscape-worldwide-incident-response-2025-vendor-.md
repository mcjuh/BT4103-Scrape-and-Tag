## KPMG Incident Response
KPMG firms approach to incident response (IR) seeks to transcend transactional engagements. KPMG firms work with clients through the cyber crisis lifecycle—from initial detection and containment to regulatory response, recovery, and long-term resilience. Our methodology is designed to be comprehensive, and integrates digital forensics, crisis management, legal and regulatory advisory, e-discovery, and recovery services, all delivered by a global network of member firms spanning more than 140 countries and territories.
