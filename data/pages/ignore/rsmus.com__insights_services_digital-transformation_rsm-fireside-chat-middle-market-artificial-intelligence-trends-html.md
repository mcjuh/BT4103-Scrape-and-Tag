# RSM fireside chat: Unveiling middle market AI trends and challenges
### Q&A with chief data officers navigating the AI landscape in real time
October 16, 2024
Generative AI Artificial intelligence Digital transformation
Artificial intelligence (AI) is revolutionizing industries, creating new opportunities for insight, efficiency and innovation. Yet, the pressure to keep pace with AI advancements can challenge companies to either innovate quickly or risk falling behind.
Access our on-demand fireside chat where we’ll dive deep into the findings from the new RSM Middle Market AI Survey 2024: U.S. and Canada. This session features a discussion on AI trends, challenges and opportunities facing midsized companies, followed by a live Q&A with chief data officers who are navigating the AI landscape in real time.
Key fireside chat topics will include:
  * How the middle market is successfully leveraging AI
  * Perspectives on the future of and the risks associated with generative AI
  * Insightful perspectives on AI strategies and maturity in the middle market


### Why view this presentation?
Whether you're just beginning your AI journey or refining your strategy, this session will offer actionable insights to help your organization navigate the evolving AI landscape.
**Presented by:**
  * George Casey, RSM US LLP
  * Sawan Dhaliwal, RSM Canada
  * Tarun Sood, American Century Investments
  * Greg Townsend, AltaMed Health Services
