KPMG is proud to announce its recognition as a strong performer by Forrester in their report entitled**The Forrester Wave™: Digital Transformation Services, Q3 2025.**
KPMG was specifically recognized for its “clear and differentiated vision, backed by an ambitious innovation roadmap", and for standing out "when it comes to transformation governance and risk management" in digital transformation with the new KPMG Velocity offering. This prestigious recognition, coupled with our continued multi-million—dollar U.S investment in KPMG Velocity, powerfully affirms KPMG firms’ unwavering commitment to industry—centric innovation, excellence and client success. It underscores KPMG professionals dedication to providing digital transformation services and our ability to help clients achieve tangible outcomes from AI — both agentic and generative AI — in today’s dynamic business landscape.

## KPMG Velocity: Empowering intelligent, agile and resilient organizations
KPMG Velocity is an AI—first way of working designed to help organizations change and thrive in today’s disrupted world. It provides AI-enabled products and services through a platform ecosystem that integrates KPMG insights, methods, experience, capabilities and data with technology. It is designed to empower clients to build and operate intelligent, agile and resilient organizations, capable of sustaining the next level of growth and value creation.
From transaction to transformation, KPMG Velocity is designed to offer a broad-ranging approach to digital transformation, helping to accelerate client journeys to achieve their outcomes. This includes strategy development, innovation leadership, AI—enabled products and services, transformation governance, and risk management.
KPMG’s Strengths Recognized by The Forrester WaveTM
  * **Strategy:** "KPMG has a clear and differentiated vision, backed by an ambitious innovation roadmap. It’s investing heavily in its Velocity platform, which supports clients in building digital foundations while driving AI adoption and resiliency." 
  * **Capabilities:** "KPMG’s capabilities stand out from the competition when it comes to transformation governance and risk management. KPMG currently shines with outstanding skills in post-merger engagements and customer experience transformation." 
  * **Customer Feedback:** "Reference clients applaud KPMG’s transformation governance and risk mitigation capabilities, especially for complex post-merger engagements in regulated industries." 
  * **Forrester’s Take:** "KPMG will appeal to organizations that want to drive AI adoption with the support of an integrated service and platform delivery model."
