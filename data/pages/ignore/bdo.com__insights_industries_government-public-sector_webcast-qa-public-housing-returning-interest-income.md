# Webcast Q&A – Public Housing: Returning Interest Income 
Article 3 min read
  * PRINT 
  * 

The following represents the questions we received and the answers we provided on our webcast “Public Housing: Returning Interest Income” held on May 21, 2025. 
### 
Is there any regulation or reason to do anything other than the First Dollar Accounting Concept?
add
This is not required by HUD and not specified in any regulation.
### 
Could cash management impact the FASS indicator for PHAs?
add
If HUD publishes guidance stating that PHAs must use operating reserves first, before drawing operating subsidy, then a PHA could see a significant impact on the FASS indicator.
### 
Is the interest earned on CDs subject to recapture?
add
Yes, the interest earned on CDs is subject to recapture if they contain grant revenue.
### 
Can you confirm that we should use operating subsidy for expenses first, then program income?
add
Currently, the PHA has the option to apply expenses to operating subsidy for program reserves first.
### 
If we draw down subsidy and expend it all, then still have expenses of $25K in the same month and we have program reserves of $35K, do we have to spend the $10K the next month before we draw subsidy down again?
add
No, you do not have to spend the $10K in the next month before drawing down subsidy again.
### 
Many banks charge fees and interest on funds – can you net the bank fees against interest before allocating the interest between subsidy and other?
add
A PHA must report interest income and expenses according to GAAP accounting.
### 
Do we need to have each CD account listed out on the General Depository Agreement or just bank accounts?
add
Both CD accounts and bank accounts need to be listed out on the General Depository Agreement. 
### 
Is there a specific deadline for remitting interest at a specific time of the year?
add
Once the audit is completed the interest should be submitted to HHS.
### 
How far back do we go to separate our subsidy and program income in our operating reserves?
add
24 CFR 990 states records must be retained for 5 years.
### 
Are administrative funds from CFP not included in this "subsidy" determination for interest calculation?
add
If administrative funds are being drawn and provided to the AMPs, these funds are considered expended on administrative costs.
### 
How do you determine how much of your reserves is operating subsidy or program income that has been accumulated over time?
add
An analysis would have to be completed to determine the funding sources.
### 
If my monthly expenses are greater than the 1/12 operating subsidy received, can I assume that $0 of my cash is operating subsidy?
add
Currently, yes, if monthly expenses are greater than the 1/12 operating subsidy received, $0 of cash is operating subsidy.
### 
If our total expenses are greater than subsidy received, would we still have to look at the timing of subsidy in, and expenses out?
add
If you use the annual approach of calculation of interest, then the answer is likely, no.
### 
Please clarify if we are calculating only on the subsidy received each month, not on operating reserves.
add
Calculation of interest should only be calculated on grant advances.
### 
We have investments that were started years ago, and nothing is being added to them – how do I know if they should be considered operating subsidy or program funds?
add
24 CFR 990 requires that records be retained for a period of 5 years. The PHA would need to determine how much grant revenue is accumulated in reserves.
### 
These examples are prospective reporting on interest income – how should PHAs address and report on any operating reserves in accounts from previous years?
add
The PHA would need to determine how much grant revenue is accumulated in reserves.
### 
Does the $500 apply to each AMP or the entire LIPH program?
add
The $500 applies to the entire LIPH program.
### 
Are gains on bond investments considered interest income?
add
Yes, the gains on bond investments are considered interest income.
### 
What about small housing authorities drawing their 1406 capital funds down during the year – would this count as operating subsidy?
add
When 1406 funds are transferred to the AMP, the PHA is not required to match expenses with subsidy and is considered fully expended.
### 
When I calculate this interest, do I use calendar year or fiscal year?
add
Interest is calculated using fiscal year. 
### 
Our public housing operating expenditures always exceed subsidy – do we have to retain funds subject to the $500 interest limit?
add
It depends on the interest calculation methodology. If you used an annual calculation methodology, this would make sense.
### 
As a fee accountant I have a client that is cash rich, great reserves, runs a great program. They do not draw subsidy down on a routine basis and typically, I am accruing an entire year of subsidy because it has not been drawn. When they do draw it in the future, can that be considered reimbursing prior years expenses?
add
It seems that program income is paying for expenses, so interest would be calculated on funds drawn from prior years.
### 
If we use the FYE balance method for interest calculations, can we pull FDS line items by category?
add
This would make sense if you were picking up actual cash outlays.
### 
Currently, we pay our bills on the 15th and the last day of the month. I have been drawing down operations at the beginning of each month. My understanding from this class is that we should move our payment schedule to the beginning of the month and the 15th of the month and I should also draw down the money a day or two before we pay our bills (always staying in the same month?)
add
You want to draw funds to best manage cash flow.
