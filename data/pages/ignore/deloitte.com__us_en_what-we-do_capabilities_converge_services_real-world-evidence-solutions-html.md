###  Faster, more trusted insight
####  Give teams the tools they need to work smarter with complex real-world data.
#####  How do you keep AI-driven RWE trustworthy and audit-ready? 
Our solutions are built with governance and scientific rigor as first principles, not afterthoughts.
  * RWE Agent keeps experts in the loop. Users review, refine, and approve medical codes and cohort logic before any query executes.
  * Every cohort definition, dataset generation step, and prompt is tracked and auditable, supporting reproducibility and the rigor that regulatory work requires.
  * DAE's configurable workflows formalize study design and execution, capturing concept approvals, prespecified protocols, and stakeholder sign-offs in one auditable system, supporting the transparency and reproducibility expectations that credible RWE depends on. 


#####  How do these solutions work with existing data infrastructures? 
Our solutions deploy directly within your environment, so your data stays under your control, and they're designed to meet organizations where they are, not require a clean-slate transformation.
  * RWE Agent works directly with data in its native formats, querying data in place within your existing data platforms, so nothing has to move to a separate environment. Analysis-ready datasets are surfaced right back into those platforms, where your teams can analyze them with their tool of choice.
  * DAE integrates with your existing data environment to surface assets already in your organization, reducing duplication without requiring migration.
  * Both solutions keep sensitive data inside your infrastructure, with nothing leaving your control. 


#####  How can these solutions help scale evidence generation? 
Together, our solutions remove the friction across the evidence generation process, streamlining the manual steps and accelerating the work that has traditionally made each study slow and resource-intensive, so teams can take on more, move faster, and generate evidence at scale.
  * RWE Agent generates rapid insights for quick decisions and produces analysis-ready datasets for formal studies, all through natural language, freeing scientists to spend their time on science rather than manual data preparation.
  * DAE helps teams find prior evidence and reuse approved cohort definitions and data assets across the organization, so no one starts from scratch and every study builds on the last.
  * HealthPrism Linked Data comes pre-linked, pre-certified, and ready to query. The patient-level linkage and HIPAA expert determination, both of which can take considerable time and effort to complete, are already done, so teams can begin work immediately rather than waiting on data preparation and certification.
