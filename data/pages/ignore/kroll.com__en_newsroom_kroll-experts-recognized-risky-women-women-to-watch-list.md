# Kroll Experts Recognized in Risky Women’s “Women to Watch” List
[Kroll](https://www.kroll.com/en/), the leading independent provider of global financial and risk advisory solutions, is proud to announce that five of its outstanding professional women have been recognized in [Risky Women’s](https://www.riskywomen.org/) _Women to Watch_ list announced in July 2025. This global initiative acknowledges and celebrates influential women leaders who are driving innovation and shaping the future of governance, risk, and compliance (GRC).
Released quarterly the [Women to Watch](https://www.riskywomen.org/profiles/page/2/?view=grid&5687=&5688=) list spotlights exceptional talent across the GRC landscape. Kroll is delighted to congratulate the following colleagues who have been recognized:
  * Rachel Posner, Chief Client Officer
  * Denyl Green, Managing Director, Cyber, Data and Resilience
  * Violet Ho, Senior Managing Director and Head of the Greater China Investigations, Diligence and Compliance Practice
  * Elizabeth M. Kay, Associate Managing Director, Investigations, Diligence and Compliance
  * Julia Wood, Managing Director and Head of the Revenue Office, Risk Advisory


This recognition reflects Kroll’s ongoing commitment to empowering women in the industry and creating pathways for leadership and impact. As a proud sponsor of Risky Women, Kroll continues to support initiatives that elevate women’s voices and showcase role models for the next generation of leaders in GRC.
Kroll continues to strengthen its partnership with Risky Women through exclusive industry events and Risky Women Radio, a podcast platform showcasing insights from leading voices. Zoë Newman, Regional Managing Director, EMEA and Global Co-Head of the Financial Investigations Practice shares her career journey and Kroll highlights on her episode _The Evolving Nature of Corporate Fraud_ , [listen here](https://www.riskywomen.org/2025/06/podcast-s8e8-evolving-nature-of-corporate-fraud-zoe-newman/).
David Blowers, President, Strategy at Kroll said, “The world of governance, risk and compliance, is complex, fast moving and a bedrock of global business. It is not easy to be successful in a field of work that requires rigour, via intense attention to detail, thoughtful solutions and continual horizon scanning. At Kroll, we are passionate about supporting diversity and inclusion in the GRC field, and we’re proud to celebrate the nomination of five of our talented women to Risky Women’s _Women to Watch_ list. Together, we’re building a more diverse and representative future for risk, regulation, and compliance.”
