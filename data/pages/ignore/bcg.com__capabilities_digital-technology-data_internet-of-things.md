# Internet of Things
Data shared by the Internet of Things (IoT) offers a powerful opportunity for businesses to design new customer offerings, deliver enhanced efficiencies, and provide unprecedented insight into how to deliver value. Our IoT consultants help unlock the next level of innovation. 
IoT leaders understand that, at its core, IoT is a fundamental component—and enabler—of [digital transformation](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/digital-transformation/overview). But winning with IoT means connecting more than just devices. It means linking your strategy with your digital journey; employing the right [emerging technologies](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/emerging-technologies/deep-tech), such as [artificial intelligence ](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence)(AI), [augmented reality](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/emerging-technologies/augmented-virtual-reality) (AR), and [blockchain](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/blockchain); collaborating with the right partners and digital ecosystems; and establishing new processes and ways of working.It’s a tall order, but by understanding the new IoT trends—such as better economics, edge computing, AI and AR, blockchain, and improved connectivity—and how best to leverage them, organizations of all types—businesses, governments, and nonprofits alike—will find that the possibilities for innovation and value are virtually boundless.

## Our Approach to IoT Adoption
To harness these trends—and create an IoT strategy that fuels true digital transformation—our IoT consulting team helps organizations consider a number of crucial questions:
  * Where do we build, and where do we buy, across IoT’s six-layer stack of devices, backbone, applications and analysis, security, communications, and services?
  * How do we identify the right partners and [digital ecosystems](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/digital-ecosystems)—because few companies will be able to do everything alone—for filling in gaps in our technical and business capabilities?
  * How do we adopt—and facilitate—new ways of working to establish cross-functional alignment and accountability?
  * How do we reengineer core business processes to capture the full value of IoT?
  * How do we develop or acquire the necessary talent, especially in such areas as analytics, AI, and software engineering?
  * How do we create a scalable technical infrastructure that ensures the fast deployment of new use cases?


BCG’s IoT consultants help organizations zero in on the answers—to unlock new revenue streams, efficiencies, and possibilities—using our IoT consulting approach that integrates strategy, technology, and human components to drive the best outcomes.

## We Drive IoT Adoption Across All Industries
Expand All 
Industrial Goods
  * **Automotive.** IoT enables powerful use cases from smart navigation and autonomous driving to mobility and electric vehicles.
  * **Engineered Products and Infrastructure.** IoT continues to enable use cases in aerospace and defense as well as machinery and industrial automation.
  * **Materials and Process Industries.** IoT has powered the industry with real-world data, especially in agribusiness.
  * **Transportation and Logistics.** The industry continues to leverage IoT data in the logistics, postal and parcel, rail, and shipping sectors.


Insurance
IoT allows insurance agencies to connect with customers in new ways and offer new pricing models based on data collected through sensors in a car.
Oil and Gas
IoTを利用すると、グリーンフィールドの資産にも、ブラウンフィールドの資産にも、まったく新しい無人オペレーションを取り入れることができます。こうして、経常経費、設備投資、リスクエクスポージャーを変えることができます。さらに、IoTの利用では、下流のB2CやB2Bにおいて、革新的な「つながる」ビジネスモデルが可能になります。
Technology, Media, and Telecommunications
  * **Technology.** Tech is a critical enabler for IoT, especially with regard to how IoT devices are managed and secured, as well as how data is collected and analyzed. This happens across the IoT stack from the device and the cloud backbone to apps and analytics.
  * **Telecommunications.** Telcos play a critical role in delivering data from device and cloud to applications. Both 5G and edge computing are important topics for realizing the value of IoT data.


Retail
IoT is a core component for trace-and-trace of vehicles for home delivery, customer experience, the physical retail space, and the connection with retail employers to ensure customer service is optimized.
Power and Utilities
IoT is a core component for trace-and-trace of vehicles for home delivery, customer experience, the physical retail space, and the connection with retail employers to ensure customer service is optimized.
Public Sector
IoT helps the public sector to transform outdated processes; improve operational efficiencies; improve transparency; and increase accountability among leadership, agencies, and citizens.
