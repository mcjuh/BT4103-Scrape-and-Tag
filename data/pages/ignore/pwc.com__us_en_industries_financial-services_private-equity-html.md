#  Private Equity 
## How PwC can help private equity firms
Private equity firms need a cash culture and an integrated plan for managing funds, improving portfolio company performance and maintaining flawless transactions.
PwC takes an integrated approach — drawing on deep private equity expertise to help identify and resolve challenges across both the transaction and portfolio lifecycle, including those unique to the fund itself.
The private equity world is complex — we help make it clearer. PwC brings the insights and experience to turn today’s challenges into opportunities, supporting some of the industry’s largest and most impactful deals.
