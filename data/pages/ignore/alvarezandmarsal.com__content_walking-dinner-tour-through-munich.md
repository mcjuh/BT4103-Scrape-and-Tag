# A Walking Dinner Tour Through Munich
In May, the Alvarez & Marsal Alumni Network gathered alumni and colleagues in Munich for the annual event. This year included a walk and dinner, reconnecting through a shared evening in the city.
The evening began with aperitifs and prosecco at the Bayerischer Hof, where guests reconnected over drinks before joining a guided walk through Munich’s historic city center. Attendees then moved to Masi Wine Bar on Maximilianstrasse for dinner and concluded at Spice Bazaar, where dessert and cocktails gave guests the chance to relax, reminisce, and build new connections. 
Hosted by Managing Directors [Steffen Kroner](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/search/steffen%20kroner?) ([Private Equity Performance Improvement](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/private-equity-performance-improvement)) and [Christoph Hoerauf](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/our-people/search/Christoph%20Hoerauf?) ([Disputes & Investigations](https://www.alvarezandmarsal.com/expertise/disputes-investigations)), the event brought the alumni community together to strengthen relationships, expand networks and reconnect current and former colleagues, highlighting the enduring bonds and shared experiences that make the A&M Alumni Network so special.
