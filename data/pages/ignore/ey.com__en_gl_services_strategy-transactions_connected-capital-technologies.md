### Secure better insights throughout the entire value chain with our AI-powered strategy and M&A technology solutions.
In today’s fast-paced world, confidently shaping your future means crafting strategies that are as dynamic and intelligent as the opportunities you face.
The AI-powered EY-Parthenon Edge suite of technologies combines sector data and decades of industry experience to help create value and deliver results across strategy, transactions and transformation.
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  Competitive Edge: AI-led M&A and strategic intelligence platform
Competitive Edge draws upon leading artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities to provide 360-degree insights and a real-time view of opportunities, risks and resilience considerations to form the heart of your strategic planning.
Competitive Edge:
    * Finds financial company data and transaction history
    * Queries company filings and news
    * Analyzes capital flows across sectors
    * Identifies targets using custom taxonomies
Find out more about [Competitive Edge.](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/competitive-edge)
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  Diligence Edge: AI Diligence Platform
Diligence Edge is an end-to-end integrated suite of tools used across the transaction lifecycle, utilizing automation and AI to accelerate data review and analysis, surface key risks, red flag items and highlight tailored deal insights.
Diligence Edge can help:
    * Highlight insights and improve M&A diligence processes
    * Bring together diverse data sources — from financial data direct from ERP platforms to social media analytics — enabling M&A practitioners to take a big-picture view of the due diligence deal process
Find out more about [Diligence Edge](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/dilligence-edge).
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  Capital Edge: M&A transaction platform
Capital Edge streamlines transaction and transformation processes using more than 30 purpose-built apps across engagement accelerators, leading practices and methodologies to drive better outcomes.
Capital Edge offers:
    * Support for buyers or sellers to develop scenario modeling, forecasting, financial and commercial planning
    * Support for project management, advanced analytics and data accelerators with real-time global collaboration in a live deal or post-deal scenario
Find out more about [Capital Edge](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy/ey-capital-edge-mergers-acquisitions-transaction-platform).
Read More  Read Less 
  * ###  Advanced Analytics
Our tech-enabled Advanced Analytics teams help turn information into connected intelligent insights (using AI), empowering you to make better informed decisions.
Businesses are faced with complex challenges and significant opportunities in today’s data-saturated world. EY-Parthenon advisors harness the power of advanced analytics to provide intelligent data-driven insights at optimized speed to help clients prepare for a better tomorrow.
From corporate strategy to capital allocation to M&A, EY-Parthenon’s Advanced Analytics teams underpin and inform planning through to execution to help create bespoke, sector-driven value for clients. This helps clients find better answers to some of their toughest questions — and sometimes uncovers questions they didn’t know they needed to ask themselves.
Find out more about [Advanced analytics](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/services/strategy-transactions/advanced-analytics).
