#  2024 RESPONSIBLE CARE® AND SUSTAINABILITY CONFERENCE AND EXPO
6 – 9 May 2024 
Miami, FL 
[ Learn More ](https://web.cvent.com/event/b583548f-6cb1-4438-91d7-8deeb67c4044/summary)
**ERM is sponsoring, exhibiting, and presenting at the 2024 Responsible Care® and Sustainability Conference and Expo hosted by the American Chemistry Council (ACC) in Miami, Florida.**
The conference is expected to bring together 300+ industry professionals including ACC member companies, supply chain partners, and other stakeholders. The agenda will focus on sustainability initiatives across the industry as well as strategic enhancements to the industry’s environmental, health, safety, and security performance program, known as Responsible Care®.
##  ERM at the Conference
ERM is a proud sponsor of the Linking Responsible Care® and ESG Reporting session and will be at booth #208 in the Expo hall.
####  Wednesday, 8 May, 2024 | 
#####  Linking Responsible Care® and ESG Reporting 
Time: 
2:45 PM-3:30 PM ET 
ERM speaker: James Margolis
Session overview: Collecting and reporting data to external stakeholders continues to be a critical component of company sustainability programs. Currently, EH&S topics comprise a majority of commonly requested metrics for many external environmental, social and governance (ESG) reporting frameworks, including topics transparently reported under Responsible Care®. Hear from industry leaders about experiences communicating their EH&S story through various ESG frameworks, and how Responsible Care implementation has prepared their organization to manage the process.
##  ERM Speakers
##  James Margolis 
Partner, ERM 
Mr. Margolis is a Senior Partner with ERM, the world’s largest pure play environmental, health, safety and sustainability (EHSS) consultancy, with over 5,000 staff in 140 offices in 40 countries. He is one of ERM’s senior management consulting practitioners and a nationally recognized expert in EHSS governance and management systems. He is a member of Northwestern University’s Kellogg Graduate School of Management’s Youn Social Impact Scholar cohort.
Jimmy has helped dozens of companies develop and implement EHS, sustainability and corporate social responsibility strategies. He works with leaders across functional areas and organizational levels to identify sustainability business risks and opportunities, develop strategies to address those risk and opportunities based on a robust business case, and implement strategies from the corporate level down through plant-level operations.
Jimmy has assisted dozens of clients develop sustainability/ESG reports, improve ESG rater/ranker scores, conduct product lifecycle assessments, implement sustainability-related information technology solutions, develop more sustainable supply chains, define corporate metrics/goals, and develop and roll out corporate policies and standards.
Jimmy has led comprehensive enterprise-wide reviews of EHSS management effectiveness for over 20 major corporations. He has helped companies develop and implement improved corporate governance programs and plant-level EHSS management systems contributing to significant reductions in environmental impacts, safety incidents, and costs, while engaging and building trust with key external stakeholders. During the course of his career, he has visited hundreds of industrial sites and developed and led dozens of EHSS management training courses, seminars and workshops.
Prior to joining ERM, Mr. Margolis spent eleven years with Arthur D. Little and six years with Deloitte. He started his career as an engineer at an oil refinery and petrochemical plant.
