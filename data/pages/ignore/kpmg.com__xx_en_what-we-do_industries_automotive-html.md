#  Automotive 
Enabling automotive organizations to master transformation in a shifting global ecosystem — with strategic foresight, operational agility, and technology-enabled performance


## Navigating complexity and unlocking long-term value
The automotive industry is going through a radical transformation. The automotive landscape has become complex: cost pressures, geopolitical uncertainty, supply chain fragility and rapidly evolving customer expectations are creating constant tension. Manufacturers and suppliers alike face mounting pressure to innovate, reduce costs, and meet evolving regulatory and customer demands — all while maintaining quality and global relevance.
To succeed, many automotive leaders need a strategic realignment. They require calculated foresight to anticipate market shifts, operational agility to adapt quickly, and technology-enabled performance to stay ahead. From responsibly integrating AI and connected technologies to enhancing compliance, transparency, and resilience across the value chain — companies are seeking trusted partners to help them navigate complexity and unlock long-term value.
Our global automotive network unites Audit, Tax, and Advisory professionals from KPMG firms around the world to deliver coordinated and multidisciplinary approaches. This team includes nearly 200 industry experts — engineers, designers, supply chain specialists, and others — who contribute extensive frontline expertise to help clients achieve their most critical goals.
