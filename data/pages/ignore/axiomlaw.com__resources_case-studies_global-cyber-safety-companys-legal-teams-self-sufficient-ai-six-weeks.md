### Axiom attorney helped:
Replace outside counsel spend with a fixed-scope, embedded attorney who builds lasting internal capability
Drive 40% efficiency gains* in contract turnaround within six weeks of hands-on enablement 
Turn institutional knowledge into reusable AI playbooks across NDAs, DPAs, procurement, and more — scaled to the whole team
### Challenge
A global consumer cyber-safety company was bringing together legal teams across business units with different contract types, stakeholders, and ways of working. Senior attorneys relied on **institutional knowledge and individual review approaches**. The client needed AI-enabled contracting introduced in a **practical, governed way** , with tacit expertise turned into reusable playbooks.
### Approach
Axiom combined **DraftPilot access with embedded legal and AI-advisory support** : five licenses deployed and an Axiom attorney working alongside the team for roughly 20 hours a week over six weeks. Near-daily enablement helped priority users build playbooks, set AI-use best practices, and adapt workflows to specialized contract types—powered by Axiom's **Tech+Talent** solution.
### Impact 
  * A top-down AI mandate became a **practical, attorney-led way of working** —teams now configure and evolve their own AI-supported contracting workflows.
  * **Reusable playbooks** reduced reliance on individual attorneys' institutional knowledge.
  * The client **retained its licenses** after enablement, converting the embedded attorney's seat to a client user.
