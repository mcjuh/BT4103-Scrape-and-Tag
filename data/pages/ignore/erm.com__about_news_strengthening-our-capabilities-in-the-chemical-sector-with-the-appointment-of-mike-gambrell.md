# Strengthening our capabilities in the Chemical Sector with the appointment of Mike Gambrell
05 February 2016
Share this page:
**In support of our growing presence and influence in the Chemical sector, we are delighted to announce that Mike Gambrell has joined ERM as a special advisor to support our service offerings to this sector.**
Mike is a former Executive Vice President with the Dow Chemical Company, retiring after 37 years with the firm. This included two years spent as an advisor to the company’s Chairman and CEO. During his tenure with Dow, he also served on the company’s Strategy Board, Sustainability Team and ex officio member of Dow’s board of director’s Environmental, Health, and Safety Committee. From 2011-2015, Mike served on the Board of Directors and strategic planning committee of the US National Safety Council, as well as being Chairman of the Campbell Institute, an organization dedicated to the promotion of excellence in environmental, health and safety issues.
In this new role, Mike will provide strategic counsel to ERM’s Board on Chemical sector.
