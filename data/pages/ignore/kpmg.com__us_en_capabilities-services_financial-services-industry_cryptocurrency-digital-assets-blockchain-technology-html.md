KPMG is a leader in providing professional services to innovative clients involved with digital assets and blockchain technology. Our professionals provide audit, tax, and advisory services across the ecosystem of digital assets—miners, exchanges, stablecoin issuers, asset managers, custodians, node hosting and staking providers, wallet providers, and other crypto-native technology providers.
Wherever you are on your blockchain journey, KPMG can provide insights and guidance through every phase. This includes, but is not limited to:
  * Organizational strategy
  * Thorough risk assessments
  * Accessing capital markets
  * Building and attesting to internal controls
  * Performing financial statement audits
  * Addressing accounting, tax, and regulatory implications such as AML and KYC
