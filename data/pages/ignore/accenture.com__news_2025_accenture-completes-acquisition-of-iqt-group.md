# **Accenture Completes Acquisition of IQT Group**
MILAN, Italy; Feb. 28, 2025 – [Accenture](https://www.accenture.com/it-it "Accenture") (NYSE: ACN) has completed the acquisition of [IQT Group](https://www.iqtconsulting.it/ "IQT Group"), a provider of engineering managed services for large infrastructure projects, based in Rovigo, Italy, [announced on December 18, 2024](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2024/accenture-to-acquire-iqt-group-strengthening-its-capabilities-for-net-zero-infrastructure-projects "announced on December 18, 2024"). The move combines Accenture’s generative AI and digital capabilities with IQT Group’s infrastructure projects expertise to help clients plan, execute and manage [net-zero](https://www.accenture.com/in-en/services/energy/towards-net-zero-transition "net-zero") infrastructure projects more efficiently.
With IQT Group, Accenture continues to expand its infrastructure and capital projects capability globally and in Europe. Last year, it acquired [BOSLAN](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2024/accenture-acquires-boslan-to-help-clients-optimize-investments-in-net-zero-infrastructure "BOSLAN"), a management services provider focused on net-zero infrastructure in Spain. In 2023, Accenture acquired advisory and management company [Anser Advisory](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2023/accenture-completes-acquisition-of-anser-advisory "Anser Advisory") in the U.S. and consulting and program management company [Comtech](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2023/accenture-acquires-canadian-capital-projects-consulting-company-comtech-group "Comtech") in Canada.
Accenture’s recent acquisitions in Italy include [Fibermind](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2024/accenture-completes-acquisition-of-fibermind-to-strengthen-fiber-and-mobile-5g-network-services "Fibermind"), a network services company; [Intellera Consulting](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2024/accenture-completes-acquisition-of-intellera-consulting "Intellera Consulting"), with expertise in the national consulting market dedicated to the innovation of Public Administration; [Ammagamma](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2024/accenture-completes-acquisition-of-ammagamma "Ammagamma"), a consulting company with Italian excellence in innovation related to Artificial Intelligence (AI); and [Customer Management IT and SirfinPA](https://newsroom.accenture.com/news/2023/accenture-expands-digital-transformation-capabilities-for-italy-s-public-sector-with-acquisition-of-customer-management-it-and-sirfinpa "Customer Management IT and SirfinPA"), two companies offering innovative services and technological solutions in the fields of Justice and Public Security.
The terms of the agreement have not been disclosed.
