#  Publicis Sapient Wins Business Intelligence Group’s 2026 Artificial Intelligence Excellence Award in Augmented Intelligence for Sapient Slingshot 
Sapient Slingshot applies AI to augment how enterprises modernize software at scale.
March 24, 2026 
Share
##  NEW YORK, NY, March 24, 2026 
Publicis Sapient, a technology company that provides enterprise AI platforms and services, today announced it has been named a winner in Business Intelligence Group’s [2026 Artificial Intelligence Excellence Awards](https://www.bintelligence.com/awards/artificial-intelligence-excellence-awards) for its AI software development platform, [Sapient Slingshot](https://www.publicissapient.com/platforms/slingshot), in the Augmented Intelligence category. This award honors organizations, products, teams and individuals that combine human insight with AI capabilities and are applying AI in ways that drive real, measurable impact.
“AI has arrived! 2026 is about execution, accountability, and results,” said [**Russ Fordyce**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/russfordyce/)**, Chief Recognition Officer, Business Intelligence Group.** “Publicis Sapient stood out because its work in augmented intelligence reflects where the market is headed: practical AI that solves real problems, earns trust, and delivers measurable value. This recognition highlights a team that is not just participating in the AI shift but helping define what meaningful progress looks like.”
“We are honored to be recognized in Business Intelligence Group’s 2026 Artificial Intelligence Excellence Awards for Sapient Slingshot’s work in Augmented Intelligence,” said **Nigel Vaz, CEO of Publicis Sapient**. “This recognition reflects the strength of our people and products approach and the trust our clients place in us to solve complex, real-world challenges. With Sapient Slingshot, we are applying AI in a way that augments human expertise, enabling organizations to modernize critical systems faster, with greater confidence and less risk. We believe the true value of AI lies in delivering tangible business outcomes responsibly, and this award underscores our commitment to that vision.”
Designed to modernize and build core enterprise software at scale, Sapient Slingshot addresses one of the most persistent challenges established organizations face: decades of accumulated technical debt embedded into legacy systems. These systems contain critical business logic, but are costly, fragile, and difficult to evolve. Sapient Slingshot enables enterprises to safely unlock and transform these environments without disrupting the services customers and operations depend on every day.
Sapient Slingshot stands out for utilizing AI to read, interpret, and extract business rules from legacy systems, preserving decades of institutional and human knowledge while enabling organizations to modernize with greater speed, confidence, and savings. The platform delivers up to 99% code-to-spec accuracy and accelerates delivery timelines by as much as 3x.
A key differentiator is Sapient Slingshot’s Enterprise Context Graph, a dynamic, living map of an organization’s software estate that connects code, architecture, and data to business rules, tribal knowledge, and operational dependencies. By combining this context with human input, Sapient Slingshot enables more informed decision-making, reduces risk, and ensures continuity and traceability across the entire software development lifecycle.
This recognition is grounded in measurable results delivered across industries. In healthcare, Sapient Slingshot enabled a large healthcare benefits provider to modernize more than 10,000 legacy COBOL screens, achieving 3× faster delivery and a 50% reduction in modernization costs. In energy, it helped a leading European producer rebuild a critical, decades-old application in just 48 hours, accelerating code generation and testing by up to 45% and removing long-term operational risk. In retail, it standardized code generation and included built-in testing for a major Latin American retailer, which improved consistency, reduced rework, and cut development level-of-effort by 20-30% during re-platforming efforts. Together, these outcomes demonstrate why Sapient Slingshot stands out in combining innovation with data and human insight to deliver real-world impact at scale.
Business Intelligence Group’s Artificial Intelligence Excellence Awards celebrate the people and organizations leading the next phase of AI adoption, where innovation is judged not just by novelty, but by impact. Winners are selected based on how effectively they are using AI to improve performance, reduce friction, solve meaningful problems, and move their industries forward.

##  About the Artificial Intelligence Excellence Awards 
Since 2013, the[ Artificial Intelligence Excellence Awards](https://www.bintelligence.com/awards/artificial-intelligence-excellence-awards) recognize organizations, products, teams, and individuals leveraging AI technology to solve real-world problems. The program evaluates advances across 46 major AI application types and 36 industries, celebrating innovations that improve the human experience and advance society as a whole. Judged by a panel of experienced business executives who provide transparent feedback and detailed scoring, the awards honor those dedicated to using AI as a force for good.
##  About Business Intelligence Group 
Business Intelligence Group (BIG) is an independent awards organization that has been recognizing outstanding achievement in business since 2012. Now in its 14th awards season, BIG operates 12 annual programs spanning innovation, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, customer service, cloud computing, sustainability, sales and marketing, workplace culture, and women's leadership. [Business award programs](https://www.bintelligence.com/awards-deadlines) include the BIG Innovation Awards, AI Excellence Awards, Fortress Cyber Security Awards, Excellence in Customer Service Awards, Stratus Awards for Cloud Computing, Sustainability Awards, SAMMY Awards for Sales and Marketing, Best Places to Work Awards, Herizon Awards, We Love Tech Awards, NAA Top Employers Award, and BIG Awards for Business.
Unlike popularity contests, BIG programs use professional judging panels and objective scoring benchmarks to identify organizations, products, and individuals making real, measurable impact. Winners receive a complete promotional toolkit — including blockchain-verified credentials, press release support, social media assets, and featured placement across BIG's global community of more than one million business professionals.
For more information about BIG award programs, nomination deadlines, and judging criteria, visit [bintelligence.com](https://www.bintelligence.com/).
