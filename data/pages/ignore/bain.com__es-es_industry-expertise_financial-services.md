## AI in Financial Services
Our research finds that 75% of financial services companies are achieving or exceeding their expected value with generative AI initiatives. What’s more, firms that embrace AI stand to reduce their customer service cost base by 20% to 30% and increase revenue by about 5%. Banks, insurers, fintechs, and wealth management firms are using AI to:
• Enhance employee productivity with AI assistants handling basic tasks• Automate higher-value routine workflows such as customer correspondence • Improve customer experience for B2C (e.g., banking chatbots) and B2B (e.g., account support for merchants using payments systems) • Facilitate risk management activities (e.g., Know Your Customer) 
[Learn how AI is reshaping financial services](https://www.bain.com/es-es/insights/?filters=industries\(200\)|featuredtopics\(1952908\))
