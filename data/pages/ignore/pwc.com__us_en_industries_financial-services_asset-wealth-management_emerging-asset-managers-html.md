Partnering for Growth and Success on the First Mile
#  Emerging Asset Managers: 
Launching a fund is a complex and pivotal undertaking. The earliest decisions you make - especially around selecting service providers - can shape the trajectory of your asset management firm for years to come.
At PwC, we understand the unique challenges emerging asset managers face as they enter the asset management industry. Beyond just compliance and reporting, including audit and tax services, you need trusted advisors who offer deep expertise, responsiveness, and a shared commitment to your long-term success.
## Why service provider selection matters more than ever
In today’s dynamic market, outsourcing, managed services, and collaboration with external partners aren’t optional - they’re essential strategic levers for emerging managers. These critical partnerships often begin before a single investment is made and continue to evolve throughout your growth journey.
Choosing the right service providers early means assembling a trusted network that supports you at every step - whether you’re structuring funds, navigating complex regulatory regimes, managing tax-efficient strategies, scaling operations, or responding to the shifting market landscape.
## Thorough, integrated solutions for emerging managers
Launching your fund requires more than compliance. It demands strategic guidance and operational readiness across multiple fronts:
**Audit services**
Facilitating transparent, rigorous financial reporting that meets investor and regulatory expectations, giving your fund credibility and confidence.
**Tax Advisory**
Designing and implementing tax-efficient fund structures and strategies that optimize your after-tax returns and help you navigate complex multi-jurisdictional requirements.
**Fund Structuring & Governance**
Advising on economic arrangements, governance frameworks, and regulatory compliance tailored to your fund’s strategy and investor base.
**Regulatory Navigation**
Anticipating evolving regulatory expectations and guiding you through global compliance landscapes.
**Operational Support**
Helping scale your back-office, risk management, and reporting functions in step with your growth plans.
**AI Solutions**
Harness AI-driven insights to enhance investment decisions, streamline operations, and manage risk. Automation of routine tasks like data reconciliation and compliance enables your team to focus on strategic growth and maintain a competitive edge.
## Four essential criteria for selecting your service providers
  1. 1. Deep expertise and industry credentials
  2. 2. The right team
  3. 3. Responsiveness
  4. 4. Fair fees for quality service


### 1. Deep expertise and industry credentials
Emerging asset managers face steep learning curves. Engage providers who have “been there” and deeply understand your sector.
  1. Look for firms with proven qualifications and track records in audit, tax, and fund services.
  2. Make sure your engagement leads bring relevant, hands-on experience.
  3. Conduct thorough reference checks - these will validate quality and connect you to a broader network.


### 2. The right team
Your service provider works hand-in-hand with your team to support your goals.
  1. Know exactly who will manage day-to-day interactions.
  2. Confirm cultural alignment and capacity to support evolving needs, especially important in audit cycles and tax reporting deadlines.


### 3. Responsiveness
Timely, clear communication is critical.
  1. Assess responsiveness throughout RFP and onboarding phases.
  2. Early impressions predict future service reliability, particularly during time-sensitive audits and tax filings.


### 4. Fair fees for quality service
Cost matters, but value matters more.
  1. Understand pricing structures fully to avoid unexpected increases.
  2. Avoid providers whose low upfront fees mask long-term costs.
  3. Balance cost with the quality, expertise, and network access you receive.
