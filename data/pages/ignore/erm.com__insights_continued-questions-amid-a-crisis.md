# Continued Questions Amid a Crisis
#### Dear SustainAbility Community –
Our March 27 newsletter acknowledged the disruption and uncertainty COVID-19 has unleashed while pointing to some uplifting societal and business responses.
Two weeks on, there is more data — about infection rates, deaths, economic fallout, government responses and more — but meaning remains elusive. Shared questions include:  _Is social distancing working and how long with it last? How high will unemployment rise?_ Individually we wonder:  _Is my family safe? How can I help my community?_
In response, many political leaders are making remarkably personal appeals, like Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau, filmed outside his home explaining ‘flattening the curve’ and why everyone must help. Daily [briefings](https://campaigns.sustainability.com/t/r-l-jhkyukc-ukhjolilj-o/)of some US Governors, like New York’s Andrew Cuomo, are now must-watch viewing for constituents. And citizens everywhere are contributing, like this [volunteer elder-support army](https://campaigns.sustainability.com/t/r-l-jhkyukc-ukhjolilj-b/) in the UK, and by following [guidance](https://campaigns.sustainability.com/t/r-l-jhkyukc-ukhjolilj-n/)on helping medical personnel.
Business leadership is advancing too. As CEO Tim Cook [shares](https://campaigns.sustainability.com/t/r-l-jhkyukc-ukhjolilj-p/), Apple has sourced some 20 million face masks for donation and pivoted employee skills to design and produce face shields for healthcare workers. JPMorganChase CEO Jamie Dimon’s April 6th [Letter to Shareholders](https://reports.jpmorganchase.com/investor-relations/2019/ar-ceo-letters.htm) addresses financial performance, but gives most ink to a seven-part section detailing how JPMC will support customers, employees, communities, shareholders and government during the crisis. And, as CEO of the world’s biggest soap-maker, Alan Jope [writes](https://campaigns.sustainability.com/t/r-l-jhkyukc-ukhjolilj-q/) about Unilever’s responsibility to help, which includes “free soap, sanitiser, bleach and food to the value of €100 million.”
At a meeting of Chief Sustainability Officers we just hosted, change management expert Michael Nord of ERM Group’s [The Fifth Business](https://campaigns.sustainability.com/t/r-l-jhkyukc-ukhjolilj-a/) talked about empathetic, authentic, transparent and consistent leadership. Cook, Dimon and Jope demonstrate this, and Marriott CEO Arne Sorenson’s employee [message](https://campaigns.sustainability.com/t/r-l-jhkyukc-ukhjolilj-f/) presents an amazing balance of rawness and determination well worth watching.
This newsletter features articles on COVID-19 relating to the tech sector and Asia Pacific’s recovery, [insights](https://campaigns.sustainability.com/t/r-l-jhkyukc-ukhjolilj-z/) from ERM Group company Critical Resource’s stakeholder advisory panel and an invitation to our April 16 [webinar](https://www.erm.com/insights/webinar-covid-19-sustainability-whats-next/) “COVID-19. Sustainability. What’s Next?” which will explore the pandemic’s impacts on sustainable development and business.
As ever, we welcome your feedback.
**Stay safe,****Mark Lee, Executive Director, SustainAbility and Partner, ERM**
