## Moments of Impact
From creating the first digital boarding passes to paving the way for future AI advancements, check out a short list featuring a few of our client firsts and key dates in our history.
1990
### The beginning
Founded in Cambridge, MA, Sapient began as a digital consulting firm focused on IT innovation. We pioneered a fixed-time, fixed-price delivery model.
2000
### Dot-com era growth
During the dot-com crash we doubled down on global growth. From 1992 to 2000 revenue scaled from $950K to more than $500M.
2008
### Mobile innovation
With Lufthansa we introduced mobile boarding passes and facial recognition technology to streamline air travel.
2013
### Dynamic customer experiences
Dunkin' Donuts partnered with us to launch digital menu boards in 3,000 locations, delivering dynamic, geo-specific recommendations connected to POS systems. 
2019
### Digital banking
With AGTB we built the first fully digital trade finance bank in months, combining adaptive architecture with agile delivery. 
2021
### Bodhi enables agentic workflows
Sapient Bodhi, our first agentic AI platform, was created with pre-built machine learning models and pre-configured AI workflows. 
2022
### Slingshot automates full SDLC
Sapient Slingshot began as a prototype blending generative AI with PSChat to accelerate developer workflows, and quickly expanded toward full-scale modernization. 
2024
### A new way to search
With Homes & Villas by Marriott Bonvoy we launched an AI-powered vacation home search tool on Azure using GPT models, shifting travel discovery to an experience-based search.
