# Global Health and Development
## From data, evidence, and analytics to measurable global impact.
[Schedule a conversation](https://www.mathematica.org/sectors/global-health-and-development#landing_contactusform)
  * [Our Services](https://www.mathematica.org/sectors/global-health-and-development#so-element)


Mathematica works with organizations worldwide to turn investments into measurable impact. We help leaders make confident decisions by applying innovative data analytics alongside proven research and evaluation methods.
Global development efforts depend on trusted evidence to strengthen programs and deliver better outcomes. Through our Global Practice, we partner with governments, NGOs, foundations, and multilateral institutions in more than 90 countries—bringing deep sector and methodological expertise to complex challenges. We work across agriculture and food systems, education and workforce development, energy, health and nutrition, and water and sanitation.
By transforming evidence into action across partners, countries, and sectors, we help organizations adapt programs, scale what works, and improve outcomes in diverse and evolving contexts.

## Our Approach
Our approach combines rigorous data collection and analysis with flexible, collaborative engagement, keeping our clients' goals at the heart of our work. We are passionate about using evidence to drive better policies, smarter programs, groundbreaking solutions, and transformative partnerships.
### Driven by Your Goals, Powered by Evidence
**Integrated Expertise:** Our experts bring deep knowledge across disciplines, regions, and methodologies—ensuring we meet each client’s unique needs with precision and depth. 
**End-to-End Support:** From initial design through final assessment, we immerse ourselves in our clients’ work, offering nuanced guidance and tailored approaches that foster agility, innovation, and real results. 
**Building Evidence Capacity:** We don’t just deliver insights—we help our clients create organizational cultures and capacity for evidence use and learning to sustain evidence-driven decision-making. 
**Holistic Ecosystem Engagement:** We go beyond traditional research and integrate qualitative and quantitative knowledge, scientific evidence, and local expertise to understand what works and support effective learning that drives the best possible outcomes. 
**Commitment to Innovation:** We invest in cutting-edge digital tools, analytics, and technologies, optimizing cost-efficiency, effectiveness, and impact through smarter data-driven decisions. 
**Strong Partnerships:** We collaborate with a broad network of global and local experts to integrate contextual, technical, and on-the-ground experience into decisions. 
## Our Services
Planning and Program Design 
We use analytical tools and models for ongoing measurement and learning to help inform how clients make progress. 
  * Landscape scans, stakeholder mapping, and evidence reviews
  * Predictive analytics and modeling
  * Theories of change development


Learning Partnerships 
Our MERL framework design helps clients strengthen their learning practices, define learning agendas, and develop portfolio- or organization-wide evidence frameworks. 
  * Learning agendas
  * Indicator framework development


Performance Monitoring and Improvement 
We help organizations use data and evidence in real time to drive adaptive management, support informed decisions, and build capacity. 
  * Rapid-cycle evaluation
  * Cross-portfolio performance monitoring
  * MIS platforms and dashboards
  * Learning capture for decision-making


Evaluation for Evidence Generation 
We uncover action-ready insights through evaluations and analytics, helping clients determine what works, what doesn’t, and how to scale for maximum impact. 
  * Rigorous impact and performance evaluations
  * Strategy reviews and portfolio evaluations
  * Theory-based evaluations for complex initiatives and systems change
  * Cost analysis and cost evaluation
  * Case studies


Evidence Translation and Dissemination 
We collaborate with clients and partners to make data and findings available and useful to shape policy, guide investment, and drive meaningful improvements. 
  * Structured learning and sensemaking workshops and activities
  * Internal products and presentations for leadership and boards
  * Peer-reviewed publications and learning products for policymakers and practitioners
  * Data visualization
  * Multimedia content and social media support

### Evaluation of GiveDirectly’s Cash-only and Cash-plus Programs in the Nakivale Refugee Settlement (Uganda)
Mathematica is conducting a randomized controlled trial (RCT) and qualitative study to help GiveDirectly understand the role unconditional cash transfers (UCTs) and business support services can play in improving livelihood outcomes for refugees in Uganda.
