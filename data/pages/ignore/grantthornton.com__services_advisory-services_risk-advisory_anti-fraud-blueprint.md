#  Anti-Fraud Blueprint 
The fraud risk landscape is changing. Is your organization protected?
Our Anti-Fraud Blueprint was developed in partnership with the Association of Certified Fraud Examiners (ACFE) to help organizations build, mature, or enhance their FRM programs. The Blueprint builds on the principles laid out in the 2023 second edition of the _Fraud Risk Management Guide_ , issued by the ACFE and the Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission (COSO), providing practical guidance for getting started and key questions to consider. It also includes an updated Enterprise Anti-Fraud Maturity Assessment Model© that can be used to measure your organization’s progress.
Fill in the form to download the Blueprint for practical guidance on the changing fraud risk landscape. For more insight, see our [**Anti-Fraud Blueprint webcast**](https://www.grantthornton.com/events/advisory/2026/04-08-the-anti-fraud-blueprint).
**What’s in the report?**
  * Fraud risk management program overview
  * Fraud risk governance
  * Fraud risk assessment
  * Fraud control activities
  * Fraud investigation and corrective action
  * Fraud risk management monitoring activities
