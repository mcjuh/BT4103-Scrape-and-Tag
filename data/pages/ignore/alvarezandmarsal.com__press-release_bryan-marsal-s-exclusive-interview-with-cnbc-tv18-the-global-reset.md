# Bryan Marsal's Exclusive Interview with CNBC TV18 - The Global Reset
Bryan Marsal, Co-Founder and CEO of Alvarez & Marsal, was featured in an exclusive interview on CNBC TV18, where he shared perspectives on the evolving global business environment and the firm’s accelerating growth in India.
During the conversation, he discussed how Alvarez & Marsal is expanding its presence in the region while continuing to support clients globally through its integrated operating model. He highlighted India’s growing importance within A&M’s global platform both as a high-growth advisory market and as a strategic talent hub supporting the firm’s international operations.
Bryan also outlined how the firm is investing heavily in the market, expanding leadership teams, and building capabilities across multiple service lines to meet rising client demand.
## **Key Highlights from the Interview**
  * **India is emerging as one of Alvarez & Marsal’s fastest-growing markets globally.**Growth in the market is being driven by rising demand across multiple service lines, including restructuring, transactions, corporate performance improvement, and infrastructure and capital projects, alongside continued expansion through new managing director hires, entry into additional sectors, and capability building across advisory practices.


  * **India plays an important role in supporting Alvarez & Marsal’s global operations through its Global Capability Center. **Teams across the firm’s worldwide network leverage the center to support internal priorities and deliver client work.


  * **India’s strong economic growth and stable leadership make it an attractive market for investment.** The country’s large, English-speaking talent base continues to support business growth and global delivery.


#### “We’ve invested heavily in India by bringing on new managing directors, entering new lines of business, and expanding into new sectors. We’re up about 65% in revenue and expect around a 65% increase in fiscal 2026 (excluding GCC). I look at India as a star market for 2026–27.”
