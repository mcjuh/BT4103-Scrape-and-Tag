#  M&A Services for private equity investors and portfolio companies 
## Buttoned up on the outside. Fired up on the inside. 
This isn’t the private equity market any of us grew up in. This is a new, bold market on the move. Ready to embrace it? 
## Collaborating to help make your vision a reality
Navigating today’s private equity market with confidence takes more than good insights. You need a guide who can help you turn insights into action.
Our global network of M&A specialists can help private equity firms and portfolio companies uncover, create, and drive new value. Because we’re like you—all business on the outside; raring to go on the inside. 
### What we provide
Our Merger & Acquisition Services span the full private equity life cycle.
#####  Diligence and Pre-deal Services 
In today’s complex environment, the pre-transaction phase is more vital than ever. We can help you identify and assess opportunities that align to your long-term vision. 
Our services include:
  * Deal origination and target screening/identification
  * Buy-side investment banking, valuation, and advisory services
  * Due diligence, including finance, IT, HR, operational, cyber, accounting, and tax
  * Acquisition accounting, reporting, and internal controls assessments
  * Deal-specific tax, legal, and regulatory risks and opportunities across industries and countries
  * Operational Day 1 planning, including carve-out assistance and transition service agreements (TSAs)


#####  Value Creation and Exit Services 
Our tailored services can help your portfolio companies drive new value and your exits achieve new efficiency. 
Our services include:
  * Revenue enhancement through growth, consolidation, and scale
  * Digital transformation and sustainable value creation through AI, automation, and other technologies
  * Sell-side support including IPO and SPAC readiness and execution, investment banking, SEC reporting, tax efficiency planning, and divestiture accounting
