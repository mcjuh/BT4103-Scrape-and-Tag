# BCG at the Munich Security Conference
What must leaders do to tackle their most pressing transnational security and international defense challenges? How can ministries of defense and the industrial base partner to build their defense posture for new realities?
Ministries of defense and the industrial base around the world are navigating tremendous change: geopolitical instability and a need to quickly ramp up capabilities in the face of technological disruption. These challenging dynamics require cross-border cooperation and the definition and advancement of common security goals.BCG’s partnership with the Munich Security Conference (MSC) connects one of the world’s top management consulting firms with the world’s leading forum for high-level defense policy experts, industry leaders, and [public sector](https://www.bcg.com/industries/public-sector/overview) decision makers, including heads of state, to exchange insights on transnational security and foreign policy.In our role, we are partnering with the MSC to release an annual report on defense innovation with actionable steps that ministries of defense and the industrial base can take to drive innovation and generate tangible results. We are also leveraging our deep experience serving [defense and security](https://www.bcg.com/industries/public-sector/defense-security) agencies and [aerospace and defense](https://www.bcg.com/industries/aerospace-and-defense-industry) companies to bring topical discussions to key MSC events throughout the year.
