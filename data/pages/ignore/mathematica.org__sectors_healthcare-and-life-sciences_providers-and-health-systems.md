Powerful tools and expert guidance for providers, health systems, and ACOs
In today’s healthcare landscape, health systems, providers, and accountable care organizations (ACOs) increasingly struggle to achieve revenue and growth KPIs while still providing high-quality patient care. Organizational, financial, and regulatory challenges often stand in the way.
  * Health systems lack the resources or expertise to mine deeper insights from price transparency data.
  * Physician practices don’t know they can use price transparency data—and don’t have the internal resources to analyze it.
  * ACOs struggle to predict their savings from Medicare Shared Savings Program.


Mathematica—with more than 50 years of leadership at the intersection of healthcare policy and data analytics—can help.
