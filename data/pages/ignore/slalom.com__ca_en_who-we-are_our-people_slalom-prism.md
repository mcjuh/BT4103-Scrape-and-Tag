##### Prism seeks to increase awareness and understanding while removing workplace barriers for all orientations, identities, allies, and those interested in learning more. 
## Mission
To build and shape a better future for LGBTQ+ Slalomers and our allies by cultivating fiercely human authenticity in our lives and work.
## Objectives
  * Partner with local community organizations supporting all orientations, identities, and allies. 
  * Create engaging educational experiences for all Slalom employees to deepen their understanding of LGBTQ+ identities, experiences, and history. PMake space for a workplace culture of belonging and authenticity through coaching and collaboration. 
  * Drive internal connection and collaboration globally at Slalom.
