# International Tax 
Helping multinational organizations succeed in today’s complex international tax environment
##  Thinking Beyond Borders 
To succeed in today’s global marketplace, companies must anticipate and understand the potential short- and long-term consequences of tax planning decisions at a coordinated, global level and in the context of their wider business objectives.
The KPMG International Tax practice professionals help companies manage the complexities of multiple tax systems and international regulations around the world. KPMG provides an array of international tax planning, consulting, and compliance services to corporations ranging from enterprises just beginning to invest internationally to large multinationals.
Our services include:
  * Global effective tax rate planning
  * Tax planning for internal restructurings (e.g., for legal entity rationalization and value chain management)
  * Intellectual property and cash access planning
  * Tax planning for cross-border mergers, acquisitions, and disposition transactions
  * International tax dispute resolution services
  * International tax reporting and compliance services 
  * International tax provision support for financial reporting
  * Tax modeling and computational services


Align tax planning with business goals 
Navigating international tax rules and balancing stakeholder priorities puts pressure on tax teams. KPMG can help keep your tax planning in -line with your overall business goals. 
Manage your effective tax rate and risks 
The KPMG multidisciplinary approach, coupled with the KPMG International Tax Reform Analyzer and other tax technology tools, can help you manage your effective tax rate and risks. 
Navigate U.S. and global tax reform developments 
As U.S. and global tax reform developments move forward, KPMG can help your company evaluate the impact of these changes and identify tax planning opportunities. 
Enhance profitability and cash flow 
By aligning tax planning with operations and finance transformation, KPMG can help your company achieve greater profitability and cash flow.
