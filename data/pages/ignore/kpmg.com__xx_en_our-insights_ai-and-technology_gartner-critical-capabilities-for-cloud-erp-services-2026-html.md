This report is a companion to the [2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Cloud ERP Services](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/transformation/2026-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-cloud-erp-services.html), in which KPMG was named a Leader. Gartner defines cloud ERP services as “services provided by third-party systems integrators to assess needs, implement solutions and evolve platforms that are transforming back-office systems through the implementation of cloud-based ERP solutions.
### About the report
The Critical Capabilities research evaluates providers across six Use Cases, including:
  * Discovery and design
  * Microsoft Dynamics-based implementation and evolution
  * Oracle Fusion-based implementation and evolution
  * SAP-based implementation and evolution
  * Workday-based implementation and evolution
  * Application managed services


This Critical Capabilities research assesses 19 providers on the most critical cloud ERP capabilities.
### What we believe this report will help you understand
  * How providers perform across cloud ERP Use Cases
  * How providers are assessed across critical capabilities
  * How to evaluate providers based on specific business and technology needs
  * How to use this research alongside the Magic Quadrant when selecting a provider


### KPMG perspective
Cloud ERP transformation is an important priority for organizations seeking to modernize enterprise systems, simplify ways of working and support better business outcomes.
KPMG firms help clients approach cloud ERP transformation as a business-led journey—bringing together strategy, process, technology and change to support enterprise transformation from planning through implementation and ongoing evolution.
