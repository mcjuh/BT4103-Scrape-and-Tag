# KPMG and Ping Identity
Flexible, large-scale identity and access management solutions for the enterprise
Driving customer, partner and employee engagement is a top digital business initiative for enterprises—but those engaging experiences must be secure and consistent across all channels and applications. Through its alliance with Ping Identity, KPMG expands its ability to deliver flexible, large-scale identity and access management solutions for the enterprise, providing secure access solutions that enable a wide range of digital transformation initiatives.
The Ping Identity Platform can be deployed as Identity-as-a-Service (IDaaS), in the enterprise’s private cloud, as on-premises software, or in combination for hybrid IT environments. Able to scale to 100’s of millions of identities, this unified, standards-based platform provides access management, multifactor authentication (MFA), single sign-on (SSO), access security, data governance, directory, and data sync capabilities.
Determining where and how to best implement these capabilities can be challenging. A leading cyber services provider, KPMG blends domain expertise, risk management methodologies, and technical specialists to help you plan, architect, configure, and implement enterprise-grade, secure IAM solutions. For companies looking for a phased approach, KPMG can lay out a roadmap for implementing specific Ping capabilities such as MFA or SSO. With best practices in regulations and compliance, KPMG professionals can also improve the security and privacy of your customer data. Leveraging the alliance between KPMG LLP and Ping Identity, enterprises can grant employees, partners, and customers simple, secure access to cloud, mobile, and enterprise applications.
To learn more, visit [Ping Identity](https://www.pingidentity.com/en.html)
