# Towards Planet Positive Chemicals: A Chemical Transformation Roadmap 
**Discover new actionable insights for the chemical industry**
ERM has partnered with the World Business Council for Sustainable Development (WBCSD), in collaboration with nine leading chemical companies, to issue the “Towards Planet Positive Chemicals: A Chemical Transformation Roadmap Enabled by the Circular Economy”. The roadmap aims to drive transformative change across the chemical industry and downstream manufacturing value chains
**The urgency for change**
Chemicals play a pivotal role in virtually every sector from healthcare to energy, mobility or communication. However, their contribution to global greenhouse gas emissions (~6.3% in 2019) and environmental degradation are undeniable. The roadmap recognizes these challenges, calling for an urgent transformation in how chemicals are produced, used, disposed of and recycled. 
**A blueprint for collaboration with actionable pathways**
The roadmap lays out a shared vision, six pathways and 33 actions to be taken by 2030 to transition toward just, net-zero, nature positive chemicals. This calls for multi-sector collaboration between chemical companies and downstream manufacturing actors, policymakers, financial institutions, and other relevant stakeholders.

## Three imperatives for the transformation
### Net-zero chemicals
Commit to reduce greenhouse gas emissions across all scopes (1, 2 and 3) by 2050.
[ read more ](https://www.wbcsd.org/resources/towards-planet-positive-chemicals/)
### Activating sustainable chemistry
Leveraging sustainable chemistry innovations to drive solutions to climate, nature, safety and equity challenges.
[ Read more ](https://www.wbcsd.org/resources/towards-planet-positive-chemicals/)
### A Just Transition for chemicals
Create transformative solutions that prioritize equity and wellbeing for workers, users and impacted communities across the entire value chain.
[ Read more ](https://www.wbcsd.org/resources/towards-planet-positive-chemicals/)
