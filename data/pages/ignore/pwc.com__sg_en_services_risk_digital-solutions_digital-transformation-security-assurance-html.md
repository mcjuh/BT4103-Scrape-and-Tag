As organisations increasingly embrace business models with digital at their core, and reliance on technology and internet grows, more sensitive data is being generated, stored, and shared among organisations, partners, and customers.
With the increase in volume of data being stored and transmitted online, data has become the new lifeblood of the interconnected business ecosystem. This has elevated the likelihood and impact of cyber attacks and data breaches.
Therefore, it is critical for organisations to develop an effective and agile approach to cybersecurity and data privacy throughout their digital transformation journey. 
## How our digital transformation security assurance services can help you 
Our digital transformation security assurance services include a suite of comprehensive solutions aimed at helping organisations address challenges in cybersecurity and privacy threats, organisational changes, and regulatory requirements as they embrace digital transformation. 
  1. Cyber and privacy risk assessment
  2. Cyber audit
  3. Security testing
  4. Cyber simulations
  5. Ongoing cyber monitoring and maintenance


### Cyber and privacy risk assessment
Today’s enterprises are digitally powered and decision making across all levels of the organisation is driven by data. As data collection, storage and analysis become more complex through growing volume, variety and velocity, organisations need to ensure proper data management and governance practices to protect the privacy of sensitive information.
[Learn more about how we can help you with privacy assessment](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/services/risk/digital-solutions/privacy-assessment.html)
### Cyber audit
We help you evaluate the design and effectiveness of your IT systems to identify gaps and vulnerabilities in your applications, infrastructure and networks and develop a mitigation plan. We also help you achieve compliance with cyber regulations and policies.
[Learn more about how we can help you with cyber audit](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/services/risk/digital-solutions/cybersecurity-audit.html)
### Security testing
Accelerated digitisation in recent years has increased the surface area for cyber attacks, as well as the prevalence and sophistication of threat actors. Cyber regulations have also become more stringent in response to growing cyber risks. Organisations need to take a more proactive approach to safeguarding their key technology systems and processes. Security testing allows organisations to identify weak points in their cyber defences, and formulate an action plan for improvement accordingly.
[Learn more about how we can help you with security testing](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/services/risk/digital-solutions/security-testing.html)
### Cyber simulations
The conversation around cyber attacks is no longer about if, but rather when, one will happen. Cyber simulations allow clients to practise their incident response skills in a safe working environment. We design scenarios and injects to simulate failures in an organisation's cyber defence, allowing us to test various aspects of crisis responses, such as incident detection, triage, escalations, communications with various stakeholders, senior management decision making, and steps taken to address members of the public and media.
[Learn more about how we can help you with cyber simulations](https://www.pwc.com/sg/en/services/risk/digital-solutions/cyber-simulations.html)
### Ongoing cyber monitoring and maintenance
Pursuing digital transformation is playing the long game. As your organisation continues on its digitisation journey, we can help you monitor the performance and health of your IT infrastructure to ensure its security and reliability and, if needed, trigger the relevant actions to resolve any issues.
