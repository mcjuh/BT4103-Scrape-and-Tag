##  Why Thoughtworks? 
About us
Being a Thoughtworker is more than just a job. It's a shared commitment to revolutionizing technology – for our clients, society and ourselves – and advocating for an equitable future. For over 30 years, we’ve delivered extraordinary impact through our culture and tech excellence, helping our clients solve complex business problems with technology as the differentiator.
[ Learn more about our purpose ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/about-us/our-purpose)
Clients that trust us
Our clients have bold ambitions. Staying competitive in a world of constant change demands ongoing digital transformation and modernization. That’s where we come in. We partner with some of the world’s leading businesses – from scale-ups to global Fortune 500 enterprises – to drive innovation and disruptive thinking, and turn big ambitions into reality.
[ Learn how we help clients harness the power of tech ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/clients)
Our application process
When it comes to interviewing for tech careers at Thoughtworks, we focus on outcomes and the belief that your interview experience should be collaborative and enjoyable. You will first speak with one of our recruiters, followed by a technical interview – possibly consisting of a pair programming session, case study or design challenge, depending on the role – and conversations about leadership, technology and our culture.
[ Learn more about our process and tips to help you prepare ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/careers/our-process)
Consultant life
One of the best questions candidates ask our recruiters is: ‘What will I actually be doing as a consultant at Thoughtworks?’ Here, you’ll work in collaborative and purpose-led teams to create impactful solutions for clients from a variety of industries. For example, you could influence the digital strategy of a retail giant, launch a new mobile application for a bank or redesign platforms using event sourcing and intelligent data pipelines.
[ Learn more about our ways of working ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/careers/consultant-life)
Entry-level careers
Our entry-level careers are designed for graduates and career changers ready to begin their tech journey. We offer structured support, continuous learning and mentorship throughout your first year at Thoughtworks. A key part of this is Thoughtworks University (TWU), our global learning program that combines immersive training, peer collaboration and real client experience to help you grow with purpose and confidence.
[ Learn more about early careers and Thoughtworks University  ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/careers/graduates)
How we care for our people
At Thoughtworks we offer well-rounded benefits to support your personal and professional development goals, as well as your health and wellbeing needs. On top of this, we also offer a variety of perks that cover many categories such as: transportation, paid and unpaid leaves, referral bonuses, a laptop buyback program and more.
[ Explore our benefits  ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/careers/benefits)
How we support your growth
At Thoughtworks we help you shape your tech career journey, your way, with interactive tools, development programs, and supportive teammates and leaders. From our first-year experience program and Thoughtworks University for entry-level joiners just starting their tech careers, to capability academies and certifications for growing technologists and leadership development programs.

## You matter
At Thoughtworks, Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI) is at the core of who we are as a company. From the moment you join us, you’ll be part of a community that values your individuality, fosters belonging and embraces the unique impact you bring.
We believe DEI can drive extraordinary change. That’s why we’re committed to creating a workplace where you feel respected, safe and valued—where everyone has a place to belong and grow.
Our DEI commitment isn’t optional or temporary; it’s been part of our DNA since we began our journey in 2010, long before it was mainstream. Our commitment is not just about it being the right thing to do, we believe that diverse teams build more innovative, resilient and responsible technology solutions to solve the greatest problems facing our clients, the industry and society.

##  Driving technical efficiency and innovation: Meet the Thoughtworkers behind the project with MYOB
Balancing innovation while maintaining and integrating existing systems and digital assets is no small feat especially for a fast-evolving business like MYOB. With internal development and integrated partnerships contributing to growth, their delivery teams needed to unlock capacity for new product development without compromising existing operations. Thoughtworkers Jiawei Liu, Na Chen, Pei Zhao and Yingjie Guan partnered with MYOB to deliver a streamlined, AI-supported approach that boosted efficiency, reduced costs and empowered teams to focus on innovation.
[ Read more about this team’s story behind the project ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/blog/careers-at-thoughtworks/Meet-the-thoughtworkers-behind-the-project-with-myob)

##  Delivering an optimized cloud platform: Meet Thoughtworkers behind the Hubbl project, Foxtel Group
We’ve all wasted hours cycling through streaming apps, looking for something to watch. Seeing the opportunity to transform the streaming landscape, Foxtel Group partnered with Thoughtworks to build Hubbl, a plug-and-play digital platform device that simplifies the streaming experience. Thoughtworkers Juanjuan Chen, Zhuowen Fan, Hao Lin and Ju Li helped turn that opportunity into reality. Their insights provide a unique look at the challenges, successes and lessons learned during development.
[ Read more about this team’s story behind the project ](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/blog/careers-at-thoughtworks/Meet-the-thoughtworkers-behind-the-hubbl-project-with-foxtel-group)
