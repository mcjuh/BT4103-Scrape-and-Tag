## Frequently Asked Questions
  * What's the difference between a merger, an acquisition, and corporate restructuring?-
A **merger** combines two companies into one organization, while an **acquisition** occurs when one company purchases another. **Corporate restructuring** is a broader strategy that can include mergers, acquisitions, divestitures, carveouts, and organizational changes designed to improve performance, reduce complexity, or reposition the business.
