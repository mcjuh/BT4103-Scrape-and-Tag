# Global project management
### Global delivery: Thrive beyond compliance
#### [Contact our global delivery team ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/global-project-management.html#form)
Navigating complex global environments requires strategic thinking and seamless execution. RSM's global delivery framework helps organizations achieve their international objectives by providing dedicated professionals, a robust methodology and the extensive reach of the RSM network. We specialize in compliance, transformation and the successful delivery of global projects, helping our clients navigate the complexities of the global marketplace.
##  Our global delivery framework 
At the heart of our success lies a commitment to three core principles. First, engagement excellence means our program and change professionals dedicate themselves to deeply understanding your unique needs and goals. We infuse rigor, quality and excellence into every program to achieve the strategic benefits you envision.
Second, a drive for innovation fuels our pursuit of better solutions. By harnessing cutting-edge technologies and approaches, we streamline programs, minimize disruption and accelerate value creation, helping you advance with confidence and agility.
Finally, our professional delivery reflects our commitment to deploying the right professionals for every engagement, consistently exceeding your expectations. We leverage RSM’s deep industry, functional and technical experience to meet and surpass your needs, providing comprehensive support and guidance.
We offer a unique blend of global reach, local talent and a client-focused approach. RSM provides access to professionals in over 120 countries, while our consultants bring deep industry and technical experience to every engagement. We use proven methodologies for successful project delivery, striving for efficiency and convenience while mitigating risks.
Above all, we are committed to a client-focused approach, tailoring our services to your specific needs and objectives. This combination of global reach, professional knowledge, proven methods and personalized service helps you receive high-quality support and achieve your international business goals.
##  The RSM advantage 
Our global delivery team offers a proven approach that drives accountability, visibility and transparency. RSM focuses on filing deadlines and obligations, mitigating risk for your organization. We offer a single point of contact for all international functions that are part of the engagement.
Contact RSM today to learn how your organization can thrive in an international setting.
##  How we can help  

Global assessment  
    
We provide comprehensive global assessments to evaluate the current status of an engagement, leveraging the extensive RSM network to understand our client’s position. This assessment includes a detailed presentation of deliverables outlining the current status, potential penalties and actionable remediation steps. 

New jurisdiction services  
    
Expanding into new markets requires careful planning and execution. Our new jurisdiction services provide a comprehensive entry plan, leveraging the RSM network to identify the most efficient structure for your operations. We deliver an actionable plan that navigates the specific requirements of each jurisdiction, helping you achieve a smooth and successful market entry.
[ Learn more about our new jurisdiction services ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/global-project-management/new-jurisdiction-services.html "Learn more about our new jurisdiction services") 

Global compliance and reporting services (GCRS)  
    
Effective global compliance requires a robust framework. Our global compliance and reporting services approach includes the governance and deployment structure necessary to manage and coordinate global project activities effectively. We provide ongoing compliance tracking and clear processes for issue and escalation management.
[ Learn more about GCRS ](https://rsmus.com/services/global-services/global-compliance-and-reporting-services.html "Learn more about GCRS") 

Global optimization services  
    
To maximize efficiency and effectiveness, we offer global optimization services. We assess your global compliance maturity, protocols and policies to identify opportunities for standardization, centralization and optimization. These lead to developing high-performing global reporting and statutory account teams, incorporating best practices and recommending process and provider improvements. We also offer support for global reporting office creation, group audit and private equity group integration. 

Jurisdiction exit services  
    
When it's time to exit a market, our jurisdiction exit services provide professional guidance. We identify entity rationalization strategies and requirements, as well as assist in the execution of these efforts, developing a comprehensive exit plan that is coordinated with multiple providers in the relevant jurisdictions.
[ Learn more about our jurisdiction exit services ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/global-project-management/jurisdiction-exit-services.html "Learn more about our jurisdiction exit services") 

New global requirements and developments  
    
The global regulatory landscape is constantly evolving. Our services for new global requirements and developments monitor statutory changes affecting entity operations and provide advice on the related impacts. We support this with clear remediation protocols and actionable plans, helping you stay ahead of regulatory changes and maintain compliance in good standing.
[ Learn more about our new global requirements and developments services ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/global-project-management/new-global-requirements-and-developments.html "Learn more about our new global requirements and developments services")
