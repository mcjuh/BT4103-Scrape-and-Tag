# KPMG US Payments
Payment modernization from a digital payments leader
##  A frictionless payment experience 
As companies venture through the evolving payments landscape and digital world, they strive to enhance their capabilities, architecture, processes, and partnerships in order to foster new value-added capabilities for their customers and new revenue streams for their organizations. A rapid increase in innovative payments and financial services products has accelerated the need for companies to transform their payment ecosystem.
Customers now have access to an abundance of financial products and are demanding a frictionless payment experience, underpinned with transparency and trust. In addition, legacy payment systems are undergoing transformation and are being disintermediated by new payment types and trends such as Real Time Payments (RTP) and ISO 20022.
At KPMG we assist our clients in navigating the new digital world by helping them modernize their payments ecosystem with services ranging from payments and product strategies and solution development through implementation of all payment types and getting our clients live in the market. 
### Three key challenges
Companies that have embraced the digital agenda are able to offer a differentiated customer experience, potentially gain market share, and overcome today’s key challenges: 
Payments product strategy
Businesses risk falling behind competitors who integrate new and emerging industry products. An enterprise payment strategy benefits both the consumer and the company.
Technology integrations
Existing payment ecosystems are frequently unable to adapt to current payment technologies. Outdated legacy systems, inability to digitize core systems, and integration effects from M&A have created complex back-end processes.
Process efficiencies
Companies are facing continuous pressures to identify inefficiencies in their processes, people, and technology. Using tools such as automation, reporting, and AI can help optimize those inefficiencies.

## KPMG offerings
### Our Payments Services include but are not limited to the following solutions and offerings:
#### Payments Platform Modernization
Provide payments modernization spanning strategy through implementation across all payments types, including wires, ACH, RTP/instant payments, and card to navigate rapidly evolving payment technologies.
#### API Product Strategy and Embedded Banking
Develop a treasury management API product strategy to drive deeper integration with client and vendor platforms to meet client needs, inclusive of ecosystem partnerships, API banking maturity benchmarking and API enablement.
#### B2B Modernization
Reimagine B2B service offerings through discovery, definition, design, and implementation of new age digital offerings.
#### Partnership Ecosystem Management
Provide organizations with a set of tools, accelerators, and frameworks to drive effective partnership ecosystems and help maximum outputs from existing and future partnerships.
#### Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) Product Optimization
Enhance adoption and responsible growth of BNPL products through product design, operational efficiencies, credit risk analytics, and regulatory compliance.
#### ISO 20022 Conversion
Perform ISO 20022 impact assessments and develop strategic roadmaps as well as provide enablement and implementation support to help our clients meet the industry mandate. In addition, we assist banks with value-added opportunities to monetize the new incremental ISO 20022 data.
#### Payment Systems Operations (PSO) Ecosystem
Establish a payments risk framework that drives an informed payments strategy, and identifies opportunities to increase operational efficiencies, control effectiveness, and compliance.
#### Commercial Treasury Client Onboarding
Develop a treasury management client onboarding experience that leverages leading digital tools, end-to-end workflow capabilities, and automation techniques to improve decrease cycle time and increase time to revenue while enhancing customer experience.
#### Billing and Invoicing Optimization
Provide strategic guidance and implementation of digital billing and invoicing to optimize front to back office synergies, improve customer experience, and enhance cost savings. 
#### Digital Asset Adoption
Provide digital asset strategy to meet growing demand from product innovation, readiness assessments to implementation.
