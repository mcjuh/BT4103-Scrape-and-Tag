# Research & Evaluation
Mathematica helps government and industry leaders invest wisely by uncovering what truly works. We generate the credible, action-ready insights needed to scale effective solutions, improve outcomes, and deliver lasting value.
## Ready to make progress and track results?
Whether you're launching something new, improving an existing program, or responding to emerging challenges, Mathematica can help.
  * [Evidence & Insights](https://www.mathematica.org/solutions/research-and-evaluation#landing_featuredwork)
  * [Related Staff](https://www.mathematica.org/solutions/research-and-evaluation#landing_staff)


### Identify what works and why
Across a range of [focus areas](https://www.mathematica.org/focus-areas), we apply experimental, quasi-experimental, and mixed-method evaluations to assess effectiveness, measure impact, and inform strategic decisions across complex programs and policies.
### Evidence for real-world decisions
From cost-effectiveness analyses to implementation and impact studies, our evaluations reflect real-world environments so you can understand what delivers value and what drives meaningful change.
### Insightful by design
We prioritize human-centered design and engage community perspectives, while our advanced survey operations use a range of tech-enabled data collection methods to capture the full picture.

### Children and Youth Behavioral Health Initiative—Evaluation Consulting Services 
Mathematica is conducting a rigorous, mixed-methods evaluation of the CYBHI to track its progress from design to implementation to outcomes. The findings will help California leaders understand what is working, what can be strengthened, and how to create lasting improvements in youth behavioral health.
