#  Digital design and modelling solutions 
As engineers and technical experts, our teams have developed powerful digital tools to solve complex design problems. 
Drawing on machine learning’s capabilities, these apps model scenarios, generate creative solutions from client requirements, and bring designers, clients and users into closer collaboration.
[ InForm Powerful KPI-driven generative design tool to optimise the design of buildings and infrastructure ](https://www.arup.com/services/digital-solutions/inform/) [ OPTIF Advanced offshore wind foundation design tool, enabling rapid, optimised solutions across the development cycle ](https://www.arup.com/services/digital-solutions/optif/) [ Rail Signalling Visualisation Enhance the efficiency and operational safety of new signalling systems ](https://www.arup.com/services/digital-solutions/rail-signalling-visualisation-tool/) [ SoundLab Explore the acoustic implications of design in a rich and immersive experience ](https://www.arup.com/services/digital-solutions/soundlab/)
