# KPMG Law Firm Mergers
Unlocking sustainable growth through a law firm merger
##  Want to set a clear strategy for your law firm merger? 
Law firms face accelerating market consolidation, rapid technological change, and growing pressure to scale. Firms that pursue strategic mergers can expand capabilities, deepen reach, create significant value, and stay competitive. But law firm mergers can also raise significant challenges: aligning commercial strategies, harmonizing cultures, and optimizing combined operations without disrupting the client or talent experience. 
Successful mergers require a compelling narrative, clear strategy, and disciplined execution. **The KPMG Law Firm Mergers** practice combines all three, along with the leadership and vision to get to a “yes.” As the strategic integration advisor in some of the largest global law firm combinations of the past decade, the KPMG Law Firm Mergers practice delivers a powerful approach that creates and captures value at every step of your deal.
We help law firms:
  1. Define the merger rationale and integration roadmap
  2. Align leadership, teams, and culture across both firms
  3. Execute integration that delivers Day 1 readiness and sustained growth


### At a glance
**What we do** : Provide strategic advisory and post-merger integration support to help law firms plan, execute, and realize value from mergers.
**Who it’s for:** Managing partners, executive committees, general counsel/chief legal officers, chief financial officers, chief operating officers, practice leaders, and integration leads navigating a law firm merger.
**Expected outcomes:** Clear deal thesis and integration roadmap, Day 1 readiness, vision and leadership alignment, and measurable value tracking post-close.
## Integrated capabilities for law firm mergers 
Law firm combinations can create opportunities to enhance capabilities, expand geographic reach, improve efficiencies, and capture additional market share, but converting these opportunities into actual value for the combined firm requires a clear strategy and disciplined execution. Our Law Firm Mergers practice combines our [**Global Legal Business Services**](https://kpmg.com/us/en/capabilities-services/tax-services/global-legal-business-services.html) and Transaction Strategy experience to support the strategic transformation and growth of law firms that are combining.
Merger strategy and deal thesis for law firm combinations
Define and articulate the merger rationale, align leadership on integration principles and decision-making structures, and translate priorities into a practical integration roadmap. This includes shaping the “transaction narrative” that helps build conviction among leaders and stakeholders.
Financial modeling, synergy analysis, and value drivers
Pressure-test key assumptions through financial and performance analysis, identify value drivers, and clarify financial implications to help inform early decisions and integration sequencing. Leadership comes away with a quantified picture of synergy potential, integration costs, and the assumptions that will most affect the outcome.
Integration planning, governance, and Day 1 readiness
Establish governance and the integration management office, build the integrated workplan and critical path forward, and drive readiness to help enable the combined firm to operate smoothly from Day 1. Rigorous planning helps protect revenue, retain talent, and preserve client confidence through the transition.
Post-merger integration, change enablement, and communications
Shape internal and external communications to maintain confidence and momentum, support cultural integration within the combined firm, and track synergy initiatives and combination outcomes. The goal is transparent value delivery, with measurable results that leadership can report to stakeholders.
