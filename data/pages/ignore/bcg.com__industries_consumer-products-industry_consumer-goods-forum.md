# BCG at The Consumer Goods Forum Global Summit 2026
BCG was proud to join industry leaders at The Consumer Goods Forum Global Summit 2026 in Vienna, Austria, to share AI-driven and sustainable growth strategies from our team of experts.
CGF’s Global Summit convened an influential group of CEOs and C-suite executives from the world’s leading [consumer goods](https://www.bcg.com/industries/consumer-products-industry/overview) [retailers](https://www.bcg.com/industries/retail/overview) and manufacturers, policymakers, and industry stakeholders to drive collective action on the sector’s most pressing challenges and most promising opportunities.BCG experts and senior industry leaders explored how to shape powerful new strategies that drive lasting value—whether reigniting growth, reducing costs, or translating into tangible business impact. Discussions also focused on how companies can move beyond AI pilots and embed these capabilities into everyday decision making, rethink their consumer engagement, and build an AI-first consumer company.

## FAQs

When was The Consumer Goods Forum Global Summit 2026?
The Consumer Goods Forum Global Summit was held from June 23-26, 2026.
Where was the Global Summit held? 
The Global Summit was held at the Austrian Center Vienna (ACV) in Vienna, Austria.
Who spoke at The Consumer Goods Forum Global Summit 2026? 
The Consumer Goods Forum Global Summit 2026 featured CEOs and senior leaders from top consumer goods and retail companies, with speakers in AI, sustainability, and supply chains.
What was the event schedule for the Global Summit 2026? 
The event schedule for the Global Summit included four days of keynotes, panels, and networking beginning at 9 AM June 23. The conference concluded on June 26 following the final sessions and closing program.
