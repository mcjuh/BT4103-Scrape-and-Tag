## How Has Technology Become a Mission-Critical Investment?
Technology is not just a line item in a ledger — [65% of respondents to BDO’s 2025 Nonprofit Benchmarking Survey](https://insights.bdo.com/rs/116-EDP-270/images/2025_NPE-Benchmarking-Overview_Infog.pdf) indicated they use AI for organizational strategy. Instead of functioning only as administrative support, technology serves as a strategic driver of mission delivery, operational success, and resilience across the organization in the following key areas:
### 
System Efficiency
add
Disparate systems do not generate real-time information. Manual processes are time consuming, increase risk to data integrity, and slow reporting, making it harder to meet stakeholder expectations. Yet many nonprofits operate with disparate or “Frankenstein” systems built over time, and those outdated platforms often cannot adapt to technological innovations like AI. Investing in technology like Workday improves efficiency by bringing people out of their silos and onto a single platform.
### 
Infrastructure Capabilities
add
The nonprofit’s organizational framework supports and enables its mission. Creating a single source of truth across that infrastructure delivers widespread benefits, including stronger risk management, improved stewardship, better grant compliance, and faster decision making in uncertain funding environments. Scalable tech solutions like Workday can enable leaders to respond quickly as priorities shift and address both current and future challenges.
### 
Mission Focus
add
Repetitive, time-consuming tasks pull staff away from a nonprofit organization’s primary initiatives. Automating routine tasks and using AI can reduce the administrative burden, improve data visibility, and promote better collaboration across all departments. A single system frees staff to concentrate more time on mission delivery
### 
Staff Support
add
Workforce strategy encompasses recruiting, onboarding, developing, and retaining employees. Disorganized workforce planning and operational problems such as manual processes often cause burnout and high turnover. Both challenges can significantly impact the nonprofit’s ability to carry out its mission. 
Integral Care, a healthcare-related nonprofit located in Texas, chose Workday to integrate its financial and human capital management capabilities. Clinicians gained access to AI tools that automated note-taking allowing them to focus on patients.
