#  Private Equity 
Fluctuating market dynamics are driving private equity (PE) firms and portfolio companies to seek additional value creation levers and new strategies to accelerate growth. PE firms need to evolve their operations and investment decisions to stay ahead of the market and deliver stronger returns.
EY, together with EY-Parthenon, helps PE firms and their holdings maximize value across the enterprise, faster. Our globally connected teams and alliances provide a full spectrum of services to unlock efficiencies, strengthen performance and accelerate enterprise-wide transformation.
With deep sector expertise, data-driven insights and advanced technology, we provide the confidence and capability to execute robust transaction strategies, drive value across the investment lifecycle, and leverage artificial intelligence (AI) for maximum impact.
Together, we are all in to support PE funds and portfolio companies to shape a more competitive future.
