#  Enterprise Assurance Services 
## A trusted partner for evolving assurance demands
Introduction  Hide page index 


## One integrated solution.
## One accountable team.
Grant Thornton's Enterprise Assurance Services bring independent attestation and certification under a single, integrated delivery model. Our services are tailored for organizations facing rising regulatory, stakeholder and board scrutiny. One governance structure, one control framework and one accountable team reduce duplication and streamline testing to deliver decision-useful reporting for your most complex assurance needs.
  * Strategic Assurance & SOC Services: SOC 1/2/2+/3, HITRUST, AIUC-1, ISO 27001, SOC for supply chain, SOC for cybersecurity, AUPs, readiness assessments, general examinations
  * Employee benefit plan audits across all major plan types
  * Sustainability assurance & advisory for ESG and evolving disclosures
  * Structured finance & transaction-focused attestation
  * Technology-enabled delivery via SOC.x®, Convene®, Hyperproof and Fieldguide
