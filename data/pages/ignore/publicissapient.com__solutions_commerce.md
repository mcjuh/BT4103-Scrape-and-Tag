How does AI improve digital commerce performance?
AI is most impactful when it runs inside live systems, not alongside them. Bodhi uses agents to personalize experiences, optimize decisions and adapt journeys while Slingshot and Sustain scale your platform and ensure it stays stable.
Can these AI platforms be used in either B2B or B2C digital commerce models?
Absolutely. Our AI platforms support both B2B and B2C digital commerce by adapting decision logic, workflows and operations to different buying behaviors without splitting the technology stack. They handle B2C needs like high-traffic discovery and checkout alongside B2B requirements such as negotiated pricing, custom catalogs and complex order flows. Teams can scale conversion and performance across business models with one platform foundation.
How do your AI platforms apply industry knowledge to digital commerce?
Our AI platforms apply industry knowledge by embedding sector-specific rules, data patterns and constraints directly into decisions and operations. That includes everything from demand and inventory shifts in retail to regulated workflows in industries like healthcare and financial services, all without rebuilding the platform for each case.
