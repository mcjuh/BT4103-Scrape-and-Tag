## Optimising Thermal Comfort, Energy Efficiency, and Weather Resistance
Building Envelope Commissioning (BEC) ensures that the exterior systems of a building perform optimally in terms of energy efficiency, durability, and safety. A BEC agent plays a crucial role from the design phase through construction and occupancy, focusing on verifying that building envelope systems, such as fenestrations, walls, wall crawl spaces, roofs, basements, and floors meet the project’s performance goals and regulatory standards.
## What We Offer
[ Design Review and Specifications Validation ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-envelope-commissioning/#collapse-7abe8626aa068dd844fb)
The BEC agent conducts in-depth reviews of design specifications to ensure alignment with performance objectives, including energy efficiency, thermal insulation, moisture control, and airtightness. This includes a close examination of material choices and detailing to anticipate and prevent potential issues.
[ Constructability Review and Recommendations ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-envelope-commissioning/#collapse-203a7c16aa068dd844fb)
To support a smooth construction process, the agent assesses constructability, focusing on how the design can be effectively implemented in the field. This includes identifying potential conflicts, challenges with installation sequences, and ensuring that the design details are practical.
[ Installation Oversight and Field Testing ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-envelope-commissioning/#collapse-daeab306aa068dd844fb)
During the construction phase, the BEC agent provides oversight to verify that installation meets design intent and project specifications. This includes observing critical installation procedures, reviewing quality control measures, and conducting field tests such as air and water infiltration tests.
[ Thermal and Moisture Performance Verification ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-envelope-commissioning/#collapse-7abe8626aa068dd84921)
Ensuring thermal integrity and moisture management is vital. The agent evaluates insulation, vapor barriers, and waterproofing components to validate their effectiveness in managing thermal and moisture transfer. Infrared thermography and moisture meters may be used for performance verification.
[ Compliance and Performance Testing Coordination ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-envelope-commissioning/#collapse-203a7c16aa068dd84921)
The BEC agent coordinates testing and inspections to verify compliance with applicable codes and standards. This includes overseeing specialized tests like water penetration, air leakage, and thermal performance, ensuring that building envelope elements perform as expected.
[ Documentation and Quality Assurance Reports ](https://aesg.com/engineering/building-envelope-commissioning/#collapse-7cbc2e56aa068dd84921)
To support a comprehensive commissioning process, the agent prepares detailed documentation, including reports on design reviews, inspection results, testing outcomes, and recommendations for corrective actions. These reports provide a quality assurance record that guides ongoing maintenance and operation.
## How We Deliver Building Envelope Performance
### Pre-Design Consultation
We work with architects and project teams from the early design stages to establish performance criteria for the building envelope, considering factors such as climate, building use, and regulatory requirements.
### Simulation and Modelling
We conduct thermal, moisture, and acoustic simulations to evaluate the façade's performance under various conditions. This informs decisions on materials, insulation, and detailing.
### Material Specification
We recommend materials and systems that optimise energy efficiency, weather resistance, and acoustic performance while considering lifecycle cost and durability.
### Testing and Verification
Our team oversees on-site and laboratory testing to validate the façade's performance, including tests for air tightness, water penetration, and thermal bridging.
### Ongoing Performance Monitoring
We provide services for the ongoing assessment of building envelope performance to ensure that façades continue to meet performance standards over time.
## Suitable Applications for Building Envelope Performance
### High-Performance Buildings
Enhancing energy efficiency and indoor comfort for buildings seeking certifications like LEED, BREEAM, or WELL.
### Renovations and Retrofits
Upgrading existing building envelopes to meet current performance standards, including improved insulation, air tightness, and weather resistance.
