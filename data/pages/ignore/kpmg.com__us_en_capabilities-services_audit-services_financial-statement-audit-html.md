# A financial statement audit designed for trust—powered by KPMG Clara AI
AI-powered, human-assured. A KPMG financial statement audit strengthens confidence blending governed AI for continuous analysis and precise risk focus, empowering our experienced professionals to provide deeper judgment—delivering stronger assurance with minimal disruption. 
##  Agents with clear human accountability 
Built for a world that demands integrity and agility, our audits deploy autonomous AI agents and operators combined with experienced professionals with AI-integrated technology designed to improve risk focus and transparency—ensuring that ultimate accountability, independence, and critical judgment remain strictly human-owned.
