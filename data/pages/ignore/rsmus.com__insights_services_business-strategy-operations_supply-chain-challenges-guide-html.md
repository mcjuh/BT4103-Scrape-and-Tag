## Achieve supply chain harmony in uncertain times
Today’s global disruptions, shifting tariffs and rising costs are wreaking havoc on supply chains. Other considerations, like environmental, social and governance (ESG) priorities and tax liabilities, only add to the complexity. The good news is that the right technology, people and processes can help your business weather uncertainties and lay the foundation for future growth. This is the essence of supply chain orchestration—a strategic approach that synchronizes core functions to build resiliency and achieve efficient, end-to-end coordination across the entire supply chain.
Implementing supply chain orchestration as a fundamental strategy can help businesses move beyond short-term fixes and unlock long-term value. By ensuring that the various components of their supply chain are working in harmony, businesses can achieve key benefits such as:
  * Lower operational costs through streamlined coordination, enhanced resource allocation and the elimination of process redundancies
  * Greater efficiency and visibility, which improve lead times, inventory management and workflow processes
  * A stronger competitive advantage by enabling quick responses to market shifts and delivering consistent, superior value to customers


RSM’s new interactive e-book—“**Supply chain orchestration: Overcome challenges and achieve harmony in uncertain times** ”—is designed to support supply chain, procurement and logistics professionals in addressing disruptions, reducing trade risks and building resiliency.
## What’s inside?
We can’t eliminate uncertainty, but we can support your efforts to manage it effectively. Our e-book includes actionable strategies rooted in supply chain orchestration to help you build alignment, enhance resilience and create harmony across your operations. Inside, you’ll find the following insights to help you confidently navigate challenges and move your supply chain forward:
**Prepare for and prioritize supply chain risk reduction** around global instability and disruptions, potential tariffs, ESG priorities and tax liabilities.
**Increase supply chain efficiency and agility** by strengthening inventory management, supplier relationships, regulatory compliance and logistics.
**Explore supply chain optimization technologies** such as automation tools powered by artificial intelligence and machine learning, as well as enterprise and business management software like Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Oracle NetSuite.
Orchestrating solutions that account for disruptions, evolving technologies and business objectives to deliver supply chain harmony is no small feat. Get the information and insights you need to respond to whatever is next.
