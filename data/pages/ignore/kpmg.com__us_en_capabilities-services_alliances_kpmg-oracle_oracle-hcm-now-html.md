### An answer for organizations intimidated by implementing Oracle HCM
Organizations with smaller employee headcounts often lack the willingness to take on an implementation of Oracle Human Capital Management (HCM)—despite the notable benefits of its industry-leading tools and features. That uneasiness is often based in fear that the implementation will be overly complex, of longer duration, and too expensive. KPMG, in collaboration with Oracle, has built an offering that helps take these specific concerns out of the equation: Oracle HCM Now.
### Accelerate time to value with fixed fees, a predefined scope, and a simplified and rapid deployment program
Unifying HR processes, talent and workforce management, employee experience, and payroll doesn’t have to be such a heavy lift when you bring in the KPMG team and our simplified approach, practice capabilities, accelerators, and assets that culminate into our Oracle HCM Now offering.
### Embracing the benefits of Oracle Human Capital Management (HCM) cloud technologies is now more accessible than ever
Learn more about how KPMG can help expedite your Oracle HCM implementation leveraging Oracle HCM Now by:
  * Going beyond traditional technology implementation to provide lasting value
  * Leveraging a unique and modern HR platform deployment model
  * Focusing on the most impactful outcomes and benefits based on your needs
  * Working with a Global Oracle Cloud Leader with over 700 projects to glean from
