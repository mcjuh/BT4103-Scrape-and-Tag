# Executive committee
## Since May 20, 2020, Capgemini Group management has been led by Aiman Ezzat, Chief Executive Officer. 
The Group Executive Committee currently comprises 38 members, including the 13 members of the Group Executive Board indicated by an asterisk (*). The Group Executive Board facilitates the carrying out of the Group’s operations. The Executive Committee helps it to define Capgemini’s principal directions, setting major strategic priorities and the associated action plans, and ensuring that these plans are effectively implemented at the operational level.
### Diversity in management
##### The Group has decided to implement **a progressive increase in both female and international representation on the Group’s Executive Committee**. The Group’s long-term objective is to achieve the same percentage of women in the Executive Committee as in the Group’s headcount. We also intend to increase female representation to 10% of positions with the greatest responsibility within Group executive leaders and, more widely, in the senior leadership community, with a target of 30% of women in executive leadership positions by 2025. These objectives are combined with voluntary internal measures and policies aiming to ensure a diversified and non-discriminatory global representation at all levels of the organization.
