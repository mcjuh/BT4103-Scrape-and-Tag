For tax departments, analysis and planning is critical to making strategic decisions. Here, tax professionals can add value by determining ways to lower corporate tax liabilities and reduce exposure to risk. A constantly changing regulatory landscape only increases the complexity of analysis and elevates its importance.
Skills and technology from KPMG and Alteryx can help. KPMG provides the tax and technology-convergent resources and experience to define a holistic data strategy. Alteryx provides automation tools that gather, move, and transform data. Together, KPMG and Alteryx can help tax professionals focus on strategic decisions, analysis, planning and risk management. The offering from KPMG and Alteryx streamlines the process from procurement to solution delivery.
