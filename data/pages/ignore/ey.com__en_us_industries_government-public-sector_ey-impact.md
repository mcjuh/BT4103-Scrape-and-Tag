### Identify community needs
#### Targeted improvement opportunities
  * Measure community needs and view clear graphics highlighting areas of opportunity to improve community outcomes.

### Secure additional funding
#### Relevant grant suggestions
  * Utilize EY Impact’s natural language search to discover and pursue grant opportunities suited to your organization’s goals. 


#### Boost your funding
  * Improve funding by leveraging grant opportunities that are less commonly applied for.


Read more  Read less 
### Generate a grant response in 15 minutes
#### Contextualized grant responses
  * Using preloaded data and past grant submissions, EY Impact keeps context across interactions, confirming that responses match prior inputs.


#### Intuitive interactions
  * EY Impact dynamically prompts users for specific details or suggest improvements based on leading practices to create stronger grant responses.


#### Industry compliance
  * Predefined templates and rules verify that responses are compliant with industry standards and meet quality expectations.
