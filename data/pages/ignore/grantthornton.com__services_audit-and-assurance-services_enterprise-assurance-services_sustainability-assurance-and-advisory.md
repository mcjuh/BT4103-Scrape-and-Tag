#  Sustainable Assurance & Advisory 
## Creating sustainable value for business and investors
## Sustainability: An executive imperative
Sustainability is front and center for investors, executives, boards & audit committees. With customers, employees and regulators increasingly focused on sustainability reporting, particularly climate-related disclosures, the imperative is pressing. The demand for transparency requires that companies work to understand how sustainability fits into their business and reporting.
Our sustainability offerings help businesses understand how to mitigate risk and transform their operational models. Grant Thornton can help organizations navigate these challenges to drive growth.
Strategy and operations
Our sustainability offerings support businesses in understanding the sustainability landscape, mitigating risk and proactively transforming their business models. Grant Thornton can help your organization navigate this complex and evolving domain, leveraging insights to drive growth and create long-term value.
Reporting and compliance
Our team helps companies produce investor-grade reporting that meets stakeholder and regulatory expectations. We help management draft disclosure reports that comply with evolving ESG regulatory requirements at a state, federal and global level, meet voluntary ESG standards and improve internal controls and processes for existing disclosures. Our services include:
  * ESG reporting
  * ESG assurance readiness


Audit and assurance
Our professionals help clients maintain stakeholder confidence in their non-financial ESG reporting by reviewing ESG-related company disclosures and providing limited or reasonable assurance over ESG and sustainability metrics in accordance with AICPA standards. Our services include:
  * ESG assurance


Integrated service offerings
Grant Thornton’s One Firm approach features a dedicated group of specialists across our advisory, audit and tax practices who focus on implementing a variety of strategic solutions. The ESG & Sustainability team regularly collaborates with these specialists to provide holistic solutions to our clients in areas such as tax; risk and scenario analysis; and forensic services.
