# West Monroe Named in 2026 Risk Consulting Services Landscape by Independent Research Firm
Firm believes its inclusion reflects its industry-first approach of embedding expertise across business functions to reflect how companies actually manage risk 
CHICAGO, September 3, 2026 — West Monroe, an AI-native global consulting firm, announced it has been named a Notable Provider in Forrester’s [Risk Consulting Services Landscape, Q3 2026](https://www.forrester.com/report/the-risk-consulting-services-landscape-q3-2026/RES200000204). The report lists West Monroe’s industry focus as financial services and insurance, healthcare, and utilities. The landscape provides an overview of the risk consulting services market and notable providers, helping leaders evaluate providers based on factors including size, geographic focus, industry focus, and business scenarios.
AI is changing how risk is managed, from how organizations identify and monitor exposure to how they design controls and make decisions. At the same time, regulatory change and technology transformation are raising expectations for risk functions to move faster and show clearer business value. The report identifies regulatory risk and compliance, risk program transformation, and technology risk management as the three core business scenarios buyers most often seek from risk consulting providers.
“Managing risk effectively takes more than risk expertise alone,” said [Dan Belmont](https://www.westmonroe.com/our-team/dan-belmont), Managing Partner, Client Strategy at West Monroe.
The challenges clients face rarely fit neatly into one function. They cut across the business, technology, operations, and regulation. Bringing those perspectives together helps organizations move from identifying risk to actually addressing it, with solutions that work in the real world and fit the realities of their industry.
Dan Belmont
Managing Partner, Client Strategy at West Monroe
Rather than operating risk as a standalone practice, West Monroe embeds risk expertise directly within its industry and functional teams. Its industry-led model brings together sector expertise with business, technology, regulatory, and operational capabilities, applying a common enterprise risk methodology while tailoring solutions to the specific risks and regulatory expectations of each industry.
Across financial services and insurance, healthcare, utilities, and more, West Monroe helps clients address risk as part of broader business and technology transformation:
  * **Financial services and insurance:** Regulatory response and remediation, financial crime transformation, enterprise risk management, and control modernization. For [one large regional commercial bank](https://www.westmonroe.com/client-results/regional-bank-smarter-kyc-practices), West Monroe redesigned KYC practices, reducing unnecessary client touchpoints by 43% while strengthening identification of more than 50 high-risk client types.
  * **Healthcare:** Data and privacy risk, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience. For [one private equity-backed healthcare provider](https://www.westmonroe.com/client-results/enabling-healthcare-m-a-through-cyber-resilience), West Monroe strengthened cyber resilience across 2,000+ endpoints in 12 weeks, secured 1,000+ workstations, and eliminated 90+ unauthorized applications.
  * **Utilities:** Regulatory risk management, grid resiliency, asset and third-party risk, and infrastructure modernization. For [one national utility](https://www.westmonroe.com/client-results/90-percent-faster-cyber-response-for-a-secure-grid), West Monroe implemented automated isolation procedures that improved cyberattack response time by 90% across 60+ critical processes.


A core element of West Monroe’s approach is connecting risk strategy to implementation. The firm helps clients move from identifying risks and defining frameworks to designing controls, implementing technology, transforming operating models, and embedding risk into major business initiatives. Its work spans:
  * **Risk Advisory & Strategy: **Enterprise risk framework design and modernization, regulatory interpretation and change management, risk assessments, governance, and policy design.
  * **Risk Transformation & Implementation:** Regulatory remediation, control design and operationalization, AI-enabled compliance automation, GRC platform implementation, and target operating model deployment.
  * **Risk Embedded in Business Initiatives:** M&A diligence and value creation, cybersecurity resiliency, financial crime transformation, and digital, cloud, AI, and platform modernization with integrated risk controls.


West Monroe’s focus on implementation reflects a broader shift in the market. Forrester notes that buyers increasingly expect risk consulting providers to help implement and operationalize recommendations rather than simply assess outcomes, driving demand for deeper technology, transformation, and implementation capabilities.
