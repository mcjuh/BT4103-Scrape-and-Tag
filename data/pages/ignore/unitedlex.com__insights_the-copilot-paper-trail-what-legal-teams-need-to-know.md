# The Copilot Paper Trail: What Legal Teams Need to Know 
## Complete the form to access the Article
### Insights into hidden data risks, retention realities, and discovery implications created by Copilot’s data storage structures. 
With Microsoft Copilot embedded in work applications, it has quickly become part of the everyday work rhythm. While users within an organization may not consider the risks from a legal perspective, they can cause hidden litigation issues based on the technology’s nuanced, and seemingly sporadic, data storage and collection practices. 
Understanding where Copilot-generated information lives and how it’s captured is essential to avoiding gaps, missteps, or unnecessary risk during litigation. In  _The Copilot Paper Trail: What Legal Teams Need to Know_ , we share findings from Copilot data mapping and strategic legal considerations for organizations using the technology.
