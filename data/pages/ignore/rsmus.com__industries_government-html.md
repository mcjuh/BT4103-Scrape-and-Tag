## Consulting services that modernize government operations
We help government services organizations across the country—including hundreds of state and local government offices and agencies—improve their performance through a comprehensive understanding of their business models, internal processes, objectives and daily realities. We ask the right questions, read between the lines, listen actively and think holistically—and we build relationships based on experience and trust.
##  We support all levels of governmental organizations, including:  

State and local governments  
    
A broader range of constituencies are challenging state and local governments to deliver more transparency, efficiency and accountability. We help state and local governments across the U.S. preserve and protect the public interest. 

School districts  
    
School districts must balance the delivery of high-quality education with the demands of financial sustainability, regulatory compliance and public trust. We offer a comprehensive suite of services tailored to the unique operational, financial and compliance challenges of K–12 public education systems. 

Federal agencies  
    
Federal agencies are turning to private sector best practices for performance and process improvements. We provide compliance and risk review services to help agencies incorporate internal controls for greater efficiency and accountability. 

Tribal governments  
    
The challenges of tribal governments are unlike those of any other entity. We identify opportunities that increase the productivity and profitability of tribal enterprises, programs and other economic development ventures.
##  Top government services industry trends 
**Governance and risk** : Organizations must strategically implement data integrity and classification practices that ensure public data is governed, managed and protected effectively.
**Cybersecurity** : As stewards of sensitive data and critical infrastructure, government organizations face persistent cyber risk. Building resilient defenses and secure digital environments is essential to ensuring compliance, continuity and public confidence.
**Modernization** : Governments are embracing cloud scalability, artificial intelligence-driven efficiency and advanced analytics to enhance public services while upholding robust security and data-driven decision making.
**Regulatory and policy changes** : Governments must navigate shifting political priorities and regulatory landscapes while adhering to federal, state and local regulations. 
**A dynamic workforce** : Government organizations are prioritizing talent attraction, retention and reskilling while leveraging technology to boost employee engagement and productivity in a rapidly evolving skills landscape.
**Grant administration** : Effective grant management ensures the transparent, compliant and efficient allocation of public funds to drive policy outcomes and community impact.

##  Challenges and risks in government services 
Government leaders are under pressure to ensure agencies function like businesses—delivering quality services, implementing efficient processes and setting standards that can withstand examination.
At the same time, government organizations must deliver results in constantly shifting economic and political environments, all while staying focused on their mission to protect the public interest.
### **RSM helps government leaders address a variety of unprecedented challenges:**
  * **Reporting and compliance requirements.** We help government entities establish controls to enhance accountability and transparency; reduce waste, fraud and abuse; and limit their exposure to risk.
  * **Risk and internal controls.** Government organizations face operational, financial and compliance risks that can hinder strategic objectives. Process breakdowns, system limitations and shifting policy environments increase exposure to oversight findings. RSM helps agencies assess risk, strengthen internal controls and build resilient processes that support accountability and performance.
  * **Business process reengineering and operational transformation.** Efforts to streamline government operations must align with mission priorities and deliver measurable results. RSM helps agencies assess current workflows, eliminate inefficiencies and redesign processes to improve performance, accountability and service delivery.
  * **Technology consulting.** We help develop tailored solutions, whether they involve implementing a new information technology environment, migrating from a legacy platform or transforming business intelligence capabilities.
  * **Aging infrastructure.** Government organizations across the U.S. face significant challenges related to aging infrastructure—both physical and technological. We address aging infrastructure through integrated strategies that combine modernization, managed services cybersecurity and stakeholder collaboration.
