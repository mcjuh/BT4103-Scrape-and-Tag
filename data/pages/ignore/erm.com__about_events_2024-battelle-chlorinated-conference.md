#  2024 Battelle Chlorinated Conference
June 2-6, 2024 
Colorado Convention Center 
[ Learn More ](https://www.battelle.org/conferences/chlorinated-conference)
**ERM is an exhibitor and leading presenter at the 2024 International Chlorinated Conference held June 2-6, 2024, in Denver, Colorado.**
Battelle's environmental engineers, scientists and professionals offer focused expertise to government and industrial clients in the U.S. and abroad. Battelle works with clients to develop innovative, sustainable and cost-effective solutions to complex problems in site characterization, assessment, monitoring, remediation, restoration and management.
The 2024 Chlorinated Conference will feature 477 platform presentations, over 650 poster presentations and 8 technical panel discussions. Attendance is estimated to be approximately 1,500 environmental professionals from 22 countries.
##  ERM AT THE CONFERENCE
ERM is exhibiting at the conference and will be presenting 23 poster presentations and 16 platform presentations.
We will also be exhibiting at Booth #300 and hosting a private client reception on June 4.
For more information on our client event, contact allison.cepukenas@erm.com.
