#  Tax Managed Services 
Tax and finance functions today face dynamic pressures in staying on top of legislative change globally, keeping pace with technology, and attracting and retaining professionals with future-focused skills. These functions face a tall order: do more, and provide more value, with fewer resources, while reducing costs amid a rapidly shifting world of risk and regulation.

## What EY can do for you
EY’s Managed Services for Tax can help you reimagine your tax and finance operating model to get ahead of evolving regulation, technology and talent demands. Significant investment in leading-edge technology, standardized processes and service centers of excellence enable us to carry out key activities more efficiently, helping you to manage risk and improve visibility, drive innovation and realize increased value from data today and into the future.
