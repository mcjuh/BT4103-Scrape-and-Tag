#  Publicis Sapient expands hiring in India to Coimbatore, Madurai, Pune and Hyderabad 
August 4, 2021 
Share
## GURUGRAM/BENGALURU, August 4, 2021
[Publicis Sapient](https://www.publicissapient.com/), the **[digital transformation company](https://www.publicissapient.com/digital-business-transformation)** , announced today its intention to expand its hiring in India to Coimbatore, Madurai, Pune and Hyderabad. This expansion is in line with the company’s flexible culture that it pioneered in 2000 with its Globally Distributed Delivery (GDD) model.
“The pandemic has taught us that people don’t need to be in a physical office to stay connected or create impact. We’ve always believed in reaching talent wherever they are. With the increasing demand for digital business transformation services, we are seeing a significant upswing in our business. In the second quarter, Publicis Groupe achieved organic growth of more than 17% and Publicis Sapient delivered organic growth in the US of over 27%. Our business expansion is driving aggressive hiring in India. I believe cities like Coimbatore, Madurai, Pune and Hyderabad offer growing technology talent in whom we would like to invest,” said Sanjay Menon, Managing Director, Publicis Sapient, India.
India is Publicis Sapient’s largest talent market and the company is committed to hiring the brightest tech minds in the country. With new ways of working and remote working options, the company plans to expand in other non-office locations and invest in building world-class talent.
