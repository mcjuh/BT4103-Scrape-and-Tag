#  Data & AI for the Enterprise 
We help enterprise organizations move from scattered data and stalled pilots to governed AI systems running in production. That means models tied to real workflows, clear ownership, traceable lineage and measurable financial impact.
## Our approach
The difference between an AI pilot and AI in production often comes down to data expertise. It fails because definitions change, lineage is unclear, controls are bolted on late and no one owns the model after launch. We fix the plumbing first by defining enterprise KPIs and decision points. Then, we design governed data architectures with lineage and access controls built in. We embed model monitoring, drift detection and audit logs before the first deployment. Finally, we ship AI into production and keep it there.
