# Zero-Based Budgeting
In a volatile market often shaped by investor scrutiny, profitable growth has become the C-suite’s top mandate. BCG’s zero-based budgeting approach frees capital to allow companies to accelerate performance, sharpen priorities, and invest where it matters most.
The C-suite is redefining [cost management](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/cost-management) from that of a constraint to a catalyst for stronger performance. As companies adapt to volatility and disruption, the ability to reallocate resources with precision has become a clear differentiator.BCG’s zero-based budgeting consulting experts help leaders across all industries rethink spending from the ground up. The result: through a zero-based transformation, we enable clients to redirect capital toward the capabilities and growth that matter most.

## Our Approach to Zero-Based Transformation
BCG helps companies deliver lasting cost and performance gains through a structured, insight-led approach to zero-based transformation. We combine [AI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence)-enabled diagnostics, proprietary benchmarks, and agile collaboration to drive real outcomes—fast.
Our zero-based budgeting consultants work side-by-side with clients to do the following:
  * **Gain a redesigned organization from the ground up.** Clients achieve leaner, more effective structures through zero-based organization redesign. We challenge legacy operating models, reimagine workflows, and eliminate low-value activities—embedding collaborative [change management](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/organization-strategy/culture-change-management) from the start to foster shared accountability across teams. 
  * **Achieve full transparency into cost categories.** Our [GenAI](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/artificial-intelligence/generative-ai)-powered diagnostics and proprietary benchmarks create a clear baseline of zero-based spending, surfacing cost anomalies in days, not months. This enables rapid identification of savings and reinvestment opportunities. 
  * **Align resource allocation to strategic goals.** We co-develop a reinvestment roadmap that embeds ROI logic into decision rights and fosters a cost-conscious culture. The result: capital is continually redeployed where it creates the most value. 
  * **Sustain the impact with real-time governance.** With a control tower model and digital dashboards, clients gain full visibility into progress. Clear tracking mechanisms and strong governance frameworks help institutionalize changes and free up funds for long-term growth.


Doing so leads to an agile, high-performing organization that captures value and sustains it.
