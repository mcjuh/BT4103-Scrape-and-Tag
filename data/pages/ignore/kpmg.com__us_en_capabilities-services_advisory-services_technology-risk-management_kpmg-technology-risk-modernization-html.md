# KPMG Modern Technology Risk
Centers of Excellence
The threat landscape in today’s volatile environment continues to evolve shifting attack vectors and variable risks. As digital transformations accelerate in business functions at a record pace, our Technology Risk network has developed the KPMG Modern Technology Risk Centers of Excellence (COE) to provide insights and help organizations evolve their capabilities to respond to digital acceleration, cloud transformation, and emerging technologies.
**Our COE can offer organizations:**
  * Highly technical subject matter professionals to execute specialist assessments, including new emerging IT delivery practices/emerging technology ​
  * ​Access to modern IT risk practices in order to mature in-house risk management and controls​
  * Design and implement controls and compliance measures real time during technology modernization and cloud efforts


**Ask yourself these questions:**
  * Do you have IT risk and compliance embedded into your technology or business transformations? ​
  * Have you deployed a cloud security and control program to monitor key risks and cyber security areas as part of your cloud modernization efforts?​
  * Have you transformed your risk and controls measures where new modern IT delivery processes have been deployed – for example around your DevSecOps processes?​
  * Have you performed a risk assessment over new technologies being deployed – AI, process automation, low code platforms?​
  * Are you able to proactively and efficiently respond to all of your stakeholder compliance requests?​
  * Are you receiving increasing requests for assurance and transparency around your risk, compliance and information security program?​
  * Are your risk and compliance efforts taking a lot of effort and are you interested in automating and monitoring controls more real time?


### Our Modern Technology Risk COE can help your organization get the answers to these lingering questions.
