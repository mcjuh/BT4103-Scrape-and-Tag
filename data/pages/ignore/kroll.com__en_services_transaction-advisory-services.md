# Transaction Advisory Services
Kroll’s Transaction Advisory Services platform offers corporate and financial investors with deep accounting and technical expertise, commercial knowledge, industry insight and seamless analytical services throughout the deal continuum.
From decision support at origination to deal closing and beyond, the Transaction Advisory Services practice supports its clients in navigating pre-transaction due diligence and post-transaction asset protection and value creation throughout the transaction lifecycle. We advise on all aspects of the investment cycle, employing an approach that focuses on value drivers and deal-breakers that are critical to investment decisions.
Combining industry knowledge with deep transaction experience, we support critical decision making with essential guidance and robust analysis. We provide our clients with the information they need to make informed business decisions for buy- and sell-side (i.e., vendor due diligence) transaction engagements, refinancings and other transaction types. 
## Transaction Advisory Services Provides Independent Support Through the Transaction Lifecycle 
Our transaction advisory services professionals provide [financial & accounting](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/transaction-advisory-services/financial-due-diligence), [tax diligence and structuring](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/transaction-advisory-services/mergers-and-acquisitions-tax-services), [information technology](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/transaction-advisory-services/it-transaction-advisory-services)/cyber, operational due diligence and accounting advisory to clients.

## Key Differentiators
  * Senior level attention throughout every phase of a transaction, which allows us to identify and communicate key deal issues early in the process
  * Dedicated professionals with strong technical and industry expertise providing a flexible approach and seamless coordination with your team and other advisors
  * Virtually no conflicts or independence issues under Sarbanes-Oxley. We can provide due diligence, valuation, projection analysis, fairness and solvency opinions, and transfer pricing on the same transactions, allowing us to be an advocate for our clients during the negotiation process
  * Access to the industry expertise of our M&A and valuation groups, which enables us to identify potential targets
  * We integrate valuation, tax (i.e., business incentives, transfer pricing), process improvement/merger integration within the due diligence process.
