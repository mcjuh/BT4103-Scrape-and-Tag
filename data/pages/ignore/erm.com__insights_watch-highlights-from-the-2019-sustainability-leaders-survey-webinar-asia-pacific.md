# Watch: Highlights from The 2019 Sustainability Leaders Survey Webinar – Asia / Pacific
#### GlobeScan and SustainAbility presented a webinar on July 9th San Francisco / July 10th at Hong Kong to discuss the results of [The 2019 Sustainability Leaders Survey](https://www.erm.com/insights/key-takeaways-from-our-sustainable-food-systems-workshop/).
Hear from global thought leaders who shared their insights on the 2019 survey findings and answered your questions on the current and future state of sustainability leadership:
  * Hendrick Rosenthal, Director of Group Sustainability, CLP
  * Wander Meijer, Director, GlobeScan
  * Bron York, Analyst, SustainAbility


The Sustainability Leaders Survey is the largest and longest-running survey of its kind and has tracked expert opinions on sustainable development leadership for over 20 years.
