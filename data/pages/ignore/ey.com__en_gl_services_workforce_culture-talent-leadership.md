#### Culture
We know that organizational culture can be intentionally shaped to support vision, mission and strategy, which are of little value without a culture to bring them to life. A culture rooted in clear and consistent strengths — one that motivates individuals and minimizes behavioral risks — drives both productivity and profitability. 
Our methodology is designed to understand an organization’s current culture and evolve its environment to meet the demands of the future. To understand culture at a granular level, we assess employee behaviors, uncovering the unique dynamics that define an organization’s environment. This in-depth analysis helps us identify both strengths and areas for growth, providing the foundation for purposeful cultural transformation aligned to your business strategy.
Our teams, informed by the latest in behavior science, help organizations get to root causes quickly and activate culture change through a top-down and bottom-up approach. In addition to working with senior leaders to set the tone at the top, we identify influencers who can activate culture change through grass roots movement supported by behavior sprints, nudging and learning tools. With culture as a lever, we also help organizations enhance transactional success and create long-term value through strategic cultural alignment.
#### Inclusiveness and Belonging
We help clients make progress toward building a truly human-centered workplace where all employees feel they belong. Our multidisciplinary approach combines data-driven insights, powerful conversations and systemic interventions to deliver measurable, sustainable progress.
Our diagnostic tools support the individual strengths of the clients we serve and help them to focus efforts in places where they can get the greatest impact and track results effectively. Organizations can leverage inclusiveness as a key enabler to success by integrating it into their business and talent strategy.
#### Creating a Talent Advantage
Organizations can achieve a Talent Advantage, as identified by the [EY 2024 Work Reimagined Survey](https://eur01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ey.com%2Fen_gl%2Finsights%2Fworkforce%2Fwork-reimagined-survey&data=05%7C02%7CAnjana.Vijayan1%40gds.ey.com%7Ca6803b6e04c74f7b088d08dd30b49df3%7C5b973f9977df4bebb27daa0c70b8482c%7C0%7C0%7C638720274496052246%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=x5KLLsHFG2aLYgXn3PPHrTfjIcPjr9yLfFXyQfVhoAU%3D&reserved=0), in the mid- to long-term by excelling across five key dimensions: talent health and flow; work technology and generative AI (GenAI); total rewards priorities; learning, skills and career pathways; culture and workplaces.

  * ##  Determining the Talent Advantage
The EY 2024 Work Reimagined Survey segmented 1,595 employer responses on 22 variables, using latent class segmentation. Talent Advantage is based on:
    * Two work technology variables (satisfaction with company processes/workflows and extent of GenAI utilization)
    * 12 variables of talent health (reward, development, culture)
    * Five variables related to employee experience (company brand, jobs and work content, quality of leaders and managers, teams, industry accomplishments)
    * Two variables relate to flexibility (when and where people work)
    * The presence of a strategic HR function
Through statistical modeling, we identified three segments — Talent Advantage (the most strategic organizations) representing 32% of the survey sample, Talent Middle (37% of the survey sample), and Talent Disadvantage (the least strategic organizations) representing 31% of the survey sample.
