## Expert valuation services tailored to your industry needs
PwC Singapore’s Valuations team provides expert and reliable valuation services to a diverse range of clients in various industries. We handle complex valuations and financial analysis, leveraging the latest industry standards and methodologies. Whether it’s for financial reporting, transaction advisory, or dispute resolution, our team is dedicated to delivering insightful and reasonable valuations that meet our clients’ unique requirements. We are committed to understanding the key drivers and complexities of various industries and are well equipped to handle any valuation needs. 
## #1 Leading mid-market deal maker internationally
Ranked by Mergermarket as the #1 financial advisory firm by deal count in 2022, the PwC Valuation Services team is a diverse, global team of industry experts serving a wide range of clients - from global multinational corporations and regional mid-sized companies to fast growing early stage startups.
## Our competency
  * Extensive experience in a wide range of industries and asset classes, delivering reliable valuation services.
  * Providing price competitive services, without compromising on quality.
  * Relevant and up-to-date valuations by leveraging on the PwC Deals network in PwC.


We assist companies to assess the value of a target company in the process of a M&A transaction. This support involves analysing the financial and operational performance of the target company, considering market trends and economic conditions, and applying various valuation methods to determine a fair market value. This helps the relevant stakeholders to make informed decisions.
##### M&A valuation report
The goal of litigation valuation is to provide objective and impartial evidence to support a legal argument, helping parties in a dispute to reach a fair resolution. We provide expert opinions on the value of businesses involved in various type of legal lawsuits from contract disputes to shareholders disputes.
##### Litigation valuation support
For tax valuation, we provide our independent opinion of the value of the business for tax-related situations, including but not limited to transfer pricing, tax restructuring, tax dispute resolution or ensuring compliance with tax laws and regulations.
##### Tax valuation support
  * We provide expert opinions on the allocation of the purchase price of an acquisition among the assets acquired. This support is used in the context of M&A to ensure that the purchase price is fairly allocated among the various assets.
  * Assets would include the assets already recognised on the Balance Sheet of the target, as well as Intangible Assets that are identified and valued throughout the purchase price allocation process.


##### Purchase price allocation report
We provide expert opinions on the value of various IP assets, including but not limited to trademarks, patents and copyrights.
##### Intellectual property valuation support
We provide expert opinions on the value of assets or businesses for audit and financial reporting purposes, ensuring companies meet regulatory requirements and maintain credibility with stakeholders.
