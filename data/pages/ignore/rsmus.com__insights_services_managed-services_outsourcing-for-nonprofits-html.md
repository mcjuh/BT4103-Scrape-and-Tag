#  Outsourcing for nonprofits
### Empowering mission-driven organizations through operational excellence
#### [Download our outsourcing guide ](https://rsmus.com/insights/industries/nonprofit/outsourcing-for-nonprofits-buyers-guide.html) #### [Connect with our team ](https://rsmus.com/insights/services/managed-services/outsourcing-for-nonprofits.html#contact)
Nonprofits are built to serve communities, not to be bogged down by back-office complexity. At RSM, we understand the unique challenges nonprofit organizations face: limited resources, rising compliance demands, and difficulty attracting and retaining skilled talent. That’s why we offer tailored outsourcing solutions that align with your mission, strengthen financial sustainability and help you focus on what matters most: your impact.
## Why outsource?
**Stabilize operations** filling critical finance, accounting, IT and human resources roles with experienced professionals.
**Increase efficiency** through streamlined processes and cloud-based technologies.
**Enhance transparency and compliance** with real-time reporting and audit-ready systems.
**Scale flexibly** to meet evolving demands without the overhead of full-time hires.
## What we offer
Our managed services are designed to meet nonprofits where they are. Whether you need full-service support or targeted knowledge, we provide:
  * [Finance and accounting outsourcing ](https://rsmus.com/services/financial-management/finance-accounting-outsourcing.html)
  * [Managed IT services](https://rsmus.com/services/digital-transformation/managed-it-services.html)
  * [Human resources outsourcing ](https://rsmus.com/services/business-strategy-operations/human-capital/human-resources-outsourcing-services.html)
  * [Risk and security outsourcing](https://rsmus.com/services/managed-services/security-risk-defense.html)


## The RSM difference
We bring more than just technical capability—we bring a deep understanding of the nonprofit sector.
RSM’s outsourcing services help nonprofits to:
  * **Maximize mission impact** by freeing up internal resources and allowing your team to focus on what matters most
  * **Enhance decision making** through improved data visibility and actionable insights
  * **Strengthen operational resilience** to support long-term sustainability and community outcomes
  * **Scale confidently with managed services** tailored for financial health and program growth
  * **Adapt to change with agile solutions** that evolve with your organization’s needs


Our professionals bring robust sector experience, and we strive to understand your unique challenges and deliver solutions that drive measurable impact.
