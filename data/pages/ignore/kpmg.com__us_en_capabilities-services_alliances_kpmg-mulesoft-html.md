Driving AI Innovation Through MuleSoft API and Integration Solutions
Artificial intelligence is often hindered by data trapped in disconnected systems, which slows down or prevents information from getting from point A to point B. To solve this, KPMG, MuleSoft, and Salesforce's Agentforce have combined to create a "trifecta for Agentic AI success," which accelerates competitive advantage through improved data access and integration for autonomous AI agents.
This partnership combines three core components:
  * **MuleSoft and MuleSoft's Agent Fabric:** Provides back-end tools for unified integration and automation. It unlocks and unifies diverse data sources, creating a central library that allows AI agents to access information beyond the Salesforce ecosystem. AI Agents become a natural part of the API economy preventing siloed, tangled, unsecured, and duplicative AI agents.
  * **Agentforce:** A front-end Salesforce tool that allows customers to build and customize autonomous, proactive AI agents for various tasks through a no-code, customizable platform.
  * **KPMG:** Implements this solution through its AI Incubator and expertise on MuleSoft's Anypoint Platform and Agent Fabric. KPMG brings proprietary frameworks, deep industry knowledge, and strategic alliances to create a unified integration network. This approach focuses on driving business outcomes and ensuring solutions are scalable and future-proof.


Together, these three elements provide a comprehensive solution that enables organizations to harness the power of AI to streamline operations, amplify human potential, and drive business growth and transformation.
