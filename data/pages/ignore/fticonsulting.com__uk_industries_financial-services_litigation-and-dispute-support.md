The costs of litigation, regulatory investigation, remediation and redress can be substantial in the banking sector. Our clients look to FTI Consulting for independent advice and opinions to preserve value and minimise costs in contentious situations. Our professionals, with multi-jurisdictional experience, work with in-house and external legal teams. Using quantitative modelling, forensic and discovery methodology that withstands court scrutiny, we provide comprehensive and robust opinions on conduct, reporting, valuations and critical judgements.
**Our Services include:**
  * Forensic investigations
  * Support to in-house legal teams and external lawyers
  * Expert opinions on valuations, accounting and professional negligence
  * Financial and Enterprise Data Analytics
  * Deep dive networked intelligence gathering
  * Advice and analysis relating to competition issues and market abuse
  * Disputes, litigation and international arbitration support
  * Advisory services on quantum and consequential loss
  * Supporting professional negligence claims as investigators and experts
  * Economic consultancy and statistical analysis to support dispute resolution
