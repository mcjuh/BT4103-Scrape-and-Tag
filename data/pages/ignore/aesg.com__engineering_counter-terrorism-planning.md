## Our Counter-Terrorism Planning Approach
AESG’s Counter-Terrorism Planning Services are meticulously designed to support organisations in creating secure and resilient environments. Our approach includes:
Security Threat & Risk Assessment  Hostile Vehicle Mitigation  Blast Engineering Services  Protective Security Modelling  Response Planning  Risk Mitigation Strategies  Integrated Development Process  Concept Master Planning 
## Security Threat & Risk Assessment
### Conduct thorough assessments to identify potential terrorist threats and vulnerabilities.
### Evaluate the likelihood and impact of identified risks to prioritise mitigation efforts.
## Hostile Vehicle Mitigation
### Design strategies to prevent and mitigate attacks involving hostile vehicles.
### Implement barriers, access control points, and surveillance systems to enhance perimeter security.
## Blast Engineering Services
### Provide expert blast resistance and mitigation solutions to protect structural and non-structural components.
### Collaborate with architects to incorporate blast-resistant features during the early design stages.
## Protective Security Modelling
### Develop predictive models to simulate potential terrorist attacks and their impacts.
### Utilise 3D modelling to visualise and address vulnerabilities in building designs.
## Response Planning
### Create response plans to ensure swift and effective actions during and after a terrorist incident.
### Coordinate with local authorities and emergency services to enhance response capabilities.
## Risk Mitigation Strategies
### Present risk avoidance, reduction, transfer, and acceptance alternatives to key stakeholders.
### Balance risk appetite with affordability through detailed cost-benefit analyses.
## Integrated Development Process
### Guide developers and architects through compliance with safety and security planning manuals.
### Influence site selection, infrastructure planning, site topography, and asset placement for optimal security.
## Concept Master Planning
### Engage at the Concept Master Planning stage to advise on site selection and assess risks from adjacent plots.
### Influence infrastructure planning, site topography, placement of critical assets, access and circulation, building orientation and shape, and safe stand-off distances between Vehicle & Pedestrian Access Control Points and Crowded Space Areas.
