#  Venture debt 
Fuelling startup growth engines in Southeast Asia 
Copy link Link copied to clipboard 
Venture debt is an alternative source of capital for startups who are facing cash constraints and have difficulty obtaining financing from banks. It can help venture capitalist ("VC") backed startups in their growth journey and create an optimal funding structure, whilst enhancing stakeholders’ value.
Although an established alternative financing source in the US, Europe, Israel and India, venture debt has recently gained traction in Southeast Asia as a mainstream financing option for high growth technology companies.
Since its emergence in Singapore in 2015, the market has seen a marked increase in venture debt activity in the region. Considered an apt support to boost the local startup ecosystem, the Singapore government has introduced key programmes aimed at providing local early stage and high growth small and medium-sized enterprises an additional financing option for business growth and expansion. 
## Benefits of venture debt
Helps founders and VC-backed startups minimise shareholder dilution at early stages of the company’s growth
Allows the extension of cash runway between fundraising rounds, helping companies achieve performance targets set by equity investors 
Supports companies facing unexpected market turbulence (e.g. economic impact arising from COVID-19) and helps speed up growth initiatives 
## Growing impact and potential
Amid COVID-19, venture debt has emerged as an important source of funding, propelling the startup ecosystem in Singapore and Southeast Asia. We see more companies turning to venture debt to quickly adapt to the new market conditions. With strong demand from startups and founders for venture debt, venture debt can help both sides thrive in the post pandemic world.
## Key considerations for founders
##### Start a conversation
  * Take time to understand how venture debt works and the key terms that come with it 


##### Plan for venture debt at the right moment
  * Raise it at the appropriate stage of your startup journey and consider raising in conjunction with an equity raise


##### Use venture debt to its strengths
  * Capital raised can be used flexibly to accelerate the company’s growth


##### Less dilution, but be aware
  * Instill discipline with regards to repayment timelines and be sure to adhere to other obligations imposed by the venture lender


##### Have a wider perspective
  * Transparency and predictability are important criteria in choosing the right venture lender
