You’ve invested in technology. But without the right strategy to build skills, shift roles, and foster adoption across your organization, that investment could stall—or worse, put you behind.
### Ask yourself:
  * Are your teams confident using AI day to day?
  * Do your people know where AI can actually create value?
  * Is your talent strategy keeping pace with how work is evolving?
