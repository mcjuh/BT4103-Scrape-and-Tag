#  Energy insights: 2026 AI Impact Survey
## Where AI is scaling and controls are not
## Energy companies need more AI controls
AI is delivering efficiency for energy companies, but governance and controls are a constraint. Our survey report shows that better controls can help companies improve proof and scalability.
**of leaders say AI controls exist, but evidence is fragmented**
**cite governance or compliance barriers for AI underperformance**
**What’s in the report?**
  * 64% of energy boards have integrated AI risk into ongoing oversight, nine points above the cross-industry average
  * 17% of energy leaders say their organizations are ready for an independent audit of AI governance today, five points lower than the average across all industries
  * 30% name regulatory changes as the biggest external pressure driving AI adoption, nearly 10 points above the cross-industry average
  * 54% cite regulatory and compliance uncertainty as their top concern about agentic AI deployment
