#  Health Built for Connected Care 
When systems, data and decisions work together across patients, providers and operations
[](https://www.publicissapient.com/industries/health#connect)
## The realities of health
Health care organizations are under pressure to improve patient outcomes, reduce costs and provide connected digital experiences while navigating legacy systems, data silos and strict regulatory requirements.
### Legacy systems limit care delivery
Aging clinical, operational and administrative systems make it difficult to share data, scale services and support modern care models.
### Patient engagement is fragmented
Disconnected workflows across scheduling, registration and care coordination create friction for patients and increase administrative burden.
### Reliability and compliance are critical
Health care platforms must remain secure, interoperable and compliant while supporting constant change and innovation.

### Improve patient engagement
Personalize interactions across scheduling, registration and care guidance to reduce friction and improve patient experience.

Apply predictive analytics to identify risks early and support more proactive, personalized care pathways.
### Automate claims and administration

### Unify health data across systems

###  Modernize digital health platforms
