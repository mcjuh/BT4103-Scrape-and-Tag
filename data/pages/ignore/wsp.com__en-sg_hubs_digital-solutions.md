#### Digital platforms
Our digital platforms perform a critical role in underpinning our advice and services. These world-leading tools allow us to collaborate across design teams, as well as with clients and the communities they serve; ultimately delivering stronger designs and advice.
DiBAM
Digital tool for bridge asset management and urgent response.
WSP’s DiBAM is a digital asset management tool that helps owners and operators develop long-term plans and access the data needed for urgent response.
GoldSET©
Sustainability evaluation across the project lifecycle
GoldSET© is a web‑based tool combining multi‑criteria analysis, geospatial data and performance forecasting to help clients evaluate options and monitor project performance.
FracMan®
World Leaders in Fractured Rock Analysis, Technology and Consulting
FracMan®, the world’s first commercial DFN software, anchors WSP’s rock engineering expertise with workflows designed to solve complex rock-related challenges worldwide.
Land iQ
Streamlining planning for government with evidence‑based decisions. 
Land iQ supports future‑focused planning by helping governments optimise land use, bringing together evidence from transport, urban design and public space studies.
Floodly
Using machine learning to predict river levels and flood risk.
WSP’s Floodly uses machine learning to predict river levels and flood risk. Its rapid predictions complement traditional hydraulic modelling, which can be slower, more costly and challenging to apply in complex urban catchments.
PRIME for Cities
GIS platform tracking land, real estate and infrastructure activity 
PRIME for Cities is WSP’s proprietary web GIS platform that tracks land development, public infrastructure and urban growth policy, providing real-time visibility of relevant planning and development activity.
FloodVue™ 
Visualizing flood risk through augmented reality
WSP’s FloodVue™ is an augmented reality tool that helps communities and landowners visualise flood scenarios by creating immersive images of severe weather impacts before they occur.
Smart Drains
Sensor-based drains to mitigate flood risk and boost resilience.
Smart Drain fits ultrasonic sensors in high-flood-risk gullies, sending real-time silt and flow data to IoT dashboards and flagging blockages so crews can clear the right drains at the right time.
Nature Vista
Turning nature-related risk into opportunity
WSP’s AI‑enabled platform that connects nature and asset data to turn complex environmental insight into clear, actionable decisions across the full project lifecycle.
Orbi INSIGHT
Digital asset management platform combining systems and applications
WSP’s Orbi INSIGHT is a digital platform for stakeholders across the organization, combining two back-end systems and four applications to support different workflows.
Canary
Risk management system for asset networks across all hazard phases.
WSP’s Canary is a risk management system that provides asset owners, managers and users with risk insights across assets and networks before, during and after natural hazard events.
