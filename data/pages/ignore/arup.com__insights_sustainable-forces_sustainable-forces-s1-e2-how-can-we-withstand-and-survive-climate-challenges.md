#  Sustainable Forces S1 E2: how can we withstand and survive climate challenges? 
We’re planning for a future that is facing significant and ongoing uncertainty.

While human resilience remains strong, we need robust, inclusive and integrated solutions to protect people and urban environments from stressors.
In this episode, we chat with Lauren Sorkin, Executive Director at the Resilient Cities Network and Kate West, Arup’s Australasia Co-Chair. Learn what resilience means, how to create a resilience plan, and how we are working together on projects worldwide to build a resilient future.
