At AESG UK, we offer Specialised Built Environment Modelling services designed to enhance the design, performance, and sustainability of buildings and infrastructure projects. Our team utilises advanced modelling techniques to provide comprehensive insights that inform decision-making throughout the project lifecycle.
### Optimise Building Performance
Ensure buildings operate efficiently, reducing energy costs and environmental impact.
### Enhance Design Quality
Improve design decisions through data-driven insights, leading to better outcomes.
### Facilitate Compliance
Help projects meet UK regulatory requirements and sustainability certifications such as BREEAM and LEED.
### Support Stakeholder Engagement
Provide clear visualisations that enhance communication with stakeholders and clients.
## Our Approach to Built Environment Modelling
We take a holistic approach to modelling, addressing various aspects of building design and performance:
Building Information Modelling (BIM)  Environmental Modelling  Performance Modelling  Lifecycle Assessment (LCA)  Simulation Techniques 
## Building Information Modelling (BIM)
### Integration of Disciplines
## We utilise BIM to foster a collaborative environment where architects, engineers, and contractors can work together seamlessly.
### Lifecycle Management
## BIM supports effective management throughout the building's lifecycle, from design and construction through to operation.
## Environmental Modelling
### Energy Performance Analysis
## We assess energy consumption patterns to identify opportunities for improvement.
### Carbon Footprint Assessment
## Our modelling quanti es emissions from building operations, guiding carbon reduction strategies, throughout the design process.
## Performance Modelling
### Thermal Comfort Analysis
## We evaluate indoor environmental quality, from early-stage orientation and massing to detailed design, to ensure occupant comfort.
### Daylight Simulation
## Our tools assess natural light levels, optimising design for both occupant well-being and energy efficiency.
## Lifecycle Assessment (LCA)
### Comprehensive Evaluation
## We conduct LCAs to understand the environmental impacts of materials and construction methods across the building’s lifespan.
## Sustainable Material Selection
## Our assessments support choices that reduce embodied carbon and promote sustainability.
## Simulation Techniques
### Advanced Computational Tools
## We employ techniques such as computational fluid dynamics (CFD) for airflow analysis and parametric modelling to optimise design variables.
### Scenario Analysis
## Our simulations allow for testing various design scenarios to determine the most sustainable solutions.
