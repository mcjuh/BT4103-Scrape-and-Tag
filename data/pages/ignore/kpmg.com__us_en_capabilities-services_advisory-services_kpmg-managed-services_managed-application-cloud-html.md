# KPMG Managed Application and Cloud Services (MACS)
Modernize, secure, and enhance your IT ecosystem with AI-enabled managed services that elevate operations and accelerate innovation
##  Why are modern IT operations critical for today’s enterprises? 
Organizations face relentless pressure to keep technology on the leading edge _and_ aligned with business goals. Yet many companies are still held back by legacy platforms and fragmented information technology (IT) operations, making it harder to keep pace with business demands. In 40% of US companies, foundational enterprise IT system flaws disrupt business-as-usual on a _weekly_ basis. Meanwhile, 56% of US technology leaders say that the cost of fixing technical debt prevents them from making investments in new technology programs1.
**_KPMG Managed Application and Cloud Services (MACS) provide an integrated, fully managed model to cover both applications and cloud infrastructure—and give IT leaders the agility and visibility they need to support the business with confidence._**

## The MACS Suite
The MACS Suite is built around three connected areas. You can choose to use these individually or together to keep your IT environment stable, secure, and adaptable. 
Cloud and application support 
Risk and security 
Governance 
### Cloud and application support
The cloud and application support delivery layer brings day-to-day operations and improvement under a single managed model that includes:
**Cloud infrastructure services** to manage your digital operations and complex cloud environments robustly and securely—from initial setup and migration to ongoing optimization and governance.
**Platform as a service (PaaS)** to deliver a secure, stable, compliant, and scalable platform with experienced support for rapid application development and deployment.
**Software as a service (SaaS)** for managing administration, integration, configuration and optimization of critical applications, including proactive monitoring and support to enhance performance, improve user adoption, and help maximize value.
**Commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) services** for stable and reliable off-the-shelf functionality that is designed to evolve with your business, from implementation and customization to ongoing support and maintenance.
**Custom application services** delivering specialized, scalable, end-to-end support for your distinct technology assets, from infrastructure management to application-level monitoring and enhancements.
**IT service and change management** for centralized control of incidents, requests, and releases.
**Enhancements and modernization** to cover planned upgrades and new feature activation.
**Data and system administration** to manage data integrity, access, and configuration.
### Risk and security
Security and resilience are embedded across the services via:
**Continuous monitoring, detection, and response** supported by automation and threat intelligence.
**Business continuity and disaster recovery** frameworks that help minimize disruption and protect critical assets.
**Ongoing observability** through unified insights, advanced analytics, and system dashboards.
### Governance
A strong governance framework helps ensure consistent accountability and compliance across the operating model through:
**Operations and maintenance** strategies that define service standards and KPIs tied to business outcomes.
**Certifications and compliance** with leading standards and regulatory mandates across jurisdictions.
**Continuous improvement** through feedback loops that translate insights into measurable progress.
