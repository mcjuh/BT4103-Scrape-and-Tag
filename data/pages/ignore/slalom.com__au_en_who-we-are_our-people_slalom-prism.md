##### Prism seeks to increase awareness and understanding while removing workplace barriers for all orientations, identities, allies, and those interested in learning more. 
## Mission
To build and shape a better future for LGBTQ+ Slalomers and our allies by cultivating fiercely human authenticity in our lives and work.
## Objectives
  * Partner with local community organisations supporting all orientations, identities, and allies. 
  * Create engaging educational experiences for all Slalom employees to deepen their understanding of LGBTQ+ identities, experiences, and history. PMake space for a workplace culture of belonging and authenticity through coaching and collaboration. 
  * Drive internal connection and collaboration globally at Slalom. 


> The LGBTQIA+ community has seen a lot of positive change in recent years, but there is still a lot of work that needs to be done.
**Emily Sinclair** Consultant, Seattle
> We want our allies to know that the change does not happen overnight within the community. You really see drastic change when you recognise and embrace intersectionality.
**Diarte Jeffcoat-McLeod** Associate Consultant, Charlotte
> When I came to Slalom, I wondered how I would come out. 10 years later, I have a hard time imagining a time when I was ever worried about being my authentic self at Slalom.
**Dan Yurcovic** Senior Product Manager, Chicago
