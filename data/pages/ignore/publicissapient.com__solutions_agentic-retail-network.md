## The realities of retail
Retailers are expected to deliver consistent, personalized experiences while managing complex legacy systems, seasonal demand shifts and growing pressure on reliability across every channel.
### Legacy systems slow execution
Retail platforms are often stitched together, making even simple changes risky and time-consuming. This adds tech debt and slows response to market shifts.
### Personalization is hard to scale
Disconnected data and manual workflows make personalization expensive and inconsistent across channels.
### Reliability matters at peak moments
Retail systems face constant pressure. Downtime, performance issues or slow fixes directly impact revenue and customer trust.
