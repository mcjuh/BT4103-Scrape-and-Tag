## [Insights](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/digital-handover-management/#Insights)
Effective digital handover is critical to ensuring a seamless transition from construction to operation, providing structured and accessible asset information for facility management. AESG’s Digital Handover Management service leverages DATA+, our in-house information management platform, to streamline the collection, validation, and delivery of digital handover documentation. Our structured approach ensures data accuracy, interoperability, and long-term usability for building operations.
## Key Services
[ Handover Document Templates ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/digital-handover-management/#collapse-7abe8626aa0680a0dfff)
We provide standardised templates for handover documentation (i.e., O&M Manuals, Asset Registers & System Manuals) and ensure compliance with project requirements and industry standards.
[ Supply Chain Information Management ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/digital-handover-management/#collapse-203a7c16aa0680a0dfff)
We manage the systematic collection, validation and consolidation of handover information and ensure consistency and completeness from all suppliers & sub-contractors.
[ Technical Authoring ](https://aesg.com/project-delivery-management/digital-handover-management/#collapse-daeab306aa0680a0dfff)
We structure, describe and compile the technical content for O&M Manuals and ensure clarity and accuracy in system descriptions, operating procedures & maintenance guidelines.
DATA+ Information Management Platform
## Data+ is AESG’s web-based information management platform that serves as a central source of truth and offers a secure, collaborative environment for all stakeholders and project teams for all handover information. By adopting a process-driven, user-friendly interface, we empower projects to produce accurate and validated information from design to operation aligned with ISO 19650.
### Automated Data Validation to reduce errors and improve data quality.
### Interoperability with CAFM, CMMS, and BIM platforms for seamless integration.
### Version Control & Auditability ensuring transparent and trackable data update.
### Custom Dashboards & Reporting for real-time insights and progress tracking.
By integrating automation, data assurance, and compliance into the digital handover process, AESG enables asset owners, operators, and facility managers to access high-quality, structured data that enhances operational efficiency and decision-making.
