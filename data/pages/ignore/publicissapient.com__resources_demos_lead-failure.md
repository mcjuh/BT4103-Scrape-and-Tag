#  Lead Failure Demo 
Leads failures are hard to spot. Issues hide across systems while the business impact is immediate and measurable. Service teams are often chasing problems without knowing where to start. Sapient Sustain shows the full path of failure in one service map, flags the systems driving the issue and surfaces the incidents teams need to fix first.

## Highlighted features 
Identify lead failures, trace issues across systems and resolve them with clear next steps.

### Real-time failure detection
<p>Detect issues at the point of failure, such as form validation errors, before they go unnoticed or impact conversion.</p>
### Service map visibility
<p>Understand how failures connect across systems, from front-end experiences to back-end services.</p>
### Automated root cause analysis
<p>Surface the exact issue by comparing logs, validation rules and configurations without digging through multiple tools.</p>
### Built-in remediation workflows
<p>Trigger agentic workflows to investigate, resolve and document issues across systems.</p>
What lead capture issues can Sustain detect?
Sustain detects lead captureissues such asvalidation errors, configuration mismatches and integration failures across systems.
How does Sustain find the root cause of lead failures?
Sustain finds the root cause by pulling logs across different monitoring tools, comparing configurations, historical maps and tracing dependencies to identify an optimal fix with a high degree of confidence. 
What happens after Sustain identifies the issue?
After Sustain identifies the issue, it triggers AI workflows to analyze the issue, generate a root cause summary and apply fixes. It also prevents ticket duplication, stores the incident, actions taken and system context so teams can resolve repeat issues faster.
