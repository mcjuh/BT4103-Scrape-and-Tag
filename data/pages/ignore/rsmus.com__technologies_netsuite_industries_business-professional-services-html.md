##  NetSuite capabilities for professional services companies 
NetSuite ERP is the leading cloud-based solution for business and professional services organizations looking to streamline and update processes. Read each section to learn more ways our NetSuite consultants can help you achieve the remarkable. 

Project management  
    
Instead of relying on multiple, disconnected tools, use NetSuite to collaborate in real time. Because it’s cloud-based, NetSuite gives you access to always-current project data—making it easier to proactively control timelines and resolve potential issues.  

Resource management and forecasting  
    
Optimize staffing and minimize bench time by ensuring that qualified resources are working on the right projects. Use up-to-date information to forecast resource usage for projects in the pipeline.  

Project accounting  
    
Because NetSuite connects project activities with company financials, you can deliver more accurate accounting and billing throughout a project’s life cycle, streamline time and expense management and quickly generate a variety of extensive reports.  

Billing and invoicing  
    
Let NetSuite automate invoicing—from one-offs to complex, recurring time- and project-based billing. Choose from several billing options: based on completing project work, achieving project milestones or following a date schedule. 

Revenue recognition  
    
NetSuite’s revenue management automation is flexible and capable of handling all scenarios for services companies, including percentage of completion based on hours or cost, revenue based on worked time, milestone revenue recognition and rateables over time.  

Timesheet management  
    
Replace high-touch time sheet processes with NetSuite’s customizable time tracking features. Whether in the office or on the road, your users can easily capture and submit detailed time sheet data.  

Expense management  
    
Use NetSuite’s impressive expense management options to improve the tracking, accuracy and timeliness of expense reporting while simplifying regulatory compliance. Enter expenses via web browser or mobile phone for go-anywhere convenience. 

Analytics  
    
Drawing from a single data source on demand, NetSuite’s full set of robust reporting and analytical tools enables you to monitor, report and analyze overall project and financial performance in real time. 
##  Benefits of using NetSuite for BPS  

Real-time visibility into performance and profitability  
    
Because you’re accessing a single source of always-fresh data, you won’t have to rely on a piecemeal understanding of performance—it’s all right there, in real time. 

Improved on-time delivery and project success rates  
    
With detailed, accurate information at your disposal, you’ll have much finer control over project delivery—including navigating the unexpected.  

Advanced resource management  
    
Increase utilization and minimize downtime. With a true view of productivity, you’re better equipped to make tactical staffing decisions and help ensure your team isn’t idle when there are tasks to be completed. 

Mobile access to time and expense management tools  
    
Working from multiple locations is the new normal. NetSuite’s convenient and intuitive mobile interface gives you the key capabilities you need when you’re not at a desktop or laptop.  

Multicompany, multicurrency tracking and automated consolidation  
    
Doing business globally means not only using multiple currencies, but also adhering to regulations from region to region. Automate financial localization worldwide. 

Cloud-based delivery  
    
There’s big processing power in the cloud, and we leverage it to deliver advanced solutions with speed and precision—no expensive hardware required.
