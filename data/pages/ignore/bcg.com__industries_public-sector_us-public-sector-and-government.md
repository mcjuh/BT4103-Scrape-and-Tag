With growing fiscal constraints and evolving threats, agencies must adopt proven strategies, strengthen core capabilities, and drive measurable outcomes.Our US government consulting team draws on deep expertise across strategy, operations, and technology—bringing proven private sector insights to help federal, state, and local agencies increase efficiency, reduce costs, and meet mission-critical objectives with confidence.

## Our Clients’ Success in the US Public Sector 
We’ve partnered with major government organizations to support—and spark—their transformations. The result: more efficient, more effective, and more innovative ways of working. Here are some examples.
220+
pain points prioritized
**Leading a strategic ERP transformation.** A major North American transit operator faced ERP challenges and relied on manual tools. BCG conducted an assessment that catalogued 750+ interfaces and analyzed 180+ processes. The BCG team prioritized 220+ pain points and finalized 80+ decisions. By enhancing automation, data accessibility, and integrated business processes, the team helped accelerate the client’s S/4 transformation.
$185m
annual cost reduction
**Guiding financial systems modernization in a federal defense agency.** BCG partnered with a U.S. federal defense client to assess modernization opportunities and improve operations. The team developed an analysis of alternatives and transformation strategy to reduce technology operating costs by ~$185M annually. The effort identified overlaps across 30+ platforms, informing implementation plans and enabling streamlined reporting and stronger audit readiness.
90%
backlog reduction
**Catalyzing state government solutions**. A state government sought to ease residents' challenges accessing essential services and improve overall service delivery. BCG collaborated with state leaders to establish a customer-focused agency, designing its operating model, funding structure, and delivery approach. The team built internal staff capabilities and implemented product management best practices, achieving a 90% backlog reduction and national recognition for innovation and impact.
