#  Faces of Change 
Faces of Change is a book published in English and Spanish to mark the conclusion of the six-year partnership with the UK and Peru to accelerate the reconstruction of schools, healthcare centres, hospitals and flood defences. 
Last updated: September 2026

The book tells the story, through photos and their own words, of the people bringing to life the international standard infrastructure delivered through the partnership between the UK Delivery Team of Arup, Mace and Gleeds and our partner in Peru the Autoridad Nacional de Infraestructura.
The images by renowned Peruvian photogapher Omar Lucas, capture the renewed confidence and hope of the staff building and operating the new facilities and flood defences, and some of the thousands of pupils, patients and residents whose communities have being transformed.
