## Assessments for Effective Noise and Vibration Management
At AESG, we provide detailed Site Noise and Vibration Measurement Surveys that evaluate existing conditions to guide the design and development of projects. By using state-of-the-art equipment and strategic modelling, we offer precise assessments that address noise and vibration issues during both the construction and operational phases of a project. Our expertise spans a wide range of environments, including industrial, commercial, and residential developments, ensuring compliance with relevant regulations and enhancing overall project success.Our team not only conducts accurate measurements but also translates the results into actionable insights that inform the design and operational decisions for your project.
## Key Services
[ Operation and Construction Site Noise and Vibration Surveys ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-7abe8626aa068fd75088)
We assess noise and vibration levels at both operational and construction sites to ensure compliance with regulations and minimise environmental impact. These surveys provide critical data for managing site activities while protecting nearby communities.
[ Noise & Vibration Impact Assessments ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-203a7c16aa068fd75088)
We offer stand-alone reports or chapters for Environmental Statements, including assessments for ventilation and overheating. These assessments help guide design decisions and ensure projects meet industry standards.
[ Construction Noise and Vibration Assessments (BS 5228) ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-7f71af96aa068fd75088)
Our assessments comply with BS 5228, providing detailed insights into noise and vibration levels during construction, ensuring that activities remain within acceptable limits to minimise disruption to nearby properties and communities.
[ Industrial/Commercial Assessments (BS 4142) ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-adafbf66aa068fd75088)
We conduct noise assessments for industrial and commercial facilities in line with BS 4142 guidelines. These assessments help to evaluate the noise impact of industrial operations on surrounding areas and ensure compliance with regulatory standards.
[ Entertainment Noise and PAVA Spillage Noise Assessments ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-f645a056aa068fd75088)
We offer tailored assessments for entertainment venues and Public Address and Voice Alarm (PAVA) systems to manage and minimise noise spillage, ensuring that noise levels remain within acceptable thresholds.
[ Gym Noise and Vibration Assessments ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-7abe8626aa068fd76e76)
Our specialists conduct thorough assessments for gyms to address both noise and vibration concerns, ensuring that the facilities meet regulatory requirements and minimise disturbances to nearby residents or businesses.
[ Noise and Vibration Complaint Assessments ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-203a7c16aa068fd76e76)
In response to complaints, we perform detailed investigations to assess the validity of noise or vibration issues and provide recommendations for mitigation to resolve conflicts effectively.
[ Noise Insulation and Workplace Assessments ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-54b13616aa068fd76e76)
We carry out noise insulation assessments to ensure that buildings meet required acoustic performance standards, as well as noise and vibration at work assessments, ensuring compliance with workplace safety regulations.
[ Predictive Noise Modelling ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-d318ece6aa068fd76e76)
We use advanced predictive noise modelling to evaluate noise impacts from industrial, commercial, road, rail, and aircraft sources. This helps us develop effective mitigation strategies to manage noise impacts throughout the lifecycle of a project.
[ Predictive Vibration Analysis ](https://aesg.com/engineering/site-noise-vibration-measurement-surveys/#collapse-ffa72cf6aa068fd76e76)
Our predictive vibration analysis identifies potential vibration issues and helps prevent disruptions in sensitive environments, ensuring the comfort and safety of occupants
