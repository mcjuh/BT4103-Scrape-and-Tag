# Building Safety, Fire and Sustainability Consultants in the UK
Delivering Innovative Solutions for the UK's Built Environment
Welcome to AESG UK, where global expertise and local insight come together to shape the future of the built environment. As a leading international consultancy, engineering, and advisory firm, we are renowned for delivering solutions across key areas such as façade engineering, fire life safety (FLS), cladding, and external wall systems. Our technical knowledge and practical experience enable us to address complex challenges in the UK's built environment, from compliance and safety to innovative design and performance optimisation. While our roots are in the critical aspects of the built environment, sustainability remains a core principle that underpins our approach. We integrate sustainable practices across our services, ensuring that each project not only meets today’s standards but also anticipates future requirements. Led by a top-tier leadership team, we deliver hands-on, bespoke strategic solutions that drive value and resilience for our clients.
## Our Commitment to the UK
At AESG, we are dedicated to advancing the UK's built environment and supporting the nation's ambitious goals for a sustainable future. Our comprehensive approach combines global best practices with a deep understanding of the UK’s regulatory landscape, enabling us to navigate the unique challenges and requirements of this market. We focus on three core service areas: Environment and Sustainability, Engineering, and Project Delivery Management. From engineering world-class façades and fire life safety systems to providing carbon advisory and environmental assessments, we deliver innovative, multi-disciplinary solutions tailored to the needs of UK businesses and communities. Our commitment goes beyond compliance—it's about driving progress, enhancing safety, and delivering exceptional project outcomes.

### Acoustics Engineering
The driving metric of building design is how people experience buildings, acoustic design is a vital component of that. Acoustics have the potential to impact human health, employee productivity and overall communication. AESG’s holistic approach to acoustic design encompasses a suite of services over the whole lifecycle of buildings, from planning to commissioning. Acoustic design criteria vary depending on the intended uses and functions of spaces, and our highly skilled professionals have extensive international experience and are familiar with the local, regional and international regulations and guidelines that shape all our solutions.
