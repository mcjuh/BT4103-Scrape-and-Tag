In 2021, the Thermal Management Division of Arlington Industries split from its parent company and sold its operations in Brazil, China, Germany, France, the UK and the U.S. to Cartesian Capital Group (“Cartesian).
Kroll’s Brazil Investment Banking Team served as sell-side advisor to the Thermal Management Division of Arlington Industries Group in Brazil in connection with the transaction.
## About Arlington Industries
Arlington Industries is an integrated design, engineering, and manufacturing company that supplies parts to the automotive and aerospace sectors.
## About Cartesian
Cartesian Capital Group is a SEC registered investment advisor that manages over $3 billion in private equity funds, providing growth equity capital to companies across a range of industries on a global basis, specializing in growing businesses, both organically and through acquisitions.
