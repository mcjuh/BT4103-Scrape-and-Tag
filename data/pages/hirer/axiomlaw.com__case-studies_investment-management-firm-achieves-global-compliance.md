# Global Compliance Achieved for Investment Management Giant
### Axiom lawyer team:
Provided 24/7 coverage with local lawyers stationed in 5 key offices across the globe
Billed hours back to each supported fund to avoid pulling from central legal budget
*Saved Client $100,000 per month on outside spend at traditional law firms
### Challenge
A leading investment management firm is overwhelmed with marketing review work. **Trapped under a hiring freeze, and with work streaming in from around the globe, the understaffed legal team is running on****fumes, and****is forced to rely on inexperienced law firm secondees.** They need more than a band-aid solution for this ongoing workstream.
### Approach
Working alongside the global head of legal and compliance, Axiom installs a team of 6 lawyers, stationed strategically across 5 key offices, to provide **consistent global coverage for teams in Asia, the US, and Europe.** Each Axiom lawyer, the go-to contact for their geo, is **adept with the Investment Advisers Act and has notable regulatory experience with the SEC or FINRA at a minimum**. The team primarily supports marketing review, and they also assist with implementing changes to standard policies and disclosures as they relate to investor-facing materials.
### Impact 
  * Ensured strategic compliance and round-the-clock coverage via local lawyers**stationed across 5 key offices around the globe**.
  * **Circumvented hiring freeze and strict headcount requirements** by relying on embedded Axiom lawyers.
  * The cost of Axiom lawyers is billed back to each fund they support**without detracting from central legal budget**.
