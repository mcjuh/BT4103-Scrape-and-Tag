## Easily explore, discover, and engage top legal talent in minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions
What can I expect after creating a free account? 
You'll have the ability to quickly find Axiom legal talent on your own terms, with guidance from our team available whenever you need it along the way. 
**1. See curated matches or browse our network:** Sign in to your Axiom account and describe your needs to instantly see Axiom talent who match your requirements or browse our extensive network using advanced search and filtering capabilities. 
**2. Review detailed talent profiles:** Dive into comprehensive profiles that highlight each legal pro's relevant experience and skills, helping you make informed decisions.
**3. Select talent to interview:** Set up interviews without the traditional back-and-forth hassles of scheduling.
**4. Your selected talent becomes an extension of your team:** Axiom facilitates onboarding with your ideal legal talent and manages the engagement from start to finish. 
Can I engage high quality legal talent through Axiom? 
Our lawyers average 18+ years of experience across law firms and in-house roles, and we target individuals with prior experience with F500 and/or AmLaw 200. We hire only 3% of applicants through rigorous vetting, including multiple interviews and background checks. Over 92% of clients say our talent equals or exceeds law firm quality. 
Which practice areas and industries do you cover? 
We cover 60+ legal areas of focus across 30+ industries. Our network not only includes lawyers, but also paralegals and legal professionals specializing in Legal Operations, Compliance, Data Privacy and Investigations.
How quickly can I get someone started? 
We typically place legal professionals within a week. Once you create an account, you will be able to see talent matches within 5 minutes and set up interviews for as soon as tomorrow.
What if the lawyer doesn't work out? 
If the lawyer does not work out, we will work with you to offer a suitable replacement. Given the timing and expense you have incurred, your Axiom contact will work with you and internal teams to find an agreed upon financial resolution for your investment and time.
How much does it cost? 
Axiom’s typical hourly rates are between $150 and $350 USD depending on the number of hours per week and the talent's practice area. Our rates are significantly lower than traditional law firms because we don't have profit-per-partner models or expensive real estate overhead. You will receive Axiom's rates for the legal professional along with their bios in your matches, and that number includes both their hourly rate and Axiom’s portion. There are no hidden or additional costs.
Can I engage part-time or project-based help? 
Yes, we offer both reserved hours (minimum 10 hours/week) and ad-hoc/as-needed arrangements. “Reserved” engagements guarantee a minimum number of hours per week, and most commonly clients opt for at least 20 hours/week, as that is where you'll see the greatest cost savings. Ad-hoc engagements are most commonly set up for 5-10 hours per week, though you are not locked into a minimum. Engagements must be set up for at least 30 days, but you can adjust hours based on workload or seasonal needs or end an engagement with just 10 business days' notice.
Your attorneys are very senior; why would they want to do this kind of work? Will their rates fit my budget? 
Lawyers and legal professionals join Axiom for a variety of reasons, including access to our client base, diversity of work, flexibility to take more extended time off between engagements, ability to work part-time, and ability to live where they want. Most of our legal talent are not interested in climbing the career ladder but desire to get back to hands-on legal practice, and many do not mind more “junior” work. In fact, our clients appreciate having more experienced legal professionals that can get the work done faster with minimal supervision. Their hourly rates are based on their years of experience, field of law, and where they live, however even our most senior legal professionals' rates are still a fraction of the cost of working with attorneys from most large law firms. 
Why do I need in-house counsel to supervise an Axiom engagement? 
Because Axiom is not a law firm, bar requirements state that Axiom legal talent be supervised by an actively licensed attorney while on engagement with our clients. Most commonly, this would be the client’s in-house counsel, however in some cases we can also work with their outside law firm. 
How does contracting and billing work? 
Once you've selected the legal professional you want to engage, you will sign an Engagement Letter and a Scope of Services. You will receive monthly invoices through email or your e-billing platform.
What is Axiom? 
Axiom is a global alternative legal services provider delivering on-demand legal talent, secondments, and AI-enabled legal services to in-house legal departments, supporting both ongoing work and complex legal projects while reducing legal costs and outside counsel spend.
