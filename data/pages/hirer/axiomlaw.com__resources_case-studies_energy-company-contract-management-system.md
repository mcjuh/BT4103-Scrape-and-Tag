# Contract Management System Roll Out for Fortune 50 Energy Company
### Axiom attorney helped:
Standardize contract outputs for the business teams without requiring legal support
Migrate and integrate acquired company contracts into new contract management system
Save client $680,000 on CMS implementation when compared to law firm equivalent*
### Challenge
A global energy company operates an electric vehicle (EV) subsidiary. Amidst heavy growth, the energy company folds in the EV subsidiary into the broader org as new division. However, they **need to implement a new contract management system (CMS**) to comply with company standards and streamline the contracting process for the division.
### Approach
An Axiom legal ops lawyer begins reviewing existing contracts relating to software sales, construction, maintenance, data privacy, and more to configure the CMS. Based on uploaded data, she **roadmaps the system workflows to generate approvals and contracts templates**. Midway through the project, the client reorganizes and merges in a newly purchased company. The Axiom lawyer migrates their records and templatizes contracts into the new CMS. Lastly, she **creates a repository for legacy contracts, automates alerts based on expiry dates, and creates a manual to train non-lawyers** on the platform.
### Impact 
  * Implemented CMS that **standardized contract outputs for the business team** to produce time-sensitive and complex sales contracts without requiring legal support.
  * Axiom lawyer adjusted on the fly to **migrate and****integrate newly purchased company’s contracts into CMS** amidst a major corporate reorganization.
  * **Created central contracts repository and training guide** for sales teams to action legacy contract renewals on time.
