## What can technology lawyers help companies handle?
Technology lawyers can help companies with product counseling, SaaS and cloud agreements, open-source software issues, website and app terms, data privacy, cybersecurity, AI governance, strategic partnerships, licensing, resale arrangements, and technology-related disputes. Axiom can help legal teams add industry-experienced legal talent for specific projects or ongoing support.
## How can technology lawyers support AI governance and compliance?
Technology lawyers can support AI governance by helping legal teams map AI use cases, assess regulatory and contractual risk, update policies, review vendor and customer terms, create approval workflows, and coordinate with product, privacy, security, and compliance stakeholders.
## What legal issues come up in SaaS, cloud, and technology transactions?
SaaS, cloud, and technology transactions often raise issues around service levels, data processing, usage rights, IP ownership, security obligations, indemnities, termination rights, reseller terms, implementation support, and compliance with privacy or sector-specific requirements.
## When do technology companies need flexible legal talent?
Technology companies often need flexible legal talent during product launches, enterprise sales cycles, privacy or security initiatives, AI rollouts, contract backlogs, fundraising or M&A activity, market expansion, or temporary gaps in the legal team. Flexible support can help teams scale without slowing commercial execution.
## How can technology lawyers help manage data privacy and cybersecurity risk?
Technology lawyers can help manage privacy and cybersecurity risk by supporting privacy notices, data processing agreements, cross-border transfer terms, data governance, audits, vendor reviews, incident response planning, and coordination between legal, security, and product teams.
## How can product counsel support technology launches?
[Product counsel](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/product-counsel) can support technology launches by reviewing product claims, user flows, data collection practices, customer terms, vendor dependencies, IP ownership, marketing requirements, privacy notices, and risk controls before launch. This helps teams identify legal issues earlier in the product development cycle.
