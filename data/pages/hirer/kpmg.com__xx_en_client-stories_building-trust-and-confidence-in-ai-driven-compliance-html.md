## Make regulatory compliance seamless
Organizations face an escalating volume of complex, time-consuming regulatory demands. The pace of change and level of scrutiny are even higher in heavily regulated sectors, such as telecommunications companies operating across multiple jurisdictions. This telecommunication organization adopted a new way to manage obligation risk at speed and scale, by using AI to strengthen compliance and navigate increasingly demanding regulations.

### Complex, time-consuming compliance demands
With an ever-growing volume of complex, time-consuming regulatory demands, the telecommunications organization's compliance capability was heavily reliant on manual controls in siloed processes. It became necessary to build the confidence levels and capability of their Board and Executive leadership teams in overseeing the company’s obligations and controls. 
### Simplified compliance, reduced risk and better outcomes
To meet this challenge, KPMG Australia worked with Microsoft to develop an AI solution designed to turn dense regulatory text into clear, actionable insights. The AI agent helps teams interpret new regulations, map them to internal controls, and assess control effectiveness — reducing a process that once took months to just days.
### Navigating compliance with speed and accuracy
Microsoft’s OpenAI capability and KPMG’s deep regulatory experience is helping to simplify compliance, reduce risk and deliver better outcomes. This telecommunication organization is leading the way, using AI to navigate thousands of local and international laws with speed and accuracy. 
## How we make the difference
By combining AI with KPMG’s deep regulatory expertise, AI agents can simplify compliance processes, reduce risk and deliver strong outcomes. With the rising volume of complex, time-consuming regulatory requirements, many organizations are looking for solutions to ensure their use of AI is reliable and trusted.
