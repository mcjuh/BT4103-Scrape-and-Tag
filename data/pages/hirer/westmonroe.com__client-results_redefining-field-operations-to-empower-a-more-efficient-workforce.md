# Redefining field operations to empower a more efficient workforce
Optimizing workforce efficiency to maximize the impact of new technology
Services
  * [Workforce & Productivity](https://www.westmonroe.com/services/workforce-and-productivity)
  * [Infrastructure Modernization](https://www.westmonroe.com/services/infrastructure-modernization-for-electric-gas-utilities)


A leading utility was [modernizing its grid](https://www.westmonroe.com/client-results/future-proofing-the-grid-results-in-8m-in-savings) with advanced metering infrastructure (AMI) technology, aiming for greater efficiency and cost savings. But it needed to rethink its field operations to fully capture these benefits. That’s where West Monroe came in. We partnered with the utility’s project sponsors and field teams to analyze how AMI would change daily tasks, define new workforce processes, and develop an optimized staffing model. Through hands-on collaboration—including workflow mapping, time studies, and task redesign—we identified process improvements that could cut required labor hours by 12%, translating to more than $6 million in annual savings. With a clear path forward, the utility is now equipped to maximize its AMI investment and scale for future demand.

Ideas into Reality
To ensure AMI technology delivered real savings, the utility partnered with West Monroe to quantify workforce impact and redesign field operations for maximum efficiency. 
Read More 
New technology changes how work gets done. The utility needed a clear understanding of how AMI would impact field technician tasks and overall workforce needs. West Monroe combined deep industry and technology expertise to measure current processes and forecast future efficiencies. We partnered with field teams to document workflows, analyze time-on-task, and pinpoint opportunities for automation. This detailed approach provided a precise estimate of labor-hour reductions and a data-driven roadmap for future staffing. Together, we laid the groundwork for lasting operational improvements.

Tackling it Together
Over 11 weeks, we collaborated with field teams, mapped processes, and built a staffing model to align workforce needs with projected AMI-driven efficiencies. 
Read More 
Success required close collaboration. We worked alongside the utility’s field operations experts to map 11 distinct technician workflows, observe task execution, and quantify the impact of AMI-driven changes. By measuring the work content of 20 technician job types, we developed a precise activity-based staffing model. This model not only projected a 12% reduction in required labor hours but also provided a foundation for ongoing workforce optimization. Our approach ensured that solutions were tailored to real-world operations, empowering the utility to implement change with confidence.

Value Created
By optimizing field operations, the utility is set to save over $6 million annually, with additional opportunities to realize $4-6 million in further efficiencies.

Through a rigorous, hands-on approach, we validated that the utility could achieve a 12% labor-hour reduction—translating to $6 million in annual savings. Beyond these immediate gains, our staffing model highlighted additional opportunities for workforce right-sizing that could unlock another $4-6 million in savings. By aligning technology adoption with operational improvements, we helped the utility turn potential efficiencies into tangible results.
