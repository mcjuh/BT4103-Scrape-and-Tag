# 63% Cost Savings on Labor Complaints for Healthcare System
### Axiom attorney helped:
Cut cost of position statements by 63% compared to outside counsel
Lead and win multiple state labor commission hearings
Ensure full compliance with California State Labor Commission and National Labor Relations Board
### Challenge
A regional healthcare company receives a steady stream of labor and union complaints, but their legal team is too small to handle them promptly. They need an experienced California labor attorney to draft position statements and responses.
### Approach
Axiom swiftly integrates a labor and employment attorney and former General Counsel into the legal team to resolve several union labor charges. For each complaint, the in-house legal team handles the initial draft of the response. The Axiom attorney, knowledgeable on both Federal and California-specific labor laws, beefs up and edits each outgoing position statement. Axiom attorney stays “on call” at no additional cost to the client to advise the in-house team and respond swiftly to any more incoming complaints.
### Impact 
  * **Cut cost of each position statement by 63%** compared to outside counsel
  * Even when position statements were disputed, **Axiom attorney led and won multiple state labor****commission hearings**
  * Each position statement received an in-depth review to ensure **full compliance with California State****Labor Commission and National Labor Relations Board**
