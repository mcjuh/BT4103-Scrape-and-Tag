# Mirfa Emirati Housing
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project consists of developing 410 Emirati type villas and associated facilities in Mirfa, Western Region of Abu Dhabi, in a plot area of 2.2 million sqm. The project is intended as a mixed-use development, with residential, retail uses, recreational spaces located along the Mirfa Beach. The site is in fact located within and in the proximity of high ecologically sensitive areas such as a marine reserve and an ecological reserve. AESG has been appointed as the environmental consultant on the project, to acquire environmental permit from the Environment Agency of Abu Dhabi.
  * Environmental Impact Assessment
  * EMMP
  * Fully-fledged Environmental Baseline Study
  * Hydronamic modelling and flushing assessment
  * Authority Permitting
