# Marsa Meydan
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
Marsa Meydan is an upcoming waterfront development located in the heart of Dubai’s Jebel Ali district. The development will offer waterfront villas, townhouses, and apartments, in addition to retail, entertainment, lifestyle and community establishments and amenities. Furthermore, the project will feature a state-of-the-art fully covered and climate-controlled marina. Covering a land area of more than total of 1.7 million square meters, the project will contribute to Dubai’s continuous growth as an urban metropolis.
As part of the required authority approvals, AESG has been appointed to provide environmental consultancy services, which includes the undertaking of comprehensive baseline surveys, terrestrial and marine ecology surveys, and an avian flight path analysis which will culminate with an Environmental Impact Assessment. The project also includes full marine and land-based scope, with hydrodynamics, flushing and plume dispersion modelling to be an integral part of the study.
  * Environmental Authority Permitting
  * Environmental Baseline Studies
  * Terrestrial and Marine Ecology Surveys
  * Avian Flight Analysis
  * Environmental Impact Analysis
