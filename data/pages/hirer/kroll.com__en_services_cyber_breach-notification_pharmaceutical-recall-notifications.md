# Pharmaceutical Recall Notifications
Kroll's expertise handling large-scale notifications for highly regulated industries helps you increase efficiencies and meet regulatory requirements.
Approximately 4,500 drugs and medical devices are recalled each year, according to the FDA. Each recall affects thousands of individuals who need to be notified as soon as possible.
Kroll has provided professional notification services since 1999 and serves clients a wide range of industries, including healthcare, pharmaceutical, and retail. Our clients rely on our experience and expertise to handle large-scale recall notifications - from mail list preparation and verification to distribution and reporting. Our focus is on managing your notification process as quickly and effectively as possible to best serve your customers.
**Case Study: National Pharmacy Class II Recall**
A national pharmacy chain contacted Kroll for assistance with a Class II drug recall notification. As a current client, the pharmacy knew that Kroll had the capabilities to quickly and accurately notify the nearly 500,000 customers affected by the recall.
The pharmacy provided a list of affected customers which was processed by Kroll to standardize data and eliminate problematic records. Notification letters began mailing within days.
**Why Kroll?**
