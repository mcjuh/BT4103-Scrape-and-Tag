### Challenge
A global energy services company faced significant budget constraints that forced the departure of two full-time employees from their legal team. Rather than backfilling the headcount with traditional hires, the client wanted to **explore****whether****a secondee armed with AI tools could reduce full-time headcount needs while maintaining legal output quality**.
### Approach
The client onboarded an experienced Axiom attorney focused on creating scalable and more efficient contracts playbooks for the team. Using DraftPilot, the **Axiom lawyers builds AI-powered playbooks for commercial and supply chain contracts**. Within six weeks, the playbooks are so successful that the client extends DraftPilot licenses to 5 additional legal team members. The Axiom lawyer ensures the foundational playbooks are being used correctly and **leads hands-on training sessions to drive****adoption** untiltheteamistrainedup on the new systems. 
### Impact 
  * **Maintained****legal output quality without backfilling** forexpensive permanent roles, enabling client to reallocate budget towards strategic investments
  * **AI-powered playbooks drove major contract efficiency gains** , preserving internal legal team bandwidth and reducing time spent on manual redlining, review, and negotiation
  * **Validated leadership's consolidation hypothesis** proving that a team of three attorneys equipped with AI tools could match the output of four
