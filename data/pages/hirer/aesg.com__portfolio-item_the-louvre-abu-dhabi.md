# The Louvre Abu Dhabi
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The Louvre Abu Dhabi includes 6,400 square meters of gallery space, comprising 23 galleries for the museum’s permanent collection, a temporary exhibition space and a Children’s Museum. It also includes a 270-seat auditorium, restaurant, shop and café. Supporting the project team during the construction final stages to ensure compliance with the sustainability targets (LEED and Estidama) is the main scope conducted by AESG team.
  * Assisting the procurement team choosing the right materials and systems
  * Managing and compiling documentation required for LEED and Estidama
  * Monitoring site practices and construction waste management

  * Architect: Jean Nouvel
