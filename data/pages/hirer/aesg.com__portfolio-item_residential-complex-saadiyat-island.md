# Residential Complex Saadiyat Island
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The residential building is located in the Cultural District of Saadiyat Island. The site area of 9,352 sqm is well connected to the city via Sheikh Khalifa Bin Zayed Highway.
AESG provided Estidama Consultancy for the project. The 49,000 m2 complex achieved a 37% energy reduction through the Estidama 1 Pearl Rating.
  * Estidama Consultancy
  * Energy Modelling
