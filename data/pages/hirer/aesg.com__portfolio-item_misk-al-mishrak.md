# MISK Al Mishrak
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project comprises the first phase of MiSK City, a visionary new development conceived as a gift from the MiSK Foundation to future Saudi generations. Situated on the tributaries of Wadi Hanifah to the West of Riyadh, the site commands spectacular views across the Wadi basin and back towards the iconic downtown skyline. LifeLine 1A precinct is composed of residential, commercial and retail components as well as a primary MiSK asset, the Culinary Arts Centre.
