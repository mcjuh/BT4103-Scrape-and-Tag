# Construction Company Breaks Ground on Smarter Diligence
[ Learn How ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/resources/case-studies/construction-company-breaks-ground-on-smarter-diligence#anchor) [ Discover Legora + Axiom ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/solutions/tech-plus-talent/legora)
### Axiom attorneys helped:
Extract material terms across hundreds of contracts in one week, half the traditional review timeline 
Save $90,000 — 90% less than a traditional outside-counsel review 
Standardize outputs across the entire deal room for fast, side-by-side comparison 
### Challenge
Ahead of a planned M&A transaction, the client needed to evaluate the material terms buried across a deal room with hundreds of contracts. While they could send this out to a firm, their deal team wanted a fast-moving partner to help them accelerate their analysis in a more cost-effective way. 
### Approach
Traditionally, law firm associates would review each contract individually, extract key provisions, and compile deal memos, a process that could run nearly $100,000 for a document set this size. Instead, Axiom paired experienced legal talent with Legora to run the analysis. The **Axiom lawyer partnered with the client to identify a dozen material terms critical to deal analysis, then configured****Legora****to extract those terms automatically across all the documents.** The extracted data was exported in a structured format the client could use immediately for deal analysis.
### Impact 
  * Received a **structured view of material contract terms across the whole document set in a week** , significantly faster than the traditional manual diligence process.
  * **Client saved 90% of total cost** compared to traditional outside counsel review, which would require hundreds of hours of associate time. 
  * By applying the same extraction logic across each document, **client received standardized outputs that made it easy to compare terms and identify potential roadblocks in the deal**. 


_*Cost savings estimates are based on average in-house counsel compensation compared to AI-enabled subscription model and consolidated team structure._
