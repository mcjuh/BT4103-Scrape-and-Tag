## Enabling faster global expansion with data-driven insights
A life sciences company was preparing to expand its sales operations into more than 15 countries over an 18-month period. To support this global growth, they needed a robust data platform capable of managing healthcare professional data across multiple regions and complying with international regulations. West Monroe partnered with the client to extend their existing data platform using [**Databricks**](https://www.westmonroe.com/partnerships/databricks), helping them automate regulatory reporting and integrate with new international [**Salesforce**](https://www.westmonroe.com/partnerships/salesforce) instances. The solution reduced manual reporting efforts, improved efficiency, and could potentially lower cloud costs by 30%—all while supporting the company's first year of international operations.

By extending the client’s Databricks platform, West Monroe streamlined data management to support their global expansion. Automation eliminated manual reporting, saving the company three days per month and enhancing overall efficiency. Regulatory reporting, which once took days, is now available on demand—enabling sales leaders to respond swiftly to market changes. We also isolated all international data in a separate cloud storage location, reducing data replication and minimizing risk by adhering to GDPR and LDPR standards. This approach could cut cloud costs by 30%, driving operational efficiency as the company scales into new markets.
