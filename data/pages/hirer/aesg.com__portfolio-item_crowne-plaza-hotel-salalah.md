# Crowne Plaza Hotel Salalah
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The existing Crowne Plaza (central within the overall project site) is located on a prime beachfront location overlooking the Indian Ocean and a 10 min drive from the newly opened Salalah International Airport.
Salalah benefits from a year-round tourism season due to the popularity during the summer (Khafeer Season) with GCC guest and the rest of the year with International guest. The objective of the project development is to extend the product life cycle of the existing Crowne Plaza Hotel for the next 20 years by renovation and refurbishment of the existing facilities.
