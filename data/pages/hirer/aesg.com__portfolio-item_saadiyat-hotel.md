# Saadiyat Hotel
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
AESG was appointed as the waste management consultant on the Saadiyat Hotel development in Abu Dhabi. The development, to be operated by Ritz Carlton, will consist of a hotel, villas, and townhouses situated in a resort style setting, with a total built-up area of 70,000 sqm.
A detailed waste collection and handling strategy, waste room layouts, and specifications were developed to ensure that the project’s design complied with Abu Dhabi regulations, best practice guidelines, as well as the hotel operator’s requirements. Collection of waste from the villas and townhouses was developed using buggies, while also minimizing visual impact to guests, and the waste room and loading bay were developed to accommodate for a compactor at the hotel operator’s request.
