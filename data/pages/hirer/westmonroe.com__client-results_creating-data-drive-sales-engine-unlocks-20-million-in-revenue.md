# Data-driven sales engine boosts revenue by $20M
Transforming lead generation to increase patient enrollment and sales efficiency

A life sciences company needed a more efficient, data-driven way to generate and distribute leads to its sales teams. Data silos and time-consuming manual processes limited their ability to effectively engage healthcare professionals. West Monroe partnered with the client to create a modern, automated lead generation engine, powered by [**Databricks**](https://www.westmonroe.com/partnerships/databricks) and integrated with Veeva. This new system delivers real-time, qualified leads, significantly improving the efficiency of sales reps. The solution generated $20 million in revenue and a 4-5x increase in patient enrollments from targeted healthcare professionals in just six months—all while saving an estimated 150 hours per sales rep annually.
