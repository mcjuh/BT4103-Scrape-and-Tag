# Meydan One Mall
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project is a large mega-mall complex of 1,445,025 square meters in Dubai with a mix of uses inclusive of retail, entertainment, recreation, an office tower, and hotels. The project will be a flagship attraction, including the world’s tallest residential tower, a mall with retractable roof, approximately 2,050 hotel rooms and suites, a 1.2 km indoor ski-slope (the world’s longest), a crystal lagoon with 300-meter-long white sand beach, water park, outdoor plaza and dancing fountain, and 45,000 sqm of office space.
AESG was brought on board to review, validate and update the current waste management strategy. The complexity of the project involved extensive zonation, segregation, and the strategic design of operational elements such as waste flows. Waste storage and treatment rooms were engineered and designed for optimum efficiency and effectiveness.
  * Waste Strategy Validation
  * Waste Engineering
  * Detailed Waste Management Design
