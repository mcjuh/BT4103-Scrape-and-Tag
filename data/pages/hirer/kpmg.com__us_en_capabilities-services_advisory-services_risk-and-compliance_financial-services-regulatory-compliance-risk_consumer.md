Compliance Reimagined – Strengthening Trust. Advancing Innovation. Accelerating Growth.

## Risk & Compliance Reviews & Transformations
We help organizations execute stronger, more efficient, and more resilient enterprise compliance programs across the lines of defense grounded in the core programmatic elements from risk assessments and monitoring and testing to integrated reporting and analytics. This involves in-depth assessments of current compliance and risk management programs, identifying program gaps and opportunities for enhancement based on our team's understanding of regulatory expectations, industry best practices and the financial institutions operational excellence objectives.
**How We Strengthen Risk & Compliance Programs**
  * Deliver risk & compliance program assessments to evaluate maturity
  * Design integrated, actionable risk and control frameworks
  * Enhance regulatory taxonomies and mapping
  * Automate testing and continuous monitoring
  * Implement AI-enable reporting, analytics and insights
  * Optimize enterprise-wide and line-of-business-specific complaint and issue management programs 


## Consumer Protection & Fairness
We help organizations turn a legal imperative into a competitive advantage by designing consumer‑centric programs that help prevent harm, elevate transparency, and build trust across the customer lifecycle. ## Client Success Story
**Turning fragmented compliance data into real-time execution intelligence**
KPMG helped a leading U.S. bank modernize its compliance data model by deploying a technology-enabled architecture that integrates process, risk, and regulatory data. The result, deeper compliance insights and traceability, faster regulatory response, and a scalable foundation for sustainable compliance. 
Client
A top ten U.S. bank
Sector
Financial services
Project
Business and risk integration
## Client challenge
As regulatory complexity increased and operating models evolved, this leading U.S. bank faced fragmentation across compliance, risk, and process data. Information critical to manage both operational and compliance risk lived in disconnected systems, creating delays in regulatory response, limited enterprise visibility, and high manual effort across the three lines of defense.
The bank recognized that traditional documentation and static process mapping were no longer sufficient. To support sustainable growth and operational excellence, leadership set-out to reimagine compliance as a near real-time, intelligence-enabled capability to drive strategic insights.
## The KPMG-led transformation
KPMG partnered with the bank to **modernize its compliance operating model** , shifting from static artifacts to a **dynamic, data-driven ecosystem**.
Together, we helped the client:
  * Design an integrated process, risk, and regulatory data architecture aligned to how the business actually executes
  * Embed regulatory obligations, risks, and controls directly into execution-level process views
  * Enable automation that converts structured data into dynamic, continuously updated process intelligence
  * Establish enterprise alignment across Compliance, Technology, and Governance teams


Rather than layering new tools onto legacy processes, the effort focused on building a scalable digital foundation that could evolve with regulatory expectations, business growth, and emerging risks.
## Outcomes that matter
The transformation delivered measurable and sustainable value:
**Real-time execution visibility** Dynamic process views automatically update as underlying regulatory, risk, or control data changes
**Faster regulatory response** Reduced manual effort and cycle time for regulatory change implementation and reporting
**Lower operating friction across the three lines of defense** A shared, consistent source of execution insight replaced duplicative documentation
**A sustainable compliance model** A governance framework designed to scale with future regulatory and business change
**Stronger foundation for operational resilience and sustainability** Improved traceability and transparency support long-term risk management, resilience, and responsible growth
## KPMG insights
Modern compliance transformation requires more than automation; it requires intentional data design and enterprise alignment, connecting regulatory requirements, risks, controls, and execution processes to deliver real‑time insight, reduce compliance operating friction, and enable sustainable regulatory change management.
