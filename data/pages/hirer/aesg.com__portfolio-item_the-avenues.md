# The Avenues
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The Avenues Riyadh is mixed-use development which will be constructed on a site approximately 390,000 square meters in area. The project site is located at the North-western area of Riyadh and is bound by King Salman Road to the North and King Fahad Road to the East. The development consists of a super regional shopping mall with two basement levels for car parking.
On top of the mall, there will be 5 Towers as following: (Office) Tower, (5 Star Hotel + Serviced Apartments) Tower, (Branded Residence/Boutique Hotel) Tower, (3 stars + 4 stars + Clinics) Tower and (Convention Hotel) Tower.
