# Data-driven sales strategy delivers 25% growth
Optimizing account strategy to increase revenue and organizational efficiency

An optical frames and lenses company wanted to streamline its sales operations across multiple brands. Their goal? To sell more efficiently and increase revenue from existing accounts—freeing up time for their sales representatives to focus on acquiring new ones. They faced challenges with sales approaches, customer experience, and operational inefficiencies. West Monroe brought a data-driven mindset to help restructure their sales organization. By analyzing more than 10 million sales records and leveraging [**Alteryx**](https://www.westmonroe.com/partnerships/alteryx) for deep insights, we created a centralized sales strategy that boosted revenue by 25% while improving organizational efficiency by 20%.

Ideas into Reality
The client sought a data-driven approach for designing their sales organization, starting with insights from analyzing over 10 million B2B transactions.

The client needed a cohesive, data-driven strategy to inform their new sales organizations design. West Monroe began by consolidating multiple years of our client’s data and external industry data sources into an industry-leading optometry database. We then analyzed more than 10 million B2B sales transactions using Alteryx to uncover key insights and trends, which proved and disproved common beliefs around their customers and sales representatives. Using Alteryx’s Business Insights package, we correlated demographics with sales activity inform product recommendations.

Tackling it Together
In six weeks, West Monroe utilized Alteryx to optimize sales activities and account strategies for over 20,000 accounts.

West Monroe collaborated closely over six weeks with the client’s sales leaders to redefine how they serviced accounts. Together, we developed an account playbook strategy that tailored service levels based on customer characteristics, from high touch for major buyers to digital for others. This approach not only prioritized accounts and opportunities but also helped determine the optimal number of sales representatives needed in each region. Our data-driven recommendations were quickly embraced by sales leaders.

Value Created

West Monroe’s data-driven recommendations transformed the client’s sales organization. By optimizing account management and consolidating workforce resources, they achieved significant growth from existing accounts and streamlined their operations. The new sales structure, guided by real-time insights, allowed the company to better align resources with customer demand, driving a 25% increase in revenue while improving organizational efficiency by 20%. These improvements not only boosted sales but also paved the way for a more sustainable, data-driven approach to future growth, positioning the company for continued success.
