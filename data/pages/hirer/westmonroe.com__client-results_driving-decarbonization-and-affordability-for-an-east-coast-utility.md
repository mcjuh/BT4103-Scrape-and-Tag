# Driving decarbonization and affordability for an East Coast utility
Partnering for emissions reduction and reliability
Services
  * [Operations](https://www.westmonroe.com/services/operations)


An East Coast utility serving over a million customers faced mounting pressure to achieve aggressive greenhouse gas (GHG) reduction targets while maintaining affordability and reliability. They needed a partner who could balance sustainability goals with operational demands—and that’s where West Monroe came in. Over three years, we co-created a comprehensive energy transition plan by aligning with internal teams, engaging policymakers, and collaborating with regulators. Together, we quantified emissions impacts, optimized regulatory strategies, and introduced practical solutions. The result? Tangible progress on sustainability and a stronger position to deliver affordable, reliable energy to their community.

  * +$50M
