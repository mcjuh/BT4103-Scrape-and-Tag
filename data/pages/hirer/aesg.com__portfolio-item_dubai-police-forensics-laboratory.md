# Dubai Police Forensics Laboratory
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
AESG has been commissioned by a Dubai Government entity to provide LEED and sustainability consultancy services for a large new government laboratory facility located in Dubai. The project, which will be one of the largest laboratories of its type in the world, is pursuing high levels of sustainability, including LEED certification from the United States Green Building Council. AESG are providing comprehensive sustainability services throughout the project design and construction.
  * Sustainable Design Consultancy
  * LEED Certification Management
  * Energy Modeling
  * Daylight Simulation
  * Independent Commissioning Agent
  * Developing Measurement and Verification Plans
  * Site Inspections
  * Material Review and Approvals
