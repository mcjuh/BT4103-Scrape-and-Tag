# Employee Investigations Wrapped up for Top E-Commerce Company
### Axiom attorney team helped:
Complete company's remaining investigations backlog ahead of schedule
Run over 100 total investigations from intake to closure with minimal client support
*Save client 29% of total budget compared to their previous services provider
### Challenge
A Fortune 50 tech and e-commerce company faces a backlog of employee investigations from their warehouses. With limited resources to handle the reviews, they**need a partner with the ability and know-how to review large volumes of complaints and run investigations from start to finish**.
### Approach
With their busy season approaching, the tech company needs rapid support to churn through a backlog of employee investigations, including but not limited to harassment, workplace violence, Title VII discrimination, and performance management. **Just two weeks after the client secured budget, Axiom supplies a first wave of 10 employee relations (ER) specialists to run the investigations process from start to finish** , from intake to case closure. Two weeks later, they onboard an additional wave of 10 to further slash the backlog. Our Axiom team **cleared the bulk of reviews 1 month ahead of schedule;** the client flexibly winds down support while maintaining an ongoing team of 5 from Axiom to handle new and ongoing matters.
### Impact 
  * Each Axiom resource managed**17-23 full-cycle investigations at a given time.**
  * **Axiom Team****Lead** assisted with onboarding, training, and report review prior to closing casesto minimize thetheclient’s management burden.
  * Axiom Talent expected to clear**3-5 complex ER investigations per week** while attaining an**average closing time of 14 days with the claimant.**
