### Challenge
A small medical equipment manufacturer is undergoing diligence ahead of a potential transaction. As part of diligence, they **need to tag and upload 750+ contracts into a central contract management system**(CMS). However, their legal team is so lean that using only internal resources would significantly delay the transaction. 
### Approach
With limited internal capacity, the legal team faced a major operational hurdle. Although they could upload the contracts manually, they estimated it would take 3 months to complete. In steps an Axiom lawyer, trained on AI software and equipped with the latest tools. In **just a week, the Axiom lawyer uploaded and tagged all 750 agreements, including 10-15 clauses within each**. What would have taken a paralegal three months working off the side of their desk was condensed into a focused one-week project. 
### Impact 
  * Saved legal team 3 months of tedious work, **freeing up the legal team to focus on ongoing matters** and high-value projects. 
  * **Eliminated major bottleneck in the diligence process** by ensuring all documents were properly categorized and available in the CMS.
