# Procurement Team Cemented for Fortune 100 Construction Company
### Axiom procurement team helped:
Negotiate and review over 2,700 complex agreements
Replaced previous contracts provider with minimal oboarding time and business disruption
Save client $550,000 on outside legal spend when compared to traditional law firm*
### Challenge
A global construction company needed to replace their LPO (Legal Process Outsourcing) vendor with a more effective long-term alternative. With looming deadlines and conflicts of interest with their existing LPO, they needed to stand up a new contracts solution with no time to spare.
### Approach
The manufacturer engaged Axiom to act as an interim solution as they looked to offshore operations. **Axiom began by bringing in a team of 4 lawyers and 6 contracts managers** , with an Axiom team lead to manage the project, delegate new work, and report up to key stakeholders. The Axiom lawyers supported **procurement and sales agreements on counterparty paper, while the contracts managers handle****NDAs.** As the project drew towards conclusion, the client was so pleased with the support they kept a contingent on to continue their support long-term.
### Impact 
  * **Negotiated and reviewed 2700+ complex agreements** during 4 months of support while providing progress reports each week to keep leadership apprised.
  * Rapidly replaced previous provider **without impacting the business or expending significant time and effort** to field a new team.
  * Axiom team overhauled and **untangled complex contracts workstream, enabling the client to outsource low-level work** to less expensive alternatives.


_*Cost savings estimates are based on actual Axiom rates compared to equivalent law firm rate averages as listed in Wolters Kluwer’s Real Rate report._
