Public sector legal teams may need support for HR policies, return-to-office policies, handbooks, co-employment issues, pay equity reviews, state-by-state employment monitoring, investigations, discrimination and harassment claims, [labor relations](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/labor-relations), negotiations, [OSHA](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/osha), and ADA. Axiom can help legal teams [support privacy and cybersecurity readiness](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/data-privacy-cybersecurity) across public-sector programs and vendors.
