# Assoufid
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
A luxury hotel located in Marrakesh, Morocco is being designed as part of a larger mixed-use master plan development. The property will accommodate the main hotel building, several independent guest room blocks, standalone villas, and various leisure and recreational facilities, with a total GFA of 185,500 sqft.
AESG has been appointed as the waste management consultant, to ensure that the project’s design and overall waste strategy comply with relevant design guidelines and best practice.
Thorough due diligence was undertaken to recommend all possible measures to reduce the impact of waste on the surrounding environment, as well as ensure a streamlined and efficient servicing strategy.
  * Waste generations calculations
  * Review of existing design and comparison against Moroccan regulations and best practice
  * Recommendations for optimization of overall waste management strategy
