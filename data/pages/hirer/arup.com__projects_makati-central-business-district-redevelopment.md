Makati CBD is the Philippines' oldest established business district, forged around financial institutions and commercial headquarters. It’s a preferred location for business, but it’s ageing. Ayala Land sought a bold, future-focused strategy to revitalise Makati and enhance connectivity, promote mixed-use redevelopment, and expand green, people-centred spaces while unifying the district’s long-term direction.
We built on Ayala Land’s established Makati 2050 Vision in developing the Strategic Framework Masterplan – integrating urban design, sustainability, land use, pedestrian-focused mobility, and redevelopment strategies. Through data-driven analysis, benchmarking, and stakeholder engagement, we identified key opportunities and delivered an actionable roadmap to guide Makati’s transformation into a more connected, sustainable, resilient, and vibrant district.
The Masterplan enables long‑term growth and redevelopment and enables the district to respond to changing economic and lifestyle needs, enhance connectivity and livability, and maintain its competitiveness. The vision also establishes a benchmark for future urban regeneration initiatives across the Philippines and the region.
## A masterplan to transform Makati CBD into a multi-use, sustainable space
Redeveloping an existing CBD is a bold move, and creating a feasible development plan requires ingenuity and creativity. We were appointed to assist with the creation of the Strategic Framework Masterplan, envisioning Makati CBD as a vibrant, sustainable, and resilient district, one that’s capable of adapting and thriving despite changes.
The masterplan would need to support the district’s continued growth, optimise land use, improve connectivity and initiate harmonious integration of residential, commercial, and public spaces. Our work addressed evolving economic, demographic, and emerging lifestyle preferences, while also considering the increasing competition amidst global challenges.
The project scope covers urban design and planning, sustainability, densification analysis, and implementation, ensuring that the district’s growth aligns with contemporary urban planning principles and enhances the urban environment.
Our team conducted a thorough analysis of baseline conditions, reviewed prior reports, global benchmarks, and case studies, and took the time to have a deeper understanding of the site. This process involved a range of internal and external consultations, site visits, geospatial analysis, and test-fitting development scenarios, all in close collaboration with Ayala Land.
## A framework for an evolving CBD
We developed a spatial framework that focusses on three key initiatives: improving connectivity, promoting mixed-use redevelopment, and enhancing the greening of the public realm. 
The Vision and Strategic Framework also include three key strategies: land use reprogramming, development of superblocks, and enhanced pedestrian circulation. 
These recommendations build the foundation for the creation of an action plan which will outline actionable strategies to implement these initiatives and act as a comprehensive roadmap toward sustainability and growth.
