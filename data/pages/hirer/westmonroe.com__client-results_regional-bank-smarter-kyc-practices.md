A large regional commercial bank needed to expand its deposit base but feared that outdated know-your-customer (KYC) practices may expose it to unmitigated compliance risk. To support growth while maintaining regulatory rigor, the bank partnered with West Monroe to rethink its commercial customer lifecycle management. Together, we co-created a target operating model that aligned teams, simplified processes, and laid the foundation for scalable technology. The result? A risk-based approach to KYC that eliminated unnecessary touchpoints and enabled smarter client acquisition. With a working KYC prototype and roadmap in hand, the bank became better equipped to grow relationships while staying ahead of compliance demands.

Ideas Into Reality
The bank brought in West Monroe to redesign its operating model and its KYC collection processes with the goal of creating an onboarding process with reduced friction—cutting client touchpoints by 43% during onboarding.

The bank sought to improve commercial customer lifecycle management while reducing risk associated with new client acquisition. West Monroe quickly identified KYC as a critical barrier to scaling deposit growth. We worked with leaders across risk, compliance, and onboarding to build a holistic operating model focused on clarity and accountability. From there, we developed a flexible risk-based KYC framework and a working prototype tool to streamline data collection. These foundational changes created a smarter onboarding experience for clients and relationship managers—reducing back-and-forth and enabling faster, more confident decision-making.
