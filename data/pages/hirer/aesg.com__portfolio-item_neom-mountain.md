# Neom Mountain
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
AESG have been commissioned by the lead architect and masterplanner to conduct environmental and sustainability consultancy services. The environmental work consists of a natural systems assessment, preliminary environmental review, scoping study and full Environmental and Social Impact Assessment (ESIA) process.
  * Preliminary Environmental Review 
  * Scoping Report 
  * Environmental and Social Impact Assessment
  * Development of comprehensive sustainability strategy at masterplan and building level
  * Environmental performance analysis
  * Waste Management Strategy
