# Contracts Platform Migration with Tech+Talent for Aerospace Company
### Axiom attorney helped:
Enable client to complete their CMS overhaul without pulling from any additional in-house resources
Re-structure data and upload 1,400 contracts into client's Ironclad system
Save client $17,000 compared to the standard non-AI competing solution
### Challenge
An aerospace technology company has wanted to migrate their legacy contracts into their Ironclad CLSM system for years.However, their **current****setup is****duplicative and lacks the structured data necessary to migrate systems**. They need to find a partner who could not only execute the migration but also clean up years of disorganized data.
### Approach
Axiom partnered with the client’s Head of Legal Operations to place an Ironclad expert to lead the contract migration and ensure quality assurance throughout the process. As the project begins, it is revealed that the contracts were in worse shape than anticipated. As a result, the **Axiom expert leads a major 6-week cleanup effort and backend reconfiguration of Ironclad** to accept properly structured data. Once configured, Axiom used Legora to **categorize,****upload, and extract key metadata (such as renewal term length) from****1400 contracts** like service agreements, [MSAs](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/master-service-agreements), [NDAs](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/nda), etc.
### Impact 
  * Re-structured data and **uploaded 1,400 contracts** into new Ironclad system
  * Enabled client to **complete long overdue CMS overhaul** without pulling from any in-house resources
  * Legora delivered **20-30% higher accuracy** vs the client’s own CMS AI tool in head-to-head comparison
  * Structured metadata and de-duplicated records **equipped the legal team with a clean, searchable contract repository** for the first time
