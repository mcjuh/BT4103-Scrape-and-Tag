## Accelerated progress, significant impact
City National Bank of Florida, a $20 billion regional bank, needed a robust digital operating model to become a regional leader. Their ambitious vision centered on transforming IT and operations using the Salesforce Financial Services Cloud, nCino Loan Origination System, Azure, and MuleSoft. West Monroe’s role extended beyond technology delivery to co-creating a digital-first operational model with lasting value. By partnering across four key projects, we helped drive impactful improvements, including $800 million in deposit growth, $1.4 billion in loans processed within 90 days, and a projected $50 million in new revenue. With data-driven insights and automation, City National Bank increased efficiency, empowered its workforce, and positioned itself to capture additional growth with agility and precision.

Value Created

The bank’s comprehensive digital transformation delivered immediate, measurable outcomes. In six months, City National Bank realized $800 million in deposit growth, primarily in private banking and commercial services, and saw a 63% increase in client-sourced lead meetings. The bank’s new nCino Loan Origination System processed over 317 loans, totaling $1.4 billion in volume within 90 days. Enabled by a streamlined, API-connected environment through MuleSoft, City National Bank is on track to generate $50 million in additional revenue. This transformation not only supports growth but also equips the bank to deliver sustained, data-driven customer experiences well into the future.
