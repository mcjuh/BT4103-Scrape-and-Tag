# Bvlgari Hotel And Residences
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The Bvlgari project at Jumeirah Bay Island, a six million square feet mixed-use development, sculpted in the shape of a titanic seahorse, will feature 100 rooms and suites in the main hotel buildings, alongside 20 hotel villas and a marina. The marine complex will cover an area of 1.7 million square feet.
AESG were appointed to provide the below:
  * Commissioning Management
  * Validation of ELV and BMS systems
