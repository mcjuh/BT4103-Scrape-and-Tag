# Health Insurer Turns Its Own Redlines Into Ten Negotiating Playbooks
[ Learn How ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/resources/case-studies/health-insurer-turns-its-own-redlines-into-ten-negotiating-playbooks#anchor) [ Discover DraftPilot + Axiom ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/solutions/tech-plus-talent/draftpilot)
### Axiom attorney helped:
Build 10 negotiating playbooks, each with its own escalation ladder, from the team's own prior redlines 
Train 8 client attorneys and paralegals to run the new playbooks independently
Drive 70%+ efficiency gains* on contract review, freeing time for strategic work 
### Challenge
The client wanted to **reduce attorney time on routine contracts** so the team could spend more of it on strategic work. That meant an **automated playbook and review platform** covering a varied set of agreement types, not a single template.
### Approach
An Axiom attorney from DraftPilot's original beta group built **10 playbooks** in the tool. Where the client had templates, those set the primary position; where agreements always sit on vendor paper, DraftPilot analyzed **prior redlines** to find what the client repeatedly pushed back on. Each playbook got a **fallback ladder** , tuned with the GC so routine matters never reached her desk. Rollout ran on **live training, a cheat sheet, twice-weekly office hours** , and 1:1 sessions for anyone who missed one.
### Impact 
  * Negotiating positions the team had won in practice became **codified, reusable****playbooks**.
  * A defined **escalation ladder** keeps routine fallbacks away from the General Counsel.
  * Coverage extends to **vendor paper** , where no template existed to start from.
