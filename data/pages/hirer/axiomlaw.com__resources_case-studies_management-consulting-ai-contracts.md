# Big 3 Management Consulting Firm Uses Axiom to Train AI Contracts Tool
### Axiom attorney team helped:
Train the AI tool to achieve 95% accuracy, surpassing benchmark goals by 15%
Review firm's database of agreements to tag and categorize clauses, data points, and custom language
*Save client $70,000 compared to equivalent solution
### Challenge
A big 3 management consultant implements a comprehensive AI tool to draft and review contracts. However, the **software is largely ineffective****out-of-the-box, so the in-house team continues to work on their contracts the old-fashioned way**. With mountains of new agreements every day, the firm needs to train their AI tool to improve its accuracy and grow internal adoption.
### Approach
Axiom sets up a team of 6 lawyers and a team lead to train the AI tool. To harness the full power of the software and warrant consistent use, the firm needs to reach at least an 80% accuracy rate. The Axiom lawyers, all deeply experienced with professional services agreements, **review a database of the firm’s executed agreements to tag specific clauses, data points, and custom language from the firm.** Over the course of 3 weeks, the **Axiom team reviews and uploads hundreds of newly tagged agreements** into the AI training program, refining the software’s database.
### Impact 
  * Axiom lawyers’ train the AI tool to**achieve 95% accuracy on new agreements** , far surpassing the initial company benchmark goal of 80% accuracy.
  * **In-house adoption of the AI tool hits 100%** the month after Axiom training is complete, allowing the firm to harness major efficiency gains.
  * Successfully trains AI model while**meeting budget goals by paying 40% less for Axiom’s professional services lawyers** vs AI company’s more generalist lawyers.
