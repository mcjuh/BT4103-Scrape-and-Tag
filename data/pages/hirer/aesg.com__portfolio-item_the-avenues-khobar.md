# The Avenues Khobar
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project’s scope of work consists of a mixed use complex comprising a shopping mall located northwest corner of the King Saud intersection and Prince Sultan Road in Al Khobar city, Saudi Arabia. The project includes 180,000 rental areas including commercial, entertainment, residential, hotel, office and health facilities.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following:
  * Fire and Life Safety Design Consultancy & CFD Analysis
  * Sustainability Certification strategy guidance & gap analysis for LEED Certification of whole property
  * Post-Contract Cost Management
