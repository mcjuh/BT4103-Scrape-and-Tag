# 1,000+ Hours Saved for Regional Airline with Axiom Tech+Talent

### Axiom tech + talent helped to:
Cut over 1,000 manual review hours for the client's legal team with AI-enabled workflow
Convert thousands of unstructured agreements into clean, usable data for the client’s internal database
*Save client $400,000 due to AI-enabled solution vs law firm equivalent
## Challenge
After a large acquisition, a regional airline **needed to harmonize the acquired business into its parent operations** , which required a clear picture of all contract obligations across the subsidiary. The contract set was large, varied in structure, and needed to be converted into a format compatible with the client’s internal systems.
## Approach
Axiom partnered with the client **to review nearly 3000 documents across a set of 15-30 different data points.** Equipped with Legora, a senior Axiom attorney ran the AI tool across several contract families to pull out info such as lease descriptions, auto-renewal terms, NDA provisions, etc. He then developed **custom workflows to upload the outputs into the client’s CMS using a custom data structure and unique set of naming conventions.** In addition Axiom supported a separate workstream in which they reviewed highly complex collective bargaining agreements against one another to compare negotiated terms, and understand which provisions were most favorable during their negotiation process. 
## Impact 
  * Experienced Axiom attorney with an AI-enabled workflow **cut over 1,000 manual review hours for the client’s legal team**
  * **Converted thousands of unstructured agreements into clean, usable data** for client’s internal database, supporting a clearer view of subsidiary obligations and operational synergies.
  * By comparing terms across complex collective bargaining agreements, the client’s team could improve their negotiations by **evaluating relative favorability across****a number of****negotiated concepts.**
