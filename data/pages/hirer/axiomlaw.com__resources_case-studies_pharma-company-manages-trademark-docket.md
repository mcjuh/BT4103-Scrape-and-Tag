# Global Trademark Docket Management for Pharma Company
### Axiom attorney helped:
Lead trademark docket management
Navigate the complex pharmaceutical product naming process
Save client 73% on IP matters compared to outside counsel
### Challenge
A pharmaceutical company struggles managing their trademark portfolio with limited in-house resources. The company relies on only one paralegal for IP support and has experienced difficulties when bringing in external help.
### Approach
As the work volumes continue to grow, the client brings in an Axiom lawyer to help manage their vast trademark portfolio and keep the workstream in-house. The Axiom lawyer partners with colleagues in the US, Australia, and Germany to support filing and clearances for new product names, apply for trademarks, and maintain thousands of trademarks in the docket.
### Impact 
  * By offloading the trademark docket management to Axiom, client’s in-house team pivoted their focus towards commercial ventures, driving overall business growth and efficiency.
  * Client navigated the complex process of naming new pharmaceutical products, expanding their offerings with confidence.
  * “Best I have felt about the trademark team in years.”- Associate General Counsel, Commercial & Trademarks
