## What can investment banking lawyers help legal teams manage?
Investment banking lawyers can help legal teams manage capital markets transactions, [M&A support](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/mergers-acquisitions), lending, derivatives, structured finance, broker-dealer issues, conflicts and anti-bribery policies, governance, entity management, regulatory change, and global compliance coverage.
## How can investment banking lawyers support capital markets transactions?
Investment banking lawyers can [support capital markets transactions](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/capital-markets) by helping review debt and equity materials, private placement documentation, structured finance documents, broker-dealer considerations, disclosure workflows, governance materials, and related transaction checklists.
## What legal support is needed for lending, derivatives, and structured finance?
Lending, [derivatives](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/derivatives), and [structured finance work](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/structured-project-finance) may require legal support for syndicated and bilateral loans, hedge fund and [private equity finance](https://www.axiomlaw.com/industries/private-equity), trade finance, ISDA documents, CSAs, MSFTAs, futures and OTC clearing, collateral terms, regulatory requirements, and documentation workflows.
## How can investment banking legal teams manage conflicts and disclosure workflows?
Investment banking legal teams can manage conflicts and disclosure workflows by maintaining clear policies, reviewing deal team and research-related restrictions, documenting potential conflicts, coordinating approvals, monitoring information barriers, and ensuring transaction materials reflect required disclosures.
## When do investment banks need flexible legal support?
Investment banks often need flexible legal support during deal surges, capital markets windows, [regulatory remediatio](https://www.axiomlaw.com/solutions/large-projects/regulatory-compliance-review)n, documentation backlogs, cross-border coverage needs, derivatives repapering, [M&A diligence](https://www.axiomlaw.com/skills/buy-side-ma-due-diligence), or temporary staffing gaps across compliance and transaction teams.
## How can investment banking lawyers support global compliance coverage?
Investment banking lawyers can support global compliance coverage by helping legal teams coordinate local requirements, maintain issue trackers, review policies, [support regulatory change implementation](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/regulatory-compliance), document controls, and provide coverage across regions when internal teams need extended or around-the-clock support.
