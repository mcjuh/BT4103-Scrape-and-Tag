# How our teams drive audit innovation and efficiency
## You have a choice. Choose audit innovation. 
A Grant Thornton client was spending two weeks manually preparing data before every audit, because its accounting system didn’t feed into the required financial statement disclosures and other workpapers. It was time for a better solution. Our alyx™ platform helped us identify an automated solution and create custom workflows. The new solution improved processes to save time and provided insights to drive better business decisions. You have a choice. If you are ready to save time, improve quality and get better insights, choose audit innovation.
