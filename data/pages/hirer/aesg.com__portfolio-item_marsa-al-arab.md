# Marsa Al Arab
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
Marsa Al Arab is located on a reclaimed peninsular to the east of Burj Al Arab and Jumeriah Beach Hotel, Dubai, UAE. The development includes a 5 star plus luxury hotel of 400 keys G+16 with total BUA 93,500 sqm, a branded serviced hotel apartment building, a marina, and an extensive resort landscape with infinity pools and private beach front.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following:
  * Façade Access Strategy
  * Detail and Tender Drawing Package
  * Façade Access Specifications
