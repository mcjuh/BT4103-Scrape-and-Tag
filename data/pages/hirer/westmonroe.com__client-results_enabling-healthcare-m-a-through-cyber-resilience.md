# Enabling healthcare M&A through cyber resilience
Private equity firm strengthens healthcare provider against cyber threats
Services

When a private equity firm acquired a healthcare specialty services provider, they knew cybersecurity couldn't wait. With cyber threats increasingly targeting post-acquisition companies, they partnered with West Monroe to conduct thorough IT due diligence and implement critical security upgrades. Our team delivered comprehensive cybersecurity improvements across more than 2,000 devices on a tight timeline. Through strategic implementation of advanced security measures and automated solutions, we strengthened their digital infrastructure, integrated robust authentication protocols, and created a more secure and well-managed digital system.

Ideas Into Reality
Our client needed to quickly strengthen cybersecurity across 2,000+ devices while maintaining business operations during their post-acquisition phase.

Working closely with the client's team, we conducted a thorough assessment of their cybersecurity landscape, identifying critical vulnerabilities and opportunities for enhancement. Amid a post-acquisition transition, the client needed to secure more than 2,000 devices without disrupting daily operations. Our approach prioritized high-impact initiatives that would deliver immediate risk reduction while setting the foundation for long-term digital resilience. We developed a comprehensive roadmap that balanced urgent security needs with operational continuity—focusing on essential upgrades to authentication systems, endpoint protection, and administrative controls.

Tackling It Together
Over 12 weeks, our team implemented multi-layered security solutions, protecting more than 2,000 endpoints through a phased transformation approach. 
Read More 
We launched parallel workstreams to maximize impact within the compressed timeline. Our technology experts collaborated with the client's IT team to implement Azure AD security controls, Thycotic Secret Server for password management, and Microsoft Defender for Identity. Through a phased, 12-week transformation, we deployed multi-layered security solutions that protected more than 2,000 endpoints. We established new administrative protocols, enhanced encryption measures, and removed security vulnerabilities. Throughout the process, we provided hands-on training and documentation, ensuring the client's team was equipped to maintain and build upon these security improvements long after project completion.

Value Created
Our solutions implemented automatic password rotation for 1,000+ systems and eliminated 90+ unauthorized applications. 
Read More 
The transformation delivered immediate and lasting impact. Beyond the quantitative improvements in endpoint security and access control, we introduced automated credential rotation across thousands of systems and removed over 90 unauthorized applications—tightening controls and reducing exposure. We established a resilient cybersecurity foundation that supports the company's growth objectives. The new security infrastructure significantly reduced the risk of costly ransomware events while freeing IT resources to focus on strategic initiatives. With enhanced monitoring capabilities, automated security protocols, and trained internal teams, the organization now has the digital resilience to thrive in today's dynamic threat landscape.
