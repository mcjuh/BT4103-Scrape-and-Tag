# Successful Spin-Off for Fortune 50 Technology Business Unit
### Axiom attorneys helped:
Generate cost savings compared to outside counsel
Quickly process a high volume of contracts
Complete a divestiture efficiently and effectively
### Challenge
Global technology company needed to update high volume of commercial and government contracts to complete **divestiture of $18B business unit.**
The company lacked enough in-house attorneys with appropriate domain knowledge to deliver this work at scale, and hiring a law firm for this project would require paying premium rates.
### Approach
Axiom quickly assembled **team of 15 attorneys** with deep knowledge of commercial and government contracts in the technology industry. **Two experienced managers** led the team of Axiom attorneys, and they delivered regular updates and reporting to provide the client clear visibility into the project’s progress. Axiom’s approach enabled the team to effectively **process all outstanding contracts across United****States, Canada, and the United Kingdom** required to successfully spin-off the business.
### Impact 
  * Axiom team’s size and relevant experience enabled them to **quickly process a high volume of****contracts** , helping client **complete the divestiture** efficiently and effectively.
  * At the client’s request, the same Axiom team began a **new workstream to stand up the new****business.** Since the Axiom attorneys already knew the needs of the business, they were able to rapidly launch the project and align with the client’s requirements.
  * The company was able to **pay meaningfully lower rates** by engaging Axiom attorneys, rather than sending this work to outside counsel.
