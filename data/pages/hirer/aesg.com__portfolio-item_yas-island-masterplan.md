# Yas Island Masterplan
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
AESG have provided Estidama Pearl Community Rating System (PCRS) consultancy on two packages of work on Aldar’s iconic Yas Island. AESG were appointed as the Independent Commissioning Agent and Estidama Construction Estidama Consultant for the West Yas 2 Peal project, which is a residential development of over 1,000 units.
In addition, AESG have were appointed for the concept and detailed master planning of the North Yas masterplan – Yas Acres, which will accommodate over 50,000 residents on a 680ha plot. AESG are also providing Estidama PVRS consultancy for the design of the 18 residential unit typologies on North Yas.
**List of Services provided**
  * Estidama design and construction management
  * Energy modeling and analysis
  * Independent Commissioning Agent
  * Sustainable design consultancy, energy and water measures
  * Waste management consultancy
  * Material reviews and trackings
