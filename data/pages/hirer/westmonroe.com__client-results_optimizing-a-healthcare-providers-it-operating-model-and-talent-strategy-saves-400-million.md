## Transforming IT to Unlock Savings
A healthcare provider needed to reduce IT costs and optimize its talent strategy amidst unsustainable operating losses. West Monroe stepped in to help reframe the client’s IT operating model, uncovering opportunities across four key pillars: employee optimization, contingent talent, outsourcing, and automation. Through this comprehensive approach, we co-created a strategy that consolidated suppliers, standardized pricing, and optimized operations—driving over $400 million in savings. Together, we transformed the client’s operating model to reduce costs and establish a sustainable, forward-thinking business enablement strategy.

West Monroe broadened their approach by re-envisioning their IT operating model, emphasizing scalability and effectiveness across multiple areas. Our team, blending deep healthcare and IT expertise, partnered with the client over 25 months to redefine IT operations. We initiated a phased approach—first targeting organizational design, then moving into managed service providers (MSPs) for key service functions, and establishing a Vendor Management System (VMS) to optimize contingent talent. By unifying supplier agreements and creating a streamlined vendor ecosystem, we standardized pricing, improved role definitions, and created measurable efficiencies. This end-to-end collaboration achieved sustainable, forward-looking improvements that elevated the client’s IT capability and controlled their contingent workforce spend.
