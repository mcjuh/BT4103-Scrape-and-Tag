# Ferguson House
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The project consists of the internal redevelopment of an existing six-storey building in London. The works include new office space on the aboveground floors and the conversion of the existing basement level into a healthcare clinic.
AESG are on board to provide the FLS Strategy for the whole building.
  * Fire and Life Safety advice to the design team
  * Production of Fire and Life Safety Strategy
  * Negotiations with Building Control Authority


  * Client: Lazari Investments Limited
  * Consultant:
  * Architect: CDS Projects
  * Developer:
  * Contractor: Cushman & Wakefield
  * Scope: Fire and Life Safety
  * Location: [London, United Kingdom](https://aesg.com/uk/)
  * Dates: 2018 – Ongoing
