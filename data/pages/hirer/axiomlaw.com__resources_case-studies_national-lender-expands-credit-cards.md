### Challenge
**National Lender** planned to expand offerings to include consumer credit cards.
In-house legal team was **stretched****thin** between executing requirements for the new product offering and existing initiatives.
### Approach
Axiom deployed a former **financial services executive and partner-level attorney** – with forty years of regulatory experience and an extensive background in payments and consumer credit matters, including knowledge of state and federal regulatory requirements – to **provide senior-level legal advice** on the credit card launch.
