### What are the main benefits clients can expect from using Axiom’s AI solutions like Draft Pilot?
Our clients benefit from: 
  1. Significant increases in efficiency, output, and consistency across contract review tasks.
  2. Broadened contract review scope without raising costs, thereby reducing operational risks.
  3. Opportunities to reconsider offshore contract review in favor of local, efficient, and experienced legal services


### Does DraftPilot automate contract review completely?
No, DraftPilot serves as a “co-pilot” rather than a replacement for legal expertise. It supports lawyers by identifying issues and enhancing efficiency, with the final decision-making remaining the attorney’s responsibility. 
### Can DraftPilot help with all types of contracts?
DraftPilot is optimized for convenience in reviewing Microsoft Word documents, works on both first- and third-party paper, and can help with converting PDFs into word format. 
### Can counterparties see that DraftPilot has been used?
No. Counterparties are unaware of DraftPilot’s application during negotiations, as all AI-generated annotations appear under the attorney’s authorship. 
### How do Axiom lawyers use DraftPilot?
DraftPilot functions as a user-friendly Microsoft Word add-in accessible through Axiom-provided laptops, facilitating seamless integration into daily workflows. 
### Can Axiom lawyers use DraftPilot on a client laptop or virtual desktop?
Yes, but only with client IT approval to install the DraftPilot add-in. Clients may also choose to purchase and implement their DraftPilot licenses to facilitate access.
### Can clients use DraftPilot directly?
While our clients are engaging Axiom legal talent, we offer monthly licenses for DraftPilot to their in-house legal team. 
### Where is the data housed?
Playbook data for DraftPilot is stored in Ireland, and OpenAI (Foundational Model for DraftPilot) has a 0-day retention policy to manage data security. 
### Will my data be used to train public AI models?
Absolutely not. Clients’ data remain confidential and secure, adhering to stringent SOC 2 Type 1 and ISO 27001 standards, ensuring privacy and protection. 
### What safeguards are in place to protect my data in DraftPilot?
DraftPilot complies with rigorous security measures, ensuring that client data is never used to train AI models. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, with strict data segregation and user-level permissions ensuring additional security. Robust safeguards are built to uphold confidentiality and integrity. For further information, please visit DraftPilot’s Trust Center draftpilot-ltd.trust.site. 
### How does the DraftPilot license fee differ from what I can get from DraftPilot directly?
DraftPilot offers an annual license fee per user at $3,000 each and charges a platform fee in addition. For certain flexible arrangements or a paid proof-of-concept, they offer a monthly $300 per user license.
Therefore, the $250 per user per month rate from Axiom is the best DraftPilot rate available in the market.
Additionally, your in-house team can get licenses from Axiom at the same price as Axiom legal talent. 
### How does DraftPilot get installed?
For engagements on Axiom laptops, we will handle the application add-in for the Axiom talent. For client virtual environments on Axiom laptops or Axiom laptops itself, we just need your IT team to push the install of the DraftPilot application from the Microsoft App Store. Once DraftPilot is available for users, we will provision the license on our end for easy login for each user.
