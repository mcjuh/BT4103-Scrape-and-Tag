# Artificial Intelligence Product Rollout for Fortune 50 Pharma Company
### Axiom attorneys helped to:
Enable client's legal team to rapidly scale up without removing support from core business verticals
Provide tech and generative AI knowledge that client lacked in-house
Produce $520,000 in cost savings compared to equivalent law firm spend*
### Challenge
A global pharma powerhouse recently established a new digital technology arm. However, the internal legal team**lacked the expertise required to navigate the complex intersection of AI, tech and pharma law**. With myriad acquisitions, partnerships, and existing/future regulations, the new digital org needed a partner to help address data privacy, AI, IP, and regulatory compliance concerns.
### Approach
Axiom deployed a tailored team of lawyers with extensive experience supporting technology roll-outs, including new AI products. The **Axiom team drafted and negotiated vendor agreements, statements of work, and affiliate agreements** with an eye towardsdata processing and privacy implications. They also issue spotted to ensure compliance with **healthcare regulations like HIPAA and anti-kickback****rules**. Additional **Axiom tech IP lawyers supported patent drafting for digital assets** which the new org was developing internally and acquiring from external partners and sources.
### Impact 
  * At its peak, a team of 7 lawyers and 1 project manager **enabled client’s legal team to rapidly scale and meet the needs of the new digital org** without taking away legal support from their other business verticals
  * **By providing tech and generative AI knowledge that the client lacked in-house** , Axiom enabled client to implement new AI tools for their customers while adhering to regulatory compliance rules
  * Slashed administrative burden of coordinating review across legal functions (M&A/Licensing, Procurement, IP, data privacy) to **streamline client’s digital asset transactions**


_*Cost savings estimates are based on actual Axiom rates compared to equivalent law firm rate averages as listed in Wolters Kluwer’s Real Rate report._
