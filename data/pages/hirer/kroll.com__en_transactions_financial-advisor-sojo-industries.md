On June 12, 2025, Sojo Industries ("Sojo") announced it received a $40 million Series B investment from S2G Investments ("S2G").
The Technology and Business Services Investment Banking practice served as the exclusive financial advisor to Sojo in connection with the capital raise.
## About Sojo Industries
Sojo is a mobile manufacturing and technology company that utilizes robotics, modularity and software to deliver innovative mobile packaging and supply-chain solutions primarily to the food and beverage industry. Sojo delivers modular, robotic packaging solutions both on-site at customer facilities and via its national footprint of brick-and-mortar variety pack manufacturing facilities across the country in Pennsylvania, California, Indiana and Texas. Sojo also has a blockchain-secured, geolocation track-and-trace software platform which drives highly granular visibility into the variety pack supply chain.
## About S2G Investments
S2G Investments is a multi-stage investment firm focused on venture and growth-stage business across food & agriculture, oceans and energy. The firm provides capital and value-added resources to companies and leadership teams pursuing market-based solutions designed to deliver greater value, improved outcomes and enhanced performance over traditional alternatives.
