# Intellectual Property Protected for National Developer
### At an estimated 64% lower rate than law firms, Axiom attorney helped the firm:
Assess and protect their intellectual property
Avoid infringement on competitors’ intellectual property
Easily work with a seasoned legal professional who knows their business
### Challenge
Development and construction firm needed to **evaluate intellectual property (IP)** portfolio in order to **protect their proprietary designs.**
The firm’s legal department **did not have any IP attorneys in-house** with enough experience to manage the work, and hiring a law firm for the engagement would have been overly expensive.
### Approach
Axiom deployed a senior attorney who has spent **19+ years** practicing IP law, including experience as a **former partner at an AmLaw 100 firm** and multiple roles in-house. Working closely with the firm’s legal department, the Axiom attorney developed an approach to help the company assess and protect their intellectual property. He **evaluated and registered multiple trademarks and copyrights.** He also provided guidance on which content the company should avoid using in its promotional materials in order to ensure it does not infringe on competitors’ IP.
### Impact 
  * Axiom attorney helped firm legally protect its valuable IP, such as images and iconographies of its buildings, in order to **preserve its brand value and differentiate it from competitors.**
  * **Flexible hourly billing** enabled the company’s legal department department to work with the Axiom attorney on an as-needed basis.
  * Ongoing access to the same Axiom attorney enabled firm to **easily work with a seasoned legal professional who knows their business** , unlike a law firm who may bring in different attorneys for different engagements.
