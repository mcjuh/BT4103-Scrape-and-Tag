# 90% faster cyber response for a secure grid
Improving isolation procedures to mitigate risks
Services
  * [Cybersecurity](https://www.westmonroe.com/services/cybersecurity-resilience)


A national utility company knew that as its IT and operational technology (OT) systems became more connected, its exposure to cyber threats grew. To safeguard critical operations, they needed a clear, reliable way to isolate OT systems in the event of a breach. They turned to West Monroe, a [partner of more than a decade](https://www.westmonroe.com/client-results/a-decade-of-building-a-smarter-and-more-secure-grid).
With deep expertise in real-time energy networks, we helped design and implement new isolation procedures, train more than 200 employees, and introduce push-button automation. The utility now can respond to cyber threats 90% faster, protecting 60+ critical processes and reducing operational risk. With stronger governance and well-trained teams, they are prepared to act decisively—ensuring reliability for millions.

Ideas Into Reality
To reduce cyber risk, the utility partnered with West Monroe to develop clear isolation procedures and implement automation for rapid response.
Read More 
With growing IT and OT interconnectivity, the utility needed a way to quickly isolate critical systems if a cyberattack compromised IT or external networks. West Monroe brought deep energy industry expertise and a hands-on approach to developing a resilient solution. 
We mapped network architecture, identified key isolation points, and designed precise procedures for both manual and automated system disconnection. By balancing technology with governance, we ensured the utility could isolate threats effectively—minimizing business disruption and maintaining operational integrity.

Tackling it Together
Over 12 weeks, West Monroe collaborated with the utility to implement and test isolation protocols, training more than 200 employees through live exercises. 
Read More 
Creating the right procedures was just the first step—we also ensured they worked in real-world scenarios. We partnered with IT and business leaders to define decision-making authority, test procedures, and refine them based on live results. 
We engaged more than 200 employees across multiple locations with hands-on training, ensuring teams knew exactly how to isolate systems in a crisis. We ran walkthroughs and live off-hour exercises to simulate cyber breaches, testing both manual and push-button isolation. In the end, the utility had confidence in its ability to respond both quickly and effectively.

Value Created
With automation and clear procedures, the utility improved cyberattack response time by 90%, securing 60+ processes and reducing operational and financial risk.

With new isolation capabilities in place, the utility can now respond to cyber threats 90% faster. This means a reduced chance of system infiltration, minimizing downtime and financial loss. Beyond technical improvements, this effort created lasting operational resilience. Employees are well-trained, governance is clearly defined, and decision-making is structured for rapid action.
