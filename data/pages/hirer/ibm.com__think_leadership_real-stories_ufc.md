#  Turning fight night into real-time insight 
[ Explore insights ](https://www.ibm.com/think/leadership/real-stories/ufc#urx)
### The challenge
UFC produces one of the most visceral, fast-moving sports experiences in the world. Every event generates enormous amounts of performance data, historical matchups and fighter statistics. But much of that data remained underused. Manual analytics workflows slowed the path from raw information to broadcast-ready insight. As audience expectations for data-driven storytelling grew, a clear imbalance emerged: the fights were scaling. Manual insight generation was not.

### The stakes
When action outpaces analysis, the fan experience takes a hit. Labor intensive workflows leave analysts wrangling data instead of narrating the fight. That drag slows insight to a crawl during live competition. And when contextual insights can’t keep pace, fans disengage—creating an opening for UFC’s biggest competitors. UFC needed a faster, smarter way to turn raw data into real time insight.

### The game plan
The organization partnered with IBM to build the UFC Insights Engine—an AI powered platform that generates unique insights and advanced analysis on demand. It also lets UFC editors mine a massive library of fight data using natural language. The result is a decisive momentum shift that turns raw data into a strategic, story-driven edge.
The impact 
The UFC Insights Engine brings high-impact, creative storylines to broadcast and digital channels, accelerates the path from data to insight and significantly expands content output capacity.
For fans 
Fans gain a deeper understanding of fighters and matchups. They also get faster, more dynamic content across every digital channel.
For teams 
Analysts spend far less time on manual data preparation. They can finally focus on strategic storytelling that elevates each event.
For the brand 
The brand now operates on scalable infrastructure built for sustained fan engagement. It also strengthens its competitive edge in a rapidly evolving, data-driven sports economy.
Conclusion 
UFC will always thrive on intensity and spectacle. With AI-powered analytics, the organization can now deliver insights as powerful as the fights themselves. In a real‑time environment, lag is a liability—whether you’re in the fight game, retail or insurance. UFC shows that scaling performance without scaling interpretation creates friction. Close that gap, and speed becomes strategy, insight becomes advantage.
