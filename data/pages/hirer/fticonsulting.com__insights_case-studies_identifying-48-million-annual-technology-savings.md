# Identifying $48 Million of Annual Technology Savings
  * May 23, 2024
  * Share


FTI Consulting professionals worked with a $2 billion global leader in publishing, education and digital products to identify $48 million of annual technology savings.
### Our Impact
  * FTI Consulting assessed the company’s portfolio and**identified opportunities for $48 million in annual technology savings.**
  * Our experts presented the client with **a defined digital product development life cycle and migration paths.**
  * FTI Consulting **validated the business case for global consolidation of seven enterprise resource planning (“ERP”) platforms.**


### Our Role
  * FTI Consulting was engaged to assess the current state of systems and integrations, infrastructure, technical debt, enterprise data management and project pipelines.
  * Our experts established a strategy for product technology to utilize a standardized product development life cycle for consistency and uniformity across the technology organization and its more than 2,000 professionals. 
  * These efforts produced a prioritized and sequenced implementation roadmap to capture technology improvements and reduce annual spend by 14%.
