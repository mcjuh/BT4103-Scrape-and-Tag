# Republic of Singapore Pavilion
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
The Singapore Pavilion, with the theme of ‘Nature, Nurture, Future’, encapsulates Singapore’s story of overcoming its physical limitations as a small island city-state and adapting itself to become a highly livable and sustainable city. The Singapore Pavilion is located in the Sustainability District of Expo 2020 Dubai.
AESG was appointed to undertake the following:
  * Fire & Life Safety Engineering Services
  * Fire protection systems
  * Fire alarm systems
  * Smoke management systems
  * Smoke management systems
  * Commission ability and maintainability of the mentioned systems
  * Means of egress components
  * Fire rating requirements of construction materials, façade, cladding, glazing and roofing system
  * Witnessing of testing and commissioning of fire safety systems
