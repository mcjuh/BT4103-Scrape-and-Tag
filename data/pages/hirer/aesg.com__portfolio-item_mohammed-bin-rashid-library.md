# Mohammed Bin Rashid Library
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
Mohammed Bin Rashid (MBR) Library will be a state of the art library building in Dubai. This Library will be a landmark building for the region and shall house various facilities including, various library spaces such as children’s, Arabic, media, international and family, café, auditorium, study spaces, conference and exhibition spaces.

  * Energy Modelling
  * Waste Management Consultancy
