### Axiom attorneys helped:
Review 286 contracts and analyze 135 customer relationships in under two weeks of a breach response

Deliver an 89% potential cost savings versus traditional law firm review* 
### Challenge
Following a cybersecurity incident involving a critical technology provider, a US client needed to identify contractual breach notification obligations across **hundreds of agreements**. Retained breach response counsel required a **rapid assessment** of notification requirements — an urgent need for a scalable alternative to manual review.
### Approach
Axiom talent, working with the client's in-house team, combined **legal expertise with AI-powered contract review** to extract notification requirements and key provisions. The team validated AI-generated outputs and delivered structured results — powered by Axiom's **Tech+Talent (T+T)** solution.
### Impact 
  * **90+ attorney hours** avoided, freeing counsel for legal strategy and stakeholder communications.
  * Retained breach response counsel could **quickly assess** notification obligations and act.
  * A **scalable, repeatable model** for future high-volume, time-sensitive reviews.


_*Based on a conservative estimate of 15 minutes per contract to identify, analyze, and document breach notification obligations across 286 agreements._
