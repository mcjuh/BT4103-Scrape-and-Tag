# NHS Net Zero Carbon Strategy
[Back](https://aesg.com/projects/)
AESG was appointed by the NHS Trust to develop a Net Zero Carbon strategy for their assets and wider operations. The project covers 3 major hospitals and involves undertaking detailed carbon calculations, extensive stakeholder engagement activities and the development of a roadmap in alignment with the NHS’ overarching target of reaching Net Zero by 2040.
AESG was appointed by the NHS to complete the following services:
  * Data Collection
  * Carbon Calculations in accordance with GHG protocol
  * Stakeholder Engagement
  * Net Zero Carbon strategy and roadmap development
