# $150 Million Cost Optimization of a Leading Managed Cloud Service Provider
  * February 23, 2024
  * Share


Private Equity Consulting  [Learn more](https://www.fticonsulting.com/industries/private-equity)
When a leading managed cloud service provider was experiencing margin pressure, FTI Consulting helped the company meet its cost optimization target and subsequently supported savings initiatives.
### Our Impact
  * FTI Consulting identified more than $150 million in total run-rate savings opportunities, enabling the client to meet its cost-savings target and implement $100 million in savings in 12 weeks.
  * Our team provided end-to-end assessment and implementation support and ensured value assurance, driving the execution of quick-win initiatives and enhancing cross-functional collaboration.


### Our Role
  * FTI Consulting ran a rapid and comprehensive cost transformation program through a combination of bottom-up and top-down approach.
  * The team assessed the two business units for EBITDA improvement opportunities — with opportunities ranging from customer profitability, services optimization, general and administrative (“G&A”) right-sizing and external spend demand optimization.
  * The team led the implementation of the recommendations in a war-room style effort to ensure that $100 million in savings was implemented within one financial quarter.
