# National Restaurant Chain Abstracts Lease Data From 1,945 Sites in 20 Weeks
[ Learn How ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/resources/case-studies/national-restaurant-chain-abstracts-lease-data-from-1945-sites-20weeks#anchor) [ Discover Tech+Talent ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/solutions/tech-plus-talent)
### Axiom attorneys helped:
Abstract data from 1,945 scanned leases across 31 service categories into one filterable master file 
Deliver in 20 weeks — 74% faster than the 18-month manual baseline* 
Complete the entire project with a two-person Axiom team, output engineered to load directly into the client's database 
### Challenge
A national restaurant chain was standing up a new lease and property database and needed to seed it with **structured, per-site data** drawn from a legacy portfolio of decades-old, non-standard scanned leases from dozens of landlords. For every store, across **31 service categories** , it needed to know who executes the service, who pays, and where the lease says so—information that lived only inside individual PDFs.
### Approach
The full portfolio was ingested into one workspace and OCR'd end to end. Axiom worked **category-first, not lease-first** —building 31 parallel tabular reviews, each running against all 1,945 stores in a single pass. Every category passed a **three-stage attorney-led QC cycle** : a 10-site sample, a validation set to catch drift on non-standard forms, then the full run, normalized to the client's taxonomy.
### Impact 
  * Delivered in **20 weeks** —work the client estimated at 18 months for one paralegal.
  * Obligations moved from **1,945 individual documents** to a single filterable master file.
  * All 31 outputs consolidated into **one workbook** , keyed by store and service type.
  * Engineered to **load directly** into the client's new database.
