### Education

Amit Kumar is a member of Boston Consulting Group's Technology, Media & Telecommunications practice, and the firm's global lead for software. He serves clients across the software, IT services, and high-tech industries, advising them on large-scale transformations, strategy, go-to-market, M&A (integrations and divestitures), and forward-looking topics such as quantum and AI.
Prior to joining BCG, Amit worked for the supply chain and manufacturing group at Unilever, India.
