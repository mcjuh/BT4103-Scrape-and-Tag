## Career Summary
[Curriculum Vitae](https://www.brookesbell.com/fileadmin/uploads/brookesbell/CVs/MOODY_I.pdf)
Ian Moody graduated with a BEng (Hons) in Material Science and Engineering and a PhD in Metallurgy, leaving Imperial College in 1995. He then spent 18 years at British Steel (subsequently called Corus and Tata Steel), the final 12 as the Technical Manager for the International Projects team. There followed 3.5 years as Product Manager for Columbia Metals, a non-ferrous stockholder specialising in copper, copper alloys, stainless steel and nickel alloys and 4.5 years as Conversion Metallurgist at NeoNickel, another stockholder specialising in titanium and nickel-based superalloys. As Product Manager at Columbia Metals, Ian advised on the purchase and sale of copper and nickel alloys worldwide and as the Conversion Metallurgist at NeoNickel, Ian managed the contract processing of nickel and titanium alloys in the UK and Europe.
## Background
During his time at British Steel, Ian was responsible for technically auditing numerous suppliers of steel products (including billets, plates, sections, bars, coils and pipes) throughout Asia and Eastern Europe as well as managing the technical supply of metals to projects across the Middle East, North Africa and South Asia. Ian was responsible for the technical supply conditions for over two million tonnes of pipe sales (including seamless, ERW, SAW and DSAW) and oversaw the production processes from the raw material sourcing (billet, coil or plate) through to finished pipe acceptance.
Has appeared as an expert witness in proceedings at the Commercial Court, London and in arbitration at the Oslo District Court and the Dubai International Arbitration Centre.
Ian joined Brookes Bell as a Metallurgist in March 2022.
