# Claudia Miller
Partner 
Claudia Miller is a Partner in Digital & Technology Transformation. She is a clinical data exchange and regulatory subject matter expert with over 30 years of progressive experience in directing and connecting data between healthcare information technology (IT) in both health system and physician practice environments. Claudia is a key IT change leader for Cures Act Information Blocking requirement readiness, provider relationship and data management, value-based care vendor solution analysis and selection, EHR implementation planning, and PMO. Recent engagements have included leading a successful provider data standardization and key care partner data exchange roadmap and playbook implementation, several Cures Act provider requirements compliance implementations, home health IT strategic planning, biomedical assessment, PMO assessment, EHR planning assessment and merger due diligence.
Prior to joining Chartis, Claudia was the Project Management Office Director of Franciscan Missionaries of Our Lady Health System for three years. She then created a new FMOLHS subsidiary, Franciscan Health and Wellness, a population health management organization that successfully increased quality and decreased cost for over 10,000 self-insured members. The program, Healthy Lives, was subsequently sold to and managed for over nineteen employers in Louisiana. 
Claudia is an active Healthcare Information and Management Systems Society (HIMSS) member and a founding member of the Louisiana HIMSS board chapter. She is a Fellow in the American College of Medical Practice Executives and a Project Management Professional with the Project Management Institute. Claudia has authored many relevant publications and has also presented at local and national HIMSS conferences. 
Claudia received her Master of Business Administration as well as her Bachelor of Arts in computer science from Louisiana State University.

###  What has been your favorite project at Chartis and why? 
My favorite project at Chartis involved a large health system in the northeast that was striving to improve clinical data exchange with their key external care partners. A difficult yet critical component to success is eliminating the use of fax. I led a team to replace archaic faxes with electronic exchange and measured the results of efficient exchange, which ultimately saved time for both patients and providers. 
###  How do your personal goals align to Chartis’ mission and vision? 
My personal goal is to enhance clinical data sharing between providers and patients. As patients, we encounter providers on different electronic health record systems. It should be simpler for each of us to have the clinical data from the other. I am privileged to work for Chartis and help connect these puzzle pieces for our provider organizations to materially improve healthcare.
