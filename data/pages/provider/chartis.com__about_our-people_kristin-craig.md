# Kristin Craig
Partner 
Kristin Craig is a Partner in Strategic Transformation at Chartis. She brings 15 years of healthcare and consulting experience working with a broad range of organizations, including multi-region health systems and IDNs, nationally ranked academic medical centers (AMCs), community hospitals and medical schools, and regional non-profit payers. She specializes in long-range enterprise strategy and planning, with expertise in driving sustainable growth, business model innovation, and collaborative partnerships. 
Prior to joining Chartis, Kristin advised healthcare executives at over two dozen organizations as a Director with PwC’s health strategy practice. Representative work included cost transformation and growth strategy for a national IDN, enterprise strategy and capability development for a leading AMC, and partnership facilitation and strategic diligence for high-profile healthcare transactions. Earlier in her career, she worked in health care policy and philanthropy with the Peter G. Peterson Foundation, where she helped launch a new center focused on accelerating value and innovation in the US healthcare system.
Kristin is also a published thought leader whose work has appeared in leading industry publications. Her writing explores topics such as the future of healthcare delivery, value-based care, and innovation in home-based care models.
Kristin received her MBA with honors from the University of Chicago’s Booth School of Business, and her BA from Columbia University.

###  Why healthcare? 
I’ve always been drawn to public service and work that makes a meaningful impact. Early in my career, I was working in economic and healthcare policy in the lead-up to the 2010 Affordable Care Act. It was an energizing time, a moment that felt like a once-in-a-generation opportunity to “bend the cost curve” and drive greater value across this complex system. I was quickly hooked. Since then, I’ve dedicated my career to working with healthcare organizations on the front lines of this transformation, helping shape a more sustainable and equitable future for the industry. 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
The most rewarding part of my career has been the power of solving complex problems as a team. I’ve always believed that 1+1>2 — that the best ideas emerge when people with different perspectives come together around a shared goal. There’s nothing more fulfilling than seeing that collective energy turn into solutions that make a real difference for our clients and the communities they serve. 
###  What is something most people would be surprised to learn about you? 
I’m a certified Hatha yoga instructor and have been practicing the same sequence of postures for more than 20 years. The consistency of the practice has become an anchor in my life, and a reminder of the power of discipline, presence, and the mind-body connection.
