# Andy Murphy
Partner 
Andy Murphy is a Partner in Performance Transformation at Chartis. He has almost 15 years of experience in Supply Chain Management for healthcare organizations. Andy specializes in supply and purchased service cost reduction, supply chain structure and process design/improvement, strategic sourcing, clinical and non-clinical product/service conversions, value analysis, inventory management, and data governance. During his time with Chartis, Andy has led performance improvement engagements involving supply and purchased service expense reduction, strategic contracting, GPO selection, data management and analytics, shared services, value analysis and clinical conversions. He has helped clients save tens of millions of dollars across all areas of supply and purchased services expense, while maintaining or improving operational efficiency, patient safety, and stakeholder satisfaction. These outcomes led to increased customer/clinician satisfaction, reduced inventory and operational expense, as well as standardization of supplies and purchased services. In addition to operational improvement and cost reduction-focused projects, Andy has served as a Supply Chain Subject Matter Expert on growth-focused engagements related to Hospital @ Home, Perioperative efficiency, mergers/integrations, and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems. Prior to joining Chartis, he worked for the Health Solutions division of FTI Consulting and The Resource Group of Ascension Health. During his time with FTI, Andy served as project manager and subject matter expert for engagements that saved clients tens of millions of dollars in annual supply and purchased service expense, while improving the efficiency of their supply chain operations. During his time with The Resource Group, he established and led interdisciplinary value analysis teams to standardize supply utilization between departments and facilities, resulting in less variation of practice as well as substantial cost savings. He managed clinical supply and service conversions ranging from surgeon gloves to coronary stents and specialty bed rentals. Andy earned a certification in the Supply Chain Operational Reference Model (SCOR-S) as an undergraduate, and a Lean/Six Sigma Green Belt Certification in 2019.
Andy holds a BSB in Supply Chain Management from Miami University in Oxford, OH and an MBA in Finance from The Quinlan School of Business, Loyola University Chicago.

###  Why did you pick Chartis? 
I chose to work at Chartis because of the firm’s healthcare-focused mission, reputation, and trajectory. I stay at Chartis because of the people, the culture, and the ability to make a broad impact with healthcare organizations across the country. 
###  Best piece of advice you’ve ever been given? 
When I started my career as an analyst, I was told that a good analyst accurately answers the question they have been asked, but a great analyst will figure out what the next question would be, and answer that question as well. I try to apply this lesson to all of my work – keep asking questions and digging further until we have meaningful insights that help clients make good decisions.
