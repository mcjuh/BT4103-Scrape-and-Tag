Rajan Prasanna is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Restructuring & Turnaround in Bangalore. With more than 20 years of corporate leadership and consulting experience in India, the UAE, and the US, Mr. Prasanna advises clients on operational and financial turnaround, business model redesign, and performance improvement initiatives.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Prasanna held multiple strategic roles at Flipkart Group. Most notably, he served as Senior Vice President of strategy and analytics at Myntra, where he successfully led multiple new business and cost transformation programs, helping Myntra achieve market-leading growth and profitability.Before Flipkart, he led the customer excellence charter at Emaar Properties Group in the UAE, handling all post-sales operations for the real estate portfolio. In addition, Mr. Prasanna previously served as Associate Principal at Bain & Company, where he advised global clients across the industrial, automotive, and consumer retail sectors on strategy, customer centricity, sales acceleration, and operational turnarounds.Mr. Prasanna earned an undergraduate degree from Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, a master’s degree in electrical engineering from Ulm University, Germany, and an MBA from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology Sloan School of Management.
