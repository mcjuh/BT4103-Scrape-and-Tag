## Executive summary
Alex is a Partner in the Advisory practice in Phoenix. Alex focuses on fraud investigations, complex international engagements, litigation support consulting, and developing data analytics solutions to identify emerging risks for organizations. He has deep expertise in forensic accounting principles, asset tracing techniques, the real estate and construction industry, and anti-bribery/anti-corruption compliance principles.
