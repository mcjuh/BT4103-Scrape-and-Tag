## Michael Koenig
#### Managing Director
Mr. Koenig is a former senior-level Department of Justice prosecutor and civil trial attorney with many years of experience leading complex domestic and international investigations across numerous industries. He leverages his experience, coupled with his deep familiarity with DOJ and other agency processes and priorities, to provide clients with robust and dependable investigative, monitoring, and related services.
Washington, DC
[+1 202 223 4700](tel:+1%20202%20223%204700) mkoenig@secretariat-intl.com
Michael Koenig draws on a wealth of knowledge and know-how from his years of service as a senior prosecutor and trial attorney at the Department of Justice to conduct time-sensitive, high-stakes investigations relating to criminal and civil antitrust matters, as well as white-collar fraud, often coordinating with domestic and foreign law-enforcement and regulatory entities. He has led numerous complex domestic and international criminal cartel investigations, and investigated and litigated merger and civil conduct relating to antitrust. He has also supervised corporate monitors and mediated disputes between monitors and monitored entities.
Mr. Koenig’s experience encompasses numerous industries, including agriculture, chemical manufacturing, electronics manufacturing, financial products, healthcare, international shipping, consumer products, real estate, forestry products, healthcare, insurance, and software engineering.
Mr. Koenig received the following distinctions for his DOJ service:
  * Attorney General’s Award for Distinguished Service (2014)
  * Federal Bureau of Investigation’s Washington Field Office Award for Outstanding Service (2019, 2016)
  * Criminal Division’s Assistant Attorney General Exceptional Service Award (2016)
  * Antitrust Division’s Assistant Attorney General Exceptional Service Award (2022, 2020, 2014)
