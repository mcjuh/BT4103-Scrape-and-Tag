### Education
  * MBA, Mercer University
  * BA, finance, Roanoke College


Kristy Ellmer is a global authority on transformation and a former Chief Transformation Officer. She has led numerous organizations around the world, in dozens of industries including energy, media, retail, and banking, through periods of profound change.
Kristy leads Boston Consulting Group's Transform practice in North America, and cofounded the firm’s Behavioral Science Lab. A leader emeritus of BCG’s global culture and change management work, she combines her data-driven insight with a deep understanding of human behavior to advise boards and C-suite executives on unlocking resilience, inspiring growth, and turning transformation into lasting outcomes. She has delivered billions of dollars of value and believes transformations provide an opportunity for leaders and organizations to thrive. Kristy is a TED speaker and coauthor of numerous publications.
