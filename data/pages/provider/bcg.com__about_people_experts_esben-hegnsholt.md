### Education
  * MPhil, University of Cambridge
  * MSc, Copenhagen Business School


Esben Hegnsholt is a leader in Boston Consulting Group's Energy Practice. He works with leading companies on strategy, capital project excellence, energy transition, and end-to-end value creation programs—with a strong emphasis on technology-driven improvement.
Esben leads BCG’s Power and Utilities Sector for Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and South America, as well as the low-carbon energy topic globally, working on the build-out of new energy businesses. He has extensive experience across energy and resource-intensive industries, including power and utilities, oil and gas, transportation, infrastructure, and mining and metals.
