## Executive summary
Greg Haberer is a partner in Grant Thornton’s Risk Advisory practice and serves as the firm’s National Lead for Risk Advisory Technology Enablement. He is the executive sponsor of CompliAI, Grant Thornton’s platform-enabled approach for modernizing SOX and internal control programs, internal audit, and enterprise risk management (ERM). Greg also leads the firm’s strategic alliances across the risk and compliance technology ecosystem—including AuditBoard, Delinea, Zania, and Fieldguide—helping organizations adopt integrated solutions that improve scale, transparency, and efficiency.
With nearly 20 years of consulting experience and advanced credentials spanning AI governance, project management, agile methodologies, and data science, Greg advises CFOs, CAEs, CISOs, and Audit Committees on AI-enabled assurance and practical, defensible AI Governance operating models. His work reflects a deep understanding of structured delivery, adaptive frameworks, and data-driven insights, enabling organizations to increase real-time visibility, reduce manual effort, and enhance assurance quality through automation, analytics, and modern program design.
Greg has served clients in the technology, healthcare, utilities, retail, financial services, media, telecommunications, and public sectors. Before joining Grant Thornton, he was with Accenture, where he managed enterprise technology, business application, and analytics implementations for both commercial and government clients. He remains committed to continuous learning and advancing industry leadership through innovation, governance excellence, and transformative risk management practices.
Prior to joining Grant Thornton, Greg worked at Accenture delivering Oracle business application and analytics implementations for public commercial and government clients.
Greg has experience providing risk advisory and technology implementation professional services for technology, healthcare, utility, retail, financial services, media, telecommunications, and public sector organizations.
