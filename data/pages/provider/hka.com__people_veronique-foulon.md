### Expertise
Veronique Foulon has over 15 years of experience in business intelligence, due diligence, risk management, and relevant roles.
Veronique has assisted in arbitration proceedings for complex global investigations. She has supported law firms in preparing investigative dossiers for submission to the UK Serious Fraud Office relating to fraud and embezzlement cases.
Veronique’s expertise includes Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) investigations, commercial arbitration, and asset tracing for clients in the aerospace, shipping, and manufacturing industries. She has led numerous pre-transaction due diligence, fraud, and litigation support investigations regarding political corruption, money laundering, and sanction circumvention.
### Background
Veronique has advised clients about managing third-party money laundering, corruption, and ESG risks. She has experience in various sectors, including manufacturing, construction & infrastructure, energy, banking, telecommunications, and hospitality.
Veronique is a member of the Association of Certified Anti-Money Laundering Specialists, the Association of Corporate Investigators, and the Women in White Collar Crime Defense Association (London chapter). She is fluent in English and French and is research proficient in German, Italian, Greek, and Russian. ​
