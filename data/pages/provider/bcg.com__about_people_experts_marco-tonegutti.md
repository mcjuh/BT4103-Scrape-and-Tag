### Education
  * MBA, cum laude, SDA Bocconi School of Management
  * International MBA exchange program, London Business School
  * MSc, management engineering, Polytechnic University of Turin


Marco Tonegutti joined Boston Consulting Group in 2005. He is the regional leader of the Energy practice for Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and South America. He also leads the Climate & Sustainability practice for the East Med and Caspian.
Marco has served many international, national, and independent energy and industrial companies across Europe, the Middle East, Asia and Africa.
In the field of oil and gas and energy EPC, Marco has worked on many strategic transformation, decarbonization, and diagnostic projects around the world. In the field of refining and petrochemicals, Marco has participated in several major transformation programs for different clients, including digital transformation, operational excellence, energy efficiency, and asset management.
Marco has also completed many client assignments to define corporate low-carbon portfolio strategies for energy players, specifically in waste-to-energy, CCUS, hydrogen, onshore wind, and photovoltaic technologies.
Before joining BCG, Marco was a consultant at two boutique consulting companies, where he developed organization and management accounting projects in the retail and fashion industries.
