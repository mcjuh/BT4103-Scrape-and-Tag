# Kristin Kellas
Partner, HealthScape Advisors 
Kristin Kellas is a Partner at HealthScape Advisors, a Chartis Company, and co-leads the Analytics and Technology team. She has dedicated her career to helping clients across the healthcare ecosystem achieve near-term and long-term goals.
As a leader of HealthScape's analytics and technology team, Kristin oversees ideation, development, and deployment of analytic approaches to deliver data-driven insights and strategic recommendations to clients. Her consulting experience includes extensive experience in strategic planning, data management, business transformation, operational improvement, transaction advisory, and government program contract capture experience.
Kristin began and has continued her career at HealthScape Advisors after earning her bachelor's degree from the University of Illinois.

###  Why did you pick HealthScape Advisors? 
I chose to and continue to choose to work for HealthScape Advisors because of the meaningful work we do, alongside talented people. I really enjoy the challenge the healthcare industry brings, and I’m energized by the opportunity to help drive change and make an impact within a company that continues to grow and evolve. HealthScape is a place where I feel challenged, valued, and inspired! 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
The most rewarding part of my career has been helping clients solve complex problems and seeing the impact of those solutions. Equally fulfilling is supporting and mentoring the people around me—supporting team members grow in their own careers and succeed is incredibly meaningful. 
###  What inspires you? 
What inspires me most is seeing and experiencing others live out their passions. There’s something incredibly motivating about watching people pour their heart into what they love—it reminds me of what’s possible when we’re aligned with purpose and driven by joy.
