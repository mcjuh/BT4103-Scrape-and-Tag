## Executive summary
Andrew will support the team with detailed and in-depth knowledge of the audit process as it relates to financial services companies.
Andrew has worked in public accounting for over eight years and is a partner in our audit practice. He has spent his career serving a wide variety of financial services clients, including community banks, mortgage banks, real estate investment trusts, and specialty finance lenders. He has also worked in internal audit roles with large banks, and provides other attestation services to his audit clients, including under Regulation AB and HUD rules.
Andrew has experience with publicly traded, private-equity funded and privately-held clients with domestic and international operations. He also spent two years working on international assignment with the GT member firm in Hamburg, Germany.
