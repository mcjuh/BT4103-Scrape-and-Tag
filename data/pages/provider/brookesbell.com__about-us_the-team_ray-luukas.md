## Career Summary
[Curriculum Vitae](https://www.brookesbell.com/fileadmin/uploads/brookesbell/CVs/LUUKAS_CV.pdf)
Ray Luukas is a chartered engineer with a First-Class Certificate of Competency and a BEng (Hons) Mechanical Engineering degree, First Class. He joined Brookes Bell in 1992 and became Equity Partner in 1997 and Senior Partner in 2007. Ray became Chief Technical Officer in 2018 and Consultant Engineer in 2024. Ray is also a Supporting Member of the London Maritime Arbitrators Association (LMAA).
## Background
His main surveying and consultancy experience is in the field of hull and machinery surveys, including hull structural failures, main and auxiliary engine damages (crankcase explosions, scavenge fires, crankshaft/bearing failures, and contamination of fuel and lube oil systems). This has included intensive involvement in major casualties, including collision, stranding, grounding, salvage and wreck removal, engine room flooding and fires, and total losses. Ray also has experience in marine electrical installation problems, both power generation and distribution.
Major salvage and wreck removal cases have included MSC NAPOLI and RIVERDANCE off the UK coast during 2008, in which Brookes Bell acted as project manager and supervisor for owners/insurers. Ray prepared ITTs, tender analysis and presentations to insurers and UK Government SOS rep (Secretary of State Representative) and Environmental Agency. In 2015, Ray was also involved with the wreck removal of GOODFAITH from Andros Island, Greece.
He also provides advice on a range contractual disputes (charter parties; sale and purchase contracts; new build contracts, etc.) involving hull and machinery issues, crane failures, new building and repair disputes, sale and purchase disputes, bunker disputes, maintenance and management disputes, redelivery (Bare Boat) disputes and damage to project cargo. 
During his time as a surveyor he has worked on many ship types, including general cargo vessels, bulk carriers, OBOs, container ships, ro-ro vessels, reefers, tankers, FSOs and FPSOs. Ray has particular experience in disputes involving high-speed craft (mono-hull and multi-hull types) and high-speed propulsion units/waterjets. 
Ray has also investigated machinery damage and fires to, and total losses by sinking of, superyachts and megayachts, new build and sale and purchase disputes.
He has prepared technical reports and has given evidence as an expert witness at arbitrations and court hearings in the UK, Germany, Norway, Denmark, Japan and USA. Ray has also participated in Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) schemes including Mediations and has acted as a 3rd Expert in Neutral Evaluations. Ray has also been appointed as Third Arbitrator for LMAA arbitration.
Before becoming a marine surveyor, Ray Luukas was at sea over a 14-year period, serving in general cargo vessels, container ships, bulk carriers, VLCC and LNG tankers in all ranks up to and including chief engineer. During 1988/89, he also carried out superintendency duties, including dockings and sale/purchase inspections. While working offshore in the North Sea, he was chief engineer of a heavy-lift crane barge.
Since the acquisition of Neale Consulting Engineers, Ray has also worked on, and overseen, land-based projects organising multi-discipline teams (mechanical engineers, tribologists, metallurgists, etc.) to investigate and provide RCA for power station turbine generator failure, swing-bridge bearing failures, large lock gate problems (Panama Canal) and offshore assets (FPSO and FSO).
