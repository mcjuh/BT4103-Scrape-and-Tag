### Education
  * MSc, modern Chinese studies, University of Oxford
  * BS, University of Wisconsin, Madison


Ed Newman leads Boston Consulting Group’s Corporate Strategy business in North America, as well as the private equity and M&A team in the Midwest. He is a core member of BCG's Principal Investors & Private Equity (PIPE), Corporate Finance & Strategy (CFS), and Industrial Goods (IG) practices. Ed has over a decade of experience supporting clients on corporate strategy, portfolio strategy, due diligence, M&A, and shareholder value engagements.
Prior to joining BCG, Ed worked at a fundamentals-driven, long/short equity hedge fund.
