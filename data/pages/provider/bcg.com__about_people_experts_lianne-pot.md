### Education
  * MBA, Harvard Business School
  * Masters in International Business, University of Maastricht


Lianne Pot is an expert on postmerger integrations and transformations at Boston Consulting Group, and a global topic leader on PMI in technology for the firm. She is also a topic leader in the media sector, especially the music industry. Lianne has deep experience in operating model, organization and talent, and culture and change management. She has worked with clients in overseeing overall IMO.
As an expert in PMI and transformations, Lianne works with clients across industries and has deep expertise in the media and technology industries. In addition, she supports media and technology clients on a broader set of topics including strategy, go-to-market transformations, and organizational design.
