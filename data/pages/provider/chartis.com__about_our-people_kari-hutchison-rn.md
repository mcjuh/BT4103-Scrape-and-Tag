# Kari Hutchison, RN
Partner 
Kari Hutchison is a Partner in Digital & Technology Transformation. She has over 20 years of healthcare experience in the vendor, provider, and consulting arenas. She has an accomplished history leading multiple enterprise EHR implementations across a wide range of healthcare organizations including large networks, academic institutions, and pediatric environments. She is seasoned in program leadership, communications, healthcare change management, and IT service management. 
Kari recently served as program director for planning through the enterprise system implementation at a pediatric organization celebrating high adoption rates and user satisfaction at go-live. She continued with the organization post live to establish a governance structure, project intake process, and continue cross-department collaboration for maximized results.
In addition to her EHR implementation experience, Kari has a deep interest in IT enablement. She is passionate about ensuring an organization has the optimal mix of IT solutions to enable operations most efficiently, and creating an IT operation to ensure those solutions are seamlessly built, tested, and communicated creating the best experience for all stakeholders.
Prior to joining Chartis, Kari was a Vice President at Impact Advisors in the Implementation practice and led the Implementation Change Management methodology.
Kari holds degrees in nursing from Marquette University and Organizational Communication and Public Relations from Michigan State University. She is currently certified in several Epic applications, holds her ITIL Fundamentals certification, and a Healthcare Change Management certification from Cornell.

###  Why healthcare? 
I have always held a deep interest in healthcare. Healthcare so prevalently impacts our entire community deeply and routinely, in large ways and small. The potential for improvement in healthcare is vast and each improvement has the opportunity to benefit us all in a significant way throughout our entire life. I can’t imagine working in another field. 
###  How do your personal goals align to Chartis’ mission and vision? 
I have a passion for the mission of healthcare, and I believe it can be made better. We’re perfectly aligned! My professional goal is to continue to expand my knowledge of Healthcare IT solutions, as well as to continually evolve my understanding in the challenges healthcare systems face. With that knowledge, I strive to help organizations find the very best and most efficient mix of those IT solutions to enable and maximize the impact to clinicians, caregivers, and patients and to help deliver those as seamlessly as possible. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
If I’m not at one of my sons’ sporting events, I’m probably planning our next family trip. I enjoy exploring new places, being out of my comfort zone, and having to reset. I also love coming home!
