#  Jasmeet Gill 
##  Principal | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
Jasmeet Gill leads Deloitte’s Insurance Core Systems Transformations practice. He is recognized for driving complex, high-impact, technology-enabled, strategic initiatives at some of the largest insurance organizations in the world. His area of focus is around technology-enabled business transformations, including core insurance operations and systems, data and analytics, and digital enablement. Gill has a proven track record of serving as a trusted advisor to client executives, and successfully partnering with business and IT teams to deliver end-to-end technology advisory to implementation services. He has deep experience with business alignment, business value, technology investment decisions, business process design, technology planning, and technology development and support.
