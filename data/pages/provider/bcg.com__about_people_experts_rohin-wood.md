### Education
  * PhD, control systems engineering (aerospace), The University of Adelaide
  * BEng, mechatronic engineering, The University of Adelaide


Rohin Wood joined Boston Consulting Group in 2014. He leads BCG X in ANZ, the firm's tech build and design business unit, for the Perth office and co-leads the BCG X Data Science team in Asia Pacific. He is also the global leader for mathematical optimization. His experience covers range of industries, including mining and metals, transportation, aerospace, telecommunications, defense, and financial services. He brings specialist knowledge in mathematical and computational modeling, statistical analysis, and optimization to his work with clients.
Rohin’s current focus is on applying analytics and artificial intelligence to optimize operations and unlock value in resources businesses, particularly mining and metals.
Prior to joining BCG in 2014, Rohin was the principal for analytics at TSG Consulting and the practice leader for analytics at Sinclair Knight Merz.
