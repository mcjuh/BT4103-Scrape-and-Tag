Jimmy Feng is a core member of Boston Consulting Group's Technology, Media & Telecom practice. He specializes in consumer electronics sales and marketing, augmented reality and virtual reality (VR/AR), strategic planning, and product development. 
Jimmy has worked for clients in a variety of technology industries including consumer electronics companies, telecom operators, telco equipment manufacturers, and industrial equipment manufacturers, as well as in private equity. In his recent engagements, he has developed a digital health care strategy for a leading telco in Taiwan, including market scanning for potential JV partners; and identified key cost and growth levers for Taiwan's incumbent telco's B2B business. 
Before Jimmy joined the firm in 2011, he was a software engineer and software project manager for five years with Motorola. He left BCG between 2014 and 2017 to join HTC’s VR business as head of APAC.
