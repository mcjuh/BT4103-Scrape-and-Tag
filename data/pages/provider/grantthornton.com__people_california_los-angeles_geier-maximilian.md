#  Maximilian Geier, CPA 
Partner, Risk Advisory Services Grant Thornton Advisors LLC 
Los Angeles, CA
[ +1 213 596 6727 ](tel:12135966727) [ +1 213 596 6727 ](https://www.grantthornton.com/bin/grantthornton/vcard.vcf?cfPath=/content/dam/grantthornton/website/people-fragments/california/los-angeles/maximilian-geier)
## Executive summary
Max is a Partner in Grant Thornton’s southern California Advisory practice with nearly 20 years of professional management consulting experience focused on IPO readiness, internal audit, Sarbanes-Oxley, business process transformation, IT audit, and regulatory compliance.
Rooted in the fundamentals of financial accounting, Max’s experience covers the full range of activities associate with the implementation of an internal audit program and Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance. His expertise is focused on pre-IPO and year-1 public companies, where initial creation of process documentation and resolution of control maters are critical. He provides program leadership and oversight of SOX compliance services covering all phases of work from design evaluation walkthroughs to year-end issuance and certification – all conforming with the latest PCAOB requirements and preferences of the external audit firm.
As a specialist focusing on first-year adoptions and SOX implementation, Max leads engagements with a consultative mindset helping organizations navigate through what is often the most challenging year. Through leading training sessions and one-on-one education and direct assistance, Max supports control owners as they begin to adopt a new way of working.
Utilizing the same consultative approach, Max helps public companies clear existing Material Weaknesses by advising on the deployment and enhancement of internal controls.
Max has broad experience with many popular business and enterprise applications, including Oracle, Peoplesoft, SAP, SQL, Windows Active Directory, and SAAS platforms. He assists organizations in identifying, enhancing, and testing internal controls over enterprise applications. Max’s experience in IT also includes performing IT control testing for Grant Thornton’s external audit clients, including private companies and SEC registrants.
