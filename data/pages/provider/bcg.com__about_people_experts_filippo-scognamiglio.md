### Honors and Awards
  * NOVA - Italian MBA Association, Former President


Filippo Scognamiglio is a lead member of Boston Consulting Group’s Tech & Digital Advantage and Financial Institutions practices areas. In his role, he has been advising technology leaders across financial institutions for over 20 years. He serves as the Functional Leader for Development and Commercialization at BCG Henderson Institute, BCG’s global think tank.
Filippo also runs BCG's Global Cloud Advisory Business, where he has been driving the firm's growth in supporting clients as they transform into efficient cloud-enabled organizations.
Prior to joining the firm, Filippo was a consultant at Roland Berger.
