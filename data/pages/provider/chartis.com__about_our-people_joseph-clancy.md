# Joseph Clancy
Partner 
Joseph Clancy is a Partner in Performance Transformation at Chartis. Joe brings nearly 25 years of experience to the firm leading and advising health systems and community hospitals. His expertise spans operations improvement, health system integration, physician integration, business transformation, and market share growth. Before transitioning to consulting, Joe spent 13 years as a hospital CEO and COO, where his leadership drove long-term growth, process efficiency, and overall excellence in quality, finance, and operations. Joe’s deep understanding and strategic insight enable him to craft tailored solutions that address both common challenges and the unique needs of his clients.
Prior to joining Chartis, Joe served as a Director in the Healthcare Operations practice at Guidehouse, where he led large-scale performance improvement initiatives. In this role, he focused on closing gaps in operational platforms and restructuring internal workflows and infrastructure to secure lasting improvements and organizational alignment. Prior to that, Joe was a Manager in the Healthcare Mergers & Acquisitions practice at PwC, where he guided organizations through the entire M&A continuum, from developing partnership strategies to executing post-merger integration plans.
Joe holds a Master of Health Administration from the Medical College of Virginia Campus at Virginia Commonwealth University and a Bachelor of Arts in health & society from the University of Rochester.

###  What do you hope to help our clients achieve? 
In today’s competitive and highly regulated environment, performance improvement is crucial for adapting to evolving patient needs, delivering high-quality care, addressing disparities in care, and ensuring long-term sustainability. I aim to help our clients identify and eliminate inefficiencies, streamline processes, and enhance patient outcomes. By emphasizing continuous improvement, our clients can better manage costs, reduce waste, and improve patient access and satisfaction. Ultimately, these efforts lead to more effective and patient-centered healthcare. 
###  One Item from your bucket list? 
Traversing the Haute Route from Chamonix in France to Zermatt in Switzerland, one of the most iconic long-distance hikes in the Alps, is a bucket list adventure for my wife and me. 
###  Best piece of advice you’ve ever been given? 
The best piece of advice I've ever received came from my teenage daughter, who shared with me the mantra she lives by: 'Do not wait for opportunity, create it.' This simple yet powerful statement has shaped my approach to both personal and professional challenges as I’ve gotten older, reminding me to take initiative, be proactive, and actively pursue the outcomes I desire rather than passively waiting for them to happen.
