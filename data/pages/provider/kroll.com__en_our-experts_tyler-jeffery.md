AT A GLANCE 
Tyler Jeffery is Director of Client Success within the Cyber and Data Resilience business, based in Raleigh, NC.
Tyler leverages over ten years of experience in breach response, finance, management and forecasting to deliver strategic vision and management for Kroll’s global Breach Notification Engagement Team. He leads a team of over 15 engagement and operations managers who are responsible for facilitating data breach response and support for single to multimillion-person events.
Before this, Tyler served as Senior Vice President of Business and Financial Analysis in Kroll’s Breach Notification business, where he undertook business and financial analysis to support the development of strategies to maintain growth, while establishing new processes for efficiency and long-term success. Tyler previously held roles as Vice President of Business and Financial Analysis and Engagement and Operations Manager within Kroll’s Breach Notification business.
Prior to joining Kroll in 2019, he served as a financial analyst at fintech company, StrategyCorps and as Originations Team Leader for the Ford Motor Credit Company.
Tyler holds a Master of Business Administration from the University of North Carolina with a Bachelor of Science in economics from Belmont University.
