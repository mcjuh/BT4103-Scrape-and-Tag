Andrej Levin is the head of BCG X, Boston Consulting Group's tech build and design unit, in Central Europe, where he leads AI-driven transformations for major corporations. He is the founder and global leader of the firm's Auto.AI product, supporting automotive companies in developing and scaling cutting-edge AI solutions.
Andrej has written numerous high-impact publications for BCG, as well as the industry and academia at large, teaches “Value Creation with AI” at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), and leads the BCG x TUM AI Strategy Research Cluster. He holds AI patents for AI-driven inventory management systems and an AI market intelligence engine.
