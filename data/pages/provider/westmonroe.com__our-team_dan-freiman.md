As Chief Financial Officer, Dan guides investment strategies that enhance West Monroe’s services while improving processes and leveraging automation to create measurable impact. With nearly two decades of experience in international finance, including as CFO of a public wireless communications business in Latin America, Dan brings a global perspective to his role. 
Before joining West Monroe, Dan was CFO of JSI and NII Holdings, Inc. He has a B.S. in accounting and an MBA in finance from the University of Maryland. Treasury and Risk Magazine previously recognized him as one of the top 40 business leaders under 40. 
## _Dan’s All-In Mindset_
A true business partner, Dan empowers his team to deliver exceptional client experiences. Outside of work, you’ll find him cheering on Washington sports teams, playing tennis, or traveling with his family. His career has taken him throughout Latin America, where he built strong cross-cultural connections.
