Ted Scott is a licensed civil engineer with more than 25 years of experience in the construction industry, specializing in dispute resolution, delay and disruption analyses, cost overruns, scheduling, and project controls. His experience spans a variety of large-scale infrastructure and commercial projects, including airports, casinos, education, high-rise commercial/residential, highways and bridges, hospitals, hotels, judicial, oil & gas, ports, power, rail, stadiums, tunnels, and water/wastewater treatment facilities.
Mr. Scott has been appointed as an expert on numerous disputes in Asia, Australia, Europe, North America, and the Middle East. He has testified in the United States Federal and State Courts and arbitrations (under ICC, AAA, and JAMS rules) as an independent expert on scheduling, project delay, loss of productivity, and quantum matters
Mr. Scott is an author and frequent speaker on a variety of construction-related topics. He also serves on the American Society of Civil Engineers’ committee to develop of a national consensus standard governing the best practice for conducting CPM schedule delay analysis in construction. He is also an officer of the Society of Construction Law North America. Mr. Scott also serves as the Society of Construction Law’s Southwest Co-Regional Director.
Before working in the consulting field, Mr. Scott worked as a civil engineer and scheduler on several large highway and bridge projects gaining hands‐on experience from the ground up.
