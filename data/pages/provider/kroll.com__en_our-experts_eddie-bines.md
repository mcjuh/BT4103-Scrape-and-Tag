AT A GLANCE 
Eddie Bines is a Managing Director in Kroll’s Restructuring practice, based in London. 
He has over 30 years of experience advising clients on restructuring and exit strategies, with a focus on delivering value through efficient legal entity rationalization and managed exits.
Eddie specialises in:
  * **Legal entity rationalization** : Streamlining global structures to reduce cost and complexity
  * **Managed exits:** Supporting clients in exiting non-core jurisdictions or operations
  * **Entity elimination:** Returning value to shareholders and creditors through appropriate processes


He has advised listed groups, private companies and owner-managed businesses across sectors including financial services, technology, real estate, aviation, hospitality, retail and manufacturing.
Eddie’s notable assignments include:
  * Leading a global entity rationalization for a U.S.-based technology group going through a Chapter 11 reorganization
  * Managing the UK exit for a subsidiary of a Hong Kong-listed financial services group; simplifying the structure of a Fortune 500 food processing group, dissolving 180+ entities in 18 months
  * Acting in numerous liquidations, returning over £100m to shareholders in a tax-efficient manner
  * Numerous liquidations returning capital totaling more than £100 million to shareholders tax effectively


Prior to joining Kroll, Eddie held senior roles at leading top four firms. He is a licensed Insolvency Practitioner and a fellow of the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales.
