Paul Aversano is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal in New York. He is the Global Practice Leader of A&M’s Global Transaction Advisory Group and maintains oversight of the firm’s operations in Japan and US Corporate Transactions Group.
For over 30 years, Mr. Aversano has led both buy- and sell-side financial and accounting due diligence projects for complex public and private company transactions, as well as transactions in the capital markets. He specializes in assessing quality of earnings and GAAP/IFRS accounting, providing accounting and transaction structuring advice for leveraged buyouts, purchase accounting, and Securities and Exchange Commission reporting.
Mr. Aversano works to deliver A&M’s services to clients globally. He leads the firm’s efforts in delivering cross-border services to private equity firms, sovereign wealth funds, and family offices, as well as to corporate and strategic buyers around the world across a wide spectrum of industries.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Aversano was a partner with EY in New York and Director of Middle Market Private Equity. He led financial accounting due diligence projects for private equity investor groups as well as the development and coordination of the firm’s go-to-market and service delivery strategies focused on the private equity market. Previously, he was with Arthur Andersen’s Transaction Advisory Services and Audit practices in New York.
Mr. Aversano earned a bachelor’s degree in accounting from Binghamton University. He is a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) in New York and a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA), the New York State Society of CPAs, and the Association of Certified Fraud Examiners. He serves on the board of directors and audit committees of both the New York Police and Fire Widows’ and Children’s Benefit Fund (Answer the Call) and PowerPlay NYC, as well as the advisory board of the University of Michigan’s Ross School of Business’ (“Ross”) Sanger Leadership Center. In addition, Mr. Aversano represents A&M on the planning committee of Ross’ Private Equity and Alternative Investments Summit. Mr. Aversano is a sought-after guest lecturer at top universities including the University of Michigan, the University of Pennsylvania and Binghamton University, as well as a leading industry voice who has frequented such broadcast channels as Bloomberg and FOX Business. He is also the recipient of numerous awards for his industry stewardship, including The M&A Advisor’s Leadership Award and Hall of Fame honor in 2024 and its Service Professional of the Year in 2015.
