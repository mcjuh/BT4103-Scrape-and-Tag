### Education
  * PhD, engineering and physical biology, Harvard University
  * MA, biochemistry, Harvard University
  * BS, biology, Massachusetts Institute of Technology


John Wu brings his deep knowledge of biology and his background in biomedical science to the Health Care practice of Boston Consulting Group. He has extensive experience working with clients in the biopharmaceuticals sector. John is interested in reshaping the health care ecosystem through technology and business model innovation. 
John’s experience covers a diverse set of topics within the health care space, including strategy, corporate development, clinical development and operations, and large-scale transformations. As a lead member of the Health Care practice, John helps his biopharma clients create value with better strategic choices, more efficient operating models, and more streamlined and engaged organizations.
