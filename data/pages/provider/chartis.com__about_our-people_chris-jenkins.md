# Chris Jenkins
Senior Partner 
Chris Jenkins is a Senior Partner in Digital and Technology Transformation. He is an innovative and results-driven healthcare technology executive with 30 years of experience in information systems leadership, operations, and strategic planning. He has served in CIO and CTO roles within large, complex health systems, leading multi-year technology strategies and enterprise-wide transformation initiatives. Chris has directed major programs including enterprise EHR deployments, IT security enhancements, technology infrastructure modernizations, and integrations of new and acquired hospitals. His collaborative leadership and deep technical acumen have consistently improved operational performance and advanced digital capabilities across the organizations he has served.
Prior to joining Chartis, Chris co-founded Healthlink Advisors and served as Executive Vice President of Digital Strategy and Enterprise Imaging, where he led advisory services focused on digital transformation and enterprise imaging strategy for healthcare organizations. During his nine-year tenure, he also held roles as Senior Vice President of Advisory Services and Vice President, guiding clients in technology modernization, IT operations improvement, and strategic planning. Before joining Healthlink Advisors, Chris spent 20 years at BayCare Health System, advancing from Technical Project Manager to Vice President of Infrastructure and CTO, and later serving as Interim CIO. In these roles, he oversaw technology operations supporting more than 25,000 users, led enterprise architecture and cybersecurity initiatives, managed multimillion-dollar budgets, and directed Cerner EMR implementation and infrastructure modernization for a multibillion-dollar health system.
Chris is an established thought leader and respected voice in healthcare IT and digital transformation. He has contributed to Perspectives by Healthlink Advisors, including _“The Next 100 Days: A Realistic Approach to Help Established CIOs Initiate Sustainable Change,”_ a framework designed to guide healthcare IT leaders in driving sustainable transformation. He has also been featured on This Week Health, where he discussed imaging AI expansion and hospital technology trends, sharing insights that help healthcare executives align innovation with organizational performance.
Chris holds a Bachelor of Science in Information Systems Management from the University of South Florida and is certified in ITIL Foundations.

###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
The most rewarding part of my career has been realizing that my job title never defined my role. My true role has always been to understand and improve healthcare for patients, clinicians, and business users. Whether guiding a health system through an EHR implementation, leading a complex PACS or ERP selection, or supporting due diligence for an acquisition, I have focused on how technology and strategy work together to make healthcare better for everyone it touches. Seeing those improvements come to life, and watching teams sustain them long after the engagement ends, has been the most fulfilling aspect of my work. 
###  What do you hope to help our clients achieve? 
I hope to help clients create clarity in the midst of complexity by aligning strategy, people, and technology so they can deliver better care more efficiently. With Chartis's expertise and resources, I am excited to help healthcare leaders move beyond short-term fixes and build lasting digital and operational foundations that support their mission. 
###  One item on your bucket list? 
It used to be running with the bulls, but I run a little slower now. My bucket list has shifted to walking the Camino de Santiago trail.
