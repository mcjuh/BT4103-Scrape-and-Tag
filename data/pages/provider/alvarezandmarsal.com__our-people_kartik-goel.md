Kartik Goel is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Business Transformation Services in Mumbai. He specializes in procurement and supply chain transformation, spanning strategy through to execution. His areas of focus include enterprise-wide cost transformation, procurement operating model redesign, strategic sourcing, end-to-end process redesign, logistics and network strategy, and digital procurement, including the design and deployment of AI-enabled tools that embed intelligence into sourcing, supplier management, and spend analytics workflows. 
Mr. Goel has over 12 years of consulting experience across India and the US, with a particular emphasis on translating complex operational diagnostics into measurable profit and loss impact. He has worked with clients across multiple sectors, including food and beverage, fast-moving consumer goods, home appliances, textiles, and retail, helping them build future-ready procurement and supply chain organizations. 
Mr. Goel’s notable engagements include procurement cost takeout and operating model redesign for leading footwear manufacturers and retailers, MRO (maintenance, repair, and operations) cost reduction for one of the world’s largest food and beverage players, logistics optimization and end-to-end operations diagnostics for a major used-car retailer in the US, digital twin strategy for a leading quick-service restaurant chain, and AI-enabled procurement tools deployment across multiple consumer and industrial clients. He has also advised on supply chain network design, distribution strategy (including new distribution center launches and third-party logistics model design), and go-to-market strategy for a multinational technology firm’s supply chain offering.
