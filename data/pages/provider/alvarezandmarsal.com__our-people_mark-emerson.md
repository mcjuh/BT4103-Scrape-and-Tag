Mark Emerson is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Private Equity Services in Cincinnati. He specializes in driving transformational change across all aspects of the CFO agenda.
Mr. Emerson’s primary areas of concentration include finance operating model design and implementation, finance organization design, finance process improvement, finance shared services, and finance M&A, including post-merger integrations and carve-outs.Mr. Emerson brings nearly 25 years of management consulting experience that spans multiple sectors, including private equity, chemicals, life sciences, industrial products, software, and professional services. He has worked with C-suite executives at Fortune 500 and mid-market companies, both public and private.Mr. Emerson’s notable assignments include: leading several finance function stand-up engagements for recently acquired private equity portfolio companies; leading the finance function carve-out and stand-up of a global chemical manufacturer prior to its initial public offering; leading the finance integration efforts as part of the merger of two global pharmaceutical companies; and leading the finance operating model design for a global software and professional services company.Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Emerson spent 17 years in EY’s Finance Transformation practice and seven years in Accenture’s Finance & Performance Management practice, where he delivered large-scale finance transformation and M&A engagements.Mr. Emerson earned a bachelor’s degree in psychology from Northern Kentucky University and an MBA from the Willams College of Business at Xavier University in Ohio.
