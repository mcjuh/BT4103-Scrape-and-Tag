Daniel Sack is a member of BCG X, Boston Consulting Group’s tech build and design division. He specializes in building digital products, especially those based on machine learning/AI. He leads BCG X in the Nordics, and co-leads BCG’s generative AI efforts. He also co-leads BCG X’s Plan AI efforts, integrating advanced analytics into end-to-end planning processes.
Dan served as the lead for BCG’s Data Science Chapter and helped build BCG X’s Delivery Excellence (DevEx) program, a system of processes and governance that ensures each team maintains the highest standards for software code and technical solutions, including responsible AI.
