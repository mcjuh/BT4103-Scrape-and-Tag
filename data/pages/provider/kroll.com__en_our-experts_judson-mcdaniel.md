AT A GLANCE 
Judson McDaniel is Director in the Expert Services practice, based in Washington, D.C. He leverages more than eight years of experience providing expert and consulting services on business valuation, economic damages, post-acquisition disputes and forensic accounting. He has advised legal counsel, corporate clients and insurers across a wide range of industries on engagements involving valuations of closely-held and public businesses, complex economic damages analyses in connection with breach of contract claims, internal corporate investigations, forensic accounting investigations and post-acquisition disputes including representations and warranties claims. He regularly prepares sophisticated accounting, valuation and damages analyses in support of expert testimony.
Judson has assisted experts in preparing written expert reports and pre-litigation memoranda, and frequently supports experts in depositions, arbitrations, trials and presentations before corporate and legal audiences. His valuation experience includes Delaware Court of Chancery and international appraisal matters, shareholder disputes, earnout disputes and lost business value claims. He also has extensive experience supporting representations and warranties insurers and transaction parties in post-acquisition disputes arising from mergers and acquisitions.
Judson is a contributing author to Kroll’s annual publication, Selected Business Valuation Case Summaries: Delaware Court of Chancery Decisions and has authored multiple editions of the review over consecutive years.
Judson holds a B.S. in business administration with a concentration in finance from The Ohio State University Fisher College of Business, and a minor in economics from The Ohio State University College of Arts and Sciences. He is a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) charterholder and holds the Accredited in Business Valuation (ABV) designation as a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA).
He is also involved in Everybody Wins DC, a shared reading and mentorship program with elementary school students in Washington, D.C.
