# Filippo Bianchi
Managing Director & Senior Partner; Global Lead, Luxury [ Milan ](https://www.bcg.com/offices/milan/default)


### Education
  * MBA, Columbia University
  * Degree in Business Administration, Bocconi University


Filippo Bianchi joined Boston Consulting Group in 2004. He is an active member of the Consumer practice and serves as the global lead for the luxury division for the firm. He previously led fashion and luxury for Central Europe, the Middle East, and Africa.
Filippo has experienced working along the entire fashion and luxury value chain, including branding and positioning, supply chain, pricing and markdown, organization and operative model, format and operations retail, ecommerce, and digital. He has also worked with clients on many mergers and acquisitions.
Before joining the firm, Filippo worked as an analyst at Accenture.
