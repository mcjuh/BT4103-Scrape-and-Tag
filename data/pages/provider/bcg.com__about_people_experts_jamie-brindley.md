### Education
  * BA, Oxford University


Jamie Brindley leads Boston Consulting Group’s retail business across Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and South America (EMESA). He is a senior leader in BCG’s Consumer practice, focusing on supporting retailers and wholesalers both locally and internationally.
Jamie works with clients mostly on commercial, category management, and strategy projects. His most in-depth experience is in modern trade food retail and wholesale, followed by DIY and non-food retailing. He has worked for traditional brick-and-mortar retailers, omnichannel players, and e-commerce pure play companies.
Before joining BCG, Jamie worked as a journalist for Czech Radio, and as an English teacher in Germany.
