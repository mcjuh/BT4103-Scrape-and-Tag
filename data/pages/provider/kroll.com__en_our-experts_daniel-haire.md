AT A GLANCE 
Daniel Haire is a Managing Director in the Real Estate Advisory practice, based in Houston.
Daniel leads the Real Estate Valuation Management practice at Kroll and has over 12 years of experience in real estate valuation and real estate appraisal review. Daniel is primarily focused on real estate appraisal management and consulting for institutional fund products, as well as appraisal review for institutional investment managers and various lending institutions. His appraisal and appraisal review work covers all primary institutional asset classes as well as alternative real estate investments. Daniel has worked in the areas of fair value accounting, lending, portfolio valuation, fairness opinions, portfolio monitoring and tax appeal. He has led valuation management and consulting engagements for financial reporting purposes in connection with a variety of real estate products including large non-traded REITs, strategic alternative closed-end funds and separate accounts. His clients include private equity firms, insurance and pension managers, institutional investment managers and a variety of lending institutions and functions (commercial mortgage backed securities, balance sheet, warehouse). Daniel has consulted on valuations and provided decision support analyses on behalf of clients for trophy real estate across the United States, including the General Motors Building, Crown Building, Two Manhattan West, 550 Madison Avenue, 50 Hudson Yards, 35 Hudson Yards, 512 W 22nd Street and 770 Broadway. He is a licensed certified general real estate appraiser in many states coast to coast.
Prior to joining Kroll, Daniel was based in Manhattan where he was the primary valuation consultant for a large multibillion-dollar non-traded REIT product as well as strategic closed-end accounts on behalf of a global investment manager. He also managed the appraisal review function for a global non-bank lender.
Daniel holds a B.A. in economics from the University of Houston summa cum laude. He is also a Designated Member of the Appraisal Institute and holds the Appraisal Institute's AI-GRS designation.
