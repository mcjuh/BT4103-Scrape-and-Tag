### Expertise
Tim Hart is a forensic accountant with over 40 years of professional practice. He has served as an expert witness for more than 30 years in disputes around the globe.
Tim’s expert testimony covers a wide range of types of damages with trial or hearing testimony in over 70 cases. Tim’s international testimony is particularly concentrated with over 50 investor-state cases (ICSID, PCA, UNCITRAL) that involve allegations such as expropriation and fair and equitable treatment, along with international arbitrations involving breach of contract. His testimony in domestic cases has covered damages stemming from breach of contract, class action, breach of fiduciary duty and complex financial frauds, including Ponzi schemes. His testimony often covers topics important to determine jurisdiction, liability, causation, in addition to damages.
Tim specialises in matters of damages, valuation, accounting, fraud, and bribery and corruption. His expert work has been concentrated in high-stakes disputes spanning across a range of industries with high concentrations in mining, energy, telecommunications, manufacturing and financial services.
