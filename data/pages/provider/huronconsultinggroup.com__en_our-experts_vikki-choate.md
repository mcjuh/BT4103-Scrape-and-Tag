# Vikki Choate
DNP, MSN, R.N., NEA-BC, CPHQ, CPPS
Vikki has over three decades of healthcare leadership experience that she brings to her role helping organizations achieve quality, safety and high reliability as they pursue transformative, lasting clinical and operational change. 
## About Vikki
Vikki is a consultant and international speaker with more than 35 years of healthcare leadership experience that she uses to help clients consistently deliver results. She specializes in guiding healthcare organizations and executives to improve the care delivery process, enhance the patient experience and create cultures of accountability, safety and high reliability. 
Vikki leads Huron’s quality, safety and high reliability work across the U.S. and in Canada. She presents regularly at national conferences and individual organizations. 
Prior to joining Huron, Vikki held leadership roles in both the payer and provider environments. Vikki has served as a system vice president of performance improvement and a chief quality officer.
