### Education
  * MSc, Ecole Nationale des Ponts ParisTech


Lucas Chaumontet leads Boston Consulting Group in South Africa, and is a member of the firm's Energy practice leadership team as global leader for Climate & Sustainability in Energy.
Lucas works primarily on engagements in energy, natural resources, process industries, and infrastructure. His expertise includes capital project development, the incubation and scaling of new low-carbon businesses, corporate transformations, and national and global energy transition initiatives.
Lucas joined the BCG Paris office in 2009, and has since worked across Europe, Asia, and Africa.
