The Software Opportunity in Building Materials
With software as a service, BCG’s Matthias Tauber explains that the way we distribute building materials will transform, as startups, software companies, and online stores drive the change.
### Education
  * MBA, with distinction, INSEAD
  * MSc, engineering, Vienna University of Technology


Matthias Tauber leads Boston Consulting Group’s operations in Europe, Middle East, South America, and Africa. He is a member of BCG’s management team in Europe.
Matthias’s client work focuses on large-scale change programs with clients in the building materials, metals and mining, and chemicals industries. Matthias has led digital transformations, cost optimizations, M&As, and postmerger integrations, as well as stand-alone transformation programs. On the digital side, Matthias has worked on transforming core businesses as well as building new business models. He has also acquired significant experience in organizational redesign—including the build-up of digital capabilities. In addition he has worked extensively with private equity firms.
