  * Country Director 
  *   * michael.adenew@opml.co.uk



Michael Adenew leads OPM’s strategic initiatives in Ethiopia, collaborating with key continental organisations such as the African Union and the UN Economic Commission for Africa, and tailoring OPM's strategies to meet the region's specific needs and in building key relationships with governments, development partners, and stakeholders.
Michael brings over 20 years of experience from working in the US, UK, and Africa at the intersection of technology, development, and public sector support. He has led major satellite and technology-related initiatives that tackled critical developmental challenges. His work with international bodies including the UN, World Bank, FCDO, USAID, and Gates Foundation, has enabled him to effectively integrate global best practices with local solutions.
Michael holds an MSc in Business Information Technology Management and a BA (Hons) in Management of Business Information from the University of Westminster. He is a member of the British Computer Society and an Associate of Project Management. As a speaker on digital transformation, he is dedicated to using technology to drive developmental progress and impactful change.

Project
###  [Strengthening Gavi’s engagement with civil society organisations and local partners](https://www.opml.co.uk/projects/strengthening-gavis-engagement-civil-society-organisations-and-local-partners "Go to Strengthening Gavi’s engagement with civil society organisations and local partners")
We provide technical assistance (TA) at national and sub-national levels to strengthen health systems and support the Government of Ethiopia and Gavi in ensuring equitable and sustainable access to immunisation under the GAVI Fund Manager contract. Through embedded TA and collaboration with civil society organisations across high-priority districts, we focus on reaching 250,000 zero-dose children and remote populations for immunisation coverage.


Project
###  [Technical assistance for health systems and immunisation strengthening in Ethiopia](https://www.opml.co.uk/projects/technical-assistance-health-systems-and-immunisation-strengthening-ethiopia "Go to Technical assistance for health systems and immunisation strengthening in Ethiopia")
We provide technical assistance (TA) at national and sub-national levels to strengthen health systems and support the Government of Ethiopia and Gavi in advancing equitable and sustainable access to immunisation. Our embedded technical advisors focus on strengthening immunisation systems with particular emphasis on equity and localisation. We enable evidence-based planning, data use and monitoring of immunisation initiatives.
