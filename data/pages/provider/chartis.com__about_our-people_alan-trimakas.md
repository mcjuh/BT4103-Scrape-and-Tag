# Alan Trimakas
Partner 
Alan Trimakas is a Partner in Strategic Transformation at Chartis. With over 14 years of experience, he has played a key role in helping health systems navigate complex strategic challenges, capitalize on emerging opportunities, and adapt to an industry undergoing profound change.
Alan’s primary areas of focus include enterprise strategy, market growth planning, partnership formation, payer strategy optimization, and financial performance improvement, helping to deliver transformative engagements that have reshaped healthcare organizations at both the national and regional levels. With deep expertise across the healthcare ecosystem—spanning hospitals, health systems, academic medical centers, physician organizations, and payers—Alan is passionate about redefining care delivery models, improving financial sustainability, and driving innovative strategies that meaningfully enhance healthcare accessibility and quality in the U.S.
Prior to joining Chartis, Alan served as a Principal at BDC Advisors, a healthcare-focused strategy consulting firm. Alan led engagements responsible for designing strategies for major market transformations, including the formation of a multi-billion-dollar regional health system through a strategic merger, where he worked closely with senior leadership to define a long-term integration strategy that aligned market positioning, care delivery, and financial sustainability objectives.
Alan began his career in KPMG’s Management Consulting Practice. Among other clients, Alan led consulting teams advising the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) on a range of health policy and operational transformation initiatives.
Alan holds a Master of Business Administration from the University of Florida and a Bachelor of Science in Commerce from the University of Virginia.

###  Best piece of advice you've ever been given? 
"Cut through the clutter." Early in my career I received the great piece of advice that true value-add in consulting isn’t about solving everything at once—it’s about identifying and addressing the core issue while tuning out distractions. Once that’s solved for, you can strategically layer in complexity rather than getting lost in it. 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
Seeing the lightbulb go off and the confidence on a client’s face when they recognize the path forward is clear has to be one of the most rewarding aspects of my work. Delivering a strategy that brings everything into focus and helps leaders navigate pivotal moments, whether by shaping a transformative growth strategy, structuring a complex partnership, or solving a financial challenge that strengthens their organization’s future, makes this work deeply fulfilling and meaningful. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
Traveling or planning my next trip. I’m an avid traveler and also, in what likely comes as a surprise to no one, a meticulous planner. Every trip is carefully mapped out with color-coded itineraries and project plans, though I always leave just enough room for (strategically planned) spontaneity.
