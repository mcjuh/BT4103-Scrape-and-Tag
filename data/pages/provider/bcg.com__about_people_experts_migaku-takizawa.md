Migaku has worked mainly with clients in the industrial goods sector, especially in the automobiles and automotive industries, as well as in high-tech industries. His recent experience includes formulating business strategy, business restructuring, digital transformation, and building governance structure.
Prior to joining BCG, Migaku worked at Toyota Motor Corporation.
