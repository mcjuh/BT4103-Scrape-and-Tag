## Kiran Sequeira
#### Managing Director
Mr. Sequeira is an expert witness on damages in complex, high-stakes international disputes. He has more than 20 years of experience advising clients in international commercial and investor-state arbitrations. He has provided expert evidence on financial, economic, accounting, valuation, and damages issues in more than 100 matters, including those before all major arbitral institutions and the US and UK courts.

Kiran Sequeira is  an expert witness on damages in complex, high-stakes international disputes. He has more than 20 years of experience advising clients in international commercial and investor-state arbitrations. Mr. Sequeira has provided expert evidence on financial, economic, accounting, valuation, and damages issues in more than 100 matters, including  before all major arbitral institutions a the US and UK courts.
Mr. Sequeira brings a hands-on approach working with clients and counsel – identifying practical solutions based on in-depth knowledge of key issues impacting each case. His industry experience includes the energy, mining, telecommunications, financial services, infrastructure, construction, real estate, manufacturing, and services sectors. Geographically, his expertise spans the globe, focusing on emerging economies in Asia, Africa, Middle East, Latin America, Russia/CIS and East Europe. He also has substantial experience valuing companies and investments in India.
He is also a licensed professional engineer who combines his valuation and damages expertise with a solid understanding of the technical issues underpinning many complex disputes. He integrates these insights into expert evidence that tribunals can rely upon.
Mr. Sequeira has been elected to the Expert Witnesses section of  _Lexology Index_(formerly _Who’s Who Legal_) every year since 2013. Since 2017, he has been named annually as one of only a few “Most Highly Regarded Individuals” by _Lexology_. Since 2019, he has been elected to _Lexology_ ’s “Thought Leaders – Global Elite” which is a select list of the world’s foremost damages experts. 
_Lexology_ notes, “Kiran Sequeira is described as ‘simply the best in the industry,’ with over 20 years of experience providing quantum analysis in international disputes. His ability to ‘anchor discussions on key points’ makes him a highly regarded expert witness in complex cases.” Clients and peers applaud Mr. Sequeira as “truly excellent,” stating that he “stands out for his strategic analysis and performance under cross-examination” and “wins plaudits for his ability to explain complex financial problems and present a ‘convincing quantum report’. Mr. Sequeira is also commended by _Lexology_ for his deep financial, economic, and accounting expertise, and for his ability to expertly “[assist] with investigations and advising on global disputes across multiple industries and jurisdictions, including energy, mining and infrastructure.”
