AT A GLANCE 
Cindy Jia is a director in the Aerospace Defense and Government (ADG) Services Investment Banking practice, based in Houston.
Cindy leverages more than 15 years of middle market M&A experience. She specializes in providing sell-side services to family-and founder-owned businesses and private equity firms. She has worked with companies in various end markets, including aerospace, defense, government services, metal distribution and servicing, oilfield services, engineering and construction, general industrials, manufacturing and business services.
Cindy’s notable ADG transactions include the recapitalization of Innoflight with The Jordan Company; the sale of Busby Metals to the Wieland Group; the divestiture of Bradken’s Specialty Products North American Operations to TerraMar Capital; the divestiture of SGL Carbon SE’s Gardena Composite Solution Business to Tex Tech Industries, a portfolio company of Arlington Capital; the sale of Blue Marble Communications to T2S Solutions, a portfolio company of Madison Dearborn Partners; the sale of Aerial Armor to Dedrone; and the sale of Citadel Defense, a portfolio company of Lightspeed Venture Partners to BlueHalo, a portfolio company of Arlington Capital. 
Prior to joining Kroll, Cindy served as a senior associate at Growth Capital Partners, L.P., a merchant and investment banking firm in Houston, Texas. 
Cindy holds a BBA in finance and management from the University of Houston. She is a FINRA-registered representative holding the Series 7 and 63 licenses.
