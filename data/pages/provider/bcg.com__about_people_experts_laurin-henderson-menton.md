Laurin Henderson Menton is a retail specialist and core member of Boston Consulting Group’s Corporate Finance & Strategy and Consumer practices with a focus on digital transformation.
Laurin advises clients on strategic topics, including corporate strategy, operating model design, and advanced analytics. She is an expert in finance topics. Laurin has extensive experience driving transformational change across finance, merchandising, and product development, for global organizations. She is a trusted partner to boards and executive officers, driving transformative programs that deliver sustained value.
Laurin leads Women@BCG for Brooklyn, and is active in recruiting. Before joining the firm, she worked at AlixPartners, Deloitte Consulting, and Aritzia LLP.
