#  Chris Smith 
##  Audit & Assurance Partner | Deloitte & Touche LLP 
Chris is an Audit & Assurance partner within Deloitte & Touche LLP’s US Banking and Capital Markets practice, which provides a broad spectrum of services to each of the banks and capital market firms listed on the Fortune 500.
Chris has more than 20 years of experience working with many of Deloitte’s largest US and international banking and capital markets clients. He works closely with audit committees and senior management on corporate governance, risk management, mergers and acquisitions, and various complex accounting and reporting matters. This often includes evaluating the use of data from operations, finance, risk, and compliance, as well as the related supporting processes, methodologies, and controls.
