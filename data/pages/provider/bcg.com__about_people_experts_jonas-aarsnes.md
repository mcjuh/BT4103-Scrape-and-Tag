Jonas Flø Aarsnes is a core member of Boston Consulting Group's global Technology, Media & Telecommunications (TMT) and Corporate Finance & Strategy (CFS) practices. He leads the TMT team in Oslo.
Jonas has extensive experience working on growth strategies, performance improvement, and transformation programs. He focuses on work in the telecommunications, media, and technology industry, but has also led a broad range of transformation and M&A related projects for private equity and industrial clients.
Before joining the firm in 2008, Jonas was a squad leader in the Norwegian Army.
