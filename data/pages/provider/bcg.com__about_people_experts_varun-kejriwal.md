Varun Kejriwal joined Boston Consulting Group in May 2012. He is a core member of the Financial Institutions and People & Organization practices at the firm, and leads the Seat-At-The-Table (SATT) agenda and marketing activities for the People & Organization practice in Asia Pacific.
Varun is an expert in digital performance management, leadership development, organization design, end-to-end customer journey digitization, post-merger integration, digital-first business build, and market entry strategy.
Recently, Varun has been focusing on helping financial institutions and public sector enterprises leverage digital to better serve employees and customers. He is a co-author of BCG’s annual FIBAC productivity report on the Indian banking industry, produced with FICCI and the Indian Banks’ Association.
