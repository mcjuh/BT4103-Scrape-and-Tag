Yuichiro Azo is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Global Transaction Advisory Group in Tokyo. He serves as Co-Head of Corporate Finance.
With approximately 20 years of transaction-related experience, Mr. Azo specializes in M&A deal execution for clients across a broad range of sectors. He has extensive experience in deal handling and negotiation, scheme advice, corporate valuation analysis, and disclosure document support. Mr. Azo brings strong, up-to-date expertise in corporate law, financial instruments and exchange acts, timely disclosure rules, and many M&A-related guidelines, among other key accounting and tax-related rules.Mr. Azo has a strong track record in M&A, including complex business integrations, carve-outs, management buyout, and delisting, especially in consumer product and retail sectors.Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Azo held senior roles at EY and Nomura Securities, leading Japanese financial institutions. In addition, he recently served as Chief Sales Officer and a full-time director of the board at a consumer product company listed at the Tokyo Stock Exchange.Mr. Azo earned a master’s degree in international finance from the University of Amsterdam.
