Burak Tansan is Boston Consulting Group’s global leader for environmental, social, and governance (ESG) in emerging markets, and chairman of the Turkey office. He has over 25 years of senior-level experience in multiple industries, with a specific focus on banking, insurance, consumer and retail, industrial goods, and conglomerates.
Burak’s consulting project experience has included large-scale transformations and post-merger integration programs for leading Turkish and multinational companies, governance and organizational model redesign for leading conglomerates and companies, and portfolio assessment and governance models.
