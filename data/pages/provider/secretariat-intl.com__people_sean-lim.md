## Sean Lim
#### Associate Director
​​Sean Lim is an Associate Director at Secretariat with over a decade of experience specialising in digital forensics investigations and eDiscovery across Southeast Asia, advising clients on complex disputes, regulatory investigations, and cross-border data matters.​ 
Singapore
[​​+65 86296681​](tel:%E2%80%8B%E2%80%8B+65%2086296681%E2%80%8B) slim@secretariat-intl.com
[Representative Experience](https://secretariat-intl.com/people/sean-lim/#)
​​Mr. Lim advises clients on complex digital investigations, disputes, and regulatory matters involving large, cross-border data environments and sensitive information. He is regularly engaged to help clients manage matters subject to court and regulatory scrutiny, including disputes, regulatory investigations, employment and shareholder matters, intellectual property issues, and post-breach response efforts involving personally identifiable information (PII).
​Mr. Lim’s work centres on forensic data preservation, digital investigations, and the management of end-to-end eDiscovery workflows, including advanced analytics and technology-assisted review. He is frequently called upon in matters requiring defensible methodologies, efficient execution under compressed timelines, and coordination across multiple jurisdictions.
​Before joining Secretariat, Mr. Lim held senior roles at specialist forensic and eDiscovery consultancies and previously worked at a global financial institution, where he supported internal investigations and worked closely with legal, compliance, and privacy teams. This experience informs his practical, commercially focused approach to advising clients operating in highly regulated environments.
​Mr. Lim is known for translating complex technical issues into clear, actionable advice for legal and business stakeholders and has delivered guest lectures on digital forensics and eDiscovery at multiple academic institutions as part of professional and academic programmes.
​Mr. Lim is recognised by  _Lexology Index_ (formerly  _Who’s Who Legal_) for his decade of experience in digital investigations.  _Lexology_ notes that Sean Lim “operates at the epicentre of matters involving regulatory scrutiny, employment disputes, intellectual-property concerns and post-breach analysis” delivering expert insight across highly complex multinational data landscapes.
