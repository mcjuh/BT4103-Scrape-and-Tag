# Meredith Deal
Senior Research Associate 
Meredith Deal is a Senior Research Associate at Chartis. Meredith has over 6 years of research experience, 4 of which in the clinical setting. Meredith is a member of the Chartis Research Team, where she works on both internal and external research projects.
Prior to joining Chartis, Meredith led research teams at Brigham & Women’s Hospital and UPMC, focusing on psychiatry and behavioral health.
Meredith holds a Master of Science in Counseling Psychology from Chatham University. She graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Exercise Science from the University of Delaware.
