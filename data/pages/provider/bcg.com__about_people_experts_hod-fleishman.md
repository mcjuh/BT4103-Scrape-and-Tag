Hod Fleishman joined Boston Consulting Group in 2016. He is vice president and Global Head of [Internet of Things (IoT)](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/internet-of-things) for BCG, as well as a core member of the Industrial Goods, Energy, and Consumer practices at the firm. He is an expert in innovation, product development and build, and business build, specifically for Deep Tech. 
Hod’s current focus is leading BCG's global work in developing the processes tools, content, and methodologies for integrating Deep Tech into sales, ideation, and build phases to help clients solve business problems. He collaborates to find business opportunities that solve key frictions for BCG's corporate clients using Deep Tech. 
Before joining the firm, Hod was founder and CEO of several startups in the fields of automotive M2M (machine to machine) and consumer IoT.
