### Education
  * EDHEC Business School


Renaud Montupet is a core member of BCG's Transform practice area where he leads BCG Transform's Special Situations unit in Europe, Middle East, South America and Africa. He is an expert in transformation, turnaround, and restructuring of stressed and distressed companies. Renaud has over 20 years of experience in operational and financial turnarounds, as well as performance improvement engagements. He is able to combine his management consulting and managerial expertise to drive operational improvement, as well as EBITDA and cash optimization. 
Renaud's experience includes cost rationalization, large headcount reduction programs, footprint optimization, business plan design and implementation, liquidity management, and cash flow improvements. He has worked with mid-market and large international corporations as well as financial shareholders, mainly in retail, oil and gas, and industrial goods. 
Prior to joining BCG, Renaud was a Managing Director at Alix Partners, where he spent 11 years delivering comprehensive turnaround programs for distressed and underperforming companies in complex turnaround situation.
