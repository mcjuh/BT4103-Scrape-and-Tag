AT A GLANCE 
Mario De Munno is Managing Director in the Transaction Advisory Services practice, based in Milan. Mario leverages more than 15 years of experience in complex domestic and cross-border transactions supporting private equity firms and corporate buyers with buy-side and sell-side financial due diligence.
Prior to joining Kroll, Mario served as Partner in the Transaction Advisory Services practice at Nexia Audirevi. Before that, he held roles at Forvis Mazars and spent eight years with KPMG Transaction Services, where he built his expertise in financial due diligence and deal advisory. Mario has extensive experience across multiple sectors, including agribusiness, ICT and professional services. He has supported Italian and pan-European private equity funds in executing buy-and-build strategies, providing rigorous financial analysis and insights that help clients navigate growth, transformation and integration challenges. Mario’s transaction advisory experience includes buy-side and sell-side mandates for private equity funds pursuing sector consolidation, cross-border platform investments and operational optimization initiatives. His work focuses on helping clients understand the key value drivers, risks and financial dynamics across the transaction lifecycle.
Mario is a certified chartered accountant and a registered statutory auditor in Italy.
