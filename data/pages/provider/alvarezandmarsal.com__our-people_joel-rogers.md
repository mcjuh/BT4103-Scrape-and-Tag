Joel Rogers is a Senior Director with Alvarez & Marsal Restructuring & Turnaround in Atlanta. With more than 20 years of experience, he brings significant financial and operational advisory expertise. Mr. Rogers specializes in working with management teams and other stakeholders in distressed business and workout situations.
Mr. Rogers has experience advising stakeholders and partnering with management teams to navigate out-of-court restructurings and large Chapter 11 cases. His engagements have included advising clients on implementing operational and performance improvement initiatives; performing strategic assessments of companies, business segments, and assets; developing business plans and capital budgeting; managing and improving working capital; leading cash flow forecasting and treasury management; negotiating with vendors and other constituents; performing debt capacity and collateral analyses; and preparing Chapter 11 and general contingency planning. He has also served in interim management roles for clients, focusing on financial reporting and cash flow management.
Mr. Rogers has advised companies across a range of industries, including Vertex Energy, Sunlight Financial, Joann Inc., Somic America, The Aarons Company, iHeartMedia, Babcock & Wilcox Enterprises, California Pizza Kitchen, ASARCO, Angelica Corporation, Brambles Limited, Stone Energy Corporation, Lane Construction, UTC Power, iGPS Logistics, Clipper Windpower, and Horizon Lines, Inc.
Mr. Rogers earned a bachelor’s degree in finance and a bachelor’s degree in mathematics from the University of Georgia. He is a Certified Insolvency and Restructuring Advisor and a member of the Turnaround Management Association.
