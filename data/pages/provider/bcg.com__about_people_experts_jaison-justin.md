### Education
  * MBA, Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University
  * BS, Chemical Engineering: University of Arizona


Jaison Justin leads Boston Consulting Group’s Technology, Media & Telecommunications (TMT) practice for the US South System and is the global topic lead for large-scale R&D transformations through Product Innovation & Engineering. He is also the Office Leader for BCG Austin.
Jaison has consulted with leading technology, media, and telecom clients on topics including strategy development, post-merger integration, go-to-market, operations, and transformation. 
Before he joined the firm, Jaison worked as a semiconductor product manager at Electro Scientific Industries, Inc., where he led marketing strategies for the semiconductor and LCD manufacturing industries.
