AT A GLANCE 
Krishna Raja is a Managing Director in the Cyber Risk practice, based in Toronto.
Krishna brings more than 15 years of experience in application security, the first eight years of which he spent performing comprehensive penetration tests, threat modeling and developing application security programs for financial, government, retail and health care organizations across Canada and the U.S.
Krishna joined Kroll through the [acquisition](https://www.kroll.com/en/newsroom/kroll-acquires-security-compass-advisory) of Security Compass Advisory (SCA) by Kroll in December 2021. Before that, he oversaw and evolved SCA’s Consulting Practice, which included over 50 cyber security professionals. Krishna has delivered a wide variety of cyber security training from exploiting and defending web applications to threat modeling at industry conferences, such as RSA and OWASP AppSec. He has also participated in initiatives that further the cyber security community, such as OWASP ASVS and the Toronto Area Security Klatch.
Krishna received a Bachelor of Science in computer science, with software engineering specialization from Western University. He also holds a certification in CSSLP from (ISC)2.
