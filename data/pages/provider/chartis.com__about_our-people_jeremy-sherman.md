# Jeremy Sherman
Partner 
Jeremy Sherman is Partner in Performance Transformation. Jeremy is a relationship-driven, innovative healthcare executive with over 15 years of experience in strategic consulting, operations, and technology solutions for healthcare providers and services companies. He has a proven track record of translating market insights into impactful strategies, driving the implementation of health tech, intelligence automation, revenue cycle process workflow design, and operationalizing and managing change to achieve sustainable financial results. He is privileged to have worked with many nationally ranked large hospitals, academic medical centers, and integrated delivery networks, as well as several pediatric institutions.
Prior to joining Chartis, Jeremy was a Senior Vice President for Janus Health Technologies, a private equity backed growth stage revenue cycle data intelligence and process automation company. His role included building and scaling Janus’ commercial team, defining the go-to-market processes, and overseeing the creation of the marketing and account management departments. Jeremy was also responsible for working with large health systems to design and deploy the Janus software platform, contributing to the overall growth of the firm.
Prior to Janus, Jeremy spent over 12 years at Huron Consulting Group, serving as the Healthcare Intelligent Automation Practice lead. His recent work also included leading large performance improvement, technology strategy, interim leadership, and workforce optimization engagements for health systems and community hospitals.
Jeremy is an active member in Healthcare Financial Management Association (HFMA) and holds a Bachelor of Business Administration from the University of Wisconsin.
