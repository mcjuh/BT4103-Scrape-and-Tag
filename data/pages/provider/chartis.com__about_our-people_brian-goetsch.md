# Brian Goetsch
Senior Partner, HealthScape Advisors 
Brian Goetsch is a Senior Partner at HealthScape Advisors, a Chartis Company. Brian advises health plans, specialty health organizations and private equity investors on a variety of strategic, financial, and operational topics.
Brian co-leads HealthScape’s Ancillary Center of Excellence with a focus on the Dental and Vision ecosystems, which includes managed care, providers, labs, and emerging technologies. In addition, he has product experience in Supplemental Health, Financial Protection, and Lifestyle offerings.
Brian has led successful healthcare engagements across a wide range of topics including product expansion and diversification, market and line of business entry, network and cost of care management, partnership strategy and execution, and inorganic growth strategies.
Prior to joining HealthScape, Brian served as a senior executive at EyeMed Vision Care leading the development and execution of its partner channel “One-Stop-Shop” strategy. During his tenure, he successfully led multiple cross-functional initiatives including EyeMed’s expansion into Medicaid, investment in its Medicare Advantage solution, and the launch of its medical-surgical vision solution. He also supported efforts to develop, implement, and contract multiple partner deals
Previously, Brian was a Director at HealthScape Advisors and started his career with Huron Consulting Group. Brian earned his Masters and Bachelors in Accountancy from the University of Illinois and was a Certified Public Accountant (inactive) and Certified Fraud Examiner (inactive).

###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
The most rewarding part of my career is seeing my colleagues advance and grow professionally. I love working side-by-side with our people and nothing makes me more excited than seeing our people succeed; whether its their first time presenting to the client, first major presentation, finalist presentation, or sale, it's super exciting to be a part of it. Gives me energy to continue to grow myself everyday. 
###  Do you have a motto or personal mantra? 
My personal mantra is "the hottest fires forge the strongest steel". I try and frame all the challenges of the day as opportunities to become stronger and more capable. I’ve found that my hardest and most demanding bosses, projects, problems, and situations have all accelerated my own personal and professional growth. The stress is temporary while the positive impact is permanent. 
###  What is something most people would be surprised to learn about you? 
I love to bake. It's an opportunity to bond with my kids and do something creative.
