#  Lauren Jackson 
##  Managing Director | Deloitte’s Investment Management practice | Audit & Assurance Managing Director 
Lauren is a Managing Director in Deloitte’s Investment Management practice. She has experience serving registered investment companies, exchange traded funds, collective investment trusts, and hedge funds, and has served many of the firm’s largest investment management clients. Lauren has been a public speaker on accounting and financial reporting topics impacting the industry and has authored previous articles on the SEC Proposed ETF Rule.
