# Robert Faix
Partner 
Robert Faix is a Partner in Digital & Technology Transformation. He has nearly 25 years of Information Technology experience, including over 20 years as a Health IT consultant developing and delivering complex technical solutions to health delivery organizations. Robert has significant experience effectively presenting complex technical concepts to Executive leadership, Boards of Directors, and various sub-committees. Throughout his consulting career, he has provided advisory services to health delivery organizations of all sizes and types with particular emphasis on the assessment of current state infrastructure, IT operations optimization, development of IT strategic plans, visioning and planning of IT services in support of new construction, data center and hosting alternatives analysis, and disaster preparedness to enhance the effectiveness of IT operations. 
Robert’s strengths include collaborating with all levels of executive, clinical, business, and IT leadership to present, evaluate, and define technical strategies while engaging IT architects, engineers, and administrators to align tactical design, process, and implementation efforts. Furthermore, he is a hands-on leader with a wide breadth of health IT experience paired with a passion for developing innovative solutions and evaluating the potential implications of emerging technologies. 
Prior to joining Chartis, Robert served as Vice President, Technology Service Line Leader, for an Illinois-based healthcare information technology consulting firm. 
Robert earned his Bachelor of Science in Management Information Systems from Youngstown State University.

###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
Hands down, the most rewarding part of my career has been the countless relationships cultivated with clients, colleagues, vendors, and other industry contacts. Many of these relationships span over 20 years and have evolved into true friendships. It’s a great feeling to take a moment occasionally and scroll through my LinkedIn contacts and reflect on how I became connected to so many people over time. 
###  Do you have a motto or personal mantra? 
Onward! Those who have been on meetings with me will often hear me conclude a meeting with this phrase. To me, it’s not only a call to action, but implies that we have a plan, a goal, and a sense of urgency for getting to the next destination. 
###  One item on your bucket list? 
More recently, I’ve taken an interest in learning to sail and the influences of that lifestyle when attempting to get from point A to point B. Making the journey part of the joy of travel and surrendering the minute-by-minute planning that air travel dictates will be a real change in mindset and something I look forward to in life.
