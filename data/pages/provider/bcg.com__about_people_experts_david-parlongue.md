David Parlongue is a member of the Travel, Cities & Infrastructure, and Health Care practices at Boston Consulting Group. His key areas of expertise include transportation and logistics, regulation, health care providers, and transaction support.
David joined the firm in 2019, bringing ten years of experience in the public sector, including as an economic advisor to Emmanuel Macron, then Minister of the Economy for France. Additional previous positions included advisor to Ministers and Deputy-Ministers and high civil servant positions in the French Treasury. Just prior to joining BCG, David was a member of the Executive Committee in charge of Strategy and M&A of CMA CGM, an international shipping and logistics key player.
