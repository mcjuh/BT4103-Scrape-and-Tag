AT A GLANCE 
Kevin Martin is Managing Director in the Agency and Trustee Services practice, based in Singapore.
Kevin is a seasoned corporate trust and banking professional with over 27 years of experience across capital markets, banking, sales and client services. He has a strong focus on growing revenue, expanding market share and delivering tailored solutions to clients in complex financing environments.
Kevin brings a strong client service mindset, underpinned by technical depth and execution excellence. His career is defined by his ability to drive commercial growth while maintaining a high standard of service and operational excellence across global markets.
Prior to joining Kroll, Kevin led business development across Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) and Australia for Deutsche Bank. Kevin has held senior roles across Singapore, Hong Kong and London, playing a key role in expanding business across Asia Pacific. He has driven business development in markets including Japan, Hong Kong, Indonesia, and Australia—building strategic relationships with clients and stakeholders in private equity, debt capital markets and M&A. At Deutsche Bank London, he was a senior member of the Debt and Agency Team, managing complex portfolios for European and American clients. In this role, he worked closely with arrangers, governments, issuers, borrowers, private equity firms and international law firms to ensure seamless execution and superior client outcomes.
Kevin also spent over seven years at Landesbank Baden-Württemberg in London, where he served as Senior Loans Administrator. There, he specialized in syndicated loans, project finance, and loan agency administration.
