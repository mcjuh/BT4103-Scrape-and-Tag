### Expertise
Timothy Harwin is a civil engineer with nearly 25 years of construction industry experience. He has acted as a delay expert witness on more than 20 occasions.
Tim has testified and been cross-examined as an expert witness in arbitration proceedings, including giving concurrent evidence (hot-tubbing). He has extensive experience in the preparation of expert witness reports for adjudication, arbitration and litigation.
Tim specialises in the analysis of delay, disruption and programming on construction projects. He has supported clients for projects with values up to US$1.5 billion and disputed values in excess of US$200 million.

### Background
Tim has a strong technical and engineering background in the delivery of large building, civil, energy, and infrastructure projects, enabling him to provide his clients with sound and reliable advice. He has provided expert services on a range of building and infrastructure disputes across Asia, Africa, Australia and the Middle East for disputes arising on building, road, rail, bridge, power stations, oil pipelines, water treatment facilities, LNG facilities, mining infrastructure and renewable energy projects.
Timothy Harwin holds degrees in civil engineering, business and construction law. He is a member of the Academy of Experts and the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators.
