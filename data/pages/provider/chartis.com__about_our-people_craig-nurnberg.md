# Craig Nurnberg
Senior Partner 
Craig Nurnberg is a Senior Partner in Performance Transformation at Chartis. He is a seasoned healthcare executive with over two decades of experience in the healthcare revenue cycle management sector. Throughout his career, Craig has been instrumental in leading multi-million-dollar projects that have consistently delivered exceptional financial outcomes for healthcare organizations. His ability to design and execute complex performance improvement projects has led to client satisfaction and long-term partnerships, embodying his "client for life" philosophy.
Prior to joining Chartis, Craig’s career began at Stockamp and Associates, where he spent five years managing large-scale revenue cycle improvement engagements. In 2008, as Stockamp joined Huron Consulting Group, Craig ascended to the role of Performance Improvement Managing Director. At Huron, he became a key member of both the Performance Improvement Leadership Team and the Revenue Cycle Leadership Team, significantly influencing strategic initiatives and driving the company’s growth selling its full suite of healthcare services.
Notably, Craig was a highly trusted leader at Huron, consistently exceeding clients’ expected targets—even during the challenging pandemic. Craig leveraged his strong client experience and led Huron to expand into new markets. Craig’s mentorship efforts were also pivotal in expanding Huron’s market presence, particularly within the revenue cycle space, where his strategic direction led to significant market penetration and client engagement.
Craig holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Management Administration from the Georgia Institute of Technology, where he graduated Magna Cum Laude with minors in Finance and Economics. Residing in Marietta, Georgia, Craig balances his professional accomplishments with a rich personal life, where he is known for his strong ties to his community and his passion for mentoring the next generation of leaders.

###  Why Chartis? 
I chose Chartis for two key reasons: the unwavering focus on clients and the exceptional quality of our teams. Collaborating with some of the brightest, most driven consultants to address one of our nation’s most pressing challenges is a cause I am deeply passionate about. By partnering with the most influential healthcare organizations, I have the opportunity to contribute to transforming and enhancing the delivery of healthcare across the country. 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
Without a doubt, the most rewarding aspect of my career is witnessing the tangible outcomes of our work with clients, along with the journey that leads us there. Time and again, it's the relationships built throughout the process that foster stronger leaders in healthcare—and in turn, make me a better consultant and a better person overall. 
###  What is something most people would be surprised to learn about you? 
I spent my childhood living in seven different states and Japan as a military brat. This unique experience exposed me to diverse cultures and perspectives, shaping who I am today. It taught me the value of understanding viewpoints different from my own and continues to influence my approach to listening and communication every day.
