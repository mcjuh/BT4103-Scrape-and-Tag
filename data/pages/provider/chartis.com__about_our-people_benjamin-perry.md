# Benjamin Perry
Partner 
Benjamin Perry is a Partner in Strategic Transformation and has more than 13 years of healthcare experience, 11 of which have been spent in consulting. His work is concentrated in enterprise strategy and organizational alignment, with a focus on supporting academic health centers and academically affiliated children’s hospitals to develop approaches to strategy alignment and management structures, including research organization, governance, and funds flow. 
Ben’s depth of experience includes the development of a five-year enterprise strategic plan for a comprehensive academically affiliated children’s hospital. He also worked with a large urban children’s hospital on the design of a strategic plan for their maternal and fetal health programs. Additionally, he completed an enterprise strategic plan for a large urban academic medical center addressing the key components of the academic mission – clinical, teaching, and research. 
Before joining Chartis, Ben served as an Administrative Fellow at Ochsner Health System in New Orleans, Louisiana assisting in the implementation of a network development strategy. His healthcare experience also includes the completion of a Thomas C. Wales Foundation Fellowship, where he designed a program to bring occupational therapy into the homes of low-income elderly adults in the greater Seattle metropolitan area. 
Ben holds a Master’s degree in health administration from the University of Washington School of Public Health and a Bachelor of Science in anthropology and comparative religious studies from Hamline University in Saint Paul, Minnesota.

###  What has been your favorite project at Chartis and why? 
My favorite project was an enterprise strategic plan for an urban academic medical center (AMC) and medical school, developing a future state vision and detailed strategies for their clinical, education, and research missions. I enjoyed the comprehensiveness of the final deliverable and how it resulted in the team interacting across the whole organization. It was also a team of very different palates that enjoyed constantly debating the next dinner location. 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
I really enjoy helping extremely complex organizations align around specific ideas and initiatives. Getting all the boats rowing in the same direction is a substantial challenge with the scale of our clients, and they are very appreciative when we can assist them in a shared understanding of their aspirations and how to achieve them. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
I love to futz around on projects for the fun of it. My house is about 100 years old, and I do vegetable gardens and bee keeping in the summer, and I have an old classic car. My 6-year old and I are always working on something and I’m looking forward to my 2-year old joining soon.
