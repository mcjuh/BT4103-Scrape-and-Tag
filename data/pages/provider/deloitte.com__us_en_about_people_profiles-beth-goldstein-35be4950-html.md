#  Beth Goldstein 
##  Audit & Assurance Partner | Deloitte & Touche LLP 
Beth is an Audit & Assurance partner serving the financial services industry and is the Wealth Management segment leader for the Audit & Assurance business at Deloitte & Touche LLP. With more than 22 years of experience, Beth has served financial services clients that range in size, complexity, and maturity with a heavy focus on wealth management. She has extensive experience with initial public offerings, as well as accounting, financial reporting, operational, and regulatory compliance matters.
Throughout her career, Beth has held leadership roles providing advice on accounting, auditing, and regulatory matters. She was also previously responsible for executing on firm strategy regarding performance management, retention, global mobility, immigration matters, employee relations, and promotions in an additional role as a talent partner.
