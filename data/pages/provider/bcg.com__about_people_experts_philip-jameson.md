### Education
  * BA, Yale University


Philip Jameson is a senior expert in organizational change at Boston Consulting Group, where he serves companies undertaking transformation. He is a coauthor of BCG's new book **_How Change Really Works: Seven Science-Based Principles for Transforming Your Organization_** , published by Harvard Business Review Press and available May 19, 2026.
Philip’s work with BCG has taken him across industries and continents. He served as Chief of Staff for the Sydney Symphony Orchestra from 2018 to 2019, during a period of transformative change. A classical musician, he is the composer of works for orchestras, string quartets, and choirs. He is passionate about the history of economics and systems for group decision making, and draws on this knowledge to help leaders navigate complexity.
