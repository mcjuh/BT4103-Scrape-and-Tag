### Education
  * MBA, Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University
  * BComm, Queen's University


Mark Harris is co-leader of Boston Consulting Group's Principal Investors & Private Equity (PIPE) practice in Canada. He works with Canadian investors across asset classes to develop fund strategy, optimize fund operations, assess new investment opportunities, and unlock tangible value at their portfolio companies. He is an expert in portfolio acceleration, fund strategy and operations, and digital growth and transformation.
Mark is the global lead for BCG’s work with pensions, and supports global pensions as they develop fund strategy and move up the maturity curve to become world-class global investors. He is currently focused on helping funds determine how digital innovation and analytics will shape their fund strategy and ultimately unlock value in their portfolios. Before joining the firm, Mark was an analyst with Scotiabank's Private Client Group.
