AT A GLANCE 
Mark Mondello joined Kroll (formerly Duff & Phelps) in 2005. He is a Managing Director in the Los Angeles office and part of the firm's Valuation Advisory practice. Mark also leads Kroll Entertainment and Media industry group. He has more than 25 years of corporate finance experience.
Prior to joining Duff & Phelps, Mark was a managing director with Standard & Poor's Corporate Value Consulting where he worked for 13 years. He started his professional career with Drexel Burnham Lambert, an investment bank based in Beverly Hills, California. Mark specializes in directing valuation studies of businesses and interests in businesses, intangible assets and intellectual property. His expertise in entertainment and media has led Mark to play a key role in numerous high profile transactions in this industry over the last fifteen years. In addition, he has provided services to many of the world's leading entertainment and media companies Mark holds an M.B.A. in finance from The Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania and a B.A. in economics from the University of California at Los Angeles.
