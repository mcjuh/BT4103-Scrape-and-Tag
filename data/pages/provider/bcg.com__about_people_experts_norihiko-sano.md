Norihiko Sano joined Boston Consulting Group in 2008. He is a core member of the firm’s Consumer, Climate & Sustainability, and Social Impact practices.
Norihiko has worked on many projects covering technology and innovation strategies, as well as industry collaborations, to solve climate change and sustainability issues, particularly in food systems.
