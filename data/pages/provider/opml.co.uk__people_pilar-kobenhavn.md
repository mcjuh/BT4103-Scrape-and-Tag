Pilar Kobenhavn (Oben) is a landscape architect and biodiversity specialist with over eight years of experience in landscape architecture, environmental planning, and biodiversity risk management, supporting infrastructure and energy projects across Indonesia. 
Most recently, he supported the World Bank and BAPPENAS under the GEF-funded Sustainable Cities Impact Program (SCIP), helping to mainstream climate resilience and biodiversity conservation into Indonesia's national Feasibility Study (FS) Guidelines and Assessment Criteria. 
He holds a Master's degree in Landscape Architecture from The University of Manchester and is particularly interested in advancing nature-positive infrastructure through the application of biodiversity safeguards and international standards.
