Sophie Gebel is a core member of Boston Consulting Group's Consumer Products (CP) and Marketing, Sales & Pricing (MSP) practices, with a focus on consumer demand and innovation.
Since joining BCG in 2014, she has been helping fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) clients build their growth strategies, drive consumer-centric and deep tech innovations, define go-to-market strategies, optimize portfolios, and run overall business transformations. Sophie is a member of BCG’s Center for Customer Insights, and has developed an expertise on food and beverage and personal care topics, in France and globally.
Prior to joining BCG, Sophie worked in the Sales & Marketing Graduate Programme of Reckitt Benckiser: as a sales rep and in trade marketing (France), and as a brand manager (UK).
