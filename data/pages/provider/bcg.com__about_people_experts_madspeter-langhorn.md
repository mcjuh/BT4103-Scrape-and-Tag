### Education
  * MSc, economics and business administration, Copenhagen Business School


Mads Peter Langhorn is an expert on the corporate strategy and shareholder value creation agenda at Boston Consulting Group. He is a core member of the Corporate Finance & Strategy practice, within which he is also part of the Europe, Middle East, and South America (EMESA) leadership team. Mads is also a member of the firm’s Travel, Cities & Infrastructure; Industrial Goods; and Principal Investors & Private Equity practices. In addition, he leads BCG’s relationships with several Nordic-based green infrastructure and infrastructure investor clients.
Since joining BCG in 2011, Mads has focused on strategy and transformation programs — performing portfolio reviews, helping shape growth agendas, and delivering large shareholder value acceleration programs. In recent years, he has concentrated on intensive green energy transition projects serving leading infrastructure investors and operators.
Prior to joining BCG, Mads worked in investment banking.
