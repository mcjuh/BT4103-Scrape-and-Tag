Trisa R Masria (Nita) is a Social Safeguards Specialist at Oxford Policy Management (OPM) with more than 20 years of experience supporting infrastructure, renewable energy, environmental management, biodiversity conservation, and community development programmes across Indonesia. Holding Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Law, she specialises in environmental and social safeguards, stakeholder engagement, social risk assessment, grievance redress mechanisms (GRM), gender, Indigenous Peoples and customary land issues, livelihood restoration, social inclusion, community health and safety, pollution prevention, and ESG-related frameworks.
