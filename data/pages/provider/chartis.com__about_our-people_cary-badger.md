# Cary Badger
Partner, HealthScape Advisors 
Cary Badger is a Partner at HealthScape Advisors, a Chartis Company. Cary has over 25 years of healthcare industry experience with deep Medicare Advantage expertise, health plan operations, product development, marketing and sales, planning and business development. ​
Cary has assisted clients with market expansion and acquisition strategies, strategic partnerships, build/buy/outsourcing, new market and product entry, and overall MA and commercial plan performance improvement.​ 
Prior to joining HealthScape, Cary most recently led the business and market development of four regional health plans at The Regence/Cambia Health to create new markets for growth, lower cost of acquisition, and implementation of strategies that prepare the organization for major shifts in government program policy. ​ 
Cary also served as founding National Director of Marketing and Database Services at AARP Services, Inc. and held leadership roles for marketing, sales, and health plan operations for Kaiser Permanente. ​ 
Cary has an undergraduate degree in microbiology from the University of Kansas, a master's degree in public health from the University of Tennessee and an Executive MBA from Stanford University. He currently serves as a Trustee for the Washington Health Benefits Trust based in Seattle, Washington.

###  What do you hope to help our clients achieve? 
In all my engagements, I strive to provide clients with objective and honest advice on difficult issues, executable solutions, and a deep understanding of how to get things done within the context of their business. No lofty aspirations, just practical execution to solve or mitigate a healthcare issue or problem, leveraging my years of experience in their shoes, if possible. 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
Coaching and inspiring our newer consultants as to what they can really achieve and learn in this business as a byproduct of their client work. Never run away from a problem you don’t understand but rather run towards it. 
###  How do your personal goals align to Chartis’ mission and vision? 
I aspire to continue to innovate and make our work at Chartis relevant and meaningful to any client we engage. Even though we solve similar challenges across our clientele and markets, every client is special and unique in their specific situation.
