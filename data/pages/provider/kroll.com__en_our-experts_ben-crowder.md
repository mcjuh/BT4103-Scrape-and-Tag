AT A GLANCE 
Ben Crowder is a Managing Director in the Expert Services practice, based in London. He is a Chartered Materials and Corrosion Engineer with over 10 years of experience in the energy sector.
Ben specializes in technical disputes work focused on materials selection, corrosion, welding, and mechanical integrity and has been engaged in a variety of projects including onshore and offshore oil and gas projects as well as renewable energy projects including hydrogen pipelines, solar farms and wind farms.
Ben commenced his career serving as a materials and corrosion engineer, performing materials and welding research, corrosion modelling, and providing technical expertise for projects located in Africa, Asia, Eurasia, Europe, the Middle East, North America and South America. He subsequently acquired practical experience working in an operating facility to support maintenance and turnarounds as a mechanical integrity consultant.
Prior to joining Kroll, Ben was an associate director at Blackrock Expert Services, which was acquired by Kroll in 2020.
Prior to joining Blackrock Expert Services, Ben was appointed as the materials, corrosion and welding technical authority for a major capital project, supporting the project from concept development to construction. Ben now applies his experience and knowledge in the dispute resolution field of international arbitration and litigation.
Ben is a Chartered Engineer, with a materials engineering degree from New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology, and a M.S. in construction law and dispute resolution from King’s College London.
