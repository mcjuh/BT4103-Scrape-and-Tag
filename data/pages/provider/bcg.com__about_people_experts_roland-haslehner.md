Roland Haslehner on Digitizing in Project-Based Industries
BCG’s Roland Haslehner explains why companies should attempt a digital transformation right now. The benefits of real-time optimization and delivering mega-projects on time and on budget are huge, and companies are often surprised at how much data they already have on hand.
### Education
  * MBA, business informatics, Johannes Kepler University of Linz


### Honors and Awards
  * Scholarship, Johannes Kepler University of Linz


Roland Haslehner joined Boston Consulting Group in 2002. He is part of the firm's global Climate & Sustainability leadership team and focuses on growth transformation for industrial goods companies. Previously, he led the greentech, equipment, and infrastructure business on a global level, and was responsible for the scale-up of the Eastern European offices across all business lines.
Roland’s extensive knowledge of engineered products, infrastructure, and metals and mining make him a valuable asset on strategic and operational projects. He has led numerous transformation and restructuring programs for family-owned businesses and major global corporations.
Roland worked for Procter & Gamble for five years before joining BCG.
