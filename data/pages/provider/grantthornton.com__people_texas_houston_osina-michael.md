## Executive summary
Michael Osina is a Corporate Tax Solutions Partner in Grant Thornton’s Houston office and has more than 20 years of public accounting experience and joined the firm in 2004. His areas of specialization include tax consulting, tax compliance, and ASC 740 tax provision services with an emphasis in the oil and gas industry.
