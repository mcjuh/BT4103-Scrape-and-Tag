# Sek-loong Tan
Managing Director & Partner [ Melbourne ](https://www.bcg.com/offices/melbourne/default)
  * [Digital, Technology, and Data](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/digital-technology-data/overview)


### Education
  * Master of Public Administration, Harvard Kennedy School
  * Master of Engineering, University of Cambridge
  * Bachelor of Arts (Engineering), University of Cambridge


Sek-loong Tan joined Boston Consulting Group in 2007 in London, and has since been based in BCG’s Melbourne, Mumbai, Kuala Lumpur, and Canberra offices. He works with government and business leaders to design and deliver on ambitious reforms. Sek has substantial experience in strategy, data and digital transformation, organizational effectiveness, and navigating geopolitical disruption. He has worked extensively with governments across Australia, Asia, and Europe on economics, skills, trade, energy, and transport.
Sek previously advised companies in a range of private sector industries including energy; financial services; and technology, media, and telecom. Before joining the firm, he was a co-founder and Director of Operations and Strategy at Teach for Australia, and senior advisor to the CEO of Asialink in Australia, where he helped establish Asialink Business.
