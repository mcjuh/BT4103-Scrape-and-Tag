## Executive summary
Cory Perry is a partner in Grant Thornton’s Washington National Tax Office, where he leads the firm’s international tax technical practice and serves as the Pillar Two service line leader. He advises multinational companies on a wide range of cross-border tax matters, including international restructuring, subpart F, GILTI/Net CFC tested income, Pillar Two implementation, foreign tax credits, and M&A transactions. His work centers on technical analysis, international tax modeling, and helping clients navigate and adapt to ongoing global tax reform.
Cory regularly assists multinational organizations in evaluating structural changes and preparing for compliance with evolving international tax rules. He also co‑chairs Grant Thornton’s Global International Tax Steering Committee, providing strategic oversight for how member firms collaborate across jurisdictions and contributing to the development of the firm’s global tax strategy. He additionally serves as chair of the AICPA’s International Tax Resource Panel and its OECD Task Force, where he helps guide the profession’s engagement with emerging international standards.
He is a frequent speaker and contributor on key topics such as tax reform, Pillar Two, subpart F, and other global tax developments. Cory has published articles with national journals and organizations, and he works extensively with clients in the financial services, technology, and manufacturing sectors, including large banks, hedge funds, and private equity firms.
