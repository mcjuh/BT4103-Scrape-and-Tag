### Education
  * MBA, University of Southern California, Marshall School of Business
  * BA, law, Keio University


Daisuke Sohta joined Boston Consulting Group in 2012. He worked for one year in BCG’s Düsseldorf office before moving to Tokyo and Nagoya. Daisuke leads BCG’s work in aerospace and defense in the Asia Pacific region. He is a core member of the firm’s Industrial Goods and Operations practices.
Daisuke has worked with clients in aerospace and defense, machinery, and automation industries on transformation, business model transformation, corporate governance, global business strategy, and procurement reform.
Prior to joining BCG, Daisuke worked at Mitsubishi Corporation.
