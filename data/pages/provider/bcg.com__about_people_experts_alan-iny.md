### Education
  * MBA, management and social enterprise, Columbia Business School
  * BS, honors, McGill University


Alan Iny has the pleasure of spending his entire working life on creativity in business. With Boston Consulting Group since 2003, he has trained thousands of people on how to think creatively, working with a wide range of clients across industries worldwide. He has a deep focus on navigating uncertainty by using scenarios to help clients think more expansively about the future. In 2025, Alan began a three-year research fellowship with the BCG Institute, exploring "the future of thinking about the future," while continuing his client work.
Alan is a member of the firm’s Corporate Finance & Strategy practice leadership team, and has expertise in innovation, transformation, and organization design. With BCG’s Luc de Brabandere, he coauthored  _Thinking in New Boxes: A New Paradigm for Business Creativity in_ 2013. The book is available in 12 languages, and Publishers Weekly called it “a must-read for anyone in a leadership position who dares to look at the world in new ways.” 
His writing with Luc de Brabandere has also appeared in various journals, including Strategy & Leadership and Technological Forecasting and Social Change. You can follow Alan on LinkedIn and subscribe to his newsletter, #WhatIfWednesday.
