### Education
  * MBA, strategy and finance, University of Texas, Dallas
  * MS, finance and MIS, University of Texas, Dallas
  * BE, chemical engineering, University of Bombay


Hardik Sheth is a leader of Boston Consulting Group's CFO Excellence practice area in North America, and a member of the Corporate Finance & Strategy and Insurance practices globally. Hardik has over 15 years of experience advising CFOs on a broad range of strategic topics so they can determine their vision, mission, strategy, organization structure, capability profiles, engagement models, and supporting processes and systems. 
Hardik has worked with clients in the insurance, financial services, life sciences, and technology industries on digitally enabled large-scale transformations, and has extensive experience in operating model redesigns, finance process and technology optimization, data and analytics, and performance management. 
In addition to BCG, Hardik has worked at Deloitte Consulting, where his focus was on helping companies transform across the enterprise, with a concentration on the middle and back office functions in the insurance, financial institutions, and technology industries.
