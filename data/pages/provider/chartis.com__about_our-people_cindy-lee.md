# Cindy Lee
Managing Partner and Chief Strategy Officer 
Cindy Lee serves as the firm’s Managing Partner and Chief Strategy Officer. Cindy advises healthcare leaders in crafting their futures through scenario planning that considers how the healthcare environment is likely to evolve in the coming years. She also specializes in organic and inorganic growth strategies, organizational design, and academic alignment/funds flow. She has worked with nationally leading academic health systems, children’s hospitals, and healthcare focused companies over the last 20 years. 
Before joining Chartis, Cindy was a Partner at Stockamp & Associates, Inc. (now part of Huron Consulting), a national healthcare consulting firm focused on revenue improvement and patient flow solutions. Prior to that, she was a consultant at CSC Healthcare and its predecessor, APM Management Consultants.
Cindy has authored and been featured in numerous articles and facilitates board planning sessions on key strategic and operational considerations for how to position the enterprise in an ever-evolving healthcare landscape.
Cindy has served on several civic boards in her community, focused on her interests in education, and community health and wellness. Cindy holds a Bachelor of Science degree in journalism and economics from Northwestern University.

###  Why healthcare? 
I love working with people to drive change and advancement, and at the core, that’s what healthcare is. In big ways and small, healthcare is meant to advance the health of our societies and the health of individuals. 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
I really enjoy helping people accomplish their goals—whether it’s my clients or my colleagues—and figuring out how to best engage people and the change management process to do so is what I find rewarding. 
###  When you’re not working, what would we find you doing? 
Something outdoors (skiing, running/hiking, concerts) but not gardening.
