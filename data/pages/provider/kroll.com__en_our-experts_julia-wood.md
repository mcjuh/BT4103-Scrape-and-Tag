# Julia Wood
##### Managing Director, Head of the Revenue Office, Risk Advisory
[Julia Wood](https://www.linkedin.com/in/julia-wood-006a4b7)[+31 20 851 5117](tel:+31%2020%20851%205117)Julia.Wood@kroll.com
AT A GLANCE 
Julia is a Managing Director and Head of the Revenue Office for Kroll’s Risk Advisory Business. She is also the Amsterdam city leader. 
Julia is responsible for sales strategy, driving organic growth and developing comprehensive solutions to help clients navigate uncertainty, manage risk, build resilience, and drive sustainable growth.
Julia has over 25 years’ experience of managing international business development and operations in professional and financial services industries. She was previously the Group General Manager of Borrelli Walsh, a leading Restructuring advisory firm, which was acquired by Kroll in 2020, where she managed the firm’s global operations. She was a member of the firm’s Executive Committee, responsible for international growth and business development.
Julia joined Borrelli Walsh in 2009 from a leading private bank, where she served as an Associate Director for new business development and delivery of the client experience in the Hong Kong market. Prior to that, she spent seven years with an international law firm, in both the Hong Kong and London offices, where she was responsible for business development and client relationship management for the Finance and Dispute Resolution Groups.
Julia is a Committee Member of Kroll’s global Network of Women, whose mission is to attract, retain and develop women and encourage a diverse and inclusive team of colleagues.
Julia has a post-graduate degree in Marketing from London Metropolitan University (formerly London Guildhall University) and a degree in Economics and Geography from the University of Exeter. She qualified as a Chartered Marketer in 2002 (Chartered Institute of Marketing, UK).
