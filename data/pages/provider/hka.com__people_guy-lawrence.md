### Expertise
Guy Lawrence is a quantity surveyor with over 35 years of construction industry experience. He has assisted the named quantum expert on 14 occasions.
Guy provides advice and support to parties and their legal representatives in adjudication and arbitration proceedings regarding both domestic and major international project disputes. He regularly acts as lead assistant to internationally renowned quantum experts and is widely experienced in the coordination of complex disputes and analysis to inform the expert’s report.
Guy specialises in all aspects of quantum including the preparation, analysis and submission of complex actual cost verification data and audits. He has a strong commercial and contractual background.

### Background
Guy has undertaken roles across all areas of quantity surveying including contract management, pre-contract procurement, tender analysis and post-contract financial administration and cost control. He has extensive industry experience ranging from rail, marine, superyachts and shipping, offshore and onshore oil and gas facilities, and floating production storage and offloading (FPSOs) to airports, civil engineering, buildings (retail, commercial, industrial, and housing), mechanical and electrical, and communications.
Guy is a member of the Chartered Institute of Building and a member of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators. He is a practising associate member of the Academy of Experts, a member of the Society of Construction Law and has completed an advanced professional award in expert witness evidence (LETAPAEWE).
