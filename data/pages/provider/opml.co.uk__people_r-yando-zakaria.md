R. Yando Zakaria is a distinguished anthropologist and social safeguards expert with more than three decades of experience in sustainable development, indigenous peoples' rights, natural resource governance, social inclusion, and community development. His extensive career spans advisory roles with the Government of Indonesia, multilateral development institutions, and international organizations, including the World Bank, Asian Development Bank (ADB), USAID, and UNDP. 
In addition, Yando has worked extensively with civil society organizations and NGO networks throughout his career, including AMAN (Indigenous Peoples Alliance of the Archipelago), Konsorsium Pembaruan Agraria (KPA), LATIN, INSIST, KARSA, and others. He is widely recognized for his expertise in environmental and social impact assessment, land acquisition and resettlement, indigenous peoples planning, conflict resolution, agrarian reform, and stakeholder engagement across infrastructure, forestry, energy, and rural development sectors. 
Yando has written over 150 articles published in mass media, academic journals, and conference papers, and has authored six books in Bahasa Indonesia, accessible through <https://independent.academia.edu/YandoZakaria>. Outside his professional work, he enjoys travelling and outdoor activities, which continually broaden his perspective on people and communities.
