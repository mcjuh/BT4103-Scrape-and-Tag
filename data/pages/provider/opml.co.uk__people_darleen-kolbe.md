Darleen is a social protection expert with a background in project management, programme evaluation and capacity-building. She specialises in the design and delivery of social protection systems, particularly focusing on gender- and shock-responsive approaches.
Prior to joining OPM, Darleen worked as a research assistant and lecturer at the University of Applied Sciences Bonn-Rhein-Sieg, where she taught courses on constitutive factors and comparative analysis of social protection systems. In this function, she also coordinated an international Master’s programme in Social Protection, and organized transfer formats such as seminars in Ghana, Tanzania, and Germany. Her consultancy experience includes advising organisations such as Save the Children, UNICEF, GIZ, Misereor and the Konrad-Adenauer foundation on the design and evaluation of social assistance programmes and capacity building for social protection practitioners.
Darleen holds a Master’s degree in Analysis and Design of Social Protection Systems from the University of Applied Sciences Bonn-Rhein-Sieg, and a Bachelor’s degree in International Business Administration from WHU – Otto Beisheim School of Management.

Project
###  [Global Alliances for Social Protection (GASP)](https://www.opml.co.uk/projects/global-alliances-social-protection-gasp "Go to Global Alliances for Social Protection \(GASP\)")
We manage a short-term expert pool for the GASP Programme. This enables rapid, low-bureaucracy deployment of experts to strengthen crisis-resilient, digital social protection systems. These experts support organisational development, capacity development, and institutional strengthening, with a strong focus on adaptive social protection (ASP) and public sector reform.
