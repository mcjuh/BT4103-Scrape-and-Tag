### Education
  * MBA, Washington University
  * BS, electrical engineering, Washington University


Sandeep Chugani leads Boston Consulting Group's office in Miami. He is a core member of the Transform, Consumer, and Operations practices. Sandeep has deep expertise in retail, operational effectiveness, and transformation and turnaround strategies. Previously, Sandeep led BCG’s retail work within the Consumer practice in North America as well as the Transformation and BCG TURN practices globally.
Since joining BCG in 2006, Sandeep has led a number of successful large-scale transformations across multiple consumer-related industries. He has worked with clients on issues related to growth strategy, organizational behavior, merchandise and marketing effectiveness, pricing strategy, global and indirect sourcing, logistics and supply chain strategy, and cash flow management and optimization. Before joining BCG, Sandeep was a senior partner at A.T. Kearney and a managing director (partner) at DiamondCluster International, working in the consumer products and retail practice.
