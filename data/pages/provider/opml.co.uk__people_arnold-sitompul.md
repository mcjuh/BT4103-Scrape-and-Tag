Dr. Arnold Sitompul leads the implementation team of the Low Carbon Development Initiative (LCDI) Programme. He provides strategic leadership and ensures the programme delivers to agreed impact. He is responsible for working with FCDO, Bappenas, and key line ministries to advance and coordinate LCDI’s objectives.
As an experienced conservation scientist with over 20 years of expertise in environment conservation, he has led many impactful conservation initiatives across Southeast Asia. Prior to joining us, he served as an Executive Director for the Conservation Science Initiative, Conservation Director at WWF where he developed natural resource conservation programme in Indonesia and Thailand. He has extensive experience in natural resource management, biodiversity conservation, nature-based solution initiative and climate change mitigation.
