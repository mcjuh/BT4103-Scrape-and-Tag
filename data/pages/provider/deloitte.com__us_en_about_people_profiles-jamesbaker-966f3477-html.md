#  James Baker 
##  Principal | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
Jamie Baker is a principal in Deloitte’s Strategy practice focused on financial services and real estate clients’ business model and operations challenges. Jamie serves a range of financial services, real estate, and banking clients and leads Deloitte Consulting’s Real Estate Industry practice. Baker has more than 20 years of industry and consulting experience working on a range of challenges, including helping clients evolve and configure their operating models, incorporate disruptive automation technologies, and execute a range of improvement or transformational initiatives.
