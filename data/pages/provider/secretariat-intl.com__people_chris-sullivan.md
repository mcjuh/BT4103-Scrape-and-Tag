## Chris Sullivan
#### Managing Director
Mr. Sullivan is a dynamic expert specializing in schedule delay and construction quantum. He is a responsive and reliable construction disputes professional with extensive on-site construction experience.
Burlington
[+1 978 790 4572](tel:+1%20978%20790%204572) csullivan@secretariat-intl.com
Chris Sullivan has a unique perspective on the needs and demands of project participants as he has direct experience as an owner, contractor, and designer. He brings this experience as he assists in resolving construction disputes and claims effectively, swiftly, and equitably.
Mr. Sullivan is here to help by providing expert construction services to assist you in managing construction projects, solving disputes, and mitigating risk. Known as an effective communicator, Mr. Sullivan has advised numerous construction projects focusing on schedule delay and construction quantum. Before joining Secretariat, he was a construction project controller for a major hospital in Boston and managed construction projects for several prominent New England contractors.
He specializes in schedule and delay analysis, quantum development and defense, productivity and labor analysis, project controls, construction cost analysis, contract administration, project and program management, and dispute resolution.
Mr. Sullivan’s industry experience includes healthcare, senior and assisted living, power plants, education, senior/student housing, mixed-use, transportation, commercial, industrial, historical renovations, utilities, water and wastewater, and disaster management.
Mr. Sullivan is lauded in  _Lexology Index,_ with the esteemed publication noting, “ _Chris Sullivan is described as ‘an exceptional claims consultant’ for his work on delay and quantum matters in complex construction disputes. His ‘remarkable ability to absorb and process large volumes of information’ is particularly valued by clients on healthcare, energy and transportation projects, among others.”_
