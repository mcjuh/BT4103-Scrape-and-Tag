# Bob Bartell, CFA
##### Vice Chairman
[Bob Bartell, CFA](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robertbartell/)[+1 312 697 4654](tel:+1%20312%20697%204654)bob.bartell@kroll.com
AT A GLANCE 
Bob Bartell, CFA, is the Vice Chairman of Kroll, based in Chicago.
As Vice Chairman, Bob provides strategic direction to the firm’s CEO and executive leadership team, manages key client and referral relationships for the firm and provides senior leadership and mentorship to many of his Kroll colleagues. Since joining the firm in 1997, Bob has held a variety of leadership positions, including President of Kroll Corporate Finance and CEO of Kroll Securities, LLC, a FINRA registered broker/dealer, global practice leader of the firm’s Fairness Opinions Practice and led the formation of the firm’s M&A advisory practice’s Financial Sponsors Group. During his tenure, Bob has specialized in a broad array of corporate finance advisory engagements, including fairness opinions, solvency opinions, M&A advisory, financial restructurings, ESOP’s, shareholder disputes, financial due diligence, strategic advisory and debt advisory.
Bob has extensive experience in cross-border [M&A](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/investment-banking/mergers-acquisitions-advisory) transactions. Notably, while working at Kroll’s London office from 2008-2010, he expanded the firm’s European Corporate Finance practice through a combination of acquisitions and strategic hires. Bob was also instrumental in opening the firm’s Corporate Finance branches in Brazil, China and India. He has advised on a number of going-private transactions involving UK, Chinese, French, Canadian and Brazilian companies listed on public stock exchanges. Before joining Kroll, Bob worked in the commercial banking department at BMO Harris. 
Bob holds an MBA from The University of Chicago Booth School of Business and a B.S. in agricultural economics from The University of Illinois-Champaign. He is a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) Charterholder and holds the FINRA Series 7, 79, 24 and 63 licenses. 
Bob has contributed his time and energy to many non-profit organizations including Camp Kesem, the National Kidney Foundation of Illinois’ Middle Market Open, Chicago Commonwealth Club, The Economic Club of Chicago, Crohn’s & Colitis Foundation, The President’s Council of the University of Notre Dame, The Chicago Blackhawks 1926 Honor Society and Lurie Children’s Hospital of Chicago.
