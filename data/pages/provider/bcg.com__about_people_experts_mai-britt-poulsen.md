Growth Mindset for Resilience Strategy
Nearly 75% of companies try to create growth in times of volatility and uncertainty, but a mere 14% actually deliver. BCG’s Mai-Britt Poulsen explains how leaders can bring strategic clarity and faster decision making to their organizations.

Mai-Britt Poulsen is the global leader of Boston Consulting Group's Consumer practice and a member of the firm’s global executive committee. Previously, Mai-Britt served as managing partner for BCG in the UK, Ireland, the Netherlands, and Belgium and as the firm’s global chief of staff. She has two decades of experience advising BCG clients worldwide.
Mai-Britt is also a core member and client service partner of the firm's Transform practice. She specializes in consumer goods and has significant experience in large-scale transformation and performance improvement programs, commercial go-to-market strategies, and other topics including leadership, change management, and enablement. She is passionate about diversity and inclusion and is actively involved in BCG’s partnership with UN Women.
Prior to BCG, Mai-Britt worked with Nordea Corporate Finance.
