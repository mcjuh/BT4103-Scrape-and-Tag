Saima has over two decades of wide-ranging experience in the education sector. She has worked in the development sector in senior leadership and advisory positions with reputed international organisations in Pakistan and Bangladesh. Since 2014 she has been working with FCDO in Pakistan as a senior education advisor, providing policy leadership and technical and operational support across the education portfolio. She has worked extensively in the areas of education policy and governance, systems strengthening and inclusive education. She has also designed and delivered programmes to improve learning at scale, providing technical assistance on curriculum, teaching and assessment. Prior to joining FCDO, Saima has worked with The Asia Foundation, Save the Children UK and Aga Khan Foundation, where she was leading strategy, partnerships, program design and implementation. She has a Masters in International Educational Development from Teachers College, Columbia University.
