AT A GLANCE 
Luis Maluf is Managing Director in the Fixed Asset Advisory Services practice, based in São Paulo. He has more than 30 years of experience in fixed asset appraisal and management.
Luis is an engineer and conducts and manages appraisal and analysis of useful lives of machinery, equipment and industrial installations for tariff revision, accounting, insurance, and buying and selling, among other areas. Luis also has extensive experience in the management and organization of fixed assets. He has worked in the following sectors: automobile, mining, electricity (generation, transmission and distribution), sanitation (water and sewage), oil and gas, telecommunications, food, pharmaceutical, sugar and alcohol, metallurgical and chemical.
Luis has been in his current position since 2010. Prior to joining American Appraisal (now Kroll), Luis worked at Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu, in Financial Advisory Services, as a senior manager.
Luis holds a B.E. in mechanical engineering from the Mackenzie Presbyterian University, São Paulo. He also holds an MBA from the Getulio Vargas Foundation's São Paulo School of Business Administration.
