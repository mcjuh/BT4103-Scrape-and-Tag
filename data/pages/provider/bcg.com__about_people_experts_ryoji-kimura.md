### Education
  * MBA, Dean’s List, HEC Paris School of Management
  * Specialized master in international risk management, HEC Paris School of Management
  * BA, Kyoto University


Ryoji Kimura is a core member of Boston Consulting Group’s Technology, Media & Telecommunications; Consumer; and Transform practices. He is the Asia Pacific People Team chair, and previously served as the global lead of the firm’s Corporate Finance & Strategy practice until August 2023.
Ryoji works closely with clients primarily in the manufacturing, telecommunications, and media industries. His experience spans corporate and business strategy, new business building, scenario-planning, large-scale transformation, and turnaround.
Prior to joining BCG, he worked as a loan officer for North Africa at Japan Bank for International Cooperation.
