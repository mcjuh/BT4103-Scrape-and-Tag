Big Data: The Transformation Imperative
An effective big data approach is no longer just the responsibility of IT; the entire business must undergo transformation. Elias Baltassis discusses how BCG can help companies define their data vision and strategy—and discover value-creating change.
### Education
  * MBA, INSEAD
  * Master’s degree, applied mathematics and computer sciences, Swiss Federal Institute of Technology (EPFL)
  * Bachelor’s degree, economics and econometrics, University of Fribourg


Elias Baltassis specializes in artificial intelligence and data and analytics at Boston Consulting Group. He is a core member of the Technology Advantage practice and of BCG X, the firm's tech build and design unit.
Elias leads analytics and AI projects across many industries and functions, and regularly works in areas such as data strategy, data governance, and data regulation. He works with clients in several European countries and the US. 
Elias has published numerous articles on these topics and is a regular speaker at leading AI industry events and conferences. He is also a lecturer at INSEAD on the topic “AI in Business.” 
Elias has more than 20 years of experience in quantitative and data-driven topics. Prior to joining BCG, he was a founding member and managing director of Opera Solutions, a leading global pure-player in data and analytics. Prior to that, he was a Partner with Bain & Co.
