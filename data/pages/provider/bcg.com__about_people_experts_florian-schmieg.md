### Education
  * MBA, INSEAD
  * BA, Business Information Systems, DHBW Stuttgart


Florian Schmieg is a leader in the Technology, Media & Telecommunications (TMT) and Principal Investors & Private Equity (PIPE) practices at Boston Consulting Group. With more than 20 years of experience in the software and technology industries, Florian advises senior executives on value creation, growth strategy, go-to-market acceleration, and due diligence.
Florian leads BCG's tech capital business in Europe, the Middle East, South America, and Africa. He partners with private equity funds along the full cycle of their technology and software investments. In addition, he chairs BCG’s corporate venture capital business in Central Europe, and has set up multiple venturing units around the globe and written numerous publications on corporate venturing.
Prior to joining the firm, Florian worked at Siemens/Siemens Venture Capital in the US and Germany, building up an internal software startup in the industrial space.
