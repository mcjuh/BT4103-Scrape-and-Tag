Drew Baiter specializes in consulting in delay analysis and time-related disputes on large complex construction projects, including infrastructure, transportation, educational, residential, commercial, hospitality, and industrial facilities construction projects. Mr. Baiter also serves as arbitrator for construction disputes in arbitration.
From 2002 to 2014, Mr. Baiter worked as a construction lawyer – first in the construction practice group of a prestigious international law firm and then for one of the largest developers in the Middle East and North Africa. In 2014, Mr. Baiter joined Secretariat, where he focused on performing critical delay analysis for complex construction projects worldwide.
_Lexology Index_(formerly _Who’s Who Legal)_ recognizes Mr. Baiter as a “Global Leader – Arbitration Expert Witnesses 2025.” According to _Lexology Index_ , peers and clients say: “Drew’s submissions are always thorough, yet clear and concise,” and “He possesses strong legal skills and understands the nuances of construction disputes.”
Mr. Baiter was honored as part of the 2024 and 2025 Lexology Client Choice Awards in the category of Arbitration Expert Witness (Mexico). He is a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators and an Arbitrator Panelist on the Construction Panel of the American Arbitration Association.
