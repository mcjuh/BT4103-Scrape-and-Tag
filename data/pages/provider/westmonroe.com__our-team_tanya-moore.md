As Chief People Officer, Tanya drives initiatives that empower West Monroe’s employees and foster a diverse, supportive culture. Tanya partners with leadership to develop the next generation of leaders, ensuring our people are fulfilled and our employee experience remains a key differentiator. 
Before joining West Monroe in 2023, Tanya was Chief People Officer at M.C. Dean and spent two decades with IBM, where she led award-winning programs that shaped the company’s transformation. She holds an MBA in organizational development from the College of William and Mary. 
## _Tanya’s All-In Mindset_
Tanya’s passion for HR started early in her career when she improved workplace culture through grassroots initiatives. Outside of work, she serves on multiple advisory boards, including the Conference Board’s CHRO Council and William and Mary’s Consulting Board.
