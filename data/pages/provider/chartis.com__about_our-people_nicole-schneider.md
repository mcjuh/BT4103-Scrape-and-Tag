# Nicole Schneider
Partner 
Nicole Schneider is a Partner at Chartis in Digital & Technology Transformation. Her career in healthcare started in Implementation Services at Epic and has spanned over 15 years, 8 of which have been in healthcare consulting. Her previous work with academic medical centers, children’s hospitals, community hospitals, and healthcare systems include IT and operational support through Epic implementations, operational readiness programs, optimization, and clinical decision support design and reengineering.
Nicole received her Master of Healthcare Administration from the University of Iowa, and her Bachelor of Environmental Design from Texas A&M University.
