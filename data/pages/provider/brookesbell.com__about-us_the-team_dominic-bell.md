## Career Summary
[Curriculum Vitae](https://www.brookesbell.com/fileadmin/uploads/brookesbell/CVs/BELL_CV.pdf)
Dominic Bell is a Master Mariner with command experience. He joined Brookes Bell as a marine consultant in January 2020, became Senior Master Mariner in October 2022 and Principal Master Mariner in July 2024. Previously, Dominic held a position as Marine Manager with Seatruck Ferries Ltd. and sailed as Master with the Isle of Man Steam Packet Company Ltd. During his 20 years at sea, he sailed on various ship types, including freight ferries, Ro-Pax and high-speed passenger ferries and various naval auxiliaries. 
## Background
Dominic has considerable ship management experience as a Marine Manager, Designated Person Ashore, Company Security Officer and Crisis Manager. 
He has extensive pilotage and ship-handling experience on various ship types, having served as a marine pilot and held pilotage exemption certificates as Master.
Dominic has carried out a variety of survey and opinion-based work since joining Brookes Bell - listed at the end of this CV. He leads Brookes Bell’s electronic navigation analysis services and has prepared replays of navigational incidents using the Company’s Marine Accident Data Analysis Suite (MADAS), which have been used during Arbitration and Court proceedings in the UK.
Dominic is regularly instructed by ship owners and management companies to advise on operations, undertake accident investigations, vessel inspections and navigation assessments. 
He has prepared technical reports and given evidence as an expert witness in Arbitration and Court proceedings in the UK and has experience participating in Mediation. 
In respect of Offshore Renewable Energy Installations (OREIs), Dominic has collaborated with other consultancies and developers, providing expert advice and assistance on the subject of Shipping and Navigation, including Navigation Risk Assessments (NRAs), and participated in a number of Planning Inspectorate public hearings.
