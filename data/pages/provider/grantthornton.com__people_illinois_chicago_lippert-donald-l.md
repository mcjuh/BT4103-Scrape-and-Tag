## Executive summary
Don Lippert is a partner with Grant Thornton specializing in property tax services. He has over 25 years of professional experience. Don has comprehensive experience in personal property and real estate valuation services and is the National Leader for property tax services at Grant Thornton. He has performed due diligence on multi-billion dollar utility projects and served as the National Deployment Leader of a Pollution Control Initiative, which reduced clients’ taxable base by taking exemptions from the market of their pollution-control equipment. Prior to joining Grant Thornton, Don was a manager at General Electric where he oversaw all aspects of the property tax process for their Energy, Healthcare, Water and Industrial businesses. Under his direction, these businesses achieved over $105 million in property tax savings. He worked at KPMG prior to his time at GE.
