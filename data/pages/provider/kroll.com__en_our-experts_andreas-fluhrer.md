# Andreas Fluhrer
##### Managing Director and Co-Head of European Restructuring
[Andreas Fluhrer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/afluhrer)[+49 89388884016](tel:+49%2089388884016)andreas.fluhrer@kroll.com
AT A GLANCE 
Andreas Fluhrer is Managing Director and Co-Head of European Restructuring at Kroll, based in Munich.
Andreas has over 20 years of professional experience in providing corporate finance services to a wide range of stakeholders (corporates, investors, banks, family offices) across EMEA and LATAM. Andreas specializes in leading companies through transformation and restructuring situations and has served as Advisor, Executive Chairman, CEO, CFO, CTO, CRO and independent board member across various industries including, manufacturing, construction, life science, and heavy industries.
Prior to joining Kroll, Andreas held the position of Senior Managing Director in FTI Consulting's Corporate Finance practice. Prior to that, Andreas was with McKinsey’s Recovery and Transformation Practice (RTS) in Madrid; and prior to that, he was part of A&M’s Restructuring Practice in Spain.
