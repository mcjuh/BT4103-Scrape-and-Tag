# Thomas Choquet
##### Director
[Issuer Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/issuer-services)
thomas.choquet@kroll.com
Thomas Choquet is a Director in the [Issuer Services](https://www.kroll.com/en/services/issuer-services) practice, based in Brussels.
With over 20 years of experience, Thomas specializes in structuring and executing corporate actions in both international and domestic markets, with a focus on complex and high-volume transactions such as debt restructurings and reorganizations.
Prior to joining Kroll, Thomas worked for The Bank of New York Mellon and Euroclear Bank. Thomas holds a bachelor's degree in business economics from HEMBC Mons.
