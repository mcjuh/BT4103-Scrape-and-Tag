Dewi Sudharta serves as the Deputy Team Leader - Low Carbon Development Initiative programme, providing senior leadership to ensure the programme's high-quality delivery, foster strong relationships with the Government of Indonesia and FCDO staff and drive the achievement of programme goals.
With over 15 years of experience in the development sector, her expertise spans governance, policy-making, and stakeholder management across areas such as poverty reduction, community empowerment, education, health, and climate. In past, Dewi has led diverse, multicultural teams and worked closely with Indonesian think tanks to support the Government of Indonesia’s efforts in transforming its marine and coastal economy in response to climate change.
