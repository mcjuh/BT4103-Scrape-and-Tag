# Judd Schneider
##### Managing Director and Tax Valuation Services Leader
[Judd Schneider](https://www.linkedin.com/in/judd-schneider-110a551/)[+1 617 378 9423](tel:+1%20617%20378%209423)judd.schneider@kroll.com
AT A GLANCE 
Judd Schneider is a Managing Director and Tax Valuation Services Leader in the Valuation Advisory Services practice, based in Boston. He also serves as the Boston Office city leader. 
Judd leverages more than 20 years of experience in the valuation of business enterprises, legal entities, debt and equity securities, intangible assets and intellectual property for the purposes of tax planning and reporting, financial reporting, merger and acquisition planning, tax reorganizations and restructurings, and intercompany transfer pricing. He has led complex valuation assignments for clients across industries ranging in size from family-owned or PE-backed domestic companies to large publicly traded multinational companies, including Fortune 500 organizations. 
Judd has considerable expertise in analyzing and valuing intangible assets for organizations in various industries, including industrials, consumer products, technology, and gaming. Judd provides clients with advisory services related to pre- and post-transaction in accordance with ASC 805 (and IFRS 3) purchase price allocations; net assets and invested capital of companies’ reporting units in connection with impairment testing pursuant to ASC 350 (and IAS 36); legal entities for internal tax reorganizations, tax planning and reporting, as well as tax controversy support; equity securities for gift and estate tax and business planning purposes; complex preferred equity instruments; and general corporate finance consulting.
Judd holds a B.S. in finance and economics from the University of Florida. He is also a Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA) charterholder and a member of the CFA Society Boston. 
Judd has co-founded StopLeukemia.org, a non-profit organization designed to provide personalized support to individuals impacted by leukemia.
