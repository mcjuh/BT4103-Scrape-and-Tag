AT A GLANCE 
Jerry Cotellesse is Senior Vice President of Enterprise Risk Retainer services in the Cyber and Data Resilience business, based in Philadelphia.
Jerry leverages more than 20 years of experience working on national security matters to co-lead Kroll’s Enterprise Risk Retainer services, enabling organizations to mitigate potential risk and respond more swiftly, flexibly and effectively to rapidly changing cyber threats.
Prior to joining Kroll, Jerry worked for the FBI's Philadelphia Field Office, where he served as a supervisory special agent on the Cyber Squad and was responsible for high-profile complex cyber investigations and intelligence matters, including those involving nation-states that threaten U.S. critical infrastructure, intellectual property and national security.
Before that, Jerry served as a supervisory special agent for the FBI, assigned to the Washington, D.C., Cyber Division. He managed the cyber program and overall strategy for various field offices, including global cyber investigations. This role required overseas assignments designed to foster long-term relationships with key partners. His previous role was in complex cyber investigations in Newark, New Jersey, where he worked as a special agent.
Prior to the FBI, Jerry worked as a computer consultant for a multitude of industries in the Philadelphia region. Jerry holds a B.S. in finance and a master’s degree in cybersecurity, as well as Certified Information Systems Security Professional.
