### Education
  * MBA, INSEAD

Prashant Choudhary is a core member of Boston Consulting Group’s Consumer; Technology, Media & Telecommunications; and Global Advantage practices in Asia Pacific. He advises consumer goods and telecommunications clients on strategy, go-to-market (route-to-market model design, digitization, sales and distribution, marketing, pricing, and customer support) within B2B and B2C contexts, as well as consumer insights, and revenue management. He is the firm’s GTM topic leader for Southeast Asia , and currently serves as the learning and development lead for consumer goods.
Prior to joining BCG, Prashant was with Procter & Gamble in Singapore and in India.
