# Daniel DePonte
Partner 
Daniel DePonte is a Partner in Strategic Transformation. He has over 10 years of consulting experience providing strategic planning and advisory services to hospitals, large health systems, and academic medical centers focused exclusively on the oncology service line. Daniel brings specific expertise in custom analytics and leverages seasoned financial acumen to guide his clients through complex strategic issues.
Daniel’s recent engagements include physician alignment and transaction design for one of the largest multi-specialty oncology physician practices in the country and completing a business plan for a client’s $100m investment in new cancer facilities and programs. He also facilitated strategic planning for several regional integrated delivery networks focused on network development and revenue optimization. Daniel is a leader within the practice’s analytics team, expanding the firm’s understanding of clinical and business data and developing the proprietary models our clients use to guide decision-making.
Before joining Chartis, Daniel worked in the telecommunications industry, holding various finance positions in both small-cap and Fortune 50 firms. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Finance and Management from Quinnipiac University.
