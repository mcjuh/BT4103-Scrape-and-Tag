### Expertise
Tony Mauritz is a Chartered Accountant with over ten years of experience. He has assisted the named expert on numerous occasions. 
Tony has provided litigation support on a range of financial and accounting matters. He has assisted clients with shareholder disputes, business interruptions, minority interest valuations, accounting irregularities, and investigations supporting collective actions. 
Tony’s expertise includes leading investigations into accounting irregularities, economic crimes, and corruption in both domestic and international cases. He performs financial analyses on bankrupt companies, identifying red flags that may indicate fraud or irregularities and procedures to support trustees in the causation investigation. 
### Background
Tony’s experience covers analyzing post-acquisition disputes in international settings, where he evaluates appropriate balances and interprets share purchase agreements. He is proficient in calculating lost profits in complex corporate contexts and conducts event studies to statistically assess shareholder damages following negative media exposure. 
Tony holds a master’s in accounting and finance, and a postgraduate degree in financial auditing. He is a member of the Royal Netherlands Institute of Chartered Accountants and is bilingual in Dutch and English.
