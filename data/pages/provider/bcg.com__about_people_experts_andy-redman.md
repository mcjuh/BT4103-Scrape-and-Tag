# Andy Redman
Managing Director, BCG BrightHouse [ Atlanta ](https://www.bcg.com/offices/atlanta/default)
  * [Organization Strategy](https://www.bcg.com/capabilities/organization-strategy/overview)


### Education
  * MPhil, technology policy, Judge Business School, University of Cambridge
  * MPA, LBJ School of Public Affairs, University of Texas at Austin
  * BSc, business, Auburn University


Andy Redman is a member of the leadership team at BCG BrightHouse, Boston Consulting Group's global creative consultancy that helps organizations uncover their timeless purpose so they can grow their people, profits, and social impact. He is also on the leadership team for education in North America, and is a core member of BCG’s Public Sector and Social Impact practices, as well as BCG Fed.
Since joining the firm in 2018, Andy has led more than 45 engagements in ten countries, including creating the strategy and blueprint to reimagine the experience at a top 20 US university; leading a high-growth government ministry to set new vision, mission, values, and behaviors; creating strategic communications during several large-scale government transformations; and leading a strategy engagement for an urban redevelopment project. He has also articulated and activated purpose for companies across various industries (transportation, government, technology, agriculture, and finance).
Prior to BCG BrightHouse, Andy spent a decade working in education, during which time he led teams at Teach For America and OneGoal to dramatically grow and scale operations while consistently achieving ambitious results. He began his career as a public sector consultant, working with more than a dozen clients in the UK and US on business strategy and performance management. He is an active community leader, recently serving as the board chair of a charter school network (the Kindezi Schools).
