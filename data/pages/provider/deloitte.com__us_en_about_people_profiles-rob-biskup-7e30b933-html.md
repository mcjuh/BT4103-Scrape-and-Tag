#  Rob Biskup 
##  Managing Director | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
Robert T. Biskup is a Managing Director in Deloitte’s Regulatory, Forensics & Compliance practice, bringing over 30 years of experience in both corporate and professional services. As a national and regional leader in regulatory compliance, forensics, and FCPA, he leverages deep expertise from his tenure as global head of compliance and assistant general counsel at Ford Motor Company. In these roles, he led global compliance programs spanning Ford, its affiliates, and operations in 44 countries, and guided the company through numerous high-profile regulatory matters. At Deloitte, he advises clients on comprehensive compliance program design, board governance, risk management, internal investigations, and culture change. He has extensive international experience, particularly in high-risk regions, and frequently engages with boards and senior leaders on sensitive regulatory and investigative matters. His background includes practicing law with Sidley Austin LLP, further enriching his expertise in compliance and investigations.
