AT A GLANCE 
Federica is a Director in the Real Estate Advisory Group (REAG) in Italy and manages Hospitality Advisory Services.
She is responsible for the Hospitality and Leisure Business practice, providing:
  * Appraisals of hotels and health care facilities for acquisitions, disposal and financing
  * Appraisals of hotels and health care facilities as the independent valuer for real estate funds
  * Business planning of hotel and health care operation
  * Market analysis of the hotel environment
  * Feasibility Studies for new developments and refurbishments


Graduated in Business Administration in Management and Marketing in 1999, Federica attended a Master of Business Administration in Finance in 2002. Federica is a Member of Royal Institute of Chartered Surveyors since 2013 (MRICS).
