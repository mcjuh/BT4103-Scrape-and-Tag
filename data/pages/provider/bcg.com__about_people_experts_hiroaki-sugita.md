Hiroaki Sugita joined Boston Consulting Group in 1994. He is a senior member of the firm’s Consumer; Industrial Goods; Technology, Media & Telecom; Global Advantage; and People & Organization practices.
Hiroaki has worked closely with clients in a wide range of industries including consumer goods and services, media, telecommunications, automotive, and industrial goods. He has particular expertise in transformation, digitization, business strategy, new business development, corporate governance, and group management. He serves as a professor at Waseda University Business School.
