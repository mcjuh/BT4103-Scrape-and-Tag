Joe Saab is the lead for digital architecture, enterprise solutions platforms, and cybersecurity for BCG Platinion in Spanish-speaking South America. Platinion is a part of the Boston Consulting Group network that provides consulting services focused on the development and implementation of business-critical IT architecture and digital solutions.
Joe is currently focused on transforming the financial services and energy industries through digitization, cloud adoption, and next-gen ERP implementation. He leads the technology agenda for the Insurance practice in Iberia and South America, and is the cloud leader for BCG Platinion in Europe, the Middle East, South America, and Africa.
Prior to joining BCG Platinion, Joe was the CTO and Director for Value Creation at IBM in Mexico. Joe is a patent holder and long-time volunteer, passionate about finding technology solutions to help treat cancer.
