# Chris Regan
Managing Partner 
Chris Regan is a Founding Partner of Chartis and is a Managing Partner in Strategic Transformation, where he co-leads the Partnerships/M&A practice. He has a wide range of experience in the healthcare field, having led over 150 engagements for provider, payer, and life sciences clients across the country over his 35-year career. He has been instrumental in growing the firm to over 1,000 consultants over the past 25 years.
Chris has led enterprise strategic planning engagements for many of the nation’s leading national and regional health systems as well as several top-tier academic health systems. He also has served as the lead strategic advisor to the Board and senior management for numerous affiliation strategy/ M&A engagements involving community-based health systems, regional and national health systems, and academic health systems.
Prior to co-founding Chartis in 2001, Chris served as President of H-Works, the consulting division of The Advisory Board Company. Prior to that, he spent 15 years at CSC Healthcare, where he was one of thirty senior partners leading the firm’s healthcare consulting practice. At CSC, Chris played an integral role in growing the firm’s strategy and clinical resource management practices. Chris is a frequent speaker at industry conferences and Board retreats. He served for nine years as a trustee of Lawrence Hospital Center in Bronxville, NY, and for eighteen years as a Trustee of Ascension Ventures. He is a current Trustee of Hamilton College and Seacor Marine Holdings. Prior to joining CSC Healthcare, Chris worked for six years in the financial services industry.
Chris holds a Master of Business Administration in Finance from New York University's Stern School of Business and a Bachelor of Arts in English Literature from Hamilton College.

###  Why did you pick Chartis? 
I was initially drawn to the opportunity to work on the industry’s more pressing challenges with super bright, highly motivated colleagues, and I continue to be inspired by the belief that through our enduring mission commitment, we can make a difference in the quality of healthcare available to all. 
###  What has been your favorite project at Chartis and why? 
My favorite project at Chartis was when I worked on a national strategy for Ascension Health in 2012-13. We helped reposition all of their 23 health ministries for success amid changing market conditions. The project was at once national in scope and locally impactful as we worked closely with local leadership in every market. 
###  Best piece of advice you’ve ever been given? 
Be developmentally driven over the long term but don’t take yourself too seriously at any one moment.
