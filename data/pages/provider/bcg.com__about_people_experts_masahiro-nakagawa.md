### Education
  * MS, engineering, Waseda University

Masahiro Nakagawa joined Boston Consulting Group in 2021. He is a core member of BCG X—the firm’s tech build and design unit, as well as the Public Sector and Technology & Digital Advantage practices. He is a leader for GenAI in Japan.
Masahiro has worked with clients in industries such as consumer goods, retail, and insurance, as well as government organizations on topics related to digital. In particular, he has rich experience in formulating digital and data strategy, operational optimization using AI and analytics, launching new businesses utilizing digital, digital government, and sales digitization.
Prior to joining BCG, Masahiro worked at McKinsey & Company, SMBC Nikko Securities and at Accenture.
