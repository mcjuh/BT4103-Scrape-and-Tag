### Education
  * MBA, high distinction, Baker Scholar, Harvard Business School
  * BA, magna cum laude, Dartmouth College


Kaelin Goulet is a lead member of Boston Consulting Group’s Consumer and Principal Investors & Private Equity (PIPE) practices. Her primary casework is at the intersection of those two practices with a functional focus on strategy and marketing and sales. Her due diligence experience covers companies in food, beverage, consumer packaged goods value chain, personal care and beauty (skincare, color cosmetics, medical aesthetics), and fitness and recreation. Kaelin is part of the leadership teams for consumer within the PIPE practice and consumer transaction integration and excellence (TIE). She has also worked with many public sector clients on their post-COVID return, particularly on understanding shifts in consumer spending. She works with the BCG Henderson Institute, focusing on growth and portfolio strategy.
Since joining the firm in 2007, Kaelin has worked in the Chicago, San Francisco and, since 2013, New York offices.
