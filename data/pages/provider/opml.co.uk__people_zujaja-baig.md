Zujaja Baig is a development practitioner with expertise in quantitative and qualitative research, policy development, analysis and evidence based impact evaluations for policy with a particular interest in poverty. She has experience of conducting interviews, field work, community engagement, economic data analysis and impact evaluations across developing countries with organizations such as Innovations for Poverty Action and the World Bank. At OPM, her work focuses on robust research and analysis for adaptive social protection. 
Zujaja is a graduate of Georgetown University with a Master in International Development Policy and has strong skills in qualitative research, quantitative research and policy analysis.
## Projects involving Zujaja Baig
Project
###  [Scoping review on disability-inclusive social protection for climate action in rural areas](https://www.opml.co.uk/projects/scoping-review-disability-inclusive-social-protection-climate-action-rural-areas "Go to Scoping review on disability-inclusive social protection for climate action in rural areas")
We conducted a scoping review about how social protection programming can support disability-inclusive climate action in rural areas. This review brought together global knowledge on a new area of practice, and identified key recommendations for disability-inclusive social protection for rural climate action at the level of policy, programming, and implementation. This assignment was completed through FCDO’s STAAR facility.
