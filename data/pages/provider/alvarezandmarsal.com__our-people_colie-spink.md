Colie Spink is a Managing Director and Middle East Regional Leader, based out of Alvarez & Marsal's Middle East headquarters in Riyadh. He is also responsible for strategic growth initiatives across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA).
Mr. Spink brings extensive experience helping businesses deliver shareholder value through transactions, growth, transformation, and turnaround. He has been instrumental in extending A&M’s distinctive hands-on advisory services into the private equity arena. 
While at A&M initially, Mr. Spink founded and led A&M’s European Transaction Advisory Group, which provides financial due diligence and other accounting-related services to private equity clients. He then started and led A&M’s European Private Equity Performance Improvement practice, which helps clients identify, quantify, and deliver meaningful value creation initiatives in their portfolio investments.
Prior to re-joining A&M, Mr. Spink helped establish A&M Capital Europe (AMCE), serving as Co-Founder and Co-Managing Partner. AMCE is a €650 million private equity fund that makes control-oriented investments across Europe and the UK. It is independent from but leverages a close strategic relationship with Alvarez & Marsal to deliver exceptional returns for investors. 
Prior to this, Mr. Spink was an Operating Partner for Bank of America’s direct private equity business in Europe and co-founder and CEO of one of their portfolio investments. He previously founded and led Bank of America’s European Emerging Markets investment banking practice. 
Mr. Spink earned a bachelor’s degree from the University of Virginia and an MBA from the Kellogg School of Management at Northwestern University. He is a Trustee and Director of the Cartoon Museum in London, a European regional board member for the University of Virginia, and a Non-Executive Director of EMED Group Ltd., the UK’s leading health and social care logistics provider.
