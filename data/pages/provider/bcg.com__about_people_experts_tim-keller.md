Tim Keller is a Managing Director of BCG BrightHouse, a Boston Consulting Group company dedicated to helping clients mobilize their leaders and people through the power of meaning.
Tim is an expert in purpose-driven transformation, positioning, product development, and brand strategy. He works with major European and Asian companies to emotionalize their strategic direction and create momentum across all hierarchies and functions.
With more than 20 years of experience, he has consulted across a wide range of industries, including insurance, financial institutions, industrial goods, energy, media, technology, telecommunications, consumer goods, travel, and logistics.
Prior to joining BCG BrightHouse in 2021, he was Managing Director and Executive Director for Strategy & Innovation at several marketing strategy firms, including Accenture. He has delivered commercially effective and award-winning results for some of the world's leading companies, including US tech companies transforming the market, and German hidden champions and institutions in the process of transforming themselves.
