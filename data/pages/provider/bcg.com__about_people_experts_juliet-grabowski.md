### Education
  * MBA, Harvard Business School
  * Bachelor of Music/Management Studies Certificate, University of Rochester


Juliet Grabowski is a core member of Boston Consulting Group's finance leadership team, responsible for leading technology and transformation in the finance function. Prior to this leadership position, Juliet was a member of the Corporate Finance & Strategy and Financial Institutions practices. She also co-led BCG’s Center for CFO Excellence globally.
She has led multiple engagements across industries to help CFOs transform their organizations, advising clients on strategic topics including organizational and operating model design, end-to-end process optimization, cost reduction, agile transformation, and leader enablement. Before coming to BCG in 2011, Juliet was a Manager of Strategy & Analytics at Digitas.
