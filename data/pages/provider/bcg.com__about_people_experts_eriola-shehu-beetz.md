### Education
  * MBA, Harvard Business School
  * B Comm, finance, University of Toronto


Eriola Beetz is the global leader of commercial banking, and the Europe, Middle East, South America, and Africa (EMESA) leader for climate and sustainability in financial institutions, at Boston Consulting Group. Eri has significant expertise in capital markets and commercial banking across multiple topics, encompassing digital transformations and customer journeys, cost as advantage, and multiple climate topics for corporate and investment banks. She has published extensively on net zero topics, including supply chains, climate transformation, and how to tackle the nature challenge and its opportunities.
Prior to joining BCG, Eri worked in the Investment Banking Division of TD Securities in Toronto.
