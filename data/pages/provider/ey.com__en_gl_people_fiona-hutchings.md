As a partner in the EY-Parthenon UK&I Analytics and AI team, Fiona helps clients unlock growth and drive value from transactions, focusing on facilitating data-driven decision-making in support of M&A, divestitures and growth strategy. Fiona collaborates with clients addressing complex issues by blending financial analysis, operational knowledge, data science, emerging technologies and artificial intelligence (AI) to support diligence, integration, exit readiness and separation.
She has global experience across the transaction spectrum, including financial due diligence, valuation, strategy and advanced analytics. Fiona has advised top-tier private equity and corporate clients in the UK&I, the US and Australia.
Fiona has a Bachelor of Commerce in Accounting from the University of Melbourne, Australia, and is a chartered accountant with the Institute in Australia and New Zealand.
#### How Fiona is shaping the future with confidence
Fiona works with clients to help them navigate uncertainty around M&A transactions, leveraging data and commercial acumen to provide better outcomes. She is passionate about diversity and championing women working in M&A, often sharing her experiences at panels and industry events.
Fiona contributes to EY NextGen Women and has judged participants in the competition for the past three years. She enjoys supporting people early in their careers and sharing practical advice on what working in transactions is like day to day. For Fiona, it’s about helping others build confidence and see a path into the industry.
