### Expertise
Amanda Yeo is a quantity surveyor with over 20 years of experience. She has assisted the named expert on 18 occasions.
Amanda has supported with quantum matters at arbitration in UNCITRAL, SIAC, ICC, KLCRA, LCIA, PCA proceedings and litigation before the High Court and the Supreme Court. She has prepared expert reports on disputes with values of up to $55 million.
​​​Amanda specialises in quantum assessments for variations, loss and expense, head office overheads, loss of profits, interest charges, and depreciation costs. ​She has also assisted the delay expert in the preparation of a chronology of events based on the documentary evidence provided by the client.​
### Background
Amanda has a background in quantity surveying and cost management, contract administration, construction claims, and dispute management with international contractors and consultancies. Her experience includes projects in the buildings, infrastructure, power and utilities, industrial, and manufacturing industries.
Amanda holds master’s degrees in construction law and arbitration and international construction management, along with qualifications in building and construction economics, quantity surveying, and building services engineering. She is a member of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators.
