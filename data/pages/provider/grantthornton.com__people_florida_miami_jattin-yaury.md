## Executive summary
As a Partner in Grant Thornton’s Tax Reporting and Advisory Services Yaury provides a broad range of tax compliance and consulting services, primarily focused on federal corporate and partnership tax matters. Yaury serves a diverse client base, working with public and private companies in a variety of industries, including manufacturing, distribution, healthcare, technology and real estate.
Yaury has a specific concentration in ASC Topic 740: Accounting for Income Taxes. Her ASC 740 expertise includes preparing and auditing income tax provisions particularly for SEC registrants.
Yaury has 17 years of public accounting experience working with large corporate taxpayers on income tax compliance, provisions, and consulting. She works with corporate groups, both public and private, with multinational and multistate presence. Yaury has 6 years of experience leading a tax M&A practice, overseeing buy-side and sale-side tax due diligence, net after-tax cash transaction modeling and transaction structuring for a regional firm.
