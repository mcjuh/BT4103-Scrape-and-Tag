### Education
  * BS, physical sciences, University of Mumbai


Krishnan Ramachandran is a core member of the leadership team of Boston Consulting Group’s Tech & Digital Advantage practice. He is a technology leader and architect with over 30 years of experience driving business outcomes through strategic technology choices, modern IT architecture, cloud strategy, engineering practices, and implementation of next-generation technologies
Krishnan has worked with many global banks to drive their transformation priorities, including strategy and implementation of cloud transformation, architecting and implementing next-gen technology and data platform for driving technology, and operations efficiencies through modern architecture.
Before joining the firm in January 2019, Krishnan was Distinguished Engineer and Global CTO for Banking and Financial Markets at IBM Consulting. He holds several patents in the areas of digital technology and artificial intelligence.
