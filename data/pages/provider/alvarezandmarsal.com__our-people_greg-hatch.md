Greg Hatch is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Corporate Performance Improvement (CPI) in Houston. He leads CPI’s ACE Academy, a program that advances and cultivates emerging leaders.
Mr. Hatch specializes in managing and delivering transformation and performance improvement initiatives focused on technology. He also has extensive experience with system implementations, CIO advisory, IT strategy, software selection and technology project management.
With more than 30 years of consulting experience, Mr. Hatch has worked with clients in various industries, including upstream energy, oilfield services, retail, distribution, heavy manufacturing and services. He brings experience in information systems strategy, software selection, operations improvement, supply chain, human resources/payroll and systems implementations, including SAP, Oracle and Lawson.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Hatch was a Managing Director with BearingPoint, Inc., where he was responsible for one of the firm's regional ERP practices. Previously, he was a partner with Arthur Andersen, where he led and delivered technology projects.
Mr. Hatch earned a BBA from Ohio State University. A Certified Public Accountant in the state of Texas, he is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA). Mr. Hatch is also a Certified Project Management Professional.
