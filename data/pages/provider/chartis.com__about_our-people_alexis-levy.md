# Alexis Levy
Senior Partner, HealthScape Advisors 
Alexis Levy is a Senior Partner at HealthScape Advisors, a Chartis Company. Alexis has helped clients across the healthcare industry on a variety of strategic, operational, and financial issues. Her consulting experience has included long range strategic planning, operational due diligence, physician engagement and alignment strategies, corporate advisory services for mergers and acquisitions, and service line planning. 
Her particular expertise is on the relationship between payers and providers, and she has significant experience working with provider-sponsored health plans, value-based transformation, and Medicare Advantage strategy and operations. 
Alexis is the co-lead for HealthScape’s Medicare Advantage Center of Excellence and has led successful engagements focused on helping Medicare Advantage Organizations grow, develop external partnerships, improve Star Ratings, identify performance improvement opportunities, develop risk adjustment strategies, and improve compliance. 
Prior to joining HealthScape, Alexis spent six years with Navigant Consulting’s Provider Strategy consulting practice. She has also held roles in hospital administration and in clinical practice. 
Alexis earned her MBA from the Olin School of Business at Washington University in St. Louis and her bachelor’s degree in occupational therapy from Boston University.
