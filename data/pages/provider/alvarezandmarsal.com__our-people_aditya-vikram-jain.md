Aditya Jain is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Business Transformation Services in New Delhi. He has over 25 years of experience in building and scaling global businesses, with a strong focus on developing industry and functional capabilities that integrate analytics and technology to drive client impact.
Mr. Jain is known for combining strategic perspective with strong execution and for building high-performing teams and scalable platforms that deliver meaningful business outcomes.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Jain was with Boston Consulting Group (BCG), where he served as Executive Director and Global Head of Research. In this role, he led a global team of 500+ industry/functional experts and was instrumental in strengthening capabilities aligned with evolving client needs. He also set up and led BCG’s Global Capability Center in India, overseeing a 900+ member team across knowledge, analytics, and digital capabilities, with a strong focus on capability building, operational excellence, and innovation.
Previously, Mr. Jain was part of the founding team at Evalueserve, where he helped scale the firm into a global organization, working with Fortune 1,000 and other large multinational companies across industries.
Mr. Jain earned a bachelor’s degree in electronics and communications engineering and a master’s degree in business administration.
