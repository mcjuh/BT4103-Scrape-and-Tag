### Education
  * MBA with honors, Northwestern University, Kellogg School of Management
  * BA, magna cum laude, Economics, Yale University 


Alejandro Navarro is a core member of Boston Consulting Group's Consumer and Climate & Sustainability practices. He leads the climate and sustainability agenda for consumer products in the Europe, Middle East, South America, and Africa (EMESA) region, and leads the design to sustainable value work globally for the Consumer practice.
Alejandro has significant experience in consumer products broadly, and more specifically, in food and beverage and beauty and personal care. He works with clients on driving broad transformation programs, developing winning corporate and business unit strategies, and helping them define and achieve their ESG ambitions. He has particular expertise in reimagining consumer product portfolios to optimize for sustainability, cost, and consumer value.
Previously based in Chicago, Atlanta, and Mexico City, Alejandro serves global clients across regions and has led engagements in English, Spanish, Portuguese, and Dutch.
