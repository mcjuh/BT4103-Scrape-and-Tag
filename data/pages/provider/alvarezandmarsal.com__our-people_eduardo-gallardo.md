Eduardo Gallardo is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Financial Advisory and Special Situations in São Paulo. He specializes in financial advisory. His primary area of concentration is debt restructuring.
With more than 15 years of financial restructuring experience, Mr. Gallardo has led the largest financial restructurings in Brazil over the past decade, successfully restructuring more than BRL 100 billion in debt through both judicial and out-of-court processes.
Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Gallardo spent seven years with Siemens LTDA and Siemens AG in São Paulo (Brazil) and Nuremberg (Germany), where he most recently served as business controller.
Mr. Gallardo earned a BBA from FGV-EAESP and is fluent in Portuguese, English, and Spanish.
