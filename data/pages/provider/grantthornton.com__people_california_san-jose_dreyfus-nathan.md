## Executive summary 
Nathan Dreyfus is Grant Thornton's leader in Silicon Valley. Prior to this role, he served as a partner in the Risk Advisory Services practice. He specialized in contract and licensing compliance services, helping organizations implement, execute and monitor contract-based relationships. He has a wealth of experience in the technology industry, especially the hardware and software spaces. His clients include large electronic equipment and component suppliers, as well as many software companies with global operations.
In addition, Dreyfus is deeply familiar with internal audit, Sarbanes-Oxley, business process improvement, system implementation (including ERP systems), segregation of duties analysis, and IT general computer controls, procedures and policies.
