#  Gina Vargas 
##  Principal | Finance & Enterprise Performance | Financial Services 
Gina Vargas is a leader with Deloitte Consulting LLP. She has more than 15 years of experience helping CFOs and finance organizations become better business partners and realize value through transformation programs. She advises clients on business process modernization, operating model redesign, and data and technology solutions. She consults predominantly in financial services and specializes in the insurance industry. Vargas also has extensive experience advising senior finance executives on global finance transformation programs, business finance and analytics, operating model design and enablement, and expense management.
