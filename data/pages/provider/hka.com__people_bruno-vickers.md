### Expertise
Bruno Vickers is an experienced investigator with nearly 20 years of experience working across Asia‑Pacific and globally. He is regularly engaged on complex investigations in support of commercial disputes and contentious matters.
Bruno has supported proceedings before courts and arbitral tribunals in multiple jurisdictions, including international arbitration forums in Europe and Asia. His investigative reports are frequently relied upon by clients and their legal advisers in contested proceedings. Bruno’s work often involves identifying and developing evidence, locating witnesses, reviewing large volumes of documents and supporting case strategy from an early stage through to enforcement.
Bruno specialises in matters involving fraud, corruption, embezzlement, sanctions breaches and other misconduct, focusing on fact‑finding, evidence gathering and asset tracing. He has expertise in tracing and documenting complex, multi‑jurisdictional asset structures, beneficial ownership and cross‑border financial flows, to support recovery and enforcement strategies. Bruno works closely with legal counsel and also advises clients on risk, due diligence and strategic intelligence matters in connection with transactions, investments, restructuring and market exit decisions.
