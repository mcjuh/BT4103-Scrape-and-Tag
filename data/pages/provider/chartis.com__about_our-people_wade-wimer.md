# Wade Wimer
Engagement Manager 
Wade Wimer is an Engagement Manager with Chartis. He has spent five years as a management consultant at the firm, advising community and academic health systems on strategic planning and performance improvement. Wade has experience across several solution types, including multi-workstream project management, patient safety and healthcare quality, workforce productivity, enterprise strategy, patient access, and funds flow redesign. Prior to joining Chartis, Wade spent ten years in the healthcare industry. Some of this time he spent supporting marketing and medical affairs efforts in the pharmaceutical industry as an ethics and compliance officer. In this capacity, he worked with pharmaceutical and physician leaders to responsibly convey the benefits and drawbacks of products to prescribers. Before his pharmaceutical work, Wade was a lead scientist at a clinical laboratory where he contributed to the design of a novel method of heavy metal analysis now used to assess toxicology levels for private and public organizations across the world.
Wade received his MBA and Master of Business Analytics from the University of Minnesota’s Carlson School of Management. He received his Master of Science in Environmental Analytical Chemistry and his Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science from the University of Illinois.
