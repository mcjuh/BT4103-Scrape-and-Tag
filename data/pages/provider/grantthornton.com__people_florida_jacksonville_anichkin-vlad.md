## Executive summary
Vlad Anichkin is a Partner in Grant Thornton’s Advisory business, helping organizations harness the power of AI and digital transformation to achieve measurable growth, efficiency, and innovation.
He advises C-suite leaders on how to turn technology disruption into competitive advantage—accelerating business performance, modernizing operations, and creating value across the enterprise. With more than two decades of experience spanning Big Four consulting, private-equity-backed ventures, and global customer experience agencies, Vlad bridges strategy and execution to deliver lasting impact.
He has led large-scale transformation programs that combine data, intelligent automation, and human-centered design to reshape how companies work and grow. His clients include leading banks, technology firms, health systems, and consumer brands seeking to unlock speed, scalability, and sustained ROI from AI and digital investments.
Before joining Grant Thornton, Vlad held senior leadership roles at Ernst & Young, PwC Digital, and UnitedLex, where he built and scaled digital transformation initiatives across industries. He brings a unique blend of creative vision, operational discipline, and growth orientation that helps organizations thrive in an era of constant change.
