Johannes Distler joined Boston Consulting Group in 2023. He is a member of the Travel, Cities & Infrastructure practice, with significant industry experience in logistics, postal, and shipping and functional experience in strategy, performance improvement, and large-scale transformation projects. In addition, he has an extensive track record leading M&A and post-merger integration projects in the Middle East and globally.
Before joining BCG, Johannes was the Chief Strategy Officer of Aramex International, the largest logistics company in the Middle East, where he was responsible for strategy, M&A, and sustainability/ESG. Before Aramex, Johannes was a Partner at Roland Berger, leading their transportation and logistics work in the Middle East.
