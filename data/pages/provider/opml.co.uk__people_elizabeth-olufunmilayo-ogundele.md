Elizabeth Ogundele is a development professional with expertise in research, policy design, the new business development, communications, and programme implementation across health, education, and social development sectors.
She currently works as an Assistant Consultant within our Health Practice, where she supports research, evaluation, and technical assistance projects in low- and middle-income countries. Her work focuses on health systems strengthening, health financing, and public sector reform, with contributions to both technical delivery, project design, and business acquisition.
Elizabeth holds an MSc in Public Health (Health Management) from the University of Lagos and a BSc in Anatomy from the University of Ilorin.
