## Career Summary
[Curriculum Vitae](https://www.brookesbell.com/fileadmin/uploads/brookesbell/CVs/PAGE_CV.pdf)
Andrew is a Marine Engineer with a Class One Certificate of Competency (Motor) with seagoing experience complemented by management and survey roles. Andrew joined Brookes Bell in May 2017 as a marine consultant.
## Background
Andrew's surveying experience includes having attended various types and sizes of vessels, including Superyachts, and reporting on a variety of incidents including main and auxiliary machinery failure investigations, fire damages, crane damages, collision damages, redelivery condition surveys/disputes, repair cost disputes, bunker quality and related machinery failure disputes, personal injury claims, and MARPOL surveys/investigations. Andrew has also performed pre-purchase surveys for Owners and condition surveys on behalf of Underwriters and P&I Clubs. Andrew has given evidence in London arbitrations and in Singapore court.
Andrew's shore-based experience includes working as a Superintendent and latterly Fleet Manager for a large container shipping company, where activities included drydocking and repair projects and overseeing the day-to-day management of the vessels.
Prior to that, Andrew worked as a Marine Engineer on deep-sea container vessels where he gained first-hand experience of slow and medium-speed engines, as well as related auxiliary machinery, systems, and plant.
