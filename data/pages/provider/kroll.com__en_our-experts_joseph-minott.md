AT A GLANCE 
Joseph Minott is a Managing Director in the Tax Services practice, based in Chicago. He is responsible for management and execution of property tax consulting engagements nationwide for clients with multijurisdictional portfolios. 
Joseph brings more than 20 years of experience in property tax consulting, focusing on multitenant shopping centers, big-box retailers, gaming, hospitality and commercial airlines. His property tax consulting experience includes pre- and post-acquisition due diligence, real estate valuation, unitary valuation, appeal management, settlement negotiations, litigation support and forecast budgeting. Joseph has represented clients in valuation disputes throughout much of the Midwest, Northeast, and Southern United States.
Prior to joining Kroll, Joseph served as Property Tax Manager at Macy's, Inc. where he testified and represented the company at tax appeal hearings before state and local taxing authorities. His responsibilities included assessment analysis, property valuation, property tax appeal management, negotiations and litigation management. Joseph began his state and local tax career with Sears Holdings Corporation where he prepared sales and use tax returns and personal property renditions for various states.
Joseph holds a B.A. from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. His professional achievements in the property taxation and valuation fields include attaining the Certified Member of the Institute (CMI) designation with the Institute for Professionals in Taxation (IPT) and successfully completing numerous courses with the Appraisal Institute. Joseph is a registered agent with the State of Tennessee – State Board of Equalization and is a licensed senior property tax consultant in the state of Texas.
