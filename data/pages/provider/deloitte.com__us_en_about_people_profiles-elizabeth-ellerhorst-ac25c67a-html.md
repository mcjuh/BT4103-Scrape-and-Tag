#  Elizabeth Ellerhorst 
##  Senior Manager | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
Elizabeth is a senior manager in Deloitte’s Sports practice, where she sits at the intersection of sports and marketing strategy. She is also a leader in Deloitte’s women’s sports initiatives–most notably, the Athena Pledge.
Backed by more than 10 years of strategy consulting experience, Elizabeth has worked with leagues, Olympic properties, and National Governing Bodies to solve their most pressing business challenges. Her primary focuses are helping sports organizations translate fan insights into deeper engagement and sustainable revenue growth.
