### Education
  * MBA, Indian Institute of Management, Lucknow
  * MBA (Dual Degree), ESCP-EAP European School of Management, Paris
  * BA (Honors), economics, St. Xavier's College, Mumbai


Sushmita Banerjee leads Boston Consulting Group’s Corporate Finance & Strategy practice for North America. She also serves as a voting member of the US Retirement Benefits Committee (RBC), which oversees the firm’s retirement programs for its staff.
Sushmita works extensively with clients on strategy, growth, organization, large-scale transformation, operational improvement, total shareholder return, post-merger integration, cybersecurity and digital identity. She is also a core member of the Social Impact practice, partnering with clients to advance their mission-driven goals in a sustainable manner.
Throughout her career at BCG, she has held multiple leadership positions across several practices. These include serving as global strategy business leader and global marketing and communications leader for the Corporate Finance & Strategy practice; leading Diversity, Equity & Inclusion efforts; and various leadership positions within the Technology, Media & Telecommunications, People & Organization, and Transform practices. She was also instrumental in developing BCG’s B2B information services unit.
Prior to joining BCG, Sushmita worked at Unilever (India and Asia Pacific) and led successful assignments in sales, brand management, and customer development. She is passionate about the arts and is deeply involved with New York’s Metropolitan Museum, serving as an advisory member of the Finance Committee of its Board of Trustees. She also sits on the Board of Directors for JUST Capital.
