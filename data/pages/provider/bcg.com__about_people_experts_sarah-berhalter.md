### Education
  * MBA, Vanderbilt University, Owen Graduate School of Management
  * BA, Phi Beta Kappa and Beta Gamma Sigma, Washington and Lee University


Sarah Berhalter is a core member of Boston Consulting Group’s Industrial Goods (IG) practice and a leader for transactions and integrations, particularly divestitures and carve-outs, in North America. She also serves as the North America people lead.
Since joining BCG in 2015, Sarah has supported clients in multiple sectors, including automotive, energy, health care, and travel. She covers topics in end-to-end carve-out/spin-off transaction strategy and execution for both the buy and sell side parties. In addition, she has experience in large-scale operating model, digital and transformation efforts with particular expertise in people and engagement strategy and operational effectiveness.
Prior to joining BCG, Sarah held management positions within the equestrian industry, where she was recognized with the Zeppa international award for management in her field.
