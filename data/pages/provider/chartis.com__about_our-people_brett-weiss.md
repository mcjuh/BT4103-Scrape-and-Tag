# Brett Weiss
Senior Partner 
Brett Weiss is a Senior Partner in Digital and Technology Transformation and co-leads the Business Technology Innovation practice. He brings over 25 years of consulting, business development, healthcare, and ERP implementation experience to the firm, specializing in guiding healthcare providers through business and process transformation using modern enterprise software.
A frequent industry speaker, Brett offers a dynamic understanding of healthcare IT, trends, and technologies available to address the challenges of a volatile market. He has extensive experience analyzing current-state business processes and aligning clients' goals with technology solutions to support operational modernization. Brett has managed complex merger acquisition engagements and software deployments with stand-alone community-based hospitals and some of healthcare’s largest independent delivery networks.
Brett most recently served as Vice President of Nordic Global where he was responsible for leading the organization’s Advisory Services growth strategy. Before Nordic, Brett was a Principal and Senior Vice President at Avaap, a management consulting and ERP implementation services firm.
Brett holds a bachelor’s degree in international business from The University of Arizona.

###  Why Chartis? 
Joining a firm with a well-established culture where people are truly heard, seen, and respected was paramount for me. I firmly believe that a positive culture fosters happiness, and happiness, in turn, drives care and quality. Chartis embodies all of these values. 
###  What have you found to be the most rewarding part of your career? 
Even after more than 25 years in the healthcare industry, I continue to find immense personal and professional satisfaction in working with clients to develop and implement strategies and solutions that help them modernize and achieve their goals. 
###  What inspires you? 
My children truly inspire me most—to listen, to share, to show compassion and empathy, to be humble, to be proud, to laugh, to cry, to be of healthy mind and body and to remind me that life can be really serious, but adding some silly into the mix makes it a whole lot more fun!
