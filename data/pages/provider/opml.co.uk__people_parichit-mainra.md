Parichit is an experienced professional in governance, regulatory affairs, and policy advisory within the digital governance space. He has worked with the Government of India, the Confederation of Indian Industry, and various media organisations, contributing to policy planning and reform projects in South Asia. Parichit holds a degree in Information Science from M.S. Ramaiah Institute of Technology, a Postgraduation in Journalism from the Indian Institute of Mass Communication, and a certification in Public Policy and Management from the Indian Institute of Management, Calcutta.
