Faaiz Gilani is an urban economist specialising in climate adaptation, infrastructure development, and multi-stakeholder engagement, with expertise in econometric modelling, policy research, and community development. At OPM, his work focuses on major climate initiatives, including the Strengthening Climate Adaptation and Resilience (SAR) programme, Catalytic Fund for Water Resource Accountability in Pakistan (WRAP) and Australia-Pakistan Water Security Initiative (APWASI), delivering actionable insights for policy design. His portfolio includes research on women's workforce participation, urban inequality, and sustainable infrastructure development.
As an advocate for sustainable development, Faaiz regularly writes for leading prestigious newspapers, recommending urban solutions to dilemmas and discussing topics related to climate change. As co-founder of Hamsukhan, he leads initiatives fostering community dialogue on economic and social challenges through public space activation.
Faaiz holds an MSc in Urban Economic Development from University College London, where he developed models evaluating gender-inclusive urban transit interventions and infrastructure projects' socioeconomic impacts, and a BSc in Economics from Lahore University of Management Sciences.
