AT A GLANCE 
Jordan Peponis is Senior Director in Construction Expert Services practice, based in Atlanta.
Jordon has 13 years of experience in providing consultation on a variety of international and domestic projects, including airports, petrochemical, natural gas extraction (wells and pipelines) and LNG facilities, oil refineries, professional sports stadiums, water transportation construction, commercial, mixed-use, hospitality and highways.
While he has provided advisory services, his experience has primarily been in the role of an independent delay expert. This includes preparation of critical path delay analyses, loss of productivity analyses, preparation of expert reports as well as evaluation of other experts’ opinions. He has given expert testimony in AAA arbitration and provided expert evidence in mediation.
Prior to joining Kroll, Jordan was Director with a global consulting firm for 12 years. Before that, he worked with a Southeastern US-based general contractor.
