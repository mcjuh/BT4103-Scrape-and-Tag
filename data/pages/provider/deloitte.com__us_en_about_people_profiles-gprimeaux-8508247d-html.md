#  Gina Primeaux 
##  Principal | US Regulatory, Risk & Forensics Leader 
[ Costa Mesa, US ](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/offices/office-locator/costa-mesa.html)
Gina is the US Regulatory, Risk & Forensics Leader. In this role, she and her team advise clients across industries to help them bolster and protect their business strategy, investments and innovations. In addition, Gina serves as the Global Regulatory, Risk & Forensics Leader. Gina believes that an organization’s long-term growth is directly tied to its risk, regulatory, and compliance operationalization and management.
Gina is a principal in Deloitte & Touche LLP. She is a leader with more than 20 years of experience and actively works to help organizations to transform their work, workforce, and workplace due to increased convergence of industries, innovation, and disruption. She advises organizations, their boards, and C-suite leaders on issues related to strategic trends, governance, financial services reform, performance improvement, forensics and investigations, information technology, and enterprise risk management.
Previously, Gina served on the US Board Council. Her other prior leadership roles include US Fintech Industry leader, US Regulatory & Legal Transformation leader and US Ecosystems & Alliances leader. Before Deloitte, Gina worked for the US Department of Treasury’s Office of Thrift Supervision.
Gina received a BA in business economics with a finance emphasis from the University of California, Riverside. She is a certified fraud examiner, certified internal auditor, and member of the Association of Certified Fraud Examiners and Institute of Internal Auditors.
