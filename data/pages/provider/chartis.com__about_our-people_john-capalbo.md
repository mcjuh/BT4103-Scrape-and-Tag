# John Capalbo
Partner 
John Capalbo is a Partner in Strategic Transformation. He brings over 18 years of experience advising academic and community health systems, provider organizations, provider-sponsored health plans, and cancer center clients. Passionate about building integrated teams and driving meaningful change, John helps clinical and enterprise leaders shape a future that finds strength in the pivotal strategic challenges that they face. His expertise spans enterprise strategy, partnership formation and integration planning, service line growth, research and academic alignment, and footprint optimization.
Before joining Chartis, John advised healthcare leaders as a Director in PwC-Strategy&’s provider strategy practice. Prior to that, he was an Associate Director at NYU’s College of Nursing, where he designed and led multiple grant-funded projects focused on improving care for older adults through academic trainings and community outreach programs.
John has authored multiple perspectives on trends in academic health, higher education, and clinical trials, and has been featured on the Next in Health podcast. He has served as a preceptor in the Master of Health Administration program at Johns Hopkins University and has volunteered strategic planning support for nonprofit organizations focused on both educational equity and Ukrainian causes.
John received his Master of Business Administration from the Stern School of Business at NYU, where he received the Dean’s Award for Outstanding Scholarship. He earned his Bachelor of Arts from the University of Pennsylvania, where he studied English and Communications.

###  Why healthcare? 
I come from a family of teachers, and the idea of dedicating your career to lifting up others—to making life better for as many people as you can—has been an undercurrent to my thinking for as long as I can remember. I didn't always know what I wanted to be "when I grew up," but everything I explored—whether that be government or law, academics, nonprofits, or artistic pursuits—built from that perspective. Healthcare is a natural extension. There aren't many other industries where you can be sure that if you dedicate your time, energy, and passion in the right way, you can markedly improve people's lives. Healthcare consulting opens the aperture even more. It's the patients we can help through our work, of course, but it's also the caregivers, the staff and administrators, the families, and the communities we can impact, as well. That's the bad and the good of it: there are so many challenges that need to be addressed, but on the flip side, so much opportunity to invoke positive change. 
###  What is something most people would be surprised to learn about you? 
I was an actor for about seven years out of college and first moved to New York to dedicate myself to that career. It was a wild ride for the time I was on it, and it really helped me evolve from an introverted, somewhat cautious kid into someone who's willing to take risks and venture into the unknown. It's funny how much I find myself still drawing on those experiences as a consultant—from realizing that my night job as a document production worker set me up for all those PowerPoints I've needed to churn out over the years, to just being willing to trust that the hours and hours of prep that you've done when working on a project (or a play) will be there when you need to open your mouth and speak. It also taught me a lot about being on a professional journey—namely that it's ok to not always know where you're going...as long as you're staying true to yourself, you'll end up where you need to be. 
###  What is one thing you can't live without? 
My espresso machine. My wife and I got really into lattes a number of years back and quickly started spending way too much to feed the addiction, so we decided to purchase a nice machine we could use at home. It's not only paid itself back 1,000x over, it's also given the two of us a daily ritual that helps us ensure we start every morning (or as many as we can) by taking a moment to connect, enjoy a warm comfort, and fuel up for the day ahead.
