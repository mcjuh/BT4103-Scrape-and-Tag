## Bryan Byrd
#### Managing Director
Mr. Byrd has more than 25 years of project and construction management experience, specializing in construction practices, critical path delay, acceleration, schedule compression, disruption, construction costs and damages.
Houston
bbyrd@secretariat-intl.com
Bryan Byrd has more than 25 years of project and construction management experience. Mr. Byrd provides construction management services and performs cost and schedule analyses on domestic and international projects, including engineering, procurement, construction, design-bid-build, EPC/EPIC, and design-build work scopes. Mr. Byrd is knowledgeable of common practices in the construction industry such as estimating, contracting, risk analysis, project management, coordination, change management, project administration, safety, schedule preparation and analysis, project controls, cost control, completion, and project close-out.
Mr. Byrd’s experience includes numerous domestic and international projects with work scopes ranging from several million USD to more than $40 billion USD. Mr. Byrd has successfully assisted clients with the resolution of several hundred complex construction and engineering disputes in more than 30 countries around the world.
Mr. Byrd has served as a construction management, scheduling, and construction cost expert and testified on project and construction management issues as well as construction practices, critical path delay, acceleration, schedule compression, disruption, construction costs and damages on domestic matters in arbitration and State and Federal courts as well as international venues.
A highly regarded expert in  _Lexology Index,_ Mr. Byrd is commended by clients and peers for his “extensive expertise, [which] encompasses critical disciplines such as risk analysis, project administration and change management, making him a valuable asset in navigating complex construction disputes globally.”
