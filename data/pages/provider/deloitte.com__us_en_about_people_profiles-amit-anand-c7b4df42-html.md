#  Amit Anand 
##  US Financial Services Industry Technology Leader | Principal | Deloitte Consulting LLP 
[ Morristown, US ](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/offices/office-locator/morristown.html)
###  Amit is the technology growth leader in the Deloitte Financial Services industry group and is a Principal with Deloitte Consulting LLP. 
With more than 25 years of experience, including working with several Fortune 500 banks and fintechs, Amit serves as the technology growth leader in Deloitte's Financial Services industry group. In this role he helps C-suite executives and business leaders in banking, investment management, insurance, and commercial real estate navigate market disruption, drive growth, and transform their organizations through digital and technology innovation.
A principal in Deloitte Consulting LLP, Amit assists clients with defining and executing digital strategies, developing new technology-led business models, and implementing large-scale transformation programs that enhance customer experience, optimize operations, and improve profitability. He is known for collaborating across both digital and operational teams, and Amit's approach is rooted in strategically unlocking the full potential of technology to achieve meaningful business outcomes.
Amit’s areas of expertise span digital banking, core banking transformation, financial crime and risk management (including anti-money laundering), and technology strategy. He has deep experience in banking platform modernization, open banking strategies, and fintech innovation. He is passionate about helping financial institutions stay competitive in a rapidly evolving landscape by leveraging technology to drive business value and operational excellence.
