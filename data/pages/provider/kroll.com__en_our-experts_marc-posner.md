AT A GLANCE 
Marc Posner is a Managing Director in Kroll’s Government Solutions practice, based out of New York. 
Marc is an attorney with 25 years of experience in a range of legal areas. He joined the firm through the [acquisition](https://www.kroll.com/en/newsroom/kroll-acquires-verus-analytics) of Verus Analytics by Kroll in July 2020. He has been practicing in the field of unclaimed property for over 10 years. Marc is the head of audit strategy for Kroll.
After a clerkship in the Federal District Court in Manhattan, Marc joined the litigation department of Davis Polk & Wardwell. In this role, he focused on complex corporate and securities litigation. 
His background also includes extensive corporate transactional experience as Of Counsel to Stratos Global, an international telecommunications provider.
Marc holds a B.A. from the University of Pennsylvania and a J.D. (magna cum laude) from the University of Pennsylvania Law School.
