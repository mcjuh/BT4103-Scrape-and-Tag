Tina Gao is Boston Consulting Group's Washington, DC office lead, and the current co-lead of the North America Asian Diversity Network (ADN). At the Washington level, she has held leadership roles, including CDC chair, Women@BCG lead, and recruiting director. She is North America Consumer & Business Services lead, and has helped to stand up and accelerate the firm's work with B2B service businesses in the Consumer practice.
Tina has expertise in wholesale distribution within multiple industries, including food service management, hospitality services, facility services, and landscape services. She has worked with public corporations, private equity owned companies, and family owned entities. She also has deep experience in large scale change—in particular, operating model and organizational design transformations, post-merger integration, direct and indirect cost optimization, merchandising transformations, and activist change management.
