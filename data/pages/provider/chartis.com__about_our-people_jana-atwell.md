# Jana Atwell
Partner, Business Management, Jarrard 
Jana Atwell is a Partner at Jarrard, a Chartis Company, and oversees business management operations at the firm.
With more than 20 years of experience – including 12 at Jarrard Inc. – Jana is primarily responsible for accounts payable, accounts receivable and human resources functions. She also focuses on legal, vendor management, financial management forecasting and projections, building and grounds, and day-to-day business management.
Prior to joining Jarrard, Jana served as Senior Operations Manager at The Buntin Group, where she was instrumental in developing and implementing streamlined office systems, policies, and procedures, including the creation and execution of a new client contract system. Before that, Jana was the full charge bookkeeper and office manager at ECO Manufacturing Corporation.
Jana studied at Aquinas College in Nashville, and she is a member of the Society for Human Resource Management.
