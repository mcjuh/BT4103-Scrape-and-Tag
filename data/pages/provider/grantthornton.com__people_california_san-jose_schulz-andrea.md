## Executive summary 
Andrea Schulz is the Head of the Technology industry and an Audit Partner based in the San Jose office. In her leadership role, she oversees the growth and operations of the firm’s Technology industry practice, which encompasses a full range of audit, tax and advisory services.
Located in San Jose, Andrea has nearly 20 years of experience working with businesses ranging from privately-held emerging growth companies to publicly traded global companies in the technology industry, focusing on the SaaS industry. She has led audits of companies experiencing rapid transformational periods due to extreme growth and IPOs. Andrea proactively teams with management teams to ensure companies attain their desired milestones. She has broad experience with the regulatory requirements of becoming public, business combinations, revenue recognition, as well as internal control matters on integrated audits.
Andrea embraces and advocates for the adoption of technology in the auditing and accounting profession to provide management with deeper insights into the business. She is a frequent speaker at professional conferences and academic events on innovating the future of audit and accounting through artificial intelligence, machine learning, and natural language processing.
