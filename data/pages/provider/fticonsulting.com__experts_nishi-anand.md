#  Nishi Anand 
##  Senior Managing Director 
## About Nishi
Nishi Anand specializes in executing revenue and operational transformations and brings extensive experience in value creation and due diligence, particularly in the healthcare and technology sectors. Ms. Anand has more than 10 years of experience driving improvements across the entire customer lifecycle — from go-to-market strategy to lead generation to sales to invoicing — to drive sustained, profitable growth.
Ms. Anand has advised C-suite, investors and boards of directors and is skilled in defining and leading growth strategy, performance management and operations efficiency initiatives for large-scale organizations. In addition, she has effectively managed various company transformations and acquisitions as an advisor and operator.
Prior to her role at FTI Consulting, Ms. Anand was a Principal at KKR Capstone, where she drove value creation for healthcare and technology portfolio companies. In this position, she led cross-functional transformational initiatives to drive topline and margin with immediate impact. Preceding KKR Capstone, she worked at BCG, where she supported healthcare and tech clients on various business strategy, revenue growth and enterprise transformation initiatives.
Relevant Experience:
  * Launched outbound sales and improved sales and marketing effectiveness for a healthcare technology business, doubling bookings within six months
  * Optimized inside sales capabilities for a B2B managed services business, doubling growth
  * Developed channel sales and marketing strategy for a software platform
  * Drove commercial excellence for a national behavioral health treatment provider **—** from patient acquisition channel strategy to changes in sales and marketing organization, processes and tools **—** reducing 30% costs and improving referral conversion
  * Ran the integration management office (“IMO”) for a national autism care provider, identifying and delivering more than $15 million in synergies
  * Supported multiyear operating model transformation for a pharma company
