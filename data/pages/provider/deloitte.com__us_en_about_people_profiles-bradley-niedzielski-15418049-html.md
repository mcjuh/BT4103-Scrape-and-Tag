#  Bradley Niedzielski 
##  Audit & Assurance Partner, Business Controls Leader | Deloitte & Touche LLP 
###  Brad is an Audit & Assurance (A&A) partner with more than 20 years of experience serving complex global financial services clients across public and private sectors. And is the National A&A Business Controls Advisory Leader 
Brad is an Audit & Assurance (A&A) partner with more than 20 years of experience serving complex global financial services clients across public and private sectors. As Deloitte’s National A&A Business Controls Advisory Leader, Brad advises clients on how to modernize controls using AI-enabled tools, providing risk coverage over emerging technologies like AI, ML, RPA, ETL, GenAI, and advanced analytics. He specializes in SOX program design, internal controls gap analysis, M&A advisory, and business controls for system migrations. Brad previously led the A&A Clients & Markets GenAI initiative and the A&A East Region finance transformation practice. A CPA in the state of New York, he is a member of the AICPA and NYSSCPA, and formerly served on the Manhattan NYSSCPA board.
