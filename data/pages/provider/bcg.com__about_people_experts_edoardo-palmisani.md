### Education
  * MBA, with Highest Honors, University of Chicago Booth School of Business 
  * Degree Magna Cum Laude, financial markets, LUISS University


Edoardo Palmisani is Boston Consulting Group's global lead for CFO excellence, as well as head of Corporate Finance for the EMESA region (Europe, the Middle East, South America and Africa), and head of the firm's Corporate Finance & Strategy practice for the East Mediterranean and Caspian.
Edoardo focuses on engagements with financial services, private equity, and sovereign wealth fund clients. He leads the firm's relationship with several large clients in EMESA across different topics and domains.
He works across clients on a range of CFO topics, including tech-enabled finance transformation, investments, corporate strategy, shareholder value creation, capital management, M&A, partnerships and post-merger integration. He also has extensive experience with fund strategy and asset and wealth management topics.
Before joining BCG, Edoardo worked in London for a leading investment bank and in the private equity sector.
