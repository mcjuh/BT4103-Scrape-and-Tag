## Executive summary
Amy Roberts is a tax partner in the Minneapolis office. She has over 30 years of public accounting and tax experience. Amy works closely with privately held businesses and their owners to help them achieve their goals.
Amy has an expansive background of professional tax experience working with closely held companies providing a broad range of services including compliance, consulting and tax planning for both business and individual tax issues. She has extensive experience working with her clients on tax structuring, acquisition and divestitures of entities, as well as federal, state and local and international tax consulting in order to minimize tax and maximize cash flow. 
Amy has a diverse practice that includes clients in the manufacturing and distribution industry as well as technology, retail, life sciences and hospitality.
