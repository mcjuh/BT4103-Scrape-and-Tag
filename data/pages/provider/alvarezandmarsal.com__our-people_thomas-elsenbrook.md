Tom Elsenbrook is a Managing Director with Alvarez & Marsal Performance Improvement in Houston. He oversees A&M’s leadership efforts in enterprise-wide performance improvement across corporate, industry, and public sector organizations in the US.
Mr. Elsenbrook works closely with senior executives of Global 1,000 companies and government leaders to accelerate performance and deliver measurable top- and bottom-line results. In his public sector work, he uses his business sector experience to help government organizations execute more effectively and deliver improved outcomes. 
Mr. Elsenbrook has helped shape A&M’s differentiated model, which combines world-class consulting talent with seasoned operators and restructuring expertise. Central to this model is an integrated, execution-oriented approach that enables clients to address complex challenges and implement change at scale, with a strong emphasis on speed, accountability, and results. 
Mr. Elsenbrook works closely with senior executives to execute enterprise and business unit strategies. His primary areas of focus include driving profitable growth, improving business process performance, and achieving sustainable cost reductions. With more than 40 years of experience, he has advised Global 1,000 companies across multiple industries, including energy, communications, high-tech, commercial services, and construction. 
Mr. Elsenbrook partners with leadership teams to align organizational structures. This includes post-merger integration and the development and execution of growth strategies through product expansion and entry into new markets. He also helps clients strengthen competitive positioning through enhanced collaboration with customers and suppliers. 
Prior to joining A&M more than 20 years ago, Mr. Elsenbrook served at Arthur Andersen as Partner In Charge of the Business Consulting Division for the West Region of the US. He also led Arthur Andersen’s North American Energy Consulting Practice. 
Mr. Elsenbrook earned his BBA (accounting) from Texas Tech University.
