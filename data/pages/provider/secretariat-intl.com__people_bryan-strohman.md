Burlington
[+1 603 738 9706](tel:+1%20603%20738%209706) bstrohman@secretariat-intl.com
Bryan Strohman is a seasoned civil engineer with more than 20 years of experience in the industry. He specializes in overcoming complex geotechnical, civil, and structural engineering challenges for critical infrastructure and heavy civil projects. His experience includes investigating, designing, and rehabilitating a variety of structures—including mechanically stabilized earth walls, slopes, foundations, piles, excavation support systems, retaining walls, drainage and stormwater infrastructure, bridges, nuclear facilities, and specialized structures such as ground-supported solar farms, cold storage facilities, and athletic fields. 
Alongside his work, Mr. Strohman’s background includes numerous publications along with many presentations and research projects in his field. His expert opinions have proven to be extremely insightful in resolving a variety of disputes through depositions, mediations, arbitrations, and trials. 
Mr. Strohman is an active participant in the American Society of Civil Engineers’ (ASCE) Embankments, Dams, and Slopes (EDS) Committee, Deep Foundations Institute’s Codes and Standards Committee, and ASTM International’s Committees for Soil and Rock (D18) and Geosynthetics (D35). He is also a Board-Certified Geotechnical Engineer (BC.GE) through ASCE’s Academy of Geo-Professionals, the highest post-license certification in the discipline, showing his dedication to fostering innovation and excellence in geotechnical engineering.
