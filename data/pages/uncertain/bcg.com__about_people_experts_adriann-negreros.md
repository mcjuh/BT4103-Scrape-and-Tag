Adriann Negreros is a core leader in Boston Consulting Group's People & Organization practice, with a focus on the travel and tourism, industrial goods, and consumer products industries. He works extensively with CEOs, operations leaders, HR executives, and frontline supervisors, and has led projects on organizational design, redefining operating models, crafting compelling employee value propositions, and leading complex change management initiatives.
Adriann leads client work, partnerships, and research on frontline labor, directly developing BCG's intellectual property and serving as the lead author of the "State of Frontline Labor" newsletter. His insights reached a global audience in November 2023 when he delivered a TED Talk in Paris addressing innovative strategies to enhance the frontline worker experience.
He previously served as an Ambassador at the BCG Henderson Institute (BHI), collaborating closely with managing director and partner Julia Dhar to pioneer a science-based framework for human-centric organizational change. He serves as a key leader in BCG's recruiting efforts for both associate and consultant positions, leading a dedicated team of specialists across all US offices to drive more inclusive recruiting strategies.
