# Global Pharmaceutical Company Restates 15 Distribution Agreements Buried in Years of Amendments
[ Learn How ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/resources/case-studies/15-distribution-agreements-buried-in-years-of-amendments-restated#anchor) [ Discover Legora + Axiom ](https://www.axiomlaw.com/solutions/tech-plus-talent/legora)
### Axiom attorneys helped:
Consolidate 40+ years of amendments across 15 territories into newly restated agreements
Save 80–90% of review time* — about 100 hours versus 5–10x longer manually 
Deliver a full change log of every amendment, ready for the client and counterparty to verify
### Challenge
A global pharmaceutical company needed to consolidate and **restate a portfolio of complex global distribution and promotion agreements**. Each country file ran several hundred pages, one nearly 1,500, with **+ amendments** , side letters, and special terms layered on the original. The team needed to know the **current contractual position** , how key provisions had changed, and how each agreement differed from what was originally signed.
### Approach
Axiom paired a **senior commercial contracting lawyer** with Legora, on a strategy covering **15 territories** across Africa, the Caribbean, and Latin America. The lawyer was chosen for international experience and prior legal-AI work. Legora **identified the specific underlying provisions and changes** supporting each response, so the attorney reviewed the Legora analysis to confirm its generated conclusions. The analysis phase came in **under budget**.
### Impact 
  * **New amended and restated agreements** delivered for all 15 territories, reflecting the current business relationship.
  * A **punch list of every agreed change** , traced back through each amendment, giving the client and distributor a record to manage the relationship against.
  * Change tables the client shares with a counterparty to verify, enabling **business management, contract remediation and future commercial work**.
