Corporate 
  * Review, consolidate, and matrix side letter obligations and communicate to business stakeholders.
  * Monitor compliance with side letter obligations and remediate as required.
  * Develop risk and compliance framework and reporting processes.
  * Provide a solution that harmonizes local and global compliance policies.
  * Support investor onboarding, inquiry response, and other Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) activities.
  * Prepare and review documents (PPMs, sub docs, offering documents, online subscription booklet for investors, etc.).
  * Act as escalation points for diligence and rolling reviews.
  * Review and draft documentation relating to suspicious activity report (SAR) investigations.


Deal Work 
  * Structure and negotiate terms of Non-Disclosure Agreements
  * Create negotiation playbooks to industrialize workflows and roadmap contractual obligations to the business.

  * Assess all regulatory obligations and required licenses.

## What can private equity lawyers help funds and portfolio companies handle?
Private equity lawyers can help funds and portfolio companies with diligence, transaction execution, investment documentation, investor onboarding, side letter tracking, portfolio company contracts, compliance frameworks, governance, financing support, and post-close operational legal work.
## How can private equity lawyers support due diligence and deal execution?
Private equity lawyers can [support M&A due diligence](https://www.axiomlaw.com/practice-areas/mergers-acquisitions) and deal execution by reviewing target company contracts, identifying key obligations, cataloging liabilities, preparing diligence summaries, supporting NDAs and engagement letters, coordinating issue escalation, and helping business teams understand legal risk before signing or closing.
## What legal support do portfolio companies need after acquisition?
Portfolio companies often need post-acquisition legal support for contract cleanup, customer and vendor agreement updates, compliance policies, governance workflows, employment matters, privacy issues, integration work, and repeatable commercial processes that help the company scale.
## How can private equity lawyers help with side letters, investor onboarding, and AML/KYC?
Private equity lawyers can help organize and track side letter obligations, support investor onboarding, review subscription materials, coordinate AML and KYC workflows, prepare escalation materials, and help legal and compliance teams maintain consistent records across funds and investors.
## When should private equity firms use flexible legal talent?
Private equity firms often use flexible legal talent during active deal periods, fund launches, investor onboarding surges, diligence backlogs, portfolio company clean-up, compliance remediation, or temporary internal team gaps. Flexible support can help legal teams scale without overbuilding permanent headcount.
## How can private equity legal teams scale contract review across portfolio companies?
Private equity legal teams can scale contract review by prioritizing high-risk agreements, using playbooks, cataloging key terms, standardizing templates, routing repeatable work to the right legal talent, and using defined workflows for commercial, procurement, customer, and vendor agreements across portfolio companies.
