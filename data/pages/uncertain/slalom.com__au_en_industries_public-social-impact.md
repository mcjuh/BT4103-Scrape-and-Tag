## Our mission
At Slalom public and social impact, we amplify the missions of our government, education, nonprofit, and philanthropy clients through innovative solutions, driving positive systemic change in the public and private sectors. 
As residents, neighbours, and end users of community systems, we have a vested interest in making every project a success. Accountable to the communities we serve, Slalom brings more than solutions: we bring a commitment to making a difference, with passion and relentless drive to create a better future. 
As a catalyst, we drive systemic impact through bridging technical silos across jurisdictional and geographic regions enhancing cooperation, interoperability, agility, transparency, and accountability among all stakeholders and the communities we serve. 
Through our insights, resources, and collective expertise, Slalom public & social impact empowers our customers to sustain and scale their impact, building better tomorrows for all.

## Our public and social impact _expertise_
#### Education 
Slalom helps our education customers meet the challenges of today and establish a strong foundation for tomorrow. We partner with colleges and universities, school districts, and other organisations that develop knowledge and deliver learning, bringing our expertise to improve the experiences of their students, teachers, and leaders.
#### Federal government 
Decades of consulting have taught us that people and processes are just as important as the right technology. We combine commercial best practices with federal expertise and collaborate with you to customise solutions that steward taxpayer dollars responsibly while delivering results.
#### Nonprofit & philanthropy 
Our nonprofit practitioners and field experts are passionate about positively impacting the communities where we live, work, and play. Nonprofit organisations across the globe partner with us to adapt to and prepare for trends shaped by evolving digital technologies, dynamic stakeholder preferences, and community needs. 
#### [State & local government ](https://www.slalom.com/us/en/industries/public-social-impact/federal?=2024-financial-services-outlook)
Government plays a key role in solving the urgent challenges of our time, from digital equity and police transparency to the overhaul of legacy health and human service, transportation, and employment systems that serve millions of residents. We help you reach solutions others will not and then deliver at scale.
